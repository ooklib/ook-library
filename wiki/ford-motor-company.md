Ford is an American automaker. The video alleges its German and French subsidiaries produced military vehicles for the Nazi regime and that founder Henry Ford was a known anti-semite honored by Hitler's Germany.

## Claims

- Subsidiaries Ford-Werke and Ford SAF produced military vehicles and equipment for the Nazi regime, with Ford-Werke using over 12,000 forced laborers by 1944. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 17:47-17:58)*
- Henry Ford was a known anti-semite, author of The International Jew, praised by Hitler in Mein Kampf and awarded the Grand Cross of the German Eagle in 1938; he allegedly had little direct involvement by the war's later years. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 18:02-18:15)*
- In 2000 Ford-Werke paid up to $26 million into Holocaust labor victim funds. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 18:24)*
