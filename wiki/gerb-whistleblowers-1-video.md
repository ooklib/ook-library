A video (about 29 minutes, dated 2024-05-05, [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EKEZ2BClIb0)) that opens [[uap-gerb]]'s series on UFO whistleblowers, reviewing the claims and credibility of each. Chapters: Intro, 1st Lt. Walter Haut, Stephen Lovekin, Conclusion. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 0:00-1:45)*

Gerb sorts whistleblowers into three tiers: ironclad reputations (David Grusch, David Fravor), those bound by sworn legal testimony (Robert Salas, Robert Jacobs, Michael Herrera), and those with intriguing but unsworn, unverifiable claims (Bob Lazar, Mark McCandlish, Bill Uhouse, Boyd Bushman). *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 0:00-1:08)* He promises later volumes on crash-retrieval, reverse-engineering, sighting and radar whistleblowers. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 29:00-29:13)*

This volume covers [[walter-haut]] (Roswell press officer) and [[stephen-lovekin]] (White House Army Signal Agency technician). Gerb states his personal belief that a non-human craft crashed at Roswell in 1947 and that it was the nexus of reverse-engineering programs, possibly [[magenta-italy-crash-1933]]-style earlier efforts being less compartmentalized. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 26:57-27:21)*
