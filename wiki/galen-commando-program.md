A program in which [[reinhard-gehlen]]'s [[gehlen-organization]] ("the Org", ASR "Galenorg"; Gehlen rendered "Galen" throughout the ASR), paid for by the CIA under [[frank-wisner]] and [[allen-dulles]], recruited East European collaborationist refugees in West Germany and trained them as paratrooper "special forces" to be dropped behind the Iron Curtain. [[eyes-wide-open]] (`ewa/shackley`) presents this as a side-story to [[ted-shackley]]'s career and floats, explicitly as speculation, that the early US Army Special Forces (Green Berets) descend from it and that Shackley and [[lucien-conein]] at the CIA's Nuremberg base had a role. Sources the host leans on: E.H. Cookridge's *Gehlen: Spy of the Century*, John Loftus's *The Belarus Secret*, Christopher Simpson's *Blowback* and *Inside the Green Berets*.

## Origins (host's account)

- The [[office-of-policy-coordination]] (reporting to State, the Army and the CIA) and the CIA's Office of Special Operations were merged into the Directorate of Plans under Frank Wisner in 1951; Dulles moved to deputy director and became director by February 1953. Under the Dulles-Wisner axis the CIA and Pentagon "really start to actively collaborate" with Gehlen from 1951. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 41:42-42:54)*
- Background per the host: Gehlen was allegedly given immunity by US Army/OSS officials including Dulles late in the war in exchange for his Soviet-secrets microfilms and agent network; he set up in Pullach (ASR "Pulak") near Munich in 1946. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 43:01-43:47)*
- Cookridge, quoted: Dulles told Gehlen that the "top priority must be given to the selection and training of agents for work in Russia"; the Dulles brothers "believed deeply that Stalin's captive nations would rise" and a "private mercenary force was to be established in Germany" for use "without involving regular US forces." *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 44:51-45:05, 46:31-46:52)*
- Host's reading: with a Soviet invasion looking unlikely by 1951, the leadership redirected the Org from defensive stay-behind ([[operation-gladio]]-type) work to offensive strike forces for a possible coup behind the Iron Curtain. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 45:28-46:31)*
- Host notes this reportedly is one of the first uses of the term "special forces" by the US. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 47:15-47:23)*

## The "triumvirate" of recruiters

All three are described (per Cookridge) as university professors turned intelligence men with double lives, tasked with recruiting from East European collaborators among the refugees in West Germany. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 47:50-48:25)*

- [[franz-six]]: Belarusian, Russian and Ukrainian elements; joined mid-1952 after release from prison. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 56:16-56:24, 59:20-59:33)*
- Dr. Michael Akhmeteli (ASR "Akmatelli"; spelling variant by my inference from the Georgian name): Georgian scholar, personal friend of the Gehlen family; his father allegedly struck oil in the Caucasus with British and American mining engineers "including future President Herbert Hoover" (host: "strange fact"); fought with the White Army, became a Slavic-studies lecturer in Germany, was linked to the Abwehr, was a colleague of Nazi race theorist Alfred Rosenberg and served as an "analyst" for Gehlen's Eastern intelligence; covered the Caucasus. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 53:21-54:47, 56:24)*
- Dr. Emil Augsburg (ASR "Agsburg"/"Emile Agsburg"): Polish-born Soviet expert, SD member and SS security police official in occupied Poland; per Cookridge he escaped via the Odessa network, found refuge in Italy under Vatican cover, and returned to Germany under an alias, never tried; covered Poland. Per official US government investigations he had worked with the Army [[counter-intelligence-corps]] in 1947-48 "as an expert on Soviet affairs" (host's link to Shackley below). *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 55:05-56:24, 56:30-56:54)*

## Training and infiltration

- Aim: parachute former collaborationists into Eastern bloc countries to contact anti-communist undergrounds and foment revolts, with paramilitary and political training combined; Luftwaffe special-operations ace Edmund Gartenfeld (ASR "Gartenfield") was hired as parachute trainer. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 59:48-1:00:28)*
- Cookridge and Loftus: drops flew from US airstrips in "Vbaden" (ASR; place name unresolved) and from an airstrip in newly NATO Greece. Loftus: recruits were trained first at Oberammergau (see [[counter-intelligence-corps]]) and then at former Wehrmacht/SS facilities at Bad Tolz (ASR "Bad Tols"). *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:00:36-1:01:26)*
- Cookridge claims the most promising commandos were sent for paratrooper training at Fort Bragg from as early as 1951. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:01:26-1:01:48)*
- Loftus (per host): the most significant contingent recruited via Six was an especially brutal division of Russian commandos (ASR "yellow Russian", unclear), many of whom later ended up in the US under assumed names. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 52:46-53:12)*
- Result per Cookridge: largely ineffective; most were caught while parachuting in or infiltrating. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:05:18-1:05:34)*

## Link to the US Army Special Forces (host's argument)

- The Lodge-Philbin Act (ASR "Lodge filin", June 1950) let the Army recruit foreign nationals; some Gehlen commandos allegedly trained at Fort Bragg in 1951 just as the 10th Special Forces Group was being formed; the 10th moved to Bad Tolz in June 1953, where the Org's commandos had trained, "and from there, the divide ... blurred increasingly until all that was left were the now ubiquitous Green Berets." Host: the Army Special Forces "trace their origins back to Galen's commandos, not the other way around." Framed as host's inference from Cookridge/Simpson. See [[us-army-special-forces]], [[lauri-torni]]. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:03:00-1:03:25, 1:03:25-1:05:18, 1:05:34-1:05:57)*
- Flagged tension: the host's own dates put the first Green Berets at Fort Bragg no earlier than summer 1952 while Cookridge's Fort Bragg training of Org commandos starts in 1951; the direction of influence is an interpretation, not something either source states. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:01:41-1:02:11)*

## Host's speculation about Shackley and Conein

Host argues (explicitly speculation, marked by his green eye icon; "this is all speculation on my part") that Conein's odd assignment as a paramilitary expert to the CIA Nuremberg base 1952-55, about a 2-hour drive from Munich, and [[ted-shackley]]'s Polish-asset recruiting there, may have served the Wisner-Gehlen commando project; and that a possible role explains Shackley's later closeness to the Green Berets. He concedes they may simply have run a sleepy base and failed at foot infiltration. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 41:33-41:42, 57:09-57:31, 59:00-59:20, 1:07:12-1:07:42, 1:11:45-1:12:41)*
