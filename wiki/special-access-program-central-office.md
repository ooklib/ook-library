The DoD Special Access Program Central Office (SAPCO), a distinct body from the
[[special-access-program-oversight-committee|Special Access Program Oversight Committee
(SAPOC)]], is the OSD-level office overseeing DoD special access programs. Gerb argues its
director's clearance/authority is high but still likely subordinate to the director of the
[[national-program-special-management-staff|National Program Special Management Staff (NPMS)]}
under [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-and-security|USD I&S]].
*([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:07:06–1:07:52)*

## Claims

- Maj. Gen. [[dawn-m-dunlop|Dawn M. Dunlop]] served as SAPCO director from 2018 to 2019, before
  and after tours connected to the [[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards 412th Test Wing]]. Gerb
  alleges she may have gatekept UFO legacy-program operations from official channels.
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:35:42–1:36:04)*
- Per the disputed [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]], then-SAPCO director Air Force
  General H. Marshall Ward gave Vice Admiral Thomas Wilson actionable intelligence regarding UFO
  legacy-program records held within the office later known as
  [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-acquisition-and-sustainment|USD A&S]]. Gerb states he
  maintains the notes' fundamental facts about Wilson being stonewalled are true, even though the
  document is disputed. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:36:04–1:36:36)*
- [[saf-aa|SAF/AAZ]] (Air Force security/special-program-oversight/information-protection
  directorate) is separately described as serving as "the Air Force Special Access Program
  Central Office" and authorizing official for Air Force SAP systems — i.e. the Air-Force-level
  SAPCO analog nested within SAF/AA. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  1:25:38–1:26:11)*
