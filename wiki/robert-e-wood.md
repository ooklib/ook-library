Retired General Robert E. Wood, Sears, Roebuck president (1928-39) and chairman (to 1954), founder of the original America First Committee in 1940, is introduced by [[eyes-wide-open]] as a hero of the "old right" whom [[charles-willoughby]] visited in 1943 to build support for a MacArthur presidential run. The host promises an in-depth treatment in Willoughby part 4 (his "chapter 4"), calling him a key funder and organizer within the circles Willoughby frequented after retirement.

## Claims

- Missouri-born, Illinois-based West Point graduate; brigadier general in WWI. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:09:13-1:09:59)*
- Founded the original America First Committee (1940), a major pressure group against US entry into WWII; the host says there is "much to say" on it later. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:09:13-1:09:59)*
- His 1943 meeting with Willoughby was "the first of many"; the two "remained political allies throughout the 1950s and 60s". *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:09:59-1:10:17)*
