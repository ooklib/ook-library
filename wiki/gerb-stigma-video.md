A documentary-style video (about 24 minutes, dated 2024-02-09) in which [[uap-gerb]] walks through the official studies and panels of the 1940s to 1960s that he alleges were used to create the [[ufo-stigma]]. The source page is [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xKArN8S9bnM). *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 0:00-2:53)*

## Claims

- Framing: he asks why civilian and military pilots were historically advised not to report UFOs and why serious scientific study is laughed at, quotes [[j-allen-hynek]]'s three "incontrovertible points" (reports persist, are global from about 140 countries, and many come from highly credible trained people), and says the narrative that UFOs are misidentified prosaic phenomena studied only by conspiracists was pushed by the US government. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 0:33-2:53)*
- Structure: [[project-sign]] (1948-49), [[project-grudge]] (1949), [[project-blue-book]] (1952-69), the [[samford-press-conference-1952]], the [[robertson-panel-1953]], the [[house-armed-services-ufo-hearing-1966]], the [[trick-memo-1966]] and the [[condon-committee]] findings, ending in 1969. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 2:53-22:25)*
- Conclusion: he says sign, Grudge, Blue Book, the Robertson Panel and the Condon Committee were all tools to ridicule the study of anomalous things in the sky "under the guise of scientific research", while real research continued behind the scenes; he says the stigma has been "wildly successful", and invites viewers to email him or join his Discord for the case files. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 22:25-24:34)*
- He links the "sophisticated disinformation campaigns" to what [[david-grush]] talks about. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 22:56-23:14)*
