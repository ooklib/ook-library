Richard Bissell is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as an OSS "old boy" and covert operator from the former [[office-of-policy-coordination]] whom [[frank-wisner]] put in charge of planning [[operation-pbsuccess]] in 1954; he entrusted [[e-howard-hunt]] with a major covert role there. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 13:38-14:19)*

## Claims

- Later "tapped to plan the Bay of Pigs operation on behalf of Allen Dulles and Richard Nixon in 1960." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 13:47-14:05)*
- The CIA spy plane (U-2) and spy satellite programs, main customers of [[itek]] hardware early on, were "directed by one Richard Bissell." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 24:49-25:13)*
- Oswald's U-2 base posting in Japan (~1957) coincided with the U-2 program being directed by Bissell, "future deputy director of plans" (host's juxtaposition, no claim of a direct link). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 44:16-44:33)*
- Wiki-internal cross-link: [[national-reconnaissance-office]] names Bissell as first NRO co-director per gerb/nro.

## Relationships

- Planner of [[operation-pbsuccess]] and [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]]; reported through [[frank-wisner]] and [[allen-dulles]]; programs served [[itek]].

## Additional claims (Operation 40)

- Deputy director of plans (DDP) for under two years by 1960, replacing [[frank-wisner]], a Dulles protege and Ivy League (Yale) man; main planner of PBSuccess; given authority over the U-2 spy plane and the evolving spy satellite programs by 1960 (technology thread: see [[national-reconnaissance-office]]). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 12:37-13:47)*
- Instructed by [[allen-dulles]] on Jan 18, 1960 to create WH/4; secured the Guatemala site; in an internal interview blamed [[joseph-caldwell-king]] for first advocating Mafia use. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 11:16-11:37, 25:43-26:09, 10:50-11:08)*
