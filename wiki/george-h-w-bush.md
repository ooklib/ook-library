George H. W. Bush appears in Eyes Wide Open's "Real Cold War" episode as a named example of the
CIA/American-elite-business "revolving door" the host argues defined the agency's leadership
throughout its history, having run an oil company before becoming CIA director in the 1970s.
Eyes Wide Open's separate "Bush the Father" chapters (`ewa/bush-1` through `ewa/bush-3`) cover
him in depth; chapter 1 is ingested below, chapters 2 and 3 are not yet.

## Claims

- Was president of the global oil company Zapata Oil, among other business ties, before becoming
  director of the CIA in the 1970s — cited by Eyes Wide Open as an example of the deep ties
  between the CIA and the American business elite. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 6:47-6:57)*

## Relationships

- Ran Zapata Oil before becoming director of the [[central-intelligence-agency]] in the 1970s.

## Additional claims (Operation 40)

- Host: in the early 1960s, around when Operation 40 was set up, Bush was "in close dealings with the top staff" of Mexico's PEMEX, where Op 40 veterans ("Chi Chi" Cano, Ricardo Chavez) were later embedded for CIA arms/narcotics cover; his Bay of Pigs connection deferred to next episode. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 49:33-49:53)*

## Additional claims (Bush the Father, Chapter 1)

Framing: Chapter 1 of Eyes Wide Open's three-part series argues that Bush, presented in official
histories as an "outsider" like John McCone, is "almost certainly the complete opposite": his CIA
ties "date back as far as the early 1950s" and he was "likely tapped as a young man out of Yale for
intelligence work." All of the following is the host's argument, drawing on Russ Baker, Joseph
Trento, Anthony Kimery and Whitney Webb. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 0:00-1:56)*

### The 1976 appointment and the Yankee-Cowboy frame
- Ford, "a man who had never won a presidential election," appointed him 11th CIA director on Jan 30,
  1976; the host notes he was not made Secretary of State or Energy Secretary, and that the agency
  had had no outsider director since [[john-f-kennedy]] appointed McCone in 1961 to replace
  [[allen-dulles]] after the Bay of Pigs. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 0:00-1:41)*
- Host's frame: in the early 1960s Bush served as "an emissary of sorts" between the Yankee world of
  Wall Street (his father) and the Texas Cowboy world of energy and "a private intelligence web to
  be topped by Langley," a "Yankee wonder kid posing successfully as Cowboy royalty." See
  [[yankee-cowboy-thesis]]. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 1:56-2:38)* *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 5:14-5:23)*
- Host lists his early anti-Castro work: "Bay of Pigs and ... an agency asset throughout the CIA's
  Caribbean operations in the early 60s" (deferred to later chapters; see [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]]).
  *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 1:56-2:14)*

### Family and Skull and Bones
- Born June 12, 1924, second-eldest son of [[prescott-bush]] and Dorothy Walker; Navy pilot in WWII;
  enlisted at Yale in 1945 and was, like his father, inducted into [[skull-and-bones]] (host: Bones
  "was a popular entryway for future spooks into the cult of intelligence"). His son George W. was
  later also a member. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 6:43-7:11)* *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 19:07-19:30)*

### How he was allegedly brought into the CIA (two theories)
- Kimery (Covert Action magazine, 1992, via host): likely recruited while at Yale, perhaps by crew
  coach Allan "Skip" Walz (ASR "Waltz"; my normalization), a former Office of Naval Intelligence
  agent; the host calls this "unceremonious," like hundreds of other Yale recruits. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 19:30-20:34)*
- Trento (via host), citing an interview with William Corson (a Marine attache to the CIA in 1950s
  Asia): Bush was recruited on the specific orders of [[allen-dulles]] as a potential "wedge" against
  Prescott; "George's insecurities were like clay to Dulles." The host notes Bush was widely seen as
  seeking his father's approval. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 20:34-21:27)*
- Graduated 1948; first job was at [[dresser-industries]], run by "Uncle" [[neil-mallon]]. Prescott's
  letter says Mallon was "well known to Alan Dulles" and helped him procure people for the CIA;
  host: an indirect admission that Bush was recruited through Mallon. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 21:27-22:54)*
- Host's personal theory: [[robert-lovett]] may have suggested sending the young Bush to Odessa,
  Texas, the "clearest link" between Prescott and Texas. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 10:29-11:01)*
- April 10, 1953: Dulles allegedly convened Mallon and Bush on a "pilot project in the Caribbean";
  weeks earlier, Bush, 29 and with no oil experience, had co-founded [[zapata-petroleum]] in
  Midland. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 33:50-34:23)* Naming note: the earlier `ewa/cold-war` episode says "Zapata Oil"
  (see [[zapata-petroleum]]).

### Nixon, Ford and the Warren Commission
- [[richard-nixon]] appointed him UN ambassador (1971), RNC chair (1974) and, per the host, "special
  envoy to China in the last weeks of his presidency"; circumstantially it "appeared" Nixon owed
  Bush, whom the host frames as a return for the Eastern establishment's support of Nixon's rise.
  Flagged discrepancy: the intro (0:30) says he was ambassador to the People's Republic of China
  under Nixon, whereas 27:52-27:59 says special envoy. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 0:23-0:37)* *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 27:36-28:14)*
- Host: Gerald Ford link to the JFK case: Ford sat on the [[warren-commission]] and later
  elevated Bush to CIA director; "the connections between Poppy Bush and the investigation of JFK's
  murder are numerous and wide reaching," deferred to later chapters. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 32:53-33:18)*
- Host: the [[presidents-intelligence-advisory-board]] was later used by both Bush presidents to
  induct associates into the intelligence bubble. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 30:40-31:12)*
