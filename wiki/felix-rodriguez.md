Felix Rodriguez is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the "Felix" of the Schlesinger memo, the purported leader of [[operation-40]], and a later CIA operator in Southeast Asia, Bolivia (alleged assassin of Che Guevara), and Iran-Contra. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 37:48-38:20)*

## Claims

- Host: son of a rich pre-revolutionary landowning family; by his own account his family was killed or imprisoned after Castro rose; he fought under [[frank-sturgis]] as an anti-Castro guerrilla, smuggled to Miami in 1960 and joined Op 40. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 38:20-38:52)*
- Host calls him a "war junkie" and notes he is suspected and accused by many of involvement in the death of DEA agent Kiki Camarena (host defers detail). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 38:13-38:58)* See [[drug-enforcement-administration]].
- The host says the Schlesinger memo is the only place he has seen Rodriguez named as Op 40's leader, but judges it plausible given Rodriguez's later CIA career and closeness to [[ted-shackley]]. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 38:58-39:19)* Inference flag: the identification of "Felix" as Rodriguez is the host's, not stated in the memo.
- After the Cuba program he became a "private hitman", employed by the CIA and Central American regimes under Operation Condor (host). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 50:12-50:30)*
- Participated in Phoenix in the early '70s, reunited in Saigon with former JM/WAVE boss Ted Shackley, who was directing Phoenix (host); host sees Rodriguez as the direct link between the Castro assassination/purge team and Phoenix. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 54:41-55:07)*
- Shown front-left in the disputed "Op 40 photo" (host: "quite clearly" him). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 54:41-54:48)*

## Relationships

- Leader of [[operation-40]] (per memo as read by the host); associate of [[frank-sturgis]] and [[ted-shackley]]; see [[jm-wave-station]], [[secret-war-in-laos]].
