University of Minnesota physicist and wartime OSRD scientist whom the Ramsays ([[scott-and-suzanne-ramsay]]) identify as a likely "Dr. G" of [[frank-scully]]'s book. Gerb relays this as inference, not proven identity. ASR renders "Terren" for Torrance.

## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Scully described one Dr. G as having "more degrees than a thermometer" from Armour Institute, Creighton and the University of Berlin, matching Tate's record (master's in Omaha, doctorate in Berlin). *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:18:40-1:19:19)*
- Tate was recruited by [[vannevar-bush]] to NDRC and OSRD anti-submarine work, serving as scientific adviser to the US Fleet commander and in OSRD field services and operations research. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:19:34-1:20:06)*
- At the University of Minnesota in 2011 the Ramsays found evidence Tate travelled to the Southwest around the time of Aztec and told a large audience that a recent trip had changed his life and could change how energy and food are produced. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:20:06-1:20:44)*
