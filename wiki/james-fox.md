James Fox is a documentary maker ("The Phenomenon"). In Cameron's telling he tried to obtain the Vandenberg film from [[alan-sandler]] and lobbied Congress to get it.

## Claims

- Cameron says Fox raised $25,000, was refused, and turned to Congress hoping for a copy in exchange for helping move the film. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:12:38-1:13:50)*
- Cameron mentions a friend who attended the Soul Conference where Fox's film screened and said the Kingman crash claim is not right. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:49:41-1:50:16)*
