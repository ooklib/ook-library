Alleged 6 May 1978 crash of a cylindrical object into a mountainside near a Bolivian town (the ASR text reads "eler Bolivia"; the name is unverified). [[uap-gerb]] calls it his favorite Moon Dust case and says it was brought to his attention by the work of the UAP caucus (he names Reps. Tim Burchett, [[anna-paulina-luna]], [[eric-burlison]] and Jared Moskowitz). *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 8:13-9:06)*

## Claims

- Gerb relays that thousands of people saw a cylindrical object hit the mountainside at 4:15 pm on 6 May 1978, with a sonic boom heard up to 150 miles away and windows cracked within 30 miles. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 8:58-9:23)*
- Army Corporal Natalio Ruiz reportedly saw it fly overhead like a gigantic wine container trailing white smoke; the Bolivian military and three jets were dispatched. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 9:23-9:38)*
- Quoted officials describe a dull metallic cylinder 12 feet long with a few dents, contents unknown, with technical commissions and a NASA expert expected, and two US officers (Colonel Robert Simmons and Major Jesse Haaste, spellings from ASR) arriving afterward. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 9:38-10:08)*
- FOIA documents show Secretary of State Cyrus Vance writing that appropriate government agencies had been contacted; Gerb says the appropriate agency was Moon Dust, which received the military's film of the object and was tasked to monitor. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 10:08-10:42)*
- Gerb says the trail ends there and he does not know whether the craft was retrieved. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 10:42-10:56)*
