*S4: The Bob Lazar Story*, with working title Project Gravitaur (transcribed "Gravitar/Gravitator"), is Luigi Vendittelli's roughly decade-in-the-making documentary and VR reconstruction of [[bob-lazar]]'s alleged S4 experience, distributed via wearenotalone.com per the video description. Lazar says he intended to stop after [[jeremy-corbell]]'s film and only agreed after Luigi promised to render exactly what he saw; the project took about three years with Lazar correcting every detail *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 5:00-6:22)*. Ramsey says the VR version produced "an overwhelming sense of dread", which Lazar says matches the real feeling; Lazar's friend [[gene-huff]] said the same *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 7:04-8:11)*.

## Claimed corroborations

- Lazar says the recreated craft's light behavior (halogen tripod lights not brightening the interior) matches what he saw, and infers it stems from the angles rather than the material *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 9:58-11:52)*.
- Lazar says the reconstruction is "100%" faithful and even restored forgotten details such as a whiteboard *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 38:12-39:36, 1:04:33-1:05:16)*.
- The alleged hangar-door photo shows nine doors and matches Lazar's account per Ramsey, with claimed pixel analysis by Ron Masters *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 26:15-26:52)*.
- Luigi's model reproduces the reversed flag position Lazar described, answering critics who said it would be invisible *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:11:02-1:11:43)*.

Nothing in the interview independently verifies the reconstruction: its only reference is Lazar's memory.
