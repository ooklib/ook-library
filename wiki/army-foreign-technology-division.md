Claimed Pentagon-based office within the Office of the Chief of Army Research and Development (OCRD) under Lt. Gen. [[arthur-trudeau]], designated in 1960 as the chief-run section of [[philip-j-corso]] and tasked to receive all "foreign technology" reports including "foreign beings and events, those not of this world". Per Corso it exploited technology of unknown origin from [[roswell-incident-1947]] and possibly other crashes by feeding it to industry and laboratories as ordinary R&D. Gerb stresses this is not the Air Force's [[air-force-foreign-technology-division]] at [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]] and that the Army office has "almost zero trace or record outside of Corso's own military documents". *(Unverified claim.)* *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 9:07-9:39, 23:05-23:22, 1:00:03-1:01:37)*

## Claims

- Corso's four assigned subjects (1960): foreign development "of this world"; foreign intelligence and developments "not of this world"; interim project officer on select R&D projects; other duties as Trudeau's special assistant. Gerb notes points 1, 3, 4 were normal and point 2 was the exception. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 49:08-49:47)*
- Budget of about 2 billion dollars (Corso, in an Art Bell / John B. Alexander interview), with RDT&E contracts; Gerb equates this to over 10.5 billion today. Corso says the Army had its own atomic labs and the night viewing lab at Fort Belvoir ([[fort-belvoir-night-vision-laboratory]]). *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 26:11-27:32)*
- Partners: the top 25 Fortune 500 industries were contacted, with board-level meetings, plus laboratories and universities, and foreign scientists such as those from [[operation-paperclip]]; Corso mentions [[bell-labs]] and [[sperry-rand]]. Details in [[corso-technology-seeding-claims]]. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 24:02-26:02, 46:44-47:14)*
- [[john-b-alexander]] wrote that the Army office "was formed as Corso stated and then disappeared shortly after his retirement". After Trudeau, [[dwight-e-beach]] led Army R&D and nothing further is found on the division. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:00:41-1:01:37)*
- Corso says [[project-moon-dust]] and Blue Fly reported materials and intelligence directly to the Air Force's foreign technology division at Wright-Patterson. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:02:17-1:02:34)*
- Gerb's hypothesis: it may have run parallel to the [[army-research-office]] (created 1958 under Trudeau) outside civilian and Congressional oversight; the R&D primer also shows University Research Initiatives, in-house independent research and IR&D as funding mechanisms that later led to UARCs and [[ffrdc]]s. Gerb likens the missing paper trail to the [[interplanetary-phenomenon-unit]]. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:01:44-1:04:22)*
- Late work: lasers, anti-missile missiles and directed-energy weapons. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 58:19-58:28)*
