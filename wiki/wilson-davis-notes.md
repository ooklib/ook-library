The Wilson-Davis notes are disputed but (per Gerb) "highly likely accurate" notes transcribed
by physicist [[eric-davis|Dr. Eric Davis]], leaked from the estate of late astronaut
[[edgar-mitchell|Edgar Mitchell]]. They recount a 2002 conversation between Davis and Vice
Admiral (and former DIA director) [[thomas-wilson|Thomas Wilson]] in the parking lot of
contractor [[eg-and-g|EG&G]] in Nevada. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 34:39–35:19)*

## Claims

- Wilson recounts finding, but being stonewalled from accessing, UFO recovery/material
  exploitation programs — protected by the DoD [[special-access-program-oversight-committee|
  SAP Oversight Committee (SAPOC)]] Senior Review Group (SRG) and "a large defense contractor,"
  which Gerb wagers is [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]].
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 35:19–35:35)*
- Per Wilson, he was told by General Michael Ward to search a special records group in the
  Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Technology (OUSDA&T) belonging to
  legacy holdover programs predating early-1990s SAP restructuring.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 35:35–35:52)*
- Per the notes, UFO programs were reorganized under SAPOC/SRG after an internal audit "almost
  revealed" them; this reorganization began 5 January 1994, when the Deputy Secretary of
  Defense significantly strengthened DoD SAP management/control structures.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 35:52–36:23)*
- A GAO May 1993 report ("DoD Special Access Programs: Administrative Due Process Not Provided
  When Access Is Denied or Revoked") discusses Navy/Air Force noncompliance with DoD regulation
  62000.2/R on SAP access; per Gerb, the Army complied more consistently — matching his broader
  sense that Army UFO material-exploitation programs operated in a stricter, controlled
  environment than the more "loose" Navy/Air Force legacy programs.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 36:23–37:16)*
- SAPOC/SRG, per Wilson, was chaired at reorganization time by Deputy Secretary of Defense
  [[john-m-deutch|John Deutch]] — though the restructuring itself was conducted by his
  predecessor, [[bill-perry|William Perry]]; Deutch immediately followed suit in the chair role,
  having just come from Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Technology (the same
  office Wilson learned held the UFO legacy files). *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  37:23–37:56)*
- Deutch also served as a consultant to TRW, [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los Alamos]], and
  [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]] before SAPOC work, and (pre-1995, possibly during his DepSecDef
  tenure) as director of [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]], working in
  SAIC's La Jolla office — the same office as [[denise-mckenzie|Denise McKenzie]].
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 38:11–38:51)*
- William Perry, who conducted the 1994 SAP reorganization, once served on SAIC's board.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 39:14–39:32)*
- Wilson spoke to two permanent SAPOC members: Brig. Gen. Michael Costanik and
  [[paul-kaminski|Paul Kaminski]] (USD A&T, preceded by Deutch); Kaminski and permanent SRG
  member [[linton-wells-ii|Lynn (Linton) Wells]] are both lifelong MITRE Corporation members.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 39:32–39:49)*
- A permanent SAP member post-restructuring: Admiral [[william-a-owens|William A. Owens]],
  former VCJCS, senior military assistant to [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]], and SAIC CEO. Gerb
  notes 2 of 6 SAPOC personnel Wilson identified were former SAIC alumni — supporting his
  identification of SAIC as the "large defense contractor" from the notes.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 39:49–40:25)*
- A "critical third man": Naval Reserve Commander [[willard-miller|Willard Miller]] attended a
  1997 meeting with VADM Wilson and [[steven-greer|Steven Greer]] (confirmed to have taken
  place); during the EG&G meeting, Davis showed Wilson a 2002 letter from Miller offering to aid
  Davis and [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] with UFO crash-retrieval research, including access to a
  senior official with ARV knowledge, a list of civilian DoD contractors involved in UFO crash
  retrieval, and a possible crash-retrieval team. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  40:33–41:22)*
