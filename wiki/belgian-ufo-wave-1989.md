From November 1989, hundreds of Belgian reports described a large flat triangle with lights underneath flying low; on the night of 30 March 1990 an object was tracked by radar and pursued by two Belgian F-16s, which could not locate it. Declassified US documents say the US told the Belgian Air Force and Ministry of Defense that no US stealth aircraft (B-2, F-117) were used. Gerb, while noting the wave is often dismissed as the Patrick Marechal hoax photo or mass delusion, floats the hypothesis that it might have been tests of a still-more-secret craft such as the TR-3B, and notes critics of [[edgar-fuche]] claim he merged the TR-3B story with the wave. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 7:43-9:05, 45:34-46:12)*
