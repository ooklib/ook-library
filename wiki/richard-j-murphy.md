Richard Murphy is a British chartered accountant, economist, and tax-justice campaigner who,
per this video's own description, is Emeritus Professor of Accounting Practice at Sheffield
University Management School, director of Tax Research LLP, and author of the *Funding the
Future* blog; his best-known book is cited as *The Joy of Tax*. In this video he argues that the
[[city-of-london-corporation]] sits at the epicenter of the global offshore tax haven network and
should be abolished as a political entity. Unlike Green & Pleasant's documentary-style narration
on the same institution, Murphy's video is explicit first-person advocacy/opinion by a named,
credentialed individual, and its content is recorded here as his argument, not established fact.

## Claims

- Self-described credentials (per the video's own description and its framing of him as its
  presenter): Emeritus Professor of Accounting Practice, Sheffield University Management School;
  director of Tax Research LLP; author of the *Funding the Future* blog; author of *The Joy of
  Tax*
  *([rjm/city-of-london](../raw/rjm/Richard J Murphy - 2026-05-17 - The secret power behind the City of London.md) description)*.
- Murphy states, as a claim about his own record, that he created country-by-country reporting —
  a system requiring large multinational corporations to report their tax-haven operations to tax
  authorities — and that he was "instrumental" in lobbying for the automatic information exchange
  process between tax havens and originating countries' tax authorities
  *([rjm/city-of-london](../raw/rjm/Richard J Murphy - 2026-05-17 - The secret power behind the City of London.md) 5:12, 5:18, 5:28)*.
- Murphy argues the [[city-of-london-corporation]] is the epicenter (not merely a beneficiary) of
  the global offshore tax haven system connecting London to Jersey, Guernsey, an ASR-mangled
  place name transcribed as "the Alaman" (likely the Isle of Man or Alderney), Cayman, Gibraltar,
  Bermuda, and the British Virgin Islands, and calls for the Corporation's abolition as a
  political entity and the removal of its corporate voting rights
  *([rjm/city-of-london](../raw/rjm/Richard J Murphy - 2026-05-17 - The secret power behind the City of London.md) 1:56, 9:27, 9:49)*.
- Murphy calls the offshore world "the aircraft carriers of the anti-democracy movement" and
  argues that "market confidence" has effectively given the financial sector a veto over
  democratic policy choices
  *([rjm/city-of-london](../raw/rjm/Richard J Murphy - 2026-05-17 - The secret power behind the City of London.md) 4:37, 4:46, 6:52)*.
