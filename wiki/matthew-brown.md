Matthew Brown, a self-identified [[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]]
whistleblower, posted a tweet on 5 June 2025 alleging UFO materials/craft were being stored at
[[naval-air-station-patuxent-river|Pax River]]'s special-project hangars, owned by
[[amentum|Amentum Corporation]], among "other fun spaces."
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:51:19–1:51:34)*

- In 2024, delivered the [[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]] report to Congress
  through Jeremy Corbell, describing IMCON's mission as ISR "of a global nature for UAPs and
  ARVs/RVs," and naming [[sentient-nro-ai-program|NRO Sentient]] as one tasked intelligence
  resource. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 39:18–40:47)*

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2 — administrative terrorism)

- In a separately embedded interview clip, Brown names an unidentified individual he describes
  as "a nexus point for legacy security" and "part of the dirty tricks department" — someone who
  matches whistleblower names "to the capabilities to go achieve X, Y, Z objectives," resulting
  in "an ecosystem flooded with disinformation and psychological operations and targeted attacks
  against whistleblowers and their allies." Brown states the individual is currently at
  [[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]], with a role Brown identifies as "deputy SAPCO for
  DARPA." Brown says he cannot reveal how he knows this "because I am communicating it for
  others," and states he and others "are done taking punches and not punching back."
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:47:12–2:49:10)*
