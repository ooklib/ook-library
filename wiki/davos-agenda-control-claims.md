Claim cluster from [[wef-insider-interview-2025]]: that the [[world-economic-forum]] does not merely influence but effectively steers Western governments and corporations through a shared agenda. Attributed to [[desiree-fixler]] with host [[winston-marshall]] partly pressing back; unverified.

## Claims

- Fixler says the WEF "shapes markets, national policy, education, media and financial flows" and that its tentacles affect every part of life; she says it controls the West but "maybe not China, Russia or Trump's America". *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 57:59, 58:07, 58:53)*
- She says the agenda is broader than net zero: transportation and heating mandates, hiring rules, open borders, digital IDs and central bank digital currency, and censorship, aimed at top-down control of resources, production, distribution and demographics. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 55:08, 55:30, 1:19:16)*
- She names Mark Carney, Keir Starmer, Macron and Ursula von der Leyen as pursuing an identical agenda, and calls it "soft power" with real effect on things voters never approved. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:01:33, 1:02:00)*
- Marshall pushes back that Schwab does not dictate: attendees swim in the same waters, hear no dissent, and implement what they absorb; Fixler adds that participants are vested interests, mostly bureaucrats with speaking notes, with a few true believers. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:02:32, 53:22, 1:15:30)*
- Fixler says corporations favour regulation because it raises barriers to entry and funds compliance jobs, and cites revolving-door careers such as Nick Clegg going to Meta. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:04:56, 1:05:21)*
- She says WEF participants dismissed her energy-cost concerns as a non-issue, calling her a flat-earther and a climate denier, and that a colleague remarked she did not struggle to pay bills. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:14:11, 1:16:04)*
- Marshall's Substack teaser claims Fixler argues the climate crisis is a hoax (not in the free portion). *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:25:42)*
