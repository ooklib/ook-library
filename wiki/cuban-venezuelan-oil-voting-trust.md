The Cuban Venezuelan Oil Voting Trust (CVT) was, per [[eyes-wide-open]], the 1950 vehicle through which [[george-de-mohrenschildt]] secured rights to nearly 50% of pre-revolutionary Cuba's oil reserves, the Texas cowboys' first international expansion "into the Dominion of the Yankees."

## Claims

- Co-founded in 1950 by de Mohrenschildt and Pantek (ASR) president Warren Smith. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 23:11-23:32)*
- Secured rights to nearly 50% of pre-revolutionary Cuban oil, thanks to a "massive capital injection" from Empire Trust Company of "John L" senior (ASR-truncated; a New York financier originally from Missouri), which had extended into Dallas. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 24:33-25:03)*
- Empire Trust's local representative and VP was [[jack-crichton]], its rep at CVT from 1950. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 24:56-25:03, 27:31-27:38)*
- Context: Cuba was the [[texas-raj]]'s hoped-for foreign frontier; the host contrasts Yankee neo-colonial holdings with the Cowboys' lack of them. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 23:32-24:33)*

## Relationships

- Founded by [[george-de-mohrenschildt]]; represented by [[jack-crichton]]. Wiki-internal observation: the transcript does not say what became of CVT after the Cuban revolution.
