Dr. Vannevar Bush is presented as the intellectual architect of the modern
[[ffrdc|FFRDC]] system and, per Gerb, a "centralized figure" in the alleged "Manhattan Project
2.0" secrecy architecture.

## Claims

- Established the MIT Radiation Lab in 1940, which aided WWII microwave radar development for
  navigation/combat until 1945; it reopened in 1951 as [[mit-lincoln-laboratory|MIT Lincoln
  Labs]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 20:42–20:59)*
- Gerb calls Bush "the grandfather of MITRE" for this lineage, and separately "the godfather of
  MITRE" in connection with Navy-sponsored WWII anti-submarine/sonar research under his Office
  of Scientific Research and Development (OSRD), which Gerb speculates may trace forward into
  MITRE's Navy-administered reverse-engineering work. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 20:26–20:34, 2:09:43–2:10:24)*
- Alleged to have led Atomic Energy Commission National Labs' custodianship/study of recovered
  nonhuman technical vehicles per classified 1948 presidential executive orders. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 19:45–20:05)*
- Aided in establishing the secrecy architecture of both the original Manhattan Project and
  Gerb's alleged "Manhattan Project 2.0." *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 20:22–20:34)*
- Conceptualized UARCs (university-affiliated research centers) and FFRDCs generally.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:01:50–1:02:10)*
- Gerb speculates a possible link between Bush-era Navy anti-submarine/sonar research and the
  "doodlebug" device Silas Newton and Leo GeBauer allegedly used to defraud investors in
  connection with the Aztec case, though this is framed as speculative. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:09:52–2:10:24)*

- Per [[j-andrew-kissner|J. Andrew Kissner]]'s 1994 statement, discs downed by the US Army from May–July
  1947 were processed and analyzed for Bush (chairman of the Joint Chiefs' Joint Research and
  Development Board, director of the Office of Scientific Research and Development), assisted by
  [[armed-forces-special-weapons-project|AFSWP]] personnel at [[sandia-national-laboratories|
  Sandia Base]]. Gerb notes Bush served on AT&T's board of directors 1947–1962 — the same period
  AT&T/Western Electric's Sandia Corporation subsidiary managed Sandia (from 1949).
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 25:33–25:50, 29:34–29:53)*

## Claims (from the "Secrecy" Vol. 1 video)

- UAP Gerb calls Bush and General [[george-c-marshall|George C. Marshall]] the two figures most
  likely to have built the security skeleton of the alleged "Manhattan Project 2.0," with General
  [[leslie-groves|Leslie Groves]] credited for the underlying conceptual blueprint.
  *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 2:20:48–2:21:06)*
- Member of the Manhattan Project's [[top-policy-group|Top Policy Group]] — the small civilian/
  political control group (with FDR/Truman, Secretary of War Stimson, Marshall, and deputy James
  Conant) that let the president retain oversight of the project while bypassing normal channels;
  as director of the Office of Scientific Research and Development (OSRD), Bush also ran the
  Manhattan Project's internal self-censorship/publication-control system, a precursor to the
  1946 Atomic Energy Act's "restricted data." *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  1:07:39–1:07:55, 1:25:04–1:25:27)*
- Co-oversaw the [[alsos-mission|ALSOS missions]] alongside Groves, Marshall, and Marshall's G2
  Colonel Boris Pash — a Manhattan Engineer District intelligence operation to secure Nazi atomic
  progress, personnel, and materials, run entirely outside standard military-intelligence
  channels. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 2:15:17–2:15:39)*
- Chaired the National Military Establishment's Research and Development Board (RDB, 1947–1948),
  created under the 1947 National Security Act as the successor to the Joint Research and
  Development Board; UAP Gerb argues the RDB was "the tip of the spear" for UFO-related scientific
  covert operations from 1947 to 1953, when it transitioned into the Assistant Secretary of
  Defense for Research and Development — the lineage that today is the Under Secretary of Defense
  for Research and Engineering. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  1:48:56–1:49:37)*
- Per an alleged 1948 classified presidential executive order, custody of recovered discs was to
  be analyzed by Bush and vested in Atomic Energy Commission National Laboratories (Sandia would
  be spun off the following year, 1949, when Truman asked AT&T/Western Electric to take over
  Sandia Base, Los Alamos, and Oak Ridge). *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  3:19:11–3:19:58)*
- [[robert-sarbacher|Robert Sarbacher]] told Canadian engineer Wilbert B. Smith in 1950 that a
  small group headed by Bush was conducting a "concentrated effort" studying flying saucers, in a
  matter rated more highly classified than the H-bomb; Sarbacher separately told William Steinman
  in 1983 he "strongly theorized" [[j-robert-oppenheimer|J. Robert Oppenheimer]]'s involvement
  alongside confirmed involvement by Dr. John von Neumann. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 1:55:26–1:56:06, 2:01:43–2:02:06)*
- Bush is "almost ubiquitously" labeled by early UFO researchers — including
  [[j-andrew-kissner|J. Andrew Kissner]] and William Steinman's *UFO Crash at Aztec* (1986) — as the
  first man to spearhead US crash-retrieval/reverse-engineering operations; a disputed 1947
  [[interplanetary-phenomenon-unit|Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit]] report separately places
  Oppenheimer (not Bush) on the retrieval team. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  1:53:34–1:54:17)*
- Established the MIT Radiation Lab and pioneered the FFRDC/FCRC and UARC (university-affiliated
  research center) concepts, revolutionizing government-university research partnerships; UAP
  Gerb calls him "the foundational individual behind our scientific and government partnership of
  industry." *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 3:12:39–3:14:52)*
