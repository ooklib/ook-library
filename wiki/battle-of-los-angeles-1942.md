An anti-aircraft barrage over Los Angeles on the night of February 24, 1942, shortly after Pearl Harbor, described by [[uap-gerb]] as one of the cases that got him into UFOs as a child. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 23:10-23:26, 24:43)*

## Claims

- Multiple radar signatures triggered a military scramble and hours of aerial bombardment; five civilians died (three car accidents, two heart attacks). The later official explanation was war nerves or a meteorological balloon. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 23:34-24:13)*
- A photo published in the LA Times on February 26, 1942 shows searchlights on what looks like a saucer-shaped craft, but Gerb notes it may have been heavily altered. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 24:13-24:36)*
- Gerb now thinks a prosaic explanation is likely. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 24:43-24:59)*
