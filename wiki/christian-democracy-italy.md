Christian Democracy (Democrazia Cristiana, the Christian Democratic Party/CDP/DC), per Eyes Wide
Open, was created and funded by the Truman administration with $350 million in US taxpayer
dollars routed through the Catholic Church, becoming the only ruling party of Italy throughout
the Cold War after allegedly winning the 1948 election through CIA/Mafia/Vatican coercion,
bribery, and violence.

## Claims

- The Truman administration began funneling legal cash to the Vatican: $350 million to establish
  the Christian Democratic Party as the Catholic Church's political vehicle, plus an additional
  $30 million to create the Catholic Action Groups anti-communist propaganda network. Eyes Wide
  Open frames this as the US "creat[ing] their party" and "obtain[ing] the papacy as an anti
  Communist ally." *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 22:31-23:07)*
- Won the 1948 election with 48% of the vote and 307 parliamentary seats, versus the Communist/
  Socialist coalition's 31% (200 seats) — a result Eyes Wide Open attributes to Mafia terror,
  Vatican propaganda/violence, and ballot-box stuffing by [[calogero-vizzini]]'s men, not fair
  democratic process. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 29:00-29:38)*
- Under Prime Minister [[alcide-de-gasperi]], ruled with eight different cabinets from 1945 to
  1953 and never once purged any members of parliament; researchers speculate former fascists ran
  rampant within the early party. Remained in power for the entirety of the Cold War, essentially
  establishing Italy as a one-party state. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 30:00-30:32)*
