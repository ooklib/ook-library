An alleged 1968 radar and visual UFO encounter at Minot Air Force Base, North Dakota, described by [[uap-gerb]] via [[kevin-knuth]]'s analysis. It is an attributed claim; Gerb says the file is declassified. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 10:54-12:24)*

## Claims

- According to base personnel, an egg-shaped UFO with skin "described as molten lava" kept about 3 miles from a descending B-52; during a 3-second radar sweep it closed to 2 miles, which Knuth computes as about 209 g and a top speed near Mach 12 (about 9,200 mph). *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 11:06-11:32)*
- Gerb notes the base supported nuclear weapons, like Malmstrom, and that the egg shape and lava-like skin recall the craft in the Kingman story (the "mother of pearl" effect, see [[kingman-arizona-crash-1953]]). *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 11:06-11:49)*
- The declassified report shows it was filed under USAF regulation 200-2 (1953), so it never reached Blue Book files or public awareness, though it was a multisensor military detection at a nuclear base. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 11:49-12:24)*
