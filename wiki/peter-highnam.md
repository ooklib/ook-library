Dr. Peter Highnam (transcribed in the source ASR as "Heinham"/"Heinum") is officially titled
principal deputy assistant secretary of defense for critical technologies within the office of
the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering ([[under-secretary-of-defense-for-research-and-engineering|
USD R&E]]). Gerb calls him "a very very very senior and experienced legacy program lead,"
referencing prior coverage of him. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 42:40–43:02)*

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2 — full career dossier)

- Full career timeline per Gerb: DARPA program manager focused on electronic warfare and airborne
  communications; director of ODNI's Intelligence Advanced Research Projects Agency (IARPA);
  director of research at the National Geospatial Intelligence Agency (NGA); deputy director and
  then acting director of [[darpa|DARPA]], 2018–2022; currently principal deputy assistant
  secretary of defense for critical technologies within USD R&E.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:54:20–1:54:47)*
- Gerb states "critical technologies" positions high in the DoD are "often an indicator of legacy
  exposure," and directly alleges Highnam has been "integral to coordinating legacy efforts
  between DARPA and USD R&E" (and, by extension,
  [[darpa-security-and-intelligence-directorate|DARPA SID]]) for at least the past 8+ years.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:53:51–1:55:06)*
