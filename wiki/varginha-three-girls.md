The three young women who say they came within about 10 feet of a live being in daylight in Varginha in January 1996, per [[james-fox]] "some of the most compelling eyewitness testimony to a nonhuman alien being". Their ages given are Katia 21, Liliane 16 and Valquiria 14 (Fox's recollection).

## Claims (a52/fox)

- They saw the being against a block wall in daylight from about 10 feet away, near the later blockade area. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 41:31-41:41, 1:04:14-1:04:40, 2:12:05-2:12:38)*
- Fox says Valquiria took the youngest sister and ran; Liliane went back for Katia, who stood frozen, locked eyes with the creature, and felt it pleading "I need help, I'm weak, I'm frightened". Fox says this resembles E.T. and asks what would have happened if Katia had taken it home. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 2:12:05-2:13:25)*
- Fox says the girls' mother Marta (Ramsey calls her the sister of the deceased military officer; the two accounts are not fully clear) refused an offer of a briefcase of American dollars and to make her daughters call it a "deranged animal or dog". *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 41:49-43:06)*
- Fox says the girls were very upset by later comments from [[ubirajara-rodrigues]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:31:09-1:31:18)*
