Lucien Conein, the "Operator," was a French-born, Kansas City-raised OSS Detachment 202 alumnus who, per Eyes Wide Open, became a main CIA point man in Vietnam under Edward Lansdale, is alleged to have connected Corsican mobsters in Saigon with Vang Pao's army in Laos, and ended as chief of covert operations for the DEA. (ASR spells him 'Kunai'/'Kane'.) *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 14:48-15:11)*

## Claims

- Served in both French and US armies; called up to the OSS in 1943; ended WWII in China under [[paul-helliwell]], training irregulars on the Indochina border against the Japanese; declined an offer from French intelligence; allegedly met Ho Chi Minh in Saigon and concluded he must be countered. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 14:57-15:31)*
- Nine years with the CIA in postwar Germany, then Vietnam from 1954 as top aide to [[edward-lansdale]]. Allegedly forged ties with the local French and Corsican mobsters, some met in Marseille during WWII. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 15:31-15:57)*
- Per Alfred McCoy, helped establish a narcotics connection between Corsican mobsters in Saigon and [[vang-pao]]'s guerrilla army; instrumental to the Strategic Hamlet program and CORDS; allegedly gave the green light to South Vietnamese generals to kill [[ngo-dinh-diem]] in 1963. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 16:12-16:42)*
- Recommended by [[e-howard-hunt]] to Nixon as White House narcotics advisor post-Watergate; ended mid-1970s as chief of covert operations for the [[drug-enforcement-administration]], later admitting he worked with Corsican mobsters there as he had under Lansdale. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 16:47-17:10)*
- Host: shows the importance of narcotics smuggling to counter-insurgency work ([[cia-drug-paradigm]]). *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 17:10-17:26)*
- Named in LAOS 1 as a member of the 1945 [[deer-mission]], described as 'counterinsurgency expert and future Ho Chi Minh Trail strategist' (ASR 'Lucien K'). *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 18:46-19:06)*

- LAOS 2: accompanied [[edward-lansdale]] to Trinquier's Saigon briefing on [[operation-x]] (ASR "Lucien K"/"Cane"), described as a "short intelligence officer with a Corsican name" who had supported the Corsican mob in southern France in WWII, Lansdale's "devious mob associated sidekick" and the parallel of [[antoine-savani]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 46:49-47:22)*

- LAOS 3 (ASR "Lucien K"/"Kan"/"K", Corsican American): OSS veteran of [[paul-helliwell]]'s Detachment 202, Lansdale's main liaison with the local French and Corsican populations in Saigon; Lansdale's official 1955 evaluation (per William Rust) called him "a good strong right-hand man in tight spots", while privately calling him "the thug". *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 39:18-40:16)*
- In the early OSS he was the main US officer liaising with the Corsican Brotherhood in the south of France on a resistance against the Nazis and says he was made an unofficial member. The transcript gives that as service in "World War I", which is presumably a host slip for WWII (flagged, not corrected). *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 40:16-40:44)*
- Host claim: in 1955 the CIA had just helped the Corsicans in Marseille restart their narcotics business; Conein's Saigon work complemented it, and he "likely connected with the Corsican expatriate mobsters" to keep the northern Laos links to Marseille alive. Per Douglas Valentine, he rubbed shoulders with the Corsican underground at the Hotel Continental, owned by father and son Mathieu and Philippe Franchini (ASR "mat"/"franchini"), the Franchinis having deep ties to Marseille syndicates. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 40:44-42:20)*
- October 1956: six-week Special Forces officer course at Fort Bragg, where instructors later called him "the first officer in charge of military free fall training within the Special Warfare Center" and credit him with the low-flying parachute technique used in Vietnam and Laos; the host is skeptical about the "true purpose" given later Bragg narcotics crimes. He returned to Vietnam in the late 1950s, early 1960s and early 1970s, training US Special Forces for Ho Chi Minh Trail work in eastern Laos. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 42:20-43:36)*
- McCoy interview (1971, at Conein's suburban home near Langley): Conein was almost boastful about his role supporting the Nhu apparatus, said "the Corsicans are smarter, tougher and better organized than the Sicilians", and later was furious at McCoy for publishing it. Allegedly given a gold medallion with an eagle crest inscribed in Corsican "for your friendship" in the mid-1960s. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 43:42-44:46)*
- Host inference: Conein forged the bond between the Diem regime and Corsican mobsters and, at least, knew of and permitted the Nhu-Corsican relationship; from 1958 the Nhu-[[air-laos-commerce]] alliance restored the opium flow. In the host's telling Conein was also the main American adviser to Nhu on the [[strategic-hamlet-program]] and would return to oversee Diem's overthrow and murder in 1963. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 44:46-45:14, 37:46-37:57, 50:10-50:17)*
