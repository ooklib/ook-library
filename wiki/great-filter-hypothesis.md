# Great Filter Hypothesis

Solution to the Fermi paradox that every civilization passes gates that decide whether it survives or destroys itself. [[uap-gerb]] speculates that harnessing fission and fusion is one such gate, and that non-human observers may watch humanity's handling of it. It is Gerb's opinion, not evidence. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 13:48-14:28, 15:23-16:10)*
