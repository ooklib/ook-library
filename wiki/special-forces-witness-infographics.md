An anonymous US serviceman, described by [[uap-gerb]] as an elderly man (he puts his age at about 74 or older) who claims to have been a Vietnam-era Army long-range reconnaissance patrol (LRRP) soldier drawn into a covert UFO crash-retrieval organization he calls "the program", out of it about 20 years when interviewed. His account reached the public through the March 2024 Infographics Show video "US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs", said to be a composite of interviews and written correspondence. Gerb treats every claim as unverified but finds the timeline consistent with real events, and calls him "our witness". Nothing is known of his real name.

## Claims (gerb/special-forces)

- Vietnam crash: in 1968 on the Vietnam-Cambodia border, his LRRP platoon (Gerb reads it as Company E, 52nd Infantry, 1st Cavalry Division, based at Phuoc Vinh, October to December 1968) saw a glowing red-orange object overhead, heard a crash seconds later and found a cracked-open egg-shaped, dull gray metallic craft with no bolts, seams or screws. Full account: [[vietnam-egg-craft-crash-1968]]. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 2:05-8:54)*
- Induction: he and his platoon mates were read into the program by force, offered the alternative of a general discharge, likely drug charges or an Article 15. The program was said to have been formally put together during the Korean War, with several rapid-reaction units on standby in the Vietnam theater because "these things are attracted to conflict". Gerb sees the timeline as compatible with the [[majestic-12]] documents and [[wilbert-b-smith]]'s 1950 information from [[robert-sarbacher]]. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 10:06-11:50, 12:35-13:00)*
- Compartmentalization: his introduction was highly compartmentalized; he was told only what he needed to know, and said he did not realize the objects were from space "until I actually saw a body". Gerb compares this to the compartment pyramid in the [[wilson-davis-notes]] and to Stringfield's 1954 retrieval witness. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 13:52-14:28)*
- Aftermath: his unit was split up and put into heavy combat; five of the original 12 died and three were badly wounded, which he suspected was an attempt to kill them off. A year after the crash (about 1969) he was officially brought into the program, and says he left about 20 years before the interview. Gerb cross-checks the 1st Cavalry LRRP casualty list and gets 16 deaths in the window, 8 hostile, and guesses five of them are his platoon mates. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 15:56-17:27)*
- Scale: he says he thinks about three craft were pulled out of theater (not only Vietnam) and that one recovery nearly started a war with China, the program gaining the upper hand only because of China's primitive early warning systems. Gerb sets this beside [[david-grush]]'s remarks on a decades-long cold war over the subject and a redacted Disclosure Project witness (10659) with Vietnam retrieval involvement. Recoveries at sea would be near impossible; Europe was another hot spot but kept a tighter lid on it. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 17:33-19:51)*
- He mentions and praises [[david-grush]] as a patriot, and confirms the involvement of aerospace contractors and sophisticated disinformation. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 15:22-15:45)*
- Facilities: he saw two collections of craft in different places. He calls Wright-Patterson and the Foreign Technology Division a "red herring" where the Soviets would come looking, saying "we faked some stuff going into Wright Patterson"; he describes co-op sites run by the government and big contractors (Raytheon, Boeing, and small firms like [[texas-instruments]]) where engineers received pieces to figure out without being told the origin. Gerb infers Wright-Patterson material after about 1969 may be program hoaxes, and sees tension with [[barry-goldwater]]'s [[blue-room-wright-patterson]] story. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 19:59-23:50)*
- Craft types: the ones that crash are almost always the eggs and the bells, sometimes disintegrating on impact and probably unmanned drones; saucers are "the crown jewels" and he took part in the recovery of only two. He does not know whether they are really spacecraft and wonders if "that's where they want us to think they are coming from". Gerb notes AARO's 1996 to 2023 shape data show the same common configurations. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 23:58-25:07, 25:39-25:54)*
- South American saucer recovery: the saucer hit a rocky outcropping, smashed the rock and took out a quarter mile of trees, relatively unharmed; see [[south-america-crash-retrievals-claims]]. Construction: single piece of material or assembled at an atomic level, entrance invisible until it opens, interiors accessible by destructive and non-destructive means. Gerb says this matches [[witness-ms]] (a Dugway craft "flawless like it had been 3D printed") and [[marine-rb]] (door panel tolerances too tight for paper). *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 25:54-29:29)*
- Biologics: the "things" inside were dead, decaying, with a crude digestive system, taller than the typical gray, large but not massive eyes, long limbs and knees "not in the right places", raised in a dimly lit environment; he thinks they were biological androids. At a UK-based retrieval two of five crew survived. See [[biological-android-hypothesis]] and [[boyle-ireland-ufo-crash-1996]]. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 29:39-34:00)*
- Live-biologics protocol: never level a weapon at a being, keep security out of sight, take hazard readings first, then send medical personnel including a lead doctor called "the Ambassador" who spoke at least English, Chinese, Spanish and Russian. The beings were let out like schoolchildren, and lived at most a few days; they could breathe Earth's atmosphere, supporting the drone or biological-computer idea. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 32:32-33:49)*
- Fabrication theory: the program suspected the craft were fabricated elsewhere (like a huge 3D printer or self-replicating facility in the solar system) and left for humans to recover, and tasked NASA to look for the heat signature but found nothing conclusive; a "donation" theory that the craft are gifts is also favored, with a darker alternative that they were left lying around because they are easy to build. Gerb ties this to [[john-von-neumann]]'s self-replicating automata, NASA documents from the [[black-vault]] (a 1977 letter about a possible UFO fragment examined at Los Alamos, a late 1970s "UFO HEAP" analysis program) and [[eric-taber]]'s desert-found egg. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 35:08-38:06, 39:17-39:41)*
- Downing attempts: he mentions failed and successful attempts in the 1990s to down craft; one failure was in Iran, which Gerb thinks is the 1976 [[tehran-ufo-incident-1976]] (Major Jafari); the witness thinks harm to people is incidental. Craft can distort perception, so recovery operatives formed "anchors" (a shared mental picture such as a big oak tree and a gray boulder) to stay grounded; Gerb links this to [[gary-nolan]] and a leaked [[advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program]] slide. He does not know what the beings want. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 38:06-39:26)*
