The 1957-58 Lebanon crisis, as summarized by [[eyes-wide-open]] in chapter 2 of [[road-to-dallas]]: the US, facing rising Arab nationalist sentiment, intervened to prop up Lebanese President Camil Shamun and his successor Fouad Shahab, both Christian Maronites whose minority rule was increasingly tenuous. It matters to the wiki because [[david-atlee-phillips]], [[miles-copeland]] and possibly [[jack-cannon]] were in the region at the time. The host says he is not an expert and gives only a sketch. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 15:19)*

## Claims

- Framing: Egyptian president Nasser's pan-Arabism supported majority Arab elements; the US Marine intervention of mid-1958 is seen as a manifestation of the Eisenhower Doctrine ([[dwight-d-eisenhower]]). Host's reading: an attempt to curb Arab nationalism by keeping the US's chosen leader in charge and the Levantine states divided (Egypt and Syria briefly united as the United Arab Republic, 1958-61); Nasser's push, he says, threatened Washington and Israel. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 15:21, 16:21)*
- Host compares backing unpopular minority Christian leaders in Lebanon to Eisenhower-era backing of Ngo Dinh Diem in Vietnam ([[ngo-dinh-diem]]). *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 15:52)*
- CIA role: [[david-atlee-phillips]] says in his memoir *The Night Watch* that he and fellow officers in Beirut in 1957 supported Maronite candidates; host says this was likely "Operation PECTATE," speculated to be the CIA Lebanon operation of 1957-58 (he cites a memo on it). Fellow officer [[miles-copeland]], a "master at the good old bags of cash method" from the 1948 Italian elections ([[italian-election-1948]]); host: Langley assembled a "dream team of CIA electioneers." *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 17:12, 17:51)*
- Outcome: Shamun ended his term and was replaced by Fouad Shahab, a former French military officer and Lebanese army commander, also a Maronite. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 18:30)*
- Narcotics angle (host thesis, from Alfred McCoy): Beirut had long been a vector for raw opium/morphine for the old "French Connection"; McCoy, quoting a 1965 Federal Bureau of Narcotics study, describes Corsican networks importing morphine from Lebanon, refining it in Marseilles labs and exporting heroin to American mafia distributors via Canada and the Caribbean. Host argues that backing the francophile Shamun and Shahab protected these dope flows, since independent Arab leadership could have jeopardized the route. See [[corsican-mafia]], [[alfred-mccoy]], [[cia-drug-paradigm]]. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 18:51, 20:11)*
- Host ties this to Cuba: Batista's Cuba was a mob and narcotics transshipment hub until early 1959, so Phillips's Beirut work between his two Cuba tours gains significance (host's speculation). *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 20:32)*
