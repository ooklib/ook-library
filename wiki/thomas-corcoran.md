Thomas "Tommy the Cork" Corcoran is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as an FDR New Deal "Brain Trust"
insider, "the first modern lobbyist," and the hub of the [[china-lobby]]. **Name note:** the ASR
transcript renders the surname "Cochran" throughout; identification as Corcoran is my
normalization (the source's own bibliography cites a Washington Post article "about Tommy the
Cork"), kept here as an alias.

## Claims

- A lawyer and New Deal Brain Trust member who spent much of the war lobbying for defense
  contractors, specifically the company that became Bechtel (then a significant shipbuilder),
  headed at the time by [[john-mccone]]; the host says his insider connections in the FDR
  administration won them contracts and made McCone, Bechtel and himself richer, and calls this
  political corruption that "would mark Tommy's entire career." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 15:24-16:55)*
- In 1941 [[franklin-d-roosevelt]] tasked him with creating secret channels of aid to Chiang's
  forces that circumvented Congress and the bureaucracy: he created the China Defense Supplies
  company, a shell to administer the Asian lend-lease program, on paper merely its legal counsel
  but "running the whole show." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 17:01-17:40)*
- Met [[claire-chennault]] in 1941, persuaded FDR to approve the American Volunteer Group (the
  [[flying-tigers]]), and used his partner [[william-pawley]] to ship the planes. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 17:40-18:48)*
- Worked as campaign manager for [[lyndon-b-johnson]] in his failed 1941 and successful 1948
  Senate bids; the host speculates his influence-peddling shaped Johnson's back-room style. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 33:03-33:49)*
- Host: with Corcoran, the China Lobby "elevated the careers" of both Johnson and
  [[richard-nixon]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 33:49-34:06)*
