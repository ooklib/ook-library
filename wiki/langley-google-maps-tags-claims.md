Borland's claim that people employed at NASA Langley posted puzzles, labyrinths, pyramids and rock formations as photos on Google Maps building entries near where he saw the 2012 triangle, discussed on [[borland-michels-interview-2026]]. Borland offers no interpretation beyond "it's real", and thinks it could be employees joking.

## Claims

- Borland says he screenshotted images years ago, including a labyrinth at the NASA Langley hypersonic facilities complex, and that the photos have since been removed from Google Maps. Michels sees the screenshots on the show. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 41:08-41:46, 3:03:35-3:05:57)*
