[[jason-jorjani]]'s claim that religious and mythic traditions record a two-faction struggle over human status, which he identifies with the UFO phenomenon. Speculation, offered as his synthesis of Vallee, Zecharia Sitchin, Plato, Genesis and Hindu texts.

## Claims

- The phenomenon spans recorded history and probably prehistory and is inseparable from Judeo-Christian, Islamic, Egyptian, Vedic and other religions; Devas/Asuras, Elohim/rebel angels/Nephilim, Olympians/Titans and Anunnaki are "the same beings", with two factions in every tradition. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 33:47, 34:09)*
- A rebel line (Enki, Prometheus, Quetzalcoatl, the Eden serpent, Lucifer) gave humans forbidden knowledge; the ruling line (Elohim/Enlil/Devas) wants humans as workers and fears parity. Eden's nakedness is read as slave status. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 34:42, 35:34, 36:25)*
- Plato's Atlantis is the best account of the rebellion: a demigod society hybridizing with and uplifting humanity, punished by Zeus (Noah's flood); Genesis 6 and the Book of Enoch preserve the excised backstory. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 38:24, 39:28, 40:12, 40:51)*
- The Anunnaki are "as clearly there" in Sumerian texts as the Olympians in Greek, and created humans as servants after failed earlier models; Jorjani speculates reptilians are lab-made dinosaur hybrids from the same "Frankenstein laboratory" that produced the grays. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:33:33, 1:34:48, 1:35:30)*
- Michels (relaying Vallee) calls sky battles over Basel and Nuremberg just before the Renaissance evidence that the rebel faction "opened a space" for human progress; Jorjani says "the bad guys won" and the control system persists. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 45:59, 50:01)*
- Jorjani draws on Richard Thompson (*Alien Identities*) for the Vedic devas as UFO entities. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 59:15)*
- Book-of-Enoch, Diana Pasulka and Vallee context: Michels says Pasulka told him Vallee is interested in fallen angels; Jorjani recounts Vallee showing him a "history of Satan" book. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 41:38, 42:18)*
