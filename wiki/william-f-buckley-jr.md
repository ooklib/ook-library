William F. Buckley Jr. is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as a Yale contemporary of [[george-h-w-bush]], a [[skull-and-bones]] initiate, and a CIA officer under [[e-howard-hunt]] before becoming a conservative media figure. His father, William F. Buckley Sr., founded Pantek Oil (ASR), the first employer of [[george-de-mohrenschildt]].

## Claims

- Studied at Yale in the late 1940s-early 1950s, overlapping with Bush for a couple of years; a Skull and Bones initiate. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 22:05-22:14)*
- Served in the CIA from 1951 to 1953 under E. Howard Hunt at the Mexico City station, before founding National Review and Young Americans for Freedom (1960) and hosting *Firing Line* for over 30 years. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 22:14-22:38)*
- Host shows a *Firing Line* interview in which Hunt, after release from prison for Watergate, talks for over an hour about the agency's inner workings while Buckley "plays dumb"; host says such "CIA on CIA TV spots" make an "Operation Mockingbird type CIA media infiltration operation almost undeniable." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 22:38-23:11)*
- His father founded Pantek Oil Company in the 1920s, which hired George de Mohrenschildt in the 1940s. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 21:48-22:05)*
