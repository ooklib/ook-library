The 22 November 1963 assassination of President John F. Kennedy in Dallas. In this episode Eyes Wide Open covers only [[e-howard-hunt]]'s alleged presence and deathbed confession; the host says his own research indicates a fundamental mob element in the conspiracy. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 6:48-11:00)*

## Claims

- Hunt was reportedly in Dallas that day and resembles the third "tramp" (identity debated). *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 6:48-7:14)*
- Hunt's alleged deathbed conspiracy list (via Saint John Hunt, Rolling Stone 2007): Lyndon Johnson, [[cord-meyer]], David Atlee Phillips, Frank Sturgis, David Morales, Antonio Veciana, William Harvey, a French gunman on the grassy knoll. Host considers it half true and possibly a limited hangout. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 9:42-11:58)*
- Speculation about CIA involvement was fueled by Hunt, [[james-jesus-angleton]], [[richard-helms]], [[cord-meyer]] and [[john-mccone]] visiting [[allen-dulles]]'s house in 1962-63. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 8:20-9:07)*

- LAOS 3: the host says [[edward-lansdale]] was identified "by a reliable source" as the man walking in the three tramps photo in Dallas on the day of the 1963 killing; he defers it. The Kennedy administration is also credited with the 1963 overthrow of [[ngo-dinh-diem]]. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 52:58-53:17, 11:28-11:35)*
