The central thesis of [[jesse-michels]]'s September 2025 episode is that the history of rocketry and the space program is entangled with mysticism, ritual and possibly communication with [[non-human-intelligence]]. This page collects the episode's pattern claims about NASA symbolism, dates and personnel; Michels himself flags the launch-timing material as "highly speculative" and raises pareidolia as the skeptical reading. All of it is attributed claim, not established fact.

## Claims

- Freemasonry: Buzz Aldrin (a 33rd-degree Scottish Rite Mason) took communion and a Scottish Rite flag to the Moon; the Masonic Grand Lodge of Ohio claimed Apollo 11 established a Masonic body on the Moon. Michels lists other alleged Masons in NASA: John Glenn, James Webb, Kenneth Kleinknecht, Gordon Cooper, Walter Schirra, Donn Eisele, Gus Grissom, Thomas Stafford, James Irwin, [[edgar-mitchell]]. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 1:24-2:45, 40:20-41:45)*
- The "33" motif: a 1950s Disney/von Braun film shows a constructed-looking feature on the Moon's far side detected at 33 degrees; NASA's White Sands launch pad and Kennedy's runway are numbered 33. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 39:16-40:28, 44:36-44:52)*
- The Apollo patch: Michels says the "A" crossbar aligns with Orion's belt (Osiris to the Egyptians) and asks whether the A stood for Apollo or Asar (Osiris); program names (Juno, Mercury, Gemini, Apollo) mix pagan pantheons; Farouk El-Baz, the Apollo landing-site planner, had a father who was an Egyptologist. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 43:14-45:34)*
- Pagan calendar dates: the [[jack-parsons]] group's first liquid-fuel test (31 Oct 1936, Samhain) and Explorer 1 (31 Jan 1958, Imbolc/Candlemas) bookend early US spaceflight; Apollo 8 launched on the winter solstice; Apollo 11's landing fell under Aquarius. Michels says the timing "seems to follow an occult template" even if not consciously planned. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 45:49-49:06)*
- The [[nrol-16-launch-2005]] (Walpurgis Night, occult patch, Big Dipper alignment) is the modern example, via [[john-ramirez]] and [[peter-levenda]].
- The Big Dipper: Michels connects it to Close Encounters, the Betty Hill abduction (via Jacques Vallee), Ryan Graves's pilots' reports, Egyptian and Taoist ritual, the Thule Society, and Chris Bledsoe's "the Lady" and "Guardians" (also the term used by the US Space Force, whose Space Delta 7 emblem shows a bear holding a key before the Dipper). *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 52:08-53:47)*
- Personnel: [[tim-taylor-nasa]] and his alleged time-travel "adjustment team" links to [[thomas-townsend-brown]]. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 54:05-56:37)*
- Legal backdrop: the National Aeronautics and Space Act (29 July 1958) is civilian but exempts activities "peculiar to or primarily associated with weapon systems, military operations, or the defense of the United States", which Michels reads as allowing NASA to withhold any breakthrough, including contact with non-human intelligence, indefinitely; he likens it to the 1954 Atomic Energy Act's "special nuclear material" secrecy (with an interjected voice, likely [[harald-malmgren]] or another guest, agreeing). *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 37:03-38:34)* See [[nasa]].
- Military-industrial echoes: [[james-t-ryder]] (Lockheed Martin) gave a 2018 talk "The Garment of God" on mysticism and technology; Jeffrey Kripal says Charles Chase of Lockheed's revolutionary projects invited him to brief Skunk Works engineers on levitation; see [[secret-machines-project]]. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 56:56-59:18)*
