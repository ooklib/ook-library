"Moment of Contact" is [[james-fox]]'s feature documentary on the [[varginha-ufo-incident-1996]]; the December 2025 re-cut is titled "Moment of Contact: New Revelations of Alien Encounters". In [[fox-ramsey-interview-2026]] Fox walks host [[chris-ramsey]] through it scene by scene with added material that did not make the cut.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says he first covered the case in a project called The Phenomenon, cut it after five or six years of work for lack of critical witnesses, and then decided to make a dedicated film. He compares deleting it to "killing your darlings". *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 21:50-22:59)*
- Ramsey says the new cut is an update of the previous version; Fox deleted the old first section (Tuba City, UFOs over Brazil 1986, photographic evidence) to make room for entirely new material, and added crash-retrieval context (Magenta, Roswell, Kecksburg, Kingman, Bentwaters) plus the congressional hearings and [[eric-davis]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 10:53-11:11, 1:25:58-1:26:44, 1:58:18-1:59:22)*
- New in this cut: maps and Google Earth graphics of the crash site, the farm family and the disc-sighting family, the police chief and radiologist and other interviews, and the pathologists' bacteria analysis. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 27:50-28:35, 38:01-38:15)*
- Fox first heard of the case around the time of his second film (Out of the Blue) and dismissed it; Jeff Sagansky, later Stanton Friedman, then [[marco-leal]] pushed him, and he has visited Brazil 15-16 years with three trips in 2025. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 12:07-16:44, 15:27-15:46)*
- Fox says the makers spent five or six years persuading Daza and others and describes their work as ongoing, with more doctors and a mayoral screening planned. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 24:37-24:46, 1:00:48-1:01:26)*
- Fox says the film has a companion effort of "a movement" with a big US announcement planned for early January. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 2:04:26-2:05:29)*
- Fox says of the reception: "I don't care what people think about this documentary if they don't watch it." *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:59:45-1:59:58)*
