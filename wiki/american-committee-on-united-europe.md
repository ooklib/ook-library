The American Committee on United Europe (ACUE), founded in 1948 with [[william-donovan]] as chairman, was described by Eyes Wide Open as a CIA cutout that promoted European federalism (the Pan-Europa movement) through front groups and lobbying networks. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 22:58-24:16)*

## Claims

- Founded 1948 by [[william-donovan]] (then legally a private Wall Street lawyer but still working with the CIA); [[allen-dulles]] was vice chairman and [[walter-bedell-smith]] (future CIA director, 1950) also served; per a 2000 Telegraph article Donovan hired ex-OSS figures who moved in and out of the CIA. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 22:58-23:50)*
- Per historian [[richard-aldrich]] (1997 article), front groups seeking ACUE/CIA support had to back the Council of Europe, the Marshall Plan/Mutual Security Act/NATO, West German integration into Europe, and 'must not be in opposition to the foreign policy of the United States'. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 23:50-24:53)*
- Funded primarily by the CIA, with money also allegedly from the Rockefeller and Ford foundations; run in practice via [[thomas-braden]]'s [[cia-international-organizations-division]]. Eyes Wide Open argues it created a favorable environment for Gladio. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 25:32-26:20)*
- Cord Meyer later said European leaders who solicited US aid demanded no publicity, since Communist propaganda could portray them as American puppets. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 27:55-28:23)*
