An unverified document that circulated online around 2021-22 (including a 4chan post), purportedly pages of a longer classified version of the [[uap-task-force]] report prepared for Congress. [[uap-gerb]] treats it with a grain of salt but finds it intriguing in [[gerb-flyby-video]]; nothing here confirms it is real.

## Claims

- Comms log: it details the cockpit voice recording of a fighter (Club 11) during an AWACS-directed intercept (AWACS call sign Brigham, plus an unclear third party BLK); the pilot and weapon system operator see a bogey closing at 2,820 knots, countermeasures activate, contacts "merge", and the crew then see an object hanging 10 to 15 ft off the starboard wing, whereupon BLK tells them not to change velocity or vector and to open their blue envelopes; Gerb says this matches the FLYBY footage. *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 9:00-11:43)*
- Authenticity clues: he says the author would need to be very familiar with pilot lingo, and that the document distinguishes aerial from submerged phenomena, which lends some credence. *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 11:43-13:07)*
- Other page: section 5 "behavioral data analysis" allegedly covers 1,292 UFO cases analyzed by joint elements of ODNI, NSA, DHS and SAP-cleared DoD experts, concluding UAP are remote or autonomous drones or a form of mechanical life, with an "organic species" deploying UAP whose flight performance shows a "stepped virtual evolution process". *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 12:24-13:32)*
- Names: it mentions M. Harmon, S. McCaron and K. Shakoya (the latest contribution being 2020), a "blackout flower report" and "layer three behavioral assessment"; Gerb found almost nothing, only a Hillary S. McCaron, a research neuroscientist at the US Army Medical Research Institute of Chemical Defense, and assumes pseudonyms. *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 13:23-14:29)*
- Appendix F section 4B is titled "DOD 1992 through 2017 high value witness interviews"; if real and if the comms log describes the FLYBY footage, Gerb says the footage would be a high value witness testimony and one of the most crucial pieces ever. *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 14:29-15:33)*
