Dylan Borland's account of a 100-150 ft black triangular craft near the NASA hangar at Langley Air Force Base, Virginia, in summer 2012, told under oath to Congress in September 2025 and expanded here. This page records his details from the American Alchemy interview ([[borland-michels-interview-2026]]); the earlier [[uap-gerb]] treatment is on [[dylan-borland]]. Unverified single-witness testimony.

## Account

- Around 1:00-2:00 a.m. on a weather-standby night, Borland was outside his barracks smoking and on the phone with a friend who was a federal officer (an 1811 criminal investigator, unnamed). He saw a light "pop up" near the NASA hangar, thinking it a weather balloon (he says balloons were tested from that hangar), and it flew across the base to him. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 26:01-27:55)*
- "A triangle manifests around it": a white light that became a triangle, stopping about 100 ft in front of and above him for 2-3 minutes. He says he cannot say whether it was active camouflage, decloaking or something else, and only reports the sequence. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 28:37-29:33, 34:42-34:54)*
- Description: a 100-150 ft equilateral triangle, flat bottom, 90-degree edges, a light on each corner and a center light two to three times larger, black metallic-flake finish, and a gold, transparent, lava-lamp-like flow pulsing around the surface. He never saw the top. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 29:53-31:12)*
- Physical effects: static electricity, a smell "like a lightning storm", and his phone overheating, freezing and draining, then recovering once the craft left. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 29:24-29:40, 31:35-33:00)*
- Departure: the center light flashed and the craft shot up, in his estimate from about 100 ft to at least 30,000 ft in 2-3 seconds (Michels computes roughly 10,000-15,000 ft/s), appearing to be a star or satellite before drifting east over the Atlantic. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 31:19-31:35, 33:05-33:51)*
- He does not know whether it came from the NASA hangar. Elsewhere he suggests it was "a task" or scan by a non-human craft at the right place and time. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 40:39-40:56, 2:47:06-2:47:29)*

## Interpretation offered

- Borland thinks the glow was ionization of the atmosphere around a charged, probably electromagnetic or nuclear-powered craft, controlling speed and direction. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 38:28-38:59)* [[jesse-michels]] connects this to [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s megavolt ionization experiments and ozone formation, matching the thunderstorm smell. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 39:06-39:38)*
- Michels compares it to a [[david-grush]] anecdote of a 300 ft triangle over a Norfolk-area worker's car that milky-ed the paint with UV, which Borland places within a couple of years of his own sighting. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 36:24-37:29)*
- Borland says he knows it was not human-made or "ours" because [[aaro]] recorded the sighting memorandum for record as unclassified while classifying his other drawings, which he reads as proof it was not a US program. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:20:59-2:21:22, 1:36:04-1:36:22)* Michels floats a 40-chess counter-hypothesis that leaving it unclassified was a deliberate move. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:21:39-2:21:54)*
- Gerb's rival reading (an electro-optic cloak on a human-built airframe, see [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b]] and [[flying-triangle-sightings]]) is not discussed here; Borland says he only reports what he saw.

## Aftermath

He went back to work, and coworkers and a tech sergeant (a former loadmaster) reacted by warning him not to talk about it. A security-forces "Raven" told him of a C-17 landing in North Dakota with a box surrounded by guards for hours. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 54:53-57:05)* He was later assumed by a colleague at [[bae-systems]] to be read into a legacy program because he talked about the triangle (*([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 58:32-59:02)*), which led to the events on [[borland-whistleblower-reprisal-claims]].
