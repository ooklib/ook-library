Claim, originated by [[ashton-forbes]], that footage purporting to show three orbs circling the flight path of Malaysia Airlines Flight MH370 (which disappeared over Southeast Asia and drew months of CNN coverage) indicates the civilian aircraft was "teleported" out of the sky by man-made exotic technology, rather than crashing conventionally. Entirely attributed and unverified.
