*Sentinels of Ether* is a novel of about 120,000 words attributed to [[jake-barber]], ghost-written under the pen name "Alva Douglas" (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 8:20-8:29)*). It was submitted in 20-page chunks to the Defense Office of Prepublication and Security Review ([[dopsr-prepublication-review-claims]]), and [[jesse-michels]] says the Pentagon spent six months on the first 20 pages and returned seven pages of redactions, from about 10 percent of the manuscript (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 8:29-8:53, 16:32)*). Michels links the manuscript in the episode description and argues the novel is a disguised account of real legacy-program operations. This is Michels' framing and Barber's claim; the book is a work of fiction and none of the plot is independently verified.

## Plot as narrated by Michels

- Alex McEwen, a tier-1 operator escorting about two dozen refugees near [[coyame-mexico]], uses the verbal IFF code "Matchbox", is met by three armed men who call him by name, is scanned with a bioscanner and executed. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 20:39-26:13)*
- The refugees are loaded into a shipping container with a mini-split HVAC unit slung under a helicopter; a Predator feed shows this to [[jsoc]] at Pope Air Force Base, where the commander (named as General Dale Steinmaker) asks the Secretary of Defense whether "agency guys" are in Mexico and is told "Black on black, sir". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 22:53-27:52)*
- F-18s from White Sands intercept on guard frequency 243 MHz, then lose power (drone feed and cockpit) near a canyon that dead-ends in a 1,500 ft mountain; the pilot ejects while the helicopters pass through a mountainside that pixelates from 3D to 2D and vanishes, into a chamber of stone and steel beams. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 28:20-37:10)* See [[coyame-mountain-base-narrative]].

## What Michels says is real, and what he proposes to check

- Refugees as "psionic cargo" mirrors [[michael-herrera-indonesia-encounter-2009]] and Barber's account of prospecting for psionic assets abroad ([[psionic-assets-and-breeding-claims]]). *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 22:04-23:13, 42:07-43:24)*
- The EMP shoot-down of the drone and F-18s matches the alleged use of temporary flight restrictions to lure and down craft ([[ufo-baiting-and-directed-energy-claims]], [[barber-toughbooks-operation-claims]]). *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 14:55-15:26)*
- Michels lists FOIA targets if the incident is real: NORAD, US Northern Command, JSOC, SOCOM and the Navy, asking for air-defense logs on unknown aircraft in the "Coyame corridor" that ignored a Navy intercept on 243 MHz in 2004. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 30:44-31:16)*
- The 2004 retrieval into a disguised Chihuahua base was already flagged by UAP Gerb; see [[jake-barber]] and [[deep-underground-military-bases]].
