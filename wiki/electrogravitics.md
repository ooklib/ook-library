Electrogravitics is the label for coupling of electric fields to gravity, coined around [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s work (the "lowbrow applied version" per Eric Weinstein's framing).

## Claims (jm/townsend-brown)

- Host thesis: "lowbrow" terms like electrogravitics were used to stigmatize vital off-the-books physics so academia and the public would not investigate; he compares it to Boeing/Lockheed calling their work "rocket science". *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 16:48-18:17)*
- Bill Gunston allegedly attributed the [[b-2-stealth-bomber]]'s performance to electrogravitic propulsion. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 28:48-29:56)*
- A 1988 [[science-applications-international-corporation]] paper for [[edwards-air-force-base]] reportedly found James Woodward's work, built on Brown's capacitor research, showed theoretical validity, and that Ferris Williams' 5D theory could unify fields; [[eric-davis]] concedes an Air Force study derived an electrogravitic coupling from a 5D continuum. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 1:30:28-1:32:13)*
