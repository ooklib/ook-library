Dennis Mariani ("Marani" in the transcript, spelling unverified) is the man [[bob-lazar]] says was his supervisor and constant escort when he began work for [[eg-and-g]] special projects: a short-haired, ex-Marine-looking man with no sense of humor who "followed me everywhere" and stared at him during the bus ride to S4 *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 19:36-20:14, 24:00-24:18)*. Lazar says Mariani flew with him on the Janet flight to Groom Lake, sat in the briefing room while Lazar read the blue project folders, led him in through the first open hangar door where a guard rebuked Lazar for touching the craft, and later told him to walk under the hovering craft *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 19:36-19:51, 41:12-41:34, 1:00:16-1:00:40, 1:07:31-1:07:50)*. He left once Lazar was introduced to [[barry-s4-lab-partner]] *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 40:06-40:16)*.

Ramsey says an anonymous, verified former EG&G contract pilot contacted the channel and recalled meeting a man he nicknamed "John" (Dennis) more than a dozen times, but then dropped out before recording; Lazar compares this with witnesses that [[george-knapp]] lines up and then loses *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 20:22-21:53)*. Treat this as an unverified second-hand claim.
