Sheffield Edwards is the CIA Office of Security official [[eyes-wide-open]] names as the working-level manager of the [[cia-mafia-assassination-plots]], picked by [[richard-bissell]] from "a somewhat obscure section of the agency".

## Claims

- Former Army man; joined in 1947 while it was still the Central Intelligence Group ([[central-intelligence-group]]) under General Hoyt Vandenberg (ASR "hoit Vandenberg"); host: early days saw Army officers installed in senior intelligence roles. Graduated West Point in 1923, the same year as [[joseph-caldwell-king]]. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:00:16-1:01:12)*
- Started at the CIA on Project Bluebird (renamed Artichoke), an early brainwashing/hallucinogen experimentation program that the host calls a precursor to, and in some ways "more sickening" than, MK Ultra; likely brought him into contact with [[sidney-gottlieb]]. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:01:44-1:02:23)*
- Tasked James "Big Jim" O'Connell (ex-FBI until 1947, CIA Office of Security from 1951) to contact [[robert-maheu]] (his former FBI colleague); the cash reward was $150,000. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:02:31-1:03:09)*
