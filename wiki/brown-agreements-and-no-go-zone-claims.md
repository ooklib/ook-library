Claims by [[matthew-brown]] on [[jesse-michels]]' show that governments have some agreement with a non-human "third party" and that the legacy program transmits this only as an oral tradition. All are attributed and unverified; Brown says he cannot speak to the validity of the lore, and only reports a "line of narrative" in the legacy program.

## Claims

- Brown believes humans made agreements with the beings, arguing from strategic behavior: the secrecy and lack of disclosure among rival states are not explainable otherwise. He points to open-source analogues: the outer space treaty duty to notify of UFO detections, and the 1971 US-Soviet arrangement on coordinating around UFOs in sensitive airspace (his recollection of dates). *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:07:58-1:09:29)*
- He says he learned that it is the legacy program's oral tradition that an agreement was struck between the US and a third party in possession of the technology, in the Truman-Eisenhower era, with two parts: areas within US borders the US must avoid (no development, military, commercial or human activity, "someone else's territory"), and access to and communication with humans of their choice. He frames it as a business agreement rather than a ratified treaty. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:11:10-1:12:37, 1:13:28-1:14:12)*
- He is skeptical of detailed versions such as the [[greada-treaty-1954]] (Eisenhower at Edwards AFB, see [[edwards-air-force-base]]), expecting disinformation to have been injected, but sees a core truth. Michels floats mountains (Mount Zyle, Mount Hayes, Mount Perdido, Mount Shasta, Secret Mountain, Inyangani) and remote-viewer [[pat-price]]-era targets as possible no-go zones; Brown finds it plausible and suggests correlating hotspots with unexploited desirable areas. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:12:37-1:14:33, 1:27:02-1:28:21)*
- He thinks the US gains little, is "submitting", and that the beings would not need agreements unless they wanted to make humans aware of "the rules". *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:09:54-1:10:48)*
- Oral tradition: after a decision to stop briefing new legacy-program entrants on its history (as [[dylan-borland]] said), there is a split between those briefed before and after, and between those wanting reform and those wanting nothing out. [[david-grush]] under oath was asked about agreements and could not give detail. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:14:40-1:16:51)*
- He believes the parties are "juniors" in a partnership between humans and a third party. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 3:27:43-3:28:19)*
- Michels ties access to humans to [[dan-sherman]]'s [[project-preserve-destiny]] account, which Brown calls credible (he says it is one of the very few UFO books he has read, and was recommended by insiders) and evidence of the agreements' implementation; he also cites the leaked [[kit-green]]-[[ed-may]] call about contactees and [[andrija-puharich]]'s "Council of Nine". *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:16:56-1:22:13)*
