Frederick Valentich was a young Australian pilot who vanished in a light plane over Bass Strait in 1978 after saying "it's not an airplane". [[ross-coulthart]] uses it as context for the [[kaikoura-ufo-incident-1978]] and for the later skeptic argument that excited witnesses are primed by the last big UFO story.

## Claims

- Valentich flew out of Moorabbin (Melbourne) in a Cessna over Bass Strait, and the haunting audio ends with his statement that it was not an airplane; the official explanation is that he crashed into the water, perhaps flying upside down, which Coulthart says is implausible for that aircraft. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 11:59-12:56)*
- Coulthart is skeptical of the farmer's hearsay that a craft commandeered the plane, and of a photograph of a distant object taken at sunset (Ramsey says he sees a fly). *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 12:56-13:39)*
- Coulthart says he would once have argued that everyone in New Zealand and Australia was hyped by Valentich and mistook natural phenomena at Kaikoura; the declassified files changed his view. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 13:39-14:35)*
- He says skeptics dismiss the [[westall-ufo-incident-1966]] students as excited children primed by the Valentich case, though Westall (1966) predates Valentich (1978), an inconsistency in the transcript as spoken. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 30:07-30:21)*
