The 10th director of the [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]] — the sitting director who
officially declassified the NRO's existence in 1992. Joined the CIA/NRO under Program B as an
engineer before rising to director. *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md) 2:20:14–2:20:23,
2:37:21–2:37:41)*

## Claims

- Per his own account, entered the intelligence community "a little indirectly": left the Air
  Force, went to [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]], worked on the ground sensor program widely used in
  Vietnam, met CIA people doing similar work, and was hired into the NRO side of CIA's Office of
  Development Engineering because of his electro-optical Air Force background.
  *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md) 2:37:41–2:38:22)*
- A "leaders of the NRO" celebration page reportedly quotes Faga with a line Gerb calls
  suspiciously on-the-nose: something to the effect that "the best intelligence is intelligence
  gathered with ideas that physics don't conform to." *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md)
  3:06:10–3:06:50)*
