ZR/RIFLE (ASR "ZRRIFLE"/"Z Rifle") was, per [[eyes-wide-open]] and the 1975 [[church-committee]] materials, the CIA's "executive action" capability for assassinations, organized in February 1961 under [[william-harvey]] at [[richard-bissell]]'s request. It institutionalized the earlier, off-the-books anti-Castro plots described in [[cia-mafia-assassination-plots]]. Scoped here to the `ewa/jfk-cia` primer. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 29:34-29:42, 1:03:38-1:04:07)*

## Claims

- Per the Church Committee timeline, ZR/RIFLE was launched in February 1961, "only about a month after Kennedy entered office"; Bissell asked Harvey, just recalled from Berlin, to establish the executive-action capability. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:03:53-1:04:07, 1:06:22-1:06:43)*
- Host: it is the most detailed primary source available, a 1967 CIA Inspector General report ordered by then-DCI [[richard-helms]] (144-page released package); the cover page says all other copies were destroyed and the committee received the "ribbon copy." Host's speculation: Helms had it drafted as a "limited hangout" for a future inquiry, and it shows Helms as an observer of the Harvey/[[sheffield-edwards]] plots rather than a prime mover, shifting blame to the "disgraced" Harvey. Host says to treat the CIA-authored report with caution and that it includes outlandish plots he earlier speculated were made up. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:04:07-1:06:22)*
- The IG report's terminology: "phase one" of the "gambling syndicate operation" (the Mafia) covers the plots under the Nixon-Dulles leadership (from late 1959), before Harvey; "phase two" is everything from February 1961 organized under ZR/RIFLE. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:06:43-1:07:41)*
- Per the Church timeline, Bissell said he was "twice urged by the White House" to establish the capability. Host, assuming JFK did not make the request, names two "prime suspects": [[mcgeorge-bundy]] (Bissell's Yale pupil who had private talks with him) and [[robert-f-kennedy]] (citing Douglas Valentine, *The Strength of the Wolf*: RFK's office proposed covert clemency deals to mobster Norman Rothman in June 1961 for help with Castro attempts). Host: this is inference; JFK himself repeatedly tried to shut the assassination program down. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:01:54-1:02:21, 1:07:41-1:09:55)*
- Harvey "took over the criminal syndicate context" in mid-November 1961 (about ten months after ZR/RIFLE was organized), reintegrating [[johnny-roselli]], [[santo-trafficante]] and others; host repeats his alternative reading that the CIA was simply extending the mob figures protection from prosecution under national-security cover. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:09:55-1:10:52)*
- Flagged inconsistency in the source: the host places Harvey at the head of Task Force W "early the next year in 1962" (29:42-29:54) but also describes him as already "put in charge of Task Force W" when ZR/RIFLE was institutionalized in 1961 (1:03:38-1:03:45) and as managing it in "1961" (1:17:28-1:17:40). *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 29:42-29:54, 1:03:38-1:03:53, 1:17:28-1:17:40)*
- Kennedy's stance: JFK "continuously tried to shut down" the assassination program; the CIA "proceeded in quiet defiance." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:01:32-1:02:21)*
