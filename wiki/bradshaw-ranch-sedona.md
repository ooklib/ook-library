Bradshaw Ranch in Secret Canyon near Sedona, Arizona, and the nearby Secret Mountain are described by [[jesse-michels]] in [[sentinels-of-ether-michels-episode-2026]] as ground zero for Sedona's high strangeness (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:22:06-1:23:11)*). The claims below are anecdotal and unverified.

## Claims

- A book, *Merging Dimensions*, covers a rumored portal on the ranch; witnesses report cargo planes descending toward mountains where nothing exists, lights shooting from canyons and men with military-style guns. The ranch was sold to the US National Forest for climate research in partnership with the [[department-of-energy]] and [[oak-ridge-national-laboratory]]. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:22:47-1:23:24)*
- [[ross-coulthart]] says there is a "deep underground military bunker built in the 1950s under Secret Mountain", and he watched a golden orb escorted by helicopters rise from behind it at 1:30 a.m. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:23:24-1:24:23)*
- Michels cites Terra Exploration Group geophysical and magnetometry surveys showing a straight man-made tunnel about 50 feet in diameter from Secret Mountain to Bradshaw Ranch to the front gate of the Clarkdale cement plant (which owner John Bradshaw says was guarded like a military site), plus large electrical power going underground; a ground-penetrating-radar check found a 50-ft-wide reinforced concrete structure just under the road. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:24:23-1:25:35)*
- Coulthart's sources allege a portal in a highly guarded facility "under the US Forest Service cover", with men patrolling and witnesses who saw people "walking into literally a wall". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 58:14-58:40)*
- Hikers reportedly meet armed men in unmarked black uniforms in Coconino National Forest, the "same ghost force" seen at other hot zones. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:25:44-1:26:10)* Compare [[deep-underground-military-bases]] and [[richard-sauder]] (whose DUMB book Michels recommends).
