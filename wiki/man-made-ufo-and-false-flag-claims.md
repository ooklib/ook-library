[[steven-greer]] divides "UAP" into three groups: real extraterrestrial vehicles ("ETVs" or NHI craft), man-made "alien reproduction vehicles" and lookalikes (the majority of what is seen, he says), and extradimensional beings that are not craft. He says the three are deliberately blended to confuse the public and officials, and that the man-made craft enable staged threats. This is Greer's claim; [[jesse-michels]] raises objections but does not dispute it directly. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 22:10-22:32, 52:09-52:19, 1:06:33-1:07:07)*

## Claims

- Gravity control: Greer says the US mastered it in October 1954 (per Richard Foch) after Nazi disc prototypes and [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s electrogravitics; Michels doubts scalable zero-point energy. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 10:56-11:14, 16:45-17:10)*
- Pilots and a manager of Edwards-based pilots are said to be his sources; craft are flown at about 1 g inside an "electromagnetic space-time bubble". *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 28:16-29:17)*
- Staged abductions: he says he met people who served on "abduction squads" made to look like aliens, that abductees were funneled through cutouts to [[budd-hopkins]], [[david-jacobs]] and [[john-mack]], and that cattle mutilations are human work; he says [[betty-and-barney-hill-abduction-1961]] was "very likely" staged, citing psychological-warfare chief C. D. Jackson visiting the Hills two weeks later (Michels supplies this detail). *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:07:25-1:07:55, 2:05:42-2:06:11, 50:26-50:52, 2:14:17-2:14:33)*
- A CIA document allegedly held by [[jacques-vallee]] (from his 1992 journal, 1985 entry) describes operations in Argentina and Brazil to abduct peasants for "psychological warfare value"; Greer says Vallee refused to release it. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:12:50-2:14:01)*
- Tic Tac: Greer says [[david-fravor]]'s 2004 object was a [[skunk-works]] craft, that a Lockheed executive later told Fravor so, and that a witness saw one offloaded from a ship in 1991; he says it was a test of how the military would react. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:04:12-1:05:24)*
- A "Raytheon triangle" at Fort Irwin with holographic decoys, whose witness was reportedly abducted and tortured, is presented as a possible template for a hoaxed alien invasion. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:32:41-2:33:26)*
- Norton Air Force Base "baby, mama and papa bear" craft shown at a 1988 air show came from the late 1950s, per Greer citing [[brad-sorenson]]. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 3:02:08-3:02:59)*
- SDI: he says Colonel Homeman told him the alien threat was added to sell Star Wars to Reagan ("we knew that was false") and that [[strategic-defense-initiative]] was a black-budget front. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:13:59-1:14:19, 3:18:30-3:19:09)*

- He says most of what [[jake-barber]] and Michels's guests encounter is human technology, and that he separates from anyone who mixes known false information into testimony. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft.
