The 1893 railroad-driven US depression, told in [[empire-of-shadows-rothschild-documentary-2023]] as the moment the [[rothschild-family]] and [[house-of-morgan]] jointly supplied the US government with gold.

## Claims

- On 9 May 1893 an over-built railroad industry collapsed and produced one of the worst depressions in history. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 25:33, 25:47)*
- President Grover Cleveland refused expansionary policy to maintain gold convertibility; with reserves days from depletion he called on [[j-pierpont-morgan]]. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 25:59, 26:28, 26:49)*
- Morgan turned to the Rothschilds, who had for three decades invested in gold and silver mining, and bought 3.5 million ounces for the government with 65 million dollars of bond proceeds; the narrator's chapter title says "Bailing Out The Federal Reserve", though the text says "for the Fed" loosely. The narrator does not allege a plot. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 27:02, 27:28)*
- The narrator concludes America thereby "enters the world scene as a new empire". *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 28:09)*
