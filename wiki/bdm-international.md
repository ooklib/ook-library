BDM International is a defense contractor with a secure facility in McLean, Virginia, where a
1985 "Advanced Theoretical Physics Working Group" conference (classified via DOE controls,
supervised by [[john-b-alexander|Col. John B. Alexander]]) was held — one year after Gen.
[[albert-stubblebine|Albert Stubblebine]] joined BDM International.
*([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 46:09–46:49)*

## Claims

- [[oki-shannon|Oki Shannon]], former manager of special projects at
  [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los Alamos]] and a figure in the
  [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]], attended and took notes at this conference.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 46:09–46:24)*
- [[ronald-moultrie|Ronald Moultrie]] (who later recommended AARO's formation) sat on BDM's
  board and reportedly scrubbed this from his LinkedIn. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  29:13–29:47)*
