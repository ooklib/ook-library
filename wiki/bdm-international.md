BDM International is a defense contractor with a secure facility in McLean, Virginia, where a
1985 "Advanced Theoretical Physics Working Group" conference (classified via DOE controls,
supervised by [[john-b-alexander|Col. John B. Alexander]]) was held — one year after Gen.
[[albert-stubblebine|Albert Stubblebine]] joined BDM International.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 46:09–46:49)*

## Claims

- [[oke-shannon|Oke Shannon]], former manager of special projects at
  [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los Alamos]] and a figure in the
  [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]], attended and took notes at this conference.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 46:09–46:24)*
- [[ronald-moultrie|Ronald Moultrie]] (who later recommended AARO's formation) sat on BDM's
  board and reportedly scrubbed this from his LinkedIn. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  29:13–29:47)*
- Acquired by [[trw|TRW]] in 1997. BDM's board also included Rear Admiral
  [[sumner-shapiro|Sumner Shapiro]], former Director of Naval Intelligence, who
  [[bobby-ray-inman|Bobby Ray Inman]] recommended in the late 1980s as a point of contact on
  recovered UFOs, and who reportedly confirmed to NASA mission specialist Bob Oechsler that he had
  studied recovered nonhuman vehicles up close. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 44:42–45:54)*
- [[mary-sturtevant|Mary K. Sturtevant]] worked at BDM on defense/arms-control research and
  technology transfer before her government career. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  2:47:45–2:48:15)*
