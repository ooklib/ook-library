The Nazca lines are geoglyphs in the desert of southern Peru, mostly animal figures, estimated created between 500 BC and 500 AD by removing stones and pebbles to expose differently colored soil. Gerb (ASR "NASA lines") presents them as the standard ancient-astronaut example. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 11:07-11:46)*

## Claims

- [[erich-von-daniken]] proposed in *Chariots of the Gods* (1968) that the lines were landing sites steering extraterrestrial ships, claiming they resemble modern airport layouts. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 12:09-12:41)*
- Gerb favors the prosaic explanation: religious offerings to gods who could see them from above, and the lines are visible from nearby hills, not only from the air; he says the theory is nonetheless fun, noting Ancient Aliens has about 30 seasons. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 11:53-13:05)*
