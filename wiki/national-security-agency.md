The National Security Agency, one of the US's "big five" intelligence agencies (alongside NRO,
CIA, DIA, NGA), is described as having such deep ties to
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] that SAIC is nicknamed "NSA West" — per
a 2018 *Baltimore Sun* article and James Bamford's *The Shadow Factory*.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 21:11–21:45)*

## Claims

- Gerb has separately implicated the NSA in UFO legacy program operations via gatekeeping
  (Tactical Reconnaissance Engineering Assessment Team, "TREAT") and management of alien
  reproduction vehicle programs like the TR3B. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 21:45–21:52)*
- Sponsored SAIC's 2002 **Project Trailblazer** — a mass-communications-data-analysis system
  that ballooned from $280M to billions amid fraud/waste/abuse complaints from NSA employees
  including [[william-binney|William Binney]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:17:04–1:17:45)*
- An unnamed retired Army public-affairs officer told Gerb he was more "terrified" of the NSA
  than of the CIA. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 21:52–22:01)*

## Relationships

- Deeply entwined ("NSA West") with [[science-applications-international-corporation]];
  sponsored SAIC's Project Trailblazer.
