Manuel Ray was a Cuban engineer who, per [[eyes-wide-open]], worked at the [[havana-hilton]] as head of maintenance and project manager under [[frank-brandstetter]] while secretly running sabotage operations for Castro's forces, and later became a CIA-backed Cuban exile leader in Florida who founded the Junta Revolucionaria Cubana (ASR "Honta Revolutionaria Kubana"; "JURE" as spoken, "Jur"). *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 34:03-34:33)*

## Claims

- Brandstetter suspected Ray of working with Castro but kept him as an informant; on 1 Jan. 1959 Ray warned him of the mob's plan to burn the hotel. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 34:33-34:42, 40:01-40:16)*
- The 29 July 1961 Senate internal-security subcommittee deposition of George Finegan (ASR "Finnegan", a University of Miami aviation-management student with deep contacts in Miami Cuban groups; host guesses FBI or CIA informant) places [[jack-cannon]] as a man close to Ray in the Miami Cuban underground, operating a rebel radio transmitter and with "transportation, high-speed boats, arms" ("the goodies"). Host: possibly Brandstetter introduced Ray and Cannon, or vice versa in Havana; explicitly speculation. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 34:49-38:31)*
- Host says he will draw on the 1961 Finegan deposition later; it also describes Finegan's knowledge of Sturgis and Hemming groups. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 35:34-36:10)*

## Relationships
