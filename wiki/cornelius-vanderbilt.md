Cornelius Vanderbilt (died 1877 aged 82) was an American shipping and railroad magnate, called "The Commodore" and, by [[most-brutal-businessman-vanderbilt-documentary-2022]], "the original robber baron". The wiki records the documentary's account, which is conventional business history rather than conspiracy claims.

## Claims

- Left school at 11 to work on his father's New York Harbour ferry; at 16 he bought his first sailboat with a $100 loan from his mother, repaid by clearing a rocky field with the help of local kids paid in free rides. *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 1:07, 1:21, 1:57)*
- Business method: undercut every competitor (at times free trips), bankrupt them, buy them out cheaply or be paid to leave, and reinvest all profits. Example: the Steamboat Association paid him $100,000 to stay off the Hudson. The narrator calls this the "capitalist blueprint", comparable to Bezos and Amazon. *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 2:06, 3:53, 4:31, 4:53)*
- Won a War of 1812 contract carrying soldiers; marketed his steamboats as "The People's Line" but was always regarded as an outsider by the wealthy elite. *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 2:56, 5:06, 5:20)*
- California gold rush: ran passengers via Nicaragua under an exclusive government deal, a route said to be about 600 miles shorter than via Panama. *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 6:15, 6:23)*
- Personal life: married his cousin at 19 and had 13 children; allegedly had his wife briefly committed to an asylum and later committed an epileptic son. After she died in 1868 he married another cousin, Frank Armstrong Crawford, over 40 years younger. *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 3:07, 3:23, 9:14, 9:40)*
- Sold his ships for railroads, consolidating independent lines into an interconnected network and, per the narrator, the largest railroad in the world. *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 10:13, 11:04)*
- Gave $1 million to found Vanderbilt University, described as the largest US charitable gift at the time. *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 11:07)*
- Died in 1877 with a fortune of about $100 million (about $200 billion today), reportedly more than the US Treasury held and about one-ninth of US currency in circulation; about 90% went to his oldest son. *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 0:12, 11:52, 12:00, 12:14)*
- MagnatesMedia's Morgan documentary says J.P. Morgan bought 250,000 New York Central shares from his son William Vanderbilt: see [[j-pierpont-morgan]]. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 7:09)*
