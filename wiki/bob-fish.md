Alleged US government contractor who was program manager for several highly classified advanced communications systems projects in the 1980s and 1990s, per [[uap-gerb]], including for the White House and executive branch intelligence agencies. Gerb notes [[ross-coulthart]] vetted him. He appears through a March 2015 email to [[john-podesta]] in the Wikileaks Clinton campaign leaks.

## Claims

- Email: Fish says one of the government programs collecting hard data on UFOs is the USAF [[defense-support-program]]. He says he was never fully briefed into DSP but met members at lunch in a TS/SCI-cleared cafeteria in El Segundo, California (transcribed "elag gundo") around Operation Desert Shield and Storm. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 8:10-9:19)*

- He also cites the 2014 DSP fact sheet line about [[aerospace-corporation]] researchers and links a defunct blog, "Top Secret Spy Satellites Spot UFOs" by Tim Schwarz, saying much of it is correct. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 10:06-10:56)*
