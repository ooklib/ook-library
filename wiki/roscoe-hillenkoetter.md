Roscoe Hillenkoetter (rendered "Hillenk Oetter" in Eyes Wide Open's ASR captions) was a US naval
officer whom, per Eyes Wide Open's account, President Truman installed in place of
[[william-donovan]] to become "the first director of the CIA," after losing faith in Donovan's
effectiveness.

## Claims

- A US naval officer, appointed by Truman to replace [[william-donovan]] as (per the host) "the
  first director of the CIA," reflecting Truman's greater respect for an actual military officer
  over Donovan. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 15:29-15:52)*
- Eyes Wide Open flags that, despite this apparent upgrade in respectability, Hillenkoetter "was
  not exactly a saint himself either" and was "quite a devious man himself" — to be elaborated in
  later episodes. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 15:52-15:59)*

## Relationships

- Succeeded [[william-donovan]] as leader of US foreign intelligence, becoming (per Eyes Wide
  Open) first director of the [[central-intelligence-agency]].
