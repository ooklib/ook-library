[[bob-lazar]]'s stated best working theory, given in this interview, is that the S4 reactor "appeared to be powered by a super heavy element, element 115", a stable isotope rather than the unstable version later synthesized in labs *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:32:04-1:32:38)*. X-rays of the reactor base plate suggested "a little accelerator, like a little cyclotron" that bombards an element 115 sample, producing an effect he originally thought was gravitational *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:32:38-1:33:06)*.

## Lazar's "other force" idea

- Lazar says he is increasingly convinced the effect is not gravity at all but an undiscovered repulsive force of nature; gravity is only a property of mass, is weak, and "shouldn't be manipulatable to that extent" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:33:06-1:34:39)*.
- "Anti-gravity" is just a label because there is no better term for the lift, and he hopes someone else has by now found the force; he has seen no craft that works like it *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:33:43-1:35:07)*.
- Explaining how it works remains a guess; there is no supporting evidence in the interview.
