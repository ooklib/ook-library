Pedro Luis Aguiar was, per [[james-fox]], the retired chief of police of Varginha who agreed to a rare on-camera interview and said the events were real. Fox says he has since died. He did not, however, admit to a photograph.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says he spent years trying to reach Aguiar and finally got the interview when a pizza stop delayed them so that he was just home with his wife and son. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 54:17-56:01)*
- Aguiar said "it is not a story, it is history"; he said he saw a creature under a tarp or net, "more brass than I have ever seen in my career", helicopters landing and going, and heard that one being was alive, one dead and one got away. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 53:59-54:17, 56:25-56:37, 1:38:04-1:38:36)*
- He also said the Americans came to Campinas and took what was there, angering local military. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:38:22-1:38:36)*
- Fox says Aguiar admitted at one point to having a photograph (one alive, one dead, a soldier and firefighter in the background) but when pressed said some things within the police or military will "remain secret"; his wife stared at him wide-eyed throughout the interview. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 58:45-1:00:07)*
- Fox says Aguiar died months later and calls the cause unknown; he was fairly healthy apart from arthritis. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 56:37-56:45, 1:00:07-1:00:35)*
