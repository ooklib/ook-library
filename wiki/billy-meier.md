Billy (Eduard) Meier is the Swiss farmer and contactee whose 1970s UFO photographs are discussed in [[lazar-ramsey-interview-2025]]. [[bob-lazar]] says he does not follow UFO lore, but that Meier's early photos, including one on a poster owned by [[luigi-vendittelli]] and pictures in a book Ramsey shows, look "exactly like the sport model" ([[lazar-sport-model-craft-claims]]), down to underside details, ports and antenna: "there's no way Billy could have gotten that without seeing it", 90-95% similarity *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:44:30-1:44:49, 1:46:54-1:48:14, 1:49:57-1:50:16, 1:51:59-1:52:11)*. Lazar thinks Meier had a real sighting and took authentic photos, then, in his "UFO researcher syndrome" theory (a term from his friend [[gene-huff]]), embellished as attention faded, producing absurd craft such as a cake with golf balls or a hubcap *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:45:03-1:46:54, 1:49:25-1:49:45)*. He thinks Meier may also have been "messed with" because the craft was a security-protected project not flown over Switzerland *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:48:29-1:49:10)*. Ramsey notes the early photos are beautiful, says "something changed", and hopes Meier can be heard *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:49:40-1:50:09, 1:52:39-1:52:57)*. The Townsend Brown episode summarized on [[bob-lazar]] similarly has its host note the disc's resemblance to Meier's "sports model". Meier's contactee claims are cataloged in the wiki's contactee pages such as [[contactee-nordic-racialism-claims]].
