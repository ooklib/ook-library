Yoshio Kodama is a Japanese crime boss and "deep state operator" whom [[eyes-wide-open]] cites in `ewa/dallas-4` as the prime example of [[jack-cannon]]'s comfort with organized crime. The host promises a fuller treatment when the series returns to [[charles-willoughby]]'s time in Japan.

## Claims

- Cannon "famously worked closely" with Yakuza members in postwar Japan, most notoriously Kodama. Willoughby's G2 army-intelligence contingent, the unit Cannon worked under, helped secure Kodama's release from Sugamo prison in 1948. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 27:20-28:00)*
- Per Kaplan and Dubro (*Yakuza*): before and during WWII he had deep ties in Japanese far-right and Yakuza circles and was a significant player in China in the late 1930s, shipping arms, radium, cobalt, nickel, copper and tungsten to Japan; his smuggling business (the Kodama Kikan / "Kodama Agency", ASR "Kodama Kikon") was valued at about $175 million by war's end, mostly plunder (diamonds, banknotes, platinum). *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 28:00-28:45)*
- Served as a rear admiral advising imperial politicians at the war's end; imprisoned in 1946; released after Willoughby and his deputy Cannon "worked behind the scenes to bring him into the US fold". Remained the major postwar Japanese narcotics smuggler and top political fixer. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 28:45-29:15)*
- Reemerged in the 1970s as an off-the-books consultant to Lockheed in Japan, helping sell the L-1011 to All Nippon Airways: the Japanese component of the Lockheed bribery scandal, which the host hopes to cover later. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 29:15-29:40)* (See [[lockheed-martin]].)
- Host: Canon "had no qualms and actually thrived within organized crime circles", so Hemming naming him among the mob's "boys" in Havana is unsurprising. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 29:46-30:15)*
