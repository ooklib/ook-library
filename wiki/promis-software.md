PROMIS ("Promise" in ASR) is the case-management software built for the US Justice Department by the small company Inslaw. This page records the oompaville account; see also [[robert-maxwell]].

## Claims

- The allegation (Gordon Thomas and Martin Dillon, [[ari-ben-menashe]] and other whistleblowers, and reportedly [[whitney-webb]]) is that it was stolen from Inslaw, given a secret Israeli backdoor and sold to intelligence services worldwide so Israel could read everything in it, with Robert Maxwell as salesman. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 25:11, 25:28, 25:36, 26:17)*
- Debunking record given by the host: special counsel Nicholas Bua (1993) found no evidence of criminal conspiracy or an Israeli backdoor; the Justice Department (1994) found no credible evidence officials conspired to steal it; the US Court of Federal Claims (1997-98) ruled the versions in question were public domain and the government free to use them. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 26:38, 26:52, 27:00, 27:09)*
- The host says Ben-Menashe and Michael Riconosciuto, the chief PROMIS whistleblower, were both judged fabricators in official findings. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 27:32, 27:40)*
