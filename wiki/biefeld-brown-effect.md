An electrogravitic propulsion concept discussed in a 1988–90
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] report (written by SAIC investigator
Dennis Cravens, with contribution from Los Alamos's Ferris Williams) for Edwards AFB's
Aeronautics Laboratory / Air Force Space Technology Center.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:31:14–1:32:11)*

## Claims

- The report discusses [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los Alamos]] and
  [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence Livermore]] work on classified power-density
  devices for [[strategic-defense-initiative|SDI]], and suggests a five-dimensional field-
  unification approach to novel propulsion. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:32:11–1:32:29)*
- Proposes anti-gravity via rotating cylinders filled with mercury, for oceanic/atmospheric
  applications — which Gerb notes matches [[edgar-fuche|Edgar Fuche]]'s description of the
  reverse-engineered "Magnetic Field Disruptor" (MFD) powering the
  [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b|TR-3B]] almost exactly (mercury-based plasma pressurized at
  250,000 atmospheres, 150°K, accelerated to 60,000 RPM, generating a magnetic vortex field that
  neutralizes gravity's effect on nearby mass by 89%). *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:32:29–1:33:51)*
- Ferris Williams also spoke at a June 2001 UFO technology conference in La Jolla, San Diego
  (near SAIC's HQ), where his research was noted as sponsored by [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]].
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:33:57–1:34:14)*
