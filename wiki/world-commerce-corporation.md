The World Commerce Corporation was [[william-donovan]]'s private company, owned by him as a
private citizen after leaving the OSS, which he allegedly used to funnel funds to mob associates
— including, per some sources, funding the 1947 Portella della Ginestra massacre.

## Claims

- According to some sources, the 1947 Mayday massacre (Portella della Ginestra) had allegedly been
  funded directly by former OSS agent Wild Bill Donovan, "now a private citizen and owner of the
  mysterious World Commerce Corporation which he used to funnel funds to the mob associates."
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 26:19-26:31)*

- LAOS 4: host: SEA Supply was founded by [[paul-helliwell]] under the umbrella of the World Commerce Corporation, founded by Wild Bill Donovan, "in a sense the first CIA cowboy". *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:03:07-1:03:26)*
