A video (about 31 minutes, dated 2024-05-26, [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Xk4X41yl2M)) that continues [[uap-gerb]]'s series on UFO whistleblowers, following [[gerb-whistleblowers-1-video]]. Chapters: Intro, Harland Bentley, Senator Barry Goldwater, RB, Conclusion. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 0:00-1:38)*

Gerb again sorts whistleblowers into those with sworn testimony (David Grusch, Robert Jacobs, David Fravor, Robert Salas, Michael Herrera) and those with astounding claims but no legal testimony or evidence (Phil Schneider, Clifford Stone, Don Phillips, "Mr Dennis"). He says all deserve a critical lens. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 0:00-1:01)*

This volume covers [[harland-bentley]] (an alleged 1958 Nike Ajax site UFO crash and an Apollo 8 overheard-radio claim), [[barry-goldwater]] (denied entry to the "Blue Room" at Wright-Patterson by [[curtis-lemay]]) and [[marine-rb]] (an anonymous Marine who allegedly guarded a disc-shaped craft in December 1963). Gerb speculates the Blue Room may be where RB stood guard. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 27:56-28:04)*

Gerb says he is investigating a possible lead to RB's identity and may ask researcher Michael Schratt for help, teases future videos on Dan Burisch and Project Aquarius, and recommends [[ryan-s-wood]]'s book Majic Eyes Only as his crash-retrieval handbook alongside [[leonard-stringfield]]'s status reports 1 through 7. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 28:12-30:48)*
