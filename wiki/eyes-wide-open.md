Eyes Wide Open is a YouTube channel (formerly "Everything You Need to Know," rebranded after a
six-year hiatus) run by a historian/researcher covering the history of American intelligence and
the CIA, drawing on published books (Tim Weiner's *Legacy of Ashes*, David Talbot's *The Devil's
Chessboard*, Paul L. Williams's *Operation Gladio*, Peter Dale Scott's works) with per-claim
citations shown on screen. This video, "The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence," is Part
1 of a multi-part "History of the CIA" series.

## Claims about self / methodology

- States every substantive claim will be cited in the video description with a page number, and
  that opinions/assumptions/theories will be flagged on-screen with a "green eye" icon, distinct
  from cited factual claims. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 2:44-3:49)*
- Frames the series around [[peter-dale-scott|Peter Dale Scott]]'s "deep politics"/[[deep-state]]
  theory, explicitly redefined for the series away from right-wing-populist usage of the term.
  *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 8:26-9:23)*
- Announces upcoming installments will cover "Operation Sunset/Sunrise" (Allen Dulles's alleged
  scheme to broker a separate peace with Nazi Germany). *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 29:36-29:53)*
- Part 2, "Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe," opens with a recap of Part 1
  (standard practice going forward, so each video "stands on its own") before covering
  [[operation-sunrise]], and closes by previewing Part 3's coverage of OSS activity in Asia
  (China, Southeast Asia, Burma) and its link to the French Connection and Gladio. Bibliography
  again drawn primarily from David Talbot's *The Devil's Chessboard* and Paul L. Williams's
  *Operation Gladio*. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 0:09-1:04, 26:50-27:06)*

- Part 3, "Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection," again opens recapping
  prior parts and states this is territory "very rarely discussed even in authoritative histories
  of the CIA." It covers the OSS's WWII-era opium facilitation for the KMT in China/Burma via
  [[paul-helliwell]] and [[civil-air-transport]], the wartime [[operation-underworld]] precedent
  with [[lucky-luciano]], and [[paul-helliwell]]'s postwar [[operation-x]] plan tying the future
  CIA to [[meyer-lansky]]'s [[national-crime-syndicate]] and the Sicilian mob — teased as leading
  into the next episode's coverage of Vatican officials and [[operation-gladio]] funding. Draws
  bibliographically on Alfred W. McCoy's *The Politics of Heroin*, James Bradley's *The China
  Mirage*, Paul L. Williams's *Operation Gladio*, Tim Weiner's *Legacy of Ashes*, and Whitney
  Webb's *One Nation Under Blackmail*. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 0:54-2:12, 29:06-29:21)*

- Part 4, "The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA," opens a new chapter in the series (the
  CIA's founding, rather than the OSS's WWII history) and lays out the host's organizing
  framework for the rest of the Cold War-era episodes: dividing the postwar world into a
  capitalist "First World," a communist "Second World," and a colonized/decolonizing "Third
  World," and arguing the CIA's central Cold War battlefield was not Moscow or Berlin but the
  Third World, where the agency worked to keep western capital in control of the Global South's
  wealth against nationalist and Marxist land-reform movements. Covers the [[truman-doctrine]],
  the [[central-intelligence-group]], and the [[national-security-act-1947|National Security Act
  of 1947]]'s creation of the CIA, and previews the next episode's coverage of the Greek Civil War
  as the CIA's first official operation. Bibliography draws on Vincent Bevins's *The Jakarta
  Method*, David Talbot's *The Devil's Chessboard*, Tim Weiner's *Legacy of Ashes*, and Naomi
  Klein's *The Shock Doctrine*, among others. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 1:37-2:23, 25:47-26:00, 26:29-26:52)*

- Part 5.1, "Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election," breaks from the usual single-episode format
  as "Part 1 of a new three-part mini-series" within the larger History of the CIA series,
  explicitly digging into [[operation-gladio]]'s origins in Italy, its ties to the Vatican, and
  pan-European stay-behind coordination. The host frames the evidence throughout as largely
  circumstantial ("we have very little in terms of smoking guns"), explicitly invites viewers to
  draw their own conclusions, and separately lays out — while flagging them as dubious —
  allegations of Soviet interference in the 1948 election for balance. Previews Part 2's coverage
  of the New York-Vatican-FBI-CIA blackmail/corruption network behind Cardinal
  [[francis-spellman]] and [[charles-poletti]]. Bibliography draws primarily on Daniele Ganser's
  *NATO's Secret Armies*, Paul L. Williams's *Operation Gladio*, and Peter Dale Scott's *Deep
  Politics and the Death of JFK*. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 1:28-1:52, 2:44-3:16, 5:02-5:28, 30:45-31:14)*

- Part 5.2, "Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite," continues the Gladio mini-series (Part 2 of
  3), shifting from Italy to domestic 1950s American parapolitics: [[joseph-mccarthy|McCarthyism]]/
  the Red Scare, [[richard-nixon]]'s rise via [[house-un-american-activities-committee|HUAC]] and
  the [[alger-hiss]] case, the alleged [[blue-suite]] sexual-blackmail circle around
  [[j-edgar-hoover]], [[francis-spellman]], [[roy-cohn]], and [[lewis-rosenstiel]], and a
  biographical capstone on [[george-white]]. States this is "probably the most cited work" the
  host has presented, drawing heavily on Anthony Summers's *Official and Confidential* (Hoover
  biography), Whitney Webb's *One Nation Under Blackmail*, David Talbot's *The Devil's
  Chessboard*, Peter Dale Scott's *Deep Politics and the Death of JFK*, and Carl Oglesby's *The
  Yankee and Cowboys War*. Frames the alleged Blue Suite blackmail dynamic as a "passive" cohesion
  mechanism binding elite networks together, explicitly drawing a parallel to the later Jeffrey
  Epstein case, and previews the next episode's coverage of the French Connection and the origins
  of Gladio in France. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 1:57-4:34, 41:20-42:24, 55:50-56:21)*
