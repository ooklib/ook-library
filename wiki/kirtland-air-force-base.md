Kirtland AFB, New Mexico, hosts a deep underground military installation and
[[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia National Labs]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
57:36–57:52)*

## Claims

- Site of a 2005 SAIC/[[air-force-operational-test-and-evaluation-center|AFOTEC]]
  CBRNE (chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear, explosive) research contract.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 57:21–57:44)*
- Originally Albuquerque Army Airfield, selected by Los Alamos's Z Division as an operational
  location (12 July 1945) and renamed Sandia Base; merged into Kirtland AFB in 1971. Hosts the
  Manzano Base/"Site Able" subsidiary installation, built 1947 by the
  [[armed-forces-special-weapons-project|AFSWP]] to store nuclear weapons, containing two
  underground installations per FOIA'd documents. Also hosts the
  [[defense-threat-reduction-agency|Defense Threat Reduction Agency]], which maintains a major
  presence there. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 16:07–17:22, 19:14–19:30,
  49:53–50:08, 2:30:59–2:31:18)*

## Claims (from the Hidden Wing video)

- Hosted the [[air-force-special-weapons-center|Air Force Special Weapons Center (AFSWC)]]
  (1949–1976), which specialized in atomic/nuclear/unconventional weaponry; AFSWC's research
  directorate employed [[eric-henry-wang|Dr. Eric Henry Wang]] from 1956 until his 1960 death,
  after his department relocated there from Wright-Patterson AFB. Wang's personal papers were
  reportedly confiscated by military intelligence upon his death and locked in a secret section
  of the Kirtland AFB library. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  3:12:42–3:15:22)*
- Hosts [[air-force-operational-test-and-evaluation-center|AFOTEC]] headquarters (alongside
  Sandia), and [[air-force-research-laboratory|AFRL]]'s Directed Energy and Space Vehicles
  directorates. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:41:08–1:41:31,
  2:47:38–2:48:00)*
- Maintains a permanent [[air-force-office-of-special-investigations|AFOSI/PJ]] station, and was
  the base of operations for counterintelligence special agent [[richard-doty|Richard "Rick"
  Doty]]. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:48:03–1:48:19,
  1:59:16–2:03:17)*
