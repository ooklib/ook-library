Terry Phillips is named by Gerb as a contact/figure at
[[air-force-office-of-special-investigations|AFOSI's Office of Special Projects]], addressed
directly on camera ("hello Terry Phillips") in connection with the AFOSI/PJ's alleged
counterintelligence role supporting [[mitre-corporation]]'s legacy activities.
*(40:42–40:56, 1:12:01–1:12:08)*

## Claims (from the Northrop Grumman & TRW Vol.2 video)

- Began his career as an Air Force officer before transitioning to AFOSI, ultimately earning
  appointment as a Senior Executive Service Tier 1 (the civilian equivalent to a one-star
  general). Served as SAP security director for the entirety of the Air Force and as executive
  director of AFOSI/PJ (Office of Special Projects) - roles in which Gerb alleges he "engaged in
  vicious reprisal campaigns against whistleblowers." Left government service in 2022 for senior
  VP and chief security officer at [[leidos|Leidos]], then in 2023 moved to VP for security at
  [[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]]. *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md)
  2:18:15-2:19:50)*
- Gerb, who names Phillips as one of his "Rogues Gallery" of UFO Legacy Program gatekeepers,
  states he considers Phillips "a truly evil individual" and "the tip of the spear" for the
  security architecture behind Northrop's UFO retrieval and reverse-engineering efforts. Cites
  investigator Ross Coulthart's independent corroboration naming Phillips as a "key gatekeeper
  figure" in a separate UAP investigation. *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md)
  2:17:36-2:20:22, 3:08:51-3:09:07)*
- Gerb theorizes directors of security operating under Phillips at Northrop include
  [[john-c-freestone|John C. Freestone]], [[kathy-andrews|Kathy Andrews]], and
  [[vince-divine|Vince Divine]], along with [[michael-dempsey|Michael Dempsey]] (VP for
  government affairs) among further executive-level figures allegedly engaging with Northrop's
  UFO portfolio. *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 2:19:52-2:21:25,
  2:50:44-2:51:02)*
- Gerb clarifies that while Phillips interfaces with legacy operations at both the Air Force (as
  a deputized asset under National Security Council-originating architecture pursuing
  insider/outsider threat protection) and Northrop Grumman, he believes these are different
  capacities - but maintains that at Northrop, Phillips "is literally in charge of the entire
  security apparatus for Northrop's breakaway programs." *([northrop-trw](../raw/
  0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 3:09:56-3:10:35)*

## Claims (from the Hidden Wing video — Gerb's primary career dossier)

Gerb dedicates an extended section of the Hidden Wing video to Phillips, calling him "a truly
evil boogeyman" and stating explicitly he considers it a personal risk to discuss him.
*([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 2:10:32–2:11:08)*

- Career timeline: began as an Air Force officer; became an AFOSI special agent around 1997; by
  2001, program security officer/special agent for AFOSI/PJ at Air Combat Command MAJCOM HQ,
  "protect[ing] special access programs for the combat air force"; by 2006, deputy director
  within AFOSI directing counterintelligence/investigative operations supporting Air Mobility
  Command; director of counterintelligence within AFOSI, 2012–2015; deputy director of AFOSI/PJ
  by 2016; from December 2016 to December 2021, outright Air Force SAP Security Director while
  concurrently serving as executive director of AFOSI/PJ — making him responsible for all Air
  Force and Space Force SAPs, plus non-Air-Force SAPs where the Air Force had been assigned
  responsibility. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 2:11:17–2:13:03)*
- On 6 August 2020, Phillips was selected for Senior Executive Service tier 1 (SES1, equivalent
  to a brigadier general/one-star admiral) — the first executive director of PJ to hold that
  rank. On an obscure AFOSI podcast ("OSI Today, My OSI Journey #16"), Phillips said his pride in
  the appointment had "grown 25-fold." *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md)
  2:13:03–2:14:26)*
- Gerb argues Phillips, as SES1 SAP Security Director and PJ executive director, likely engaged
  in "aggressive legacy program protection" and insider/outsider threat mitigation against
  whistleblowers — including [[david-grush|David Grush]] and [[dylan-borland|Dylan Borland]] —
  possibly via AFOSI/PJ's [[guardian-angel-program|Guardian Angel]] FBI-liaison mechanism.
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 2:15:11–2:17:00, 2:26:58–2:27:22)*
- On a farewell podcast episode recorded on the eve of his OSI retirement, Phillips said of his
  tenure, "we define the art of the possible" — describing PJ's approach to security review as
  finding conditional paths to "yes" for classified-program construction/operations rather than
  reflexively saying "no." *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 2:19:45–2:22:18)*
- Immediately upon retiring from PJ, Phillips was appointed senior VP and chief security officer
  at [[leidos|Leidos]] (spun out of [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] in
  2013; merged with Lockheed Martin's IT sector in 2016). From 2023 to present, Phillips has
  served as VP for security and chief security officer at
  [[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]]. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md)
  2:22:18–2:25:38)*
- A 15 July 2025 Black Vault FOIA release (obtained by John Greenwald) on DoD Inspector General
  project D2021-DEVSN-116.0000 (an evaluation of DoD's UAP handling) shows Phillips's name
  appearing repeatedly, engaging in classified UAP conversations with the DoD IG alongside former
  AFOSI commander Brig. Gen. Terry Bullard, and being positioned as a subject-matter expert on
  UFOs — e.g. in a 4 March 2021 meeting Bullard could not attend. A separate 15 July 2025 FOIA
  release places Phillips (as PJ executive director) alongside representatives of
  [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-acquisition-and-sustainment|USD A&S]],
  [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-and-security|USD I&S]] SAPCO, Army SAPOC, and
  Navy Special Programs Division as a senior UFO point of contact.
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 2:27:29–2:29:44)*
- Gerb's proposed interrogatory for Phillips: "Mr. Phillips, during your tenure as director of
  OSI/PJ and Air Force Special Access Program Security Director, did you actively engage in
  reprisals against UFO whistleblowers? Did you, alongside the PJ cadre, leverage administrative
  terrorism, loss of pension/benefits, and threats to life and well-being against
  whistleblowers?" *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 2:30:03–2:30:31)*
- Phillips's PJ/Air-Force-SAP-security successor is [[lee-m-russ|Special Agent Lee M. Russ]],
