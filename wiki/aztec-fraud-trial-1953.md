The Denver prosecution of [[silas-newton]] and [[leo-gebauer]] over the [[doodlebug-magnetic-detector]], prompted by investor Herman Flater with [[jp-cahn]]'s help, which is the main reason Aztec was written off as a hoax. [[uap-gerb]] reads it as targeted retaliation for the leak, based on [[william-steinman]]'s analysis of court records and the Ramsays' work.

## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Federal judges in four states declined the case; local charges were filed 10 October 1952 (interstate transport of stolen property, then "worthless machinery"), arrests followed on 18 October 1952, and the FBI found 32 of 33 investors satisfied. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 50:35-51:57)*
- Trial ran 10 November to 29 December 1953. Flater claimed a $250,000 loss and denied signing a contract giving him an interest in three devices though witnesses saw him sign; the prosecution's expert, Dr. James Meredith of the Colorado School of Mines, tested a $445 surplus device, not Gebauer's. Defense witnesses reported successful finds, including a rancher who sold 89% of a well for $1.75 million. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 51:57-55:17)*
- Both men were found guilty but never sentenced and fined nothing beyond Newton paying $18,000 in costs, which Gerb finds odd; he asks why they were charged at all. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 55:49-56:41)*
- Gerb cites later reprisals against disclosers such as [[jonathan-weygandt]] and [[mark-mccandlish]] (IRS targeting) as parallels. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 56:48-57:11)*
- The Ramsays argue the airman "George"'s statement that the Air Force was upset not with Scully but with who leaked to him supports the retaliation reading. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:39:59-1:40:22)*
