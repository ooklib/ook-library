Philippe Thyraud de Vosjoli (ASR variants above; the description lists "thyraud de vosjoli") was a major figure in French intelligence (SDECE, ASR "STEK"/"SD") who, per [[eyes-wide-open]], was its main liaison for the western hemisphere, moving between Cuba, Canada, the US, Mexico and the Caribbean. Normalization of the spellings is my inference from the description's keyword list. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 41:15-41:47)*

## Claims

- Present at the [[havana-hilton]] on 1 Jan. 1959, watching [[frank-brandstetter]]; Brandstetter put him on the first flight out of Havana with US embassy officials. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 41:15-41:24, 46:40-47:13)*
- In the early 1960s a major whistleblower: claimed SDECE and de Gaulle's cabinet were filled with KGB moles per a Soviet defector he handled ("Martell", ASR); the "Martell/Sapphire affair" culminated in [[john-f-kennedy]] writing to de Gaulle. Host says he may return to this. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 41:47-42:19)*
- Per [[peter-dale-scott]] (*Dallas 63*), citing European sources, he turned double agent serving the CIA and then the OAS (Secret Army Organization, rogue French generals opposed to de Gaulle's Algeria policy). Scott quotes an Italian Piazza Fontana source: in the early 1960s he was an intermediary between CIA leaders including [[richard-helms]] and OAS organizer Jacques Soustelle (ASR "Susted"). *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 42:19-43:38)*
- A 1987 internal biography of [[john-mccone]], unredacted under the 2025 JFK Files Act, says the French embassy in Cuba agreed to be the CIA's "Cuban eyes and ears" with de Vosjoli, SDECE's Washington representative, reporting directly to McCone. The host asks why this passage stayed one of the only redactions in the 53-page document and speculates about hiding a French element in the [[kennedy-assassination]]. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 43:52-44:50)*
- Per Scott he appeared on the Gulf Coast in the early 1960s gathering intel among OAS veterans, e.g. Captain Jean Souetre (ASR "Suet"), who the host says was caught in Dallas on 22 Nov. 1963. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 44:55-45:23)*
- On 22 Nov. 1963 he flew from Washington to Acapulco to take refuge with [[frank-brandstetter]] at Las Brisas ("for unexplained reasons", quoting his autobiography via Scott). Host: this "could hint at his knowledge or even involvement" in the assassination; the host frames these as questions. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 45:23-46:40)*
- A quoted passage from the Brandstetter biography says he later shared confidences on "communist penetration of security agencies." *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 47:13-47:37)*
