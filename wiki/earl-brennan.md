Earl Brennan was the OSS director in Italy who, following orders from [[william-donovan]],
reached out to the Vatican to identify allies for the anti-communist struggle, initiating the
alliance that helped install [[calogero-vizzini]] as a postwar Sicilian administrator.

## Claims

- A subordinate of OSS director Wild Bill Donovan, took the additional step of finding adequate
  administrators for post-war Italy by reaching out to the Vatican. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 13:54-14:12)*
- Liaised with Monsignor [[giovanni-montini]], the Vatican's undersecretary of state (and future
  Pope Paul VI), about potential allies in the anti-communist struggle; Montini allegedly
  suggested the OSS ally with "Don Carlo" [[calogero-vizzini]], boss of bosses of the Vizini-
  Agostino crime family — a suggestion also backed by [[lucky-luciano]]. Brennan relayed the
  message to Donovan, who ordered an airdrop communique to Vizzini's estate requesting his
  assistance with the invasion of Sicily. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 15:50-16:29)*
