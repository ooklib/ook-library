Alger Hiss, a respected State Department New Dealer and architect of the United Nations, was the
target of the 1948 HUAC perjury trial that launched [[richard-nixon]]'s career and, per Eyes Wide
Open, entangled [[john-foster-dulles]], [[allen-dulles]], and [[henry-luce]]'s Time magazine.
The host stresses that Hiss's actual guilt or innocence remains genuinely contested among
historians.

## Claims

- A deeply respected Washington establishment figure, a Harvard graduate, and one of the head
  architects of the United Nations as an FDR-era New Dealer in the Foreign Service; his
  connections transcended party lines, including being granted the presidency of the
  [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace|Carnegie Endowment for International Peace]] by
  [[john-foster-dulles]] in 1947. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 16:01-16:36)*
- Accused by [[whittaker-chambers]], a senior Time magazine editor, of having been his courier in a
  1930s Soviet spy ring in Washington, DC. Hiss vehemently denied the allegations before HUAC and
  was ultimately convicted only of perjury (for lying about not knowing his accuser), not
  espionage or treason. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 15:42-15:55, 19:38-19:51)*
- The host notes that since 1948, declassified Venona Papers proved some contemporaneously
  convicted figures (e.g. Julius and Ethel Rosenberg) were indeed guilty of Soviet spying, but that
  historians remain divided on Hiss's complicity even accounting for Venona. The host's own
  speculative theory is that the case may have been at least partly engineered to give
  [[john-foster-dulles]] a pretext to purge New Dealers from a future State Department, and to
  legitimize [[richard-nixon]]'s rising anti-communist profile. Explicitly flagged as
  unverifiable "pet theory." *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 20:25-22:15)*
