The Korean War (from June 1950) appears in [[eyes-wide-open]]'s War Conspiracy 1 as the event that
institutionalized peacetime military spending and gave the CIA's [[civil-air-transport]] its first
opportunity for rollback.

## Claims

- Truman, "still scarred by the loss of China" and the McCarthyite/China Lobby blowback, ordered US
  troops to support the South under a UN directive when North Korean troops crossed the 38th parallel
  in June 1950 (the host says "30th parallel," an apparent slip; he says "38th parallel" later, flagged).
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 38:21-38:43, 45:12-45:23)*
- The first major US Army campaign of the Cold War; Truman's first defense budget after entry was
  $50 billion per James Bradley. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 38:43-39:07)*
- When the US neared the Yalu, China intervened; by late 1950 it was a stalemate at the 38th
  parallel; what began as rollback became containment. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 45:04-45:23)*
- The war's contracts, especially aviation, reignited the arms industry; see [[military-industrial-complex]].
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 41:01-41:27)*
- Same period: the CIA bought [[civil-air-transport]] and began planning [[operation-paper]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 43:54-45:49)*
