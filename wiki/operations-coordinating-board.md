Corso's own FOIA-released military documents show him as a member, which Gerb uses to rebut critics who say Corso was never NSC staff; his role was as intelligence staff officer attached to the NSC. It succeeded the [[psychological-strategy-board]] in practice. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J.
