The nearly decade-long CIA program that armed and trained, per [[eyes-wide-open]], an army of approximately 40,000 Hmong fighters under [[vang-pao]] for the secret war in [[laos]]. It was launched in early 1961 on an 18-page proposal by [[bill-lair]] approved via [[desmond-fitzgerald]] and [[allen-dulles]]: "arm the first thousand men and then we'll see". *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:11:26-1:12:02)*

## Claims

- By mid-1962 the host says the CIA was handed the Laos conflict, and Momentum "started to run full swing"; the "secret war was online". *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:18:26-1:18:45)*
- Foreshadows privatized black ops: the CIA's use of proprietaries and shell companies to mask illegality and evade Congress; the host says these networks funded unapproved operations via narcotics and gun-running and opened the door to personal profiteering. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:02:40-1:04:38)*
- Predecessors: [[operation-brotherhood]] (contact), [[paru]] (model), and [[operation-white-star]] (parallel Army effort).

## Relationships

- Run by [[bill-lair]] with [[vang-pao]] and the [[hmong-people]]; authorized by [[allen-dulles]]; part of [[secret-war-in-laos]]; funded in part by [[narco-counterinsurgency]] per the host.
