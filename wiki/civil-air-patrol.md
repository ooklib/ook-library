The Civil Air Patrol is described as "an ostensibly civilian appendage to the full-blown Air Force." The Louisiana unit's pilots were "particularly spooked up" and increasingly associated with CIA gun and drug running through the 1970s-80s. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 41:29-41:59)*
