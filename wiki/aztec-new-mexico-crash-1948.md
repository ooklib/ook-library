An alleged additional 1940s UFO crash retrieval near Aztec, New Mexico, which Gerb speculates
may have also fed into [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]'s alleged technology-exploitation program
alongside [[roswell-incident-1947|Roswell]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:02:44–1:02:55, 2:04:13–2:04:23)*

## Claims

- Gerb speculates the "doodlebug" device that Silas Newton and Leo GeBauer allegedly used to
  defraud investors in connection with the Aztec case may have had technological development
  origins in Navy-sponsored WWII anti-submarine special projects under
  [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]]'s OSRD — framed explicitly as speculative ("a real tangled
  web"). *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:09:52–2:10:24)*

## Claims (from the "Secrecy" Vol. 1 video)

- UAP Gerb states William Steinman's 1986 book *UFO Crash at Aztec: A Well-Kept Secret* is the
  definitive exploration of the case, alongside the work of Scott and Suzanne Ramsey; Steinman's
  investigation chased down "Mr. G" — an amalgamation figure representing Silas Newton and Leo
  GeBauer, key figures who disclosed crash-retrieval knowledge in connection with the case.
  *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 2:02:13–2:02:48)*
- Steinman's book names an alleged "Aztec saucer recovery scientific team" led by
  [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]], including three accused original
  [[majestic-12|Majestic 12]] members (Dr. Detlev Bronk, Dr. Lloyd Berkner, Dr. Jerome Hunsaker),
  plus Dr. Carl A. Highland, Dr. John von Neumann, and Dr.
  [[j-robert-oppenheimer|J. Robert Oppenheimer]]. UAP Gerb cautions Steinman did not
  meticulously document his sourcing for this roster; Highland is Steinman's most independently
  corroborated name, resurfacing in Colorado court records as a hostile witness against Newton and
  GeBauer. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 2:02:57–2:03:44)*
- UAP Gerb speculates Steinman may have derived the Oppenheimer/von Neumann names either from the
  disputed [[interplanetary-phenomenon-unit|IPU]] documents (leaked in the early 1990s, after
  Steinman's 1986 publication and his 1983 communications with Sarbacher) or from
  [[robert-sarbacher|Robert Sarbacher]]'s 1983 statements to him that von Neumann was "definitely
  involved" and Oppenheimer likely so. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  2:03:55–2:04:15, 3:15:14–3:15:32)*

## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Gerb's full treatment ([[gerb-aztec-video]]): on 25 March 1948 a wobbling disc, seen by three radar sites, crashed on Hart Canyon mesa about 12 miles northeast of Aztec; witnesses describe a 99.9 ft light-metal disc with no rivets or welds, an 18 ft by 6 ft central crew cabin, and two bodies seen slumped at a punctured porthole, with 12-16 bodies found after a rod opened a panel. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:46-2:34, 5:17-5:46, 12:48-15:30)*
- Steinman adds an IPU security team from Camp Hale, General George C. Marshall relaying to [[vannevar-bush]], a scientist team, glyphic instrument panels, a plastic "pamphlet" sent to cryptologists William Friedman and Lambros Callimahos, [[detlev-bronk]] ordering dry ice (freezing gear allegedly from Paul Schaerer's Air Research) and autopsies in Blue Book/Sign report no. 13, with bodies about 40 lb, webbed fingers and no digestive tract or red blood cells; Scully instead describes near-perfect humanoids. Bodies allegedly went to Wright-Patterson. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 15:30-21:22, 26:37-26:57)*
- The disc was reportedly taken apart into three equal segments plus cabin at interlocking seams, moved by M25 tank transporters on concrete pads over about two weeks, and driven to Los Alamos, where it stayed over a year. Gerb links the interlocking-disassembly detail to [[sumner-shapiro]]'s 1989 account to [[bob-oechsler]], the rotatable cabin to [[fouche-controversy|Edgar Fouche]]'s TR-3B account, and the Bureau of Reclamation equipment records the Ramsays found. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 21:22-27:07)*
- Gerb ties the crash to early legacy-program structure: [[j-andrew-kissner]]'s 1994 sources on 2-4 discs, Project Supremacy, 1948 executive orders giving the AEC custody, and the AFSWP. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 27:15-30:30)*
- Discussion of sources and objections: [[silas-newton]], [[leo-gebauer]], [[jp-cahn]], [[aztec-fraud-trial-1953]], [[dr-gee-scientists]], [[aztec-eyewitness-accounts]], [[scott-and-suzanne-ramsay]], [[interplanetary-phenomenon-unit]], [[john-spencer-carr]]. Gerb concludes the case likely happened but may conflate several events, including a 30-foot craft at Farmington. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 2:12:39-2:14:05)*

Old") who saw a photo of an intact tilted brushed-aluminum disc near Farmington, New Mexico before 1950, porthole with a pencil-sized puncture, diamond drills and torches failing, 4 ft charred humanoids in woven flight suits and a 30-something foot diameter. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 2:10:19-2:12:32)*
