AUTEC is the US Navy's Major Range and Test Facility Base (MRTFB) located in the "Tongue of the
Ocean," a deep-water trench near the Bahamas dropping to ~2,000 m, used for testing advanced
submarines, weapons, and sonar systems. *(1:25:00–1:25:17)*

- Gerb reckons the object stations further east, in the Blake–Bahama Basin (dropping to
  ~4,000 m into the Atlantic abyssal plain), near but not necessarily within AUTEC's range.
  *(1:25:34–1:25:43)*
