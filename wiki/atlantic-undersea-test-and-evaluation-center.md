AUTEC is the US Navy's Major Range and Test Facility Base (MRTFB) located in the "Tongue of the
Ocean," a deep-water trench near the Bahamas dropping to ~2,000 m, used for testing advanced
submarines, weapons, and sonar systems. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:25:00–1:25:17)*

## Claims

- Gerb suspects AUTEC personnel have direct firsthand experience relevant to the alleged
  [[tic-tac-factory-uso|Tic Tac Factory]] program, though he states he lacks direct knowledge
  confirming this. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:25:17–1:25:34)*
- Gerb reckons the object stations further east, in the Blake–Bahama Basin (dropping to
  ~4,000 m into the Atlantic abyssal plain), near but not necessarily within AUTEC's range.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:25:34–1:25:43)*
