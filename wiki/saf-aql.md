SAF/AQL ("Air Force Special Programs"), a directorate under [[saf-aq|SAF/AQ]] (SECAF ATNL),
"directs SAP advanced technology development programs and monitors new technologies which may
satisfy operational requirements or capitalize on technology opportunities in coordination with
SAF/AQR." It manages development and execution of the Air Force's most highly classified and
sensitive acquisition programs, and serves as the principal SAP acquisition adviser to the SECAF,
Under Secretary of the Air Force, and SAF/AQ, liaising with other services, defense agencies, and
federal departments. It also plans, programs, directs, and accomplishes RDT&E for "promising
advanced air, space, and cyber capabilities." *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md)
44:00–46:26)*

## Claims

- Gerb wagers that within AQL and [[saf-aqr|AQR]]'s joint scope of "coordinat[ing] on Air Force
  RDT&E SAPs and ... monitor[ing] new technologies," their charter covers deep-sixed RDT&E SAPs
  focused on recovery and exploitation of "technologies of unknown origin."
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 48:41–49:11)*
- [[randall-g-walden|Randall G. Walden]] (later RCO director) served as technical director for
  the Air Force Acquisition Directorate of Special Programs (i.e. AQL) earlier in his career.
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 56:34–57:08)*
- Gerb states in his conclusion that some testimony he has personally gathered has "directly
  named" AQX (of the AQL/AQX/AQR trio) in connection with legacy-program activity.
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 3:20:41–3:21:13)*
