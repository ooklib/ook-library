Cameron recounts interviews with Penn State president [[eric-a-walker]], who allegedly admitted being at a 1950 Wright-Patterson crash briefing and told researchers that unless they understood ESP they would not be taken in by the "control group", a group of "elites" of von Neumann calibre.

## Claims

- Steinman, told by [[robert-sarbacher]] that Walker attended a Wright-Patterson briefing (with Vannevar Bush and von Neumann), phoned him in 1983 and 1987; Walker allegedly said "I was there. So what?" and that he had known of them for 40 years. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:53:44-1:55:31)*
- Asked about MJ-12, Walker asked about ESP and said if you were invited into the group "I would know"; Cameron says Ben Rich later asked Jan Harzan (ASR "Hartson") the same question. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:47:02-1:47:44, 1:48:46-1:49:03)*
- Walker said "we promised not to tell" when asked about communication, and said "how do you know they didn't get up and walk away?" of Kingman; Cameron says a Soul Conference attendee heard a leading figure say Kingman was the crash "they walked away from", leaving the craft intact. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:49:26-1:50:39)*
- Cameron says Walker admitted being at Kecksburg (1965), was IDA board chairman and worked with Vannevar Bush in 1947, and that Kit Green was thrown out of Walker's Penn State office after asking about crash saucers. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:48:00-1:48:33, 2:35:19-2:36:29)*
- Cameron says Walker told them curiosity is not a reason to change classification rules: "you're just curious." *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:56:09-1:56:22)*
- Michels notes [[harald-malmgren]] also served at IDA and Cameron says he believes Malmgren; Cameron mentions Bob McGuire of IDA. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:51:42-1:53:07)*
