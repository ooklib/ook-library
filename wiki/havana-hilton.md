The Havana Hilton is the Conrad Hilton flagship hotel in Havana, whose grand opening was in March 1958, about 15 minutes' walk from the US embassy and CIA station. [[eyes-wide-open]] argues in chapter 5 of [[road-to-dallas]] that under manager [[frank-brandstetter]] it acted as a "covert nucleus for Army intelligence" in 1958-59 and a "melding of worlds" between July 26th revolutionaries and American and European intelligence officers. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 11:33-12:16, 40:50-41:15)*

## Claims

- Open only about 9 months before the revolution ended; the host says it "would act as a centerpiece for US intelligence recruiting." *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 33:48-34:10)*
- Staff: Cuban engineer [[manuel-ray]] was head of maintenance/project manager; Brandstetter suspected him of working for Castro but kept him as an information source. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 34:03-34:42)*
- New Year's Day 1959: Ray warned that the mob planned to burn the hotel down as a symbolic anti-Yankee gesture; Brandstetter confronted the crowd and persuaded them to leave (for about 15 minutes); later July 26th men came to confiscate food but otherwise the hotel was unharmed. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 39:45-40:32)*
- Castro entered Havana on 8 January 1959; Brandstetter prepared the presidential suite, Castro at first declined but later made the entire building his temporary personal headquarters with an office in the penthouse. Host: this gave Brandstetter an inside look at the first days of the Castro government, "a massive boon to the US intelligence apparatus." *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 40:32-40:57)*
- French SDECE agent [[philippe-thyraud-de-vosjoli]] was present on 1 Jan. 1959 and left on the first flight out with US embassy staff, arranged by Brandstetter. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 41:15-41:24, 46:40-47:13)*
- Brandstetter left 13 Feb. 1959 after hosting Fidel "for the past month or so." *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 47:44-47:58)*
- Host: Castro's rise about 8 months after the opening made it a bad time for Conrad Hilton to open his new flagship hotel. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 11:42-12:07, 13:54-14:09)*
