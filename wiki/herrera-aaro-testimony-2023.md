The alleged late-April 2023 interview in which [[michael-herrera]] testified to [[aaro]] about his 2009 Indonesia encounter, described in [[gerb-herrera-2-video]] and earlier in [[gerb-herrera-1-video]]. Only Herrera's and Joey's accounts exist; the wiki records them as claims.

## Claims

- Herrera says it felt like an interrogation, that [[sean-kirkpatrick]] was the only attendee he can name (he signed an NDA about the location and the other attendee) and that Kirkpatrick took no notes and asked only three, identical questions about who the operators were. Herrera says he does not know who they were, only roughly which group and background. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:13:53-1:15:03)*
- AARO said it could obtain satellite imagery of the day, but a year later had not contacted him with imagery for him to point out features on. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:15:03-1:15:44)*
- He calls Historical Report Volume 1's account (extraterrestrial vehicle, US special forces) a false, purposeful omission, says a fellow Marine in the room can confirm he never said it, and says he signed several documents that may have included a memorandum for record; Joey mentions a Black Vault FOIA release of a matching form and criticizes the report's emphasis on "belief". *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:12:37-1:13:49, 1:15:44-1:17:24)*
- Herrera says he first testified to AARO that the container cylinders might be humidity control for drugs, before he learned they held oxygen and people. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 22:01-22:26)*
