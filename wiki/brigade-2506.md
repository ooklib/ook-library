Brigade 2506 was the CIA-trained Cuban exile army of the Bay of Pigs, trained at a Guatemalan camp the host calls Base Trax (ASR "base tracks"/"Fort Trax"). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 26:09-26:22)*

## Claims

- Host: recruitment ran through JM/WAVE in Miami, where [[e-howard-hunt]] (alias "Eduardo" among exiles) was political officer coordinating weapons and cash; the exile army topped out a little over 1,500 combatants, not the 10,000 once hoped for. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 23:37-24:12, 27:56-28:49)*
- Camp: a plantation of Roberto Alejos (brother of Guatemalan ambassador Carlos Alejos), first ~100 Cubans by August 19, 1960; 430 Cuban and CIA staff by Nov 4, 1960, at 7,000 ft on muddy volcanic ground near working coffee plantations; host says Castro learned of it via Miami spies and a Guatemalan spy or Cuban double agent. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 25:43-26:16, 30:16-31:13)* Secured by [[richard-bissell]]; Guatemala had hosted the 1954 coup ([[operation-pbsuccess]]).
- The plan shifted from guerrilla toward an amphibious assault; trainers were mostly CIA contractors and ex-gun-runners; shell companies likely overseen by [[paul-helliwell]] financed vehicles and weapons. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 31:13-31:53)*
- Host: it was an "open secret" by April 17, 1961; the host suggests that may have been partly intentional and that the publicized failure overshadowed less public successes such as [[operation-40]]. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 25:03-25:31)*
- Led by [[manuel-artime]]; allegedly infiltrated by Castro agents; Sheeran claim: see [[e-howard-hunt]]. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 29:37-30:16)*
- Host: its elite side group was [[operation-40]], described by Felipe de Diego as the Brigade's "intelligence branch." *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 41:33-41:44)*
