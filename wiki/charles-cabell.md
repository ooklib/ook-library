Charles Cabell, a West Point graduate, served as [[allen-dulles]]'s deputy director of the [[central-intelligence-agency]] from 1953 until late 1962, when, per [[eyes-wide-open]], President Kennedy forced him to retire for his role in the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]]. His brother [[earle-cabell]] was Dallas mayor at the time of the [[kennedy-assassination]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 26:44-27:15)*
