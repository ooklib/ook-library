The CIA-Mafia assassination plots are the alleged 1960-61 (and later) schemes to kill [[fidel-castro]] using [[national-crime-syndicate]] figures, which [[eyes-wide-open]] uses in the episode "The CIA-Mafia Legend" as a window onto the alliance of intelligence, organized crime and private capital. The host also argues that the popular story of these plots is itself a "legend" (see [[cia-mafia-legend]]).

## Origin and chain of command

- September 1960, Brown Derby, Hollywood: [[robert-maheu]] proposes to [[johnny-roselli]] an alliance between the CIA and the mob in Miami, Louisiana and Chicago to kill Castro; second meeting at the Kenilworth Hotel, Miami with [[sam-giancana]] ("Sam Gold") and [[santo-trafficante]] ("Joe"). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 0:07-1:44)*
- Host: the mob wanted its lost Havana casinos back (Trafficante, [[carlos-marcello]], [[meyer-lansky]] hardest hit; Lansky reportedly put a million-dollar bounty on Castro), aligning with the CIA and Yankee businessmen who also lost assets. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 33:15-34:39)*
- Chain: [[richard-nixon]] and [[allen-dulles]] as top planners; Dulles delegated to [[richard-bissell]] (deputy director of plans); Bissell picked the CIA Office of Security and [[sheffield-edwards]] as working-level manager; Edwards tasked James "Big Jim" O'Connell (ex-FBI, joined the CIA Office of Security in 1951, host: another possible Hoover mole) to contact Maheu; $150,000 reward put up, with Maheu to pose as the front for private businessmen. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 59:44-1:03:17)*
- Hougan (via host) theorizes it "remains a distinct possibility" that the plan was actually paid for by business leaders and the CIA origin story was the cover; the host adds that oil tycoons in the Southwest became key financiers of later anti-Cuba plots, making CIA vs business elite "almost impossible" to distinguish. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:03:17-1:03:57)*
- Host: the early CIA leaders chose former Army intelligence officers for the two key jobs (Edwards here, [[joseph-caldwell-king]] for early [[operation-40]]), foreshadowing Army-intelligence/Pentagon involvement in later exile groups such as Alpha 66. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:01:12-1:01:44)*

## Plots described

- Gangland-style street hit (Hougan's candidate shooter: Cuban exile Rolando Masferrer, "el Tigre", ASR "Masera"/"M farer"/"Farah"; ex-Batista senator with a private paramilitary squad, later financed privately, not by the CIA) was dropped as too risky. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:04:06-1:05:51)*
- Giancana and Roselli proposed poison instead. Bissell had already ordered poison development for U-2 pilots' cyanide pills via the Technical Services Division of [[sidney-gottlieb]], who had also prepared poison for the Lumumba plot in the Congo. Host cross-note: the U-2 program and the anti-Castro project were both run by Bissell. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:05:51-1:08:11)*
- First plot: Maheu delivered six poison tablets to Roselli to give to Castro at his favorite restaurant. Other schemes (citing Kinzer's *Poisoner in Chief*): LSD-sprayed microphone, depilatory to make his beard fall out, poison cigars, poisoned diving suit, exploding conch shell. Some sources estimate 600 attempts. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:11:31-1:13:00)*
- Host's own speculation: the plots were meant to land before the 1960 election as an "October surprise" for Nixon, but did not materialize. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:08:11-1:08:50)* JFK, after taking office, wanted anti-Cuba activity to be as deniable as possible; CIA and mob continued with Gottlieb's poison and Trafficante's agents. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:09:06-1:09:59)*

## Protection and aftermath

- Peter Dale Scott (quoted): after [[robert-f-kennedy]]'s February 1960 *The Enemy Within* named Giancana and Trafficante, the CIA gave both men protection by involving them in the plots; the host reads this as shielding useful mob assets from RFK and holding off prosecutions on "national security" grounds; he says [[j-edgar-hoover]]'s FBI was itself allied to these mobsters in many cases (to be covered next episode). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:14:42-1:16:19)*
- Trafficante possibly a double agent for Castro (Hougan: cushy jailing, allowed to leave; Castro skipping his usual restaurant the day of the poison-pill plot) vs. Roselli's remark that Trafficante simply "sat on" the plots. See [[cia-mafia-legend]]. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:16:27-1:19:33)*
- Giancana killed 19 June 1975 before testifying; [[johnny-roselli]] testified to the [[church-committee]], then was found dead in an oil drum in August 1976; Trafficante appeared before the House Select Committee on Assassinations in 1978, downplaying his role as a "Spanish interpreter", denying involvement in JFK's murder, and died of natural causes in 1987 aged 72. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:20:28-1:22:28)*
