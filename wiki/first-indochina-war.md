Eyes Wide Open's account of the 1946-54 colonial war between the returning French administration and [[ho-chi-minh]]'s Viet Minh coalition, which the host uses as the setting for the origin of [[narco-counterinsurgency]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 0:21-0:42)*

## Claims

- Fighting began at once on French re-entry in 1946; Hanoi erupted in December 1946; the French stayed dominant in the south (Cochinchina, Saigon), whose allies were religious minorities (Catholics, incl. the future [[ngo-dinh-diem]]'s family, and the syncretic Cao Dai sect). *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 0:21-2:01)*
- Saigon had a large Corsican expatriate community; the [[corsican-mafia]] of Marseille controlled much of the East-West narcotics trade and by the mid-1950s Marseille was "Europe's heroin laboratory"; Saigon Corsicans handled the logistics side and were "valuable allies" to French intelligence (the SDECE). *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 2:18-3:56)*
- Per Christopher Goscha and [[alfred-mccoy]] (quoting Giap), the Viet Minh saw the war as political, the French as a conventional military exercise; the host argues the French misunderstanding fed a self-reinforcing cycle. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 4:54-7:53)*
- Budget deficits and a strong, antiwar French Communist Party limited France's means; the host says [[operation-gladio]] "had not had time to mature" and the CIA would later "turn the tables" against the French Communists. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 9:00-9:50)*
- Host digression on counterinsurgency theory: portable radios, the AK-47 (1947/49) and RPG-7 (1958) empowered insurgents; Soviet and Chinese sponsorship made Vietnam an archetypal proxy war. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 10:07-14:34)*
- The war was an "unnecessary conflict"; French adoption of counterinsurgency came "too little too late", and took place "largely nowhere else but Laos" via [[operation-x]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 16:25-16:55)*
- The French joined NATO in 1949; the host says the US sent nearly $3.5 billion to France by 1954 for the war (citing Covert Action magazine no. 28), and that [[civil-air-transport]] had supported the French throughout. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 34:08-34:44)*
- Ended by [[battle-of-dien-bien-phu]] and the [[geneva-conference-1954]].
