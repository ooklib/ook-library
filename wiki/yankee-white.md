Yankee White is a real, publicly acknowledged White House [[special-access-program|SAP]]
designation — a single-scope background investigation standard required for personnel providing
direct support to the President and White House. Gerb cites it as an example of a legitimate
White House SAP that **was** properly reported to Congress, contrasting it with the alleged
"non-covert action" White House SAP loophole legacy programs are said to exploit instead. See
[[covert-action-program]]. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:28:54–1:29:09)*
