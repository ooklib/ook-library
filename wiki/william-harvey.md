William King Harvey, per [[eyes-wide-open]], was the CIA's "assassination czar" during the Kennedy years: an ex-FBI counterintelligence man turned CIA Berlin station chief who was called back to Washington in February 1961 to run the "executive action" program [[zr-rifle]] and, in early 1962, was placed at the head of Task Force W, the CIA's Cuba action group. This page is scoped to what the `ewa/jfk-cia` primer says; later "Road to Dallas" episodes are expected to cover him in more depth. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 29:14-29:54)*

## Claims

- Career before Cuba: joined the FBI in December 1940 (host's ASR renders his birthplace as "Bronny, Indiana"; unresolved); in July 1947 left the Bureau. The official story (per David Corn) is that he resigned when [[j-edgar-hoover]] ordered him to the Indianapolis office after a night of drinking violated the two-hour-call rule. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:11:18-1:11:37, 1:12:35-1:13:13)*
- Host's theory (explicitly speculation "in my estimation"): because [[robert-maheu]] ("Mayhew" in the ASR) also joined the FBI in December 1940 and left in 1947, and both went to work for the CIA immediately, Hoover may have been "implanting bureau moles" in the new CIA from day one to keep apprised of its operations. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:11:37-1:11:44, 1:13:19-1:14:04)*
- Joined the CIA's first class of agents in 1947; first role was in [[james-jesus-angleton]]'s counterintelligence shop. Quoting Corn: the FBI "was middle America" and the agency "the establishment," but Harvey had counter-espionage experience few in the CIA could equal. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:14:24-1:15:03)*
- Credited with correctly identifying British spy Kim Philby as a Soviet double agent in 1951, while Angleton overlooked his friend; the host notes a "more recent revisionist theory" that Angleton himself may have been a double or triple (Soviet and Israeli) agent and defers it to a future episode. Harvey's reward was the West Berlin station, "the busiest spy base in the world." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:15:03-1:16:56)*
- Bissell asked him in February 1961 to build the executive-action capability; in Harvey's (Church Committee timeline) account the White House had "twice urged" Bissell to do so. Host suspects [[mcgeorge-bundy]] or [[robert-f-kennedy]], not JFK. See [[zr-rifle]]. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:06:22-1:06:43, 1:07:43-1:08:01, 1:08:01-1:09:55)*
- In November 1961 he "took over the criminal syndicate context developed in phase 1" (per the 1967 Inspector General report), i.e. [[johnny-roselli]], [[santo-trafficante]] and the others. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:09:55-1:10:20)* Host: by the time of the IG report he was "by then disgraced." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:05:43-1:05:50)*
- Helms placed him at the head of Task Force W in early 1962; Helms "would lean on Harvey and the JMWAVE station" to proceed with anti-Cuba operations in defiance of the president. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 29:42-30:01)* Harvey ran the rogue South Florida operators from [[jm-wave-station]] as his "own small thief" (ASR; probably "fiefdom") and clashed with RFK "near weekly." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:17:40-1:18:06)*
- Lansdale allegedly gave him the nickname "the American James Bond" during their work together on the Cuba program. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:17:01-1:17:23)*
- His Berlin protege [[ted-shackley]] was elevated to head JM/WAVE in early 1962. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:18:06-1:18:35)*

## Relationships

- Employed by the [[federal-bureau-of-investigation]] (1940-47) and then the [[central-intelligence-agency]].
- Ran [[zr-rifle]] for [[richard-bissell]] and under [[richard-helms]]; worked alongside [[edward-lansdale]] on the Cuba show ([[operation-mongoose]]).
- Associate of [[robert-maheu]]; mentor of [[ted-shackley]]; used [[jm-wave-station]] as his Miami base.

## Additional claims (The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE, ewa/jmwave)


- Head of Task Force W (the Cuban Division) at Langley and Shackley's boss; brought Shackley from Berlin. Hotheaded and frustrated by [[robert-f-kennedy]]'s oversight: he complained to DCI [[john-mccone]] about advance approval of "major operations" and detail such as "the gradient on the beach." Feud with RFK, "one of several" RFK feuds (also [[j-edgar-hoover]] and [[lyndon-b-johnson]], per host). *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 38:13-40:40)*
- Per John Sherwood (via Trento), Shackley turned on Harvey by cultivating RFK. Church Committee: April 1962 U-Haul of arms for [[johnny-roselli]] with Shackley's help under [[zr-rifle]]. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 40:46-43:39)*
- RFK blamed him ("We'd been working with him for a year and no accomplishments"); RFK reportedly demanded his late-1962 transfer; sent as Rome station chief in early 1963, replaced by [[desmond-fitzgerald]]. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 45:28-46:15, 50:47-51:10)*
- Assassination angle: host notes speculation that Harvey "went rogue" in Rome. Via David Talbot's *The Devil's Chessboard*, HSCA researcher Dan Hardway said documents showed Harvey travelling a lot in the weeks before the assassination, and that his memo making the case against Harvey on classified paper "has since disappeared." Church Committee timelines show his last contact with Roselli in June 1963. The host calls Rome "often overlooked," promises coverage later and declines to "get too hung up" on Harvey. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 46:15-48:42)* See [[kennedy-assassination]].
- Alcoholism; retired 1968 and returned to Indiana; died of a heart attack in Indianapolis in 1976 aged 60, a little under a year after Church Committee testimony; the host says he cultivated Operation Gladio in Rome. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 48:42-49:27)* See [[operation-gladio]].
