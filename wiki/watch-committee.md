The "watch committee" is the name, per the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]], that a three-person team gave itself: a security director (former NSA), a program director and a corporate attorney who oversee an alleged embedded UFO crash-retrieval/reverse-engineering special access program. UAP Gerb repeatedly stresses the term. Claims below are attributed to the notes as retold by Gerb. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 11:11-11:28)*

## Claims

- Of seven program managers [[thomas-wilson|Wilson]] called after finding an "unusual records group," four referred him to the same program, run by these three people; the four were described as "part of it in different compartments placed in different layers of the compartment's pyramid" (Gerb: unclear whether adjacent reverse-engineering or material-exploitation programs; he links it to [[david-grush]]'s nested-programs claim). *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 11:11-11:44)*
- They said they formed the committee out of necessity to protect themselves after a "near disaster" - a past audit that almost blew their cover and led to a "tug of war" with the Pentagon over money; the auditor was briefed and toured the program, and a formal agreement was struck with SAPOC. Strict access criteria followed, which they refused to disclose. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 14:02-14:56)*
- Wilson flew to meet all three in a secure-vault conference room and was told he would not be read in; his tickets were valid but he was "not on the bigot list." They showed him pages of [[bigot-list|bigot list]] data (1990-1993): all civilians (scientists, technicians, engineers, managers), no politicians or White House, Clinton or Bush administration names, but some Pentagon, NSC and other department names. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 15:03-16:14)*
- They confirmed to Wilson only that the program was not weapons, intelligence, spec ops or logistics ("it's okay to say it"), and declined to discuss Roswell, Holloman AFB landings, [[majestic-12]], the leaked documents or the Zamora and Bentwaters cases. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 17:43-17:57)*
- When Wilson phoned them by conference call, they reacted with "confusion and agitation," asking who he had talked to; he demanded a formal briefing using his DIA authority, and they agreed to meet. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 13:07-13:54)*
