Jorjani's claim (drawing on Joseph Farrell and on Plimpton Hydrick's *Critical Mass*) that the Nazis had a real, secret nuclear weapons programme and traded it, and possibly a more advanced weapon, to the United States. Not established; his own speculation is layered on the authors' arguments, and Michels says he would not "rest anything" on the Heisenberg transcripts.

## Claims

- Ronald Richter set up a "fusion" project on Huemul Island near Bariloche for Juan Peron in the late 1940s and early 1950s; IAEA investigators (dated 1951 by Jorjani) found a complex facility that did not resemble known fusion and it never produced weapons. Jorjani visited Bariloche while hiking. See [[ronald-richter]]. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:05:18, 1:10:30, 1:10:39)*
- Two eyewitness accounts of German nuclear tests in late 1944-early 1945 (Baltic Sea, and the Thuringian forest with a local power cut). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:12:06)*
- The Heisenberg enriched-uranium programme was a red herring and cover; a black programme used electromagnetic isotope separation (later used at Oak Ridge) and possibly laser enrichment, and reached fission weapons. Jorjani also claims Iran uses laser enrichment. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:13:57, 1:14:28, 1:15:16)*
- Hydrick argues the fissile material at Trinity was insufficient for Fat Man and Little Boy, and that in 1945 a submarine deal with the SS supplied highly enriched uranium, with Nazi help on the plutonium bomb. Michels notes the Farm Hall wiretap transcripts (Heisenberg's men quickly estimated the fissile mass at Hiroshima). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:18:54, 1:19:13, 1:17:41, 1:18:16)*
- Why not use them? (1) Perhaps they had a fusion weapon with no fission trigger, a "clean" bomb, making atom bombs "matches", and traded fission technology for amnesty, the SS network's incorporation into the CIA and wartime loot in Argentina while keeping the fusion bombs. (2) Using nukes would have brought total war and lost the long game: see [[thousand-year-reich-worldview-war-claims]]. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:20:16, 1:21:53, 1:23:06)*
