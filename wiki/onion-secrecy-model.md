Gerb's recurring metaphor for the layered-compartmentation secrecy architecture of the alleged
[[ufo-legacy-programs|legacy programs]]: "layers of secrecy protect and are protected by
seemingly infinite other layers that indeed protect core secrets." *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 55:44–55:52)*

## Claims

- Gerb states [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]] "innovated the onion secrecy model within the legacy
  programs," introducing compartmentation and managed-need-to-know techniques used at scale
  today. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 15:53–16:01, 48:39–48:57)*
- MITRE's security architecture was described to Gerb (by a source) as "the origin of the Onion
  model." *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 55:44–55:52)*
- Compared to [[john-craven]]'s "seventh veil" analogy — even highly cleared individuals may
  never know if they've reached the innermost compartment. See [[seventh-veil-analogy]].
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 56:07–56:38)*

## Claims (from the "Secrecy" Vol. 1 video — full articulation)

**Note on attribution:** in this later, more comprehensive video, Gerb presents "The Onion" as
his own explicit analytical framework/"executive summary" device for organizing the entire
two-part investigation — distinct from the earlier MITRE-video claim (above) that a named source
called MITRE's internal security culture "the origin of the Onion model." Both are Gerb's framing
choices, not established fact; treat "The Onion" throughout as UAP Gerb's own coined
conceptual lens.

- Defines the model explicitly: "layers upon layers of secrecy where the center of the onion...
  is always hidden and obscured by another layer of protection and secrecy," applicable not just
  to the modern day but back to the alleged 1947 inception of the
  [[manhattan-project-2|Manhattan Project 2.0]]. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  10:48–11:20)*
- States he learned of an "onion briefing" — a real, recurring briefing format allegedly given
  "hundreds of times" to senior statesmen and flag officers by a self-described Navy/FFRDC USO-
  activity security individual, who told Gerb: "I was on the legacy program when I found out
  there were others." Gerb explicitly declines to make this individual's other claims the focus of
  the investigation. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 17:39–18:06)*
- Lists specific historical "layers" he intends the two-part series to peel back: Manhattan
  Project secrecy architecture; classified presidential executive orders under Truman and
  Eisenhower (see [[presidential-emergency-action-documents]]); the 1954
  [[atomic-energy-act-1954|Atomic Energy Act]]; Cold War [[special-access-program|SAP]]
  expansions and oversight reforms; the [[aaro|ATIP]] perception-management campaign; David
  Grush's investigation; and Sean Kirkpatrick's AARO tenure. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 11:29–12:11)*
- Identifies the model's "outermost layer/skin" as disinformation and narrative management
  (including the ATIP/[[to-the-stars-academy|To The Stars Academy]] partial-transparency effort,
  which he says originates from within the onion itself via the
  [[national-security-council|NSC]] and [[james-clapper|James Clapper]]), and its deepest
  identified layer as "unrivaled secrecy" — numerous minor layers between the SAP layer and the
  core secrecy structure not individually significant enough to warrant dedicated sections.
  *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 27:48–28:53, 30:58–31:12)*
- Names specific program-protection/counterintelligence "antibody" offices that enforce inner
  onion layers: DoD/component-level [[special-access-program-central-office|SAPCOs]], Air Force
  OSI/Office of Special Projects, DARPA's Security and Intelligence Directorate, DOE's Office of
  Intelligence and Counterintelligence, and the Air Force Life Cycle Management Center's
  Information Protection Directorate. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  30:17–30:49)*
- Explicitly rejects [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]]'s claim that the entire UFO legacy-program
  portfolio is held within contractors specifically to avoid FOIA — Gerb counters "the legacy
  security apparatus is nowhere near that simple," citing an on-screen government interviewee's
  remark that government-held activity is FOIA-accessible while contractor-held activity is not
  as only one small piece of a much larger structure. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 31:12–32:01)*
