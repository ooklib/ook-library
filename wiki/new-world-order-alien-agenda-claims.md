In the Q&A of the 1995 lecture ([[schneider-las-vegas-lecture-1995]]) [[phil-schneider]] ties the "New World Order" (which he says is above the US government and is slowly replacing it via the UN) to an alien agenda. All unverified claims.

## Claims

- The NWO takes its orders "100 percent" from aliens and wants the planet for itself; many "one-worlders" do not know this and are seen by the aliens as "bags of food". *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 57:22, 58:19)*
- Eleven alien civilizations visit Earth, all known to the US military; nine are "bad news", two are benevolent but have supposedly left, and Valiant Thor is called a repudiated Venusian, a Pleiadian-like with six digits and two hearts. Speech is high-speed "whale song" to human ears. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 55:53, 56:24)*
- The hostile aliens feed on human glandular secretions such as adrenochrome; the Atlanta CDC allegedly has countermeasures; AIDS, Ebola and hantavirus are described as engineered "designer diseases". *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 58:36)*
- "Global 2000" as a plan to cut the population by five sixths, about five of six billion people, and enslave the rest; alien takeover to stay "in the background" for the next 20-40 years. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 57:22, 1:03:21)*
- The underground bases and 39 prison "boot camps" will become internment and slave-labor sites under martial law, with executions of the old and infirm; people are trained by ex-KGB and Spetsnaz on US soil; police are taught "martial law research". *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 20:00, 22:09, 52:55, 1:08:30)*
- Workers are held by secrecy oaths, with pay of 4,000 to 40,000 dollars a month. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 1:09:39)*
