SS program founded in 1935 to breed a racially "pure" blond, blue-eyed population. The documentary calls it one of the most secretive Nazi projects and ties it to the occult Aryan-Atlantean origin myth held by the secret societies.

## Claims

- Believed to have produced over 8,000 children; testimony from Gisela Heidenreich (ASR "Gazella"), daughter of an SS officer father. Mothers had to meet racial criteria and could name an SS man as father without marriage. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 31:16,)*
- The program also allegedly took thousands of children from Poland and northern countries, put them in homes and placed them with German families to be "Germanised". *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 33:14,)*
- Origin: the Nazi elite believed the Aryan race went back some 10,000 years to Atlantis and had been soiled by mixing. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 30:05,)*
