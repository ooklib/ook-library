The Georgetown Set is the informal Washington, DC social circle of former OSS agents, CIA executives, sponsored journalists, and Truman-era officials that [[eyes-wide-open]] describes (drawing on David Talbot's *Brothers* and a Politico Magazine article) as an unofficial forum where intelligence operations and media narratives were coordinated. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 14:19-15:26)*

## Claims

- Members and attendees named by the host: [[frank-wisner]] (center until his 1958 breakdown and 1965 suicide), [[richard-helms]], Tracy Barnes (Bissell's deputy, Bay of Pigs organizer), [[desmond-fitzgerald]], [[allen-dulles]], [[james-jesus-angleton]], [[richard-bissell]] (a "gregarious schmoozer" since the early 1950s), [[cord-meyer]]. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 14:27-16:28, 18:11-18:23)*
- Journalists: [[thomas-braden]], Philip Graham (OSS under Donovan, World Bank, Washington Post), David K. Bruce, and the Alsop brothers; Joseph Alsop's house is called a "salon" (Talbot) and Alsop is "the journalist of choice of the [[china-lobby]]". *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 16:34-17:29)*
- Officials: Dean Acheson, William Averell Harriman, John McCloy and [[paul-nitze]], author of [[nsc-68]]; host says they advised presidents into the 1980s. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 18:57-19:31)*
- Host: the symbiosis (journalists get scoops, spies get press access) makes the parties a possible "origin point" of Operation Mockingbird; Cord Meyer (CIA propaganda chief) set up fronts like Radio Free Europe and allegedly coined "compatible left". *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 17:29-18:57)* See [[cia-international-organizations-division]].
- [[john-f-kennedy]] was allegedly at a few functions; Alsop recommended Lyndon Johnson at the 1960 convention (host: possibly just a strategy to win the South; Johnson was not a member); rumors said Dulles or subordinates fed Cuba intelligence to the Kennedy camp, which let Kennedy attack Nixon from the right on Cuba while Nixon, bound by Bay of Pigs secrecy, could not respond. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 19:31-21:44)* See [[lyndon-b-johnson]], [[richard-nixon]].
- Host uses the set to argue [[richard-bissell]] likely heard the ideas behind [[joseph-caldwell-king]]'s memo in these circles. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 14:05-14:27)*
