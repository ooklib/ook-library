Elroy John Center was a senior research chemical engineer at [[battelle-memorial-institute]] from 1939 to 1957, and co-author of the titanium-alloy analytical-methods section of Battelle's 1949 report. According to a 1994 MUFON (Mutual UFO Network) Ohio report relayed by [[uap-gerb]], he told his daughter's date in 1958 that he had been responsible for a project studying parts retrieved from a flying saucer. This is an unverified second-hand account.

## Claims (gerb/battelle)

- Gerb says Center worked at Battelle 1939-1957 and authored, with an A. C. Eckert (ASR), the "analytical methods for titanium based alloys" subsection of report 2, about the titanium purity needed for nitinol. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 8:46-9:23)*
- Gerb cites the summer 1994 Ohio UFO Notebook by Dr. Irene Scott and the MUFON board piece "The Ohio UFO Crash Connection and Other Stories": an informant who came to MUFON in 1992 dated Center's daughter in 1958 in Columbus, and Center allegedly said he was responsible for a project requiring study of parts retrieved from a flying saucer; the report has a section decoding the I-beam. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 9:23-10:24)*
- Gerb argues that since the nitinol work was declassified only in 2010, this account is credible unless Center really worked on crash-disc material, likely Roswell wreckage, and talked after leaving Battelle in 1957/58; he calls it "another kernel of credibility". *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 10:41-11:10, 15:01-15:34)*
