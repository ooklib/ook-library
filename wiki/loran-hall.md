Loran Eugene Hall (ASR "Lauren Eugene Hall"; also "Lorenzo Hall", given as an alias) is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the archetypal low-grade recruit among the mercenaries who flooded Havana in 1959 and later filled the roster of [[interpen]]. He recurs in the [[road-to-dallas]] series as a suspect in various JFK theories and through the [[odio-incident]]; the host says this chapter covers only his 1959 background and that he will return "closer to the assassination". The normalization "Loran" (the wiki's and the bibliography's spelling) is my inference; the ASR says "Lauren". *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 8:04-8:26)*

## Claims

- Born 1930 in Newton, Kansas; drifted between jobs (including a short Boeing plant job that ended after he fell off a plane); claimed Marine service but there are no records; instead served as a weapons specialist in the Army 1947-52, partly in Germany. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 10:49-11:33)*
- Left Kansas for Cuba in March 1959, abandoning a separated wife and four children (per FBI interview of his father, Jack Hall, taken in 1959 and retrieved in 1964 after Hall's name arose at the [[warren-commission]]). The father said he had "no idea" why his son went; the host uses Hall as the "bottom end of the recruit pool". *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 11:33-12:07, 17:08-17:49)*
- Arrested by Cuban police less than a month after arriving for "not getting along with Captain Salazar", his superior, having joined Castro's men including [[gerry-patrick-hemming]] and [[frank-sturgis]] who were planning a Nicaraguan invasion; he said he "thought he was working for Dr. Fidel Castro". *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 16:29-17:08)*
- His FBI file says he met "Captain Frank Fiorini" (Sturgis) and two other Americans, Lieutenant Jack Sheay and Captain Rex Sunderland; host identifies "Jack Sheay" with [[jack-cannon]] via Hemming's HSCA testimony. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 17:58-18:38)*
- Hemming told Senate investigators in 1963 (as the host phrases it) that he believed "Lorenzo Hall" was involved in the JFK assassination, and in a 1993 Argosy interview with Dick Russell said he called Texas from Miami about 1:30-2:00 p.m. on the day and was told Hall was in Dallas, and that Hall might have used a Johnson-type breakdown rifle. Host: the Interpen men's willingness to finger their own is why they are "problematic" to researchers; he speculates Interpen served as "distractions" and "additional paths". *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 8:26-9:52)*
- Later Warren Commission episode: Hall (with Lawrence Howard and William Seymour, Interpen associates) was named by an FBI report as one of the three men at Sylvia Odio's apartment; per host and Larry Hancock, the FBI supplied this evidence after Odio's inconvenient testimony, and none of the three remembered being there. Hall initially made a partial confession naming Seymour as the "Leon Oswald", later retracted. See [[odio-incident]]. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 11:58-14:00, 15:11-16:04)*
- Host speculation: the FBI's interest in Hall from 1959 on (interviewing his father) might indicate Hall was scouted or used by the [[federal-bureau-of-investigation]], or that his confession was a "fall guy" arrangement or Interpen training ("take the blame and move on"). *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 14:17-16:29)*

## Relationships

- Recruit of [[gerry-patrick-hemming]] and member of [[interpen]]; met [[frank-sturgis]] in Havana 1959; linked via [[odio-incident]] to [[lee-harvey-oswald]] and the [[kennedy-assassination]] (contested).

## Additional claims (Road to Dallas Ch. 4)

- Per his FBI file, shared a Cuban jail cell with [[santo-trafficante]] (1959); [[gerry-patrick-hemming]] met him there as a fellow prisoner and, per the same document, did not see him again until January 1963 in Los Angeles; Hall then followed Hemming to Florida as a "groupy" around [[interpen]]. Hemming denies Hall was a member; the host doubts that. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 15:25-17:06)*
- Host: Hall's cell also connects him to the "gangster named Ruby" who visited Trafficante ([[jack-ruby]]); asks why the Warren Commission never asked Hall about it. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 17:13-18:35)*
