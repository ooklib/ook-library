Air Force General H. Marshall Ward is named in the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]] as one of two officials (with [[bill-perry]]) who told [[thomas-wilson]] to search a special records group. Other wiki pages call him "Michael Ward" per the SAIC transcript; the name is ASR-sensitive. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 9:49-10:29)*

## Claims

- In April-June 1997 Wilson's 45-day investigation was guided by Ward and Secretary of Defense Bill Perry (Gerb's wording; the memo's own reference may be to Perry's deputy), who directed him to records-group files in the office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Technology - a "special project records group not belonging to usual special access programs," a subset of "unacknowledged carve outs, waived programs" organized in 1994 by Perry. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 9:49-10:29)*
