General Arthur Trudeau, per [[philip-j-corso]]'s claims, had direct knowledge of UFO crash
retrievals and entered the role of head of Army OCRD (Office of the Chief of Research and
Development) in spring 1958 — a few months before [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]'s creation.
*([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:05:22–1:05:38)*

## Claims

- Under Trudeau, OCRD created the [[army-research-office|Army Research Office (ARO)]] in spring
  1958, directly coordinating with Navy and Air Force research and development — a fact Gerb
  wonders may indicate "MITRE crossover." *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:05:38–1:05:54)*
- Corso claims Trudeau's Army Foreign Technology Division was mandated to recruit trusted
  defense-industry partners to adapt Roswell-derived technology. *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:04:16–1:04:41)*

## Relationships

- Directed the Army Foreign Technology Division that employed [[philip-j-corso]]; founded the
  [[army-research-office]] in 1958.
