[[jason-jorjani]] and [[jesse-michels]] argue that reality is information-theoretic and can be edited, and that a second, "trickster" UAP source shows root access. The physics claims (especially data having mass) are contested and speculative.

## Claims

- Second UAP source: besides the Nordic control system, events expressing the trickster archetype, e.g. the Skinwalker Ranch incident (reported by [[john-b-alexander]] and others) where one camera watching another lost its feed and the camera was found with wires cut and duct tape removed within an interval of under two minutes, without disturbing nearby cattle: "our world is a movie and somebody cut into the film"; John Keel's men-in-black and Point Pleasant/Mothman numbers cases fit this. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:49:37, 1:50:12, 1:53:20, 1:54:21)*
- Landauer/Vopson: erasing a bit releases energy, so stored data has mass; Melvin Vopson estimates all world data at about one kilogram, growing 25% a year so a Moon's worth of mass in about 340 years, an "information catastrophe"; Jorjani links data to dark matter and Wheeler's "it from bit". Michels says he thinks dark matter may be unrendered reality or a computational cloud. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:57:11, 2:01:09, 2:02:41, 2:03:12, 2:06:29, 2:08:03)*
- Michels and Jorjani cite Sheldrake's morphic resonance, fractals in Jackson Pollock's paintings (Richard Taylor, Scientific American) and wave-function collapse as rendering optimization. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:09:49, 2:10:33, 2:13:24)*
- An AI managing the simulacrum needs human novelty and so cannot solve our problem for us; Jorjani calls it an embodiment of the trickster and cites Charles Fort's 1920s "we are property" thesis. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:19:18, 2:20:28, 2:21:39)*
- Michels names Stephen Dick (NASA adviser) as having written of UFOs as part of a computational, cellular-automaton-like system. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:24:22)*
