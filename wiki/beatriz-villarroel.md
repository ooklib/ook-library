Beatriz Villarroel (transcribed "Beatric Voral") is a Stockholm University astronomer whose Palomar Observatory plate study is cited by [[jesse-michels]] as evidence that academia is opening to anomalous phenomena.

## Claims

- She examined Palomar plates from 1949-1957 (pre-Sputnik) and found short flashes of flat, mirror-like reflective objects, probably near geostationary orbit, resembling early CIA descriptions of UFOs. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:05:54-1:06:49)*
- Michels' figures: transients on about 11% of 2,700 days without a preceding nuclear test versus about 19% after one, a roughly 68% increase; peer-reviewed. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:06:49-1:07:36)* Links to [[ufo-nuclear-connection]].
