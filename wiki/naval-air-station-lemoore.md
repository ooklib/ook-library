The Navy's West Coast master jet base in California's central valley, home to Hornet and Super Hornet strike fighter squadrons. In [[gerb-castle-video]] it is one of Gerb's candidate homes of the triangle in [[castle-29-palms-triangle-encounter-1997]]; ASR renders it "Lamore".

## Claims (gerb/castle)

- Gerb says the Navy assembled air crews from Lemoore with those from Fallon for the "Hunter Air" concept in the [[hunter-warrior-advanced-warfighting-experiment-1997]]. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 59:41-1:00:28)*
- He reports a 2001 testimony (given as 2012 sightings) of Navy aviation maintenance administrator Angelo Asetta of a slow triangular object with white glowing points above FRC West at Lemoore, seen four times between February and October 2012 with no response from the station. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:08:10-1:08:39)*
- He says Lemoore is one of few facilities with "Poncho 3" clearance into the [[r2508-restricted-airspace-complex]], joining [[edwards-air-force-base]], [[china-lake]] and [[air-force-plant-42]] in his list of legacy-program-linked sites. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:11:17-1:13:18)*
