[[jason-jorjani]]'s thesis that the "NHI" behind UFOs are not non-human but a Nordic-looking human faction that regards itself as overlord, uses masks and deception, and manages a control system over Earth. His time-travel explanation is speculation.

## Claims

- Sources cited: [[ingo-swann]] (Nordics on the Moon and Mars), Joe McMoneagle (Mars 1,000,000 BC), Travis Walton (Nordics after robotic grays), Betty Andreasson (angelic Nordics; grays record deeds; a "source" light that sent Jesus), and the 1950s Italian Bruno Sammaciccia case where Nordics warned of the soulless "Contrari". *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:14:24, 1:18:23, 1:20:15, 1:22:55)*
- The grays are cybernetic robots serving the Nordics, so "good Nordics versus bad grays" (including the Eisenhower story) is a good-cop/bad-cop bait and switch; Sammaciccia himself wondered about SS eugenics programs continued underground. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:22:07, 1:23:55)*
- Because "NHI" is a misnomer, the deepest secret is a Hegelian master-slave dialectic in which masters appear as angels; the real deception is that they lie, as in Vallee's fairy-folk folklore. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:01:10, 1:25:12)*
- Contactees who claim benevolent Martians, Venusians or Pleiadians (Adamski, Van Tassel, Billy Meier) are distrusted; see [[contactee-nordic-racialism-claims]]. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:24:52)*
- Time scale: Nordics appear on Mars millions of years ago and today because a group of humans achieved time travel (perhaps in 2050 or 3100) and intervene "hyperdimensionally" in past epochs; the Martian cryo-chambers McMoneagle saw were waiting for rescue. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:31:19, 1:31:58)*
- Zero-point electrogravitic craft are "flying time machines" (citing 1950s Martin Aircraft's "G engines", [[electrogravitics]], and Michael Masters); [[lynn-buchanan]] said pilots at a hidden California facility were trained to cope with time displacement. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:26:32, 1:27:40, 1:29:37)*
- Related sighting: Jorjani says [[john-b-alexander]] told him of two Nordics in a Las Vegas casino who read his mind, judged "he's not part of the program", and moved on. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:51:12)*
- Michels says he struggles with race-specific claims (Nordics, reptilians); Jorjani replies that abstraction is a psychological defense, and disclosure will not happen until the power structure is dismantled. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:45:34, 1:46:25, 1:47:05)*
- Why no disclosure: sovereignty, psi recognition and inability to defend, as in Havana syndrome. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:26:08, 2:28:30)*
