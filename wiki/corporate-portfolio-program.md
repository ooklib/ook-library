A DoD program giving defense-industrial-base corporations like
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] access to DoD executives, scientists,
and management, with broad access to special access programs. Established in 2020 under then
Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment [[ellen-lord|Ellen Lord]].
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 22:19–22:50)*

## Claims

- Lord joined SAIC's advisory board in July 2021 — shortly after establishing the program that
  gives SAIC (and similar corporations) this privileged access. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  22:50–23:00)*

- Also cited in Gerb's Lockheed investigation as giving DIB contractors like Lockheed and SAIC
  access to DoD executives, scientists, and management, with broad special-access-program
  portfolio access. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 30:47–31:14)*

## Relationships

- Established by [[ellen-lord]]; gives [[science-applications-international-corporation]] and
  [[lockheed-martin]] privileged DoD/SAP access.

## Claims (gerb/off-world-tech)

- Gerb says SAIC is eligible under the program, which gives contractors broad access to SAP portfolios, and ties it to [[david-grush]]'s description of contractors working with DoD on UAP programs; he suggests Crane's contracts fall under SETA and R&D with money split between acknowledged and unacknowledged SAPs. *([gerb/off-world-tech](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-07-17 - Off-World Technologies Division - UAP Technology Reverse Engineering.md>) 34:33-35:40)*
