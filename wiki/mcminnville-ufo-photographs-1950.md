Two photographs of a metallic disc taken by farmer Paul Trent near McMinnville, Oregon, in 1950, described by [[uap-gerb]] as among the most famous UFO photographs ever taken. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 3:34-3:47)*

## Claims

- On May 11, 1950 Evelyn Trent saw a slow, metallic disc-shaped object approaching from the northeast, called her husband Paul, and he took two photographs near their home. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 3:40-4:12)*
- The negatives were loaned to astronomer [[william-k-hartmann]] of the [[condon-committee]] (1967). Gerb says the committee was basically told to explain UFOs away as swamp gas and weather balloons, yet Hartmann found the photos remarkable. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 4:12-4:45)*
- Hartmann noted the underside of the craft appeared lighter than the underside of a nearby oil tank, which he attributed to atmospheric extinction, implying the object was farther away than the tank and not a small object hung on a string as debunkers suggest. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 4:45-5:15)*
- Hartmann is quoted as writing that this was one of the few UFO reports in which geometric, psychological and physical factors all appear consistent with an extraordinary, silvery, metallic disc tens of meters in diameter flying within sight of two witnesses. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 5:15-5:40)*
