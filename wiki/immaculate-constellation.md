An alleged National Security Council unacknowledged Special Access Program (USAP), established in
2017 with the mission of "collecting imagery intelligence on unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP)
and ARV/RV [alien/adversary reproduction vehicles] utilizing tasked and untasked intelligence
resources," acting as "a nexus for collecting, analyzing, and disseminating intelligence on the
activities, capabilities, and locations of anomalous aerospace threats." In 2024, whistleblower
[[matthew-brown|Matthew Brown]] delivered the Immaculate Constellation report to Congress through
Jeremy Corbell. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 39:52–40:39)*

Gerb repeatedly stresses **IMCON is not a UFO legacy program** — it is a reactionary USAP,
established the same year as the Rubio/Gillibrand congressional UFO inquiries, likely originating
from the National Security Council to give tasked/untasked IC assets "eyes and ears" on nonhuman
craft and derivative technologies (parallel to, but siloed from, decades-older legacy programs).
*([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 42:16–43:47, 3:05:02–3:06:10)*

## Claims

- Gerb speculates the 2017 MITRE program-control shift may factor into or connect with
  Immaculate Constellation, allowing [[james-clapper]]'s NSC crash-retrieval portfolio better
  "situational awareness" over the reverse-engineered assets. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:15:52–2:16:19)*
- Gerb notes the program's actual roots likely precede 2017, citing the NRO's "Sentient" AI
  program (deployed ~2016, with roots traceable to ~2010–2011) as a parallel example of
  pre-dated program origins. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:16:04–2:16:29)*
- Self-identified whistleblower [[matthew-brown|Matthew Brown]] tweeted on 5 June 2025 that UFO
  materials/craft were stored at [[naval-air-station-patuxent-river|Pax River]]'s special-project
  hangars, owned by [[amentum|Amentum Corporation]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:51:19–1:51:34)*

- Relies heavily, per Gerb, on [[sentient-nro-ai-program|NRO Sentient]] as a tasked intelligence
  resource — IMCON was established in 2017, but Sentient's core buildout occurred in 2016.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 41:57–42:16)*
- IMCON report cases reviewed by Gerb: a large disc using cloud cover over INDOPACOM and a tic-tac
  transiting the Atlantic over SOUTHCOM, both allegedly captured via OPIR platforms Gerb
  attributes to the NRO. Notably, no crash imagery appears anywhere in the report — Gerb argues
  crash retrieval is outside IMCON's scope since crash sites (unlike tracked transits) aren't
  predictable/episodic. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 46:14–48:12, 1:01:33–1:02:27)*
- Gerb theorizes [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]] — TTSA's DIA/CIA/NRO liaison, 2017–2021 — made an
  authorized or unauthorized disclosure of IMCON-adjacent "episodic visit" data to
  [[tom-delonge|Tom DeLonge]] in 2017, 7 years before Brown's public disclosure.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 55:58–57:18)*
