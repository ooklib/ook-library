Armed DOE anti-nuclear-terrorist security force with arrest-on-site authority that guards nuclear facilities and moves weapons across the US. [[uap-gerb]] treats it as a less likely identity for the black-clad armed men [[jonathan-weygandt]] met in Peru in 1997, and links it to the [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team]].

## Claims (gerb/weygandt)

- Gerb says NEST personnel were not armed operators, so the black-clad men were probably not NEST; he offers the SRT as a second, "far less likely" candidate. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 52:21-52:39, 1:08:14-1:08:38)*
- He says the SRT had arrest authority on site and at least in the 1980s wore all-black fatigues, and in the late 1990s guarded Los Alamos and Lawrence Livermore. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:08:38-1:08:56)*
- He says SRTs were trained by Wackenhut (ASR "Wacken Hut"), a private security firm that guarded legacy-program sites and once chaired by [[bobby-ray-inman]] (who allegedly told Bob Oechsler recovered UFOs may one day be available for research); he found no evidence SRTs ever operated outside the US but suggests one could travel with NEST assets. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:08:56-1:09:46)*
