In the early and mid 2010s a dispute developed over [[edgar-fuche]]'s credibility. Critics were [[jeremy-rys]] ("Rice", alienscientist.com) and YouTuber David Hamilton (not to be confused with [[bill-hamilton]]); defenders included author Andrew Johnson ("Secret Space Program", extensive interviews with Fouche) and researcher Dan Benkert (co-author with Michael Schratt). Gerb says he has "zero skin in this game" and only relays the arguments.

## Claims

- Rice wrote the TR-3B account had "no technical value" and was likely fabricated to merge the Nazi Bell device with the [[belgian-ufo-wave-1989]] triangles, objected to the mercury plasma, called Fouche deceitful and alcoholic, and claimed Fouche sent him a photo of himself with the F-117 prototype crew that was lifted from an "F-117A" magazine article whose photographer replied that Fouche "doesn't know what he is talking about". *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 45:11-47:47)*
- Gerb faults Rice's leading emails to the photographer, notes the Hamilton and Rice videos ("Edgar Fouche fake documents", 2013) were later removed, and that Fouche's July 2014 Outpost Forum reply addressed only small document inconsistencies (indentations, pressure, degradation) and that the military service documents were obtained by Hamilton himself via FOIA. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 47:47-49:45)*
- Hamilton allegedly told Benkert in a 2012 Skype chat "I work for an agency that monitors things you shouldn't do... I now control Ed not you... the exposer becomes a tool of control." *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 49:45-50:37)*
- Gerb's own view: the most valid criticism is that Fouche never worked on the TR-3B himself and relays secondhand information from five friends, but the story is "not as simple" as gloming onto the Belgian wave. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 1:13:27-1:14:16)*
