The US Army's Major Range and Test Facility Base (MRTFB) at [[dugway-proving-ground]], run by the [[army-test-and-evaluation-command]] (ATEC) and, per Gerb, largely funded by the Joint Services Chemical and Biological Defense Program. Gerb's thesis is that WDTC and the southern [[utah-test-and-training-range]] together host a joint Army/Air Force UFO reverse-engineering program. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 7:31, 9:00, 9:25, 34:15, 55:48)*)*

## Claims

- About 77% of acknowledged WDTC work in fiscal year 2013 was for the Chemical and Biological Defense Program (CBDP), per a GAO figure Gerb cites. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 57:36, 57:45, 1:11:09)*)*
- Leadership crossovers with [[battelle-memorial-institute]]: director Ryan W. Harris was a Battelle project manager at Dugway (from 2000) before joining DPG in 2004 and becoming WDTC test director in 2012; Greg Frank was a Dugway chemist (1984-85), then Battelle project manager and later Battelle Science and Technology International EVP; Anders Weborg, ex-director of WDTC's special programs division (now DOE), was a Battelle test director earlier. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:01:13, 1:01:37, 1:01:53, 1:02:17, 1:02:49, 1:03:11, 1:03:26, 1:03:33)*)*
