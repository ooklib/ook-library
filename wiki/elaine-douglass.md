UFO researcher who received an anonymous paper copy of the [[deneb-report]] and passed the original to [[leonard-stringfield]]; she died in 2014. Her website (elainedouglass.com, as transcribed) reportedly hosted documents, perhaps a scan of the original, but the pages on the Coyame incident are missing from the web archive. Her family reportedly meant to maintain the site.
