  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 33:56–34:31)*

  study of the discs' "skin" — possibly involving [[battelle-memorial-institute|Battelle]] and
  [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]], with DOE's Office of Intelligence and Counter Intelligence (OICI)
  as the alleged program manager. Gerb speculates Kirkpatrick's post-AARO Oak Ridge role may
  connect to this. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 1:17:36–1:18:04, 1:18:23–1:19:41,
  1:19:35–1:19:42)*
