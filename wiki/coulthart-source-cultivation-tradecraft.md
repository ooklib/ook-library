[[ross-coulthart]] describes how he found and protected legacy-program sources, in effect a case study in journalism inside this subject.

## Claims

- He asked what kind of people and scientists would be in such a program, targeted scientists, and wrote about 160 old-fashioned handwritten letters, mailed with a stamp because paper is less trackable; in a couple of cases he dropped a letter in a mailbox unstamped to show he had been there. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 50:04-50:50, 2:32:54-2:33:35)*
- He read university theses by aerospace people and looked for authors who had written on propulsion 25 years earlier; some responded. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:33:01-2:33:50)*
- He does not use Signal or WhatsApp for sensitive sources; he uses dead-letter drops, the dark web, in-person meetings or postal mail. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 51:15-51:51)*
- He was inspired by his earlier work exposing the Echelon communications-surveillance system (with Duncan Campbell), which showed him how easy interception is. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:31:32-2:32:43)*
- A turning point was a Georgetown bar meeting where a person he knew in a very senior uniform leaned across and said "it's real, Ross". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 53:37-54:29)*
- His NewsNation role (see [[ross-coulthart]]) means people now find him more easily. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 53:21-53:37)*
