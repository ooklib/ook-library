The US Navy's research arm. In Gerb's Navy video it is the alleged staging point where retrieved undersea craft are stored and then handed to defense contractors and FFRDC/UARC partners, together with the Office of Naval Intelligence in his four-part "monitor, collect, store, exploit" model. All of this is Gerb's hypothesis and Sharp's alleged sourcing, not established fact.

## Claims (gerb/navy)

- Per [[chris-sharp]]'s third-version article, recovered undersea craft are "transferred to the Office of Naval Research, which subsequently hands them over to defense contractors for detailed analysis." *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 10:49-10:58)*
- Gerb lists ONR-linked UARCs (the applied physics laboratories at Johns Hopkins and the University of Washington, the applied research laboratories at Hawaii, Texas at Austin and Penn State) and the Center for Naval Analyses as the missing intermediary between the Navy and contractors. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 13:19-15:29)*
- Gerb notes the ONR showed interest in ESP by 2013, with a program to train personnel on intuitive decision-making, and that in 1951 an ONR physicist told a magazine that all UAP reports were mirages or balloons. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 14:30-14:55, 32:52-33:10)*
- Woods Hole manages projects for the ONR such as Project Sundance. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 46:20-46:27)*
- Gerb points out that ONR has a major Chicago department less than 40 miles from Great Lakes Naval Station, where a craft was allegedly stored in 1973. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:17:23-1:17:31)*
- Gerb speculates the ONR is "almost a staging point for housing craft" and wonders whether Army and Air Force retrievals are also staged at naval locations. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:44:16-1:44:41)*
