Edward Lansdale is described by Eyes Wide Open as a legendary CIA counter-insurgency expert and, per Alfred McCoy, Allen Dulles's main liaison in southern Vietnam, under whom [[lucien-conein]] served as top aide from 1954. The host defers fuller coverage to the First Indochina War episode. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 15:37-16:15)*

## Claims

- Conein and Lansdale worked to pacify the Laos-Vietnam border. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 16:12-16:19)*

- LAOS 2: in July 1954 he arrived in Saigon as leader of "MAG" (host: the Military Assistance Advisory Group, "set up by the American CIA" under civilian cover to evade Geneva's limits on troops), a charismatic Special Forces vet fresh from the Philippines Huk campaign, "a close personal friend to CIA director Alan Dulles", handpicked to advise Diem on counterinsurgency and to lead early stages of the Ho Chi Minh Trail war; with [[lucien-conein]] as second in command he was briefed by [[roger-trinquier]] on [[operation-x]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 45:39-47:48)*
- He later claimed to have signaled disapproval of narco funding; the host is skeptical. Dulles, by contrast, was impressed. The host frames Lansdale/Conein as mirror images of Trinquier/[[antoine-savani]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 47:15-48:13)*
- The episode preview says LAOS 3 covers the creation of South Vietnam and Lansdale's impact on counterinsurgency. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 50:01-50:18)*

## Relationships

- LAOS 2: briefed by [[roger-trinquier]] on [[operation-x]]; see [[ngo-dinh-diem]].
