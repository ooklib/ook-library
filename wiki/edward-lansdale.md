Edward Lansdale is described by Eyes Wide Open as a legendary CIA counter-insurgency expert and, per Alfred McCoy, Allen Dulles's main liaison in southern Vietnam, under whom [[lucien-conein]] served as top aide from 1954. The host defers fuller coverage to the First Indochina War episode. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 15:37-16:15)*

## Claims

- Conein and Lansdale worked to pacify the Laos-Vietnam border. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 16:12-16:19)*

- LAOS 2: in July 1954 he arrived in Saigon as leader of "MAG" (host: the Military Assistance Advisory Group, "set up by the American CIA" under civilian cover to evade Geneva's limits on troops), a charismatic Special Forces vet fresh from the Philippines Huk campaign, "a close personal friend to CIA director Alan Dulles", handpicked to advise Diem on counterinsurgency and to lead early stages of the Ho Chi Minh Trail war; with [[lucien-conein]] as second in command he was briefed by [[roger-trinquier]] on [[operation-x]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 45:39-47:48)*
- He later claimed to have signaled disapproval of narco funding; the host is skeptical. Dulles, by contrast, was impressed. The host frames Lansdale/Conein as mirror images of Trinquier/[[antoine-savani]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 47:15-48:13)*
- The episode preview says LAOS 3 covers the creation of South Vietnam and Lansdale's impact on counterinsurgency. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 50:01-50:18)*

- LAOS 3: a former San Francisco advertising executive turned WWII OSS propaganda officer, said to report only to [[allen-dulles]] when sent to Saigon on 1 June 1954 with the [[saigon-military-mission]]. In the Philippines he helped Ramon Magsaysay crush the Huk rebellion (e.g. the "vampire" rumor tactic, a civil-affairs psychological-warfare bureau, land reform and relief); the host also relays Sterling Seagrave's claim that Lansdale ran a $5 million-budget team of Filipino assassins, and notes his Philippines years remain a "massive blank spot". *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 14:47-18:08)*
- Speculative explanation of his rise: as a G2 officer he allegedly located Yamashita's loot in 1945 and secured a covert slush fund; see [[yamashita-gold]]. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 18:26-24:16)*
- In South Vietnam: helped [[ngo-dinh-diem]] win the 1955 election with CIA cash ("recently revealed"); armed and trained Diem loyalists against the [[binh-xuyen]] in the [[battle-of-saigon-1955]], importing Filipino forces; hired Napoleon Valeriano for Diem's guard. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 28:11-28:25, 33:36-35:49)*
- Host says Lansdale kept a "bureaucratic barrier" between his official counterinsurgency work and the narcotics work of subordinates such as [[lucien-conein]]; privately called Conein "the thug". His time as Diem's premier adviser ended in 1957; he was shipped to the Pentagon and later worked with the JM/WAVE CIA station in Miami on the Castro assassination program, where he met CIA station chief Ted Shockley (ASR), who will figure in the Laos war. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 39:57-40:16, 41:27-41:39, 52:34-52:58)*
- Kennedy-assassination link: the host says a "reliable source" identified Lansdale as the man walking in the photo of the three tramps in Dallas on 22 November 1963, deferring it; see [[kennedy-assassination]]. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 52:58-53:17)*
- His influence on Diem was outweighed by [[wesley-fishell]]'s, who had Diem's trust "that Diem never really provided to Lansdale or Conein". *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 53:29-54:01)*
