Medieval castle in northern Germany (ASR: "bevelsberg", "devilsburg") that Heinrich Himmler turned into the SS's premier "Ordensburg" (castle of the order). The documentary presents it as the heart of the Nazis' esoteric belief.

## Claims

- Built in the 10th century, adapted over centuries, taken over by the SS in the 1930s; intended as the centre of Germanic ideology for centuries. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 3:42,)*
- Ritual crypt under the North Tower in the shape of a beehive hut; construction halted in 1943 after Stalingrad. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 4:20,)* The unproven suggestion is that senior SS generals' ashes would have been kept in urns there. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 11:34)*
- Theo Paijmans (Dutch researcher): the SS had several Ordensburgen, each training a function; Wewelsburg was the premier one, attended by only about a dozen high-ranking officers who practised secret rituals and pagan worship. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 5:03,)*
- Main hall with 12 pillars and 12 Sig runes and a black sun wheel on the floor; the black sun linked by a speaker to hollow-earth ideas. The exact rituals are unknown. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 9:19,)*
- One eyewitness allegedly saw twelve SS generals sitting in silent meditation. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 7:51)*
- The narration also says the Nazis "restored" it to house relics such as the Ark and the Grail. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 12:21)*
