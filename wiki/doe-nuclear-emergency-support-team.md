The US government's premier response unit for radiological/nuclear accidents or incidents,
operated by DOE's semi-autonomous National Nuclear Security Administration (created 2000). Its
authority derives from the 1954 Atomic Energy Agreement. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
1:14:50–1:16:06)*

## Claims

- Gerb identifies NEST as the likely direct-response, on-site scientific team ("men in DOE
  mission-oriented protective posture/MOP gear") described by USMC Lance Cpl. [[jonathan-weygandt|
  Jonathan Weygandt]] at his 1997 Peru UFO crash encounter, arriving via US Army CH-47s alongside
  tier-1 direct-action personnel. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:13:59–1:16:15)*
- Gerb notes legacy programs additionally allegedly utilize DOE/NNSA Special Response Teams
  (SRTs) and the Office of Secure Transportation (OST) for crash retrievals and material
  transport. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:16:31–1:16:46)*
- Historically staffed by contractor/FFRDC personnel (not DOE/DoD employees directly), including
  former [[eg-and-g|EG&G]], Raytheon, [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence
  Livermore]], [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los Alamos]], and
  [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]]. In November 1997, months after Wagant's encounter,
  NEST considered (but did not execute) deployment to Bolivia/Colombia over debris risk from the
  failed Mars 96 probe launch — intelligence Gerb notes came via US Space Command's DSP
  satellites, whose payload/ground-processing systems Sandia has helped field.
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 1:30:15–1:30:31, 1:30:48–1:33:40)*
- Became a DOE/NNSA team after the NNSA's creation in 2000; per DOE Order 153.1A, the
  [[office-of-secure-transportation|Office of Secure Transportation]] "may provide
  transportation for NEST nuclear weapons, nuclear weapon components, and SNM within the
  continental United States." *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 1:34:01–1:34:09,
  1:44:10–1:44:27)*

## Claims (from the "Secrecy" Vol. 1 video)

- NEST's core forensics element, the Nuclear Forensics Material Analysis Program (NF-MAP), is
  tasked with delivering forensic results on nuclear materials found outside regulatory control;
  UAP Gerb hypothesizes "with near total certainty" it is a paramount component of alleged UFO
  crash-retrieval efforts, feeding recovered nonregulatory materials into key NNSA facilities
  (Los Alamos, Lawrence Livermore, Oak Ridge, Pacific Northwest, Y-12). *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 1:17:33–1:19:17)*
- NEST was created under Executive Order 12656 under the primary authority of the
  [[atomic-energy-act-1954|1954 Atomic Energy Act]]. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 1:16:38–1:16:53)*
