Jesse Michels' March 2026 American Alchemy episode "NASA Chief: We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!" (about 2h33m) with Dr. [[charles-buhler]], lead scientist of [[nasa]]'s Electrostatics and Surface Physics Laboratory at Kennedy Space Center and co-founder of [[exodus-propulsion-technologies]]. Buhler reports about 2,000 vacuum-chamber and in-air experiment variations showing a propellantless thrust force ("the Exodus force") that persists after the power is switched off, which Michels frames as a vindication of [[thomas-townsend-brown]] and the [[biefeld-brown-effect]]. The last hour is a physics discussion with [[david-chester]] about Buhler's quantum-electrodynamics explanation. The episode is speculative claim-making by an experimenter; nothing in it has been independently replicated or peer-reviewed, as Buhler himself acknowledges.

Victor Bertrandias (1952 gravitator demonstration in Los Angeles, "I think I just saw a flying saucer and it frightened me"), and a deathbed-confession audio from Jacques Coron about a 1956 vacuum-chamber test in Paris. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 4:37-5:53)*
