Pergamon Press was the scientific publisher [[robert-maxwell]] founded in 1951. oompaville describes its model as one of the century's most brilliant and poisonous business ideas. See [[maxwells-data-dynasty-video-2026]]; for the propulsion-paper gatekeeping allegation see [[epstein-atlantis-gravity-claims]].

## Claims

- Scientists write research (publicly or university funded) and referee it for free, then journals sell it back to university libraries; Maxwell signed up scientists directly, bypassing academic societies, and launched journals by the hundred, each a small monopoly libraries must buy, hence today's paywalls. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 12:15, 12:30, 13:01, 13:31, 13:47)*
- By the early 1990s Pergamon ran more than 400 scientific journals. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 13:56)*
