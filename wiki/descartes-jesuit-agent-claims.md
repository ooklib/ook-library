Claims by [[jason-jorjani]], discussed with [[jesse-michels]], that the mechanistic scientific paradigm was deliberately engineered to serve the Catholic Church. Speculative philosophical history, not established fact.

## Claims

- Jorjani says René Descartes was a hired Jesuit agent, effectively a military-intelligence attaché to Catholic armies burning Protestant villages, sent to infiltrate the Rosicrucian order and build a false mechanistic science. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 10:02, 11:13)*
- Effect claimed: everything not reducible to mechanism is left to the Church, so the public gets a "soul-crushingly nihilistic" science and stays with the Church; Giordano Bruno's hermetic-alchemical path was the road not taken, and Michels points to Frances Yates's Rosicrucian Enlightenment (John Dee, Edward Kelly, the Winter King). *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 10:26, 52:14, 53:12)*
- Kant read Swedenborg's *Arcana Coelestia* (extraterrestrials, clairvoyance, telekinesis), then built the "unknowable thing-in-itself" to cover the occult; Jorjani says Kant admitted the material was politically destabilizing and "storming heaven" would follow if psi were trained widely. Jorjani sees the same move in some abstract UAP theorizing. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 6:25, 8:00, 17:37)*
- Kuhn: paradigm shifts are political coups; asked who is "in the driver's seat", Jorjani answers "the religious control system on the planet". *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 24:35, 30:22)*
