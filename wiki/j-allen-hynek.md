J. Allen Hynek (ASR "heinik") is presented by [[uap-gerb]] as the scientific adviser to [[project-blue-book]] and "one of the OG whistleblowers". *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 11:44-12:08, 14:41)*

## Claims

- Gerb says Blue Book ran 1952 to 1969, analyzed 12,618 reports and left 701 unexplained, and that its stigma (swamp gas, weather balloons) stems from the [[robertson-panel-1953]] and the [[condon-committee]]. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 12:08-13:04)*
- In May 1972 Hynek wrote that Blue Book was a cover-up and that the issue was glossed over, and that it was possibly a front for a more classified group (ASR "AF f o n-x SG") and he was told not to pursue cases. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 13:04-13:37)*
- In 1979 he said the Air Force's unwritten law was "don't rock the boat" and that it debunked interesting cases as balloons or planets; he said the Condon committee ignored key evidence and that Project Grudge was more public relations than science. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 13:37-14:41)*
- Gerb says that on 13 April 1975 Donald Rumsfeld told Hynek at the White House he had no need to know whether a secret UAP study existed after Blue Book. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 14:10-14:26)*
