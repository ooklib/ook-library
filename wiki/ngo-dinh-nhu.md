Ngo Dinh Nhu (ASR: "no din new", "noin new", "new"; normalization is my inference) was [[ngo-dinh-diem]]'s brother and most trusted adviser, described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the corrupt, scheming half of the regime, controller of the secret police and of the narcotics network the French left behind. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 36:54-37:21)*

## Claims

- Formed a lobbying group for Diem's presidency around the time [[william-donovan]] and [[edward-lansdale]] arrived in Saigon. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 27:48-28:01)*
- Many speculate Diem was a mere figurehead behind Nhu, who ran the "ruthless Can Lao" secret police; he developed the [[strategic-hamlet-program]]. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 37:02-37:34)*
- Host, citing [[alfred-mccoy]]: from 1958 on Nhu, with [[lucien-conein]] likely working alongside, revived the Laos-to-Saigon opium trade to fund his secret police and intelligence network, which was organized under Dr Tran Kim Tuyen's Office of Social and Political Study. The ASR renders this "Dr tran Kim tuan" / "tuen" (spelling unverified). McCoy is quoted: Tuyen "commanded a vast intelligence network that included the CIA finance, Special Forces, the military security services and most important the clandestine Can Lao party". Tuyen allegedly reemerged after the regime's fall to help Air Marshal (and future vice president) Nguyen Cao Ky operate the network in the mid-to-late 1960s. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 39:07-39:18, 49:05-49:57)*
- Introduced to the owner of one of the Corsican-run charter airlines, "Bavans Rock Francis", forming the alliance behind the resumed opium flow from 1958; see [[air-laos-commerce]]. Host's inference: Conein, who confirmed the Nhu-Corsican relationship to McCoy, at least knew of it and let it persist. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 44:56-45:48, 47:26-47:39)*
- Host claim: CIA officer [[william-colby]], Deputy Station Chief from 1959, also advised Nhu on the Strategic Hamlet program and had a close personal relationship with him; the host suggests they may have influenced each other, and thereby Colby's later Phoenix Program. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 38:04-38:59)*
- His secret police was equipped and trained through the [[michigan-state-university-vietnam-advisory-group]]. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 55:33-55:45, 57:37-57:50)*
