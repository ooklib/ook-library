Reichsführer-SS and, per the documentary narration, "second in command only to the Führer". The Banijay documentary portrays him as an "active occultist" who personally directed the SS's ritual life at [[wewelsburg-castle]] and pursued holy relics.

## Claims

- Wanted a centre where men "could come and become true Germans", melding Aryan-race ideas with Germanic gods and volkish beliefs; the SS meditated and held seances there. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 2:50)*
- Spent a couple of years, with concentration-camp labour, building Wewelsburg, and designed its crypt personally. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 4:20,)*
- Visited the castle at every opportunity and was received with ceremony; the inner circle is said to have been twelve SS generals plus Himmler (13). *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 8:34,)*
- Ordered the castle blown up at the end of the war so the Allies would not learn its secrets; too little explosive was used. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 11:53)*
- Obsessed with the Knights Templar as "pin-up boys" of his new religion and with finding the Ark of the Covenant, Holy Grail and Spear of Destiny; sent expeditions to Tibet and Africa and, per Ross Andrews, possibly agents to Royston Cave. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 35:15,)* See [[himmler-relic-quest-claims]].
