Aurora and Astra are rumored code names for a high-speed black triangle reconnaissance aircraft allegedly made by [[lockheed-martin]] and tested at Groom Lake.

## Claims (jm/townsend-brown)

- Evidence cited: 1987 congressional budget line item of $2.3 billion, USGS sonic booms, a 1989 North Sea sighting reported in Jane's, and late-1990s UK MoD documents on black triangles with exotic propulsion. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 42:16-43:06)*
- Bob Lazar said he saw an Aurora at Groom Lake but found it unimpressive next to the disc; host reads that as amplifying the UFO narrative while downplaying human tech. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 43:25-44:02)*
- Host thesis: the ET narrative shielded deep-black projects; [[ben-rich]] complained about tech-protection costs; Lockheed used [[bob-lazar]] as a recruitment tool and the Skunk Works director's link to [[tom-delonge]]'s TTSA and lobbying (Mike Turner) against the Schumer bill fits. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 41:27-44:59)*

## Relationships

- Attributed to [[lockheed-martin]]; rival to [[b-2-stealth-bomber]].

## Additional claims (gerb/tr-3b)

- Fouche places the TR-3B ("Astra" = Advanced Stealth Technology Reconnaissance Aircraft) as the most exotic model within the Aurora program, which also covers the pulse-detonation "Pulsar" seen by [[brad-sorenson]]; Gerb cites the DOD Aurora procurement line ($80M FY86, $2.3B FY87, double the [[b-2-stealth-bomber]] cost) and the possibly-linked TR-3A (see [[boscombe-down-crash-1994]]). *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 27:44-29:06, 1:08:40-1:09:35)* See [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b]] and [[edgar-fuche]].
