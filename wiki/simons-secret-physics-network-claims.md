[[eric-weinstein]] suggested on Joe Rogan's show that [[renaissance-technologies]] might house a secret physics program, and [[matthew-pines]] elaborates on the fact pattern around [[jim-simons]] with [[jesse-michels]]. Pines explicitly says he has no smoking gun and that it "just seems quite a curious set of coincidences".

## Claims

- Pines: Simons was an NSA-linked cryptologic code-breaker, a leading mathematician, controlled the Stony Brook math department, and built a hedge fund with a reportedly 30 percent a year return and "never a down year", which would be "an immense black box" concentrating math and physics talent. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 56:04-57:28)*
- He notes the Simons Foundation funds frontier physics, AI and mathematics, and that its head David Spergel was tapped to chair the [[nasa-uap-independent-study-team]] whose report basically said there was nothing there. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 57:36-59:27)*
- Pines says Simons also engineered a Stony Brook and Battelle partnership to co-manage [[brookhaven-national-laboratory]], a Department of Energy lab doing classified work near his fund. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 58:14-58:38)*
- Pines says [[brian-keating]], a skeptic-leaning cosmologist and podcaster, is Simons' godson and a friend of Weinstein; Michels says Keating has always been "super cool" with him and he does not think he is operating in bad faith. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 59:35-59:51, 1:00:15-1:00:23)*
- Michels recalls that townspeople near Brookhaven said a UFO crashed and was collected in 1992, and links that to UFO attention on nuclear assets. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 1:00:32-1:01:01)*
- Pines: "Exactly what I would do if I was the government" is to set up a funding source and a private research group; if legacy programs exist it would be "incompetent" not to. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 59:51-1:00:12)*
- Michels adds that the [[robert-mercer]]-founded Renaissance did semantic analysis and early language-model work, fitting a team one would apply to NHI communication (see [[ai-and-nhi-communication-claims]]). *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:52:59-3:53:24)*
