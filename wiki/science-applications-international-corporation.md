Science Applications International Corporation (SAIC, called "SIC" throughout the ASR
transcript) is a huge, historically employee-owned DoD/IC contractor Gerb frames as more
intimately entwined with UFO legacy program operations "than any other American defense
contractor." *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 3:58–4:16)*

**As with the MITRE video, all claims here are a single investigator's allegations, not
established fact** — see `CLAUDE.md` for the wiki's framing.

## Corporate history

- Founded 3 February 1969 by Dr. [[j-robert-beyster]], stacked from the start with General
  Atomics scientists; one of the first employee-owned companies, using ownership as a
  recruitment/retention tool. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 7:16–8:26)*
- Also packed its board/staff from inception with "generals, diplomats, spies, and cabinet
  officers," quoted as having "better access to the Pentagon than the government's own
  attorneys." *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 8:26–9:06)*
- First government contract: Defense Atomic Support Agency, calculating nuclear device
  yields. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 9:37–9:45)*
- By 2007: workforce of 44,000+ (larger than the combined DoL/DoE/HUD workforce at the time),
  major offices in San Diego and Washington DC; described by *Vanity Fair* (2007) as a "body
  shop in the brain business" selling expertise in weapons, homeland security, surveillance,
  computer systems, and information warfare. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 9:45–10:11)*
- Maintains locations near US military/RDT&E facilities: [[naval-surface-warfare-center-crane|
  NSWC Crane]], Nellis AFB, China Lake NAS, [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]], and
  [[crystal-city-virginia|Crystal City]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 10:28–10:58)*
- **27 September 2013**: SAIC split into a $4B spinoff retaining the SAIC name, renaming the
  original entity [[leidos|Leidos]] — driven by federal acquisition conflict-of-interest
  provisions that barred bidding on new contracts given existing ones. Gerb treats "SAIC" as
  referring to both entities going forward. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 16:59–17:41)*
- **16 August 2016**: Leidos merged with Lockheed Martin's Information Systems & Global
  Solutions division ($5B acquisition); that division was renamed Leidos Government Services
  Inc. on 30 November 2017. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 17:41–17:58)*
- **2018**: SAIC acquired [[scitor-holdings|Scitor Holdings]] for $790M (all-cash), a
  CIA DS&T contractor for satellite SIGINT/ELINT. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  18:06–18:32)*
- **2018**: SAIC also purchased [[agility|Agility]] for $2.5B — a company spun off in July 2012
  from L3 Communications (later L3Harris), which had itself acquired the Virginia government
  services group of Emergent Information Technologies in 2001 (formerly Steven Meyers &
  Associates, formerly [[decision-science-applications|DSAI]]/DSA Acquisitions).
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 16:00–16:52)*

## Claim — corporate acquisitions/associations as UFO-program indicators

- Gerb frames SAIC's acquisition pattern (DSAI→...→Agility→SAIC, plus Scitor) as structurally
  similar to Northrop Grumman's acquisition of TRW/Teledyne Ryan and connections to the
  [[aerospace-corporation|Aerospace Corporation]] — i.e., a pattern of contractor consolidation
  he associates with UFO program lineage. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 10:58–11:17)*
- Scitor's old Chantilly, VA building shared a corporate park with SAIC, the
  [[aerospace-corporation|Aerospace Corporation]], and the [[national-reconnaissance-office|
  NRO]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 20:13–20:30)*

## Claim — black budget / misappropriation of funds

See [[katherine-austin-fitts]] and [[denise-mckenzie]] for the detailed testimony. SAIC is
named among corporations Fitts identified as responsible for obfuscating DoD/HUD fund
accounting (alongside Lockheed Martin Information Systems, IBM, Dyncorp).
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 51:44–52:08)* McKenzie's 1992 testimony describes SAIC
running large contracts with zero deliverable activity, funded by generic "we're working on
it" form letters. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 54:38–56:03)*

Gerb explicitly connects this to the [[strategic-defense-initiative|Strategic Defense
Initiative]]: SAIC was, by 1985, the largest SDI contractor by value (more than General
Dynamics, Northrop, Raytheon, and RAND combined) and "the best represented contractor" for
particle-beam weapons from 1978 onward; Gerb argues the same fraud/waste pattern extends to
UFO program funding skimmed from SDI. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:07:16–1:08:20)*

## Claim — contract-failure pattern ("fraud/waste/abuse")

- **Project Trailblazer** (2002): SAIC-led consortium (with Boeing, Booz Allen Hamilton) for
  the [[national-security-agency|NSA]], initially $280M, ballooned to billions; NSA employees
  (including [[william-binney|William Binney]]) filed IG complaints over fraud/waste; DoD IG's
  2005 report was 90%+ redacted; whistleblowers were raided by the FBI. SAIC was then awarded
  the follow-on "Execute Locust" contract worth $361M. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:17:04–1:18:40)*
- **Virtual Case File** (VCF): $124M contract with the [[federal-bureau-of-investigation|FBI]]
  for a central data repository; called "the most highly publicized software failure in
  history" after 3 years. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:18:51–1:19:21)*
- 2005 unsolved burglary at SAIC's La Jolla facility: theft of a desktop and four laptops
  containing personal data on thousands of current/former employees (including former CIA/NSA/
  Pentagon personnel). *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:19:34–1:20:04)*
- 1987–1995: allegedly defrauded the government with non-functioning flat-panel LCD prototypes
  for a USAF fighter-jet display contract; early 2000s: charged with fraud for false cost/profit
  statements on USAF base waste-cleanup contracts. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:20:13–1:20:50)*

[[project-stargate]] for detail.

## Claim — Off-World Technologies Division (NSWC Crane)

Gerb re-presents [[randy-anderson]]'s testimony and identifies SAIC as the likely contractor
running the alleged "off-world technologies division" beneath [[naval-surface-warfare-center-
crane|NSWC Crane]], based on SAIC's extensive, sometimes-scrubbed contracts with Crane's JXN
(Weapon Systems Division) and JXW (Maneuver, Surveillance and Engagement Division, containing a
"special capabilities branch," JXWT). *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:46:24–2:00:36)*
Cited contracts: a since-deleted 2020 $133M JXW mission-engineering contract, a since-deleted
2023 $249M weapons-division-instrumentation IDIQ contract, and a 2010 $351M contract supporting
Crane's "joint special operations response department" (linked to [[jsoc|JSOC]]).
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:58:39–1:59:36)* SAIC also has significant presence at
[[naval-air-station-patuxent-river|Pax River]] via a joint venture with [[amentum|Amentum]]
called FSA. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:51:41–1:52:22)*
