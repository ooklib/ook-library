Arthur Young is named by [[peter-levenda]] as the inventor of the Bell helicopter who attended the 1952-53 Maine seance with his wife Ruth Forbes Paine Young, and whose family later hosted [[ruth-paine]].

## Claims

- Levenda says the Youngs lived in Pennsylvania and that Ruth Paine visited them a few months before the assassination, discussing the Russian defector in her house. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 15:54, 19:27, 19:39)*
- Surviving correspondence has Young's wife telling her daughter-in-law not to speak about Oswald. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 19:55)*
