The 1907 banking crisis, in which [[j-pierpont-morgan]] organised a private rescue of failing banks, as told by MagnatesMedia.

## Claims

- Bank runs left banks near bankruptcy; the narrator says the government reportedly had tellers count money slowly to slow withdrawals. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 13:47, 14:01)*
- President [[theodore-roosevelt]], despite disagreeing with Morgan, called on him; Morgan summoned leading financiers to his Madison Avenue library, had them deposit large sums in smaller banks, and locked the door until they agreed by morning. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 14:12, 14:32, 14:41, 15:02)*
- The US Treasury added 25 million dollars; Morgan, aged 70 and with a bad cold, is credited with averting a depression, but people noticed their dependence on one man and "something needed to change". ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 15:09, 15:17, 15:38)*
