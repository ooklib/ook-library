The NSC 5412 Committee ("Special Group"), established by President Eisenhower on 15 March 1954
under National Security Council directive NSC 5412, was an interdepartmental body created to
review and approve covert US operations, and formally defined "covert operations" for US policy
purposes while designating the CIA as executive agent for them. UAP Gerb argues Eisenhower used
this structure — one level more deeply hidden than the Manhattan Project's
[[top-policy-group|Top Policy Group]] — to conceal ongoing oversight of the alleged "Manhattan
Project 2.0" UFO legacy programs, including a hidden [[majestic-12|MJ-12]] control group.
*([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:31:42–1:32:00)*

## Claims

- Original membership: the deputy under secretary of state, the deputy secretary of defense, the
  president's special assistant for national security affairs, and the CIA director (as the
  group's action officer). *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:33:12–1:33:40)*
- Modeled explicitly, per UAP Gerb, on the Manhattan Project's Top Policy Group: keep readin
  personnel and the control group small, minimize the paper trail, and maintain presidential
  control through a trusted intermediary. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  1:32:35–1:32:53)*
- Gordon Gray — an accused original [[majestic-12|MJ-12]] member per the disputed Eisenhower
  Briefing Document, and Truman's assistant secretary of the Army at the time of MJ-12's alleged
  founding — later served as Eisenhower's national security adviser (1958–1961), making him a
  member of the 5412 Committee itself; UAP Gerb reads this personnel continuity as evidence MJ-12
  (or its successor) continued covertly as the control group for the UFO legacy-program portfolio
  through the 5412 Committee's tenure. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  1:33:40–1:34:13)*
- UAP Gerb theorizes this alleged UFO control-group continuity persisted through the 5412
  Committee's later evolution into Nixon's 303 Committee, before "going into a black box" or
  breaking apart into siloed containers during the 1980s SAP reforms discussed under
  [[special-access-program]]. He nonetheless believes an NSC element retained oversight of a
  singular UFO crash-retrieval portfolio to the present day, involving DOE, the Air Force, the
  CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology, and select primes/FFRDCs — the structure he argues
  the *Age of Disclosure* documentary partially outlined while conspicuously omitting the NSC's
  role, to protect [[james-clapper|James Clapper]]. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  1:34:13–1:35:14)*
- Because official NSC meetings require full council presence, informal meetings among 5412-type
  members with relevant expertise were held off the books and often went unrecorded — a structural
  feature UAP Gerb argues let Eisenhower create "the impenetrable UFO control group that would
  ultimately usurp our system of checks and balances." *([secrecy-1](../raw/
  jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:37:50–1:38:14)*
- UAP Gerb connects the 5412 Committee/303 Committee evolution's gradual erosion of traditional
  presidential oversight to remarks in Eisenhower's farewell "military-industrial complex" speech
  about safeguarding against "unwarranted influence... sought or unsought." *([secrecy-1](../raw/
  jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:38:24–1:38:50)*
