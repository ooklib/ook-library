H. L. Hunt is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the third, richest and most ideological of the East Texas oil "Titans": an outspoken anti-communist and segregationist funder of activist and militia groups, whom "to many" was an "unrepentant fascist." (Not to be confused with [[e-howard-hunt]].)

## Claims

- Born in Illinois; settled first in Arkansas (said to have run cotton plantations); legend says he turned his last $100 into over $100,000 at New Orleans poker tables, which the host says is disputed. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 11:28-11:59)*
- Bought the original East Texas plots of the discoverer, Joiner (ASR "Marian DOD Joiner"); per Robert Bryce, Joiner, broke and in debt, accepted $1.3 million (about $30,000 up front, rest in royalties) in "the biggest theft since the invention of slot machines." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 11:59-12:47)*
- Fortune estimated at $2-3 billion at his death; Hunt Oil remains a large independent. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 12:47-13:04)*
- Spread his fortune across "seemingly independent" activist organizations and nativist militia groups; the host previews his support for the Minutemen and General Edwin Walker's segregationist activism in upcoming parts. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 13:04-13:52)*
- Had three wives simultaneously and 15 children; son [[nelson-bunker-hunt]] later a private backer of the Contra "death squads" (host's phrase); son Lamar Hunt created the Kansas City Chiefs. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 13:52-14:32)*
- Host: the public face of cowboy excess, but "by no means the only one" of the three who got his hands dirty. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 14:41-15:04)*
