Rose des Vents ('Compass Rose', allegedly named after the NATO insignia; also called Alcazar or Mission Rainbow 48; ASR: 'Rose de van', 'Jose Von') was, per Eyes Wide Open, the main French Gladio stay-behind network, organized by French intelligence and the CIA after [[plan-bleu-1947]] failed. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 39:48-40:26)*

## Claims

- Origins traced (via a Gladio paper by a researcher rendered 'Gerald'/'agua' by ASR) to two French Special Forces officers who recruited [[francois-de-grossouvre]]; the network was simultaneously called Alcazar or 'Rainbow Mission 48' (for the year founded). *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 40:00-40:34)*
- Structure: France split into zones each headed by an intelligence official who hired underlings who hired three more men each, with no links between top and bottom; De Grossouvre said he was in contact with at most ten people. A FOIA document (1999, CIA European division) on [[operation-zipper]], a joint CIA-BND program involving [[reinhard-gehlen]], is shown as a comparable pyramid diagram. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 41:21-42:45)*
- Former CIA officer [[edward-barnes]] told The Nation (6 April 1992, interviewed by [[jonathan-kwitny]]) the program 'consisted of several dozen men individually recruited by the agency each of whom was to build a small network of his own recruits'. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 42:45-43:49)*
- Short-lived: Barnes claimed it was ordered closed as early as 1953; [[constantin-melnik]] said [[charles-de-gaulle]] ordered it shut in 1959, feeling threatened by the network's militancy, a year after the May 1958 crisis (rogue generals' failed coup over Algeria). *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 44:02-45:10)*
