Major Roger Trinquier (ASR renders "Tranier", "Tran", "Tank", "teni"; normalization to Trinquier is my inference from the episode description, which names "Major Roger Trinquier") is presented by Eyes Wide Open as a French counterinsurgency expert and future theorist who arrived in Saigon in late 1951 for his third tour and built [[operation-x]], which the host calls "arguably the single most impactful innovation to counterinsurgency work during the beginning of the Cold War": [[narco-counterinsurgency]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 16:55-17:34)*

## Claims

- Nominally an intelligence agent under the newly formed [[mixed-airborne-commando-group]] (MACG), a special-forces division under French intelligence. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 17:04-17:18)*
- Understood the French were being humiliated by the Viet Minh, knew the operation had to be "lean, extremely cheap", and conceived the idea that "the war paid for itself" and was fought by indigenous allies, not the French. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 17:41-18:11)*
- Described by [[alfred-mccoy]] as the "cold-blooded scientific tactician"; he took the profits generated by [[antoine-savani]]'s drug logistics and used them to arm, feed and pay Hmong mercenaries. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 21:12-21:56)*
- Ran the four-phase Hmong recruitment into the maquis (see [[operation-x]]). *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 27:32-29:53)*
- Late April 1954, now commander of the MACG, he flew to the Plain of Jars with silver bars, bought 3,000 of [[touby-lyfoung]]'s best men, and sent them, after an 8-day boot camp, to relieve Dien Bien Phu; commanders included the young [[vang-pao]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 36:34-37:21)* See [[battle-of-dien-bien-phu]].
- Packed up at Cap Saint-Jacques in late July 1954 after Geneva and was ordered back to France. He later wrote the counterinsurgency textbook *Modern Warfare*, which per the host made no mention of narco profits; he revealed them only to McCoy in the early 1970s. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 42:45-43:54)*
- Sent a letter to McCoy's publisher Harper and Row after *The Politics of Heroin* (1972) confirming McCoy's accounts except one point: he says the remaining funds in the "black box" (caisse) were returned to French intelligence rather than used to compensate the Hmong fighters, which disappointed him. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 44:02-44:57)* (The host dates his testimony to McCoy both "1970" and "early 1970s".)
- Went on to lead counterinsurgency in the Algerian War, where the host says torture "took center stage". *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 44:49-45:06)*
- Briefed [[edward-lansdale]] and [[lucien-conein]] in Saigon in July 1954 on Operation X and told them the infrastructure remained ready to reactivate. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 45:39-47:48)*
