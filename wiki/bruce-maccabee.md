Bruce Maccabee (ASR: "McCabe"/"McAfee") is a Navy physicist and civilian UFO researcher who wrote about UFOs and the FBI. In the `jm/cover-up` episode, [[ron-pandolfi]] (head of the CIA's "weird desk") is said to have told the Clinton administration that its science adviser should be briefed by Maccabee, then called Maccabee to give with about a day's notice the lecture he had once given the CIA. [[jason-samosa]] and [[jesse-michels]] treat this as a sign the administration was fobbed off: the science adviser did not even get a CIA UFO officer, and William Nepal (ASR) suggests they wanted to push it away. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 47:34, 48:57, 49:22, 49:46, 50:02)*
