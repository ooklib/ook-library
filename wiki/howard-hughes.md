Howard Hughes is the aviation billionaire [[eyes-wide-open]] uses to illustrate wartime
procurement profiteering and the intertwining of aviation lobbying with the Cold War state. This
page also covers his firms Hughes Aircraft Company and Transworld Airline (TWA).

## Claims

- Billionaire heir of Hughes Tool Company (fortune from an oil-drill-bit patent); bought TWA stock
  from 1938 and founded Hughes Aircraft Company in California in early 1939, "just in time" for wartime
  contracts. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 23:09-23:41)*
- Citing Jim Hougan's *Spooks*, the host says Hughes was a fraud who took large sums from the Air
  Force and War Department while delivering little: the Hercules H-4 "Spruce Goose" (funded by an
  $18 million grant, finished in 1946, flown once in November 1947 a few feet off the water)
  ballooned to a cost the host puts at $246 million in 2023 dollars. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 23:41-24:47)*
- Senate investigation: Hughes had received about $60 million in Air Force contracts that "had not
  delivered a single aircraft" while Hughes Aircraft grew from four to "880,000" employees (figure
  as spoken; almost certainly an ASR/host error, flagged not corrected), largely by manufacturing other
  companies' planes such as Boeing's and Lockheed's. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 24:47-25:29)*
- Summer 1947: the Senate War Investigating Committee, led by Senator Owen Brewster (a friend of
  PanAm founder Juan Trippe), probed Hughes; Hughes alleged PanAm sought a worldwide overseas-flight
  monopoly and that Brewster tried to blackmail him into merging TWA with PanAm. The host finds
  the claims plausible since PanAm would get the monopoly on flights to Southeast Asia throughout
  the second Indochina War, contributing (per [[peter-dale-scott]]) to its growth. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 25:29-26:36)*
- Hughes proved the Spruce Goose could fly and handed it to the Air Force, dispelling the inquiry.
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 26:36-26:59)*
- Postwar: TWA and PanAm (both deploying the Boeing 707 with the "good friend" of Trippe, Boeing
  chairman Bill Allen) became the two biggest Cold War airlines; Hughes Aircraft pioneered
  helicopters (Hughes Helicopters) and built the first air-to-air guided missile, the "A4F Super
  Falcon" (as spoken; 1955), starting a long missile-maker history. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 26:59-28:06)*
- The Hughes-PanAm rivalry is called "indicative of the underlying military industrial complex
  interests" seeping into politics by 1947. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 28:06-28:25)*
- **UFO-thread bridge:** the wiki's UFO-legacy pages independently mention Hughes Aircraft as a
  former client of an attorney tied to the Zodiac program claims
  (see [[zodiac-crash-retrieval-program]] and [[richard-dolan]]). This episode makes no UFO claim
  about Hughes.
