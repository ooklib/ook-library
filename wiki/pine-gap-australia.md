Ground station in Australia for [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]] Program B, a joint NRO/CIA
satellite-SIGINT program. Today jointly run by the Australian Defence Force, CIA, NSA, and NRO.
Gerb frames its remote location and "spook-run" character as a recurring subject of UFO
legacy-program discussion. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 22:54–23:17, 2:38:29–2:39:00)*

- [[bill-perry|William Perry]] chaired the "Perry Panel" advising Program B on overhead SIGINT
  collection systems while at the NRO (1955–1997). *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  2:22:23–2:22:52)*

  of the Pine Gap facility. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:39:00–2:39:17)*

  NRO/CIA DS&T coordination on satellite reconnaissance elements. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 1:25:56–1:26:47)*
