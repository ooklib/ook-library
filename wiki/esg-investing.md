Environmental, social and governance investing, described by [[desiree-fixler]] in [[wef-insider-interview-2025]] as a "marketing scam" and the core of a multi-trillion-dollar industrial complex. All statements are her claims.

## Claims

- The term dates to the 2004 UN paper "Who Cares Wins"; it stayed niche until after the 2008 crisis and went mainstream after 2020. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 14:18, 15:09)*
- It let Wall Street rebrand after the financial crisis and bailouts ("a shield"), with Goldman Sachs' "vampire squid" image as an example, and Jamie Dimon's 2019 Business Roundtable statement backing stakeholder capitalism as the elite adopting it. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 9:38, 43:14)*
- Slapping ESG, green or SDG labels on funds let managers charge roughly double the fees. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 8:44, 9:00)*
- Became mandatory from 2020 as a "license to operate", and via the EU sustainable finance action plan (2018) and regulations from March 2021 with about 500 new reporting line items. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 11:37, 24:08, 24:44)*
- ESG funds underperformed: she cites the S&P 500 up over 80% in five years against a clean-energy index down over 30%, and says the earlier Harvard research linking ESG to profitability was debunked. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 22:17, 23:07)*
- European oil majors (BP, Shell, Danish oil and gas) shifted into renewables and now write off those investments while US majors outperformed. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 20:00, 21:11)*
- The industry feeds consultants, data providers, law firms, PR agencies, lobbyists and academia, which lobby for more regulation. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 40:16, 41:22, 42:25)*
- The backlash began in 2022 with greenwashing stories, underperformance, and US red-state pension controllers objecting to BlackRock default ESG funds. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 48:59, 1:07:32)*
- She claims net zero pushed up energy prices and, in her opinion, contributed to the Ukraine war by de-industrializing Germany; she calls the climate crisis "manufactured" while accepting climate change. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 50:12, 1:03:18)*
