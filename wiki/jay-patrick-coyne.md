Aide to Robert Cutler and later a Kennedy White House national-security staffer (ASR "Jay Patrick Coin"). [[geoffrey-cruickshank]] calls him an overlooked figure, a friend of [[allen-dulles]] and "sort of like a double agent for the deep state". *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 57:46-58:59)*

## Claims

- Cruickshank: on 28 June 1961 Coyne saw Dulles at 11:00 a.m. and was in the five-person 3:30 p.m. meeting where [[john-f-kennedy]] allegedly asked about MJ-12 operations relating to Cold War psychological warfare, which Cruickshank reads as Coyne tipping off Dulles beforehand; the date matches Dulles's calendar (compare the 1995 document discussed in [[majestic-12]] "Additional claims (jm/kissinger)"). Speculative. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 58:02-59:01)*
