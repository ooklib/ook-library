At the Hereticon panel ([[nell-pasulka-hereticon-panel-2024]]) [[karl-nell]] argues that UFOs/UAP are "indisputable" via three lines of evidence: first principles, testimony from people in a position to know, and data. All items are Nell's assertions as stated on stage; none is independently verified here. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 2:55-3:24, 10:25-10:34)*

## First principles

- Nell argues the laws of physics are the same everywhere, that life appeared on Earth at the earliest possible time (blue-green algae in 3.5-billion-year-old stromatolites, possibly life 4.1 billion years ago, viable microbes in space, microbes revived from 2-billion-year-old rock), implying either rapid evolution or panspermia. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 3:32-4:42)*
- The Sun is a relatively young Population I star and there are older Sun-like stars, giving other civilizations billions of years' head start. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 4:42-5:06)*
- Faster-than-light travel is "not even needed" for his case, but the [[miguel-alcubierre]] solution (1994) to Einstein's equations gives an effective FTL mechanism requiring negative energy; he points to the Casimir effect and dark energy as evidence negative energy exists, and calls it a theoretical, not engineering, solution. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 5:06-6:10)*

## Testimony

- Nell lists Barack Obama and Donald Trump ([[barack-obama]], [[donald-trump]]), UK Admiral of the Fleet Lord Hill-Norton, Israeli space chief Haim Eshed, Canada's [[paul-hellyer]], [[christopher-mellon]], [[david-grush]] (with two naval aviators before the House Oversight Committee) and [[luis-elizondo]] as figures who have said UFOs/NHI are real. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 6:24-7:32)*
- He cites the 2023 bipartisan legislation from Senators Schumer, Rounds, Rubio and Vance and others, saying Congress is "not being told the truth" ([[uap-disclosure-act-2023]]). *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 7:32-8:08)*

## Data

- The Flir, Gimbal and Go Fast videos (2004, 2015) that the Pentagon has said are neither US nor adversary craft; Gordon Cooper's 1951 chase over West Germany and his 1979 UN testimony. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 8:08-8:38)*
- Radar incursions over the northern US ballistic missile warning system in the 1970s; the 1967 ICBM readiness changes at [[malmstrom-minuteman-incident-1967]] and an alleged Soviet analogue; the standing US-Soviet agreement to notify each other if UFOs affect early-warning systems ([[us-ussr-nuclear-accident-agreement-1971]]). *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 8:38-9:26)*
- The 1952 Washington DC radar-visual sightings and the resulting press conference; the 1994 Ruwa (Zimbabwe) school landing and a 1966 Westall (Australia) incident as "landings in daylight". *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 9:26-10:06)*
