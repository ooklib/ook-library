Pat Price was an SRI remote viewer in the early psychic-spying programme. [[jason-jorjani]] speculates he was recruited into a separate black unit that used psychokinesis against intelligence targets, developed moral reservations, and was killed for it. Unverified inference offered without documentation.

## Claims

- Individuals from the psi programmes tried to recruit remote viewers into a unit that used psychokinesis against US intelligence targets; some refused; "I think other people were successfully recruited like Pat Price". *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 15:12, 16:00)*
- Jorjani ties this to Soviet attempts to stop mouse hearts with psychokinesis and to Lyn Buchanan's ability to enter Saddam Hussein's mind. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 13:11, 13:50)*
