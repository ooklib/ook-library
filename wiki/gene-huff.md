Gene Huff ("Jean Huff" in one transcript passage) is described by [[bob-lazar]] as a friend he confided in before going public. When [[luigi-vendittelli]] showed him the S4 reconstruction he reportedly called it "really creepy, almost an ominous feeling", which Lazar treated as proof Luigi got the atmosphere right *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 7:42-7:59)*. Lazar credits Huff with coining "UFO researcher syndrome", the tendency of researchers to escalate claims as attention fades ([[billy-meier]]) *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:45:03-1:45:20)*. Huff is not connected here to other wiki entities; the spelling is unverified.
