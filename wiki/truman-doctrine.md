The Truman Doctrine is [[harry-truman]]'s March 1947 declaration to Congress positioning the
United States as an overt enemy of global communism, made while requesting military aid for
anti-communist forces in Greece. Eyes Wide Open treats its wording, and the year of its
declaration, as pointedly coincident with the [[national-security-act-1947|National Security
Act]].

## Claims

- Delivered to Congress in March 1947 while requesting military aid to anti-communist forces in
  the Greek Civil War; Truman stated "I believe that it must be the policy of the United States
  to support free people who are resisting attempted subjugation by armed minorities or by
  outside pressure." Eyes Wide Open marks this utterance as the start of the Cold War proper.
  *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 22:15-22:39)*
- The host argues the doctrine's stated ideals of "freedom" and support for "the oppressed" would
  manifest, per the next episode, in immediate US support for fascist paramilitaries and a
  "deeply unpopular monarchist government" in postwar Greece. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 22:40-22:57)*
