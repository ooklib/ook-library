Dark triangular craft (typically three white corner lights, a dull red or amber central light, "structural work" underneath, 200-300 ft in many reports) are reported worldwide and cluster in 1970-2000, unlike the smooth, seamless craft associated with NHI. UAP Gerb argues this pattern, plus the Immaculate Constellation report labeling some triangles ARVs, suggests they were human-built reproduction craft ([[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b]], [[xf-131-super-sentinel]]).

## Cases cited

- Immaculate Constellation ([[immaculate-constellation]]): a 2009 equilateral triangle rotating 500-1,000 m above the ocean over Indo-Pacific Command collection vessels (with a sweeping light bar), and a fighter-jet-sized triangle under 200 m over a vessel, both reported as reproduction craft; AARO lists triangles as 3-4% of reports. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 3:31-5:08)*
- [[belgian-ufo-wave-1989]] and its 30 March 1990 F-16 chase. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 7:43-9:05)*
- Croton Falls NY, 28 Oct 1993 (Hudson Valley flap): biomedical engineer Jim Cook saw a triangle about 15 ft above the water with nine red lights and a red beam probing the water. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 52:02-52:42)*
- St. Clair, Illinois, 5 Jan 2000: police reported a black triangle about 75 ft by 40 ft near Scott AFB with a multicolored light band. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 52:42-53:53)*
- Madison, Wisconsin, 22 Nov 1985: a dull triangle whose underside looked like "the back of a refrigerator", quoted by Michael Schratt; Gerb suggests a heat-exchanger for a prototype propulsion system. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 53:53-54:26)*
- Colin Saunders, UK, March 1999: a 50 ft triangle bobbing at about 15 degrees, skin like flowing liquid mercury, with a raised pattern on top and bottom that he took as a docking mechanism; Gerb links the skin to Jonathan Wayant's crash-retrieval "mother of pearl" description and notes airborne aircraft carriers are not unknown in human design. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 54:26-55:29)*
