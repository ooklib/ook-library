The claims about a two-tier US government UAP structure that [[michael-herrera]] says the [[herrera-black-program-insider]] told him, as summarized in a Reddit post by [[joeyisnotmyname]] and read out by Gerb. [[uap-gerb]] cautions that none of it is verified and could be disinformation.

## Claims

- The USG has a blue group, programs with proper congressional oversight (AARO, AATIP, Blue Book, etc.), and a black group founded in the 1940s to reverse engineer NHI technology, with no oversight or accountability. Gerb says this mirrors the [[wilson-davis-notes]]. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 26:44-27:16)*
- The black group has reverse engineered NHI craft to an unknown degree and uses them operationally; the flying craft are called "boring tech", with other technologies "far more exciting" and "utilitarian for humanity". *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 27:16-27:39)*
- EMP-like weapons can target and disable UAP; crash-retrieval programs lure UAP to locations where EM weapons disable them and craft and bodies are recovered. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 27:39-27:56)*
- The program recruits people with "psionic abilities", referred to as P3, often from third-world nations, to interface with and control NHI technology, bargaining with security for their families; this is allegedly the operation Herrera stumbled upon. See [[psionic-assets-and-breeding-claims]]. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 27:56-28:20)*
- Later in the video a person who came forward to Greer says the containers seen in Indonesia were for transporting people. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 15:35-16:08)*

## Claims (gerb/herrera-2)

- Herrera relays that factions exist within the reverse-engineering programs, a war between "good guys" who want disclosure and congressional oversight and "bad guys" who want to keep the technology and "commit crimes against humanity"; he says the group is also the enforcement arm of political corruption, with assets in local, federal and military law enforcement and government. His proposed fix is for the good faction to "flip the tables" and take control or neutralize the bad guys, with laws and US Marshals executing. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:47:11-1:54:18)*
- Aerospace contractors allegedly pay politicians to resist oversight (Herrera cites resistance to the AARO/domain push), while only small groups within Boeing, Lockheed, Raytheon and Northrop Grumman know. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:54:34-1:55:25)*
- The insiders allegedly say people are taken, drugged and used as "biological equipment" or "pink assets" in programs, promised a way out of their situations; Herrera says he could not sleep for a week and hopes the programs can become known and voluntary. See [[psionic-assets-and-breeding-claims]]. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:30:09-1:31:14, 1:58:48-1:59:36)*
- Herrera relays that TFR-area civilian aircraft have been hit with UAP weapons, that reverse-engineered craft are tested by hanging them from balloons and shooting them at high altitude and on the ground, that the insiders' craft are EMP-proof and immune to bullets and missiles, and that insiders now struggle to tell their own craft from NHI craft. He also relays speculation that NHI could end humanity if the program keeps shooting down craft. See [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b]]. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:49:53-1:53:16, 1:55:34-1:57:59)*
- Herrera says he has never met NHI himself and that his claims about NHI abilities (interdimensional travel, passing through solid objects) are speculation from what insiders told him. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:51:12-1:53:08)*
