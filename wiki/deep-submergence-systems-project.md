- Per Craven, DSSP was created to satisfy the operational needs of one project, [[operation-sand-dollar]], a compartmented naval program hidden within [[polaris-program]] that marked global seafloor items of national-security interest for salvage. Craven was briefed in 1965. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:03:25-1:03:50)*
