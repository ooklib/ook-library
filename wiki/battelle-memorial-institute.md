Battelle Memorial Institute is a contractor that administers multiple FFRDCs on behalf of the
DOE and other agencies, and is described by Gerb as "no stranger to this channel."
*([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:24:11–1:24:35)*

## Claims

- Runs multiple FFRDCs including [[oak-ridge-national-laboratory|Oak Ridge]] (where
  [[sean-kirkpatrick|Sean Kirkpatrick]] joined as CTO post-AARO),
  [[savannah-river-national-laboratory|Savannah River National Labs]], and the
  [[national-biodefense-analysis-and-countermeasures-center|National Biodefense Analysis and
  Countermeasures Center]] — all of which Gerb suspects are involved in UFO legacy programs.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:24:35–1:24:50)*
- Possibly involved in studying shape-memory alloys from the 1947
  [[roswell-incident-1947|Roswell]] wreckage; ran a parallel-to-Project-Blue-Book study called
  "Project Stork"; and has been associated with UFO legacy-program research at
  [[dugway-proving-ground|Dugway Proving Ground]]. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:24:18–1:24:27)*
- Comprises (with Texas A&M University and the University of California)
  [[triad-national-security|Triad National Security LLC]], the administrator of
  [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los Alamos National Labs]] on behalf of the DOE.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:23:25–1:23:55)*
- [[ronald-moultrie|Ronald Moultrie]] served on Battelle's board, reportedly scrubbed from his
  LinkedIn, while simultaneously recommending [[aaro|AARO]]'s formation — a conflict of interest
  per Gerb. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 29:13–29:47)*
- Gerb draws a parallel between Battelle's Triad structure (a Battelle-formed LLC managing DOE
  sites via a trio of institutions) and [[science-applications-international-corporation|
  SAIC]]'s own 2004 joint venture RDS LLC with [[eg-and-g|EG&G]] and Parsons for the DOE's
  National Energy Technology Laboratory. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:24:50–1:25:06)*
