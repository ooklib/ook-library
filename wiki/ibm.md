IBM is an American technology company. The Brofessor Stein video alleges that through its German subsidiary Dehomag it supplied the Nazi regime with punch-card and data-processing machines used for censuses and tracking systems.

## Claims

- Through Dehomag it provided punch-card and data-processing machines from the early 1930s, essential to Nazi censuses and tracking systems that identified Jews and other targeted groups across Germany and occupied Europe; the data allegedly enabled efficient deportations and camp internments, with IBM machines operating in places like Dachau. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 8:02-8:29)*
- IBM allegedly sent technicians to train SS personnel and kept supplying equipment, parts and punch cards. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 8:32)*
- In 1937 CEO Thomas Watson received the Order of the German Eagle from Hitler; even after WWII began IBM allegedly kept aiding the regime and refused to sell off Dehomag, keeping control from New York; in 1940 it created a Dutch branch that helped identify Jews for deportation. IBM stated in 2001 that it had limited control over Dehomag. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 8:37-9:05)*
