A 26 Sept 1994 case file (cited by Gerb from [[ryan-s-wood]]'s "Majic Eyes Only") quotes the November 1994 Air Force Monthly: an unusual crash landing at the UK Boscombe Down facility, after which an unmarked CIA-operated Boeing 737 and a DC-8 visited and two days later the wreck was flown on a C-5 to [[air-force-plant-42]] at Palmdale. Rumor said it was a flying triangle, speculated to be a "TR-3A". Gerb notes TR-3A and TR-3B are often used interchangeably, and that TR-3A is variously a pulse-detonation "pumpkin seed" (like Sorenson's 1988 unmanned craft) or a delta-shaped piloted fighter with electrogravitic mass reduction. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 40:02-42:01, 1:08:40-1:09:35)*
