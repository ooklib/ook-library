The SOL Foundation, as [[uap-gerb]] describes it, is a group of scientists and academics, military personnel and government and policy figures that seeks to educate the public on the academic legitimacy of UAP and to push toward a post-disclosure world. Gerb lists as members or participants [[christopher-mellon]], [[david-grush]], Stanford pathologist [[gary-nolan]], physicist [[kevin-knuth]] and [[karl-nell]], and calls their credibility "beyond reproach" (a promotional characterization). *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 1:40-2:11)*

## Claims

- Nell spoke at the symposium in November 2023 at the Nolan laboratory of the Stanford School of Medicine; some of his slides leaked at the time, but the full talk only appeared in February 2024. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 2:11-2:37)*
- The symposium also included talks by Grusch, Mellon, Knuth, Nolan and [[jacques-vallee]], which Gerb planned to review. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 21:34-21:49)*
- Nell's campaign plan treats academic bodies like the SOL Foundation as the channel for scientific research on UAP, still at risk because of persisting stigma. See [[nell-uap-campaign-plan]]. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 12:37-12:54)*
