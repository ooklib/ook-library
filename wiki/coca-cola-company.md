Coca-Cola is an American beverage company. The video tells the story of Fanta, created by its cut-off German subsidiary during WWII.

## Claims

- Coca-Cola was popular in pre-war Germany and sponsored the 1936 Berlin Olympics; once the war began the German subsidiary lost syrup supplies to Allied embargoes and Nazi trade restrictions. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 18:30-18:48)*
- Managing director Max Keith created Fanta from local ingredients (mainly whey and apple pulp), launched in 1941; it had no ties to military production. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 18:51-19:12)*
- After US entry into the war official contact was cut, but Keith kept selling Fanta with large profits for the company; after the war Coca-Cola regained control of the subsidiary, registered Fanta in the US in 1949 and expanded it globally. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 19:12-19:37)*
