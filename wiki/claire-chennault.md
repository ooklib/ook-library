Claire Chennault was a former US Army Air Force captain who formed a private mercenary air force
("the Flying Tigers") for [[chiang-kai-shek]] using covert American war funds, later repurposed by
[[paul-helliwell]] into [[civil-air-transport]]'s heroin-smuggling operation.

## Claims

- Lobbied FDR to fund a private air force for Chiang Kai-shek; this became "the Flying Tigers."
  Described by Eyes Wide Open as "a fairly incompetent pilot," he trained US Army flunkies to fly
  surplus American war planes against advancing Japanese forces — efforts the host calls "largely
  insignificant to the war effort" but which directly led to the creation of
  [[civil-air-transport]], which repurposed his rundown air force into a heroin-smuggling
  operation. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 11:34-12:12)*

- Per McCoy, approached State (rebuffed) then [[paul-helliwell]], who referred him to [[frank-wisner]] at a George Washington Hotel meeting; later said [[operation-paper]] aimed to trap China in "giant pincers". *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 15:09-16:31, 22:17-22:36)*
