The 1954 conference (delegates from Vietnam, France, Russia, China, Britain and the US) that ended the [[first-indochina-war]] after [[battle-of-dien-bien-phu]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 38:10-38:26)*

## Claims

- The American delegation was led by [[john-foster-dulles]], brother of CIA director [[allen-dulles]]; the host says the CIA was "fully apprised" via an ally in Geneva and paramilitary advisers (incl. CAT) at Dien Bien Phu. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 38:26-38:49)*
- Peace settled 20 July 1954: independence for Cambodia, Laos, and a Vietnam split at the 17th parallel into a communist North and capitalist South; near-total French and American military exit, advisers capped at 342 per power, troop freeze until 11 August 1954. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 38:49-39:41)*
- Laos was covered by a side agreement (Cessation of Hostilities in Laos): the French kept influence via training the Royal Lao Army, two permanent bases (one at Seno on the Thai border, one outside the capital), a 3,500-man garrison plus 1,500 trainers. The host reads this as Western diplomats preparing Laos as a forward position. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 39:41-40:38)*
- US National Security Council meetings of 8 and 12 August called Geneva a "disaster" that "completed a major forward stride for communism". *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 41:02-41:27)*
- Elections were mandated: Laos and Cambodia 1954, separate Vietnams 1955, unified 1956; Western policymakers feared [[ho-chi-minh]] would sweep them. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 41:27-41:52)*
- On 20 August [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] approved an NSC paper, "Review of US Policy in the Far East": work with France only to build indigenous internal-security forces, give economic aid directly to Laos, Cambodia and [[ngo-dinh-diem]]'s South, and back any anti-communist, pro-US political opponent. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 42:01-42:45)*
- The French were soon absorbed in Algeria until 1962. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 40:54-41:02)*

- LAOS 3: host describes the partition of French Indochina into four entities (Cambodia, Laos, and North and South Vietnam along the 17th parallel) and the mandated 1956 reunification referendum that never occurred; French forces mostly to exit by October 1954. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 2:37-3:14, 4:47-5:12, 28:33-29:15)*

- LAOS 4: host: Geneva barred foreign troops from Laos but not foreign civilians, so CIA operated under civilian and USAID cover; France kept about 5,000 soldiers on two bases. The 1962 agreement (14 states, July 23, 1962) then formally renewed neutrality. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 17:30-18:29, 1:17:31-1:17:59)*
