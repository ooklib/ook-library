Marco Leal is the Brazilian researcher and co-producer of [[moment-of-contact-documentary]], whom [[james-fox]] credits as "boots on the ground" and "exceptional".

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says he met Leal on his first Brazil trip, when Leal had been working the case for about six years; Leal spent five or six years massaging contacts to get witnesses such as [[carlos-daza]] to come forward. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 16:44-16:53, 24:29-24:46)*
- Leal found the radiologist through students at a school, and the hospital contact behind [[italo-venturelli]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:33:20-1:34:14, 1:44:04-1:45:23)*
- Fox lists other researchers who came before him: Claudio Kovo, A. J. Gavard, [[ubirajara-rodrigues]], Pacini, Marco Patise; and credits Alen Cross (co-producer). *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 16:44-17:08, 2:01:10-2:01:28)*
