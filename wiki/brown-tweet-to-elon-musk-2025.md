Brown: no further comment. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:54:05-1:54:37)*

Matthew Brown).md>) 1:54:45-1:56:52)*

He says Amentum owns parts of [[eg-and-g]] according to its history. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft.
