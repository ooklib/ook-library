[[grant-cameron]] recounts that Frances Swan, a channeler in Eliot, Maine (near Betty Hill's neighborhood), was used by [[wilbert-b-smith]] and by Navy intelligence, culminating in a 1959 session at which a Navy officer allegedly began automatic writing and channeling before [[arthur-lundahl]] and Blue Book's Robert Friend. Cameron cites an 11-page memo he received from [[jacques-vallee]]. Everything is Cameron's paraphrase of documents he describes.

## Claims

- Swan channeled a blond "Alpha" being; Navy intelligence visited in 1954, and Smith allegedly asked her contact to overfly Ottawa on 1 August 1954, after which his flying saucer observatory recorded a large spike on 8 August. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 49:43-51:40)*
- Cameron says Ottawa then shut down [[project-magnet]]: Smith could keep the equipment but not speak for the government. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 51:40-51:49)*
- In 1959 two Navy men (one with a CIA pass) returned; Swan taught one to channel, and the group said a UFO passed the window in front of the Capitol when asked for proof. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 53:05-54:32)*
- Blue Book chief Robert Friend was flown in for a repeat that produced no proof ("the time isn't right"); his general reportedly said another agency had it, hands off. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 54:44-55:30)*
- Cameron reads Lundahl as running a channeling operation and gathering material from many experiencers. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 55:30-56:12)*
