Garry Nolan (spelled "Gary" in the transcript) is a Stanford microbiologist, described by [[diana-pasulka]] as a Nobel nominee with a foundation dedicated to studying people with anomalous experiences. He appears here only through Pasulka's remarks. Other pages in this wiki may refer to him under a different spelling. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 16:01-16:09, 19:09-19:33)*

## Claims (jm/nell-pasulka)

- Pasulka says she was relieved when he "came out as himself", and met him around 2014, when he shared research on effects (radiation-burn-like injuries) on people. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 16:01-16:09, 1:00:34-1:01:15)*
- She says he was recruited by the government to work with "people in the program" on UAP, and says the CIA "came knocking at his door" with files of people with anomalous experiences whose injuries resembled electromagnetic radiation effects. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 19:17-19:33, 1:00:43-1:00:59)*
- She compares this to the burns of the stigmata ([[st-francis-stigmata-seraphim-account]]). *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 19:00-19:19)*
