Vito Genovese (ASR-misspelled "Geneviese"), a Mafia figure, was given complete control of Italy's
harbors via his dock workers union by military governor [[charles-poletti]], allegedly enabling
the free flow of narcotics through Italy's ports.

## Claims

- [[charles-poletti]]'s appointment as military governor of Italy allowed the free flow of dope
  through Italy's ports by giving Vito Genovese's dock workers union complete control of Italy's
  harbors. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 17:12-17:19)*
