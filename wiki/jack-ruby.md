Jack Ruby (Rubenstein), the Dallas nightclub owner who killed [[lee-harvey-oswald]], is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as a late-1950s gunrunner to Cuba, first to Batista's forces for mob friends (Havana casino manager Lewis McWillie, who managed [[meyer-lansky]]'s Tropicana, per the host), also to Castro. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 39:29-39:53)*

## Claims

- Per John Roselli (via [[peter-dale-scott]]): "Ruby was hooked up with Trafficante in the rackets in Havana" ([[santo-trafficante]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 40:01-40:16)*
- After his conviction, Ruby told a jail-cell friend "they're going to find out about Cuba, ... about the guns, ... about New Orleans, ... about everything" (per Scott, ~5 years after). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 40:25-40:47)*
- Host: he went insane after a visit from MK-Ultra-affiliated psychologist Dr. Louis Jolyon West ("a story for another time"). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 40:54-41:11)*
- Host says he flew arms with Barry Seal and David Ferrie's circle; the host's "core circle" of figures all knew each other before 1961 (framed by the host, not established). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 44:41-45:05)*

## Relationships

- Killed [[lee-harvey-oswald]]; linked to [[santo-trafficante]], [[meyer-lansky]]; see [[kennedy-assassination]].

## Additional claims (CIA-Mafia Legend)

- Host (citing [[peter-dale-scott]]): Ruby, a Chicago mobster, played a key role in the outfit's wire-service war win and was sent to Dallas alongside a group of [[sam-giancana]] cronies to expand the wire service and the Chicago outfit's influence there. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 53:07-53:38)*

## Additional claims (Bush and the Cowboys, Chapter 2)


- Host: emigrated from the Chicago underworld to Dallas via the mob's wire-service expansion, as [[johnny-roselli]] did to LA. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 30:11-30:26)*

## Additional claims (The Road to Dallas Chapter 1, ewa/dallas-1)

- Host: Ruby (called the "future Lee Harvey Oswald assassin") and [[david-ferrie]] got their start running guns to Cuba during the [[cuban-revolution]]; arms trafficking to Cuba was driven largely by organized crime and intelligence assets. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 7:01-7:32)*

## Additional claims (Road to Dallas Ch. 4)

- Per HSCA testimony of [[lewis-mcwillie]] (known Ruby since 1951 in Dallas) and Larry Hancock, Ruby called McWillie his "idol"; the host says his organized-crime ties went unexplored by the Warren Commission. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 14:06-14:52)*
- Reportedly with McWillie coordinated and personally participated in 1959 gun shipments to Castro from the southern US for [[santo-trafficante]] interests; [[edward-browder]] flew for the operation. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 18:35-19:26)*
- Per Hougan citing a CIA report, an "American gangster named Ruby" frequently visited Trafficante in Cuban detention (host's inference: Jack Ruby), where Trafficante's cellmate was [[loran-hall]]. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 17:20-18:35)*
- Host: Ruby and McWillie were low-level "foot soldiers"; supervision came from [[norman-rothman]]. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 21:34-22:03)*
