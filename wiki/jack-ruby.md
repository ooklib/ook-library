Jack Ruby (Rubenstein), the Dallas nightclub owner who killed [[lee-harvey-oswald]], is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as a late-1950s gunrunner to Cuba, first to Batista's forces for mob friends (Havana casino manager Lewis McWillie, who managed [[meyer-lansky]]'s Tropicana, per the host), also to Castro. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 39:29-39:53)*

## Claims

- Per John Roselli (via [[peter-dale-scott]]): "Ruby was hooked up with Trafficante in the rackets in Havana" ([[santo-trafficante]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 40:01-40:16)*
- After his conviction, Ruby told a jail-cell friend "they're going to find out about Cuba, ... about the guns, ... about New Orleans, ... about everything" (per Scott, ~5 years after). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 40:25-40:47)*
- Host: he went insane after a visit from MK-Ultra-affiliated psychologist Dr. Louis Jolyon West ("a story for another time"). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 40:54-41:11)*
- Host says he flew arms with Barry Seal and David Ferrie's circle; the host's "core circle" of figures all knew each other before 1961 (framed by the host, not established). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 44:41-45:05)*

## Relationships

- Killed [[lee-harvey-oswald]]; linked to [[santo-trafficante]], [[meyer-lansky]]; see [[kennedy-assassination]].
