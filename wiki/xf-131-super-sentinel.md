The XF-131 Super Sentinel is the name allegedly used by disgruntled Northrop and Lockheed engineers for a reverse-engineered triangular craft reported by Antelope Valley residents, as relayed through the illustrator [[bill-mcdonald]] and his drawing "Tehachapi Triangle". It is unverified; the engineers' identities are undisclosed.

## Claims

- In spring 1992 McDonald met four engineers at a Denny's in Antelope Valley, California: two from [[northrop-grumman]] (working at the site UFO researchers call Tejon/Tehachapi, see [[tahone-radar-cross-section-facility]]) and two from [[lockheed-martin]], who said they worked for [[ben-rich]] at Skunk Works on the radar cross-section range at Helendale. All complained of extreme compartmentalization and security threats (jail, pension loss) and wanted the story told. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 57:42-58:57)*
- Gerb ties the Helendale claim to [[steven-greer]]'s later accusation that Helendale, near [[edwards-air-force-base]] and [[air-force-plant-42]] in Palmdale, is a black-budget UAP reverse-engineering site, and notes McDonald said the site's flood-control channel doubles as a test runway or platform, some 5 years before Greer named it. Fouche said the senior TR-3B designer would have been Ben Rich. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 59:04-1:01:07)*
- Fox contracted McDonald's drawing to make the computer render of the craft for the X-Files pilot-season episode "Deep Throat", in which Mulder learns the craft is a Roswell-derived reverse-engineered vehicle. Gerb notes rumors that Chris Carter was asked by military brass how he knew so much but finds no credible source, and cites ex-FBI agent John Dusza's claim that season one drew on true cases. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 1:01:15-1:06:34)*
- Differences from the TR-3B: much smaller, a pyramid-like raised top (most similar to the 1999 Kirby-in-England sighting, see [[flying-triangle-sightings]]), and a large maneuverable light under the belly with three pairs of lights along each side. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 1:08:25-1:08:40, 1:12:15-1:12:41)*
