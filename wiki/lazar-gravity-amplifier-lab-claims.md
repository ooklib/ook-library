[[bob-lazar]] describes hands-on demonstrations given to him by [[barry-s4-lab-partner]] in the S4 lab, using a small reactor, a "gravity amplifier" and an emitter laid out on benches with no visible connections between them *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 53:36-55:30)*. These are uncorroborated recollections from about 35 years earlier.

## Force-field demonstration

- The reactor sphere pushed back on Lazar's hands like opposing magnet poles; the first two or three inches were "elastic" and beyond that he could not move it an inch. It was a "real force field" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 53:44-54:29)*.
- When Barry moved the reactor off the bench and repeated, the force was not transferred to the bench, so it "just goes away" rather than pushing the object off *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 54:29-55:12)*.
- The reactor powered the emitter, and "nothing's connected together" ("the Twilight Zone") *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 55:12-55:39)*.

## Candle "frozen in time"

Barry lit a candle at the emitter's focal point, rotated the emitter and the flame stopped moving; Barry said it was "frozen in time", and Lazar objected that if so no photons would reach him. He could not put his hands in; rotating the emitter back made it a candle again *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:25:22-1:26:17)*. Lazar links this with [[project-looking-glass-s4]].

## Miniature black dot

Rotating the emitter further produced a tennis- or baseball-sized black dot in which light was bent away, without pulling objects toward it; Lazar wonders how a gravity machine could act on photons only *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:26:17-1:27:38)*. Lazar mentions a separate laser test that he did at home *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:27:42-1:27:52)*, and regrets that things "didn't work out" and he did not let Barry finish the experiments *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:27:52-1:28:26)*.

## Other observations

- The craft's skin carried high voltage and corona discharge; shorting it did nothing, "it didn't care" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:06:52-1:07:21)*.
- A conventional HF/VHF radio worked to someone in the craft despite the gravity field, which still confuses Lazar *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:06:09-1:06:45)*.
- Lazar says the "magic" is gone for things he can only watch, but hands-on interaction is frustrating because he wants to make it work *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:30:05-1:30:27)*.
