George Kennan (ASR-misspelled "George Keenan"), director of the State Department's Policy
Planning Staff, authored the first numbered CIA memo, NSC Document 1/1, framing Italy as the key
Cold War battleground, and later recommended a naval invasion of Italy to nullify any Communist
electoral victory.

## Claims

- Authored NSC Document 1/1, "The Position of the United States in Respect to Italy," issued
  November 14, 1947, which stated: "as far as Europe is concerned, Italy is obviously the key
  point. If communists were to win elections there, our whole position in the Mediterranean and
  possibly Western Europe as well would probably be undermined." Eyes Wide Open frames the Truman
  administration and CIA as viewing the battle for Italy as the single most important battleground
  of the emerging Cold War. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 9:57-10:47)*
- Per plans he drafted, the State Department recommended a naval invasion of Italy if the
  communists won the 1948 election, nullifying any potential win — Eyes Wide Open concludes
  "democracy was not a possibility for post war Italy." *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 29:38-29:53)*
