Disputed 14 July 1954 memorandum from Robert Cutler (special assistant to the president) to Gen. [[nathan-twining]] saying the "MJ-12 SSP briefing" should take place during the already scheduled 16 July White House meeting. It is the only alleged MJ-12 paper physically found in official archives (National Archives, Box 189), and the skeptics' usual first target. Gerb-side coverage is in [[majestic-documents]].

## Claims (rc/mj12)

- Coulthart: [[jaime-shandera]] and [[william-moore]] went to the National Archives on a tip (a postcard whose return address was "box 189"); the memo bears an archives stamp, its presence in the box is undisputed, and it acknowledges "MJ-12". Coulthart says it has been dismissed by almost everyone as a forgery and that he found the 1980s-only security marking the most persuasive point against it. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 50:44-52:21, 59:06-59:35)*
- [[jed-davis]]: it was planted, likely by CIA and/or AF OSI, to discredit MJ-12 preemptively; "there are things wrong with it": Cutler was out of the country, Eisenhower's and Twining's calendars show no meeting on the dates in it, "TOP SECRET RESTRICTED SECURITY INFORMATION" was NSC wording only from the Nixon era, and it lacks the NSC letterhead/watermark of the real Cutler-Twining memo of 13 July 1953 (an extraordinary all-day NSC meeting on 16 July 1953, with covert-entrance instructions). The "Reese's Pieces" clue in the accompanying letter nods to the archivist (Reese) and to E.T. (1982). Its one merit: it acknowledges MJ-12 as a real 1940s-50s compartment. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 52:26-56:43)*
- [[geoffrey-cruickshank]] disagrees: the memo can be seen at College Park, Tom Whitmore photographed it at higher resolution, and Philip Klass would have noticed a date alteration; the Box 189 postcard came from Addis Ababa where [[scotty-miller]] was station chief. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 56:51-57:40)*
- Cruickshank calls it Davis's one deviation; Coulthart says everything else in the documents fits the historical record "except for the Cutler Twining memo". *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 14:41-14:51, 24:42-24:58)*
