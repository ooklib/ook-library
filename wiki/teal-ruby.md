Teal Ruby is a 1970s program named, alongside [[talon-gold|Talon Gold]], by metallurgical
engineer Albert Bruce Collins to the late Leonard Stringfield as oriented to track and possibly
down UFOs — part of Collins's broader claim connecting SDI-era programs to NRO satellites
allegedly detecting UFOs as far back as the 1960s. Gerb defers deeper coverage of Collins's story
to his prior NRO project. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md)
41:57–42:28)*
