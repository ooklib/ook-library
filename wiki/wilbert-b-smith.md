Wilbert B. Smith was a senior radio engineer in the Canadian government's Department of Transport who, in 1950, asked for Canada's first official UFO investigation. His memo relays four claims from an unnamed "source within Washington", identified by UAP Gerb as [[robert-sarbacher]].

## Claims

- 1950 memo to the controller of telecommunications proposed studying magnetism as propulsion, citing four points: UFO matter is the most highly classified subject in the US government, higher than the H-bomb; flying saucers exist; modus operandi unknown but a small group headed by [[vannevar-bush]] is making concentrated effort; the matter is of tremendous significance to US authorities. *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 0:01-0:56, 5:02-5:24)*
- The memo led to [[project-magnet]] and Project Second Story. *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 5:24-5:48)*
- Historian Arthur Bray found both the memo and Smith's handwritten notes of his meeting with Sarbacher in the engineer's estate; Smith said he had read Frank Scully's *Behind the Flying Saucers* and Sarbacher replied its contents were "fundamentally correct". *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 6:17-7:09)* See [[frank-scully]].
