[[david-icke]]'s claim, in [[icke-ramsey-interview-2026]], that the global cult and its non-human directors are close to fusing humans with artificial intelligence through a technologically generated electromagnetic field, that [[donald-trump]] is a front man trashing the old system to enable it, and that news events and UFOs are diversions. The technical and medical assertions (including about vaccines and graphene oxide) are Icke's and are not supported in the source; the wiki records them only as attributed claims.

## Claims

- Goal of the astral agenda: "the fusion of humans with artificial intelligence", after which AI becomes the human mind and people stop asking who they are or who runs the world. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:06:16-1:07:00, 1:31:28-1:31:43)*
- Trump: Icke says he warned supporters in the 2024 campaign that Trump is a "con man" who would trash the present system while "facilitating the rapid expansion of the AI digital control system"; Trump is surrounded by AI oligarchs (he lists Musk, [[peter-thiel]], Marc Andreessen, David Sacks, Sam Altman, Larry Ellison), and JD Vance is Thiel's protege, so a Thiel-to-Vance succession is expected. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:18:45-2:20:32, 2:23:48-2:23:55)*
- "Creative destruction" or the "totalitarian tiptoe": the cult repeatedly trashes the status quo (the first world war and Versailles, the Great Depression, the second world war with the UN, Bretton Woods, IMF, World Bank and CIA created after it) so a more extreme system can replace it; the present "trashing" via wars and tariffs makes way for an AI and digital control system. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:20:58-2:23:39)*
- He points to the "Stargate" $500 billion AI data-centre announcement (by Ellison and Altman, two days after the inauguration) as confirming his prediction. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:24:09-2:24:32)*
- 6G, low-orbit satellites (about 11,000 SpaceX now, 50,000 planned, with Bezos following) and towers would create a global technological electromagnetic field through which frequencies can be sent to the human mind to create a "centrally dictated hive mind". He cites, in his book *The Road Map*, a Chinese whistleblower who said Chinese humans' behaviour is already dictated by such a field. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:24:32-2:26:31)*
- The COVID "fake vaccine" is, he says, a receiver in the body: doctors and scientists he has consulted see self-replicating structures under electron microscopes, including graphene oxide, which he says superconducts electricity, changes how the brain processes information and amplifies electromagnetic fields. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:26:39-2:27:59)*
- Ray Kurzweil (favoured, he says, by Bill Gates) has said humans will be connected to AI via "the cloud" by 2030 until human thinking is "negligible or deleted". *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:28:08-2:29:04)*
- Diversions from AI expansion: the Iran war, "Trump", UFOs (Icke says 2026 will be big, with the Spielberg film "Disclosure Day" coming in June) and the shooting of Charlie Kirk. Ramsey and Icke agree this pulls attention away. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:29:12-2:29:51)*
- The US-China "AI race" is a psychological ruse: the cult is borderless, Chinese and American researchers collaborate on research papers, and a global AI control system is being built across the US, China, Europe and Russia. Ramsey adds that the Department of Energy tweeted that the AI race would be "the next Manhattan project". *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:30:05-2:32:02)*
- His decades of "the plan for humanity" warnings are, he says, more and more vindicated, and much of the alternative media stuck in an "eddy" of politics and finance instead of questioning beyond the box. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 3:18-4:56, 5:22-6:15)*
