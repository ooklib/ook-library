The Director's Initiatives Group at the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, as described in [[borland-michels-interview-2026]]: a unit under DNI Tulsi Gabbard that a Senate hearing with James Erdman III concerned, with a stated remit of finding out what the government has hidden. [[dylan-borland]] alleges it committed felonies against whistleblowers.
