The well-known USAF Foreign Technology Division at [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]], which operated from 1961 to 1991, evolved from the Air Technical Intelligence Center and sat under Air Force Systems Command, reportedly working on recovered Russian MiGs. Gerb repeatedly distinguishes it from Corso's Army office ([[army-foreign-technology-division]]) and notes that, given stove-piping, it "would not be shocking" if it also engaged with technology of unknown origin. Corso says [[project-moon-dust]] and Blue Fly reported to it. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:00:03-1:00:34, 1:02:17-1:02:34)*
