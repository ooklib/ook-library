The US war of conquest against Filipino resistance after the expulsion of Spain (from 1899), as narrated by [[eyes-wide-open]] in the context of the MacArthur family: [[arthur-macarthur-jr]] led US forces and was military governor; [[william-howard-taft]] led the transition to civilian rule; Douglas MacArthur first served in the islands in 1903 during the Moro Rebellion (the "second phase", per the host).

## Claims

- After Spain's expulsion, local resistance began "the first of many guerrilla wars of the 20th century"; Arthur MacArthur declared in May 1900 that the rebels were crushed. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 7:03-8:12)*
- Host: the early occupation was "especially brutal", with wholesale killing of civilians and concentration camps; roughly 15,000-20,000 Filipino and 4,200 American combatants killed, plus an estimated 200,000 civilians dead of starvation. Second phase, the Moro Rebellion, was starting as Taft left in February 1904. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 18:06-18:39)*
- Douglas MacArthur arrived in 1903 as an engineer lieutenant and befriended future presidents Manuel Quezon and Sergio Osmena. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 23:38-24:09)*
