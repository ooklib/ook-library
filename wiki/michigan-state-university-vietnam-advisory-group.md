The Michigan State University Vietnam Advisory Group (MSUG; ASR "MS ug") was a technical-assistance program to [[ngo-dinh-diem]]'s South Vietnam led by [[wesley-fishell]] under MSU's Center of International Programs and funded entirely by the US Operations Mission, which the official description says "channeled all US aid to South Vietnam". [[eyes-wide-open]] presents it, on the strength of a 1966 Ramparts article, as an academic front for CIA police and paramilitary advisers. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 54:07-54:35)*

## Claims

- Ramparts (1966, "University on the Make") reported MSU ran educational projects in 13 countries before South Vietnam, including Colombia, Taiwan, Turkey and Brazil. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 54:35-54:59)*
- Four key programs per Fishell's official MSU biography: public administration, help creating a constituent assembly, help writing a constitution, and, "most controversially", support for the regime's policing including hands-on technical help reforming the police and the secret police of [[ngo-dinh-nhu]]. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 55:05-55:45)*
- Former MSU professors told Ramparts a five-man CIA unit sat on the official organization chart as the largest section of the police-administration division. Quote: the CIA unit "was self-contained and appeared on an official organization chart". *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 55:45-56:21)*
- The group allegedly imported hundreds of revolvers, light arms, tear-gas canisters, handcuffs and other police gear and brought in police officers and even FBI agents; per Ramparts, "the East Lansing contingent helped secure Diem's dictatorship and provide the base and the arms for the secret police" that made Madame Nhu and her brother infamous. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 57:12-57:57)*
- Host's reading: either a CIA front for extra paramilitary trainers circumventing the Geneva accords, or a covert way into a regime [[edward-lansdale]] struggled to penetrate; he says the same secret police was later hooked to Laotian opium profits by the CIA, making the MSU and CIA missions "complementary" (he adds "it's all allegations"). *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 56:21-56:33, 57:50-58:14)*
- Host analogy: like the CIA-backed "Chicago boys" economists who staffed Pinochet's early administration in Chile after the 1973 coup, an example of academia as cover for spycraft. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 59:26-1:00:16)*
