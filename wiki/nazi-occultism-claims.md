## Claims

- 1945: US forces found Nazi documents in a southern German cave recording occult expeditions (Tibet, runestones, pagan sites, a Holy Grail quest). Himmler turned Wewelsburg into a ritual center and created the Ahnenerbe to argue Aryans descended from Atlantis. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 24:39-25:46)*

- Michels notes earlier episodes linked Hitler to the 1933 [[magenta-italy-crash-1933]] and rumored saucer tests. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 23:53-24:20)*
- [[hermann-oberth]] and [[wernher-von-braun]] are the rocket lineage; von Braun was captured in Bavaria while Henry Kissinger was allegedly helping run counterintelligence, had his record scrubbed, and was sent to build the US space program; FBI documents allegedly show he once sent classified material to Soviet counterparts without discipline. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 30:33-31:34, 38:43-39:09)* See [[henry-kissinger]].
