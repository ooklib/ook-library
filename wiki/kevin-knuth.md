Kevin Knuth is described by [[uap-gerb]] as a physics professor (ASR "Kevin canth", described as New York University; affiliation as stated by Gerb) who modeled the flight characteristics of anomalous aerial vehicles in famous cases. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 3:14-3:49)*

## Claims

- Gerb cites his paper "Estimating flight characteristics of anomalous unidentified aerial vehicles" for computing the g-forces implied by the 1986 [[japan-airlines-flight-1628-incident-1986]]. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 3:14-4:20)*

## Claims (gerb/uso-casebook)

- Gerb links a 1970 Kamchatka lake USO case (boat engine stalled) to the vehicle disruptions Knuth discussed at the SOL Foundation, and compares an Australian 1960s objects-submerging-without-disturbing-water case to the HMNZS Southland case Knuth described. See [[soviet-uso-cases]] and [[australian-uso-cases]]. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 9:22-9:46, 14:41-15:02, 26:16-26:32)*

## Claims (gerb/knuth)

- Gerb introduces Knuth as a University at Albany physics professor (PhD, University of Minnesota) working on information physics, quantum-mechanics foundations and Bayesian analysis, vice president of UAPx (a nonprofit field-research group) and research affiliate of Avi Loeb's Galileo Project. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 1:09-1:42)*
- Gerb summarizes the 25 September 2019 paper in Entropy by Knuth, Robert M. Powell and Peter Reali, "Estimating Flight Characteristics of Anomalous Unidentified Aerial Vehicles", which estimates speed and g-force for well-documented multi-sensor cases: a 1951 case (ASR "Beane"), the 1986 [[japan-airlines-flight-1628-incident-1986]] and the 2004 [[2004-nimitz-encounter]]. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 1:49-2:37)*
- In his November 2023 [[sol-foundation]] talk Knuth says he is skeptical of people who assume they witnessed an alien spacecraft, notes that unfamiliar engineering can look like anomalous physics, and criticizes the vagueness of the term UAP. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 3:01-3:59)*
- He shows seven UFO images (Mosul orb 2016, 1943 foo fighters, Costa Rica 1971, Weyauwega 2003, Vancouver 2005, Green Bay 2007, Trindade 1958) and lists the five (or six, with biological effects) UAP characteristics attributed to "L Alando" (Luis Elizondo, inferred), stressing sudden acceleration and hypersonic velocity without signatures. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 3:59-7:32)*
- Nimitz: for the Tic Tac's drop from 28,000 ft to sea level (transcribed as "78 seconds", likely garbled) Knuth's lower bound is about 5,000 g (Gerb: 5,400 g). With a deliberately conservative 1,000 kg mass, the power is about 1,100 GW, which Gerb equates to ten times total US nuclear output. Knuth adds there is no sonic boom, fireball or energy deposition at the stop, energy equal to about 250 Tomahawk missiles exploding. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 7:39-9:11, 14:17-15:04)*
- JAL 1628: Knuth calculates a speed of about 269,000 mph. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 12:32-12:40)*
- Under such constant accelerations the craft could reach high fractions of light speed; Gerb quotes the paper as saying they would make excellent interstellar craft. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 15:13-15:34)*
- Luminosity: from a August 1953 Canadian Air Force photograph (analyzed by [[bruce-maccabee]], summarized by [[jacques-vallee]]) he derives luminosities of about 2,000 to 30,000 "men Mega" (units unresolved in ASR), suggested as a propulsion byproduct. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 15:38-16:30)*
- Fields: electronics failures (e.g. [[tehran-ufo-incident-1976]]) and the 1983 "McCampbell" car-stalling case imply electric fields of about 3 x 10^6 V/m and around 10^9 J; Faraday-effect rings seen with polarizing filters imply magnetic fields of about 10^11 to 10^13 (units as transcribed). *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 16:36-18:08)*
- USOs: a transmedial object entering water at about 100 mph with no splash (analysis by SCU), a February 1987 case north of New Zealand where a 150 ft by 800 ft USO followed the HMNZS Southland, closed 20 km in under half a minute (average about 4.5 g) and reportedly drained the ship's power, and a list of eight older transmedial cases. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 18:08-19:43)*
- Minot: Knuth analyzes the 1968 [[minot-afb-ufo-incident-1968]]. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 10:54-11:32)*
- Knuth cites [[hermann-oberth]]'s 1954 lecture on measured saucer speeds. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 9:13-10:50)*
