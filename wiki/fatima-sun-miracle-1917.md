In the `jm/cover-up` episode [[jason-samosa]] describes the 1917 Fatima events in Portugal as a UFO-relevant case the Vatican tried to constrain: children saw a lady; about 70,000 people gathered; lawyers and doctors described a silvery disc that zigzagged and seemed to descend, after which the sun reappeared behind clouds. The Vatican's line was that this was the sun, while, Samosa says, a prophecy paper from the children was confiscated ("the secrets of Fatima"). He uses it as an example of the Vatican resisting information that could revolutionize doctrine. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 2:19:51, 2:20:18, 2:20:43, 2:21:25, 2:22:14)*
