Clare Boothe Luce (ASR "Clare Booth Loose") was a playwright, journalist, magazine editor, wife of [[henry-luce]] and later Republican congresswoman from Connecticut (1943-47). [[eyes-wide-open]], citing her biographer Sylvia Jukes Morris, describes her as [[charles-willoughby]]'s lover from 1940 and, in the host's inference, his direct line into the [[time-life]] media machine.

## Claims

- Visited Manila in 1940 as a Life correspondent to write a MacArthur profile; began an affair with Willoughby (newly separated from his first wife Hanna in 1940). She said he was "the one man that I could have run away with". The profile ran in Life on 7 December 1941, the day of Pearl Harbor. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 47:52-48:09, 49:16-50:26)*
- Host describes the Luces' publicly open marriage (Morris: both "wandered off the reservation"), calls Henry Luce a "China-born Skull and Bones inducted media tycoon", and says her affairs included [[joseph-kennedy]] while he was ambassador in London. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 48:09-49:09)*
- Host's inference (not evidence): Willoughby "pulled some strings" with her to get [[douglas-macarthur]] glowing press; MacArthur appeared on Time/Life covers a "staggering" number of times. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 50:26-51:05)*
- Reunited with Willoughby in Washington in summer 1943 (he stayed a block from her apartment) and San Francisco in July 1944, where her 18-year-old daughter Ann Clare Brokaw (ASR "Anne Clare Brock"/"Brockaw") also wrote of being infatuated with him; Ann also was seeing John Kennedy in the late 1930s while Clare saw Joe Kennedy (host). Wartime letters continued; she stayed in his life after his 1951 remarriage. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 51:05-51:25, 1:10:44-1:11:11, 1:17:26-1:18:50)*
- Host: shared Willoughby and MacArthur's politics, backed the 1944 MacArthur bid, saw America's future in the Pacific, and would keep "swirling in the very same circles" as Willoughby and Robert Wood after the war (to come). *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:11:05-1:11:37)*
