William (Bill) Perry, as Secretary of Defense, enacted 1994 reforms significantly strengthening
DoD SAP management/control structures — specifically the
[[special-access-program-oversight-committee|SAPOC]] and its Senior Review Group — restricting
temporary-employee and non-need-to-know access. Gerb believes this was directly tied to
protecting UFO programs. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:44:52–1:45:32)*

## Claims

- Gerb cites the "Wilson–Davis notes" as discussing Perry, [[paul-kaminski]], and this 1994 SAP
  reorganization, which he believes are substantially accurate. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:45:32–1:45:50)*
- Per the SAIC investigation: as Deputy Secretary of Defense, conducted the actual 5 January
  1994 SAPOC/SRG restructuring (later inherited by [[john-m-deutch|John Deutch]] as chair); once
  served on [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]]'s board — which Gerb calls
  "once could be chance, two is a pattern" given Deutch's parallel SAIC ties.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 37:36–37:56, 39:14–39:32)*

- Served at the NRO 1955–1997, where he chaired the "Perry Panel" advising NRO Program B on
  overhead SIGINT collection systems, ground-based at [[pine-gap-australia|Pine Gap, Australia]].
  Named among Gerb's 13 alleged NRO-linked gatekeepers. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  2:22:23–2:22:52, 2:50:59–2:51:14)*
