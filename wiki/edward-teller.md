Edward Teller, father of the hydrogen bomb, appears in two roles in this source: as the fifth "credible witness" to Brown's experiments and as the figure Lazar said he met before hiring at S4.

## Claims (jm/townsend-brown)

- Teller watched a Brown demo at his Berkeley home with Linda Brown and Teller's wife Augusta present, and said he had no idea what made it work; his wife said it was nice to hear him say so. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 20:18-20:56)*
- His demo convinced Floyd Odom to invest in [[guidance-technologies]]. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 25:07-25:19)*
- [[bob-lazar]] was reportedly recruited after meeting Teller. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 33:12-33:18)*
