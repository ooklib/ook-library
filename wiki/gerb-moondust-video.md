A documentary-style video (about 15 minutes, dated 2024-01-24) in which [[uap-gerb]] follows the FOIA paper trail of [[project-moon-dust]], which he presents as a possible Department of Defense UFO crash retrieval program tied to the Air Force and [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]]. The source page is [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6ZuHLgVtKu8). He says he plans a website to archive the documents he uses. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 0:00-1:37)*

## Claims

- Framing: after David Grusch and Schumer's 2024 NDAA legacy-program language, a DoD UFO reverse-engineering and crash-retrieval program is "no longer just speculation"; he says only a couple hundred people in the program's 80-plus-year history have been read in. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 0:26-0:58)*
- Gerb argues [[edward-j-ruppelt]] concluded UFOs had an "interplanetary explanation" and suggested a parallel, more complete classified investigation in April 1952, so Moon Dust could be an inconspicuous crash retrieval program next to the official programs. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 1:37-2:51)*
- Documents shown: a 1961 USAF draft policy, a 1967 review of the 1127th USAF Field Activities Group, a 1961 Karachi sighting memo, a 1968 Nepal disc memo, a 1976 Canada memo, and 1978 New Zealand and Bolivia cases; details are on [[project-moon-dust]] and [[bolivia-ufo-crash-1978]]. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 2:51-10:56)*
- The 1992-1994 Air Force denials and the alleged destruction of files are covered in [[moon-dust-senate-inquiries-1992-1994]]; Gerb links them to the early-1990s reorganization into special access programs described by [[thomas-wilson]]. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 10:56-13:32)*
- Gerb notes the pressure-vessel shape of the 1978 New Zealand fragments resembles [[david-fravor]]'s Tic Tac description (propane tank or pressure vessel without inlets or outlets). *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 7:39-8:04)*
- He calls 1969-2008 (Blue Book's end to the AARO predecessor) the "Dark Ages" with no official US UAP investigation group. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 12:54-13:16)*
- In the outro he rejects the view that Moon Dust only collected foreign and domestic space vehicles, and names further unacknowledged programs he wants to cover: [[zodiac-crash-retrieval-program]], Blue Fly, and the [[cia-office-of-global-access]]. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 13:40-14:29)*
