Khun Sa (ASR: "kuna"), Chinese-Burmese warlord known as the "king of opium," rose in Shan State among the
remaining Hui contingent left after [[li-mi]]'s defeat in 1961, per Eyes Wide Open; he held power
until the mid-1990s. The host notes few documentaries mention his origins under the CIA-funded
troops of [[operation-paper]]. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 46:56-47:28)*
