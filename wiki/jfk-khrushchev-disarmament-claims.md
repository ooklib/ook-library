In [[sheehan-michels-interview-2025]], [[danny-sheehan]] argues that [[john-f-kennedy]] was killed because after the [[cuban-missile-crisis]] he and Khrushchev secretly agreed to destroy nuclear warheads and to merge their space programs, with Kennedy demanding a UFO briefing in June 1963 to share with Khrushchev. This is Sheehan's (and Michels's) narrative, presented as motive for the plot in [[sheehan-jfk-assassination-claims]].

## Claims

- Sheehan says Kennedy exchanged about 18 secret, off-channel letters with Khrushchev via Norman Cousins after the missile crisis, agreeing to disassemble and destroy (not shelve) warheads, and that Khrushchev proposed Pope John XXIII broker and verify the process; John XXIII died in early June 1963. Sheehan also says China agreed not to pursue a nuclear program. *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 42:12)* *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 47:38)* *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 1:02:37)*
- Joint space program: Kennedy proposed combining Soviet and US space efforts as an economic stimulus to replace weapons spending. *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 48:43)*
- UFO briefing: Sheehan says on June 5, 1963 Kennedy asked CIA director [[john-mccone]] (formerly head of the Atomic Energy Commission) to fully brief him on UFOs, in order to share it with Khrushchev before joint space ventures. Sheehan says he only learned this via [[harald-malmgren]]'s account: as a 27-year-old McNamara aide costing X-ray missile defense during the Cuban missile crisis, Malmgren heard a UFO was knocked out of a nuclear plume in the Bluegill test, was taken to Sandia (Kirtland) to see debris (Lawrence Preston Gist, described as Jeff Bezos's maternal grandfather, is named), felt the debris "speak" telepathically, and was briefed by [[richard-bissell]] on "otherworld technologies" and past crashes including [[magenta-italy-crash-1933]].
