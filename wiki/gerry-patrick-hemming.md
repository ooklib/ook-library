Gerry Patrick Hemming (ASR: "Jerry Patrick Heming" throughout the transcript; the normalization to Hemming is my inference from the wiki's usual spelling and the video's cited "Hemming and Oswald document 1977") is introduced by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the second American "soldier of fortune" of the [[road-to-dallas]] series, a longtime collaborator of [[frank-sturgis]] and founder of the mercenary group Interpen (covered in later chapters). *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 22:40-23:00)*

## Claims

- Born 1937 in Los Angeles, 6'5"-6'6", roughly 15 years younger than Sturgis; the host says the LA origin fits his later role as a "public frontman for the paramilitary underground". *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 22:40-23:13)*
- Enlisted in the Marine Corps April 1954 (host: he was reportedly 17 and lied about his age); CIA and FBI files give service dates April 19, 1954 to October 17, 1958. A CIA report of April 1977 says he served "in Japan with a US Marine Airwing"; a 2008 Miami Herald piece calls him an "aviation control tower operator". *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 24:42-25:36)* (The host says "his 2011 personnel file at CIA"; the year is probably an ASR/host slip since the report is dated 1977 and the Herald piece 2008; flagged, not resolved.)
- Host observation: Hemming's last two years in Japan (1957 to Nov 1958) overlap with [[lee-harvey-oswald]]'s posting at Atsugi Air Base, so he could have been at Atsugi or in the same circles; the host calls this suggestive but possibly coincidence. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 25:36-26:37)*
- Hemming repeatedly claimed to have met Oswald in Los Angeles in 1959 at the Cuban consulate while recruiting Americans for Castro's military. In his 1976 Argosy interview with Dick Russell he said the 26th of July coordinator called him aside about a Marine who might desert, that the Marine described himself as a radar operator, and that Hemming "thought he was a penetrator" on "the naval intelligence payroll" and told the 26th of July leadership to get rid of him. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 26:37-28:05)*
- Host: the story fits Oswald's reported timeline (posted in LA in early 1959, reportedly said he wanted to fight with Castro), but Hemming's "high tales", his associations with others swirling around Oswald and the assassination, and his eagerness to be the public face of the Gulf Coast paramilitaries make his testimony "somewhat questionable". *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 28:05-28:43)*
- Host's opinion (explicitly flagged as speculation): Hemming and Interpen may have been "created and funded precisely to add additional noise and confusion" to the Gulf Coast, a "chaos agent" and "distracting lawn furniture within the conspiracy canon"; he notes Hemming gave abundant interviews, including a TV spot as early as 1962. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 23:13-24:37, 28:43-29:23)*
- Per a CIA info file, left for Cuba on February 18, 1959 (about five months after discharge); through 1959-60 made several trips between Cuba, Florida and Los Angeles recruiting mercenaries and arranging weapons shipments for the new regime. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 29:16-29:50)*
- Host: possibly "flipped" by US intelligence while in Japan, since his later activities became increasingly suspect (to be shown in later chapters). *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 30:51-31:10)*

## Additional claims (ewa/dallas-3)

- Per Hemming's FBI file (host): Hemming joined Sturgis in Cuba around February 1959; by April 1960 the file says he "was then employed by the Cuban Revolutionary Air Force at San Julian as a parachutist and was then engaged in training Nicaraguans", showing the Nicaragua invasion plans persisted through the first two years of the regime. He met Sturgis (air-force security chief) while serving as a paratrooper at Pinar del Rio, and the link lasted through the secret war; the origins of [[interpen]] lie there. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 5:01-5:50, 6:46-7:57)*
- In 1978 HSCA testimony he identified "Jack Sheay" as really Jack Cannon (two N's), naming him among Americans working with Camilo Cienfuegos; see [[jack-cannon]]. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 19:00-20:37)*
- Told Senate investigators he believed [[loran-hall]] ("Lorenzo Hall") was involved in the JFK assassination, and in the 1993 Argosy interview said Hall was in Dallas that day. Host: the willingness of Hemming's men to accuse one another is why the network is "problematic" and fuels his speculation they were "distractions". *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 8:26-9:52)*
