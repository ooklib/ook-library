The Rif War (early 1920s to mid-1920s, "5-year conflict" per the host) pitted Berber tribesmen of Spanish Morocco, led by Abd el-Krim, against Spain and later France. In [[eyes-wide-open]]'s Willoughby biography it is the setting where [[charles-willoughby]] first did intelligence work and met [[francisco-franco]]. (ASR variants "Riff", "Rift".)

## Host's account

- Background: Spain's colonial decline left it only the northeastern Moroccan protectorate; Berber fighters organized into a guerrilla army and a short-lived "Republic of Riff". At the Battle of Annual (host: "disaster of Anual"), about 3,000 Rifians reportedly killed 13,000+ of 20,000 Spanish (host's figures), toppling several Spanish governments. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 19:58-22:58)*
- Abd el-Krim later died in exile in Cairo in February 1963; Mao, Ho Chi Minh and Che Guevara reportedly cited him on guerrilla warfare (host's aside). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 21:21-21:56)*
- France intervened; Marshal Lyautey was replaced by [[philippe-petain]] in 1925. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 23:28-24:35)*
- Host thesis: the war sits at the intersection of colonialism and fascism, and inspired future fascist leaders; the "Africanists" around [[francisco-franco]] later formed the Falangist leadership. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 24:41-25:53)*
- Chemical warfare: Spanish and French air forces "indiscriminately" used chemical weapons dispensed by low-flying aircraft, which the host says led to their victory. Whether Willoughby was involved is posed as "certainly a possibility" (speculation, no evidence given). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 28:26-28:59)*
- Willoughby was ostensibly a US Army observer but, per a William Dean article the host found only in French translation ("Americans in the Riff Rebellion"), was embedded with the mercenary American volunteer air unit "Escadrille Chérifienne" (ASR "Escadri Sheriffen", Sheriffian Squadron). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 27:22-28:15)*
- Willoughby's August 1925 *Infantry Journal* article "Spanish campaigns in Morocco" frames the war in racial terms (quoted on [[charles-willoughby]]). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 32:44-34:09)*
