Jesse Michels' solo American Alchemy episode of about 1h36m, published 2026-05-21 (video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OOr98r51Sj0), in the series' "American Alchemist" format, here a walkthrough of [[jake-barber]]'s novel [[sentinels-of-ether]]. Michels opens with the Department of War release (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:14-4:19)*, see [[department-of-war-ufo-release-2026]]) and NASA astronaut sightings (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 4:25-6:44)*, see [[gene-cernan]]), introduces Barber, then retells the book's opening chapters as narrated fiction interleaved with his own commentary on what might be real. All substantive assertions are attributed claims; Michels repeatedly flags his foglet and hologram discussion as speculation (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 53:06-53:14)*).

## Structure and themes

- The manuscript's backstory (missing Panasonic Toughbooks, a burned operation, and the plan to use pre-publication review as a discovery tool) is in [[barber-toughbooks-operation-claims]] and [[dopsr-prepublication-review-claims]].
- The setting is [[coyame-mexico]], "Mexico's Roswell", and the opening ambush, JSOC reaction, F-18 chase and vanishing mountain are summarized in [[coyame-mountain-base-narrative]] and [[sentinels-of-ether]].
- Refugees moved in shipping containers link to [[michael-herrera-indonesia-encounter-2009]], first told at the [[national-press-club-disclosure-event-2023]] hosted by [[steven-greer]], and to [[psionic-assets-and-breeding-claims]].
- Michels' explanation of the "mountain" is [[foglets-and-programmable-matter]] (J. Storrs Hall's utility fog), with [[ben-rich]] and [[bradshaw-ranch-sedona]] as supporting lore.
- Psionic summoning of UFOs and the CE5 comparison are in [[ce5-contact-protocols]]; the drugged, chipped "pink assets" and the "psychic joystick" pilot interface are in [[psychic-joystick-brain-interface-claims]], with R&D lineage through [[john-norseen]], [[lockheed-martin]], [[darpa-n3-program]] and [[salvatore-pais]].
- The closing survey of mountain-linked sites ([[bradshaw-ranch-sedona]], [[skinwalker-ranch]], Pat Price's Mount Hayes) is in [[mountain-base-network-claims]] and ends with the breakaway-civilization thesis (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:30:25-1:32:14)*, [[richard-dolan]]).

## Framing by the host

- Michels argues the book is fiction only in name: "The best-kept secrets don't need to be classified. They're just too insane to be taken seriously. They're self-stigmatizing." *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 8:53-9:08)*
- He says disclosure will be "spoon-fed to us in fiction" and that, in the tradition [[tom-delonge]] established with *Secret Machines* (see [[sekret-machines-project]]), the manuscript may encode real truth and use the Pentagon's own redactions as authentication. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:32:07-1:33:16)*
- His thesis is that "the big secret isn't a mother ship. It's you": human consciousness is the real technology, and the black world spent 70 years trying to monopolize it. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 10:01-10:29, 1:33:39-1:34:17)*
- He treats the Department of War release as a possible distraction from Epstein and Iran, but says UFOs can be real and also be used as a distraction. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 3:09-3:45)*
- Sponsor reads (Incogni, Plaud, MUD\WTR) and a hiring pitch are skipped here.
