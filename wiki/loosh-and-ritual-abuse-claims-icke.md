[[david-icke]]'s claim, in [[icke-ramsey-interview-2026]], that non-human entities feed on the low-vibration energy of human fear and suffering ("loosh", a term he credits to [[robert-monroe]]) and that the "global cult" stages wars, deprivation and ritual abuse to produce it. These are extreme, unverified allegations about named categories of people and are recorded only as Icke's assertions.

## Claims

- Loosh: fear, conflict, war and suffering emit frequencies that a "chaotic" consciousness can absorb whereas joy and love it cannot; the cult's job is to generate events that trigger these states, and he says human history is endless war and deprivation. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:12:10-1:14:38)*
- Icke says that in the early 1990s he found people he investigated took part in satanic ritual including child sacrifice and pedophilia, and that "the whole foundation of this cult in the human realm is pedophilia and Satanism". *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:41:09-1:41:55, 1:05:32-1:05:48)*
- Ancient gods and pantheons and their sacrifices were the same astral entities as now; sacrifice was once open and is now secret. Former Satanists have told him rituals maximize the victim's terror, entities feed on the energy, and participants drink adrenochrome (adrenaline chemistry from terror) as an elixir; he adds that stories of adrenochrome use in Washington, "both parties", are related. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:42:04-1:44:46)*
- Ramsey asks whether loosh is like adrenochrome for these beings; Icke says it is how they get high and also sustenance. He points to the world wars, the then-current Iran war, and events since 7 October 2023 in Gaza as mass suffering that "empowers them". *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:44:46-1:45:38)*
- His counter is to live as consciousness rather than a human, observing the drama without being emotionally pulled in, so that attention and "loosh" are not fed to the system. Trump, he says, is a "drama queen extraordinaire" and the perfect front man for a drama-driven attention drain. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:45:50-1:48:27, 1:47:00-1:47:28)*
- Ramsey proposes that a game developer would stress-test the simulation with chaos and that hardship may be purposeful; Icke rejects this as the reincarnation "lessons" story. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:38:01-1:39:29)*
- Icke says the Savile and Epstein cases are "gateways to the cesspit" of this Satanism and pedophilia network, which is "the cement that holds the global cult together" and is compartmentalized so most secret-society members do not know. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:15:16-2:16:38)*
