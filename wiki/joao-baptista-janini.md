Joao Baptista Macuco Janini (transcribed "Jao Baptista Makuko Janini") is described by [[james-fox]] as a Brazilian forensic pathologist and founder of the IPD, whose tissue analysis of [[marco-chereze]] in 1996 he says found an unprecedented bacterial infection.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says Janini has done over 50,000 autopsies with his company since the 1960s and has "never seen a case like this" before or since, and has now put his endorsement, stamp and signature on the report on camera late in his career. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 19:41-19:54, 1:11:08-1:11:39, 1:14:01-1:14:30)*
- Janini told Fox the bacteria probably came from under the being's nail, via a scratch in the armpit. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:11:49-1:12:07)*
- Fox interviewed Janini and Dr. Armando Fortunato at the IPD; Fortunato then phoned neurosurgeon [[italo-venturelli]], leading to the hospital scene. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:45:46-1:47:02)*
