[[ross-coulthart]] argues that Congress has been kept largely oblivious to the legacy program, that a select few senators have recently been "read in", and that this is being used to blunt momentum for disclosure law. These are his claims and inferences from background conversations.

## Claims

- Before 2021 many in Congress, including members of the Gang of Eight, were genuinely oblivious; Coulthart says he pulled rank to reach two of them and that people who denied any program to him in 2018-2020 turned evasive after the Pentagon's 2021 acknowledgment that UAPs are real. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 31:56-32:38, 35:23-35:57)*
- The intelligence community and military disregard oversight because they believe Congress cannot be trusted with secrets (and, he says, "they're probably right" because both parties leak). *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 33:17-33:42, 40:44-41:20)*
- Certain Gang of Eight members "have been read in", and he expects the Pentagon and intelligence community to cite that as an excuse when the truth comes out. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 35:57-36:20)*
- He says he is "pretty sure" Senator [[john-mccain]] was aware of legacy retrieval and reverse engineering, second-hand from someone who knew him. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 36:20-36:48)*
- He says he knows a certain unnamed Gang of Eight senator (he guesses a Democrat) was told by a source what he now lies to the public about. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 38:15-38:47)*
- In the past year or two the program has begun briefing select senators (not the House) in order to protect itself against lawsuits if it is disclosed. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 39:07-39:25)*
- Constitutional lawyers he spoke to see no basis for a president being barred from a briefing (as [[bill-clinton]] was when he asked); Coulthart is told a secret executive order was signed probably in the [[harry-truman]] administration, and another under [[dwight-d-eisenhower]], enshrining the program and keeping it from Congress. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 39:31-40:28, 34:54-35:19)*
- He is told that senior senators and majority leaders were taken aside and given just enough privileged information, which shut down congressional momentum; late last year it had looked like new disclosure laws were coming, including whistleblower protections for contractors. See [[uap-disclosure-act-2023]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:13:00-1:14:37)*
- He says witnesses unknown to the public have testified to the Senate Intelligence Committee, Senate Armed Services and House oversight, and that [[david-grush]] gave "times, dates, places, names, literally the door numbers". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:12:08-1:12:47)*
- UFO/UAP caucus members are doing the right thing but are not powerful; the gatekeepers have gone quiet after lobbying by the defence and intelligence community. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:16:39-1:17:13)*
- [[jake-barber]] testified to Congress at Coulthart's team's instigation, and per Coulthart described the blue-on-blue incident and the belief that his team was set up by a private corporation to be murdered; Coulthart does not think Barber "opted out" but has done all he can within the law. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:14:37-1:16:14)*
- He thinks the Pentagon's current wording (no evidence of extraterrestrial engagement) leaves wiggle room, citing [[chris-sharp]] being denied the term NHI by Susan Gough of the Pentagon. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:03:46-1:04:21)*
