A second-hand account [[danny-sheehan]] tells of a dying Minnesota Army clerk-typist, Oscar Wolf, who said he worked on a Project Blue Book unit reviewing about 700 unexplainable cases and later accompanied his commander to S-4, where he saw hovering craft and a live extraterrestrial being being interviewed through two-way glass. Sheehan's recording of the interview was corrupted.

## Claims

- Sheehan says around 2001-2002 he flew to northern Minnesota to hear Wolf at a hospice; Wolf was a US Army clerk with a special [[project-blue-book]] unit, and he and the commander were both members of the Worldwide Church of God. *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 1:46:42)* *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. - At Area 51 and then S-4 Wolf reportedly saw three or four craft hovering in underground "little garages" (echoing [[bob-lazar]]) and, through a two-way mirror, a roughly five-and-a-half-foot being with a large head and eyes in a jumpsuit communicating telepathically with the commander; Wolf refused to enter because he thought it "demonic".
