Allen Dulles (transcribed "Alan Dulles" in Eyes Wide Open's ASR captions) was an
[[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]] officer during WWII who went on to become the CIA's first
civilian director. Eyes Wide Open, drawing heavily on David Talbot's *The Devil's Chessboard*,
calls him "arguably one of, if not the most important person in modern American history" and
"one of the most reprehensible characters in American history."

## Claims

- Born into the American elite: his maternal grandfather John W. Foster was secretary of state
  under Benjamin Harrison, and his uncle by marriage was Woodrow Wilson's secretary of state.
  Born in Watertown, NY; graduated Princeton; law degree from George Washington University.
  *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 18:26-19:02)*
- Before the war, worked as a Wall Street lawyer at [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan &
  Cromwell]], where his brother [[john-foster-dulles]] (future Eisenhower secretary of state)
  was a partner. Eyes Wide Open alleges a "severe conflict of interest" between Sullivan &
  Cromwell's client list — which included Nazi-adjacent firms such as
  [[ig-farben|IG Farben]] and Krupp AG — and Dulles's OSS duties, which nominally reported to
  FDR's anti-Nazi war effort. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 19:02-20:16)*
- Elected president of the [[council-on-foreign-relations]] in 1927. Eyes Wide Open frames his
  use of that platform to push an interventionist "make the world safe for democracy" line as
  aligned with his access to the OSS's operational reach. At the CFR's 1946 meeting he argued
  the US should not go too far denazifying Germany because "most men of the caliber required to
  run the new Germany suffer a political taint" (i.e., were Nazis) — cited by the host as
  evidence of Dulles's Nazi sympathies. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 20:17-21:22)*
- Eyes Wide Open alleges Dulles (via Sullivan & Cromwell) continued serving [[ig-farben|IG
  Farben]] and the Nazi Reich's interests through the "revolving door" between Wall Street and
  government even during the war, defying State Department/presidential orders regarding dealing
  with Nazis. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 23:03-24:16)*
- Met Hitler in 1933 while on a State Department diplomatic mission; per the host's account,
  wrote back to State Department colleagues expressing curiosity in the rising fascist party,
  saw potential business deals with Nazi Germany, and admired propagandist Joseph Goebbels'
  "frankness and sincerity." *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 24:39-25:11)*
- Eyes Wide Open frames Dulles (along with the OSS leadership generally) as rabidly
  anti-communist, viewing Nazi Germany as a useful anti-Soviet "bulwark" — a stance the host
  says gave rise to alliances between the OSS and high-ranking Nazi officials and to postwar
  "stay-behind" organizations of armed former Nazis, foreshadowing the next episode's "Operation
  Sunrise/Sunset," Dulles's alleged scheme to broker a separate peace with Nazi Germany.
  *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 25:59-29:35)*
- Stationed at the OSS office in Bern, Switzerland for most of the war, using its neutral ground
  to parlay with Nazis and fascists without attracting Allied attention. Met with Nazi General
  [[reinhard-gehlen]] (at [[fort-hunt-virginia|Fort Hunt, Virginia]], after Gehlen's surrender)
  and recruited Gehlen's network as a postwar stay-behind anti-communist asset, seeding both the
  [[gehlen-organization]] and the future [[operation-gladio]]. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 2:25-5:40)*
- Worked closely in Bern with banker [[thomas-mckittrick]] of the
  [[bank-for-international-settlements|Bank for International Settlements]]; along with his
  brother [[john-foster-dulles]], allegedly worked to shield BIS-held Nazi client funds (IG
  Farben, Merck KGaA) from FDR's [[operation-safe-haven|Operation Safe Haven]] confiscation
  policy, destroying paper records of the transactions himself. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 6:21-8:24)*
- Along with [[william-donovan]], worked to save select Nazi war criminals from Nuremberg
  justice: successfully securing [[hjalmar-schacht]]'s release (via Donovan circumventing
  prosecutor Robert Jackson) while judging [[hermann-goring]] too high-profile to save.
  *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 10:05-12:53)*
- Devised and ran [[operation-sunrise]], the scheme to rescue and protect SS General
  [[karl-wolff]] in exchange for blocking Soviet-aligned Italian partisans; delegated the field
  rescue to [[gerhart-von-schulze-gaevernitz]] to avoid the appearance of insubordination
  himself, then used destroyed evidence, falsified character reports, and a forged psychiatric
  diagnosis to keep Wolff out of Nuremberg prosecution, eventually writing Wolff's exculpatory
  trial affidavit personally. Wolff allegedly blackmailed Dulles over the affair, including
  writing to President Truman. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 13:40-21:41)*
- Viewed by [[james-jesus-angleton]] (OSS station chief in Rome, later CIA counterintelligence
  chief) as "a second father and a mentor." *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 23:36-23:40)*
- Along with Donovan, "loved" [[paul-helliwell]]'s proposal to fund OSS covert action via
  narcotics trafficking — first backing his China opium-facilitation plan, then his postwar
  [[operation-x]] scheme. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 10:56-11:08, 14:27-14:41, 19:49-19:55)*
- Sullivan & Cromwell's client list — including [[united-fruit-company|United Fruit]] and the
  [[anglo-iranian-oil-company|Anglo Iranian Oil Company]] — is cited by Eyes Wide Open as
  evidence of the CIA's later conflicts of interest, since Dulles (as CIA director under
  Eisenhower) and his brother [[john-foster-dulles]] (as secretary of state) worked to overthrow
  the governments that threatened those firms' interests: [[jacobo-arbenz]] in Guatemala (1954)
  and, one year earlier, [[mohammad-mossadegh]] in Iran (at the British government's/AIOC's
  request). *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 5:42-6:24)*
- While nominally practicing law at [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]] between 1945
  and 1947, continued liaising with former OSS colleagues to plot the future CIA's course,
  drafting a 1946-47 work plan with [[william-donovan]] largely based on Donovan's vision of "a
  highly funded top secret unaccountable agency" with unlimited global reach. Elected president
  of the [[council-on-foreign-relations]] in 1945 (Eyes Wide Open's Part 1 separately cites a
  1927 CFR presidency), which the host frames as another venue for remaining connected to the
  postwar security state. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 20:40-21:21, 21:23-21:52)*
- In 1947, called before the Senate Armed Services Committee to lay out the architecture of the
  future CIA — the outline Truman ultimately approved, forming the basis of the
  [[national-security-act-1947|National Security Act of 1947]]. Also presented the proposal to a
  June 1947 congressional committee in DC. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 21:23-21:52, 22:57-23:11)*

- Per David Talbot, met [[richard-nixon]] in 1947 aboard the Queen Mary during a European
  fact-finding mission, allegedly convincing Nixon to support the [[marshall-plan]] and beginning
  an alliance marrying the East Coast Republican establishment with the emerging western
  conservative movement. Per former Justice Department Nazi-hunter John Loftus (allegations the
  host stresses are unverifiable), Dulles's partnership with Nixon may run deeper: Loftus alleged
  Nixon discovered documents in 1945 indicating Allen and [[john-foster-dulles]] had laundered
  Nazi funds during the war, and that Nixon agreed to silence in exchange for secret campaign
  funding. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 9:48-11:19)*
- In 1948, John Foster Dulles hoped to be installed as secretary of state (and Allen as CIA
  director) under a [[thomas-dewey]] presidential administration; the brothers backed Dewey's
  campaign against Truman, and Nixon reportedly consulted with John Foster (who agreed the
  [[alger-hiss]] trial should proceed so long as Foster's own prior sponsorship of Hiss to the
  [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace]] presidency was not revealed). *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 18:51-19:38, 19:57-19:57)*
- In 1956, nephew [[avery-dulles]] (son of [[john-foster-dulles]]) was ordained a Catholic priest
  by Cardinal [[francis-spellman]], despite the Dulles family's Protestant background — a
  synchronicity the host notes without resolving, given Spellman's simultaneous closeness to CIA
  activities in Italy. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 35:24-36:16)*
- Vice chairman of the CIA-cutout [[american-committee-on-united-europe]] (1948) under [[william-donovan]]; as deputy director was persuaded by [[thomas-braden]] to create the [[cia-international-organizations-division]]. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 22:58-23:32, 25:51-26:10)*

- Per David Talbot (Detachment 202 episode), after being fired by JFK over the Bay of Pigs, Dulles hosted [[e-howard-hunt]], [[james-jesus-angleton]], [[richard-helms]], [[cord-meyer]] and [[john-mccone]] at his home in 1962-63; Hunt sought his help for a private intelligence career and helped on his memoirs; researchers speculate a sinister purpose tied to the assassination (speculation). Also called the 'big three coups' director ([[operation-pbsuccess]]). *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 5:37-5:47, 7:35-9:07)*

- LAOS 2: after Lansdale and Conein's briefing by Trinquier, Dulles was "initially skeptical" of narco funding per Lansdale's account but the host says he was "particularly impressed" by Trinquier's cunning and saw that the French reached the same conclusion as the CIA did with [[operation-paper]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 47:48-48:31)*

- LAOS 3: sent the [[saigon-military-mission]] on 1 June 1954; the host says it was his personal project and Lansdale reported only to him; impressed by Lansdale's Philippines results. A 1954 remark to the NSC, quoted, says a majority of Vietnamese supported the Viet Minh after Dien Bien Phu. Host finds Dulles compelling as the authority "higher than Nixon" behind the MSU program. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 14:29-15:18, 18:08-18:21, 29:32-29:55, 56:47-57:05)*

- LAOS 4: Dec 28, 1958 NSC statement that "drastic changes" in the Lao government were needed; per [[bill-lair]] visited his Thai training camp in 1953; authorized [[operation-momentum]] by short cable in early 1961; then fired after the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]]. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 37:07-37:32, 1:00:50-1:01:13, 1:11:19-1:11:47, 1:16:28-1:16:42)*

## Relationships

- OSS officer under [[william-donovan]]; future director of the
  [[central-intelligence-agency]] (per later episodes of this Eyes Wide Open series).
- Worked at [[sullivan-and-cromwell]] alongside his brother [[john-foster-dulles]].
- President of the [[council-on-foreign-relations]] (1927, per Part 1; 1945, per Part 4).
- Sullivan & Cromwell's client relationship with [[ig-farben]], [[united-fruit-company]], and
  [[anglo-iranian-oil-company]] is alleged to have created conflicts of interest with his OSS/CIA
  roles.
- Recruited [[reinhard-gehlen]]'s network as a US stay-behind asset, seeding the
  [[gehlen-organization]] and [[operation-gladio]].
- Worked closely with banker [[thomas-mckittrick]] of the
  [[bank-for-international-settlements]]; allegedly circumvented [[operation-safe-haven]] with
  [[john-foster-dulles]].
- Devised and ran [[operation-sunrise]], protecting [[karl-wolff]] (rescued in the field by
  [[gerhart-von-schulze-gaevernitz]]) and [[hjalmar-schacht]] (with [[william-donovan]]); could

  [[sullivan-and-cromwell]]; presented the agency's architecture to the Senate Armed Services

- Alleged 1947 alliance (and possible campaign-funding arrangement) with [[richard-nixon]]; backed

- Nephew [[avery-dulles]] ordained a priest by [[francis-spellman]] in 1956.
- Vice chairman of [[american-committee-on-united-europe]]; created [[cia-international-organizations-division]] at [[thomas-braden]]'s urging.
