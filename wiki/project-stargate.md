Project Stargate was a joint, secret Army/DIA/CIA/SRI-International-contractor program (begun
1977) investigating military use of psychic phenomena ("remote viewing"). It went by multiple
code names by sponsoring agency: Gondola Wish, Stargate, Sunstreak (CIA), Grill Flame (INSCOM),
Centerlane (DIA). *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:42:34–1:43:21)*

## Claims

- Originally conducted at [[sri-international|SRI International]], sponsored at times by the
  NSA, CIA, DIA, and US Army [[army-intelligence-and-security-command|INSCOM]].
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:37:52–1:38:06)*
- Key early sponsor: [[albert-stubblebine|Maj. Gen. Albert Stubblebine]], founder of INSCOM.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:43:12–1:43:28)*
- Consolidated under the "Stargate" name and moved to
  [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] in 1991, continuing there through at
  least 1994 (Gerb wagers longer). *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:43:21–1:43:36,
  1:43:36–1:43:44)*
- SAIC published 10 primary studies on remote viewing/psi phenomena, coining "anomalous
  cognition" (AC — information transfer absent known sensorial stimuli, i.e. remote
  viewing/clairvoyance) and "anomalous perturbation" (AP — psychokinesis, influencing matter by
  an unknown process); SAIC's official position was that AC was verified but AP was not.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:44:44–1:45:34)*
- Employed "agents" (subjects displaying AC) and "beacons" (individuals providing direct
  sensorial focus for the AC receiver). *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:45:34–1:45:56)*
- Gerb draws a direct parallel to UFO legacy programs: an embedded interview clip speculates
  Stargate-style remote-viewing programs may have spawned "out of a need to try and unlock
  something we have in a warehouse somewhere" (i.e., recovered NHI technology).
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:37:59–1:38:54)* [[david-grush|David Grush]] separately
  made a similar connection discussing Stargate and CIA remote-viewing documents.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 2:03:29–2:04:14)*
- Physicist [[eric-davis|Dr. Eric Davis]]'s *Teleportation Physics Study* (2001–2003, written
  for AFRL) devoted a section to "psychokinetic teleportation," theoretical/experimental work
  Davis traced back to the SRI-International/Stargate lineage. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:37:44–1:38:06)*
- Gerb links anomalous-cognition/consciousness-interface themes to
  [[philip-j-corso|Corso]]'s claims (biologics as part of a craft's "guidance system," 1961
  brainwave-guidance project), [[jake-barber|Jake Barber]]'s more recent psionics claims, and
  [[randy-anderson|Randy Anderson]]'s account of a sphere reacting to human consciousness at
  [[naval-surface-warfare-center-crane|NSWC Crane]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:38:54–1:40:00, 1:53:10–1:53:46)*
