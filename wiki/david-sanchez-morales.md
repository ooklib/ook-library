David Sanchez Morales ("El Indio") is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as one of the CIA's most ruthless early assassination experts and the assassination trainer of [[operation-40]]. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 43:25-43:48)*

## Claims

- Born to a Mexican father and an indigenous mother; enlisted in the Army in 1946, served at Fort Bragg; joined the CIA in the early 1950s (host: it is unclear how an obscure Army corporal became an assassination expert). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 43:42-44:19)*
- Postings across Latin America with future PBSuccess/Bay of Pigs colleagues [[e-howard-hunt]] and David Atlee Phillips; two years at the Havana station with contacts in the anti-Castro underground and Batista officials. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 44:19-44:52)*
- Reunited with Hunt in Miami to train the Op 40 cadres; in a May 25, 1961 interview he described 39 selected Cubans trained as case officers. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 44:52-45:48)* See [[operation-40]].
- Quote attributed by the host to Thomas Clines (ASR "Klein"), a future JM/WAVE deputy: "if you saw Morales walking down the street of a Latin American capital you knew a coup was about to happen." *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 45:48-46:12)*
- Alleged: openly claimed a role in killing both Kennedys, saying in 1973 he "was in Dallas when we got the son of a [expletive] and I was in Los Angeles when we got the little bastard"; host suggests this candor may have led to his "heart attack" death in 1978 weeks before his House Select Committee on Assassinations testimony. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 46:12-46:45)* Unverified claim as reported by the host. See [[kennedy-assassination]], [[robert-f-kennedy]].
- Later worked under USAID cover training Hmong guerrillas in the secret war in Laos, then Phoenix in South Vietnam, both under former boss [[ted-shackley]]. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 46:51-47:15)*
