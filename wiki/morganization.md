The tactic of taking over underfinanced companies, streamlining management and merging them into one dominant player, which [[j-pierpont-morgan]] applied to the railroads and which came to be named after him.

## Claims

- MagnatesMedia says Morgan saw railroads (unlike steel under Carnegie or oil under Rockefeller) as too fragmented and price-cutting, and answered with consolidation and economies of scale so the merged firms could set prices. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 5:12, 5:26, 5:47)*
- He took over underfinanced railroads, merged them, crushed remaining competitors, and took an active board role, refusing help unless given full control; the narrator says this stabilised the industry and attracted European money. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 6:00, 6:19, 6:47, 6:51)*
- By the narrator's account Morgan came to own one third of American railroads when railroads were 60% of stock-market capitalisation. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 7:31)*
