The Hmong maquis leader in Laos in Operation X, rendered by the ASR as "Lea Tubi", "tuby", "tub Le Fong", "tub Leong" and "Tu Le Fong". The normalization to Touby Lyfoung is my inference; the transcript never spells the name. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 30:32-30:48)*

## Claims

- First Lao opium-monopoly representative of the colonial era, a prominent highland warlord, and the first Hmong to attend a French high school; French intelligence's man of choice. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 30:32-30:48)*
- Coordinated opium deliveries to French dirt airstrips (later called Lima sites when the Americans arrived); the dope was loaded onto returning DC-3s that brought arms and food. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 30:48-31:05)*
- Spring 1953: his maquis provided backup for French artillery at the Plain of Jars, enabling a French base with a steel-mat airstrip housing 12,000 troops. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 31:44-32:16)*
- Late April 1954: traded 3,000 of his best men to [[roger-trinquier]] for silver bars; they went to [[battle-of-dien-bien-phu]] under commanders including [[vang-pao]] ("one of tub Le Fong's top lieutenants"). *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 36:41-37:13)*
