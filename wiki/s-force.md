The "S force" is the label [[danny-sheehan]] gives to a roughly 15-man team of anti-Castro Cuban exiles and mafia gunmen, hand-picked by [[santo-trafficante]] in 1960 for a plot to kill Fidel Castro, Che Guevara and other Cuban leaders, and later allegedly reactivated to kill [[john-f-kennedy]]. The name and structure appear in this wiki only through Sheehan's account; treat it as unverified.

## Claims

- Structure: a "triangular" three-gunman crossfire team for a moving target, drawn from paramilitary bases on Noma Key, Swan Island, the Everglades and two sites on Lake Pontchartrain; recruits were flown to Fort Huachuca, disappeared from the records, then trained at Clint Murchison Jr.'s ranch in "Waka", Mexico (ASR; likely Oaxaca), under CIA's Carl Jenkins in a program codenamed ZR Rifle. *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 24:00)* *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 25:27)* *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 26:36)*
- Members named: [[david-sanchez-morales]] (called "the Mexican", the crack shot), Rip Robertson (field operations commander), Felipe Vidal Santiago. Sheehan says [[frank-sturgis]] and [[e-howard-hunt]] were CIA liaisons to the exile groups, not necessarily S force members. *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 5:15)* *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 1:22:27)*
- Funding: casino skim carried in Cadillacs to Miami and Mexico City. See [[sheehan-jfk-assassination-claims]]. *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 27:01)*
- Reactivation: Sheehan says the team was a "sleeper cell" that Dulles and the China-lobby group merely had to release. *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Danny Sheehan).md>) 1:06:53)*
