Rockefeller Brothers Inc. was one of the first post-war venture-capital firms, founded in 1946 by the Rockefeller brothers and led by [[laurance-rockefeller]]; it was reformed into Venrock in 1969. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 22:04-22:26)*

## Claims

- Host's own assertion after "quite a deep dive": RBI "appears" to have been an intelligence front or a way for the Rockefellers to liaise with the CIA; concrete information is "very difficult to find." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 22:26-22:50)*
- Primary investor in military technology, aerospace and aviation firms; initial partners were the five brothers and sister Abigail plus two silent partners: Harper Woodward (Pentagon procurement during the war; the host says an early director of [[civil-air-transport]], linking the [[china-lobby]] to Rockefeller interests) and Thaddeus Walkowicz (ASR "walowitz"; MIT aerospace engineer, 11 years in the Air Force). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 22:50-23:44)*
- Woodward and Walkowicz went on to found [[itek]] with RBI funding. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 23:44-23:53)*
- Not to be confused with the Rockefeller Brothers Fund or Rockefeller Foundation (host's warning). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 22:11-22:18)*
