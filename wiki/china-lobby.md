The China Lobby is the informal grouping of American (and Chinese) business, media and political
interests that [[eyes-wide-open]] presents as the first of the "elite circles" behind the
[[war-conspiracy]]. Per the host (citing James Bradley), it existed unofficially since at least the
mid-19th century and hardened into a political vehicle in the late 1930s-40s around the KMT.

## Claims

- Made up of influential American and Chinese business interests sympathetic to a pro-Western China;
  early incarnations sought a "westernized and christianized" China integrated into the
  Anglo-American sphere. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 5:22-6:11)*
- The host says it was "born out of the trans-Pacific opium trade": American merchants crossing
  the Pacific made fortunes in exploitative manufacturing/textiles or opium, e.g. Warren Delano,
  [[franklin-d-roosevelt]]'s maternal grandfather, who with other families made an ancestral
  fortune importing Chinese opium. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 6:11-7:15)*
- It crystallized as a rigid political vehicle in the late 1930s-early 40s, rallying around the
  "notoriously corrupt" Kuomintang under [[chiang-kai-shek]], who had ties to the Chinese opium
  industry and the Shanghai [[green-gang]] (used as enforcers in the 1927 "White Terror," 5,000 to
  10,000 union members massacred per the host). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 7:22-8:48)*
- Its Washington face was [[t-v-soong]]; [[henry-luce]] was its most effective American propagandist.
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 9:21-11:00, 12:35-13:17)*
- The host distinguishes a "China Yankees" side (investment interests) from the "China Cowboys"
  (the [[oss-detachment-202]]-style "China hands" of the OSS). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 7:34-7:41, 20:03-20:16)* (Terms as
  the host uses them, echoing the [[yankee-cowboy-thesis]] vocabulary; the host does not tie them
  to Oglesby here.)
- New post-war lobby "crystallized" through covert channels: [[thomas-corcoran]]'s China Defense
  Supplies shell company and the [[flying-tigers]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 14:47-14:54)*
- The 1949 "loss of China" produced the formal birth of the Cold War China Lobby; Truman and every
  president until at least the mid-1970s feared their own "loss of China" moment. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 31:07-31:39)*
- It recruited young politicians: [[richard-nixon]] (1950 California Senate race) and, through
  Corcoran, [[lyndon-b-johnson]]; the host says China Lobby hands "continue to pop up" throughout
  both men's careers. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 31:53-32:16, 33:03-34:13)*
- Favored policy: rollback (see [[nsc-68]]). The host says the CIA's [[civil-air-transport]]
  finally allowed the agency to push a rollback of communism in China via [[operation-paper]].
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 45:23-45:49)*
- Preview: "many of the China Lobby figures" reappear in the CIA's 1954 Guatemala operation
  ([[operation-pbsuccess]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 50:01-50:22)*
