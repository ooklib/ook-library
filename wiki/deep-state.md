The "deep state" (or "deep politics") is a theoretical framework, credited by Eyes Wide Open to
[[peter-dale-scott]], positing that behind the US's elected government stands a largely
unelected, unaccountable wing of government and allied institutions acting to guard elite/
corporate interests. Eyes Wide Open explicitly adopts and redefines the term as the organizing
lens for its whole "History of the CIA" series, distancing it from the term's more recent
"hijacking" by right-wing populist/Trump-era usage.

## Claims

- Eyes Wide Open's working definition: "any unelected officials that work to undermine the
  democratic will of the people of the country," primarily but not exclusively the United
  States. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 9:08-9:23)*
- The host explicitly distinguishes this usage from the codified "deep state" language used by
  the American right, especially since the rise of Trump/right-wing populism, which he says
  "muddies the water" for serious discussion of the concept; he notes the US is "not the only
  country in the world with a deep state" — he argues most democratic capitalist countries have
  one. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 7:51-8:23)*
- By this series' definition, the deep state includes: the Department of Defense (Pentagon,
  Joint Chiefs of Staff) and the broader "security state" (including the Department of Homeland
  Security); the [[federal-bureau-of-investigation|FBI]], framed as the domestic counterpart to
  the CIA; the defense/military-industrial-complex industries; industries with indirect military
  links such as oil/fossil fuels; think tanks and institutions such as NATO and the IMF;
  organized-crime elements such as the mafia; church institutions such as the Vatican (flagged
  as relevant to the later-covered Operation Gladio); and, at the network's alleged center, the
  [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 9:25-10:59)*
- The host caveats that these institutions are "not always working explicitly together" in an
  organized conspiracy, but argues they "undeniably all have shared interests" — capital, US
  empire, and anti-communism — that cause them to move "in unison" as a network he calls "the
  blob." *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 10:59-11:49)*
