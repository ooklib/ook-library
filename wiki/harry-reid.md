Former Senate Majority Leader Harry Reid, a Gang of Eight member, sponsored the DIA's
[[aawsap|Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP/OAP)]] and directed ~$21
million to the DIA and [[bigelow-aerospace-advanced-space-studies|Bigelow Aerospace]] for work
that included investigating Skinwalker Ranch.

## Claims

- [[david-grush|David Grush]], on the Judicial Watch interview, recounted meeting Reid roughly
  nine months before his death, briefing him on the legacy program topic and seeking his "thought
  leadership" as a former Gang of Eight member and Senate Majority Leader. Sitting in Reid's
  living room, Reid reportedly told him: "Yeah, I knew we had UFO material. I was denied access
  for decades." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:11:31–1:12:28)*
