Carl T. Compton was MIT president (1930-1948), per the host. In the Malmgren interview he is the man who allegedly offered a paint-restoration worker's teenage son a full scholarship, and a name that recurs in UFO-history claims.

- [[harald-malmgren]] says Compton came into a Newport/Boston mansion his father was restoring, talked with him about photons and offered to matriculate him at MIT on full scholarship; the host frames this as Malmgren being "identified" and tracked young. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 11:31-15:45, 19:03-19:56)*
- Host: Compton served on FDR's National Defense Research Committee, worked with [[vannevar-bush]], led the Rad Lab radar effort; his top technical aide was [[john-trump]]. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 20:42-21:20)*

Reinhardt (ASR), Eric Walker and John von Neumann; John Greenewald's FOIA to the FBI returned that records "were destroyed August 30th, 2006." *(All second-hand.)* *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft.
