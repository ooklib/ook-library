The practice, reported in the media, of Wall Street banks hiring children and relatives of Chinese officials to win business. Lei's Real Talk states that JPMorgan was fined hundreds of millions of dollars by the SEC for it, and that Citigroup, Goldman Sachs, Credit Suisse, Deutsche Bank, Morgan Stanley and UBS were also reported to have similar programs. *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 5:04,)*

- Lei alleges the program's true prototype was [[jeffrey-epstein]]'s October 2009 advice to [[jes-staley]] to build a China entity that gave officials' families titles, pay and jobs inside [[jpmorgan-chase]], with a laundering function for family wealth. This origin claim is Lei's inference, not established. *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 4:00,)*
