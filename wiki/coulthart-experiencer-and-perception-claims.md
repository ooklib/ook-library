[[ross-coulthart]] says he has moved from mocking experiencer accounts to taking them seriously as witness evidence, and describes several reports he has received. All second-hand and unverified.

## Claims

- He says [[eric-davis]] asserts he was briefed by [[george-h-w-bush]] about mantids, reptilians and Nordics (Coulthart notes this was before Congress, not necessarily under oath), while [[david-grush]] testified under oath. Species talk from program insiders: the Grays, short and tall, are "almost unanimous". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:09:52-1:10:24)*
- Five years ago he would have "laughed them out of town"; he told [[whitley-strieber]] the same. He wrestles with the experiencer phenomenon in the [[john-mack]] tradition. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:38:39-1:39:31)*
- For him the real mystery is consciousness rather than nuts-and-bolts craft; many people describe creative or scientific "downloads". After he casually said "the blue guys", hundreds of people wrote to him about benevolent blue humanoid beings met in liminal dream states. See [[consciousness-core-secret-claims]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:39:49-1:41:32)*
- One European contact photographed hieroglyphic images after such an encounter; a person Coulthart knows who is associated with the NSA reportedly said they looked like ancient Sumerian symbology. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:41:32-1:42:19)*
- He cites the "slide 9" briefing (see [[uap-task-force]]) as saying the phenomenon can manipulate human perception ("cognitive inputs"). *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:44:34-1:45:11)*
- An unnamed very senior Australian minister said he saw a giant elliptical metal disc over a Sydney square that his press secretary could not see: "I can't say for sure that it was there, but it wanted me to know that it was real." Coulthart thinks one phenomenon may present itself in different ways. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:45:16-1:49:17)*
- People telling him of mantid visits say the mantids tell them to engage with Coulthart; he says he sees "not a trace of guile". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:47:52-1:48:39)*
- He and Ramsey discuss whether benevolent feelings are induced (citing Dan Burisch's account, Jake Barber's love experience) and that a military or counterintelligence view naturally treats a perception-manipulating entity as a threat. See [[nhi-counterintelligence-and-polygraph-claims]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:49:17-1:51:16)*
- Cattle and human mutilations are mentioned as not "friendly", crediting Karyn Brazil (spelling unverified; "Karis Brazil") with describing killed and mutilated people. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:51:52-1:52:17)*
- He is speaking to a woman in her late 80s whose doctor found signs she had carried a pregnancy she does not remember, and who says benevolent beings showed her the plan for the fetus. He asks how to assess such witnesses, after five or six similar accounts giving them Mack-like credibility, while warning that many drawn to the subject are mentally ill. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:52:17-1:54:08)*
- Ramsey says witnesses often withhold "the craziest part"; Coulthart agrees to give safe space while cross-examining (see [[coulthart-critique-of-credulity-in-ufology]]). *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:54:08-1:55:45)*
- Answering audience questions he says he would ask an ET whether they created humans, whether consciousness survives death, and who the good and bad guys are. He also speculates about prior civilizations, the age of the Sphinx and Great Pyramid, interference in human evolution (citing Michael Denton) and mantids as possible insect descendants, and says he would choose to interview a reptilian for their sense of humour. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:36:43-2:41:53, 2:51:43-2:53:52)*
