Robert Maheu is the private-intelligence operative whom [[eyes-wide-open]] presents as the CIA's cutout to the mob in 1960 and, simultaneously, as [[howard-hughes]]'s political fixer. The ASR transcript renders the name "Mayhew" / "Mayu"; the normalization to Maheu is my inference from the description text, which spells it "Maheu" and from the previous episode's preview (see [[operation-40]]). The host frames him as an "emissary" bridging the legitimate "Overworld" and the underworld.

## Career (host, drawing on Jim Hougan's *Spooks*)

- Born in Waterville, Maine, 1917; joined the FBI in December 1940 (recruited while at Georgetown), worked in counterintelligence flipping foreign spies during WWII; sent post-war to a dead-end bureau assignment in Waterville and left the FBI in 1947 at age 30 despite a reportedly solid relationship with [[j-edgar-hoover]]. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 8:49-9:28)*
- Stated reason for leaving: to start a business with his wife, "Dairy Dream Farms"; then 1952-54 at the Small Defense Plants Administration ("some sort of military-industrial complex rubber stamp agency"); in 1954 he founded the private detective firm Robert A. Maheu and Associates. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 9:28-10:02)*
- **Sheep-dipping theory:** Hougan theorizes 1947-54 was a cover story ("sheep dipping") used by the CIA or FBI to hide continued involvement. Evidence cited: his SDPA file shows "10 years comp time" toward his pension by 1952 although his official FBI service was 1940-47 (6.5 years); the CIA put his firm on a monthly retainer as soon as it was founded. The host adds that Maheu, like William King Harvey, may have been a Hoover-loyal FBI mole implanted into the new CIA (created the same year, 1947); he says the CIA drew on three factions early on: ex-OSS, ex-FBI, ex-Army intelligence. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 10:02-12:54)* Labeled as the host's and Hougan's theory, not established fact.
- His firm became known as the "Mission Impossible agency", taking domestic tasks too risky for CIA staff (the CIA is "technically not allowed to operate on US soil"); the host says it was said to have inspired *Mission Impossible* creator Bruce Geller. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 12:54-13:25)*

## Jeddah Agreements job (1954) and Nixon

- Per Hougan, [[richard-nixon]] worked with the CIA in early 1954 to bring in Maheu's firm to sink [[aristotle-onassis]]'s Saudi tanker deal; Maheu's firm ran illegal wiretaps and a smear campaign; Maheu himself claimed the deal was foiled by evidence of a bribe to the Saudi royal family that he convinced the CIA was a national-security issue. See [[jeddah-agreements]]. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 17:32-18:29, 21:06-21:23)*
- The host thinks Nixon and Maheu likely stayed in touch 1954-60 and that Nixon may have recommended Maheu to Hughes (host: "hard to say"). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 22:11-22:56)*

## Hughes fixer

- Hired by [[howard-hughes]] in 1955 or 56; for over 15 years acted as his political fixer, gopher and "eyes and ears"; Maheu claimed he never met Hughes in person, only by telephone. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 22:49-23:37)*
- Per a Las Vegas Sun article the host cites, a Watergate investigator claimed Maheu paid out the $50,000 cash portion of a $100,000 Hughes bribe to Nixon through [[charles-rebozo]] ("babe" Rebozo per ASR). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 27:03-27:35)*
- The host speculates Maheu "may have created the bridge" between the underworld and Hughes, given he had worked with [[johnny-roselli]] in the early 1960s. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 33:01-33:15)*

## The CIA-Mafia approach

- September 1960, Brown Derby restaurant, Hollywood: Maheu, on behalf of the CIA, proposed to Roselli an alliance with the mob in Miami, Louisiana and Chicago to assassinate [[fidel-castro]]. Roselli then brought him to Miami's Kenilworth ("Kennelworth" per ASR) Hotel to meet "Sam Gold" ([[sam-giancana]]) and "Joe" ([[santo-trafficante]]). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 0:07-1:44)* See [[cia-mafia-assassination-plots]].
- Contact was routed through ex-FBI colleague James "Big Jim" O'Connell of the CIA Office of Security, who told Maheu to pose as the front for private businessmen putting up a $150,000 reward. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:02:31-1:03:17)* See [[sheffield-edwards]].
- Maheu delivered six poison tablets to Roselli for the first plot. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:11:31-1:11:48)*
- Per the 1981 Chicago Tribune article the host cites, Maheu and his employee Joseph Shimon knew about the conception of the plots but nothing of their execution beyond what Roselli reported. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:18:39-1:18:53)*
