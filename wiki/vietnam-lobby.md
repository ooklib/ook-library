"Vietnam Lobby" is the colloquial term (from Joseph Morgan's book *The Vietnam Lobby: The American Friends of Vietnam, 1955-1975*, in the bibliography) for the men Diem was introduced to on his 1951-54 US tour, "intent on seeing further US involvement in Vietnam". [[eyes-wide-open]] shows a full list on screen and says it later grew into "an all-encompassing blob across the US deep state", promising more later. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 12:11-12:47)*

## Claims

- Early names given in the narrative: Cardinal [[francis-spellman]] (Archbishop of New York), Washington Catholics [[joseph-kennedy]] and Congressman John F. Kennedy, and, in spring 1951, [[william-donovan]], described as an early Vietnam lobbyist and then Ambassador to Thailand. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 10:43-11:40, 11:47-12:06, 27:36-27:48)*
- The host speculates that as a fellow Catholic Donovan could have recommended Diem to [[allen-dulles]] in 1954, and notes the irony that the Kennedy administration would see Diem's overthrow in 1963. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 11:28-11:35, 12:00-12:11)*
- A related claim: State Department official John W. Haynes said support for Diem was "rammed through single-handedly through our intelligence and military communities", and CIA officer Richard Bissell reportedly noted deep CIA involvement in Diem's appointment. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 27:10-27:36)*
