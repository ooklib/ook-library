Former SAIC administrative employee Denise McKenzie is a legendary figure in the "UFO Legacy
Program financial analyst community" for her 2001 testimony about fund obfuscation at
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]]. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
54:38–54:55)*

## Claims

- Introduced to SAIC in 1992 while working retail; recruited by a co-worker, "Sophia," to
  SAIC's La Jolla, San Diego campus. Given the job immediately, with no clear department
  assignment. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 55:56–56:34)*
- Worked in SAIC's military contract division; noticed a highly classified biological division
  with strict access control on the same campus. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  56:50–57:06)*
- Found that numerous large SAIC/DoD contract files (worth millions) contained only generic
  "it's being worked on" form letters, some signed by senior SAIC personnel, with zero actual
  activity. Reported this to her recruiter Sophia, who grew furious and had McKenzie hand over
  all files, then told her to "forget all about the matter."
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:00:38–1:02:12)*
- Was harassed by supervisor Stuart Stanley; left after he failed to help her when she choked on
  food. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:02:12–1:02:29)*
- Later found "Sophia" listed on a CIA website of deceased agents, under a different first name
  but the same last name — Sophia had supposedly died in 1987/88, years before McKenzie's 1992
  employment interaction with her. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:02:38–1:03:06)*
- Not the only 1992 SAIC employee to allege fund "mischarging" — a 2007 *Vanity Fair* article
  quotes a 1992 sworn deposition describing mischarging as "institutionalized" at SAIC.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:03:58–1:04:19)*
- Testimony featured in [[steven-greer|Steven Greer]]'s 2001 disclosure briefing document; Gerb
  located her March 2001 videotaped interview and has attempted outreach to her.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 54:46–55:56)*
