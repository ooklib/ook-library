Eugène Deloncle was a former French naval engineer who, per Eyes Wide Open, founded [[la-cagoule]] in 1936. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 3:11)*

## Claims

- Founded La Cagoule in 1936; after the 1940 defeat moved to Vichy France and worked with Pétain's regime, then returned to Paris to expand his Mouvement Social Révolutionnaire (MSR), which sought a unified fascist France and had a tenuous relationship with the Nazis. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 3:11-3:23, 6:26-7:37)*
- Killed in a shootout with Gestapo agents in his Paris apartment in January 1944; the MSR continued without him until the Allied arrival. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 7:37-7:49)*

## Relationships
