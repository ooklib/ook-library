Term for non-human bodies recovered from crashes. In the [[corso-affidavit-1998]] the Walter Reed autopsy report calls the creature an "extraterrestrial biological entity"; Gerb notes the same phrase appears in the [[majestic-12]] special operations manual, which describes EBE-1 and EBE-2, with EBE-1 closely matching Corso's description. Corso says the name was coined by the OCRD group and "we never used the letters" as such. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 15:04-15:13, 18:52-19:18, 52:18-52:44)*

## Corso's interpretation

- The beings were "clones", made by "some other intellect" for space travel, because human muscles and brains cannot travel in space; the Roswell craft's failures with fine guidance mean the being was part of the guidance system, which inspired brain-wave control research ([[corso-technology-seeding-claims]]). *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 52:18-54:11)*
- Gerb links this to modern claims of consciousness-linked craft (a senior scientist's account of a mind-interface) and to [[leonard-stringfield]]'s report from a Bell Labs source of headband transceivers worn by recovered aliens in the early 1950s. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 53:38-56:10)*
- Corso lists hostile acts attributed to EBEs: abductions, mutilations, spying on atomic installations, interfering with missile and space tests, crashing aircraft, tampering with human and animal genomes; the threat was treated like the Soviets, a "fig leaf policy". Gerb contrasts this with [[david-grush]]'s remark that people were hurt but without attributable malicious intent. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 38:51-39:51)*
- Corso: "extraterrestrials never gave humans a thing"; Gerb asks what agreements with NHI (per Grusch) could then involve. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 35:15-36:20)*
