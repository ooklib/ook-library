General Phao Sriyanon (ASR: "Fou", "Fa", "faou cenon") was, per Eyes Wide Open, the corrupt deputy
then director of Thailand's National Police, son-in-law of coup leader Phin Choonhavan, and the
main recipient of US aid under [[operation-paper]]; rival of Marshal [[sarit-thanarat]].

## Claims

- Built a US-equipped Thai Border Police and aerial recon unit that eclipsed the regular army;
  Prime Minister [[plaek-phibunsongkhram]] reportedly feared the US was positioning him to overthrow
  him. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 27:37-27:44, 35:02-35:43)*
- Controlled the Golden Triangle-to-Bangkok dope flow under cover of anti-narcotics work; delivered
  weapons to the border and took opium back; in 1955 Phibun reprimanded him for awarding himself
  $1 million for seizing 20 tons of opium, which he said was KMT-owned Burmese opium. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 35:50-36:32, 41:16-41:53)*

- LAOS 4: host (ASR "General Po"): commanded the Thai Border Patrol Police, deeply involved in the opium trade, funded via SEA Supply for the CIA-trained [[paru]]; the identification with Phao is my inference from context. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 8:43-8:54, 55:40-56:16, 1:01:44-1:02:05)*
