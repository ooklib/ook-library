According to [[most-brutal-businessman-vanderbilt-documentary-2022]], [[cornelius-vanderbilt]] ran a Nicaragua passenger route for the gold rush and, while on a European vacation in 1853, left it to partners "Morgan and Garrison" who made a deal with William Walker, who had seized power in Nicaragua, transferring his exclusive rights to themselves. *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 6:38, 6:55, 7:03)*

## Claims

- Vanderbilt's letter: "Gentlemen: you have undertaken to cheat me. I won't sue you for the law is too slow. I'll ruin you." *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 7:17)*
- His response: a rival transit company at low prices, a refusal to carry passengers to or from Nicaragua (a blockade), lobbying neighbouring countries not to recognise Walker, and, in the narrator's words, helping "start a war to completely overthrow the new government." *([mm/vanderbilt](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2022-05-06 - The Most BRUTAL Businessman In History.md>) 7:33, 7:40, 7:45, 7:48)*

## Relationships

- Involves [[cornelius-vanderbilt]]. The partner "Morgan" is not identified with the [[house-of-morgan]] by the source.
