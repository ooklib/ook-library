American oil magnate (1839-1936), presented by MagnatesMedia as the world's first billionaire and the founder of the dynasty later described in [[rockefeller-family]]. The documentary treats him as a man with two sides, a ruthless monopolist and a systematic philanthropist, quoting his biographer that "his good side was every bit as good as his bad side was bad". All claims below are the narrator's account, not verified fact.

## Early life

- Born 1839 into a poor farming family; his father "Devil Bill" was a con-artist selling fake herbal remedies as "Dr. William Levingston", kept a second wife in another state, and was accused (unproven) of sexual assault when John was 10. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 1:13, 2:13, 3:03, 3:25)*
- Left school at 16 and, after six weeks of rejections, got a bookkeeping job in Cleveland on 26 September 1855, which he celebrated as "Job Day"; he repeated "I am bound to be rich!" each morning and gave about 10% of his pay to charity from the start. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 4:17, 4:50, 5:23, 5:59)*
- With chemist Maurice Clark he founded a produce firm (Clark and Rockefeller), inflating their announced investment to win bank loans; he paid $300 for a substitute rather than serve in the Civil War. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 6:46, 7:38, 8:06)*

## Oil empire

- Backed Samuel Andrews's Cleveland kerosene refinery with a $4,000 loan, bought out Clark for $72,500 in 1865 and renamed the firm [[standard-oil]] with partner [[henry-flagler]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 9:17, 11:25, 12:34)*
- Used secret railroad rebates, price wars and the Cleveland Massacre to reach roughly 90% of US refining; see [[standard-oil]] and [[south-improvement-company]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 15:55, 21:25, 28:38)*
- After the 1893 depression he hired Baptist minister [[frederick-gates]] to fix his poor personal investments and had his son [[john-d-rockefeller-jr]] trained by him. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 34:07, 34:35, 35:00)*
- Bought the Mesabi iron range, forcing a truce with [[andrew-carnegie]] and later a $88.5 million stock sale to [[j-pierpont-morgan]]: see [[mesabi-iron-range-deal]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 35:24, 39:14)*
- Retired without a public announcement, still owning about 30% of Standard Oil; [[ida-tarbell]] and [[theodore-roosevelt]] then made him a national villain, and the company was dissolved in 1911: see [[standard-oil-dissolution-1911]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 39:59, 40:26, 43:48)*

## Philanthropy and legacy

- Called himself "a soldier of God"; founded the [[rockefeller-foundation]] in 1913, giving about $540 million personally. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 46:50, 47:16, 47:30)*
- Died at 97; his son said "There was only ever one John D. Rockefeller." *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 49:12)*
