The North American Aerospace Defense Command, headquartered at a Space Force base in Colorado per [[uap-gerb]], and overseen with US Northern Command (USNORTHCOM) in his account. In [[gerb-fastwalkers-video]] he casts it as the origin of the [[fastwalker-designation]] and a long-time UFO tracker that has refused to release data.

## Claims

- 2013 FOIA: NORTHCOM and NORAD told [[black-vault]] that fastwalker documentation is currently and properly classified under Executive Order 13526, and released only two papers. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 2:24-3:25)*
- 1979 FOIA: NORAD estimated over 18,000 man-hours and $155,000 to satisfy a UAP tracking data request and later refused a fee waiver. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 3:48-4:22)*
- Hynek: [[j-allen-hynek]] allegedly said in January 1967 that NORAD has tracked UFOs since 1957 and that every continental US military Blue Book case is designated a NORAD case. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 7:28-7:53)*
- With the Air Force Foreign Technology Division it studied fastwalkers seen by [[defense-support-program]] sensors. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 4:43-5:08)*
