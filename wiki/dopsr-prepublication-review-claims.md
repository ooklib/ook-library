The Defense Office of Prepublication and Security Review (DOPSR, spelled "Doppser" in the ASR transcript) is the Defense Department system that clears former personnel's writings for publication. In [[sentinels-of-ether-michels-episode-2026]] [[jesse-michels]] alleges that [[jake-barber]] turned it into a "Trojan horse": he had a ghost writer craft the manuscript, sliced it into 20-page chunks and watched which lines the Pentagon blacked out. This is Michels' account of Barber's strategy, and the redactions are not independently documented.

## Claims

- The system is "designed to silence him. For most people, it's a muzzle." *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 16:10-16:25)*
- The first round took six months and returned seven pages of redactions, escalated to subject matter experts in classified special access programs ([[special-access-program]]). *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 8:29-8:53, 16:32-16:38)*
- Michels: "Jake used the redaction process backwards as a discovery tool", submitting material he suspected was classified and drip-feeding chapters to see "which lines made the Pentagon flinch". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 16:38-17:06)*
- By this reading redactions authenticate the content: "the redactions only serve to confirm it", and "the Pentagon's own bureaucracy becomes the delivery mechanism". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 16:32-17:13, 1:33:01-1:33:16)*
- Michels speculates that if only two chapters triggered "a firestorm of black ink", the rest of the manuscript may hide more. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:33:01-1:33:16)*
- Counterpoint implicit in Michels' own remark: fiction can be "self-stigmatizing", so classified truths may not need classification to stay hidden. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 8:53-9:08)*
