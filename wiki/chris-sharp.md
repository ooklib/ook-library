Chris Sharp, a journalist with the Liberation Times, is cited by Gerb for his article "UFO
allegedly stored at East Coast Naval Facility as material transfer claims resurface," which
reported sources describing a vehicle or vehicles of unknown origin stored at
[[naval-air-station-patuxent-river|Pax River]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:18:02–1:18:21)*

## Claims

- His 20 September 2024 Liberation Times article ("Paradigm Changing UFO Transparency
  Legislation Fails in Congress for Second Consecutive Year") reported that multiple crash-
  retrieval programs are orchestrated by the CIA DS&T (and its Directorate of Operations/maritime
  branch), [[national-underwater-reconnaissance-office|NURO]], the Navy,
  [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]], Space Command, NGA, and JSOC; foreign-soil retrievals
  involve [[cia-office-of-global-access|CIA OGA]] and JSOC. Gerb calls it "a cornerstone" of his
  own retrieval-team research, while arguing Sharp misses the NGA/NRO/160th SOAR logistics
  pieces. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:05:25–1:07:20, 1:10:39–1:11:06)*

- Began reporting on two separate instances in which [[bigelow-aerospace-advanced-space-studies|
  BASS]] attempted to transfer UFO craft materials out of [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]] to
  the DIA's OAP program (2008–2011); reported the second attempt involved creating the
  [[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]] PAP, thwarted by CIA DS&T deputy director
  [[glenn-gaffney|Glenn Gaffney]]. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 1:57:56–1:58:15,
  2:56:53–2:57:18)*
