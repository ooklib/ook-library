[[grant-cameron]] says a woman sent him photographs of about 100 of roughly 250 pages of a study prepared for the Johnson White House at the urging of [[james-e-mcdonald]], whose conclusion he paraphrases as UFOs being real metal objects. Cameron did not obtain the rest, and the author (recalled as "Rand") is unverified.

## Claims

- The study's panel supposedly included the head of the weird desk, the head of Skunk Works, a two-star general from Wright-Patterson and the head of the NRO, which Cameron says mirrors To The Stars Academy's list. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 47:29-48:03)*
- Cameron says Johnson told the researcher to go to hell and that McDonald's supersonic-transport opposition created leverage. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 46:48-47:13)*
- McDonald mentioned working on a presidential study while interviewing witnesses at the Australian schoolyard landing case. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 47:13-47:25)*
