Randall G. Walden, senior executive service member and former director/program executive officer
of the Air Force Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO), is a figure Gerb previously named in his
(as-yet unformally-cited) Air Force legacy-programs project and revisits here. *([northrop-trw](
../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 57:03–57:23)*

## Claims

- CV includes a classified flight-test-squadron command out of Nellis AFB, Nevada (1997–1998),
  technical director for the Air Force Acquisition Directorate of Special Programs (SAF/AQL), and
  director of Air Force Test and Evaluation (AFTTE) — a component of Gerb's alleged
  [[hidden-wing-air-force-program|"hidden wing"]] test-and-evaluation portfolio.
  *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 57:23–57:59)*
- Elected to the [[aerospace-corporation|Aerospace Corporation]]'s Board of Trustees on 7 June
  2023. Gerb frames this as a pattern he sees often: senior DoD/IC legacy-program figures "trade
  in their stars and stripes for a pinstriped suit" on the corporate side, this time at an FFRDC.
  *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 57:59–58:34)*
- From 2019 to 2021, [[vince-divine|Vince Divine]] served under Walden as deputy director of
  security for the Air Force RCO. *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md)
  2:27:57–2:28:14)*
- Gerb states Walden and [[edward-c-aldridge|Edward C. Aldridge]] both served "very senior UFO
  legacy program roles within an Air Force capacity" and both went on to executive roles at the
  Aerospace Corporation, calling this pattern notable. *([northrop-trw](../raw/
  0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 1:10:45–1:10:54)*

## Claims (from the Hidden Wing video — primary career dossier)

- Commissioned in the Air Force in 1982, Walden "served as a flight test engineer within Air
  Force Material Command at [[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards Air Force Base]], California,
  [[naval-air-station-patuxent-river|Pax River]] Naval Air Station, Maryland, and Nellis Air Force
  Base, Nevada," participating in development, ground, and flight testing of numerous aerospace
  weapons systems. From 1997 to 1998, he commanded a classified flight-test squadron out of
  Nellis AFB. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 55:45–56:42)*
- Served multiple roles within the Air Force Directorate of Special Programs
  ([[saf-aql|SAF/AQL]]), including deputy chief of the advanced technology division, chief of the
  special studies division, and technical director, before becoming director of
  [[aftte|Air Force Test and Evaluation (AFTTE)]] immediately prior to his RCO directorship.
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 56:42–57:08)*

  capabilities-office|RCO]] facilitates "over 30 programs" equally or more secret than the B-21

  Lockheed Skunk Works model (Kelly Johnson's rules) and the early NRO's streamlined
  requirements/acquisition process, and publicly thanked
  [[air-force-office-of-special-investigations|AFOSI]] for safeguarding RCO's most secretive
  programs. Gerb calls Walden's speech answers dry, even "something fed back from ChatGPT," but
  recommends it. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 57:16–58:53)*
- Gerb notes that Walden and [[edward-c-aldridge|Edward C. Aldridge]] both held "very senior UFO
  legacy program roles within an Air Force capacity" before moving to executive positions at the
  [[aerospace-corporation|Aerospace Corporation]] — a pattern Gerb calls notable.
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 3:23:25–3:23:41)*. Cross-referenced in
