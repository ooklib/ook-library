[[david-icke]]'s claim, in [[icke-ramsey-interview-2026]], that a US military study of [[robert-monroe]]'s Gateway Process found astral travellers meeting many reptilian entities that they collectively called "alligators", which he took as confirmation that reptilians originate in the astral. Icke does not name the study, and the claim is his summary without a document.

## Claims

- Icke says Monroe (of the Monroe Institute) developed a way to project consciousness into the astral, and that the US military studied the Gateway Process; the astral travellers in the study "came across so many reptilian entities" that they gave them the collective name "alligators". He calls this a "massive confirmation" of his conclusion. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 23:43-24:57)*
- Ramsey ties Monroe's "loosh" idea to the entities keeping humans in a low vibration. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 13:19-13:41)*
- Icke says Monroe projecting into another reality is the "reverse" of what reptilian entities do when moving between dimensions. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 53:31-53:55)*
