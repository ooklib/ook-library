Wright-Patterson Air Force Base in Dayton, Ohio, was headquarters of Air Materiel Command (AMC)
and Air Research and Development Command (ARDC) — forerunners of the modern
[[air-force-material-command|Air Force Materiel Command]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:01:26–1:01:50)*

## Claims

- Alleged destination of the [[roswell-incident-1947|Roswell]] crash wreckage, evaluated here
  after 10 years, allegedly leading directly to [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]'s 1958 founding.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 12:07–12:24, 1:54:37–1:54:52)*
- Chain of custody for the alleged MITRE reverse-engineering program is said to run from ARDC
  (out of Wright-Patterson) to the US Navy, likely ONI and NAVAIR, and then MITRE upon its
  founding. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:55:11–1:55:24)*
