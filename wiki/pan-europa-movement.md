The Pan-Europa Movement, as used by Eyes Wide Open, refers to the post-war push for a federated Europe to counter the Soviet bloc, which the host says was perpetuated by the British and American power elite even before WWII and which the CIA promoted through [[american-committee-on-united-europe]] front groups. Note: the transcript never names Richard Coudenhove-Kalergi or the Paneuropa Union; the wiki records only what the video says. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 22:07-22:34)*

## Claims

- Second step of a two-tier Anglo-American plan (first step: Franco-German reconciliation and economic rebuilding via the [[marshall-plan]]): create a 'pan-Europa' to counter the Warsaw Pact. The idea was unpopular in war-torn Europe, especially regarding Germany and Italy, so the CIA 'stepped in'. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 20:30-22:58)*
- Eyes Wide Open argues today's European Union in many ways achieved this goal, and that integration let the US form consensus among allies on foreign policy and the need for a Gladio stay-behind. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 22:14-22:34, 28:36-29:15)*
