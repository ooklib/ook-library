# Malmstrom Minuteman Missile Shutdown (1967)

Alleged 24 March 1967 event at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] in which a hovering object coincided with ten Minuteman ICBMs going into fault status. Central case in the [[ufo-nuclear-connection]] video by [[uap-gerb]].

## Claims

- Gerb says [[david-grush]] directly confirmed the case in an interview with [[ross-coulthart]] (NewsNation) and in his 2023 Newsweek article. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 8:17-8:25)*
- [[robert-salas]], first lieutenant and on-duty commander of an underground launch control facility (490th Missile Squadron), was told by the flight security controller of erratic lights, then of a pulsating red oval object, 30-40 ft across, hovering over the front gate with guards' weapons drawn. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 8:25-9:41)*
- Alarms sounded and all ten missiles showed red fault status with guidance and control failure; the object then left. The missiles stayed inoperable that night with no permanent damage. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 9:41-10:20)*
- Squadron commander Col. George Eldridge allegedly said it was not an exercise and could not be explained; an Air Force security/intelligence officer told the crew it was classified secret. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 10:20-10:42)*
- Salas swore an affidavit in 2010, and three other men swore affidavits: Robert C. Jamison (ASR "Jameson"), Patrick McDonough, and Dwynn Arneson (ASR "dwin C arneson"). Gerb gives them as USAF first lieutenant, Airman First Class, and officer in charge of communications respectively, but the transcript ordering is ambiguous. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 10:42-11:08)*
