Whitney Webb is an investigative journalist and author of *One Nation Under Blackmail*, described by oompaville as "very heavily sourced and very heavily contested". Only her role in [[maxwells-data-dynasty-video-2026]] is recorded here.

## Claims (as relayed by oompaville)

- She argues the Maxwell family deliberately controlled the world's sensitive information for intelligence services, on the same public evidence (company filings, FBI contract filings) but with intent inferred; the host says she is open about the inference. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 19:34, 19:49, 20:03)*
- Her core thesis: [[ghislaine-maxwell]] watched her father turn information into money and power, and after his collapse rebuilt the family trade with [[jeffrey-epstein]] using leverage (black book, hidden cameras, flight logs); the sexual abuse being "the bottom rung" and a means to gather leverage on those who already hold leverage over the masses. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 21:37, 22:13, 22:21, 22:42)*
- She claims the Maxwell-to-Epstein handoff was an intelligence operation running since the 1980s, resting on [[ari-ben-menashe]]. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 23:04, 23:14)*
