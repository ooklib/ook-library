The National Program Special Management Staff (NPMS), sitting under the
[[under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-and-security|USD I&S]]'s dual-hatted "director of
defense intelligence" role (specifically under "sensitive activities and special programs,"
SASP), runs coordination between National Security Council special access programs (SAPs) and
the US Department of Defense/intelligence community. Gerb calls the director of NPMS "one of the
most critical jobs in the entirety of the United States that ... most people have actually never
even heard of" — "usually the most cleared person in the entire DoD besides the Secretary of
Defense," and even more cleared than the director of the DoD Special Access Program Central
Office (SAPCO). *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:06:47–1:08:27)*

## Claims

- Gerb theorizes that UFO legacy programs run out of the National Security Council heavily
  feature involvement from USD I&S, and that the director of NPMS is one of the most integral
  "above board" roles in the entirety of NSC-led UFO programs. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:08:40–1:08:56)*
- Gerb notes the echo between NPMS's "sensitive activities and special programs" (SASP)
  categorization and the naming of [[saf-aa|SAF/AAH (sensitive activities)]] and
  [[saf-aql|SAF/AQL (special programs)]] as suggestive of a shared conceptual framework across
  Air Force and OSD-level program structures. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  1:08:18–1:08:39)*

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2)

- [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]] ("Lou Alzando") served as NPMS director from 2013 to 2017,
  under [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-and-security|USD I&S]] — the same office
  [[james-clapper|James Clapper]] held from 2007 to 2010 before becoming DNI. Gerb reiterates
  that this makes Elizondo, during those years, "usually the most cleared person in the entire
  DoD besides the Secretary of Defense," directly undercutting Elizondo's public framing of
  himself as merely a mid-level [[advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program|ATIP]]
  administrator. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:02:35–1:03:41,
  2:25:19–2:25:44)*
- Gerb argues this NPMS role — coordinating between NSC special access programs and the DoD/IC —
  is the real explanation for Elizondo's "far more intimate history with national programs, USD
  INS, and the National Security Council than he has ever led on." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  35:40–35:57)*
