George de Mohrenschildt (ASR "George deor Shield" and variants; description spells "George DeMohrenschildt") is the Dallas Russian-emigre geologist whom [[eyes-wide-open]] presents as a hinge between the Bush family, the Cuban oil ventures of the Texas cowboys, and [[lee-harvey-oswald]].

## Claims

- 1952: joined the "Texas Russians," the wealthy anti-communist White Russian exile oil community in Dallas (executives, geologists, engineers, surveyors); the host says Neil Mallon's [[dresser-industries]] job and Dallas Council on World Affairs likely connected Bush to that milieu. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 16:59-17:47)*
- Aristocratic German-Russian family with intelligence ties back to WWI: his uncle Ferdinand was one of Tsar Nicholas's ambassadors to Washington urging the Wilson administration to join the war; Russ Baker notes Wilson's secretary of state Robert Lansing was the uncle by marriage of [[allen-dulles]] and [[john-foster-dulles]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 17:47-18:35)*
- Moved to Dallas in 1952 where he befriended young [[george-h-w-bush]]; Bush already knew the family through George's much older brother [[dimitri-von-mohrenschildt]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 18:35-18:51)*
- Host: it is likely through Dimitri that the CIA was introduced to George. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 18:51-19:00)*
- Arrived in the US in 1938 with a doctoral dissertation titled "The economic influence of the United States on Latin America," which the host says foreshadowed his role as an off-the-book CIA asset posing as an oil executive in Latin America. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 21:23-21:56)*
- A geologist first hired in the 1940s by "Pantek" oil company (ASR; founded in the 1920s by William F. Buckley Sr., father of [[william-f-buckley-jr]]); left in 1950 to co-found the [[cuban-venezuelan-oil-voting-trust]] with Pantek president Warren Smith. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 21:48-22:05, 23:11-23:32)*
- Host: the friendship with Oswald came to national attention "a little over a decade later." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 17:38-17:56)*
