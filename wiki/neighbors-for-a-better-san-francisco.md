Neighbors for a Better San Francisco is a political-spending group described by More Perfect
Union as, at one point, the biggest single spender in local San Francisco politics. The video
presents it as informally linked to the [[bohemian-grove]] club through overlapping donors and
founders.

## Claims

- Described as having been the biggest big-spender in local San Francisco politics at one point,
  active in city council and mayoral races and on ballot-measure campaigns
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 11:53, 12:02)*.
- Named donors/figures cross-checked against the Grove list by Boguslaw and Sean Morrow: Nick
  Podell, William Oberndorf, and Stephen Merrill, all confirmed present on the Grove list; John
  Atwater, founder and former chairman of Prime Finance/Prime Realty, is named as one of the
  group's big backers
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 12:09, 12:17, 12:24)*.
- Called "basically just an offshoot of the Bohemian Grove" on camera by one of the reporters,
  based on this donor overlap
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 12:17)*.
- Most recently focused spending on fighting a San Francisco ballot measure that would have
  marginally increased property taxes on the very wealthy to fund affordable housing. Top donor
  William Oberndorf is noted as having used his charitable foundation to give $2,000 to the
  Make-A-Wish Foundation and $200,000 to Neighbors
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 12:27, 12:35, 12:46)*.
- Described (like the Heritage Foundation) as one node of a wider network of linked organizations
  sharing a small pool of donors and board members across San Francisco
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 12:46, 12:59)*.
- A tenants union is reported to have organized specifically to fight John Atwater's Prime Realty,
  winning victories across every building Prime owns
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 16:35, 16:45)*.

## Relationships

- Overlapping membership with [[bohemian-grove]] (alleged donor/founder overlap; called an
  "offshoot" by More Perfect Union).

## Speculation flag

The "offshoot" characterization is a rhetorical claim made on camera by a More Perfect Union
reporter based on a small number of shared names between two lists, not a documented formal or
financial relationship between the two organizations; recorded here as an attributed editorial
claim.
