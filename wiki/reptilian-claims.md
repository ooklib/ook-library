A conspiracy theory proposed by [[david-icke]] of shape-shifting reptilian aliens taking control of Earth by assuming human form and political power, summarized by [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 25:04-25:24)*

## Claims

- Introduced in Icke's 1999 book *The Biggest Secret* (ASR "the big secret"): tall, blood-drinking reptilian humanoids from the Alpha Draconis system living in underground bases and in plain sight; the Rothschilds, the Bushes and the British royal family are named as reptiles. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 25:24-25:57)*
- It has become one of the most popular conspiracy theories, with internet users hunting for lizard-eye glitches in videos of leaders. Gerb says [[denver-international-airport]] has leaned into it with reptile posters. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 26:06-26:32)*

## Claims (a52/icke)

- In [[icke-ramsey-interview-2026]] [[david-icke]] recasts the theory: reptilians and other entities are mostly astral beings that operate through a "global cult" and human-non-human hybrids, not only physical creatures; shape-shifting is the observer decoding a different information field. He also says the reptilian brain (R-complex) carries fight-or-flight, fear and worship, and that not all reptilians are evil. See [[astral-control-system-and-archon-claims-icke]] and [[icke-shape-shifting-and-royal-family-claims]]. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 23:18-23:34, 35:14-36:01, 41:06-41:38, 55:21-55:28)*
- He says the trigger for accepting the idea was 12 people telling him of human-to-reptile shifts in 15 days in 1996, and [[credo-mutwa]]'s confirmation from Zulu legend. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 17:04-17:37, 15:32-16:18)*
- He says the astral travellers of a US military Gateway Process study called the reptilian entities they met "alligators" ([[monroe-gateway-process-alligators-claim]]), and that the royal families are hybrids. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 24:15-24:57, 2:05:33-2:06:37)*
- [[chris-ramsey]] raises the [[lerta-files]] as a purported reptilian interview corroborating his account. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 54:03-54:31)*
