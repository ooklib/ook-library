The SDECE was France's post-war external intelligence agency, created (per Eyes Wide Open) around 1946-47 to replace the earlier Direction Générale, and 'filled with hardline cold warriors'. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 15:15-15:42)*

## Claims

- Alongside the CIA and British SAS collaborators, allegedly organized [[plan-bleu-1947]]; its director headed the official investigation into the plot. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 15:42-16:34)*
- After Plan Bleu was foiled, French intelligence and the CIA organized a new stay-behind, [[rose-des-vents]]. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 39:48-40:07)*

- LAOS 2: the host says Saigon's Corsican underground were "valuable allies to French intelligence, the SDECE" (ASR "sdce"), and describes [[operation-x]] as a French intelligence program run through the [[mixed-airborne-commando-group]]; profits were returned to French intelligence at war's end per [[roger-trinquier]]'s letter. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 3:39-3:56, 17:04-17:18, 44:25-44:41)*

## Relationships

- Organized [[plan-bleu-1947]] and [[rose-des-vents]] with the [[central-intelligence-agency]].
- LAOS 2: French intelligence behind [[operation-x]] ([[mixed-airborne-commando-group]]).

## Additional (ewa/dallas-5)

- [[philippe-thyraud-de-vosjoli]] is described as SDECE's (ASR "STEK") chief liaison for the western hemisphere, present at the [[havana-hilton]] on 1 Jan. 1959, later a whistleblower on KGB moles and per Peter Dale Scott a double agent for the CIA and the OAS. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 41:15-43:38)*
