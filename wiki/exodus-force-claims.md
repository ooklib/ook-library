The "Exodus force" is [[charles-buhler]]'s and [[andrew-arjima]]'s name for a claimed propellantless thrust seen when high voltage is applied to asymmetric electrode/dielectric devices in a Faraday cage, in air and in high vacuum, measured on scales, pendulums and spinners. [[jesse-michels]] presents it as a validated, better-instrumented version of the [[biefeld-brown-effect]] of [[thomas-townsend-brown]]. All results are self-reported and unreplicated by independent labs; Buhler admits peer review may take 20-30 years and he is taking the patent-office route instead.

## Experimental claims

- Scale: about 480 V in room air lifts a device by about 0.1 g (about 1 mN); flipping the device inverts the sign; ion neutralization and re-taring were done. In vacuum a dual-thruster vertical spinner deflected 14 mm, about 2.5 mN, actuated by Bluetooth inside indium-tin-oxide grounded walls. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 54:47-58:44)*
- Buhler says about 2,000 variations (each tested multiple times), all DC; that DC removes magnetic explanations; and that the force remains with power off: "if you put something on the scale and we turn it off... the force remains." He states energy conservation is not obeyed classically. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 59:39-1:01:23, 1:33:02-1:33:34)* Chester notes the claimed persistence is the hardest part to accept and wants it confirmed as unlimited in time. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 2:18:52-2:19:36)*
- Buhler says he cannot think of a person who examined it in detail and left skeptical, though only "a couple of dozen" have seen it. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:01:23-1:02:07)*
- Levers: voltage, dielectric breakdown strength, permittivity, geometry, static dissipation, signal type; the force scales with field squared and is higher in vacuum because the field limit rises from about 10^6 to 10^8 V/m. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 41:44-42:36, 52:36-53:09)* Barium titanate tests were uninteresting; bismuth is of interest. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 42:51-43:22)*
- Buhler says the classical version of the force has a surface part (electrostatic pressure) and a volume part (divergence of E), but he thinks only a quantum-mechanical account can explain it. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:32:16-1:33:06)*
- Buhler suggests spacecraft momentum anomalies in the Van Allen belts (speed-ups or slow-downs needing extra fuel) could be this force. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:31:45-1:32:14)*
- Buhler distinguishes weight change from mass reduction and rejects the "antigravity" label; Drew calls it warp drive; Buhler is not claiming spacetime bending. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:09:36-1:09:56, 1:36:05-1:37:19)*

## Controls and the ion-wind objection

See [[ion-wind]]. Controls listed: Faraday cage around the whole device with force measured on the cage, reversal of orientation, high vacuum, grounded indium-tin-oxide shielding, and a scale test. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 50:23-53:47)*

## Theory (QED third-order claim)

- Buhler starts from two charges and second-order time-independent perturbation theory in quantum electrodynamics (the derivation of Coulomb's law via virtual-photon exchange), then tries the third order, which has 12 terms, multiplies charges rather than adding them (giving the asymmetry classical superposition cannot) and includes scalar/"dark" photon exchange or one-sided emission and absorption, from which he argues momentum conservation yields thrust. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:53:46-2:01:40, 2:03:56-2:05:44)*
- [[david-chester]] cautions that virtual particles cannot be external lines, that standard QED perturbation theory is not usually time-independent in that sense, that tree-level diagrams are the classical limit, and that Buhler should study renormalization, self-energy and the classical limit; he calls the experimental results among the most convincing he has seen and says he cannot think of a prosaic explanation. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 2:05:57-2:13:20, 2:16:42-2:18:52, 2:24:44-2:25:33)*

## Related and rival work

- Falcon Space's Biefeld-Brown replication: Chester found it suggestive but not conclusive, worrying about field emission, discharges and magnets. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 2:19:48-2:23:40)*
- Musha (Japan, ex-Honda) submerged capacitors in transmission oil (no ion wind) and reported thrust; Michels says the group went quiet without retracting. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:51:23-1:51:53)*
- Podkletnov (Tampere) and Ning Li on spinning superconductors; Buhler says NASA reproduced it without seeing the effect. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:37:31-1:38:15)*
- [[sonny-white]] (NASA Eagleworks) and Casimir-effect claims. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:04:44-1:05:54)*
