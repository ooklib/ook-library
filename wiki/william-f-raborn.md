Rear Admiral William F. Raborn headed development of the [[polaris-program|Polaris]] submarine
missile program before retiring from the Navy in 1963, then served as Director of National
Intelligence (1965–66) and was recruited to senior
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] roles.
*([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 9:13–9:20, 43:08–43:35)*

## Claims

- Raborn was "plucked for senior SAIC roles to stack the contractor's payroll with scores of
  influential members of the national security establishment," per Gerb.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 43:19–43:28)*
- The Polaris program he led is separately connected by Gerb to
  [[john-craven|John Piña Craven]]'s Deep Submergence Systems Project and Operation Sand
  Dollar, an alleged UFO crash-retrieval program "buried" within the Polaris program's
  structure. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 43:43–45:33)*

- Polaris-program testing occurred at [[tonopah-test-range|Tonopah Test Range]], where the Navy's
  Naval Air Special Weapons Facility conducted special-weapons testing; Gerb ties this to
  [[operation-sand-dollar|Operation Sand Dollar]] and its Lockheed-built DSRVs.
  *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 1:02:47–1:06:09)*
