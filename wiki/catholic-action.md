Catholic Action (the Catholic Action Groups), per Eyes Wide Open, was an anti-communist
propaganda network run by the Church, created with $30 million in US funds and later used as a
Vatican Bank front group to distribute laundered cash to mob associates.

## Claims

- An additional $30 million (beyond the $350M used to establish the Christian Democratic Party)
  was sent to create the Catholic Action Groups, an anti-communist propaganda network run by the
  church. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 22:46-23:00)*
- The $65 million dumped into the Vatican Bank in 1948 was paid out through Vatican front groups
  such as Catholic Action, "a Vatican aid organization that was perfect for doling out laundered
  cash to mob associates." *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 28:14-28:34)*
