Skinwalker Ranch in Utah's Uinta Basin is a long-running paranormal and UFO hotspot, formerly owned by [[robert-bigelow]] and now by Brandon Fugal. [[jesse-michels]] cites it in [[sentinels-of-ether-michels-episode-2026]] as a place where craft appear to enter and exit mesas (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:26:10-1:28:46)*). This page is new and records only what this source says; the ranch is likely to appear in other sources.

## Claims

- The Ute tribe calls it cursed ground, "the path of the skinwalker"; drones malfunction, helicopters lose power and equipment dies. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:26:10-1:26:37)*
- Fugal describes Black Hawks circling with no transponders and a UAP that dropped into the east field below a helicopter and exited from a mesa; sensors malfunctioned at the time. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:27:03-1:28:07)*
- [[robert-bigelow]] tells of a UFO flying lazy figure-eights at low altitude and shooting into a mountain without an explosion, "as if the stone was a doorway". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:28:07-1:28:46)*
- Michels frames this as the same "vanishing into mountains" pattern as [[coyame-mountain-base-narrative]] and [[bradshaw-ranch-sedona]]. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 55:16-55:34, 1:22:16-1:26:18)*
