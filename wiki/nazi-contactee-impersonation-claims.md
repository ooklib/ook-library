A cluster of second-hand items Jorjani uses to argue that Nazi-linked humans posed as extraterrestrials. Each is anecdotal and unverified.

## Claims

- Reinhold Schmidt (1957, Nebraska, as recalled): saucer with a transparent interior, two women and four men who spoke high German, asked about the US satellite programme and predicted failures. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:16:36, 2:17:13, 2:18:22)*
- [[betty-and-barney-hill-abduction-1961]]: Barney Hill's first statements said the entities looked like Nazis ("the evil face"). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:19:14, 2:19:42)*
- Desmond Leslie's daughter allegedly commented that her father always said Nazis contacted George Adamski and posed as Venusians and Martians. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:21:01, 2:21:41)*
- [[paul-mellon]] allegedly told his grandson John Warner IV that in 1944 he and [[allen-dulles]] stood on a German flying disc in a Czechoslovak hangar. Michels attributes to [[harald-malmgren]] the Magenta craft's exfiltration via Nazi rat lines. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:22:15, 2:23:10)*
