Jacob "Jake" Esterline is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the CIA's task force chief of WH/4 and its main on-the-ground director of the Bay of Pigs planning. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 22:10-22:21, 23:11-23:24)*

## Claims

- Assigned by [[joseph-caldwell-king]] in early 1960 to lead the working-level team devising simultaneously the Castro assassination and the Cuban invasion operations. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 22:10-22:29)*
- Career: started in OSS Detachment 101 in Southeast Asia as a paramilitary expert, "clearing the way" for [[paul-helliwell]] and his [[oss-detachment-202]] men in Miami (host's phrasing); postwar posted across Latin America; reportedly paramilitary adviser for [[operation-pbsuccess]]; called from Venezuela in early 1960. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 22:29-23:24)*
- Organized and directed both the elite training for [[operation-40]] and the broader combat training of [[brigade-2506]] in Guatemala. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 23:24-23:37)*
- Host: the PBSuccess group was largely transferred to the Bay of Pigs operation about six years later. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 23:05-23:11)*
