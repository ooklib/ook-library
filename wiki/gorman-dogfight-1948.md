The Gorman dogfight is a 1 October 1948 encounter over Fargo, North Dakota, in which National Guard second lieutenant George F. Gorman, flying a P-51 Mustang, says he chased a small light that outmaneuvered him for about 20 minutes. [[uap-gerb]] retells it as one of four air-force cases in [[gerb-air-force-encounters-video]]. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 0:47-5:59)*

## Claims

- Gerb says [[edward-j-ruppelt]] wrote it up as one of three classic 1948 incidents that "proved to Air Force intelligence specialists that UFOs were real"; he says he will cover the other two later. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 0:51-1:13)*
- Account: Gorman was a WWII veteran on a cross-country flight; around 9:00 pm he saw a light near a Piper Cub and, after checking with air traffic control at Hector airport, found no other traffic. He pursued at 350-400 mph and could not corner it; it passed 500 ft over him, appearing as a 6-8 inch ball of light whose blinking stopped and luminosity grew as it sped up. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 1:13-2:51)*
- The object made steep vertical climbs twice, once leaving his P-51 stalled after a 9,000 ft climb and once when he dived from 14,000 ft at full power; he abandoned pursuit at 9:27 pm. Hector controller L. D. Jensen watched it pass overhead through binoculars but could not see the blinking. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 2:51-3:37)*
- Gorman swore an account on 23 October 1948 saying the maneuvers showed "thought behind" them, that the object obeyed inertia, that he blacked out briefly turning with it, and that it out-turned, out-sped and out-climbed his aircraft. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 3:37-4:33)*
- Official follow-up: [[project-sign]] (created after Lt. Gen. Twining's assertion that flying discs were "real and not visionary or fictitious", in a letter to Gen. Schulgen) interviewed Gorman and witnesses and checked the P-51, which reportedly was measurably more radioactive than other fighters, leading investigators to conclude it had flown close to an "atomic powered object". Sign reports were to go to the Army and Navy Research and Development Board, the USAF Scientific Advisory Board and the [[atomic-energy-commission]]. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 4:33-5:28)*
- Prosaic explanations: Sign first offered weather balloons and jets, then attributed the radiation to less shielding at 14,000 ft, and concluded Gorman chased a lit weather balloon or Jupiter whose feats only appeared fantastic from his frame of reference. Gerb ranks it among the two most compelling cases. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 5:28-5:59,)*
