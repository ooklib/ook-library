Area 6 is a little-known top-secret R&D facility on Yucca Flat within the
[[nevada-national-security-site|Nevada National Security Site]], existing since the 1950s (dirt
airstrip reconstructed in 2005) and featuring a hangar complex able to hold 15+ MQ-9 Reaper
drones. It was first publicly discussed in 2016 by NNSA spokesperson Darwin Morgan. Gerb argues
Area 6 — with a documented [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]] presence and a Lockheed
Martin-operated aerial operations facility — is a strong candidate site for the DARPA-linked
"Advanced Group Six" exotic-propulsion RDT&E reported by [[richard-dolan|Richard Dolan]]'s 2021
source. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:19:04–2:21:10)*

## Claims

- Per Morgan, Area 6 performs sensor and UAV testing for the Air Force, Army, Navy, DARPA, and
  the [[defense-threat-reduction-agency|Defense Threat Reduction Agency]]; its runway/apron were
  built by Bechtel Nevada (site operations contractor) for $9.6M. Funding is buried in the Nevada
  Field Office's "strategic partner program" ($84M of the site's total FY2015 budget of $662M,
  ~13.5%). *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:20:04–2:22:05)*
- A 20,000+-image EG&G/Nevada Test Site photo lab collection (1972–1992+, reviewed in full by
  Gerb) includes photographs of Sandia's compound and control rooms at Area 6 dating to the
  1970s–1980s, alongside images of Area 19 (another Sandia operations/maintenance facility) and
  Janet Airlines aircraft. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:18:23–2:19:04,
  2:22:52–2:23:22)*
- The "aerial operations facility" (AOF) at Area 6 was established in 2001 (per a 2004 NNSA/DOE
  environmental assessment) "to construct, operate and test a variety of unmanned aerial
  vehicles" — airframe modifications, sensor operation, onboard computer development — and is
  operated by [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]]. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md)
  2:23:22–2:26:12)*
- N20R, a twin-turboprop Janet Airlines aircraft otherwise associated with Area 51, has been
  tracked (per the Lazy G Ranch website, as recently as 2020) flying to an unlisted airport,
  NVH1, located at Area 6's aerial operations facility on the Yucca Dry Lake.
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:26:34–2:27:50)*
- Gerb: "the connection here[:] Lockheed Martin, Sandia National Labs and UFO Legacy programs" —
  he believes Area 6 and [[tonopah-test-range|Tonopah Test Range]] are "intimately connected."
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:27:57–2:28:04)*
