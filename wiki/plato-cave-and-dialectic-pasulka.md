[[diana-pasulka]] argues that Plato's *Republic* ends with two "strange" devices, the allegory of the cave and the myth of Er (a soldier's near-death experience), because Plato was traumatized by Athens killing his mentor Socrates and could not show how a just society is possible; what he offers instead is performative: a community practicing a "dialectic", a "mystical conversation" about justice and goodness. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 29:35-31:26, 45:36-48:46)*

## Claims

- She says she only began truly reading Plato after encountering UFO believers, the concealment of UFOs and, she says, harassment of whistleblowers that she witnessed firsthand. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 45:14-46:05)*
- Michels and Pasulka read Er as anamnesis, a soul that crosses the river of forgetfulness between incarnations, and Michels says Plato probably went through the Eleusinian mystery rituals; Pasulka says the literal reading is "abhorred" in graduate school and goes against both liberal and conservative readings. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 48:55-50:46)*
- She says early Christians used the cave allegory: the 1940s discoveries of early Christian libraries (Nag Hammadi, not named) show Plato's *Republic* influenced them. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 50:46-51:19)*
- The "apocalypse is structural" (always happening; the Roman destruction of the Temple in 70 was one), and the "out" is the dialectic community. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 29:42-31:26)*
- Late in the follow-up she ties this to the "Invisibles", the whistleblowers who might keep secrecy, and to the Buddhist triad (Buddha, Dharma, Sangha): salvation is communal. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:17:04-1:18:58)*
