The 1961 CIA-organized invasion of Cuba by anti-Castro exiles. Per Eyes Wide Open, [[e-howard-hunt]] organized it as political liaison to exile groups and trained exile mercenaries in Guatemala; many speculate it was partly financed by [[paul-helliwell]]'s secret banking network, and researchers consider it the Hunt/CIA grievance behind later JFK-plot speculation. The host plans several episodes on it. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 6:21-6:46, 12:14-12:31)*

## Claims

- JFK's refusal to provide air support left Hunt embarrassed and, per the host, emboldened against Kennedy; JFK then fired [[allen-dulles]]. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 7:15-7:39)*

- LAOS 4: host: the invasion's start on April 17, 1961 cancelled [[operation-milpond]]; it soured Kennedy on the CIA and led to the firing of Allen Dulles and Richard Bissell; Cuban exiles later became a paramilitary pool. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:15:52-1:16:42, 58:35-58:58)*
