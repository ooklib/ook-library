The London School of Economics was founded in 1895 by the [[fabian-society]], per Green &
Pleasant, with the explicit stated aim (Sidney Webb's words) "to teach political economy on more
modern and more socialist lines." Located a short walk from Whitehall, the video frames it as a
central piece of the Fabians' [[permeation]] infrastructure, training generations of civil
servants, economists, and world leaders.

## Claims

- Founded 1895 by the Fabian Society; Sidney Webb's stated intention was to teach political
  economy "on more modern and more socialist lines"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 7:00)*.
- Alumni cited as evidence of Fabian/LSE influence on foreign governance: Jawaharlal Nehru
  (India), Lee Kuan Yew (Singapore, who later said Fabian ideas required tempering with
  practicality)
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 7:51, 8:15)*.
- William Beveridge, who had been trained as a researcher under Beatrice Webb, was made LSE
  director by the Fabians in 1919
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 19:56)*.
- The "Fabian Window" stained-glass artwork (designed by George Bernard Shaw in 1910, stolen
  1978, resurfaced at Sotheby's in 2006) was unveiled at LSE on 20 April 2006 by then-Prime
  Minister Tony Blair, who said Fabian/Shaw "values would be very recognizable in today's Labour
  Party" while standing in front of a literal wolf-in-sheep's-clothing emblem
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 25:29, 25:53)*.

## Speculation flag

The characterization of LSE as founded specifically to serve a socialist "permeation" agenda is
Green & Pleasant's framing of Sidney Webb's own quoted words; the school's later academic
independence and ideological diversity are not addressed by this source.
