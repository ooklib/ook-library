## Claims

- Blamed Rockefeller for the death of his mentor [[thomas-a-scott]], beginning their rivalry. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 29:02, 29:11)*
- Forced by ore shortage into the [[mesabi-iron-range-deal]], then sold Carnegie Steel to [[j-pierpont-morgan]] to form [[us-steel-corporation]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 36:43, 37:13, 39:24)*
- Ended "far behind" Rockefeller in the fight for richest man after 1911. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 45:05, 45:12)*
