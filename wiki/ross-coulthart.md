Ross Coulthart, Australian investigative journalist (host of *Need to Know* with Bryce Sable),
is cited by Gerb recounting [[david-fravor|David Fravor]]'s claim that [[ben-rich|Ben Rich]]
privately told him the 2004 Nimitz "tic tac" originated from Lockheed Skunk Works.
*([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 1:14:05–1:16:19)*

## Claims

- States on *Need to Know*: "I now know categorically that the tic tac is Lockheed Martin
  technology" — a claim Gerb says has been met with skepticism, outrage, and intrigue by the UFO
  community, and criticizes for lacking a constructed thesis or evidence trail despite
  Coulthart's confidence. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 1:15:43–1:16:33,
  2:50:59–2:52:08)*
- Frames the alleged coverup as the US government hiding advanced capability from foreign
  adversaries out of fear of appearing behind. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md)
  1:16:03–1:16:26)*
