A short case-compilation video (about 14 minutes, dated 2024-02-01) in which [[uap-gerb]] covers four military air-force UFO encounters he says are underrepresented compared with the Gimbal and Go Fast videos: the US Gorman dogfight, a Finnish case, a Peruvian case and a Mexican case. The source page is [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6cVe-hdMTCE). The description says some cases are legendary in their countries and some were found in official USG Project Sign or Moon Dust documents. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 0:00-0:47)*

## Claims

- Framing: he says he expects viewers know the foo fighters, the David Fravor Tic Tac and the Manises and Tehran cases, and asks about official air-force encounters that "flew under the radar"; military sightings are credible because of multi-sensor detection and trained pilots. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 0:00-0:47)*
- Case tellings: [[gorman-dogfight-1948]], [[finnish-pori-ufo-sighting-1969]], [[arequipa-peru-ufo-incident-1980]] and [[campeche-mexico-ufo-incident-2004]]. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 0:47-12:41)*
- Ranking: he finds the Gorman and Peruvian cases most interesting (the most sensor data and the most compelling case for non-prosaic origin), leans towards a UFO explanation for the Finnish case because of the pilots' "ironclad testimony", and rates the Mexican case as the one with the highest probability of a prosaic (flare) explanation while saying more analysis is needed. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 13:06-13:54)*
- He credits the YouTube channel whose P-51 recreation clips he used, invites viewers to his Discord where he shares case files, and promises a part two with more cases. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 12:41-14:16)*
