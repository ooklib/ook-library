A mass sighting of a boomerang or V-shaped formation over Phoenix, Arizona on March 13, 1997, one of the largest in US history, per [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 21:25-21:47)*

## Claims

- The official explanation is flares and a formation of A-10 Thunderbolt II aircraft. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 21:39-21:57)*
- Arizona governor [[fife-symington]] observed the event, was denied an official inquiry, and later said he is a pilot who knows nearly every machine that flies and it was bigger than anything he had seen; in a 2017 CNN editorial he described a huge, silent delta-shaped craft with a distinctive leading edge and enormous lights. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 21:47-22:41)*
- Gerb says the flare explanation cannot cover some sightings such as [[jeremy-corbell]]'s 2023 Palm Springs footage, and given hundreds of witnesses he cannot rule out an anomalous craft. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 22:47-23:10)*
