DARPA's Security and Intelligence Directorate (SID) is a support directorate under the DARPA
director tasked with "planning, executing, and directing the information, personnel, industrial,
information assurance, and physical security programs at DARPA and at specific contractor sites."
Gerb frames SID (alongside [[air-force-office-of-special-investigations|AFOSI/PJ]] and the
[[doe-office-of-intelligence-and-counterintelligence|DOE's OICI]]) as one of the three
"antibody" program-protection offices central to Vol. 2, and states he "personally fears" SID.
*([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:49:29–1:51:35; also
[gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:02:32–2:02:50)*

## Claims

- SID's mission: "develop, manage, and implement programs that facilitate the secure and
  successful accomplishment of DARPA's mission while protecting DARPA technical and
  administrative personnel, information, property, and ensuring business continuity." Its Program
  Security Representatives (PSRs) are not stovepiped by category — they cover fundamental
  research, controlled unclassified information, collateral, SAP, SCI, and other compartmented
  activities across DARPA's portfolio, "frequently for technologies that exist nowhere else."
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:50:08–1:51:35)*
- Gerb dates SID's founding to at least 2003 (earliest routing-line reference he found, on a
  DARPA Information Processing Techniques Office document). *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  1:51:42–1:51:57)*
- Gerb alleges he is aware of former DARPA SID directors who "engaged in serious crimes to
  silence whistleblowers and prevent program exposure," including — he states without further
  detail — wet-works operations, and calls for Congress to construct interrogatories against
  DARPA SID and its Support Services Office (SSO) under penalty of prosecution. He declines to
  name the individual. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:52:07–1:52:51)*
- SID's administrative reporting line runs to the DARPA director and then to
  [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-research-and-engineering|USD R&E]]; Gerb names
  [[peter-highnam|Dr. Peter Highnam]] as the individual within that chain of command he considers
  "legacy program." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:52:55–1:53:51)*
- Since 2012, SID has maintained an "intimate partnership" with contractor
  [[system-high|System High]], awarding it three consecutive multi-year multi-security-services
  contracts (most recently a 2022 5-year award). *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  1:55:06–1:55:53)*
