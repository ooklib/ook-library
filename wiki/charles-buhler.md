Dr. Charles Buhler (transcribed "Buer", "Beller", "Pewer" in the auto-transcript) is the lead scientist of [[nasa]]'s [[nasa-electrostatics-and-surface-physics-laboratory]] at Kennedy Space Center (part of Swamp Works), a prospective president of the Electrostatics Society of America, and co-founder of [[exodus-propulsion-technologies]]. [[jesse-michels]] presents him as the most credentialed person to have reported a [[thomas-townsend-brown]]-style thrust force in vacuum, described in [[exodus-force-claims]]. His employer does not sanction the propulsion work.

## Claims

- Background: PhD in theoretical condensed-matter physics at Florida State University (working at the National High Field Laboratory); his PhD topic is high-temperature superconductivity; joined the KSC lab as a postdoc under its founder, expecting electrostatics to be a solved subject, then found even tribocharging unexplained; earlier worked as a consultant for Exxon Mobil. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 23:01-23:58, 37:42-37:46, 35:57-36:06)*
- He says he is the lead electrostatics scientist in all of NASA "by default" because NASA has one electrostatics lab and he leads it. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 14:50-15:04)* Michels stresses that credential as the reason skeptics cannot dismiss the effect as basic electrostatics. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 7:52-8:15)*
- Conventional contributions cited by Michels: showing that brush discharges do not occur in high vacuum, and that in a tilted vat of same-size particles the ones that roll over others become positive (charging tracks collision dynamics, not size). *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 19:22-21:36)*
- He has "always believed there had to be a better way" than Newton's-third-law rockets, and spent about two decades on converting electromagnetic field momentum into mechanical momentum (confounded by "hidden momentum") before finding a force that needed no fields' momentum at all (2018). *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 26:56-28:24, 2:31:05-2:32:03)*
- First saw an anomalous force around 2010 with his future brother-in-law and mentor Dr. Sid Clemens; the effect grew after meeting [[andrew-arjima]]. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 32:10-34:15)* His wife works in NASA's Launch Services Program and prompted the link between Arjima's needle setup and his own work. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 37:52-38:14)*
- His childhood UFO sightings and the [[cocoa-beach-lights-encounter]]; he says he does not think they relate to his propulsion work. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:07:56-1:22:01)*
- He says no military or DARPA body has contacted him; he and his wife were approached by NASA's own UAP study effort but declined because it was an instruments group with no physicists. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:39:53-1:41:38)*
- Not affiliated: he dislikes the term antigravity, is "not there yet" on bending spacetime, and does not want to claim teleportation or wormholes. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:36:05-1:37:19)*
