Travis Walton is the Arizona logger who reported a 1975 abduction; he appears here only as a comparison. [[chris-ramsey]] says Walton's descriptions of a glowing craft with arches underneath resemble the craft [[bob-lazar]] worked on ([[lazar-sport-model-craft-claims]]) and offers to arrange a Walton meeting; Lazar says he believes Walton is "telling the truth as he knows it", and that [[luigi-vendittelli]] has met him *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:43:07-1:44:30)*. The 1975 sighting is not otherwise detailed in this source.
