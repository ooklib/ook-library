The Remembrancer is a permanent, unelected office of the [[city-of-london-corporation]], created
in 1571 under Elizabeth I, whose holder sits inside the House of Commons as the Corporation's
liaison to Parliament. Green & Pleasant presents the office's 454-year continuous seat as the
clearest example of the City's institutionalized, unremarked-upon access to the machinery of
government.

## Claims

- Created 1571 under Elizabeth I; has run continuously ever since. The Corporation itself
  describes the role as "the channel of communication between the city and parliament," and it
  comes with a seat inside the House of Commons chamber
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 9:23, 9:30, 9:37, 9:44)*.
- Green & Pleasant corrects a popular misconception: the Remembrancer does not sit behind the
  Speaker's chair, contrary to some published claims — Parliament has stated plainly that he sits
  in the under-gallery, facing the Speaker from the far end of the chamber. He cannot vote, speak,
  or intervene in proceedings. Officially he is one of roughly 15 "parliamentary agents" who work
  on private legislation
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 9:53, 10:01, 10:09)*.
- The video's emphasis: a post created to watch legislation on behalf of one financial district
  has held a permanent place in the Commons chamber since before the Spanish Armada (1588), and
  the rules allowing it have never been seriously reviewed. In 2013 a Green MP asked the Speaker
  whether the post still needed a seat in Parliament; nothing happened. Green & Pleasant stresses
  this is documented plainly in Hansard, the official parliamentary record — "not a conspiracy
  theory," simply unmentioned
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 9:13, 10:18, 10:25, 10:34, 10:42, 10:50)*.
- Proposed as one of three non-radical reforms the video suggests: withdrawing the Remembrancer's
  seat, as the 2013 Green MP proposal envisioned
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 17:32)*.

## Relationships

- Created and operated by the [[city-of-london-corporation]] as its permanent liaison inside the
  UK Parliament.

## Speculation flag

The office's existence, creation date, and non-voting status are stated by Green & Pleasant as
matters of public parliamentary record (Hansard, Parliament's own clarifications). Framing
language ("nobody ever elected," the emphasis on 454 unbroken years) is the source's rhetorical
characterization and is recorded here as such, per this wiki's "claims, not fact" convention.
