Marco Chereze (spelled many ways in the auto-transcript) was, per [[james-fox]], a 23-year-old military police officer who allegedly grabbed a being with his bare hands on the evening of the [[varginha-ufo-incident-1996]] and died of infection within weeks.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says Chereze was off duty when fellow military police officer Eric Lopes, who had orders to find "something unusual, some sort of creature", invited him along; at dusk a creature ran across the road and Chereze leapt out of the car and grabbed it, placing it on his lap in the back seat. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:03:50-1:05:00)*
- They took it to a clinic, where a doctor allegedly refused it ("get that thing out of here"), then to Regional Hospital. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:05:06-1:05:36)*
- Chereze went home with a smelly, greasy, oily substance on his skin, tried to wipe it off with rubbing alcohol, and within weeks fell ill. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:07:55-1:08:15)*
- Fox says Chereze told cardiologist Dr. Cesario Furtado the truth "in confidence" hoping to be cured; Furtado says he "threw the kitchen sink" at the infection, without effect. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:08:22-1:09:47)*
- He died at 23, perfectly healthy beforehand, leaving a pregnant wife who gave only one interview (to Dr. Roger Leir, 2002); the family had trouble getting the autopsy report and the cause was recorded as general infection of unknown cause. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:09:52-1:10:59)*
- Fox says the cause of death is analysed in [[marco-chereze-autopsy-claims]]; Fox insists the being was not aggressive and that the scratch was accidental. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:17:20-1:17:46)*
