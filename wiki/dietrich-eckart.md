Thule Society member and, per the documentary, an "absolutely devastating influence" on Hitler's thinking (ASR: "Eckhart").

## Claims

- With Alfred Rosenberg, named as a Thule member among the Nazi ruling elite; both held that the material world was evil and a higher spiritual realm the goal. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 14:43,)*
- Led a secret society meeting in a Munich beer hall attended by Hitler, Himmler and others; taught Hitler rituals and the development of willpower and "magnetism". *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 27:42,)*
- Believed he was in touch with "Secret Chiefs", variously fallen angels, underground beings or extraterrestrials. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 28:30)*
