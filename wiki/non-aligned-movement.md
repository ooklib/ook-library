The Non-Aligned Movement is the anti-colonial coalition of Third World states that, per Eyes
Wide Open, formed in the 1960s to remain neutral between the American and Soviet Cold War blocs
— a stance the host argues was nonetheless still untenable to the CIA, since Non-Aligned states
generally supported the land reform policies that threatened western corporate interests in the
developing world.

## Claims

- Formed in the 1960s by states such as Egypt, Indonesia, India, and the Democratic Republic of
  the Congo, embracing a "third way" outside the communist-capitalist paradigm to maintain
  autonomy and neutrality in the Cold War. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 12:15-12:30)*
- Per Eyes Wide Open, still untenable to the CIA and its "elite benefactors" because Non-Aligned
  nationalist leaders generally believed in land reform, setting up a recurring Cold War pattern
  the host says the series will trace: nationalist leaders pushed toward Marxism by harsh CIA
  opposition, then forced to seek Soviet/Chinese support to complete their revolutions.
  *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 12:30-13:01)*

## Relationships
