Neutralist half-brother prince and first prime minister of independent [[laos]], per [[eyes-wide-open]] (ASR variants "suvana Puma", "suvan Puma"). Repeatedly tried to build a government of national unity including the [[pathet-lao]] led by his half-brother Prince Souphanouvong, which the host says Washington, the CIA and the State Department would not tolerate. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 15:54-16:25, 21:42-22:01)*

## Claims

- Founder-leader of the neutralist Lao National Progress Party (1950), advocating non-alignment in the tradition of Tito, Nasser and Nehru; neutrality was broadly popular with the Buddhist population but the movement had largely disappeared by the late 1960s. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 15:59-17:10)*
- 1957 deal with Souphanouvong to integrate Pathet Lao troops; the 1958 unity government; forced out in July 1958 after US aid was cut, and made ambassador to France. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 30:36-31:10, 35:22-36:10)*
- Reinstated by [[kong-le]] in August 1960 and again proposed a national-unity government; the CIA-backed [[phoumi-nosavan]] broke off negotiations and re-took the capital. Souvanna was reinstated as prime minister again in 1962 and remained in power for most of the civil war, but with diminished power. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 46:52-47:33, 49:34-49:47)*
- See [[laos-coups-1958-1960]] for the full sequence.

## Relationships

- Opposed by [[phoumi-nosavan]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency]]; restored by [[kong-le]]; leader of the neutralists alongside the [[pathet-lao]] and right-wing factions in [[laos]].
