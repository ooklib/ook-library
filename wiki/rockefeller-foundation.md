Philanthropic foundation created in 1913 by [[john-d-rockefeller]] with [[frederick-gates]] and [[john-d-rockefeller-jr]]. The Eyes Wide Open pages elsewhere in the wiki (see [[rockefeller-family]]) place its China Medical Board in the mid-1910s; this source presents it only benignly.

## Claims

- Focused on medicine and education; Rockefeller had given almost $150 million by 1913 and $540 million personally in total, with Gates and Junior adding $537 million through the foundation. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 47:16, 47:21, 47:30)*
- Credited with funding research against hookworm, malaria and meningitis, lifting the American South from poverty and building hundreds of schools and two prestigious research institutions. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 48:04, 48:13, 48:20)*
- The narrator says donations were sometimes labeled image-management, but he also gave anonymously. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 47:03, 47:09)*

## Relationships

- Founded by [[john-d-rockefeller]], [[frederick-gates]], [[john-d-rockefeller-jr]]; part of the [[rockefeller-family]] ecosystem.
