Dan Sherman, author of "Above Black", a former Air Force electronic intelligence airman who claims a genetic-management program ([[project-preserve-destiny]]) gave him the ability to communicate with non-human intelligence. Described in the [[matthew-brown-michels-interview-2026]] by [[jesse-michels]], who had interviewed him. All claims are Sherman's, relayed by Michels, and unverified.

## Claims (as relayed by Michels)

- He was in continuing education in Maryland and entered an NSA complex where senior Air Force staff told him his mother was abducted while pregnant and he was genetically altered in the womb, giving him hypersensitivity to communicate with non-human intelligence. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:17:03-1:17:58)*
- He was given binaural headphones, told to lock onto and "collapse" a sine wave on a graphical interface and took pills; he communicated with entities he named "Bones" and "Spock", who supplied coordinates and descriptions of people being abducted, turning him from electronic intelligence to "alien signals intelligence". *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:18:01-1:19:44)*
- Michels says a FOIA request on Project Preserve Destiny was forwarded from the Air Force to the NSA, the Italian bases (San Vito dei Normanni) match, and a very senior former official says the NSA building is real. Brown says the book is one of the few UFO books he read, on insiders' advice, and reads it as evidence of long-established standard procedures implementing agreements ([[brown-agreements-and-no-go-zone-claims]]). *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:18:22-1:20:34)*
- Michels compares the methods to [[andrija-puharich]]'s (per Greg Malazzi's documentary "Mind Traveler") and to the contactees who would have been identified to Sherman and checked on by human assets (leaked [[kit-green]]-[[ed-may]] call). *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 1:20:34-1:22:13)*
