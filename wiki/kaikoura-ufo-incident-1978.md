The Kaikoura incident was a December 1978 wave of sightings, radar returns and film taken from a cargo aircraft off the New Zealand coast. In this source [[ross-coulthart]] treats it as the case that first convinced him something real was going on, because the official squid-boat-light explanation does not survive the declassified New Zealand files.

## Claims

- Coulthart recalls his father, a small-town doctor and total skeptic, telling him in 1978 that he had seen a UFO and had told the local policeman, who had also seen it. Years later Coulthart found the policeman's logged report confirming the sighting in the New Zealand archives. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 10:23-11:41)*
- The Kaikoura incident came about a week or so after that, and shortly after the [[frederick-valentich-disappearance-1978]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 10:49-11:59)*
- Newspapers quickly asserted the objects were squid-boat lights refracted on cloud; Coulthart says the declassified New Zealand files show that explanation was nonsense, and even New Zealand Air Force investigators accepted that neither the squid-boat nor the Venus explanation washed. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 14:08-14:40, 19:24-19:34)*
- The objects were caught on radar. Wellington air traffic controller John Cordy (since deceased) told Coulthart he and a colleague saw radar returns moving around an Argosy cargo aircraft carrying newspapers up the east coast of the South Island. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 14:40-15:47)*
- Aboard the Argosy, Australian journalist Quentin Fogarty, cameraman Dennis Grant (later a top Australian political correspondent, who still has his notes) and another crew member filmed objects that came up to the cockpit window, and Grant's notes reportedly match in real time what Cordy was seeing on radar 50-60 miles away. Pilot Captain Bill Startup was, per Coulthart, a nuts-and-bolts man angered at the implication he had made an error. Other pilots had seen the objects the previous nights. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 15:47-16:56, 20:57-21:37)*
- [[bruce-maccabee]] analysed a copy of the film for Fogarty and the Australian network and found the objects' movements anomalous. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 20:14-20:52)*
- Fogarty made a film and wrote a book titled "Let's Hope They're Friendly". The Wellington controllers who corroborated the film were, per Coulthart, unpopular and professionally reprimanded. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 18:43-19:05)*
- Coulthart says that as a young New Zealand Herald reporter about six years later he asked former prime minister Sir Robert Muldoon (transcribed "Sir Robert Moldun", whom the transcript calls prime minister of Australia) about it, and that Muldoon chuckled and said the Americans wanted it shut down as quickly as possible. Coulthart calls this an "ontological shock". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 17:02-18:26, 21:46-22:12)* Note: the transcript calls him prime minister of Australia while Coulthart was working at a New Zealand newspaper; the country and identity are unverified.
