Reinhard Gehlen was the head of German military intelligence on the Eastern Front during WWII
who, per Eyes Wide Open, surrendered to the US with a cache of anti-Soviet intelligence and was
recruited by [[allen-dulles]] to run a postwar stay-behind intelligence network — the seed of
both the [[gehlen-organization]] and, per the host, the future [[operation-gladio|Operation
Gladio]].

## Claims

- Ran the German military intelligence apparatus on the Eastern Front, where his agents
  reportedly hunted fugitive Jews (sentencing them to Nazi camps or death squads) and got much of
  their intelligence from torturing Soviet prisoners of war. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 2:41-3:12)*
- Foreseeing German defeat, devised a plan to defect to advancing American troops in southern
  Bavaria with a case of microfilmed intelligence on the Soviet Union, using it to leverage a
  personal audience with [[allen-dulles]] of the OSS. He was flown to comfortable quarters at
  [[fort-hunt-virginia|Fort Hunt]], Virginia for the meeting. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 3:15-3:58)*
- Dulles was impressed by Gehlen's resume and decided to send him back to Germany with US
  military handlers to run his existing organization as a stay-behind anti-communist asset,
  forming clandestine guerrilla armies of former fascists and Hitler Youth organized into
  "werewolf squads" — later renamed "Gladiators" under the future CIA's [[operation-gladio]].
  *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 4:01-4:52)*
- Was eventually reinstated as head of the West German intelligence service, the BND, in the
  1950s — cited by the host as illustrating "the clear revolving door between the Nazi party and
  the future framework of Western European security." *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 5:07-5:25)*
- Eyes Wide Open frames Gehlen, alongside [[karl-wolff]] and [[junio-valerio-borghese|the Black
  Prince]], as one of the three men who would go on to recruit the future "Gladiators" to fight
  European leftists, run false-flag operations, and keep European fascism alive in a hidden
  network. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 26:13-26:27)*
- Allegedly paid [[jean-violet]] ~6,000 DM annually as a 'special connection' and, per whistleblower [[hans-langemann]], 30,000 DM once for [[le-cercle]]; also a component of the CIA-BND [[operation-zipper]]. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 34:34-35:41, 42:22-42:39)*
