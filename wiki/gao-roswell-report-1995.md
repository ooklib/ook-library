- The probe was to "determine if the US Air Force or any other US government agency possessed information" on the alleged Roswell crash. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:56:31-1:57:02)*
