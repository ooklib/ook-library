Historian whose 1972 *The Politics of Heroin* the host calls the touchstone of intelligence-narcotics research and the main source for [[operation-paper]]. Cited alongside Douglas Valentine (*The Strength of the Wolf*), [[peter-dale-scott]] (article "Operation Paper: The United States and Drugs in Thailand and Burma"), Whitney Webb, Pierre-Arnaud Chouvy, Arne Kislenko and Victor Kaufman. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 3:31-4:32)*
- Cited in LAOS 1 for: Lao nationhood as a 'fiction' of colonial powers and Cold War negotiators; Laos's 60 tons of raw opium in the 1920s vs China's 6,380 tons; and Lao people scattered across the Golden Triangle (bibliography cites *The Politics of Heroin* pp. 94-96, 113-115, 126, 292). *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 9:28-9:44, 16:20-16:35)*

- LAOS 2 (main source): quoted for the "Corsican Machiavelli" ([[antoine-savani]]) and "cold-blooded scientific tactician" ([[roger-trinquier]]) labels; the four-phase [[operation-x]] recruitment; the [[binh-xuyen]] and CAT-at-Dien-Bien-Phu material; and Trinquier's candid interviews and 1972 letter to McCoy's publisher. The episode cites *The Politics of Heroin* many times, plus Cockburn and St. Clair's *Whiteout*, Kruger's *The Great Heroin Coup* and Peter Dale Scott's *The War Conspiracy*. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 21:04-21:31, 27:39-29:53, 43:46-44:57)*

- LAOS 3: the main source on [[lucien-conein]]: McCoy's 1971 interview at Conein's home near Langley; on Nhu's opium-funded secret police under Dr Tran Kim Tuyen; and the phrase "Cold War opium boom". Bibliography cites *The Politics of Heroin* pp. xi-xii, 203-206, 294-296. The host says McCoy "is hesitant to say so directly" that Conein forged the Diem-Corsican bond. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 43:42-44:46, 48:19-48:25, 49:17-49:44)*
