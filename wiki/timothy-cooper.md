UFO researcher who was the recipient of many [[majestic-documents]] via anonymous mailings and mailbox drops. His father, Master Sergeant Harry B. Cooper, was a USAF printing-management NCO at Air Defense Command (1957-63) who worked around UFO photographs and classified documents; Ryan Wood suggests friends made through that work later gave material to Tim. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 40:20, 41:52, 42:15)*

## Claims

- Received the anonymous June 1999 letter with the [[burned-memo]] from a source claiming to be a CI officer. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:02:19)*
- Investigated [[thomas-cantwheel]] and swore a five-page affidavit shared with [[stanton-friedman]] and [[dr-robert-wood]]. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:12:33)*
- Received the [[bowen-manuscript]] in 1999 from the FOIA office at Fort Meade. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:34:54)*
