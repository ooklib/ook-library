Claims by Lei's Real Talk about emails from the DOJ Epstein documents in which [[jeffrey-epstein]] allegedly taught Wall Street bankers how to cultivate the CCP elite through guanxi (relationship networks). The host's readings are attributed claims; the emails themselves are not reproduced in the transcript.

## Claims

- Lei says a 2009 email from Epstein to [[jes-staley]] (then a senior JPMorgan executive, later Barclays CEO) spells out "guanxi" for him and tells "arrogant Wall Street elites" to stop bringing thick legal contracts to China, since inside the party contracts are "just paper". *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 0:42,)*
- Epstein allegedly argued that CCP officials lack not money but a promise of survival for themselves and their families, so a bank should become the elite families' private overseas safe and placement office for children and relatives. *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 1:40,)*
- Scripted meeting lines: never say "I'm here to invest and make money", instead say "I've come here to seek your guidance" and praise China's civilization and governance. *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 2:30)*
- Disguised bribes: not gold or cash but a school recommendation letter for their children, introductions to Nobel laureates, or helping a relative "recover lost antiques" at a New York auction. *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 2:54)*
- October 2009 email: create a dedicated China initiative with its own advisory board including Politburo-linked figures, effectively a shelter for officials' relatives, with fancy titles, large salaries and jobs for their children; Lei calls it the prototype of the [[sons-and-daughters-hiring-program]]. *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 4:00,)*
- Epstein allegedly also suggested credit default swaps as secret, untraceable asset-management channels for Chinese officials, which Lei frames as a money-laundering route into New York. *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 5:49,)*
- Lei credits this coaching for [[jpmorgan-chase]]'s outsized China footprint: first wholly foreign-owned futures company (2020), first wholly foreign-owned securities company (2021), full ownership of an asset manager (2023). *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 7:26,)*
- September 2, 2010: Staley emails Epstein that he had the most senior JPMorgan meeting in China except for Jamie Dimon's with [[wang-qishan]], spent almost two hours with the head of the CCP organization department (ASR renders the name "Duian"/"Leuancha"; the host says he would join the Standing Committee) and was "seeing the prince this afternoon". Lei reads the prince as then-vice-president [[xi-jinping]] and calls Epstein "the back room" to JPMorgan's "front stage". *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 8:26,)*
- 2012: after Xi's installation in November, Epstein allegedly texted David Stern about a New York Times piece on the ex-wife of Desmond Shum (author of Red Roulette), reputed financial proxy of the Wen Jiabao family. Stern allegedly replied that the new leadership planned a massive probe of Wen family asset structures, designed by Zeng Qinghong (ASR "Jang Zaming"; also "Jiang Zemin" is possible) to clear the field for Xi. Lei says this shows Epstein's network read the power struggle early. *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 11:57,)*
- A May 13, 2019 email with redacted sender and recipient reads roughly "when Xi and Wang Qishan are removed or step down, break the radical [line], install our guys, wreak havoc on collaborations in the West". Lei treats it as Epstein's circle already discussing replacing Xi, "kingmaking", and calls Epstein a geopolitical speculator betting on regime shifts. The sender is unknown and the transcript gives the quote only approximately. *([lei/epstein-backchannel](<../raw/lei/Lei's Real Talk - 2026-02-23 - Epstein’s Backchannel - The Secret Pipeline Between Wall Street and Beijing.md>) 14:10,)*

## Caveats

- Lei's framing is strongly interpretive (for example "hostage diplomacy", "power harvesting machine"); the transcript does not show the underlying emails, and the 2019 email's provenance is admitted unknown.
- The transcript's ASR garbles several Chinese names, so identifications here are the wiki's best reading.
