Jed Davis (host's spelling "Jed"; ASR sometimes "Jet") is a lawyer and independent researcher who publishes on Twitter and Substack as "MJ12 Logic". He researches the history of US intelligence institutions from 1945 to the early 1950s using declassified CIA FOIA material, and Coulthart credits him with showing that the author of the [[majestic-documents]] "knew a hell of a lot" about material declassified only recently. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 8:02-8:53)*

## Claims

- He found the number 834021 on an MJ-12 document, searched the CIA FOIA reading-room portal and found a single file with that number, declassified 22 June 2022; every page carries it, so in his view it could not have been fabricated by anyone outside the compartment. See [[cia-file-834021]]. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 10:11-11:50, 36:53-37:44)*
- Gives a 1946-47 chronology of the intelligence build-up before Roswell: the December 1946 Dulles/Donovan/Vandenberg/Bush meeting, the January 1947 CIG-R&DB agreement, the May 1947 R&DB intelligence panels (his unpublished finding), the [[nia-meeting-1947-06-26]] and Hillenkoetter's two proposals. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 15:12-24:20)*
- Treats the July-September 1947 record (Wright's diary trip on 4 July, Truman's off-record meetings, the Air Force chiefs at the White House 8-9 July, Bronk's dossier, Forrestal's revolver registration, the dossier packet order matching the [[eisenhower-briefing-document]]) as "too many things for it to be a coincidence". *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 32:55-35:28, 39:10-39:57, 1:16:08-1:18:11)*
- Believes the MJ-12 documents are real "with that one exception": the [[cutler-twining-memo]], which he calls the "BS memo" and thinks was planted by CIA and/or AF OSI to make the whole subject radioactive; he says he was earlier too hard on it because it does confirm MJ-12 as a historical compartment. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 52:26-56:43, 1:09:34-1:12:04)*
- Details the Goudsmit letter and CIA-SI-28-55 numbering, see [[goudsmit-letter]]. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 1:00:22-1:03:36)*
- Argues (as does Cruickshank) that anyone hoaxing the documents would need knowledge across many operations; compares MJ-12 to MK-Ultra, which survived only because 20,000 files were misfiled in a finance-records warehouse. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 1:10:17-1:10:51)*
