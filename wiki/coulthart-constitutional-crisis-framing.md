[[ross-coulthart]] links the UAP cover-up to the Epstein files and to the American constitutional order, arguing all are symptoms of a broken accountability structure. These are his political opinions.

## Claims

- He says a Truth and Reconciliation model (from his reporting on post-apartheid South Africa) is a better route than prosecutions, but notes the program's people are not under government oversight. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:24:25-1:26:22)*
- He thinks Congress's Epstein momentum resembles the outrage behind the [[church-committee]] and hopes it could carry UAP, while doubting an "elite" or "deep state" story he once dismissed as paranoid. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:26:33-1:28:07)*
- The Washington Post and [[watergate-scandal]] led to oversight reforms, yet through it "the one secret they sat on was the UFO secret". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:29:56-1:30:47)*
- He says [[allen-dulles]] and [[james-jesus-angleton]] did illegal things and withheld information from elected authorities, which led [[john-f-kennedy]] to want to break the CIA up; he does not believe Kennedy was killed by a lone assassin, and says he is "in no doubt" Dulles's conspiracy was integrally connected to UAP because Kennedy also wanted nukes gone. See [[kennedy-assassination]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:32:03-1:33:24)*
- He says Australian friend [[geoffrey-cruickshank]] believes the MJ-12 documents are "almost certainly real", based on Truman handwriting comparison and references to things released only in 2023. See [[majestic-12]], [[mj12-disinformation-hypothesis]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:33:31-1:34:22)*
- He argues presidents (Clinton, Obama, Ford, Carter named) making defence decisions without briefing shows the state is broken. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:34:45-1:35:28)*
