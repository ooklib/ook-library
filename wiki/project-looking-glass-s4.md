Project Looking Glass is, per [[bob-lazar]], the S4 project studying the fact that the craft produces its own gravitational field and hence whether the flow of time can be distorted, not time travel but perhaps milliseconds of change *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 42:09-42:32)*. Lazar links it to the candle "frozen in time" demonstration by [[barry-s4-lab-partner]] ([[lazar-gravity-amplifier-lab-claims]]) *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:24:48-1:26:17)*. Host [[chris-ramsey]] compares it with the alleged Vatican-suppressed Chronovisor ([[chronovisor-claims]]), which Lazar had never heard of *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 44:45-45:49)*. Lazar says interfering with time would be "the most powerful weapon of all" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 44:27-44:45)*.

The name overlaps with the unrelated alleged time-viewing "Looking Glass" claims of other sources; this page records only Lazar's briefing-binder description.

## Relationships

- Part of [[lazar-briefing-projects-trio]]; sibling of [[project-galileo-s4]] and [[project-sidekick-s4]].

## Claims (a52/lazar-secret)

- Lazar says time is the big project but gravity is prerequisite, since without the propulsion machine and power source the others cannot start; he has seen no sign of time viewing. Ramsey relays second-hand claims of a sphere called Looking Glass used through consciousness to peer into time: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 32:48-34:21)*, see [[s4-project-directives-claims]].
