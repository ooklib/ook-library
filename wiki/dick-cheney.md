- David Grush called Cheney "Darth Vader himself" and "the closest person" to a "mob boss" figure
  he was aware of within the UFO legacy-program structure, stating activities "really had central
  leadership" until Cheney left office in 2009. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 3:19–3:53)*

  [[christopher-mellon|Christopher Mellon]] (1997–1998). *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 1:48:02–1:49:02)*
