[[jason-jorjani]]'s claim (from a Substack piece, "Nuking the Matrix") that nuclear detonations are a way to detect or escape the simulation, and that this explains UFOs' focus on nuclear weapons. Speculative.

## Claims

- Nazi computer scientist Konrad Zuse first proposed a universe as a "calculating space" (Rechnender Raum) and that extremely high-entropy events such as nuclear blasts could reveal the "mesh of the matrix"; Michels adds the idea of high-energy physics (Tesla, [[thomas-townsend-brown]], CERN) as an Archimedean lever and portals at Area 51, and Jorjani cites 167 Q-cleared witnesses in Robert Hastings's book. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:11:24, 3:12:20, 3:13:03, 3:13:59)*
- The only constant Nordic message is disarmament; the entities do not object to nukes on moral grounds (vitrified ancient sites, Mars), but nukes would expose the matrix, and the control system was their psychological response to learning they are in one. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:14:07, 3:14:35)*
- Sir Horsley (a British wing commander and aide to the Queen, per Jorjani's *Closer Encounters*) in the 1950s was approached by Nordic-looking "Miss Markham" and "Mr. Janice", who asked him to help disarm Britain; he objected that nukes were the only defence against them, she smiled "well done, boy", their flats were empty when authorities checked, and 20 years later he controlled the British nuclear arsenal. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:15:37, 3:16:26, 3:17:12)*
- Michels adds gorilla-mask lore from Annie Jacobsen's *Area 51* and a portal-at-a-test-site speculation. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:08:35, 3:09:50)*
