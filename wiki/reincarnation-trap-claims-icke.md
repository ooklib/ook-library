[[david-icke]]'s claim, in [[icke-ramsey-interview-2026]], that reincarnation is real but is a scam that recycles consciousness back into the matrix so its controllers do not "run out of people" to feed on. He offers it as his interpretation of near-death experiences and past-life research, not as anything demonstrated.

## Claims

- At death the biological computer stops decoding the simulation and consciousness enters the astral, where it is put "on the wheel of samsara", crossing a "river of forgetfulness" to come back for "lessons to evolve", which Icke calls "a total scam". *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 51:25-52:29)*
- He says near-death accounts share a tunnel, light, life review and being sent back with an unexplained mission, and that what lies past the "threshold" is not what experiencers are led to believe; the reincarnation cycle exists because feeding entities need people to keep returning after miserable lives. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 20:35-22:14, 1:20:03-1:21:30)*
- Returning souls pass through a "very powerful electromagnetic field" that wipes the mind like a blank sheet; on leaving, near-death experiencers meet a light, spirit guides, loved ones and robed elders and defer to them as authorities just as they do on Earth, which he says is the trap. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:22:39-1:24:43)*
- Ramsey brings up Dr. Michael Newton's hypnotic-regression work (*Journey of Souls*, thousands of sessions on planning the next incarnation); Icke's reply is to dismiss the "life lessons" framing and the idea that hardship is deliberate. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:17:10-1:17:59, 1:39:21-1:40:27)*
- The Buddhist notion that the best chance to leave the matrix comes immediately after death is accepted by Icke, and enlightenment (expanded self-identity as infinite awareness) is the way off the wheel; he says he returns "only by choice" and came this time for the work that began in 1990. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:24:59-1:26:19, 1:29:01-1:31:20)*
- He thinks the controllers cannot prevent his return, and that "God" cannot intervene because anything entering the low-frequency field would be affected by it. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:31:43-1:32:23)*
- He quotes comedian Bill Hicks's "life is like a ride" monologue and "we are the imagination of ourselves", and says the conspiracy in one line is "to hijack humanity's imagination of itself". *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:35:20-1:37:52)*
