The Church Committee is the mid-1970s Senate investigation of the intelligence agencies that [[eyes-wide-open]] names as the source of declassified material on the [[cia-mafia-assassination-plots]]. In this episode it appears only through the plots; the page is a hub for future ingests.

## Claims

- Kinzer's *Poisoner in Chief* is cited by the host as drawing on information declassified during the Church Committee and the House Select Committee on Assassinations. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:12:05-1:12:19)*
- Johnny Roselli testified in June and September 1975 and again in April 1976; this was "the first time that the CIA Mafia plots were made public". [[sam-giancana]] was killed on 19 June 1975 days before he was to testify; Roselli went missing 28 July 1976 and was found 7 August. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:20:28-1:21:40)*
- [[santo-trafficante]] was not called before it; he appeared before the House Select Committee on Assassinations in 1978 instead. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:21:40-1:22:13)*
