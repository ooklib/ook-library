[[leonard-stringfield]]'s pseudonymous physicist "Dr. Epigoni" (as transcribed) told him in the late 1960s and early 1970s he worked on a "Project Heaven" (later "Project Blue Heaven") special project at [[edwards-air-force-base]]. Recorded from [[uap-gerb]]'s summary of Stringfield's status report 6 in the Edwards video; all claims are second-hand and unverified.

## Claims (gerb/edwards-afb)

- Epigoni said he worked closely with Oppenheimer, Einstein and Teller; a flying saucer and crew had landed at Edwards, the craft kept in a tightly guarded hangar while the occupants were taken to a secure building. The 12 bodies, 5 ft tall in silvery metal suits, had eyes of one color, nose, mouth, teeth and oil-like blood. Researchers left them alone because there was no deterioration and another craft might reactivate them. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 34:06-35:02)*
- The craft had no rivets, seams or welding, was a classic saucer with a dome 40 ft across, and made of materials scientists "never dreamed could exist", with no way to enter. Epigoni said Project Blue Heaven was still active in 1990 under the DOD, with the Navy running two or three similar projects. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 35:02-35:32)*
- Gerb finds the strangest detail to be that the craft touched down and the beings walked out and lay on the ground, which he links to the craft-donation theory, [[david-grush]]'s idea that some retrievals are unattended finds, and the [[holloman-landing-film-claims]]; he ties it to the biological-robot idea. Stringfield and a fellow investigator thought Epigoni changed the body count from 12 to 4 after consulting superiors. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 35:32-36:27, 45:30-46:20)*
