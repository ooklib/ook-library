A map of alleged connected underground UFO facilities and tunnels across the American west, published in [[bill-hamilton]]'s 1992 *Cosmic Top Secret* and supplied in 1990 by a source "TA" (partly Thomas Castillo, controversial in the Dulce story). [[uap-gerb]] analyzes it in the last chapter of his DUMB video. The full list of sites shown, as compiled by the wiki owner, is under "Sites on Gerb's map" in [[deep-underground-military-bases]]. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:19:48, 1:19:56, 1:20:03)*

## Gerb's reading

- Network spans the west/southwest with major clusters in Southern California, New Mexico and Colorado; [[edwards-air-force-base]] connects to many spots, some marked "R&D" (UFO R&D without a DUMB); an underground base marked "cat" is Catalina Island; Area 51 links to Tonopah. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:20:11, 1:20:20, 1:20:29, 1:20:46)*
- Unconnected solo DUMBs include Fort Huachuca (Gerb: heavily involved in UFO programs; Army intelligence under [[albert-stubblebine]]; a Joint Interoperability Center) and, in New Mexico, Dulce, Los Alamos, Sandia/Kirtland; also Cheyenne Mountain and a base in Chihuahua, Mexico that Gerb links to [[jake-barber]]'s novel *Sentinels of Ether* (2004 retrieval-team flight into a disguised mountain base). *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:20:55, 1:21:10, 1:21:28, 1:21:37, 1:21:53)*
- Overlaid on RAND's [[dumb-tunnel-and-maglev-network]] VHST map, routes coincide through northern Arizona/New Mexico, split at Southern California toward San Diego and Sacramento, run north to Portland/Washington, and hit Denver (large FEMA underground complex). Gerb argues UFO DUMB networks have central hubs and local branches. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:17:05, 1:22:10, 1:22:17, 1:22:26, 1:22:35)*
- Overlaid on the 2019 DoD lab/range map, matches include Fort Huachuca, Cheyenne Mountain, Dugway (and its two MRTFBs), Los Alamos, White Sands, Sandia and Kirtland (Air Force Research Laboratory space vehicles and directed energy directorates); Groom Lake is absent from the DoD map; also a "fourth estate" DMEA in Northern California. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:22:49, 1:23:48, 1:24:03, 1:24:10, 1:24:19)*
- R&D-only marks include the National Strategic Research Institute (a UARC founded 2012, marked in 1990-92, which Gerb finds striking), Fort Sill (UFO R&D), NSWC Indian Head/McAlester detachment (labeled storage; cf. [[randy-anderson]] at [[naval-surface-warfare-center-crane]]), Fort Leavenworth research unit and Fort Hood Army Research Unit (not marked as DUMB despite Stringfield's reports). *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:25:01, 1:25:26, 1:25:43, 1:25:51, 1:26:06)*
- Additional DUMB marks Gerb wants investigated: Fort Stockton, Fort Irwin, Vandenberg AFB, Chocolate Mountains range, 29 Palms, Hutchinson AFS, Peterson AFB, Kingsley (NE), Tulsa, Riverton (WY). Sauder has spoken with Hamilton about the map. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:26:20, 1:26:28, 1:26:42, 1:26:51)*
