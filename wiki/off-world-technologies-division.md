The "Off-World Technologies Division" is a program named by Green Beret witness RA ([[randy-anderson]]), who says that between 2013 and 2015 a contractor instructor took him and one other TS/SCI soldier deep beneath [[naval-surface-warfare-center-crane]] and casually announced they were entering it. The account was first told publicly by [[uap-gerb]] in [[gerb-off-world-tech-video]]. Gerb says he found no other mention of the name anywhere. All of this is one anonymous witness's uncorroborated claim.

## Claims

- The division was unlabeled, sat behind several checkpoints and an elevator descent, and looked far more advanced than the rest of Crane; RA and the other soldier were held in a viewing room overlooking a lab, with the contractor guide and one staff member present. *([gerb/off-world-tech](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-07-17 - Off-World Technologies Division - UAP Technology Reverse Engineering.md>) 11:41-14:11)*
- Two objects were on display: a basketball-sized seamless, liquid-looking metallic sphere with a hand-sized indent that appeared to float off its podium, and a gauntlet-like clear tablet projecting hieroglyphic-like symbols. RA says the guide said both interact with consciousness (only some people trigger a response) and that the Navy and contractor did not understand how to operate them. *([gerb/off-world-tech](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-07-17 - Off-World Technologies Division - UAP Technology Reverse Engineering.md>) 14:11-19:04)*
- The tablet was considered a weapon because it discharged an energy pulse and injured someone during recovery; RA stresses this was second-hand talk between the two staff, who spoke casually as if at lunch. *([gerb/off-world-tech](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-07-17 - Off-World Technologies Division - UAP Technology Reverse Engineering.md>) 19:29-21:29)*
- Gerb compares the sphere surface to the "mother of pearl" effect of the 1997 Peru craft ([[jonathan-weygandt]]) and the cognitive effects to [[gary-nolan]]'s research and the anchors in [[special-forces-witness-infographics]]. *([gerb/off-world-tech](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-07-17 - Off-World Technologies Division - UAP Technology Reverse Engineering.md>) 15:20-19:29)*
- Gerb's speculation: the guide was an [[science-applications-international-corporation]] employee, and SAIC's 2020 $133M Crane mission-engineering contract (special capabilities branch) may fund the division. *([gerb/off-world-tech](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-07-17 - Off-World Technologies Division - UAP Technology Reverse Engineering.md>) 35:40-36:10, 49:37)*
- Gerb finds one possible lead: the alleged FDR memo "Special Committee on Non-Terrestrial Science and Technology" in the MJ-12 leaks, discussed by [[ryan-s-wood]], which names [[vannevar-bush]]. *([gerb/off-world-tech](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-07-17 - Off-World Technologies Division - UAP Technology Reverse Engineering.md>) 40:39-41:55)*
- Gerb also relays a second witness, "Martin", who allegedly described a clear basketball-sized sphere from a briefcase that chose who it interacted with. *([gerb/off-world-tech](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-07-17 - Off-World Technologies Division - UAP Technology Reverse Engineering.md>) 52:39-53:20)*
