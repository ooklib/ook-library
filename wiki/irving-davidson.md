Isaac Irving Davidson (description: "I. Irving Davidson") was, per [[eyes-wide-open]], the Washington "super lobbyist" for [[clint-murchison]], his sons and an array of dubious clients, and the network hub that let the Texas oil independents reach Washington, organized crime, Latin American dictators and Israel. The host says he will "continue to come up in future episodes."

## Claims

- John Davis (Mafia Kingfish): "the epitome of Washington lobbyist, a schemer and promoter with a vast international network of powerful acquaintances." The host compares him to Roy Cohn (see [[roy-cohn]]) and Roger Stone: power brokers for hire. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 39:48-40:28)*
- Clients: the Somoza, Duvalier and Trujillo families (Nicaragua, Haiti, Dominican Republic), Israeli defense firms, and organized-crime figures including [[carlos-marcello]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 40:43-41:24)*
- Late 1960s-early 1970s he worked to prove [[jimmy-hoffa]]'s innocence; per John Davis he had earlier used Hoffa to get a "huge Teamsters pension fund loan" for Murchison. Hoffa was released Dec 23, 1971 when [[richard-nixon]] commuted his sentence. Host speculates Nixon and Davidson may have met at Del Charro, perhaps as early as the early 1960s; "hard to say." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 41:24-42:41)*
- Lobbied for Israel Aerospace Industries (founded by his "good friend" Al Schwimmer, whose prior employers the ASR renders "loed and tww"; my inference Lockheed and TWA, flagged) and Israel Military Industries (Uzi). Per Jonathan Marshall (Dark Quadrant) his work was closer to arms sales, e.g., over 100 Israeli staghound armored cars to Somoza's government, and he told Nixon's 1960 campaign manager that "Israeli military intelligence hopes Nixon would become president." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 42:41-43:44)*
- Host speculation: it is "likelihood" he was moonlighting as an Israeli spy for Mossad (ASR "mad") or military intelligence. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 43:44-44:00)*
