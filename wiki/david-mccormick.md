David McCormick is a hedge-fund billionaire turned US Senator from Pennsylvania. More Perfect
Union presents his appearance on the leaked 2023 [[bohemian-grove]] attendance list, and a
cluster of campaign donations from fellow attendees, as an example of the club's continuing
alleged political influence.

## Claims

- Appears on the 2023 Bohemian Grove attendee list in the Mandalay camp, the same camp listed for
  Michael Bloomberg and Henry Kissinger (Kissinger's last recorded Grove attendance before his
  death) *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 14:36, 14:44, 14:49)*.
- At the time of the 2023 encampment, McCormick had already lost a Pennsylvania Senate primary to
  Dr. Oz; two months after the encampment he announced another Senate run, and this time won
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 14:49, 14:59, 15:06)*.
- [[daniel-boguslaw]] cross-referenced the Mandalay camp attendee list against McCormick's public
  campaign donors and found 11 men from that camp had donated to his campaign, not counting
  additional dark-money contributions
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 15:06, 15:14, 15:18)*.
- As Senator, appeared alongside President Trump and Bechtel CEO Brendan Bechtel at an event
  announcing a major AI-investment push for Pennsylvania data centers and power plants; see
  [[bechtel-corporation]] for the related contracting claims
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 15:18, 15:28, 15:31)*.

## Relationships

## Speculation flag

More Perfect Union implies, but does not directly prove, a causal link between McCormick's Grove
attendance, the donor overlap Boguslaw found, and his subsequent Senate win; this wiki records the
donor-overlap finding as reported fact from public donation records but the causal narrative
around it as the video's own interpretive argument.
