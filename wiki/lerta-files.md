## Claims

- Ramsey says the "Lerta files" (early 2000s, allegedly in Sweden) describe a control system working with governments and colonies of reptilians underneath Asia, and that it corroborates parts of Icke's account. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 54:03-54:31)*
- Icke's reply is that entities can be physical and non-physical, "all of the above", with some within the earth at human frequency, while the real control point is the astral dimension. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 54:44-55:08)*
