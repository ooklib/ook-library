Regional Hospital in Varginha, Minas Gerais, is where per [[james-fox]] a captured being was taken in January 1996, and where [[italo-venturelli]] is still head neurosurgeon. A second hospital, Humanitas, is also named.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says there is a plaque outside the hospital stating that a live being was brought there; staff at reception told him it was brought through the back door. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:05:36-1:06:41)*
- He says one neurosurgeon worked there that night; Venturelli says the ICU bed is where the being lay. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:06:25-1:06:33, 1:49:04-1:49:25)*
- Fox says the hospital staff caught the crew filming in the ICU and expelled them. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:51:09-1:54:33)*
- Marco Chereze was treated at Regional Hospital or Humanitas (Fox is unsure) and died in the ICU; both hospitals had cameras used to film surgeries. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:08:15-1:08:37, 50:34-50:57)*
