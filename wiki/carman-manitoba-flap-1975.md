A 1975 wave of luminous-object sightings around Carman, Manitoba (about 35 miles southwest of Winnipeg) that began in February 1975 and drew [[grant-cameron]] into UFO research. A CKY television crew filmed a light jumping from ground level to about 5,000 feet, and the footage appears in the 1979 documentary "UFOs: Past, Present and Future" ("UFOs Has Begun" in the ASR).

## Claims

- Cameron describes a slowly pulsing, dead-light plasma object crossing the road at 100 to 150 feet, and a second night of "ping pong ball" jumps in front of 20-plus people. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 6:08-11:38)*
- The CKY film (13 May 1975, cameraman credited as Alan Carr in the documentary narration) was shot by a film editor who filled in; Cameron says the original film is missing. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 26:44-28:03)*
- Pilot Bob Diemert told Cameron the lights were there "for the nuclear weapons" and recounted an Air Force pilot who refused an order to ram an object cornered over a weapon. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 12:10-13:20)*
- Cameron links the Manitoba activity to the 1975 opening of the Stanley Mickelsen ABM site in North Dakota (about 100 more missiles, closed by November 1975, roughly $6 billion) and to the Grand Forks and Minot fields. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 13:20-15:47)*
- Cameron says late October to November 1975 saw UFOs over Loring, Wurtsmith, Minot and Malmstrom-area bases, and that every night Travis Walton was missing (5 to 10 November) an object was over a US nuclear site. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 41:47-42:30)*
- Cameron distinguishes modalities that change over time (nuclear sites in the 1950s, then crop circles, mutilations, orbs) and says there were no orbs or light language in 1975. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 29:24-30:24)*
