Major General John K. Singlaub, the "General," is portrayed by Eyes Wide Open as a leading figure of American counter-insurgency and guerrilla warfare: an OSS Detachment 202 alumnus who served in Korea, China, Laos and Vietnam, ran the US Army side of the secret war in Laos, and later became a central figure in Iran-Contra dark-money networks. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 12:34-13:10)*

## Claims

- Allegedly trained paramilitary forces in Nicaragua and Afghanistan; the US Army official in charge of the secret war in Laos in the 1960s-70s, supporting [[vang-pao]]'s forces. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 12:53-13:06)*
- Relieved from duty in 1977 for criticizing Carter's Korea troop drawdowns; then, funded by Texas billionaire [[nelson-bunker-hunt]], founded the [[western-goals-foundation]] (funding the Contras in Nicaragua and Honduras), led the U.S. Council for World Freedom (1980; ASR 'U.S Council on world freedoms') and consulted for Geo Militech, a liaison between arms dealers and anti-communist guerrillas in Afghanistan and Central America. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 13:16-13:58)*
- Host says his 1980s role paralleled Helliwell's earlier one, managing organizations and shell companies moving untraceable funds; both guerrilla armies were tied to the narcotics trade ([[cia-drug-paradigm]]). *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 13:58-14:30)*
- In a documentary clip he describes [[mitchell-werbell]] as an expert in special weapons such as silencers, respected by Special Forces people; the host says they appear to have worked together later. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 19:27-20:19)*
- Named in LAOS 1 as a member of the 1945 [[deer-mission]], described as 'future MACV-SOG leader' (ASR 'makave so'). *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 18:46-19:06)*
