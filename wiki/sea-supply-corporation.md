Sea Supply Corporation (ASR: "SE Supply", "CE Supply") was, per Eyes Wide Open, an American-
incorporated shell company created by [[paul-helliwell]] to route weapons and equipment to
[[li-mi]]'s KMT force in Burma while masking US involvement.

## Claims

- Opened an office in Bangkok headed by Sherman Joost (former OSS Detachment 101 Burma guerrilla
  leader) on Helliwell's behalf, with [[alfred-cox]] (Hong Kong CIA station chief, CAT's CIA
  man). *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 32:22-33:27)*
- Named [[willis-bird]]'s import-export firm its general agent. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 33:20-33:38)*
- Remained active after official aid ended; [[phao-sriyanon]] allegedly picked up weapons from it and
  exchanged them at the Thai-Burma border for opium. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 41:16-41:39)*

## Relationships
