One of [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]'s six FFRDCs, formerly known as "C3I FFRDC," sponsored by the
DoD and the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering (USD R&E), located in
McLean ("MLAN"), Virginia. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 42:14–42:30)*

## Claims

- Quoted mission: to give the US and allies "the ability to anticipate and solve the most
  consequential national security problems requiring integration across missions,
  capabilities, organizations, and nations." *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 43:02–43:18)*
- Works with air/space forces on space warfighting and rapid acquisition; with DoD/services on
  integrated air and missile defense and joint C2; with the IC on strategic deterrence and
  advanced analytics — several described with a knowing "wink wink" by Gerb, implying covert
  UFO-program overlap. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 43:18–43:52)*
- Primary contract/administrative agent is AFLCMC (Air Force Life Cycle Management Center), part
  of [[air-force-material-command]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 43:52–44:09)*
- Available to any DoD or IC element requiring RDT&E expertise, though Gerb states MITRE's
  legacy-program involvement he's aware of "revolves specifically around the US Navy," not the
  Air Force administratively. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 44:30–44:58)*
- Dr. [[peter-highnam]] (principal deputy assistant secretary of war for critical technologies,
  USD R&E) is cited as a "very very very senior and experienced legacy program lead" connected
  to this office. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 42:40–43:02)*
