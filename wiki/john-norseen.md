John Norseen (ASR: "Norsine") was, per [[jesse-michels]], a former Navy pilot turned neuroengineer and weapons designer for [[lockheed-martin]], funded by NASA and [[darpa]] for mind-reading research, who published a 1999 report on "Biofusion" (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:10:55-1:11:27)*). Michels presents his work as the Lockheed prototype for the brain-machine flight control [[jake-barber]] describes; this is an inference by Michels, not a claim by Norseen.

## Claims

- Biofusion: a brain-computer interface that turns thoughts into commands with "no joystick or buttons", mapping a pilot's electrical pattern to headings and injecting corrections into the pilot's mind via electromagnetic waves; Norseen theorized "brain prints" (like fingerprints, per his colleague Duncan Lurie) that could be captured and run back to induce states like aggression, calm or ecstasy. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:11:27-1:13:41)*
- A technical writeup by "autonomous019" cites a Lockheed Martin R&D report titled "Biofusion, reflexive control in neuroergonomic integration in adaptive cockpits", funded under contract N66001, a prefix tied to the Navy's Space and Naval Warfare Systems Center in San Diego (now Naval Information Warfare Center) but which NASA describes as an aviation weather information systems project; Michels reads this as a program buried in a weather contract. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:13:45-1:15:27)*
- Michels notes the San Diego ties are near the 2004 Nimitz Tic Tac sighting, and cites [[ross-coulthart]]'s claim that the Tic Tac is Lockheed technology. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:14:31-1:14:59)* See [[2004-nimitz-encounter]] and [[tic-tac-factory-uso]].
- His work vanishes from public view in the early 2000s; a colleague relays that before a planned lecture at the Naval War College in Newport he emailed that he had "RVed" (remote viewed) the upcoming lecture, signed "John not heard nor seen", and about two weeks later he was found dead in a motel room. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:15:36-1:17:10)* Michels offers no cause of death; the reading of the email as code is the colleague's.
- Roughly 20 years later a Lockheed patent by Charles Chase and Gerald Yonas ("cognitive enhancement using feedback") describes a closed-loop brain-state stimulation system; Chase says he does not know Norseen. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:17:18-1:18:14)*
