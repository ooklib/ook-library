Operation Mongoose was the Kennedy White House's post-Bay of Pigs anti-Castro program, described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as "a primarily non-lethal regime change operation" meant to spur a popular uprising inside Cuba, devised by the Kennedy brothers with [[edward-lansdale]] as their "self-appointed covert action guru." It is the White House track of what the host calls Kennedy's "two-track" Cuba policy, the other track being the CIA's own, largely unauthorized operations out of [[jm-wave-station]]. This page holds the `ewa/jfk-cia` primer's account only. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:03-1:47, 2:25-2:48)*

## Claims

- Goal: revolution "led domestically by the Cuban people themselves," ideally with a cooperative CIA and its Miami network of Cuban exiles; the CIA, however, did not want to hand its Gulf Coast paramilitary programs to the White House. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:17-2:05)*
- Lansdale was brought into the White House in November 1961 to run it; [[robert-f-kennedy]] headed the White House Cuba task force; [[mcgeorge-bundy]] sat on the Special Group Augmented. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 45:43-45:56, 47:17-47:30, 55:35-55:49)*
- Timeline: about three months after Lansdale arrived (February 1962) he presented a month-by-month declassified timetable; components were infiltration of CIA-backed exiles plus "hit-and-run raids" on Cuban facilities and industries, likened to "hundreds of small pin pricks"; the popular uprising was pencilled in for October 1962. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 58:17-59:29)*
- Host: this was "exceedingly optimistic and outright delusional" to CIA staff who understood the situation in Cuba; per David Corn, Lansdale "confessed little was known of the real situation inside Cuba." Many authors call Mongoose a "showpiece" rather than a real attempt to dislodge Castro. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 58:05-58:17, 59:29-59:53)*
- Lansdale compared it to the American Revolution (quoted via Talbot, *Brothers*); per Talbot, CIA and Pentagon officials "knew that Operation Mongoose had no chance of succeeding and that a military invasion was the only way." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:00:00-1:01:06)*
- Host: alongside the planned invasion route, the CIA's "chosen way" was "liquidation of the leader," via [[zr-rifle]], which the agency continued "in quiet defiance." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:01:12-1:01:43)*

## Relationships

- Run by [[edward-lansdale]] for [[john-f-kennedy]] and [[robert-f-kennedy]]; opposed in spirit by CIA hardliners ([[william-harvey]], [[richard-helms]]); see [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]], [[jm-wave-station]], [[fidel-castro]].
- Wiki-internal observation: earlier episodes (`ewa/operation-40`) already described the post-Bay-of-Pigs guerrilla reorganization as "Bobby Kennedy's show" under Mongoose.

## Additional claims (The CIA's Blond Ghost, ewa/shackley)

- Host framing: Mongoose (White House-directed sabotage/regime change, restarted early 1962) is the backdrop for [[ted-shackley]]'s JM/WAVE tenure; he ran the station 1962-64, "king of his own thief" (ASR, probably fiefdom). *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 0:01-0:32, 1:19:44-1:19:59)*

## Additional claims (The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE, ewa/jmwave)


- Launched February 1962 jointly under [[robert-f-kennedy]] and [[edward-lansdale]]; the host says it directly caused the massive expansion of [[jm-wave-station]] personnel and activity over two years, and that funds flowed "to seemingly any and all Cuban exiles" for eight months (February-October 1962). Lansdale's timetable slated a Cuban uprising for October 1962; the host calls it a pipe dream even without the missile crisis. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 0:50-1:25, 5:42-6:41)*
- The White House Special Group Augmented (RFK, Lansdale, [[mcgeorge-bundy]]) competed with CIA's Task Force W under [[william-harvey]]; the "10,000 small pinprick strategy" of sabotage raids (e.g., the Matahambre mine) mostly counterproductive. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 38:56-39:35, 42:20-44:49)*
- Missile crisis "largely signaled its end"; on January 4, 1963 Bundy said "There is well-universal agreement that Mongoose is at a dead end." *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 53:29-56:00)*
