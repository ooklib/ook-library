SAF/AQ, the Office of the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, Technology and
Logistics ("SECAF ATNL"), sits within the headquarters of the Air Force (HAF) under the
Secretary of the Air Force. It oversees Air Force research, development, acquisition, and
program-sustainment activities with an annual budget over $60 billion across more than 550
acquisition programs, including the F-35 and B-21 Raider. Gerb states his central thesis for
this investigation up front: "the vast majority of Air Force UFO Legacy Program operations
reside within" SAF/AQ. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 17:56–18:22,
32:33–34:33)*

## Claims

- Gerb's recurring witness "Ed" — a retired Air Force officer with firsthand experience in the
  [[hidden-wing-air-force-program|Hidden Wing]] programs, previously discussed via Steven Greer's
  notes — served at the Pentagon connected to SAF/AQ following his time at the
  [[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards 412th Test Wing]]. Ed reported that in consecutive fiscal
  years, 30–40% of total Air Force TOA (total obligation authority) budget was slashed and sent
  to the Air Force black budget before review by Air Force financial management (SAF/FM) — which
  Gerb frames as a direct example of misappropriation feeding derivative-vehicle/ARV TN
  programs. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 35:38–39:08)*
- Reviewing 14 past holders of the SECAF ATNL position, Gerb finds 8 of 14 had pre-government
  careers at one or more of Lockheed Martin, SAIC, Northrop Grumman, TRW, LTV
  Aerospace/E-Systems/Aerospace Corporation, or General Dynamics — contractors Gerb has
  separately accused of UFO legacy-program involvement. Named examples:
  [[lawrence-j-delaney|Lawrence J. Delaney]] (1991–1992) and [[sue-c-payton|Sue "Susie" C.
  Payton]] (2006–2009). *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 39:29–43:44)*
- SAF/AQ works closely with [[air-force-research-laboratory|AFRL]] (SAF/AQ appoints AFRL's
  commander as a technology executive officer for select programs) and partners with the
  [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-acquisition-and-sustainment|USD A&S]], which Gerb had
  separately identified as integral to NRO UFO legacy programs — raising the question of whether
  the National Security Council uses this USD(A&S)–SAF/AQ (specifically [[saf-aqr|AQR]])
  partnership and its "technology transfer vessels" to slash-budget or hide materials.
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 34:10–34:48, 47:36–48:16)*
- Gerb identifies the [[rapid-capabilities-office|Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO)]], which sits
  within SAF/AQ, as the vehicle that lets Air Force acquisition run UFO legacy-program RDT&E
  outside congressional/Air Force oversight. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md)
  51:04–55:25)*
- Lt. Gen. [[donna-shipton|Donna D. Shipton]] served as military deputy in the SAF/AQ office
  (SECAF ATNL), responsible for "research and development, test, production, production support,
  and modernization of Air Force programs worth more than $60 billion annually," from August
  2022 to December 2023, immediately before taking over as commander of the Air Force Life Cycle
  Management Center. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 1:01:10–1:02:10)*
