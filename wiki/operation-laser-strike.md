US Southern Command (SOUTHCOM) classified counter-narcotics operation in Peru during the 1990s, the setting of [[jonathan-weygandt]]'s alleged 1997 UFO crash encounter ([[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]]). Everything here is as described by [[uap-gerb]] from open-source documents; parts of the operation are said to remain classified.

## Claims (gerb/weygandt)

- Gerb describes it as one of numerous classified US worldwide counter-narcotics operations under SOUTHCOM, involving up to 20 agencies (USMC, DEA, Air Force, CIA, National Guard, Army Special Forces and the Peruvian Air Force), with three phases targeting illegal airspace use, alternate transport modes and production. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 7:22-8:03)*
- It used TPS-43 and relocatable over-the-horizon (ROTHR) radar and, Gerb says, possibly covertly satellites of the [[defense-support-program]]; it followed the smaller 1996 Operation Green Clover in Peru and Colombia. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 8:03-8:28)*
- From April 1996 to April 1997 its radar tracked 45,000 aircraft, 5,390 "tracks of interest", 150 suspected traffickers and 21 forced or shot down, with the exact shootdown count classified. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 8:28-8:56)*
- Three primary sites were Pucallpa (ASR "Pukulpa/Bukalpa"), Andoas and [[iquitos-peru]] (ASR "Aikitos/Iikitos"), where a Marine Forces Atlantic (MARFORLANT) riverine training team trained Peruvian forces; SOUTHCOM authorized up to 14 special forces deployments in 1997, and the [[us-army-7th-special-forces-group]] supported South American counterdrug work. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 9:03-9:34)*
- Weygandt's USMC Marine Air Control Group 28 (MACG-28) from Cherry Point replaced aging Hawk batteries with Stinger and Avenger platforms and provided perimeter security for radar installations. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 7:13-7:30)*
- Gerb says he confirmed that MACG-28 Marines deployed to Laser Strike, and that Hawk batteries were phased out of Laser Strike in 1997 (the Marine Corps kept Hawk until 2002). *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:30:10-1:30:25, 1:32:21-1:32:28)*
- Gerb proposes Weygandt was stationed not at Pucallpa but at USAF radar Detachment 5 in Iquitos, because a Marine he contacted would not discuss Laser Strike bases (Pucallpa has been publicly linked to Laser Strike since 2001) and the C-130 flight from Lima fits Iquitos (about 630 miles) better than Pucallpa (about 300 miles). *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:12:36-1:14:15)*
- Gerb says USAF personnel at Detachment 5 he identified were Lieutenant Colonel Gayla Biles, Second Lieutenant Randall Rothman and Staff Sergeant Robert Macau of the 649th Combat Logistics Support Squadron, none of whom he could reach. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:27:02-1:27:44)*
