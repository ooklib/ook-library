[[matthew-pines]] and [[jesse-michels]] discuss AI as central to the alleged UFO program: for classification, for identifying UAP, and for decoding communication with non-human intelligence. Much is speculation; one key item is hearsay from a former senior DoD official.

## Claims

- Pines: at a National Security Council meeting reported back by Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz, a senior NSC staffer responded that "we have classified entire areas of fundamental physics before, don't think we can do the same for AI", rebutting the claim AI is "just math". (Pines calls this hearsay.) *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:47:08-3:47:31)*
- Pines says a very senior former DoD official who managed collections of special access programs (SAPs), including AI ones, described a meeting with AARO and was told "AI, quantum, and the Grusch stuff" are a triangle; Pines notes such officials routinely give "cover" briefings and "quantum" is a government catch-all. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:47:53-3:50:10)*
- Pines says the program [[michael-shellenberger]] wrote about apparently uses AI to identify UAP and maybe ARVs by characteristic morphology (see [[sentient-nro-ai-program]], [[immaculate-constellation]]). *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:50:19-3:50:42)*
- He argues AI is good at exploring and translating across "real space" conceptual distance (like decoding whale and dolphin sounds), so a top priority would be an encoding between human and NHI concepts, as in the film Arrival, with AI "maybe already extremely important". *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:51:07-3:52:59)*
- Michels notes [[robert-mercer]] of [[renaissance-technologies]] and the NSA's decryption skills match that task; Pines says physics itself is like decrypting the world. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:52:59-3:54:12)*
- Michels raises [[danny-sheehan]]'s claimed Carter-era look at Blue Book files showing a crashed craft with hieroglyph-like symbols and asks if AI could decode them; Pines imagines a compartmentalized program with biological, materials, propulsion-physics, xenolinguistics and sociocultural branches, and says "you need some Immaculate Constellation to quarantine some of the comms". *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:54:50-3:56:55)*
- Pines guesses the government would explore non-transformer AI architectures in secret, because its secrecy requirement rules out huge infrastructure builds, and that there may be "orthogonal vectors" beyond OpenAI and Anthropic. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:57:11-3:58:45)*
- He warns AI-enabled online manipulation degrades society's ability to make sense of a disclosure. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:58:50-3:59:35)*
- A RAND initiative for global control of compute is mentioned by Michels; see [[rand-corporation]]. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:47:33-3:47:53)*
