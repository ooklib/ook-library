Cay Sal is a small Bahamian islet detached from the main archipelago, about 62 miles (100 km) south of the Florida Keys and 31 miles (50 km) north of Cuba. [[eyes-wide-open]] presents it as an ideal staging ground for amphibious operations against Cuba, and as a locus where [[zapata-offshore]] and [[howard-hughes]] overlapped.

## Claims

- Name roughly translates from Spanish as "salt island" (a point raised by a patreon supporter), which the host speculates inspired the salt-themed cryptonyms in [[wubriny-and-wusaline]]. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 19:46-20:34)*
- A 1958 FBI memo: [[howard-hughes]] leased Cay Sal a year earlier from Clarence O. Moody, described as Hughes's middleman in delivering support to Cuban exiles; [[zapata-offshore]] leased the plot next to it. The memo also places Zapata Offshore offices in Marathon, Florida, and a Hughes front, the Panella's Fish Company, whose docks served as a helipad for Hughes Tool Company helicopters. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 20:34-22:28)*
- Newspaper articles: a small landing strip existed on the island, previously used by the Bahamian Army for training. Host speculation: a transshipment point for CIA-backed arms traffickers from Louisiana (centered on [[david-ferrie]] and the [[civil-air-patrol]], with [[jack-ruby]] and [[barry-seal]] named as smugglers), perhaps through the airstrip. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 22:58-23:44)*
- Bush's rig "Scorpion" was installed off Cay Sal in 1958; Trento and Russ Baker speculate it served as a training site for Cuban exiles. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 24:37-25:09)*
- Nearby No Name Key (west of Marathon) hosted a famous privately funded Cuban exile camp; many speculate the No Name Key group was funded by Hughes, and that it later relocated to a Louisiana camp speculated to have trained Kennedy's future assassins (host defers detail). *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 21:23-21:56)*
