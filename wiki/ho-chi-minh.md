Ho Chi Minh (ASR "hoochi Min", "hoi Min", "hoan") is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the Vietnamese resistance leader whom OSS agents of the [[deer-mission]] aided in 1945, and to whom Washington's refusal of support is called a "massive foreign policy mistake". Name normalization from ASR is my inference.

## Claims

- Suffering from malaria in 1945, he was treated by OSS Detachment 202 agents parachuted into rural Vietnam ([[deer-mission]]). *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 18:43-19:06)*
- In 1945 he and his Viet Minh, the main threat to the Japanese, appeared "nationalist first and communist second", open to cooperation with the West; OSS reports recommended backing him. He quoted the American Declaration of Independence. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 19:29-20:09)*
- This changed when American agents learned he had spent a long time in the Soviet Union and was an "ardent communist". *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 20:09-20:22)*
- Feb 1946 cable to President [[harry-truman]], evoking the Atlantic Charter and the San Francisco (UN) Charter, "largely ignored" as Truman focused on the Cold War, expecting the KMT to win in China. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 21:15-22:21)*
- Host's counterfactual: had Truman backed him, Vietnam might have stayed neutral like Yugoslavia; he calls it a "massive foreign policy mistake". Speculation flagged as the host's opinion. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 22:21-22:55)*
