Jacques Vallee is the French-American computer scientist and ufologist whose books (notably Messengers of Deception) and published journals are the main intellectual frame for guest [[jason-samosa]] in the `jm/cover-up` episode. The episode uses him as a source for a "control system" or belief-shaping reading of UFOs, for the AAWSAP-era journals, and as validation of the Tom DeLonge/Podesta story. This page records what the episode attributes to him; the wiki treats these as claims about his positions.

## Claims

- Samosa says Vallee looks at UFOs' impact on society and belief systems rather than the extraterrestrial hypothesis, and has said we may be engaging with an artificial intelligence created by a non-human intelligence; the phenomenon may be "designed to interface with our mind" like a dream. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 5:52, 6:29, 1:05:17)*
- Vallee warns in Messengers of Deception that a mass appearance of UFOs could let cults blossom while trust in government collapses. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 11:04)*
- Samosa cites Vallee's most recent journals as recording, in early 2013, rumors from the White House of a planned "confirmation, not disclosure", and hints that the [[aawsap]] scientists concluded an existential threat may have been detected. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 43:19, 44:16)*
- Samosa says the journals validate the [[tom-delonge]]/[[john-podesta]] contacts and show Vallee was skeptical of what they were trying to achieve. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 41:10, 44:39)*
- Vallee's journals reportedly recount [[diana-pasulka]] relaying that [[tim-taylor-nasa]] said not to talk about hybrids. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 2:08:58)*
- The "Capella" database of about 260,000 cases with seven levels of analysis is presented as the biggest study of UFO data, and Samosa urges focusing on it rather than scripture. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 44:07, 1:04:18)*
- Vallee reportedly cited a private European bank of the 1800s as the possible origin of the hidden power behind this; Samosa suggests [[charles-hambro]]'s bank would fit. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:38:59)*
- In Messengers of Deception a source called Major Murphy suggests people built flying saucers in the war years and that their real advantage was "psychotronic effect", adding "why are you talking about the Nazis, other people became involved". *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:27:34)*
- Tom DeLonge's original story (contact at Lockheed Skunk Works) was validated by Vallee, per Samosa. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 35:29)*
