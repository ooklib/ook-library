The original notes and manuscript of [[philip-j-corso]], about 176 pages, released in Italy in 2003 and in the US in 2011, after Corso's death in 1998 at age 83. UAP Gerb treats it as Corso's unfiltered testimony and as the credible counterpart to the co-written bestseller [[the-day-after-roswell-book]] (about 130 pages longer). Gerb calls it "tough reading", repetitive, but useful. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 4:32-5:58, 1:23:40-1:23:57)*

## Claims and contents

- Released because Corso disliked the bestseller: per his son [[philip-corso-jr]], Corso marked up everything he did not say or like but gave up halfway; [[john-b-alexander]] and others saw the manuscript before his death and remarked that sensational details in the book were missing from it. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 5:05-5:42)*
- Absent from the manuscript, present in the book: Corso intimidating the CIA director of covert operations into self-deleting, the [[strom-thurmond]] foreword, the first chapter narrating the Roswell crash in detail, links to Roscoe Hillenkoetter and the [[majestic-12]] list (in the manuscript Corso acknowledges the [[eisenhower-briefing-document]] but says he never saw it at the NSC or White House). *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 5:51-6:43, 1:08:03-1:08:22)*
- Its introduction lays out the 1947-1958 "dark ages" of Army R&D, the 1958 consolidation under [[arthur-trudeau]], the "golden age" 1958-63 and the seeding of TUO into industry ([[corso-technology-seeding-claims]]). *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 22:24-23:47)*
- The manuscript omits the 1947 Fort Riley body sighting of [[corso-affidavit-1998]] (which is in the book) but places the [[corso-white-sands-nhi-encounter-claims]] at the very end. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 43:46-44:11, 1:16:33-1:16:47)*
- States that extraterrestrials "never gave humans a thing" voluntarily: every advantage came by accident or force ([[ebe-extraterrestrial-biological-entity-claims]]). *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 35:15-35:40)*
