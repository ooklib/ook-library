A short documentary-style video (about 16 minutes, dated 2024-01-21) in which [[uap-gerb]] covers the 1979 Manises incident, which he calls the most famous UFO case in Spain and the first recorded emergency landing of a commercial airliner because of UFOs. The source page is [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K_BZkOAw6E0). Gerb uses the original pilot radio audio and flight rendering from the YouTube channel MPC Flight Recreations and links it in the description. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 0:00-1:19)*

## Claims

- The case is told in [[manises-ufo-incident-1979]]; Gerb closes by saying he thinks "this case is legitimate". *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 15:11-16:00)*
- He relates the Mirage F1 avionics jamming to the [[2004-nimitz-encounter]] (where [[david-fravor]] says the objects jammed the jets' radar and sensors) and to the [[tehran-ufo-incident-1976]]. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 9:26-11:51)*
- He runs through a skeptical "debunk panel" (refinery flares, planets, a US Fleet electronic-warfare explanation) and rejects it as too many coincidences. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 11:58-15:11)*
- He says he is inspired to make more standalone case documentaries, including a global air-force UFO encounter video with cases from the US and Peru, and that iceberg part three is still coming. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 16:00-16:33)*
