Dr. J. Robert Beyster (ASR-mangled as "Baster") founded
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] on 3 February 1969.

## Claims

- Served in the US Navy during WWII; earned a PhD in nuclear physics in 1950; worked at
  Westinghouse's Atomic Power Division on the nuclear submarine program; joined
  [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los Alamos]] as a research physicist; chaired the accelerator
  physics department at General Atomics. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 7:16–7:48)*
- Self-described as "not the brilliant flash of inspiration type of entrepreneur ... rather ...
  a persistent builder type." *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 7:48–7:56)*
- Immediately stacked SAIC with General Atomics scientists and made it one of the first
  employee-owned companies, using ownership as a recruitment/retention tool; also packed the
  payroll and board with generals, diplomats, spies, and cabinet officers from the company's
  inception. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 7:56–8:58)*
