A roughly 2h23m interview (uploaded 2026-01-09, DEBRIEFED ep. 70) in which [[chris-ramsey]] (Area52) sits down with filmmaker [[james-fox]] for a scene-by-scene breakdown of the new cut of his documentary [[moment-of-contact-documentary]] ("Moment of Contact: New Revelations of Alien Encounters"), about the alleged 1996 [[varginha-ufo-incident-1996]] in Brazil. The video opens with a viewer letter about a craft seen over Austin Studios (2022), then an exclusive clip of neurosurgeon [[italo-venturelli]]; Ramsey says he is "as near 100%" convinced as he can be, while Fox says it is the best documented recent case he knows of. Everything is testimony and second-hand relay of what witnesses told Fox; the wiki records it as attributed claims.
