Lewis Rosenstiel, a former Prohibition-era bootlegger who became a legitimate businessman as
founder of the Schenley Liquor Corporation, is described by Eyes Wide Open as a "cunning but
hot-headed operator and financier" within Mafia circles who habitually bugged his own properties
and used blackmail as a tool — allegedly hosting the [[blue-suite]] parties and serving as
[[j-edgar-hoover]]'s liaison to mob boss [[meyer-lansky]].

## Claims

- Per Whitney Webb, was a cunning but hot-headed operator and financier within Mafia circles who
  loved to secretly record his guests and use blackmail; allegedly bugged his liquor
  distilleries, including bathroom stalls. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 24:22-24:47)*
- A recurring anecdote (per his New York Times obituary) holds that Rosenstiel met Winston
  Churchill on a 1920s boat cruise in the French Riviera, and that Churchill tipped him off about
  the coming end of Prohibition, telling him to "prepare for the return of liquor sales in the
  United States." *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 24:47-25:12)*
- Like other former bootleggers, became a useful ally to deep-state operatives due to his dual
  allegiances between his organized-crime past and legitimate alcohol-industry present; remained a
  close ally to [[j-edgar-hoover]] and likely acted as Hoover's liaison and middleman to
  [[meyer-lansky]]. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 25:12-25:36)*
- Per author Peter Dale Scott, along with Hoover, effectively created Senator [[joseph-mccarthy]]'s
  entire "witch trial": Hoover's list of communist subversives, meant to go to McCarthy, was
  passed through US Army intelligence and then leaked to the Jewish League Against Communism — a
  Rosenstiel front group — which passed it on to McCarthy. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 23:42-24:16)*
- In 1960, five years after the alleged Blue Suite events, Rosenstiel co-founded the J. Edgar
  Hoover Foundation with fellow ex-bootlegger [[joseph-kennedy|Joseph Kennedy]] (father of John and
  Robert Kennedy). *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 25:42-26:04)*
- Alleged (per [[susan-kaufman]]'s testimony) to have hosted and bugged the [[blue-suite]] parties
  at the Plaza Hotel, at which Hoover, [[francis-spellman]], and [[roy-cohn]] were present; Cohn
  was also, per Kaufman, caught in bed with Rosenstiel. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 33:27-33:52, 40:44-40:50)*
