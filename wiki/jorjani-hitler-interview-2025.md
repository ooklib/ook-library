American Alchemy episode of 2025-11-01 (about 3h58m, a returning-guest sequel to [[jorjani-michels-interview-2025]]) in which [[jesse-michels]] interviews philosopher [[jason-jorjani]] about a hypothesised Nazi/SS survival inside the American deep state, Nazi saucer and "Bell" technology, Antarctica and Atlantis, the Nordics, and a trickster super-intelligence. Almost everything is Jorjani's speculation or second-hand material he cites from his book *Closer Encounters* (2021) and from Joseph Farrell, Nick Cook and Igor Witkowski; Michels is sympathetic, adds context, and occasionally pushes back (Nazi-Nordic alliance, JFK disarmament, communism, Ted Owens).
