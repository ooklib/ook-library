A two-part taxonomy of CIA business cover, as presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] from former CIA officer Victor Marchetti (as related in [[jonathan-kwitny]]'s *The Crimes of Patriots*). The host uses it to classify [[zapata-offshore]] as a "front."

## Claims

- Proprietary: a company that looks private but is wholly owned by the agency, with seed capital from the agency and no private shareholders, yet which performs the business it advertises. Kwitny's examples: [[paul-helliwell]]'s companies in Southeast Asia, principally Sea Supply Corp in Bangkok (ASR "C Supply Corp"), and [[civil-air-transport]] (later [[air-america]]). Proprietaries may spend their own profits but can go to Langley for cash. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 16:02-17:31)*
- Front: "an organization whose purported business is a sham kept in place to provide a cover for other activities," a place for agents to get mail or phone calls and answer "what are you doing here." Host: Zapata Offshore was less about drilling than providing cover for assets. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 17:31-18:12)*
- Host's thesis: Bush and his cohort, "the core of which would end up at the center of the Iran Contra affair," pioneered a privatized, decentralized model of intelligence funding and plausible deniability, brought in at the "ground floor" of a Dulles "pilot project" (see [[allen-dulles]]); as a politician Bush later tapped this web for widespread criminal operations. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 18:12-19:06)*
- Host: Bush was "a key pioneer in the privatization of American intelligence," a process accelerating after the Bay of Pigs and the Kennedys' push for control. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 34:38-35:01)*
- Comparison: the CIA's venture arm [[in-q-tel]] (1999), which the host says seeded part of Palantir (ASR "Peter Teal's paler"), a data software firm serving the intelligence community and Pentagon; Devine's activities were "the same operations as their successors in InQTel, albeit at a much smaller scale." *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 5:04-6:02)*
