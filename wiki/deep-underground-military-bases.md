Deep underground military bases (DUMBs), also called subterranean facilities ("STFs", or "stiffs" in ASR), are the subject of a ~1h38m [[uap-gerb]] survey. Gerb's framing: acknowledged DUMBs undeniably exist (NORAD's Cheyenne Mountain, Raven Rock), so the live question is whether some unacknowledged ones host UFO legacy program RDT&E, crash-material storage and reverse engineering, and whether they are linked by tunnels and maglev trains. Everything below is Gerb's argument or the claims of his sources, not established fact. He leans heavily on [[richard-sauder]]'s three books and on [[bill-hamilton]]'s 1990s work, and says he has spoken with "nearly half a dozen" veterans who traveled to STFs. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 0:00, 1:31, 3:33, 3:41, 3:49)*

## Acknowledged and documented background (Gerb)

- Acknowledged sites: NORAD Cheyenne Mountain Complex (2,000 ft down) and Raven Rock / Site R; Warrenton Army Training Center (claimed CIA covert facility, per Fletcher Prouty and an Inquiry article) and a Navy underground site near Sugar Grove, WV run for the NSA. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 5:08, 5:32, 8:13, 8:43, 8:52)*
- Army Corps of Engineers deputy engineering director Lloyd A. Duscha said in 1989 that several of the most interesting Corps underground facilities were "still classified"; a 1985 Corps survey said hardened underground facilities are limited by cost, not technology; a 1959-61 five-part Corps manual assumes such facilities already exist and advises inconspicuous portals and camouflage. Gerb links this to witness MS's small-building Dugway entrance. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 4:15, 6:53, 18:21, 19:32, 19:39)*
- RAND ran 1950s-60s deep-underground studies for the USAF (1959 symposium; 1960 study of 12 sites, one at Santa Barbara). 1964 Corps proposals for 12 giant structures up to 4,000 ft deep included Yuma County, AZ and Inyo County, CA (shared with [[china-lake]]). *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 16:47, 17:26, 17:34, 20:06, 20:15, 21:18)*
- The 1957 Green Mountain (Huntsville) rocket-factory and "junior Pentagon" plan, and a 1991 DARPA/Army Missile Command paper on detecting hidden cavities (buildings can conceal shafts) are cited as precedent. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 15:21, 24:48, 25:13)*
- Gerb infers most DUMBs were built 1961-1989 (some as ICBM/continuity-of-government sites), connecting tunnels from about 1974, and maglev systems a decade or so after that. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 23:40, 1:35:00, 1:35:47)*
- Money: UFO legacy programs run on the black budget; cites Katherine Austin Fitz's $21 trillion unauthorized spending 1998-2015. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 7:18, 7:42)*

## Nazi origins claim

Gerb traces US DUMBs to Nazi underground factories (Ohrdruf-area "subterranean towns", Riese/"Wurm"-type complexes) and [[operation-paperclip]]: a 1947 Air Materiel Command request for German technicians for an underground plant program, including Todt organization director Xavier Dorsch. He speculates [[hans-kammler]] ran early UFO-legacy programs underground (Mednick Hill), was secretly taken into US custody, and with Dorsch is a candidate "architect" of modern US UFO/DUMB legacy programs. He ties this to [[magenta-italy-crash-1933]] as the Nazi-held crashed disc. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 9:22, 10:33, 11:04, 11:19, 12:08, 12:36, 14:22, 14:39)* Hermann Kemper's 1934-38 "Rohrbahn" vacuum-tube maglev concept is offered as a Paperclip-era maglev precursor. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 11:30, 12:13)*
