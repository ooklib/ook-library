Phil Schneider was a self-described geologist, structural engineer and underground-tunneling specialist who lectured in 1995 that he had spent 17 years working on classified government projects. The wiki records only his claims as made in the November 1995 Las Vegas lecture ([[schneider-las-vegas-lecture-1995]]), not verified fact. He is a foundational figure of the "alien underground base" strand of UFO lore that predates and differs from the contractor-legacy-program framing used by [[uap-gerb]].

## Claimed background

- Says he worked 17 years for the US government as geologist, aerospace and structural engineer, 11 of them at Groom Lake, S-2 and S-4 ([[groom-lake]]), holding a level-three clearance with a "Riyfactor" hardened factor given to a handful of civilians, and working in over 70 countries and for NATO. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 1:01, 4:40, 18:10)*
- Names employers and clients (the ASR list is garbled, mentioning Aerospace of France and Los Alamos laboratories) and [[eg-and-g]]; says he co-invented shaped-charge blasting methods and a laser/maser "rock deflorating" technique that melts rock and coats tunnel walls with a glass-like layer. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 2:00, 4:00)*
- Claims to have helped build 13 deep underground military bases, including sealed rooms over a mile deep near Dulce and additions at Groom Lake, S-2 and S-4, and submarine "DUMB 2" bases off the continental shelf. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 9:36, 9:50)*
- Says his father was a WWII U-boat captain captured by the French and turned over to the US Third Army and Navy, who later became a doctor in Navy aerospace medicine and helped develop small nuclear motors ("grid bed"), which Schneider ties to flying-saucer prototypes; he says he only learned this on his father's deathbed. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 15:52, 16:46)*

## Claims about his own life

- Says he quit after learning the bases were being used "to subvert the Constitution", cut up his clearance and mailed it back, was arrested and made to finish three weeks, and never collected his final paychecks. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 19:39)*
- Says he has survived 13 attempts on his life since the start of 1995 (shot, run off the road, hand-to-hand combat), a figure also given by the introducer, and that a friend, Ron Rummel, was murdered after they published the "Star Wars is part of the New World Order" spoof; the death was ruled a suicide but is now acknowledged as homicide. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 1:59, 14:44, 41:39)*
- Says he was the survivor of the [[dulce-underground-base-incident-1979]], listing severe radiation-type injuries. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 43:24)*
