Generalissimo Chiang Kai-shek led the Nationalist Chinese (KMT) forces in China's WWII-era dual
conflict against Japan and Mao Zedong's communists. Per Eyes Wide Open, unlike Mao's hard
anti-opium stance, Chiang funded his war effort by consolidating and taxing China's opium trade,
a policy that drew [[paul-helliwell]] and the OSS into narcotics facilitation.

## Claims

- Led the KMT/Nationalist Party in a decades-long civil war against Mao Zedong's communist
  People's Liberation Army, interrupted by a short-lived 1935-1940 anti-Japanese united front.
  Unlike Mao (who launched an anti-opium campaign leading to 82,000 arrests after taking power in
  1949), Chiang funded his armies by selling opium to Chinese addicts, consolidating the
  previously decentralized opium industry under his control via the [[green-gang]] crime
  syndicate; author Whitney Webb is cited as calling Nationalist China "a full blown narco
  state." *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 8:34-9:59)*
- [[paul-helliwell]], the OSS liaison working with the KMT, proposed the OSS help facilitate
  Chiang's opium trade to ensure continued anti-communist funding; [[claire-chennault]] separately
  lobbied FDR to fund a private air force for Chiang (the "Flying Tigers"), later repurposed into
  [[civil-air-transport]]'s heroin-smuggling route between Burma and China. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 10:19-11:46)*

- [[paul-helliwell]] of [[oss-detachment-202]] helped Chiang's Nationalists against Mao's Communists from Yunnan. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 2:50-3:03)*

- After fleeing to Taiwan in 1949, his China Lobby pushed US involvement; Taipei was pressured (Burma, UN, US) to withdraw [[li-mi]]'s force, agreeing in Sept 1953. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 11:36-13:38, 43:17-44:22)*
- LAOS 1: his KMT sent Gen. Lu Han's (ASR 'luhan') 200,000-man army from Yunnan to expel the Japanese from Indochina; in 1946 France and the KMT agreed that France regained its colony while renouncing its concessions in China. Host says he was uninterested in Indochina, focused on the civil war. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 20:22-20:58)*
