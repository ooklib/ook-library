Summary of the criticism of [[philip-j-corso]] as presented (and partly rebutted) in [[corso-investigation-video-2025]]. Gerb says most criticism targets [[the-day-after-roswell-book]], not the [[dawn-of-a-new-age-manuscript]], and that even critics ([[john-b-alexander]], [[jacques-vallee]]) stress his story "never wavered", like "a tape recorder". *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:08:22-1:09:19)*

## Critics' claims

- [[stanton-friedman]] (interview clip): no validation, no index, a PhD colonel listed 90-plus false claims; a Nobel-winning physicist's discovery preceded Corso's period; [[strom-thurmond]] withdrew the foreword; [[j-edgar-hoover]] had "nothing but bad things" to say of Corso; Corso only made lieutenant colonel after 22 years; the Eisenhower Library says he never attended an NSC meeting. Gerb objects to Friedman associating Corso with [[bob-lazar]] and notes Corso never claimed to be a "world savior" in his manuscript. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:10:24-1:14:39)*
- [[jacques-vallee]]: integrated-circuit claims not documented facts. [[john-b-alexander]]: night-vision genealogy fully accounted for; wrote Corso a letter listing issues with the book. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:09:35-1:10:24)*
- July 2024 [[black-vault]] (John Greenewald) FBI release: no UFO content; a 1964-65 background check for a House immigration/national-security subcommittee job; unfounded claims such as a list of Fabian socialists given to Trudeau; spreading CIA-sourced claims that [[lee-harvey-oswald]] was an FBI informant; and a remark from "another government agency" calling Corso "a parasite". Gerb notes these were cross-references, not main files, and that no documents on the Army foreign technology division were released. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:14:39-1:16:33)*
- Gerb's own concerns: [[corso-white-sands-nhi-encounter-claims]]; and Corso is often called "Colonel" though Gerb only verifies Lt. Col. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:16:33-1:22:19, 1:22:35-1:22:51)*
