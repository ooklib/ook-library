Kathy Andrews is a director of security at [[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]], another figure
Gerb places under [[terry-phillips|Terry Phillips]]'s security apparatus.
*([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 2:23:43–2:23:52)*

## Claims

- Career: VP of operations for L3 Communications (2004–2006), then security manager at Northrop
  Grumman Information Systems (2006–2015). *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md)
  2:23:52–2:24:10)*
- Gerb flags a "bizarre" roughly two-year gap: from 2015 to 2016 Andrews left Northrop to serve as
  assistant group supervisor at [[johns-hopkins-applied-physics-laboratory|Johns Hopkins
  University's Applied Physics Laboratory (APL)]], a Navy-sponsored University Affiliated
  Research Center (UARC) — which Gerb frames as the "opposite side of the same coin" from FFRDCs —
  before returning to Northrop as senior security manager and eventually director of security
  from 2016 to present. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md)
  2:24:10–2:25:08)*
- Gerb speculates UARCs like APL are sought by specific UFO-related SAPs/WOAPs for challenging
  R&D work, citing an unrelated example (a California-based UARC allegedly tacked onto an early-
  2000s Army program adapting electro-optic cloaking from a recovered nonhuman vehicle onto the
  Next Generation Combat Vehicle) and wonders whether Andrews's stint reflects similar
  personnel-swapping between Northrop and APL, which have co-shared office space with
  [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]] and [[mit-lincoln-laboratory|MIT Lincoln Laboratory]] since 2021.
  *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 2:25:08–2:26:36)*
