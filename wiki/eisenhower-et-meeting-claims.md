Asked by [[jesse-michels]] about the rumored 1954 Eisenhower meeting, [[steven-greer]] says he has French Ministry of Defense reports that a meeting happened (in 1954 or 1956) but that it was not the mythology of a treaty: the ETs tried to redirect the nuclear arms race and Eisenhower was inclined to comply, but [[allen-dulles]] and [[paul-mellon]] refused and cut the president out. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 3:28:53-3:30:05)*

## Claims

- Steven Lovekin, a young Signal Corps soldier at the White House around 1959-60, allegedly saw Eisenhower doodling ET craft and was told Eisenhower no longer controlled the issue. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 3:30:05-3:30:33)*
- Greer says Eisenhower died bitter and that Kennedy later told Air Force One chief steward Bill Holden that he knew the sightings were real but the matter was "out of my hands"; Kennedy then fired Dulles. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 3:30:41-3:31:28, 1:34:41-1:35:23)*
- A 1963 Kennedy letter to John McCone urging coordination with the Soviets on "unknowns" is raised by Michels; Greer says its provenance is unknown but it "comports with the facts". *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:33:54-1:34:41)*
- Greer says the Kennedy assassination was over UFOs; he cites a 1968 Robert F. Kennedy letter to a UFO researcher. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 6:08-6:24, 1:32:17-1:33:50)*
