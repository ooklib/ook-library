Richard "Rick" Doty (rendered "Rick Dodie" in the ASR transcript), a former AFOSI
counterintelligence special agent stationed at [[kirtland-air-force-base|Kirtland AFB]], is
infamous for his engagement in psychological operations against Paul Bennewitz and, more
recently, for a series of disclosure-adjacent claims and interviews. Gerb states he finds it
intriguing that Doty "now has seemingly turned a new leaf" and aligns with disclosure
initiatives, yet remains controversial. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
1:48:03–1:48:27)*

## Claims

- Doty claims he was offered a job in 2019 by the US Space Force to work with
  [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]] in disinformation, and refused. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:48:37–1:48:53)*
- In an hour-and-a-half interview with Steven Greer for the documentary *Unacknowledged*, Doty
  said he was briefed in the summer of 1979 into a special access program regarding "the US
  government's investigation and contacts with extraterrestrial[s]" and the Air Force's
  involvement, with the access code name "Yankee Black" (distinguished from "Yankee White,"
  access to the White House). *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  1:49:05–1:50:24)*
- A Washington Post article (which Doty's testimony appears to echo/contradict) later claimed
  that UFO whistleblowers had been tricked by a fake Air Force SAP designed to haze officers,
  called "Yankee Blue." Gerb notes that if this were genuinely a fabricated hazing program, there
  should be no obstacle to releasing the supporting document, especially since journalists have
  claimed to possess it. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:50:51–1:51:28)*
- Gerb states he will not give weight to Doty's testimony generally, due to his past
  counterintelligence/disinformation role, characterizing Doty's approach as likely "truths mixed
  in with misdirection, lies, and red herrings" — classic disinformation technique — rather than
  straightforward disclosure. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  1:51:28–1:51:51)*
