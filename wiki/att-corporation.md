The telephone company that, per [[uap-gerb]], President Truman put in charge of managing [[sandia-national-laboratories]] in 1949, and that developed the transistor in the 1940s (through [[bell-labs]]). Gerb floats it as an early suspect for UFO involvement in the private sector. This page records Gerb's speculation, not established fact.

## Claims (gerb/del-rio)

- Gerb says Sandia, on the property of [[kirtland-air-force-base]], was managed by AT&T from 1949 until 1993, when it was taken over by [[lockheed-martin]]. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 25:10-25:29)* *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 26:50-26:59)*
- Gerb notes two redacted entries in [[steven-greer]]'s [[disclosure-project]] witness list: "witness from ACC with knowledge of ET technology transfer to AT&T," and a "president of [blank]" with knowledge of the ET technology transfer program who "has been on radio sighting show" and "may have useful pseudonyms." *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 25:29-26:10)*
- Gerb ties this to the alleged December 1950 retrieval at [[el-indio-guerrero-crash-1950]], whose wreckage was sent to Sandia, and to [[edgar-fuche]]'s claim that part of the alleged TR-3B reverse-engineering came from Sandia. He wonders if AT&T is one of the early suspects. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 26:10-26:59)*
