The Quandt family are BMW's largest shareholders. The video alleges they built much of their wealth through Nazi collaboration.

## Claims

- Patriarch Gunther Quandt and son Herbert employed around 50,000 forced laborers from concentration camps producing batteries, weapons and ammunition via Accumulatoren-Fabrik (transcribed 'Akumulan Fabriagi'), and took over Jewish-owned businesses confiscated by the Nazis. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 3:49-4:18)*
- Herbert's stepmother Magda divorced Gunther to marry Joseph Goebbels, Hitler's propaganda minister. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 4:20-4:28)*
- After the war the Quandts became major BMW shareholders; BMW itself had supplied the Nazi military with arms and aircraft engines using slave labor from Dachau. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 4:28-4:39)*
