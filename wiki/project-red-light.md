Project Red Light is an alleged Area 51 program to fly and exploit recovered UFOs and alien reproduction vehicles. [[uap-gerb]] traces the name through several sources and speculates that [[northrop-grumman]] managed it. All of it is unverified.

## Claims

- [[leonard-stringfield]]'s files include an April 5, 1980 letter relayed by researcher Michael Schratt (Gerb: "Shrat") about a witness "MH" who claimed radio maintenance work at the Nevada atomic proving grounds in 1961-1963 and participation in Red Light at Area 51: test flights of a recovered UFO shipped from [[edwards-air-force-base]], silent and not conventionally powered, with personnel rotated every six months and rumors of a West Coast defense contractor struggling to reverse engineer components. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 53:22-54:33)*
- [[edgar-fuche]] said [[eg-and-g]] was awarded an indefinite contract for Red Light; Gerb links this to the alleged nuclear-mishap recovery and security role of EG&G and to [[bob-lazar]]'s S4. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 54:41-55:21)*
- [[arnold-house]]'s friend said the Area 51 program to test NHI vehicles and exploit materials was called Red Light, with Northrop paychecks, underground areas near Lancaster and Palmdale, Anza, and March Air Force Base. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 52:33-53:22)*
- Gerb draws a parallel to the joint Edwards 412th Test Group and Area 51 program described by the witness "Ed", and concludes Northrop "could have served as a project manager", possibly still ongoing, with smaller firms like "Alon Science and Technologies" helping. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 54:33-55:36,)*
