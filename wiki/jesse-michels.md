Jesse Michels is the host of the American Alchemy YouTube channel (registry channel `jm`), which conducts long-form interviews on UFO/UAP, deep politics and secret science. The channel's sponsor/advertisement segments are not recorded in this wiki.

- Interviewed [[harald-malmgren]] with [[pippa-malmgren]] in April 2025, and did a follow-up call three days before Malmgren's death; the resulting video is a memorial and frames the account as first-hand. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 7:35-7:59, Description)*
- Supplies extensive host commentary (marked as host speculation in this wiki): the Bluegill/Excalibur/SDI link, the Magenta and Knights of Malta narrative, the MJ-12 and continuity-of-government reading, and the Chase Brandon novel parallel. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 1:17:31-1:29:06, 1:36:01-1:41:14, 3:42:35-3:43:06, 3:45:04-3:46:04)*
- Guest interviews referenced in this transcript: Matthew Pines, Danny Sheehan, Eric Weinstein. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 10:15-10:22, 3:01:36-3:01:52, 2:47:45-2:47:52)*
