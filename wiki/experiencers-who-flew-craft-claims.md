Cameron says he collected 36 people (a 747 pilot, a retired Air Force colonel, Whitley Strieber, Chris Bledsoe and others) who report flying a craft during an experience, not in a government facility, typically with a guide standing behind them. He distinguishes these from Lazar, and he says the government does not fly anything.

## Claims

- Cameron says a retired Air Force F-16 colonel found himself on a craft with a guardian-like presence behind him telling him he already knew what to do, and he flew it by releasing the controls; Cameron links the left-behind figure to the spirit guides in Michael Newton's work. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:51:03-2:53:16)*
- Strieber allegedly told Cameron someone behind him laughed when he planned to steal a craft. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:53:24-2:53:47)*
- [[chris-bledsoe]] described a black beehive pedestal, spinning hexagons and a window opening onto the Cape Fear River. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:53:47-2:54:58)*
- Ron Johnson allegedly imagined the Milky Way, felt one second of intense g-force, and saw it out the window; Cameron says he has multiple witnesses told "it is within you". *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:55:06-2:55:41)*
- Cameron relates this to remote viewing (the viewer is instantly at the target) and to the Sufi line Greer quotes. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:55:41-2:56:13)*
