The Men in Black are described by [[uap-gerb]] as suit-clad government agents said to question, threaten, harass and sometimes assassinate UFO experiencers, a staple of ufology folklore. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 8:30-8:54)*

## Claims

- Gerb traces the legend to June 27, 1947, when [[harold-dahl]] said he saw six donut-shaped objects over Puget Sound near Maury Island, one of which dropped debris that killed his dog; the next day a man in a black suit took him to a diner, recounted his sighting in detail and warned him not to speak of it. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 8:54-9:56)*
- Gerb argues the "Men in Black" can be men in military fatigues rather than suits, citing Roswell's Jesse Marcel and the girl whose fireman father saw the craft, and [[robert-jacobs]]'s footage of a UFO disabling a warhead. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 9:56-10:34)*
- Gerb's opinion: the suited MIB are a total hoax that distracts from the credible officials suppressing legitimate sightings and footage. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 10:34-11:06)*
