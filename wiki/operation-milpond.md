Top-secret US Air Force bombing operation, approved by the Kennedy White House, that was to bomb the Plain of Jars in April 1961 but was cancelled on April 17, 1961, the day the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]] began. Source: [[eyes-wide-open]] LAOS 4. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:14:15-1:15:52)*

## Claims

- In late March 1961 about a dozen unmarked B-26s flown by [[air-america]] pilots and former USAF pilots landed at Takhli, Thailand; led by Major Harry C. "Heinie" Aderholt (ASR "heiney aderholt"), who is credited with inventing the Lima site (dirt airstrip) also useful for loading opium. Pilots were given unofficial Royal Lao Air Force status on April 16; the bombing orders were cancelled the next day. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:14:15-1:15:52)*
- The host says the cancellation reflected Kennedy's new skepticism of the CIA and national-security advisers after the Bay of Pigs, quoting Ted Sorensen; Kennedy later fired [[allen-dulles]] and Richard Bissell. The bombing of Laos was restarted only under Johnson. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:15:52-1:17:18)*
- Same day as the March 23 televised press conference, an SC-47 reconnaissance aircraft (code-named "Rose Bud" per ASR "roseb") was shot down over Laos, killing the crew but for Army attache Major Lawrence Bailey. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:13:46-1:14:15)*
