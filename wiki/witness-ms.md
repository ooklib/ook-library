Pseudonymous Dugway contractor witness ("M.S."; ASR renders "Ms"). Per [[uap-gerb]], MS was a US Navy auxiliary-division sailor (USS Theodore Roosevelt, 1990-2000), then from 2009 to 2013 a maintenance painter and sign technician for contractor "C Martin" (an ASR-rendered name; identity uncertain) at [[dugway-proving-ground]]. He carried a level-five proximity badge but no security clearance. Gerb says he found MS through lapses in site security in the DPI archive (witness ID 10655) after [[steven-greer]] described him at the June 2023 National Press Club whistleblower symposium, and that MS was approached by the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence but declined because he is a current railroad employee. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 29:44, 31:37, 32:23, 32:33, 32:56, 33:12)*)*

## Claims

- 2009, about four months into the job, covering for a sick apprentice, MS read electric meters at roughly 15 facilities. At Avery he entered an unnumbered, unmarked "ghost building" and had an M16 pushed into his chest by an armed guard. Inside a clean-room-like area were "white coats" and a disc hovering 10-12 inches off the ground under its own power, silently, with a bottom door/section removed. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 35:09, 36:21, 39:28, 40:28)*)*
- Gerb summarizes the craft as roughly 10 ft tall and 20 ft across, brushed-metal, seamless and fastener-free ("like it was just casted ... 3D printed ... Flawless"), with a floating conical component underneath that Gerb compares to [[bob-lazar]]'s reactor model, and an electronic display with coordinate-like mapping. About six lab technicians were present; Gerb speculates they were ATEC and [[battelle-memorial-institute]] scientists. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 44:07, 44:23, 44:30, 44:45, 45:01)*)*
- Guards took him to the Garrison building in the Ditto Technical Center where two men in black suits (FBI/CIA/"NSA Men in Black" per MS's and Gerb's guesses) interrogated him for six hours; he was released and later reached out to insiders. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 46:20, 46:38, 46:48)*)*
- He relayed [[witness-gp]]'s statements on the craft's origin, the mapping system and the underground base; a current railroad co-worker allegedly also confirmed the base and claimed to have helped dig its tunnels. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 47:22, 49:53, 50:00)*)*
- Gerb intends to re-interview MS, GP and the railroad co-worker, and had first spoken with MS in June 2024. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:11:32, 1:11:41, 1:11:49)*)*
