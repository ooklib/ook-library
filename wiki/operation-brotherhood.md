A CIA-funded aid mission that, from January 1957, staffed emergency hospitals in the Plain of Jars region of [[laos]] with Filipinos, marking the CIA's first incursion there, per [[eyes-wide-open]] LAOS 4. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 25:20-25:44)*

## Claims

- Ran alongside the International Voluntary Services (IVS, called a precursor of the Peace Corps by Frederick Benson), the first US aid organization in Laos (1956), working with Hmong refugees on the Plain of Jars; the host says those people became the backbone of the CIA's secret army. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 24:25-25:08)*
- Host notes the "intersection once again of the CIA and Filipino assets" as with [[edward-lansdale]] in Vietnam. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 25:39-25:57)*
- Acted as a recruitment tool: CIA case officer Stuart Methven met [[vang-pao]] at an Operation Brotherhood hospital in Ban Ban in August 1959, starting a relationship that became a rural airdrop program. J. Vinton Lawrence (2016 interview, per host) called Methven "a catalyst for the whole thing". Discrepancy flagged: the host also says Methven's "first meeting with General Vang Pao" was in 1960 via a USAID-associated mission. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 26:18-27:18)*
- The early CIA-USAID efforts culminated in 1958's Operation Booster Shot; see [[laos-coups-1958-1960]]. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 27:18-27:31)*
