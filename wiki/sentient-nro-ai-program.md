Sentient is a real, FOIA-confirmed [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]] "ongoing research and
development program" combining "problem-centric intelligence, multi-INT end-to-end, and trusted
machine automation" to automate the collection-tasking-processing-exploitation-dissemination
(TCPED) cycle. Development reportedly began around 2010, with core buildout in 2016.
*([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md) 36:02–36:33, 42:05–42:16)*

**This page catalogs UAP Gerb's speculative claim** that Sentient is used to identify and divert
anomalous (UFO-related) sensor data before it reaches standard NRO/NGA analyst feeds — this is
unverified inference, not documented Sentient functionality.

## Claims

- Described by former NGA director Robert Cardillo as using "automated inferencing" to aid
  intelligence collection. *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md) 36:33–36:42)*
- On 2 June 2022, the NRO released a document ("Recent Sentient Highlights," from data collected
  6 May 2021) describing Sentient's detection of a possible airborne object under 10 meters in
  diameter, "tic tac shaped," resembling objects detected by US Navy aircraft/vessels in
  redacted UAP operating areas; the NRO publicly ruled out sensor artifact/focal-plane anomaly.
  *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md) 37:21–38:07)*
- Gerb theorizes Sentient (or systems like it) is used to flag and remove anomalous imagery/
  signals data from standard analyst feeds before digital encoding, routing it instead to more
  "cleared" personnel — his explanation for why rank-and-file NRO/NGA analysts haven't
  whistleblown on UFO imagery. *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md) 35:03–36:02, 38:07–38:27)*
- In 2021, the NRO contributed Sentient data to the ODNI's report to Congress on the UAP task
  force — which Gerb notes contradicts a 2015 NRO statement claiming it had "no documentation
  related to UFOs" and that the topic was "outside the scope of NRO responsibilities."
  *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md) 38:27–39:06)*
- Per whistleblower [[matthew-brown|Matthew Brown]]'s 2024 [[immaculate-constellation]] report to
  Congress, NRO Sentient is one of the "tasked" intelligence resources feeding Immaculate
  Constellation's global UAP/ARV ISR mission. *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md) 39:18–40:47)*
- Gerb speculates [[david-grush|David Grush]], during his NRO/NGA liaison work, may have been
  exposed to Sentient-collected UFO imagery or crash-site imagery, given his stated inability to
  discuss "crash imagery" in open congressional session. *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md)
  57:41–1:01:20)*
