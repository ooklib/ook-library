Vernon Bowen's typed history of UFO cases to 1960, "Encyclopedia of Flying Saucers", submitted to the Air Force in 1960 and reportedly never returned; it reached [[timothy-cooper]] in 1999 through a FOIA designation at Fort Meade, stamped Top Secret Magic and full of marginal comments by [[vannevar-bush]]. Gerb calls it the original that connects [[project-moon-dust]] to the Majestic documents. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:26:51, 1:34:37)*

## Claims

- Original onion-skin paper; Wood says forensic ink/pencil analysis dates Bush's writing to 1962 ("Moon Dust"). *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:28:04, 1:38:06)*
- Bush's comments include "What is Don Menzel doing?" and, beside a question whether the craft could be man-made experimental vehicles, "this should not get out", read by Wood as concern about gravity-control craft. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:29:14, 1:29:39)*
- A section sample notes Mussolini and Hitler exposed to a craft in 1942, with Bush underlining a paragraph; Gerb links this to [[magenta-italy-crash-1933]]. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:36:56)*
- Gerb ties the insect-like beings mention to [[robert-sarbacher]]'s account. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:35:29, 1:36:42)*
