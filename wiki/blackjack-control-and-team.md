Two alleged entities that [[steven-greer]] says a source inside Blackjack control described: "Blackjack control", a facility that takes part in reverse engineering, and "Blackjack team", operating in conjunction with it. The wiki records what Greer and [[uap-gerb]] say; Gerb finds almost nothing public on the term.

## Claims (gerb/edwards-afb)

- A July 1991 alleged classified [[national-reconnaissance-office]] document, about civilian flying saucer groups organizing protests in Nevada, lists need-to-know operations (Royal Ops, Cosmic Ops, M Ops, Magi Ops), several USAF Special Forces units (26th, 64th, 65th, 527th), Nellis AFOSI, Groom Lake / Dreamland / Area 51 S4, "AquaTech and C-spray" and a Special Operations group with "one Blackjack team". Greer says he gave it to Admiral [[thomas-wilson]] (see the Wilson-Davis memo) who recognized one of the entities as run by a contractor. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 38:45-39:31)*
- Greer's source: Blackjack control is Nellis-based, while Blackjack team is stationed at Edwards; in a recorded talk Greer instead places Blackjack control among the Edwards-related facilities and the Blackjack team at Nellis, so Gerb notes the two are reported inconsistently. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 39:39-40:37)*
- Greer's 2009 list for the president names Edwards AFB as a "constellation" including Haystack Butte, China Lake, George AFB, the retired Norton AFB and Table Mountain Observatory. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 40:03-40:28)*
- Gerb's only public "Blackjack" is a 2010s-2020s [[darpa]] program for a low earth orbit network built with Raytheon-owned contractors; given the 1991 date he doubts the two are related. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 40:44-41:17)*
