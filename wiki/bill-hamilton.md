Late UFO researcher, per [[uap-gerb]] the first to produce the term and public image of the alien reproduction vehicle (ARV) and a handler of witnesses such as [[mark-mccandlish]] for [[steven-greer]]. Author of *Cosmic Top Secret* (1992) and an early-1990s VHS lecture "UFOs and Underground Bases". Since the late 1980s he focused on the [[antelope-valley]], and especially the Northrop Tejon ("Tahachapi"/"Ant Hill") radar cross-section site. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 36:15, 36:22, 36:30, 36:38, 37:10, 39:03)*

## Informant claims relayed by Hamilton (per Gerb)

- A local man on 170th Street West near Tejon reported the ground opening like a missile silo and a saucer taking off; Edwards AFOSI allegedly silenced him. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 43:43, 43:51)*
- Contractor "Chuck" dug tunnels beneath the Ant Hill, with round doorways and red/green ID lights. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 44:14, 44:22)*
- A "Project Star Talk" source described underground lasers to "bring in UFOs" and a UFO in an underground hangar at a McDonnell Douglas facility. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 44:30, 44:46)*
- A source "Gober" (name unclear in ASR) claimed work at Tejon, Helendale and the Nevada range with terrifying alien technology, 14-16 day rotations, and a command center under Haystack Butte connected to the Ant Hill. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 45:03, 45:10, 45:19)*
- Other informants: experimental discs flown from Edwards, ETs held under Edwards, work under an Edwards North Base hangar, electric ground vehicles and maglev links. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 45:28, 45:37)*
- A source (NASA consultant, unnamed Colonel) worked at underground sites at China Lake, Edwards and the Nevada test site linked by maglev, entering via a DUMB under White Sands. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 51:02, 51:10, 51:18)*

Gerb notes Hamilton's map source "TA" is partly Thomas Castillo of the controversial Dulce story. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:19:56, 1:20:03)*
