The Air Force Life Cycle Management Center (AFLCMC), headquartered at
[[wright-patterson-air-force-base|Wright-Patterson AFB]], is one of six major operational centers
under [[air-force-material-command|Air Force Material Command (AFMC)]] — alongside the Air Force
Test Center (AFTC) and Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) — responsible for total life-cycle
management of all Air Force aircraft, engines, munitions, and electronic systems, including
IT/C4ISR systems, armaments, and installation/infrastructure. Gerb notes AFLCMC is also the
primary contract/administrative agent for [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]'s National Security
Engineering Center FFRDC. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:58:14–1:59:21; also
[gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 44:09–44:23)*

## Claims — the Information Protection Directorate (AFLCMC/IP)

AFLCMC's **Information Protection Directorate (IP)** is, per Gerb, "the premier program
protection element for Air Force acquisition and sustainment," and one of the three named
program-protection "antibody" offices of Vol. 2 alongside
[[darpa-security-and-intelligence-directorate|DARPA SID]] and
[[doe-office-of-intelligence-and-counterintelligence|DOE's OICI]].
*([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:59:49–2:00:36)*

- IP "enhances the ability to acquire and support war-winning capabilities by providing a
  professional security workforce and risk-based processes to protect technology and information
  from unauthorized disclosure or compromise throughout a program's life cycle." Its protection
  reach extends across AFMC's other operating centers (AFTC, AFRL), the Air Force Test and
  Evaluation directorate (AFTTE) special programs division, and all Air Force Major Range and
  Test Facility Bases (MRTFBs). IP also interfaces with the Air Force's component-level SAPCO,
  [[william-e-mcclure|SAF/AAZ]] — the same office Gerb alleges was bypassed by
  [[edward-c-aldridge|Edward C. Aldridge]] for "outside activities."
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:00:13–2:01:14)*
- [[terrence-l-reynolds|Terrence L. Reynolds]], current IP director, oversees "information
  security, personnel security, industrial security, acquisition security, international program
  security, operations security, scientific and technical information program, and program
  protection planning" across the Department of the Air Force. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  2:01:24–2:01:57)*
- [[donna-shipton|Lt. Gen. Donna D. Shipton]] currently commands AFLCMC; prior to this she served
  as military deputy in [[saf-aq|SAF/AQ]] (Aug 2022–Dec 2023), "responsible for research and
  development, test, production, product support, and modernization of Air Force programs worth
  more than $60 billion annually." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:57:48–1:58:22)*
