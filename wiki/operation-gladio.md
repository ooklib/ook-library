Operation Gladio, per Eyes Wide Open, was the future CIA's Cold War program of clandestine
anti-communist "stay-behind" armies across Western Europe, seeded by [[reinhard-gehlen]]'s
postwar "werewolf" fighters (renamed "Gladiators") and later expanded through figures like
[[karl-wolff]] and [[junio-valerio-borghese]]. The video only introduces the operation's origins;
Eyes Wide Open indicates it will cover it in more depth in future installments.

## Claims

- Grew out of Gehlen's "werewolf squads," renamed "Gladiators," which formed the backbone of the
  postwar European political climate and were activated at war's end to play an instrumental role
  in West German and broader European politics. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 4:45-5:07)*
- Described as "the CIA's secret and murderous campaign against European communists"; by the end
  of this episode, its groundwork is set through the recruitment efforts of [[reinhard-gehlen]],
  [[karl-wolff]], and [[junio-valerio-borghese|the Black Prince]], who will go on to recruit
  future "Gladiators" to perpetuate violence against leftists, orchestrate false-flag events, and
  keep European fascism alive in a hidden network. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 25:56-26:27, 26:34-26:37)*
- In Italy specifically, [[junio-valerio-borghese]] was installed as commander of Gladio forces,
  organizing an army of 40 groups (10 sabotage, 6 espionage/propaganda/escape, 12 guerrilla
  divisions) funded via US State Department "operational resources" funds and trained on
  Sardinia, drawn from hardline fascists and Italian monarchists. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 25:21-26:07)*
