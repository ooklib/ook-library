## Claims

- Working on the Puget Sound near Maury Island on June 27, 1947 he saw six donut-shaped objects about half a mile up; debris struck and killed his dog; he took pictures and footage. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 8:54-9:26)*
- The next day a man in a black suit met him at a diner, described the sighting in detail, said this proved he knew much more, and told him not to speak of it or bad things would happen. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 9:26-9:56)*
