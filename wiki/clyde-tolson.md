Clyde Tolson, [[j-edgar-hoover]]'s long-time second-in-command at the FBI, is widely speculated by
historians to have been Hoover's lover; Eyes Wide Open cites allegations that compromising 1940s
photographs of the two men were held independently by [[james-jesus-angleton]] and
[[meyer-lansky]].

## Claims

- Countless historians and scholars have speculated Tolson and [[j-edgar-hoover]] were gay lovers:
  both were lifelong bachelors who traveled everywhere together and were the subject of gossip-page
  speculation. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 36:21-36:39)*
- As early as 1946, several sources allege [[james-jesus-angleton]] and [[meyer-lansky]] separately
  possessed compromising photographs of Hoover receiving oral sex from Tolson; electronics expert
  and CIA affiliate Gordon Novel claimed Angleton showed him the photos in 1967, and former OSS
  officer John Weitz alleged Angleton showed him the same photos at a 1950s dinner party,
  identifying the two men in them as Hoover and Tolson. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 36:39-37:49)*

## Relationships

- Hoover's long-time FBI deputy and alleged lifelong companion/lover.
- Alleged, with Hoover, to have been photographed compromisingly by parties tied to
  [[james-jesus-angleton]] and [[meyer-lansky]].
