The Necronomicon published in December 1977 (666 leather-bound copies) is the occult book with which [[peter-levenda]] has been associated since 1975. He says it grew from a manuscript salvaged from a book dealer's burning shelves and that Strategic Air Command wrote to praise it. Provenance and content are Levenda's account; Lovecraft scholars, he concedes, reject the link.

## Claims

- After his friend in the church circle discovered "monks" running a large rare-book theft ring above a strip club in Queens, the friend burned priceless books, and Levenda kept a manuscript with Greek lettering that he read as "Necronomicon". *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 3:06:37, 3:08:49, 3:09:16, 3:09:55)*
- Herman Slater of the Warlock Shop had the translation and the only copy; the translation work was completed on 12 October 1975, and Larry Barnes, a Lovecraft fan whose father owned a large East Coast lithography press, financed publication. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 3:11:57, 3:12:22, 3:22:43)*
- The book was funded via barter-space ads in Omni, Psychology Today and High Times, drew letters from military bases, and included a letter from Strategic Air Command that Levenda cannot now attribute to a signer. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 3:23:23, 3:25:22, 3:25:43)*
- Content: a Sumerian-flavored pre-Islamic grimoire; Levenda derives "Cthulhu" from Sumerian Kutu-lu, "man from the underworld", and cites Kutha in Iraq. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 3:14:17, 3:16:36)*
- Levenda says SAC interest fits its mind-control and paranormal research interests, not an institution-wide belief, and notes Michels's claim that Levenda inspired Johnny Depp's *Ninth Gate* character, which Levenda hedges as something Polanski's scouts told people. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines!
