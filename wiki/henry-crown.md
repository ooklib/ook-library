Henry Crown is introduced by [[eyes-wide-open]] as a "low-key businessman" who will recur in later [[road-to-dallas]] episodes: a major investor in Las Brisas (Acapulco), a significant Hilton shareholder, majority shareholder of General Dynamics from 1959 to his death in 1990, and owner of the Empire State Building 1951-61. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 14:09-14:40)*

## Claims

- General Dynamics rose in the 1950s as a top arms manufacturer with a massive Texas-anchored expansion; in 1962, with help from [[lyndon-b-johnson]] and Robert McNamara, it won the largest DoD contract to that time, the TFX program, which became the F-111 ("Aardvark", ASR "Arvar") used by the Air Force from late Vietnam into the Gulf War. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 14:40-15:35)*
- Host: the TFX procurement scandal over questionable practices and backroom deals "would come to haunt LBJ and McNamara in the last days of JFK's presidency," then disappeared after JFK's death and LBJ's ascension. Detail is promised for later. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 15:35-15:58)*
- Host speculation: Crown's military-industry contacts may explain why Las Brisas became the preferred destination of astronauts and aerospace figures. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 23:22-23:53)*
