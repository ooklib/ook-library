Arthur Lundahl headed the CIA's National Photographic Interpretation Center, the U-2 and SR-71 imagery analysis unit. In [[grant-cameron]]'s account he is the first head of the CIA "weird desk" and was the man who briefed Kennedy on the Cuban missile crisis with imagery boards.

## Claims

- NPIC was housed over a broken-down car dealership in a rundown part of Washington so Russians would not find it; locals allegedly thought money was being printed there. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 49:11-49:36)*
- Lundahl was a friend of [[james-e-mcdonald]] and, Cameron says, obsessed with UFOs; [[hal-puthoff]] later asked Cameron for a list of documents from Lundahl's collection. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 48:03-48:44)*
- Cameron says Lundahl received the 1959 Frances Swan channeling demonstration: [[frances-swan-channeling-claims]]. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 53:41-55:30)*
- Cameron says Lundahl received an award from Kennedy for the missile-crisis briefing and talked about "really weird stuff" when dying. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 56:12-56:36)*
- Cameron gives the lineage of the weird desk as Lundahl, then [[kit-green]], then [[ron-pandolfi]]: [[cia-weird-desk-claims]]. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 45:48-45:55)*
