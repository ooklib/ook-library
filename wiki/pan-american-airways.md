Pan American Airways (PanAm), founded and long chaired by Juan Trippe, is presented by
[[eyes-wide-open]] as a "crown jewel" of the postwar aerospace industry embedded in the US
establishment and intelligence world.

## Claims

- [[william-pawley]] headed its "Chinese branch," the China National Aviation Company. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 18:23-18:41)*
- Per [[howard-hughes]], PanAm lobbied to become the head of a worldwide overseas-flight monopoly
  and Senator Owen Brewster (friend of Trippe) tried to force a TWA merger; PanAm was "eventually
  granted" the monopoly on overseas flights to Southeast Asia throughout the second Indochina War,
  which per [[peter-dale-scott]] "undoubtedly contributed to their tremendous growth" in the 1960s-70s.
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 25:29-26:30)*
- Trippe was a good friend of [[boeing]] chairman Bill Allen; TWA then PanAm were the first to
  deploy the Boeing 707, helping Boeing become a major consumer and military aircraft maker. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 27:05-27:29)*
- Supplied decommissioned planes and off-duty pilots to [[civil-air-transport]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 44:35-44:53)*
- The host says TWA and PanAm interests "intersect and intervene" through the rest of the
  presentation, rubbing shoulders with intelligence agents. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 28:18-28:45)*
