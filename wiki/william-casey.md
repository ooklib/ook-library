William Casey appears in Eyes Wide Open's "Real Cold War" episode as another named example of
the CIA/American-elite-law-firm "revolving door," having been a partner at the corporate law
firm Hall Casey Dickler and Howley before becoming CIA director under President Reagan.

## Claims
