The theory that microbial life is distributed through the universe by space dust, meteors, asteroids, comets and planetoids, summarized by [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 7:14-7:30)*

## Claims

- Gerb's example: Mars once had life; an impact kicked up microbe-laden soil that reached Earth. He lists three types: lithopanspermia (between solar systems, as stated in the transcript), ballistic panspermia (between planets of one system) and directed panspermia (humans seeding other planets). *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 7:30-8:27)*
