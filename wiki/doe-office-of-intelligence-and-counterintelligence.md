The Department of Energy's Office of Intelligence and Counterintelligence (OICI, "DOE IN") is,
per its mission statement, tasked to "inform national security decision makers and mitigate
threats to the DOE enterprise and the nation's energy security by providing unique scientific and
technical intelligence and expertise." Gerb names OICI as the third of his three named
program-protection "antibody" offices in Vol. 2, alongside
[[darpa-security-and-intelligence-directorate|DARPA SID]] and
[[air-force-life-cycle-management-center|AFLCMC/IP]] — noting the DOE, unlike component
military branches, maintains its own dedicated SAP structure entirely, "not a component-level
SAPCO, but its very own SAP." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:02:15–2:03:19)*

## Claims

- OICI is DOE's primary interface with ODNI and other intelligence agencies, specializing in
  foreign nuclear capabilities/activities, counterintelligence (espionage and insider threats),
  nuclear material security, critical infrastructure protection, and support to DOE's national
  laboratories. Per ODNI, its mission is to "protect, enable, and represent the vast scientific
  brain trust resident in DOE's laboratories and plants." Though small, it maintains a classified
  budget drawn from FY2026's $81.9 billion National Intelligence Program budget.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:03:19–2:03:47)*
- Gerb traces OICI's institutional roots to the Manhattan Project's [[alsos-mission|ALSOS
  missions]] (covered in Vol. 1) — "basically the blueprint for a parallel intelligence apparatus
  for legacy activities completely removed from standard chains of command."
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:04:20–2:04:50)*
- Gerb is "particularly interested" in OICI's **Nuclear Materials Information Program (NMIP)**,
  established under NPD-48/HSPD-17, given the [[atomic-energy-act-1954|1954 Atomic Energy Act]]'s
  liberal use of "special nuclear material," "transclassified foreign nuclear information
  (TFNI)," and "non-contract foreign intelligence information" categories — tools he argues, per
  [[david-grush|David Grush]], are used to "transclassify" UFO material into nuclear secrets. He
  wagers OICI's nuclear programs also see engagement with the UFO portfolio.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:04:50–2:05:16)*
- OICI maintains 14 Field Intelligence Elements (FIEs) and 15 counterintelligence field offices
  at DOE labs/facilities/sites worldwide, including at [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los
  Alamos]], [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]], [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|
  Lawrence Livermore]], and [[oak-ridge-national-laboratory|Oak Ridge]] — the same national labs
  Vol. 1 identified as recipients of custody over recovered NHI vehicles under alleged 1948
  Truman-era classified executive orders. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  2:05:49–2:06:30)*
- Gerb calls OICI "still an emergent piece of the puzzle" that is "exceedingly difficult to
  crack," and states he plans a dedicated Department of Energy investigation covering it further.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:06:46–2:07:00)*
