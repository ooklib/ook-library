Amentum Corporation is a defense contractor accused by
[[matthew-brown|Immaculate Constellation whistleblower Matthew Brown]] of housing UFO materials
at [[naval-air-station-patuxent-river|NAS Patuxent River]]'s special-project hangars.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:51:19–1:51:34)*

## Claims

- SAIC has "significant presence" at Pax River, supporting NAWCAD and the Ship and Air
  Integration Warfare Division, similarly to Amentum's hangar presence.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:51:41–1:51:56)*
- SAIC and Amentum formed a joint venture, **FSA**, which by 2020 had secured IDIQ federal
  contracts exceeding $1.3 billion. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:52:13–1:52:29)*
