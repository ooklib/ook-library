Private security firm founded in 1954, later renamed G4S Solutions, that [[uap-gerb]] treats as "synonymous with UFO legacy programs" and as a revolving door for military and intelligence figures. It appears here as a hypothesised source of the black team in [[castle-29-palms-triangle-encounter-1997]] and as the firm that recruited [[roderick-castle]] in 2002; see [[castle-triangle-origin-thesis]].

## Claims (gerb/castle)

- Gerb says Wackenhut historically ran perimeter security for DOE and DoD installations including the Nevada Test Site, [[groom-lake]] (where it caught Bob Lazar and friends camping to watch test flights) and [[los-alamos-national-laboratory]]. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 40:56-41:17, 44:46-45:11, 1:35:27-1:35:50)*
- It allegedly served as a CIA front: Gerb cites a March 1963 memo in the JFK files about Wackenhut wishing to serve as CIA cover, found by researcher "Blocked Epistemology", and Edward S. Herman's 1989 *The Terrorism Industry* on its board's intelligence ties. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:35:50-1:36:23)*
- Board members Gerb names: [[bobby-ray-inman]], [[frank-carlucci]] (whom Gerb has wagered was with Brad Sorenson at the 1988 Norton air show, see [[flux-liner-arv-saga]]) and Adm. [[william-f-raborn]], the Polaris director who also sat on SAIC's board and whose program hid the [[operation-sand-dollar]] deep-submergence work. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:36:37-1:38:14)*
- From 1987, per the General Accounting Office, Wackenhut subcontracted DOE guard services and provided the [[doe-special-response-team]] units, which Gerb says were its own employees; he speculates such a unit was the team Castle met. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:39:12-1:40:12, 1:40:48-1:41:27)*
- Gerb tells the story of Richard Mingus, a Federal Services guard at the Nevada Test Site, who in 1982 reported an armed attack on an Area 6 test site with a live bomb exposed above ground, escalated to President Reagan, only to learn the attackers were a Wackenhut drill. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:43:19-1:44:06)*
- In 2002 it sent Castle a recruiting invitation seeking helicopter experience; Castle thought it odd that they seemed to know his history as a flight-medic trainee. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 40:44-44:02, 1:34:05-1:34:31)*
