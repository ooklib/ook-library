Pippa Malmgren is [[harald-malmgren]]'s daughter, described by [[jesse-michels]] as a former special assistant to President George W. Bush on the National Economic Council. She co-hosts the interview, relays what her father told her off air, and supplies context on his career.

- Says she has long asked her father how, at 27, he ended up as joint liaison between the Joint Chiefs and the President during the Cuban Missile Crisis, and suggests he was "tracked" from youth (AEC and Oak Ridge correspondence, the paint-bucket meeting with [[carl-compton]]). *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 17:24-17:34, 19:25-19:56)*
- Says the Bluegill "tagalong" was casual to her father's generation because they knew it was not Russian or Chinese, and that people were probably encouraged not to ask questions. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 59:37-1:00:10)*
- Says Bissell told her father that he would "need to pass it along in the future at the right time"; the host frames the interview as fulfilling that. *(Per the video description:* *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) Description)**)*

Bush contemplated UFO disclosure with Stephen Hadley. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft.
