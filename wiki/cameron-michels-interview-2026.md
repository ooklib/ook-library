Jesse Michels' American Alchemy episode of 2026-09-15 (about 3h03m) is a long interview with [[grant-cameron]], a Canadian UFO researcher active since 1975. Cameron moves from his own 1975 Manitoba sightings near a US missile complex through Canadian and CIA history (Wilbert Smith, Arthur Lundahl, the CIA "weird desk"), the Emenegger and Sandler Holloman film story, the leak of the Wilson-Davis notes, Bob Lazar, and his contacts with Eric Walker and Ron Pandolfi, and ends with his thesis that the deepest secret is consciousness rather than hardware. All content is Cameron's account or Michels' commentary; much is second-hand or explicitly speculative.
