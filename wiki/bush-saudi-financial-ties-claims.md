The 911mmry compilation ends with a clip claiming that the Saudi royals and their associates have given the Bush family, their friends and related businesses $1.4 billion over three decades, contrasted with the president's $400,000 salary, and asking whether the Bushes think about "what's best for the Saudis" rather than the American public. The speaker (unnamed; the clip has a satirical tone) says "$1.4 billion just doesn't buy a lot of flights out of the country, it buys a lot of love". *(*([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 4:05)*)* It adds that the relationship with a regime Amnesty International condemns as a widespread human rights violator would "come back to haunt the Bushes", and after 9/11 it was an embarrassment they preferred no one ask about. *(*([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 5:44)*)*
