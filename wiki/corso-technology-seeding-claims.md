[[philip-j-corso]]'s central claim: Roswell material was fed by the [[army-foreign-technology-division]] to industry, laboratories and universities as ordinary R&D so the Army would gain a competitive edge, with firms allowed to take patents provided they returned benefit to the Army, "the people and the world if necessary". Nothing went on paper as alien. Gerb finds it parallels modern claims about contractors and unacknowledged waived special access programs. *(Unverified claim.)* *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 19:36-20:47, 20:47-21:20)*

## Claimed technologies

- Image intensifiers/passive night vision from the [[fort-belvoir-night-vision-laboratory]] with help from 48 industries; [[john-b-alexander]] challenged this after speaking to lab director Dr. Lou Cameron, who denied an ET eye lens helped. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 28:22-28:52, 1:10:07-1:10:24)*
- Fiber optics (a "broken wiring harness" of multicolor strands), used by [[bell-labs]]; contentious since French fiber-optic principles date to the 1840s and Bell improved it in the 1970s. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 28:52-29:50)*
- Super-tenacity fibers (composites, stealth; the book says Kevlar via DuPont 1965); molecular alignment of materials; fluidic amplification (heart pump). *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 30:46-32:32)*
- Transistors and integrated circuits: a stack of wafer-like components leading to the "micro module" (Corso quotes a 50-year and 200-year timeline compressed to 5 years); Gerb notes Bell's 1947 point contact transistor and Texas Instruments' Jack Kilby IC of 1958-59, and that [[jacques-vallee]] says Corso's IC claims are undocumented. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 32:24-33:57, 1:09:51-1:10:07)*
- The laser (Bell Labs, 1960) as the beings' long-range communication; anti-missile missiles for the Star Wars program ([[strategic-defense-initiative]]); brain-wave control of aircraft and missiles from the idea that the beings were part of craft guidance. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 34:05-34:21, 38:19-38:51, 53:23-53:54)*
- Corso: "we never quibbled over who discovered or invented a development"; extraterrestrials gave nothing voluntarily, so technology was taken, echoing [[brad-sorenson]]'s [[flux-liner-arv-saga]] claim that Roswell wreckage was taken without consent. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 34:30-35:40, 37:14-37:39)*
- Contractors named: [[bell-labs]] (AT&T/Western Electric; AT&T ran [[sandia-national-laboratories]] from 1949), [[sperry-rand]] and universities. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 29:58-30:46, 37:39-38:19)*
