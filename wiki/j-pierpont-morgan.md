Head of the [[house-of-morgan]] and, in the [[empire-of-shadows-rothschild-documentary-2023]] narrative, the man Grover Cleveland called on to save the gold reserve.

## Claims

- In the [[panic-of-1893]] he raised 65 million dollars in government bonds and negotiated with the [[rothschild-family]] to buy 3.5 million ounces of gold "for the Fed"; the bonds sold out in 30 minutes. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 26:28, 27:28, 27:35, 28:01)*
- His son Jack later led the house in World War I: see [[house-of-morgan]]. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 30:39)*
- Born 1837 to [[junius-spencer-morgan]]; sickly childhood, a year alone in the Azores, worked at a Wall Street firm for George Peabody & Co, married Amelia Sturges in 1861 (she died of tuberculosis four months later). ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 0:47, 1:22, 1:51, 2:48, 3:10)*
- Paid 300 dollars to avoid Civil War conscription and financed a resale of old Hall carbines to the government at over 600% markup, which the narrator calls unpopular war profiteering. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 3:35, 3:50, 4:04)*
- Founded Drexel, Morgan & Co with Anthony Drexel in 1871 (later J.P. Morgan & Co, precursor of JPMorgan Chase). ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 4:34, 4:39)*
- Applied [[morganization]] to railroads, buying 250,000 New York Central shares from William Vanderbilt (see [[cornelius-vanderbilt]]) and underwriting 40 million dollars of Northern Pacific bonds. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 7:09, 7:22)*
- Fought [[theodore-roosevelt]] over Northern Securities, built [[us-steel-corporation]], and led the rescues of [[panic-of-1893]] (his syndicate bought 65 million dollars of gold bonds with 3.5 million ounces of gold; Cleveland could sign under an old Civil War statute, and the bonds sold out in 22 minutes; the narrator says he profited and bankers then donated to the Republican nominee) and [[panic-of-1907]]. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 12:32, 12:46, 12:54, 13:15, 13:38)*
- Testified before the [[pujo-committee]], died in Rome on 31 March 1913 aged 75; source hub: [[most-jp-morgan-owned-america-documentary-2023]]. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 17:15, 17:54)*
