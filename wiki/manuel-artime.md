Dr. Manuel Artime is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the "public face" of the CIA's anti-Castro program and head of [[brigade-2506]] and of his Movimiento de Recuperacion Revolucionaria (MRR, ASR "mr"/"M de reol"). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 26:16-27:11)*

## Claims

- Cuban physician who fought alongside Castro against Batista, served briefly in the revolutionary government, fled Cuba in 1959 to Mexico, where CIA man Bernard Barker (a future Watergate burglar, per the host) first recruited him. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 26:22-26:46)*
- The MRR was chosen as the sponsored political organization to form a "government in arms" on the Cuban coast: the plan was to hold territory for 72 hours, declare a government so that, per the host's account of international law, the Neutrality Act would be void and foreign powers could support it overtly, prefiguring wider US intervention. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 27:00-27:56)*
- The Schlesinger memo (as read by the host) says liberal exiles feared Op 40 would end by installing a right-wing dictatorship "presumably under Artime"; host: the true plan was a pro-agency puppet dictator. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 39:19-39:53)* See [[operation-40]].

## Relationships

- Leader of [[brigade-2506]]'s political wing; recruited by Barker; see [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]], [[e-howard-hunt]] (liaison to exile groups).
