Grant Cameron is a Canadian UFO researcher (YouTube and X handle "whitehouseufo") who has investigated since the 1975 Manitoba "Charlie Red Star" flap and specializes in presidential and government UFO records, FOIA filings and Wilbert Smith's archive. Michels says his name shows up whenever he traces a deep thread. He authored "Beyond Managing Magic 2: Landing the Plane" and a book on people who say they flew the craft (ASR "UFO Skypods"). He also says he made the Wilson-Davis notes public.

## Claims and activities

- Says a May 1975 sighting near Carman, Manitoba, made him a lifelong researcher; he was studying at university and had been raised expecting to become a minister. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 6:06-9:49, 11:38-12:02)*
- Says he recovered all of Wilbert Smith's files, including Canadian embassy correspondence that is not in the official Canadian archives. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 32:52-33:21)*
- Says he is a presidential-library researcher: he finishes the UFO material in about three hours because there are almost no documents, then reads oral histories to understand how presidents decided. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:53:24-1:53:38, 2:09:39-2:10:35)*
- Says he filed 47 FOIA requests at one second after midnight on 20 January to be first in line, including one for Reince Priebus briefing documents. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:10:35-2:13:27)*
- Describes the leak of the Wilson-Davis notes: [[wilson-davis-memo-leak-account-cameron]]. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:14:25-1:19:25)*
- Says he has spent 25 years chasing [[ron-pandolfi]], sourcing his information from people around him rather than Pandolfi himself. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:25:02-2:26:07)*
- Says he now holds that the core secret is consciousness and a portal/time-travel technology, not nuts-and-bolts hardware. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:36:53-1:37:00, 2:47:41-2:47:58)*
