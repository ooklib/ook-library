Former director of the [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|National Geospatial-Intelligence
Agency (NGA)]]. Described [[sentient-nro-ai-program|NRO Sentient]] as using "automated
inferencing" to aid intelligence collection. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 36:33–36:50)*

## Claims

- Gerb suggests Cardillo was possibly involved in blockage of the Lockheed Martin OAP "Kona Blue"
  UFO-materials transfer, conducted by the CIA DS&T and other parties.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 36:42–36:50)*
- Curiously ended up on the board of [[peraton|Peraton]] — originally the Lockheed Martin
  Enterprise Integration Group, an arm containing many of Lockheed's black-budget projects,
  severed from the company and acquired by Veritas Capital shortly after the failed 2010 Kona
  Blue transfer. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 36:50–37:14)*
- Gerb's Lockheed investigation names Cardillo, alongside [[glenn-gaffney|Glenn Gaffney]] and
  possibly [[mary-sturdivant|Mary Sturdivant]], as a possible final authority blocking
  [[james-t-ryder|James Ryder]]'s attempted [[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]] material transfer,
  based on research by Rob Jones (*Subrosa*), Eric Davis's statements, and Chris Sharp's
  reporting — while noting this is alleged, not definitive.
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:34:10–2:35:21)*
- Cardillo served as DDNI for Intelligence Integration at the time of the transfer, and separately
  held roles as the sixth director of NGA and deputy director of the DIA.
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:34:39–2:35:02)*
- Joined Peraton's board of advisers in May 2019 — the same entity Rob Jones theorizes may have
  ultimately received some of Lockheed's divested UFO materials via the EIG sale.
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:38:00–2:38:19)*
