Major General Dawn M. Dunlop (referred to by the ASR transcript at points as "Don Dunlop")
served as director of operational capability requirements, deputy chief of staff for strategy,
integration and requirements within the headquarters of the Air Force from 2018 to 2019, and in
that period also served as director of the DoD [[special-access-program-central-office|Special
Access Program Central Office (SAPCO)]]. Gerb identifies Dunlop as an individual he believes "did indeed serve to gatekeep UFO legacy
program operations from official channels" and possibly operated within programs at a T&E level
themselves. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 1:35:42–1:36:04)*

## Claims

- Gerb draws a structural parallel to the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]], in which
  then-SAPCO director Air Force General H. Marshall Ward gave Vice Admiral Thomas Wilson
  actionable intelligence about UFO legacy-program records housed within the office later known
  as [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-acquisition-and-sustainment|USD A&S]] — Gerb argues it is
  not unreasonable to think the SAPCO director role continues to serve a gatekeeping function to
  this day, possibly subordinate to the director of the
  [[national-program-special-management-staff|NPMS]]. *([hidden-wing](../raw/
  -IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 1:36:04–1:36:52)*
- From 2009 to 2012, Dunlop served as vice commander and then commander of the
  [[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards 412th Test Wing]]. Gerb notes witness "Ed" received letters of
  commendation signed by Dunlop during his time working within the
  [[hidden-wing-air-force-program|Hidden Wing]] legacy program at the 412th, and calls on the
  administration to subpoena Dunlop. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md)
  1:36:52–1:37:16)*
