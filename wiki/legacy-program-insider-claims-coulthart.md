[[ross-coulthart]] says he has spoken with more than a dozen, probably 15 or more, people who operate or have operated inside the "legacy retrieval and reverse-engineering program", and that he is in no doubt it exists. This page collects what he says about how the program is organised and behaves. All of it is attributed, unnamed-source testimony.

## Claims

- He says he corroborates a claim only when two or three independent witnesses who do not know each other tell the same story, and that this is what he has for the program's existence. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 45:15-45:44)*
- There is "a control group, a cabal", mostly people in private aerospace (Battelle, Lockheed Martin, Northrop, Boeing, Raytheon), often coming up through CIA science and technology, seen as a safe pair of hands and periodically bringing trusted politicians into the loop. It "doesn't abide by any rules". Compare [[ufo-control-group]], [[battelle-memorial-institute]], [[lockheed-martin]], [[northrop-grumman]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 37:07-38:15)*
- He says the program treated Congress with contempt for 50-60 years, and that trillions, not billions, of dollars were spent without taxpayer knowledge or consent, hidden inside other programs as [[david-grush]] has explained. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 38:55-39:04, 41:36-42:35)*
- The program uses waived unacknowledged special access programs (his "WOAPs"), the top tier above unacknowledged SAPs, nominally reported to the Gang of Eight; see [[special-access-program]] and [[congressional-oversight-and-read-in-claims-coulthart]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 33:42-34:29)*
- "Noble cause corruption" or "process corruption": program insiders decided not to honour the Constitution or report to Congress. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 41:28-41:50)*
- A former very senior NASA official who also held a top defence department executive role told Coulthart he knows nothing about the program; Coulthart says the person three rungs down at NASA was the gatekeeper for the legacy program and had run a lunar reconnaissance mission relevant to anomalies on the moon. He suspects the program hides NASA photographs and data. See [[nasa]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:20:01-1:21:56)*
- Numbers: at the very tip perhaps 24 people, but thousands read in overall; he compares it to the Manhattan Project with its roughly 130,000 staff, one traitor (Klaus Fuchs) being able to blow it, and says the program's surveillance level is "insane", judged by the measures his sources require. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:21:56-1:22:53)*
- The insiders are patriots proud of the program, not angry at it. Coulthart says they think secrecy has subsumed the original rationale and there is no good reason left to keep it secret. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 54:39-55:01, 1:23:13-1:24:15)*
- He thinks people involved include special forces tier-1 operators from the UK, US and Australia who described retrievals of obviously non-mundane technology; and that a well-resourced team (perhaps [[department-of-energy]] related, from the "DOE jackets" in the Peru story) deploys to investigate recovery sites. See [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:18:16-2:20:26)*
- Coulthart says he believes some retrieval sites are not yet publicly known beyond [[magenta-italy-crash-1933]], [[kingman-arizona-crash-1953]], [[aztec-new-mexico-crash-1948]] and [[roswell-incident-1947]], and that he has seen imagery of scientists investigating one of them. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:18:16-2:18:51)*
- Insiders sometimes died: he says "some of them have" got a bullet in the back of the head, and that people have been murdered, intimidated, bullied and had clearances stripped to protect the secret. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:26:22-1:26:33, 1:30:47-1:31:14)*
- [[david-grush]] and [[jake-barber]] first reached him via people in the program, and he thinks both may know more than they say, though he does not buy that they are part of a controlled disclosure. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:17:20-1:18:28)*
