Claims by [[matthew-brown]] in the [[matthew-brown-michels-interview-2026]] that consciousness is manipulated by remote technological means. Brown says his fields at the Pentagon exposed him to frontier science and its transmutation into capabilities, but that he is not a scientist and limits himself to public sources; "I don't want to get myself in jail". *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 2:57:58-3:00:33)*

## Claims

- Biotech (genetic editing, synthetic biological constructs, and the interplay of directed energy with modified biology) is a rapidly advancing, under-recognized trend that "overlays" the phenomenon. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 3:18:33-3:19:29)*
- He has "very good reason to believe" the mind is "no longer sovereign": it can be interfaced remotely, people can definitely be made to "hear" things by directed energy, dreams are subject to technological manipulation including implantation at a distance (he points to MIT work) and ideas can be implanted in waking life; he says he is certain, that this is public knowledge (voice-to-skull, pulsed microwave, light modulating DNA) and that a motivated party could replicate it from gray literature. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 3:19:29-3:21:01, 3:23:05-3:23:37, 3:25:33-3:25:49)*
- Michels adds Colin Ross' books on MK-Ultra ("CIA Doctors") and directed energy, Michael Levin's bioelectric morphology and Havana syndrome, and calls it suppressed science; Brown agrees. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 3:21:01-3:23:05)*
- On consciousness: he says consciousness is "part of the game", that integration with technology is key and that measurable evidence of consciousness surviving death, if not classified, would trigger a re-examination of priorities, not Armageddon. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 2:19:58-2:23:35)*
- He treats [[extended-electrodynamics]] and [[thomas-bearden]]'s claims as a limited hangout around a core truth: non-transverse waves, near-instant transmission, energy extraction that is reversible and dangerous, with the Russians well versed; he learned of it through [[kirk-mcconnell]]'s prior appearance on the show. He also floats bismuth and repulsive magnetic behavior (Lazar-related), and says reverse engineering has produced propulsion and beamed power that avoid onboard power sources, with orbital power beaming for indefinite surveillance. He is certain some craft come from outside the atmosphere, without knowing whether interstellar or interplanetary. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 3:00:33-3:08:45)*
