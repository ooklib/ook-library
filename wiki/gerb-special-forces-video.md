A video (about 46 minutes, dated 2024-06-16, [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcvuglS7ps4)) in which [[uap-gerb]] analyzes line by line the March 2024 Infographics Show video "US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs", the testimony of an anonymous Vietnam-era soldier who says he was read into a crash-retrieval organization called "the program". Chapters: Intro, Vietnam UFO Crash, The Program, Craft & Material Handling, Craft, Biologics, Hypothesis & Conclusion. Gerb calls it possibly the most crucial firsthand whistleblower account of all time. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 0:00-1:55)*

Gerb notes the Infographics narrator says the account is based on interviews and written correspondence edited into one timeline, so it is unclear whether it is a real anonymous whistleblower. Gerb tests the story against Vietnam-era units and operations, other witnesses (mostly redacted [[steven-greer]] [[disclosure-project]] entries), [[leonard-stringfield]]'s status reports, and [[david-grush]]'s claims. His conclusion: the timeline (October to December 1968 crash, October to December 1969 induction, service 1969 to 2004) is fairly confident, the South American and Irish crash matches are medium confidence, and his speculative candidate for the identity is [[john-m-riggs]]. See [[special-forces-witness-infographics]] for the claims themselves. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 39:41-45:06)*
