DARPA's Security and Intelligence Directorate is listed among the program-protection/
exposure-mitigation services safeguarding the [[ufo-legacy-programs|legacy program]] pyramid.
*([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 6:39–6:54)* DARPA is also based in/near [[crystal-city-virginia|Crystal City]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 40:07–40:14)*

Gerb states he "personally fears" DARPA's Security and Intelligence Directive (SID), calling it an
office "not afraid to employ wet works to silence insider or outsider threats to exotic RDT&E
programs." *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:02:32–2:02:50)*

- Per [[richard-dolan|Richard Dolan]]'s 2021 source, a leaked classified UAP Task Force report
  described DARPA-spearheaded, "Advanced Group Six"-managed exotic-propulsion RDT&E at Area 51
  and [[tonopah-test-range|Tonopah Test Range]]; separately, 2016 NNSA spokesperson Darwin Morgan
  named DARPA as a customer of [[area-6|Area 6]]'s aerial operations facility (UAV testing,
  operated by [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]]) on the Nevada National Security Site. Gerb
  speculates Area 6 may be the physical home of "Advanced Group Six."
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:19:00–2:21:10, 2:26:12–2:26:29)*

- [[vince-devine|Vince Devine]] (later Northrop Grumman director of security/SAPCO) served as SAP
  functional area lead for DARPA's Strategic Technology Office (via ManTech).
  *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 2:27:05–2:27:22)*
- [[donald-c-winter|Donald C. Winter]] worked under DARPA in the 1980s on the
  [[strategic-defense-initiative|Strategic Defense Initiative]], primarily the
  [[talon-gold|Talon Gold]] laser-tracking program. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 39:00–39:24)*
