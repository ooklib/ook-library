In [[karl-nell]]'s reading, as relayed by [[uap-gerb]], the Schumer Amendment ([[uap-disclosure-act-2023]]) deliberately targets UAP proper, not "TNO" (technology of unknown origin, per Gerb's transcript of the slide). The claimed difference: UAP have no prosaic attribution and non-human origin, whereas a TNO temporarily resists identification only because of observation limits but has a known human origin, foreign or domestic. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 4:39-6:36,)*

## Claims

- The amendment's UAP definition: any object operating or capable of operating in outer space, the atmosphere, or on or under the ocean, lacking prosaic attribution due to performance characteristics and properties not previously known to be achievable under commonly understood physics. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 5:18-5:48)*
- Terminology strategy: shifting UFO to UAP, USO to USP, flying saucer to UAP and ET to NHI strips stigma, and these terms now sit in legislation. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 4:07-4:39)*
- Six UAP characteristics are cited, the sixth being physical or invasive biological effects, which Gerb speculates was added at [[gary-nolan]]'s urging. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 4:39-5:11)*
- Terms of reference: "non-human intelligence" appears at least 18 times; a "legacy program" is any government or private effort to collect, exploit or reverse-engineer UAP material or NHI biological evidence; the eminent domain clause covers crash debris, vehicles and technology lacking prosaic attribution or known human manufacture. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 7:17-8:37)*
- Nell says [[project-blue-book]] spent most of its effort (94% or 80%, depending on statistics) on explainable TNO-type cases. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 6:12-6:36)*
