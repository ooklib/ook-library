Cameron proposes that Majestic-12 may be a continuity-of-government structure, with UFO issues handled under a heading that does not seem to apply. Evidence he cites: John Alexander's remark, a Canadian emergency-management chief's deathbed claim, Blue Book files sending Kecksburg material to Mount Weather, and a FEMA head at the post-Kecksburg meeting.

## Claims

- [[john-b-alexander]] said MJ-12 was real but was about continuity of government, and to look at the people on the list. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:03:37-2:03:54)*
- [[paul-hellyer]] told an Ontario audience (ASR "Brford") that the head of Canadian emergency management, dying of ALS, told him "it's all real" and that he was taken to Langley, then Area 51, and sat inside a craft. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:04:03-2:05:28)*
- Blue Book files on the 1965 Kecksburg crash mention Michigan metal debris and sending material to "project Greengrass", which Cameron says is Mount Weather; Walker said he was at Kecksburg, and the FEMA head was in Johnson's first meeting the day after. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:05:37-2:07:25)*
- Michels raises a false-flag alien-invasion contingency; Cameron replies that presidential libraries suggest bureaucratic layers, not schemes. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:08:07-2:10:35)*
