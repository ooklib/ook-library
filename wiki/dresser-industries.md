Dresser Industries, an oil-derrick component maker, is the firm where [[george-h-w-bush]] got his
first job in 1948; [[eyes-wide-open]] presents it, citing Joseph Trento, as one of the first of many
oil companies used by the intelligence community as fronts.

## Claims

- Controlled by [[brown-brothers-harriman]] since 1928; on acquiring it, new partner
  [[prescott-bush]] appointed friend and fellow Ohio native [[neil-mallon]] president despite no oil
  and gas experience; Mallon stayed about three decades. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 21:27-22:17)*
- Per Trento, a firm that "would often collaborate with the CIA by giving agents cover
  assignments." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 21:27-21:55)*
- Prescott Bush sat on its board by 1942, and the host says it "would become instrumental to the
  rise" of his son. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 12:21-12:45)*
- Baker (via host): in October 1945 Dresser expanded into Whittier, California, [[richard-nixon]]'s
  hometown, the same month a "large New York financial house" representative allegedly went to
  California to find a Republican challenger to Jerry Voorhis (ASR "Voris"); the host asks whether
  that representative was Bush or Mallon. Speculative. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 26:18-27:15)*
- April 10, 1953: [[allen-dulles]] met Mallon, its president, and Bush about a "pilot project in the
  Caribbean." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 33:50-34:14)*

## Relationships

- Owned by [[brown-brothers-harriman]]; led by [[neil-mallon]]; [[prescott-bush]] director;
  first employer of [[george-h-w-bush]].

## Additional claims (Bush and the Cowboys, Chapter 2)


- Bush's first job there was, per the host, a cover job: he was sent to Odessa in 1948; its head [[neil-mallon]] tagged along to help him form Texas contacts and founded the Dallas Council on World Affairs in 1951; Mallon hosted Dallas's [[crusade-for-freedom]] chapter, funding [[radio-free-europe]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 0:38-1:09, 15:39-16:11, 16:59-17:14, 21:01-21:17)*
- After "5 years of doing seemingly nothing related to oil and gas extraction," Bush left to found [[zapata-petroleum]], described as "graduating from his cover job at dresser." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 44:58-45:37)*
- Host: Dresser stayed independent for five decades "presumably continuing to serve as an intelligence front on the side" (speculation) until its 1998 merger with rival [[halliburton]], then led by [[dick-cheney]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 46:11-46:52)*

## Additional claims (Bush and the CIA, Chapter 3)

- Host speculation: Dresser was in negotiations with Hughes Tool Company for its patented drill bit in the late 1940s-early 1950s while Bush worked there, suggesting an early Bush-[[howard-hughes]] link. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 24:05-24:37)*
