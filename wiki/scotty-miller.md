Scotty Miller (ASR "Scotty Mile/Miler") is a former CIA counterintelligence officer whom [[geoffrey-cruickshank]] identifies as the probable anonymous leaker behind the later [[majestic-documents]] releases. This is Cruickshank's inference, not an established fact,, whereas Gerb's wiki entry describes the [[burned-memo]] source only as an anonymous "Source S-1" counter-intelligence officer.

## Claims

- Cruickshank is "99% sure" that the important memo and the letter accompanying the [[burned-memo]] were written by Miller, described as a counterintelligence agent for [[james-jesus-angleton]]; the letter says Miller saw [[jaime-shandera]] and [[william-moore]] "getting the runaround" from a group called the Aviary, so he decided to set things right and leak to [[timothy-cooper]]. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 26:02-26:40)*
- Miller was New Mexico chapter president of the [[association-of-former-intelligence-officers]], had observed Cooper at meetings, judged him trustworthy, and Cooper's father had peripheral UFO dealings in the Air Force. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 26:40-27:14)*
- Cruickshank says Miller fed documents into the incinerator when [[richard-helms]] (in office two weeks in 1973, per Cruickshank) ordered files burned, and saved the burned memo and others out of anger at [[richard-nixon]]. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 48:18-48:58, 1:08:53-1:09:19)*
- The postcard pointing Shandera and Moore to Box 189 (see [[cutler-twining-memo]]) came from Addis Ababa, where Miller was station chief 1960-64 and so knew the postal system. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 57:31-57:46)*
- Cruickshank places him among Helms loyalists with Angleton and Ray Rocca. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 1:09:02-1:09:19)*
