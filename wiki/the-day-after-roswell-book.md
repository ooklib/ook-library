The 1997 global bestseller co-written by [[philip-j-corso]] and (per Gerb) Bill Burns [sic; ASR/likely William Birnes], published for the 50th anniversary of [[roswell-incident-1947]]. UAP Gerb discards it as the basis of analysis because it allegedly contains embellishments "possibly inserted by the other co-writer", and because critics ([[stanton-friedman]], [[john-b-alexander]], [[jacques-vallee]]) aim almost all criticism at it. See [[corso-criticism-claims]]. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 4:32-5:11, 1:08:22-1:08:40)*

- Reportedly Corso had only 24 hours to edit the book, with no control over creative liberties. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:09:19-1:09:35)*

- Added material: Corso forcing a CIA covert-ops chief to self-delete, the Roswell-desert first chapter (Corso was not present), Hillenkoetter/MJ-12 details, "world savior" tone. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 5:51-6:43, 1:23:00-1:23:40)*
