Jesse Marcel Sr. was the 509th Bomb Group intelligence officer dispatched to recover the Foster Ranch debris in July 1947. Only the claims [[uap-gerb]] relays in one video are recorded here; see [[roswell-incident-1947]] for the wider case.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-1)

- Gerb says Marcel recovered wreckage from the debris field found by rancher Mac Brazel on the Foster Ranch, loaded it in a Jeep and showed pieces to his family, including his son Jesse Marcel Jr., on the way home. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 3:07-4:19)*
- Gerb says Marcel was photographed in [[roger-ramey]]'s office with weather-balloon foil for press purposes and, in a 1978 interview with Stanton Friedman, admitted he was ordered to take part in a cover-up. He then ordered his family not to talk, and moved to Fort Worth. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 4:51-5:21)*
- Haut says Marcel privately admitted the involvement to him in 1980, and relays that Marcel and his son saw markings on the wreckage. Gerb links the "I-beam" material Marcel showed his son to the metal piece in [[stephen-lovekin]]'s account. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 9:03,)*
