Naval Air Station Patuxent River ("Pax River") is headquarters for
[[naval-air-systems-command|NAVAIR]], the Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD),
and the US Navy Test Pilot School (USNTPS), and is one of ~23 US DoD Major Range and Test
Facility Bases (MRTFBs). *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:14:52–1:16:48)*

## Claims

- Described by Gerb as the "nexus point" for Naval Aviation Research, Development, Test and
  Evaluation (RDT&E) — where new aircraft/aviation systems undergo flight testing and systems
  integration. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:15:03–1:15:20)*
- NAVAIR PEOs at Pax River are, per Gerb, where the alleged MITRE reverse-engineering legacy
  activity is housed both administratively and operationally, alongside MITRE's own MLAN, VA
  facility. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:14:52–1:15:03, 1:18:29–1:18:37)*
- Rep. [[eric-burlison]] reportedly took a White House staff site visit to Pax River looking for
  hangars that might have stored recovered vehicles; he did not find any but was impressed by
  the infrastructure, and separately toured a facility reportedly slated for a vehicle transfer
  from [[lockheed-martin]] to Bigelow Aerospace. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:17:11–1:17:53)*
- Chris Sharp (Liberation Times) reported sources describing a vehicle or vehicles of unknown
  origin stored at Pax River, in an article titled "UFO allegedly stored at East Coast Naval
  Facility as material transfer claims resurface." *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:18:02–1:18:21)* See [[chris-sharp]].
- Listed as a facility MITRE should be required to name/preserve records for under the
  congressional interrogative's preservation act. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:51:32–1:51:49)*
