"Special Operations Manual: Extraterrestrial Entities and Technology, Recovery and Disposal" (SOM1-01, nicknamed "Psalm 101" in transcript ASR), an alleged 1954 "Top Secret Majic Eyes Only" manual for [[majestic-12]] units. Leaked in 1994 as undeveloped 35mm film in a green box to aviation writer Don Berliner; the Woods developed it in a darkroom. Rated by [[ryan-s-wood]], [[dr-robert-wood]] and [[stanton-friedman]] as equal or better in authenticity than the [[eisenhower-briefing-document]]. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 12:44, 14:37, 27:22)*

## Contents (per Gerb's summary)

- Purpose: MJ-12 treats UFOs as highest-order national security; public knowledge would have negative impact; danger if advanced tech reaches adversaries; MJ-12 established 24 Sept 1947 on recommendation of Bush and Forrestal. Goals: recovery by any means of ET materials/devices and entities, special teams, secure secret facilities in the US, covert operations "in collusion with the CIA" at home and abroad, and absolute secrecy. NHI viewed in 1954 as a benign presence with technology far beyond human science; the group already held several dead biologics. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 28:24, 29:09, 30:34)*
- Craft taxonomy: metallic discs (50-300 ft, dome, three landing-gear sets), very rare cigar/"Tic Tac" shapes (up to 2,000 ft, above lower atmosphere, 7,000+ mph), ovoid/"ice cream cone" 30-40 ft, and newer triangles (up to 300 ft) described as some of the earliest mentions of flying triangles. Gerb links the conical shape to the 1976 Tehran and Cash-Landrum cases. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 31:31, 32:12, 33:02, 43:26)*
- Two EBE types: Type 1 humanoid 5 ft-5 ft 4, chalky yellow skin, almond eyes; Type 2 "classic gray" 3 ft 5-4 ft 2, large black eyes, blue-gray skin, non-mammalian. Recovered materials 1947-1953 (Roswell to [[kingman-arizona-crash-1953]]) reportedly like aluminum foil/magnesium sheeting with inscriptions, matching Jesse Marcel's account. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 33:17, 34:47)*
- Press blackout, witness discrediting and deception; crash procedures; functional-looking craft assigned to "Red Team" (Wood notes Blue Lab at Wright-Patterson). *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 35:17, 35:43, 36:09)*
- Classification/disposition table: intact craft and fragments to "Area 51 S4"; media, maps and charts to Kirtland AFB Building 21; deceased entities to Wright-Patterson "Blue Room"; living entities to OPNAV BBS-01 (possibly Bermuda Biological Station, unproven). "Loss of EBE life is acceptable." *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 38:34, 42:45, 43:10)* Public-disclosure contingency plan MJ-1 1949 is referenced but missing, as are the reference appendix and UFOB identification guide (retyped by Woods to fit the table of contents). *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 36:56, 37:20, 37:52)*
- Change-control page: stamp Kirtland AFB, N.MEX (correct 1954 abbreviation); MJ-1 and MJ-4 amended it 1954-58 with initials EWL and JRT. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 13:33, 27:47, 28:02)*

## Authentication arguments

- A retired US Government Printing Office officer said the raised "Z"s were an artifact of a hot-lead press, fonts, em-dashes and "first aid" capitalization were period-correct, and guessed the CIA printed it. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 25:45)*
- Other hallmarks: "craft tape", "screw driver" as two words, "N.Mex", the typo "desicant", Albuquerque phone-book matches for the initials living near each other on Kirtland's Perimeter Road. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 53:34, 44:38)*
- Rebuttals to skeptics: odd fonts appear in other 1950s documents (a 1952 NSA-founding memo); restricted-data classification co-existing with Top Secret is legitimate under the 1947 Atomic Energy Act; Area 51 was already under contract in 1951 and Groom Lake had a borax mine. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 54:22, 55:04, 55:58, 56:05)*
- Jan Aldrich's 1996 critique (no grid/photo instructions, "satellite" pre-Sputnik, interchange of MJ-12/Majic 12/Majestic 12) is called by Gerb largely fluff. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 57:25)*
- A Navy yeoman, Dale Bailey, allegedly saw the manual when destroying classified UFO material in 1976; Wood is locating files. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 53:11)*
- Harry B. Cooper (father of [[timothy-cooper]]) worked in printing at Air Defense Command 1957-63; an LeMay commendation is offered as context for why leaks reached his son. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 40:20, 41:52)*
