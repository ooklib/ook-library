Alcide De Gasperi (ASR-misspelled "Alice Doug Gasperi"), Christian Democratic prime minister of
Italy from 1945 to 1953 across eight different cabinets, never purged fascist-linked members of
parliament, per Eyes Wide Open.

## Claims

- Under Prime Minister De Gasperi, the Christian Democrats ruled with eight different cabinets
  from 1945 to 1953 and never once purged any members of parliament; to this day researchers
  speculate that former fascists ran rampant in the early Christian Democratic Party.
