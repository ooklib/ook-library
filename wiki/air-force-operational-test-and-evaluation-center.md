AFOTEC contracted [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] in 2005 for
biological/chemical/radiological/nuclear/explosive (CBRNE) work at
[[kirtland-air-force-base|Kirtland AFB]], NM, per SAIC SVP Chuck Zang's public statement about
assessing "new and emerging technologies." *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 57:13–58:56)*

## Claims

- Historically supported the [[deputy-under-secretary-of-defense-advanced-systems-and-concepts|
  Deputy Under Secretary of Defense for Advanced Systems and Concepts (DUSD ASC)]] for
  non-traditional acquisition programs. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 58:56–59:14)*

## Claims (from the Hidden Wing video)

- AFOTEC is "a direct reporting unit under US Air Force headquarters" and "the Air Force
  Independent Test Agency responsible for testing under operationally realistic conditions,"
  operating since January 1974. Gerb notes that, like other nominally independent FFRDCs, "there
  is no such thing as independence when dealing with waved, unacknowledged special access
  programs." AFOTEC's [[aftte|AFTTE]] Policy, Programs and Resources Division acts as its T&E
  policy focal point, alongside Air Force MAJCOMs and Space Force field commands.
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:39:35–1:41:07)*
- AFOTEC is headquartered at [[kirtland-air-force-base|Kirtland AFB]] (also home to
  [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia National Labs]]), and operates four major detachments at
  Eglin AFB, Hill AFB (within the UTTR), [[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards AFB]] (412th Test Wing),
  and Nellis AFB (within the NTR). Gerb theorizes AFOTEC elements support
  [[aftte|AFTTE]] operations on MRTFBs dealing with T&E of technologies of unknown origin and
  reverse-engineered airframes. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  1:41:08–1:41:56)*
