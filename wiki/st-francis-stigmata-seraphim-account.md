In the panel [[diana-pasulka]] reinterprets the 13th-century stigmata of St Francis of Assisi as an "angel contact event", the iconic case that "convinced" her to appear. This is her scholarly reading, not an established finding. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 16:39-17:29)*

## Claims

- In the primary sources Francis meets a "seraphim" that descends with light and an eye in its middle; the best of the competing reports, from Brother Leo (about 15, watching), says the being "dealt harshly" with Francis. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 17:29-18:26)*
- Francis was injured, the injuries became the "wounds of Christ" tradition, and the event was kept secret between Francis and Leo; he most likely died from the wounds. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 18:26-19:00)*
- She says the burns on his hands "comport one to one" with what the CIA is studying in people who have had UFO encounters, via [[gary-nolan]] (a Stanford microbiologist and Nobel nominee, per Pasulka) to whom "the CIA came knocking". *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 19:00-19:33)*
- Michels, relaying [[peter-thiel]]'s Rogan remarks, notes the last thing Francis wrote is the Greek letter Tau, meaning "conversion", and reads the encounter as a "forcing function"; Thiel's argument is that aliens with warp technology must be angels or demons because of dual-use technology. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 37:39-38:43)*
