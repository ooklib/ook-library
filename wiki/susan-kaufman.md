Susan Kaufman, ex-wife of bootlegger-turned-liquor-magnate [[lewis-rosenstiel]], is the source of
the central allegations opening this Eyes Wide Open episode: an eyewitness account of the
[[blue-suite]] sex parties at New York's Plaza Hotel in the 1950s.

## Claims

- Testified in a 1970 affidavit to the New York State Legislative Committee on Crime (sealed to
  this day) that she was brought by her husband into Room 233 of the Plaza Hotel — the "Blue
  Suite" — where she witnessed Rosenstiel engaged in sexual activity with [[j-edgar-hoover]],
  [[francis-spellman]], [[roy-cohn]], and several unidentified young men. She broke her public
  silence in the 1990s, testifying from her home in France. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 0:00-1:32)*
- Also stated she personally caught [[roy-cohn]] in bed with her then-husband Rosenstiel prior to
  being brought to the Blue Suite, and remarked this was "not normal behavior for a lawyer and his
  client." *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 40:44-40:56)*
- Reported that at the orgies she witnessed, Hoover was addressed by others present using the
  female name "Mary Hoover" — matching an independent account from Allen Dulles's mistress Mary
  Bancroft. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 38:04-38:10, 39:51-39:57)*
