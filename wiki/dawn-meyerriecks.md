Dawn Meyerriecks (the ASR transcripts across Gerb's videos render the surname inconsistently as
"Meyer," "Merix," "Myrix," and "Myer Ricks" — this wiki treats all four as one person given the
matching career details) began her career at [[trw|TRW]] (later bought by
[[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]], which Gerb directly implicates in derivative-technology
RDT&E) as technical staff, 1981–1993, went on to serve as a JPL project manager and CTO of the
Defense Information Systems Agency, then as Assistant Director of National Intelligence for
Acquisition, Technology and Facilities
([[deputy-director-of-national-intelligence-for-acquisition-technology-and-facilities|ADNI
ATNF]]) from 2009 to 2013 — a role Gerb pairs with its DoD counterpart, the Under Secretary of
Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S), as imperative to legacy-program operations,
specifically through NRO acquisitions — and finally as Deputy Director of the CIA's Directorate
of Science and Technology from 2013 to 2021 (8 years), immediately after which she joined
[[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]'s corporate board. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md)
1:42:24–1:43:22, [gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:32:03–2:32:56)*
