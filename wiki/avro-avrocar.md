The Avrocar (ASR: "avocar") was a joint US-Canadian disc aircraft project led by John Frost. The source frames it as an attempt to rebuild the Nazi disc program.

## Claims (jm/townsend-brown)

- Host claims Miethe (ASR: "Maita"), captured via Brown's task force, ended in Canada on it; Frost approached [[victor-schauberger]] in 1953; the project nominally failed in 1961 but moved to Wright-Patterson as Project Y / Silver Bug. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 57:11-58:46)*
- Henry Kwanda (Romanian engineer) consulted on Project Y and, with Jacques Coron, brought Brown to France. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 59:14-59:39)*
- Curtis LeMay allegedly angrily refused someone entry to the secret room at Wright-Patterson. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 58:46-59:05)*
- The Avrocar reportedly hovered 10 feet and wobbled uncontrollably, tied by [[nicap]] lore to "fluttering leaf" UFO reports. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 1:10:37-1:10:55)*
