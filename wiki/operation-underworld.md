Operation Underworld was, per Eyes Wide Open, a later-declassified series of WWII-era liaisons
between the [[office-of-naval-intelligence|Office of Naval Intelligence]] and American Mafia
figures, struck after the 1942 SS Normandie fire, trading dock security for
[[lucky-luciano]]'s early release from prison.

## Claims

- After the SS Normandie, a troop-transport ship, was set ablaze in New York Harbor in 1942 by
  [[lucky-luciano]]'s associates (who controlled the New York Dock Workers Union) and the attack
  was blamed on Nazi sabotage, the embarrassed Office of Naval Intelligence approached the
  Luciano/[[meyer-lansky]]-controlled union for help securing the docks against further such
  incidents; this led to a deal, later declassified as Operation Underworld, comprising a series
  of ONI-Mafia liaisons for domestic coastal security and support for the 1943 invasion of Sicily.
  ONI secured Luciano's cooperation from his prison cell in exchange for his early release and
  deportation to Sicily; the deal guaranteed the Luciano-controlled dock union complete control
  over New York Harbor security and, per Eyes Wide Open, the "free flow of narcotics from
  European and Cuban heroin labs." Luciano was released in 1946 and deported to Sicily, where he
  reorganized the Sicilian Mafia. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 24:29-26:14)*

## Relationships

- ONI recruited [[lucky-luciano]] and his dock union; presented by Eyes Wide Open as the direct
  precedent for [[operation-x]]'s CIA-Mafia partnership model.
