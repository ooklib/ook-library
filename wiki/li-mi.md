General Li Mi (ASR: "Lee Me", "Lei Me") commanded a KMT remnant (93rd Division, 26th and 8th Army
elements) that retreated from Yunnan into Burma after the 1949 communist victory. Per Eyes Wide
Open, his largely Hui-Muslim troops became the first CIA-armed "narco army" under
[[operation-paper]].

## Claims

- Crossed into Burma with remnants of the 93rd Division, 26th and 8th Armies; a further ~5,000 KMT
  who entered northern Laos were dealt with by the French and repatriated to Taiwan. Most of his men
  were Hui from Yunnan, historically opium growers who supplied Bangkok. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 12:19-13:25)*
- Armed by the CIA via Thailand, grew to about 4,000 men, built permanent training bases, and
  launched failed invasions of Yunnan in June and August 1951-52. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 36:32-39:38)*
- After US aid ended his men settled in Shan State, married local women, expanded opium
  production, and kept trading with Thai General [[phao-sriyanon]]'s police; Burma found three
  morphine refineries at a KMT base in May 1959. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 40:03-41:39, 46:11-46:31)*
- Evacuated to Taiwan in 1953-54; remaining KMT forces were overwhelmed by a joint Burmese and
  Chinese communist army on 26 January 1961 and retreated into Laos, where some reportedly
  merged with CIA-backed Hmong. Former troops fed the rise of [[khun-sa]]. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 44:07-47:47)*
