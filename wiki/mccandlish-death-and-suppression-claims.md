Claims about the professional and personal consequences [[mark-mccandlish]] said he suffered after speaking about the ARV, his 2021 death, and Gerb's unresolved suspicions. Recorded as claims; the death was officially an apparent suicide and Gerb states no proof.

## Claims (gerb/mccandlish)

- Sorenson told McCandlish the accurate blueprint was "probably one of the most dangerous illustrations you've ever done" and advised tearing up the original. After the 2001 [[disclosure-project]] appearance McCandlish said he was warned, lost all his defense clients, was threatened on the phone and had money and cars taken by the IRS; he said he lay awake with a 9mm under his pillow and regretted being nearly coerced by [[steven-greer]] to name names, including Sorenson. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 54:59-56:27)*
- On 13 April 2021, aged 69, McCandlish was found dead of an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound after speaking to friend Rick Price and his girlfriend. According to Robert D. Morningstar he was to testify to the Senate on UAP in June 2021 and had been in contact with Senator Rubio. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 59:12-1:00:08)*
- Sorenson's replies to Gerb: McCandlish "was a stupid dramatic selfish fool who died because he could not keep his mouth shut", and Gerb should say goodbye to everyone he loves if he investigates, and would "never see it coming"; Sorenson said he protected McCandlish until he could not. Gerb reads the threats as an attempt to ward off amateurs. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 21:27-22:31,)*
- Gerb lists the Zero Point documentary's deaths of free-energy figures: Stefan Marinov (1997 window fall), Eugene Mallove (beaten to death 2004), Arie de Geus (2007), Mark Tollen/Toman (2009) and Amy Eskridge (2022, ruled self-inflicted), plus [[james-allen-filmmaker]]. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 56:27-57:44)*
- Gerb wonders whether Sorenson's protection ended when McCandlish planned to swear testimony to Congress. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 1:07:44-1:08:15)*
