Liz Truss is cited by Green & Pleasant as the second named example in the video's 40-year "same
room" narrative — the shortest-serving Prime Minister in British history, whose exit the video
attributes to a market reaction against a budget the [[city-of-london-corporation]]'s financial
markets rejected.

## Claims

- Became Prime Minister in 2022; her chancellor announced a budget of large unfunded tax cuts,
  and the bond market reacted within hours. Borrowing costs rose so violently that the Bank of
  England had to intervene to stop pension funds from collapsing. Truss resigned after 49 days —
  the shortest premiership in British history. Green & Pleasant's summary line: "Liz Truss was
  elected by Conservative members, appointed by the Queen and removed by the bond market"
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 15:07, 15:15, 15:22, 15:30)*.

## Relationships

- Cited by Green & Pleasant as removed, in effect, by a market reaction rooted in the
  [[city-of-london-corporation]]'s financial system.

## Speculation flag

The 2022 mini-budget, bond-market reaction, and 49-day premiership are documented public events;
the framing of her removal as effectively decided "by the bond market" rather than through
ordinary political process is Green & Pleasant's rhetorical characterization, recorded here as an
attributed claim.
