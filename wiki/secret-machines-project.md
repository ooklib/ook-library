*Secret Machines* is the [[tom-delonge]] / [[peter-levenda]] book and project whose thesis, per [[jesse-michels]], is that UAP are "mystical machines" and human consciousness is central. Michels says senior Skunk Works figures Robert Weiss, Steve Justice and Eric Schrock, and Air Force Research Lab commander Neil McCasland, advised it, and that these defense technologists keep returning to consciousness in conversation. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 58:34-59:26)*

- Jeffrey Kripal (Rice University), on the Danny Jones podcast: Lockheed's revolutionary projects director Charles Chase invited him to give a private talk to top engineers on levitation among shamans, saints and monks. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 58:05-58:51)*
