Peraton is the current name of a corporate chain originating as [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed
Martin]]'s Enterprise Integration Group (EIG) — "a unit deeply involved in highly classified
systems engineering for US intelligence agencies." Per researcher Rob Jones's *Subrosa*, Veritas
Capital acquired EIG for $815M in cash in 2010, rebranding it the SI Organization Inc., which
inherited a significant portion of Lockheed's black-program work (NSA, NRO, CIA, and private
DIB contractors). *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:35:41–2:36:44)*

## Claims

- Corporate chain: SI Organization (2010) → merged with Kinetic under Veritas Capital, rebranded
  Vencore (2014) → merged again under Veritas Capital to form Perspecta Inc. → taken back private
  by Veritas Capital in 2021 for $7.1B, forming Peraton. Peraton subsequently acquired Northrop
  Grumman's federal IT and mission support services for $3.4B.
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:36:44–2:37:25)*
- [[robert-cardillo|Robert Cardillo]] — who Gerb alleges may have helped block Lockheed's
  [[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]] material transfer while Deputy DNI for Intelligence
  Integration — joined Peraton's board of advisers in May 2019 and remains on it, which Gerb
  flags as notable given Peraton's origin in Lockheed's divested black-program unit.
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:38:00–2:38:19)*
- Per Jones's theory (which Gerb finds compelling but labels speculative), Lockheed may have used
  this 2010 corporate transaction to successfully offload UFO-related materials or programs after
  the [[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]] PAP effort was blocked. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:37:25–2:38:34)*

- Retired SAF/AA official [[william-a-davidson|William A. Davidson]] joined Perspecta's board of
  advisers after leaving Air Force service — Gerb ties this to Rob Jones's *Subrosa* research on
  Veritas Capital's UFO-legacy-program interest. Around 2018,
  [[stephanie-osullivan|Stephanie O'Sullivan]] joined Peraton's board, sharing it with former NGA
  director [[robert-cardillo|Robert Cardillo]], whom Gerb (per Jones's research) suspects may have
  helped block Lockheed's Kona Blue material transfer. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 1:01:23–1:01:52, 1:40:03–1:40:24)*
