Three military videos, labeled Go Fast, Gimbal and FLIR (Gerb says "Fleer that is a drone", a joke on the FLIR1 audio), classified but released through the efforts of [[luis-elizondo]] and [[christopher-mellon]] and published via the New York Times by Leslie Kean and Ralph Blumenthal, per [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 26:32-27:11)*

## Claims

- Gerb focuses on Gimbal, filmed in 2015 off Florida by an F/A-18's targeting pod: the pilot said the object moved with no visible means of propulsion and stayed stationary in severe winds. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 27:11-27:35)*
- He says a clip of the video was never declassified, confirmed by the pilots and Mellon, in which the craft rendezvouses with other craft and takes a V formation. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 27:35-27:59)*
- Skeptics such as [[mick-west]] say Gimbal is a bird or a jet moving away with a lens artifact; Gerb says he trusts the trained pilots over "some dude who sits in his basement". *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 27:51-28:20)*
