Terrence L. Reynolds directs the Information Protection Directorate (IP) of the
[[air-force-life-cycle-management-center|Air Force Life Cycle Management Center (AFLCMC)]],
overseeing "information security, personnel security, industrial security, acquisition security,
international program security, operations security, scientific and technical information
program, and program protection planning" across the Department of the Air Force. Gerb states he
does not single Reynolds out as an evidentiary target, but includes him as an addendum
illustrating how far IP's program-protection reach extends within Air Force acquisition.
