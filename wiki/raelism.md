## Claims

- It teaches that an extraterrestrial species, the Elohim, created humanity; Buddha, Jesus and Muhammad were misread Elohim, and 40 human-Elohim hybrids served as prophets. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 18:26-18:55)*
