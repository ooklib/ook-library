The hypothesis, aired in [[uap-gerb]]'s video, that recovered craft occupants are engineered biological drones or "avatars" rather than true living creatures. All claims are attributed.

## Claims (gerb/special-forces)

- [[special-forces-witness-infographics]] says the dead beings had a crude digestive system able to process only basic proteins, so the program thought they were like biological androids; live ones lived a few days, could breathe Earth's air and reacted only to basic stimuli. His theory: "whatever's making these things just creates a biological crew when it needs one". *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 29:39-33:49)*
- Gerb quotes [[gary-nolan]]: an intelligence would not land among angry monkeys but send an intermediary that looks like them but isn't them, and "most of what we think we're seeing are avatars, biological robots" per the intelligence community's current view, "a hypothesis". Gerb also cites [[david-grush]] on recovering dead pilots and Victor Marchetti's claim of "little gray men" on ice at Wright-Patterson, and says biologics are among the most hoaxed aspects of the phenomenon. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 30:35-31:29, 34:37-35:00)*
- Gerb says he has been drawn to the biological-android theory for about a decade, recalling a Mysterious Universe podcast episode around 2017. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 45:06-45:57)*
