Paul Kaminski served as Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Technology (USD A&T)
from 1994 to 1997 and as a permanent member of the Special Access Program Oversight Committee
(SAPOC). Gerb treats him as central to the 1994 SAP-governance reforms he ties to protecting
UFO programs. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:43:41–1:44:22)*

## Claims

- Served on [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]'s board from 2017 to 2025. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:44:22–1:44:33)*
- Gerb ties Kaminski heavily to the "Wilson–Davis notes" narrative, which he believes to be
  substantially accurate, discussing [[bill-perry]], Kaminski, and the 1994 SAP reorganization.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:45:32–1:45:50)*
- The 1994 reforms under [[bill-perry]] significantly strengthened SAPOC and its Senior Review
  Group (SRG), restricting "temporary employees" and those without need-to-know from accessing
  programs — reforms Gerb attributes at least partly to protecting UFO programs.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:44:52–1:45:32)*
- Per [[thomas-wilson|VADM Thomas Wilson]]'s testimony ([[wilson-davis-notes]]), Kaminski was
  one of two permanent SAPOC members Wilson spoke to directly (with Brig. Gen. Michael
  Costanik), serving as USD A&T, preceded in that role by [[john-m-deutch|John Deutch]].
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 39:32–39:49)*

- Was NRO from 1971 to 1976 and "pioneered the development of a major NRO reconnaissance
  satellite system"; joined the NRO Technical Advisory Board as of 2007. Named among Gerb's 13
  alleged NRO-linked gatekeepers. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:24:53–2:25:10,
  2:50:59–2:51:14)*
