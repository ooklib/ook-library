James Rigney is, per UAP Gerb, the man who copied the Wilson-Davis notes from the estate of [[edgar-mitchell]] and put them into the public arena. Gerb's account relies on the show notes (Rigney interview) and on Rigney's Richard Dolan podcast appearance. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:02-1:18)*

## Claims

- Around 2013 Rigney formed a close relationship with a confidential source at a US space conference; the source had close ties to many Apollo astronauts. When Mitchell died (February 2016), the family invited the source to help disperse the estate, much of which was marked for destruction; the source contacted Rigney about UFO-related documents. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:09-1:50)*
- Rigney asked for and was granted access, copied numerous documents including the Wilson-Davis memo, and was named publicly after appearing on [[richard-dolan]]'s podcast. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 3:42-3:59)*
- Gerb: nobody knows how Mitchell came into possession of the memo. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 3:28-3:35)*
