The composite of anonymous scientist informants that [[silas-newton]] and [[frank-scully]] cited as the sources of the Aztec story; Scully said they had "more degrees than a thermometer". [[uap-gerb]] says up to eight or nine scientists were folded into one "Dr. G" and that [[jp-cahn]] wrongly collapsed them into [[leo-gebauer]].

## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Dr. G told Scully the craft was recovered intact, went to Los Alamos, ran on magnetism and had a plastic pamphlet with undeciphered symbols, and showed him a cube-like radio-sounding device and small gears and discs from the craft; Scully's widow said she saw photographs. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 33:55-34:41)*
- Candidates Gerb discusses are [[carl-a-highland]] (Steinman's choice), [[john-torrance-tate]] (the Ramsays' likely choice), GSI figures Eugene McDermott and J. Clarence Carter ([[geophysical-service-inc]]), and the biophysicist Arnold Schmidt, appointed NDRC investigator by [[vannevar-bush]] in 1941; one G lived in Denver and could fly to the site in two or three hours. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 44:35-44:42, 1:15:56-1:19:19)*
