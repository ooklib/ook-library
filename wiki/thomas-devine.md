Thomas Devine is, per [[eyes-wide-open]], the "mystery man" co-founder of [[zapata-petroleum]] with [[george-h-w-bush]]: an MIT graduate and (per his 2019 New York Times obituary as cited by the host) CIA staff officer since 1949. The host's central thesis in this chapter is that Devine was the CIA case officer running Bush's oil firm as an intelligence front in the Caribbean. This is the host's interpretation of declassified CIA memos, not established fact.

## Claims

- Host: Devine's obituary says he was a CIA staff officer from his 1949 graduation, which "almost guarantees" Zapata was "some sort of agency front." He came from the same Northeastern "Yankee" world as Bush rather than the Southwestern cowboy world, and was later inducted into the [[council-on-foreign-relations]]. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 1:39-2:29)*
- A November 29, 1975 CIA memo (to the deputy director of operations, shown on screen) calls him a former CIA staff employee who became an "oil wild catting associate with Mr Bush," said they co-founded Zapata together, and ties his effort to a terminated project with cryptonym WUBRINY (or "LP dictum"). *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 2:36-3:09)*
- A 1968 CIA memo sent to the Department of Security calls him a "personal friend and unofficial Foreign Affairs advisor" to Congressman Bush, and a "cleared and witting contact in the investment banking firm which houses and manages the proprietary Corporation of WUSALINE." See [[wubriny-and-wusaline]]. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 3:15-3:53)*
- Host's argument: Devine was coordinating CIA corporate-cover operations in the Caribbean, using Bush and [[george-de-mohrenschildt]] as cutouts in the Gulf of Mexico; he likely pumped seed capital into CIA shell companies and was "likely the CIA case officer" over Bush's business activities. The host compares the model to the later CIA venture arm [[in-q-tel]]. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 4:32-6:26, 6:50-7:04)*
- Alleged (via Russ Baker): Devine and de Mohrenschildt met secretly in New York in April 1963 about de Mohrenschildt's Haiti operations. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 6:26-6:50)*
- Alleged: Devine and Bush traveled together to Vietnam in late 1967 / early 1968 during Bush's first House term, coinciding with the ramp-up of the Phoenix Program. The host speculates the two would also have met [[ted-shackley]] earlier at [[jm-wave-station]] (1962-64) while expanding their firms; details deferred. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 7:04-8:18)*
- Host's summary: Devine is "the key to understanding Bush's early relationship with CIA" as a "roving emissary" for Zapata on the outside but working for Langley. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 8:18-8:42)*
- Flagged: the host says he is unsure how to pronounce the name ("deine or Dine"); the spelling "Devine" is used, consistent with the earlier episode ([[zapata-petroleum]], bush-2). The "Wu Briney" rendering in the ASR is the project name, and the host himself says it "may have been" Devine's code name.
