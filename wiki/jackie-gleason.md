Jackie Gleason, the actor, was famously obsessed with UFOs and built his house to resemble a flying saucer. In jm/kissinger he is the subject of an anecdote about [[richard-nixon]].

## Claims

- Per Gleason's ex-wife, one night in the 1970s a possibly drunk Nixon arrived unannounced and took Gleason to see alien bodies at a secret facility; the shaken Gleason told the story at home and it later reached the tabloids. Michels says its veracity is debatable (bragging, venting or fake) and infers only that if Nixon was interested in UFOs, [[henry-kissinger]] would have heard about it. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 47:12, 47:20, 47:27, 47:34, 47:41, 47:48, 47:56)*
