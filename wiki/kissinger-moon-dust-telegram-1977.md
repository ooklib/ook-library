In January 1977, just before leaving office, Secretary of State [[henry-kissinger]] sent a telegram to the US embassy in Ottawa requesting a report on "possible returned space objects", subject line "Moon Dust". [[jesse-michels]] presents it as "the most intriguing clue of all" in the episode and the closest thing to a documentary link between Kissinger and crash-retrieval work.

## Claims

- Text and context as described by Michels: the telegram asked for a report on possible returned space objects under the Moon Dust subject line. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 51:52, 52:00, 52:09)*
- [[project-moon-dust]] is described as a Cold War USAF program to recover foreign space debris that, per declassified documents, extended to unidentified objects; example documents cited include spherical objects in New Zealand farmland, a fireball and silent aircraft near a Bolivian crash site, and a Wright-Patterson-bound report on a 1957 Afghanistan event rivaling Roswell. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 52:09, 52:18, 52:26, 52:35, 52:43, 52:50, 53:07)*
- Michels's own caveat: the telegram does not prove Kissinger was involved, only that he was aware of Moon Dust; he calls it a "breadcrumb". He says the procedures resemble those in the MJ-12 documents and legacy-program accounts. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 53:14, 53:22, 53:31)*
