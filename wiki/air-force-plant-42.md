Air Force Plant 42, in Palmdale, California, is a government-operated airfield complex in the
Antelope Valley used by Lockheed Martin Skunk Works, Boeing Phantom Works, and Northrop
Grumman's Advanced Technology Development Center — the only three contractors with access to
its complex. It is one of four Air Force plants nationwide, managed by the Acquisition,
Environmental, and Industrial Facilities Division at [[wright-patterson-air-force-base|
Wright-Patterson AFB]]. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 45:51–46:34)*

- Gerb has separately accused Plant 42 of operating as an integral piece of American Southwest
  UFO RDT&E, alongside the former Norton AFB, the Lockheed and Northrop radar cross-section
  ranges, and Boeing Phantom Works. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 46:42–47:24)*

  secondhand witness Mark McCandlish: McCandlish stated high-value witnesses were flown from
  Norton AFB to Plant 42 aboard a military Boeing 727 to be shown the Flux Liner ARVs. A hangar
  now serving as Skunk Works' facility was originally built by Lockheed in the 1960s for

  47:24–48:14, 48:26–49:07)*

  alluding to nonhuman-life knowledge and radical materials/aircraft-design capabilities.

  [[northrop-advanced-technology-and-design-center|Northrop Advanced Technology and Design Center

  36:37–37:06, 3:06:22–3:06:41)*

- Gerb's Hidden Wing video reiterates Plant 42 as one of four Air Force plants nationwide (a
  "GOCO," government-owned/contractor-operated institution) providing exclusive access to just
  three contractors — Boeing, Lockheed Martin, and Northrop Grumman — who work there closely
  with pilots from the [[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards Test Pilot School]]. Gerb again ties this

  AFTC's TPS at Edwards). *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 2:05:50–2:08:02)*
