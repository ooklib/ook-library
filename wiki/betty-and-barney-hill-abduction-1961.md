The 1961 Betty and Barney Hill case is the best-known US abduction case. [[jesse-michels]] notes a Netflix project on it; in the [[steven-greer]] interview Greer says it "very likely" was staged by humans with stage craft because the described mechanism is not consistent with interstellar technology, and that by 1961 fully operational man-made electrogravitic craft already existed. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 50:12-50:52)*

## Claims (gerb/iceberg-1)

- [[uap-gerb]] retells the case: on September 19, 1961 the Hills, driving home from Niagara Falls/Montreal near Lancaster, New Hampshire, saw an erratic bright light near the Moon and Jupiter that descended to about 80-100 ft, looked like a large pancake, and showed 8-11 humanoids in its windows; one figure allegedly told Barney telepathically to "stay where you are and keep looking". *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 13:10-14:58)*
- The 178-mile drive took 7 hours instead of about 4, leaving 3 hours of missing time; under hypnosis Barney recalled six figures and terrifying eyes, and Betty recalled a star map in the control room, which some read as showing Zeta Reticuli, with a UFO-report member giving odds of at least 1,000 to one against a random configuration and Carl Sagan rebutting it in *Cosmos*. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 15:58-18:12)*
- Betty had five nights of vivid dreams beginning about 10 days after; Barney told NICAP the beings were "somehow not human". *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 14:58-15:50)*
