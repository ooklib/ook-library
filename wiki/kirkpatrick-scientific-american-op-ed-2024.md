An opinion piece by former [[aaro]] director [[sean-kirkpatrick]] in Scientific American, published in January 2024, arguing that AARO found no evidence of extraterrestrials and that conspiracy-minded whistleblowers are misguided or unsubstantiated. The wiki holds only [[uap-gerb]]'s characterization of it in [[gerb-kirkpatrick-video]], not the article itself; treat every description below as Gerb's paraphrase. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 0:00-4:10)*

## Claims about the op-ed (per Gerb)

- It opens with Carl Sagan's "extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence", a quote Gerb says he despises because extraordinary science is often done without evidence. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 1:43-2:07)*
- It alleges the reverse-engineering story is a 2008 conspiracy perpetuated by AATIP under a small group: [[luis-elizondo]], [[harry-reid]], [[hal-puthoff]], [[eric-davis]] and [[christopher-mellon]]. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 3:39-4:10)*
- It calls it inconceivable that such a program would be shared with only 50 to 100 people; Gerb calls this irrational given how SAPs work. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 3:16-3:39)*
- It claims that no whistleblower in the public eye came to AARO despite numerous invitations, and Gerb says this is false. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 5:23-6:12)*
- It describes AARO as unwaveringly committed to bringing clarity to the issue, which Gerb rebuts with the DoD Inspector General finding of no overarching UAP policy. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 7:08-7:56)*
- Gerb also cites Kirkpatrick's 2023 LinkedIn post, which he calls an emotional appeal casting AARO staff as diligent scientists against whistleblowers with unsubstantiated claims. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 2:37-3:16)*

## Responses quoted by Gerb

- [[david-grush]] said several times he tried to approach AARO and Kirkpatrick and never heard back; Gerb notes he chooses to believe Grusch, who has testified to Congress. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 5:23-5:40)*
- [[christopher-mellon]] tweeted that he introduced Kirkpatrick to former AATIP director Lou Elizondo, [[eric-davis]] and [[hal-puthoff]], who each spent hours briefing him in a classified setting and received no feedback, and hoped the pending congressional report would describe the claims and how AARO evaluated them. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 5:40-6:44)*
- [[luis-elizondo]] tweeted that many people he knows personally gave detailed information to Kirkpatrick's office and that if AARO will not tell Congress the truth, they will. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 6:44-7:08)*
