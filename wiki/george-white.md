George White, a New York City-based Federal Bureau of Narcotics agent (the FBN, precursor of the
DEA), struck a 1946 deal with [[lucky-luciano]] releasing him on parole to Sicily, working
alongside [[james-jesus-angleton]] ahead of the 1948 Italian election. Eyes Wide Open's Part 5.2
closes on a dedicated biographical section dubbing White (per Peter Dale Scott) "Mr. Deep
Politics" for his criss-crossing between the Mafia and three-letter agencies, and uses his career
to introduce the show's "spies vs. operators" model of CIA personnel.

## Claims

- A senior member of the OSS's X-2 counterintelligence program under [[james-jesus-angleton]] (in
  charge of the Italy desk and specifically Sicily during the 1948 elections), in 1946 White
  reached a deal with [[lucky-luciano]] to release him on parole to Sicily; Angleton allegedly also
  ensured Luciano was deported alongside Detroit mob boss [[frank-coppola|Frank Kapola]].
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 25:16-25:57)*
- Nicknamed "Mr. Deep Politics" by Peter Dale Scott for his career crisscrossing between American
  Mafia circles and "three-lettered agencies." First discussed in the prior episode regarding
  [[lucky-luciano]]'s WWII-era cooperation under [[operation-underworld]] (working then for the
  Office of Naval Intelligence); worked for the FBN to approach New York Governor
  [[thomas-dewey]] in 1946 to extradite Luciano to Italy as a wartime reward — the same Dewey who
  had originally imprisoned Luciano in 1936 as a state attorney. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 46:17-47:55)*
- Worked simultaneously for the OSS/later CIA and the FBN, giving him unique cross-agency access,
  but per the host's research was "always more loyal" to the OSS/CIA than the FBN. Started his
  intelligence career as a WWII-era instructor at the New York staging ground for new OSS agents
  headed to Europe/the Pacific, where he personally trained future CIA spymasters
  [[frank-wisner]] (future director of Plans), [[richard-helms]], [[william-colby]], and —
  "maybe most significantly to White's career" — future counterintelligence chief
  [[james-jesus-angleton]], with whom he formed a strong working relationship. White's comfort
  collaborating with mobsters was, per the host, likely noticed by Angleton, who would himself
  become closely aligned with organized crime. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 49:44-50:57)*
- Illustrates a host-proposed evolution in CIA personnel structure: an early "spies" class of Ivy
  League Wall Street/corporate-law elites (Angleton, Allen Dulles, Richard Helms — "the executive
  class," decision-makers of "the company") versus a later "operators" class of rougher,
  non-elite, often Midwestern/Southern agents (White himself — 5'7", 200 lb, a former narcotics
  detective — along with Bill Harvey and Frank Sturgis) who carried out the CIA's "dirty work":
  rubbing shoulders with mobsters, delivering cash, or (per White specifically) breaking into
  Watergate. Compared directly to Angleton: both had intimate organized-crime connections, but
  White handled operational tasks while Angleton negotiated deals and financial infrastructure with
  [[meyer-lansky]] and [[paul-helliwell]]. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 51:03-53:14)*
- In 1950, before his 1952 recruitment to run MK-Ultra's Operation Midnight Climax (setting up
  safe houses in New York and San Francisco and procuring victims from Mafia pimps for CIA
  scientists), White worked with [[joseph-mccarthy]]'s 1950 committee investigating communist
  influence in the State Department, and was separately present that same year on the
  [[kefauver-committee|Kefauver Committee]] investigating organized crime, performing an "operator"
  role on both whose exact nature is unclear — the host's "pet theory" is that White served as a
  CIA attaché during the Red Scare. White testified to the Kefauver Committee, on behalf of the
  OSS, that the agency "remained aloof" from and had no awareness of [[operation-underworld]]'s
  collaboration with Luciano — per the host, "almost certainly a blatant lie" given the CIA's
  later documented collaborations with Luciano-connected elements in Italy and with
  [[meyer-lansky]] and [[santo-trafficante]] in Cuba. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 53:19-54:23)*
- Per his own diary, was present in Rome in 1948, where he met with [[william-donovan]] (then a
  private citizen); White also had allegations of funding Mafia elements in Italy shortly before
  the 1948 election. In 1950, [[meyer-lansky]] separately took a trip to Sicily to meet
  [[lucky-luciano]] around the same period the CIA moved to place southern French docks under
  Corsican Mafia control and fought to keep Golden Triangle opium fields under KMT-allied warlord
  control; White would go on to return "with a mysterious French mobster under CIA employ,"
  teased as a subject for a future episode. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 54:43-55:50)*

## Relationships

- Federal Bureau of Narcotics agent; worked with [[james-jesus-angleton]] to secure
  [[lucky-luciano]]'s parole and deportation, approaching Governor [[thomas-dewey]] for the
  extradition.
- OSS wartime instructor who personally trained [[frank-wisner]], [[richard-helms]],
  [[william-colby]], and [[james-jesus-angleton]].
- Exemplifies the CIA's non-elite "operator" class, contrasted with Angleton's "spy" class.
- Present on [[joseph-mccarthy]]'s 1950 committee and the [[kefauver-committee]]; falsely denied
  OSS knowledge of [[operation-underworld]] to the latter.
- Met [[william-donovan]] in Rome, 1948; later recruited to run MK-Ultra's Operation Midnight
  Climax.
