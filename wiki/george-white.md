George White, a New York City-based Federal Bureau of Narcotics agent (the FBN, precursor of the
DEA), struck a 1946 deal with [[lucky-luciano]] releasing him on parole to Sicily, working
alongside [[james-jesus-angleton]] ahead of the 1948 Italian election.

## Claims

- A senior member of the OSS's X-2 counterintelligence program under [[james-jesus-angleton]] (in
  charge of the Italy desk and specifically Sicily during the 1948 elections), in 1946 White
  reached a deal with [[lucky-luciano]] to release him on parole to Sicily; Angleton allegedly also
  ensured Luciano was deported alongside Detroit mob boss [[frank-coppola|Frank Kapola]].
