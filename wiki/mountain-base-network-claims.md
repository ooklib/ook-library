In the closing chapters of [[sentinels-of-ether-michels-episode-2026]] [[jesse-michels]] connects [[coyame-mexico]], [[bradshaw-ranch-sedona]] and [[skinwalker-ranch]] with CIA remote-viewing claims about a network of mountain bases, and concludes with a "breakaway civilization" thesis. These are Michels' speculative synthesis of anecdotes.

## Claims

- In the CIA remote viewing program in 1973 [[pat-price]] described a large underground base inside Mount Hayes, Alaska, and named three more: Mount Perdido in Spain, Mount Inyangani in Zimbabwe and Mount Zyle in Australia. Years later [[joe-mcmoneagle]], given the same coordinates blind, saw the same mountains with underground command centers connected like nodes of a network, and saw Perdido built by humans (helicopters, cargo crates), not by non-human intelligence. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:28:46-1:29:41)* See [[project-stargate]].
- Michels: once a private company reverse engineers enough non-human technology it can leap decades or centuries ahead, giving "a parallel civilization with its own cities, its own private armies"; mountains show geomagnetic anomalies, disappearances and craft entering and leaving. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:29:55-1:30:32)*
- He echoes [[richard-dolan]]'s "breakaway civilization" and posits jointly operated human and non-human bases "with entirely insulated and separate chains of command", with the control system operating "parapsychologically" and explaining missing-scientist cases and dead-end gravity, nanotech, fusion and free-energy technology trees ([[free-energy-suppression-claims]]). *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:30:25-1:31:51)*
- Reasoning: it is as crazy to think NHI exists but has not infiltrated government and aerospace as to believe in NHI at all; disclosure will be "spoon-fed to us in fiction". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:31:51-1:32:23)*
- Related lore he cites for humans hiding holographic or hard-to-see facilities includes [[bob-lazar]]'s holographic book (via [[robert-bigelow]]), [[randy-anderson]]'s Crane account and Greer's Raytheon-hologram whistleblower; see [[coyame-mountain-base-narrative]].
- Michels' Skywatcher note: Barber's attempt to recreate legacy UFO efforts openly through a private company, Skywatcher, "has hit some snags" and the promised demonstration and egg landing did not happen. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:32:23-1:32:46)*
