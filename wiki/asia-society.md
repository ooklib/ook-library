Rockefeller III as an open forum for American business to network with Asian political and business leaders. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 19:42-19:59)*
