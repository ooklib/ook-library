The Collins Elite is a name used in ufology for a purported group or program investigating whether UFOs are demonic. In the `jm/cover-up` episode [[jason-samosa]] says the label is used by insiders (naming [[jacques-vallee]] and [[george-knapp]]) to refer to multiple things, and that he cannot say with confidence who they are.

## Claims

- Per [[nick-redfern]]'s book Final Events, a funded program traces back to shortly after Roswell, when investigators (as in Ruppelt's account) worked through hypotheses: human, Russian, Nazi technology, extraterrestrial, and an occult overlay. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 16:10, 16:38)*
- Redfern's sources allegedly say the CIA Directorate of Plans unlocked funding in 1952 for about 12 people working full-time; Samosa notes that Directorate of Plans funding is associated with covert operations such as Mockingbird and Gladio. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 17:07, 17:31)*
- A second component: the [[the-family-fellowship]] network, whose members [[alonzo-mcdonald]] and [[james-baker]] were allegedly involved in UFO-related White House briefings under Reagan, so a "demonic, don't investigate" shutdown might come from the Family rather than the 1952 program. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 17:52, 20:03)*
- A third component: a briefing to Reagan with people from CIA S&T (presumably Kit Green's group), the Collins Elite, and another group. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 19:30)*
- The [[air-force-office-of-special-investigations]] is said to have overseen the beginnings of the Collins Elite theory. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 2:25:46)*
- Samosa treats the demonic reading as a possibly valid overlay but warns it is circular when used to validate the Bible. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 14:29, 15:10)*
