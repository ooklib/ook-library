A proposed 1963-64 deep bunker 3,500 ft beneath Washington, DC, reached by elevator from the Pentagon. Officially never built; the National Military Command Center was the interim facility. [[uap-gerb]] argues it was built and is a denied site tied to UFO programs. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 53:11, 53:19, 53:25, 53:41, 56:33)*

## Documented proposal (per declassified memos Gerb cites)

- 7 Nov 1963 (three weeks before JFK's assassination) memo: [[robert-mcnamara]] proposing meetings on a National Underground Command Center; designed to survive 200-300 Mt hits.
