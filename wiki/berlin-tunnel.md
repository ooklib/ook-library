CIA wiretap operation of the mid-1950s run from the Berlin base under [[william-harvey]]; in [[eyes-wide-open]]'s `ewa/shackley` it is the "crowning achievement" of [[ted-shackley]]'s Berlin years.

## Claims

- Harvey led a team that dug a roughly 1,400-foot tunnel from the edge of West Berlin under the Soviet-controlled sector to tap underground Soviet/East German communication lines; the most successful listening post into East Berlin until Soviet authorities discovered it on April 24, 1956. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:16:05-1:16:38)*
- Shackley's role was "relatively minor," coordinator with British intelligence (per David Corn), but inclusion in Harvey's group boosted his rise. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:16:38-1:17:04)*
- [[edward-lansdale]] allegedly labeled Harvey "the American James Bond" over it; host says its true intelligence value "remains in question" but it was a propaganda victory. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:17:04-1:17:21)*
