The "Del Charro set" (ASR "delcho") is [[eyes-wide-open]]'s name for the informal circle of Texas oil cowboys, politicians and organized-crime figures that met at [[clint-murchison]]'s Hotel del Charro in La Jolla, California (ASR "La Hoya"), near San Diego, and the adjacent Del Mar racetrack. The host calls it "the cowboy equivalent" of the [[georgetown-set]] and a "private forum where the Cowboys can interface with the powerful forces in Washington."

## Claims

- Murchison bought the hotel in 1951; it hosted his and [[sid-richardson]]'s "dearest friends in the establishment." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 31:40-32:05)*
- An informal forum for "intelligence scheming, backroom deals and interfacing between legitimate political figures and underworld characters." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 32:05-32:28)*
- Organized crime context: host recaps the Chicago mob's wire-service expansion into Texas and California (Johnny Roselli to LA, Jack Ruby to Dallas), their use as intelligence assets, and quotes Whitney Webb (One Nation Under Blackmail) that the "Southern Rim" offered organized crime unprecedented opportunities. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 30:04-31:17)*
- [[j-edgar-hoover]], a friend of Murchison, had visited with [[clyde-tolson]] since the early 1930s (per Anthony Summers); Murchison allegedly comped everything (about $460,000 over 18 summers). *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 32:28-33:44)*
- Hoover's horse-race betting: allegedly at his request Murchison and Richardson bought Del Mar in 1954 from Al Hart, an alleged Chicago-outfit-tied liquor wholesaler said to have worked in Capone-era bootlegging (host speculates he may have crossed paths with [[joseph-kennedy]], "but I have yet to find a substantial" connection); Hoover allegedly sent two FBI men to intimidate Hart into selling. Ownership went to Murchison's charity "Boys Inc.," which the host suggests was a tax dodge/money laundry (Jane Wolfe's book: comparable tracks netted over $1M a year vs. about $50,000 net at Del Mar). *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 33:44-36:27)*
- Murchison allegedly covered Hoover's losses and let him keep wins: "risk-free gambling," the host says, is unofficial bribery buying his loyalty in overlooking Texas/Louisiana schemes, alongside the simultaneous alleged Northeastern mob blackmail via the [[blue-suite]] parties of [[roy-cohn]]. The host also cites [[peter-dale-scott]] that Hoover "would immediately work to cover up the conspiracy to kill Kennedy." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 36:27-38:23)*
- Guests reportedly included [[johnny-roselli]] (poolside with Hoover, per a Del Charro employee), Vice President [[richard-nixon]], and [[john-connally]] (as Richardson's lobbyist); the host says many key players of the upcoming [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]] were in the set "long before the invasion was even contemplated." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 38:23-39:31)*
- Lobbyist [[irving-davidson]] was "the glue" tying these elements together. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 39:31-39:48)*

## Relationships
