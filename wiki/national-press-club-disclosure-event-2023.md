A national press club conference in Washington, DC on 12 June 2023 hosted by [[steven-greer]], where [[michael-herrera]] testified about a 2009 Indonesia encounter ([[michael-herrera-indonesia-encounter-2009]]). [[jesse-michels]] says [[jake-barber]] attended as part of his crash-retrieval work, having been "sent there on behalf of our employer" to red-team whistleblowers, do threat assessments and report back, possibly for arrest (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 37:28-38:44)*).

## Claims

- Michels describes Greer as "notorious" for finding disgruntled insiders and drawing publicity to their stories. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 37:37-37:55)*
- Barber says he was in "this weird place between becoming a rogue whistleblower or rounding up the whistleblowers", and, hearing Herrera, chose to join forces rather than run counterintelligence on him: "I saw a good Marine up there". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 38:04-38:26, 43:24-43:49)*
- Barber later told the story of the Toughbooks operation ([[barber-toughbooks-operation-claims]]).
