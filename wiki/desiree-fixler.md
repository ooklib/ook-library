Former investment banker (career from 1994, London and Frankfurt, trained at JP Morgan) turned ESG specialist and, in her own telling, the whistleblower whose 2021 disclosures triggered the US and German ESG-fraud cases against [[dws-group]]. She now campaigns against ESG, net zero and the [[world-economic-forum]] agenda. All statements below are her own account, given to [[winston-marshall]] in [[wef-insider-interview-2025]].

## Claims

- She started in investment banking in 1994, moved into socially responsible investing in the mid-2000s and calls herself "one of the original people" and a "chief architect" of ESG from 2005 to 2021, building buy-in for the agenda at other firms though not working for [[klaus-schwab]]. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 2:24, 3:29)*
- She was trained at JP Morgan (see [[jpmorgan-chase]]). *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:17:44)*
- In 2020 she became chief sustainability officer of DWS, "roughly $1 trillion" of assets, part of Deutsche Bank. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 7:20)*
- She says she raised only internally that the CEO's claim of over 450 billion euros of ESG-aligned assets (out of about 800 billion) was unsupported, perhaps closer to 120 billion, plus fudged gender ratios and claims of AI-driven ESG risk systems. She was fired weeks later and the annual report came out unchanged. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 27:47, 31:10, 32:07)*
- She says DWS then briefed Bloomberg that she underperformed and had questionable ethics, revoked her German work permit and left her homeless, and that she moved in with her parents at about 49. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 33:00, 33:52)*
- After a Wall Street Journal story (arranged through an acquaintance who used to run the paper) the SEC, FBI and DOJ called her within two days. She says this led to a Frankfurt raid by about 50 federal agents, executive dismissals, US and German guilty findings on ESG misstatements, a 14% market-cap drop (over a billion in a day) and legal costs in the tens of millions. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 36:05, 36:54, 37:20)*
- She insists "whistleblower" is inaccurate as she stayed internal and DWS went external. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 35:06)*
- After the firing she was invited onto the UK FCA's sustainability advisory board and a WEF futures council on responsible investing, seen wrongly, she says, as a green "Joan of Arc". She left the FCA board and the WEF in December 2024. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 45:09, 1:18:46)*
- By 2022 she was pushing back in these rooms, and says a colleague, Ian Hirsty Alley (ASR, name uncertain), urged her to stay as a dissenting voice. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:10:21)*
- She met [[klaus-schwab]] at WEF events in San Francisco and Dubai and found him "very unimpressive", stiff, and drawn only to the most famous people present. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:20:28)*
