Oki Shannon, former manager of special projects at [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los
Alamos]] and a character in the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]], attended the 1985
Advanced Theoretical Physics Working Group conference at [[bdm-international|BDM]]'s McLean, VA
facility, supervised by [[john-b-alexander|Col. John B. Alexander]].
*([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 46:09–46:49)*

## Claims

- His notes from the conference discuss a major engineering project under
  [[bobby-ray-inman|Adm. Bobby Ray Inman]], naval intelligence on underwater UFO hotspots near
  Golfo San Matías, Argentina, and mention of an organization that "collects, manages, and
  destroys UFO information." *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 46:49–47:12)*
- The notes include an action item to approach [[donald-kerr|Donald Kerr]] "for influence and
  money," and a separate list entry reading "Don K/et" alongside other names, which Gerb
  suspects also refers to Kerr. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 47:12–47:21,
  2:07:35–2:07:58)*
- Gerb states he would "be really interested to speak to Oki Shannon" for more detail.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 2:07:27–2:07:35)*

## Relationships

- Former [[los-alamos-national-laboratory]] employee; figure in the [[wilson-davis-notes]];
  documented the 1985 conference discussing [[donald-kerr]] and [[bobby-ray-inman]].
