The account [[danny-sheehan]] gives in [[sheehan-michels-interview-2025]] of why and how the 1963 [[kennedy-assassination]] happened. In his telling, no single agency but a small network around [[allen-dulles]] as consigliere of [[brown-brothers-harriman]] and the China lobby reactivated the [[s-force]], an assassination team originally built for [[richard-nixon]] and [[howard-hughes]] against Fidel Castro, once [[john-f-kennedy]] moved toward destroying nuclear warheads and a joint US-Soviet space program (see [[jfk-khrushchev-disarmament-claims]]). Everything below is Sheehan's narrative, much of it second-hand and unverified.

Sheehan calls it the same architecture as Iran-Contra. *([jm/sheehan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-05-31 - UFOs & JFK - He Knew Too Much! (Ft. Sheehan cites Trafficante as source and says Hughes's messages referred to Nixon as "the president".
