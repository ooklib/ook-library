CE5 is [[steven-greer]]'s method of "initiating" peaceful contact with non-human intelligence by coherent thought and meditation rather than technology. He says it was co-created in an October 1973 experience on a Blue Ridge mountain near Boone, North Carolina, where after a disc sighting he met a roughly 5-foot being with deer-like eyes and lost about four hours. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 36:04-41:31)*

## Claims

- Origin: a childhood sighting around 1963 in Charlotte; a near-death experience at 17; meditation from 1973. He says the being was from "thousands of years in the future" (his framing). *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 35:56-36:20, 37:42-38:15, 41:12-41:31)*
- Field results: four craft allegedly materialized near Pensacola in 1992 with about 60 participants; he says the intelligence community "came after me like a pack of Rottweilers" once it saw the method work. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 34:12-34:36, 2:38:07-2:38:27)*
- Ethic: he insists on "invite", not "summon", and calls the alien names (grays, Nordics, reptilians) racist labels for dozens of benign species. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:37:26-2:37:56, 3:25:40-3:26:23)*
- He also describes technology-assisted consciousness and consciousness-assisted technology (from his 1991 paper and his first book, dated 1999), and says [[wernher-von-braun]] used a psionic interface to transmit a speech to spokesperson Carol Rosen. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:33:41-2:36:16)*
- Critics: Michels raises "flares" allegedly used at CE5 events; Greer calls this fabricated. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 3:06:44-3:07:31)*
- Greer says these events show the beings do not need man-made craft and that [[jake-barber]]'s consciousness-contact ideas are compatible. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 31:20-31:30, 1:59:34-1:59:47)*
- Greer calls the CIA remote-viewing program ([[project-stargate]]) the "cheap end" of psionics and CE5 a route to the high-end transdimensional interface of consciousness and technology. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 31:04-31:28)*
