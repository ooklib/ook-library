Charles de Gaulle, per Eyes Wide Open, led France's provisional post-war government (1944-46), returned in 1958 to found the Fifth Republic and, allegedly, shut down [[rose-des-vents]] in 1959 and created the [[service-d-action-civique]] to bring France's secret paramilitary network under presidential control. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 10:24-10:49, 44:52-46:13)*

## Claims

- Stepped down in 1946 amid protests over centralization; his Gaullist movement overtook the MRP by the late 1950s. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 10:18-10:49)*
- Refused key cabinet posts to PCF members after the Communists won a plurality in October 1945. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 12:16-13:13)*
- Allegedly consolidated secret paramilitary networks under his control after fears about links between Rose des Vents and the 1958 coup attempt, cutting out the CIA and NATO. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 46:01-46:13)*
