The Office of Policy Coordination (OPC), per Eyes Wide Open, was the innocuously named covert
operating arm consolidated within the newly created CIA, distinct from the agency's
uncontroversial intelligence-gathering side, and headed for its early history by
[[frank-wisner]].

## Claims

- With the CIA's 1947 creation came a new organizational structure, subsequently divided into two
  functions: intelligence gathering (largely uncontroversial, akin to a "news agency for the
  president") and covert operations, "largely consolidated under the innocuously named Office of
  Policy Coordination." The OPC acted as the CIA's covert operating arm and would lead many of
  the CIA's global operations and insurrections throughout the Cold War in the name of
  anti-communism. For the beginning of its history, the OPC was headed by [[frank-wisner]], who
  would oversee the foundation of Gladio throughout Europe. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 18:56-20:01)*
- The CIA and OPC's first operation abroad was in Italy in 1948, where the Communist Socialist
  Coalition Party threatened to win a super-majority in the first postwar national elections.
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 20:01-20:15)*
