## Claims

- The "future JM wave station chief" and Felix Rodriguez's close associate. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 39:05-39:19)*
- [[david-sanchez-morales]] later worked under his "former boss Ted Shockley" in Laos and Phoenix. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 46:57-47:15)*
- Op 40 veteran "Chi Chi" Cano attached himself closely to Shackley and his deputy Thomas Clines (ASR "Klein"); Clines, with Naval intelligence asset and ex-CIA agent Ed Wilson, embedded assets in PEMEX for arms and narcotics smuggling cover; Shackley and Clines, "now ostensibly private businessmen", did late-1970s/early-1980s weapons deals tied to Iran-Contra (host, "as we will see later"). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 48:41-50:06)*
- Directed Phoenix in Saigon by the early '70s, where [[felix-rodriguez]] rejoined him. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 54:55-55:07)*
