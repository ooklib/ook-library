Career-long profile of the CIA officer known as "the Blond Ghost": Counter Intelligence Corps in occupied Germany, CIA Nuremberg and Berlin, [[jm-wave-station]] chief in Miami (1962-64), Laos (1966-68), Saigon and the Phoenix program (1968-c.1972), head of the Western Hemisphere Division, associate deputy director for operations (1976), then a private-business/Iran-Contra logistics network in the 1980s. Earlier sections below are from other episodes; the dedicated episode `ewa/shackley` covers his early life and 1927-1958 career in depth, with the section "Overview and career arc" onward. Everything here is a claim by [[eyes-wide-open]] or by sources it cites (chiefly David Corn's *The Blond Ghost*), not verified fact. ASR renders the name as "Ted Shockley"/"shakley" (normalization to Shackley by inference; the same spelling issue appears in earlier episodes).

## Claims

- The "future JM wave station chief" and Felix Rodriguez's close associate. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 39:05-39:19)*

- Op 40 veteran "Chi Chi" Cano attached himself closely to Shackley and his deputy Thomas Clines (ASR "Klein"); Clines, with Naval intelligence asset and ex-CIA agent Ed Wilson, embedded assets in PEMEX for arms and narcotics smuggling cover; Shackley and Clines, "now ostensibly private businessmen", did late-1970s/early-1980s weapons deals tied to Iran-Contra (host, "as we will see later"). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 48:41-50:06)*

## Overview and career arc (Eyes Wide Open, "The CIA's Blond Ghost")

- Opening framing: [[william-harvey]] brought him in early 1962 to head [[jm-wave-station]], which per Hinckle and Turner (*The Fish Is Red*) "operated on a budget of well over $500 million a year and employed between 600 and 700 American personnel" in 1962-64, not counting thousands of Cuban exile assets. He was brought in largely to manage the growing pool of human-intelligence assets after the Bay of Pigs. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 0:01-1:56)*
- Host judgment: he became "maybe the man who was single-handedly the most responsible for the CIA's paramilitarization throughout the 1960s"; his gift was asset tracking, data sheets and organization charts rather than cocktail-party spycraft, which let the CIA run a full secret war in Laos by the end of the decade. Nickname "Blond Ghost," which he reportedly disliked. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:56-3:51)*
- Career per host: post-war Germany under Harvey; JM/WAVE; Laos station chief 1966-68 ([[secret-war-in-laos]]); Saigon station chief 1968 to roughly 1972, "rolling out" the CIA-Pentagon Phoenix program first conceived by [[william-colby]], a mid-career mentor; 1972 head of the Western Hemisphere division, playing "an instrumental role" in Project FUBELT, the coup against Salvador Allende. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 5:52-8:05)* Flagged discrepancy inside this episode: the coup date is given as September 11, 1973 at 8:05 but Kissinger's role in the overthrow is later dated "1972" at 34:50, presumably a slip. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 8:05, 34:42-34:57)*
- Per researcher Joseph Trento (host's attribution), he became CIA's lead on the Israeli account beside [[james-jesus-angleton]], a relationship he would call on "in an official and unofficial capacity" in the 1980s. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 8:05-8:36)*
- Reputation as a "mastermind bureaucrat," human-intelligence expert and "expert paymaster" for illegal covert operations; forced out by the Carter White House but only after being promoted in 1976 by DCI [[george-h-w-bush]] to associate deputy director for operations, "two rungs under the directorship." Host: Shackley and Bush "had likely crossed paths a decade and a half earlier, both as young CIA assets in the Caribbean" (speculation). *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 8:44-9:49)*
- After [[halloween-massacre]] (demoted December 1977, retired 1979), in the 1980s he coordinated and owned "a dizzying network of shell companies" tied to arming the Contras; host: VP Bush was "likely apprised." He was forced to testify in September 1987 before the Senate Select Committee on Secret Military Assistance to Iran and the Nicaraguan Opposition; host: do not expect to glean much from his evasive testimony (ASR fragment "ki and avoidant"). See [[narco-counterinsurgency]], [[william-casey]]. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 10:31-11:36)*
- Elusiveness: only one widely published photo of him front and center (author photo on *The Third Option*) and, the host believes, no video anywhere; sparse in CIA memos and mainstream press before his 2002 death. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 4:43-5:36, 11:44-12:25)*
- Books and their reliability: see [[the-third-option]]. Host's source caveat on David Corn's 1994 *Blond Ghost* (only full-length biography; sources possibly "limited hangouts"): see [[the-third-option]].

## Early life (per David Corn, via the host)

- Born July 16, 1927 in Springfield, Massachusetts to Eleanor (Lena Anna Sedova), a first-generation Polish immigrant, and Theodore Shackley Sr., a salesman and drinker; parents married 1925 in Hartford, split 1929, divorced 1937. Host: the father's alcoholism may explain Shackley's lifelong avoidance of alcohol. Rumors at Langley that he was an orphan were false. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 23:12-24:44)*
- Raised in Queens, then partly by a woman Corn speculates was a Polish grandmother in New Jersey (where he likely learned Polish); mother's second husband Justin Manning moved them to West Palm Beach, Florida, under 100 miles from Miami. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 24:44-25:47)*
- Brilliant high schooler; repeatedly tried to enlist during the war but was barred until the very end; enrolled in the Army in October 1945 and joined the [[counter-intelligence-corps]] (Polish an asset). *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 26:40-27:36)*
- Discharged February 22, 1947; attended the University of Maryland rather than an Ivy League school (legend: a football promise to a friend despite a Princeton scholarship offer). Spy Master theorizes he would have become DCI with an Ivy education; host reads that as self-consolation. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 36:30-37:29)*

## CIC in Germany, 1945-47

- 970th CIC detachment; Spy Master mentions only one line: interest in intelligence as a career began in "Berlin in 1946 to 47." Corn: worked DP camps, interviewing refugees for war criminals, ex-Gestapo and "subversive Poles," and sought people on automatic-arrest lists. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 29:43-31:49)*
- Host speculation: CIC service put him near the roots of Operation Paperclip and the rat lines, secrets that "clued [him] into one major government secret" and made him a CIA candidate. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 31:49-33:27)*
- Trained at the European Theater Intelligence School, Oberammergau, mid-1946 with [[henry-kissinger]] allegedly among instructors; host notes Shackley later ran Laos and Vietnam while Kissinger oversaw the bombing that supported his irregulars and had a role in Project FUBELT ("safe to say the student did not drift far from the teacher"). Editorial link by the host. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 33:27-35:06)*

## Recruitment and Nuremberg, 1951-55

- Called up as an Army reservist for Korea, then summoned to the temporary buildings by the Lincoln Memorial and recruited into the CIA's Office of Special Operations for his CIC record, Polish and German; OSO and [[office-of-policy-coordination]] soon merged under [[frank-wisner]]'s Directorate of Plans. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 37:52-39:19)*
- Posted in 1952 to the CIA base at Nuremberg, a "comparative backwater," as a case officer smuggling East European assets behind the Iron Curtain, likely targeting Poland; station chief was [[lucien-conein]]. Corn: Shackley recruited and interrogated DP-camp candidates, Conein readied them for crossing the Czechoslovak border; Conein remembered him "calm and cool, a detailist" working late, but downplayed the relationship. Corn portrays the work as largely futile. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 39:43-40:36, 57:31-58:54)*
- Speculation: the host suggests Shackley and Conein may have supported the [[galen-commando-program]] (paratroopers from Gehlen's network, argued to be the roots of the Green Berets, see [[us-army-special-forces]]), which would explain Shackley's later ease with special forces and rapid promotion; he stresses this is unverified and may simply have been a sleepy base. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 56:30-57:31, 1:07:26-1:12:41)*

## Berlin, 1955-58

- Summoned to Frankfurt (CIA in the old IG Farben headquarters) then to West Berlin, where he met his "first true mentor" [[william-harvey]]; Corn: the Berlin base had 40-50 case officers and 150 other employees; Shackley quickly became Harvey's top agent. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:12:55-1:14:14)*
- Specialty: briefing, paperwork and organization charts; Corn says colleagues called him a "fabricator," and in Laos his briefing of Senator Stuart Symington (ASR "Simington") on the Armed Services Committee reportedly secured continued funding for the secret war. Host: "spin and damage control," plausible deniability, foreshadowing his Iran-Contra financial channels. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:14:14-1:15:58)*
- Coordinated with British intelligence on the [[berlin-tunnel]] (role "relatively minor"). Stayed in Berlin until fall 1958, then short postings in Japan and Langley 1959-60, then Miami, early 1962. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:16:38-1:17:04, 1:19:07-1:19:36)*
- Host thesis: unusual as a CIA operator, he was one of few who honed intelligence first and covert action second, making him "the ultimate insider spook" for the Dulles-era shift toward covert action. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:17:53-1:19:07)*

## Laos and special forces

- Corn: as Laos station chief 1966-68 he held weekly meetings with General Westmoreland with Colonel [[john-singlaub]], MACV-SOG commander (who had served with [[lucien-conein]] under [[paul-helliwell]]'s OSS Detachment 202); Shackley and Singlaub reappear in Iran-Contra funding. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:10:41-1:11:45)* See [[lauri-torni]] for a Green Beret death shortly before Shackley took the Laos post.
