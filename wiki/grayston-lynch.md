Grayston Lynch is a special forces veteran and CIA paramilitary trainer quoted by [[eyes-wide-open]] (via David Corn's *The Blond Ghost*, Hinckle and Turner) as an eyewitness to life inside [[jm-wave-station]] under [[ted-shackley]]. ASR renders the first name "Grreyston/Grston". Scoped to `ewa/jmwave`; the host says later Road to Dallas chapters return to paramilitary men on the ground.

## Claims

- Host: had just returned from [[operation-white-star]] in Laos in 1960 (used as another example of the East Asia to Gulf Coast link); despite explicit orders not to put Americans on the ground he landed at the Bay of Pigs with [[brigade-2506]] ("Brigade 256" in ASR), evaded capture and went back to JM/WAVE to train exiles. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 30:23-31:00)*
- Told Corn that Shackley read a raid proposal so fast it seemed like skimming, then weeks later quoted whole passages; Lynch's conclusion: "You didn't try to (bull) him." *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 31:06-31:31)*
- Said of [[robert-f-kennedy]]'s visits: "I'd come to the safe house in the morning and they would say Bobby says this, Bobby says that." *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 41:48-42:01)*
- Said the boat provided to the saboteurs on the Matahambre copper mine plot was old and in bad shape and died midway at sea. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 44:15-44:29)*
