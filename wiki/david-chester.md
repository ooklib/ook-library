David Chester is a theoretical physicist introduced by [[jesse-michels]] as an MIT undergraduate and UCLA PhD specializing in general relativity and quantum field theory, "the smartest and best-credentialed guy who will entertain the quacky stuff", and a friend with whom Michels has discussed [[extended-electrodynamics]]. He co-hosts the last hour of [[buhler-michels-interview-2026]].

## Claims

- Introduced and credentialed. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:52:26-1:53:14)*
- Critiques [[charles-buhler]]'s third-order QED explanation: momentum is conserved in QED and virtual radiation cannot be external lines, the classical limit corresponds to tree-level diagrams, and Buhler should read up on renormalization and self-energy; also that the QED coupling alpha appears at each vertex. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 2:05:57-2:08:34, 2:24:44-2:27:56)*
- Says the experimental results are "in the top 10 most convincing things I've seen" and he cannot think of a prosaic explanation, while wanting the power-off persistence tested carefully. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 2:16:42-2:19:36)*
- Reviewed Falcon Space's Biefeld-Brown replication and found it suggestive but not conclusive. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 2:19:48-2:21:09)*
