Daimler-Benz (now Mercedes-Benz) is a German automaker. The video alleges close cooperation with the Nazi regime.

## Claims

- Its board included many Nazis; it produced military vehicles, Luftwaffe aircraft engines and the bulletproof 770 car used by Hitler, Mussolini and Hirohito, and by 1942 had fully shifted to war production. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 0:56-1:21)*
- In 1944 nearly half of its 60,000 workers were forced laborers, some loaned to other companies for profit; postwar it was denazified and resumed production under American oversight, and in 1988 it contributed $12 million to a reparations fund. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 1:21-1:46)*
- Later bought 88% of Auto Union (Audi) in the late 1950s. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 7:37-7:40)*
