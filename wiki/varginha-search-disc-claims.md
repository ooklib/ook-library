A disc-shaped craft said to have flown low and slowly back and forth over Varginha after the crash, allegedly looking for the beings, and a separate 1996 night photograph or footage of an orange glowing disc.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says a whole family (mother, husband and daughter), who did not want to go on camera, watched a spinning glowing disc for 20-30 minutes flying low, coming back, in a grid-search pattern over the neighbourhood, and got angry when asked about helicopters. Their home is blocks from where the girls saw the being and where Chereze grabbed the second. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:21:48-1:25:26)*
- Fox's drone shot shows the search area matches the disc's path; he says other unconnected townspeople told the same story. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 35:35-37:37, 1:25:42-1:25:58)*
- Fox says the family were later visited by Americans (perhaps NASA); see [[ubirajara-rodrigues]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:29:18-1:30:55)*
- Footage of a glowing orange disc over a residential area at night from 1996, "just to the south" of Varginha and months later, was added by Fox; Ramsey compares it to Chuck Clark's disc, Carlos Diaz's photos and [[bob-lazar]]'s S4 craft. Fox discovered the location link himself and is "high conviction". A child in the clip says "it's a disc, Mom". *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 28:42-29:53, 1:26:44-1:29:09)*
