Civil Air Transport was a nominally commercial airline run by the Nationalist Chinese government
(and later the CIA), created with [[paul-helliwell]]'s backing out of [[claire-chennault]]'s
"Flying Tigers" mercenary air force. Per Eyes Wide Open, it was the operational vehicle for
Helliwell's first opium-smuggling route and the direct ancestor of [[air-america]].

## Claims

- Created with the help of [[claire-chennault]], a former US Army Air Force captain who had
  lobbied FDR to fund a private mercenary air force ("the Flying Tigers") for
  [[chiang-kai-shek]], repurposing that rundown air force into a heroin-smuggling operation.
  *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 11:21-12:12)*
- The OSS's first devised route: CAT planes shipped weapons to Nationalist fighters in Burma
  (a forward operating base for the KMT outside China's borders in the opium-rich Shan Plateau),
  then loaded heroin for the return trip — a small-scale model Eyes Wide Open says was later
  replicated worldwide (Afghanistan/Turkey for opium, Latin America for cocaine) and which
  transformed the Shan Plateau into "the biggest opium production region on the planet."
  *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 12:12-13:20)*
- Would eventually be reformed into [[air-america]], the CIA's full-fledged airline that shipped
  narcotics and weapons throughout Indochina during the Vietnam War — a topic Eyes Wide Open says
  it will cover when the series reaches the Vietnam War. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 12:46-12:59)*
