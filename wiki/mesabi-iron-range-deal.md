Sequence of 1890s-1901 deals in MagnatesMedia's account by which Rockefeller's Minnesota iron ore, a truce with Carnegie and Morgan's purchase produced [[us-steel-corporation]].

## Claims

- Gates and Junior bought the Mesabi range, a 120-mile vein of high-quality ore, and undercut [[andrew-carnegie]] by selling ore to his competitors. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 35:24, 35:29, 36:16)*
- Carnegie, short of ore, agreed to buy at least 600,000 tons a year and ship it over Rockefeller-owned railroads for a secret rebate, with a pledge that Carnegie would not buy Mesabi land and Rockefeller would not build a steel mill. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 36:43, 37:13, 37:42)*
- [[j-pierpont-morgan]] saw he could unite ore and steel into one monopoly, "U.S. Steel", and bought the mines from [[john-d-rockefeller-jr]] for $88.5 million in stock (about $3.1 billion today), then bought Carnegie's business; Rockefeller's US Steel stock rose to over $200 million. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 38:19, 39:14, 39:24, 39:31)*
