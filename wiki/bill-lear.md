- A 1956 Jane's-Defence-Weekly-attributed article said Lear was "already figuring out gravity control"; he reportedly cited American anti-gravity research as a reason to believe in flying saucers. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 45:28-45:56)*
- Lear's headquarters were across the street from [[guidance-technologies]]; Linda Brown recalls the two inventors lunching almost daily; a video shows Lear at [[agnew-bahnson]]'s lab watching Brown's gravitator. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 45:56-46:11)*
- Lear, [[curtis-lemay]] and RAND figures held meetings with Brown in 1967 before Guidance Technologies shut down. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 25:35-25:48)*
