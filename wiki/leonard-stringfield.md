Leonard Stringfield, a UFO crash-retrieval investigator Gerb describes as "legendary," produced
a series of "Crash Retrieval Status Reports" analyzed throughout the SAIC video.

## Claims

- Status Report 6 ("Inner Sanctum," 1991) analyzed the 1989 [[mattituck-bay-crash-1989|
  Mattituck Bay, Long Island]] crash retrieval. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:12:44–1:13:00)*
- Stringfield's notes record a conversation (via one "Mr. Hines") with a Bell Labs-connected
  informant describing a UFO retrieval witnessed on an Air Force base flight line in the early
  1950s, including a cutaway view of the recovered craft and an alien "headband transceiver";
  one man who allegedly kept a piece of the craft disappeared and was never found.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:41:25–1:42:06)*

- Crash Retrieval Status Report 6 ("The Inner Sanctum," 1991) also analyzed the testimony of
  [[albert-bruce-collins|Albert Bruce Collins]] ("Barnabas"), sourced via researcher Tim Cooper,
  regarding a 1947 UC Berkeley warehouse UFO and 1960s NRO-founding claims.
  *([nro](../raw/GM1Qx9vuo2c_nro.md) 26:33–26:57)*
