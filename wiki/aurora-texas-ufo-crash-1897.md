[[uap-gerb]] calls the alleged 17 April 1897 crash near Aurora, Texas the first alleged UFO crash retrieval he is aware of. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 18:27-18:45)*

## Claims

- Per a Dallas Morning News article of 19 April 1897 as summarized by Gerb, a craft hit a windmill on a farm, its lone pilot was dead and "not an inhabitant of this world", the metal resembled aluminum and silver and weighed several tons, the wreckage was thrown in a well and the pilot buried. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 18:36-19:50)*
- In a 1980 Time interview 86-year-old resident Etta Pegues (ASR "Etna pegu") said the story was fabricated to draw attention to a town bypassed by the railroad. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 19:50-20:11)*
- MUFON's 1973 investigation led by Bill Case reportedly found two eyewitnesses (Mary Evans, whose parents saw an alien body, and Charlie Stevens who saw a smoking airship), a grave marker resembling a flying saucer and metal-detector readings; the cemetery refused exhumation, the marker vanished and a 3-inch pipe was placed at the grave, after which readings stopped. MUFON called its evidence inconclusive and did not rule out a hoax. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 20:11-21:51)*
- Gerb does not know what to think but finds it interesting. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 21:51-21:59)*
