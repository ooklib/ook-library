Claims, mostly by [[jason-jorjani]] (drawing on John Brandenburg, [[ingo-swann]] and Joe McMoneagle), that Mars hosted a Nordic-type civilization destroyed in a nuclear war. Speculative and contested.

## Claims

- John Brandenburg (physicist, Sandia/Livermore) argues excess xenon-129 and argon-40 in Mars's atmosphere, especially an isotopic signature at Cydonia ("Sedonia" in ASR) matched only by Earth thermonuclear test sites, shows a natural-impossible nuclear event; Jorjani says Brandenburg estimated an "Empire State Building's worth" of nuclear weapons and that Carl Sagan phoned Brandenburg to ask why he was "causing so much trouble". *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:06:50, 1:08:00, 1:10:06)*
- Swann's early-1980s team saw exploded, honeycombed cities; in 1984 Joe McMoneagle (Monroe Institute) remote viewed "Mars 1,000,000 BC" for the CIA and saw ruins and 12-14 foot Nordic-type people, then a scout ship finding a stormy, volcanic Earth needing modification; Jorjani says the report is declassified. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:06:23, 1:10:56, 1:12:30)*
- Jorjani reads this as the Nordics scouting a primordial Earth and building the Moon; see [[artificial-moon-claims]]. He notes the same people appear today, so the ruins prove nothing about extraterrestrial origin (time travel; see [[nordic-control-system-claims]]). *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:12:30, 1:31:19)*
- He concludes the entities are not averse to nuclear use on moral grounds, contra their disarmament pleas; see [[nuking-the-matrix-claims]]. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:14:35)*
