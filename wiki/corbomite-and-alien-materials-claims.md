At the 1995 lecture ([[schneider-las-vegas-lecture-1995]]) [[phil-schneider]] showed table samples he said were alien-derived materials. The transcript renders "corbomite" as "Mite" (the video description calls it corbomite, "element 140"). All are unverified claims by the speaker.

## Claims

- A heavy rock 2.5 times the weight of uranium, non-radioactive, made of rare-earth clays, powdered metals and an "alien element" from crashed saucers; used in every stealth aircraft and in small amounts in black helicopter skins. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 27:29)*
- Very pure titanium mixed with alien elements, withstanding over 7,000 F, used in a Phoenix-class submarine fleet of 106 boats built at two a month, each with about 10,000 lb of titanium hull costing 28 million dollars, diving over 7,000 feet, and carrying 11 small "grid bed" nuclear motors no bigger than a podium, each equal to three Enterprise-class carriers. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 27:54, 28:16, 29:32)*
- The metal is supposedly stockpiled, possibly mined on the moon (which he says he cannot prove) or refined in orbit, with a secret shuttle flight per week. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 29:32)*
- Scalenohedral crystals of niobium, titanium, rhenium and copper oxide developed at Los Alamos through [[eg-and-g]], used in all black jets; a very light lithium-plus-alien-element compound used in propulsion. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 30:37, 32:18)*
- "Alien" or "Aurora drops": coating dripping off black jets after hypersonic flight, harder than lava rock; and a man-made rock 11 times harder than diamond, withstanding over 10,000 F, used as windows in Mach 5-plus aircraft. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 32:43)*
- A 140-element periodic table versus 105 in schools, as evidence of concealed elements. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 24:06)*
- A fragment of Roswell craft skin given to him aged 14 by a Navy archives friend of his father, said to have been shattered with liquid nitrogen and a hydraulic hammer, then analyzed and adapted ([[roswell-incident-1947]]). *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 36:00, 36:52)*
- Agatized organic samples and an imprint of a 360-blade mechanical part in a 220-million-year-old rock offered as evidence of ancient technology, with an alleged 100,000-year-old chipping tool from Jordan. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 34:10)*
- Says military technology outpaces the public sector by 44 to 45 years per year. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 1:07:00)*
