The alleged capture of one or more small beings in Varginha and their handling at two hospitals, as relayed by [[james-fox]] in [[fox-ramsey-interview-2026]].

## Claims (a52/fox)

- A local newsman was told by firefighters netting the first being that it was "just an animal"; he says it screamed like a child or baby. Fox says people threw rocks at it and some thought it was a devil because of its red eyes; nobody at the time thought of it as extraterrestrial. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 52:10-53:27)*
- Fox says the captured being in the morning was taken by firefighters, and that police chief [[pedro-luis-aguiar]] later saw a creature under a tarp, more brass than in his whole career, and rumour that one was alive, one dead and one escaped. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 53:37-54:17, 1:38:04-1:38:36)*
- [[marco-chereze]] and Eric Lopes took a second creature that evening (see [[marco-chereze]]); at Regional Hospital it was allegedly brought in the back rather than the front "because it was scary". Fox says there is still a nurse practitioner working there who was present that night. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:05:14-1:06:41)*
- A radiologist (anonymous, filmed from the back with disguised voice, teaching at a local school) says the military brought something in a child's body bag, it stank, X-rays were taken in silence, the military took all the films and told him "this never happened; you were never here". Fox says it took years to persuade him, in 2021. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:33:03-1:35:06)*
- [[varginha-military-x]] drove the creature to the hospital and then trucks from Humanitas Hospital to a military base; he also saw a being in a box. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:35:50-1:37:37)*
- Fox says the doctors at both hospitals filmed surgeries and had cameras, and that the police, fire department and military took photos; there are "at least five different sources" of video and photographic evidence which Fox has not seen. Fox says he talked with people who saw them, including the chief of police, and that a fake video is circulating that should not be sent to him. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 49:21-51:27)*
- Fox says he would release any video for free and have firsthand witnesses check it first, and that a video shot by a second transporter was nearly reached before the man was summoned to his base. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 47:31-48:43, 49:28-50:00)*
- Venturelli's account of telepathic contact and the being's appearance is on [[italo-venturelli]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:49:54-1:53:59)*
- Fox says the hospital staff "are not denying it"; there is a public plaque. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:06:41-1:06:48)*
