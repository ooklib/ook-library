The Psychological Strategy Board (PSB) was a Truman-era body created to unify psychological-warfare efforts across US agencies; its successor was the Operations Coordinating Board. In jm/kissinger it is the institutional bridge between [[henry-kissinger]]'s early consulting career and the hidden UFO history.

## Claims

- Kissinger consulted for the PSB and its successor in 1951, 1952 and 1955; a declassified CIA document is cited for the PSB's mission of "winning hearts and minds" and for the claim that only a select few were deemed capable of staffing it. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 18:21, 18:29, 18:36, 18:44, 18:52, 19:00)*

Jackson; special assistant to Eisenhower, CIA-Pentagon liaison, later head of Life magazine, which withheld the Zapruder film's moving footage) was another. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 19:08, 19:16, 19:32, 19:41, 19:56)*
