The Maynard Consortium is a fictional private international organization described in the DeLonge/A.J. Hartley novel series [[sekret-machines-project]], founded in the late 1940s, with finances obscured through shell companies that appear and disappear, and with power over geopolitics beyond the UFO issue. In the `jm/cover-up` episode [[jason-samosa]] proposes that the real-world [[world-commerce-corporation]] fits the description, and [[jesse-michels]] compares it with [[ben-rich]]'s "international corporate board" and [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s [[caroline-group]]. This is speculation about a fiction's real-world referent, not an established identification.

## Claims

- [[jim-semivan]] reportedly says the book is very interesting but declined to explain why; DeLonge co-authored fiction with A.J. Hartley so as to tell sensitive history obscured as fiction. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:15:15, 1:15:32)*
- Samosa lists points of match: founded in the 1940s, an unremarkable name, shell companies, Donovan/Stevenson/Hambro/Dulles as leaders (for the WCC), and the alleged mass money flows of 1964 that the book calls "sweep". *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:16:04, 1:24:26, 1:36:50)*
- "Sweep" is left obscure in the book; people assume it is SWIFT, but Samosa says he has not identified it, though he links 1964 to [[moneyrex]] and the Bahamas buildup. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:37:06, 1:37:36)*
- Michels adds that [[ben-rich]] wrote of an international corporate board governing the UFO issue, and that Brown ran into an international group of industrialists that took up exotic technology. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:17:26, 1:17:46)*
