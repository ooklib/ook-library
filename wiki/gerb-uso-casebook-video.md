A video (about 28 minutes, dated 2024-04-26, [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yn-83KbgRN8)) in which [[uap-gerb]] follows up his first USO video ([[gerb-uso-video]]) with a case book of unidentified submerged objects from the 18th-20th century, worldwide rather than US-only. Chapters: Intro, Project Blue Book, Soviet USO Files, Australian USO Files, Pre 20th Century USO, Maritime Light Wheel, Conclusion. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 0:00-1:17)*

## Claims

- Of [[project-blue-book]]'s 13,000+ sightings only about 2% (258) are ship sightings, 13 rated good, and a handful describe USOs; see [[blue-book-uso-cases]]. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 1:17-6:27)*
- The Soviet Union has a trove of USO reports, mainly via Stonehill and Mantle's *Russia's USO Secrets*; see [[soviet-uso-cases]]. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 6:27-10:27)*
- Australia has declassified USO/UFO files and many civilian stories; see [[australian-uso-cases]]. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 11:09-15:05)*
- Pre-20th-century cases and the recurring maritime light wheel are covered in [[pre-20th-century-uso-cases]] and [[maritime-light-wheel]]. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 15:11-24:52)*
- Conclusion: the similar experiences across Blue Book, Australian and Soviet files (Tic Tac shaped craft, cigars, fireballs leaving the water, egg shapes, light balls dancing, objects hovering above water without disturbing it, as in the case [[kevin-knuth]] discusses) suggest answers to the phenomenon may lie in the oceans. He is torn on whether light wheels are craft or an anomalous natural phenomenon, blames OPNAV 3820 and [[janap-146c]] for the thin Blue Book record, mentions unsourced Chilean incidents (a fleet of saucers approaching a craft), and would like to ask [[george-knapp]] whether the UFO files he allegedly smuggled out of Russia include USOs. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 24:50-28:10)*
