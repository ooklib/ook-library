The "New Look" is the codename [[eyes-wide-open]] gives to [[dwight-d-eisenhower]]'s policy of building a massive arsenal of tactical nuclear weapons as a deterrent to Soviet and Chinese efforts in the third world, instead of committing large conventional US forces. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 1:42-1:58)*

## Claims

- The host says the policy brought a dramatic rise in the US nuclear arsenal and set off ongoing calls from hawks to use nuclear weapons "in limited capacities": he cites MacArthur suggesting limited nukes in Korea to Truman, and Barry Goldwater advocating nuclear use in Vietnam in the 1964 campaign (source-list items; no wiki pages for these). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 1:58-2:49)*
- Alongside New Look, Eisenhower's foreign policy was overt "massive financial and military aid" to client states (Thailand, Laos, [[ngo-dinh-diem]]'s South Vietnam) paired with CIA covert missions. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 2:56-3:25)*
- In the conclusion the host calls Eisenhower's farewell warning "ironic" because he did little to quell nuclear spending under New Look and simply funneled military spending to Southeast Asian clients. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 1:00:22-1:00:54)*
