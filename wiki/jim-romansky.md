James "Jim" Romansky was a volunteer firefighter and machinist who says he was among the first at the Kecksburg impact site in December 1965. [[uap-gerb]] calls him probably his favorite eyewitness of any case. All details below are attributed claims. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 13:46-14:09)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:08:49-1:09:13)*

## Claims

- Romansky's fire company was dispatched to search for a downed aircraft. He heard by walkie-talkie that the site had been found, and says the object lay half buried at about a 30 degree approach angle, having broken tree limbs and left a trench about 25 ft long, with no signs of fire. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 14:09-14:32)*
- He describes a large egg or acorn shaped bronze-to-gold metallic object, about 10-12 ft long and 8-10 ft in diameter, with a flat bottom, a "bumper" ring about 8-10 inches wide bearing hieroglyphic-like markings of "dots, rectangles and circles", and no doors, windows, seams, rivets or propulsion. As a seasoned machinist he says it looked made in a liquid-metal mold and he never saw that metal color before. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 14:41-16:39)*
- He says the markings were not Russian or Polish, checking with his father who could read both, which he offers against the Soviet space capsule theory. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 16:46-17:30)*
- On the 1993 Montel Williams show he said two men in trench coats with stiff crew cuts ordered him out, then a uniformed military force arrived within 15 to 20 minutes with flashlights, having taken over a farmhouse up the hill, and the fire hall was soon full of military with guards. He says the firemen were told to be quiet. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 17:37-19:45)*
- He says his daughter (Marine Corps) and son (Air Force) were made to sign statements that they would not request information about Kecksburg, and that his son was told he could not seek duty at Wright-Patterson, where Romansky says "my UFO is sitting". *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 19:52-21:11)*
- He later took [[stan-gordon]] to the exact crash site, which Gerb says matches the account of [[bill-bulebush]], who Gerb believes never spoke to Romansky. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 21:11-21:33)*
