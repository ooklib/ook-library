Avery Dulles, son of [[john-foster-dulles]] and nephew of [[allen-dulles]], was ordained a
Catholic priest (and later cardinal) by [[francis-spellman]] in 1956 — a "strange synchronicity"
the host flags given Spellman's simultaneous closeness to the CIA in Italy and the Dulles
brothers' Protestant family background.

## Claims

- In 1956, Cardinal [[francis-spellman]] ordained Avery Dulles — [[john-foster-dulles]]'s son and
  [[allen-dulles]]'s nephew — as a Catholic priest, later cardinal, despite the Dulles family's
  Protestant background; Avery had undergone a personal religious conversion to Catholicism. At
  the time, Allen and John Foster Dulles were both serving in the Eisenhower administration, and
  Spellman was "adjacent to many CIA activities in Italy." The host frames this as a "strange
  coincidence, or maybe not" illustrating how symbiotic the CIA/mobster/power-broker blackmail
  circles were. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 35:24-36:16)*
