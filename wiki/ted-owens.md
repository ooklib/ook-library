Alleged psychokinetic adept whose biography *PK Man* was written by Jeffrey Mishlove, as told by [[jason-jorjani]]. He claimed powers from mantid beings called "Twitter and Tweeter" who saw him as the most promising PK adept since Moses. Jorjani says Owens caused fires and a civilian airliner crash (Norwegian tourists) to be taken seriously, and that Mishlove considered whether Owens caused the Challenger disaster after letters threatening the space shuttles by name; Owens died soon after. Michels compares the pattern to Chris Bledsoe and [[jake-barber]]. All unverified.
