Claims about what happened to the Kecksburg object after it left the woods, as told to [[stan-gordon]] and [[leonard-stringfield]] and relayed by [[uap-gerb]]. All are unverified witness claims. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 51:50-52:47)*

## Claims

- A witness who contacted Gordon before the 1990 Unsolved Mysteries broadcast says he was on the Air Force security team at Lockbourne Air Force Base near Columbus, Ohio, and guarded the object in the early morning of 10 December 1965 after it arrived by truck and was backed into a hangar with security greatly tightened. It stayed only a short time before being moved to Wright-Patterson. Gerb notes Lockbourne was a Strategic Air Command base of the blue-beret [[blue-beret-claims]] elite guard. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 8:40-8:49)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 51:50-52:39)*
- Witnesses including a Pittsburgh musician, Jerry Betters (41 in 1965, notarized statement), say they saw the acorn-shaped object on a large flatbed being hauled out under a tarp, with hieroglyphic-like writing, while soldiers with rifles chased them away. Betters says the truck had trouble on the field and the object was about a little bigger than a Volkswagen. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 23:59-26:09)*
- [[myron-brick-delivery-witness]] says the object was in a Wright-Patterson warehouse behind a special lead-lined brick order, with Navy personnel trying in vain to cut in. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 54:13-57:27)*
- Gerb notes the Wright-Patterson (see [[blue-room-wright-patterson]]) location fits [[jim-romansky]]'s statement that "my UFO is sitting" there. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 20:55-21:03)*
- Stringfield also cited an insider called "Uncle" (via an intermediary, 1993) who said Kecksburg involved a crashed spy plane while Fort Riley and Johannesburg cases were true. Gerb uses it to show Stringfield reported opinions against Kecksburg but still investigated it for more than a decade. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 53:34-54:13)*
