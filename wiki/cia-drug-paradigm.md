"CIA drug paradigm" (also "drug financing paradigm" or "global narcotics paradigm") is Eyes Wide Open's term for the alleged decades-long pattern in which narcotics smuggling funds, or is tolerated to sustain, American covert and proxy operations. The host credits [[paul-helliwell]] as a critical driver and the [[oss-detachment-202]] alumni as its practitioners. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 0:59-1:11, 2:44-2:50)*

## Claims

- Host claims narcotics-affiliated operations in Southeast Asia, Europe and Latin America are "intrinsically linked by one continuous flow of funds" and that this was "by design." *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 22:34-22:46)*
- [[lucien-conein]] is said to have ensured dope kept flowing to fund secret armies in Laos; [[john-singlaub]] is said to have played a 1980s role analogous to Helliwell's 1950s-60s, managing shell organizations that moved untraceable funds to forces the US could not openly support. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 14:16-14:28, 17:15-17:26)*
- An unnamed Helliwell colleague reportedly claimed (1980 Wall Street Journal) that he gathered wartime intelligence with five-pound shipments of opium, "three sticky brown bars." *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 3:19-3:39)*

- Ep. 6 frames [[operation-paper]] as the first case of the model (called [[narco-counterinsurgency]]); source [[alfred-mccoy]]. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 0:31-2:44, 30:40-31:55)*
