## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2 — disinformation lineage)

Gerb frames Blue Book as the middle link in a decades-long official-investigation disinformation
lineage: the Air Force was tasked in 1948 with investigating UFOs via **Project Sign**, which
became **Project Grudge** in 1949 (running until 1951), superseded by **Project Blue Book**
(1952–1969). *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:09:03–2:09:25)*

- A "massive schism" in these official investigations occurred with the 1953
  [[robertson-panel-1953|Robertson Panel]]. Gerb cites Air Force Capt.
  [[edward-j-ruppelt|Edward J. Ruppelt]] (director of Grudge and Blue Book), whose 1956 book *The
  Report on Unidentified Flying Objects* recounted a 1948 Project Sign memo he observed
  concluding UFOs were of "interplanetary origin" — a memo Ruppelt said was ordered destroyed by
  General Hoyt Vandenberg (an accused original [[majestic-12|Majestic 12]] member).
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:09:25–2:10:03)*
- Gerb connects this to Rep. [[eric-burlison|Eric Burlison]]'s naming of file
  AF-ATIC-FILM-0352 — a 1952 Ruppelt-narrated briefing film titled "Flying Saucer Talk," held (per
  Gerb, oddly) at MIT Lincoln Labs, an FFRDC descended from [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]]'s MIT
  Radiation Lab — and never volunteered for mandatory National Archives (NARA) collection,
  something Gerb suggests is because the film depicts sensitive classified material regarding
  non-human technical vehicles. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:10:03–2:10:59)*
- Project Blue Book ended with the 1969 **Condon Committee** — see [[condon-committee]] — a
  playbook Gerb argues was reused with the [[robertson-panel-1953|1953 Robertson Panel]] and again
  with the 2022 establishment of [[aaro|AARO]]. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  2:11:00–2:11:48)*
