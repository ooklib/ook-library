Junio Valerio Borghese, "the Black Prince," was a former member of the Catholic black nobility
and fascist Italian aristocracy who, per Eyes Wide Open, was rescued from war-crimes prosecution
by [[james-jesus-angleton]] and installed as the commander of [[operation-gladio]] forces in
Italy.

## Claims

- A former member of the Catholic black nobility with deep ties to the Vatican; led fascist
  resistance in northern Italy even after Italy's 1943 armistice with the Allies, commanding a
  short-lived "Solo Republic" and a militia group called "Xmas" that Eyes Wide Open says
  "perpetuated untold horrors" on Communist partisans funded by the Italian Communist Party
  (PCI). *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 23:56-24:36)*
- Crossed paths with [[karl-wolff]] at a villa on Lake Garda, also attended by
  [[james-jesus-angleton]], where he agreed to cooperate with [[operation-sunrise]] in exchange
  for clemency for his crimes. Arrested for war crimes in May 1945 in Milan; Angleton personally
  secured his release. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 24:40-25:10)*
- Angleton and [[william-donovan]] briefly considered installing him as king of a
  Catholic-dominated southern Italy, before opting for the lower-profile plan of making him
  commander of [[operation-gladio|Gladio]] forces in Italy, funded via US State Department
  "operational resources" funds for the ostensible purpose of forming an Italian police force
  and military. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 25:12-25:38)*
- Organized his shadow army into 40 groups — 10 sabotage, 6 espionage/propaganda/escape (a
  euphemism, per the host, for smuggling war criminals to South America and the Middle East), and
  12 guerrilla-fighting divisions — trained on the island of Sardinia and drawn from hardline
  fascists and Italian monarchists. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 25:43-26:07)*
- Framed alongside [[reinhard-gehlen]] and [[karl-wolff]] as one of three men who would go on to
  recruit the future "Gladiators." *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 26:13-26:27)*
