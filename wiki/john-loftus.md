John Loftus, a former Justice Department investigator and self-described Nazi hunter, is cited by
Eyes Wide Open (via David Talbot's *The Devil's Chessboard*) for allegations of a secret 1945-47
deal between [[richard-nixon]] and [[allen-dulles]] over Nazi-funds-laundering documents. The host
explicitly flags Loftus's allegations as unverifiable.

## Claims

- Alleged, based on classified documents he says he read, that [[richard-nixon]] came across Nazi
  files in 1945 while working as a naval officer in a torpedo factory on the American East Coast —
  documents allegedly indicating [[allen-dulles]] and [[john-foster-dulles]] had facilitated
  laundering Nazi funds during the war. Loftus further claimed Nixon and Dulles struck a deal:
  Nixon's silence in exchange for Dulles secretly funding Nixon's 1946 congressional campaign
  against Jerry Voorhis. The host stresses these specific allegations are "not verifiable" but
  "not entirely unfounded" given the series' broader Nazi-funds-laundering narrative, and that only
  the later, independently corroborated 1947 Queen Mary meeting between Nixon and Dulles is
  established fact. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 10:22-11:33)*

## Relationships

- Alleges a hidden [[allen-dulles]]-[[richard-nixon]] arrangement predating their documented 1947
  meeting.
