David Ferrie (ASR "Ferry"; source list spells Ferrie) led the Louisiana [[civil-air-patrol]] group; the host calls him "an eccentric man and by many accounts a psychopath and a pedophile," and, per Daniel Hopsicker and Jim Garrison, a CIA asset in Baton Rouge in the mid-1950s (attribution is ambiguous in the ASR). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 41:50-42:14)*

## Claims

- Recruited teenage [[barry-seal]] and another teenage pilot into CAP in the mid-1950s; Seal served alongside [[lee-harvey-oswald]], and former members reportedly identified Ferrie and Oswald in a 1955 training photo. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 42:06-43:29)*
- Ferrie and Seal later flew arms to Cuba. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 44:41-44:49)*
