A 345-page (at least; ASR-garbled total) CIA file bearing the number 834021 on every page, declassified 22 June 2022 and concerning the Nazi [[operation-paperclip]] scientists. The same number (ASR "83421") appears in the corner of an MJ-12 document leaked decades earlier, which [[jed-davis]] and [[geoffrey-cruickshank]] present as an authenticity data point. Presented on Coulthart's show as an argument, not verified by the host; he is a self-declared devil's advocate here.

## Claims

- Cruickshank: Davis used metadata from the [[majestic-documents]] to find declassified CIA references with six/seven-digit numbers "a person could not possibly guess"; this one refers to the Paperclip scientists, another to the UFO subject (see [[goudsmit-letter]]). He believes the documents were leaked by disgruntled CIA officers. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 9:10-9:56)*
- Davis: he searched the number in the CIA reading room, one file came up; only someone with access to that heavily compartmented file could have fabricated it. The file begins around August 1947. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 10:11-11:50, 36:53-38:42)*
- Why Paperclip and UAP: Cruickshank names [[allen-dulles]] as the common thread (Canaris question in Congress, 27 June 1947; Ciano diary via Edda Mussolini; Knights of Malta passports for Nazis; "controlled the UFO files in the CIA with James Angleton"). Coulthart notes allegations that Paperclip scientists were taken from Los Alamos to view Roswell debris, which Davis says the IPU late-July report mentions. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 11:53-14:17, 37:44-38:29)*
