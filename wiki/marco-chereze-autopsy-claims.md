The autopsy report of [[marco-chereze]] is the centrepiece of the medical evidence in [[james-fox]]'s [[moment-of-contact-documentary]]. In [[fox-ramsey-interview-2026]] host [[chris-ramsey]] reads a consolidated English translation of the necropsy report and Fox relays the comments of forensic pathologists. All of it is second-hand relay of what Fox and the doctors say; the report itself is not in the source.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says forensic pathologist Dr. Joao Baptista Macuco Janini, founder of the IPD (the acronym is not spelled out), has done over 50,000 autopsies since the 1960s and told Fox on camera he had "never seen a case like this". Fox says Janini and Dr. Armando Fortunato (forensic pathologist, former municipal health minister who did the autopsy) put their names on camera for the new cut. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 19:23-19:54, 1:10:52-1:11:39, 1:14:59-1:15:21)*
- Ramsey reads the findings: multifocal pneumonia in both lungs, septic pulmonary thromboembolism, early myocarditis, acute liver congestion, and an armpit abscess growing abundant Staphylococcus bacteria (transcribed "scopfairy", presumably S. sciuri, inferred). *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:12:24-1:13:35)*
- Points said to make it strange: this staphylococcus usually infects dogs and cats and is extremely rare in humans; no prior surgery or medical device; no immune deficiency; and unusual antibiotic resistance, so no antibiotics worked. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:13:35-1:15:37)*
- Janini says the bacteria likely entered through a small skin break, possibly from the being's nail scratching Chereze's armpit; some have suggested deliberate biological manipulation, like Komodo dragon saliva, though the doctor says he cannot be certain. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:11:49-1:12:07, 1:16:11-1:16:56, 1:19:43-1:19:58)*
- The report's conclusion (per translation) says the "hypothesis considered by many" is an encounter with a strange being that "could use this bacteriological weapon". Ramsey notes the doctor wrote this without afterthought. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:18:47-1:19:40)*
- Fox says Janini described it as "an advanced bacteria unlike anything he's seen on Earth", a known strain but evolved in a way "nothing could stop"; Fox admits he is a "neophyte" on the science. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:17:53-1:18:16)*
- Fox and Ramsey suggest the skin secretion could be the being's excrement or waste excreted through pores (Fox compares a Reddit claim of a molecular biologist who saw thousands of pores on frozen beings); an injected foreign excretion could cause a similar reaction, so the being was not hostile. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:17:03-1:17:20, 1:18:25-1:18:39, 1:52:22-1:52:53)*
- Fox says Chereze died two to three weeks after the encounter and that no other person who touched the creature is known to have died. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:21:27-1:21:37)*
