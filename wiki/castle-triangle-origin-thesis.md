[[uap-gerb]]'s working theory about who and what [[roderick-castle]] saw in [[castle-29-palms-triangle-encounter-1997]]: a human-made alien reproduction vehicle (ARV) with an accompanying black special mission unit, run jointly by the Navy and Air Force and exercising unseen inside the [[hunter-warrior-advanced-warfighting-experiment-1997]]. All of this is Gerb's hypothesis; he says he has no evidence for parts of it.

## Claims (gerb/castle)

### Why not the Marines

- Gerb says he finds no evidence that the USMC takes part in UFO programs, unlike the Army, Air Force and Navy, and that the Marine air station at Yuma is not an MRTFB; the triangle and team operated in the exercise area to train alongside "blue" friendly assets while hidden from them, and the failure here led to handling procedures such as the way Marines were treated later in Peru. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 11:19-13:06, 49:16-52:09)*

### Navy and Air Force lineage

- He calls the Navy the sponsor "with very strong conviction", citing the [[center-for-naval-analyses]] report, the late-planning Navy adjunct ([[naval-air-warfare-development-center]], [[naval-air-station-lemoore]]), NAWDC's triangle sightings (a 2002 Fallon report; a 2001 and 2012 Lemoore report by Angelo Asetta) and the [[naval-undersea-warfare-center-keyport]] depot claim. He suggests the craft could have been stationed at Lemoore, Fallon, [[edwards-air-force-base]] or [[china-lake]]. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 54:25-1:00:43, 1:06:26-1:08:39, 1:53:25-1:54:34)*
- He notes the northwest departure heads toward the [[r2508-restricted-airspace-complex]] (whose southern edge is about 44.5 miles northwest of Emerson Dry Lake), managed by Fort Irwin, China Lake and Edwards, with Lemoore also holding "Poncho 3" access, and toward the [[antelope-valley]] triangle-report region. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:11:17-1:13:18, 1:26:39-1:27:16)*
- He links the Air Force side to [[witness-ed]] (Nellis, then the Edwards 412th Test Wing as an electronic-warfare director dealing with reverse-engineered craft) and says Ed has blocked him on every platform. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:13:36-1:17:36)*

### TR-3B parallels

- Gerb compares the craft to [[edgar-fuche]]'s TR-3B: size (250 feet, and a 600-foot model vs Castle's 200-300 feet), rounded corners, non-flush corner lights, a concave center resembling the mercury-plasma accelerator ring, vent ridges, plus Fuche's claim that military test-pilot elites from Air Force and Navy schools flew it. He notes Top Gun at NAWDC is a fighter school, not a test pilot school, and the Navy's test pilot school is at Patuxent River. He adds a similar concave center in [[salvatore-pais]]'s 2017 inertial-mass-reduction patents. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:17:52-1:26:33, 1:55:13-1:57:14)*
- He concedes the alternative that it was an [[xf-131-super-sentinel]] kept at the Tejon or Helendale radar cross-section sites and flown over R-2508 by test pilots, and calls the TR-3B identification the more likely, and notes the Corbell-listed plant addresses and Greer's claim about the [[tejon-radar-cross-section-facility]] electromagnetic-pulse ranges. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:27:40-1:33:48)*
- He compares the flux liner as a crude ARV ("Model T of anti-gravity vehicles") to argue a craft can mix prosaic and reverse-engineered parts; see [[flux-liner-arv-saga]]. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:22:16-1:23:33)*

### The team as Wackenhut-run DOE SRT

- Gerb speculates the black team was not JSOC or SOCOM but a private [[wackenhut]] special mission unit, perhaps a DOE [[doe-special-response-team]] (SRT, trained and staffed by Wackenhut, arrest authority, all-black gear in the 1980s, guards of Los Alamos and Livermore in the late 1990s) that could hide retrievals under the [[atomic-energy-act-1954]] "transclassified foreign nuclear information" classification, like the [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team]] he proposed in the Weygandt case. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:34:46-1:35:27, 1:38:36-1:43:19)*
- He says he has heard second-hand rumours of crashed craft moved across the US, haphazardly labelled nuclear material and guarded by SRTs, and that Wackenhut had forward bases in Peru in the late 1980s and early 1990s per Edward S. Herman's *The Terrorism Industry*, so the same team type may be what [[jonathan-weygandt]] met, though he offers no evidence for this and had earlier leaned toward Army Special Forces. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:38:36-1:39:00, 1:42:31-1:42:54, 1:51:23-1:52:32)*
- The 2002 Wackenhut letter to Castle is read as a possible attempt to recruit him for helicopter-borne "Blue Boys" crash-retrieval rapid-response teams. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:34:46-1:35:27)*

### Test on blue assets

- Gerb extends the idea to the 2004 Nimitz Tic Tac as a possible Skunk Works vehicle testing on unwitting Navy forces, echoing [[ross-coulthart]]'s statement that he believes with high conviction it was Lockheed technology, while saying he has his own framework of non-human and human-made Tic Tacs. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 52:16-54:19)*
