Admiral Bobby Ray Inman had one of the most extensive intelligence/defense careers of any
figure discussed on Gerb's channel: director of the [[national-security-agency|NSA]], deputy
director of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], vice director of the
[[defense-intelligence-agency|DIA]], director of naval intelligence, director of
[[national-underwater-reconnaissance-office|NURO]], and board member (later chairman) of
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] in the 1980s.
*([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 9:06–9:13, 13:26–13:43, 42:45–42:54)*

## Claims

- Per [[colonel-steve-wilson|Colonel Steve Wilson]], Inman also ran
  [[decision-science-applications|DSAI]]. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 12:07–12:13)*
- Wilson claimed Inman was present at the alleged first successful flight of a man-made
  anti-gravity [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b|ARV]] on 18 July 1971, while still serving in
  the Navy. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 13:18–13:33)*
- 1985 conference notes by [[oki-shannon|Oki Shannon]] discuss "a major engineering project"
  under Inman. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 46:49–46:57)*
- Went from, in 1989, discussing recovered UFOs "becoming available for technological research"
  with NASA mission specialist Bob Echler, to, by 2022 (Project Unity), saying he found
  "plausible answers for all UFO sightings." *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 42:54–43:08)*
- Worked on [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]]'s NSA-sponsored Project
  Trailblazer as a board member. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:17:21–1:17:29)*
