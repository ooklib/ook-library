The Anunnaki are ancient Mesopotamian gods that [[zecharia-sitchin]] reinterpreted as extraterrestrials, as summarized by [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 30:52-31:23)*

## Claims

- Sitchin claims they came from an undiscovered ninth planet, Nibiru, about half a million years ago to mine gold, blended their DNA with Homo erectus to create slave miners (modern humans), and fled when melting Antarctic ice caused the great flood. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 31:15-32:05)*
- Gerb notes [[david-icke]] claims the reptilians are the Anunnaki ([[reptilian-claims]]). *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 32:05-32:11)*
- Gerb rejects the gold-mining story but says it is not insane that an ET civilization influenced or tampered with human DNA. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 32:20-32:52)*
