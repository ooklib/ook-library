Al Bielek (ASR: "Alpic", "Albic", "Al Bilic") is the conference host and fellow speaker who introduces [[phil-schneider]] in the 1995 lecture ([[schneider-las-vegas-lecture-1995]]). Schneider says Al has ridden the underground high-speed maglev trains linking the bases, at nearly Mach 2 on a cushion of air about three quarters of an inch above the rail, coast to coast in about an hour and a half, and refers questions about Tesla to him *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 0:45, 9:11, 9:18, 53:30)*. Schneider directs listeners to Al's booth (number 125) *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 1:10:00)*. Identification with the [[philadelphia-experiment]] storyteller is inferred from the name only and is not stated in this source.
