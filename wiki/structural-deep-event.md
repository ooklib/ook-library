A "structural deep event" is, per Eyes Wide Open (citing [[peter-dale-scott]]), a historical event that remains unclear to the public through obfuscation or cover-up and is "large enough to affect the whole fabric of society." The concept is the theoretical frame of [[road-to-dallas]]. This page records the host's usage, not an independent validation of the theory.

## Claims

- A "deep event" is any historical event that stays unclear or unintelligible to the public, usually due to obfuscation or cover-up; they are usually attributed to intelligence agencies, big business, organized crime or corruption, often at the intersection of all of these. Deep events lie on a scale from minor ones that pass unnoticed to structural ones. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 4:41-5:19)*
- Host's list of structural deep events: the [[kennedy-assassination]] (called the first), the [[watergate-scandal]], the Iran-Contra scandal, 9/11 and, most recently, the COVID pandemic. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 5:19-5:36)*
- Host claims a similar group of operators and a continuity of networks recur from Cold War structural deep events into 9/11, so studying them in order illuminates later ones. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 5:39-5:57)*
- Host frames them as "a look behind the curtain": moments when the ruling class and the "so-called deep state" briefly must make themselves visible to cover their tracks. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 14:04-14:48)*
- Host contrasts "siloed" single-chain-of-command assassination theories with a structural view in which many groups had prior knowledge. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 6:50-7:29)*
