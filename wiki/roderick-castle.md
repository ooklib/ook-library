Roderick "R.J." Castle III is a USMC sergeant (E5) who says he encountered a 200-300 foot black triangular craft and about 30 armed, unmarked men in the Mojave Desert in March 1997, and who is now (per [[uap-gerb]]) determined to swear his testimony before Congress. Gerb describes him as a witness rather than a whistleblower, and now a veteran who works with veterans suffering from PTSD. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 7:55-8:14, 1:45:41-1:45:48)*

## Claims (gerb/castle)

- Castle served in the Marine Corps from 1994 to 1999 and rose to sergeant; his MOS was 6531 (aircraft ordnance technician), with VMA-513 "Flying Nightmares", an AV-8B Harrier squadron at MCAS Yuma, part of MAG-13 of the 3rd Marine Aircraft Wing; he led a team of six to eight Marines in aviation ordnance and trained as a helicopter flight medic. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 7:55-8:21, 10:48-11:19, 13:06-13:23)*
- His alleged encounter is [[castle-29-palms-triangle-encounter-1997]]. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 19:48-20:12)*
- Gerb says he never expected to talk: Castle says that without the change in energy around the subject over the past two or three years he would have taken it to his grave. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 28:52-29:12)*
- In 2002, about three months after leaving the New York Army National Guard, he received a recruiting letter from [[wackenhut]] seeking helicopter experience (UH-1 Huey, AH-1 Cobra), which Castle now suspects was an attempt to recruit him as an asset. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 42:04-43:21)*
- In early 2025 he reached out to [[david-grush]], then to [[steven-greer]], who introduced him to Rep. [[eric-burlison]]; he was photographed with Greer, Burlison and [[michael-herrera]] in late July 2025. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:44:43-1:45:23)*
