The physical design and physics claimed for the "flux liner" alien reproduction vehicle, as drawn by [[mark-mccandlish]] from [[brad-sorenson]]'s description and theorized by McCandlish, as presented in Gerb's video ([[gerb-mccandlish-video]]). All attributed claims; Gerb says physics is not his strength.

## Design as described by Sorenson

- Three saucers identical in shape, differing only in size: about 20-24 ft, 60 ft and 120-130 ft, "Jello mold" bottoms with sloping sides, a dome on top and a submarine-style door; Sorenson later called them daddy, mommy and baby bear. The smallest had four ejection seats on a central vertical beam. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 13:00-13:23,)*
- The craft looked worn and crudely built: chipped thick lead paint (said to shield the crew from x-rays), smudges and fingerprints, messy filament windings on the crew sphere holding pressure, unlike the seamless, rivetless craft of typical UAP crash accounts. Sorenson thought they were built by scientists who did not know composite layup. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 14:26-15:05,)*
- No windows: a ring of camera bumps fed a synthetic vision system that slewed pairs of cameras to the pilot's head orientation and presented alternating left and right views to shuttered goggles, similar to the Apache gun-slaving system. The crew seats, cameras and radial oxygen tanks looked off the shelf. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 15:22-17:41)*
- The crew compartment was separate from the propulsion, which made no noise, had no moving parts and emitted no exhaust. A pilot control ball allocated power across the propulsion sections. Above an enormous capacitor array (about 14 inches thick, per McCandlish) sat a radial arm that could extend outside, which McCandlish guessed was for sample collection. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 17:19-17:58,)*
- The exhibit showed a cutaway drawing on an easel and a video loop of the craft hovering over a dry lake bed, hopping sideways three times and vanishing straight up. The three-star general said they could reach light speed or better; Sorenson recalled 5-8 times light speed, 85 g tolerance and no inertial forces in the local area. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 12:05-12:43,)*

## McCandlish's theory

- Sorenson thought the Biefeld-Brown effect might apply ([[biefeld-brown-effect]], [[electrogravitics]]) but the craft were said to draw on quantum fluctuation (zero-point) energy. McCandlish called it a giant "souped-up Tesla coil" to negate gravity and inertia, the "Model T of antigravity vehicles". *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 17:58-18:44,)*
- The pilot's control: a bowl with a laser diode scanning a dome of sensors for each of 48 capacitor sections, like a helicopter swash plate. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 39:01-40:45)*
- Three overlapping electromagnetic fields (Tesla-coil windings, central windings, a plasma ring) said to work like an explosively pumped flux compression generator to pinch a "doorway" into zero-point energy, which would reduce mass and allow faster-than-light travel. Gerb ties it to Los Alamos flux compression research since 1975, the Alcubierre drive ([[miguel-alcubierre]]) and [[karl-nell]]'s remarks. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 40:45-42:12,)*
- McCandlish also drew parallels to Nazi saucer claims, Die Glocke and patents by associates of [[thomas-townsend-brown]], and possibly [[nikola-tesla]]. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 38:00-38:23)*

## Gerb's commentary

- Gerb says the crude worn look fits a poor-man's copy and contrasts it with alleged recovered craft, and links it to [[witness-ed]]'s zero-point comments. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 14:26-14:50,)*
- He notes the two differences between Sorenson's 1990 account and McCandlish's: metal versus composite materials, and whether generals were present at the demonstration. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 1:05:47-1:06:32)*
