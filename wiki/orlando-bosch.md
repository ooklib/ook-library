Orlando Bosch and his associates are presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as [[operation-40]] veterans turned deniable Condor-era assassins and terrorists. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 50:23-50:37)*

## Claims

- Host: Bosch, Orlando Garcia and Ignacio Novo were suspected in the September 21, 1976 Washington killing of Orlando Letelier (Allende's defense minister, later a critic of Pinochet), reportedly on contract from Chile's DINA. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 50:30-51:17)*
- Ricardo "Monkey" Morales: Op 40 commando and explosives expert suspected of building the Letelier car bomb; part of the CIA Cuban Congo crew until 1965 with Mexican asset Ricardo Chavez; arrested in the late '60s for several crimes including 1968 Miami bombings but freed as an FBI informant ("prosecution proof", per a 1978 New York Times article); star witness at Bosch's 1968 trial. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 51:17-52:05)*
- Bosch: convicted of firing a bazooka from Key Biscayne at a Polish freighter bound for Cuba, paroled 1972, moved to Venezuela then the Dominican Republic, headed an anti-Castro group (host's rendering "coordination of United revolutionary organizations") blamed for the 1976 bombing of Cubana flight 455 (73 killed); possibly hired by Venezuelan intelligence or a Venezuelan private intelligence firm. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 52:05-52:43)*
- Per Gaeton Fonzi (HSCA), Morales at the 1968 trial produced a tape referencing "a fellow named Hunt" who "gave $15,000 to some representatives of Cuban power to place bombs"; host: probably [[e-howard-hunt]]; alternative reading, Morales was trying to implicate the CIA. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 52:55-53:49)*
