F. Mark Wyatt, a CIA agent in Italy, claimed in a 1998 CNN documentary that the PCI was directly
funded with Soviet "black bags of money," while also admitting he helped deliver millions of
dollars to the Christian Democrats — a claim Eyes Wide Open treats as dubious given his conflict
of interest.

## Claims

- The major allegations of Soviet interference in the 1948 election come from Wyatt, who claimed
  in a 1998 CNN documentary that the PCI was funded directly by "black bags of money" out of the
  Soviet compound in Rome, and that he helped deliver millions of dollars to the Christian
  Democrats, who would go on to win. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 23:28-23:51)*
- Eyes Wide Open treats these claims as "dubious at best," per Italian historian Alessandro
  Brogi, who characterizes actual Soviet support for the PCI as only "ad hoc last-minute
  diplomatic and financial support," in stark contrast to Wyatt's claimed $10 million/month
  figure — noting Wyatt's own conflict of interest as a CIA agent, and that Stalin was largely
  disengaged from supporting western communist parties elsewhere (Greece, Yugoslavia).
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 23:57-24:34)*
