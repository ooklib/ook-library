Jesse Michels interviews [[dylan-borland]] for about 3h19m on American Alchemy, published 2026-08-05 (video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K4gYHs84BIc). Borland, a former Air Force 1N1 geospatial (imagery) intelligence analyst in the drone program and later a [[defense-threat-reduction-agency]] contractor, retells his 2012 Langley black-triangle sighting, his claimed exposure to a legacy program while at [[bae-systems]], and the alleged reprisals that followed his 2025 whistleblowing to the [[intelligence-community-inspector-general]], [[aaro]] and Congress. All claims here are Borland's own attributed, unverified assertions, and he repeatedly answers "hypothetically" or "pleads the fifth" rather than confirming specifics.

- The sighting itself is catalogued in [[borland-langley-triangle-2012]], with Michels supplying a Townsend Brown-style ionization explanation for the craft's glow (*([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 38:22-39:38)*), see [[thomas-townsend-brown]].

- The AARO false memorandum, fabricated fourth drawing, clearance "admin withdrawal", VA testing and brake lines are in [[borland-whistleblower-reprisal-claims]].
- Borland's claims that craft of non-human origin are held and studied, that a genetics/biologics component exists, and that baiting and shootdown techniques were briefed to AARO are in [[ufo-baiting-and-directed-energy-claims]] and [[legacy-program-claims-borland]].
- The Langley-specific side threads are [[langley-air-force-base]], [[langley-drone-incursions-2023]] and [[langley-google-maps-tags-claims]].
- The closing philosophical section (humans not in charge, humans as a resource, Enoch, simulation, exit paths) is in [[nhi-control-and-simulation-claims]], with Borland's late friend [[paul-thigpen]].
- Michels raises [[jim-lacatski]]'s hull-breach claim and "UFOs as puzzles" (*([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 45:22-48:25)*), [[salvatore-pais]]'s patents and [[oke-shannon]]'s five-dimensional model (*([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:12:58-1:18:21)*), [[project-greenglow]] at BAE (*([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:33:33-1:35:15)*), and [[glenn-gaffney]] with the alleged [[lockheed-martin]]-to-[[robert-bigelow]] material transfer (*([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:03:10-1:03:42, 2:28:26-2:29:29)*).
