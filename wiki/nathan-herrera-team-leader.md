A former USMC team leader of [[michael-herrera]] on the USS Denver deployment, who publicly claims Herrera fabricated the Indonesia story. Only surname-less references appear; this page records the dispute as told by Herrera, [[joeyisnotmyname]] and Gerb in [[gerb-herrera-2-video]].

## Claims

- Herrera says Nathan was not part of the six-man group on the ridge and led him on a different mission, so his "we didn't see a UFO" statement is not contradictory; it was a daytime mission with no night vision and no aliens. Joey argues that if Herrera had invented the story he would simply have claimed a second real mission with Nathan, and that the only weight Nathan's account has is Herrera's denial of such a mission. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:18:52-1:21:42)*
- Nathan supplied a photo of two people on a helicopter that he says shows Herrera; Herrera says it is not him and knows who it is. Someone matched camouflage between it and the Daily Mail photo, which Herrera dismisses because the MARPAT pattern repeats across uniforms and the facial structure differs. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:22:22-1:24:11)*
- Herrera says Nathan does not like him and the feeling is mutual; he says Nathan was vetted by Kirkpatrick and framed as though he could have seen the UFO. See [[herrera-aaro-testimony-2023]]. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:19:12-1:24:20)*
- Herrera has not encouraged the other Marines to come forward; a former Marine's leave-me-out text was in the Daily Mail article, and Herrera says the Senate intelligence committee asked him to relay a message, with names already given to the Pentagon and AARO. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 36:54-37:59)*
