US Army Special Forces training program for the Royal Lao Army, launched July 1959 (also called Project Hotfoot and Operation Ambidextrous per the host), which circumvented the Geneva neutrality provisions using civilian cover. Source: [[eyes-wide-open]] LAOS 4. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:06:29-1:07:15)*

## Claims

- Special Forces personnel dressed in civilian clothes under the cover of the "National Geodetic Society survey Association" (as ASR renders it) trained irregulars in rural villages; the first independent US program of American trainers working directly with Lao forces. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:06:35-1:07:15)*
- Annie Jacobsen is quoted that most White Star trainees were illiterate and many did not know Laos was independent or had an army. About 150 advisers in 12 teams, per Timothy Castle; some teams trained irregulars, likely the [[vang-pao]] Hmong. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:07:22-1:08:32)*
- Host: its results proved ineffective; captain [[kong-le]] was trained under it and then toppled [[phoumi-nosavan]] in 1960. Priorities shifted in late 1960 to the CIA and the Hmong ([[operation-momentum]]). *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:08:25-1:08:58)*
