Cameron says the most important recent thread is portal or time-travel technology, not craft. He cites a video in which [[ron-pandolfi]] says a breakthrough allows people to go into the next world and come back, tales of portals at Mount Shasta, and a former aircraft building that [[tim-taylor-nasa]] photographed as the alleged "jump room" site tied to [[andrew-basiago]]'s story.

## Claims

- Cameron says a Panama Canal cruise video (posted by Pandolfi's wife) records him saying "people have always wondered what it's like to go into the next world. There's been a breakthrough... next time John goes to the desert, a number of us will go into the next world and come back again"; Pandolfi allegedly called Cameron a crook and a thief for reposting it. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:23:52-2:24:53)*
- Doug Alt, a painter friend, allegedly told Cameron the portal story was real; Pandolfi's circle allegedly talked about "actuators" pulled from portals. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:25:02-2:28:13)*
- Cameron says he interviewed people who entered a Mount Shasta portal in 2014-2015 (a 10-foot being with sparks on his chest, two attendants with iPad-like devices). *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:28:13-2:29:02)*
- Tim Taylor showed Cameron phone photographs of space paintings, a tunnel of balls, a 1956 or 1957 Chevrolet with a postcard reading "I wish I could travel through time and space to be with you" and a former aircraft building; he said it was where "the jump room" was. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:18:55-2:20:31)*
- Cameron says the CIA jump-room story places the room off an elevator on the fifth floor, jumps to Mars and Obama; he asked Basiago and later photographed the building. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:20:39-2:21:45)*
- Cameron says a time-travel or portal technology is likelier than working interplanetary ships, and that tests suggest "white world" mind technology such as a 20,000-neuron rat brain flying an F-22 simulator. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:46:32-2:47:58, 2:48:54-2:49:33)*
