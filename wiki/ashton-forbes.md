Ashton Forbes is a non-physicist investigator who argued on Twitter/X (around 2024) that footage of Malaysia Airlines Flight MH370 shows the plane "teleported" out of the sky by man-made technology, based on videos showing three orbs circling. [[salvatore-pais]] and [[jesse-michels]] discuss his claim on [[pais-michels-interview-2025]].

## Claims (jm/pais)

- Argued the MH370 civilian aircraft, which disappeared over Southeast Asian territory, was "teleported" out of the sky by man-made (not necessarily ET) technology, based on footage showing three orbs. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 1:47:47-1:48:21)*
- Michels says Pais told Forbes, on Forbes's first podcast appearance ("Hard Truths" podcast), that the footage showed the [[pais-effect]] "in action." *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 0:15-0:40, 1:49:25-1:49:38)*
- Engineer Dave Rossi reportedly proposed there must be a fourth orb (representing a wormhole "destination," paired with the three-orb "point of origin" implied by Pais's black-hole framing), which Michels says is why Forbes now also goes by "Four Orbs." *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 1:48:30-1:49:13)*
- Pais praises Forbes as a non-physicist ("no physics pedigree") who has made rapid, surprising progress studying the physics on his own in under a year, and cites him as an example that non-specialists ("Joe the plumber") could contribute the key idea to unify the field. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 1:53:23-1:53:56)*
