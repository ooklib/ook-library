Frank Sturgis (born Frank Fiorini) is introduced as an unofficial CIA-asset gunrunner who armed and trained Castro's guerrillas in the late 1950s, later a Bay of Pigs operative and Watergate burglar. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 36:45-37:09)*

## Claims

- Trained Fidel's men in guerrilla warfare from his WWII Marine Raider experience; told the 1978 House Select Committee on Assassinations that Raiders were trained for "behind the lines activities for instance assassinations." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 37:00-37:33)*
- Never an official employee; an unofficial asset of several spooks, notably [[e-howard-hunt]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 37:51-38:08)*
- The host says he may have taken the name Sturgis from Hunt's 1949 novel character "Hank Sturgis" (or from his stepfather), suggesting the two men met as early as the mid-1940s (speculation; both hypotheses given). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 38:08-38:50)*
- Speculated (by "some") to be one of the three tramps in Dallas with Hunt; both later Nixon-era Watergate plumbers. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 38:58-39:21)*
- Claimed he helped Castro until it became clear in 1959 he was a communist (host finds the hedging-bets explanation hard to accept). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 45:46-46:11)*

## Additional claims (The Road to Dallas Chapter 1, ewa/dallas-1)

This is the dedicated Sturgis episode of [[road-to-dallas]]; the host calls him "the everywhere man of the Cold War" and "arguably the most connected intelligence asset of the Mid Cold War", who fought with and against Castro, trained exiles in South Florida, fought "under several flags" and became a key player in Watergate. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 7:46-8:14, 9:04-9:16)*

### Name and early life

- Changed name from Fiorini to Sturgis officially in September 1952, per interviews to distance himself from an estranged father who abandoned his mother; per the biography *Warrior* (Jim Hunt and Bob Risch; ASR "Bob Ris") he still went by Fiorini until at least the 1970s Nixon "plumbers"/Watergate period. The host uses "Sturgis" throughout. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 8:14-9:04)* (Compare the earlier-episode speculation that he took the name from a [[e-howard-hunt]] novel character, above; this episode gives only the father explanation and does not mention Hunt.)
- Born 1924 in Norfolk, Virginia to first-generation Italian parents; per a 1977 High Times interview wanted to be a Catholic priest before "patriotism became a substitute for priesthood". Served in WWII as a Marine Raider in the Pacific. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 9:16-10:00)*
- The host, quoting *Warrior*, describes the Raiders as a quasi-paramilitary/intelligence battalion (behind enemy lines, surveillance, disrupting communications), and argues WWII Marine veterans became indispensable to early Cold War CIA and special-forces work, listing William "Rip" Robertson, William R. Corson and [[bill-lair]] (in Thailand) alongside [[gerry-patrick-hemming]]. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 10:13-11:27)*
- Host: he would come to be employed as a CIA asset later, "although the agency would never admit that". *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 11:27-11:41)*

### Army intelligence and the Haganah (an "often overlooked" period)

- After Marine discharge in 1945 he was a Norfolk police officer, joined the Naval Reserve in 1947 (Norfolk Air Base), and on August 30, 1948 joined the Army; per *Warrior* he was posted to Germany in General Lucius Clay's honor guard, and per High Times he was in Heidelberg with the Army Security Agency (Army signals intelligence). *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 11:58-12:52)*
- In Germany he reportedly befriended and had a brief relationship with an unnamed Jewish Hungarian actress who was a Haganah agent; by his own 1977 High Times account he assisted her as a courier "over a period of years" and would have helped in Israel, but lost contact. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 12:52-14:10)*
- A 1975 Argosy article ("Killing the Kennedys", F. Peter Model) says he was a "former US Marine and Haganah mercenary during the first 1948 Israeli Arab war." The host cautions this is a single unsourced mention, and notes the timeline is tight (joined the Army August 1948; war ended March 1949), so at most an 8-month window; he floats, purely as speculation, that Sturgis was an off-the-books US military adviser to the Israeli paramilitary under Army cover. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 14:17-15:47)*
- Host says he will revisit "the Israeli aspect of the JFK assassination" later and that Sturgis's early contact with the IDF's precursor and Israeli intelligence "may have translated into further work for Israeli intel elements on the eve of the death of the president" (a promised future claim, not developed here). *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 15:47-16:12)*
- By 1952 he was back in Norfolk as a bartender, part-owner of the Top Hat Bar. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 16:25-16:39)*

### With Castro (1957-59)

- Allegedly first met [[fidel-castro]] in 1957 in Miami while visiting his uncle Angelo and his Cuban wife, who were plugged into the anti-Batista underground; *Warrior* says the meeting was at a fundraiser. His aunt's family was close to [[carlos-prio-socarras]]. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 17:21-17:46, 19:45-20:04)*
- Peter Dale Scott ([[peter-dale-scott]], *Deep Politics and the Death of JFK* p. 178, as quoted by the host): Castro accepted such Americans' services "because of their ability to procure surplus or allegedly stolen US military supplies for his troops." *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 20:16-20:43)*
- Sold his Top Hat share and moved to Miami by 1957; flew night flights from Miami to Santiago de Cuba carrying arms; joined Castro in the Sierra Maestra in 1957 as ad hoc paramilitary adviser and head of Castro's ramshackle small-plane air fleet; in 1958 moved to the Escambray (ASR "Eskimri"), flying guns and supplies onto short strips (host says "Rocky Mountain ridges", an evident slip). *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 20:46-21:29, 30:04-30:23)*
- In 1977 he described Castro's force as "less than 400 men, ragged ... shotguns, pitiful men" and said he helped train officers and men in the same type of warfare as in the South Pacific. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 21:29-21:57)*
- After the victory he was, per the host, rewarded with prominent positions in Castro's government and marched into Havana with the 26th of July movement; loyal "at least on the surface". Around him, hundreds of American soldiers of fortune arrived, some undercover for US intelligence; see [[cuban-revolution]]. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 29:58-30:51)*
- Host: he and [[gerry-patrick-hemming]] would work together "all through the early 60s in the secret war against ... Fidel Castro" and become "the most visible American faces in the growing anti-Castro underground", which "brings some of their motives and associations into question." *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 22:22-22:40, 23:41-23:54)*
