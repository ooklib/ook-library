[[matthew-pines]] speculates, in conversation with [[jesse-michels]], on how the second [[donald-trump]] administration would treat UAP and legacy-program information. This is analysis, not reporting; Pines says he does not know what Trump has been told.

## Claims

- Pines believes Trump has been briefed on some aspects of the legacy programs and knows "at least broad contours" that it is real, and that his first reaction would be to ask how to use it as leverage in geopolitical bargaining. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 24:04-24:38)*
- Pines predicts that any disclosure would be the subset that maximizes leverage over rivals, with the rest kept secret. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 26:04-26:34)*
- The Senate language about "foreign technology surprise" and broadening awareness of "historical exotic technology antecedents" previously provided by the federal government for research and development is read by Pines as an implicit admission that China has a baseline of knowledge, and that a "tech tree" the US had pruned for 70 years is now being grown by China. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 27:03-28:20, 29:43-30:35)*
- Pines argues this China competition is why disclosure pressure has grown over the last ten years. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 30:35-30:39)*
- He says the Trump 2.0 coalition (Vance, Kennedy, Musk and others) is interested in the topic, that Musk "seems to have changed his tune", and that the question is who gets assigned the problem; he still prefers pushing the [[uap-disclosure-act-2023]] framework over an ad hoc truth-and-reconciliation committee. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 4:02:27-4:03:14, 4:05:35-4:05:52)*
- He calls a "perestroika" (glasnost-style reform of decayed institutions) the closest historical analogy to the election result. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 2:04-2:29)*
