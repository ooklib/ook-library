Episode 1 of the six-part documentary series *Inside Secret Societies* (uploaded by Banijay Documentaries, 2023-07-20, about 46 minutes), framed as an investigation of the Nazi elite's occult beliefs and secret societies. Narration and interviewees (Theo Paijmans, Michael Fitzgerald, Andrew Gough, Ross Andrews and others, several with ASR-garbled names) present the [[thule-society]], the [[vril-society]], [[heinrich-himmler]]'s [[wewelsburg-castle]], the [[lebensborn-program]] and the SS relic quests as the hidden influences on the Third Reich. It does not mention [[rudolf-hess]], [[martin-bormann]], [[hjalmar-schacht]], [[prescott-bush]] or [[operation-paperclip]] except for one line that the designers of the alleged Nazi flying saucer "went on to work in America and Canada". Everything below is attributed claim; the narration itself hedges some items ("it's believed", "we don't know").
