[[matthew-pines]] argues that the alleged UFO legacy programs are not a single vertically integrated cabal but a decayed, fragmented set of fiefdoms, and that the Senate's push (including the [[uap-disclosure-act-2023]]) is about accountability of rogue programs more than public disclosure. Pines mixes analytic inference with second-hand reports he cannot verify.

## Claims

- Pines assumes the WWII-to-1960s programs were federal projects run under the Manhattan Project and Atomic Energy Act secrecy structure, consistent with [[david-grush]]'s account, and that a shift came in the 1970s (Nixon shock, stagflation, neoliberal privatization), after which programs split between what the government held and what it did not. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 31:41-32:38, 33:55-34:23)*
- He says Grusch's testimony implies elements of the US government have been "rogue", with parts of the defense industrial base as loose networks (not necessarily [[lockheed-martin]] as a corporation), which is why the disclosure act targeted eminent domain over privately held material. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 34:23-35:03)*
- Pines says the interest of Gillibrand, Rubio and others "has not really been disclosure, it's been accountability": finding programs elected officials did not know about, and getting official structures to take back control. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 35:03-35:38)*
- He does not believe in a tightly integrated vertical control structure because of principal-agent problems and the compartmentalization required by secrecy, expecting a heterogeneous, horizontal set of pieces with minimal coercion. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 1:02:31-1:04:25)*
- Pines and Michels describe a "disjointed cargo cult" with no ultimate gatekeeper, partly pro-disclosure and partly anti-disclosure CIA factions, and local contractor incentives; Pines suggests disclosure is happening because the once-coherent programs "decayed and lost the ability to maintain control". *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 2:47:55-2:49:26)*
- Pines describes disgruntled program staff (a contractor "Bob" in a Lockheed warehouse paid less than at Palantir, ten years without progress, threatened with death for talking, seeing only their slice), and says Grusch said people on the inside want the stuff released. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 2:49:35-2:50:47)*
- Pines' account of the bureaucratic history: the UAP Task Force at ONI had access to grind against CIA, NSA and the Air Force; when it moved into [[aaro]] it was "compromised by the CIA Air Force guys" like [[sean-kirkpatrick]]; pressure went up to the [[national-security-council]] where Jake Sullivan reportedly "kicked the can" to the next administration. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 2:51:28-2:53:57)*
- He cites the Senate finding that "credible evidence indicates that relevant UAP records have been inappropriately classified and withheld" from elected officials, saying only the president and vice president are the elected officials in the executive branch. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 2:54:43-2:55:17)*
- Pines says the UAP Task Force was an interagency task force with mostly part-time members and a few full-time ones such as [[david-grush]], and mentions [[karl-nell]] as a source of self-reports. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:04:02-3:05:12)*
- He cites Helen McCaw's Sol Foundation white paper on UAPs and financial stability risk, and worries about Fed and Treasury bureaucrats "not showing up for work" if the topic dropped abruptly. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 2:57:05-2:58:31)*
- The "Ben Rich" line about an international board of directors and the [[tom-delonge]] narrative of a [[maynard-consortium]]-like boardroom are discussed as possibilities Pines finds less plausible than fragmentation. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 1:01:14-1:02:31)*
