United Fruit Company is a US agricultural corporation Eyes Wide Open cites as the paradigm case
of the CIA's alleged capture by corporate interests: a client of [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan
& Cromwell]] (the Dulles brothers' law firm) that, per the host, lobbied the CIA to overthrow the
Guatemalan government in 1954.

## Claims

- A client of [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]], alongside the
  [[anglo-iranian-oil-company|Anglo Iranian Oil Company]]. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 5:50-5:54)*
- Lobbied the CIA to overthrow the democratically elected left-wing government of Guatemalan
  President [[jacobo-arbenz]] in 1954, an operation Eyes Wide Open says [[allen-dulles]] (as CIA
  director) and [[john-foster-dulles]] (as secretary of state) both worked to carry out, replacing
  Arbenz with a "brutal western-friendly dictator." Ran its Central American and Caribbean
  plantations "like feudal kingdoms," per the host, making land-reform-minded governments an
  existential threat to its business model. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 6:04-6:24, 11:30-11:34)*
