Jack Parsons (1914-1952) was a self-taught Pasadena rocket chemist who co-founded the Caltech-area rocketry group that became [[jet-propulsion-laboratory]] and developed jet-assisted takeoff (JATO) boosters; he was also a devoted occultist. [[jesse-michels]] uses him as the lead example of the "occult rocket scientist" and of scientists who saw themselves as receivers of non-human inspiration.

## Claims

- Born in Pasadena in 1914; expelled from military school after blowing up a toilet block; with Ed Forman built homemade rockets, then joined Frank Malina under Theodore von Karman testing engines in the Arroyo Seco, later the JPL site. Michels notes that Caltech physicists such as Fritz Zwicky and Robert Millikan doubted rockets, and that Thomas Townsend Brown's earlier spaceflight ideas fell on deaf ears there. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 14:20-16:47)*
- JATO: first successful flight 1941; by 1942 the military ordered 20,000 units per month; the FBI then took interest in his occultism. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 17:00-18:05)*
- Member and Pasadena head of the [[ordo-templi-orientis]] (O.T.O.), led by Aleister Crowley, practicing "sex magick"; ran a mansion of artists and magicians and believed the rituals could open doorways and contact non-human intelligences. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 18:05-19:50)*
- Conducted the [[babalon-working]] with [[l-ron-hubbard]]; Michels wonders whether it "opened something up in 1946" given the later surge in UAP reports; Parsons believed he was being guided by non-human intelligence. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 20:08-21:25)*
- Would recite Crowley's "Hymn to Pan" at rocket tests; Jacques Vallee reports a rumor of a Mojave ritual where a blonde woman claiming to be from Venus appeared, which Michels calls a possible first "Nordic" encounter. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 22:06-22:44)*
- Pushed out of JPL; died in a 1952 home-laboratory explosion, officially an accident though "some found the circumstances suspicious"; has a lunar crater, and some JPL insiders say "Jack Parsons lives". *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 22:44-23:27)*
- His group's first successful liquid-fuel test fell on Halloween 1936 (Samhain); see [[nasa-occult-symbolism-claims]].
