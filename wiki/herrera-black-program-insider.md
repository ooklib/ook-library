An unnamed person who allegedly works in the crash-retrieval side of a black reverse-engineering program and who allegedly met [[michael-herrera]] in 2023. His existence and the meeting are supported, per [[joeyisnotmyname]] and Gerb, by unpublished evidence; nothing he said is verified, and Gerb says it could be purposeful disinformation. The wiki treats all of his statements as attributed claims.

## Claims

- He allegedly wanted to meet Herrera in person after the [[national-press-club-disclosure-event-2023]] and asked [[steven-greer]] to connect them. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 15:20-16:22)*
- He describes himself as pro-disclosure, in the program for decades, with a focus on crash retrieval; his statements are summarized on [[blue-group-black-group-claims]]. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 26:19-26:36, 30:44-30:52)*
- Joey infers from the location Herrera was taken to that the insider has considerable authority, since a civilian could not normally be brought there; Joey knows the insider exists (others corroborate) but not whether anything he says is true. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 29:28-30:44)*
- Gerb wonders why he would trust Herrera; his suggested answers are a scoop or disinformation operation (he recalls Paul Bennewitz being fed false information by the CIA in 1989) or a pro-disclosure figure using Herrera as a vessel, since Herrera is already comfortable with whistleblower risk. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 30:53-32:39)*
- A few weeks after the meeting he told Herrera he was writing a book with a ghostwriter to mix truth and fiction, "Sentinels of Ether", about a 2004 Mexico event in which a black-program paramilitary group wipes out a JTF team while moving humans in shipping containers to a hidden mountain base; Gerb compares the approach to Sekret Machines by [[tom-delonge]]. Herrera had expected the meeting to yield evidence, and the book was it; Gerb and Joey find the connecting threads thin. See [[sentinels-of-ether]]. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 32:39-34:00)*
- He allegedly sent a two-chapter manuscript under DOPSR review and describes a scheme to include deep-black details to see whether reviewers flag them; he is quoted as intending to "put the DoD in a pickle" and gain "inverse intelligence" from the response. See [[dopsr-prepublication-review-claims]]. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 34:00-35:19)*
