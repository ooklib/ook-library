On 8 May 2026 the Department of War (the Pentagon's renamed public-facing identity in this source) released hundreds of UFO-related documents, videos and audio after [[donald-trump]] ordered release of government files on extraterrestrial life and UFOs (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:38-2:03)*). [[jesse-michels]] reports its web page had 500 million hits and judges it "a UFO starter pack", not the vault of an alien autopsy tape or historical crash-retrieval photos (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 2:03-2:34)*). All figures are Michels' statements and are not verified here.

## Claims and commentary

- Much of the material "wasn't even newly declassified" and had been "hiding in plain sight"; the release now merely puts a stamp of approval on it, which is enough for establishment voices such as Neil deGrasse Tyson to say it is probably real. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:24-2:52)*
- Michels thinks the timing is cynical, likely a distraction from Epstein, Iran and other problems, but says podcasters calling UFOs a scop are wrong: "Someone can tell you to look at the moon and pick your pocket, but that doesn't make the moon fake." *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 3:00-3:45)*
- He describes a possible management strategy: blurry videos, a poorly curated batch, stigmatizing anything with a bad reaction, and inching forward if it goes well. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 3:45-4:17)*
- It puts [[nasa]] in a bind because the Pentagon now says [[gene-cernan]]'s astronaut sightings were unresolved. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 4:25-4:55)*
- Michels' view that the government wants "a slow and steady disclosure drip" contrasts with his own aim to speed things up. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 6:35-6:44)*
