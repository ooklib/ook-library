A 1954 Joint Army Navy Air Force publication (directive 146c) that, per [[uap-gerb]], required both airborne and waterborne UFO sightings to be reported under service communication instructions for vital intelligence sightings (Gerb guesses "merant" means maritime intelligence). *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 5:08-5:35)*

## Claims

- Civilians and pilots were prohibited from discussing officially reported sightings, as reports fell under the Communications Act of 1934 and espionage laws, with a prison sentence and a ,000 fine for violators. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 5:35-5:50)*
- Together with USAF regulation 200-2 (1953, sending official UFO reports to ATIC rather than [[project-blue-book]]) and the Navy's OPNAV directive 3820 of 23 January 1954 (flying object reports to the directors of ATIC, Air Force intelligence and Naval intelligence; a copy is in Keyhoe's *The Flying Saucer Conspiracy*), Gerb says it marks the "slow descent into the dark ages of UFO reporting". *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 5:50-6:58)*

## Claims (gerb/uso-casebook)

- In the follow-up USO video Gerb again blames OPNAV 3820 and JANAP 146(C) for the thin official USO record in Blue Book, and compares a 1977 Soviet directive requiring ships to report UFOs to them (see [[soviet-uso-cases]]). *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 0:26-0:44, 7:53-8:15, 26:50-27:04)*
