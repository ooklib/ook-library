Bill McDonald is a freelance commercial and forensic-reconstruction illustrator (and sworn law-enforcement professional, per UAP Gerb) whose UFO-related work includes the EBE-1/EBE-2 designs with [[dr-robert-wood]] for the [[special-operations-manual-som1-01]], Roswell craft art, and the 1976 Tehran incident. Gerb spoke with him and rates him highly. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 56:38-57:33)*

- Says four Northrop and Lockheed engineers told him in 1992 of the [[xf-131-super-sentinel]], and drew the "Tehachapi Triangle"; he maintains their testimony was true, has sworn to protect their identities, and says he confirmed the Tejon claim through friends of his father and his father's mentor Robert Scandrett of Rockwell International's Strategic Systems Division. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 57:42-1:01:31)*
