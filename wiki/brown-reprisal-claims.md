Reprisal claims made by [[matthew-brown]] in the [[matthew-brown-michels-interview-2026]]. Brown says he keeps many things "close to my chest" because of a lack of proof and because he is dismissed as "the UFO guy".

## Claims

- His house was repeatedly broken into: nothing of value was taken except his civilian passport, his grandfather's ashes were left in the alley by the garbage, and his wife's documents were laid out in a row as if photographed; the only sign of entry was a small forced basement window that he says an adult could not use, and there is a police report. He compares it to the Stasi practice of moving furniture. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 3:09:49-3:11:17)*

Matthew Brown).md>) 3:11:32-3:13:38)*
- Regarding the Air Force OSI, he says it was not his experience but someone's, with proof in one case of an AFOSI officer messing with a whistleblower and family. See [[air-force-office-of-special-investigations]]. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft.
