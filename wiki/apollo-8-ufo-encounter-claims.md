Two linked, unverified claims about the December 1968 Apollo 8 lunar mission (the video gives 11 October 1968, which appears to be an error). [[harland-bentley]] says he overheard mission control radio traffic about a saucer-like craft paralleling the capsule, and a redacted witness in [[steven-greer]]'s [[disclosure-project]] list allegedly saw an uncut film by astronaut Frank Borman of six UFOs descending onto the Moon. [[uap-gerb]] says he finds "zero references or lines of research" for either.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-2)

- Bentley, doing NASA classified contracting in California in 1967-1968 while "our astronauts were doing a loop around the moon", says he overheard a Houston-crew exchange about a "bogey coming in at 11 o'clock", the crew asking permission for collision avoidance, then saying it was not necessary because the object was now paralleling their course: a saucer-like ship with portals in which they could see movement or beings, photographed it, and it left after some thousands of miles. Houston and the astronauts treated it as no big deal, and the one man with him told him "you did not hear anything". Gerb concludes it was Apollo 8 by inference. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 9:33-11:53)*
- Gerb notes Borman had reported a "bogey" on Gemini 7 in December 1965 (which the media called a UFO sighting) and that Borman said it was just the rocket's booster. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 12:26-13:15)*
- Disclosure Project: a redacted government witness (number 101179 per Gerb) whose bio says he saw the official uncut NASA film of six UFOs descending onto the lunar surface, taken by Borman on Apollo 8. Gerb found Greer's files and emails on the Disclosure Project archive: Greer planned to meet the source in April 2009, the source traveling from France with a high-profile French astronaut; handwritten notes of February 2009 labeled "AB" discuss hoaxes and trustworthy people. Gerb cannot tell whether Borman is mentioned. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 12:02-14:11)*
- Gerb infers the source is Alain Boudier, who was appointed around then as president of the French 3AF-Sigma (3AF unidentified aerospace phenomena) division, from emails showing Greer's "Source One, A Boudier" delivering briefing documents to French President Nicolas Sarkozy in March 2009. He says he has reached out to Boudier. Gerb also speculates NASA pilots (Charles Bolden, a four-flight shuttle veteran) named in a forwarded French article might be the redacted names in the handwritten notes. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 14:11-15:33)*
