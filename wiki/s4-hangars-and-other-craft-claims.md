[[bob-lazar]] repeats that S4 has nine hangar doors and that "there are several, actually nine, flying saucers ... of extraterrestrial origin" out there *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 0:01-0:11, 26:47-26:52)*. He glimpsed other bays only once, when all inner bay doors were open across from the lab door; he saw parts of at least three craft and infers other propulsion teams *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:37:33-1:38:14)*. He heard and saw no evidence of underground facilities at S4 but cannot rule them out *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:36:47-1:37:14)*.

## Other craft he mentions

- A "jello mold" shape and a craft like a square "carnival hat" standing up with a projectile-style hole and bent metal *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:38:21-1:38:32)*. Ramsey says the latter is said to have been found in water.
- Lazar says "the Navy is behind all this stuff" and speculates the Navy found some craft intact underwater, giving them "dibs"; he is "fascinated by what might be going on in the water" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:38:41-1:38:59, 1:42:01-1:42:19)*.

## Underwater and transmedium comparison

Ramsey says [[luis-elizondo]] told him of crisp 4K footage of a city-block-sized round object moving at about 500 knots underwater near an oil rig, and that Navy rear admiral Tim Gallaudet described large craft at 400-500 knots underwater. Lazar agrees this would need advanced technology and suggests civilizations could hide underwater *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:40:24-1:42:01)*. Both statements are second-hand recollections of others' claims.
