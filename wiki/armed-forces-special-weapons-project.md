The Armed Forces Special Weapons Project (AFSWP), a joint Army-Navy venture to manage nuclear
weapons, was established 29 January 1947 by Secretary of War Robert Patterson and Secretary of
the Navy James V. Forrestal (an alleged original [[majestic-12|Majestic 12]] member). AFSWP and
Los Alamos's Z Division both established operations at Sandia Base the same month; a subsidiary
location, Manzano Base ("Site Able"), began construction there in 1947 to store nuclear weapons
and eventually housed two underground installations. Gerb treats AFSWP as the direct
organizational ancestor of the modern [[defense-threat-reduction-agency|Defense Threat Reduction
Agency (DTRA)]] and a recurring name in the earliest alleged UFO legacy-program crash-retrieval
claims. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 18:19–19:30)*

## Claims

- Per [[j-andrew-kner|J. Andrew Kner]]'s 1994 statement, AFSWP personnel stationed at Sandia
  Base supported military analysis of discs downed by the US Army from May–July 1947, alongside
  Air Materiel Command. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 27:07–27:23)*
- The Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit (IPU) report (dated July 1947, given to researcher Timothy
  Cooper in 1995) describes a UFO recovery operation near a July 1947 New Mexico fall of objects
  (i.e. Roswell) in which a special radiobiological team, a Special Engineering Detachment (a
  Manhattan Project-linked technical-personnel term), and a security detail from Sandia Base
  secured the crash site "under orders from Colonel Sherman V. Hasbrouck of the Armed Forces
  Special Weapons Project." Hasbrouck was a real Army colonel who worked closely with Leslie
  Groves and helped establish AFSWP. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 38:12–40:31)*
- Gerb notes Kner's 1994 statement could not have derived from the 1995-released IPU documents,
  raising the possibility the IPU documents drew on (or independently corroborate) Kner's
  account. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 39:36–39:57)*
- Much of the ~11 sq mi Coyote Test Field at Sandia, New Mexico, was built in the late 1940s
  under AFSWP. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 55:10–55:31)*
- AFSWP is the first entity in an unbroken organizational lineage — AFSWP (1947–59) → Defense
  Atomic Support Agency (1959–71) → Defense Nuclear Agency (1971–96, began supporting
  [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team|NEST]] in 1974) → Defense Special Weapons Agency
  (1996–98) → [[defense-threat-reduction-agency|DTRA]] (1998–present) — that Gerb argues gives
  weight to whistleblower [[dylan-borland|Dylan Borland]]'s claim that DTRA holds old AEC
  extraterrestrial-related files. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 2:31:47–2:33:21)*
