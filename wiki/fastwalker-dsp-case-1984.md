An alleged 5 May 1984 detection by a [[defense-support-program]] satellite of an object that approached the satellite, curved away and left. [[uap-gerb]] presents it in [[gerb-fastwalkers-video]] as speculative and unproven; it rests on an anonymous source and defunct web pages.

## Claims

- Origin: an anonymous source told journalist Joe Stefula (transcribed "staula"), described as a former Army criminal investigation command member, that a highly classified DSP satellite detected a UAP that came within 1.8 miles (another quoted account says 15 miles) of the satellite before flying below it over the Indian Ocean at about 22,000 mph, changing course and flying back into space. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 10:58-11:29, 12:43-13:14)*
- Details: the object was tracked for another 9 minutes until it disappeared; Stefula says the DSP printout for that date shows an event at the same time with the same characteristics. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 12:50-13:47)*
- Sources: Gerb says he found only two now-destroyed web pages and an archived blog with three illustrative images and a possible recreation of the top secret incident report summary, whose terminology (UCF listing, "St in the field of view") he cannot decipher. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 11:29-13:31)*
- Judgment: Gerb says it cannot be proven or denied without a full DSP report analysis, so it remains speculative; he finds it similar to the incident [[bob-fish]] described to [[john-podesta]]. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 11:46-11:54, 13:47-14:03)*
