A video (about 1h15m, dated 2024-09-12, [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgBTMzFd-hg)) in which [[uap-gerb]] gives a long treatment of the alleged December 1965 [[kecksburg-ufo-crash-1965]], one of his most requested crash-retrieval cases. Chapters: Intro, The Crash, The Witnesses, The Craft, Dr. Eric A. Walker, President Lyndon B. Johnson, After the Crash, Bodies?, Conclusion. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 0:00-1:30)*

Gerb leans mainly on the research of [[stan-gordon]] and [[leonard-stringfield]], [[leslie-kean]]'s NASA lawsuit, and [[grant-cameron]]'s 2024 document release on [[lyndon-b-johnson]]'s schedule (linked in the description as "Grant Cameron Files"). He covers the fireball and military response, the witnesses [[jim-romansky]], [[bill-bulebush]] and Jerry Betters, the craft description and rebuttal of the meteor and Soviet-capsule explanations, [[eric-a-walker]]'s alleged presence, [[johnson-schedule-after-kecksburg-1965]], the [[kecksburg-wright-patterson-transport-claims]] and the [[kecksburg-bodies-claims]]. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:06:35-1:14:38)*

His verdict: he "leans towards" a legitimate UAP retrieval, thinks something tangible crashed and it was not a meteor, doubts a human-made capsule, and notes that official documents are scant because NASA says its files were lost. He announces possible future videos on the [[blue-beret-claims]] and Fort Maguire, on [[the-bell]] and [[nick-cook]]'s work, and on witness Clifford Stone's Kecksburg claims. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:08:02-1:08:42)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:09:13-1:09:45)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:14:14-1:14:38)*
