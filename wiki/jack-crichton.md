Jack Crichton (ASR "Kriton"/"Kingon"; the name is confirmed by the video description) is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as "Dallas's private spymaster," a central figure in the private intelligence network the Texas oil cowboys allegedly built in the 1940s-50s because they could not rely on the CIA. Louisiana-born, Texas-based.

## Claims

- Empire Trust Company's Dallas representative and a vice president there. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 24:56-25:12)*
- "Plugged into every powerful political figure in Texas" and acted "as a sort of enforcer" for the cowboy interests; provided the Texas Cowboys a private intelligence network that aided their overseas expansion. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 25:12-26:36)*
- Attended Texas A&M, graduating in the same class as future Dallas mayor [[earle-cabell]] (mayor when Kennedy was shot; Cabell's brother [[charles-cabell]] was CIA deputy director). *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 26:36-27:15)*
- Served in the [[office-of-strategic-services]] in Europe in WWII; afterward offered his intelligence services to the Dallas establishment; from 1950 Empire Trust's rep at [[cuban-venezuelan-oil-voting-trust]] (with [[george-de-mohrenschildt]]). *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 27:15-27:38)*
- 1952 (Russ Baker): tasked to acquire oil rights from the Franco government in Spain for a circle including [[clint-murchison]], allegedly via contacts from his OSS days (the latter is the host's assumption). *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 27:38-28:10)*

## Relationships

- Agent of the [[texas-raj]]; colleague of [[george-de-mohrenschildt]] via CVT; OSS veteran.
