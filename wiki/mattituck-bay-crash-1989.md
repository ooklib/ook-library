An alleged UFO crash retrieval near Mattituck Bay, off the south shore of Suffolk County, Long
Island, NY, on the early morning of 29 September 1989 — analyzed by
[[leonard-stringfield|Leonard Stringfield]] (Status Report 6, "Inner Sanctum") and the Long
Island UFO Network (LI UFO). *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:12:44–1:13:45)*

## Claims

- Per LI UFO/Stringfield, the crash was not an accident but "a carefully planned and executed
  military intelligence operation to down the craft," per an anonymous Air Force intelligence
  colonel, using a Star Wars ([[strategic-defense-initiative|SDI]]) weapon; the craft crashed
  into a dune and was retrieved. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:13:45–1:14:11)*
- The unnamed colonel additionally claimed 18 US servicemen were killed by onboard biologics
  using a device generating "extremely low frequency sound waves" capable of destroying
  molecular cohesion — a claim Gerb states he can find no corroborating record for.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:14:11–1:14:27)*
- Multiple eyewitnesses (including a commodities broker) reported helicopters transporting a
  large object and military vehicles with police escort toward
  [[brookhaven-national-laboratory|Brookhaven National Labs]]; a Brookhaven scientist source
  ("Dr. Nick," previously worked at Wright-Patterson studying UFOs) described a large wedge/
  boomerang-shaped object of unusually low mass being recovered and transported there, downed by
  an AIL Electronics-built weapon. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:14:38–1:15:13)*
