The most contested part of the [[kecksburg-ufo-crash-1965]]: rumors that occupants or bodies were recovered. [[stan-gordon]] said in 2003 he had heard rumors that two small bodies were found at the impact site but accepted them as hearsay with no evidence. All claims below are unverified and attributed. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:00:51-1:01:21)*

## Claims

- [[myron-brick-delivery-witness]] added in 1998 that inside the warehouse he saw a body 4-5 ft tall on a workbench under a white sheet, with a three-fingered left hand and dark green or brownish "lizard" skin, weighing about 80 lb. He said he waited because of fear and poor health. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:01:30-1:04:14)*
- A witness "Joel" told Gordon in late 2002 he hid in the woods and saw an Army general strike the object with a probe that flew out of his hands, after which a hatch swung open and Joel saw "two fingers and an unusually long arm" inside. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:04:14-1:05:24)*
- Bill Weaver, a teenager, saw four men in white "Moon suits" from a light-colored box van carry a 4-5 ft square box on a four-handled stretcher to the object; Gerb says this may support the body claim. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:05:24-1:06:20)*
- Don Sebastian reported a scream from the forest heard by no one else; Gerb left it out. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:12:29-1:12:54)*
- Gerb contrasts Myron's late addition with [[robert-willingham]]'s changed account in the 1955 Del Rio case. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:11:49-1:12:14)*
