Finnish Waffen-SS veteran turned US Army Green Beret, used by [[eyes-wide-open]] (`ewa/shackley`) as an example of the "collaborationist" lineage of the early [[us-army-special-forces]] persisting into the Laos/Vietnam era. ASR: "Lorie Tory", later "Larry Thorne"; the bibliography spells the surname "Torni" and the first name "Lauri" is my inference from "Lorie".

## Claims

- Former member of the Finnish Volunteer Battalion of the Waffen-SS; smuggled to the US through Finnish-Swedish rat lines, arriving by 1950 via a convoluted route through South America; arrested by the FBI a few years later as an illegal alien. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:08:11-1:08:41)*
- A private law of August 12, 1953 let him immigrate legally, obtained through a Finnish diplomat acquaintance (ASR "Juan Pavo Fleming") whose lawyers were at Donovan and Associates, the firm of [[william-donovan]]; host: "interesting intersection." *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:08:41-1:09:14)*
- Joined the Army in 1954 under the Lodge Act, scouted as a special-forces recruit for his Waffen-SS sabotage experience; US citizen 1957; served 1958-62 at Bad Tolz with the 10th Special Forces Group, "the evolution of Galen's original special forces commandos" (host). *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:09:14-1:09:52)*
- Died October 18, 1965 when his helicopter was downed under unclear circumstances on Operation Shining Brass, an early special-forces mission on the Ho Chi Minh Trail; remains found 1999, buried at Arlington 2003, believed to be the only SS member buried there. Host: a little under a year before [[ted-shackley]] took over the CIA's Laos operation. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:09:52-1:10:32)*
