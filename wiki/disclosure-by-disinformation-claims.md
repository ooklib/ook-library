Cameron and Michels discuss a tactic in which intelligence or program insiders release true material alongside falsities, or through discreditable messengers, so the truth gets out but is debunked and "radioactive", inoculating the public. Cameron applies it to the Holloman film, Bob Lazar, the CIA's Hollywood liaison and Bill Moore. All are claims and hypotheses, not shown facts.

## Claims

- Michels cites a Rand paper on mixing truth with falsity; Cameron says once a case is discredited the story circulates but nobody believes it. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:09:29-1:10:29)*
- Cameron reads the Lazar story as putting out a real claim via someone with poor reputation: [[lazar-counterintelligence-hypothesis-cameron]]. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:20:10-1:21:04)*
- Cameron says CIA Hollywood liaison [[chase-brandon]] took film people on tours so they would fictionalize sensitive material; Emenegger similarly had a CIA man on set. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:32:43-1:35:11)*
