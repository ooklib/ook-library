Barry Seal is presented as Ferrie's teenage recruit who "eventually became a long-standing CIA pilot" and "the most prolific drug smuggler in US history," flying between Colombia, Central America and Mena, Arkansas during the governorship of Bill Clinton. Sources cited: Daniel Hopsicker, *Barry and the Boys*. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 42:14-42:39)*

## Claims

- Reportedly flew for the CIA in the Bay of Pigs invasion and for [[air-america]] in Laos; believed to have started flying guns to Cuban forces in the late 1950s. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 42:14-42:23, 42:32-42:47)*
- Born 1939 like Oswald; served in Civil Air Patrol under [[david-ferrie]] alongside [[lee-harvey-oswald]] (host's reading of Hopsicker photos, July 23, 1955 camp photo). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 43:29-44:01)*
- Went directly from CAP to gun-running for the CIA in Cuba. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 44:01-44:08)*
