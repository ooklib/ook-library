Sicilian mobster Salvatore Giuliano is alleged, alongside [[frank-coppola]], to have been behind
the 1947 Portella della Ginestra ("Mayday") massacre of leftist workers in Sicily.

## Claims

- According to some sources, Giuliano and Detroit mob boss [[frank-coppola|Frank Kapola]] had been
  behind the 1947 Mayday massacre, where Mafia men opened fire on a crowd of rallying workers in
  Sicily, killing eight and wounding 33. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 26:01-26:19)*
