Lao general and, per [[eyes-wide-open]], the CIA's chosen strongman in [[laos]] 1958-1962. ASR variants: "pumi novan", "pumi no saan", "Pumi"; normalization to Phoumi Nosavan is my inference from the spelling and role (not stated as such in the transcript). Not to be confused with Prime Minister Phoui Sananikone, a confusion the host himself flags. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 37:57-38:12)*

## Claims

- Studied at a French war college; "discovered" by CIA agent Jack Hay (ASR; the name is as rendered in the transcript) in 1957 and flown back to Vientiane to head the CIA-backed Committee for the Defense of the National Interest (CDNI), a quasi-secret group of right-wing Royal Lao Army generals. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 38:00-38:22)*
- Nephew of [[sarit-thanarat]], which the host says made him an even better fit given Thai-Lao ties. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 39:21-39:46)*
- Took Vientiane in a bloodless coup on Dec 30, 1959; the CIA also helped rig the April 1960 election to legitimize him; was ousted by [[kong-le]] in August 1960, then retook the capital in December 1960 with CIA/[[air-america]] support from his hometown Savannakhet and became defense minister. See [[laos-coups-1958-1960]]. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 43:16-43:33, 44:18-44:36, 47:38-48:20)*
- Opium: host says Phoumi and his officers took part in and facilitated the opium trade to keep forces active during lapses in US support; in 1962 (under Kennedy) he assigned the covert opium-funding model to General [[ouane-rattikone]], who admitted to [[alfred-mccoy]] in the early 1970s that Phoumi's opium profits totalled at least $35,000 a month in early 1962. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 50:02-50:56)*
- [[bill-lair]] and his team relocated to Savannakhet in late 1960 and, by Lair's own account, helped Phoumi retake Laos from Kong Le. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:09:16-1:09:47)*
