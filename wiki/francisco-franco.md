Francisco Franco, later fascist dictator of Spain, appears in [[eyes-wide-open]]'s Willoughby biography as the Spanish Legion commander whom [[charles-willoughby]] met in the [[rif-war]] and idolized for life. Everything below is the host's account of the video.

## Claims

- Franco and Willoughby were born nine months apart in 1892; when the American captain met the Spanish lieutenant colonel he was "apparently immediately impressed" (host's speculation about the impression). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 26:52-27:11)*
- Two years after Annual, Spanish dictator Primo de Rivera "assigned a relatively unknown Colonel Francisco Franco" to command the Spanish Legion in Morocco, where he implemented novel counterinsurgency strategies. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 22:58-23:21)*
- Host: the Rif War links colonialism to fascism. Franco (future dictator of "Falangist Spain") and Marshal [[philippe-petain]] (future leader of Vichy France) commanded the two colonial forces; Franco surrounded himself with "Africanists" who later filled the Falangist hierarchy, e.g. General Emilio Mola, one of three leaders of the July 1936 coup. Pétain stayed close to Franco, becoming ambassador to Franco's Spain in 1939 and working to increase ties while heading Vichy. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 24:35-26:12)*
- Franco reestablished the "kingdom of Spain" in 1947 with himself as figurehead, which the host says would have delighted the aristocratic Willoughby; Spain's nominal neutrality in WWII let Willoughby praise Franco without appearing treasonous. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 31:39-32:37)*
- Willoughby reportedly toasted Franco in the 1950s as "the second greatest general in the world, the first being Douglas MacArthur" ("as the story goes"). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 34:29-34:48)*
- Chemical warfare: in the later years of the conflict, "especially after Franco took over", Spanish and French air forces used chemical weapons against the Rifians (host, citing a Witter/Hayns essay). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 28:26-28:45)*
