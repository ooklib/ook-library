Richard Helms, future CIA director, is noted by Eyes Wide Open (citing Jefferson Morley) as one
of the future CIA spymasters personally trained by [[george-white]] at the New York OSS staging
ground during WWII, alongside [[frank-wisner]], [[william-colby]], and [[james-jesus-angleton]].

## Claims

- Per author Jefferson Morley, [[george-white]] personally trained the future class of CIA spy
  masters at the New York staging ground for new OSS agents during the war, including future
  director of Plans [[frank-wisner]], future CIA Director Richard Helms, [[william-colby]], and —
  "maybe most significantly to White's career" — future CIA counterintelligence chief
  [[james-jesus-angleton]]. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 50:12-50:38)*

- Host notes (Detachment 202 episode) that Helms got his start in China with the OSS like Hunt and the other Detachment 202 men; per Talbot he was among CIA guests at [[allen-dulles]]'s house in 1962-63. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 8:37-8:50)*

## Additional claims (JFK and the CIA)


- ASR normalizations here are my inference: "Institute La Ros" -> Institut Le Rosey, "Mgera"/"McGara" -> Gates W. McGarrah, "Xner" -> Exner.
- Elevated to Deputy Director of Plans on February 17, 1962 to replace [[richard-bissell]]; host says he "somehow completely skirted" the Bay of Pigs from his European perch, so to the Kennedys he looked like a fresh face, but his appointment let the "old boys" stay in control. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 17:12-17:59)*
- Background: born in Pennsylvania to Herman Helms (Alcoa executive); maternal grandfather Gates W. McGarrah was the first president of the [[bank-for-international-settlements]] (1930-33); per Prados the father cashed out before the 1929 crash; attended the Swiss elite school Le Rosey at the same time as the future Shah of Iran, whom he allegedly befriended. Host: this pedigree made his entry into the OSS/CIA "seamless." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 18:00-20:31)*
- Iran: CIA helped overthrow Mossadegh in 1953; in April 1973 Nixon named the outgoing DCI ambassador to Tehran (to December 1976); per Joseph Trento he helped secure weapons for the Shah from old colleagues [[ted-shackley]], Thomas Clines and Ed Wilson, whose networks "evolved into Iran Contra." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 20:31-21:41)*
- Career: UPI correspondent in Europe in the late 1930s; recruited into the OSS propaganda unit via UPI bureau chief Frederick Exner (ASR "Xner"; normalization is mine); coordinator of intelligence in Germany; allegedly bunked with [[allen-dulles]] in Paris in 1945; saw the Operation Rusty memos about absorbing [[reinhard-gehlen]]'s network and opposed the integration "largely on the grounds of security," yet the network became the BND. Stayed through the Central Intelligence Group, was a CIA founder, top agent at the [[office-of-policy-coordination]] under [[frank-wisner]], and ran the Eastern European branch (foreign branch M). *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 21:41-25:07, 28:42-28:49)*
- Host: originator and main overseer of MK Ultra and Operation Chaos (domestic spying on leftist groups and unions); as DCI (1966-73, appointed by [[lyndon-b-johnson]] replacing McCone) he ordered all MK Ultra and Chaos files destroyed and kept much from the [[church-committee]]. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 25:07-25:32, 27:13-28:12)*
- Quoting *The Fish Is Red*: "Mr. Cool, as in ICE"; he starved operations he disliked rather than lopping them off. Quoting John Seigenthaler (ASR "Seganler"; my normalization): "It was clear that McCone was out of the loop. Dick Helms was running the agency." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 26:30-27:00, 28:22-28:42)*
- Reunited as DDP with [[william-harvey]], whom Bissell had made head of [[zr-rifle]], and in early 1962 placed Harvey at the head of Task Force W; host says he would lean on Harvey and [[jm-wave-station]] to proceed "in defiance of the president." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 28:56-30:01)*
- Ordered the 1967 IG report on the assassination programs; host speculates it was a limited-hangout document that minimized his own role. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:04:07-1:05:43)*
