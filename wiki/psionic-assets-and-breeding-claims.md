The discussion in the [[matthew-brown-michels-interview-2026]] of alleged use of humans with psionic potential to lure craft, and the hypothesis that this is cover for selective breeding. Note the framing: the breeding hypothesis is speculation floated mainly by [[jesse-michels]], with [[matthew-brown]] engaging but saying he has no evidence and only "N of one, N of two" stories.

## Claims

- Michels recounts the [[jake-barber]] and [[michael-herrera]] stories (an Indonesian disaster zone, human cargo, "P3" people with psionic predisposition potential, called "pink assets" or "human meat", with EEG-linked operators luring craft that are then shot with directed energy). Brown says he has only heard these allegations and cannot support them, but that governments have provably pursued such capabilities for decades (see [[project-stargate]]) and he would "not be surprised"; he has no evidence that the legacy program traffics people and thinks private interests likely do. He notes Barber's book chapter had seven of about 20 pages redacted. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 2:43:34-2:47:12, 2:46:22-2:46:38)*
- Michels: the shootdown/bait story may be cover for a deeper agenda in which such people are bred over generations to concentrate the potential, "super psychic child" three or four generations down, possibly running in families (he cites Gary Nolan's basal-ganglia findings, Rh-negative blood). Brown adds that people he knows who interacted with "questionable groups" say the groups are more interested in females, which fits a breeding focus, and urges the community to look at "bad actors" exploiting empaths, saying it has been penetrated by intelligence services and organized crime. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 2:52:29-2:55:30, 2:53:44-2:53:59)*
- Brown says technologies might need a consciousness interface or be gated by genetics, which would be a reason to acquire and keep such people under control, and that this may explain why the [[jeffrey-epstein]] files show an interest in people with psi or empathic abilities, including experiencers and abductees. He speculates that unwitting civilian remote viewers are cheaper and more deniable than the Stargate model. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 2:47:24-2:48:36, 2:41:52-2:43:37)*
- Michels finds the burn rate ("sundowner" dissociation), the lack of repeatable success and a small number of craft implausible for a shootdown mission and doubts there are thousands of such craft. *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 2:49:39-2:52:29)*
- Brown says a reason for interest in humans may be human potential, psychic potential, technological trajectory (a subservient or proxy race, or less cooperative options) and possibly "riding along" via non-local consciousness or harvesting suffering (loosh; see [[robert-monroe]]). *([jm/matthew-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-29 - Pentagon Whistleblower UNLOADS on Alien Hybrid Breeding Programs (ft. Matthew Brown).md>) 2:55:46-2:57:44, 2:12:52-2:15:40, 2:50:05-2:51:14)*
