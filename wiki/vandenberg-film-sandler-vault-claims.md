Cameron says Emenegger told him a film of a UFO interacting with a dummy Atlas warhead at [[vandenberg-air-force-base]] (the 1964 incident linked to Bob Jacobs) was never returned and sits in [[alan-sandler]]'s vault in Oregon. Cameron says [[luis-elizondo]] and others have seen the video, though Michels says he does not know what to make of it.

## Claims

- Cameron says an analyst who worked for the Robertson panel viewed the film and called it one in a million. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:13:53-1:14:09)*
- Cameron says he and Angela Joiner arranged for her to visit Sandler alone, but she brought in [[james-fox]]; Sandler asked $25,000 for documentary use, then refused on legal advice. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:11:45-1:13:08)*
- Fox reportedly went to Congress, telling staff the film was a classified, government-owned item with the name Quintel on its cover, and expected a copy in exchange for helping move it. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:13:08-1:13:50)*
- Michels asks why Sandler does not release it; Cameron says he sees many people sitting on documents. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:14:17-1:14:40)*
