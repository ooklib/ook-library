Brown Brothers Harriman (ASR: "Brown Brothers Herman") is the Wall Street "white shoe" bank formed
in 1931 by the merger of W. A. Harriman and Co with Brown Brothers, portrayed by [[eyes-wide-open]]
as the home of the "Yankee" establishment: [[prescott-bush]], [[averell-harriman]], Roland
Harriman and [[robert-lovett]]. Names/dates below follow the host, who leans on Russ Baker's *Family
of Secrets* and Joseph Trento's *Prelude to Terror*.

## Claims

- W. A. Harriman and Co was the namesake bank of the Harriman railroad family; George Herbert Walker
  (father of Dorothy Walker, [[george-h-w-bush]]'s mother) became its president shortly after 1921,
  and hired his son-in-law [[prescott-bush]] in 1926. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 8:26-8:55)*
- It was a "meeting ground for future top Yankee bureaucrats": Prescott Bush, the Harriman brothers
  and future Truman Defense Secretary [[robert-lovett]] were all partners. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 9:28-9:50)*
- 1931: merged with Brown Brothers "after the great stock market crash." Host: it is "maybe most known
  today" as a US bank that dealt "illegally" with German industrial interests in WWII; per Trento,
  Bush managed the Fritz Thyssen account (see [[prescott-bush]]). *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 11:10-12:06)*
- Host: all partners were involved to some degree, "maybe most guilty" [[averell-harriman]], who
  then architected Lend-Lease; Democrats and Republicans at the bank aligned on "continued profits
  and class solidarity." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 13:08-15:12)*
- It controlled [[dresser-industries]] from 1928; [[neil-mallon]] was installed as its president.
  *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 21:27-22:10)*
- Its offices at 44 Wall Street allegedly also housed [[allen-dulles]]'s private postwar
  intelligence agency (Trento via host). *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 17:34-17:49)*
- Represented legally by [[sullivan-and-cromwell]], the Dulles brothers' firm; the host says they
  still work together. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 16:17-16:48)*
- Host's personal theory: Lovett, a Brown Brothers colleague from Texas, may have suggested that
  Prescott send his son to Odessa, Texas after Yale in 1948. Labeled as the host's own speculation.
  *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 10:29-11:01)*
