SRI International originally conducted the [[project-stargate|Project Stargate]] remote-viewing
research, sponsored at times by the NSA, CIA, DIA, and Army INSCOM, before the program moved to
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] in 1991.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:37:52–1:38:06)*

## Claims

- Following the Manhattan Project, [[edward-dahl|Dr. Edward Bushnell Dahl]] became chairman of
  Stanford University's physics department and a senior member of SRI. On 27 March 1953, SRI
  supplied key scientific assistance for the upcoming Operation Upshot–Knothole atomic tests, two
  months before the 1953 [[kingman-arizona-crash-1953|Kingman, Arizona]] crash, in which Dahl was
  separately named as a coordinator. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md)
  1:17:19–1:20:23)*
