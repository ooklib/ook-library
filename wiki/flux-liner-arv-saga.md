Alleged 12 November 1988 sighting by designer [[brad-sorenson]] of three man-made "flux liner" flying saucers (nicknamed Mama, Papa and Baby Bear) in a classified hangar exhibit at the Norton AFB air show. Relayed for two decades second-hand by illustrator [[mark-mccandlish]]; see [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b]] and [[air-force-plant-42]] for related material from earlier Gerb videos.

## Claims ([[uap-gerb]])

- Sorenson attended with an unnamed senior US government client, was passed off as the client's aide and shown a classified exhibit populated by senior DoD/IC/contractor figures (he recognized only ex-Senator Alan Cranston): the losing B-2 prototype, small VTOLs, a remote-piloted "pumpkin seed" craft called Aurora armed with 120+ nuclear warheads, and behind a partition three hovering saucers. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:26:29-1:27:54)*
- Sorenson found the finish sloppy (lead-paint-like); a three-star general showed video of a disc jittering and skipping over a dry lake bed before leaving frame; the officials alluded to 1947 and to retrofitting technology "taken from something else" for human operators; the three-star said the vehicles had been "all across our solar system". *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:27:54-1:28:49, 1:29:20-1:29:35)*
- Gerb says he found a purposely hidden 1990 Aviation Week & Space Technology interview with Sorenson that confirms McCandlish's account, turning it into first-hand testimony; Gerb believes the story is true though details may be "stretched" (perhaps marketing). He says Sorenson has issued death threats at him and notes the mysterious deaths of McCandlish and documentarian James Allen. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:28:49-1:29:53)*
- Gerb reads it as a benchmark that by 1988 the programs had emulated aspects of non-human craft ("poor man" recreations, not perfect emulation) and cannot explain why it was shown at an air show, speculating Sorenson was a program insider wanting transparency. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:29:53-1:30:46)*
- Context: [[cold-war-reorganization-1978-1994]]; Gerb elsewhere cites [[eric-davis]] as saying no successful ARV derivation has occurred and disagrees. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 2:12:06-2:12:27, 2:16:00-2:16:15)*
