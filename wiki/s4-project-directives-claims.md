In the closing segment of [[lazar-ramsey-followup-interview-2026]], [[bob-lazar]] restates his S4 job and Chris Ramsey revisits the three briefings covered in [[lazar-briefing-projects-trio]].

## Claims

- Lazar says he and [[barry-s4-lab-partner]] had two tasks on the power and propulsion system: directive one, duplicate it with existing materials; directive two, be able to disable it from a distance with existing materials, with no budget limits; he guesses only the second was achieved and hopes so: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 31:04-31:46)*
- He does not know whether Barry was briefed on the "blue documents" or discussed them: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 30:49-31:07)*
- Ramsey summarizes the three briefings as [[project-galileo-s4]] (gravity propulsion), [[project-sidekick-s4]] (weaponization) and [[project-looking-glass-s4]] (time dilation, peering into time): *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 31:57-32:32)*
- Lazar says he would stay where he was, because you cannot reach the other two without the gravity machine and its power source: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 32:48-33:26)*
- Asked whether time manipulation succeeded, Lazar says "you don't see it anywhere" and if so it is well covered: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 33:26-33:45)*. Ramsey relays second-hand accounts from people who spoke to alleged program witnesses of a sphere called Looking Glass that is operated consciously to peer into time, which he suggests could be the progression of the S4 work: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 33:45-34:21)*, compare [[chronovisor-claims]] and [[consciousness-core-secret-claims]]
- Lazar hopes for time viewing rather than "directed energy" weaponization, but says weaponization "is always going to be number one": *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 34:21-34:36)*
