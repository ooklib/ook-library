Cord Meyer replaced [[thomas-braden]] in 1954 as head of the [[cia-international-organizations-division]] and, per Eyes Wide Open, was the husband of Mary Pinchot Meyer, JFK's alleged mistress. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 27:16-27:36)*

## Claims

- Later remarked that European leaders soliciting US aid required no publicity, because Communist propaganda could otherwise depict them as puppets of American imperialism. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 27:55-28:23)*
- Wife Mary Pinchot Meyer allegedly had an affair with JFK and was mysteriously killed almost a year after the assassination; [[james-jesus-angleton]] allegedly helped cover up details of her murder (deferred to a later episode). *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 27:22-27:48)*

- Named as a guest of [[allen-dulles]] in 1962-63 and, in [[e-howard-hunt]]'s alleged deathbed confession, second in the hierarchy of a JFK conspiracy (host skeptical). *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 8:50-8:53, 10:13-10:17)*
