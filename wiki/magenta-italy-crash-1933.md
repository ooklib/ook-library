Cited by Gerb as an example of a prior alleged UFO crash retrieval before Roswell; he assesses
this and other pre-Roswell retrievals were most likely of unmanned craft, distinguishing them
from the more consequential [[roswell-incident-1947|Roswell]] event. *(1:01:02–1:01:11)*

## Claims

- Per the SAIC video, [[hans-kammler|Hans Kammler]] ("Hans Comler" in the ASR transcript),
  director of numerous Nazi special-weapons projects, may have been tasked with studying this
  crashed craft; he went missing near the end of WWII but was claimed to have worked with
  Americans afterward. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:10:46–1:11:00)*

## Claims (from the "Secrecy" Vol. 1 video)

- UAP Gerb again cites the Magenta case (before discussing Roswell) as evidence some US officials
  may have had earlier awareness of nonhuman craft, while still treating 1947 Roswell as the true
  origin point of organized, empowered US retrieval/exploitation efforts.
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 19:12–19:34)*
