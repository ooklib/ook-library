The "secret war" in [[laos]] is the nearly two-decade conflict that [[eyes-wide-open]] begins to chronicle in the Season 2 series LAOS 1-4. This page holds the overview claims from the introduction (LAOS 1); later episodes will add detail. The host dedicates the project to the people of Laos and to Vietnamese, Lao, Thai, Cambodian, Burmese and American victims of the Southeast Asian wars. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 0:00-0:44)*

## Claims

- Eisenhower's briefing: per Arthur Schlesinger, outgoing president [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] told the incoming Kennedy that Laos, not Vietnam, worried him, calling it "the key to Southeast Asia" and slipping into civil war; Kennedy was unconvinced, allegedly pronouncing it "Laos" like the insect, believing Americans wouldn't support a campaign in such a country. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 0:44-1:48)*
- Through the 1960s Laos went from moderate instability to a war-torn battlefield of many guerrilla armies. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 1:48-2:02)*
- Bombing: begun under Johnson and "massively expanded" under [[richard-nixon]]; per the Guardian, Laos is the most bombed country per square kilometre in history, on average one planeload of bombs every 8 minutes from 1964 to 1973. In 2021 there were 63 accidents from unexploded ordnance and clearance is expected to take up to 100 years. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 2:22-3:19)*
- Why it is rarely discussed: Americans were not the main ones dying (pilots and CIA officers died, but most bloodshed fell on locals), and serious research into French or US activity there "inevitably leads to the darkest and dirtiest dimensions of modern foreign policy". *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 3:19-3:55)*
- First CIA secret war: host says Laos was the first country where the CIA was tasked with a full-on secret war, and that from 1964 to 1971 the CIA was primarily in charge of the US campaign, developing and perfecting policies later replicated in other paramilitary campaigns; "the test run for decades of secret narco-fueled guerrilla wars", done covertly and without official Congressional approval. He flags parallels with the CIA campaign in Afghanistan. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 3:55-4:38)*
- Even before the CIA and before independence, the French colonial administration set up "some of the first narco counterinsurgency programs ever", later repurposed by the CIA "to much more effective and devastating effect" (previewing [[operation-x]] and the French Indochina War in LAOS 2). See [[narco-counterinsurgency]]. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 4:38-4:58, 24:38-24:57)*
- Framing: a place "where wars had no rules, where opium could buy armies and where the CIA became an agency of war". *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 5:00-5:12)*
