Gerhart von Schulze-Gävernitz was the OSS agent [[allen-dulles]] tasked with personally running
the field rescue of SS General [[karl-wolff]] under [[operation-sunrise]], to avoid the
appearance of insubordination himself.

## Claims

- Son of an illustrious European family that had funded Hitler's early rise before breaking away
  from the Nazis after the family was cut off by the party. Dulles had previously helped the
  Gävernitz family smuggle their money out of Germany during the war, a favor Dulles later cashed
  in by tasking Gävernitz with saving Wolff. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 15:08-15:44)*
- Organized the rescue team, accompanied by OSS agent Don Jones ("Scotty"), that extracted Wolff
  from his Lake Como estate on the night of the operation, safely returning to Chiasso, Switzerland
  by 2 a.m.; wrote about the mission "sentimentally" in his diary years later. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 15:44-17:11)*
- Later appeared at Wolff's 1948 Hamburg denazification trial and provided a personal character
  witness on Wolff's behalf, contributing to Wolff receiving only a four-year sentence.
  *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 20:19-20:32)*
