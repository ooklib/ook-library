[[grant-cameron]] argues that recovering crashed craft and bodies is not the core secret; the insiders' shared conclusion is that they do not understand how it works, and that mind, ESP and consciousness are the real subject. Michels agrees that hardware cannot be the deepest layer.

## Claims

- Cameron cites [[jimmy-carter]] and [[barack-obama]] as saying answers are uncertain, and [[jim-semivan]] as saying "bodies and crafts" do not explain anything and that he would bet a year of his pension they "haven't got a clue". *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:37:00-1:38:10)*
- Semivan allegedly took a 6 to 8 hour CIA briefing from contractors and military and was told "all the psi stuff is right", after which he walked around for three months disturbed. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:37:33-1:38:50)*
- Cameron says [[jim-lacatski]] told of breaching a craft in 2011 in a congressional facility with [[harry-reid]] and will not say what was inside; Cameron adds Jack Sarfatti's story (ASR "Sarati") of a Navy friend on the East Coast whose craft admits only those who vibrate correctly and opens like molten metal. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:38:50-1:40:54)*
- Cameron compares roughly 20 near-death accounts of living rock buildings that open like liquid metal and larger inside than outside; he tells the story of a Mormon experiencer, Ron Johnson, taken to a spirit-world building with crystal tables showing past lives. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:40:54-1:44:40)*
- Michels asks about mountain bases; Cameron says beings crossing time and space need no base and the phenomenon may be more etheric than physical. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:45:06-1:45:51)*
- Cameron cites [[ben-rich]]'s remark (as relayed by Jan Harzan (ASR "Hartson")) asking "what do you know about ESP" and Walker's identical question: [[eric-walker-esp-control-group-claims]]. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:47:13-1:47:44)*
- Cameron says he now thinks the mind and portal technology, not nuts-and-bolts hardware, is what the government has: [[portal-and-jump-room-claims]]. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:47:41-2:47:58)*
