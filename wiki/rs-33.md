Alleged top-secret Italian scientific/administrative group ("special research office of 1933") set up under [[benito-mussolini]] to examine the [[magenta-italy-crash-1933|Magenta]] craft and control information about UFO reports. Known only from documents given to [[roberto-pinotti]] in 1996.

## Claims

- Headed by Mussolini with foreign minister [[galeazzo-ciano]], air marshal [[italo-balbo]], astronomer Gino Cecchini (ASR "Gino saini"), and possibly [[guglielmo-marconi]]. *([gerb/magenta](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-18 - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash.md>) 2:34-3:05, 9:17-9:33)*
- An RS/33 stamp appears on the second and third Stefani telegrams; the protocol memorandum's cover note refers to "the RS 33 office", written personally by the sender without secretaries. *([gerb/magenta](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-18 - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash.md>) 7:01-8:18)*
- Nine-step protocol: notify the mayor; recover the aircraft; arrest witnesses via the OVRA (secret police); route reports to the central meteorological office at La Sapienza in Rome; suppress news; publish short articles attributing sightings to meteors, planets, halos; Air Force reports need RS/33 approval (decisions "unappealable" up to the Duce); exclude any other scientific body, including the pontifical university; charge expenses to the Reale Accademia d'Italia budget. *([gerb/magenta](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-18 - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash.md>) 10:35-12:28)*
- Gerb notes points 8 and 9 resemble USAF Regulation 200-2 (1953) and the overall structure resembles alleged [[majestic-12]], calling RS/33 "an Italian precursor" of the later US body. *([gerb/magenta](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-18 - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash.md>) 12:28-13:23)*
- The addressee "De Santi" is almost certainly a pseudonym for [[tomaso-david]]. *([gerb/magenta](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-18 - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash.md>) 8:18-8:41)*
- Proposed dissolution after the Nazis took control of the craft in 1943. *([gerb/magenta](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-18 - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash.md>) 13:40-13:47)*
- Gerb says the Ciano Diaries do not mention RS/33, which he finds understandable given secrecy. *([gerb/magenta](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-18 - The 1933 Magenta, Italy UFO Crash.md>) 9:42-9:58)*
