The Portella della Ginestra massacre ("1947 Mayday massacre"), per Eyes Wide Open, was a Mafia
attack on rallying leftist workers in Sicily, allegedly carried out by [[frank-coppola]] and
[[salvatore-giuliano]] and funded by [[william-donovan]] via the [[world-commerce-corporation]].

## Claims

- According to some sources, [[frank-coppola|Frank Kapola]] and Sicilian mobster
  [[salvatore-giuliano]] had been behind the 1947 Mayday massacre, where Mafia men opened fire on
  a crowd of rallying workers in Sicily; eight were killed and 33 wounded. Allegedly, the attack
  had been funded directly by former OSS agent [[william-donovan]] (by then a private citizen) via
  the [[world-commerce-corporation]]. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 26:01-26:31)*
- Set the tone for the "age of terror" in Italy: per Claire Sterling's *Octopus*, the Sicilian
  mob committed on average three murders a week between 1944 and 1960, and per Peter Dale Scott,
  another 498 people (mostly PCI/Italian Socialist Party members) were murdered in 1948 alone.
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 17:53-18:05, 26:31-26:46)*
