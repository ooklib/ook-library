USMC training center in the southern California desert, host of the [[hunter-warrior-advanced-warfighting-experiment-1997]] and the setting of [[castle-29-palms-triangle-encounter-1997]]. Gerb says he rejects the idea that the base itself hosts legacy-program work.

## Claims (gerb/castle)

- Gerb says he recalls Jeremy Corbell's 2023 video of a supposed triangle over 29 Palms, argued to be flares, but considers it not relevant; he disagrees with those who look for legacy-program operations at the base and instead reads the Hunter Warrior experiment area as a place for black programs to exercise. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 16:02-16:51)*
- The encounter site lies within a 7.5-mile radius of Emerson Dry Lake, northwest of 29 Palms and about 90 miles southeast of [[edwards-air-force-base]], in restricted airspace R-2501C, with the craft leaving northwest toward the [[r2508-restricted-airspace-complex]]. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 20:37-21:25, 1:26:39-1:26:54)*
