Itek ("itch" in the ASR; normalization my inference from the "Spy Capitalism: ITEK and the CIA" source listing) is the "intelligence technology firm" founded by Harper Woodward and Thaddeus Walkowicz with [[rockefeller-brothers-inc]] funding. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 23:44-23:53)*

## Claims

- Citing [[peter-dale-scott]], Itek developed technologies for the CIA including spy satellite components and cameras for the U-2 spy plane, "a key piece of the electronic intelligence boom." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 23:53-24:07)*
- The CIA spy plane and spy satellite programs, "the main customers of Itek hardware in the early days," were directed by [[richard-bissell]]; the host uses this to argue RBI was an intelligence front profiting from the intelligence complex. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 24:49-25:29)*
- Walkowicz later sat on Panel II of the [[special-studies-project]] and is described as co-founder of "the CIA affiliated technology firm that did spy satellite technology and cameras for the U-2 spy planes." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 29:11-29:37)*
- Wiki-internal cross-link (not stated in this transcript): the [[national-reconnaissance-office]] page (from gerb/nro) also names Bissell as an NRO co-director and the Corona satellite program; this transcript does not mention the NRO or Corona by name.
