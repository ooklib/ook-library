Operation Safe Haven was a Roosevelt-administration policy, per Eyes Wide Open, that sought to
confiscate Nazi assets from banks around the world, out of fear that hidden Nazi wealth could
fund a postwar German resurgence — a policy the [[allen-dulles]]/[[john-foster-dulles]] brothers
are alleged to have circumvented on behalf of their [[bank-for-international-settlements|BIS]]
-linked clients.

## Claims

- Implemented by the Roosevelt administration to confiscate Nazi assets held in banks worldwide,
  out of fear that the German elite could use such slush funds to "bide their time" and later
  regain control for the Reich. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 6:56-7:15)*
- Directly impacted the Dulles brothers, since two of their major German clients at
  [[sullivan-and-cromwell]] — [[ig-farben]] and Merck KGaA — had funds stored at
  [[bank-for-international-settlements|BIS]]. Allen and John Foster Dulles allegedly worked to
  keep those BIS funds with their Nazi clients until war's end, circumventing the policy via a
  shell-company network and destruction of paper records. Eyes Wide Open frames this as
  foreshadowing the brothers' later, more brazen circumvention of official policy as CIA director
  and Secretary of State under Eisenhower. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 7:15-8:44)*

## Relationships

- Implemented by [[franklin-d-roosevelt]]'s administration.
- Circumvented, per Eyes Wide Open's allegation, by [[allen-dulles]] and [[john-foster-dulles]]
  on behalf of their clients' funds at [[bank-for-international-settlements]].
