A 1951 project run by USAF Major [[dewey-fournet]], described by [[uap-gerb]] as an investigation of both aerial and undersea UAP undertaken with US Navy help while Fournet was a sitting [[project-blue-book]] member. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 1:05-1:31)*

## Claims

- It was "curiously" extra-governmental, hence exempt from FOIA requests; Gerb says he does not know whether that was directed by US government elements. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 1:31-1:47)*
- In 1953 it briefed the CIA, but its findings allegedly never reached Congress or the public. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 1:47-1:55)*
- Only three case files survive (cases 26, 27 and 28). Case 26: US Navy Lieutenant George P. Williams and a nine-man Fleet Logistics Air Wing crew saw a transmedium UFO leave the ocean between Keflavik, Iceland and Newfoundland, described as elliptical or cigar-shaped, at least 200 ft, with a red-orange glow, and approaching at a "terrifying closing rate". Case 27: a South African Airways Constellation crew saw a silvery disc in an extinct volcanic crater near the Kenya border, which shot straight up. Case 28 is not discussed. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 1:55-3:04)*
