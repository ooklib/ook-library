NASA mission specialist Bob Oechsler (rendered "Echler" in the ASR transcripts and in some earlier wiki pages) who in 1989 recorded a phone call with [[bobby-ray-inman]] in which Inman allegedly volunteered [[everett-heinman]] as the person to speak to about recovered UFOs becoming available for technological research, and was then put in touch with former Director of Naval Intelligence [[sumner-shapiro]], who reportedly said he had studied a recovered craft up close. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:30:57-1:33:44)*

Shapiro's account, per [[uap-gerb]]: teams disassembled craft with interlocking components (which had to be reassembled in exact sequence), packed them and shipped them to various laboratories - which Gerb compares with the [[aztec-new-mexico-crash-1948]]. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:33:17-1:33:52)*
