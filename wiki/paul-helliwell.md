Colonel Paul E. Helliwell served as chief of special intelligence for the
[[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]] in China during WWII. Per Eyes Wide Open, a figure "very
rarely mentioned in any history of the CIA," Helliwell is nonetheless credited by the host as the
architect of the CIA's alleged decades-long entanglement with global narcotics trafficking and
organized crime, first in wartime China/Burma and then, via "Operation X," in postwar New York.

## Claims

- While liaising with [[chiang-kai-shek]]'s KMT forces in China's opium-rich Yunnan province,
  observed the KMT funding its war against Mao's communists by selling opium, and proposed to
  [[william-donovan]], [[allen-dulles]], and [[james-jesus-angleton]] that the OSS facilitate the
  KMT's opium trade to guarantee continued anti-communist cash flow — "the beginning of the CIA's
  long, storied history in international narcotics smuggling." OSS leadership enthusiastically
  funded the plan, including with looted Nazi funds secured by Dulles and Donovan.
  *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 9:08-11:21)*
- Culminated this effort in the creation of [[civil-air-transport]], a nominally commercial
  Nationalist Chinese airline (built from [[claire-chennault]]'s "Flying Tigers" mercenary air
  force) that shipped weapons to KMT forces in Burma and returned with cargo holds of heroin —
  a small-scale model Eyes Wide Open says would be replicated globally for the rest of the CIA's
  history (Afghanistan/Turkey for opium, Latin America for cocaine). *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 11:21-13:20)*
- Eyes Wide Open frames Helliwell's plan as a deliberate funding workaround: with the OSS's
  peacetime future uncertain and [[harry-truman]] resistant to creating a CIA he feared would
  become "an American Gestapo," an off-the-books, narcotics-funded intelligence apparatus let
  Wild Bill Donovan and Dulles continue OSS-style operations without the formal consent of the US
  government — a rationale the host says persisted even after Congress created and funded the CIA
  in 1947, given oversight risk to a taxpayer-funded black budget. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 14:06-16:32)*
- After Truman's 1945 postwar budget allocated no funds for a new spy agency, hatched a second,
  more ambitious plan presented to Donovan, Angleton, and Dulles: code-named [[operation-x]], it
  would use the small, high-priced heroin market among Harlem jazz clubs and patrons in New York
  City as a "testing ground" for a permanent, unaccountable OSS/CIA narcotics funding pipeline,
  drawing on his China connections to international heroin smugglers. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 18:48-21:55)*
- Ran Operation X's financial machinery in partnership with [[meyer-lansky]] through a
  Miami-based shell company they created together, the [[general-development-corporation]].
  *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 21:34-21:44)*

- Per the Detachment 202 episode: officer of [[oss-detachment-202]], led its secret intelligence unit in Yunnan under the title chief of special intelligence in China; a 1980 Wall Street Journal quote from colleagues says he gathered information with opium shipments ("three sticky brown bars"). Postwar settled in Miami, worked for the CIA, founded a law firm assisting CIA operations and [[castle-bank-and-trust]], and set up shell companies laundering dirty funds into South Florida real estate -- the host's "CIA paymaster." Many researchers (unsubstantiated per the host) see him as a benefactor of the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]]. Described as "the most often overlooked CIA figure" and main character of the upcoming French Connection series; his fingerprints are seen in [[e-howard-hunt]], [[john-singlaub]], [[lucien-conein]] and [[mitchell-werbell]]. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 2:26-4:46, 12:14-12:22, 21:33-21:41)*

- Per Eyes Wide Open, introduced [[claire-chennault]] to [[frank-wisner]] (leading to CIA funding of [[civil-air-transport]]) and created [[sea-supply-corporation]] for [[operation-paper]]. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 15:48-16:31, 32:22-32:34)*
- Cited quote (1980 WSJ): he gathered wartime information with shipments of opium, "three sticky brown bars"; described as the series' main character and architect of the American side of the French Connection. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 10:39-11:34)*
- A Thai consulate was allegedly housed in his Miami law firm offices in 1953-54. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 45:20-45:53)*
- Named in LAOS 1 as a member of the 1945 [[deer-mission]] to [[ho-chi-minh]], described as 'the future American architect of the French Connection'. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 18:46-19:06)*

- LAOS 2 correction: the host retracts calling the China/Burma narco-smuggling operation "Operation X" (see [[operation-x]] for the flagged discrepancy with *ewa/mafia-allies*), saying it was likely an early iteration of [[operation-paper]]. He still names Helliwell, with his "shadow banking empire", as an example of the sophisticated CIA paymasters who would supersede the French black box. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 19:30-19:57, 27:11-27:18)*
