## Additional claims (jm/kissinger)

[[jesse-michels]] builds the case that Kissinger may have overseen the UFO legacy program. Michels repeatedly hedges: most links are circumstantial "breadcrumbs", and he says "solid links between Kissinger and UFO programs are rare". *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 49:11, 50:19)*

**Core allegation.** [[maria-wang]], widow of [[eric-henry-wang]] (Wright-Patterson Special Studies chief; his department moved to the [[sandia-national-laboratories]] complex at [[kirtland-air-force-base]] in 1956), told researcher [[william-steinman]] that her husband reported directly to Kissinger, that Kissinger oversaw much of his work on recovered craft and visited their home. Wang died in 1960, before Kissinger held office. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 3:51, 4:35, 4:43, 5:00, 5:22, 5:32, 6:14, 6:22)*

**CIC and Nazi-technology exposure (1945-47).** Drafted into the US Army and recruited into the [[counter-intelligence-corps]]; denazification work; at 22 in charge of the town of Bensheim; later assigned to Oberammergau (Bavarian Alps), where Messerschmitt's design branch had been relocated and [[wernher-von-braun]] and about 400 scientists were gathered under [[hans-kammler]]; Kissinger was one of the first officers to occupy the barracks in 1945. Michels frames this as placing him "at the crime scene" of [[operation-paperclip]] and the rumored Nazi anti-gravity work ([[victor-schauberger]], interrogated by CIC for nine months per [[nick-cook]]; CIC agent Neil Gershimer allegedly tricked him into signing his work to the US, IP ending up at [[brookhaven-national-laboratory]]). Whether he knew of exotic propulsion is called "speculative". *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 8:55, 9:10, 9:19, 9:44, 9:50, 9:58, 10:07, 10:24, 10:41, 11:12, 11:37, 11:44, 11:48, 12:16, 12:23, 13:03, 13:11, 13:20, 13:33)*

**Harvard, CFR, Rockefeller.** Continued CIC ties at [[harvard-university]]; correspondence with [[allen-dulles]]. Consulted for the [[psychological-strategy-board]] (1951, 1952, 1955) and directly for its head [[gordon-gray]]. Invited by [[mcgeorge-bundy]] to the [[council-on-foreign-relations]]; in 1955 directed a CFR study group on nuclear war, chaired by Gordon Dean and briefed by [[j-robert-oppenheimer]], producing *Nuclear Weapons and Foreign Policy*. Approached by [[nelson-rockefeller]] (Quantico panel; then director of the [[special-studies-project]], with [[edward-teller]]). Michels notes that CFR study-group alumni (Eisenhower, Dulles, [[richard-bissell]], George Kennan) overlap with alleged MJ-12 circles. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 13:42, 17:32, 17:46, 18:07, 18:21, 21:39, 21:46, 21:53, 24:30, 25:20, 27:24, 27:31, 27:40, 28:06, 28:42, 28:51, 28:59)*

**Sandia link.** A redacted Sandia history says [[don-carter]] invited Kissinger to a seminar at Kirtland in the early 1960s. Michels ties this to the [[bluegill-triple-prime]] debris sent to [[lawrence-preston-gise]]'s Albuquerque office. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 31:28, 31:50, 32:06, 32:15, 33:10, 33:17)*

**MJ-12 speculation (host).** Michels argues that even if individual [[majestic-documents]] are forged or disinformation, an oversight body is plausible; that Kissinger's CIC background, CFR group and Rockefeller ties intersect with MJ-12 roles; that [[detlev-bronk]] (Rockefeller Foundation president, named in MJ-12) supports a Rockefeller link; that MJ-12 may have evolved from a Truman-era committee into an Eisenhower-era structure with elite think tanks as its backbone and later outsourced to private contractors; and that some argue Rockefeller money and a "PI-40"-style group made Harvard a cover for MJ-12 advisory work. He stresses "there's no direct proof". *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 37:43, 38:04, 38:12, 38:21, 38:44, 39:00, 39:16, 39:23, 39:46, 39:54, 40:02, 40:19, 40:26, 40:35, 41:05, 41:12, 41:20, 41:27)*

**Kennedy and Nixon years.** Approached by Kennedy's team in 1958 on long-range problems and weapons; Michels suggests Kissinger may have viewed UFOs like tactical nuclear weapons, as a "stabilizing element requiring secrecy". Kennedy's June 1961 request to [[allen-dulles]] for an MJ-12 briefing (per a disputed leaked document whose date matches a Dulles-JFK meeting revealed in the 2022 JFK files) is discussed as context. Kissinger was sidelined in the 1961 Berlin crisis, then became [[richard-nixon]]'s national security adviser (1969-75) and Secretary of State (1973-77), with the host floating that he was brought in to oversee "a secret technological arms race" in UFO reverse engineering. Kissinger was linked to the 303 Committee (covert-action oversight; successor of the [[nsc-5412-committee]] and precursor of the 40 Committee), which some researchers say is interchangeable with MJ-12. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 42:15, 42:39, 43:03, 43:17, 43:25, 44:10, 45:11, 45:35, 45:52, 46:03, 46:12, 48:25, 48:38, 48:47, 49:11, 49:53)*

**Moon Dust telegram.** In January 1977 he sent the [[kissinger-moon-dust-telegram-1977]]. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 52:00)*

**Secrecy philosophy and legacy.** Quoted in 1968: "The only way secrecy can be kept is to exclude from the making of the decisions all those who are theoretically charged with carrying it out"; William Shawcross on his control of NSC processes.
