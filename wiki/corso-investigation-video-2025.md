UAP Gerb's roughly 90-minute video of 2025-01-03 analysing the claims of Lt. Col. [[philip-j-corso]] about the US Army's [[army-foreign-technology-division]] seeding technology of unknown origin (TUO) from crashed craft such as [[roswell-incident-1947]] into US industry. Gerb deliberately sets aside the bestseller [[the-day-after-roswell-book]] and studies only the original manuscript [[dawn-of-a-new-age-manuscript]] ("pure" Corso, without co-writer embellishment). It is part of the [[uap-gerb]] series on legacy programs and is the first video to treat the Army's R&D chain as the possible architecture of [[ufo-legacy-programs]]. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 0:00-4:49)*

- Intro, Corso's career, the 1998 affidavit ([[corso-affidavit-1998]]), the manuscript's introduction ([[corso-technology-seeding-claims]]), Corso's testimony and the Roswell file ([[roswell-file]]), the [[army-foreign-technology-division]], the [[ufo-working-group-1987]], criticism ([[corso-criticism-claims]]) and conclusion. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. ## Gerb's main takeaways

- The one thing to take away: the Army had an R&D program in the 1960s called the foreign technology division for which "there are no documents and no data"; Trudeau retired in 1962 and was followed by [[dwight-e-beach]] in command of Army R&D. Gerb has FOIA requests out. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:31:16-1:31:46, 1:26:40)*
- If true, it would be one of the first structured US legacy programs, using corporations ([[bell-labs]], Fortune 500 firms), universities and semi-private institutions ([[ffrdc]] and UARC types), a template that "could extend to today". *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:27:12-1:28:14)*
- Gerb cannot judge the technology-seeding claims (integrated circuits, metallurgy) but finds the Army R&D history (centralisation under [[arthur-trudeau]] in 1958, the [[army-research-office]]) accurate. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:25:03-1:25:49)*
- Gerb still finds the late-manuscript [[corso-white-sands-nhi-encounter-claims]] bewildering and "a sour taste". *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:24:05-1:24:36)*
- Cross-links Corso's structure to modern claims: [[david-grush]], [[eric-davis]] and [[wilson-davis-notes]], [[edgar-fuche]], [[chris-sharp]], and the [[ufo-working-group-1987]] intelligence links. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 9:39-10:39, 29:58-30:39, 1:05:44-1:07:39)*
