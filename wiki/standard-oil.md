Oil-refining company built by [[john-d-rockefeller]] into the dominant US monopoly, nicknamed "The Octopus" for its pipeline network. MagnatesMedia presents it as the archetype of predatory monopoly, ended by a federal antitrust suit in 1911 ([[standard-oil-dissolution-1911]]).

## Claims

- Rockefeller renamed the firm Standard Oil, built a second refinery, exported kerosene to Europe and brought in [[henry-flagler]], who saw business as war. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 12:34, 12:49, 13:10)*
- Secret rebates: Erie gave a 75% discount and New York Central a $1.65 rate against $2.40 official, after the Pennsylvania Railroad left Cleveland; rivals paid full price and were then squeezed in price wars; the narrator notes rebates were not yet illegal. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 14:48, 15:02, 15:46, 16:44)*
- The [[south-improvement-company]] scheme of 1872 collapsed within two months under protests and an oil-miners' boycott, but the fear it created let Standard buy 22 of 26 Cleveland refiners in six weeks (the "Cleveland Massacre"). *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 18:32, 20:29, 21:25)*
- Buyers were offered a non-compete contract or a job inside Standard; after the 1873 panic it bought refineries in Pittsburgh, Philadelphia and New York, hoarding barrels and machinery, booking railroads and pushing rebate-driven price rises to force sales. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 22:03, 23:54, 24:02, 24:09)*
- A pipeline network along the eastern seaboard earned it the nickname "The Octopus"; a leading newspaper called it "the most cruel, impudent, pitiless and grasping monopoly that ever fastened upon a country". *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 24:48, 25:02, 25:23)*
- When [[thomas-a-scott]]'s Pennsylvania Railroad built refineries and pipelines, Rockefeller cut kerosene prices and got other railroads to cut freight rates; the resulting wage cuts are said to have triggered one of America's most violent strikes (Pittsburgh losses: 500 freight cars, 120 locomotives, 27 buildings). Standard then signed a deal guaranteeing the Pennsylvania 47% of its traffic in return for a large secret rebate, competitor shipment data and 20 cents a barrel; it then controlled almost 90% of refining. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 26:07, 26:49, 27:10, 27:50, 28:04, 28:38)*
- Vertical integration after the 1885 Ohio find: it bought land, drilled, piped and retailed, and its chemists deodorized sulfurous "skunk oil"; the by-product gasoline became a profit source with the automobile. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 32:07, 33:04, 33:22, 33:49)*
- Convicted in 1911 of monopolization, predatory pricing, rebates, espionage, secret ownership of rivals and bribery of hundreds of politicians; broken into 34 companies: see [[standard-oil-dissolution-1911]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 42:47, 43:39, 43:48, 44:51)*

## Relationships

- Founded by [[john-d-rockefeller]] with [[henry-flagler]]; adversary of [[thomas-a-scott]]; attacked by [[ida-tarbell]] and [[theodore-roosevelt]]. See [[rockefeller-family]] and [[rockefeller-first-billionaire-documentary-2023]].

## From Brofessor Stein (nazi-brands)

- The video says Standard Oil, via a German subsidiary in which it held 50%, dominated Germany's fuel market by 1935, had close ties to [[ig-farben]] and tried to form a cartel (Standard over oil, Farben over chemicals), allegedly withheld synthetic rubber technology from the US, used forced labor including at a Stutthof sub camp, and was party to a blocked $24 million deal to transfer Hungarian plants to Nazi control; it is today Exxon Mobil. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 12:46-13:37)* See [[nazi-era-corporate-collaboration-claims]].
