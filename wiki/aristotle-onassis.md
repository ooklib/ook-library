Aristotle Onassis is the Greek shipping magnate whom [[eyes-wide-open]] (drawing on Jim Hougan's *Spooks*) says was targeted by [[richard-nixon]], the CIA and [[robert-maheu]]'s firm in 1954 over the [[jeddah-agreements]]. This episode records him only in that context.

## Claims

- Made his initial fortune in Argentina during WWII buying discounted ships; owner of the largest private yacht (the Christina O) and of the Monte Carlo casino, dubbed "king of Monaco" (which the host says surely irked Prince Rainier); second husband of Jackie Kennedy. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 13:57-14:37)*
- Suspected of Nazi sympathies during the war, having worked closely with the Perón government in Argentina; host says the collaborationist angle is "a whole can of worms" for a future video. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 18:37-19:02)*
- Profited massively from the 1956 Suez crisis after the Saudi deal fell through (Hougan: over 100 ships, tankers making a million dollars per departure from the Gulf). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 21:23-21:48)*

## Relationships

- Target of [[robert-maheu]] and [[richard-nixon]]; used [[hjalmar-schacht]] as intermediary.
