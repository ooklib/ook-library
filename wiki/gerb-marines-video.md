A short documentary-style video (about 17 minutes, dated 2024-01-18) in which [[uap-gerb]] takes a break from the iceberg series to cover two US Marine veterans who allegedly encountered craft abroad and were threatened by unmarked men. The source page is [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HnxylCl68Tg). Gerb says he sets these against the better-known Navy and Air Force pilot testimony (David Fravor, Ryan Graves). *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 0:00-0:35)*

## Claims

- Case one: [[jonathan-weygandt]]'s 1997 Peru account, told in [[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]]. Gerb finds it credible, citing body language and the DOE and nuclear-commando link. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 13:19-13:41)*
- Case two: [[michael-herrera]]'s 2009 Indonesia account, told in [[michael-herrera-indonesia-encounter-2009]]. Gerb is "torn" on it because of [[steven-greer]]'s emphasis on the alien reproduction vehicle theory. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 14:33-14:51)*
- Gerb argues that the Wilson-Davis memo, [[ross-coulthart]], [[david-grush]] and even [[bob-lazar]] all say the recovered technology is far beyond human comprehension, so he doubts humans have reverse engineered NHI craft, with the black-triangle [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b]] (and the [[belgian-ufo-wave-1989]] triangles) the most plausible human-made exception. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 14:51-16:10)*
- Gerb plans to try to contact Herrera (a Denver resident) and get more from Weygandt, and to return to iceberg levels three and four. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 12:42-13:05, 16:18-16:48)*
