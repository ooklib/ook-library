The Deer Mission was, per [[eyes-wide-open]], one of the first American intelligence missions into Vietnam, sent by the [[office-of-strategic-services]] in mid-1945 as the Allies took Burma. The video's bibliography cites two "Deer Mission" sources (a CIA reading-room document and a HistoryNet article) for this. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 18:33-18:46)*

## Claims

- Team named by the host: [[paul-helliwell]] ("future American architect of the French Connection"), [[lucien-conein]] ("counterinsurgency expert and future Ho Chi Minh Trail strategist" per the host; ASR "Lucien K"), and [[john-singlaub]] ("future MACV-SOG leader", ASR "makave so"), all OSS [[oss-detachment-202]] agents, who parachuted into rural Vietnam to deliver medical care to a malaria-stricken [[ho-chi-minh]]. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 18:46-19:06)*
- The host notes this may seem contrary to American interests but that 1945 differed from 1953: the Chinese Civil War was undecided and many believed the KMT could not lose. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 19:06-19:29)*
- Note: the Season 1 [[oss-detachment-202]] episode had Helliwell running the Yunnan intelligence unit; LAOS 1 puts him (with Conein and Singlaub) on this Vietnam mission. Not reconciled here; both are the host's claims.
