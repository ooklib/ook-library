The Tax Justice Network is a research organization cited by Green & Pleasant as the source of the
headline estimate for the scale of the offshore financial network the video attributes to the
[[city-of-london-corporation]] and its associated Crown Dependencies and Overseas Territories.

## Claims

- Together with a 2017 documentary, *The Spider's Web*, named the City/Crown-Dependency/
  Overseas-Territory arrangement "Britain's Second Empire" — the City as the spider, the
  territories as its web
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 13:39, 13:49)*.
- Its most recent aggregate estimate, cited by the video: the United Kingdom and these
  territories together account for close to a quarter of all corporate tax revenue lost
  worldwide. Green & Pleasant notes the organization's methodology "has its critics" but calls
  the resulting scale "hard to wave away"
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 13:57, 14:05, 14:14)*.

## Relationships

- Investigates and estimates the fiscal impact of the [[city-of-london-corporation]]'s offshore
  network.

## Speculation flag

The "close to a quarter of global corporate tax revenue lost" figure is the Tax Justice Network's
own aggregate estimate as relayed by Green & Pleasant; the video itself flags that the
methodology has critics, and this wiki records the figure as an attributed claim rather than an
independently verified fact.
