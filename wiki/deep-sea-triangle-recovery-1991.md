An alleged early-summer 1991 recovery of a triangular, charcoal-gray craft from about 1.5 miles down in the North Atlantic roughly 250 nautical miles from Aberdeen, Scotland, told by a retired Marine Corps lieutenant colonel who called Art Bell's Coast to Coast AM on 19 April 2002 and gave only the first name Mark. Gerb calls it "the most interesting part" of his Navy video and says he is still vetting it and trying to identify Mark, the naval lieutenant commander and Bell's original sketches. Unverified anecdote from a single anonymous caller.

## Claims (gerb/navy)

- Mark said he was assigned at Camp Pendleton and Okinawa, did weekend rescue diving with a Navy crew, befriended a Navy lieutenant who became commander of a DSRV unit, then was reassigned to a DSRV unit in Virginia doing deep-sea rescue and recovery, sent to rig downed aircraft and "any anomaly". *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 48:20-49:36)*
- A survey vessel picked up a radioactive signal, a second vessel with side scan found a vessel smaller than any known submarine, and Navy authorities feared a weapon or power source. The DSRV crew was steamed out from Virginia to a location roughly 250 miles from Aberdeen. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 57:26-58:58)*
- Gerb estimates the site as the Rockall Trough, west of Aberdeen, where depths exceed 8,000 ft. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 56:00-57:01)*
- On diving, the crew found something more like an aircraft than a submarine, a 60 to 70 ft charcoal-gray triangle, rounded at the corners and flat across, nose-down in mud with about two-thirds exposed, no windows, rivets, seams, bolts or cockpit, with geometric hieroglyphic-like markings in the skin whose characters repeated. Mark said "I don't believe it's Russian" and the lieutenant commander answered "if it is, it's not Russia right now". Mark and the lieutenant commander privately thought it extraterrestrial but did not say so over comms for fear of psychological evaluations and interception. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 58:58-1:02:00)*
- Gerb compares the hieroglyphic-like markings with the [[kecksburg-ufo-crash-1965]] bell and [[danny-sheehan]]'s Blue Book crash photos. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:03:10-1:03:42)*
- After 12 hours a marine archaeologist was taken down and sketched the craft. He judged from silt buildup that it had been in place at least 30 to 40 years. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:03:42-1:05:12)*
- On the third day they recovered it on the fourth dive, using thrusters to clear silt from the midsection and rigging it rear first, sizing it against an F-14. It ascended two and a half to three times faster than expected, implying it was extremely light. The underside was completely smooth with no piping or exhaust, and it measured 68 to 70 ft, 6 to 8 ft longer than an F-14. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:05:21-1:07:21)*
- Within four hours the crew was steamed off site with no debrief, threat or warning, although Mark had signed lengthy NDAs on joining the DSRV team. Asked if he believed it was alien, Mark said he could not say, only that it did not belong there and could not have been built 30 to 40 years earlier. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:07:39-1:09:05)*
- Gerb's analysis: the 30 to 40 year burial puts the crash between 1951 and 1961, which he links to the 1962 [[bluegill-triple-prime]] recovery and to triangular craft "very new and very rare" in the alleged 1954 [[special-operations-manual-som1-01]] (Majestic 12). Gerb usually attributes triangles to human prototypes such as the TR3B or [[xf-131-super-sentinel]], but here treats it as an anomaly. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:10:15-1:11:32, 1:41:06-1:42:10)*
- Gerb speculates this fell under [[operation-sand-dollar]], since Sand Dollar targeted radioactive or nuclear-emitting objects on the seafloor, and notes that the only other undersea retrieval he can think of is Shag Harbor in 1967. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:40:09-1:41:06)*
