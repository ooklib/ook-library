Secret discounts on freight rates ("rebates") and payments of a share of rivals' freight fees to the favored shipper ("drawbacks"), the core tactic MagnatesMedia says [[john-d-rockefeller]] used to build [[standard-oil]].

## Claims

- Rebates gave big shippers preferential treatment over small ones and were kept secret from competitors, who paid full price on the same trains; the narrator says they were not yet illegal. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 15:40, 15:46, 16:36, 16:44)*
- Rockefeller's rebates let him start price wars he knew rivals could not survive. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 15:55, 16:02, 16:16)*
- Under the [[south-improvement-company]] scheme drawbacks meant Standard collected 50% of the freight paid by rivals. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 18:53, 19:02)*
- [[theodore-roosevelt]] passed a bill punishing rebates before the wider suit ([[standard-oil-dissolution-1911]]). *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 40:46)*

## Relationships

- Practiced by [[standard-oil]] and [[john-d-rockefeller]].
