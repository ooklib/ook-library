[[jason-samosa]] argues in the `jm/cover-up` episode that the secrecy side, faced with prolific researchers (he names UAP Gerb, saying he pasted all Gerb transcripts into one document and Word broke at about a thousand pages), has cheap options beyond leak prevention: cultivate researchers as sources by feeding a few good pieces of information, then add unnecessary false information that spreads through the community, and turn active analysts into passive recipients. He also cites a CIA counterintelligence playbook about manufacturing infighting (from a video of his) and points to petty researcher feuds as the easiest way to destroy the community. He says Corey Goode/Gaia material discredits legitimate secret-space-program discussion. All of this is Samosa's speculation. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:52:37, 1:53:02, 1:54:06, 1:55:02, 1:57:04)*
