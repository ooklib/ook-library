US Navy biomedical research laboratory network unit with offices in Lima and [[iquitos-peru]], which [[uap-gerb]] speculates was the place [[jonathan-weygandt]] was detained and interrogated after the 1997 Peru retrieval ([[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]]). This is Gerb's hypothesis, not Weygandt's claim.

## Claims (gerb/weygandt)

- Gerb notes NAMRU laboratories worldwide (NAMRU San Antonio, NAMRU-3 in Sigonella, NAMRU Indo-Pacific in Singapore, NAMRU-South in Lima and Iquitos, NAMRU-D at Wright-Patterson) research infectious diseases and biodefense, are multinational environments, and that German officials have visited the Lima site. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:24:20-1:25:23)*
- He proposes it as the holding site because Weygandt recalled permanent structures, many nationalities (Americans, two Chinese and a fluent-English German in black fatigues with holstered pistols) and an underground interrogation room, and it would suit quarantine after contact with the craft's fluid. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 37:38-39:17, 1:23:22-1:25:46)*
- Gerb lists the problems with the theory himself: Weygandt was held only one to two days, received no medical procedures, the multinationals were armed operators rather than diplomats, and the site was underground. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:25:46-1:26:37, 1:54:10-1:54:26)*
- He also floats that craft materials or liquid may have been transferred to NAMRU-D at [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]] or that the craft went to the [[office-of-naval-research]]. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:52:40-1:53:04)*
