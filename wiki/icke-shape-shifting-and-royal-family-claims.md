[[david-icke]]'s claims, in [[icke-ramsey-interview-2026]], about how reptilian shape-shifting works, why he came to accept it, and his assertion that the British and Dutch royal families and other "bloodline" elites are human-reptilian hybrids. The stories are second-hand and anonymous, and the claims about named living or dead individuals are recorded only as attributed assertions from a conspiracy author with no corroboration offered.

## Claims

- In 1996 in the US, Icke says, he met 12 separate people within 15 days who described seeing a human shift into a reptilian form; he has since collected many such accounts and treats them as cross-referenced. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 17:04-17:37, 1:59:23-2:00:19, 2:10:22-2:10:33)*
- Mechanism: entities are hybrids with a human "information field" and a non-human field; observers decode whichever is dominant like Wi-Fi into a holographic picture, so a "shift" is a change of decoded field in the observer, triggered by high emotion or blood-ritual frenzy, and not a solid body changing. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:00:38-2:01:46, 2:02:44-2:03:05, 2:04:55-2:05:33, 35:14-36:01)*
- A woman he met after a Vancouver talk (a businesswoman, "visibly shaking") said that her boyfriend, angry that she kept his 1997 book *The Biggest Secret* by the bed, sprouted a tail during sex and briefly appeared reptilian before reverting. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:02:51-2:04:47)*
- He says he has met non-humans "a few times" (not detailed) and that major players in the global cult are hybrids; royal families are hybrids, which he argues is why royal bloodlines were seen as special with a "divine right to rule", and as royal rule faded the bloodlines moved into politics and the "dark suit professions" of finance, marrying for genetics. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:05:33-2:06:37, 2:08:02-2:08:51)*
- He names the British royal family (and gestures at the Dutch) as hybrids, saying people including participants in rituals saw royals shift in ritual, and that the Queen and Queen Mother in a "reptilian state" went "absolutely freaking wild" ripping flesh of a sacrifice in an adrenochrome stage; these are described as secondhand from unnamed informants. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 2:02:03-2:02:44, 2:06:22-2:06:46, 2:13:34-2:14:35)*
- Diana thread: after Diana's death he was invited to the House of Lords, met a woman who said a close friend had been Diana's best friend of nine years (an alternative healer at the Hope Clinic near Regent's Park).
