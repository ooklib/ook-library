[[jesse-michels]] says Cernan experienced bizarre flashes, distant blinking objects and blinding flashes of light in the dark while sleeping "like a train coming at you", and insisted "Something is out there", to which Houston replied "We don't doubt it" (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 4:44-5:19)*). Michels says [[nasa]] has been "gaslighting the public" since 1958, and that a NASA historian in Japan (Takanori Jouen in the ASR, a close friend of Cernan) told him something "mind-blowing" whose content is not audible in the transcript (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 4:25-6:26)*).
