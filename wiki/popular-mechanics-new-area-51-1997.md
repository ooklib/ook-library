A June 1997 *Popular Mechanics* article by Jim Wilson arguing the Air Force was moving secret testing out of Area 51 (overexposure) to Utah, citing a 1997 Estes/Goldin agreement to share "redundant assets" and a 1996 NASA statement that Michael Army Airfield would serve X-33 missions alongside the Utah launch complex (Green River, part of White Sands). Reporters were warned of being shot down when overflying and denied satellite imagery. Gerb calls the "shut down" framing false but notes a photo showing a note "call Admiral Inman at Science Applications International" ([[bobby-ray-inman]]). *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 17:36, 17:44, 18:07, 18:37, 19:26, 19:50, 20:05, 20:14, 21:12, 21:21, 21:28)*)*

Gerb ties it to other claims of "Area 51 to Area 52" moves: Edgar Fuche's source of a 1992 contingency plan relocating exotic platforms (to Utah, Colorado, Alaska, Greenland, Diego Garcia and S-4), Jane's writer James Goodall's 1990 report of black programs at Groom Lake, and "Ah" saying 35-40% of Area 51 moved to Utah. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 22:19, 22:27, 22:52, 23:33, 26:14)*)*
