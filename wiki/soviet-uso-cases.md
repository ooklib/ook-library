Gerb's selection of Soviet-era USO reports, drawn mainly from the case log of *Russia's USO Secrets* by Paul Stonehill and Philip Mantle (transcribed "manle"), plus a 2006 Russian documentary on USOs. He stresses many are just stories without direct access to Soviet declassified files, and says he only discusses ones he can track down additional reporting on. All claims are attributed. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 6:27-7:14)*

## Claims

- Gerb says Soviet UFO stigma has been as oppressive as the American one, and would like to cover it in its own video. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 6:27-6:43)*
- 7 October 1977, the submarine repair ship Vulga (transcribed "vulga"; captain "tanton", as transcribed) in the Barents Sea: radar detected an object about 60 miles away; nine bright discs arrived and circled the vessel for 18 minutes, with ship communications malfunctioning and returning to normal after they left (Gerb compares the [[tehran-ufo-incident-1976]] instrument failures). Fleet Admiral Nikolai Ivanovich Smirnov then issued a directive on mandatory UFO reporting by Soviet hydrographic, scientific research and reconnaissance ships, authorized by naval officer and ethologist Vladimir Azhazha and signed by Naval Deputy Chief of Staff P. (name garbled), analogous to [[janap-146c]] and OPNAV 3820. The case is also in [[jacques-vallee]]'s *UFO Chronicles of the Soviet Union: a Cosmic Samizdat* (pp. 29-30). *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 7:14-8:32)*
- August 1970, Kamchatka lake (transcribed "Kim chatka Lake"), per Soviet scientist Valentin Psalomshchikov (as spelled in the link list) via *Russia's USO Secrets*: hydrologists in a motorboat saw a dome of water burst from the lake about a kilometer away, from which rose a gray oval object 40-60 m long that climbed to about 100 m and hovered; the boat engine stalled until it accelerated away. Gerb notes the engine disruption parallels effects [[kevin-knuth]] discussed at the SOL Foundation. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 8:32-9:46)*
- 15 June 1977, Soviet ship (transcribed "nooget"; Gulf of "Gil Ecuador", as transcribed) at night: a white luminescent sphere rose from the water, circled the ship, hovered 20 m above, zigzagged higher and dived back, relayed from the writings of ethologist Felix Ziegel. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 9:46-10:19)*
- A clip from a 2006 Russian documentary shows a submarine captain discussing the phenomenon; Gerb recommends the whole 45-minute film as the best exploration of the Soviet side. *([gerb/uso-casebook](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-26 - USO Case Book_ Unidentified Submerged Objects Throughout History.md>) 10:19-10:43,)*
