Nuclear physicist turned UFO researcher. In 1983 he learned of Smith's 1950 memo and tracked down [[robert-sarbacher]]; he is also credited by Gerb with making the MJ-12 documents public. *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 11:22-11:44, 15:37-15:44)*

## Claims

- Recorded interview: Sarbacher described a meeting at Wright Field about a crash, where "one of the fellows" said the beings, if people, were made like insects with no skin, allowing extreme acceleration; Friedman asked if the crash was 1947 (so Oppenheimer would have been involved) and Sarbacher replied "very likely". Sarbacher named an unliked, smug attendee from Philadelphia, later identified as [[eric-a-walker]]. *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 11:44-13:10)*
- The R&D Board was fascinated by UAP flight characteristics; Sarbacher assumed gravity manipulation explained them. Gerb wonders whether this relates to the 1951 Lubbock Lights. *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 13:10-13:52)*
