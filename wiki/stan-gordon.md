Stan Gordon is the Pennsylvania UFO researcher whom [[uap-gerb]] calls the most seasoned Kecksburg investigator. Gerb builds his Kecksburg video primarily on Gordon's research and that of [[leonard-stringfield]]. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:11-1:26)*

## Claims

- Gordon interviewed [[jim-romansky]] from 1987, was taken by him to the crash site, and reports that witnesses fitting [[eric-a-walker]]'s description were mentioned at the site. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 13:52-14:09)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 21:11-21:26)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 40:11-40:18)*
- He obtained FOIA documents from US Space Command and the Naval Surveillance Center saying Cosmos 96 entered the atmosphere at about 3:18 a.m. EST over Canada on 9 December 1965, which he takes to rule out Cosmos 96 as the Kecksburg object. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 28:46-29:04)*
- He received notarized statements from WHJB staff about tape confiscation ([[john-murphy-whjb]]). *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 12:36-12:45)*
- In his 2003 crash retrieval conference paper he called the two-bodies rumor unsupported hearsay, but then recorded the 1998 body claim by [[myron-brick-delivery-witness]] and a 2002 witness "Joel" ([[kecksburg-bodies-claims]]). *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:00:56-1:01:30)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:04:14-1:05:24)*
- He made documentaries including "Kecksburg: The Untold Story" (1998, per the description) and a 1993 film; the Unsolved Mysteries episode and "The New Roswell" also cover the case. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:07:29-1:07:37)*
