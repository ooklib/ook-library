Decision Science Applications, Inc. (DSAI), founded in Virginia on 4 January 1977, is described
in its own materials as an employee-owned "technical services company with expertise in
military command and control systems, commercial information management systems, software
development, electrical engineering, telecommunications, and physics."
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 11:17–11:45)*

Gerb states there is no concrete evidence DSAI was spawned from
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]], "though it is likely."
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 15:07–15:22)*

## Claims

- Per whistleblower [[colonel-steve-wilson|Colonel Steve Wilson]] (speaking to Dr. Richard
  Boland), DSAI and SAIC "were one and the same," with DSAI's board including directors of
  relevant "UFO black budget divisions" from IBM, Lockheed, Teledyne Ryan, Aerojet Electro
  Systems, etc.; Wilson also claimed [[bobby-ray-inman|Bobby Ray Inman]] ran DSAI.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 11:45–12:13)*
- Named on Wilson's "map of Star Wars City" as a contractor paramount to alleged
  [[strategic-defense-initiative|SDI]]-embedded UFO programs. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  13:52–15:07)*
- Corporate lineage (researched via a now-deleted Reddit account, "Still Chill Trill"): DSAI
  merged into DSA Acquisitions, Inc. in 1998; purchased by Steven Meyers and Associates for
  ~$31M (finalized 21 Aug 1998); Steven Meyers and Associates then acquired Emergent Information
  Technologies and took its name; on 6 December 2001, Emergent sold its Virginia government
  services group to L3 Communications; in July 2012 that unit spun off as
  [[agility|Agility]]; in 2018, [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] bought
  Agility for $2.5B — completing an indirect link from DSAI's assets to SAIC and
  [[l3harris|L3Harris]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 15:31–16:52)*

## Relationships

- Disputed spinoff of [[science-applications-international-corporation]]; its corporate
  descendants (via Agility) were reacquired by SAIC in 2018.
