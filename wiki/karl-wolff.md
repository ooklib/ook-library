SS General Karl Wolff was Heinrich Himmler's chief of staff and the officer in charge of the
Nazi occupation of northern Italy, whose rescue from Italian partisans by [[allen-dulles]]'s
network became the central episode of [[operation-sunrise]].

## Claims

- Himmler's chief of staff and a high-ranking Nazi war criminal, charged with the occupation of
  northern Italy after the 1943 collapse of the Italian fascist government; hated by Italian
  resistance fighters for revenge killings of civilians and torture of partisans. Reportedly had
  a close personal relationship with Hitler, who paraded him as an "ideal Aryan specimen" at
  Nazi functions. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 13:43-14:37)*
- On April 26, 1945, surrounded by Italian partisans at an Alpine estate on Lake Como; fit
  Dulles's vision of a separate peace to block Soviet-aligned partisans from reaching the Eastern
  Front, and was extracted by an OSS-organized rescue convoy under [[operation-sunrise]].
  *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 13:40-17:04)*
- After being rearrested by American tanks and Italian partisans in May 1945, received special
  treatment in custody due to letters from Dulles recommending him as a "moderate gentleman,"
  including better food, permission to wear his SS uniform and sidearm, and eventually a POW camp
  with a lakeside resort and spa in Gmunden, Austria, where he was reunited with his family and
  personally delivered his yacht. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 17:33-18:31)*
- Dulles destroyed OSS documents labeling Wolff a mass murderer complicit in the Holocaust, and
  Chief Prosecutor Robert Jackson at Nuremberg instead received a falsified report calling him a
  "moderate Nazi" and "gentleman." Wolff testified at Nuremberg only as a witness against
  [[hermann-goring]], not as a defendant. When his name resurfaced for prosecution, Dulles forged
  a psychiatric assessment diagnosing him with psychosis to have him committed to a mental
  facility instead. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 18:42-19:31)*
- Allegedly blackmailed Dulles over his insubordination in running Operation Sunrise, including
  sending a letter to [[harry-truman]] outlining details of the operation. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 19:37-19:59)*
- In 1948, transferred to Hamburg for a "denazification" trial rather than a war-crimes trial;
  the judge read a glowing affidavit personally written by Dulles, and Gävernitz testified as a
  character witness. Sentenced to only four years, later reduced to time served; a free man in
  Europe by June 1949. In the 1950s formed a neo-fascist party ("Rexon Ferret," per the host's
  pronunciation) and, in 1956, a men's club for former SS officers, while working as an
  advertising executive in postwar West Germany. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 19:59-21:36)*
- Framed alongside [[reinhard-gehlen]] and [[junio-valerio-borghese]] as one of three men who
  would go on to recruit the future "Gladiators" of [[operation-gladio]]. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 26:13-26:27)*
