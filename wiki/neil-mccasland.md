General William Neil McCasland is described in the `jm/cover-up` episode as a general who ran the Air Force Research Laboratory at Wright-Patterson, where Roswell wreckage was rumored to have been taken, and as a key adviser of [[tom-delonge]]. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 33:42, 38:14)*

## Claims

- After the first phone call with DeLonge he reportedly warned that DeLonge might find people stumbling around an elephant like the blind men. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 32:49)*
- Samosa lists him at a meeting with Michael Kerry, Robert Vice (Skunk Works president) and [[john-podesta]], per the Podesta emails. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 40:06)*
- Michels says McCasland is now missing, having vanished from his New Mexico home days after [[donald-trump]] announced UFO disclosure, leaving his phone and tracking devices but taking a gun; he calls him one of the few sources of modern disclosure. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:16:25, 1:17:07)*
- [[sekret-machines-project]] was reportedly inspired by conversations with McCasland; an adviser (McCasland or Hayden, unclear) reportedly told the authors that genocides may be a function of the phenomenon. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:16:25, 2:10:49)*
