A reported May 1996 incident near Boyle, Ireland, in which locals allegedly saw a meteor or craft misjudge its approach, shear trees and land in a lake. [[uap-gerb]] says he "may have found" it as the match for a UK-based retrieval described by [[special-forces-witness-infographics]] (medium confidence); the witness never names Boyle.

## Claims (gerb/special-forces)

- Gerb: locals saw a meteor or aerial craft shear trees and land in a lake; several occupants were allegedly taken into custody by a retrieval team; significant military activity, including American soldiers, continued for six months and local police were told to stay away. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 31:29-32:16)*
- The witness (as relayed by Gerb) says Europe has a default agreement that recovered materials go to the US, "the price you pay for having a superpower watch your back". At the UK crash two of five crew survived with light bruising; the beings stood around aimlessly, the door opened on its own, and they were let out of the craft like school children. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 32:08-33:17)*
- Gerb also frames Europe as a place the witness says stays "always ready" for conflict, which attracts the objects. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 43:08-43:26)*
