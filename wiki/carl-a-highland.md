Hamburg-born geophysicist (doctorate 1923, came to the US in 1935, taught at the Colorado School of Mines) who owned the Highland Research and Exploration Company in Denver. [[william-steinman]] names him a real "Mr. G", a [[majestic-12]] member and a firsthand participant in the Aztec recovery. He is Steinman's most independently corroborated name, resurfacing in Colorado court records as a hostile witness against Newton and Gebauer. Every claim below is attributed to Steinman or [[uap-gerb]]'s reading of him.

## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Steinman suggests [[leo-gebauer]] and [[silas-newton]] were a targeted leak from Highland; Highland was involved in early magnetometer use for geophysical exploration and submarine detection, and Steinman claims he and Gebauer lived and worked together in Antarctica in 1935, which Gerb says lacks exhibited evidence. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 43:06-44:00, 1:06:52-1:09:27)*
- Some suggest Highland was the original scientist X speaker at the March 1950 University of Denver lecture until Newton took his place, and researcher Richard Ogden reportedly deduced Highland as Dr. G in a 1960s manuscript. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 44:08-44:35)*
- Per Steinman's account, MJ12 learned of the leak, forced him out of the lecture, briefed him on the coming trial and asked him to testify against Gebauer's devices; Highland said he could not with a clear conscience, and Dr. James Meredith of the Colorado School of Mines geophysics department (a co-worker) testified instead. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:09:27-1:10:29)*
- The Ramsays place Highland in the American Southwest at the time of Aztec, which Gerb notes conflicts with the USAF airman "George" being unaware of him on site. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:37:50-1:38:07)*
