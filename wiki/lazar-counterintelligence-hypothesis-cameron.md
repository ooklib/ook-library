Cameron argues Bob Lazar did work on real non-human craft but that a superior who wanted the material out placed him deliberately, expecting him to be discredited and become a "plausible liability". He says [[john-lear]], seen as a wild card, was the intended messenger, and that the plan backfired when George Knapp's broadcast made it viral.

## Claims

- Lazar phoned [[edward-teller]] in November, was on the base by 6 December with a Q clearance, which Cameron says cannot be issued in three weeks. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:21:12-1:21:37)*
- Cameron says Lazar told Lear "it's real" and was told to return in six months; Lazar received around 125 documents including a story placing the Dulce shootout at Area 51 instead, which Cameron reads as a two-story tactic. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:21:44-1:23:04)*
- Cameron relies on Tim Good for the claim that Lazar visited the site only five or six times and that his 1988 W-2 shows roughly $900, so Lazar took Lear and others to see the test flights after being cut off. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:24:05-1:24:47)*
- Lear filmed a test on 22 March 1989 at about 8 miles from Groom Lake; afterward Lazar was allegedly held at gunpoint near Indian Springs but not fired, and later refused an invitation to return. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:25:08-1:26:34, 1:29:34-1:30:13)*
- Cameron says the Knapp interview happened by accident when another guest cancelled, and that the land grab after Lazar's story pushed the public farther from the base. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:30:47-1:31:48)*
- Cameron says he cannot explain Lazar's walk under a craft, suggesting electrostatics or a staged demonstration. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:50:31-2:50:44)*
