[[jonathan-weygandt]], a Marine Lance Corporal, says he came upon a metallic egg-shaped craft embedded in a cliff face near a downed-aircraft site in Peru in 1997 and was arrested by unmarked men in black fatigues and DOE rain jackets. The account is Weygandt's as retold by [[uap-gerb]]; the interview clips are from Weygandt's 2001 [[disclosure-project]] interview with [[steven-greer]]. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 7:51-8:14)* The source calls the airbase "pukala" (ASR, probably Pucallpa) and the name "Weant/Wan/wigant" (ASR of Weygandt).

## Claims

- In February 1997 Weygandt was transferred to the airbase for Operation Laser Strike, a US SOUTHCOM counter-narcotics mission involving up to 20 agencies (USMC, CIA, USAF), as a Stinger Avenger gunner on perimeter security with the Marine MAG-28 team under Master Sergeant Chris Diggins and Major Bob Cole. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 1:16-1:54)*
- He recalled USAF personnel discussing tracking of UFOs with strange characteristics; Gerb notes the base had TPS-43 radar, a relocatable over-the-horizon radar and CIA Cessna Citation spotter aircraft. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 1:54-2:25)*
- On a late-night guard detail in March or April he, Staff Sergeant Montra, Sergeant Allen and Sergeant Atkins were ordered to secure the crash site of a possibly friendly downed aircraft (Gerb shows an A-37 shootdown on 25 March that sent a target into a fireball). They found a huge gash in the land where something had landed. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 2:25-3:12)*
- He, Atkins and Allen went ahead while other Marines stayed at the aircraft site. Weygandt saw an egg-shaped craft with its narrow end jutting out of a cliff face, metallic but with a constantly changing liquid-like surface (gasoline dripping into water), large vents like fish gills, a dull humming that instilled fear, and a large gash in its side that he guessed came from a Hawk missile. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 3:12-4:22)*
- As Atkins and Allen ordered him away, men in black fatigues, hazmat suits and DOE rain jackets came from two landed US Army CH-47 helicopters; he was arrested, stripped of gear, cable-tied, taken aboard a CH-47 and interrogated and verbally attacked for 15 hours. The men had no name tags and were about 30s to 40s; a lieutenant colonel made him sign unread paperwork, he was held with USAF personnel for three weeks and then sent back to the States. Some jackets read "DOE". *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 4:22-5:33)*
- Weygandt went quiet until 2023 and never sought profit. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 7:51-8:14)*

## Gerb's analysis

- Gerb's guess is that the men were the DOE Special Response Team ("nuclear commandos"), which has arrest authority to protect DOE labs and assets and in 1997 was staffed by contractors like [[eg-and-g]]; the SRT is part of NEST ([[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team]]), composed of special forces, scientists, technicians and engineers plus the US Army 52nd EOD, with technical information protected like special access programs, intelligence from the [[national-reconnaissance-office]] and CIA, and DOE contractors including EG&G, Raytheon and [[lockheed-martin]]. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 5:40-7:01)*
- He notes the DOE and the Nuclear Regulatory Commission were formerly the [[atomic-energy-commission]], which he says is named in the 2024 UAP NDAA legislation from Chuck Schumer as the reason UAP records were misclassified as "transclassified foreign nuclear material" and exempted from disclosure; this gives Weygandt's account credibility in Gerb's opinion. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 7:01-7:51)*
- He compares the egg shape with [[eric-taber]]'s egg-shaped craft at [[groom-lake]] (smooth, no rivets, no visible entry points) and with the humming fear or calm effects in other cases. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 8:14-8:39, 14:00-14:23, 15:30-15:48)*
- Gerb believes the story, judging it credible from Weygandt's body language and thinking the men were the NEST program and DOE-affiliated SAPs uncovering a crash retrieval. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 13:19-14:08)*
