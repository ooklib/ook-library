John Murphy was the news director of radio station WHJB in Greensburg, Pennsylvania, who covered the Kecksburg event. All details below are attributed claims, mainly from [[stan-gordon]]'s research as relayed by [[uap-gerb]].

## Claims

- A mother called the station saying she and her children saw a fiery object fall into the woods with blue smoke rising, which Murphy took as sincere and the first physical evidence. He went to the woods, where a state police fire marshal told him "you better get your information from the Army", then saw Army and Air Force men at the state police barracks. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 8:57-10:51)*
- He produced a radio documentary "Object in the Woods". Gerb says his wife and other employees said men approached him at the station and the program was heavily altered, opening with a disclaimer that no agency had contacted the station and that some witnesses feared reprisals from the Army and withdrew their segments. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 10:59-12:36)*
- Gordon says he received notarized statements from station employees that US government elements confiscated news tapes of eyewitness accounts. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 12:36-12:45)*
- Murphy reportedly died in a hit-and-run shortly after and never publicly said he entered the woods, but in 2003 his wife said he told her he had seen a strange metallic object on the ground. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 12:45-13:12)*
