According to [[philip-j-corso]], in 1960-61 Lt. Gen. [[arthur-trudeau]] handed him a file box ("a three drawer file") inherited with the [[army-foreign-technology-division]], asking him to study it and propose a program. Corso says it held a bundle of multicolored wires (fiber optics), an integrated-circuit chip, a laser-like cutting instrument, a paper-thin high-strength metal, field reports and Walter Reed autopsy reports that matched what he saw at Fort Riley ([[corso-fort-riley-ebe-sighting-1947]]). Trudeau told him to "give me a program". *(Unverified claim.)* *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 47:14-49:08, 49:56-50:21)*

## Claims

- Autopsy reports and photographs showed decomposed bodies about 4 ft tall, large heart and lungs, skin and bone built to protect vital organs from cosmic radiation or gravity, and a transparent film over the eye that led Trudeau to push passive night vision ([[fort-belvoir-night-vision-laboratory]]). *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 50:03-50:50)*
- The files mentioned no food, water, refrigeration, medical, toilet or recreation facilities; Corso and OCRD concluded the beings were cloned or expendable, and that the craft was a reconnaissance vessel returning to a mothership ([[ebe-extraterrestrial-biological-entity-claims]]). *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 50:50-51:47)*
- Corso hints at multiple crashes but gives no count beyond Roswell and a July 1947 San Augustine, New Mexico crash of a craft with no seams or rivets. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 51:47-52:10)*
