Richard Nixon appears in this episode as a junior California congressman who built his
anti-communist brand via [[house-un-american-activities-committee|HUAC]] and the
[[alger-hiss]] case, and whose alleged 1947 alliance with [[allen-dulles]] the host frames as the
beginning of a partnership between the Eastern "Yankee" establishment and the rising western
"Cowboy" Republicans (per Carl Oglesby's framework — see [[yankee-cowboy-thesis]]).

## Claims

- A junior congressman from California in the late 1940s and outsider to elitist Washington
  circles, born in relative poverty in rural California; unseated five-term New Dealer Jerry
  Voorhis to win his House seat. Built his political brand as an ardent anti-communist
  [[house-un-american-activities-committee|HUAC]] prosecutor. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 9:11-9:42)*
- Per David Talbot, met [[allen-dulles]] in 1947 aboard the Queen Mary on a European fact-finding
  mission; Dulles allegedly convinced Nixon to support the [[marshall-plan]] and began marrying
  the East Coast Republican establishment with the emerging western conservative movement.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 9:48-10:22)*
- Per former Justice Department Nazi-hunter John Loftus (allegations the host stresses are
  unverifiable but "not entirely unfounded" given the series' prior Nazi-funds coverage), Nixon
  allegedly found documents in 1945, while a naval officer, indicating [[allen-dulles]] and
  [[john-foster-dulles]] had laundered Nazi funds during the war — and allegedly struck a deal to
  stay silent in exchange for Dulles secretly funding his 1946 congressional campaign against Jerry
  Voorhis. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 10:22-11:19)*
- The host frames the Dulles-Nixon alliance through Carl Oglesby's "Yankee and Cowboy" framework
  (see [[yankee-cowboy-thesis]]) — Nixon exemplifying the expansionist "Cowboy" camp (alongside
  Lyndon Johnson and Ronald Reagan) forging a short-lived peace with the internationalist "Yankee"
  establishment (the Dulles brothers) throughout the 1950s. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 11:39-14:26)*
- Following testimony by Time editor [[whittaker-chambers]] alleging a Soviet courier ring
  involving [[alger-hiss]], Nixon prosecuted Hiss before HUAC; per the host's "pet theory," the
  case may have been staged partly to give Nixon legitimacy as a rising anti-communist star while
  Nixon in turn agreed with [[john-foster-dulles]] not to expose Dulles's own prior sponsorship of
  Hiss. Hiss was convicted only of perjury. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 15:35-19:51, 21:04-22:15)*
- Named as an alleged [[le-cercle]] member after his time in office. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 36:22-36:37)*

- Per the Detachment 202 episode, [[e-howard-hunt]] served as one of his "plumbers" ([[watergate-scandal]]), and Hunt recommended [[lucien-conein]] to him as White House narcotics advisor. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 9:10-9:42, 16:47-16:55)*
- LAOS 1: the bombing of [[laos]] that began under Johnson was 'massively expanded' under Nixon; see [[secret-war-in-laos]]. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 2:26-2:33)*

- War Conspiracy 1: recruited by the [[china-lobby]]; in the 1950 California Senate race he echoed its line (citing Stephen Ambrose, vol. 1) that "communism requires a worldwide resistance," won, and as Eisenhower's vice president was, at least rhetorically, an advocate of rollback and "the archetypal cold warrior." The host says the China Lobby elevated him and [[lyndon-b-johnson]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 32:05-33:03, 33:49-34:06)*
