Edward Browder Jr. (ASR: "Bowder"; the FBI document is quoted as "Edwin Browder Jr." at one point, flagged as the host's own variance) is a pilot named by [[eyes-wide-open]] in `ewa/dallas-4` as part of the mob-run 1959 gun-smuggling network to Cuba.

## Claims

- A December 1, 1963 FBI memo in the final Warren Commission volume names him as an associate in the [[jack-ruby]]/[[lewis-mcwillie]] gun operation; author Daniel Hopsicker (*Barry and the Boys*) identifies him as one of its major pilots. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 18:49-19:19)*
- A 1955 FBI file places "Edwin Browder Jr." as pilot and gun runner for the CIA's 1954 Guatemalan coup ([[operation-pbsuccess]]) and for an attempted coup against Costa Rican President Jose Figueres the next year; caught in Mexico City with illegal weapons and briefly imprisoned. Hopsicker confirmed via an interview with Browder's son that this is the same man. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 19:19-20:14)*
- Host: with [[barry-seal]] and [[david-ferrie]], one of three CIA-affiliated pilots rubbing shoulders with the mob gun networks, hinting at an earlier CIA-mafia alliance on the Gulf Coast than the Castro assassination plots. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 20:14-21:34)*
