The Draft Eisenhower movement swayed [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] to run as a Republican in 1952.
[[eyes-wide-open]] treats it as the origin of the "future interventionist Republican party."

## Claims

- Yankee Republicans including [[prescott-bush]], the Dulles brothers ([[allen-dulles]],
  [[john-foster-dulles]]) and the Rockefellers ([[rockefeller-family]]) were instrumental. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 23:33-23:51)*
- Host's lineage: these forces "paved the way" for the interventionist Republican party, first under
  [[richard-nixon]], then the neocons "birthed in the Nixon White House," given power under Gerald
  Ford and "let loose under the two Bush presidencies." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 23:51-24:21)*
- Rewards: John Foster Dulles as Secretary of State, Allen Dulles as CIA director (1953),
  [[nelson-rockefeller]] as special adviser, [[robert-lovett]] on the
  [[presidents-intelligence-advisory-board]]. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 28:36-29:13)*
