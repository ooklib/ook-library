FINAiUS video of 2023-11-18 (about 39 minutes), a solo-produced finance-history documentary tracing the [[rothschild-family]] from an 18th-century Frankfurt ghetto to the present, with the [[house-of-morgan]] as the rising American rival. Unlike most of the wiki's sources it says nothing about UFOs; it is included as background on dynastic finance and the "power structures" theme. The narration is conventional business history (front running, war financing, forex, railroads, gold) rather than conspiracy claims, and it frames the family's dominance as information, speed and family unity. It does not allege a hidden control system; readers of other sources in the wiki (for example the British power-structure channels) may map it onto their claims, but that mapping is not made here. ASR/censorship note: the transcript renders the family name as "Roths.child" and "Jewish" as "Juw".
