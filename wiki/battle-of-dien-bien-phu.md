The March-May 1954 decisive French defeat in the First Indochina War (ASR renders the name many ways). Eyes Wide Open frames it as a failure of conventional thinking, and dwells on two overlooked aspects: the CIA's [[civil-air-transport]] and the [[operation-x]] Hmong maquis. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 32:46-36:19)*

## Claims

- The French built a fortified base ("the Hedgehog") in the Thai Federation of Tonkin to seal the Vietnam-Laos crossing; 16,000 troops were sent. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 32:54-33:26)*
- Per McCoy, [[civil-air-transport]] (host: the CIA's airline, the future [[air-america]]) was contracted in 1954 by the French to bring troops and supplies; before 1952 the CIA had coordinated CAT airlifts in Thailand and Burma under [[operation-paper]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 33:26-34:08)*
- On 6 May 1954 a CAT C-119, piloted by James McGovern, crashed near "bot La", killing five of six aboard: the first US casualties in Indochina were CIA contractors, in Laos, per the host. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 34:52-35:32)* (This is a host framing; the crash is placed in Laos, the battle in Vietnam.)
- Viet Minh guerrillas hauled Soviet-provided artillery up the surrounding mountains; the siege lasted 24 days. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 35:32-35:55)*
- Late April 1954: [[roger-trinquier]] bought 3,000 of [[touby-lyfoung]]'s men with silver; after 8 days' boot camp 850 went under [[vang-pao]] to cut Viet Minh supply lines: "largely successful" at disruption but "not a decisive factor". The base surrendered 8 May; the Hmong went home to Laos. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 36:34-37:53)*
- Vang Pao, then 22, "would be the CIA's chosen asset in Laos", his army the main operators of the secret war; he "waited for his new clients to arrive". *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 37:38-38:10)*
