The Eugenics Society (earlier the Eugenics Education Society) was Britain's leading eugenics
advocacy body in the early-to-mid 20th century. Green & Pleasant alleges deep, direct personnel
overlap between this organization and the founders/leaders of the [[fabian-society]], and traces
its later institutional lineage into welfare-state and end-of-life policy circles.

## Claims

- H.G. Wells was a founding member of the British Eugenics Society
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 17:57)*.
- George Bernard Shaw lectured to the Eugenics Education Society in 1910, reportedly calling for
  "an extensive use of the lethal chamber"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 15:04)*.
- William Beveridge sat on the society's consultative council until the late 1950s (he died in
  1963), and on 16 February 1943 — the same afternoon the House of Commons debated his Beveridge
  Report — delivered the Galton Lecture to the society, titled "Eugenic Aspects of Children's
  Allowances," telling members his welfare blueprint was "eugenic in intent and would prove so in
  effect." The children's-allowance scheme is alleged to have been developed within the Eugenics
  Society with the twin aims of encouraging more children among "the educated professional
  classes" while limiting births among poor households
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 18:38, 18:58, 19:15, 19:32)*.
- Julian Huxley served as president of the society and, per the video, coined the word
  "transhumanism" in 1957
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 4:30)*.
- The video claims the organization did not disappear but renamed itself over time, becoming the
  Voluntary Euthanasia Society, which in 2006 became Dignity in Dying
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 19:40)*.
