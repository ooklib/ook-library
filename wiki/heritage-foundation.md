The Heritage Foundation is a conservative American think tank, co-founded in 1976 by Joseph
Coors Sr. (of the Coors beer family) and [[edwin-feulner]]. More Perfect Union frames it as part
of a "dark money" network with alleged institutional and personnel ties to the [[bohemian-grove]]
club, and as the publisher of [[project-2025]].

## Claims

- Co-founded in 1976 by Joseph Coors Sr. — son of Adolph Coors, father of Pete Coors — together
  with other Bohemian Club members, per More Perfect Union
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 7:16)*.
- Described as part of a network that has "influenced, led and funded pro-corporate politics for
  decades," with all named organizations in that network connected to the Bohemian Club through
  funding, founders, or board/director overlap; Heritage is said to have had major policy
  influence during the Reagan administration
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 7:24, 7:36, 7:42)*.
- Co-founder Edwin Feulner remained on Heritage's board until his death in late 2025, and appeared
  on the leaked 2023 Grove attendance list at age 82
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 8:00, 8:06, 8:13)*.
- Published [[project-2025]] ("Mandate for Leadership 7... the Conservative Promise"); More
  Perfect Union alleges Heritage's board was "like a quarter Bohemian Club guys" during the
  months the document was being written
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 13:48, 14:24, 14:32)*.

## Relationships

- Overlapping membership with [[bohemian-grove]] via co-founder [[edwin-feulner]] and Joseph Coors
  Sr.
- Authored/published [[project-2025]].

## Speculation flag

The claim that Heritage's board composition and Bohemian Club membership are causally connected
to the content or timing of Project 2025 is More Perfect Union's own inference from documented
board/membership overlap, not a claim the video attributes to any primary Heritage or Grove
document.
