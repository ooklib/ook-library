UFO researcher, author of *Majic Eyes Only*, and (with his father [[dr-robert-wood]]) curator of the Majestic documents site majesticdocuments.com and the site specialoperationsmanual.com. He is the guest of [[uap-gerb]]'s 2024-08-25 video. Gerb calls him one of his biggest inspirations in ufology. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:30:32, 1:31:05)*

## Claims (his own, as stated in the interview)

- The Majestic corpus is about 3,500 pages released over 19 years from seven different sources; he has rated every document on a weighted authenticity scale (direct witnesses, forensics, provenance) and, at the time of the interview, says he has no credible evidence that any document listed on his site is faked. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 5:11, 15:49, 1:04:18)*
- He rates the [[eisenhower-briefing-document]], the [[special-operations-manual-som1-01]] and the IPU documents (see [[interplanetary-phenomenon-unit]]) highest. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 15:49, 16:11)*
- He argues the SOM1-01 cannot be Soviet disinformation as [[john-b-alexander]] reported: exposing a retrieval programme with such a credible document would make no psychological-warfare sense. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 50:01, 51:30, 52:30)*
- He judges the Aquarius document "informed fluff" with nothing checkable, placed in Friedman's "gray basket" (his "neutral basket"). *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:05:40, 1:06:31)* See [[aquarius-document]].
- He located two people with the initials on the SOM1-01 change-control page (Albuquerque 1954-56 phone book and Kirtland records) and hired private investigators; one "JRT" he met said he was not the right man. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 44:10, 45:42)*
- Suspects the Majestic name and group persist today, split into hardware and bodies compartments working with private firms such as the Aerospace Corporation, the Carlyle Group, EG&G and RAND. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:22:50, 1:23:59)*
- He trained a ChatGPT-based tool, UFOdex (ufodex.com), on 500-800 UFO books; Gerb says he uses it for research. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:31:30)*
