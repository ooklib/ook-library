A Wikipedia article on the Coyame UFO crash existed from 2008, then in September 2012 the user "Mercy11" radically rewrote it to add names for a 15-man US recovery team and for four dead Mexican soldiers; the page was deleted in 2014 by admin Scotty Wong, who soon retired the account. [[uap-gerb]] finds the whole page's existence "incredibly suspicious" and regards the names as unverifiable. *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 31:45-32:08)* *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 34:36-34:52)*

## Claims

- Before the edit, the article (since 2008) only summarized the [[deneb-report]] and cited a UFO Casebook article and the book *Mexico's Roswell* with nothing new. *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 31:45-32:08)*
- Mercy11 added: at the US military radar airbase four Huey helicopters and a 15-man team headed to Coyame, the group being Captain Lawrence Merley, Lieutenants Randall Bishop, Eduardo Ramirez, Benjamin Rhodes and Jerome Smith and Sergeant Terrence Miles, all retired US Armed Forces personnel, who entered Mexico after intercepting a Mexican radio communication. Gerb notes "retired" would suggest experienced private-industry or intelligence-adjacent operators rather than active military. *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 32:08-33:14)*
- Mercy11 also named the dead Mexican soldiers in an olive green jeep: Captain Rogelio Arguez Gonzalez, Sergeant Teofilo Margarito Puebla, Corporal Jose Trinidad Martinez and Corporal Ricardo Velasquez, dead from asphyxiation. *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 33:14-33:47)*
- Torres and Uriarte asked Mercy11 for the source; he pointed to the UFO Casebook article and the UFO Hunters episode "Mexico's Roswell: The Coyame Crash", which contain none of this, and later said he probably saw it at another site and failed to record the attribution. Nobody has found it. *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 33:47-34:44)*
- Gerb's search for the US names: Merley yields only a Utah man who left for the Armed Forces late in WWII; Bishop matches an Indiana man (1932-2020, Persian Rifles member, Air Force air traffic controller in Korea, later a teacher), which Gerb connects to the Korean-War and Indiana details of the [[special-forces-witness-infographics]] story; Rhodes only an American Legion post commander in Delafield, Wisconsin (1989); Terrence Miles a Senior Chief Petty Officer in a 1993 Guantanamo Bay Gazette (which contradicts "retired"); Jerome Smith (if "Smit" is a misprint) an Army lieutenant colonel and World War II veteran who moved to the US Public Health Service (commander, retired 1985; died 2006); Eduardo Ramirez a World War II private first class with a Distinguished Service Cross. He says some names could be legitimate, all, or none, and is working with [[ryan-s-wood]] on tracking service records. *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 34:52-37:25)*
