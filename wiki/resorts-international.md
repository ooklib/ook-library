Resorts International — originally the obscure Mary Carter Paint Company, in which
[[thomas-dewey]] invested in the 1950s — is alleged by Eyes Wide Open (citing Whitney Webb and
other researchers) to have become a CIA/Mafia money-laundering front, later owning high-profile
Bahamas casinos/resorts and briefly passing through [[donald-trump]]'s hands.

## Claims

- Whitney Webb and other authors point to [[thomas-dewey]]'s investment in the obscure Mary Carter
  Paint Company in the 1950s; the investment matured as the company was renamed Resorts
  International, based in the Bahamas, going on to own several high-profile casinos and resorts —
  and becoming, per the host, "a key money laundering vector for the Mafia."
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 48:02-48:27, 48:54-49:02)*
- Intrinsically tied to [[castle-bank-and-trust]], the Bahamian bank founded by [[paul-helliwell]].
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 48:27-48:35)*
- Briefly owned by [[donald-trump]] beginning in 1986 — a synchronicity the host flags given
  [[roy-cohn]]'s closeness both to [[meyer-lansky]] (rumored to be behind Resorts International)
  and to Trump. Cohn died in 1986, the year of Trump's acquisition; Lansky was also dead by that
  point. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 49:21-49:41)*

## Relationships

- Originally Mary Carter Paint Company; [[thomas-dewey]] was an early investor.
- Tied to [[castle-bank-and-trust]]; rumored (per Whitney Webb) to be controlled by
  [[meyer-lansky]].
- Briefly owned by [[donald-trump]] from 1986.
