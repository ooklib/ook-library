The second official USAF UFO investigation, which [[uap-gerb]] says ran through 1949 (records updated up to 1999, with a 707-page report published in 1960) and was formed specifically to alleviate public anxiety. All claims below are Gerb's or Hynek's as relayed by him.

## Claims

- Started in the same year that [[project-sign]] was shut down by Gen. [[hoyt-vandenberg]]. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 4:13-4:32)*
- Gerb says it was tasked to explain sightings as balloons, aircraft, optical illusions, planets or mass hallucination, for example marking the Lubbock lights (four engineering professors seeing 20 to 30 lights in a semicircle) down to migrating birds, an explanation he calls unsubstantiated and not shared by the professors. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 4:32-5:45)*
- [[j-allen-hynek]] is quoted as calling Grudge "less science and more of a public relations campaign". *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 5:45-5:59)*
- Gerb says he read the whole Grudge and Blue Book reports and may do a most-interesting-cases video. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 4:41-4:56,)*
