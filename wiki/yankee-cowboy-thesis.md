The Yankee-Cowboy Thesis, developed by [[carl-oglesby]] in his 1976 book *The Yankee and Cowboys
War* and echoed by [[peter-dale-scott]] in *Deep Politics and the Death of JFK*, divides the US
power elite into two rival camps transcending party lines. Eyes Wide Open adopts the framework
loosely to explain the alleged [[allen-dulles]]-[[richard-nixon]] alliance and previews using it
for the series' future Kennedy-assassination chapters.

## Claims

- The "Yankees" (Oglesby's term) — also called the Eastern Establishment or, per Carroll Quigley,
  the "Anglo-American establishment" — are the WASPy old families of New York/Upper East Coast
  (Rockefellers, Roosevelts, Morgans), oriented around internationalist institutions (Council on
  Foreign Relations, UN, IMF, World Bank), believing in global institutions, American integration
  into foreign markets, and empire-building via covert action and financial pressure. Since the
  camps transcend party lines, the host notes Democrats like FDR and JFK belong here too, as do
  the [[allen-dulles|Dulles brothers]]. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 12:09-12:57)*
- The "Cowboys" or Western Establishment are largely Texas/California-based, with wealth
  concentrated in oil, gas, and the emerging aerospace industry — an ascendant post-WWII faction
  including the Bush family and figures such as [[richard-nixon]] (as VP and later president),
  Lyndon Johnson, and Ronald Reagan. This camp is expansionist, favoring overt warfare and direct
  confrontation over the Yankees' "meticulous and Machiavellian" covert-network approach.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 13:04-14:15)*
- Peter Dale Scott uses parallel language in *Deep Politics and the Death of JFK*, calling the same
  two camps "globalist internationalists" (Oglesby's Yankees) and "nationalist expansionists"
  (Oglesby's Cowboys). Oglesby's own book uses the framework mainly to explain the Kennedy
  assassination and Watergate as bookends of a "Yankee Cowboy War." *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 14:26-14:57)*
- The host frames the 1947 [[allen-dulles]]-[[richard-nixon]] Queen Mary alliance as exemplifying a
  "short-lived peace" between the two factions throughout the 1950s, and states the framework will
  be used "much more loosely" once the series reaches the Kennedy assassination, but is offered
  as "a compelling framework" for understanding Cold War elite politics generally.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 14:15-15:19)*

## Relationships

- Authored by [[carl-oglesby]]; independently echoed by [[peter-dale-scott]].
- Applied by the host to [[allen-dulles]] (Yankee) and [[richard-nixon]] (Cowboy).

## Additional claims (CIA-Mafia Legend)

- Host: Nixon and Hughes as the "rising Cowboys" (new Southwest oil/gas/military-industrial money) vs Yankee old-money multinationals (Aramco's Rockefeller-descended firms); the mob "realigning" from the Eastern establishment towards the Cowboys; the anti-Castro coalition became an anti-Kennedy coalition that aligned with Johnson. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 2:00-3:26, 4:37-5:11, 15:41-16:21)* See [[jeddah-agreements]], [[howard-hughes]].
