[[peter-levenda]] gives [[jesse-michels]] a documentary, cautious version of the "Nazi saucer" thesis: real occult institutions in the SS, real saucer-shaped projects, and a global Nazi asset network, but no evidence of a Nordic-alien link. He says the Roswell debris could be "exotic human technology" and that he would love a crashed saucer to exist but doubts the US has one from the 1940s.
