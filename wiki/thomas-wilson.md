Vice Admiral Thomas Wilson, former DIA director, is the central figure (with
[[eric-davis|Eric Davis]]) of the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]] — recounting his
2002 attempt to access UFO recovery/material-exploitation programs and being stonewalled.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 35:02–35:27)*

**Distinct from** [[colonel-steve-wilson|Colonel Steve Wilson]], a separate and more
controversial whistleblower discussed earlier in the same video.

## Claims

- Attended a 1997 meeting with Naval Reserve Cdr. [[willard-miller|Willard Miller]] and
  [[steven-greer|Steven Greer]] — confirmed by Gerb to have taken place.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 40:33–40:50)*
- Told by General Michael Ward to search a special records group in the Office of the Under
  Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Technology for legacy holdover programs predating
  early-1990s SAP restructuring. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 35:35–35:52)*
- Spoke to two permanent SAPOC members: Brig. Gen. Michael Costanik and
  [[paul-kaminski|Paul Kaminski]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 39:32–39:40)*

- Told by General Michael Ward to search a special records group of the
  [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-acquisition-and-sustainment|Office of the Under Secretary of
  Defense for Acquisition and Technology (OUSDAT)]] for legacy holdover programs from before
  early-1990s SAP restructuring; eventually contacted the "watch committee" (a retired NSA
  security director, a program director, and a corporate attorney), who showed him a 1990/1993
  bigot list but denied his read-in request. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:09:24–2:12:02)*
