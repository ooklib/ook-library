Chris Ramsey is the host of the Area52 (DEBRIEFED) YouTube channel and podcast, styled "Area52 Investigations", with a Patreon and Discord "intern" membership, merchandise as "Invisible College", and a second channel Area52Clips. He interviews UFO/UAP figures; this wiki registers the channel as `a52`. In [[lazar-ramsey-interview-2025]] he interviews [[bob-lazar]] after trying the VR recreation from [[project-gravitaur-s4-documentary]] *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 5:00-7:22)*. He says Luis Elizondo was a previous guest *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:40:24-1:40:32)*, and that an anonymous verified former EG&G contract pilot who remembered [[dennis-mariani]] agreed to appear and then vanished *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 20:22-21:30)*. He also solicits questions from paying "interns" and relays them *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:50:26-1:50:52)*.
