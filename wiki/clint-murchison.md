Clint Murchison Sr. of Tyler, Texas (the ASR renders "Merkerson"/"merkus"; normalization is supported by the video description, which spells Murchison) is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the national face of the "Texas Raj": an ostensibly decent East Texas oil tycoon who, allegedly, quietly built a private influence and intelligence network centered on the [[del-charro-set]] in La Jolla, California, and bribed [[j-edgar-hoover]] with free holidays and gambling.

## Claims

- Of Tyler, the largest city in East Texas; founded Southern Union Gas Company, one of the first developers in the East Texas patch, and parlayed the fortune into political influence in Texas and later Washington. The host notes he pronounces the name "Murkus." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 6:16-6:57)*
- Supported both parties to ensure support whoever won; per Anthony Summers a typical Democrat until Truman attacked the oil depletion allowance in the late 1940s, after which he and the Texas establishment turned toward the GOP. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 7:18-7:48)* See [[texas-raj]].
- Prominent supporter of the [[draft-eisenhower-movement]] in 1952 and later a major donor to [[richard-nixon]]; he and Richardson were "prominent boosters" of Nixon in the 1960 and 1968 campaigns. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 9:40-9:48, 38:53-39:00)*
- His son Clint Murchison Jr. founded the Dallas Cowboys. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 14:32-14:41)*
- 1952: per Russ Baker, [[jack-crichton]] was sent to Spain to acquire oil rights from the Franco government for a circle including Murchison, while General [[charles-willoughby]] was also in Spain; the host says the overlap is "probably not a coincidence." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 27:46-28:57)*
- Host: "despite the fawning press" Murchison was "secretly assembling a private Intel network of his own" around the Hotel del Charro and the adjacent Del Mar racetrack in La Jolla. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 29:14-29:39)* Quote attributed to him: "money is like manure, if you spread it around it does a lot of good." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 31:33-31:40)*
- Bought the Hotel del Charro in 1951; per Summers he made sure "Edgar and Clyde wanted nothing," allegedly paying all of Hoover's expenses (about $460,000 across 18 summers). *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 31:40-33:44)*
- In 1954, allegedly at Hoover's request, he and [[sid-richardson]] bought the Del Mar racetrack from liquor wholesaler Al Hart (after Hoover allegedly sent two FBI men to lean on Hart) and put it in the trusteeship of his charity "Boys Inc." The host reads this, citing Jane Wolfe's family hagiography figures (comparable tracks netting over $1M a year vs. about $50,000 net here), as a possible tax dodge and money laundry. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 33:51-36:27)*
- Per Summers he covered Hoover's gambling losses at Del Mar and let him keep winnings, which the host calls unofficial bribery buying the FBI director's loyalty. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 36:27-37:29)*
- Employed [[irving-davidson]] as lobbyist; per John Davis, Davidson used [[jimmy-hoffa]] to secure a "huge Teamsters pension fund loan" for Murchison. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 39:48-40:03, 41:39-41:55)*
