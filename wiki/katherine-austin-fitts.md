Katherine Austin Fitts, former Assistant Secretary for Housing and Urban Development, released
the 2004 paper "Black Budget of the United States," identifying trillions of dollars in
unauthorized US black-budget spending. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 48:48–49:12)*

## Claims

- Identified $21 trillion in unauthorized DoD/HUD spending from 1995–2015, much on
  UFO-related "clandestine military R&D." *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 49:05–49:19)*
- Discussed deep underground military bases (DUMBs), covert space economies, breakaway
  civilizations, [[strategic-defense-initiative|SDI]] as an alleged counter-extraterrestrial-
  threat program, and black-budget UFO operations on Danny Jones's and Tucker Carlson's shows
  and on "Dark Journalist." *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 49:19–51:10)*
- Estimated up to $60 trillion may have been spent on secret space programs/UFO technologies,
  suggesting "technologies of unknown origin" (TUO) may be worth up to $150 trillion.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 51:10–51:18)*
- While at HUD, found that IT/accounting-systems providers — [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin
  Information Systems]] (later merged into [[leidos|Leidos]] in 2015), IBM, Dyncorp, and
  [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] — obfuscated/omitted data on where
  DoD funds were directed, and refused to provide her requested account data.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 51:53–52:35)*
- Described these contractor conglomerates as effectively unaccountable to anyone but their own
  contracting parties, running like "Joe's Market on a huge scale."
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 52:42–53:57)*
- Praised [[richard-dolan|Richard Dolan]]'s *UFOs and the National Security State* as the best
  book describing the post-WWII mechanisms creating the black-budget infrastructure.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 49:41–50:04)*

- Gerb's Lockheed investigation cites her 2004 paper directly, noting she identified $21 trillion
  in unauthorized DoD/HUD spending 1995–2015, much on UFO-related "clandestine military R&D," and
  that "a large proportion of the nation's wealth is being illegally diverted into secret,
  unaccountable channels" — including via IT/accounting providers such as IBM, Dyncorp, SAIC, and
  [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]] (and Lockheed Martin Information Systems, later merged into
  Leidos in 2015). *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 15:26–16:45)*
