Harald Malmgren (13 July 1935 - 13 February 2025) was, per the host, a "whiz kid" aide to Defense Secretary [[robert-mcnamara]] and presidential adviser to [[john-f-kennedy]], [[lyndon-b-johnson]], [[richard-nixon]] and Gerald Ford, later an adviser to many foreign leaders (every Japanese prime minister since Tanaka, per [[pippa-malmgren]]). In this final interview (recorded days before he was hospitalised, with a follow-up phone call three days before his death) he claims to have directly handled UFO debris in 1962 after the [[bluegill-triple-prime]] nuclear test and to have been briefed on "otherworld technologies" by [[richard-bissell]]. Everything below is his uncorroborated account or the host's/daughter's relay of it.

## Claims about UFO material

- Says that in 1962, while running the cost assessment for an anti-missile system, he read the test report showing an unexpected "tagalong" object that joined an incoming warhead and was knocked down by the X-ray blast; "tagalong" was the group's word for such objects. He asked what it was and was told he had no need to know, despite a blanket Q clearance, presidential clearance and top secret. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 58:40-59:03, 1:00:18-1:00:25, 1:03:06-1:03:51)*
- Says he was sent to Los Alamos, met [[lawrence-preston-gise]] (AEC Albuquerque), who had "things that have come down" on his desk and told him to pick them up and "play with it". He says the material "felt weird because it didn't feel anything" and was heavy, and that Gise said this was "not the first case" of such objects and told him to do his own research. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 1:11:05-1:12:16, 1:13:12-1:15:20, 1:16:21-1:16:39)*

Harald Malmgren).md>) 1:12:16-1:13:04, 1:16:46-1:17:13)*
- Says that a few weeks after his Los Alamos visit JFK, LBJ and their teams went to Los Alamos, which by his daughter's account had never happened for a president, and that what they wanted to know about was the tagalong, not the test. *(Daughter Pippa's framing, Malmgren agrees;* *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. He was excited by a newly posted 500-page Brown workbook and plans to read it. Host notes [[john-trump]] retrieved Tesla's papers. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft.
