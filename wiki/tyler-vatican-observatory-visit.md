Pasulka recounts a trip with "Tyler", the pseudonym for the NASA mission controller she wrote about in [[american-cosmic]] (identified elsewhere in this wiki with [[tim-taylor-nasa]]), to the Vatican archives and the Vatican Observatory. All of this is Pasulka's account, relayed by Michels; the pseudonymous individual is not independently identified in this source. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:09:35-1:10:08)*

## Claims

- They looked at records on Joseph of Copertino (the levitating saint) and the bilocating Sister Maria de Agreda, and met the Jesuit Peter Gomel (transcribed; papal adviser, spoke about the Templars). *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:10:15-1:10:46)*
- At the Vatican Observatory at Castel Gandolfo (astrophysicist monks), Pasulka expected and saw a conversion experience; Tyler spent time in the archives with originals from Copernicus and Kepler and on electromagnetism. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:10:46-1:11:44)*
- Michels highlights that the observatory "knew who he was" before they arrived: the director (transcribed "Brother Guy Consolmagno") introduced them to young European Space Agency scientists, and when Tyler was introduced by his real name "the whole place erupted", to Pasulka a sign that others knew what Americans did not. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:11:44-1:13:11)*
- Michels says Ryan Bledsoe told him that Tyler had told his father [[chris-bledsoe]] he was part of a NASA and Bahamas-based group presided over by [[thomas-townsend-brown]] involving time-travel technology; Michels says he would discount it "if I didn't know so much about Townsend Brown". Pasulka says Tyler was always talking about time travel. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:13:11-1:14:30)*
- Michels says Brown died "penniless" and unrecognized and that he is convinced his work made it into the B-2, with a "ridiculously low" payout from Northrop; Pasulka says Tyler is one of the "Invisibles", people whose families do not know what they did. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:14:40-1:16:33)*
- Pasulka says she helped one such family write a eulogy after an "Invisible" died, and that many of the Invisibles seem "done with this kind of life" and are outing themselves. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:16:33-1:17:20)*
