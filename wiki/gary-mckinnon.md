Gary McKinnon is the British hacker who in 2001 accessed US military and [[nasa]] systems while searching for UFO evidence and faces a lingering US extradition warrant. [[jesse-michels]] retells parts of his own earlier interview with McKinnon during [[buhler-michels-interview-2026]].

## Claims

- Michels: McKinnon used basic blank-password techniques in 2001 to enter NASA, Navy, Army, CIA and DIA systems, is barred from the US and on an Interpol list, and was given safe harbor by then-Prime Minister Theresa May. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:42:32-1:43:26)*
- McKinnon reportedly queried the Johnson Space Center, where [[donna-hair]] said UFOs were airbrushed from images in Building 8, and saw a silvery cigar/Tic Tac-shaped object near Earth in the early 2000s, before [[david-fravor]]'s Nimitz sighting. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:43:26-1:44:11)*
- He allegedly found a list of about 40 "non-terrestrial officers" and fleet-to-fleet transfers of materials, many high-k dielectrics and molybdenum; Michels' speculation that this implies an off-Earth supply chain of materials relevant to Brown-type experiments; Buhler had not heard of such space manufacturing. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:44:11-1:47:10)* Unverified and speculative.
