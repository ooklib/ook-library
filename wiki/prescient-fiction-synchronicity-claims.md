A theme of the [[levenda-michels-interview-2025]] is that reality seems "choreographed": events are described in fiction or by mystics before they occur. [[peter-levenda]] prefers a "mechanism" (as Jacques Vallee put it, a control mechanism) over a person, and says "the glitch is the proof" that reality is malleable; he does not claim to know how it works.

## Claims

- Maurice Maeterlinck, author of *The Blue Bird* (the name behind Operation Bluebird, "the first MK Ultra"), wrote a play, *The Cloud That Lifted*, about a political leader shot from a grassy knoll by a man who might work for Russia named Alec, which Levenda says was Oswald's alias in Minsk. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 1:23:38, 1:24:13, 1:24:33)*
- Michels adds Morgan Robertson's 1898 *Futility, or the Wreck of the Titan*, Dean Koontz and COVID, Trump's ear before the 2024 shooting, the Baron Trump books and a novel foretelling the Texas Tower sniper. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 1:25:22, 1:26:02, 1:26:31)*
- Levenda cites the Indonesian multi-calendar culture (Javanese pasaran) as a civilization attuned to coincidence, and a Java cemetery ritual of temporary marriage and divorce he visited but did not witness. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 1:27:46, 1:29:04, 1:31:11)*
- Levenda's own chance encounters (Hunt's colleague next to his desk, the Orthodox church, Amsterdam) are offered as examples of attention "attracting" things, possibly retrocausally. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 9:28, 9:38, 49:25)*
