General Charles Willoughby was Douglas MacArthur's intelligence chief in the Pacific in WWII, during the occupation of Japan and the first year of the Korean War. [[eyes-wide-open]] flags him as a coming "main character" and "maybe the most overlooked critical player in the private paramilitary networks surrounding Kennedy's death." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 28:10-28:39)*

## Claims

- Mid-1952: in Spain negotiating with the Franco government for US Army and Navy bases, coinciding with [[jack-crichton]]'s and [[clint-murchison]]'s oil-rights efforts; the host says this is "probably not a coincidence." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 28:10-28:49)*
- Host: he would "work closely with the Texas Raj later in the decade in funding far-right causes and the emergent militia movement." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 28:39-28:57)* (Detail deferred to later episodes.)

- Linked to the [[texas-raj]] and, by the host's later framing, to the [[kennedy-assassination]] paramilitary networks.

## Willoughby biography, part 1 (ewa/willoughby-1)

Source: "America’s Fascist Spymaster - Willoughby 1", first of the host's Willoughby series (Patreon release June 24 2025; YouTube 2025-12-19). Part 1 covers his origins, early army postings and fascist worldview; his WWII/Pacific and Japan work is deferred to later parts (Willoughby 2 not yet ingested). All statements are [[eyes-wide-open]]'s claims.

### Framing and thesis
- Host says Willoughby is often called "the worst intelligence chief in American history" and cited as a failure in the Pacific and in the first months of the Korean War before retiring shortly after MacArthur was relieved. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 0:27-0:51)*
- Host's counter-thesis: his battlefield estimates were wrong but his real skill was "black ops and covert undergrounds"; over a 30-plus-year career he "wove an international network of reactionary forces" of operators, organizations, foundations and clubs, linking European, American and East Asian networks on five continents. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 1:23-2:02)*
- Host argues he worked to bring [[operation-gladio]] concepts to the US in the 1950s-60s and was a "godfather" of what conspiracy circles call "domestic Gladio"; he places Willoughby as "maybe the key figure within a proposed army intelligence angle" of Nov. 22 1963 ([[kennedy-assassination]]). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 2:02-2:31)*
- Series plan stated: part 2 MacArthur and WWII in the Philippines; part 3 Japan after WWII and underground networks with [[jack-cannon]]; part 4 his post-retirement work as facilitator of "a massive private intelligence network with global ties", which leads back into [[road-to-dallas]]. Host also calls Willoughby a sponsor of activist groups after retirement and a speaker at anti-communist conferences. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 0:58-1:16, 2:37-3:53)*
- Note (discrepancy in host's own framing): the video is announced as a "four part series" (description, 2:31) while the Dallas 1 outro (see above) said two parts, and this video's closing only says part two follows next week; treat the series length as unsettled.

### Origins and identity
- Officially born March 8 1892 in Heidelberg as Adolf Carl Weidenbach (ASR "Videnbach"), son of a Prussian minor Junker, Baron T. von Weidenbach, and Emmy Willoughby of Baltimore. Host cites Frank Kluckhohn's August 1952 article: the father's name did not appear in a standard catalog of German gentry (listed differently, "with only one P" per ASR), suggesting the noble lineage may be embellished; host calls this his "penchant for mythmaking". *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 5:00-6:13)* (Name spellings are ASR renderings; normalization to "Weidenbach" is my inference.)
- Host: chip on shoulder as a man who never attended West Point or grew up in America; saw himself as a Junker gentleman in a US uniform. His American patriotism came "more so from a shared anti-communism and a desire to import ... strict elitist customs of his European homeland" than from America itself. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 6:13-7:04)*
- Studied at Heidelberg and the Sorbonne, linguistics/philology; fluent in German, French, Spanish, English by 18; later mastered five languages plus half a dozen more. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 7:04-8:06)* At 18 (1910) he moved to Maryland with his mother for an unexplained reason, adopted the name Charles Andrew Willoughby, 6'3" and over 200 lb, and joined the US Army. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 8:13-8:50)*
- Graduated Gettysburg College 1914 (BA) during a break in service. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 8:50-9:09)*

### Early army career
- 1916-17 [[pancho-villa-expedition]] under Pershing, stationed at Nogales, Arizona. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 13:00-13:27)*
- WWI: embedded with Allied flyers in France, learned to fly, trained pilots at the aviation training center at Issoudun (ASR "Esoddun"); host says little is known; largely avoided combat, ended as captain, then taught machine-gun use at Fort Benning, his first classroom teaching. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 15:18-15:36, 17:46-18:15)*
- Met [[william-donovan]] and [[walter-bedell-smith]] on the Mexican expedition (per Dick Russell); host notes a later "quiet rivalry" between Donovan's [[office-of-strategic-services]] and Willoughby's Army G-2 in the Pacific. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 13:34-15:13)*
- Early 1920s: sent as US Army "observer" to Spanish Morocco during the [[rif-war]] - his first intelligence mission and "beginning of his new specialty". Per a William Dean article seen only in French translation, he was embedded with the American mercenary "Escadrille Chérifienne" air unit, likely coordinating volunteer flyers. Host speculates on whether he was involved in aerial chemical warfare ("certainly a possibility"; no evidence). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 18:18-18:51, 26:38-29:07)*

### Fascism and Franco
- Met [[francisco-franco]] in the Rif, born the same year; host says the meeting began a lifelong Hispanophilia and a "compelling case" (unproven) that Willoughby's fascist ambitions started there. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 26:38-27:16, 28:59-29:20)*
- Host: he was arguably the most outspoken fascist in the US Army; [[douglas-macarthur]] called him "my friendly fascist". *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 26:25-26:38)*
- 1953 Chronoscope interview, his only televised interview per the host's research: asked what US policy toward Franco Spain should be, he advised ignoring criticism of forms of government, judged only by solvency, reliability and usefulness as an ally, and gave an "unqualified yes" to using Spain in the war against the Soviet Union. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 29:45-31:26)* (The Dallas 5 and Bush 2 pages already place him in Spain in mid-1952 negotiating bases; see Claims above.)
- Host's reasons Franco appealed: Catholicism within fascism, Franco's 1947 nominal restoration of the monarchy (Willoughby's aristocratic tastes), and Spain's WWII neutrality, which let sympathizers back it without seeming treasonous. Host adds Willoughby also admired Mussolini's Italy "albeit more privately" (host's assertion, no source given). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 31:26-32:37)*
- August 1925 *Infantry Journal* article "Spanish campaigns in Morocco": host says it views the war through a racial lens (civilized white Christian soldiers versus "unwashed, dark-skinned Muslim masses") and quotes a passage about a "wave of race consciousness" in the "Muhammadian world" and "a flood of brown and black ... taught to kill" white men. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 32:44-34:16)* (Host presents it as Willoughby's own words.)
- 1950s toast to Franco as "the second greatest general in the world, the first being Douglas MacArthur" ("as the story goes"); host says he then followed MacArthur for a decade as his "friendly fascist". *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 34:29-34:48)*
