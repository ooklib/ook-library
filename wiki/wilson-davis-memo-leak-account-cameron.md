[[grant-cameron]] describes how the [[wilson-davis-notes]] reached the public: he says [[edgar-mitchell]]'s family discarded the UFO files after his death, an Australian aerospace collector kept them, and James Rigney (spelling unverified; ASR "Riggney") photographed pages and showed Cameron two of them on an iPad at a conference. The account is Cameron's; the chain of custody is not independently documented.

## Claims

- Cameron says he recognized Oak Shannon (who he calls the real deal from [[eric-davis]]'s advanced physics working-group notes) and Eric Davis's name on page one. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:15:40-1:17:18)*
- Rigney later sent 15 pages plus three other documents: an alien autopsy document naming [[kit-green]], a condolence letter from Mitchell to Charles Lindbergh's widow, and one about meetings of a group Cameron calls NIS (ASR). *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:17:26-1:18:23)*
- Cameron feared Davis could lose his clearance and the document would "cut throats"; he decided to give copies to anyone who asked, and one recipient, an AI expert, put it online months before Dolan found it and it went viral. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:17:42-1:19:09)*
- Cameron says it proved that a legacy program exists and is cut off from high-level people without a need to know. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:19:09-1:19:25)*
