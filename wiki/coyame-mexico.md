Coyame (population about 1,230 per [[jesse-michels]]) is a border town in Chihuahua, Mexico, called "Mexico's Roswell" after the book of that name by researchers Noe Torres and Ruben Uriarte (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 17:37-18:36)*). It is the setting of the opening of [[sentinels-of-ether]].

## Claims

- Locals talk of colored lights winking in and out around the mountain peaks and of small orbs and square objects that seem to emerge from the landscape. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 18:44-19:03)*
- The 1974 crash story: a midair collision between a small Mexican plane and a UFO; a Mexican recovery team allegedly never returned and, per a leaked report that surfaced in the 1990s, the whole team ends up dead and the object is airlifted out by US forces. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 19:03-19:55)*
- Michels suggests Coyame may be a "sandbox for clandestine ops" thanks to isolation, dark skies and odd geology, and that locals no longer discuss the lights. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 19:55-20:14)*
- Barber's 2004 retrieval into a disguised Chihuahua base is noted at [[jake-barber]] and in the DUMBs coverage ([[deep-underground-military-bases]]). See also the fictional [[coyame-mountain-base-narrative]].
