Dr. Robert Sarbacher, a respected physicist and 1950s consultant to the DoD's Research and
Development Board under the Truman administration, is a recurring named source in claims tying
[[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]] to early UFO crash-retrieval efforts. UAP Gerb notes he has
discussed Sarbacher "ad nauseam" in prior videos on the [[aztec-new-mexico-crash-1948|Aztec]]
case. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 1:54:24–1:54:42)*

## Claims

- In 1950, Canadian government senior radio engineer Wilbert B. Smith wrote to Canada's
  Department of Transport requesting Canada's first official UFO investigation, specifically into
  magnetism as a propulsion mechanism. This letter — classified top secret in Canada until 15
  September 1969 and obtained by researchers Arthur Bray and Stanton Friedman in 1978 — recounted
  three claims Sarbacher (an unnamed "source within Washington" at the time) had told Smith: the
  UFO matter was the most highly classified subject in the US government, rating higher than the
  H-bomb; flying saucers exist; their modus operandi was unknown but a concentrated study effort
  was being led by a small group headed by Dr. Vannevar Bush; and the entire matter was considered
  by US authorities to be of tremendous significance. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 1:54:42–1:55:58)*
- In subsequent 1980s interviews with UFO researcher William Steinman, Sarbacher discussed Dr.
  Eric A. Walker — a man who self-admittedly attended the 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO crash.
  *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 1:56:10–1:56:34)*
- In 1983 communications with Steinman, Sarbacher stated he was aware Dr. John von Neumann was
  "definitely involved" in 1940s–1950s crash-retrieval programs, and separately "strongly
  theorized" that [[j-robert-oppenheimer|J. Robert Oppenheimer]] was as well. Three years later,
  Steinman published *UFO Crash at Aztec: A Well-Kept Secret* (1986), listing a purported
  "Aztec saucer recovery scientific team" that UAP Gerb suspects may derive partly from
  Sarbacher's comments (or, alternatively, from the disputed Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit
  documents). *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 2:01:43–2:04:15)*
- UAP Gerb treats Sarbacher's Bush-focused claims as consistent with, and reinforcing, his broader
  case that Bush was "almost ubiquitously" labeled by early researchers as the first man to
  spearhead UFO crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering operations. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 1:53:50–1:54:07)*
