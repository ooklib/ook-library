[[david-icke]]'s account, in [[icke-ramsey-interview-2026]], of how a non-human control system operates from an "astral" dimension of the same simulation, expressed through reptilians and other entities, a "global cult" of secret societies and one overarching "super-psychopathic" consciousness that Gnostic sources call the demiurge. It is the layer he says explains UFO abductions, near-death experiences, shape-shifting and elite behaviour; all of it is his own assertion.

## Claims

- Icke says his research in the early 1990s uncovered a fiercely compartmentalized "global cult", a network of secret societies driving world events, whose inner circle is the vehicle in our reality for an agenda coming from another dimension; the "non-human aspect" entered his work in the mid-1990s. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 7:34-7:55, 11:44-12:10, 15:32-16:01)*
- Dimensions share the same space on different wavelengths like radio stations; the astral level nearest ours can cause interference, which he offers as the explanation for ghosts and paranormal events. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 35:14-36:01)*
- Abduction experiences (with common themes) and near-death experiences are, in the end, part of the same control system; the great majority of so-called extraterrestrials, reptilians and others are in the astral dimension, and control ultimately originates there. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 21:18-21:27, 22:59-23:26, 55:04-55:08, 57:05-57:13)*
- UFOs and apparitions "appearing from nowhere" are objects or entities changing frequency into and out of visible light, which the "system" does not want explained. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 28:02-29:00)*
- Some entities are physical and live "within the earth" at human frequency; he says elites and human science interact with them in deep underground military bases that open out into areas where reptilians live, citing 1990s-era informants who described weigh-in/weigh-out procedures, higher clearance the deeper one goes, and "caged children" at the bottom, and former military guests on his iconic.com who described working alongside reptilians and grays. Not all reptilians are evil, he says, but the ones he exposes are "deeply unpleasant", and reptilian seems a dominant form among several. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 54:44-54:57, 55:37-56:57, 55:21-55:28, 57:19-57:34)*
- He says a "pattern of agreement" across religions points to the same unseen manipulators under different names: Satan and demons, shaitan and the jinn, archons, the demiurge or Yaldabaoth, Zulu beliefs, Scientology. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 58:47-1:00:03)*
- The all-seeing eye and pyramid on the dollar bill's Great Seal symbolize this consciousness: a hierarchy running from astral archons and demons down through the cult into the human world, and it is "super psychopathic", with lack of empathy and compassion deleted (he cites the Hare checklist). *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:00:12-1:01:22, 1:03:07-1:03:44)*
- He calls empathy the "failsafe mechanism of human behavior", argues its absence explains child abuse and satanic sacrifice, and cites the alleged US and Israeli bombing of a school in Iran on the first day of the then-current conflict as evidence of deleted empathy. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:04:10-1:05:53)*
- Religion, in his view, was created to divide people into rigid "perceptual prison cells" and to set up dualities (good and evil, God and the devil); people fear both the devil and God, but "it's actually the same entity", worship being a reptilian-brain trait that the "fake god" consciousness wants. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:08:36-1:09:20, 1:09:27-1:11:41, 1:11:48-1:12:10)*
- Ramsey suggests reptile imagery reflects the psychological image of fear (Jung's snake) and that entities may project whatever form people can perceive; Icke agrees that astral entities can manifest as they choose, comparing fairies and 1800s airships to modern saucers. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:14:31-1:16:31)*
- Icke says he rejects Lawrence Spencer's book of letters with "Matilda Mroy" (ASR; a woman said to have contact with a being from the Roswell crash, and whom Ramsey says had Scientology ties) about Earth as a prison planet for the worst souls, but sees "similar themes"; he holds that people were trapped by having the headset put on, not sent as prisoners. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 1:26:40-1:27:56)*
- Ramsey asks about the [[lerta-files]] and the Chitauri from [[credo-mutwa]]; Icke does not comment on the specifics beyond "all of the above" (physical and non-physical). *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 13:51-14:16, 54:03-54:48)*
