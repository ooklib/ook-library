The Central Intelligence Group (CIG) was the short-lived intermediate US intelligence agency
that, per Eyes Wide Open, President [[harry-truman]] reluctantly created on 24 January 1946
under the War Department, bridging the gap between the wartime [[office-of-strategic-services|
OSS]]'s 1945 dissolution and the CIA's creation via the [[national-security-act-1947|National
Security Act of 1947]].

## Claims

- Created reluctantly by Truman on 24 January 1946 after months of bureaucratic lobbying from the
  FBI (under [[j-edgar-hoover]]) and the Pentagon's Joint Chiefs of Staff, both of whom wanted to
  become the sole postwar US foreign-intelligence authority; former OSS agents ultimately won the
  fight. Per Eyes Wide Open, Truman's stated purpose was merely to get a daily intelligence
  briefing, not realizing he had opened the door to the future CIA. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 16:31-17:24)*
- Its first operation, in Romania, tried to mobilize the "National Peasant Party" and
  [[frank-wisner]]'s wartime stay-behind network of German fascists against the Soviet Union;
  the network was quickly rounded up by Soviet intelligence and sent to the gulags, and the CIG's
  misstep allegedly hastened Romania's formal absorption into the USSR. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 17:35-18:37, 19:16-19:37)*
- Eyes Wide Open frames the CIG's Romania failure as teaching the future CIA a lasting lesson: a
  massive gap in intelligence/counterintelligence experience existed between the fledgling
  American apparatus (in effect only since 1941) and the Soviet Cheka/KGB (running since 1917),
  which the host says pushed American covert action away from direct operations behind the Iron
  Curtain and toward the developing world instead. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 19:41-20:27)*
- Reformed into the modern CIA under its second director, Air Marshal [[hoyt-vandenberg]], with
  the help of [[allen-dulles]] (who, though nominally back at [[sullivan-and-cromwell]], continued
  liaising with former OSS colleagues and, with [[william-donovan]], drafted a 1946-47 work plan
  for the future agency). *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 20:29-21:07)*

## Relationships

- Created under the War Department; failed Romania operation used [[frank-wisner]]'s wartime
  stay-behind network.
- Led by [[hoyt-vandenberg]] as second director; reformed into the [[central-intelligence-agency]]
  under the [[national-security-act-1947]].
