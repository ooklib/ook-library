Sid Richardson was, per [[eyes-wide-open]], the second of the three "Titans of East Texas" oil, best friend and frequent partner of [[clint-murchison]], and a prominent Democratic hold-out among the [[texas-raj]].

## Claims

- Anthony Summers (via host): "the wildcatter stereotype, a rough-tongued bachelor with a limp and a penchant for bourbon and late night poker games." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 9:55-10:11)*
- Balanced Murchison's GOP turn by remaining a prominent hold-out for Texas Democrats: a very early supporter of Lyndon Johnson in his failed 1931 congressional campaign (ASR "first failed Congressional Campaign in 1931") and successful 1937 run. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 10:11-10:34)* See [[lyndon-b-johnson]].
- Helped elevate LBJ's fixer, bagman and protege [[john-connally]], later trusting him as executor of his massive fortune (Richardson was a lifelong bachelor without heirs). *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 10:34-11:05)* Connally lobbied for him in the [[del-charro-set]] days. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 39:08-39:15)*
- With Murchison, bought the Del Mar racetrack in 1954 (see [[del-charro-set]]). *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 34:23-34:31)*
- Murchison and Richardson were seen as respectable, standup businessmen by the rest of the country, unlike [[h-l-hunt]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 11:05-11:28)*

## Relationships

- Partner of [[clint-murchison]]; patron of [[lyndon-b-johnson]] and [[john-connally]].
