Chief of the CIA's Far East Division in early 1961, per [[eyes-wide-open]] LAOS 4; "a longtime CIA agent and an entrenched member of the old boys network". *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:10:32-1:10:54)*

## Claims

- [[bill-lair]] says Fitzgerald was good friends with [[allen-dulles]] and [[william-donovan]], had never met Lair, but told him to draft an 18-page cable to Langley, which he recommended to Dulles, leading to [[operation-momentum]]. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:10:38-1:11:47)*
- Later served as deputy director of plans for two years (1965-1967), the period when "Ted Shockley" (ASR; presumably Ted Shackley) would be running the shop. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:10:54-1:11:12)*

## Relationships

- Friend of [[allen-dulles]] and [[william-donovan]]; approved [[bill-lair]]'s proposal.

## Additional claims (Operation 40)

- Named as a Georgetown Set attendee, "future overseer of the CIA's early incursions into Laos"; host also says he was later involved in anti-Cuba activities in Miami. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 15:59-16:22)*

## Additional claims (The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE, ewa/jmwave)


- Host: replaced [[william-harvey]] as head of the Cuba show in late 1962 (until Kennedy's death), White Shoe lawyer, fought alongside Chinese nationalists in WWII, hired 1950 and sent to East Asia under [[frank-wisner]]'s Office of Policy Coordination, Harvard educated and part of [[richard-bissell]]'s Georgetown set ([[georgetown-set]]); first CIA official to authorize training of Hmong guerrillas in Laos; promoted to Western Hemisphere Division in 1964 and to deputy director of plans once [[richard-helms]] became deputy director (the host says 1965 or 1966, ambiguous in the ASR), holding it until 1967. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 51:10-53:06)*
- Per David Corn, mentor to [[ted-shackley]]; the host says Fitzgerald oversaw the Laos war from Langley "as his acolyte Shockley ran the game on the ground," and reads Harvey's dismissal as the origin of the mentor relationship (host's inference). *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 52:05-53:24)* See [[secret-war-in-laos]].
