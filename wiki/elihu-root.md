Elihu Root, per [[eyes-wide-open]], was a New York corporate lawyer, Secretary of War (1899-1904) and later Secretary of State and US senator, and the second man of the host's "triumvirate" with [[william-howard-taft]] and Theodore Roosevelt. ASR renders him "Ellie Root" / "Ellie Hu Root".

## Claims

- Before office he served wealthy clients including Chester A. Arthur, William C. Whitney and E. H. Harriman, "father to banker and Skull and Bones alumnus" [[averell-harriman]]. He supported Roosevelt's 1880s New York mayoral run and was seen by McKinley as a man of Wall Street connections; he managed colonial expansion (Cuba, Philippines) as war secretary. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 11:08-12:01)*
- Mentor to Taft; used his position to get [[arthur-macarthur-jr]] recalled from Manila in July 1901. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 17:34-17:47)*
- Host: after leaving government in 1909 and serving as a New York senator, he created the [[council-on-foreign-relations]] in 1918, "arguably the most influential US think tank of the 20th century" and, after Rockefeller interests adopted it in the 1930s, "the beating heart of US internationalism". *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 20:18-21:05)*
