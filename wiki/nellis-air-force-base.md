Air Force base in Nevada, home of the [[nevada-test-and-training-range]] (the 98th Range Wing; transcribed "998th") and the Combined Air and Space Operations Center. In [[uap-gerb]]'s Edwards video it is the Nevada end of the joint Nellis/Edwards program alleged by [[witness-ed]].

## Claims (gerb/edwards-afb)

- Ed says he was at Nellis 1999-2002 as a first lieutenant, worked in Building 200 under the range's first commander, was briefed at S4 and saw man-made craft on the range. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 14:19-14:36, 16:08-16:14, 19:26)*
- Gerb cites a [[lockheed-martin]] case study on updating the intelligence collection and processing system for the USAF Warfare Center and the Nellis combined air and space operations center, using an [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency]] two-phase classification system, and speculates such systems could monitor UAP and crash retrievals. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 13:19-14:19)*
- Greer's source claims a "Blackjack control" facility is Nellis-based; see [[blackjack-control-and-team]]. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 39:39-40:37)*
