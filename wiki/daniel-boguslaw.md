Daniel Boguslaw is an investigative journalist (publishing independently as "Deeper States") who
brought More Perfect Union a leaked full membership list of the [[bohemian-grove]] club and a
separate attendee list for its 2023 Summer Encampment, and co-produced this video with More
Perfect Union senior writer Sean Morrow.

## Claims

- Reached out to More Perfect Union with what he described as the full Bohemian Club membership
  list *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 0:52)*.
- Says he obtained the "original list of 2017" — a full membership list — by repeatedly showing up
  to a source's office until the source agreed to hand it over; through that source's connections
  he was subsequently able to obtain further lists, including the 2023 Grove encampment attendee
  list, broken down by camp
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 3:21, 3:31, 3:40)*.
  **Unverified claim:** the wiki has not independently confirmed the authenticity or provenance of
  these lists; they are recorded here as Boguslaw's/More Perfect Union's own account.
  A separate, unnamed source is described as having subsequently put the reporting team "on the
  right track" for drawing conclusions from the otherwise overwhelming volume of names
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 3:49, 3:56)*.
- Co-produced the video (credited as Co-Producer alongside Sean Morrow) and is stated to be
  planning to release the full list publicly after the video's publication, inviting viewers to
  dig through it themselves
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 17:10, 17:18)*.
- Cross-referenced the 2023 attendee list against public campaign-donor records for Senator
  [[david-mccormick]], finding 11 men from the Mandalay camp who had donated to McCormick's
  campaign — not counting additional dark-money contributions
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 14:36, 14:49, 15:06, 15:14)*.
- Runs a Substack/YouTube channel under the name "Deeper States"
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 0:16)*.

## Relationships

- Investigates [[bohemian-grove]]; source of its leaked membership and attendance lists.

## Speculation flag

Boguslaw's central sourcing claim — obtaining a genuine, complete Bohemian Club membership and
attendance list from an anonymous office contact — is presented in the video as his own
first-person account and is not independently corroborated by any other source in this wiki.
