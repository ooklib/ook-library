"The Road to Dallas" is Eyes Wide Open's long-form Season 3 series on the [[kennedy-assassination]] network. Chapter 0 (`ewa/dallas-0`, published 2025-11-13) is a thesis and methodology episode; Chapter 1 (Frank Sturgis) followed 2025-11-14 at 5:00 p.m. Eastern. This page records the host's stated thesis and preview, as the host's claims.

## Method and thesis

- Host says he takes a "structural and materialist view of history" and will focus on the networks around the crime, not the logistics in Dealey Plaza; he says those networks give "a true picture of the clandestine history of the US Empire during the Cold War" and the history that led to today. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 1:10-2:04)*
- He does not aim to "solve" the case and doubts the shooters can be convincingly proven; means, motive and who benefited matter more. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 2:04-2:33)*
- He argues the killing mattered for entrenching the [[military-industrial-complex]], empowering intelligence agencies ([[central-intelligence-agency]]), increasing paramilitary operations, and cementing US-Israel bonds. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 2:33-2:58)*
- Host views JFK and RFK as having diverged from the Cold War "security blob" (see [[john-f-kennedy]]) and as "untenable" to the ruling class. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 3:04-4:33)*
- Multi-asset thesis: key figures such as [[lee-harvey-oswald]] were likely assets of several agencies at once, and many groups likely had foreknowledge; the host names [[james-jesus-angleton]] as the CIA man most clued in. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 7:19-8:29)*
- Elevator pitch (his guess): CIA plus Cuban exiles plus possibly an international gunman; he promises to give each other plot "its time to shine" and to focus on how plots intersect (network analysis). *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 8:31-9:39)*
- Aim: make "assassination studies" a respectable field of modern history, comparable to WWII, Korea or Vietnam history. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 9:41-10:24)*

## The five angles previewed

1. **False flag** - the assassination may have been anticipated and allowed by elements of the US ruling class to blame the Soviets and trigger a hot war; retired army intelligence figures with a network of activists and think tanks tried to paint Oswald as a Soviet agent with Moscow or Havana behind the plot; links to development of [[operation-gladio]]. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 10:37-11:18)*
2. **Militia** - early contours of the later militia movement (nativist, anti-communist paramilitary undergrounds that rose in the 1980s-90s around Ruby Ridge, Waco and the OKC bombing) trace to the 1960s JFK ecosystem; the paramilitary underground as a "repressed power center" to be tapped by "dark forces." *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 11:18-11:51)*
3. **International conspiracy** - Israeli angle, French Algerian secret army (ASR renders it as such; host's wording), and potential Gladio elements. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 11:55-12:16)*
4. **Army intelligence deception** - an army intelligence plot alongside the CIA one, including an overlooked domestic network, with army intelligence figures appearing in pre- and post-revolutionary Cuba. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 12:17-13:02)*
5. **Military-industrial complex and Texas** - see [[lyndon-b-johnson]]: Texas oil, General Dynamics and organized crime; a future [[war-conspiracy]] video on General Dynamics, LBJ and Robert McNamara. *([ewa/dallas-0](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-13 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 0 - JFK Assassination Thesis.md) 13:04-14:04)*
