General Motors is an American automaker. The video alleges its German Opel division supplied vehicles to the Nazis and that its leadership supported Hitler.

## Claims

- Opel supplied vehicles to the Nazis, including the Opel Blitz truck, considered important to the war machine; GM's leadership allegedly opposed the New Deal and supported Hitler. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 11:30-11:42)*
- Vice President James D. Mooney met top Nazi officials in the 1930s and received the Order of Merit of the German Eagle. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 11:45-11:50)*
- The US later paid GM $32 million for bombing its German plants; a 1974 Senate investigation revisited its wartime role, and GM denied involvement, claiming the Nazis had full control of Opel. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 11:50-12:07)*
