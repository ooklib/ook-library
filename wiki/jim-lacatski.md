Dr. Jim Lacatski, one of the founders of the DIA's [[aawsap|OAP/AAWSAP]] program, claimed in a
book/interview to have gained access to the interior of a recovered craft of unknown origin —
describing a "streamlined configuration suitable for aerodynamic flight, but no intakes,
exhaust, wings, or control surfaces." *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md)
1:53:01–1:54:00)*

- Stated he would lie to Congress if ever subpoenaed on the subject, which Gerb cites as grounds
  for caution around his other claims — alongside similar credibility concerns about
  [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]]. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md)
  1:52:57–1:53:01, 1:54:38–1:55:14)*
