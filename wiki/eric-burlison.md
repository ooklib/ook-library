US Representative Eric Burlison (ASR-transcribed throughout as "Berles"/"Burles") is a
congressional figure active on UAP disclosure issues, whose released legal interrogative to
[[mitre-corporation|MITRE]] is the news hook for this video. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 10:24–10:32)*

Note: the transcript separately references a "Representative Burchett" regarding "deep
underwater USO bases" comments *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:25:43–1:25:52)* — likely Rep. Tim Burchett, a distinct person
from Burlison; not otherwise detailed in this source.

## Claims

- Sent (with [[david-grush]]) a 10+ page legal interrogative letter to MITRE, received by
  [[christopher-land]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:50:21–1:50:45, 2:16:40–2:16:46)*
- In an embedded interview clip, describes meeting a Navy-source whistleblower alongside Rep.
  [[anna-paulina-luna]], describing underwater activity reminiscent of the film *The Abyss* and
  "a structure in the deep ocean" — which Gerb ties to the [[tic-tac-factory-uso]].
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 14:39–15:53)*
- Took a site visit to [[naval-air-station-patuxent-river|Pax River]] with White House staff
  looking for hangars that might store recovered vehicles; found none, but was impressed by the
  infrastructure and saw a facility reportedly slated for a Lockheed Martin–to–Bigelow Aerospace
  vehicle transfer. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:17:11–1:17:53)*
- Credited (with David Grush) for raising public awareness of FFRDCs' role in UFO crash-retrieval
  programs. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:02:03–2:02:15)*

- On the SecoActivo podcast, stated legal interrogatories drafted with [[david-grush|David
  Grush]]'s help were sent not only to MITRE but also to the CIA, the
  [[aerospace-corporation|Aerospace Corporation]], and the Navy — the CIA declined to answer and
  redirected them to AARO, while the Aerospace Corporation was the only other entity to respond,
  sending representatives to a secure in-person DC briefing. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 52:37–55:14)*
