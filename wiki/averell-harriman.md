W. Averell Harriman, older brother of Roland Harriman (Roland is folded in here, no page), is
presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the Democratic counterpart to Republican [[prescott-bush]] inside
[[brown-brothers-harriman]]. ASR renders the name "Herman"/"Harryman"; normalization is my inference.

## Claims

- Skull and Bones member "alongside" Prescott Bush and Roland; later Truman's Secretary of Commerce
  (1946-48), New York governor (elected 1955, one term) and foreign-policy adviser to presidents up to
  Lyndon Johnson. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 8:55-9:28)*
- Host: after working with Bush on Thyssen's steel profits, he became "main architect" of British and
  Soviet Lend-Lease from 1941, while his bank still served German interests until 1942; "maybe most
  guilty" of the partners. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 13:15-14:34)*
- Host thesis: Democrat/Republican differences "evaporate" at the top, with Harriman and Bush at the
  same bank. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 14:57-15:12)*

## Relationships

- Partner at [[brown-brothers-harriman]]; [[skull-and-bones]] member; associate of [[prescott-bush]]
  and [[robert-lovett]] (whose father ran Harriman-family railroads).

Harriman, who was a client of [[elihu-root]]. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 11:22-11:33)*
