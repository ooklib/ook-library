The Varginha incident is an alleged January 1996 event in Varginha, Minas Gerais, Brazil, in which a cigar-shaped craft is said to have crashed near the city, and one or more small beings were seen, captured by firefighters and military police, and taken to two hospitals before American personnel allegedly took custody. In this source [[james-fox]] presents it as the best documented recent crash case in the world; Fox and host [[chris-ramsey]] treat the testimony as credible while acknowledging that no footage or photographs have surfaced. Fox says the only US news coverage in 1996 was one dismissive Wall Street Journal article about "stinky aliens". *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 10:31-11:46, 17:08-17:34)*

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox dates it "almost exactly 30 years ago", with the crash on about 20 January 1996 (he specifies a FOIA window ending a week or so after January 20th). *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 11:46-11:51, 1:40:08-1:40:21)*
- A cigar-shaped craft, about the size of a large school bus, with a gash at the back and white vapour, was seen struggling in the sky before crashing; see [[varginha-crash-site-and-daza-claims]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 27:02-27:50, 31:22-31:38)*
- The military blockade around town came several days after the crash; how the beings got from the crash site to town is speculation (Fox mentions waterways and an escape pod theory). *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 34:27-35:35)*
- Fox says there were possibly up to five beings, though the documentary addresses three: one captured alive, one dead, and one missing or escaped, per police chief [[pedro-luis-aguiar]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 53:28-54:17)*
- Firefighters allegedly netted the first being in the morning; that evening [[marco-chereze]] and Eric Lopes grabbed a second; see [[varginha-capture-and-hospital-claims]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 52:10-53:28, 1:02:30-1:05:21)*
- The being was taken to Regional Hospital and Humanitas, then allegedly trucked to a military base and flown out by American personnel; see [[varginha-usaf-landing-and-foia-claims]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:36:19-1:37:46)*
- Chereze died within weeks of an aggressive infection; see [[marco-chereze-autopsy-claims]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:07:55-1:21:37)*
- Fox says everyone who has gone public has "everything to lose and nothing to gain", and that skeptics after seeing the testimony look to him "more insane" than the witnesses; Ramsey adds he suspects such skeptics of being part of the cover-up. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 39:23-40:07)*
- Fox says all firsthand witnesses he asked, including Daza and Venturelli, immediately agreed to a public polygraph. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 33:14-33:33)*
- Fox notes a plaque outside Regional Hospital records that a live being was brought there. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:05:36-1:06:41)*
