[[ross-coulthart]] says the one thing he has been told but has not been able to confirm to his satisfaction is the qualitative nature of a secret space program. He treats it as plausible but unproven.

## Claims

- A trusted person got very emotional and described a friend of his dying on the moon, would not say how, and Coulthart does not know what to make of it. The conversation took three and a half to four years to cultivate, and the person would not communicate on any known comms system. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 43:50-44:24, 48:55-49:38)*
- Coulthart suspects many of the wackiest secret space program claims are deliberate intelligence-community disinformation to discredit the idea, but that the real thing may be the operation of jury-rigged non-human craft flown by human psionic operators to the moon and beyond (he says Tom DeLonge has come closest to hinting at this). *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 44:29-45:15)*
- He finds [[gary-mckinnon]] credible, likes that one of the ship names McKinnon reported was "Hill and Cott" (which he reads as [[roscoe-hillenkoetter]], founding CIA director and MJ-12 member turned 1950s disclosure activist). *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 46:00-46:39)*
- He points to the officially acknowledged US Air Force astronaut program at Edwards Air Force Base as a data point and speculates it "went black", operating from deep underground bases in the continental US, possibly linked to but not run by Space Force. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 47:03-47:55)*
- Only one source claimed to be read in on the secret space stuff. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 48:36-48:55)*
