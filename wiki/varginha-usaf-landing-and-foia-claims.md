[[james-fox]]'s claims about an alleged unauthorized US Air Force aircraft landing at Campinas in Brazil during the [[varginha-ufo-incident-1996]] and his FOIA request for flight logs.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says a radar operator testified that an aircraft coming from outside Brazil, lacking authorization and not radioing the tower, was allowed to land by a high-level Brazilian military official after being diverted from Sao Paulo to Campinas; Fox refers to it as a USAF landing. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:36:19-1:37:15)*
- [[varginha-military-x]] says he drove the trucks to the base and then to Campinas, and metal boxes were loaded; "logically" it is where the Americans stepped in. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:37:15-1:37:46)*
- Fox says the police chief said the Americans came to Campinas and took the items, which angered local military. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:38:22-1:38:36)*
- Fox filed a FOIA request (2021-22) for all US-bound flights in a window of about 10 days before and 8 days after 20 January 1996, guided by retired USAF colonel Frederick Clausen (fighter pilot, 1980 UFO encounter, transcribed "Frederick Clausen" and "Frederick Frederick") and another team member. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:38:46-1:40:34)*
- The CIA answered years later (about 2025) with a "neither confirm nor deny" citing a 1949 act; Fox and Ramsey say [[christopher-mellon]] and others find it odd that the CIA, not the Air Force, responded. Fox proposes a sitting member of Congress file similar FOIAs for other dates. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:40:34-1:42:35)*
- Fox says a DIA source told him the bodies most likely went to the East Coast of the United States and would say no more; Ramsey guesses "Maryland" or a floating island. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:42:43-1:43:23)*
- Fox says he still does not know how long the live being survived. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:37:46-1:37:56, 1:43:23-1:43:27)*
