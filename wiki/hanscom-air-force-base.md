Hanscom Air Force Base, Massachusetts, is where [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]] opened its first
program office, close to [[mit-lincoln-laboratory|MIT Lincoln Labs]] involvement.
*([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 40:56–41:14)*

## Claims

- AFOSI/PJ (Office of Special Projects) maintains permanent offices both at Hanscom and in
  [[crystal-city-virginia|Crystal City]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 40:42–40:56)*
- Gerb states Hanscom served as a station for "Witness Ed," a source discussed across his
  other projects, who was exposed to further UFO legacy program elements here (with MIT
  Lincoln Labs involvement) after his time at Nellis AFB and before Edwards AFB's 412th Test
  Wing. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 41:05–41:27)*
- Air Research and Development Command (ARDC) was reportedly "pretty close with MITER" at
  MITRE's founding office here. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:05:14–2:05:22)*

## Relationships
