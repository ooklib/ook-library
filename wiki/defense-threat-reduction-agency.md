The Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA, colloquially "Ditra"), a DoD combat-support agency for
countering weapons of mass destruction and CBRNE threats, was formed in 1998 as the latest
evolution of an unbroken organizational lineage running back to the 1947
[[armed-forces-special-weapons-project|Armed Forces Special Weapons Project (AFSWP)]] — the same
AFSWP stationed at Sandia Base and named in [[j-andrew-kissner|J. Andrew Kissner]]'s testimony and the
disputed Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit documents. DTRA has ~2,100+ employees (66%+ contractors)
and an annual budget of roughly $2 billion. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md)
2:28:18–2:33:21)*

## Claims

- Lineage: AFSWP (1947–59) → Defense Atomic Support Agency (1959–71) → Defense Nuclear Agency
  (1971–96, began supporting [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team|NEST]] in 1974) → Defense
  Special Weapons Agency (1996–98) → DTRA (1998–present). *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:31:47–2:32:24)*
- Maintains a major presence and involvement at White Sands, the
  [[nevada-national-security-site|Nevada National Security Site]], and
  [[kirtland-air-force-base|Kirtland AFB]] (home of [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]]);
  works closely with Sandia and the [[national-nuclear-security-administration|NNSA]] on
  projects like "global threat reduction," and with the [[office-of-secure-transportation|
  Office of Secure Transportation]] and Special Response Team on establishing "safe havens."
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:30:36–2:31:18)*
- Whistleblower [[dylan-borland|Dylan Borland]] (on the Weaponized Podcast) said that while
  working at DTRA as an all-source analyst, a colleague responsible for collecting Archives Act
  document submissions "happened to sit next to me" on Borland's last day and described old
  Atomic Energy Commission files, discovered during that collection process, that concluded UFOs
  were extraterrestrial and "this isn't exactly for our benefit." Borland said he did not
  personally view the files. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:29:19–2:30:36)*
- [[area-6|Area 6]], a top-secret R&D/UAV-testing site on the Nevada National Security Site,
  performs work for DTRA among other government customers (Air Force, Army, Navy, DARPA), per
  2016 NNSA spokesperson Darwin Morgan. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md)
  2:20:04–2:20:23)*
- West Desert Test Center director Ryan W. Harris — a figure Gerb has separately identified as a
  likely legacy-program member with a Battelle Memorial Institute background — was also a former
  project engineer on DTRA-sponsored projects. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md)
  2:33:28–2:33:58)*
- Gerb's revised hypothesis: the NNSA works with DOE FFRDCs to operate UFO crash-retrieval and
  recovery-transportation logistics via OST, NEST, and the Special Response Team, with DTRA
  likely acting as a support agency "under the guise of national nuclear security" across all of
