[[grant-cameron]] says a 2016 Hillary Clinton campaign plan would have used [[john-podesta]], a seven-year approach with subpoena power and nine people under the president, and that her disclosure ambitions echoed [[laurance-rockefeller]]'s 1990s initiative. The plan collapsed, Cameron says, when the Podesta emails leaked and she lost.

## Claims

- Podesta allegedly told a reporter to ask Clinton about aliens; the November 2015 Kimmel show never asked, which frustrated her, and on a later show she said she would get to the bottom of it. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:40:20-2:41:26)*
- Cameron says Podesta, John Mc-something (ASR: McCay) and Robert Weiss were "the three kings" whose cover was blown by the email leak and who then disappeared, requiring a "B team" ([[luis-elizondo]]) later. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:39:47-2:40:20)*
- After Podesta's January 2016 meeting with [[tom-delonge]], Weiss allegedly wrote to ask for follow-up; Cameron says [[jim-semivan]] and Kit Green first interrogated DeLonge over borderline classified content in his book (Cameron does not name it; the wiki links DeLonge's books under [[sekret-machines-project]]) before concluding nothing was classified. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:37:40-2:39:47)*
- After the loss Chris Bledsoe allegedly told Cameron the green light was still on and bigger; To The Stars launched on 11 October 2017 after a 9 to 10 month gap. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:41:26-2:41:58)*
- Cameron says Rockefeller files show correspondence going to the science adviser through Hillary Clinton's mailbox, and that Bill Clinton told Rockefeller at the ranch it was like the tar baby: [[presidential-tar-baby-claims]]. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:42:06-2:43:25)*
- Cameron says Budd Hopkins told him Rockefeller announced he would get disclosure while feeding him breakfast, and that Pandolfi was called to brief the White House team. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:32:47-2:34:05)*
