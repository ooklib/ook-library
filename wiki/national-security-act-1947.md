The National Security Act of 1947 is the piece of US legislation that, per Eyes Wide Open,
created the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], the [[national-security-council|National
Security Council]], the Secretary of Defense position, and the US Air Force, in a single act
signed by President [[harry-truman]]. The host frames it as ranking with the Patriot Act among
the most consequential-for-civil-liberties pieces of American legislation.

## Claims

- Signed by Truman in 1947 after a congressional committee (convened June 1947) heard
  [[allen-dulles]] present the proposal for the future agency. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 22:57-23:26)*
- Created the [[national-security-council|National Security Council]], a new direct line between
  the White House and the Joint Chiefs of Staff; created the Secretary of Defense position,
  unifying all military branches under one civilian leader; created the US Air Force
  (previously part of the Army), with [[hoyt-vandenberg]] (outgoing [[central-intelligence-group|
  CIG]] director) as its new leader; and created the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central
  Intelligence Agency]] as an independent civilian intelligence agency with its own operating
  budget, staff, and global reach. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 23:34-24:27)*
- Eyes Wide Open argues the act nowhere explicitly authorizes the CIA to conduct regime-change
  operations, assassinations, coups, or other illegal acts, but that 11 "innocuous" words —
  authorizing the agency to perform "other functions and duties related to intelligence
  affecting national security," beyond correlating/evaluating/disseminating intelligence — became
  the legal basis the host says the agency cited for all such covert activity for the rest of its
  history, starting the following year in the Greek Civil War. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 24:38-25:47)*
