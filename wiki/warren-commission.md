The Warren Commission was the Johnson-convened 1964 investigation of JFK's murder that
[[eyes-wide-open]] calls "the official investigation and coverup." This page records only the
Bush-chapter-1 mentions of its membership.

## Claims

- Members named by the host: Senator Richard Russell (chair of Senate Armed Services under whom
  [[prescott-bush]] served), [[allen-dulles]] and Congressman Gerald Ford. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 32:22-33:10)*
- Host: Prescott Bush was a close friend of Dulles, Ford later appointed [[george-h-w-bush]] CIA
  director in 1976, so "the connections between Poppy Bush and the investigation of JFK's murder are
  numerous and wide reaching." Deferred to later chapters. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 32:37-33:18)*

## Relationships

- Investigated the [[kennedy-assassination]]; members include [[allen-dulles]].
