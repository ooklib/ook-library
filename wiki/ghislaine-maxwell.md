Ghislaine Maxwell is [[robert-maxwell]]'s daughter and [[jeffrey-epstein]]'s associate; ASR renders her "Glenn/Galen Maxwell". In this source she appears only via [[jason-jorjani]]'s speculative account of an Atlantis, gravity and Nordic-breeding fixation; see [[epstein-atlantis-gravity-claims]].

## Claims

- Jorjani says she was obsessed with Atlantis from childhood, named the Epstein submarine "Atlantis", dived Bahamian sites, and with Epstein met Fidel Castro to seek permission to dive a Cuban site. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:36:07, 2:36:51, 2:37:22)*
- Jorjani says her Swedish live-in male aides had their hair and genetic material collected. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:34:09)*
