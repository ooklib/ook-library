Roy Cohn, chief counsel to Senator [[joseph-mccarthy]] during the Red Scare hearings, is
introduced by Eyes Wide Open as the series' first "Deep State Operator" — a political fixer whose
shadowy, ever-changing allegiances spanned respectable politics, organized crime, the Vatican,
and, decades later, [[donald-trump]].

## Claims

- Born in New York City to Jewish parents; his father Al Cohn was a New York Supreme Court justice
  active in Democratic politics, while his mother's family, the Marcuses, brought both prestige and
  infamy: maternal grandfather Joseph Marcus founded the Bank of United States, which his uncle
  Bernard Marcus expanded aggressively (5 branches in 1925 to 62 by 1930) before its 1930 collapse
  and fraud scandal — an event widely blamed for helping trigger the broader New York bank run at
  the start of the Great Depression, and which landed Bernard Marcus in prison and tainted the
  Marcus/Cohn family name. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 26:58-28:56)*
- Per Whitney Webb and Anthony Summers, was by most accounts a gay man; Webb transcribes an alleged
  first-ever correspondence between Cohn and [[j-edgar-hoover]] — Cohn requesting information on
  communist subversion — described by the host as strikingly informal and familiar for a first
  contact between a federal police chief and a Senate committee counsel. Per Hoover biographer
  Anthony Summers, Cohn got his Justice Department job through Hoover-affiliated contacts, and
  Hoover personally recommended him to McCarthy and attended the celebration of his appointment as
  chief counsel — beating out a young Justice Department attorney, [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert
  Kennedy]], for the senior role. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 30:50-32:54)*
- As an attorney, represented [[lewis-rosenstiel]] and, "very closely," [[francis-spellman]]; by
  many accounts also mentored [[donald-trump]] and is credited with giving Trump the idea behind
  *The Art of the Deal*, an approach Cohn allegedly learned from childhood-friend's father Gene
  Pope Sr. Per Gene Pope Jr. (Cohn's childhood friend), Spellman was a critical mid-century New
  York power broker with an "ultimate say" on city bureaucratic appointments, and rumors held
  Spellman was secretly gay and had "favorite young priests" — a proclivity, per the host, shared
  by Cohn. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 33:21-34:58)*
- Alleged (per [[susan-kaufman]]) to have been caught in bed with her then-husband
  [[lewis-rosenstiel]] before being present at the [[blue-suite]] parties; also alleged to have
  been the one who procured the young men present at those parties. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 40:36-41:06)*
- The host flags Cohn's continued closeness to the New York power elite and the Nixon, Reagan, and
  future Trump political circles as raising further questions of blackmail throughout elite
  political networks. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 41:06-41:18)*
