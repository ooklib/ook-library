The Rothschild family is presented in FINAiUS's 2023 documentary ([[empire-of-shadows-rothschild-documentary-2023]]) as the most formidable banking dynasty of the 19th century, founded by [[mayer-amschel-rothschild]] in Frankfurt and spread through five houses in Frankfurt, Vienna, Naples, Paris and London. The wiki records this as FINAiUS's account, which is mainstream business history in tone, not a claim of hidden control.

## Claims

- Mayer Amschel sent four of his five sons to Vienna, Naples, Paris and London and, dying in 1812, urged them to put family unity first and above all; the documentary credits this bond and their "unique way of doing business" for their long success. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 3:43, 7:18, 7:25)*
- War financing was profitable: a network of banking houses let them charge fees up to 8% on currency exchange, and they were among the earliest successful forex traders, using private couriers, horses and pigeons to get information first; currency speculation became their most profitable business. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 10:24, 10:42, 11:06, 11:21)*
- [[nathan-mayer-rothschild]] became, per the narrator, the richest man in the world; his 1836 death was called "one of the most important events for the city and perhaps for Europe" by the Times, but the decentralised system kept the family thriving under youngest brother James in Paris. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 11:29, 12:14, 12:37, 12:50)*
- In the 1840s James moved into railways, syndicates and other people's money; the French house nearly failed in the revolutionary crisis and the London house, under Lionel, arranged a loan to bail it out, prioritising family. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 13:52, 14:18, 15:53, 16:26)*
- Lionel de Rothschild was elected Liberal MP for the City of London in 1847 (a documentary interviewee says he used his money to buy press) and insisted on swearing only on the Old Testament: see [[city-of-london-corporation]]. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 16:51, 16:58, 17:16, 17:27)*
- They correctly predicted France would lose the 1870 war and declined to lend, while [[junius-spencer-morgan]] did: see [[franco-prussian-war-loan-1870]]. After James died in 1868 the family fragmented and put 37% of its portfolio into Egypt and Indian railways. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 21:27, 23:44, 23:57)*
- Having once underestimated America, they moved into it after the rise of Morgan; in the [[panic-of-1893]] they supplied the gold, invested for three decades in gold and silver mining and gold-standard bond issues, that Morgan sold to the US Treasury. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 25:15, 27:02, 27:13)*
- Natty Rothschild, head of the London bank, lobbied for expanded naval spending before World War I; in the war family members reportedly ended up on opposing sides. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 29:01, 30:20)*
- The family declined between the wars (a 1935 Jewish Chronicle piece is quoted saying so); Nazi occupation of Paris forced the French Rothschilds (Edouard, Robert, Maurice) to flee and led to asset seizure and confiscations. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 30:51, 32:45, 33:05, 33:17)*
- Postwar, [[nm-rothschild-and-sons]] rebuilt on government financing and stayed a private club "financing elites while evading public scrutiny"; it lent to [[robert-maxwell]]. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 34:02, 34:28, 34:49)*
- By 2023 the family is diversified into investment banking, wealth and asset management, private equity and philanthropy. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 38:22)*

## Relationships

- Founded [[nm-rothschild-and-sons]] (London); patriarch [[mayer-amschel-rothschild]]; London founder [[nathan-mayer-rothschild]].
- Rival and collaborator of the [[house-of-morgan]]; bought gold for the US Treasury deal in the [[panic-of-1893]].
- Financed [[robert-maxwell]]; the documentary says their involvement in his death "remains unclear".
- Sold gold to the British government via the [[east-india-company]] purchase of 1814.
