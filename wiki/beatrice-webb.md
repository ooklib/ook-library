Beatrice Webb (1858–1943) was a leading [[fabian-society]] figure whose 1909 Minority Report to
the Royal Commission on the Poor Laws is credited by Green & Pleasant as the ultimate blueprint
for the British welfare state, and who co-authored an increasingly uncritical apologia for
Stalin's USSR with her husband [[sidney-webb]].

## Claims

- Authored the 1909 Minority Report to the Royal Commission on the Poor Laws; the video traces a
  39-year "pamphlet to legislation" pipeline from this report through the 1942 Beveridge Report
  to the 1948 NHS
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 10:31)*.
- [[william-beveridge]] worked as a researcher on this 1909 report before the Fabians made him
  LSE director in 1919
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 19:56, 20:04)*.
- With Sidney Webb, published *Soviet Communism: A New Civilization* (1935 first edition
  carried a question mark in the title; the 1937 edition, published amid Stalin's Great Terror,
  dropped it; the 1941 edition defended the purges)
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 21:32, 21:49, 22:02)*.
- Received a 1938 letter from George Bernard Shaw proposing eugenic "weeding out" of undesirable
  strains as a response to "the Jewish question"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 16:36)*.

## Relationships

- Married to [[sidney-webb]]; co-author of *Soviet Communism: A New Civilization*.
- Trained [[william-beveridge]] as a researcher; her 1909 report is the traced origin of his
  1942 welfare-state blueprint.
- Corresponded with [[george-bernard-shaw]] on eugenics.
- Critiqued (as one of "the Webbs") by [[george-orwell]] in *The Road to Wigan Pier*.

## Speculation flag

The "39-year pamphlet to legislation" pipeline is the source's own causal narrative connecting
her 1909 report to the 1948 NHS; treated here as an attributed claim, not an established causal
chain.
