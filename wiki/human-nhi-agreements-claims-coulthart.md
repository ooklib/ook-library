[[ross-coulthart]] says he believes agreements have been made between non-humans and humans and that the US government was involved, though he calls it unconfirmed with insufficient data points.

## Claims

- He points to his interview with [[david-grush]], where a line in Grusch's pre-publication security review (see [[dopsr-prepublication-review-claims]]) approved him to talk about "agreements", and Grusch "just can't go there" when asked. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:06:07-1:07:12)*
- Coulthart believes such agreements exist and the US was involved, whether by a president or an agency such as the CIA "on behalf of the human race", and worries about the ethics, and that the deal may have been struck 60-70 years ago "with the wrong ones". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:07:20-1:08:15)*
- Ramsey says he has heard the same story. Coulthart cautions that disinformation is sprinkled in (he names [[richard-doty]] as a source of "droplets of falsity"). *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:08:15-1:08:46)*
- Ramsey raises the idea of "a treaty with aliens" being hard to tell the public. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:35:28-1:35:44)*
