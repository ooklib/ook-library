Isabelle Maxwell is [[robert-maxwell]]'s daughter and [[christine-maxwell]]'s twin. She is alive and, per the host, never charged. See [[maxwells-data-dynasty-video-2026]].

## Claims

- With husband David Hayden she worked alongside Christine on the Magellan search engine. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 17:05)*
- She became president of the Israeli email-security firm Commtouch (ASR "Comtouch"), took it public on NASDAQ in 1999 with a $20 million Microsoft investment, and became a pipeline carrying Israeli technology to Silicon Valley; in her own words a chance "to continue her father's involvement in Israel". *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 18:14,)*
- [[whitney-webb]]'s reading treats this as part of a continuous intelligence operation; the host flags this as Webb's interpretation. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 19:34,)*
