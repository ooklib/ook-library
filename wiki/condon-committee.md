Physicist Edward Condon's 1969 committee marked the official end of [[project-blue-book|Project
Blue Book]]. Gerb frames it as reusing the same "reduce public concern" playbook as the
[[robertson-panel-1953|1953 Robertson Panel]], and argues that same playbook was reused again in
2022 with the establishment of [[aaro|AARO]]. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
2:11:00–2:11:40)*

## Claims

- J. Allen Hynek — Blue Book's longtime scientific consultant — directly stated the Condon
  Committee outright ignored key evidence. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  2:11:34–2:11:40)*

## Relationships

- Ended [[project-blue-book]]; analogous, per Gerb, to the [[robertson-panel-1953]] and [[aaro]]
  in a recurring official-disinformation lineage.
