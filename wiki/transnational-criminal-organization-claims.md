[[steven-greer]] asserts that UFO secrecy is run not by the US government but by a transnational, self-funded criminal organization that sits above presidents and Congress, has its own air force and navy, and steals from the legal black budget. All of this is his (often second-hand) assertion; he offers no documents in the interview and admits he has no craft or body to show. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 23:21-24:18, 2:27:08-2:27:16)*

## Claims

- Lineage: Greer calls it "what used to be Majestic 12" and says the last name he can pin to a document (an NRO paper from the 1990s) was "Magic" or "Magi", the Majority Joint Intelligence Committee; [[majestic-12]] members such as [[detlev-bronk]] link it to old industrial families, and he calls the MJ12 papers a possibly counterintelligence-seeded mixture of truth and falsehood. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 23:21-23:48, 54:18-54:32, 58:35-58:59)*
- Origins: a WWII carry-over from [[allen-dulles]] and [[paul-mellon]], via Mellon's grandson John Warner IV, who allegedly said his family were fascists and that Mellon saw a Nazi disc; Greer adds that [[prescott-bush]], Henry Ford and IBM's Watson backed fascism. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 8:16-10:05, 9:25-9:41)*
- Reach: Greer says Senator Inouye described a secret government above the law (he says he is nearly quoting), that an NSA-level tool stack "penetrates time and space", and that a 1997 source told him at least 10,000 people were paid $10 million or more each. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 23:56-24:18, 24:29-24:38, 1:43:47-1:44:12)*
- Recruitment: Greer says most people have a price; he says in 1992 General [[albert-stubblebine]] offered him $2 billion to join and was witnessed by Greer's wife. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 24:52-25:41, 43:26-43:57)*
- Money: he says a legitimate $2 billion B-2 costs under $100 million to build and the rest goes to illegal man-made UFO projects; he puts the total missing at "8 to 12 trillion minimum". *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:44:24-2:44:56, 3:32:44-3:32:56)*
- Corporations: he names [[raytheon]], [[northrop-grumman]], [[lockheed-martin]] ([[skunk-works]]), and Bechtel ([[bechtel-corporation]], said to build deep underground bases) as "thugs" with wet-works teams; he links [[battelle-memorial-institute]] to gifted-and-talented programs (a claim Michels relays from a government source). *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:20:13, 1:25:07-1:25:21, 2:27:41-2:28:41, 1:42:55-1:43:11)*
- Programs are alleged to be shielded from those nominally in charge: [[thomas-wilson]] (J2), Gen. Patrick Hughes (DIA), the CIA director and Defense Secretary under Clinton, Senate Intelligence investigators, and even the commander of Atlantic Command in a 1980s radar incident. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:21:04-1:24:07, 2:42:37-2:43:05, 2:45:34-2:47:37)*
- Presidential control: he says only insiders such as [[george-h-w-bush]] and [[dick-cheney]] had any control; he says [[donald-trump]] has "virtually no knowledge". *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:35:23-1:35:48)*
- Pressure and violence: he alleges wet-works threats against a retired Fortune-50 chairman, an unspecified 2023 attempt on his own life (a sawed bike-seat bolt), the deaths of Bill Kirby and the [[stan-meyer]] group, and planted child-pornography threats against a crash-retrieval witness. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 17:07-17:32, 3:10:51-3:11:35, 1:45:56-1:46:18, 3:33:13-3:34:47)*
- Blackmail and politics: he says Woodward was naval intelligence and Watergate removed [[richard-nixon]] because Nixon was about to disclose; that Linda Tripp was placed to entrap [[bill-clinton]] ([[watergate-scandal]]); and that [[jeffrey-epstein]] was probably an operation to build blackmail files, tied to a senior national-security figure held on a short leash. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:15:38-2:19:06, 2:19:23-2:20:02)*
