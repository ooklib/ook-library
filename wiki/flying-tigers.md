The Flying Tigers (American Volunteer Group) were, per [[eyes-wide-open]], a covertly arranged
mercenary air force for [[chiang-kai-shek]]'s KMT, created in 1941 by [[thomas-corcoran]] and
[[claire-chennault]] under [[franklin-d-roosevelt]]. The episode uses them as the origin of
[[civil-air-transport]].

## Claims

- Mid-1941: Roosevelt learned that a retired "US Air Force lieutenant general" (the host's phrase;
  compare the [[claire-chennault]] page and [[civil-air-transport]], which describe him
  as a former Army Air Force captain, as recorded from `ewa/mafia-allies`, a **discrepancy in the
  sources, flagged not resolved**) Chennault had been hired by Chiang as private air-force adviser;
  the KMT had few planes and no trained fighter pilots. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 14:47-15:24)*
- The US could not overtly arm the KMT without taking a side in China's civil war, so aid went
  through covert channels; the Flying Tigers were one. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 14:05-14:47)*
- Corcoran's China Defense Supplies shell company arranged the group; Chennault later renamed it
  "the Flying Tigers" and Walt Disney designed its insignia. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 17:34-18:18)*
- [[william-pawley]] redirected over 100 Curtiss-Wright P-40 fighters (originally destined for the
  UK) from Burma to Chennault in southwestern China as the first contingent. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 18:23-19:00)*
- Roosevelt signed a secret executive order letting the group recruit US military reserve
  officers under civilian cover as "flying instructors"; it ran like a mercenary air force with
  competitive salaries and a bounty per downed Japanese plane. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 19:00-19:21)*
- Publicity: reporter Joseph Alsop was embedded in 1942; his brother Stewart Alsop served in the
  [[office-of-strategic-services]]; the host says both remained China Lobby, later CIA, mouthpieces
  as journalists (compare the [[laos-coups-1958-1960]] page for Joseph Alsop in Laos, 1959). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 19:21-19:55)*
- The group mostly operated in the China-Burma-India theater, where it intersected with the OSS
  "China hands." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 19:55-20:16)*
- In 1946 Chennault and his group reorganized as the private [[civil-air-transport]], co-founded
  with [[whiting-willauer]]; a second spin-off, Flying Tiger Line (cargo, for Taiwanese
  interests), was later partly directed by Anna Chennault, who after Claire's early death in 1958
  publicized both airlines. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 28:58-29:58)*
