A video (about 31 minutes, dated 2024-05-17, [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8S9qdRWSnD8)) in which [[uap-gerb]] separates two alleged crash retrievals near Del Rio, Texas that researchers often conflate: the 1955 [[willingham-langtry-crash-1955]] (with a signed affidavit) and the 1950 [[el-indio-guerrero-crash-1950]] named in the [[eisenhower-briefing-document]]. Chapters: Intro, Eric Davis MJ12 and Confusion, Colonel Robert B. Willingham, 1955 Willingham Encounter, Willingham Crash, Crash Material, Bodies?, William Draeger, 1950 vs. 1955, 1950 El Indio UFO Crash, Conclusion. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 0:00-1:04)*

Gerb opens with [[eric-davis]]'s June 2018 Coast to Coast AM remark to [[george-knapp]] that Del Rio, Texas was a 1950s case alongside Roswell, and then gives four reasons the two cases are distinct (incinerated versus three large pieces, dates, 75 miles south versus 60 miles northwest of Del Rio, and Distant Early Warning radar not existing until 1952). *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 1:12-3:04)* *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 20:44-21:36)*

His verdict: the Willingham story is "just a story" until the bodies contradiction and the changed affidavit details are resolved, while the December 1950 case is interesting for its context (a nationwide alert, an FBI memo, [[sandia-national-laboratories]], the [[atomic-energy-commission]] and [[att-corporation]]). He suggests the MJ-12 documents mix disinformation and factual information, similar to what [[stanton-friedman]] claimed. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 27:15-28:49)*

He teases the next episode as [[gerb-whistleblowers-1-video]]'s Volume 2 and a possible NHI origins and taxonomy video at 5,000 subscribers, which was a deleted slide from [[karl-nell]]'s [[sol-foundation]] presentation. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 29:04-29:47)*
