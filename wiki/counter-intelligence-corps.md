US Army counterintelligence and constabulary-style unit. In [[eyes-wide-open]]'s `ewa/shackley` it is [[ted-shackley]]'s first intelligence employer (1945-47, 970th Detachment, occupied Germany) and the vehicle through which the host speculates he touched Nazi-scientist recovery and the origins of the [[gehlen-organization]] commando pool.

## Claims

- Origins: formed as intelligence police in World War I with the American Expeditionary Forces; revived as the Counter Intelligence Corps in 1941 for stateside investigative counterintelligence; resembled military police. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 28:30-28:53)*
- Post-1945 occupied Germany: ordered to act as a constabulary in the US zone; work included tracking fugitive Nazi elites, first to detain and later to prosecute or employ under secret agreements (Shackley 1946-47 interviewing DPs and hunting people on automatic-arrest lists, per Corn). *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 29:11-29:43, 31:06-31:49)*
- Host: high-value targets included Nazi party officials, senior officers and scientists, putting the CIC "at the front lines" of what became Operation Paperclip (V-2 rocket staff) and possibly the rat lines to South America, the US and Canada. Framed as speculation about what Shackley "could have" known. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 31:49-33:20)* Wiki-internal observation: German-scientist recovery is covered in [[wernher-von-braun]] and [[hans-kammler]] pages from other sources; this transcript does not connect the CIC to any UFO/UAP topic.
- Occupied Japan comparison (Harry Brunette in *Yakuza*): "You had to be Lily White to get into CIC and turn coal black to stay in"; the badge was authority to "break in and steal some documents." Host infers similar conduct in Germany. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 27:36-28:17)*
- European Theater Intelligence School at Oberammergau (Bavarian Alps; later the NATO school): Shackley reportedly trained there in mid-1946, where a recently CIC-relieved young [[henry-kissinger]] was allegedly an instructor under Army military intelligence; host: Shackley "very well might" have been his pupil. The same Oberammergau/Bad Tolz area later hosted the [[galen-commando-program]] training. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 33:27-34:20, 35:06-35:53)*
- Host speculates CIC scouts like Shackley may have identified future Gehlen commando recruits. Per US government investigations, Emil Augsburg worked with Army CIC in 1947-48 as a Soviet-affairs expert. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 35:53-36:02, 56:38-56:54)*
