*American Cosmic* is [[diana-pasulka]]'s book on UFO phenomena as a modern quasi-religion; Michels calls it "the modern ufology Bible" and gives 2018 as its date, with her 2023 follow-up *Encounters* and the 2014 *Heaven Can Wait* on purgatory. The pseudonymous "Tyler" in the book is identified elsewhere in this wiki with [[tim-taylor-nasa]]. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:09-1:48, 22:33-22:57)*

## Claims (jm/nell-pasulka)

- Pasulka says she named it *American Cosmic* because nobody had talked about American cosmism. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 25:10-25:19)*
