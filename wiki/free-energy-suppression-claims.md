[[steven-greer]] says the "really high-tech stuff" (free energy, antigravity, teleportation) was boxed up because it would end oil, gas, coal and the petrodollar system and threaten "hundreds of trillions of dollars" in assets. He says he has "100 percent knowledge" that scalable zero-point energy exists; [[jesse-michels]] says he is unsure. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 16:04-16:45, 17:01-17:10, 2:21:52-2:22:24)*

## Claims

- A retired Fortune-50 chairman (unnamed) allegedly approached Greer, was threatened by a wet-works team, and said free energy could have existed decades ago. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 17:10-17:51)*
- Inventors and their fates: [[stan-meyer]]'s water-fuel work, Pons and Fleischmann cold fusion ("on to something"), Joe Firmage (a tech entrepreneur Greer says was gaslit and later jailed), and Bill Kirby (a zero-point device allegedly found dead in 1995 in a supposed canoeing accident). *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:45:56-1:47:07, 2:11:11-2:11:26)*
- Meyer explanation: he says Meyer's high-voltage water splitting works by "ball lightning" micro-events perturbing the zero-point field. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:51:22-2:52:22)*
- Solution: dump designs open source on blockchain to billions of people at once, not patents or venture capital; he warns "money grubbing" actors. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:48:13-1:48:20, 2:52:39-2:52:54)*
- Peter Thiel is said to have almost been steered into a $150 million energy mistake by cutouts; Greer says he passed a heads-up to Eric Weinstein. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:11:48-2:12:07)*
- Medical technology (limb regrowth, spinal repair) is said to exist at a lab near El Paso but is withheld. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:21:07-2:21:29)*
- Timeline: he says a transition would take 15 to 30 years; petrodollar and the CIA-linked creation of Aramco are cited as why it is blocked. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:57:11-1:57:37, 2:22:00-2:22:24)*
- Michels also mentions Tesla and [[thomas-townsend-brown]] work; Greer says Eric Davis called Brown a phony, which Greer reads as counterintelligence. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 15:15-15:45, 1:28:52-1:29:07)*
