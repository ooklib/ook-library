Richard Nixon's 1972 executive order "Classification and declassification of national security information and material". [[uap-gerb]] calls it "the first domino" that ended the [[golden-era-1947-1978]].

## Claims

- Established the formal framework for what became special access programs ("black programs"), moving from Eisenhower-era ad hoc practice to structured policy while keeping congressional and inter-agency awareness bottlenecked. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:04:39-1:05:31)*
- Gerb argues Nixon, like [[lyndon-b-johnson]], was "well-versed and involved" in legacy activities, and that the aggressive expansion of unacknowledged SAP precursors in the 1970s-80s (protecting acquisition programs, stealth aircraft and, in his opinion, UFO retrieval) exposed the ecosystem to unwanted eyes. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:05:42-1:06:22)*
- Congress reacted with a 1973 House Committee on Government Operations report on "widespread and uncontrolled growth" of special-access labels used by agencies with and without classification authority; Gerb finds it striking that this describes exactly the golden-era pattern of hiding in code-word access. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:06:22-1:07:32)*
- In the closing recap Gerb mis-numbers it "11056" (an ASR/speech slip) and says it "started to create some problems". *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 2:38:26-2:38:42)*
