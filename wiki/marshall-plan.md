The Marshall Plan, per Eyes Wide Open, was a $13.7 billion US financial-aid program offering war-
torn European countries conditional aid in exchange for organizing their societies under
capitalist lines — and, alleged unverified claim, a major skimmed funding source for the nascent
CIA's illegal covert operations across the continent, including Gladio.

## Claims

- Presented war-torn European countries a conditional offer: financial aid in exchange for
  organizing society under capitalist lines and establishing moderate capitalist ruling parties;
  the US/British strategy of "fighting for hearts and minds" with a $13.7 billion carrot,
  contrasted with the "hidden and deadly stick" of the CIA/NATO's Operation Gladio.
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 8:15-8:39)*
- As recently disclosed, over 5% ($685 million) of Marshall Plan funds were skimmed by the CIA to
  fund illegal covert operations across the continent during the early Cold War — a massive money
  laundering scheme with little public visibility into where the funds actually went, though Eyes
  Wide Open says money "was running rampant" through Mafia circles, church institutions, and
  underground far-right networks in former Axis-occupied states. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 8:52-9:37)*
- Framed as the first step of a two-part US/British plan to bind France and West Germany economically as a bulwark against the Warsaw Pact, the second being a federated Europe ([[pan-europa-movement]]); ACUE-backed groups had to be in line with its rollout. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 21:09-22:14, 24:24-24:36)*
