A retired US Army general whom [[uap-gerb]] speculatively (and, he says, weakly) proposes as the identity of [[special-forces-witness-infographics]]. Gerb earlier, in his preceding video, relayed a claim by a witness called "TB" that a general showed classified UAP-derived cloaking technology to a BAE Systems employee in 2004 and was investigated, demoted a star and retired; Gerb says that general is John M. Riggs. That earlier claim is not in this wiki's sources beyond this video.

## Claims (gerb/special-forces)

- Gerb notes Riggs enlisted in 1965, was commissioned a second lieutenant in 1969, flew about 1,000 hours as a helicopter pilot (earning a "distinguished cross", as transcribed), and was forced to retire in 2004-2005, which lines up with the witness's program tenure of 1969 to 2004. He speculates Riggs could have flown a rapid-reaction unit in Vietnam, later worked on the administrative side with Congress, and been stationed in Germany at the time of the UK-based crash; afterwards he became an aviation and strategic defense consultant. Gerb stresses this is a theory resting on dates and coincidences, that it "doesn't hold much water", and invites other guesses. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 41:04-44:07)*
- Gerb believes "TB's" claims are credible and that Riggs is well aware of UAP-derived technology, reverse engineering and crash retrieval, but offers nothing besides dates to link him to the Infographics account. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 43:26-43:58)*
