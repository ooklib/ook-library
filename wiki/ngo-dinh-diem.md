South Vietnam's president, killed in 1963. Per Eyes Wide Open, [[lucien-conein]] eventually gave the green light to South Vietnamese generals to kill him, one of the last defining moments of the Kennedy presidency. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 16:35-16:45)*

## Claims

- Killed in 1963 with alleged Conein sign-off. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 16:35-16:45)*

- LAOS 2: the host says Diem's family was among the "significant Catholic convert community" allied with the French in Saigon, and that the [[binh-xuyen]] pirates "would challenge his authority almost as soon as he became president" (deferred to LAOS 3). He was to be established before the 1956 elections, and [[edward-lansdale]] was sent to advise him. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 1:38-1:45, 25:04-25:19, 41:52-42:08, 46:43-46:49)*
