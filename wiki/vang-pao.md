Vang Pao led the guerrilla army in the mountains of Laos's Golden Triangle that received US operational support in the secret war, per Eyes Wide Open (ASR: 'Vang Powers', 'Vangpow'). The host promises to track his development in the Indochina episodes. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 12:59-13:16)*

## Claims

- Singlaub provided operational support to his forces. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 12:59-13:08)*
- Conein allegedly helped establish a narcotics link between Corsican mobsters in Saigon and Vang Pao's army. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 16:17-16:24)*

- LAOS 2: as a young man (host: age 22 at Dien Bien Phu) he had worked for the French as an interpreter and was one of [[touby-lyfoung]]'s top lieutenants; he led 850 "Mountain Warriors" toward Dien Bien Phu in April 1954 and cut some Viet Minh supply lines. Host: he "would be the CIA's chosen asset in Laos", his army the main operators of the secret war. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 36:57-38:10)* The host says his name "surely came up" when [[roger-trinquier]] briefed [[edward-lansdale]] and [[lucien-conein]] in July 1954. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 47:39-47:48)*

- LAOS 3: the host notes the 1958 revival of the Laos opium trade coincided with the first CIA contacts with Vang Pao's Hmong forces in the northern highlands, suggesting the new Saigon business "was established in order to bolster" the CIA's secret army. Speculation; detailed next episode. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 47:39-48:07)*

- LAOS 4: first contact: CIA case officer Stuart Methven met him at an [[operation-brotherhood]] hospital in Ban Ban in August 1959 (and, per the host's later line, in 1960 via a USAID mission; flagged inconsistency); J. Vinton Lawrence called Methven the catalyst. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 26:18-27:18)*
- LAOS 4: first met [[bill-lair]] in January 1961; reply to Lair: "you give us the weapons and we'll fight the Communists"; the CIA authorized arming the first 1,000 men, launching [[operation-momentum]] (about 40,000 fighters). *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:09:52-1:12:02)*
- LAOS 4: host: he was a top lieutenant of Hmong leader "Tu Leung" (ASR; presumably [[touby-lyfoung]]) in the Hmong maquis who fought with the French, and the CIA had known of him since the end of the first Indochina war; his army would regroup on the Plain of Jars. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 24:07-24:19, 26:31-26:45, 26:03-26:11)*
