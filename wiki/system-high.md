System High, founded in 2005, is a security-services contractor whose mission statement frames
it as "the only choice when the mission must be protected," delivering "the most advanced
protection and security solutions to secure and strengthen critical missions, programs,
operations, and intelligence activities." Since 2012, it has held an "intimate partnership" with
[[darpa-security-and-intelligence-directorate|DARPA's Security and Intelligence Directorate
(SID)]], including a third consecutive 5-year multicurity-services contract awarded in 2022.
*([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:55:06–1:56:30)*

## Claims

- Gerb notes System High was founded in 2005 — "an important year" in his broader legacy-program
  narrative, marking the period he alleges specific contractors (Northrop Grumman among them)
  began independently-funded (IRAD) "breakaway" UFO retrieval/exploitation activity.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:55:45–1:55:54)*
- Beyond DARPA SID, System High holds IDIQ (indefinite delivery, indefinite quantity) contract
  vehicles with the Navy, the Missile Defense Agency, and other agencies/services.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:56:22–1:56:40)*
- Board member [[rob-howe|Rob Howe]], System High president from 2019 to 2025, previously managed
  "special activities" at [[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]], served as a program security
  officer for [[saf-aq|SAF/AQ]] (Air Force Acquisition), and served in the Air Force at the Air
  Force Test Center (AFTC) — "all major elements within UFO legacy programs," per Gerb.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:56:40–1:57:13)*
