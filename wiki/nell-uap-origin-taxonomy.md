An omitted backup slide from [[karl-nell]]'s [[sol-foundation]] deck, as provided by [[matthew-pines]] and described by [[uap-gerb]], proposing a taxonomy of UAP and NHI origin hypotheses in three categories: physical, psychophysical and metaphysical. Gerb says he is unsure where to start with it and offers to do a tier list. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 15:39-15:48,)*

## Claims

- Physical origins range from natural phenomena, humans, intergalactic ETs and protohumans to stranded gods, unknown regions of spacetime, disconnected hyperspace, 3D avatars or holograms, colliding or parallel universes, macro quantum fluctuations and pangalactic entanglement. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 17:33-18:12)*
- Metaphysical categories include supernatural forces, incorporeal life forms, ascended masters, angelic or demonic beings and artificial realities. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 18:12-18:30)*
- Gerb says he personally identifies more with the physical range of explanations. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 18:38-18:46)*
