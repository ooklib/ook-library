A mid-1980s (about 1985-86) wave of sightings of lights and a large lit triangle in southeast New York, Brewster area, recalled by [[charles-buhler]] as his first strong UFO experience and stated by him to have run for several days with weekly press and TV coverage.

## Claims

- Buhler, then 11 or 12, was working his father's garage haunted house when friends pointed out six bright white lights hanging silently in formation over the trees; cars followed them for days. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:10:52-1:12:50)*
- Around the same time, other children claimed two men in 1920s dress told them "close your eyes and tell it to land"; Buhler connects this to men-in-black lore. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:13:03-1:13:52)*
- Michels plays archive witness clips describing a rim of lights in a triangular shape 50-60 yards wing tip to wing tip. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:14:05-1:14:46)* Compare [[flying-triangle-sightings]].
- Buhler says this sparked his physics career. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:12:18-1:12:34)*
