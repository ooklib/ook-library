Paul Nitze is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as an "obscure" figure whose [[nsc-68]] made the
militarized Cold War and permanent peacetime military spending policy. **Name note:** ASR renders
"Paul nits/nitz"; normalization to Nitze is my inference (the source's listing names NSC 68), kept as
aliases.

## Claims

- Director of policy planning at the State Department when he wrote [[nsc-68]] in April 1950,
  the memo in which the host says "rollback" was first coined. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 35:15-35:28)*
- The host calls him "the originator of the concept of the militarized Cold War": the view that
  defeating the USSR required an overwhelming increase in US military spending, "the forefather of the
  US as a global police force," and a root of neoconservatism and the war on terror. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 35:34-36:34)*
