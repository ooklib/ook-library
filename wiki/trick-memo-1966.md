A 1966 memo by Robert J. Low, second in command of the [[condon-committee]], to two University of Colorado administrators, made public by [[james-e-mcdonald]] in 1967. [[uap-gerb]] treats it as the blueprint of the [[ufo-stigma]]. The memo link in the description points to the NICAP archive.

## Claims

- Low said they could expect the study to demonstrate that UFO observations had no basis in reality, "conclusions before findings", and Gerb calls him a hardened skeptic with a paper trail showing he served in the CIA before teaching at CU Boulder (an alleged CIA asset, per Gerb). *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 16:36-17:10,)*
- The quoted "trick": describe the project so the public sees an objective study while the scientific community sees non-believers with almost zero expectation of finding a saucer, by stressing the psychology and sociology of witnesses rather than the physical phenomenon. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 17:05-17:53)*
- Gerb: this "perfectly details" how the stigma would operate, forget the sighting and question the observer's psychology. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 17:53-18:09)*
- Related: in late 1967 Condon said in a public lecture that government should not study UFOs because the subject was nonsense, adding "but I'm not supposed to reach that conclusion for another year". *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 16:11-16:34)*
