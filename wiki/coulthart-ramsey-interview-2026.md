A roughly 2h55m interview in which [[chris-ramsey]] (Area52) sits down in person with Australian investigative journalist [[ross-coulthart]], who had travelled from Australia, uploaded 2026-02-20 as DEBRIEFED ep. 76. Coulthart describes how his journalistic assumption that UFOs were an American black program collapsed, recounts New Zealand and Australian cases he grew up with, and lays out what he says he has learned from more than a dozen people connected to a legacy retrieval and reverse-engineering program. He then discusses congressional oversight failures, the ethics of downing craft, experiencer accounts, his critique of credulity in ufology, a psionic "inviting" session at Esalen, and audience questions. All substantive claims are Coulthart's, mostly second-hand from unnamed sources, and are recorded here as attributed claims.
