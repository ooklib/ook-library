Two photographs said to have been taken in 1967 by USAF Captain Harvey Williams while flying a C-47 at 12,000 ft about 25 miles southwest of Provo, Utah. [[mark-mccandlish]] displayed them at the 2001 [[disclosure-project]] press conference and said the disc matched the ARV in all proportions and details, up to the synthetic vision system.

## Claims (gerb/mccandlish)

- McCandlish argued the vision-system bubbles were larger in 1967 because cameras were larger, showing an ARV evolution to the 1988 craft that used off-the-shelf parts. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 36:11-37:29)*
- Gerb notes Provo is under 100 miles from [[dugway-proving-ground]], where [[witness-ms]] claims a hovering craft in the Aviary building and a deep underground base ([[dugway-deep-underground-military-base]]). *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 37:36-38:00)*
