Denver oilman (born 19 July 1897; Baylor, Yale, post-graduate work in Berlin) who was the main public source of the Aztec crash story to [[frank-scully]]. He was convicted of fraud in 1953 over the [[doodlebug-magnetic-detector]] but never sentenced. [[uap-gerb]] argues the fraud case looks like retaliation for leaking the story. All claims below are attributed.

## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Newton claimed no firsthand experience of the crash beyond handling retrieved equipment and souvenirs; his information came from close confidants, the group of scientists called "Mr. G" or "Dr. G" ([[dr-gee-scientists]]). He invited Scully to the Mojave Desert to test the doodlebug, then introduced him to Dr. G. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 32:21-33:55)*
- On 8 March 1950 he lectured at the University of Denver on a saucer crash under the pseudonym "scientist X", replacing the original speaker (possibly one of the Gs, or [[carl-a-highland]]); radio salesman George Kohler of KMYR set it up. The recording and Newton's chalkboard drawings survive in Scully's archive. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 34:47-35:58, 44:08-44:18)*
- The day after the lecture, Air Force OSI agents came looking for Kohler and interrogated him about Newton, saucers and cube-like radios from the crash; on 30 September 1950 Army CIC and FBI staged a Denver hotel sting to intercept crash photographs, and the FBI kept files on Newton, including a 31 March 1950 letter to Hoover. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 35:58-37:26, 47:03-47:52)*
- Gerb says the crash "gears" Newton showed [[jp-cahn]] were replicas, so Cahn's aluminum test proved nothing about the real items. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 40:49-41:27)*
- Newton's unfinished 1954 autobiography (found in Scully's archive by the Ramsays) shows his fascination with magnetics and belief that saucers fly on magnetic principles, his admiration for [[wilbert-b-smith]], and the complaint that he told the story always as hearsay yet was "lied about" and framed; a 1950 wire recording has him describing a "top magnetic scientist" who told him a saucer landed 16 miles east of Aztec and that about seven magnetic-group scientists examined it. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 57:11-1:06:20)*
- Carl Flock's 1970s claim of a Newton diary saying the military asked him to spread a bogus Aztec story was never backed by a produced diary; Flock admitted his source was Newton's nephew. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 59:39-1:00:30)*
- Gerb reasons Newton knew oil and gas scientists who went into secret anti-submarine work for [[vannevar-bush]] in the Second World War, which explains his access to both magnetic detection devices and crash information. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:01:06-1:01:57)*
