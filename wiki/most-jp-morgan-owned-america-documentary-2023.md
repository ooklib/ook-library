MagnatesMedia video of 2023-06-24 (about 19 minutes, narrated by John Frazer), a conventional business-history mini-documentary on [[j-pierpont-morgan]]. Like the other tycoon sources in the wiki it has no UFO content and no hidden-control claims; it is recorded as power-structure background alongside the [[house-of-morgan]], [[rothschild-family]], [[rockefeller-family]] and [[cornelius-vanderbilt]]. The narrator's framing is that Morgan was so dominant that the economy "quite literally depended" on him, which is why the Federal Reserve was created. The ShipStation sponsor read is ignored; ASR misspellings: "Theador" = Theodore, "Republic nominee" = Republican, "hall carbine" = Hall carbine.
