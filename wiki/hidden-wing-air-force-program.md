"The Hidden Wing" is Gerb's name — coined and given a full dedicated investigation in this
video — for an alleged Air Force test-and-evaluation (T&E) program or family of programs focused
on the recovery, storage, exploitation, and derivation (RSED) of nonhuman-intelligence (NHI)
craft, derivative/adapted technologies, and alien reproduction vehicles (ARVs). Gerb states this
is his central thesis for the entire project: "if you discount the entirety of this video, all 3
hours of it, please remember what I've discussed here about this massive T&E project I call the
Hidden Wing." The program is run out of the "Western Ranges" — the airspace and test
infrastructure around the [[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards 412th Test Wing]], the Nevada Test and
Training Range (NTR), and the Utah Test and Training Range (UTTR) — using pilots drawn from both
the US Navy Test Pilot School at [[naval-air-station-patuxent-river|Pax River]] and the Air Force
Test Center's (AFTC) Test Pilot School (TPS) at Edwards. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 2:45:20–2:46:18, 3:17:31–3:18:17)*

Gerb had previously investigated the program (in the [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]] video, spending
"three plus hours" on it) as an Air Force analog he used to illustrate MITRE's own alleged
"hidden SSO" structure, describing it as operating below the western ranges of Edwards' 412th
Test Wing and AFTC, performing T&E on recovered NHI vehicles, derivative airframes, and ARVs.
*([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 52:21–52:55)*

## The witness "Ed"

Much of Gerb's Hidden Wing account rests on the testimony of a recurring witness he calls "Ed" —
a retired Air Force officer whose CV (which Gerb says he has personally held and read) spans
service as a flight-test engineer, time within the Air Force Test Center at the
[[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards 412th Test Wing]] as an Air Force officer, and later a civilian
role at the Pentagon. Gerb was originally introduced to Ed's account through Steven Greer's notes
and documents (Ed approached Greer directly), and initially cross-checked Greer's retelling
against Ed's CV; Gerb states that "due to recent events I am keeping intentionally vague," his
confidence in Ed's testimony has since grown "10,000-fold" via new independent insight into Ed's
firsthand experience. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 36:01–37:37)*

- Ed possesses years of firsthand experience within a "hidden wing" out on the Western Air Force
  ranges — an extensive waved/deep-sixed program set across the Nevada Test and Training Range,
  the Edwards 412th Test Wing, Dugway, and the Utah Test and Training Range (all MRTFBs), testing
  and evaluating recovered nonhuman vehicles as well as derivative/ARV airframes using exotic,
  non-conventional propulsion. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 35:49–36:33)*
- Per the MITRE video, Ed was reportedly exposed to further legacy-program elements at
  [[hanscom-air-force-base|Hanscom AFB]] (with MIT Lincoln Labs involvement), after his time at
  Nellis AFB and before his time at Edwards' 412th Test Wing. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 41:05–41:27)*
- Ed's CV is "riddled with acquisition," and following his time within the Hidden Wing, Ed served
  in the Pentagon connected to [[saf-aq|SAF/AQ]]. There, Ed learned that during consecutive fiscal
  years, 30–40% of total Air Force TOA (total obligation authority) budget was slashed and sent to
  the Air Force black budget before analysis/assessment by Air Force financial management (SAF/FM)
  — misappropriated funds Ed says supported T&E of derivative vehicles/ARVs.
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 37:37–38:54)*
- Ed's Office of Primary Responsibility (OPR) while at Edwards was [[aftte|AFTTE]] (Air Force
  Test and Evaluation), and he received letters of commendation signed by then-412th vice
  commander/commander Maj. Gen. [[dawn-m-dunlop|Dawn M. Dunlop]].
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 2:44:05–2:45:02, 1:36:52–1:37:16)*
