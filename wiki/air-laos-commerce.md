Air Laos Commerce (ASR "air La commercial") was, per [[eyes-wide-open]], one of four Corsican-run small charter airlines in Southeast Asia founded after the French withdrawal to carry opium from the Plain of Jars in northern Laos to Saigon and Bangkok; the early network was nicknamed "air opium". *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 44:56-45:42)*

## Claims

- Its owner is named in ASR as "bavans Rock Francis"/"Bavant Francis", a "flamboyant" Corsican based in Vientiane, introduced to [[ngo-dinh-nhu]]. The spelling is unverified, and "bavant" may be a first-name/title mangling. The host notes he shares a surname with Marseille mobster Marcel Francis (who took over his organization in 1958, the year of the alleged Nhu-Conein deal) but says no researcher has convincingly linked the two. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 45:42-46:18)*
- An August 1963 Life magazine article, "The Opium Must Go Through", interviews air-opium pilot "Rene anal Bal" (ASR; name not normalized here), who says he officially works for Air Laos Commerce, managed by a "debonair, pencil-mustached Corsican" named Francis in Vientiane, and flies dope out of Hmong villages in northern Laos to southern trade ports. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 46:23-47:10)*
- Host thesis: this is "the Asian side of the famed French Connection": Laos opium exports dropped 1954-58 during Diem's crackdown on the [[binh-xuyen]], then the Nhu-Francis alliance from 1958 restored the trade and put cash back in Hmong highland hands, coinciding with the first CIA contacts with [[vang-pao]]'s Hmong. He adds that Laotian opium then reached Marseille via Corsican mobsters, destined for North America (McCoy's "Cold War opium boom"). *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 47:10-48:37)*
- Host's inference: [[lucien-conein]], made an honorary Corsican Brotherhood member, forged the bond between the Diem regime and Corsican mobsters. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 44:46-45:14)*
