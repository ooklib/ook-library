Claims by [[dylan-borland]] on [[borland-michels-interview-2026]] about how he was treated after 2023-2025 whistleblowing over what he learned at [[bae-systems]] and saw at Langley. These are his allegations; he says he holds emails, texts and records, but nothing here is independently confirmed. The ODNI-related program-name claims are in [[rubiks-cube-program-claims]].

## Why he came forward

- After BAE colleagues, whom he believed were read into a legacy program, lost their jobs (one about two weeks before a 2023 event), and a person linked to the [[uap-task-force]] said lives were in danger, he decided to go to Congress, the [[intelligence-community-inspector-general]] and [[aaro]] to protect them. He says he did it to save lives, not because of UFOs. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:46:40-1:47:38, 1:49:03-1:49:36, 1:52:04-1:53:12)*
- He met congressional staffers in a SCIF on March 3 and then AARO at the end of March, telling them "what they needed to confirm there was a program". He refused to name the people or the program. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:53:12-1:53:35, 1:56:29-1:56:37, 2:01:33-2:02:00)*
- He did not know [[david-grush]] and says he never spoke with him. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:51:02-1:51:11)*

## AARO and ODNI

- AARO (then under [[sean-kirkpatrick]], later Jon Kosloski per the transcript's ASR "Kuzowski/Closki") sent an incorrect memorandum for record, asked him to make corrections, and, he says, later sent ODNI a false version while he held the signed true one. He says the false one inserted details to fish for names. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:58:05-2:00:22)*
- He says the ODNI [[odni-directors-initiatives-group]] told journalists he drew a fourth drawing and had committed treason, though a fourth drawing does not exist and contradicts the AARO report; he calls it falsified evidence for an offense carrying the death penalty. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:02:51-2:04:20)*
- He says former ICIG [[chuck-mccullough]] told him the whistleblowers legally did nothing wrong in what they disclosed. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:06:19-2:06:40)*
- He says [[tim-phillips-aaro]] acknowledged that Borland drew two drawings and that AARO was told what to classify by a security classification guide, and asks for that guide to be released. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:36:47-1:37:22)* He also says AARO had no original classification authority and had to poll industry for guidance. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:22:33-2:22:56)*
- He argues that disclosing "hypothetical classified information" to journalists undercuts any national-security defense for secrecy. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 7:04-7:46)*

## Legal theory

- Borland says the 2024 intelligence authorization changes (redefining "credible" and "urgent") let ICIG whistleblowers disclose CIA and Department of Energy secrets without espionage prosecution, and suggests the "born secret" restricted-data doctrine of the 1954 Atomic Energy Act was the counter-argument. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:03:42-1:05:17)* See [[atomic-energy-commission]].
- He notes the law bars classifying information to hide embarrassment, crime or finances, and claims that is happening. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:27:37-2:28:17)*

## Clearance, medical and physical

- Clearance "administrative withdrawal": after leaving a company, a clearance lapses after two years unused; a request for an updated SF86 sent to a former employer can lapse it within 30 days, leaving the holder in limbo. He says this was used against him (2015-2018 at BAE, again after his [[defense-threat-reduction-agency]] contract ended in 2024). He says he never failed a polygraph or had a violation. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:42:25-1:46:03)*
- VA experience: the VA denied he had done his drone-imagery job while treating him for PTSD, put him in a 2016-2017 program where he was polygraphed while shown flashing images and made to count backward from 100 by 7 to an air horn, and gave SSRIs that he says made him suicidal, prompting the psychiatrist to quit. He alleges a "confirmed CIA" doctor there knew where he worked. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:07:41-2:12:04, 2:13:04-2:14:22)*
- He links this loosely to government study of experiencers and cites a leaked phone call involving [[kit-green]], [[ed-may]] and Uri Geller about testing experiencers with MRIs and remote viewing ("hybrids"). *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:15:54-2:17:10)* See [[alien-human-hybridization-claims]] and [[project-stargate]].
- Per police, his car's brake lines were cut after he left BAE; he keeps a dead man's switch. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:18:37-2:19:34)*
- He believes his phone was duplicated by ODNI and that calls are monitored. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:49:41-1:50:23)*
- He mentions a 2024 database of classified documents on JWICS that he says NSA purged, and says he legally accessed databases as a counter-WMD analyst after 2023. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:18:25-1:18:50, 1:37:30-1:38:58)* See [[national-security-agency]].
