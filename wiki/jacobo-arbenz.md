Jacobo Arbenz (transcribed "Yacobo Arbens" in Eyes Wide Open's ASR captions) was the
democratically elected president of Guatemala overthrown by the CIA in 1954, per Eyes Wide Open,
at the lobbying of [[united-fruit-company|United Fruit]] and under the direction of
[[allen-dulles]] and [[john-foster-dulles]].

## Claims

- Democratically elected left-wing president of Guatemala, overthrown by the CIA in 1954 and
  replaced with what the host calls a "brutal western-friendly dictator," at the request of
  [[united-fruit-company|United Fruit]]. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 6:04-6:21)*

- Overthrown in [[operation-pbsuccess]] (1954), in which [[e-howard-hunt]] was instrumental. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 5:29-6:16)*
