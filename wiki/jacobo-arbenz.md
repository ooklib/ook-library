Jacobo Arbenz (transcribed "Yacobo Arbens" in Eyes Wide Open's ASR captions) was the
democratically elected president of Guatemala overthrown by the CIA in 1954, per Eyes Wide Open,
at the lobbying of [[united-fruit-company|United Fruit]] and under the direction of
[[allen-dulles]] and [[john-foster-dulles]].
