Gerb calls it part one of two: the Castle interview was to be released a week later, and the analysis should not be viewed without it. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 0:00-10:21, 45:22-45:30)*

## Notable claims by Gerb

- Gerb says Castle is a witness rather than a whistleblower, that he first heard of him and began vetting him in late April 2025, and that Castle then reached out to David Grusch and to [[steven-greer]], who introduced him to Rep. [[eric-burlison]]; a photo circulated in late July showing Castle with Greer, Burlison and [[michael-herrera]]. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:44:36-1:45:48)*
- Gerb says he flew to Castle's home, spent a couple of days with him, found him very nervous but gracious, and hopes to present Castle's testimony and this thesis to a congressman. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:58:40-1:59:17)*
- Gerb states he has zero evidence for the SRT hypothesis and no public evidence connecting the [[center-for-naval-analyses]] to legacy programs, only the words of his sources. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 58:02-58:19, 1:52:24-1:52:32)*
- Gerb says the 3D renders in the video leave the top of the craft blank because Castle saw only the underside and part of the side. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:56:32-1:56:49)*
