Lyn Buchanan, a former Army remote viewer (ASR "Lin Buchanan"). [[jason-jorjani]] says Buchanan told him in his living room of a trip to a deep underground facility in California. Only Jorjani's second-hand account exists in the wiki.

## Claims

- Jorjani says Buchanan psychically spied on Saddam Hussein during the first Gulf War and became ill when Hussein was ill. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 13:02)*
- Buchanan was taken to a "shack" whose floor was an elevator descending hundreds of feet to a facility where wreckage of a UFO that had collided with an aircraft was reconstructed FAA-style; he was asked to remote view the craft's guidance system and was told pilots were being trained to fly UFOs while managing time displacement. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:28:01, 1:29:26, 1:29:37)*
