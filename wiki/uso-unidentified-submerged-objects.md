Unidentified submerged objects (USOs) are UFOs seen entering, leaving or operating beneath water; the term overlaps with "transmedium UAP". [[uap-gerb]] argues USOs show the same shapes as aerial UAP (triangles, orbs, discs, cigars) and that their movements are anomalous in any fluid medium, so he sees no real difference between transmedium UAP and USOs. All specific cases below are attributed claims. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 0:00-0:33)*

## Claims

- Historical thread: [[operation-interloper]] (1951-53, [[dewey-fournet]]), a 24 April 1952 Air Force intelligence letter forwarding nine submarine-UFO reports (with ONI help; the reports are said to be unavailable), and [[janap-146c]] (1954) requiring waterborne UFO reports to be filed under secrecy penalties. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 1:14-6:44)*
- Gerb notes the Navy sightings listed in the 1950s come via Timothy Good's *Need to Know*, citing [[leonard-stringfield]], who was allegedly told of thousands of [[office-of-naval-intelligence]] UAP reports from 1952 to 1956. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 3:38-4:07)*
- The sender of the nine reports, "AFOIN-X(SG)", is read by Gerb as Air Force intelligence and (his inference) a special group; he notes [[edward-j-ruppelt]] said Air Force intelligence ran a fuller UFO investigation parallel to [[project-blue-book]], and that CUFON's 1979 FOIA requests about the group were denied on national-security grounds. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 4:07-5:08)*
- Jacques Vallee's 1985 journal ([[jacques-vallee]], *Forbidden Science: Pacific Heights*) says a source at the National Aviation Reporting Center on Anomalous Phenomena told him a large collection of UFO and USO files was stored at Norton Air Force Base, a site Gerb links to Steven Greer's alleged 1996 briefing to Congress ([[steven-greer]]). *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 7:05-7:38)*
- Unverified 2017 story: astronomer Marc D'Antonio, president of a company with Navy contracts, was taken on a submarine where sonar picked up a "fast mover" at several hundred knots (submarines and torpedoes roughly 40 knots); the commanding officer said to "log it and dog it"; a senior naval figure later told him USOs were common and that a program existed to log and speed-classify them, with reports going into a vault. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 7:46-8:55)*
- [[oke-shannon]]'s notes from the 1985 Advanced Theoretical Physics Working Group at [[bdm-international]] in McLean, published in 2022, list a Navy intelligence underwater UFO hotspot near Golfo San Matias off Argentina, plus land hotspots such as Mount Hayes, Alaska. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 9:03-9:54)*
- [[john-ramirez]] (former CIA) claimed in 2022 to have heard of a relationship between the Office of Naval Research and the CIA's Office of Scientific and Weapons Research, at its weapons intelligence, nonproliferation and arms control center, where USO materials are recovered and transferred to the CIA. Gerb calls this an unverified rabbit hole for a future video. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 9:54-10:24)*
- Cases: [[luis-elizondo]] (2022, Terry Virts interview, unnamed source) described a helicopter crew recovering test-fired cruise missiles near a Caribbean island above the Puerto Rico Trench, where a huge round black object twice rose beneath them, the second time swallowing the missile as a frogman climbed the rope; USN petty officer John Bowman (2010, USS Carl Vinson off Haiti) saw a roughly 20 ft white tic-tac object in the water dart into the depths, and was not debriefed; petty officer Kevin Thomas (1991, USS Nimitz) claimed a triangle near 1 km long rose from the ocean with no water dripping, then men in civilian clothes questioned him. Gerb says he cannot find Thomas's service record and approaches it skeptically. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 16:45-20:46)*
- Cases from the Gallaudet paper: the 2004 Tic Tac ([[2004-nimitz-encounter]]) hovering above roiling whitewater, the 2013 Aguadilla, Puerto Rico CBP thermal video (40-120 mph, entering and exiting the Atlantic, about 95 mph underwater, splitting in two), and a 1960 Shelburne Harbour, Nova Scotia case in which Royal Canadian Navy divers allegedly saw two disc-shaped craft on the seabed with occupants repairing one, and filmed it. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 14:26-16:29)*
