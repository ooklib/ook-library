Eyes Wide Open's term for a CIA strategy in which armed proxies are given a short initial injection of
aid and then fund themselves with the narcotics trade, off the books and beyond congressional
oversight; the host says [[operation-paper]] is its blueprint. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 30:40-31:55, 49:31-49:51)*

## Claims

- Two rationales: untraceable profits give plausible deniability, and the force becomes self-reliant.
  The host says it recurs in Afghanistan, Nicaragua, Guatemala and Laos. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 30:40-31:49)*
- Paired with a "client state" stepping stone (Thailand for Burma) and proprietary firms
  ([[civil-air-transport]], [[sea-supply-corporation]]). *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 48:39-49:31)*
- LAOS 1 claims the [[french-indochina]] administration set up 'some of the first narco counterinsurgency programs ever', later repurposed by the CIA, and calls Laos 'the test run' for decades of narco-fueled guerrilla wars; see [[secret-war-in-laos]]. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 4:16-4:58)*
