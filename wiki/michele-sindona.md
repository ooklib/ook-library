Michele Sindona is described in the `jm/cover-up` episode (as "Mikuel Syndona", with the speaker unsure of the pronunciation) as a mob financier who had worked with [[james-jesus-angleton]] and helped the mafia move money covertly. By 1964 the mafia allegedly asked him to launder all its money, and he created [[moneyrex]]. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:37:47, 1:38:10)*

He is said to have acquired banks, with the Vatican bank ([[vatican-bank]]) and [[charles-hambro]] taking the remaining shares. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:38:36, 1:38:44)*
