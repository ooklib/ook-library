A roughly ten-year [[bae-systems]] (formerly British Aerospace) study of antigravity, described in a public book by Dr. Ron Evans, raised by [[jesse-michels]] in [[borland-michels-interview-2026]] (transcribed "Greenlow").

## Claims

- Michels says Evans, a British researcher, describes Greenglow as a big project that developed interesting frameworks but concluded by debunking everything; Michels says he does not necessarily believe it and thinks it may be a cover. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:34:00-1:34:26)*
- Borland notes BAE has Falcon Works as its answer to Skunk Works, joint ventures with [[lockheed-martin]] Skunk Works, and that [[david-fravor]], the pilot in the [[2004-nimitz-encounter]] ("Commander David Fraver"), reportedly works at BAE, which Borland says he cannot confirm. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:34:53-1:35:29)*
