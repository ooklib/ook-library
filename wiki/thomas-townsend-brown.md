Harald Malmgren).md>) 2:34:43-2:35:15, 2:40:32-2:40:44, 2:58:10-2:58:34, 3:43:55-3:44:29)*

Harald Malmgren).md>) 2:40:44-2:41:06, 2:41:39-2:41:54)*
- Host: LeMay was interested in Brown's work (unverified). *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 2:34:11-2:34:17)*

## Biography and effect (jm/townsend-brown)

Jesse Michels' 2024 documentary, based on Paul Schatzkin's biography *The Man Who Mastered Gravity* and interviews (Jan Lundquist, Linda Brown, Nick Cook), presents Brown as a "Tesla meets Dostoevsky"-type figure, "the original UFO architect" and godfather of American dark science; all below is the host's or interviewees' claim.

- Born 1905 in Zanesville, Ohio, wealthy family; wireless telegraph at 12; a Catalina teenage encounter with a ball of light said to have started his life work. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 7:34-8:20, 1:40:47-1:41:35)*
- Biefeld-Brown effect (see [[biefeld-brown-effect]]): asymmetric capacitor, negative plate chases positive, allegedly in vacuum too; tested by Jacques Coron in Paris in 1956; the 1990 Air Force test used only 19 kV; Michels calls the Wikipedia page "blatantly manipulated". *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 8:53-12:38, 1:47:34-1:47:44)*
- "Five reliable witnesses": Coron, [[agnew-bahnson]], Maj. Gen. Victor Bertrandias (1952: "I saw a model of a flying saucer"), Paul Biefeld (affidavit) and [[edward-teller]]. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 12:38-20:56)*
- 1943 FBI file: knew more about radar than anyone in the Navy; Navy played dumb on FOIA and lost the "TT Brown electrogravity device" appraisal. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 21:01-22:40)*
- Alleged disinformation tactic: after a 1950 Pearl Harbor demo was compromised by a Soviet spy janitor, he showed only the fluid-dielectric "lifter" version, the "wounded prairie chicken routine". *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 22:47-24:26)*
- Alleged transfer to [[b-2-stealth-bomber]] via [[guidance-technologies]] and Northrop, and to Aurora work ([[aurora-astra]]). *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 25:07-29:56, 42:10-43:13)*
- Later work: "The Structure of Space" (early 1940s at Martin; gravity wells/hills, wave riding), lost 1929 "minor quantum" paper, sidereal radiation, revival of the ether; see [[extended-electrodynamics]]. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 1:20:26-1:28:16)*
