Air Force base at Hampton Roads, Virginia (not to be confused with the CIA's Langley, Virginia), adjacent to a separate [[nasa]] research campus with its own gates and fence. The site of [[dylan-borland]]'s 2012 sighting ([[borland-langley-triangle-2012]]) and the 2023 drone incursions ([[langley-drone-incursions-2023]]).

## Claims

- Borland notes the CIA/Langley confusion, describes NASA Langley as where lunar-mission pressurization and space-flight technology was developed, and says he saw weather balloons tested from the NASA hangar. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 43:27-44:13, 27:14-27:48)*
- Borland says employees who work at NASA Langley tagged puzzles, labyrinths, pyramids and hieroglyph-like images on Google Maps, see [[langley-google-maps-tags-claims]]. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 41:08-41:46)*
- Borland places the base in the I-81 corridor of UFO reports between Norfolk/Langley, Winchester, Harrisburg, Washington and [[naval-air-station-patuxent-river]], a corridor he links to early CIA director [[roscoe-hillenkoetter]]'s New York board role, and Pittsburgh's Bettis atomic power lab and Kecksburg ([[kecksburg-ufo-crash-1965]]). *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 10:37-12:23)*
- Gerb had earlier suggested Air Combat Command (headquartered at Langley) could be behind an operational triangle, see [[dylan-borland]].
