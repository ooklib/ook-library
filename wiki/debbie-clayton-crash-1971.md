Witness Debbie Clayton's account to [[leonard-stringfield]] (status report 6, 1991) of a daytime crash about 15 miles outside [[edwards-air-force-base]] in 1971. Unverified.

## Claims (gerb/edwards-afb)

- Debbie and her girlfriend's family investigated a crash from the sky and found an acorn-shaped craft: seamless, no windows, apparently one-piece, dark greenish-gray, faintly warm, with no dust clinging to its shell. Gerb compares it to the [[kecksburg-ufo-crash-1965]] object. An Air Force car and truck arrived within about 14 minutes and men with rifles ordered civilians off. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 36:27-37:34, 45:30)*
