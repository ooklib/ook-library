Neutralist, populist, anti-corruption Lao paratrooper captain who on August 9, 1960 seized Vientiane and overthrew the CIA-backed [[phoumi-nosavan]] regime, per [[eyes-wide-open]]. ASR renders him "Kong Le", "KLA" and "Kong". *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 45:45-46:20)*

## Claims

- Demanded an end to corruption and foreign interference and the reinstatement of [[souvanna-phouma]]. He is quoted saying US aid "had corrupted many government officials", and his ethos is summarized as "why should Lao fight Lao". *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 46:20-46:52)*
- Host notes the irony that he had been trained by US Special Forces a year earlier, under [[operation-white-star]], which the host takes as proof that White Star failed to produce a Lao army subordinate to US interests. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 46:03-46:10, 1:08:25-1:08:46)*
- Driven out of Vientiane in December 1960 after three days of street fighting. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 47:53-48:08)*
- Host: he was "by no means wrong" in his anti-corruption assessment; foreign funds mostly lined the pockets of bureaucrats and generals. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 48:20-48:45)*
