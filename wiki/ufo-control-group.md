Top layer of Gerb's [[ufo-legacy-programs|legacy program pyramid]]: a now "nebulous position"
loosely overseen by a committee of 27, formed in 1994 specifically to hide the legacy programs
from President [[bill-clinton]]'s disclosure appetite. *(4:38–4:56)*

## Claims

- Historically embodied by the NSC's covert-activities oversight structure — the 5412
  Committee, Special Group, and 303 Committee. *(4:56–5:03)*
- Last "ruled with an iron fist" by [[dick-cheney]] as Vice President; when Cheney left office
  in 2009, [[james-clapper]] took over as "mob boss" over the fractured legacy ecosystem.
  *(5:03–5:18)*
- Gerb terms the highest-status insider ("MJ1" in Majestic 12 terminology) as the late
  [[donald-kerr]]. *(1:38:51–1:39:06)*

## Claims (from the "Secrecy" Vol. 1 video — origin narrative)

- UAP Gerb traces the control group's historical lineage directly to the Manhattan Project's
  [[top-policy-group|Top Policy Group]] — a small civilian/political oversight body (FDR/Truman,
  Stimson, [[george-c-marshall|Marshall]], [[vannevar-bush|Bush]], Conant) that let the president
  retain personal control while bypassing normal government channels. He theorizes Truman carried
  this model onto the newly formed [[national-security-council|National Security Council]] in
  1947, and that Eisenhower then "deep-sixed" it one level further by creating the NSC's
  [[nsc-5412-committee|5412 Committee ("Special Group")]] on 15 March 1954, using the same
  principles: small readin membership, minimal paper trail, presidential control via a trusted
  intermediary. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:32:35–1:33:12, 1:36:48–1:37:24)*
- Ties the disputed [[majestic-12|MJ-12]] group directly to this lineage: the Eisenhower Briefing
  Document places the founding MJ-12 meeting on 24 September 1947, the same date Truman's real
  appointment records confirm a meeting occurred between Truman, Forrestal, and Bush; Gerb also
  notes accused MJ-12 member Gordon Gray later became Eisenhower's national security adviser
  (1958–1961) and thus a literal 5412 Committee member — evidence, per Gerb, that MJ-12 (or its
  successor) continued as the control group through the 5412 Committee's tenure and its
  evolution into Nixon's 303 Committee. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  1:29:51–1:30:20, 1:33:40–1:34:13)*
- States he still believes an NSC element retains oversight of "one singular UFO crash-retrieval
  portfolio" to the present day, spanning DOE, the Air Force, the CIA's Directorate of Science and
  Technology, and select primes/FFRDCs — the structure he argues the *Age of Disclosure*
  documentary partially outlined while omitting the NSC's coordinating role specifically to
  protect [[james-clapper|James Clapper]], who (per [[david-grush|David Grush]]'s own on-record
  statements) "was well aware of the crash retrieval issue, managed the crash retrieval issue,"
  and placed people in critical roles both publicly and non-publicly while DNI, alongside deputies
  Stephanie O'Sullivan and Michael Dempsey. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  1:34:34–1:36:13)*
- Reiterates that oversight was "fully scattered into the wind" after 1994, when
  [[bill-perry|William Perry]] reorganized [[special-access-program-oversight-committee|SAPOC]]
  into a "quasi government and industry group of just over two dozen individuals," and that
  [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] was "the last man who possessed the tradecraft and Machiavellian
  politics to hold together this fractured shell" until leaving office in 2009, after which
  [[james-clapper|James Clapper]] became the closest thing to a "mob boss" — himself a markedly
  weaker figure than Cheney. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 3:03:48–3:04:14,
  2:22:06–2:22:38)*
