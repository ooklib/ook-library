In [[eyes-wide-open]]'s War Conspiracy 2 the Rockefeller family is presented as a second elite network ("what I'll be calling the Rockefeller Network") operating alongside the [[china-lobby]], with long involvement in both Asia and Latin America, oil interests, and CIA-linked technology finance. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 17:26-17:48)*

## Claims

- China Medical Board investment since the mid-1910s (under the Rockefeller Foundation); the family funded the majority of the [[council-on-foreign-relations]] from the 1930s onward. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 17:48-18:21)*
- Post-war the clan was headed by five brothers, sons of John D. Rockefeller Jr.; the host names four: John D. III (philanthropy, China Medical Board, CFR; founded the [[asia-society]] in 1956), [[nelson-rockefeller]], [[laurance-rockefeller]] (venture capital, [[rockefeller-brothers-inc]]) and David (Chase Manhattan Bank executive, later chairman and CEO; the host says he had a "cozy relationship" with [[henry-kissinger]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 18:59-19:50, 21:16-21:47)*
- Latin American oil interests: the host says the Rockefellers were "deeply involved" in Latin American oil and threatened by [[jacobo-arbenz]]'s nationalization push; [[nelson-rockefeller]] was inside the Eisenhower government. Quoting Suzanne Jonas, the [[united-fruit-company]] integrated "the Rockefellers' Standard Oil interests, the Morgans and the Boston Blue Bloods." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 10:20-11:24)*
- Oil in Southeast Asia: Socony Mobile executives, hosted by the Asia Society in the early 1960s, allegedly advocated direct US intervention in Southeast Asia; host notes South China Sea offshore drilling doubled 1964-68 and says oil interests are "very rarely mentioned" in Vietnam War discussion. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 20:07-21:16)*
- In 1959-60 Castro nationalized "Rockefeller owned Jersey Standard Oil refineries" along with United Fruit plantations. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 48:37-48:53)*
- The Rockefeller Brothers Fund (founded 1940, led by Laurance per the host) sponsored the [[special-studies-project]], which the host calls a private think tank to institutionalize the "perpetual war economy." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 25:38-26:26)*

## Relationships
