A short footage-analysis video (about 17 minutes, dated 2024-02-05) in which [[uap-gerb]] performs a critical analysis of the FLYBY footage, a low-quality clip of a saucer-shaped object beside a jet's wing. The source page is [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qD1KnudMCO0). The description says the clip shows 3 of the 5 observables of [[luis-elizondo]] (no visible means of propulsion, instant acceleration, antigravity). *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 0:00-1:40)*

- Framing: he calls it one of his favorite pieces of alleged UFO footage and, if real, the best closely filmed UFO encounter in the public realm; he steps away from his "documentary style fact analysis" for some speculation. *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 1:14-1:39,)*
