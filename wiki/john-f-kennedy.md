US president from January 21, 1961. In [[eyes-wide-open]] LAOS 4 he is the president who inherited [[laos]] from [[dwight-d-eisenhower]], approved and then cancelled the planned bombing ([[operation-milpond]]), and pursued a diplomatic settlement. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 52:54-53:18, 1:08:52-1:09:10)*

## Claims

- Took office after a close win over Nixon following his hawkish advisers at first; Eisenhower warned him about Laos. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 52:59-53:18, 1:08:52-1:09:10)*
- March 23, 1961 televised press conference (quoted: "all we want in Laos is peace not war... a truly neutral government not a cold war pawn"), which the host says some scholars read as prepping the public for intervention; he had ramped up US Air Force activity over Laos. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:12:15-1:13:46)*
- After [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]] he viewed the CIA with high skepticism, fired [[allen-dulles]] and Richard Bissell, and relied on White House staff. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:15:52-1:17:05)*
- July 23, 1962: 14 states, including the US, France, both Vietnams, the USSR and China, signed a new Geneva agreement on Lao neutrality; the host says it left the CIA and North Vietnam operating unfettered and effectively handed Laos to the CIA. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:17:31-1:18:39)*
