US president from January 21, 1961. In [[eyes-wide-open]] LAOS 4 he is the president who inherited [[laos]] from [[dwight-d-eisenhower]], approved and then cancelled the planned bombing ([[operation-milpond]]), and pursued a diplomatic settlement. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 52:54-53:18, 1:08:52-1:09:10)*

## Claims

- Took office after a close win over Nixon following his hawkish advisers at first; Eisenhower warned him about Laos. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 52:59-53:18, 1:08:52-1:09:10)*
- March 23, 1961 televised press conference (quoted: "all we want in Laos is peace not war... a truly neutral government not a cold war pawn"), which the host says some scholars read as prepping the public for intervention; he had ramped up US Air Force activity over Laos. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:12:15-1:13:46)*
- After [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]] he viewed the CIA with high skepticism, fired [[allen-dulles]] and Richard Bissell, and relied on White House staff. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:15:52-1:17:05)*
- July 23, 1962: 14 states, including the US, France, both Vietnams, the USSR and China, signed a new Geneva agreement on Lao neutrality; the host says it left the CIA and North Vietnam operating unfettered and effectively handed Laos to the CIA. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:17:31-1:18:39)*

## Additional claims (JFK and the CIA)


- Host frames the primer as opening Season 3 ("The Road to Dallas"): after the Bay of Pigs, JFK wanted to "splinter the CIA into a thousand pieces," but the agency stayed "more powerful than ever," and the Gulf Coast paramilitary "morass" is what "countless researchers" speculate spawned the plot that killed him, a plot the host says he will advance toward through the season. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 0:01-0:38, 3:32-3:44)*
- Shakeup: Dulles forced out (resignation announced November 29, 1961, a day after JFK gave him the National Security Medal), [[charles-cabell]] and [[richard-bissell]] out by early 1962; JFK personally appointed only one of the three replacements, [[john-mccone]], "his most critical mistake" per the host. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 5:55-8:34)*
- Host: Kennedy habitually appointed Republicans to "doomed to fail" posts (McCone at CIA, McNamara at Defense, Henry Cabot Lodge as 1963 ambassador to South Vietnam) to shield Democrats; this is described as speculation ("it has been speculated"). *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 8:47-9:40)*
- Offered Bissell a new science-and-technology department at CIA (allegedly including development of the Lockheed A-12 U-2 replacement); Bissell declined and left for IDA. See [[richard-bissell]]. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 15:47-16:42)*
- Anti-assassination stance: host argues Kennedy "clearly and consistently" refused to sanction CIA assassinations of foreign leaders, while acknowledging a "dark Camelot" revisionist strain; he notes that much of the evidence for JFK's sanction comes from people tied to [[zr-rifle]] ([[william-harvey]], [[ted-shackley]]). Dick Goodwin is quoted: "We can't get into that kind of thing or we would all be targets," and, on the CIA's alleged Trujillo plot, "The United States is not to get involved in any assassinations. I'd like to get rid of Trujillo, but not that way" (via Talbot, *Brothers*; also in *The Fish Is Red*); Trujillo was gunned down under a month later in what the host calls a CIA-affiliated plot. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 30:34-31:52, 1:02:21-1:03:26)*
- Host: shaken by the Lumumba assassination (photo February 13, 1961). The host says at different points that Lumumba was killed by "Belgian and CIA backed militias" and that it "was not the CIA who ultimately assassinated Lumumba, but Belgian-backed rebels" (internal tension in the source). *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 31:59-32:52)*
- Host: some White House aides "potentially knew about and hid" the CIA's continued executive-action work, possibly including [[robert-f-kennedy]], who was more lenient than JFK on assassination and "may have even sanctioned" continued mob use; the host says RFK's stance is "unfairly projected" on JFK. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 33:11-34:36)*
- Named Lansdale to run [[operation-mongoose]] from November 1961, and made RFK head of the White House Cuba task force. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 45:43-45:56, 47:17-47:30)*
- Host mentions speculation of an "Israeli element" in the assassination motivated by JFK's attempts to rein in the Israeli nuclear program (to be covered "quite a bit later"); the McCone appointment is tied to Israeli-nuclear reporting. See [[john-mccone]]. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 12:47-13:13)*
