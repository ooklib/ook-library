[[diana-pasulka]] describes how research for her 2014 book *Heaven Can Wait* (on purgatory and Catholic "ascent narratives") led her to catalogue European, Canadian and US Catholic reports of aerial phenomena from about 1200 to 1800: discs, light orbs, "flying houses" (the House of Loreto), sometimes read as souls from purgatory needing prayer. A friend remarked they sounded like a Spielberg movie; she kept them as data and later called them "angel contact events". *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 11:58-14:33)*

## Aerospace interest

- After she published the material she says she received inquiries from many people at aerospace companies, who asked what people saw, the color of the light, what the objects looked like and, most interestingly to her, whether people reported injuries; they did. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 14:33-15:19)*
- One correspondent, pseudonymized "Tyler" ([[tim-taylor-nasa]]), was a space-program insider and government liaison to Elon Musk for certain missions, who told her that debris from UFO crash sites had been acquired and reverse engineered; this was in 2016, and she went with pseudonymous people, while [[gary-nolan]] has since come out under his own name. Pasulka says Tyler is a mission controller working in the Space Force. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 15:19-16:24)*
- She met Nolan around 2014 and says he was recruited to work with people "in the program"; she saw some of his research on radiation-burn-like effects on people and found comparable injuries in the historical record. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:00:26-1:01:24)*
- Pasulka says she "never proposed" that people try to contact these things, given the injury history: learn about it, "but don't engage", and return to one's own religious tradition. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:01:24-1:01:54)*

## Framing

- Pasulka treats these accounts as an academic (religious-studies) not a believer; her shock was that highly credentialed scientists treated them as real. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 16:24-16:39)*
- Michels adds that historical angel encounters can be "extremely violent" and painful, not cherubic. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 59:08-59:32)*
- Hierarchies: Thomas Aquinas and the Syrian Neoplatonist Iamblichus (transcribed "amus") made angel taxonomies with described effects such as "irradiated light". *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 25:36-26:01)*
