Late in the [[dawn-of-a-new-age-manuscript]], [[philip-j-corso]] recounts that while commanding the missile firing range at Red Canyon (White Sands) in 1957-58, he sheltered in an abandoned mine and met face to face a non-human being that communicated telepathically. The being could not leave because nearby active radar interfered with its craft; asked what he would get for lowering radar coverage, it answered "a new world if you can take it". Corso then had the radar shut down. He drew the being and told the story to [[john-b-alexander]], [[george-knapp]] (a lost "weaponized" interview) and [[jacques-vallee]]. *(Unverified claim.)* *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:16:33-1:18:10, 1:24:36-1:24:54)*

## Claims and notes

- [[danny-sheehan]] says an 8mm home movie Corso made for his grandchildren, shown to Sheehan, tells the same story, but dates it to July 1945 at Alamogordo and White Sands near Trinity; Gerb corrects the date to 1957-58 and notes Sheehan finds this "extraordinarily persuasive" and that aerospace firms would obfuscate. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:18:19-1:21:28)*
- Corso and some Roswell researchers theorise radar interfered with craft navigation, causing crashes; Gerb is skeptical. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:17:35-1:17:52)*
- Gerb's reservations: the being differs from the Fort Riley body, it is not in the affidavit, it was told long after the initial disclosures and is relegated to the manuscript's end. He first read it as a dream or metaphor. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 1:21:37-1:22:19, 1:24:05-1:24:45)*
