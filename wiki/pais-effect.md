The "pais effect" is [[salvatore-pais]]'s own coined term (named partly in homage to physicist Abraham Pais, Einstein's biographer, not out of self-regard, per his own account) for controlled motion of electrically charged matter — from solid to plasma — driven by accelerated vibration and/or spin, producing extremely high energy densities and electromagnetic energy fluxes. This is a claim from a single patent-holder's own physics framework, not an established or peer-reviewed effect; treat as attributed and speculative.

## Claims (jm/pais)

- Defined as controlled motion of electrically charged objects, usually from solid to plasma, via accelerated vibration or spin, generating extremely high energy densities and hence electromagnetic energy fluxes; specifically involves cold plasmas driven far from equilibrium (e.g. xenon gas oscillating in an annular channel under a 100 THz alternating electric field). *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 27:03-27:55)*
- Derived from Oliver Heaviside's version of Maxwell's equations coupled with a harmonic oscillator; scaling analysis of this combination is what Pais says yields the extreme electric/magnetic field values (~10^18 V/m) in his patents. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 13:15-13:50, 21:33-22:14)*
- Said to polarize the quantum vacuum, described via the analogy of pushing on one side of a sloppy water-filled ziplock bag: a pressure gradient is set up and the vacuum's own enormous energy density (Pais cites ~10^44 units) then "does work" for the device. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 29:35-29:58)*
- Said to also underlie a claimed room-temperature-superconductor mechanism (a bipolaron-based mechanism, per [[victor-lagno]]) distinct from standard BCS theory; this version is termed by Pais the "pais-effect-driven room-temperature superconductor." *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 40:24-40:42, 46:28-46:42)*
- Pais says only ~10^-8 of the electrical charge needed to demonstrate the effect (10^0) was achieved in an experimental Navy high-energy-electromagnetic-field-generator effort. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 26:10-26:29)*
- [[jesse-michels]] says that on seeing the Ashton Forbes MH370 "orbs" footage, he immediately thought of the pais effect, and that Pais himself told [[ashton-forbes]] the same on Forbes's first podcast appearance. See [[ashton-forbes]]. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 0:15-0:40, 1:48:03-1:49:31)*
- Michels analogizes the effect, which Pais says operates "far from equilibrium," to Pais's own remarks elsewhere in the interview about equilibrium versus disequilibrium in physics generally (e.g. renormalization, phase transitions). *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 1:33:23-1:33:41)*
