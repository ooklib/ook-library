Two-term US president (outgoing in 1961) who, per [[eyes-wide-open]] citing Arthur Schlesinger, briefed President-elect John F. Kennedy that [[laos]], not Vietnam, was his chief Southeast Asian worry. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 0:44-1:07)*

## Claims

- Had supported Laos with millions of dollars in aid and military equipment; called it "the key to Southeast Asia" and saw it slipping into civil war. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 1:10-1:23)*
- Host: "at least half correct" as Laos became a war-torn battlefield, though mistaken on Vietnam; he understood Laos's importance. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 1:48-2:10)*

- LAOS 4: his administration funded the Royal Lao Army and, per the host, dispatched the CIA and State Department to meddle in Lao politics; his warning to Kennedy about Laos is recontextualized in LAOS 4. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 28:16-28:36, 52:59-53:24)*

As Vice President he had [[richard-nixon]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 4:14-5:00, 48:46-49:09, 32:42-32:49)*
