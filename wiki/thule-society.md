Occult/nationalist society in Munich, founded (as told by [[jason-jorjani]]) by Baron Rudolf von Sebottendorf after his initiation in Kurdistan into an Iranian order he calls "Aleak" and describes as surviving Mithraists. Thule is the Germanic conception of Atlantis, an island continent whose super-civilisation the Thule Society believed progenitor of the Aryan race, drawn partly from Blavatsky's Theosophy. Jorjani says the concept of Vril (from Bulwer-Lytton's *The Coming Race*, 1871) was important to it, but he found no independent Vril Society.

## From Banijay's "Secrets of the Nazi Elite" (banijay/nazi-elite)

Michael Fitzgerald says the Thule Society had occult overtones but was primarily politically oriented, a romantic mix of Grail stories, Germanic folklore and science fiction, that it "visualised its enemies as dead" and also committed many murders. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 13:07, 14:21, 14:29)* Members allegedly included Alfred Rosenberg and [[dietrich-eckart]], who held the material world evil and believed ancestors lived underground and would return to rule. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 14:43, 15:23)* The narration links it with the [[vril-society]] and the pre-war Bavarian seance. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 18:36)* See [[nazi-elite-secret-societies-documentary-2023]].
