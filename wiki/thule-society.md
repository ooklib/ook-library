Occult/nationalist society in Munich, founded (as told by [[jason-jorjani]]) by Baron Rudolf von Sebottendorf after his initiation in Kurdistan into an Iranian order he calls "Aleak" and describes as surviving Mithraists. Thule is the Germanic conception of Atlantis, an island continent whose super-civilisation the Thule Society believed progenitor of the Aryan race, drawn partly from Blavatsky's Theosophy. Jorjani says the concept of Vril (from Bulwer-Lytton's *The Coming Race*, 1871) was important to it, but he found no independent Vril Society.
