National Security Council directive signed by [[harry-truman]] in June 1948 that authorized the CIA to conduct covert operations through the [[office-of-policy-coordination]] (OPC, initially "Office of Special Projects", renamed 1 September 1948). Ordinary history; the UFO claims are [[uap-gerb]]'s.

## Claims

- OPC was to "plan and conduct covert operations" alongside the Joint Chiefs and to operate independently of other CIA components, which Gerb likens to the siloed chains of oversight of the legacy programs; he notes [[david-grush]] said OPC was involved in UFO efforts in his Judicial Watch interview. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 30:45-31:54)*
- Gerb "understands" NSC 10/2 also established the steering group for UFO retrieval, study and exploitation, hidden within the directive and commonly called MAG 12 / [[majestic-12]]: "there was a real Majestic 12", a term program gray beards still use. It comprised key NSC, CIA and Air Force individuals. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 31:54-32:40)*
- Gerb still champions the leaked [[eisenhower-briefing-document]] (MJ-12 formed 24 September 1947) as possibly real, and suggests earlier consolidation efforts began in September 1947, but says the durable power structure sits under the NSC covert-activities oversight from NSC 10/2 onward. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 32:40-33:38)*
- Extends earlier `gerb/secrecy_1` claims (which placed MJ-12 inside the 1954 [[nsc-5412-committee]]) by pushing the NSC hiding place back to 1948.
