Edward Barnes is a former CIA officer who, per Eyes Wide Open, told The Nation in 1992 that he was involved in the French stay-behind and described its structure. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 43:18-43:49)*

## Claims

- Said the French program consisted of several dozen individually recruited men each building small networks, that he could meet at most ten recruits at once, and that it was ordered closed as early as 1953. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 43:23-44:21)*
