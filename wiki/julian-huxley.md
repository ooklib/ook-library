Julian Huxley (1887–1975), the biologist, is described by Green & Pleasant as first director of
UNESCO, president of the British [[eugenics-society]], and the man who coined the term
"transhumanism" in 1957. He was the brother of [[aldous-huxley]], George Orwell's French teacher
at Eton.

## Claims

- Became first director of UNESCO; served as president of the British Eugenics Society; coined
  the word "transhumanism" in 1957
