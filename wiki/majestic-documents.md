The corpus of allegedly leaked documents about [[majestic-12]] and related bodies, held by [[ryan-s-wood]] and [[dr-robert-wood]] at majesticdocuments.com (over 30 documents discussed in the video; Gerb says 33-35 of the reports are listed). Gerb's framing: the group and documents cannot be judged without studying origins, content, provenance and links to acknowledged declassified files. The FBI declared the first document "bogus"; skeptics include Philip Klass, and Jan Aldrich criticized the manual. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 0:00, 0:48, 57:25)*

## Origins and provenance (per Ryan Wood and Gerb)

- About 3,500 pages, 109+ documents, 1984-1999, from seven sources. First was the [[eisenhower-briefing-document]] received by TV producer Jaime Shandera in 1984 on undeveloped film; the FBI took it to other agencies, none claimed it, and it was declared bogus despite [[stanton-friedman]]'s authentication; other copies later surfaced with different marginalia and hole-punching. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 5:11, 5:36, 6:19)*
- The [[special-operations-manual-som1-01]] came in 1994 as undeveloped 35mm film to Don Berliner. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 12:44)*
- Three major sources per Gerb: [[thomas-cantwheel]] (IPU report and field order, Hillenkoetter "magic" memo, first annual report, JFK-to-CIA-director memo), "Selena", alleged daughter of Cantwheel (ten short memos, the Twining "White Hot" report, an Eisenhower-to-Oppenheimer memo, a Twining-to-Truman directive), and "Source S-1"/Source One (the [[burned-memo]]). All to [[timothy-cooper]]. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:11:52, 1:12:00, 1:12:33)*
- Delivery methods: two undeveloped-film drops (1984, 1994), mailings from Sacramento, mailbox drops placed in Cooper's box without postage, and a mailing from the FOIA office at Fort Meade of an original onion-skin manuscript (see [[bowen-manuscript]]). *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:07:39, 1:08:04, 1:08:40)*
- Why leaks occurred: Wood says nobody knows who leaked or why. He notes a Bush-to-Eisenhower letter about "celestial gifts" (Eisenhower said be quiet and win the war); Gerb notes leaks came after the [[robertson-panel-1953]]/Condon era low. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:06:40, 1:07:06)*
- Gerb's own theory: after the 1954 [[atomic-energy-act-1954]]-era AEC agreement that allegedly moved NHI craft/bodies into "trans-classified foreign nuclear materials", documents dated after 1954 hold far less operational detail; Wood agrees Air Force officers "got the rug pulled out" and one leaked the manual. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:16:48, 1:17:39)*

## Documents corroborated in official archives (per Wood)

- Cutler-to-Twining memo (Robert Cutler, National Security Council, "NSC MJ-12 special studies group", 1954), original onion skin in the Library of Congress manuscript division. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 58:48)*
- A Joint Intelligence Objectives Agency memo (Paperclip-related, College Park) bearing "CIA MJ-12". *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 59:34)*
- The Army's acknowledgment that the [[interplanetary-phenomenon-unit]] existed. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:00:04)*
- [[stanton-friedman]]'s 1989 National Archives check: the Truman administration was indeed briefing Eisenhower on 18 Nov 1952. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 10:20)*
- Friedman quote: EBD, TFM (Truman-Forrestal memo) and CTM (Cutler-Twining memo) "almost certainly genuine", the Psalm 101 (SOM1-01) memo "very likely genuine". *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 14:37)*

- Wood notes the original list surprisingly included Carl Sagan (ASR "Carl Sean"), a noted debunker, but Friedman found correspondence with other members. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 24:06)*

## Gerb's conclusion

Gerb does not argue every document is valid but concludes MJ-12 "likely was and possibly is" a real group tasked with study, exploitation, reverse engineering and crash retrieval, today probably involving the private sector; the files are a snapshot of the early players and procedures. In earlier videos he had thought the files a mix of information and disinformation. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:34:05, 1:38:24)*
