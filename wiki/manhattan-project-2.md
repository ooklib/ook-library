Gerb's nickname for the entire alleged [[ufo-legacy-programs|UFO legacy program]] apparatus,
drawing an explicit parallel to the secrecy and scale of the original Manhattan Project. Gerb
argues the name is more than metaphor: per [[david-grush|David Grush]], the UFO legacy-program
portfolio's original security apparatus was an adapted, abridged version of the Manhattan
Project's own security architecture, "plucked" and overlaid onto the UFO issue because that
architecture had already proven effective for atomic-bomb secrecy. *(2:32–2:53,
[secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 33:32–33:56, 35:35–35:54)*

## Claims

- [[vannevar-bush]] is cast as aiding in establishing the secrecy architecture of both the
  original Manhattan Project and this alleged "2.0" effort. *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 20:22–20:34)*
- Gerb frames the Roswell wreckage's arrival at [[wright-patterson-air-force-base|
  Wright-Patterson]] as setting this "Goliath National Effort" ablaze. *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:01:11–1:01:26)*

## Claims (from the "Secrecy" Vol. 1 video — the fuller thesis)

- Gerb's central thesis of this two-part investigation: the alleged UFO legacy-program apparatus
  formed and matured over roughly 1947–1954, directly copying five "foundational security
  pillars" from the original Manhattan Project: **compartmentalization**, **organizational
  architecture**, **physical security**, **classification and information control**, and
  **political shield/cover**. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 38:20–38:52,
  36:08–36:32)*
- **Compartmentalization**: ported over General [[leslie-groves|Leslie Groves]]'s strict
  need-to-know practices, mirrored in claims by [[david-grush]] and (with explicit low
  credibility) [[bob-lazar|Bob Lazar]] about teams unable to interface across disciplines, and in
  claims from an unnamed alleged Navy/FFRDC legacy-security source that personnel held multiple
  cover roles (e.g. scientists doing accounting work) to keep the number of read-in people as
  small as possible. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 40:20–41:50)*
- **Organizational architecture**: the [[manhattan-engineer-district|Manhattan Engineer
  District]]'s mundane cover-name/cover-district structure and short chain of command (Groves
  reporting directly to [[george-c-marshall|George Marshall]]) is compared by Gerb to the modern
  Air Force [[rapid-capabilities-office|Rapid Capabilities Office]] and the
  [[hidden-wing-air-force-program|Hidden Wing]] program's administrative lineage through Air
  Materiel Command / Air Research and Development Command / [[air-force-special-weapons-center|
  AFSWC]] / [[air-force-material-command|AFMC]]. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  42:31–52:52)*
- **Physical security**: recovered-vehicle custody delegated to AEC/DOE/NNSA national labs
  (Sandia, Los Alamos, Oak Ridge), plus a "hiding in plain sight" strategy Gerb traces to the
  Manhattan Project's Chicago Pile-1 (built under a squash court at the University of Chicago, so
  secret the Chicago mayor was not informed) — a technique he says persists in modern legacy-
  program sites disguised as ordinary corporate parks, office buildings, or derelict facilities
  (e.g. the [[tahone-radar-cross-section-facility|Tahone radar cross-section facility]]).
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:04:11–1:07:14)*
- **Classification and information control**: the 1946 Atomic Energy Act's "restricted data" and
  the 1954 [[atomic-energy-act-1954|Atomic Energy Act]]'s expanded "special nuclear material" and
  "transclassified foreign nuclear information" categories are treated as a separate statutory
  classification "track two," parallel to and independent of ordinary executive-order-based
  classification/[[special-access-program|SAP]] channels. *([secrecy-1](../raw/
  jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:09:56–1:11:02)*
- **Political shield/cover**: the Manhattan Project's [[top-policy-group|Top Policy Group]] is
  presented as the direct template for the [[ufo-control-group]], carried forward via the 1947
  National Security Act/[[national-security-council|NSC]] and Eisenhower's
  [[nsc-5412-committee|5412 Committee]]. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  1:24:35–1:25:04, 1:36:48–1:37:24)*
- Gerb dates the alleged formation/maturing of this apparatus to roughly 1947–1954, after which it
  "stood stalwart" for several decades until fracturing during the 1980s
  ([[special-access-program|SAP]] reforms, the [[yellow-fruit|Yellow Fruit]] scandal) and finally
  losing all centralized leadership after the 1994
  [[special-access-program-oversight-committee|SAPOC/SRG]] reorganization and [[dick-cheney|Dick
  Cheney]]'s 2009 departure from office. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  36:15–36:32, 3:02:49–3:04:51)*
- Key personnel Gerb argues carried the Manhattan Project's security architecture forward into
  the "2.0" effort: [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]] and [[george-c-marshall|George C. Marshall]]
  (both credited with building the skeleton of the secrecy structure), with
  [[leslie-groves|Leslie Groves]] credited for the underlying conceptual blueprint. Gerb states he
  does not believe Groves personally continued into the "2.0" era, as Groves retired shortly after
  the Manhattan Project, whereas Bush and Marshall (until roughly 1950–1951) did.
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 2:20:48–2:21:13, 3:15:32–3:16:08)*
- Gerb explicitly frames "Manhattan Project 2.0" as his own coined analytical term/nickname, not
  a name used by any official source — a framing device for the two-part investigation, distinct
  from claims of established fact.
