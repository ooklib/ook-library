Honeywell International, an American publicly traded multinational conglomerate with aerospace
as one of four major business lines, has administered [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia
National Laboratories]] since 2017 via a purpose-created subsidiary, National Technology and
Engineering Solutions of Sandia LLC (NTESS) — succeeding [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]]'s
1993–2017 tenure via the Sandia Corporation. Gerb states he "firmly believes" Honeywell engages
with UFO legacy programs. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 53:05–53:38)*

## Claims

- Former aerospace/defense/electronics/IT contractor Sperry Rand — named by Col.
  [[philip-j-corso|Philip Corso]] as integral to the Army Foreign Technology Division's material
  exploitation efforts — went defunct in 1986; its former divisions became part of Lockheed
  Martin, Raytheon, Northrop Grumman, and Honeywell. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md)
  53:38–54:06)*
- More to Design (M2 Design), the small Florida architecture firm Gerb ties to early Sandia/Z
  Division facility construction (see [[sandia-national-laboratories]]), also completed work at
  Kirtland AFB for Sandia's manager Honeywell, at the aptly-named Sperry Flight Systems.
  *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 57:12–57:35)*
