Robert Emenegger produced "UFOs: Past, Present and Future" (1974-75) with [[alan-sandler]] and wrote a bestselling companion book. [[grant-cameron]] spoke with him for years and reports his account of being given Air Force material about a UFO landing at Holloman.

## Claims

- Emenegger told Cameron he worked at Gray Advertising, which Cameron says lots of people call a CIA front, and that a botched Vietnam documentary job led to the UFO project; Sandler owned the studio and Emenegger did the work and music. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 57:24-58:26)*
- Around 1973 he was called to Norton Air Force Base, where a security manager asked for eight documentaries on subjects like dolphins and 3D holography plus a hidden UFO one; he knew Nixon chief of staff H. R. Haldeman from school. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 58:56-59:44)*
- He said a CIA man (recalled as "Betsky") was on set throughout, that Haldeman's office phoned every day during taping, and that he received a Nixon thank-you letter he later denied having. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:03:15-1:03:42, 1:34:59-1:35:11, 2:01:29-2:03:06)*
- He told Cameron he gave a copy of the Holloman film documentary to Spielberg, whose production crew included Spielberg's sister Annie, and that Spielberg's mother preferred her son's version of the landing. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:04:06-1:04:50)*
- He said a DARPA tour in 1974 showed a man speaking to a computer with his mind and a synthetic voice answering. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:31:56-1:32:43)*
