*Age of Disclosure*, a 2025 documentary directed by Dan Farah, features
[[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]] and Jay Stratton, among others, presenting a purported outline of
the UFO "legacy program" structure. Gerb treats the film as a continuation of
[[advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program|ATIP]]'s partial-disclosure/narrative-control
campaign rather than a genuine disclosure effort, though he states his criticism is aimed at
Elizondo and Stratton, not at director Dan Farah or the film's production quality.
*([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:34:20–2:34:47)*

## Claims

- The film presents the legacy program (singular) as spearheaded by the CIA's Directorate of
  Science and Technology (DS&T), with the DS&T deputy director as "the big bad mob boss," and the
  DOE, Air Force, and contractors sitting on equal footing beneath it. Gerb calls this
  "demonstrably false on so many levels," arguing it omits the Army, Navy, Office of Naval
  Intelligence, DIA, and FFRDCs/national labs entirely, and — most importantly — omits the
  National Security Council, which he argues actually runs the portfolio. He theorizes the chart
  was crafted specifically to protect [[james-clapper|Clapper]]'s and Elizondo's ATIP-era roles
  and to "dictate who gets to catch the live hand grenade when disclosure comes."
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:35:25–2:38:16)*
- Elizondo and Stratton in the film discuss ATIP as if it were a real, appropriations-funded
  program, naming [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] and Eric Davis as "ATIP scientists" — which Gerb
  calls "brazen nonsense" given ATIP's status as an informal, unfunded working group. See
  [[advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program]]. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  2:21:04–2:22:12)*
- The nerve of including [[james-clapper|Clapper]] in the film is itself, per Gerb, "an amnesty
  plea and misdirect on the legacy program structure." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  2:34:20–2:34:36)*
