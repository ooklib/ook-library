Air Force Captain Edward J. Ruppelt served as director of Project Grudge and, later,
[[project-blue-book|Project Blue Book]]. In his 1956 book *The Report on Unidentified Flying
Objects*, Ruppelt recounted having observed a 1948 Project Sign memo concluding UFOs were of
"interplanetary origin" — a memo he said was ordered destroyed by General Hoyt Vandenberg, an
accused original [[majestic-12|Majestic 12]] member. Gerb notes Rep.
[[eric-burlison|Eric Burlison]] has separately called out file AF-ATIC-FILM-0352, a 1952
Ruppelt-narrated briefing film titled "Flying Saucer Talk," held at MIT Lincoln Labs and never
volunteered for mandatory National Archives collection — which Gerb suggests is because it
depicts sensitive material regarding non-human technical vehicles.
