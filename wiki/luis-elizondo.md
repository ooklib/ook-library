## Claims

- Served as [[to-the-stars-academy|To the Stars Academy]]'s director of global security and
  special programs (2017–2021), performing liaison duties with the DIA, CIA, and
  [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]]. Also had "extensive experience" with National Security
  Council-run programs under the Obama administration, possibly involving
  [[james-clapper|James Clapper]], per Gerb's theory that AATIP itself was a cover program under
  NSC directives for "limited disclosure." *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 56:19–56:42,
  3:01:21–3:02:22)*
- Gerb theorizes Elizondo — "more likely than not" — made an authorized or unauthorized
  disclosure of [[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]]-adjacent "episodic visit"
  data to [[tom-delonge|Tom DeLonge]] in 2017, the same year IMCON was established, 7 years before
  [[matthew-brown|Matthew Brown]]'s public disclosure. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  55:58–57:18)*

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2 — extended distrust chapter)

Gerb devotes a lengthy section of Vol. 2 to Elizondo, stating explicitly: "I do not stand with
Alzando, nor do I trust Alzando," while emphasizing this does not extend to whistleblowers like
[[david-grush|David Grush]], [[dylan-borland|Dylan Borland]], or [[matthew-brown|Matthew Brown]],
whom he continues to support. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:19:21–2:20:42)*

- Served as director of the [[national-program-special-management-staff|National Program
  Special Management Staff (NPMS)]] from 2013 to 2017, under
  [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-and-security|USD I&S]] — making him, per Gerb,
  "usually the most cleared person in the entire DoD besides the Secretary of Defense" during
  those years, a fact Gerb says is never mentioned in Elizondo's public self-presentation as an
  [[advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program|ATIP]] administrator.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:02:35–1:03:26, 2:25:19–2:25:53)*
- Gerb's central theory: ATIP was a [[james-clapper|James Clapper]]-authorized cover to "talk
  about the onion outside of the onion," with Elizondo — leveraging his NPMS role — as the
  natural person "to spearhead this job." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  1:02:06–1:02:35)*
- In a Jeremy Corbell interview, Elizondo claimed ATIP was a "sub-SAP" to [[aawsap|OAP]]; Gerb
  states this is false, since ATIP was an informal, unfunded working group with no SAP
  umbrella/compartment structure — "digging himself into more hot water" by making the claim.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 3:07:08–3:07:56)*
- In a Matt Ford interview, Elizondo repeatedly dodged direct questions about whether ATIP
  originated from the National Security Council/Clapper, saying only "I worked for Jim Clapper
  ... I can't speak for Jim" and declining to characterize his own "special access world" role
  beyond vague generalities. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:22:29–2:24:23)*
- Gerb states Elizondo's pre-Grush public disclosures focused narrowly on Roswell (already
  "cultural zeitgeist," safe territory), and that after [[david-grush|Grush]] went public in
  2023, Elizondo's team was "forced to reposture," appearing "caught off guard and nervous" in
  the 2024 congressional UAP hearings when pressed on crash-retrieval-program access — at one
  hearing stating "I signed documentation three years ago that restricts my ability to discuss
  specifically crash retrievals." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:27:32–2:29:08)*
- Gerb theorizes Elizondo, Christopher Mellon, retired Maj. Gen. Neil McCasland, and John Podesta
  (Hillary Clinton's campaign manager) executed ATIP's partial-disclosure mission via
  [[tom-delonge|Tom DeLonge]] and [[to-the-stars-academy|To the Stars Academy]], seeking to
  position Hillary Clinton as "the disclosure president" — a campaign that failed when Clinton
  lost the 2016 election and TTSA's science-focused efforts stalled; Elizondo left NPMS in 2017
  to join TTSA. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:29:23–2:30:00, 2:33:41–2:34:11)*
- Gerb repeats his theory that ATIP's post-2020s successor is the 2025 *Age of Disclosure*
  documentary, in which Elizondo and Jay Stratton falsely (per Gerb) frame ATIP as a real,
  appropriations-funded program, misidentify [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] and Eric Davis as "ATIP
  scientists," and present a legacy-program organizational chart Gerb calls "demonstrably false"
  — placing the CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology as the singular top of the program
  (omitting the National Security Council entirely, which Gerb argues is intended to protect
  [[james-clapper|Clapper]] and Elizondo's own past). *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  2:34:20–2:38:16)*
- Gerb states outstanding questions remain: whether Elizondo disclosed elements of
  [[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]] to [[tom-delonge|Tom DeLonge]] in 2017,
  and what his and Clapper's true motive for ATIP was. He closes by directly addressing Elizondo:
  "you have a chance to still be an American hero ... if there is information you are holding on
  to, individuals you are protecting, lies you are telling, you will go down as a villain."
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:57:57–3:20:38)*
