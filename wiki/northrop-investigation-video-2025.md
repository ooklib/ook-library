UAP Gerb's roughly one-hour video of 2025-01-27, the first Northrop Grumman installment (the later "Vol.2" is covered by [[trw]] and [[northrop-grumman]] under `gerb/northrop_trw`). It follows earlier Gerb investigations of [[battelle-memorial-institute]] and [[lockheed-martin]] and asks whether [[northrop-grumman]], "alongside Lockheed the paramount aerospace corporation" in legacy programs, runs material exploitation and crash retrieval teams for technologies of unknown origin (TUOs). Gerb admits there is far less of a paper trail than for Lockheed or Battelle ("no project stories, no direct mention"), so the case rests on Northrop's acquisitions and a handful of whistleblower claims. Everything below is Gerb's argument or third-party testimony, not established fact.

- Northrop legacy operations: the 1968 electro-aerodynamics paper, [[bill-hamilton]]'s Cosmic Top Secret, the [[tejon-radar-cross-section-facility]] and underground-base claims. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 22:44-34:48)*

## Gerb's main takeaways

- Northrop bought companies that already had legacy programs, with [[trw]] as the key case: TRW built [[aerospace-corporation]] (1960), was allegedly principal investigator for its first roughly 29 years, is linked by [[richard-dolan]] to [[zodiac-crash-retrieval-program]] and chaired by [[eric-a-walker]]. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 10:03-15:38)*
- Hypothesis: TRW's inflated space contracts surfaced in the 1990s audit that, per [[wilson-davis-notes]] ([[thomas-wilson]] to [[eric-davis]]), nearly blew the cover of the [[watch-committee]] and led to reorganization under the [[special-access-program-oversight-committee]] senior review group; Northrop's 2003 settlement of the TRW overcharging suit (about $111M, "almost $200M today") is read as the paper trail. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 16:53-18:26,)*
- Gerb's own theory: Northrop is more secretive than Lockheed, offering no divestment of material, and likely does material exploitation, [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b|ARV]] testing and project management of [[project-red-light]] at [[edwards-air-force-base]] with the 412th Test Group, possibly still ongoing. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 1:00:44-1:02:27)*
- Gerb is working on a thesis that select contractors' C4ISR programs host deep-buried special access programs for crash retrieval and ARV data, tied to [[strategic-defense-initiative]] funding and the alleged [[defense-advanced-research-center]]. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 6:09-9:05)*
