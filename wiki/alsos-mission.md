ALSOS (a pun on Leslie Groves's surname, transliterated into Greek) was a little-known WWII
Manhattan Engineer District intelligence operation to determine how close Nazi Germany had come
to developing its own atomic weapons, and to secure German atomic materials, scientists, and
documents ahead of Soviet forces. T-Force was the operational arm — supplied by the Supreme
Headquarters Allied Expeditionary Force — that physically secured sites for ALSOS teams to
exploit. UAP Gerb hypothesizes this pairing directly inspired the architecture of clandestine
modern UFO crash-retrieval teams. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 20:33–20:47,
2:19:47–2:20:19)*

## Claims

- Proposed by General [[george-c-marshall|George C. Marshall]] in 1943 to solve a structural
  problem: the Manhattan Project's secrecy was so total that standard military intelligence could
  not be briefed in to assess Nazi atomic progress, leaving military intelligence "fully
  unequipped" to do so. Overseen by General [[leslie-groves|Leslie Groves]], Marshall, Marshall's
  G2 (Assistant Chief of Staff for Intelligence) Colonel Boris Pash, and
  [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]]. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  2:14:08–2:15:39)*
- Conducted in three phases with an original detachment of 13 personnel (interpreters and five
  scientists): Phase 1 (Italy) gathered scientific-development intelligence and secured key
  persons/laboratories/information; Phase 2 (France) searched for German scientists and captured
  nuclear materials (uranium, heavy water) and documents; Phase 3 (Germany, late in the war)
  continued securing Nazi personnel/facilities/research with the explicit goal of denying any of
  it to Soviet forces. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 2:15:39–2:16:41)*
- T-Force units were lightly armed, highly mobile, and rapidly deployed to secure a site — with
  methods sometimes described as having "echoes of the Gestapo" — before special investigators
  (the ALSOS scientific/intelligence teams) moved in to exploit what had been captured; T-Force
  captured a subcritical experimental Nazi nuclear reactor, uranium ingots, heavy water, and
  scientists including Werner Heisenberg, in some cases inside Soviet-controlled territory.
  *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 2:17:50–2:19:19)*
- UAP Gerb argues the ALSOS/T-Force pairing — a small, mobile, direct-action element that secures
  a site ahead of a specialized scientific exploitation team, operating entirely outside standard
  military-intelligence channels and in open competition with an adversarial power — "directly
  mirrors" his model of modern UFO recovery teams: a tier-1-adjacent direct-action element
  securing a crash site until a DOE/NNSA [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team|NEST]]-style
  scientific team arrives to manage removal, transport, and exploitation, all under an
  intelligence apparatus answering to legacy-program elements rather than standard DoD/IC
  channels. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 2:19:47–2:20:19)*
- UAP Gerb notes General Groves reportedly disliked the ALSOS mission's name, given its
  transparent reference to his own surname. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  3:16:17–3:16:32)*
