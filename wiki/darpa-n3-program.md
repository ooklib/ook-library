DARPA's N3 program, launched in 2018, aims at bidirectional brain-machine interfaces for military use, including control of unmanned aerial vehicles (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:18:56-1:19:18)*). [[jesse-michels]] cites it as the open, documented descendant of the [[john-norseen]] and Lockheed brain-interface lineage relevant to [[jake-barber]]'s psionic piloting claims ([[psychic-joystick-brain-interface-claims]]). This page records Michels' description of a public program; his link to UFOs is speculative.

## Claims

- A team led by [[battelle-memorial-institute]] advanced to phase 2 per Battelle's 2020 press release, working with the [[air-force-research-laboratory]] and Carnegie Mellon on "BrainSTORMS" (brain system to transmit or receive magnetoelectric signals): nanoscale devices injected into the bloodstream and guided by magnets into brain regions for bidirectional interfacing. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:19:25-1:20:12)*
- Michels: it "could be a stepping stone towards warfighters eventually interfacing with aerial vehicles" or simply an advance on capabilities established years earlier; Battelle "is all over" legacy-program research. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:20:12-1:20:32)*
