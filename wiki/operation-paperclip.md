Operation Paperclip is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] in chapter 5 of [[road-to-dallas]] as "the oft-discussed US counterintelligence corps directed program that sought to smuggle out German rocket scientists in the immediate post-war period and to install them in positions in the nascent military-industrial complex and eventually at NASA." *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 19:38-19:56)* This page is a light stub scoped to that video; other wiki pages ([[wernher-von-braun]], [[counter-intelligence-corps]], [[hans-kammler]]) discuss Paperclip from other sources.

## Claims

- Host suspects (speculation) that [[frank-brandstetter]] had a larger covert role in Paperclip than he admits, given his discovery of V-1 "crossbow" sites, his post in northern Italy near the Austrian border in May 1945 when Walter Dornberger and von Braun defected in western Austria, and his later friendship with Dornberger and NASA figures. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 18:50-22:19)*
- Dornberger is described as the mechanical engineer and former Wehrmacht artillery general who oversaw Peenemünde and von Braun's team, and who also oversaw the Mittelwerk underground V-2 factory and its slave labor from the Mittelbau-Dora camp after the August 1943 RAF Operation Hydra raid; he went on to roles at Bell Aircraft and Boeing. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 20:04-21:19)*
