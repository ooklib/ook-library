Henry Luce, owner/publisher of [[time-life|Time Life]], is presented by Eyes Wide Open as an
arch-conservative media ally of [[allen-dulles]] and a fellow Skull and Bones alumnus of
[[george-h-w-bush]], whose outlet employed [[whittaker-chambers]] and later held the Zapruder
film out of public view for years.

## Claims

- Ran Time magazine as an arch-conservative "close personal friend" of [[allen-dulles]], and could
  "always be counted on" for positive coverage of Dulles's OSS/CIA work, including a glowing
  article on [[operation-sunrise]]. Served with Dulles on the board of the National Committee for
  a Free Europe, a front group Dulles created to fund Radio Free Europe as a CIA propaganda
  center. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 16:47-17:20)*
- A member of Yale's Skull and Bones secret society, alongside future CIA director and President
  [[george-h-w-bush]], who was also a close personal friend. The host notes he plans a dedicated
  future video on Skull and Bones and declines to go deep here. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 17:27-17:47)*
- His [[time-life|Time Life]] outlet purchased and held the Zapruder film out of public view for
  five and a half years until 1969, a delay the host flags as a likely favor to parties allegedly
  involved in the JFK assassination. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 17:47-18:15)*
- Employed [[whittaker-chambers]], the main witness against [[alger-hiss]] in the HUAC case — flagged
  by the host as suspicious given Luce's closeness to the Dulles brothers. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 18:15-18:35)*

- War Conspiracy 1: born in Shanghai to a missionary; "the christianization of China was the supreme effort of Luce's life" (his biographer, per the host); at 14 moved to the US and in 1916 enrolled at Yale, joining [[skull-and-bones]]; founded Time Inc. and used it to propagandize for the KMT; reconnected in 1940 with [[t-v-soong]]; Time named Chiang and Soong Mei-ling "people of the year" in 1937. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 10:54-13:17)*
