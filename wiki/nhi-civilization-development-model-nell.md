Asked by Michels for a modern scientific framework for beings "higher on the consciousness or evolution chain", [[karl-nell]] critiques the Kardashev scale (Nikolai Kardashev, 1964; type I planetary, type II star, type III galaxy energy use) as "not very instructive" for UAP because total civilization energy usage cannot be inferred from observed craft or recovered material. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 26:01-27:25)*

## The alternative

- He recommends a "universal civilization development model" based on what a civilization has, knows and is. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 27:25-27:40)*
- Have: energy density rather than energy magnitude (fire, smelting, nuclear reactions, perhaps warping space-time or "impacting the laws of physics"). *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 27:40-27:57)*
- Know: a "physics of information", credited to [[jacques-vallee]]: how much of reality's nature a civilization knows, how much complexity it can manage, its thought time-constant, parallel paths contemplated at once, with consciousness coming into play. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 27:57-28:22)*
- Be: an ecological-evolutionary dimension (chemical basis, how far along, perhaps a million or a billion years ahead, omnivore versus herbivore ancestry). *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 28:22-28:47)*
- The resulting phase space would hold a range of beings; distance in it, by analogy with a Hamming distance, might dictate whether relations are symbiotic or competitive. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 28:47-29:02)*
