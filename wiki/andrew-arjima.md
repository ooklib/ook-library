Andrew "Drew" Arjima (spelling from the auto-transcript, unverified) is an engineer of about 35 years' experience who co-founded [[exodus-propulsion-technologies]] with [[charles-buhler]]. Unlike Buhler he came to the effect through [[electrogravitics]] in the [[thomas-townsend-brown]] tradition, which Buhler called slightly "pretentious"; Drew has even called the thrust "warp drive".
