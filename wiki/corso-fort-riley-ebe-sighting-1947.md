[[philip-j-corso]] recounts that as post duty officer at Fort Riley, Kansas, in July 1947, he was shown five crates in the veterinarian section by a master sergeant; he lifted one and saw a small gray body floating in fluid with a spindly-armed, oversized head for 10 to 15 seconds. The sergeant said five trucks from an airfield in New Mexico were heading via Route 40 to Wright Field ([[wright-patterson-air-force-base]]). He put it in "the back of my head", and later matched it to Walter Reed autopsy reports in the Roswell file ([[roswell-file]]). *(Unverified claim.)* *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 15:56-18:11, 44:11-46:44, 48:27-48:43)*
