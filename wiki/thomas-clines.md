Thomas Clines, rendered "Thomas Klein" throughout the ASR of `ewa/jmwave` (normalization to Clines is my inference, based on the spelling used on [[jm-wave-station]] and [[operation-40]] from earlier episodes), is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the young [[jm-wave-station]] case officer who became [[ted-shackley]]'s closest lieutenant. This page holds only what `ewa/jmwave` says; the Iran-Contra period is deferred by the host.

## Claims

- Met Shackley soon after his February 1962 arrival; CIA since the late 1940s "at the inception of the agency," moved to Caribbean operations in 1960 to train Cuban exiles at a facility in Panama. Host calls him a gregarious "people person" and key recruiter of Cuban assets, the foil to the cold, paperwork-driven Shackley. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 32:10-32:42)*
- Per David Corn (host's attribution), he was initially unsure about Shackley and considered quitting, disillusioned by the Bay of Pigs and the loss of his assets, but soon became Shackley's sidekick and his "eyes and ears on the ground" among the exiles, delivering orders and reporting from Little Havana. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 32:42-33:59)*
- Formed close ties with Cuban exiles, "most prominently Raphael Chi-Chi Cano" of [[operation-40]]; the host says the two would "reunite in the 70s and 80s as key arms traffickers within the Iran Contra networks" under Shackley. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 32:58-33:23)*
- Followed Shackley to Laos and Vietnam and, after the CIA was reorganized under Carter, set up front companies for him. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 33:28-33:46)*
- Shackley's memoir *Spymaster* notes that [[david-sanchez-morales]] was Klein's direct boss before Shackley arrived, which the host says explains Klein's link to Cano and the Op 40 squad. A photo from the tangodown63 blog allegedly shows Morales and Klein at a later CIA reunion (caption dating it to the anniversary of Che Guevara's death). *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 35:14-36:20)*

## Relationships

- Employed by [[central-intelligence-agency]] and [[jm-wave-station]]; sidekick of [[ted-shackley]]; subordinate of [[david-sanchez-morales]] before Shackley's arrival.
