The Cuban Revolution as framed by [[eyes-wide-open]] in Chapter 1 of [[road-to-dallas]]: the host's argument is that the roots of the JFK assassination lie "as much in Dallas in 1963 as ... in Havana in 1959 and 60", because the Gulf Coast anti-Castro groups of the early 1960s trace their origins to bonds formed between disillusioned Cuban revolutionaries, American adventurers, spies and mercenaries in and after the revolution. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 0:00-1:17)*

## Claims

- Lasted roughly 1953 to January 1, 1959, with most fighting in the last two years. On New Year's Day 1959 [[fidel-castro]] overthrew dictator Fulgencio Batista (called "nearly universally hated"), who went into exile in the Dominican Republic. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 2:19-2:54)*
- The 26th of July movement was a broad coalition, not explicitly communist before 1959; Raul Castro and Che Guevara (ASR "Cheavara") were the communist element who, by the standard narrative, brought Fidel into their ranks. The host notes others speculate Fidel hid his leanings to court US support and that this "will likely never be fully settled"; by mid-1959 Castro had committed Cuba to communism (Guevara at the Bank of Cuba, Raul purging the political class and army). *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 2:54-4:06)*
- The shift was a betrayal to landowning, organized-crime-affiliated and petty-bourgeois elements of the coalition; the host says the most militant Miami exiles of the early 1960s came from Castro's own former ranks. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 4:06-4:48)*
- Three overlapping motives drew Americans ("Yankee revolutionaries") to Castro: opportunism, adventure, and US intelligence (CIA or Army intelligence cover); the host says the US intelligence community made a serious effort to co-opt the 26th of July movement as Batista weakened, and that many soldiers of fortune also moonlighted as spies or informants. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 4:48-6:31)*
- Americans brought military experience, US contacts and ties to gun-smuggling networks, procuring surplus US military gear, usually through intelligence connections; the host names [[david-ferrie]] (Louisiana [[civil-air-patrol]] pilot and "long-standing CIA asset") and [[jack-ruby]] as two men later tied to the JFK assassination who got their start running guns to Cuba, and ties the arms trade to organized crime and intelligence assets as in [[war-conspiracy]] part two. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 6:31-7:32)*
- After the victory, "hundreds" of American soldiers of fortune flooded Havana, some for personal gain and some undercover for the CIA or other US intelligence to gauge the "malleability" of the new government. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 30:31-30:51)*
- Financing: [[carlos-prio-socarras]] reportedly funded Castro; see that page. Arms: [[frank-sturgis]] flew arms from Miami to Santiago de Cuba and joined Castro in the Sierra Maestra; [[gerry-patrick-hemming]] recruited mercenaries after the victory.

## Relationships

- Led by [[fidel-castro]]; funded by [[carlos-prio-socarras]]; American participants [[frank-sturgis]] and [[gerry-patrick-hemming]]; precursor to the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]] and the Gulf Coast exile network of [[jm-wave-station]] and [[operation-40]].

## Additional claims (The Road to Dallas Chapter 2, ewa/dallas-2)

### CIA Havana station, 1959-60

- Host: after the revolution the CIA station at the US embassy in Havana was staffed by many former PBSuccess planners ([[operation-pbsuccess]]). When Castro took power in January 1959, Langley (per John Prados) shuffled out the station chief, William Caldwell (described as sympathetic to Castro and hostile to Batista), for a hardliner, James Null (ASR "Null"; kept as spoken). Both are "obscure"; host speculates Caldwell might be [[joseph-caldwell-king]] and Null could be fronting for higher-profile officers brought in 1959-60, before Eisenhower severed relations in January 1961. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 1:16, 3:22)*

- *The Fish Is Red* (Hinckle and Turner) says Null helped Cuban dissenters such as [[manuel-artime]] escape to South Florida, and that Null was at the CIA station in Madrid at the time of the JFK assassination; the host notes Franco Spain was a hub for international fascists (he names Otto Skorzeny, ASR "Scorzani") that some researchers tangentially link to the assassination, but says this may be irrelevant. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 3:52, 4:32)*

- Host: [[david-atlee-phillips]] returned to Havana in 1959 after Lebanon (he had also been in Cuba 1955-56 under Batista); [[e-howard-hunt]] is said to have been active there "for a time"; [[frank-sturgis]] was also roaming Cuba in 1959; David Morales was a Phillips colleague; and Army intelligence officer [[jack-cannon]] is claimed by [[peter-dale-scott]] to have penetrated Castro's guerrilla operation in the late 1950s. Host frames 1959 Havana as the origin point of the secret war against Castro and "ground zero for the future assassination of JFK." *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 0:20, 6:20, 25:25)*

- Host's Guatemala/Che note: see [[operation-pbsuccess]]. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 12:00)*

## Additional claims (ewa/dallas-3)

- Host: after January 1, 1959 the island became "a soldier of fortune Woodstock"; the demand for arms stayed steady; ambitions turned to Hispaniola (Trujillo's Dominican Republic, Haiti) and to Nicaragua; the host says the CIA, FBI and Army intelligence all had a significant presence, making Havana in 1959-60 "a real mecca for intelligence asset recruitment". *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 3:08-4:28, 14:39-14:55)*
- Host's hypothesis: Fort Hood and [[jack-cannon]] were vectors of US Army surplus weapons to Cuba, first to the revolutionary army and later to the anti-Castro underground (speculation, no smoking gun). *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 3:00-3:17, 24:33-25:05)*
- Camilo Cienfuegos, commander of the armed forces, disappeared when his plane went missing in October 1959; circumstances unclear, with speculation of a role by Raul Castro. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 19:09-20:04)*
- Early 1959 Havana CIA station was "loaded with top covert operations men" scouting recruits for the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]] and later [[operation-mongoose]]; see [[david-atlee-phillips]], [[david-sanchez-morales]], [[e-howard-hunt]]. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 33:10-34:59)*

Mob-run gun-smuggling to Castro after the revolution ([[lewis-mcwillie]], [[jack-ruby]], [[norman-rothman]], pilot [[edward-browder]]) is described as the "framework" for later events. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 14:52-15:25, 30:33-30:58)*

## Additional (ewa/dallas-5)

- Host: Castro's rise sent "a wave of anxiety and panic" through American casino owners. Lansky sold his Hotel Nacional casino stake in 1958 to a group headed by Louisiana golfer/gambler Michael Julius McLaney (ASR "Mlan/Mlany"), reportedly bankrolled by Carroll Rosenbloom (owner of the Baltimore Colts, later LA Rams, per a 1963 Miami Herald article); McLaney was "left holding the bag" and later, per the host, lent his brother's property near Lacombe, Louisiana to the mob and associates as a bomb and weapons store for a future Cuba invasion ("much more on this later"). *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 1:07-2:05)*
