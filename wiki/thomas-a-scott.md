President of the Pennsylvania Railroad in MagnatesMedia's account, Rockefeller's ally in the [[south-improvement-company]] and then his defeated rival.

## Claims

- Proposed the 1872 secret railroad-refiner alliance. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 18:01, 18:18)*
- Later built his own pipelines and refineries, prompting [[john-d-rockefeller]] to wage a price and freight war on him; he cut jobs and wages 20% and surrendered. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 26:07, 26:16, 26:49, 27:29)*
- He suffered a stroke a year later and died; he had been the mentor of [[andrew-carnegie]], who blamed Rockefeller, starting their rivalry. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 28:46, 29:02, 29:11)*

## Relationships

- Rival of [[john-d-rockefeller]] and [[standard-oil]]; mentor of [[andrew-carnegie]].
