Kodak is an American photography company. The video alleges its German subsidiary kept operating and producing munitions components during WWII.

## Claims

- Kodak AG was placed under trustee control so it could keep functioning if the US and Germany fought; it produced fuses, detonators and triggers with slave labor at Stuttgart and Berlin plants, and French factories also supported the German effort. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 10:23-10:53)*
- New York headquarters allegedly did not appear to control the German operations but did control subsidiaries in neutral Switzerland, Spain and Portugal, through which goods made in German territory were funneled into the US. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 10:53-11:17)*
- Hitler adviser Wilhelm Keppler (transcribed 'Villim Kepler') had strong ties to Kodak and other US firms; Kodak faced no penalties during or after the war. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 11:17-11:27)*
