Ubirajara ("Uber") Rodrigues (the source's transcription varies widely) is described by [[james-fox]] as a well-known lawyer from Minas Gerais and early Varginha researcher who later turned against the case. The spelling of the surname is uncertain because the transcript is garbled.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox lists him among the researchers who deserve credit and says he went quiet after 1996. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 16:44-17:08, 43:28-44:37)*
- Fox says the disc-sighting family named him as present when American visitors, whom the wife thought were from NASA, told them "we were never here, this never happened", and that Fox realised this was the same researcher. Fox says he was the lawyer who once demolished skeptics' arguments and now will not talk to anyone. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:29:18-1:30:55)*
- Fox and Ramsey conclude that a researcher can be compromised, and that the girls were upset by things he later said. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:30:55-1:31:18)*
