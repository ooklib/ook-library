Scitor Holdings, a defense/space/intelligence contractor, was acquired by
[[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] in 2018 for $790M (all-cash), retaining
most original employees. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 18:06–18:24)*

## Claims

- Per Tim Shorrock's 2008 book *Spies for Hire*, Scitor was a critical CIA Directorate of
  Science and Technology contractor for satellite SIGINT/ELINT: "Scitor may be unknown, but it
  is definitely not a small player." *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 18:24–18:32,
  20:06–20:13)*
- In 2005, held a national symposium on MASINT ("measurement and signature intelligence")
  signature technology with contractors including [[bae-systems|BAE Systems]] and SAIC.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 19:52–20:06)*
- Its old building in Chantilly, VA (14660 Lee Road) shared a corporate park with SAIC, the
  [[aerospace-corporation|Aerospace Corporation]], and the [[national-reconnaissance-office|
  NRO]] — which Gerb calls "curious and possibly suspicious." *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  20:13–20:30)*
