David Grush is a former intelligence officer and prominent UAP whistleblower whose public
statements and congressional testimony recur throughout Gerb's video as corroborating
context for the alleged reverse-engineering program.

## Claims

- In an embedded interview clip, Grush summarizes the reverse-engineering program: "if I were to
  ... summarize the 90 plus years of history, it is a reverse engineering program to garner some
  kind of insight ... not a lot of the things that we've learned from it are ... directly ripped
  off the technology we found, but it has inspired other innovations that made its way into
  other US classified programs." Gerb treats this as directly describing the MITRE program.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 0:54–1:21, 58:44–59:49)*
- States technologies produced are not "bombastic triangles or directed energy weapons" but
  systems integrated into warfighter support — again aligned by Gerb with the MITRE program's
  alleged output. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:21:28–1:21:45)*
- Names [[james-clapper]] and [[stephanie-osullivan]] directly as IC figures aware of and
  managing the crash-retrieval issue, calling on them to be "greater leaders" on transparency.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:40:56–1:41:49)*
- Co-signed (with Rep. [[eric-burlison]]) the 10+ page legal interrogative letter sent to
  [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:50:21–1:50:30, 2:16:40–2:16:46)*
- Credited (with Rep. Burlison's office) for bringing wider public attention to the importance
  of FFRDCs in UFO crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering programs. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:02:03–2:02:15)*

- From 2021 until leaving federal service in April 2023, served as senior GEOINT capabilities
  requirements officer for [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|NGA]], lead for US
  SPACECOM's National Support Team, senior technical adviser for UAP/transmedium issues, and
  liaison to the UAP task force in his NRO and NGA roles. A 7 May 2021 FOIA'd communication shows
  NGA, NASC, DIA, and NRO consulted with the UAP task force on GEOINT-asset availability.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 57:41–58:49)*
- Told Rep. Moskowitz he had personally reviewed both "overhead collection" and other strategic/
  tactical platform imagery he "could not even explain prosaically," but stated he could not
  discuss in open session whether such imagery applied to crash sites. Gerb reads this as
  suggesting Grush was exposed to NRO Sentient-collected UFO/crash imagery, possibly shared with
  [[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]]. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  59:10–1:01:20)*
- Discussed a "new secret Cold War" between the US and adversaries (Russia, China) to master
  nonhuman technology, driven by "feudalistic dominance" motives, and separately confirmed direct
  knowledge of misappropriation of funds used to supply UFO legacy-program operations.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:45:48–1:47:10, 2:19:01–2:19:22)*

- In 2023 discussed misappropriation of funds and IRAD (Independent Research and Development)
  being used to fund UFO legacy-program operations, confirming US corporations "over-charge" the
  government for certain technology to divert additional money into such programs via IRAD.
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 12:20–13:02)*
- Discussed Lockheed's attempted divestment of UFO crash materials to the OAP program, a claim
  that "became quite tangible" around the November 2024 House Oversight hearings.
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 1:57:32–1:57:49)*

- In a Megan Kelly interview, said he was "certainly disturbed" learning how UFO legacy programs
  were controlled — "a self-licking ice cream cone, unaccountable. Everybody owns a piece. Nobody
  owns the outcome" — naming the late [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] as the closest thing to a

  *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 21:53–23:12)*
