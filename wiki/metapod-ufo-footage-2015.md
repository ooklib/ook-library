A roughly two-minute clip filmed on 6 September 2015 near El Escorial, Madrid, Spain, showing a pod-shaped craft with a solid reflective back and a transparent, glass-like front face. [[uap-gerb]] analyzes it in [[gerb-metapod-video]] and finds it compelling, without proving authenticity. Origin and authenticity are unverified.

## Claims

- Origin: on 7 September 2015 a man calling himself Modesto emailed the YouTube channel UFOVNI offering the video he and a friend filmed the day before, saying it was near El Escorial toward Las Navas del Marqués, that the object made no noise, descended vertically and left, and that the friend saw something living with limbs moving inside; the friend said it was not the first time he had seen it and that his late father had also seen it. Gerb notes the email mixes up the two names, possibly a pseudonym slip. *([gerb/metapod](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-12 - Incredible UFO Footage - METAPOD.md>) 3:28-6:15)*
- Spread: the original UFOVNI upload and a 2017 reupload were taken down (Gerb uses a stabilized version); it appeared on a Portuguese forum on 8 September 2015 (which compared it to the 2006 Monterrey clip) and on the Third Phase of the Sun blog on 11 September 2015. *([gerb/metapod](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-12 - Incredible UFO Footage - METAPOD.md>) 3:05-3:28, 7:03-7:44)*
- Craft: a solid metallic back and transparent front face; some (Third Phase of the Sun) see a being inside, Gerb thinks it is a sunlight reflection. He says it shows instantaneous acceleration (an altitude drop), no visible propulsion and low observability, plus standstill in wind, linear travel away, and rotation about its axis that reverses direction. *([gerb/metapod](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-12 - Incredible UFO Footage - METAPOD.md>) 7:58-9:58)*
- Audio: a Reddit user translated the voices; the audio sounds noise-reduced, either in post-processing or by the camera. *([gerb/metapod](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-12 - Incredible UFO Footage - METAPOD.md>) 10:11-10:39)*
- Debunks: balloon (including a custom balloon tent), object on a string, or CGI based on three jitter frames; Gerb says [[mick-west]]'s Metabunk thread ascribes the jitter to a balloon in air currents, and that the string explanation is the same used against the McMinville photographs. *([gerb/metapod](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-12 - Incredible UFO Footage - METAPOD.md>) 12:14-13:11)*
- Alleged look-alikes: the 1998 "Los Paracaidistas de Maipu" footage from Peru (five or more pods), the 2006 Monterrey "witch" video in Mexico (a back view), a 2021 Vancouver video (three objects, two ascending fast), alleged 2022 frames from a US Navy Blue Angel cockpit recording (second-generation, with four other signatures; Gerb is still verifying it) and a 2023 Texas hang-glider video he rates most likely prosaic. He says the pod may resemble the Gimbal clip outline but declines to claim that. *([gerb/metapod](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-12 - Incredible UFO Footage - METAPOD.md>) 13:20-17:07)*
