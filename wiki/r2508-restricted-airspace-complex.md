Complex of restricted areas, military operations areas and other special-use airspace over the upper Mojave Desert, managed by the National Training Center at Fort Irwin, [[china-lake]] and the 412th Test Wing's Air Force Test Center at [[edwards-air-force-base]]. [[uap-gerb]] uses it as a geographic anchor for [[castle-triangle-origin-thesis]].

## Claims (gerb/castle)

- Gerb says the Castle encounter took place in R-2501C (a separate area), while the triangle and the black team both left northwest toward R-2508, whose southern edge is about 44.5 miles from Emerson Dry Lake. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 21:07-21:25, 1:26:39-1:27:16)*
- The complex, near the [[antelope-valley]], hosts bombing ranges, supersonic corridors and refueling areas, and Gerb lists the Edwards, China Lake, Fort Irwin and Lemoore "Poncho 3" cleared entities, plus [[air-force-plant-42]] and the radar cross-section plants, as connected to his legacy-program claims. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:11:17-1:13:18)*
