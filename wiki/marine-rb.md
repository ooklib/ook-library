"RB" is an anonymous former US Marine who claims to have guarded an alleged non-human disc-shaped craft in December 1963. [[uap-gerb]] says neither his claims nor his identity can be verified and he has never gone public. The account is in [[leonard-stringfield]]'s archive and was compiled by researcher and CAD artist Michael Schratt.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-2)

- Background: served in the Marines 1960-66 as a weapons technician and corporal with an interim top secret clearance; Stringfield's UFO Crash Retrieval Status Report 5 lists the case "Marine guards a disc-shaped vehicle 1963", arranged via researcher Michael B. Johnstone. In the 2000s Michael Schratt read Stringfield's archives in Cincinnati and reconstructed the story; Schratt interviewed Johnstone in 2015. Gerb says the 1986 interview found RB in his late 40s. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 21:39-22:54,)*
- Full account of the event: [[marine-rb-craft-guard-1963]]. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 22:54-26:20)*
- Gerb notes RB is listed on [[steven-greer]]'s witness list as a redacted name numbered 10138, with no documents leading to his identity; Gerb says he may have a lead to the name. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 23:10-23:27,)*
- Gerb's assessment: the lost photograph (a flood around 1983) is a red flag but he gives it a pass until he knows more, since RB never went public or sought money; RB's wife reportedly did not believe him until she saw the photo. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 29:13-29:46)*
