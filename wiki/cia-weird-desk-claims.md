Cameron describes the "weird desk" as the place at the [[central-intelligence-agency]] where UFO, paranormal, psychic and remote-viewing material is received, a small desk (Kit Green said he spent about 10 percent of his time on it) that ran from [[arthur-lundahl]] to [[kit-green]] to [[ron-pandolfi]]. Cameron says he does not know who holds it now that Pandolfi is retired. This is a lineage from Cameron's sources, not a documented organizational chart.

## Claims

- Lineage stated: Lundahl, then Green, then Pandolfi. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 45:15-45:55)*
- Cameron says Pandolfi has been at the CIA since 1984 and is rumored to brief presidents on Chinese technology; he reportedly does the briefings and "carries the keys" to all compartments. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:26:38-2:27:02, 2:33:29-2:34:12)*
- Cameron notes the membership of the Johnson-era McDonald panel (head of the weird desk, head of Skunk Works, a two-star general at Wright-Patterson, head of the NRO) and compares it with the To The Stars Academy roster. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 47:29-48:03)*
- Cameron says Green was read in on much but could not get read into the autopsy program. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:36:53-2:37:33)*
