Nick Redfern is an author of UFO books. In the `jm/cover-up` episode [[jason-samosa]] relies on his book Final Events as the source for the claim that a CIA Directorate of Plans-funded program (the [[collins-elite]]) investigated a UFO-occult correlation from 1952, and that [[james-baker]] and [[alonzo-mcdonald]] attended a Reagan-era UFO briefing. Redfern is said to have met people who validated other sources he had. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 16:26, 17:15, 19:24)*

## Relationships

## Claims (gerb/kingman)

- Gerb ([[gerb-kingman-video]]) credits Redfern with exposing Judy Wolcott's Kingman claim as false: her husband had not died in Vietnam, and her daughter said she often lied. Gerb also cites Redfern's writing on Kingman. *([gerb/kingman](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-05 - The 1953 Kingman, Arizona UFO Crash.md>) 17:59-18:44)*
