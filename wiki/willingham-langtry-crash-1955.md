An alleged 1955 downed disc near Langtry, Texas, on the Mexican side of the Rio Grande about 60 miles northwest of Del Rio, reported by [[robert-willingham]]. Often confused with the [[el-indio-guerrero-crash-1950]]. Gerb notes there is no contemporary report of the sighting. All below are Willingham's claims as relayed by [[uap-gerb]].

## Claims (gerb/del-rio)

- On a spring day in 1955, Willingham (serial number and call sign given as "CD1 195" and "Willie Eddie") and other F-86 pilots escorting B-47s from Carswell AFB on a Cold War simulation heard by radio that fast unidentified signatures had been picked up by Distant Early Warning radar in Canada heading south. A B-47 pilot reported an unknown aircraft moving south, and Willingham saw a bright object like a star approach to within 35 to 40 miles at an estimated 2,000 mph, about four times his F-86, with a surface like "magnesium steel." Other pilots including George Smithson said they saw it in the debrief. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 6:17-7:33)*
- The object turned 90 degrees toward the Mexican border, emitted "a lot of sparks", tilted down about 45 degrees and fell out of view west of Del Rio. Willingham got permission from Denver flight control ("we want to know where it was too") to fly to Langtry via San Angelo and saw a disc-shaped wreck at about 800 ft that had skidded about 300 ft on the Mexican side. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 7:33-10:01)*
- His friend Jack Perkins, a Pennsylvania electronics engineer in the Civil Air Patrol (named after his 2002 death), flew with him in a 1947 Aeronca Champion; Gerb says he faced no reprisals. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 10:01-10:32)*
- They arrived about 4:00 p.m. The object had broken into about three sections: a dome 12 to 15 ft long resting 50 ft beyond a flattened disc 20 to 25 ft across, split in two and half embedded in a sandy mound, with debris "as big as a good sofa chair." Mexican soldiers in jeeps and cars formed a perimeter but did no retrieval; a Lieutenant Martinez from Mexico City said "the American Air Force will be here very soon to clean this all up." Gerb could find no record of Martinez and compares the pattern to the 1978 Bolivia retrieval in his [[gerb-moondust-video]]. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 11:55-13:48)*
- Willingham took a shiny metal fragment, rigid, very light, grayish silver, with honeycomb holes on one side and no markings, which resisted deformation and torch cutting. He kept it 4 days without photographing it, sent it to a USMC metallurgy lab in Hagerstown, Maryland, and was later told no such person or records existed. He gave an oral and written report to his reserve commander, Colonel Miller, and received threats from a USAF intelligence general ("don't say anything about what you saw"). Gerb says the material shares little with the Roswell shape-memory alloy studied by [[battelle-memorial-institute]]. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 13:48-15:37)*
- Gerb offers context, not proof: an 18 July 1955 unclassified document shows a USAF Security Service commander in San Antonio requesting data on B-47 UFO sightings from the Northeast Air Command; the 14 May 1954 Major Charles Scarborough Marine reserve jet encounter over Dallas (16 signatures in groups of four); the 1966 CIA memo on exploitation of a metallic fragment from a UFO recovered in the Congo; and [[project-moon-dust]]. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 10:32-11:52)* *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 15:37-16:18)*
- The independent witness [[william-draeger]] adds details. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 19:24-20:44)*
