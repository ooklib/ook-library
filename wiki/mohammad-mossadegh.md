Mohammad Mossadegh (transcribed "Muhammad Mozadek" in Eyes Wide Open's ASR captions) was the
democratically elected prime minister of Iran, overthrown by the CIA in 1953 at the request of
the British government and the [[anglo-iranian-oil-company|Anglo Iranian Oil Company]], per Eyes
Wide Open — the coup cited as the direct precedent for the CIA's 1954 overthrow of
[[jacobo-arbenz]] in Guatemala.
