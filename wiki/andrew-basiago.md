Andrew Basiago is a lecturer who claims he time-traveled as a child, including to Lincoln's Gettysburg Address. [[grant-cameron]] says he had dinner with him, found him impressive rather than crazy, and could not confirm which building on the site he visited.

- Cameron asked Basiago whether he had been in the front or back building and got "I don't remember, I was a little kid". *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:21:01-2:21:17)*
