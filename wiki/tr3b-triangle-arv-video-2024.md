UAP Gerb's 2024-11-30 video (about 1h17m, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B7JP0uX0GwY) is a deep dive into black triangular UAP and the claim that at least some are human-built "alien reproduction vehicles" ([[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b]]), prompted by the Nov 2024 [[immaculate-constellation]] report. It is the fullest single treatment in the wiki of [[edgar-fuche]]'s TR-3B testimony, and it adds a second, independent triangle lead: the [[xf-131-super-sentinel]] drawn by [[bill-mcdonald]]. As always, everything below is Gerb's presentation of claims, not established fact.

## Structure and main claims

- Intro: triangles were historically tied to deep-black human projects rather than non-human intelligence (NHI), and Gerb notes the Immaculate Constellation report (public in the fall 2024 hearings) labels some triangles as ARVs, the same term [[brad-sorenson]] heard at the 1988 Norton AFB show ([[flux-liner-arv-saga]]) and that [[eric-burlison]] says he had heard before. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 0:00-3:19)*
- Statistical framing: AARO lists triangles as only 3-4% of UAP reports, [[project-blue-book]] has only four anomalous 1950s triangles (per Don Berliner's "Blue Book Unknowns"), and UFO-report timelines show a triangle flap in the 1970s-2000, peaking in the 1980s-90s, leading Gerb to hypothesize that legacy programs began building and testing triangular ARVs in the 1970s. See [[flying-triangle-sightings]]. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 3:27-6:38)*
- Cites the disputed [[special-operations-manual-som1-01]] (Psalm 101, dated 1954) for an isosceles triangle of about 300 ft described as new technology, and raises the question of whether 1950s triangles were NHI, breakaway-civilization or precursor-program craft. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 6:47-7:37, 1:15:39-1:16:11)*
- Speculates the [[belgian-ufo-wave-1989]] radar/F-16 event of 30 March 1990 may have been test flights of a TR-3B over an Allied nation. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 7:43-9:05)*
- Fouche chapters: his biography, five sources, [[defense-advanced-research-center]], the TR-3B design and propulsion, the [[fouche-controversy]], with the [[boscombe-down-crash-1994]] and the [[sekret-machines-project]] "Astra" passage as parallels. *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 9:28-45:08)*
- Conclusion: Gerb distinguishes the TR-3A, TR-3B, possibly TR-3E, and the XF-131 as separate things often confused, and proposes at least two ARV families held by DOD, the Air Force 412th Test Group and legacy programs: the pretty flux-liner saucer (electrogravitics, magnetohydrodynamics or zero-point energy) and the less capable triangles (electrogravitics or MFD/MHD), both "a human attempt to reproduce something fantastic". *([gerb/tr-3b](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-11-30 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - TR-3B and the Flying Triangles.md>) 1:06:40-1:16:59)*
