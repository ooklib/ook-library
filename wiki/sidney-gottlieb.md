Sidney Gottlieb is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the CIA's "master chemist", head of the Technical Services Division (the agency's R&D branch), architect of its mind-control programs (Bluebird, Artichoke, later MK Ultra) and main producer of poisons for the Castro plots. The ASR spells the name variously ("Sydney got Le", "gotle"); normalization is my inference. The host says he "deserves his own episode".

## Claims

- Bissell ordered cyanide pill poison development for U-2 pilots downed over Soviet airspace; the task went to Gottlieb's division. In the last days of the Eisenhower presidency Gottlieb was tapped to deliver poison ampoules in a briefcase to the Congo for the effort against Patrice Lumumba; Lumumba was killed in January 1961 by Belgian-backed rebels in breakaway Katanga, and Gottlieb's poison went unused; he was then put to work on Castro. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:06:31-1:08:11)*
- Cooked up the plots' gadgets (per Kinzer's *Poisoner in Chief* as cited by the host): LSD aerosol for Castro's microphone, beard-depilating toxin, poison cigars, poisoned diving suit, exploding conch shell. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:12:05-1:13:00)*
