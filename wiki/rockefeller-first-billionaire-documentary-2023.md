MagnatesMedia video of 2023-11-19 (about 49 minutes), a conventional business-history documentary on [[john-d-rockefeller]] and [[standard-oil]], framed as a rags-to-riches story that is "at times inspiring, and at times... chilling". It has no UFO content and no claims of hidden control; it is recorded as power-structure background alongside the [[rockefeller-family]], the [[house-of-morgan]] and [[cornelius-vanderbilt]]. Its distinctive contribution is the Rockefeller-Carnegie-Morgan steel chain ([[mesabi-iron-range-deal]], [[us-steel-corporation]]) and the [[theodore-roosevelt]] antitrust suit. ASR note: no notable misspellings; the Skool and ShipStation sponsor reads are ignored.
