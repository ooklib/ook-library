A roughly 40-acre area of [[dugway-proving-ground]]'s [[west-desert-test-center]] bordering Michael Army Airfield, and the focus of the 388th Range Squadron out of Hill AFB. Gerb notes it yields very few search results and is missing from DPG visitor guides, unlike the neighboring Ditto Technical Center, and identifies it as the likely above-ground site of an alleged joint Army/Air Force UFO reverse-engineering program. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 33:44, 36:53, 37:00, 38:03)*)*

## Claims

- Per a July 2012 Air Force environmental assessment, Avery historically ran radiological studies and radioactive-material handling (including for the Atomic Energy Commission's radiobiological laboratories) and has an internal rail system for hauling radioactive materials; listed buildings include 1010 test operations, 1012 photo operations and maintenance, 1016 classified storage, 1038 general storage and the "102" Avery tunnel entrance. Gerb bets [[witness-ms]]'s encounter occurred in building 1010. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 38:11, 38:19, 38:34, 38:56, 39:04, 39:20)*)*
- Gerb theorizes the rail system carries non-human craft and materials to unmarked Avery buildings (test operations, classified storage). *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 56:10, 56:17)*)*
