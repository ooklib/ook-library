John Connally (ASR "Connelly"), future Texas governor, is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as [[lyndon-b-johnson]]'s early political fixer, "bagman" (Robert Bryce's term) and protege, elevated by [[sid-richardson]].

## Claims

- Hit, per the Warren Commission "anyways," by the same "magic bullet" as JFK while riding in front in the Dallas motorcade on November 22, 1963. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 10:43-10:56)* See [[warren-commission]].
- Richardson made him executor of his fortune. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 10:56-11:05)* In the 1950s he was often present at the [[del-charro-set]] working as a lobbyist for Richardson. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 39:00-39:22)*
