Frank Coppola (transcribed as "Frank Kapola"), a Detroit mob boss, was deported to Sicily
alongside [[lucky-luciano]], and is alleged to have been behind, with [[salvatore-giuliano]], the
1947 Portella della Ginestra ("Mayday") massacre of leftist workers.

## Claims

- Angleton allegedly ensured Luciano was deported alongside Detroit mob boss Frank Kapola.
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 25:51-26:01)*
- According to some sources, Kapola and Sicilian mobster [[salvatore-giuliano]] had been behind the
  1947 Mayday massacre (Portella della Ginestra), where Mafia men opened fire on a crowd of
  rallying workers in Sicily, killing eight and wounding 33; allegedly, this attack had been
  funded directly by former OSS agent [[william-donovan]] (by then a private citizen) via his
  [[world-commerce-corporation]], which he used to funnel funds to mob associates.
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 26:01-26:31)*
