Bob Lazar says that soon after he went public through [[george-knapp]] (weeks, not months) [[dennis-mariani]] phoned him and asked to meet at the Union Plaza Hotel in Las Vegas in a different, unusual tone than before: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 12:31-14:07)*. The account is Lazar's alone.

## Claims

- Lazar brought friends as witnesses this time, went in first, spotted Mariani and had a friend confirm him: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 14:28-14:44)*
- Walking in, he passed other faces he recognized from S4 security coming from Mariani's direction and told the friend (transcribed "Jean") that something was wrong; he thinks they were security and that Chuck Payne was not among them: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 14:47-15:46)*, see [[chuck-payne]]
- Mariani would not look at him or respond even when Lazar stood next to him and said his name, and Lazar concluded that "this whole thing reeks": *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 15:05-15:28)*
- Lazar guessed Mariani might be sympathetic, but the setup did not read that way: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 14:14-14:28)*
- Ramsey notes that an EG&G Janet co-pilot (flying into Groom Lake around 1987-91) reportedly knew Mariani and had learned of his death, and that Lazar has seen photos of a gravestone people believe is Mariani's; Lazar says Mariani was a few years older than himself: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 11:11-12:23,)*, see [[dennis-mariani]] and [[lazar-eg-and-g-recruitment-and-s4-access-claims]]
