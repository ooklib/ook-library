The 1948 Italian general election, per Eyes Wide Open, was "arguably the first Gladio operation"
and the CIA/OPC's first covert operation abroad — a coordinated campaign by the CIA, the Mafia,
and the Vatican to prevent the [[italian-communist-party|Communist-Socialist coalition]] from
winning a feared super-majority.

## Claims

- The CIA and its covert-operations arm, the [[office-of-policy-coordination|Office of Policy
  Coordination]] (headed by [[frank-wisner]]), had their "first operation abroad" in Italy in
  1948, where the Communist Socialist Coalition Party was threatening to win a super-majority in
  the first postwar national elections. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 20:01-20:15)*
- The anti-communist coalition of the Mafia, the Vatican, and the CIA worked to bring Italy into
  the Western fold via money laundering, hush payments, propaganda, and bloodshed — "and that is
  where Gladio began." *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 23:00-23:15)*
- On election day, [[calogero-vizzini|Don Calo]]'s men stuffed ballot boxes and bribed election
  officials while [[pope-pius-xii]] awaited results. Final results: the Christian Democrats won
  48% of the vote (307 seats); the Communist/Socialist coalition reached only 31% (200 seats),
  nearly 30% below their projected total. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 29:00-29:38)*
- Even the election's outcome may not have mattered: per plans drafted by [[george-kennan]], the
  State Department recommended a naval invasion of Italy if the communists had won, nullifying
  any potential victory. Eyes Wide Open concludes "democracy was not a possibility for post war
  Italy." *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 29:38-30:00)*
