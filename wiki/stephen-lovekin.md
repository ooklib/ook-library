Stephen Lovekin was a US Army technician who worked in the White House Army Signal Agency during the Eisenhower and Kennedy administrations and appeared in [[steven-greer]]'s [[disclosure-project]] (2001). He claims knowledge of [[project-blue-book]]'s classified content, of Roswell wreckage and bodies, and of Eisenhower losing control of the UFO effort. All below are his claims as relayed by [[uap-gerb]], who finds him "quite credible" apart from an inflated title.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-1)

- Credentials per Gerb: entered the Army in 1958, joined the Pentagon's radio frequency engineering office, then the White House Army Signal Agency in 1959 as a technical specialist with a top secret clearance, serving under Eisenhower and Kennedy; Gerb cites multiple independent sources including ufologist "Joe margia" (transcribed; likely Joe Murgia), and says Lovekin attended the 1998 briefing with Greer, [[thomas-wilson]], [[will-miller]] and others recorded in the [[wilson-davis-notes]], which Wilson confirmed. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 13:06-14:16)*
- Credibility flaw: the Disclosure Project billed him "Brigadier General," but Gerb says that was a rank in the North Carolina State Guard, a volunteer militia with no official military standing, so Greer embellished or did not vet it. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 13:16-13:38,)*
- Lovekin says Blue Book was discussed openly in the office and that its classified sections held "highly scientific" material for particular military personnel, including many photographs (from Air Force, Marine and civilian pilots, some foreign) and "between two and 300 cases of lock on" (radar lock-on), none of which appears in the declassified Blue Book reports. Gerb ties the lock-on point to Maj. Hector Quintanilla's false claim to Congress that Blue Book had zero such cases, later corrected by [[j-allen-hynek]]. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 14:25-16:49)*
- Lovekin says the Air Force was very concerned about Blue Book and that talking about UFOs was the fastest way to ruin a career; Gerb connects this to AFR 200-2. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 16:49-17:21)*
- Lovekin claims a Colonel "Hollard" (Gerb suggests a Greer misspelling of Hollowguard or Hollowberg) showed a yardstick-like metal piece bearing symbols, said to come from the 1947 New Mexico crash and to be from a box of material the military was working on, something like reverse engineering. Gerb links it to the "I-beam" material Jesse Marcel showed his son, which he says many people claimed to have seen. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 17:29-18:57)*
- He says a discussion the next day covered extraterrestrial bodies, "either three or five," with one partially alive. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 19:06-19:46)*
- Eisenhower: Lovekin says the UFO problem was split among various agencies to keep it cheap and secret, and that Eisenhower "got sold out," lost control, and came to see corporations taking control of the matter, which Lovekin ties to the farewell warning about the military-industrial complex. In a separate interview with [[grant-cameron]] he said he attended Camp David with Eisenhower and overheard him on a call about the [[washington-dc-ufo-incident-1952]], and that Eisenhower was later kept out of UAP information. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 19:46-24:41)*
- Threats: Lovekin says an older CIA and DIA officer told him revelations about the phenomenon could get someone "erased," that threats started in 1947 when the Air Force got absolute control, and that they would go after family; he strongly suspects people were murdered to protect the secret. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 24:41-26:10)*
- Gerb's own inference: the [[ufo-legacy-programs]] Grusch describes lost congressional and presidential oversight around the 1960s as parts moved to private corporations. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 28:11-28:34)*
