The "Blue Room" is the alleged restricted area at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base where UFO-related material was rumored to be stored, made famous by [[barry-goldwater]]'s statements that [[curtis-lemay]] refused him entry. All claims below are attributed to [[uap-gerb]] and his sources.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-2)

- Goldwater's letters and 1975 interview describe a room or building at [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]] holding collected UFO information, which he was denied access to and which he called "Above Top Secret". *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 16:43-19:05)*
- Blue Room FOIA: Gerb cites a 1991 response from USAF Col. Eddie L. Anderson to a request from Brian Parks that materials relating to a "blue room", in this case a film, were destroyed on 9 September 1965, which Gerb calls curiously near the time of Goldwater's request. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 21:07-21:30)*
- Gerb speculates that the anonymous Marine [[marine-rb]], who guarded a disc-shaped craft in December 1963 at an undisclosed base three hours' flight from Cherry Point, may have guarded the Blue Room, with Wright-Patterson one possible location in that range. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 21:34-21:53,)*
- Gerb plans a full video on Wright-Patterson. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 19:05-19:12)*
