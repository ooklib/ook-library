John Lear, CIA-contract cargo pilot, aviation-record setter and 1980s UFO circuit figure, is the son of [[bill-lear]] and the man who introduced [[bob-lazar]] to [[george-knapp]] in 1987-89.

## Claims (host thesis, jm/townsend-brown)

- Lear introduced Lazar to Knapp, who broke the story; Jesse Michels says Corbell/Knapp told him Lear "could barely tie his shoes" but had a wide filter and was duped. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 34:04-34:33)*
- Michels alleges Lear was "doing the bidding of the CIA": he claimed to have left the Agency in 1983 but per Knapp may have flown for it into the 1980s. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 34:38-34:55)*
- Lear ran a UFO newsletter, spoke at Las Vegas conferences, joined MUFON, wrote anonymous pieces with Bill Cooper and visited Paul Bennewitz after Richard Doty had (Bennewitz reportedly "spooked"). *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 35:49-36:40)*
- In the 1970s he and photographer Jim Goodall camped outside Area 51/Groom Lake; security knew him; in 1987 he leaked the F-117 to KLAS. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 37:52-38:23, 40:24-40:51)*
- Host thesis: Lear was used to flood the zone with alien disinformation to shield terrestrial exotic-propulsion breakthroughs traced to [[thomas-townsend-brown]] via his father. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 46:38-46:55)* He was reportedly emotional on seeing footage of his father with Brown. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 46:28-46:38)*
