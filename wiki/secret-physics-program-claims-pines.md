[[matthew-pines]] argues, as first-principles inference and not evidence, that the US government almost certainly ran and runs secret fundamental-physics research, and evaluates unconventional physics ideas linked to UFO propulsion. Pines is a self-described non-physicist and stays agnostic on most of them.

## Claims

- Pines: "it's very clear to me that the US government has been conducting secret fundamental physics research", because WWII showed physics is the top strategic domain; he expects a systematic recruitment pipeline finding prodigies at 12-13 (as [[harald-malmgren]] was spotted), and says nerds doing math leave little footprint. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:40:04-3:41:16, 3:42:25-3:45:14)*
- He says the government is good at creating secret programs but poor at stimulating innovation, so "they have these programs and gotten nowhere" is possible; cases like Ning Li disappearing could mean either a suppressed independent breakthrough or an effort to absorb anything nearby. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:45:23-3:46:27)*
- Michels notes declassified CIA memos on exotic gravity research named places such as Purdue, Indiana, Princeton and MIT, and calls the mid-century gravity work "suspicious". *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:41:54-3:42:17, 3:39:43-3:39:56)*
- Michels cites [[thomas-townsend-brown]] as having made more progress than meets the eye tying electromagnetism to gravity and being obsessed with time travel. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:20:32-3:20:48, 3:28:41-3:29:35)*
- On the 1957 conference ([[chapel-hill-conference-1957]]), Michels (via Weinstein) says Hermann Bondi asked about negative mass and energy anomalies; Pines links this to the Casimir effect and the Alcubierre drive, notes general relativity only conserves energy locally, and says he "doesn't rule it out" without the fundamental theory. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:33:57-3:37:00, 3:38:33-3:39:13)*
- Pines is agnostic on scalar fields and torsion tweaks to Einstein's equations, comparing "bells and whistles to explain UFOs" to Ptolemaic epicycles. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:31:29-3:33:57)*
- He finds a high-intensity neutrino-beam communication through the Earth (for nuclear command redundancy) "not physically implausible", relating to [[neutrino-light-detector-claims]] in the wiki. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:29:51-3:31:22)*
- Michels recalls a "Stargate" film funded by the Air Force and wonders about deeper joint programs with NHI; see [[project-stargate]]. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:20:18-3:21:15)*
