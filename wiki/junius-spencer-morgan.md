British-based American banker who took over George Peabody & Company in 1864 and built it into the [[house-of-morgan]].

## Claims

- Chose to lend to France in the 1870 war when the [[rothschild-family]] and Barings declined, judging that France's diversified economy and tax system would repay: see [[franco-prussian-war-loan-1870]]. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 21:39, 21:52, 22:10)*
- Raised over 10 million pounds through his syndicate and gave France access to American arms, netting about 1.5 million pounds when France prepaid in 1873. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 22:23, 22:29, 23:06, 23:14)*
- Died later in a traffic accident, making J. Pierpont head of the house. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 26:35)*
- MagnatesMedia describes him as a wealthy banker and junior partner at George Peabody & Co, London, who sent his sickly son to the Azores and feared John was too rash. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 0:47, 1:22, 1:51, 2:07)*
