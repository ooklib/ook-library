Los Alamos National Laboratory is a DOE/NSA National Laboratory FFRDC founded during the
Manhattan Project. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 19:32–19:37)*

## Claims

- Cited among the AEC National Labs (alongside Sandia, Oak Ridge) allegedly designated by
  1948 classified executive orders as custodians for recovered nonhuman technical vehicles,
  under [[vannevar-bush]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 19:45–20:05)*
- [[donald-kerr]] served as director of Los Alamos from 1979 to 1985, prior to a career Gerb
  frames as emblematic of the government–FFRDC "revolving door." *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:39:14–1:39:25)*
- Cited among AEC National Labs given custody of recovered discs per 1949–1954 classified
  executive orders. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:06:33–2:06:47)*
- Its Z Division (forerunner of [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]]) began operations at
  Sandia Base in July 1945; transferred to AEC control 1 January 1947. Los Alamos personnel
  reportedly supported [[j-andrew-kissner|J. Andrew Kissner]]'s alleged AEC-lab custody of Roswell-era
  recovered discs, and hosted [[project-twinkle|Project Twinkle]] (1952). One of the NNSA's three
  multi-mission-laboratory FFRDCs sponsoring [[nevada-national-security-site|Nevada National
  Security Site]] experimental programs. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 16:07–17:22,
  27:39–28:49, 1:48:01–1:48:18)*
