Joeyisnotmyname is the Reddit and Twitter pseudonym of an independent researcher with a 3D-modeling background who has investigated [[michael-herrera]]'s claims since 2023 and appears as guest in [[gerb-herrera-1-video]]. Everything below is his own account or Gerb's report of it, and much of his evidence is unpublished.

- Joey contacted [[uap-gerb]] after the Marines video, says he has investigated Herrera deeply, talks with him weekly, and has enough evidence that Herrera met an insider in a black reverse-engineering program. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 1:22-2:16)*

He stresses this proves the context, not the UFO. *([gerb/herrera-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-28 - Michael Herrera - UFO Whistleblower (ft. Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 9:01-12:23)*

Joeyisnotmyname).md>) 12:23-13:27)*
