General Development Corporation was a Miami-based shell company created by [[paul-helliwell]]
and [[meyer-lansky]] to run the financial side of [[operation-x]], laundering unaccountable
heroin-trade proceeds. Eyes Wide Open notes it was "one of many such shell companies and banks
that would emerge throughout this history."

## Claims

- Created by Helliwell and Lansky in Miami as the vehicle through which Lansky ran Operation X's
  financial network. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 23:35-23:44)*
- Received the "unaccountable drug money from the jazz clubs and the Harlem streets," which was
  then diverted to the Sicilian mob (organized by [[lucky-luciano]]), which in turn funded the
  far-right underground network that made up [[operation-gladio]]. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 28:30-29:06)*

## Relationships

- Co-founded by [[paul-helliwell]] and [[meyer-lansky]]; financial hub of [[operation-x]].
- Proceeds diverted to [[lucky-luciano]]'s Sicilian mob and onward to [[operation-gladio]].
