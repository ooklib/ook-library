Third son of [[mayer-amschel-rothschild]], sent to London to run the family's English house; in [[empire-of-shadows-rothschild-documentary-2023]] he is the driver of the family's 19th-century dominance.

## Claims

- Arrived with 20,000 pounds; by his own account he took three profits in the textile trade (raw material, dyeing, manufacture) and grew it to 60,000, and the narrator adds that he smuggled textiles and precious metals. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 5:19, 5:50, 6:13, 6:19)*
- By 1808 he had sales reputedly of 800,000 pounds and planned a bank larger than his father's. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 6:34, 6:49)*
- In 1814 he bought 800,000 pounds of [[east-india-company]] gold, knowing the Duke of Wellington needed coin (he held many of Wellington's bills at a discount), arranged its transport to Portugal and France, and called it the best business he ever did. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 9:30, 9:44, 10:04)*
- Died 28 July 1836 aged 58, surprising given the family's longevity; the Times called it a major event for the City and Europe. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 11:46, 12:07)*

## Relationships

- Founded [[nm-rothschild-and-sons]]; son Lionel later took the London house; brother James led the family after 1836.
