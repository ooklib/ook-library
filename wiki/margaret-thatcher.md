Margaret Thatcher is cited by Green & Pleasant as the first named example, in the video's
40-year "same room" narrative, of a UK government acting on behalf of the interests of the
[[city-of-london-corporation]].

## Claims

- In 1986, Thatcher's government deregulated the London Stock Exchange in a single day — the
  "Big Bang" — after the City had wanted it for years. Twenty-two years on, the deregulated
  banking system collapsed, with the House of Commons Library putting the peak cash cost of the
  resulting bailout at £137 billion, borne by the British taxpayer
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 14:31, 14:41, 14:50, 15:00)*.

## Relationships

- Cited as having acted, per Green & Pleasant, on behalf of the interests of the
  [[city-of-london-corporation]] via the 1986 Big Bang deregulation.

## Speculation flag

The 1986 deregulation date and the £137 billion bailout figure are matters of public record; the
causal framing (that the City specifically "wanted" and "got" this policy, and that this
directly enabled the 2008 crash) is Green & Pleasant's interpretive claim, recorded here per this
wiki's convention rather than as independently verified fact.
