In the 1995 Q&A ([[schneider-las-vegas-lecture-1995]]) [[phil-schneider]] answers a question on Colorado by saying there are about four underground bases, one being Denver International Airport, which he jokingly calls an "air base spaceport", with eight levels beneath the tarmac *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 47:39)*. He adds that Denver has three fairly small bases, some only 500 feet deep and built 35 to 40 years earlier for equipment storage, plus bases at Fort Collins and Colorado Springs *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 52:55)*. This is an unverified claim; the transcript is the only source in the wiki.
