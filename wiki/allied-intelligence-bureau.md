The Allied Intelligence Bureau, formed in Brisbane in 1942 to share intelligence between the Allied armies in the Pacific, headed within [[charles-willoughby]]'s offices. [[eyes-wide-open]] (quoting David Foy) calls it "MacArthur's OSS": an undercover-warfare organization spanning intelligence and special operations groups.

## Claims

- After the March 1942 arrival in Melbourne, [[douglas-macarthur]] moved headquarters to Brisbane; Willoughby established his offices under the AIB. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 59:34-1:00:08)*
- The AIB was the vehicle for MacArthur's resistance to a separate [[office-of-strategic-services]] presence: MacArthur rejected a 1941 approach by a Coordinator of Information journalist and in July 1942 blocked [[william-donovan]]'s request (supported by the Pentagon) for an OSS presence in Australia. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:00:08-1:02:16)*
- Related wartime units named: the Counter Intelligence Corps in the Pacific reported to Willoughby's G-2 in Brisbane ([[counter-intelligence-corps]]); the Allied Translator and Interpreter Section (ATIS) produced a 683-page list of Japanese officers from documents found on a Tao Maru lifeboat in March 1943. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:03:13-1:04:35, 1:12:56-1:13:03)*
