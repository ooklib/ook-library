[[ross-coulthart]] argues that ufology treats celebrity witnesses as untouchable and that they should be cross-examined the way a court would. This page records his opinions and his specific comments on named figures.

## Claims

- He rejects "do you believe in UFOs?" as a question of religious faith; evidence, not belief, matters. He now finds videos nearly useless without multiple independent witnesses willing to sign statutory declarations or radar corroboration, and says AI fakes are making video unusable. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:11:16-2:12:51)*
- He says he would recognise a video as an insect (parallax) within seconds, as with a French correspondent's "4,000 mph" clip, and is polite about it. He finds [[mc-west]] useful though increasingly a debunker rather than a skeptic. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:07:44-2:09:22)*
- On [[bob-lazar]]: he has approached Lazar a couple of times but thinks Lazar avoids his "blowtorch" interviews. He believes Lazar almost certainly worked at Area 51, which is officially denied, because the late Dave Fuhof (who ran logistics there) had heard of S4 and spoke to people who saw Lazar at Area 51. He does not mind the brothel allegation but wants to raise the MIT/Caltech record and the element 115 isotope objection (see [[element-115-and-lazar-reactor-claims]]). *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:59:38-2:02:09)*
- He would like to interview [[travis-walton]] about inconsistencies he perceives in his reading. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:04:05-2:04:49)*
- On [[chris-bledsoe]], Ramsey says he has seen row lights over the ocean with the Bledsoes but urges caution because followers link it to faith. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:10:02-2:11:16)*
- On Larry Warren: see [[rendlesham-forest-incident-1980]] and [[larry-warren]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:55:45-1:59:38)*
- He argues people who refuse cross-examination make him question their motives, while Ramsey ends interviews that turn hostile; both want "a safe and vulnerable space". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:05:29-2:06:15)*
- He describes the UFO community as "a sort of a sewer of snide barbs" and unhelpfully divided. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:08:37-1:09:03)*
