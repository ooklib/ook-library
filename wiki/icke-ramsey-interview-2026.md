A roughly 2h33m interview (uploaded 2026-03-27, DEBRIEFED ep. 81) in which [[chris-ramsey]] (Area52) talks with [[david-icke]], introduced as a former BBC sports broadcaster and author of more than 20 books including *The Biggest Secret*. It is almost entirely Icke's metaphysical and conspiratorial worldview rather than UFO case material: reality as a simulation decoded by a "biological computer" body, reptilian and other non-human entities operating from an "astral" layer through a "global cult", energy-feeding ("loosh"), a reincarnation trap, and an accelerating AI and 6G "hive mind" agenda. Ramsey asks sympathetic follow-ups and offers his own out-of-body experiences, but the wiki records every substantive assertion as Icke's claim, none of it verified. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 0:00-1:13)*
