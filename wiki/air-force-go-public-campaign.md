Gerb's claim that in 2009-2012 the Air Force, specifically the hidden wing in the Air Force Test Center at the Edwards 412th Test Wing, under a two-star general with a "weirdly dataless career", conceived and killed a campaign to publicly reveal exotic airframes derived, reverse engineered and matured on the Western Ranges, without crediting non-human technology. Gerb does not know why it was conceived or why it was killed in 2012, and says he is familiar with it but has not discussed it publicly. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 2:05:54-2:06:57)*
