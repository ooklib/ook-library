Frank Wisner is introduced by Eyes Wide Open as "the head of covert operations in the future
CIA," who alongside [[james-jesus-angleton]] developed the OSS's working relationship with mob
boss [[meyer-lansky]] as part of [[operation-x]]. The channel flags him as a recurring figure for
future episodes; Part 4 separately covers his earlier wartime role as OSS station chief in
Romania.

## Claims

- Developed a working relationship with [[meyer-lansky]] alongside [[james-jesus-angleton]],
  central to launching [[operation-x]]'s mafia partnership. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 22:31-22:39)*
- Per Eyes Wide Open, the charming son of a rich Mississippi landowner, so privileged as a child
  he "didn't even dress himself" — a servant did it for him. Attended a prestigious private
  school and the University of Virginia, where he belonged to the secretive "Society of Seven"
  (whose membership is only revealed upon a member's death); graduated with a law degree and
  joined a Wall Street law firm, following what the host frames as the standard OSS-to-elite
  pipeline. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 17:58-18:53)*
- Served as OSS station chief in Romania during WWII, where he organized a community of German
  fascists into an anti-communist "stay-behind" force to fight a future Soviet invasion —
  foreshadowing the future [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]] network in Western Europe. This
  network was activated by the [[central-intelligence-group|Central Intelligence Group]] after
  the war but was quickly rounded up by Soviet intelligence and sent to the gulags, a failure
  Eyes Wide Open says hastened Romania's absorption into the USSR. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 18:53-19:16, 19:19-19:37)*
