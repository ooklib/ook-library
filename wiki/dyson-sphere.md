A Dyson sphere is a theoretical megastructure conceived by Freeman Dyson to enclose a star and harvest its energy, as explained by [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 7:40-8:23)*

## Claims

- Gerb ties it to a type II civilization on the [[kardashev-scale]] and says SETI has begun looking for Dyson sphere signatures. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 7:40-8:39)*
- He says it is not feasible with current engineering but might be built by von Neumann self-replicating probes harvesting asteroids and moons. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 8:39-9:10)*
