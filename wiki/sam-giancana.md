Sam "Momo" Giancana was boss of the Chicago outfit, one of the three main nodes of the [[national-crime-syndicate]] in [[eyes-wide-open]]'s model. ASR renders him "Gian kan"/"Gian kanana"; the normalization is my inference. In the plots he is first introduced to [[robert-maheu]] under the alias "Sam Gold".

## Claims

- Introduced at Miami's Kenilworth Hotel by [[johnny-roselli]] as "Sam Gold", later revealed to be Giancana. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:19-1:35)*
- Chicago outfit expanded in the postwar years to the Southwest by taking over the wire services (Texas, LA, Hollywood); it won the Chicago wire-service war with [[jack-ruby]] playing a key role, and sent Ruby to Dallas with a group of cronies (host citing [[peter-dale-scott]]). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 49:59-50:49, 53:07-53:38)*
- Rumor (host): [[joseph-kennedy]] worked with Giancana during Prohibition, and with his help swung the 1960 Chicago vote to JFK; ballot-stuffing rumors persisted in the Nixon-Hughes camp. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 50:02-50:32)*
- Had comparatively minor Havana holdings lost to Castro's nationalizations. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 34:09-34:16)*
- At the Miami meeting Giancana and Roselli proposed poison instead of a "Gangland hit", starting the plots' poison obsession. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:06:00-1:06:15)* See [[cia-mafia-assassination-plots]].
- Per [[peter-dale-scott]] (quoted), the CIA gave Giancana and Trafficante protection after [[robert-f-kennedy]]'s Feb 1960 book *The Enemy Within* named them. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:14:42-1:15:06)*
- Shot dead in his own house with a .22 pistol on 19 June 1975, days before he was to testify to the [[church-committee]]; the host notes he was never given the chance to testify. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:20:36-1:20:53, 1:22:36-1:22:44)*
