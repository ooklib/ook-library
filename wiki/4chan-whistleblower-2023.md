An anonymous poster on 4chan, in April 2023 (shortly before [[david-grush]] went public),
claimed intimate knowledge of UFOs, specifically USOs. Gerb states this account, alongside a
"24th STS Reddit post," has stood out to him as unusually credible among many clearly-nonsense
whistleblower claims. *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:28:19–1:28:45)*

## Claims made by the poster

1. UFOs are primarily unmanned drones. *(Gerb: agrees.)* *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:29:06–1:29:26)*
2. UFOs are built to spec each time they are deployed. *(Gerb: no independent knowledge, but
   plausible given the MITRE "tic tac factory" study.)* *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:29:26–1:29:33)*
3. UFOs are created by a mobile construction facility that hides in the ocean. *(Gerb: agrees
   this is one of several sources of UFOs.)* *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:29:33–1:29:47)*
4. The construction facility destroys anything that comes close to it and disappears for days
   when approached aggressively. *(Gerb: strongly disagrees — engineers on the program described
   the vessel as "more or less harmless.")* *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:29:47–1:30:04)*
5. The US believes the facility has been active on Earth for at least 100 years. *(Gerb: no
   knowledge either way.)* *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:30:04–1:30:12)*
- Additional claims Gerb has no knowledge on: the facility/mobile construction unit is
  burger/pear-shaped; smaller vehicles have gravity-manipulation engines that occasionally fail
  near the planet's surface; and the ratio of manned to unmanned craft from this USO.
  *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:31:23–1:31:45)*
- Additional claims Gerb disagrees with: that shooting down UFOs from this USO has "failed
  miserably," and that craft from the unit contain "element 115" (Gerb is "highly suspicious"
  of this, attributing it to the poster filling gaps with wider UFO-community lore).
  *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:32:01–1:32:22)*
- When asked "why do you not know anything about the last 2 years," the poster replied: "Moved
  on from the project because of new management that didn't trust the older proven methods" —
  which Gerb treats as strong corroboration of MITRE's alleged 2011–2017 leadership/briefing
  changes. *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:32:22–1:33:06)*

## Gerb's overall assessment

- Believes this individual may have been on the MITRE program or close with sources who were,
  but is wary because the poster claims more knowledge than any of Gerb's own vetted engineer
  contacts who were formally read into the "Tic Tac Factory" program. *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:30:20–1:31:00)*
