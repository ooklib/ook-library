Coulthart thinks it may be an attempt by pro-disclosure people to inject urgency, or just "Pentagon titlet" (gossip), and lists Y2K, 2012 and 2021 as earlier scares. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:44:02-2:46:35)*

Ramsey fears something that can "tear a hole in the fabric" of things.
