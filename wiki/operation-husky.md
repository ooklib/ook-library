Operation Husky was the 1943 Allied invasion of Sicily, for which the OSS obtained Mafia
intelligence and guidance (via [[operation-underworld]] and [[lucky-luciano]]) — laying the
groundwork for the postwar Mafia-Vatican-CIA alliance in Sicily and southern Italy.

## Claims

- The emphasized wartime goal of the dirty alliance between organized crime and the US security
  state (Operation Underworld) was to obtain Mafia intelligence to help with the Allied invasion
  of Sicily, otherwise known as Operation Husky. In 1943, the OSS was charged with implementing
  the psychological warfare plan drafted by the Joint Chiefs of Staff and the ONI to bring Sicily
  under Allied control; the plan had been dropped in close consultation with [[lucky-luciano]],
  who from his prison cell shared the location and identities of key Mafia figures who could help
  administer Sicily once Mussolini's forces were expelled. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 12:14-13:19)*
- [[calogero-vizzini]]'s men guided American tanks through Sicily as part of the operation,
  leading to "an astounding military victory" on the island. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 16:29-16:43)*
