[[karl-nell]] lists six reasons a government would withhold UFO/NHI information, offered as a general framework rather than a specific allegation. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 19:41-20:45)*

## The six reasons

- National security (not revealing to an adversary what you know). *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 19:48-20:04)*
- Lack of a plan for a profoundly impactful subject, including technology with unknown ramifications. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 20:04-20:13)*
- Societal disruption, the "ontological shock" of realizing "angels and aliens" exist. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 20:13-20:28)*
- A covert agreement from which people benefit from the status quo. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 20:28-20:37)*
- Potential misdeeds and the desire to cover them up. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 20:37-20:45)*
- Ordinary organizational intransigence and low priority. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 20:37-20:45)*
