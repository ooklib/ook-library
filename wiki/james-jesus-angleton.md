James Jesus Angleton was the OSS station chief in Rome during WWII and, per Eyes Wide Open, would
go on to be the CIA's director of counterintelligence into the 1970s. Nicknamed "the ghost" or
"the cadaver" (this episode's closing chapter title) for his elusive, skeletal presence, he
viewed [[allen-dulles]] as a mentor and personally helped extract fascist collaborators including
[[junio-valerio-borghese|the Black Prince]].

## Claims

- Son of Hugh Angleton, owner of the National Cash Register Company and president of the American
  Chamber of Commerce in Italy, who also worked with the OSS during the war — likely the reason
  his son was put in charge of the OSS's X2 counterintelligence program in Italy. A Yale graduate
  and avid poet, deeply paranoid throughout his career, skulking through offices accusing
  colleagues of Communist sympathies and searching rooms for hidden bugs. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 22:10-23:07)*
- Despite his paranoia, described as "a master of deception," present in the background of many
  of the OSS and CIA's most significant operations; an Anglophile, ruthless anti-communist, and
  devout Roman Catholic, "largely amoral," and — "most importantly to his career aspirations" —
  viewed Allen Dulles "as a second father and a mentor." *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 23:07-23:40)*
- While stationed in Italy, helped Dulles smuggle key fascist elements out of occupied territory,
  most notably [[junio-valerio-borghese|Junio Valerio Borghese ("the Black Prince")]]; attended a
  meeting at a villa on Lake Garda (also attended by [[karl-wolff]]) at which Borghese agreed to
  cooperate with Operation Sunrise in exchange for clemency. When Borghese was arrested for war
  crimes in May 1945, Angleton personally secured his release and picked him up from his
  detention center in Milan. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 23:43-25:10)*
- Along with [[william-donovan]], briefly considered a scheme to install Borghese as king of a
  Catholic-dominated southern Italy, before opting for the less conspicuous plan of using him as
  commander of [[operation-gladio|Gladio]] forces in Italy. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 25:12-25:24)*
