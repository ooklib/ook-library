James Jesus Angleton was the OSS station chief in Rome during WWII and, per Eyes Wide Open, would
go on to be the CIA's director of counterintelligence into the 1970s. Nicknamed "the ghost" or
"the cadaver" (this episode's closing chapter title) for his elusive, skeletal presence, he
viewed [[allen-dulles]] as a mentor and personally helped extract fascist collaborators including
[[junio-valerio-borghese|the Black Prince]].

## Claims

- Son of Hugh Angleton, owner of the National Cash Register Company and president of the American
  Chamber of Commerce in Italy, who also worked with the OSS during the war — likely the reason
  his son was put in charge of the OSS's X2 counterintelligence program in Italy. A Yale graduate
  and avid poet, deeply paranoid throughout his career, skulking through offices accusing
  colleagues of Communist sympathies and searching rooms for hidden bugs. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 22:10-23:07)*
- Despite his paranoia, described as "a master of deception," present in the background of many
  of the OSS and CIA's most significant operations; an Anglophile, ruthless anti-communist, and
  devout Roman Catholic, "largely amoral," and — "most importantly to his career aspirations" —
  viewed Allen Dulles "as a second father and a mentor." *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 23:07-23:40)*
- While stationed in Italy, helped Dulles smuggle key fascist elements out of occupied territory,
  most notably [[junio-valerio-borghese|Junio Valerio Borghese ("the Black Prince")]]; attended a
  meeting at a villa on Lake Garda (also attended by [[karl-wolff]]) at which Borghese agreed to
  cooperate with Operation Sunrise in exchange for clemency. When Borghese was arrested for war
  crimes in May 1945, Angleton personally secured his release and picked him up from his
  detention center in Milan. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 23:43-25:10)*
- Along with [[william-donovan]], briefly considered a scheme to install Borghese as king of a
  Catholic-dominated southern Italy, before opting for the less conspicuous plan of using him as
  commander of [[operation-gladio|Gladio]] forces in Italy. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 25:12-25:24)*

- Alongside [[frank-wisner]], developed the OSS's working relationship with Mafia boss
  [[meyer-lansky]], central to [[paul-helliwell]]'s [[operation-x]] narcotics-funding scheme.
  *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 22:31-22:39)*

- As the CIA official in charge of the Italy desk, specifically Sicily, during the 1948 elections,
  worked with FBN agent [[george-white]] on a 1946 deal releasing [[lucky-luciano]] on parole to
  Sicily, and allegedly ensured Luciano was deported alongside Detroit mob boss
  [[frank-coppola|Frank Kapola]]. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 25:16-25:57)*

- Trained as a future OSS agent by [[george-white]] at the New York staging ground for new OSS
  agents during WWII; White and Angleton formed a strong working relationship, and White's
  comfort collaborating with mobsters was, per the host, "likely noticed" by Angleton, who himself
  became closely aligned with organized crime throughout his career. Along with [[allen-dulles]]
  and [[richard-helms|Richard Helms]] and [[william-colby]] (also trained by White), Angleton is
  classed by the host among the CIA's Ivy League "spies"/executive class, as opposed to
  working-class "operators" like White, Bill Harvey, and Frank Sturgis who carried out the
  agency's "dirty work." *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 50:17-50:57, 51:31-52:20)*
- As early as 1946, several sources allege Angleton and [[meyer-lansky]] separately possessed
  compromising photographs of [[j-edgar-hoover]] receiving oral sex from deputy
  [[clyde-tolson]]; CIA affiliate Gordon Novel claimed Angleton showed him the photos in 1967 to
  demonstrate he was not "all powerful," and former OSS officer John Weitz alleged Angleton showed
  him the same photos at a 1950s dinner party. Authors are divided on whether Angleton gave the
  photos to Lansky or vice versa. Eyes Wide Open frames the alleged leverage as evidence the CIA
  and Mafia shared an interest in coercing the FBI to permit continued narcotics/organized-crime
  activity. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 36:21-38:44)*
- Allegedly helped cover up details of the murder of Mary Pinchot Meyer, wife of [[cord-meyer]] and JFK's alleged mistress; deferred to a later episode. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 27:22-27:48)*

- Named as a guest at [[allen-dulles]]'s home in 1962-63 alongside [[e-howard-hunt]] (Detachment 202 episode, per Talbot). *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 8:33-8:37)*
