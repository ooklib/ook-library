Former Air Force base in San Bernardino, California, site of the 12 November 1988 air show at which [[brad-sorenson]] said he was taken to a classified exhibit in a hangar at [[air-force-plant-42]] ([[flux-liner-arv-saga]]).

## Claims (gerb/mccandlish)

- The show was attended by [[mark-mccandlish]]'s friend Sorenson and a client; the military 727 to Palmdale left from Norton. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 5:01-7:10)*
- Lt. Col. John Williams, an electrical engineer for construction at Norton in 1981-1982 who testified for the [[disclosure-project]] in 2000, said the base had a facility so secret even the wing commander could not enter, rumored to house a UFO. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 35:30-36:02)*
