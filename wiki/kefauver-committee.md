The Kefauver Committee, a 1950 Senate committee investigating organized crime's influence in the
United States, is named for Senator Estes Kefauver. Eyes Wide Open notes [[george-white]] was
present on it — in an unclear capacity, speculated by the host to be as a CIA attaché.

## Claims

- A 1950 Senate committee investigating the influence of organized crime in the United States,
  named after Senator Estes Kefauver. [[george-white]] was present that same year, having also
  worked with [[joseph-mccarthy]]'s committee on communist influence in the State Department; on
  both committees White performed his role as an "operator," though it is unclear precisely what
  that role was — the host's "pet theory" is that White served as a CIA attaché during the Red
  Scare period. White testified before the committee, falsely claiming (per the host) that the OSS
  "remained aloof" from and had no awareness of [[operation-underworld]]'s collaboration with
  [[lucky-luciano]]. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 53:26-54:23)*
