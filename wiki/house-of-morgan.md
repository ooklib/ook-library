The Anglo-American banking house that grew out of George Peabody & Company through [[junius-spencer-morgan]] and [[j-pierpont-morgan]] and, in the [[empire-of-shadows-rothschild-documentary-2023]] account, displaced the [[rothschild-family]] as the dominant financier.

## Claims

- Junius Morgan took control of George Peabody & Company in 1864 and, seeing government finance and wars as the big opportunity, financed France in 1870: see [[franco-prussian-war-loan-1870]]. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 19:41, 19:47, 20:07)*
- J. Pierpont Morgan became head after Junius died in a traffic accident and organised the gold-for-Treasury bond deal in the [[panic-of-1893]]. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 26:35, 27:28)*
- In World War I the bank, led by Jack Morgan, was the key financier for the US government and lent to European allies, overshadowing the Rothschilds' scale. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 30:33, 30:39)*

## Relationships

- Led by [[junius-spencer-morgan]], then [[j-pierpont-morgan]]; partnered with and eclipsed the [[rothschild-family]]. Compare American tycoons in [[rockefeller-family]].
