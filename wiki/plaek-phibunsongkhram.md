Thai prime minister ("Phibun") who aligned Thailand with Japan in WWII, was ousted, then returned in
the 1947 coup and became a figurehead for the military; per Eyes Wide Open he was overthrown by
[[sarit-thanarat]] in 1958.

## Claims

- Authorized poppy growing in northern Thailand in 1947 (restarting a taxed industry). *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 25:41-27:07, 29:42-29:54)*
- Feared the US was positioning [[phao-sriyanon]] against him; reprimanded Phao in 1955 over an
  opium-seizure reward. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 35:20-35:38, 41:39-41:53)*
