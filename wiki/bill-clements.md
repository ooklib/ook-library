Bill Clements Jr. (ASR "Clemens") is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as president of SEDCO oil company, a large international oil firm, who teamed up with [[george-h-w-bush]] to develop [[zapata-offshore]].

## Claims

- Bush moved to Houston and teamed with Clements on Zapata Offshore. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 10:34-10:57)*
- From 1968 (starting with [[richard-nixon]]) a prominent GOP backer; rewarded as deputy secretary of defense under Nixon and Ford (1973-77) and elected Texas governor for non-consecutive terms (1979-83, 1987-91), the first Republican elected governor of Texas since 1870 (host's figures). His governorships coincided with Bush's vice presidency and presidency. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 10:57-11:53)*
- Host: Bush's arrival in Texas coincided with the GOP's rise there, and allying with Bush "appeared to spell good fortunes" for those chosen, Clements being elevated "in lockstep with poppy Bush." *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 11:53-12:24)*
