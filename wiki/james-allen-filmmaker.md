Young film-maker who produced the 2014 documentary "Zero Point: The Story of Mark McCandlish and the Flux Liner" (the video description spells it 2014, Gerb once says 2004). Died in 2014 within about 2.5 months of a sudden illness, a month before the film's release.

## Claims (gerb/mccandlish)

- [[mark-mccandlish]] says pathology on Allen found radioisotopes such as uranium, thorium, beryllium and cadmium not found naturally, and concluded he had been poisoned. Gerb counts his death among the suspicious deaths around the topic (see [[mccandlish-death-and-suppression-claims]]) and dedicates the video to Allen and McCandlish. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 57:59-59:12,)*
- Gerb draws many clips from the documentary and recommends it. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 35:22-35:30,)*
