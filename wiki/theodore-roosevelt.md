US president from 1901 after McKinley's assassination, portrayed by MagnatesMedia as Morgan's adversary and, in 1907, reluctant partner.

## Claims

- Roosevelt ordered the Justice Department to sue Morgan's Northern Securities Corporation under the Sherman Antitrust Act and the company was split up; Morgan had been shielded by his closeness to McKinley. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 8:27, 8:45, 8:54, 8:58)*
- Morgan is quoted hoping the first lion in Africa "does his duty" by Roosevelt. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 9:08)*
- In 1907 Roosevelt called on Morgan to rescue the banks: see [[panic-of-1907]]. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 14:27)*

## Relationships

- Opponent then partner of [[j-pierpont-morgan]].

## From MagnatesMedia on Rockefeller (mm/rockefeller)

- Ran against [[standard-oil]] as a campaign promise to take down monopolies, and launched the federal suit that ended in the [[standard-oil-dissolution-1911]]; the suit outlasted his presidency. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 40:40, 42:26, 43:15)* His earlier rebate law is noted in [[railroad-rebates]].
