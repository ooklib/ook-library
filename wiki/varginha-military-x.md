"Military X" is the pseudonym [[james-fox]] uses for a Brazilian military witness who allegedly drove the captured creature to the hospital and later trucks from Humanitas Hospital to a military base, and saw a being in a box. He appeared on camera from behind with his voice disguised.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says the man told him early on that "all the money in the world" would not make him come forward; Fox built trust over FaceTime, family talk and personal stories, then insisted on going right away when the man wavered, and got the interview. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 45:31-47:10)*
- The next day a second man, involved in transport and said to have shot video at a hospital, was to be met with Marco Leal posing as Military X's cousin; instead Military X got a call from his base asking whether he had met an American filmmaker and confirming his address, and the second man was summoned to the base. Fox says a day's delay would have meant the interview was never obtained and that he "probably would have seen the footage". *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 47:31-48:43)*
- Testimony that he drove the trucks from Humanitas Hospital to a military base and then from the base to Campinas, and that metal boxes were loaded; see [[varginha-usaf-landing-and-foia-claims]]. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:37:15-1:37:37)*
