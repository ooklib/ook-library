Fulgencio Batista, Cuban army general and dictator, is the counterpart to [[meyer-lansky]]'s Cuban gambling scheme in `ewa/dallas-4` ([[eyes-wide-open]]).

## Claims

- Per *Meyer Lansky: Mogul of the Mob*, Lansky first befriended him in 1932, shortly before the 1933 Sergeants' coup that made him nominal army chief of staff but in reality the man running the government until 1944 (elected constitutional president 1940-44). He returned in a 1952 military coup overthrowing [[carlos-prio-socarras]] and led a "brutal dictatorship" until Castro overthrew him in 1959. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 3:55-4:51)*
- Per Doc Stacher's recollection, Lansky showed Batista suitcases of cash in a hotel; the syndicate guaranteed him $3-5 million a year in return for a casino monopoly at the Hotel Nacional "and everywhere else on the island where we thought tourists would come", plus a cut of profits. The host says this gave the [[national-crime-syndicate]] unprecedented influence. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 7:47-8:41)*
- Hemming (Argosy 1993, via the host): the mob wanted "their boys" on both sides, with Batista and with Castro, in the 1958 overthrow. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 24:49-25:14)*

## Relationships

- Business partner of [[meyer-lansky]]; ousted by [[fidel-castro]] in the [[cuban-revolution]]; deposed [[carlos-prio-socarras]] in 1952.
