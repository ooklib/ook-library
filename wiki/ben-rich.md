Ben Rich, the second director of [[skunk-works|Lockheed Skunk Works]] (1975–1991), is famous in
UFO research for a series of alleged statements interpreted by some as an admission that
Lockheed possessed physics-defying, possibly nonhuman-derived technology.
*([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 1:17:17–1:18:11)*

## Claims

- At a 1993 UCLA speech, allegedly stated: "We already have the means to travel among the stars,
  but these technologies are locked up in black projects, and it would take an act of God to
  ever get them out to benefit humanity. Anything you can imagine, we already know how to do."
  Corroborated by attendee and aerospace engineer Tom Keller (2010 MUFON article) and former
  MUFON director John Haren, both of whom recalled Rich ending the talk with a slide of a black
  disc departing Earth and the words "we now have the technology to take ET home."
  *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 1:18:11–1:20:12)*
- Per a secondary account attributed to "Tarzan," in Q&A after the same talk, Rich reportedly
  stated humanity had found "an error in the equations" (possibly Maxwell's equations) enabling
  faster-than-expected interstellar travel, and, when asked how ESP works, replied "all points
  in time and space are connected... that's how it works." Gerb flags "Tarzan" as later charged
  with serious crimes regarding a minor, urging caution with this specific account.
  *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 1:20:12–1:21:10)*
- Jane's defense writer James Goodall — a personal friend, reportedly among the last people to
  speak with Rich before his death — recounted Rich telling him: "Ah, anything you can imagine
  that you've seen at Star Trek and this and that, we already have done at the skunk works... It's
  not at Area 51. It's out in the desert there... we have things out in the desert that is 50
  years beyond what you can comprehend." *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md)
  1:21:16–1:23:36)*
- David Fravor recounted Rich (via Godsmack singer Sully Erna, Fravor's neighbor) telling him the
  2004 Nimitz "tic tac" "came out of the Lockheed Skunk Works... it's one of ours," a test to see
  how the US military would react. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md)
  1:14:05–1:15:22)*
