Mitchell WerBell III, the "Mercenary," is described by Eyes Wide Open as the most obscure Detachment 202 alumnus: an OSS veteran turned private counter-insurgency adviser and arms developer (Psionics, sound suppressors, the Mac-10) who served governments across the globe. Sources are mainly a December 1970 Atlanta magazine article (via the Washington Post) and Gaeton Fonzi's *The Last Investigation*. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 17:32-18:25)*

## Claims

- Per his own account, son of a Russian-American liaison to the White Russian forces; born in Pennsylvania; joined the OSS and worked under [[paul-helliwell]] in China behind Japanese lines. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 17:39-18:06)*
- In 1948 bought the Dobbs estate near Atlanta and founded paramilitary and defense research firms, notably Psionics, said to have patented the first submachine-gun suppressors and the Mac-10; Psionics was absorbed by the Mac-10's manufacturer. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 18:25-19:03)*
- [[john-singlaub]] says in the documentary *American Mercenaries* that WerBell supplied suppressors and special weapons in Vietnam. He himself said he was not a CIA employee but worked independently. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 19:22-19:34, 20:19-20:31)*
- Worked for the Trujillo regime (Dominican Republic), Batista (Cuba) and Duvalier (Haiti), via [[environmental-industries]]; in 1966 was involved in a coup against Duvalier. Told Fonzi he sat in Miami with "a goddamn million dollars in cash for the guy who was going to take Fidel out"; the host draws a potential link to Miami paymaster [[paul-helliwell]] (speculation). *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 20:31-21:41)*
