Podcaster and YouTube interviewer behind The Winston Marshall Show (source key `wm`), whose episodes cover the World Economic Forum, the Great Reset and elite power structures. He interviewed [[desiree-fixler]] in [[wef-insider-interview-2025]].

## Claims

- In the interview he adds his own reading of [[klaus-schwab]]'s 2020 book The Great Reset: that its "trilemma" of democracy, sovereignty and global governance is resolved by defending global governance, and that Schwab's 2022 follow-up The Great Narrative is full of climate-catastrophe language despite calling itself hopeful. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 56:59, 57:03, 1:09:26)*
- He argues the WEF is at minimum influencing and, in Fixler's stronger claim, controlling the West, and presses her on whether its power is soft or hard. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 58:34, 1:01:25)*
- He says Schwab has been found to have embezzled funds, citing a Wall Street Journal story, and was forced to step down (his characterization, presented as fact in the video and not verified here). *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:19:43)*
