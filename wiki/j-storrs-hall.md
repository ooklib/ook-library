J. Storrs Hall is the scientist credited with the utility fog concept of foglets ([[foglets-and-programmable-matter]]). [[jesse-michels]] cites him in [[sentinels-of-ether-michels-episode-2026]] (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 50:32-51:29)*), including his claim that a lot of the work needed for real nanotechnology was actively suppressed, which Michels uses to explain why civilian nanotech lags Star Trek replicators (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 56:21-56:42)*). ASR renders the name "J. Stores Hall".
