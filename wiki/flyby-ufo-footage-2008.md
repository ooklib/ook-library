A roughly 20-second, sub-240p clip of a disc-shaped object moving beside the wing of a jet, first traced to April 2008 and uploaded to YouTube on 14 May 2008. [[uap-gerb]] analyzes it in [[gerb-flyby-video]] and finds it unexplained, without claiming it is authentic. Origin and authenticity are unverified.

## Claims

- Origin: it appeared on 14 May 2008 on a channel called "Danny Lampkin" (long dead) with the description that he was not sure about it but downloaded it for viewers to decide; Gerb traces it back only to about April 2008, when theufochronicles posted it as "Flying Saucer UFO filmed from jet window". *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 0:00-3:55)*
- Footage: it is a second-generation screen recording; reflections suggest a camcorder inside a military cockpit (possibly a Gentex MBU-23P respirator mask), and Gerb guesses a NASA chase F-18A with a rear-mounted camcorder, noting the port-side wing with empty wingtip pylons matches an F/A-18 Super Hornet view. *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 3:55-5:27)*
- Sound: a loud muffled noise and possibly a faint female voice; Gerb offers two possibilities, original cockpit audio re-recorded, or ambient noise of the screen-recording location, noting it resembles the ambient sound of the 2019 Navy sphere-into-water video posted by [[jeremy-corbell]] (recorded aboard the USS Nimitz per Gerb) and citing a Reddit user (VCAmaster) who found the audio waveform does not cut at the footage cut. *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 5:27-8:02)*
- Craft: almost identical to the saucer described by Lt. [[robert-jacobs]] in his warhead shootdown footage, with a top protrusion like half a ping-pong ball as in the [[mcminnville-ufo-photographs-1950]] images, flying tilted as in many saucer accounts; even [[bob-lazar]] claimed discs fly "belly first". *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 8:02-8:58)*
- Proximity: Gerb says the footage would show a UFO within about 15 m of an aircraft, and links it to the alleged comms log in [[alleged-uaptf-classified-report-leak]]. *([gerb/flyby](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-05 - Incredible UFO Footage - FLYBY.md>) 3:13-3:28,)*
