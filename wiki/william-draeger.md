A former USAF member and Spanish speaker who, per [[uap-gerb]], appears in [[leonard-stringfield]]'s crash-retrieval reports as "Del Rio, Texas 1950" although his account concerns the [[willingham-langtry-crash-1955]]. Presented by Gerb as an independent witness; all below are his claims as relayed.

## Claims (gerb/del-rio)

- Draeger was hired as an interpreter for a Japanese documentary about a UFO crash involving a colonel near the Mexican border. Gerb has not found the documentary but has seen screenshots in [[noe-torres]]'s book. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 19:24-20:07)*
- In an affidavit, Draeger says he contacted a Mexican general who commanded that border area; the general said "yes I know about that. I don't have any papers or documents to prove it but due to my position I know about that." On camera, with [[robert-willingham]] and the crew present, the general denied and evaded questions about the incident and UFOs. Draeger calls him beyond reproach and a "military celebrity" in Mexico. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 20:07-20:44)*
- Gerb notes the Stringfield entry's 1950 date is an example of the two cases being mixed up. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 19:24-20:07)*
