Bluegill Triple Prime was the fourth-of-series high-altitude Bluegill nuclear test of 1962's [[operation-dominic]] at Johnston Island, an X-ray-based missile-defense experiment. In this transcript [[harald-malmgren]] and host [[jesse-michels]] allege it was also a UFO shootdown. All claims below are attributed.

## Claims

- Host: Operation Dominic / Fishbowl ran April-October 1962 with 31 nuclear tests; Starfish Prime caused an EMP over 1,400 km; the last were the Bluegill tests using a novel X-ray-based missile-defense concept, and Malmgren was put in charge of the cost assessments. *(Host, background:* *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 53:26-54:38)**)*

Harald Malmgren).md>) 54:48-55:41, 57:30-57:51)*

Harald Malmgren).md>) 57:51-59:03, 1:02:27-1:02:46)*
