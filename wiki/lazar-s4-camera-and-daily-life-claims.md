- Food at S4 came from generic vending machines (M&Ms, a coffee/imitation chicken soup/hot chocolate machine, sandwiches); his usual snack was peanut-butter crackers *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:53:12-1:54:00)*.
