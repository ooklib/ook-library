Two US Navy submersibles, Mystic (DSRV-1) and Avalon (DSRV-2), operated 1970 to 2008 and built by Lockheed Missiles and Space Company. Officially they were for rescuing the crews of stranded submarines. Gerb argues they and unbuilt sister craft, the deep submergence search vehicles (DSSVs), were used for clandestine retrieval, and uses them to interpret [[deep-sea-triangle-recovery-1991]].

## Claims (gerb/navy)

- Two DSRVs were operational 1970 to 2008, transportable by truck, C-5 or naval vessel to rescue submarine crews within 72 hours, with a guidance system Gerb calls more complex than Apollo 11's. No rescue was ever performed because no American submarine sank after 1971. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 49:43-50:44)*
- The vehicles were born from the 1964 [[deep-submergence-systems-project]], founded out of the Navy special projects office partly after the loss of the USS Thresher, and their 1970-72 introduction dovetails with NURO's 1969 founding. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 50:44-51:24)*
- [[john-craven]] wrote that many highly classified missions could not be accomplished without a DSRV system and that it would be available for "intelligence mission impossibles". *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 51:24-52:08)*
- The DSSP planned six DSRVs plus DSSVs intended for seafloor exploration and recovery with a 20,000 ft operating depth, never officially built. Gerb hypothesizes the vessel in the 1991 case was a DSSV. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 55:16-55:59, 1:09:30-1:09:59, 1:39:35-1:40:09)*
- A 2011 Nuclear Regulatory Commission email about the Fukushima tsunami says both US coasts had a "very operational ready" DSRV, cited as support for the Virginia-based crew. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 57:09-57:18, 1:40:42-1:40:51)*
- Officially the Mystic and Avalon depth was just over a mile, and using Marine divers was unusual, which Gerb flags as a reason for more vetting. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:09:13-1:09:30)*
