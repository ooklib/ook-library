Franklin D. Roosevelt was President of the United States when, per Eyes Wide Open, the surprise
attack on Pearl Harbor necessitated the creation of the
[[office-of-strategic-services|Office of Strategic Services]] in 1942.

## Claims

- Created the OSS in 1942 as a response to the United States being caught off guard by the
  attack on Pearl Harbor in 1941. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 6:37-6:49)*
- Appointed [[william-donovan]] as OSS director, likely on the strength of Donovan's WWI-era
  intelligence background as US Coordinator of Information. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 13:18-13:26)*

- At the Casablanca Conference, promised Stalin he would not sign a separate peace with Germany
  and would follow a policy of unconditional surrender — the policy [[allen-dulles]]'s
  [[operation-sunrise]] allegedly defied. Implemented [[operation-safe-haven]] to confiscate Nazi
  bank assets worldwide, a policy the Dulles brothers allegedly circumvented for their clients'
  funds at the [[bank-for-international-settlements]]. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 5:57-6:06, 6:56-7:00)*

- Eyes Wide Open alleges FDR's maternal grandfather Warren Delano built the family's fortune
  shipping heroin/opium from Shanghai and Hong Kong to the United States, part of a broader
  pattern of "illustrious American families" enriched by the 19th-century opium trade.
  [[claire-chennault]] separately lobbied FDR to fund a private mercenary air force for
  [[chiang-kai-shek]] using covert American war funds, becoming "the Flying Tigers" and later
  [[civil-air-transport]]. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 4:17-4:29, 11:39-11:46)*

- War Conspiracy 1: the host notes his maternal grandfather Warren Delano's fortune came from the opium trade ([[china-lobby]]); in 1941 he tasked [[thomas-corcoran]] with secret aid channels to [[chiang-kai-shek]], approved the [[flying-tigers]] and signed a secret executive order letting them recruit US reserve officers. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 6:24-6:49, 17:08-17:34, 19:00-19:14)*
