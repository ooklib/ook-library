The "Alien Reproduction Vehicle" (ARV) concept — a human-built craft reverse-engineered from
recovered nonhuman technology — is most associated in the UFO research community with the
"TR-3B," a claimed triangular anti-gravity craft.

## Claims

- A *Jane's Defense Weekly* article postscript reportedly stated that anyone skeptical of
  anti-gravity science "even for the US Air Force" should consider SAIC's electric-propulsion
  study for the Air Force's Astronautics Laboratory (later Phillips Lab), which investigated
  inductive coupling between electromagnetic and gravitational forces for spacecraft
  propulsion. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:19–2:00)*
- An embedded interview clip states an ARV is "based on advanced anti-gravity and zero-point
  energy propulsion systems," manufactured by a consortium including
  [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]], [[northrop-grumman|Northrop]],
  [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]], and others. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:06–1:19)*
- [[edgar-fuchs|Master Sgt. Edgar Fuchs]] claimed the TR-3B's maiden operational flight (mid-
  1990s) was administered by NRO/NSA/CIA and built primarily by Lockheed, Boeing, Northrop, and
  Teledyne Ryan, funded by [[strategic-defense-initiative|SDI]] money, and powered by a
  reverse-engineered "Magnetic Field Disruptor" (MFD) developed at
  [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]] and [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence
  Livermore]] labs. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:11:31–1:11:46, 1:32:39–1:33:41)*
- A 1988–90 SAIC report (author Dennis Cravens, with contribution from Los Alamos's Ferris
  Williams) on the [[biefeld-brown-effect|Biefeld–Brown effect]] proposed anti-gravity via
  rotating mercury cylinders — a propulsion method Gerb notes matches Fuchs's description of
  the TR-3B's MFD almost exactly. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:31:14–1:33:04)*
- In the MITRE video, Gerb explicitly states MITRE's alleged reverse-engineering program does
  **not** produce TR-3Bs, tic tacs, or power sources — implying a different set of institutions
  (per this SAIC video: SAIC, Lockheed, Northrop, Sandia, Livermore) handle ARV/TR-3B work.
  *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.txt) 58:56–59:08)*
- [[bob-lazar|Bob Lazar]] separately claimed to have reverse-engineered UFO propulsion systems
  at Area 51's S4 site while employed by [[eg-and-g|EG&G]]. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  21:52–21:59, 1:21:52–1:21:59)*
