Alleged Nazi-era secret society. The Banijay documentary (ASR: "vril", "real", "brill", "thrill") describes it as meeting in Munich (the Four Seasons Hotel is named as a meeting place) with about a dozen leading Nazis planning world rule. Note that [[jason-jorjani]] in another source says he found no independent Vril Society ([[thule-society]]); treat the documentary's account as contested folklore.

## Claims

- Named after the "vril" force in Bulwer-Lytton's *The Coming Race*, with subterranean dwellers and a black-sun-powered inner world reachable via the poles. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 16:05,)*
- Pre-war seance in a Bavarian country house attended by Munich elite and by the Thule, Vril, Brothers of the Black Stone and Black Sun groups (per Andrew Gough): a veiled woman said she was in contact with a planet "Aldebaran", 64 light years away in Taurus, channelled messages in a German Templar script and ancient Sumerian, and a blueprint for a free-energy spacecraft. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 18:04,)*
- Female mediums (named Maria Orsic and "Sigrun") with long hair as "antennae" supposedly channelled the extraterrestrials; Nazi engineers attended. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 24:56,)*
- Theo Paijmans says original Vril documents survive, a rare pamphlet in one library describing a novel technology (a mix of avant-garde and esoteric language, needing "only one machine") and inviting readers to join. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 23:25,)*
- The narration says this contact "led to" real UFOs the Nazis hoped would win the war. See [[nazi-saucer-program-claims]]. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 23:08)*
