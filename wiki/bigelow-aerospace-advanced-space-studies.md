Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies (BASS) is Robert Bigelow's LLC awarded a DIA contract
(HHM402-08-C-0072, 22 September 2008) under the [[aawsap|OAP]] program to research advanced
aerospace technology, initiated by Sen. Harry Reid
(2007–2008, ~$22M budget over 5 years). *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md)
1:50:35–1:51:51)*

## Claims

- Per a leaked 10-month report dated 30 July 2009, BASS was tasked to research 12 designated
  technology areas: lift, propulsion, control, power generation, spatial/temporal translation,
  materials, configuration/structure, signature reduction, human interface, human effects,
  armament, and supporting areas. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 1:55:31–1:56:14)*
- By mid-2009, BASS reported substantial progress procuring an underground vault "to pursue and
  acquire recovered hardware and biological samples from the corporate world and/or private
  individuals and groups" — a 5,000 sq ft facility with 3-ft-thick walls, 15–20 ft below ground,
  multiple tunnels and security doors, targeted for operational status by late 2009/early 2010.
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 1:56:14–1:57:28)*
- BASS was the proposed recipient of [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]]'s divested UFO
  materials via [[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]]. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md)
  1:57:28–1:58:00)*
- Gerb connects BASS to a "leftover idea" — Project Forum, a proposed 2010s program to "raise the
  level of awareness of the importance of ET and the potential disclosure in North America,"
  beginning in California — which he theorizes influenced [[to-the-stars-academy|To the Stars
  Academy]]'s later founding. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 1:29:39–1:30:12)*
