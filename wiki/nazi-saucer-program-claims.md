[[jason-jorjani]]'s account (drawing on Joseph Farrell, Nick Cook and Igor Witkowski) that SS engineers at the Skoda Works near Prague built saucer-shaped craft and the "Bell" power/propulsion device, and that some of the resulting technology surfaced at Roswell. All of it is speculative and second-hand.

## Claims

- Jorjani says the Vatican held a dossier on the [[magenta-italy-crash-1933]] object and that it was studied in a joint SS-Italian working group outside Prague, chosen as a compromise third country; he wrote *Closer Encounters* (2021) before Grusch's Magenta testimony and reinterpreted the Skoda site afterwards. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 34:45, 36:02)*
- Unclear how much of Skoda was reverse engineering and how much German aeronautical work: the saucer airframe with boundary-layer suction vents evolved naturally from 1930s aerodynamics, and a perforated metal ("Luftfahrt/Luvam" as heard) is claimed to match a metal at Roswell per [[philip-j-corso]]. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 37:00, 38:26)*
- Corso's Roswell items (Kevlar, Velcro, night vision, fiber optics, lasers) are said by Farrell to have been invented in 1940s Germany; the German IBM subsidiary Dehomag allegedly made smaller transistors than the American ones. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 40:05, 41:01)*
- The early saucer airframes used rotating rockets underneath. A parallel project under SS General [[hans-kammler]] developed the [[the-bell]]: rotation of a mercury-thorium mixture by electromagnets, alternating current plus pulses of high-voltage direct current, an alleged over-unity plasma-fusion power source. Jorjani cites a very old Serbian source who told him it began in 1937 rather than the early 1940s. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 42:26, 44:01, 45:00)*
- It levitated when switched on and had to be chained down; plants near it turned to mulch; the first team of scientists died; lead scientist Ernst Grawitz (an SS medical officer under Mengele) and Walter Gerlach are named; survivors reported time flowing at different rates; a ceramic chamber is said to have shielded a subject in a torsion field (Michels's extrapolation to time travel). Nazi human experiments (Mengele, cell block 9) are noted. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 48:48, 49:32, 51:52)*
- Alternate name "die Glocke" / "Der Bienenstock" (the beehive), from a buzzing sound; UFO witnesses also report buzzing. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 53:00)*
- Integration of the Bell into the saucer airframe is dated 1944; [[otto-skorzeny]] saw a demonstration at Skoda and became obsessed with a psychological-warfare use. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 56:34, 57:23)*
- Michels adds that the first saucer patent was Henri Coanda's in 1937 (he later worked on the Avrocar) and that Tesla and [[thomas-townsend-brown]] arrived at similar ideas independently, so the Nazis need not have invented ZPE propulsion. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 53:54, 54:35)*
- One year after the war (1946) Spanish newspapers reportedly described Skorzeny with Franco launching saucer-shaped rockets from the Mediterranean coast toward America (per Farrell's *Saucers, Swastikas and Psyops*); one year later, Roswell. Jorjani concludes "the Nazis created the crisis at Roswell", staged as psychological warfare, while stressing that grays and other crashes (Vallee's post-Trinity crash in *Trinity*) are not Nazi. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 57:38, 58:38, 1:02:46, 1:03:07)*
- Jorjani suspects the Bell time effect is linked to the Missing 411 pattern of temporal disorientation ([[missing-411-claims]]). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:05:05)*
