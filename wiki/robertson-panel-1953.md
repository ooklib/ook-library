A 1953 panel that, per Gerb, "served as a debunking panel to reduce public concern regarding
UFOs." *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 13:04–13:18)*

## Claims

- [[colonel-steve-wilson|Colonel Steve Wilson]]'s alleged [[project-pounce|Project Pounce]] can
  oddly be observed referenced in the Robertson Panel report.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 13:04–13:11)*

- Gerb frames the panel as one of a decades-long series of USG UFO-disinformation efforts
  alongside the Roswell coverup, the Condon Committee, and AARO. Evidence entries presented
  14–17 January 1953 included Projects Grudge and Blue Book, [[project-stork|Project Stork]]
  ([[battelle-memorial-institute|Battelle]]'s parallel UFO investigation), Project Twinkle at
  Sandia Base, and an "outline of investigation of UFOs proposed by Kirtland Air Force Base,
  Project Pounce" — the latter traced by Gerb to
  [[william-a-matheny|Brig. Gen. William A. Matheny]]'s 1952 F-94C interceptor proposal.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:39:39–1:41:10)*

## Relationships

- Allegedly references [[project-pounce]] (originated by [[william-a-matheny]]) and
  [[project-stork]].

## Additional claims (rc/mj12)

- The [[goudsmit-letter]] (CIA, released 2001, addressee redacted till 2 April 2026) says [[samuel-goudsmit]] did not recall [[lloyd-berkner]] on the panel but did recall an Ohio State astronomer, presumably Hynek; the panel met 14-17 Jan 1953. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 1:01:17-1:02:40)*

## Claims (jm/nell-pasulka)

- Nell calls the panel's recommendation to debunk, misdirect the public and infiltrate public UFO groups "established fact" that nobody disputes. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 20:45-21:15)* See [[non-disclosure-reasons-nell]].

## Claims (gerb/iceberg-1)

- [[uap-gerb]] quotes the panel's aim as to "strip the unidentified flying objects of their special status" and the aura of mystery, formed after the [[washington-dc-ufo-incident-1952]], and says this led to the decades-long stigma around UFOs. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 38:11-38:41)*

## Claims (gerb/iceberg-2)

- Gerb says the panel stripped UFOs of special status and fed the swamp-gas/weather-balloon stigma, per [[j-allen-hynek]]'s account. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 12:32-13:56)*

## Claims (gerb/kirkpatrick)

- Gerb compares [[sean-kirkpatrick]]'s explaining UAP as Chinese drones to the panel's explaining them as swamp gas and weather balloons (he dates the panel 1954). *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 8:41-8:56)*
