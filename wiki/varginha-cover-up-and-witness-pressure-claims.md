The alleged suppression of the [[varginha-ufo-incident-1996]] as relayed by [[james-fox]] and host [[chris-ramsey]]: a military blockade, threats to reporters, bribes and men in suits, and the disappearance of several researchers and witnesses.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- A military blockade began several days after the crash; all three television reporters, on camera for the first time, said they were told "one more question and you're going to jail" and the words "national security" repeated. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 34:18-34:36, 37:53-38:51)*
- Fox says the military tried to write off the girls' sighting as a local man with a birth defect (a "Mene" or similar), which he calls absurd since everyone knew him. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 38:57-39:21)*
- The family of the disc witnesses, the girls' mother and Daza all report that men (called foreigners or American, in suits, one speaking heavily accented Portuguese) told them not to talk, offered dollars, or threatened them. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 30:04-30:27, 41:49-43:06, 1:29:18-1:30:55)*
- Fox says many town residents moved away or went silent. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 43:28-44:56)*
- Researchers: Fox says Claudio Kovo and A. J. Gavard have died, Marco Patise still talks, Pacini (Pakuchini) was shot at and threatened and went to live with his mother for about 20 years but is back working with Fox, and [[ubirajara-rodrigues]] went quiet. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 43:28-44:37, 1:32:25-1:32:59)*
- Fox says the accounts fit a pattern: witnesses like Lonnie Zamora (1964) and Herbert Schirmer (1962) quit their jobs after ridicule. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:31:34-1:32:09)*
- Fox says the Americans "almost got away with it", that he only learned of American involvement after a decade. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 17:34-17:50)*
- Ramsey compares the pattern to the [[aztec-new-mexico-crash-1948]] hoax narrative. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:06:48-1:07:40)*
