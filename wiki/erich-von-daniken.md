Author of *Chariots of the Gods* (1968), which per [[uap-gerb]] proposed that the Nazca lines were extraterrestrial landing sites. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 12:09-12:41)*

## Claims

- Claimed the Nazca patterns look like modern airport designs and used this as evidence for extraterrestrial use. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 12:26-12:41)*

## Relationships

- See [[nazca-lines-ancient-astronaut-claims]].

## Claims (gerb/iceberg-2)

- Gerb summarizes von Daniken's ancient astronaut claims (pyramids, moai, Stonehenge, Ezekiel's angels as UFOs, Sodom as a nuclear blast) and says he dislikes the building-technique claims but finds it plausible the phenomenon influenced ancient culture and religion. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 23:15-25:19)*
