The Bohemian Club is a private, invitation-only, men-only social club founded in San Francisco in
1872, headquartered in a six-story building on the edge of the Tenderloin. Since 1878 it has held
annual summer encampments in the Northern California redwoods, since expanded into the roughly
2,700-acre Bohemian Grove property near Monte Rio, Sonoma County. More Perfect Union — opening the
video with the line "the conspiracies are true" — presents the club as a real, functioning node
where presidents, CEOs, bankers, lobbyists, Supreme Court justices and CIA directors informally
network and, it alleges, shape US policy outside any public accountability. **This is More Perfect
Union's editorial framing and should be read as an attributed claim, not an established fact**;
the wiki records it as such per the schema's "claims, not verified fact" convention. This is the
wiki's first entry in what may become a broader "US power structures" thread, parallel to (but not
currently source-linked to) the existing British power-structures material
([[fabian-society]], [[chatham-house]], [[city-of-london-corporation]]).

## Claims

### What the club is and how membership works

- Started in 1872 as a hangout for local journalists, later admitting artists and intellectuals,
  then wealthy patrons ("because those other guys don't have any money") who could afford the
  clubhouse and, from 1878, the summer encampments
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 2:23, 2:28, 2:50)*.
- Membership and attendance are meant to be completely private. Journalist [[daniel-boguslaw]]
  obtained what he presented to More Perfect Union as the full membership list, plus the full
  attendee list for the 2023 Summer Encampment, broken down by the smaller "camps" members are
  divided into
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 0:52, 1:04, 3:21, 3:31, 3:40)*.
- Alleged/reported attendees named in the video, largely as list entries rather than individually
  profiled: presidents, CEOs, bankers, lobbyists, Supreme Court justices and CIA directors in
  general; specifically named: Charles Koch, Pete Coors (son of Joseph Coors Sr, grandson of
  Adolph Coors), Clarence Thomas gift-giver Harlan Crow, Bill Draper and his son Tim Draper
  (venture capital), Steven Bechtel Sr, Caspar Weinberger, George Shultz, Edwin Meese, John
  McComb, Michael Bloomberg, Henry Kissinger, Paul Pelosi and a nephew who works for Harlan Crow
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 0:45, 7:01, 7:16, 7:42, 7:47, 6:35, 6:44, 8:37, 9:00, 16:18)*.
- The club's rituals include the "Cremation of Care" ceremony and burning a large effigy in
  tribute to an owl statue; the club's motto/slogan is "Weaving spiders come not here," which More
  Perfect Union reads as an explicit (if theatrical) instruction that business is not supposed to
  be discussed on the grounds
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 0:24, 2:59, 9:45, 9:52)*.
- Donald Trump is alleged NOT to be a member and reportedly tried and failed to join; sourced to
  Mary Moore's statement "Trump is not welcome at the Grove... He's not on the list"
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 13:07, 13:16)*.
- Two former Grove employees (a driver and a skeet-shooting instructor), speaking anonymously,
  described a workplace structured to minimize member-worker interaction, and described watching
  members perform a full-cast production of Macbeth with Colin Powell in the lead role
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 10:37, 10:46, 10:53)*.
- Grove valets won a class-action lawsuit alleging wage theft, misclassification, and other labor
  violations, including unpaid overtime
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 16:45, 16:47, 17:00)*.

### Alleged policy influence — the Reagan era

- More Perfect Union says it uncovered, at the Reagan Library, a 1981 memo from presidential
  assistant Edwin Harper to Alan Greenspan referencing "the paper we discussed at the Bohemian
  Grove" — a document titled "Social Security Reform Proposals." Alex Lawson of Social Security
  Works, contacted by the reporters, endorsed the claim that the 1983 Social Security cuts
  originated from a discussion at the Grove
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 5:24, 5:35, 5:41, 5:49, 5:56, 6:05)*.
  **This is an unverified claim** attributed to More Perfect Union's own archival research and a
  single outside expert's endorsement, not independently confirmed by this wiki. (The 1983 Social
  Security reform itself does not yet have a separate wiki page.)
- A cited book claims a Reagan-era advisor was persuaded to cut capital-gains taxes by venture
  capitalist Bill Draper while "sat semi-naked at the Grove"; More Perfect Union credits this
  change with enabling the high-risk investment model that produced the modern venture-capital/tech
  industry *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 6:15, 6:26, 6:35)*.

- See [[neighbors-for-a-better-san-francisco]] for the claim that this SF political-spending

  cost of living comparable to San Francisco; the Tenderloin, where the club's SF building sits,
  is described as the poorest part of San Francisco
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 11:18, 11:25, 11:30, 11:38)*.

## Speculation flag

More Perfect Union opens this video with the framing statement "the conspiracies are true," and
presents its reporting as confirming decades of speculation about the Grove. Per this wiki's
schema, that framing — and every substantive causal claim in this page (that specific policies
were "planned at the Grove," that named political/economic outcomes trace directly back to
conversations there) — is recorded as More Perfect Union's/Daniel Boguslaw's attributed
journalism, not independently verified fact. The underlying membership and attendance lists
themselves are described by the source as leaked/acquired documents whose authenticity this wiki
has not independently checked.
