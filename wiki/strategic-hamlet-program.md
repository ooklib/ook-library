South Vietnam's Strategic Hamlet program, developed by [[ngo-dinh-nhu]], forcibly displaced entire villages into what the host calls "heavily fortified concentration camps" to root out communist infiltrators. [[eyes-wide-open]] says it dealt a massive blow to rural goodwill for [[ngo-dinh-diem]]'s government, and that CIA figures advised it. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 37:21-37:46)*

## Claims

- [[lucien-conein]], Lansdale's right-hand man, was the main American adviser to Nhu in the early stages; from 1959 CIA Deputy Station Chief [[william-colby]] also advised Nhu on it. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 37:46-38:15)*
- The host notes it was scrapped after Diem's 1963 assassination and the US assumption of the conflict, and suggests Colby's involvement with Nhu may have influenced his later Phoenix Program, a "Search and Destroy Mass assassination program" he developed alongside Saigon station chief Ted Shockley (ASR; to figure in the Laos episodes). *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 38:15-38:59)*

## Relationships

- Run by [[ngo-dinh-nhu]]; advised by [[lucien-conein]] and [[william-colby]].
