The Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program, the controversial DoD office
[[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]] claimed to direct, work for, or operate under in various
capacities. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:34:22–1:34:32)*

## Claims

- Much discussion exists over ATIP versus [[aawsap|AAWSAP]] (Advanced Aerospace Weapon System
  Applications Program); Gerb maintains ATIP was likely a "white world cover" for more
  classified programs under AAWSAP, though he declines to fully unpack that in this video.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:34:32–1:35:01)*
- In July 2018, journalist George Knapp released a leaked version of 38
  [[dirds|Defense Intelligence Reference Documents (DIRDs)]] created under ATIP.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:34:14–1:34:22)*

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2 — revised theory: ATIP as NSC narrative cover)

**Note on revision**: In this video Gerb states outright that ATIP was "an informal working group
that was unfunded" and explicitly **not** a sub-SAP of [[aawsap|AAWSAP/OAP]] — a correction to
[[luis-elizondo|Elizondo]]'s own public claim (made to Jeremy Corbell) that ATIP was a "sub-SAP"
to OAP, which Gerb calls false given ATIP had no SAP umbrella/compartment structure at all.
*([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 3:07:08–3:07:56)*

- Gerb's central Vol. 2 theory: ATIP operated as a [[james-clapper|James Clapper]]-given "cover"
  — while Clapper was [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-and-security|USD I&S]]
  (2007–2010) transitioning to DNI (2010–2017) — functioning as a way "to talk about the onion
  outside of the onion." He speculates Clapper's motives are unknown but wagers ATIP was
  "partially a cover for National Security Council intelligence SAPs," with
  [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]] — [[national-program-special-management-staff|NPMS]] director,
  2013–2017, under USD I&S — as the person "who better to spearhead this job."
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:01:49–1:02:35)*
- ATIP's primary operational years, per Gerb, ran roughly 2009–2017, overlapping almost exactly
  with Clapper's DNI tenure and Elizondo's NPMS directorship. He calls it "extremely suspicious"
  that Elizondo held one of the most-cleared positions in the entire DoD (NPMS director) during
  the same years he claims to have merely run an informal, unfunded UAP working group.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:25:19–2:25:53)*
- Distinguishes ATIP from [[aawsap|AAWSAP/OAP]] (out of the DIA, 2008–2012, ~$22M budget,
  sponsored by Sen. [[harry-reid|Harry Reid]]) — per [[david-grush|David Grush]], one of OAP's
  express missions was to be the intended recipient of the [[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]] DHS
  prospective-SAP transfer of UFO materials from [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]]. Gerb states
  Elizondo and Jay Stratton, in the 2025 *Age of Disclosure* documentary, falsely describe ATIP as
  a real, appropriations-funded program and misidentify figures like [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]]
  and Eric Davis as "ATIP scientists" — "brazen nonsense," since ATIP had no funding of its own.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:21:04–2:22:12)*
- ATIP is framed as one half (with [[aaro|AARO]]) of Vol. 2's disinformation/narrative-control
  chapter: Gerb argues ATIP sought a partial-disclosure campaign, positioning
  [[luis-elizondo|Elizondo]] and allies to establish Hillary Clinton as "the disclosure
  president," emphasizing UFOs as a national-security threat and a non-human presence, while
  deliberately omitting crash retrieval, reverse engineering, NHI taxonomy, and the legacy
  program structure itself. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:26:49–2:27:20)*
- Prior to [[david-grush|Grush]] going public, Elizondo's public disclosures focused narrowly on
  Roswell — already "cultural zeitgeist" and safe to discuss — never mentioning custodianship of
  the wreckage; after Grush's 2023 testimony, Elizondo's team was "forced to reposture" and began
  discussing crash retrieval and legacy programs, visibly "caught off guard" in the 2024 UAP
  congressional hearings when pressed on crash-retrieval-program access.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:27:32–2:29:08)*
