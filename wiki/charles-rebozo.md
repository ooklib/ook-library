Charles B. "Bebe" Rebozo (ASR "Raboso") is the Cuban-American real estate developer and "shady financier" and longtime friend of [[richard-nixon]] who, per [[eyes-wide-open]] citing Henrik Kruger, Nixon met in 1943 while working at the Office of Price Administration. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 54:23-54:38)*

## Claims

- Advised Nixon on investments in Batista's Cuba; Nixon bought Key Biscayne property (his "winter White House"), so Castro's nationalization personally affected him. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 54:38-55:05)*
- Long-standing ties with organized crime figures ([[santo-trafficante]], [[meyer-lansky]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 55:31-55:48)*
- Unverified rumor flagged by the host: persistent rumors of a "gay affair" between Rebozo and Nixon, on which the host speculates a mob blackmail scenario, analogous to the [[blue-suite]] blackmail of [[j-edgar-hoover]] (host's speculation only). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 55:05-56:27)*
- Named in a 1958 Tallahassee Democrat article as a member of a bipartisan committee to bring the 1960 conventions to Miami Beach, chaired by [[paul-helliwell]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 56:35-57:14)*
