Jesse Michels interviews [[matthew-pines]], a national-security analyst (crypto and Bitcoin Policy Institute background, director of intelligence at a cybersecurity firm), for about 4h12m on American Alchemy, published 2024-11-15, days after the 2024 US election (video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LpLFWdsIU7M). The conversation ranges from Trump-era politics and the "perestroika" analogy through UAP legislation, a speculative history of how CIA and legacy-program functions were privatized, a possible physics program funded through hedge-fund money, Wolfram-style theoretical physics and consciousness, and back to disclosure strategy, AI and non-human intelligence. Almost everything is Pines' own analysis or second-hand reports he says he cannot confirm; all claims here are attributed and unverified.
