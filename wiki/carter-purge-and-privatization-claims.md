[[matthew-pines]] and [[jesse-michels]] propose that [[jimmy-carter]]'s purge of senior CIA ranks (see [[halloween-massacre]]) pushed officers who had overseen legacy-program functions into the private sector, where they kept their skills and contacts but lost institutional accountability. Pines says outright he has "no evidence one way or the other" and that this is a fact pattern, not a smoking gun.

## Claims

- Pines argues that after Carter fired hundreds of senior CIA officers, "those guys didn't just disappear": they knew how to run covert operations and generate black money, and the programs then "delaminated" into a part the government kept and a part it did not. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 33:04-34:23)*
- Michels and Pines discuss the Allen Dulles thesis: the CIA formed at the behest of the [[brown-brothers-harriman]] investment group; Dulles reactivated [[s-force]], "created by [[howard-hughes]]", against Kennedy (per [[danny-sheehan]]); Pines cautions that Sheehan may have his own interests, for example in defending James McCord's Watergate role. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 40:51-42:17, 49:56-50:07, 2:38:26-2:39:59)*
- Michels asks if purged CIA officers ended up at the [[carlyle-group]], citing [[jacques-vallee]]'s Forbidden Science diaries in which insiders repeatedly mention Carlyle; Pines calls the idea plausible but unevidenced. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 50:07-50:46, 1:01:05-1:01:43)*
- Michels retells the [[mark-mccandlish]] and [[brad-sorenson]] story: a defense financier took Sorenson to Palmdale and showed exotic one-offs such as Aurora/Astra and three bell-shaped "flux liners" (alien reproduction vehicles) with wear. He says researchers think the financier was [[frank-carlucci]], Carter's deputy CIA director, Reagan's defense secretary and Carlyle-associated. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 50:51-52:23)*
- Pines describes a "revolving door" and "infinite shell game" between CIA, private industry and secret government compartments, with private intermediaries (for example port facilities in great-power competition) as a prosaic analogy. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 52:23-53:45)*
- Pines says funding is the key question: official classified budgets versus black money from criminal or financial operations; he says he would watch for "black box" sources of money with plausible program connections. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 54:53-55:50)*
- He says the same arc applies to institutions: personalized in the Dulles era, formalized into bureaucracies for the Cold War, and now "repersonalized", with institutional "code rot". *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 46:57-48:49)*
