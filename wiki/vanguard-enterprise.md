A donation-funded project by [[dylan-borland]] and [[matthew-brown]] to support UAP whistleblowers and their families, promoted at the end of [[borland-michels-interview-2026]] and linked in the video description (vanguardenterprise.org, spoken in the transcript as "vanguard.org").

## Claims

- Goal: provide medical, financial, spiritual and emotional help so whistleblowers do not lose homes or jobs, and to give future whistleblowers guidance (scrub online history, expect doxxing, use a dead man's switch, be put in touch with those who went through it). *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 3:07:20-3:11:29)*
- It says other whistleblowers and civilians support it, and a person named "Jeff" recently came fully on board; Borland does not name others. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 3:07:50-3:08:30)*
- Borland says he is not paid and contrasts it with paid speaking fees. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 3:09:12-3:09:20)*
- He frames the whistleblowers ([[david-grush]] and his witnesses, Borland, Brown, [[michael-herrera]], [[jake-barber]]) as experiencers and whistleblowers who are a natural control group for studying the psychological effects. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 3:09:38-3:10:25)*
- He references the death of [[matthew-sullivan]] and says it motivated the cause. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:46:54-1:47:08)*
