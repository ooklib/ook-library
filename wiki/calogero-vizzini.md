Calogero Vizzini ("Don Carlo"/"Don Calo"), the boss of bosses of the Vizini-Agostino Sicilian
Mafia family, guided American tanks through Sicily during Operation Husky in exchange for
postwar political power, and per Eyes Wide Open personally directed ballot-box stuffing in the
1948 Italian election.

## Claims

- Per an OSS/Vatican-brokered suggestion (via [[giovanni-montini]] and [[earl-brennan]]),
  [[william-donovan]] ordered an airdrop communique to Vizzini's estate requesting his assistance
  invading Sicily; Vizzini's men guided American tanks through Sicily, leading to "an astounding
  military victory" on the island. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 16:22-16:43)*
- In return, was appointed mayor of Villalba, and immediately murdered the town's police chief;
  a series of other Mafia appointments followed throughout Sicily after the American invasion,
  including [[charles-poletti]] as military governor of Italy. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 16:43-17:24)*
- On 1948 election day, worked with his men to stuff ballot boxes and bribe election officials in
  favor of the Christian Democrats. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 29:00-29:10)*
