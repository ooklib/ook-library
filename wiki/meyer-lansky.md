Meyer Lansky, per Eyes Wide Open, was "often considered the most prolific and wealthy Mafia boss
in US history," the financial mastermind behind the [[national-crime-syndicate]], and
[[paul-helliwell]]'s partner in running [[operation-x]]'s money-laundering infrastructure. The
host flags that Lansky's Cuban casino/mob connections will become important later in the series
when it covers the Kennedy assassination (per `raw/index.md`, covered at length in the later
`ewa/dallas-4` episode, "Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid").

## Claims

- Developed a working relationship with [[frank-wisner]] and [[james-jesus-angleton]] on behalf
  of the future CIA. Had united the Jewish, Italian, and Irish mobs into the nationwide
  [[national-crime-syndicate]], becoming a millionaire as a Prohibition-era bootlegger and later
  introducing the mob to legal gambling in Cuba, where he owned several casinos — a connection
  Eyes Wide Open explicitly flags as important context for the future Kennedy-assassination
  chapters of the series. Considered "the mob's accountant" and mastermind of its sprawling
  criminal social network. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 22:31-23:28)*
- Ran all the financial aspects of Operation X's global network in partnership with
  [[paul-helliwell]] through the Miami shell company [[general-development-corporation]]. Was
  closely associated with [[lucky-luciano]], a fellow National Crime Syndicate boss.
  *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 23:28-23:53)*

- As early as 1946, alleged (alongside [[james-jesus-angleton]]) to have possessed compromising
  photographs of FBI Director [[j-edgar-hoover]] receiving oral sex from his deputy
  [[clyde-tolson]]; authors are divided on whether Angleton gave Lansky the photos or vice versa.
  In 1950, took a trip to Sicily to meet old friend [[lucky-luciano]], around the same period the
  CIA moved to place southern French docks under Corsican Mafia control. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 36:47-38:24, 55:10-55:23)*
- Rumored by researchers such as Whitney Webb to be behind [[resorts-international]] (formerly
  Mary Carter Paint Company); the company was briefly owned by [[donald-trump]] beginning in
  1986, the same year [[roy-cohn]] — closely associated with both Lansky and Trump — died; Lansky
  was also dead by that point. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 49:21-49:41)*

## Relationships

- United the mob into the [[national-crime-syndicate]]; considered its financial mastermind.
- Alleged, with [[james-jesus-angleton]], to have held compromising photographs of
  [[j-edgar-hoover]] and [[clyde-tolson]].
- Rumored to be behind [[resorts-international]].
- Ran [[operation-x]]'s finances with [[paul-helliwell]] via [[general-development-corporation]].
- Close associate of [[lucky-luciano]].
- Relationship with the future CIA developed by [[frank-wisner]] and [[james-jesus-angleton]].

## Additional claims (CIA-Mafia Legend)

- Host: "the mob's accountant" and moneyman for five decades; charged with tax evasion in 1970 (prosecuted formally only in 1972), moved to Israel under the law of return, deported after two years, and walked free on technicalities; the host argues Lansky could not be prosecuted the way [[lucky-luciano]] could because of his ties to the "Overworld". *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 46:41-48:46)*
- Host: hardest hit by Castro's Cuban nationalization alongside Trafficante and [[carlos-marcello]]; reportedly had "his hands in every casino joint" in Cuba and personally put a million-dollar bounty on Castro; Florida node with Miami and the Bahamas. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 34:16-34:39, 50:49-51:13)*
- Host: a 1969 Orlando Sentinel article claimed Lansky had substantial Swiss holdings and used Canadian-Russian banker John Pullman (ASR "pulman") as his Swiss banker; host links this to the "Rosemark" Swiss loan to Hughes' TWA (speculative). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 31:55-32:18)* See [[howard-hughes]].
- Host: Prohibition-era, young Lansky and Bugsy Siegel bootlegged alongside Italians in the same New York neighborhoods. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 43:22-43:39)*

## Additional claims (Road to Dallas Ch. 4 - Lansky's dedicated episode)

Note: `raw/index.md` and the earlier summary above describe this episode as "Cuban Tabloid"; the transcript itself never explains that phrase and is a history of the mob in Cuba. Source base is mainly the 1979 book *Meyer Lansky: Mogul of the Mob* (Eisenberg, Dan, Landau), written from interviews with Lansky in 1970s Israel; the host warns it veers into hagiography but is "a pretty great read" with details unavailable elsewhere. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 2:51-3:55)*

- Prohibition produced a truly national crime syndicate linking Italian and Jewish gangs of the Northeast and South; after 1933 the wealthy mob looked to Cuba for resorts and casinos, and Cuba also became a hub for European dope heading to the US. Lansky came up with the Cuba idea and, per the host, became de facto leader of the nationwide network in 1936 when [[lucky-luciano]] was convicted (prosecutor [[thomas-dewey]], later governor and Luciano's releaser; see [[operation-underworld]]). While Luciano was in Clinton prison ("Siberia") Lansky ran things. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 0:41-2:51)*
- Befriended [[fulgencio-batista]] in 1932; in 1933 convened a meeting in Luciano's suite at the Waldorf Towers with Bugsy Siegel (his number two since childhood, later emissary to Los Angeles), Moe Dalitz (ASR "Mo Dallots", Cleveland boss, later "Mr. Las Vegas" and Desert Inn holder, the hotel later purchased and lived in by [[howard-hughes]]) and Phil Kastel (ASR "Castell", later pushed out of Louisiana by [[carlos-marcello]]). Each agreed to put in half a million dollars for Cuba. Per Doc Stacher (Lansky's main Cuban middleman), Dalitz was how Lansky and New York gangsters met [[jimmy-hoffa]], whom Dalitz knew from Detroit's Purple Gang; Hoffa later commandeered the Teamsters Pension Fund. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 4:51-7:47, 6:15-6:48)*
- Stacher's account: cash-filled suitcases were shown to Batista; the syndicate guaranteed $3-5 million a year for a casino monopoly plus a cut. The Hotel Nacional casino was owned and operated directly by Lansky and his brother Jake. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 7:47-9:15)*
- Convened the [[havana-mob-conference-1946]] at the Hotel Nacional. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 9:22-13:05)*
- With [[santo-trafficante]] as the second most prominent Havana mob operator after him; Lansky "got out of dodge" in the weeks before Castro took power (per host), unlike Trafficante. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 13:16-13:27, 14:52-15:06)*
- Host frames the [[national-crime-syndicate]]'s Cuba stake as the seed of its role in later anti-Castro operations; the episode's thesis is that post-revolution Havana was a melting pot creating linkages that lasted until Nov. 22, 1963, with gun-smuggling networks giving "the framework for the eventual assassination". *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 2:32-2:51, 30:33-30:58)*
