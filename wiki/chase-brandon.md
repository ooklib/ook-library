Chase Brandon is a former CIA officer who acted as the agency's Hollywood liaison and wrote the novel "The Cain Conundrum" (ASR: "Christos Conundrum") about the CIA's handling of Roswell. Cameron says the name is a pseudonym and that Brandon disappeared without writing the promised UFO book.

## Claims

- Cameron quotes Brandon: "If you want a good read, read my book. If you want to learn something, read between the lines"; the manuscript went through eight reviews. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:32:56-1:34:26)*
- Cameron says Brandon's job was to walk filmmakers through CIA headquarters so they would follow the path the agency prefers. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:34:26-1:34:59)*
- Michels summarises the novel (a mathematician, an OSA offshoot of the OSS, a crystal tablet that reads timelines, a Majestic-12-like committee). *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:33:20-1:34:09)*
- Cameron links Brandon's crystal to a story he attributes to [[richard-doty]] about a crystal showing the crucifixion, and to a Utah experiencer's spirit-world crystals. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:41:36-1:44:14)*
