An alleged crash of an egg-shaped craft in the Vietnam-Cambodia border jungle in late 1968, described by [[special-forces-witness-infographics]] and dated by [[uap-gerb]] to October to December 1968 in the area of Company E, 52nd Infantry (LRRP), 1st Cavalry Division, based at Phuoc Vinh. Entirely an unverified single-witness claim.

## Claims (gerb/special-forces)

- The witness says his platoon was on a long-range reconnaissance patrol tracking supply routes from Cambodia and marking sites for a B-52 raid the following year; Gerb links this to the covert [[operation-menu]] campaign of March 1969 and to Company E, 52nd Infantry, which operated 1967 to 1969 and was moved to Phuoc Vinh in late October 1968. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 2:05-4:54)*
- An object emitted a bright red-orange glow, appearing to melt; seconds later a large crash and dull thump followed. The platoon assumed a US plane, avoided long-range radio for fear of Soviet interception, and moved to secure the site, where they saw an egg-shaped, dull gray metallic craft, seemingly cracked open, made of a single piece with no bolts, seams or screws; the only prosaic idea was a NASA or Soviet space probe. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 4:54-5:47)*
- Radio reports drew Navy, Air Force and SOF officials who were already read into the program; the infantry pulled perimeter security widely and were kept away from the craft, which took two days to remove; a pair of F-4 jets circled overhead. Gerb ties the F-4s to a 1986 claim by [[jacques-vallee]]'s contact James McCampbell that wild weasel F-4s carried UAP-detection equipment, and to a redacted [[steven-greer]] witness (10272) who installed radar assemblies on F-4s. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 7:40-9:56)*
- Gerb offers context cases: Disclosure Project witness 10622 (Navy radioman on the USS Bon Homme Richard, June 1968 messages on 11 tracked UAP), a 9 September 1968 Marine base encounter at Dong Ha, NICAP case 6811 in Albany, Georgia (22 November 1968), a 15 December 1968 French egg-shaped object, the 1967 [[malmstrom-minuteman-incident-1967]], [[eric-taber]]'s egg-shaped craft and the mother-of-pearl skin in [[jonathan-weygandt]]'s Peru case. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 2:21-7:40)*
