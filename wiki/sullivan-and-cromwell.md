Sullivan & Cromwell was a prestigious Wall Street law firm employing both
[[allen-dulles|Allen]] and [[john-foster-dulles|John Foster Dulles]] before WWII. Eyes Wide Open
centers the firm's client relationships with Nazi-adjacent German industrial firms as the crux
of an alleged conflict of interest running through the Dulles brothers' wartime and postwar
intelligence careers.

## Claims

- Client list included many Nazi-adjacent firms, notably [[ig-farben|IG Farben]] (manufacturer
  of the Zyklon B gas used in Nazi gas chambers) and Krupp AG (a German arms manufacturer).
  *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 19:29-19:45)*
- Eyes Wide Open alleges the firm's intelligence-agency alumni (the Dulles brothers) never
  stopped serving their former Sullivan & Cromwell clients even once in government/OSS roles,
  framing this as an early example of the private-sector/intelligence-agency "revolving door."
  *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 22:59-24:03)*
- Per the host, John Foster Dulles insisted the firm's correspondence to its German clients be
  signed off "Heil Hitler." *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 25:19-25:25)*
- Also represented [[united-fruit-company|United Fruit]] and the
  [[anglo-iranian-oil-company|Anglo Iranian Oil Company]] — clients Eyes Wide Open later ties to
  the CIA's 1954 and 1953 overthrows of [[jacobo-arbenz]] and [[mohammad-mossadegh]],
  respectively, once [[allen-dulles]] became CIA director. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 5:49-5:54)*

## Relationships

- Employed [[allen-dulles]] and [[john-foster-dulles]].
- Represented [[ig-farben]] as a client, alleged to have created a conflict of interest with
  Allen Dulles's OSS duties.
- Also represented [[united-fruit-company]] and [[anglo-iranian-oil-company]], tied by Eyes Wide
  Open to later CIA-backed coups in Guatemala and Iran.
