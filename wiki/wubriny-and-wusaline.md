Two CIA cryptonyms that appear in declassified memos about [[thomas-devine]] and [[george-h-w-bush]] as read by [[eyes-wide-open]]. Which name maps to which person or entity is the host's (and unnamed "researchers'") interpretation.

## Claims

- A Nov 29, 1975 CIA memo says Devine's effort fell under a terminated project named WUBRINY (or "LP dictum"); a 1968 memo refers to the "proprietary Corporation of WUSALINE" housed at an investment banking firm where Devine was a "cleared and witting contact." *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 2:52-3:53)*
- Host: many interpret WUBRINY as Devine's code name or project; researchers theorize WUSALINE was Bush's CIA cryptonym. The host argues both name salt (brine, saline solution), so both likely fell under one program: CIA corporate cover in the Caribbean. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 3:53-4:41)*
- Host speculation: "Cay Sal" ("salt island") may have inspired the salt-themed cryptonyms. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 20:02-20:26)* See [[cay-sal]].
- Flagged: the host says he is "disregarding the cryptonym for a second"; the pairing of names with people is inference. Spellings are ASR renderings ("Wu Briney", "W saline").
