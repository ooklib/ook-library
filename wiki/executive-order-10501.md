Executive Order 10501 ("Safeguarding official information in the interest of the defense of the United States"), signed by [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] in November 1953.

## Claims ([[uap-gerb]])

- Removed the "restricted" classification, introduced the confidential/secret/top secret framework, and stripped classification authority from 28 agencies. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 48:03-48:43)*
- Gerb says this led agencies to use unofficial ad hoc special-access controls and code words; this "top secret code word era" produced the "wild wild west" days of the legacy programs. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 48:43-49:03)*
- Superseded in effect by [[executive-order-11652]], which formalized special-access controls. Also see [[special-access-program]].
