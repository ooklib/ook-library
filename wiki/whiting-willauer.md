Whiting Willauer was a lawyer and China Lobby "hand" who co-founded [[civil-air-transport]] and
brokered its sale to the CIA. **Name note:** the ASR renders "Whitting Willow" / "Willow";
normalization to Willauer is my inference (the transcript does not spell it), kept as aliases.

## Claims

- Lawyer; during the last years of WWII, director of the Far East branch of the Foreign Economic
  Administration; co-founded [[civil-air-transport]] in 1946 with [[claire-chennault]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 29:10-29:29)*
- The host says he would become "a key asset to the CIA in their future coups in Latin America"
  and that China Lobby figures recur in the Guatemalan operation ([[operation-pbsuccess]]).
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 29:23-29:35, 50:07-50:22)*
- In 1950, through [[paul-helliwell]], he was introduced to [[frank-wisner]] of the
  [[office-of-policy-coordination]]; CAT, struggling in the postwar contract drought, was
  purchased by the CIA with Helliwell as broker. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 43:54-44:35)*

## Relationships

- Co-founder of [[civil-air-transport]]; associate of [[claire-chennault]], [[paul-helliwell]] and [[frank-wisner]].
