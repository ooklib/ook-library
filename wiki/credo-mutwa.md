A South African Zulu shaman and storyteller who, per [[david-icke]] and host [[chris-ramsey]] in [[icke-ramsey-interview-2026]] (ASR "Kredo Mudwa", "Kredo Muchwa"), gave Icke a long interview in the late 1990s that included the Chitauri, tall blond "Nordic" beings who could disappear, and his own abduction by grays. The page records what these two say of him; his own views are not directly sourced.

## Claims

- Ramsey says he watched the roughly six-hour interview and that it included the Chitauri (essentially reptilian-like beings), Nordic-like tall blonds who could vanish, and grays, forming a comprehensive picture of the species and their roles. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 13:41-14:33)*
- Icke says the relationship was an exchange (Mutwa told ancient African and Zulu stories, Icke told modern findings) and that "we told the same story"; Icke had already concluded a reptilian non-human layer existed, and Mutwa's confirmation from ancient legends added enormously, along with his classic gray-abduction account. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 14:41-15:19, 15:32-16:18, 17:45-18:12)*
- Icke says the influence was "not as much as you would think" because he arrived at reptilians from 1990s whistleblowers, military people, experiencers and public accounts. *([a52/icke](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-03-27 - The Alien Plan is in FULL Motion - David Icke  DEBRIEFED ep. 81.md>) 14:41-14:54, 16:37-17:04)*
