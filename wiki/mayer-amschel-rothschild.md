Founder of the [[rothschild-family]] banking dynasty in 18th-century Frankfurt, per [[empire-of-shadows-rothschild-documentary-2023]].

## Claims

- Barred from land and most trades in the Frankfurt ghetto, he began as a textile trader and moved to gold coins and antiques. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 0:49, 1:05, 1:31)*
- As court agent for Prince William of Hesse-Kassel he bought coins with borrowed money and sold them to the prince, profiting from both the 8% commission and what the narrator calls "front running". *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 1:48, 2:01, 2:19)*
- By 1782 he had amassed a large fortune and become the prince's investment manager, and he sought a dynasty that would outlast nations. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 2:33, 3:23)*
- He sent sons to Vienna, Naples, Paris and London; his health failed by 1812 and he urged family unity above all. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 3:49, 7:18)*
