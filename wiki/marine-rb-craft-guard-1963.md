The alleged December 1963 two-week guard detail over a disc-shaped craft at an undisclosed military base, as told by [[marine-rb]] and relayed by [[uap-gerb]]. Nothing is verifiable.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-2)

- RB says he was a guard in charge of security for a large metal building on an undisclosed base for two weeks in December 1963; he boarded a windowless plane from Cherry Point Marine Corps Air Station, North Carolina, and flew three hours to a location he would not reveal. Gerb notes Wright-Patterson is one possible place in that range. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 22:54-23:42)*
- The craft: disc-shaped, about 40 ft long, totally symmetrical with an ovoid cross-section, silvery like polished aluminum, seamless except for a hatch-like seam so tight "you could not get a razor blade in the crack", no landing gear, fins or propulsion, window-like features one foot apart around the edge that looked like smoked glass, almost hamburger-shaped, sitting 5 ft off the ground on scaffolding. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 23:42-24:33)*
- Technical crews with three or four other guards present tried drilling and cutting with torches near the hatch (metal got hot but the soot wiped off leaving the surface undamaged) and a high-powered laser powered by two semi-trailer generator vans, whose beams deflected and damaged the ceiling. Civilian scientists made specific mentions of aliens, which convinced RB it was not human-made. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 24:33-25:11)*
- Security: color-coded badges matched color-coded areas, a red badge gave total access, and a white taped circle around the craft could not be crossed even by USAF generals; RB says he stopped the secretary of the Navy from crossing it. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 25:11-25:42)*
- He says he took a photo with a small German pocket camera that was lost in a flood around 1983. One day the craft was prepped on a flatbed trailer and shuttled to another base for "security reasons". *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 25:42-25:58)*
- No bodies were seen, but he heard secondhand of recovered bodies of beings 39 inches tall with no thumbs stored at an underground base in a western state. On leaving the Marines he signed a security oath carrying a 10-year prison sentence and a $110,000 fine. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 25:58-26:20)*
- Gerb compares the ultra-strong material and tight seam to Machu Picchu stonework and finds the case among the most fascinating military encounters he has covered. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 28:37-29:06)*
