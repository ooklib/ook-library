David Paulides's *Missing 411* books on disappearances in national parks, as retold by [[jason-jorjani]] (who cites them in *Closer Encounters*), with a link to UFO sightings and a claim that the FBI holds case files. Jorjani connects the temporal effects to the [[the-bell]] and to a "transtemporal" pocket; Michels compares to Travis Walton, the Bermuda Triangle and vortex sites. Anecdotal.

## Claims

- Trained hikers vanish leaving food and weapons, children's remains turn up months later at higher elevation; UFO sightings in the same areas; the FBI allegedly holds case files. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:59:19, 3:00:02, 3:01:41, 3:01:57)*
- Temporal disorientation: a woman lost for a day or two meets a hiker who had been missing for a decade; silence of insects before disappearance; Walton's four days with a full beard; accelerated tree growth; a Zimbabwe mountain near Ruwa where people vanish in mist. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:02:07, 3:02:25, 3:04:35, 3:05:05)*
