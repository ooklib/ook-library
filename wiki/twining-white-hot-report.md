Alleged September 1947 report by Gen. [[nathan-twining]] (parts I-V) assessing recovered lenticular aerodyne objects; one of the documents supplied by "Selena". Ryan Wood says it credits [[james-v-forrestal]] and [[vannevar-bush]] with forming a top secret R&D intelligence group. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:12:17, 1:18:26, 1:20:28)*

## Claims

- It lists the mission team: Capt. Charles Buchanan (untraced), Col. Robert Ginsburg, Capt. Richard Glass, Maj. Gen. Stephen Chamberlain, Maj. George McDonald, Adm. Paul Lee, [[leslie-groves]] and team, James Lay Jr., Thomas Lynch (Treasury), von Karman, [[j-robert-oppenheimer]], James Doolittle and Albert Einstein (ASR names). Wood says one could write a book on this document. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:20:28, 1:22:12)*

## Additional claims (rc/mj12)

- [[geoffrey-cruickshank]]: delivered to [[harry-truman]] after 19 Sept 1947 (a Friday) for weekend reading; contains propulsion details and descriptions of the bodies, no wings, flaps or conventional engine. Truman's diary entry for the Sunday reads "all sorts of things facing me". The host presents this as Cruickshank's reading of a disputed MJ document. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 41:00-44:09)*
- Twining's 23 Sept 1947 secret-level memo (a lesser-classified version for allies; a "limited hangout") says flying saucers are real; Cruickshank says Harry Turner's 1971 document quotes it. Truman wrote on 24 Sept that he approved the document and kept the copy. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 44:09-45:25)*
