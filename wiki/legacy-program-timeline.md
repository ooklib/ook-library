Hub page for [[uap-gerb]]'s synthesis video "80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering: A Timeline" (2026-09-18, `gerb/timeline`), a ~2h44m macro-level chronology of over three dozen milestones in the [[ufo-legacy-programs]], from the 1933 Magenta crash to 2026. Gerb splits the lifespan into three epochs, each anchored by an "[[estimate-of-the-situation]]" from a witness he considers credible: the [[golden-era-1947-1978]] ([[wilbert-b-smith]]/[[robert-sarbacher]], later [[j-andrew-kissner]]), the [[cold-war-reorganization-1978-1994]] (Kissner's 1994 statement) and the [[modern-era-1994-present]] ([[david-grush]]). Everything below is Gerb's assessment, not established fact; he stresses the timeline is built only from his own crash-retrieval/reverse-engineering research and omits non-human-intelligence "agreements" and contact protocols (e.g. Dan Sherman's Project Preserved Destiny) as outside his expertise. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 9:59, 10:35-10:57, 2:42:15)*

Gerb's headline thesis: a once-unified national effort, housed in the National Security Council's "covert activities oversight structure" (NSC 10/2, [[nsc-5412-committee]], 303 Committee) under presidential control, was deliberately fractured from 1972-1985 by oversight reforms and scandals, and in 1994 moved out of the NSC into a quasi-industry/government [[ufo-control-group|control group of 27]]; he estimates only 40-50 living people can see across the silos ("grains of rice in a rice bowl"). *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 12:33-15:32, 1:53:40-1:54:58)*

## Agreement with and extension of earlier Gerb videos

The timeline restates, in compressed form, claims from `gerb/secrecy_1`, `gerb/secrecy_2`, `gerb/mitre`, `gerb/sarbacher`, `gerb/northrop_trw`, `gerb/wilson-davis` and others; each linked entity page carries the detail. Notable new or sharpened points: (1) Gerb now states flatly that a "real Majestic 12" was created under [[nsc-10-2]] (June 1948), placing the group inside the NSC covert-action structure, while leaving open the earlier Eisenhower Briefing Document date of 24 Sept 1947; (2) [[executive-order-12036]] (1978) is named as the trigger of the fracturing; (3) 1994 is dated as the move out of the NSC; (4) he asserts he does not know what became of the named MAG 12 after 1978. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 32:02-33:30, 1:17:07-1:17:23, 1:53:03)*
