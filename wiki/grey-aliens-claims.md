The archetypal extraterrestrial: 3-5 ft, gray skinned, hairless, oversized head, large black eyes, small or absent mouth and nostrils, per [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 32:34-33:18)*

## Claims

- Gerb says the gray may trace to 1917, when Aleister Crowley supposedly contacted a being named Lam, but became popular with the [[roswell-incident-1947]] crash bodies and the [[betty-and-barney-hill-abduction-1961]] case. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 33:18-34:02)*
- He cites shares of abduction reports involving grays: about 50 percent in Australia, 73 percent in the US, 48 percent in continental Europe, 12 percent in the UK (figures as stated, unsourced). *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 34:02-34:18)*
- Theories he lists: biological drones of other civilizations (emotionless, hive-minded), future humans (less muscle, indoor life, screens enlarging eyes), or beings evolved on an Earth-like planet; he says he somewhat believes a non-human intelligence resembling the classic gray exists. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 34:18-35:21)*
