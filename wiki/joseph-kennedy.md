Joseph Kennedy, father of John and Robert Kennedy and, like [[lewis-rosenstiel]], a bootlegger
turned legitimate businessman, co-founded the J. Edgar Hoover Foundation with Rosenstiel in 1960 —
a connection the host calls "strange and fascinating."

## Claims

- In 1960, five years after the alleged [[blue-suite]] events, [[lewis-rosenstiel]] and Joseph
  Kennedy — another former bootlegger turned legitimate businessman — together founded the J.
  Edgar Hoover Foundation, a charitable foundation dedicated to the FBI chief. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 25:42-26:10)*
