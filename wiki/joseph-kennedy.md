Joseph Kennedy, father of John and Robert Kennedy and, like [[lewis-rosenstiel]], a bootlegger
turned legitimate businessman, co-founded the J. Edgar Hoover Foundation with Rosenstiel in 1960 —
a connection the host calls "strange and fascinating."

## Claims

- In 1960, five years after the alleged [[blue-suite]] events, [[lewis-rosenstiel]] and Joseph
  Kennedy — another former bootlegger turned legitimate businessman — together founded the J.
  Edgar Hoover Foundation, a charitable foundation dedicated to the FBI chief. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 25:42-26:10)*

- LAOS 3: among the powerful Catholics (with his son Congressman John F. Kennedy) to whom [[francis-spellman]] introduced [[ngo-dinh-diem]] on his US tour; the host notes the irony that the Kennedy administration later saw Diem overthrown in 1963. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 11:21-11:35)*

## Relationships

## Additional claims (CIA-Mafia Legend)

- Host: often theorized that Kennedy made part of the family fortune from bootlegging through Canadian liquor houses (Bronfman and Lewis Rosenstiel, "Rosen steel" per ASR; see [[lewis-rosenstiel]]), remaining close to Rosenstiel afterward; extensive Hollywood organized-crime ties in the 1920s; excluded from the "Italian mafia" frame as an Irish American. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 43:39-44:28)*
- Host: rumored bootlegging-era work with [[sam-giancana]] and rumored help swinging Chicago for JFK in 1960 (rumor). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 50:02-50:32)*
- Host citing Drosnin: Kennedy majority-owned RKO, later bought by Hughes; Hughes resented that Old Joe "had stolen" the White House from him; the two did not circulate in the same circles per Drosnin. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 24:34-25:34)* See [[howard-hughes]].
