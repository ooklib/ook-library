Fort Hunt, Virginia was a US military base where [[reinhard-gehlen]] was flown to comfortable
quarters and personally met [[allen-dulles]] after defecting with anti-Soviet intelligence at the
end of WWII, per Eyes Wide Open.

## Claims

- Gehlen's meeting with an advancing American unit in southern Bavaria was, unusually, followed
  up not in Bern but at Fort Hunt, Virginia — Gehlen was "shipped off on a DC3" there to
  comfortable quarters for a personal meeting with Dulles. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 3:51-3:58)*

## Relationships

- Site of [[allen-dulles]]'s recruitment meeting with [[reinhard-gehlen]].
