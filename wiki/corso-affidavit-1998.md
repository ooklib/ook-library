Sworn statement released in July 1998 (shortly before Corso's death) by Citizens Against UFO Secrecy (CAUS), signed for attorney Peter Gersten to support a lawsuit against the Department of the Army. In it [[philip-j-corso]] swears he was a member of Eisenhower's National Security Council and head of the foreign technology desk of Army R&D; that on about July 6, 1947 at Fort Riley, Kansas he saw a 4-foot non-human creature (four-fingered hands, thin legs, incandescent-bulb-shaped head, almond eyes, slit mouth); and that in 1961 he came into possession of the "Roswell file" ([[roswell-file]]) containing field reports, medical autopsy reports (done at Walter Reed Hospital) and technical debris, in which the creature was called an "extraterrestrial biological entity". *(Unverified claim.)* *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J. Corso - US Army UFO Technology Research & Development.md>) 12:53-15:13)*
