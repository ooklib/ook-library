Lyndon Johnson appears in War Conspiracy 1 as one of the two politicians (with [[richard-nixon]])
whom [[eyes-wide-open]] says the [[china-lobby]] "effectively elevated," and whom the host calls
"maybe the most responsible for the escalations of violence in Southeast Asia."

## Claims

- In 1964 he made "the fateful decision to intervene unilaterally in South Vietnam" after two
  decades of selling Americans on an "inevitable" war. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 0:13-0:32)*
- As a congressman he sought a Texas Senate seat in 1941 (unsuccessfully) and 1948 (successfully);
  [[thomas-corcoran]] managed both campaigns. He became Senate Majority Leader, "master of the
  Senate." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 33:03-33:36)*
- Series preview: a later War Conspiracy episode covers Johnson's ties to the military-industrial
  complex. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 51:19-51:39)*
