Deutsche Bank is a major German commercial bank. The video alleges it was a key financier of the Nazi regime. See also [[dws-group]], its asset-management arm, covered in a different source.

## Claims

- Called a key financer of the Nazi regime: it fired Jewish employees, seized Jewish assets, and loaned funds to build the [[ig-farben]] and Auschwitz facilities using stolen Jewish gold. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 13:37-13:52)*
- It allegedly helped the Nazis bypass sanctions, sell looted gold, and expand control over banks in occupied territories. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 13:52-14:00)*
- Wartime director Hermann Abs was arrested after the war but never convicted and became chairman in 1957. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 14:00-14:08)*
