Neil Mallon (the ASR renders "Malin"/"Malan"; normalization is my inference from the recurring
context) was president of [[dresser-industries]] and an "uncle-like figure" to [[george-h-w-bush]],
who named a son Neil after him. [[eyes-wide-open]] argues, from a Prescott Bush letter found by Russ
Baker, that Mallon's real role was helping [[allen-dulles]] procure people for the CIA.

## Claims

- Prescott's Ohio friend, installed as Dresser president in 1928 with no oil experience; gave Bush
  his first job out of Yale. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 21:27-22:24)*
- Prescott's letter: Mallon "well known to Alan Dulles and has tried to be helpful to him in the CIA,
  especially in the procurement of individuals to serve in that important agency." Host reads this
  as an indirect admission that Mallon brought Bush into the CIA. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 22:24-22:54)*
- April 10, 1953: attended a meeting with Dulles and Bush on a "pilot project in the Caribbean." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 33:50-34:14)*
- Possible identity of the "representative from a large New York financial house" who scouted
  California in October 1945 (host speculation). *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 27:00-27:15)*
