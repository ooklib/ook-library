Zapata Petroleum is the oil company [[george-h-w-bush]] and partners founded in Midland, Texas in
1953. Chapter 1 ends just as it is introduced; the host defers the details to later chapters (bush-2).

## Claims

- Founded "a couple of weeks" before April 10, 1953 in Midland by the 29-year-old Bush "and partners";
  he "had no previous experience in oil exploitation." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 34:07-34:23)*
- Coincidence of timing (host: "coincidentally or maybe not so"): [[allen-dulles]] met
  [[neil-mallon]] and Bush on April 10, 1953 about a Caribbean "pilot project." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 33:50-34:14)*
- Naming discrepancy (flagged): the earlier `ewa/cold-war` episode calls it "Zapata Oil"; this
  episode says "Zapata Petroleum." Treated here as the same company (my inference).
