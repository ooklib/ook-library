Zapata Petroleum is the oil company [[george-h-w-bush]] and partners founded in Midland, Texas in
1953. Chapter 1 ends just as it is introduced; the host defers the details to later chapters (bush-2).

## Claims

- Founded "a couple of weeks" before April 10, 1953 in Midland by the 29-year-old Bush "and partners";
  he "had no previous experience in oil exploitation." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 34:07-34:23)*
- Coincidence of timing (host: "coincidentally or maybe not so"): [[allen-dulles]] met
  [[neil-mallon]] and Bush on April 10, 1953 about a Caribbean "pilot project." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 33:50-34:14)*
- Naming discrepancy (flagged): the earlier `ewa/cold-war` episode calls it "Zapata Oil"; this
  episode says "Zapata Petroleum." Treated here as the same company (my inference).

## Relationships

- Founded by [[george-h-w-bush]].

## Additional claims (Bush and the Cowboys, Chapter 2)


- Host's claims: founded at the end of March 1953 by Bush with Hugh and Bill Liedtke (ASR "litkey"), John Overbey (ASR "Overby"), the local landman, and Thomas Devine (ASR "deine"), who is called "a relatively unknown man"; it appeared to be the "pilot project in the Caribbean" discussed with [[allen-dulles]] and [[neil-mallon]] on April 10, 1953, and marked Bush's graduation to an "independent oilman like Clint Murchison and HL Hunt" ([[clint-murchison]], [[h-l-hunt]]). Details deferred to later chapters. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 44:50-46:11, 47:46-47:54)*
- Discrepancy flag: `ewa/bush-1` says founded "a couple of weeks" before April 10; this episode says end of March 1953 and "2 weeks earlier"; consistent.

## Additional claims (Bush and the CIA, Chapter 3)

- Host: named after the 1952 film *Viva Zapata!* per Bush himself; the host finds the name ironic and doubts it was really his favorite film. Original financing from silent partners including Bush's maternal uncle Herbert Walker Jr. and Eugene Meyer, owner of the Washington Post; Hugh and Bill Liedtke (ASR "litkey") of Tulsa, "wildcatters" Bush met in Midland, were the oil experts, who per Russ Baker led Zapata to 127 consecutive wet holes. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 0:03-1:39)*
- [[thomas-devine]], the MIT graduate and CIA staff officer (per his 2019 obituary), is called Bush's likely CIA case officer; the host concludes Zapata was "almost certainly a CIA front company" though it made big profits on Permian Basin/East Texas land. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 1:39-2:06, 6:50-9:22)*
- The Liedtkes took a buyout (~$800,000, allegedly from Herbert Walker) around 1957-58 and merged with South Penn Oil to form Pennzoil in 1963, leaving Bush behind. See [[zapata-offshore]] for the offshoot. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 12:47-13:47)*
- Host's note: the CIA's official code name for the Bay of Pigs invasion was "Operation Zapata," which he says "cannot be written off as a mere coincidence" given Zapata Offshore's alleged role (the host's assertion). See [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]]. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 19:15-19:31)*
- Discrepancy flag: bush-2 named Overbey as a co-founder; this chapter calls him Zapata's landman. Consistent with bush-2's "local landman" description.
