Claims in the `jm/cover-up` episode that some presidents were briefed on UFO matters and others were shut out. All of this is [[jason-samosa]]'s research-based speculation and [[jesse-michels]]' commentary.

## Claims

- Samosa: [[richard-nixon]], [[jimmy-carter]], [[ronald-reagan]] and [[george-h-w-bush]] all seem to have received briefings, whereas [[bill-clinton]] "had to fight" for information and still did not get it; [[barack-obama]] may have had a similar experience. He notes [[ross-coulthart]]'s remark that Republican presidents seem to get information and Democrats not, and explains it as insiders (Bush family) versus outsiders (Clinton, Obama). *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 45:56, 46:13, 46:30, 46:51)*
- Michels disagrees that Nixon was an insider, describing him as an outsider from Whittier who wanted to cut back the CIA. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 47:12)*
- Clinton's push: asked [[ron-pandolfi]] and got the [[bruce-maccabee]] briefing; [[laurance-rockefeller]] pushed the Clintons toward disclosure; Clinton and Obama both remarked that Area 51 holds nothing. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 47:34, 48:18, 52:10)*
- Samosa reads the fobbing-off as a pattern: earlier administrations were full of religious networks ([[collins-elite]] figures, the [[the-family-fellowship]], the [[knights-of-malta]]) with roots in the OSS. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 50:14)*
- The contrast with real programs: only about $22 million is publicly tied to [[aawsap]]/AATIP versus the trillions a legacy program implies. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 54:38, 55:25)*
