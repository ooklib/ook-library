OSS Detachment 202 was, per Eyes Wide Open, the group of OSS agents deployed to China and Southeast Asia in 1944-1945 ostensibly to support Allied forces and subvert Japanese occupation. The host argues it was an "incubator" for a future class of American clandestine experts and Cold War guerrilla-warfare, counter-insurgency and drug-financing practitioners. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 0:00-2:25)*

## Claims

- Deployed to China and Southeast Asia in 1944-45 for covert operations against the Japanese-held territories. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 0:00-0:19)*
- Led in part by [[paul-helliwell]], who ran its secret intelligence unit in Yunnan helping [[chiang-kai-shek]]'s Nationalists against Mao's Communists; Yunnan lay within the future [[golden-triangle]]. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 2:26-3:19)*
- Alumni named by the host: [[e-howard-hunt]] (the "Spook"), [[john-singlaub]] (the "General"), [[lucien-conein]] (the "Operator"), [[mitchell-werbell]] (the "Mercenary"), and [[richard-helms]], who the host says also got his start in China with the OSS. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 1:26-1:35, 8:44-8:48)*
- The host frames its alumni as architects of Cold War guerrilla warfare and the "drug financing paradigm" ([[cia-drug-paradigm]]), and as evidence of a continuous funding flow linking Southeast Asia, Europe and Latin America. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 0:54-1:26, 22:16-22:56)*
- Per LAOS 1, its agents Helliwell, Conein and Singlaub parachuted into Vietnam in 1945 on the [[deer-mission]] to treat a malarial [[ho-chi-minh]]. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 18:36-19:06)*

- War Conspiracy 1: OSS commander on the ground in Southeast Asia (per host) was [[paul-helliwell]]; his team supported Chiang against Japan in Yunnan and found KMT opium funding. Host: the OSS China hands were "grizzly and cutthroat" operators who left a lasting legacy on the CIA in covert action, paramilitary work and narcotic smuggling; called the "China Cowboys" half of the [[china-lobby]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 20:03-22:04)*
