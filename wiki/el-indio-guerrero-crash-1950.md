An alleged crash of a Roswell-like craft on 6 December 1950 near El Indio, Texas and Guerrero, Mexico, described only in the disputed 1952 [[eisenhower-briefing-document]]. Details are sparse. [[uap-gerb]] argues it is a separate case from the [[willingham-langtry-crash-1955]]. All below are attributed claims.

## Claims (gerb/del-rio)

- The briefing document says the craft hit at high speed and was nearly obliterated ("totally incinerated") and the recovered material went to the Atomic Energy Commission facility at [[sandia-national-laboratories]], New Mexico. El Indio is about 75 miles southeast of Del Rio. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 1:49-2:39)* *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 20:44-21:36)*
- Dennis Stacy's 1995 Omni article "Crash at El Indio" reports his 1990 to 1994 search; a local resident, Senor Flores, said a ball of fire fell on an adjoining ranch north of the river and started a grass fire, and a day or two later a military contingent from Piedras Negras blocked the site and hauled something away by truck. Flores could not say whether the vehicles were Mexican, American or mixed. Stacy thinks it may instead describe a 16 January 1944 Civil Air Patrol plane crash. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 21:44-23:18)*
- Context per Gerb: a 6 December 1950 nationwide US alert (recorded in the transcript as 6 January 1950, apparently an error) at 10:30 a.m. Eastern about a large number of unidentified aircraft approaching the northeast at high altitude, about 40 signatures at 32,000 ft, and cancelled by Gen. [[roger-ramey]] at 1316 hours; and a confidential 8 December 1950 memo to [[j-edgar-hoover]] saying Army intelligence was on alert for data on flying discs. Gerb wonders if the FBI memo concerns the alert or a retrieval, noting the later Guy Hottel memo discussed crash retrievals and bodies. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 23:08-24:22)*
- Todd Zechel allegedly obtained a declassified USAF document saying the military recovered a foreign object on 6 or 7 December 1950 and took it to Carswell AFB, but Gerb discounts it because Zechel would not provide the document. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 24:29-24:53)*
- The Sandia angle: Sandia sits on [[kirtland-air-force-base]], and Truman handed its management to [[att-corporation]] in 1949. Gerb argues this makes AT&T an "early suspect" for a private-sector UFO role. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 24:53-26:34)*
