[[ross-coulthart]] separates disclosure into two issues and says government fear of conflating them is the biggest impediment to admission. He also expects no imminent presidential disclosure.

## Claims

- Issue one is whether a non-human intelligence is engaging with the planet, to which he says the answer is "indubitably yes". Issue two is whether the public has a right to know the technology developed from recoveries. Officials tell him revealing the first forces revealing the second, and he disagrees, comparing it to how the public does not know how to make a thermonuclear bomb; he says scalar or particle-beam weapons, if they exist, should not be public. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 54:39-55:34, 58:03-59:08)*
- The cost of admitting the first would also include explaining the murders, cover-ups and the phenomenon itself, which is multifaceted and hard to pin down as "NHI". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 56:17-57:18)*
- He is not being told by anyone that Trump will announce disclosure, and does not buy Mark Christopher Lee's view; Trump knows and Coulthart has spoken to his son, and there is family interest, but no sign of intent, even though he thinks Trump is the president with the "kajones" to do it, perhaps around Roswell's anniversary in July. See [[donald-trump]], [[trump-disclosure-leverage-claims]]. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 57:18-57:56, 1:35:44-1:36:30, 2:17:51-2:18:10)*
- He also thinks the military-first mindset is tragic: the "bearers of this paradigm-shifting secret are people with guns", and Ramsey adds the 1950s "space brothers" era was closer to collective disclosure. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 59:15-1:00:26)*
- He thinks a future admission would let the defence and intelligence community use the "read in" senators as cover. See [[congressional-oversight-and-read-in-claims-coulthart]].
