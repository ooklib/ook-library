Chuck Payne is the name of a security guard at S4 that [[bob-lazar]] says he had never given anyone, not even [[george-knapp]], until a conversation filmed for *S4: The Bob Lazar Story* ([[project-gravitator-s4-documentary]]). Whether the name is real is unverified; the man is not identified.

## Claims

- Lazar says he deliberately withholds a few details about S4 so that anyone claiming to have been there can be tested on them, and that Payne was one of these: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 1:12-2:18,)*
- Payne was one guard among many but stood out as an extrovert: the guards were otherwise tight-lipped and had minimal interaction, while Payne would say "Hey, Bob" and talked to everybody, so anyone at S4 would know his name: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 4:11-5:34)*
- Lazar does not know his rank, only that he seemed "a special entity", perhaps in charge of the guards, and says he was not the one who roughed him up more than the others; the guards were "all from the same mold" and part of the stress inoculation: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 7:10-8:01)*
- Lazar has not tried to find him and does not know if he is alive; he asks people not to harass guards, who "were just doing their job": *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 6:27-6:37,)*

## The deleted scene

Vendittelli says Lazar volunteered the name in Las Vegas about three years earlier, when the crew (Motivo, Art and Project Gravitator crews) shared an Airbnb and were filming Lazar with a Delorean "time machine" in the desert, after Vendittelli showed him progress on the S4 build and said he did not want to push but would love something Lazar had never shared: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 9:30-11:03)*. The first cut of the film included the name, but because it was not expanded the team removed it, fearing it would only annoy viewers: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 8:16-9:04)*.

## Identity caveats

The host and Vendittelli warn that a Googled "Chuck Payne" is a different person, reportedly a senator or governor (the transcript says both), whose inbox should not be flooded, and Lazar confirmed from a photograph that it is not him: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 9:04-9:20,)*. The host adds that many Chuck Paynes exist, from retired army to intelligence, and that people who found [[dennis-mariani]] might find this one: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 9:20-9:30,)*.
