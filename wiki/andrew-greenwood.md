Andrew Greenwood was the science teacher at Westall High School who, per [[ross-coulthart]], watched the 6 April 1966 objects with over a hundred students and later said he was threatened for talking about it.

## Claims

- He had spoken previously and anonymously to [[james-fox]] and then met Coulthart. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 24:48-25:05)*
- Coulthart says Greenwood was precise and scientific, describing a metallic-coloured elliptical object and declining to say it was solid because he had not touched it. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 27:52-28:16)*
- He said an Air Force officer and a civilian threatened him to say he was an alcoholic; he agreed to speak to Coulthart, who says he has since died. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 26:44-27:52, 30:33-30:40)*
