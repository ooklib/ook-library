E. Howard Hunt, the "Spook," is presented by Eyes Wide Open as a founding CIA member who began under Helliwell's OSS Detachment 202 in China, ran CIA operations from Mexico, helped organize the 1954 Guatemala coup and the Bay of Pigs, became a Nixon "plumber" in Watergate, and left a disputed deathbed confession about the Kennedy assassination. He wrote spy novels under pen names. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 4:48-5:29)*

## Claims

- Career: OSS China under [[paul-helliwell]] (per his memoir); CIA office in Mexico; instrumental in [[operation-pbsuccess]] (1954). *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 5:02-5:37)*
- Per David Talbot, served as political liaison between Langley and anti-Castro Cuban exile groups in Florida and Louisiana for the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]] and reportedly trained exile mercenaries in Guatemala; many speculate it was partly funded by Helliwell's banking network. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 6:16-6:46)*
- Reportedly spotted in Dallas on 22 Nov 1963; researchers say he resembles the third of the "three tramps" photographed after the assassination (identity debated). The host says he had a motive after the Bay of Pigs failure. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 6:48-7:32)*
- Per Talbot, after the Bay of Pigs he and other CIA men visited the fired [[allen-dulles]]'s household in 1962-63 (guests named: [[james-jesus-angleton]], [[richard-helms]], [[cord-meyer]], [[john-mccone]]); Hunt sought Dulles's help finding private intelligence work and helped on Dulles's memoirs. Researchers speculate the meetings had a sinister purpose tied to the assassination (speculation). *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 7:35-9:07)*
- Nixon "plumber"; instrumental in the [[watergate-scandal]] burglary; testified to the Senate; served 33 months. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 9:10-9:42)*
- Deathbed confession (via son Saint John Hunt, Rolling Stone 2007) allegedly listed, in hierarchy, Lyndon Johnson, [[cord-meyer]], David Atlee Phillips, Frank Sturgis, David Morales, Antonio Veciana, William Harvey and a "French gunman on the grassy knoll" -- see [[kennedy-assassination]]. The host doubts it (no mob figures, Hunt himself absent, alleged coercion by his sons, band plug) and calls it "probably half true", likening it to a limited-hangout info dump. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 9:42-11:58)*
- Later recommended that Nixon hire [[lucien-conein]] as White House narcotics advisor. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 16:47-16:55)*
