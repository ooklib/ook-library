A commentary video (about 17 minutes, dated 2024-02-18) in which [[uap-gerb]] opens a series on the private sector's role in the UFO phenomenon with [[battelle-memorial-institute]], [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tISTJRPOqFo). The chapters are intro, Project Stork, Special Report No. 14, exotic metallurgy, EJ Center, Jacques Vallee, AARO/Battelle/Moultrie and conclusion. Gerb says Grusch, Elizondo, Mellon, Eric Davis and Harry Reid have all said defense contractors are involved in reverse engineering and crash retrieval. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 1:26-1:56)*

## Claims

- Gerb presents [[project-stork]] (1952-53) as Battelle's machine-indexing contract for ATIC (Air Technical Intelligence Center) and its role in reassuring the public about UFOs. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 1:56-3:49)*
- He treats [[special-report-14]] (1955) as contradicted by Battelle's 1949 nickel-titanium work, see [[battelle-nitinol-study-1949]]. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 3:49-8:43)*
- He relays the [[ej-center]] story as a possible link between Battelle and flying-saucer parts. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 8:46-11:10)*
- He cites [[jacques-vallee]] and [[james-fox]] on the Joe Rogan Experience (episode 1574, 4 December 2020) as pointing to Battelle as the private contractor holding UFO materials. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 11:12-12:47)*
- He notes [[ronald-moultrie]]'s alleged Battelle board seat and his oversight of [[aaro]] and [[sean-kirkpatrick]]. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 12:51-14:42)*
- Gerb deliberately omitted a 2023 online "Battelle biologist" leaker's alien taxonomy claims as unsupported. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 15:51-16:47)*
- He promises similar videos on Lockheed Martin and the Carlyle Group. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 15:34-15:51)*
