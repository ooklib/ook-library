Edwin Feulner (transcribed inconsistently as "Ed Fuelner"/"Ed Feulner" in the source's automated
captions) co-founded the [[heritage-foundation]] in 1976 and remained on its board until his
death in late 2025. More Perfect Union presents him as a direct personnel link between the
[[bohemian-grove]] club and the drafting of [[project-2025]].

## Claims

- Co-founded the Heritage Foundation in 1976 with Joseph Coors Sr.
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 7:16)*.
- Appeared on the leaked Bohemian Grove 2023 attendee list at age 82, and remained on Heritage's
  board until his death in late 2025
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 8:00, 8:06, 8:13)*.
- A video clip shows Feulner, described as having attended the Grove "for years," introducing
  "Mandate for Leadership 7... called Project 2025, Mandate for Leadership, the Conservative
  Promise," saying "a sincere thank you from this old man who was around the first time it all
  happened" (referencing the Reagan administration). More Perfect Union states this clip is dated
  to just months before a Grove encampment that year
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 13:34, 13:41, 13:48, 14:02, 14:08)*.

## Relationships

- Founded [[heritage-foundation]] (with Joseph Coors Sr.).

## Speculation flag

The characterization of Feulner as a living link between the Reagan-era Grove network and
Project 2025's drafting is More Perfect Union's editorial framing of his own on-camera remarks and
attendance-list appearance; the video does not present a document showing Project 2025 content was
directly authored or discussed at the Grove.
