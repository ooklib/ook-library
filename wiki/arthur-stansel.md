Arthur Stansel is the decorated WWII veteran and engineer who is the primary witness to the alleged 1953 [[kingman-arizona-crash-1953]]. The pseudonym "Fritz Warner" appears in other wiki sources ([[eric-henry-wang]]). In jm/kissinger, [[jesse-michels]] retells his account as the opening of the episode.

## Claims

- While consulting on blast effects at the Upshot-Knothole tests (the 19 May 1953 "Harry" shot, 32 kt), Stansel was ordered with 15 other specialists to leave belongings, flew to Phoenix, and was bused with blacked-out windows to a site near Kingman, Arizona; told a secret test vehicle had crashed, his job was to determine impact velocity. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 0:26, 0:40, 1:09, 1:19, 1:27, 1:34, 1:42)*
- He describes two deep saucers fused together, about 30 ft across, dull brushed metal, buried about 20 in in sand, with no landing gear, no visible damage and a small open hatch; in a nearby tent were several 4-ft humanoid bodies on a table. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 2:02, 2:11, 2:19)*
- Michels's credibility case: three Purple Hearts, a Bronze Star, a career in physics/engineering at Wright-Patterson, and a declassified Upshot-Knothole report placing him on site. He says [[christopher-mellon]] recently claimed the saucer was recovered and studied for decades. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 2:33, 2:43, 2:52, 3:00)*
- Named alongside him in records was the head of Special Studies at Wright-Patterson, [[eric-henry-wang]]. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 3:09)*

## Claims (gerb/kingman)

- Gerb ([[gerb-kingman-video]]) presents Stansel as one of two "extremely credible" Kingman witnesses. Fowler's 1973 affidavit under the pseudonym "Fritz Warner" (also transcribed "Fitz Werner") was signed 7 June 1973 with Fowler as witness, with the identity later confirmed as Arthur Stansel Jr. Gerb notes his name is often misspelled "Stancil". *([gerb/kingman](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-05 - The 1953 Kingman, Arizona UFO Crash.md>) 1:15-7:31)*
- Background per Gerb (from Richard Geldreich Jr.'s Medium research): Wright-Patterson USAF aircraft test/mechanical engineer for Air Material Command, D-Day veteran, worked on the Manhattan Project and 16 years on Apollo, at the Nevada Proving Ground in May 1953. Unlike Bob Lazar, Gerb says his background can be verified. *([gerb/kingman](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-05 - The 1953 Kingman, Arizona UFO Crash.md>) 7:31-8:40)*
- Gerb argues the 1973 date rules out Stansel riding the Roswell wave, and stresses he worked in Air Material Command's Installation Division under [[eric-henry-wang]] and reported to [[edward-dahl]]. *([gerb/kingman](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-05 - The 1953 Kingman, Arizona UFO Crash.md>) 8:47-11:14)*
