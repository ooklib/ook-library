Operation Sunrise (per Eyes Wide Open's account, also referred to in Part 1 as "Operation
Sunset") was [[allen-dulles]]'s scheme, run out of the OSS station in Bern, Switzerland, to
secretly rescue and preserve elements of the German wartime government and SS in exchange for
their cooperation against Soviet-aligned forces in postwar Italy — centered on the rescue and
protection of SS General [[karl-wolff]]. This video (Part 2 of the "History of the CIA" series)
is the episode this operation is named for.

## Claims

- On April 26, 1945, SS General [[karl-wolff]] was surrounded by Italian partisans at an Alpine
  estate at Lake Como; Dulles saw saving him as a way to block Soviet-aligned Italian partisans
  from reaching the Eastern Front and guarantee the West a say in postwar Italy, and code-named
  the plan Operation Sunrise. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 13:40-14:58)*
- Rather than run the operation himself (at risk of insubordination against FDR's unconditional
  surrender policy), Dulles delegated it to [[gerhart-von-schulze-gaevernitz]], who organized an
  armed rescue team — accompanied by OSS agent Don Jones ("Scotty"), who had earned the Italian
  partisans' trust fighting alongside them and then provided cover for the convoy — to extract
  Wolff from Lake Como on the night of the operation. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 14:58-17:11)*
- After Wolff's later arrest by American tanks and Italian partisans, Dulles used the promise of
  immunity, forged/falsified reports describing Wolff as a "moderate Nazi" and "gentleman,"
  destroyed OSS documents on Wolff's war crimes, and a forged psychiatric diagnosis to keep him
  out of Nuremberg prosecution; Wolff, aware of Dulles's insubordination, allegedly used the
  knowledge to blackmail Dulles into continuing to protect him, including sending a letter to
  [[harry-truman]] outlining details of the operation. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 18:42-19:59)*
- Also encompassed Dulles and [[william-donovan]]'s efforts to save other high-ranking Nazis from
  Nuremberg justice, including [[hjalmar-schacht]] (successfully) and [[hermann-goring]]
  (unsuccessfully, deemed too high-profile). *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 10:05-12:53)*
- Wolff was ultimately sentenced to only four years in 1948-49 (later reduced to time served) for
  SS membership, thanks to a personal affidavit from Dulles and character testimony from
  Schulze-Gävernitz; he was a free man in Europe by June 1949 and went on to found a neo-fascist
  party and an SS-veterans' club in West Germany in the 1950s. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 19:59-21:41)*
- Also connects to [[james-jesus-angleton]] and [[junio-valerio-borghese|the Black Prince]]: the
  Black Prince agreed to cooperate with Operation Sunrise at a meeting also attended by Wolff and
  Angleton, in exchange for clemency. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 24:40-24:58)*
