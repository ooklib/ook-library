A group of cases [[uap-gerb]] proposes as candidates for the South American saucer recovery described by [[special-forces-witness-infographics]], who says the saucer hit a rocky outcropping, smashed the rock and took out a quarter mile of trees but was relatively unharmed. Gerb searched the 1969 to 2004 window using [[ryan-s-wood]]'s Majic Eyes Only and rates the match medium confidence.

## Claims (gerb/special-forces)

- Peru, 11 November 1975: [[leonard-stringfield]]'s note of 23 March 1978 records Bob Barry saying a craft was retrieved near Lima; unmanned, 12 ft high and 8 ft in diameter, with a metallic outer surface of material deemed non-terrestrial. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 26:24-27:20)*
- Chile, 1978 (Gerb reads "Pozo Ros" as a place name from Bob Pratt's files): a year later an explosion woke about 3,000 people; engineers judged it an air burst; American operatives in NASA coveralls questioned witnesses; Pratt's FOIA requests to NASA and the CIA got no data. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 27:20-27:52)*
- Argentina, 3 October 1980 (Stringfield status report 2): a saucer-shaped fireball came down and exploded near 7:00 p.m.; Captain Carlos Lima of the Argentine Air Force space research division found four circular burned spots 10 to 18 m across, apparently from liquid fuel or a very hot material. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 28:00-28:39)*
- Gerb excludes the 1997 [[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]] and the 1978 Moon Dust retrieval in Bolivia (see [[bolivia-ufo-crash-1978]]) because those craft are not saucer-like, and mentions the Varginha, Brazil case as another possible match. *([gerb/special-forces](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-16 - US Special Forces Confession - I Recovered Crashed UFOs - Fact or Fiction.md>) 26:31-28:56)*
