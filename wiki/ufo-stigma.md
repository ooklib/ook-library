The social ridicule attached to studying UFOs, which [[uap-gerb]] argues in [[gerb-stigma-video]] was deliberately engineered by the US government, the CIA and the USAF in the 1950s and 1960s to lump UFOs with ghosts, Bigfoot and conspiracy theories. This is Gerb's thesis, not an established fact.

## Claims

- Description of the stigma: mentioning UFOs is met with ridicule, pilots and military and political figures avoid the subject, and the study became "pseudoscience". *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 0:00-2:53)*
- Mechanism as Gerb tells it: [[project-grudge]] was tasked to explain sightings away and was a public-relations campaign (per [[j-allen-hynek]]), [[project-blue-book]] kept information from the media, the [[robertson-panel-1953]] recommended stripping UFOs of "special status", AFR 200-2 kept credible reports from Congress and the public, and the [[trick-memo-1966]] and [[condon-committee]] shifted attention to the psychology of witnesses. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 4:32-18:09)*
- Outcome: the Condon report, its praise in the press, the 1969 National Academy of Sciences review and a 1970 AIAA statement lumped UFOs with ghosts and Bigfoot, letting the government say "UFOs do not exist and we do not study them" while, per Gerb, study continued in secret. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 18:16-22:24)*

## Relationships

- Attributed by Gerb to the [[robertson-panel-1953]], the [[condon-committee]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency]]; related to [[project-blue-book]] and [[project-grudge]].

## Claims (gerb/kingman)

- Gerb ([[gerb-kingman-video]]) says AFR 200-2 stove-piped UFO reporting to USAF intelligence and away from [[project-blue-book]], so fewer credible reports reached Congress and the public, fueling the stigma; he speculates this was meant to protect crash retrievals such as Kingman. *([gerb/kingman](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-05 - The 1953 Kingman, Arizona UFO Crash.md>) 11:14-12:16)*

## Claims (gerb/knuth)

- Gerb opens by saying academia does not conduct hard analysis of UFOs because of powerful stigma, and that claims of craft violating known physics are actively scoffed at, framing [[kevin-knuth]] as a rare accomplished exception. *([gerb/knuth](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-03-10 - The Physics of UFOs - Dr. Kevin Knuth.md>) 0:00-0:32)*
