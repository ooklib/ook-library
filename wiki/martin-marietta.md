Martin Marietta was founded in 1961 (predecessor Glenn L. Martin Company began securing Air
Force ICBM contracts in 1955), with a focus on missile and space systems — the Titan rocket
family, Pershing and Sprint missiles, FGM-148 Javelin, and space vehicles including Viking 1
and 2 and the Mars Polar Lander. It merged with the Lockheed Corporation on 15 March 1995 to
form [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]]. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md)
16:37–17:56, 24:38–25:11)*

## Claims

- In 1993, prior to the Lockheed merger, Martin Marietta made a series of acquisitions Gerb
  argues entwined it with the alleged UFO portfolio: General Electric Aerospace, General
  Dynamics Space Systems Division, and the management contract for
  [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia National Laboratories]].
  *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 25:11–27:11)*

  company was confident gravity control could be achieved... within years," alongside GE, Bell
  Labs, Sperry Rand, Boeing, and Lockheed. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md)
  27:30–28:46)*

  alleged 1994 operational debut of the [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b|TR-3B]] (whose
  propulsion Sandia allegedly reverse-engineered), and one year before the 1995 Lockheed merger

  *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 40:36–41:01)*
