Interpen (International Penetration Force, also called the No-Name Key Group in official memos and reports) is the mercenary band of [[gerry-patrick-hemming]] in the early-1960s secret war against [[fidel-castro]]. In chapter 3 of [[road-to-dallas]] [[eyes-wide-open]] gives only its origin story and defers "much more on No-Name Key later".

## Claims

- Interpen was not founded until 1961 in Florida, as the supposed paratrooper unit of [[frank-sturgis]]'s Anti-Communist International Brigade; the host traces its origins to revolutionary Cuba in 1959, where Hemming met Sturgis (then security director of Castro's air force, met at the Pinar del Rio air base) and some future recruits. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 7:02-7:57)*
- Recruit quality: the host uses [[loran-hall]] as the example of the "riff raff" available; members named in the chapter include Hall, Lawrence Howard and William Seymour (Hall's close associate, arrested with him several times in 1963). *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 7:57-8:11, 13:44-14:09)*
- Host speculation: Interpen's members were willing to accuse each other and talk openly, so the group may have served as "distractions" and "additional paths"; its activities were "coded in a strange sense of artifice and theater". *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 9:21-10:01)*
- Host speculation: Interpen men in the [[odio-incident]] episode may have been trained to "take the blame and move on". *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 15:53-16:12)*

## Relationships

- Founded by [[gerry-patrick-hemming]]; sponsored by [[frank-sturgis]]; roster included [[loran-hall]]; related to [[cuban-revolution]] origins; the [[odio-incident]] involved its associates.
