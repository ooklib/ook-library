Alan Sandler owned the film studio behind "UFOs: Past, Present and Future" and, in [[grant-cameron]]'s account, is the man who made the CIA connections (including two CIA regression specialists used on the Travis Walton case) and who now holds a Vandenberg film in a vault in Oregon.

## Claims

- Cameron says Sandler arranged two CIA regressionists who worked on the Walton regression and whose tape never went into their documentaries. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 58:35-58:59)*
- Sandler allegedly reacted with fury and a threat to sue when the British Museum drawing similarity to the Holloman being was raised. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:07:55-1:08:19)*
- Sandler supposedly asked $25,000 for use of the Vandenberg film, then refused after his lawyer's advice: [[vandenberg-film-sandler-vault-claims]]. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:12:38-1:13:08)*
