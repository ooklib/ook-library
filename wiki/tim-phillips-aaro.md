Tim Phillips, disgraced former deputy director of [[aaro|AARO]], claimed on 30 July 2025 that
[[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]] was established "as the home for all this paranormal UFO
crap," and alleged (without providing examples, per Gerb) that multiple sworn congressional
witnesses cite Kona Blue to justify their claims. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md)
2:00:52–2:01:27)*

Not to be confused with [[terry-phillips]] (AFOSI), a different figure named in Gerb's MITRE
investigation.

## Claims

- Gave inconsistent public accounts of a declassified Majestic-12 "Psalm 101" document — on-air
  with Event Horizon claiming it had no images/diagrams, off-air to other podcasters claiming it
  was "chalk full of images, diagrams, figures" on material packaging.
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:01:49–2:02:14)*
- Gerb compares his pattern of "emotional lash outs" against [[david-grush|David Grush]] (via
  LinkedIn) to former AARO director [[sean-kirkpatrick|Shawn Kirkpatrick]]'s similar pattern (via
  podcasts). *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:01:33–2:01:49)*
