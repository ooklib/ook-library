Whitley Strieber is the author of *Communion* and the archetypal 1980s abductee/contactee. In the `jm/jorjani-religion` episode he is discussed by [[jesse-michels]] and [[jason-jorjani]], including a light-hearted on-camera touch of the alleged implant in his ear.

## Claims

- Jorjani says Strieber's father was tied to military intelligence, the Striebers are Texas Germans, and Strieber was enrolled in a summer camp at Randolph Air Force Base where, he alleges, he underwent physical torture and mind-control experiments; he was also taken, at about age seven or eight, to a "school for special children" in Baja California where they were shown horrific scenes, given implements and made to believe they had committed the butchery, an MK Ultra-like child dissociation programme. Jorjani says Strieber "doesn't want to talk about" this, based on public statements over 20 years. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:55:17, 2:55:31, 2:56:31, 2:57:31)*
- Jorjani says Strieber has recounted that while a grey straddled him in his Catskills cabin he saw military and intelligence figures standing around, including a former schoolmate later in the CIA, implying a mind-control production of the phenomenon. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:58:35, 2:59:15)*
- Michels adds that Strieber told him James Jesus Angleton refused to recruit him ("not you"), which may mean he was already an unwitting asset. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:59:47)* See [[james-jesus-angleton]].
- Jorjani links this to MK Ultra "Manchurian candidate" partitioning, Sirhan Sirhan, and Martin Cannon's *The Controllers*, and to reincarnation/possession studies that suggest the psyche is a conglomerate that can split. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:00:34, 3:01:35, 3:02:06, 3:04:39)*
