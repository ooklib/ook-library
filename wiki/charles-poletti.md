Charles Poletti (ASR-misspelled "Puletti"), New York's former lieutenant governor, was appointed
military governor of Italy under the Allied military government after the Sicily invasion —
described by Lucky Luciano as "one of our good friends" — and gave the mob control of Italy's
docks.

## Claims

- Most notably among a series of Mafia-linked postwar Sicilian appointments, former New York
  lieutenant governor Charles Poletti — who, per Lucky Luciano, was "one of our good friends" —
  became the military governor of Italy under the Allied military government.

- Essentially allowed the free flow of narcotics through Italy's ports by giving
