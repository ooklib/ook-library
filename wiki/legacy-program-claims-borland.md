Borland's general claims about the UFO legacy program and non-human craft, as stated in [[borland-michels-interview-2026]]. He says his knowledge came from colleagues at [[bae-systems]], corroborated after 2023, and that he was not directly on a program. See [[ufo-legacy-programs]] for the wider ecosystem.

## Claims

- He is sure that craft "not made by humanity at the time we recovered them" exist, held in hangars or underground and studied on the ground, but says he never claimed extraterrestrials or "little grays", and does not know where they come from ("future or future past" is a hypothesis he floats). *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 53:19-54:30)*
- He knows of a genetics component ("what Dave called biologics"), and believes [[david-grush]] that pilots were recovered. He does not know of "bodies and freezers". *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:01:35-1:02:18)*
- Two BAE colleagues had access to a specific program with a historical backlog; once read in and digging into the backlog they got into trouble. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 59:02-1:01:16)*
- He believes remote viewing, mind projection, ESP and MK-Ultra-type programs are under one umbrella with UFOs (with [[jim-lacatski]]'s Skinwalkers at the Pentagon), a CIA-adjacent structure; Michels names [[glenn-gaffney]] of CIA S&T, as [[eric-davis]] does, as a last leader of the legacy program. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:02:18-1:03:42)* See [[operation-kona-blue]], [[project-stargate]], [[cia-directorate-of-science-and-technology]].
- He claims cell-phone video within 100 ft of triangles exists in government hands, and high-resolution imagery, and that these would "end the conversation". *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 52:03-52:30, 2:21:54-2:22:19)*
- He says a transnational agreement among governments, and probably with the non-human intelligence, must exist because China or Russia could otherwise embarrass the US, echoing themes of [[transnational-criminal-organization-claims]]. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:23:05-2:24:13)*
- He puts a "mid-2000s" turning point: something changed, such as a breakthrough by China, Russia, a third party or an arrival, and reports increased around aircraft carriers and nuclear sites. He rejects Blue Beam-style false-flag narratives as unlikely but concerning. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:27:01-1:28:21)*
- On disclosure he predicts limited disclosure of "we are not alone" with immunity for the past; Michels cites concerns that criminal legacy activity will be swept in. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:39:47-1:40:38)*
- He recounts that Kona Blue documents show government capability for false memories and mind manipulation, so an experiencer cannot tell whether CIA, a foreign state or an NHI did it. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:24:38-2:25:57)* See [[operation-kona-blue]].
- Michels claims that [[matthew-brown]]-type witnesses say the craft seem "made for purpose"; Borland says capability makes shape changes odd. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:47:29-2:48:50)*
- He believes [[glenn-gaffney]] is the CIA gatekeeper to name; quoting [[george-knapp]]'s congressional statement about a [[lockheed-martin]] to [[robert-bigelow]] transfer of unusual material stopped by Gaffney, he asks why no securities lawsuit followed. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:28:26-2:29:29)*
- On the [[oke-shannon]] channel: Borland says Shannon was a big topic on classified servers, and that [[jesse-michels]] frames a red-team hypothesis in which AATIP, [[aawsap]] and the [[uap-task-force]] fed civilian ideas back into the legacy program. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:18:25-1:20:10)*
