Commander Georges Loustaunau-Lacau (rendered by ASR as 'Lustano La Co'; first name given garbled in the transcript, so the identification is the wiki's inference) led the [[corvignolles]], the intelligence arm of [[la-cagoule]], and later turned up in the first alleged French Gladio operation. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 5:22-5:36)*

## Claims

- Organized the Corvignolles within the French army; joined Pétain's Vichy regime before forming a resistance movement, was foiled and shipped to the Mauthausen camp until war's end. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 5:22-7:19)*
- Arrested in [[plan-bleu-1947]]; Eyes Wide Open treats this as a direct link between the pre-war fascist underground and institutionally supported Gladio operations. Released after six months and reportedly given a distinguished service order by the British, which the host reads as tacit British support. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 19:01-19:34)*
