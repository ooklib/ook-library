A set of anecdotes Gerb gathers, chiefly from [[leonard-stringfield]]'s crash retrieval status reports, claiming that downed craft and biologics were held at US Navy installations. Gerb presents them as background for Chris Sharp's claim that undersea recoveries are stored at Office of Naval Research locations. All are secondhand claims.

## Claims (gerb/navy)

- Derward "Buddy" Hack: in a 1978 letter Stringfield was tipped that Hack, a chief petty officer radar observer at Naval Air Station Sunnyvale in 1952, disappeared, having (per his family) wandered into an unguarded hangar and seen a huge saucer-shaped craft with rows of windows; guards threatened him. His mother confirmed both the disappearance and the story, quoting him as saying "it was certainly no aircraft of ours". *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:12:26-1:13:16)*
- Joan Jeffers (6 February 1979 letter): a retired USAF colonel was shown a USAF film at a 1956 commander's call at Naval Air Station Brunswick, Maine, by Major Lester Goldberg (a real officer, commander of the 654th Aircraft Control and Warning Squadron) showing a silver disc on the ground and at least three small, gray, hairless, apparently dead bodies on tables. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:13:16-1:14:17)*
- Stringfield's anonymous Lieutenant Colonel X said in 1975 that bodies were being flown to a secret naval installation on a Pacific island. Gerb lists candidate Navy sites (Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Center on Andros Island, the Pacific Missile Range Facility in Hawaii) and mentions Richard Sauder's research on underwater bases. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:14:17-1:15:07)*
- Lieutenant Colonel X also connected to the alleged Majestic 12 [[special-operations-manual-som1-01]], which sends dead biologics to the Wright-Patterson Blue Room and living entities to "OPNAC BBS01", which Gerb guesses to be OPNAV plus the Bermuda Biological Station (now BIOS). *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:15:14-1:15:46)*
- Yuma, Arizona: in status report 3 case 15B witness AF and his brother Pete, a former Marine who claimed to have photographed a saucer at the Naval air base in 1967, backed away after alleged interference; Stringfield saw this as a ploy or an example of silencing. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:15:46-1:17:14)*
- Great Lakes 1973: see [[great-lakes-naval-station-craft-1973]]. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:17:14-1:20:04)*
- In 1994 former New Mexico state representative [[j-andrew-kissner]] said sources at White Sands and the Naval Research Laboratory briefed him on recovered discs in 1947, given to AEC custody and studied at Los Alamos, Sandia, RAND and MITRE, with the programs signed into a 1948 Truman executive order coordinated by the NSC with support from Air Force Systems Command and the Research and Development Board under Vannevar Bush. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:20:10-1:21:06)*
- In 2005 USAF veteran Steven Walker claimed a large empty hangar at Diego Garcia was the site of a classified silent arrival with the base locked down; Gerb links this to [[edgar-fuche]]'s claim that TR3B programs moved to Dugway and Diego Garcia in the early 1990s. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:21:06-1:22:10)*
