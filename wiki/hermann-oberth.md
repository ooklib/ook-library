Hermann Oberth, "father of German rocketry" and mentor to [[wernher-von-braun]], performed
extensive study on UFOs, per Gerb. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:10:31–1:10:46)*

## Relationships

- Mentor of [[wernher-von-braun]].

## Additional claims (jm/nasa-pagan)

- Michels: born in Transylvania, wrote *The Rocket into Planetary Space* (1923), later publicly supported the reality of UFOs (1954 lecture arguing sightings since the 1400s, extraterrestrial visitors monitoring nuclear and military sites) and said "we have been helped" by "the people of the other worlds"; not part of the official Paperclip transfer, which took his student von Braun. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 26:39-28:41)*

## Claims (jm/cover-up)

- [[jesse-michels]] says an audio exists of Oberth saying the Germans had help, and that Japanese researcher Takano Joseen met him and discussed UFOs; Oberth "truly respected the beings actually riding in UFOs". *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:11:09, 1:11:27, 1:11:43, 1:11:59)*
