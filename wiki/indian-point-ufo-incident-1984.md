# Indian Point Nuclear Station UFO Incident (1984)

Alleged 24 July 1984 sighting over the Indian Point nuclear plant at Buchanan, New York, covered by [[uap-gerb]] as evidence of UFO interest in nuclear power.

## Claims

- Security police reported a conically shaped UFO, as long as three football fields, with white, yellow and blue oscillating lights. The plant allegedly lost motion sensors, alarms and security control computers as it passed overhead. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 11:47-12:14)*
- Over the next two days [[nuclear-regulatory-commission]] agents allegedly took over plant security operations and confiscated video and audio records. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 12:14-12:33)*
- Around the same time the NRC allegedly created an incident file for a black-triangle UFO over Cooper Nuclear Station, Nebraska (Gerb cites a Black Vault FOIA document). *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 13:00-13:16)*
