Second-hand claims about a UFO reverse-engineering program code-named "Rubik's Cube" and about drawings Borland allegedly gave the ICIG and [[aaro]], reported by [[jeremy-corbell]] and [[george-knapp]] from an ODNI official and discussed by [[jesse-michels]] and [[dylan-borland]] in [[borland-michels-interview-2026]]. Borland neither confirms nor denies, saying he "cannot confirm or deny anything".

## Claims

- Corbell says an ODNI agent told him Borland reported to the ICIG a UFO program called Rubik's Cube. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 5:40-6:48)*
- Corbell also said Borland drew a non-human-intelligence "pyramid power source" and a hieroglyph; Michels describes it as an electromagnetic, possibly nuclear-principled power source that cannot be controlled or throttled. Borland "pleads the fifth", speaking only in hypotheticals, and uses a Model T versus 2026 Ferrari analogy for handing advanced tech to a lower-tech civilization. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:08:12-1:10:24)*
- Michels connects it to [[luis-elizondo]]'s sandbox toy analogy for a shared, possibly free-energy technology; Borland offers to pick apart Elizondo's book "Imminent" and says it names [[bae-systems]] as an inheritor of UFO technology. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:10:24-1:12:58)*
- On whether the hieroglyph suggests non-human origin, Borland invokes the fifth. He cites hieroglyphs in reports of [[kecksburg-ufo-crash-1965]] and Roswell, and Michels adds Socorro 1964. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 1:29:33-1:30:42, 1:33:33-1:33:50)*
- Borland argues the government cannot claim the program is fake while classifying a drawing of it, and cites Corbell's report that a "fourth drawing" was alleged. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:04:28-2:05:27)*
- Michels' concept of a "Rubik's cube" as a petri-dish design, where UFOs give humanity things that break known priors, is a Michels/Borland interpretation, see [[nhi-control-and-simulation-claims]]. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:59:38-3:00:09)*
