In 2013, stealth-aircraft researcher Joseph Jones claimed the existence of Project Brilliant
Buzzard, a codename for an airborne surveillance/tracking program setting up an electromagnetic
shield around the US using an enormous boomerang-shaped craft with near-indefinite loiter time.
*([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:11:46–1:12:10)*

## Claims

- Allegedly primarily contracted to [[boeing|Boeing]]. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:12:10–1:12:19)*
- The craft was allegedly responsible for ~25,000 eyewitness sightings between 1983–1989 known
  as the "Hudson Valley Boomerang." *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:12:19–1:12:27)*
- Allegedly a program born from [[strategic-defense-initiative|SDI]].
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:12:19–1:12:27)*

## Relationships
