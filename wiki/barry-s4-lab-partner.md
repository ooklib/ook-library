"Barry" is the unidentified S4 colleague [[bob-lazar]] says was his assigned lab partner under the "buddy system", the only person he was allowed to discuss work with *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 34:43-35:17)*. Lazar says Barry had been alone for a while (Lazar apparently replaced a departed partner) and greeted him "like a puppy", eager to show off the system *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 35:08-35:31, 57:09-57:32)*. Barry lived on site during rotations, kept large O-rings for clipping badges, and taught Lazar the badge trick *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 30:00-30:11, 35:49-35:57)*.

Barry gave the first hands-on demonstration of the reactor's force field, the gravity emitter and the candle and black-dot experiments ([[lazar-gravity-amplifier-lab-claims]]), and led Lazar into the craft ([[lazar-sport-model-craft-claims]]) *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 53:22-55:55, 1:00:44)*. Barry also told Lazar the briefing binders contained deliberate junk, so that leaks could be traced to individual readers *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 48:30-48:53)*. Lazar guesses Barry is now retired and invites him to make contact *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:28:31-1:28:52)*.
