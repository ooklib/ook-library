# Jacobs Vandenberg UFO Tape Incident (1964)

Alleged incident on 14 September 1964 in which film of a dummy ICBM warhead test from [[vandenberg-air-force-base]] supposedly showed a saucer-shaped object firing beams of light at the dummy warhead. Told by [[robert-jacobs]]; presented by [[uap-gerb]] as one of the most credible UFO-nuclear cases.

## Claims

- Jacobs, an Air Force lieutenant, commanded a 100-man optical instrumentation unit at Vandenberg filming nuclear payload and missile launches from the Western Test Range near Big Sur. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 1:42-2:04)*
- On 14 Sept 1964 the unit filmed a three-stage ICBM test carrying a dummy warhead with decoy chaff, built to the size and weight of a real warhead; Jacobs saw nothing unusual live, only a smoke trail off into space at stage-three separation. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 2:11-3:25)*
- Within a day or two his commander, Major Florence J. Mansman, summoned him to a screening with three men in gray suits, later said by the major to be the director of the Office of the Chief Scientist and two agents. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 3:34-3:52)*
- The film allegedly showed an object entering the frame at the same speed in polar orbit, firing a beam of light at the warhead, circling and firing three more times, then leaving; the warhead fell out of frame. Jacobs described a classic saucer, two saucers pressed together with a half-ping-pong-ball hemisphere on top, from which the beam came. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 4:18-5:39)*
- Mansman allegedly ordered silence ("this never happened") and told Jacobs to explain it away as "laser tracking strikes" if ever asked; Gerb notes lasers were in their infancy in 1964. Mansman later said the gray-suited men cut out the UFO footage with scissors and took it in a briefcase. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 5:46-6:49)*
- Jacobs stayed silent 18 years, then spoke publicly in 1982 because he had signed no NDA and was never told the case was classified; he says he then faced harassment and threatening calls. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 6:33-7:26)*
- MUFON reported the case in 1985; Mansman allegedly confirmed Jacobs's account in writing to Paramount Pictures staff making a documentary, referencing the January 1989 MUFON journal. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 7:26-7:50)*
- Jacobs testified under oath to AARO and [[sean-kirkpatrick]] on 10 February 2023, per Gerb. *([gerb/nukes](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-29 - UFOs and Nuclear Weapons - A Fascinating Connection.md>) 7:50-8:07)*
