Frank "Brandy" Brandstetter (ASR "Brandansteader"; the description spells it Brandstetter) is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as an obscure former US Army intelligence lieutenant colonel who managed the [[havana-hilton]] in 1958-59 and afterwards the Las Brisas hotel in Acapulco, and who, in the host's phrasing, was "not so much an operator himself, but more so a shared contact" linking Army intelligence, the [[central-intelligence-agency]], the anti-Castro exile underground and foreign services. The host uses him as the capstone of the Cuba segment of [[road-to-dallas]]; the main source is the biography *Brandy, Our Man in Acapulco* (Carlisle and Monetta, 1999), which the host found via a footnote in [[peter-dale-scott]]'s *Dallas 63*. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 11:15-13:21)*

## Biography (per the host, from the biography)

- Born 1912 in the Austro-Hungarian Empire into a line of minor Polish/Hungarian aristocrats and soldiers; moved to New York at 16 and worked in hotels (Hotel St. Moritz in the 1930s, meeting guests such as J. Edgar Hoover and an exiled Baron Louis Rothschild). Enlisted January 1941. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 16:58-17:54)*
- Served throughout the war as a G2 (Army intelligence) personal aide to General Matthew Ridgway (ASR "Rididgeway"), a father-and-son relationship (Ridgway stood as father at his wedding); received a Bronze Star for thwarting a rebellion at a German POW camp, worked on the 82nd Airborne's deployment, interrogated prisoners and hunted high-value Nazi officials in France and the Low Countries. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 17:54-18:50)*
- Claims his unit was among the first to find the "crossbow" V-1 launch sites in the Low Countries. Host speculation: this, plus later closeness to NASA and to Walter Dornberger, "raises my suspicions" that he had a larger covert role in [[operation-paperclip]] than he admits, since he was also posted in northern Italy near the Austrian border in May 1945 when Dornberger and [[wernher-von-braun]] defected. The host says this is speculation. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 18:50-22:19)*
- Left the Army April 1947 as lieutenant colonel but, by his own admission, remained an off-the-books military-intelligence source and reserve officer for life, sometimes on his own dime, briefing Army intelligence about his hotel run-ins. Married the daughter of a powerful San Francisco lawyer of the "cabinet table" whose clients included Santa Fe Railway and [[bechtel-corporation]]. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 16:04-16:36, 24:27-24:52)*

## Las Brisas, NASA and Henry Crown

- Famous for decades of managing Las Brisas in Acapulco, called an international intelligence hot spot; guests named by the host include the Bronfman family, NASA officials and Apollo astronauts, [[lyndon-b-johnson]] and family, Tito, and Conrad Hilton. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 13:30-14:09)*
- Partial owner in later life; [[henry-crown]] was a major investor. Host speculates Crown's Pentagon/industry contacts made Las Brisas the preferred resort of astronauts. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 14:09-14:24, 23:22-23:53)*
- Befriended Walter Dornberger (Paperclip scientist) after supposedly meeting him at the Apollo 11 launch in 1969; Dornberger was a frequent Las Brisas guest. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 21:05-21:37)*
- A 1972 newspaper article ("Resort Owner Tied to Astronaut Favors") alleges Apollo crews did favors for Brandstetter: Apollo 7 photographed the hotel from space, Apollo 11 planted a small pink Las Brisas flag on the moon, and Apollo 15 named "Brandy Crater" near Hadley Rille. The host calls these "simply rumors" that are "not all that far-fetched". *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 22:36-23:22)*

## Army reserve activities in Texas, 1954-58

- Yearly reserve duty in Texas: Cloverleaf 1 at Fort Hood (1954); Nov. 1955 at Fort Sam Houston, giving a 15-minute briefing on Israel and the Arab states to the Fourth Army command (a year before Suez); 15 days in 1956; in 1957 provost marshal of the Fourth US Army Area Intelligence School for two weeks, whose review topics included handling foreign visitors, control of classified funds, "Q" nuclear clearances and record checks for the [[counter-intelligence-corps]], the [[atomic-energy-commission]] and ONI. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 24:52-26:03, 28:59-30:21)*
- Worked in Dallas in the mid-1950s for Continental Restaurants Corporation and served in the 488th Strategic Intelligence Reserve Unit headed by [[jack-crichton]] (ASR "Jack Kiteon"; host corrects his own earlier "Kitchen" pronunciation). With Crichton he worked on "a study of the capability of Soviet oil fields"; host notes Crichton's alleged early-1990s trip to the ex-USSR for Texas oil firms. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 26:33-28:22)*
- Also gave [[clint-murchison]] a resort feasibility study, which fizzled. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 27:22-27:48)*
- His commanding officer at the 1957 Fort Sam Houston school was Col. George Lumpkin, deputy chief of Dallas police, who later drove the pilot car of Kennedy's motorcade and, per the host, with Crichton played a "suspicious" role in debriefing Marina Oswald (promised for later); Lumpkin also served in the 488th and, six years before, had awarded Brandstetter a commendation (ASR timing "6 years earlier" as spoken). Host: these "coincident run-ins alone put Brandstetter solidly in the JFK assassination nexus". *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 30:37-31:46)*
- Cannon parallel (credit to Patreon subscriber "Riley Ken"): Brandstetter was at Fort Sam Houston in August 1958 while manager of the Havana Hilton, his last such assignment; in October 1958 [[jack-cannon]], then provost marshal at nearby Fort Hood, was brought to Fort Sam Houston as a "mental patient". Host asks whether Cannon was trained by Brandstetter, and suggests the two probably met in Havana. Host labels this coincidence-based speculation. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 28:36-29:59, 31:57-33:40)*

## Havana Hilton, 1958-59

- Plucked by Conrad Hilton to open the hotel in 1958; see [[havana-hilton]] for the New Year's Day 1959 events, Castro's occupancy, [[manuel-ray]] and [[philippe-thyraud-de-vosjoli]]. Host: "not even a stretch to assume that Brandy was scouting assets" there. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 33:48-34:10, 38:31-38:40)*
- Befriended [[david-atlee-phillips]] in Cuba after the revolution; when Phillips founded [[association-of-former-intelligence-officers]] in 1975, Brandstetter asked that non-CIA officers (Army, Navy) be admitted, became its first non-CIA member and recruited Army/Navy intelligence veterans for it. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 38:40-39:27)*
- Left Havana for Miami on a one-way ticket on 13 February 1959. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 47:44-48:07)*
- On 22 Nov. 1963 [[philippe-thyraud-de-vosjoli]] flew from Washington to Las Brisas to take refuge with him. *([ewa/dallas-5](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-12 - The Secret History of the Havana Hilton - The Road to Dallas Chapter 5.md) 45:23-46:40)*
