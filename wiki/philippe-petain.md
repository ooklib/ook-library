Marshal Philippe Pétain, "hero of Verdun" and later head of Vichy France, appears briefly in [[eyes-wide-open]]'s Willoughby 1 as the French commander in the [[rif-war]]. ASR renders the name "Philip Pen" / "Philip Peng" (normalization is my inference). Thin page: recorded for the host's colonialism-to-fascism thread.

## Claims

- France intervened in the Rif War because Abd el-Krim's republic threatened its own North African colonies; it first sent Marshal Lyautey (ASR "Leote"), who fell ill and was replaced in 1925 by Pétain. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 23:28-24:35)*
- Host: Franco and Pétain, "two future leaders of fascist governments", led the two colonial forces; Pétain later became "chosen leader of the Nazi wartime puppet state known as Vichy France", stayed close to Franco, and was ambassador to Spain in 1939. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 24:49-26:12)*

## Relationships

- Fought in the [[rif-war]] alongside [[francisco-franco]]'s Spanish forces.
