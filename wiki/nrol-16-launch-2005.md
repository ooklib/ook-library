The NROL-16 mission (payload said to be named Prometheus) launched on a Titan 4 rocket at 12:50 a.m. on 30 April 2005, Walpurgis Night, per [[jesse-michels]] relaying [[john-ramirez]]. It is his modern example of ritual symbolism at [[national-reconnaissance-office]] launches. All of this is highly speculative pattern-finding.

## Claims

- The mission patch showed a pelican, a sun, several pentagrams, a Bigfoot silhouette and palm trees; the pelican matched [[ron-pandolfi]]'s code name. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 49:15-50:02)*
- The rocket headed northeast toward the Big Dipper, which Michels compares to a "walking" ritual in the 1977 Necronomicon (best done at midnight facing the northeast when the Great Bear "hangs by its tail"); the number five (0:50 launch, year 2005, five pentagrams) is called sacred. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 50:08-51:05)*
- [[peter-levenda]] suggests that people around such launches encouraged [[tom-delonge]] to study Prometheus; remaining Titan 4 rockets went to the Air Force museum at Wright-Patterson and to McMinnville, Oregon, site of the 1950 Trent UFO photos. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 51:05-51:48)*
