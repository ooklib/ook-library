Yellow Fruit was an unacknowledged special access program (USAP) run out of the US Army's Special
Operations Division (SAD), exposed by a 1983 DoD internal financial audit. UAP Gerb treats its
exposure — and the resulting 1980s SAP oversight reforms — as a pivotal event in the alleged
fracturing of the centralized "Manhattan Project 2.0" UFO legacy-program apparatus into today's
siloed structure. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 2:38:47–2:39:13)*

## Claims

- Purpose: providing additional operational security and counterintelligence for the Central
  American Contra mission and other classified Army operations, run under the Army's Special
  Operations Division (SAD) — one of several covert military intelligence organizations, per the
  US Army War College, used to circumvent congressional restrictions and reporting requirements
  levied on the CIA. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 2:38:53–2:40:16)*
- Its cover was blown due to cost discrepancies found in internal audits, implicating Lieutenant
  Colonel James E. Duncan, who had provided top cover/support from a staff position within the
  Army's Assistant Chief of Staff for Intelligence (G2) — a pattern UAP Gerb connects to General
  [[george-c-marshall|George Marshall]]'s earlier use of his own G2 to run the
  [[alsos-mission|ALSOS missions]] outside standard channels. Duncan subsequently retired and set
  up a private consulting firm (BSI) to continue covert operations under private cover — a "data
  masking" technique UAP Gerb says he has observed elsewhere, including Air Force
  [[hidden-wing-air-force-program|Hidden Wing]]-era general officers in the late 2000s, and CIA
  shell companies like [[e-systems|E-Systems]] (formerly LTV). *([secrecy-1](../raw/
  jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 2:40:35–2:41:54)*
- Army Chief of Staff John Wickham stated Army leadership had never been fully briefed on the
  "black programs" run by SAD, indicating Army brass had no visibility into their own service's
  covert activities. The revelation led to courts-martial, prison time, and significantly
  increased SAP oversight, and directly contributed to the broader Iran-Contra scandal's exposure.
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 2:41:54–2:42:22, 2:37:19–2:37:36)*
- UAP Gerb's central thesis: SAP architecture organizes similar critical program information into
  a single Tier-1 "umbrella" SAP, with narrower Tier-2/Tier-3 compartments and subcompartments
  nested beneath it — the same matrix outlined in DoD Directive 5205.07. Because Yellow Fruit
  handled a multifaceted mission (Contras plus other covert activities), he argues it functioned
  as exactly this kind of umbrella USAP, and hypothesizes it is "highly probable" that individual
  UFO-related compartments sat nested within the Yellow Fruit umbrella and were nearly exposed by
  the 1983 audit — structurally analogous to how he argues [[operation-sand-dollar|Project Sand
  Dollar]] (a maritime UFO/USO retrieval program per Dr. [[john-craven|John Craven]]) was nested
  inside the cover of the [[polaris-program|Polaris]] submarine program. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  2:48:39–2:49:51, 2:53:26–2:54:04)*
- UAP Gerb theorizes the Yellow Fruit exposure, combined with Reagan's Executive Order 12356
  ending the prior "Wild West" era of loose top-secret-codeword readon access (see
  [[special-access-program]]), was a major driver of legacy programs "burrowing deeper" into
  cover offices during the mid-1980s — such as the Air Force's [[saf-aa|SAF/AA]] "outside
  activities" allegedly established by [[edward-c-aldridge|Edward C. Aldridge]] — culminating in
  the 1994 [[special-access-program-oversight-committee|SAPOC/SRG]] reorganization under
  [[bill-perry|William Perry]]. He connects this same chain of events to the
  [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]]' account of a "watch committee" telling Vice Admiral
  Thomas Wilson their program was "formed out of necessity" after "an audit investigation ... led
  to them, and it wasn't supposed to, nearly outed" the program. *([secrecy-1](../raw/
  jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 2:45:17–2:48:29)*
