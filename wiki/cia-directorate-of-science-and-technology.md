CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology is listed among "program administrator" agencies in
Gerb's [[ufo-legacy-programs|legacy program pyramid]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 5:18–5:43)*

## Claims

- Deputy director role passed through [[donald-kerr]] (2001–2005), [[stephanie-osullivan]],
  and [[don-meyer]] (2013–2021) — each of whom went on to senior IC or
  [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]/Aerospace board roles. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:39:43–1:39:50, 1:43:02–1:43:14)*
- Spawned the [[cia-office-of-global-access|CIA Office of Global Access]] in 2002/2003, a
  logistics coordinator for foreign UFO crash retrievals harnessing
  [[jsoc|JSOC]] resources, established under [[donald-kerr]]'s watch as deputy director.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 18:42–18:50, 47:21–48:09)*
- Co-founded, alongside the US Navy, the
  [[national-underwater-reconnaissance-office|National Underwater Reconnaissance Office
  (NURO)]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 47:38–47:54)*
- [[scitor-holdings|Scitor]] was a critical CIA DS&T contractor for satellite SIGINT/ELINT
  before being acquired by [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] in 2018.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 18:24–18:32)*

- A second deputy-director lineage identified in the NRO video: [[doug-wolf|Doug Wolf]] (founded
  [[cia-office-of-global-access|OGA]] in 2003), [[dawn-meyerriecks|Dawn Meyerriecks]] (8 years,
  now a [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]] board member), [[everett-heinman|Everett Heinman]] (also
  director of [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]] Program B), and [[glenn-gaffney|Glenn
  Gaffney]] (career began at [[national-underwater-reconnaissance-office|NURO]]; accused in the
  "Kona Blue" UFO-materials blockage). [[mark-moyahan|Mark Moyahan]] served as DS&T chief of
  policy/interim chief of staff. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:28:52–2:33:07,
  2:44:52–2:47:12)*
