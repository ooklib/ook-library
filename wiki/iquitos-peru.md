Peruvian Amazon city that [[uap-gerb]] proposes was the operating base of [[jonathan-weygandt]]'s Marine detachment and the focus for his alleged 1997 crash retrieval ([[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]]). The identification is Gerb's inference; Weygandt never named it.

## Claims (gerb/weygandt)

- Iquitos hosted USAF radar Detachment 5, the MARFORLANT riverine training team supporting [[operation-laser-strike]], and the Navy Medical Research Unit ([[namru-south]]); Gerb finds only one 1990s-onward mention of Detachment 5. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:13:46-1:14:30)*
- Gerb reasons that Weygandt's account of hours of driving and trekking (about 2 to 4 hours, so roughly a 90-mile radius, plus or minus 25 percent) puts the crash near the Colombian or Brazilian border, and that Weygandt likely misremembered Bolivia as the border 5 to 10 miles away. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:15:02-1:18:21)*
- 1998 narco routes passing through Iquitos and the Bolivian traffic Weygandt's radar tracked are cited as consistent. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:14:46-1:14:53)*
