The East India Company, chartered in 1600 and headquartered on Leadenhall Street in the City of
London, is presented by Green & Pleasant as the second pillar (alongside [[lloyds-of-london]]) of
the City's historical role financing and administering the British Empire.

## Claims

- Chartered in 1600. At its height it governed something like 60 million people in India, with a
  private army at times roughly twice the size of Britain's own
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 12:17, 12:25)*.
- Grew opium in India and sold it into China against Chinese law; when China moved to stop it,
  Britain sent the Royal Navy — the Opium Wars, out of which Hong Kong became British
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 12:33, 12:41)*.
- Its former Leadenhall Street headquarters site is now occupied, per the video's on-the-ground
  research, by the Lloyd's of London building, with no plaque marking the site's history — one
  historian who went looking found nearby only a plaque commemorating a postage stamp
  *([gp/older-than-parliament](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-06-03 - The Government Inside Britain That's Older Than Parliament.md) 12:41, 12:50)*.

## Relationships
