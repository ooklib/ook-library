Ingo Swann was a leading SRI psychic (remote viewer) associated with what became [[project-stargate]]. In this source he appears only via [[jason-jorjani]]'s retelling of his Moon and Mars viewings.

## Claims

- In the 1970s Swann was taken blindfolded by helicopter from the SRI programme by a man calling himself "Axelrod" to an unnamed black unit and tasked to view a blind target that turned out to be the Moon. Jorjani infers his handlers had access to lunar photographs, like those Carl Wolf says he saw (see [[artificial-moon-claims]]). *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:02:39, 1:04:40)*
- Swann says he saw tall, statuesque Nordic people who told him to leave, and naked slave laborers in makeshift shelters, probably under a transparent dome. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:05:45)*
- In the early 1980s Swann and five other viewers, blind, viewed Mars and saw honeycombed ruined cities with signs of internal explosions, built by the same Nordics; see [[mars-nuclear-war-claims]]. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:06:23)*
- Michels notes that many original Stargate people came from Sidney Gottlieb's technical staff; Jorjani says the wider psi programmes were a vestibule for a darker black unit. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 16:32, 2:50:47)* See also [[scientology-remote-viewing-claims]] for a different Swann-report claim from Danny Sheehan.
