[[uap-gerb]]'s inference that a 1949 [[battelle-memorial-institute]] research contract on nickel-titanium alloys, a decade before nitinol's official discovery, shows the Air Force analyzing shape-memory metal recovered at [[roswell-incident-1947]]. This is Gerb's own stated inference, not an established finding.

## Claims (gerb/battelle)

- Gerb says Battelle was contracted by [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]] in 1949 (progress reports covering 1 September to 21 October 1949 on titanium alloys) and that the bulk of the work focused on nitinol, officially discovered in 1961 by William J. Buehler; he stresses the alloy's superelasticity and shape memory and the difficulty of making it (vacuum arc or induction remelting). *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 4:16-6:26)*
- He compares it to the Roswell material Jesse Marcel allegedly described as light metal that returned to shape, says Gen. Ramey had Marcel pose with balsa and foil, and says the debris was reportedly shipped to Wright-Patterson. He states: "I am directly inferring" that Roswell led to nitinol and the 1949 contract. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 6:26-7:31)*
- The work was an [[atomic-energy-commission]] research project; Gerb says it carried a "restricted" marking rather than classified, which he calls a private-sector need-to-know standard of the kind David Grusch has said private industry uses to avoid SAP-cleared access, and says the studies were not declassified until 2010. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 7:31-8:43)*
- The report's subsection on analytical methods for titanium alloys was written by a Battelle chemist, [[ej-center]]. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 8:46-9:23)*
