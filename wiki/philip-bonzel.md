Philip Bonzel is named by [[salvatore-pais]] as the USPTO primary patent examiner who reviewed his patent applications.

## Claims (jm/pais)

- Pais says Bonzel declared his patent material public domain/unclassified after a phone conversation, and worked with a Navy attorney (Mr. Marloot) across all five applications; three became patents (high-frequency gravitational-wave generator, high-energy electromagnetic generator, inertial-mass-reduction device), two did not (including the room-temperature superconductor). *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 20:07-20:48)*
- Pais says Bonzel was "in awe" and said the claimed electric/magnetic field strengths (~10^18 V/m) were "not possible," noting even neutron stars cannot produce such fields — yet allowed most of the applications regardless. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 21:08-21:25)*
- Pais says Bonzel did not consider the high-Q-factor link between the (granted) high-frequency gravitational-wave patent and the (rejected) room-temperature-superconductor patent, and argues that by his own enablement logic Bonzel should therefore have passed the superconductor patent too. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 23:03-23:27)*
