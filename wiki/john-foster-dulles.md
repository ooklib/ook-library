John Foster Dulles was a partner at [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]] and elder
brother of [[allen-dulles]]; he later served as US Secretary of State under President
Eisenhower (a role outside this video's scope, to be covered in later Eyes Wide Open episodes).

## Claims

- A partner at the Wall Street law firm Sullivan & Cromwell, whose client list included
  Nazi-adjacent firms such as [[ig-farben|IG Farben]] and Krupp AG. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 19:06-19:13)*
- Eyes Wide Open alleges he "arguably admired Nazis openly," was "even more of a Nazi sympathizer
  than his brother," insisted correspondence from Sullivan & Cromwell to their German clients be
  signed off "Heil Hitler," and publicly displayed admiration of Nazism until his death in the
  mid-1960s. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 25:11-25:31)*

- Set up a network of shell companies to shuffle [[bank-for-international-settlements|BIS]]-held
  Nazi client funds ([[ig-farben]], Merck KGaA) through complex transactions, allegedly to keep
  the funds out of reach of FDR's [[operation-safe-haven]] confiscation policy until the war's
  end; his brother [[allen-dulles]] destroyed the paper trail from Bern. Eyes Wide Open frames
  this as the earliest display of the brothers' "cunning efficiency" working as a team, foreshadowing
  their later roles as CIA director and Secretary of State under Eisenhower. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 7:32-8:55)*

- Sponsored [[alger-hiss]] to become president of the [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace]]
  in 1947; when Hiss was accused of espionage the following year, Foster's own connection to Hiss
  became a liability given the brothers' hopes to be installed as secretary of state and CIA
  director under a [[thomas-dewey]] presidential administration. [[richard-nixon]] reportedly
  consulted with Foster, who agreed the Hiss trial should proceed so long as his own association
  with Hiss went unrevealed. Hiss was ultimately convicted only of perjury, preserving the
  brothers' reputations, though Dewey's campaign was defeated by Truman regardless.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 16:29-19:57)*

- LAOS 2: led the American delegation at [[geneva-conference-1954]]; brother of CIA director [[allen-dulles]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 38:26-38:34)*
