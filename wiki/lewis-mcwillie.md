Lewis McWillie (ASR spellings "Mcquilly", "McQuilly", "Mcwilly", "McQuey"; normalization to McWillie is my inference, matching the HSCA testimony the host cites) was a Havana casino operator for [[santo-trafficante]] and a close friend of [[jack-ruby]], per [[eyes-wide-open]] in `ewa/dallas-4`.

## Claims

- Managed Trafficante's Tropicana Club (host: "a man I mentioned previously"); elsewhere in the same episode described as pit boss at the Sans Souci under [[norman-rothman]]. Flagged: the host names both clubs for his role. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 13:55-14:06, 22:16-22:30)*
- Per his House Select Committee on Assassinations testimony, he had known Jack Ruby since 1951, meeting at a Dallas restaurant; per Larry Hancock, Ruby called him his "idol". The host says this friendship shows Ruby's deep organized-crime connections, left unexplored by the Warren Commission. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 14:06-14:52)*
- Involved in gun trafficking to Cuba during and after the revolution, shipping guns for the mob to Castro; in 1959 reportedly with Ruby coordinated and personally participated in shipments from the southern US on behalf of Trafficante interests, with pilot [[edward-browder]]. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 14:44-15:00, 18:35-19:26)*
- Host: he and Ruby were low-level "foot soldiers" moving goods; the real intelligence links are one echelon higher (Rothman). *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 21:34-21:57)*

## Relationships

- Friend/associate of [[jack-ruby]]; worked for [[santo-trafficante]] under [[norman-rothman]].
- Part of the Cuba gun network alongside [[edward-browder]], [[barry-seal]] and [[david-ferrie]] (per host).
