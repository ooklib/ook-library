Willis Bird was a former OSS China colleague who ran an import-export business (World Commerce
Corporation, Bangkok) and acted as the CIA's main point man in Thailand during [[operation-paper]].

## Claims

- General agent for [[sea-supply-corporation]]; married the daughter of a Thai Air Force officer;
  organized the Naresuan Council, a parallel forum with top Thai generals (incl. [[phao-sriyanon]]) and
  Sherman Joost outside official diplomatic channels. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 33:13-34:09)*
- A Nov 1951 memo from William Turner to Robert Joyce says he shared top-secret memos with the Thai
  police chief and handed the police "a lot of military equipment." *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 34:28-35:02)*
- Per McCoy, [[william-donovan]] told him to contact the future CIA. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 44:56-45:14)*
