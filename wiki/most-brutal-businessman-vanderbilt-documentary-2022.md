MagnatesMedia video of 2022-05-06 (about 13 minutes), written and voiced by John Frazer, a conventional business-history mini-documentary on [[cornelius-vanderbilt]], "the original robber baron". Like the other tycoon sources in the wiki it has no UFO content and no hidden-control claims; it is recorded as power-structure background alongside the [[rockefeller-family]] and the [[house-of-morgan]]. The narrator frames Vanderbilt's rise as the "capitalist blueprint" (undercut, bankrupt or buy out rivals, reinvest everything) and compares it to Jeff Bezos and Amazon. ASR note: "Corenlius" and "barron" are transcript misspellings; the ShipStation sponsor read is ignored.
