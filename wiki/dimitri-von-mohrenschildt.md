Dimitri von Mohrenschildt (ASR "Demitri Von Moren Shield"; description spells "Dimitri Von Mohrenshildt") is the older brother of [[george-de-mohrenschildt]], described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as a longtime Western intelligence asset and the link between the de Mohrenschildt family and [[george-h-w-bush]].

## Claims

- Immigrated from Russia in the 1920s; enrolled at Yale (called a hotbed of spy recruitment), then taught at the Loomis school in Connecticut (ASR "Lumis"), where he was "believed" to have taught John D. Rockefeller III and his brother Winthrop. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 18:43-19:27)*
- Met his wife Betty Hooker, then married to fellow faculty member Roland Hooker; after they divorced she remarried Dimitri in the 1930s and he adopted her son Edward Gordon Hooker, who became George H. W. Bush's roommate at Andover. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 19:17-19:58)*
- Host: it is "pretty indisputable" he was a US intelligence asset (host's assertion); fluent in German and Russian; OSS in WWII; named as a co-founder of [[radio-free-europe]], funded through [[crusade-for-freedom]], whose Dallas chapter was hosted by [[neil-mallon]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 19:58-21:17)*

## Relationships

- Brother of [[george-de-mohrenschildt]]; via stepson, a link to Bush's Andover years; OSS veteran.
