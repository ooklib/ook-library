Colonel Joseph Caldwell King, head of the CIA's Western Hemisphere Division, is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as an obscure figure who wrote the first CIA policy proposal against Castro, dated December 11, 1959, calling for the "elimination" of Castro. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 8:23-8:45)*

## Claims

- West Point graduate (mid-1920s), worked for his father's mining company in Mexico, then Nelson Rockefeller's Office of the Coordinator of Inter-American Affairs in WWII (viewed as an intelligence front), and joined the CIA in 1947. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 8:45-9:17)* See [[nelson-rockefeller]].
- Host: some sources give him a role in the 1954 Guatemala coup, without specifics. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 9:22-9:35)* See [[operation-pbsuccess]].
- [[allen-dulles]] approved the memo but pencilled out "eliminate", replacing it with "removal"; host asks whether King was a fall guy for men who wanted assassination but could not say so. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 9:50-10:44)*
- Richard Bissell, in an internal interview, said King and not himself first advocated using Mafia elements. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 10:50-11:08)* See [[richard-bissell]].
- On January 8, 1960 CIA history says an anti-Castro branch was first discussed; on January 18 Dulles told Bissell to form WH/4; King assigned [[jacob-esterline]] as task force chief. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 11:16-11:37, 22:10-22:21)*
- The Call of Duty: Black Ops game includes a fictitious Intel item with King's name and authorization for Operation 40 (host). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 33:11-33:23)* See [[operation-40]].

## Relationships

- Superior of [[jacob-esterline]]; memo approved by [[allen-dulles]]; see [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]], [[fidel-castro]].
