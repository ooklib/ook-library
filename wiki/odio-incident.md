The Odio incident is the September 1963 visit of three men to the Dallas apartment of Sylvia Odio, daughter of a Cuban exile leader, one of whom later reportedly identified himself as "Leon Oswald". [[eyes-wide-open]] introduces it briefly in chapter 3 of [[road-to-dallas]] as "one of the key pieces of evidence breaking the lone nut narrative" and promises a fuller treatment later; this page holds only the chapter 3 summary.

## Claims

- Odio's [[warren-commission]] testimony described three men: two dark-complexioned men speaking broken English and one fluent English-speaking white man. Afterward a caller claiming to be the white man phoned and stressed that his name was "Leon". Her sister, also present, confirmed the white man introduced himself as "Leon Oswald"; Odio realized after the assassination that he was [[lee-harvey-oswald]]. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 12:07-13:20)*
- The Commission struggled to fit this into its lone-nut narrative. The evidence naming [[loran-hall]], Lawrence Howard and William Seymour (all [[interpen]] associates) as the visitors was provided by the FBI after Odio's testimony (per Larry Hancock's *Someone Would Have Talked*, as cited by the host) in a "somewhat dubious" report; none of the three remembered being there and all eventually denied involvement. Hall partially confessed, naming Seymour as "Leon Oswald", then retracted. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 13:20-14:09, 15:03-15:44)*
- Host: the FBI was "influencing or ... guiding" the Commission's handling of the incident. *([ewa/dallas-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-28 - Cactus Jack Canon Does Havana - The Road to Dallas Chapter 3.md) 15:03-15:11)*
