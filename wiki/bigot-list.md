A "bigot list" is a roster of individuals authorized (read in) to a special access program. In the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]], [[thomas-wilson]] was told he was "not on the bigot list" for the alleged UFO program even though he was deputy director of the [[defense-intelligence-agency]]. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 15:03-15:10)*

## Claims

- The [[watch-committee]] showed Wilson pages of the 1990-1993 list: civilian scientists, technicians, engineers and managers, plus some Pentagon, NSC and other-department names; no politicians or White House staff. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 15:33-15:57)*
- [[will-miller]], quoted in a Joe Murgia (ASR "Joe mgia") interview relayed by Gerb, says the term originates from the World War II British invasion of German-occupied territory and today is simply a list of SAP/USAP authorized individuals, varying by program and sensitivity. *([gerb/wilson-davis](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-09 - The Wilson Davis Memo and US Secret UFO Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 26:59-27:22)*
