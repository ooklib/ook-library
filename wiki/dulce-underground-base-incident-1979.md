[[phil-schneider]] claims that in late August 1979 he was part of a team working through [[los-alamos-national-laboratory]] at Dulce, New Mexico, when drilling of a shaft into an underground cavity led to a deadly encounter with aliens that left 66 people dead. This is a single uncorroborated first-person claim from the 1995 lecture ([[schneider-las-vegas-lecture-1995]]).

## Claims

- Four holes several thousand feet deep were drilled over a couple of days; one kept returning dirt, putrid odors and broken machine bits, lasers and boring equipment came up damaged, and a probe lowered down never came back. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 43:24)*
- Schneider says he descended in a protective suit with sensors, a rock hammer and a Walter PPK pistol, and found himself about 7 feet from a "7-foot-tall stinking alien" and shot it; he says he had been given "ET training" but most others had not, and that the government had officially answered "no" to whether aliens were in the area. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 44:49, 45:37)*
- Says 66 people died (Secret Service agents, government workers, geologists); he was burned, lost a lung, fingers and toenails, spent 400-plus days in radiation isolation and has a hole in his chest. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 43:24, 45:37)*
- Says only two other survivors exist, in nursing homes in Canada, which regards them as heroes and refuses US officials access; one cannot talk and the other will not. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 46:23)*
