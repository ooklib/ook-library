The Electrostatics and Surface Physics Laboratory is [[nasa]]'s only electrostatics laboratory, part of Swamp Works at [[kennedy-space-center]], led by [[charles-buhler]]. It is the institutional credential [[jesse-michels]] leans on in [[buhler-michels-interview-2026]]; Buhler stresses that the Exodus propulsion work is not done there and is not NASA-sanctioned.

## Claims

- Founded roughly 26 years earlier by Dr. Carlos (name garbled "Gier/Kay" in the transcript, probably Carlos Calle), after a physicist's summer at KSC. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 15:25-15:42)*
- Origin: 1960s accidents, including a spin-test-facility solid-rocket ignition that trapped 11 people and the Apollo fire, with about 14 NASA deaths attributed to electrostatics. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 15:42-16:17)*
- Work: dust mitigation, called one of the two greatest challenges for long-term lunar or Martian presence because dust limited Apollo crews to three EVAs; the Electrodynamic Dust Shield (EDS) uses embedded electrodes and was tested on the Moon about a year before the interview (thermal radiator, glass and camera EDS variants). *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 16:35-18:53)*
- No one pays NASA to study electrostatics generally; funding is case by case. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 16:42-17:01)*
