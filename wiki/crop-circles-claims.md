Large geometric structures pressed into fields, usually wheat, sometimes attributed to paranormal or extraterrestrial causes but widely considered hoaxes, per [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 2:16-2:44)*

## Claims

- Gerb says the craze began in 1991 when pranksters Doug Bower and Dave Chorley (ASR "Dave chle") took credit for the first wave using planks, rope and baseball caps; skeptics explain other techniques such as a rope-and-board method and a way of bending crops after rain found in Hungary in 1992. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 2:36-3:19)*
- Gerb considers them extremely likely human-made and not interesting. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 3:19-3:34)*
