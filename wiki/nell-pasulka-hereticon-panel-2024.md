## Nell's four "heresies"

Nell summarizes four heresies for the Hereticon audience: (1) non-disclosure of uncomfortable aspects of reality is a universal constant across cultures and time; (2) there is a hierarchy of being that includes non-human intelligence; (3) cosmism sits at the root of both the Russian and US space programs; (4) consciousness is a component not only of metaphysics but of physics itself, so that agnosticism is "not an optional worldview". *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 21:46-22:26, 36:44-37:33)*
