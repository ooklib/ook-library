Mount Weather is an underground continuity-of-government complex in Virginia, roughly 60 miles from Washington, with communications to the military and embassies. Cameron cites it as the alleged headquarters for UFO material.

## Claims

- Cameron says Kecksburg crash information was sent to "project Greengrass" at Mount Weather, and [[steven-greer]] witness Steven Lovekin (ASR "Lovkin") described Eisenhower doodling UFO material there. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:05:52-2:06:52)*
- Cameron calls it a Cheyenne-Mountain-type underground city where a new government could be installed. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 2:07:49-2:08:07)*
