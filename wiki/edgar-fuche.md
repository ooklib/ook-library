Former USAF Master Sergeant Edgar Fuche (the ASR transcripts render this variously as "Fuchs" and
"Fuché"), a whistleblower Gerb has covered before,
claims knowledge of the reverse-engineered [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b|TR-3B]], though he
could not provide material evidence for its existence. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
1:09:05–1:09:20)*

## Claims

- During 1998 disclosures, discussed the [[defense-advanced-research-center|Defense Advanced
  Research Center (DARC)]], allegedly stationed underground near Area 51/Groom Lake, working on
  UFO reverse engineering. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:09:20–1:09:35)*
- Stated DARC was "bolstered" in the mid-1980s with [[strategic-defense-initiative|SDI]]
  funding, and estimated up to 35% of SDI's budget was siphoned to UFO black programs.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:11:13–1:11:22)*
- Claimed the TR-3B's maiden operational flight (mid-1990s) was administered by the NRO, NSA,
  and CIA, built primarily by Lockheed, Boeing, Northrop, and Teledyne Ryan, funded by SDI
  money, using a reverse-engineered Magnetic Field Disruptor from
  [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]]/[[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence
  Livermore]]. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:11:31–1:11:46, 1:32:39–1:33:41)*

- Stated TR-3B prototyping began in the 1970s (first gravity-warping flight early 1970s),
  consisting of several 250 ft prototypes pre-1994 and a 600 ft operational model post-1994,
  piloted from the Naval Test Pilot School (Pax River) and Edwards AFB Test Pilot School; claimed
  the design was kept hyper-compartmentalized under primary contractors Lockheed, Boeing,
  Northrop, and Teledyne Ryan, managed by the NRO, NSA, and CIA, and employed electrochromatic-
  coating cloaking that reduced the craft's mass by 89%. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  1:50:47–1:53:52)*

- Accused [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed]] of operating as a prime contractor to the triangular alien
  reproduction vehicle TR-3B — a claim Gerb calls one of the most-discussed reasons for
  Lockheed's centrality in UFO legacy-program speculation. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 4:08–4:25)*

- In the Sandia video's opening/closing clips, states: "Sandia and Livermore Laboratories
  developed a reverse engineered MFD and I believe the government will go at any lengths to
  protect this technology" — the fullest on-camera statement of his claim that
  [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]] and [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence
  Livermore]] (not the airframe contractors) reverse-engineered the TR-3B's propulsion.
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 1:08–1:26, 1:53:44–1:54:03)*
