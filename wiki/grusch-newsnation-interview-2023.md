The first on-camera television interview of [[david-grush]] (NewsNation, June 2023, re-posted 2025-04-02), conducted by [[ross-coulthart]] and introduced by Elizabeth Vargas. Grusch, a former Air Force intelligence officer who co-led the UAP portfolio at the [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency]], claims the US government runs a decades-old crash retrieval program holding non-human craft, and that the [[uap-task-force]] was refused access to it. NewsNation says it confirmed his credentials but had not seen or verified the alleged proof. The 2025 upload adds an editor's note that in March 2025 Rep. [[eric-burlison]] hired Grusch to his staff. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 0:03-2:07)*

## Claims

- Grusch says the UAP Task Force "was refused access to a broad crash retrieval program" of "non-human origin technical vehicles", and that the US has "spacecraft from another species", "quite a number", some landed and some crashed. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 6:23-6:53)*
- Sources were current and former senior intelligence officers, many known to him for years, who named the program, gave oral testimony and provided documents; he interviewed people who did not know each other to rule out a cover for another program, and took about four years before filing his complaint. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 7:01-7:24,)*
- He prefers the term [[non-human-intelligence]] to "extraterrestrial", floating that the craft might come from higher-dimensional space collocated with ours (explicitly a theory). *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 7:42-8:13,)*
- He has never personally seen non-human beings or a craft, only "photos" and "reports"; he says recovered craft sometimes contain "dead pilots". *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 8:31,)*
- Materials were briefed to him as having engineered isotopic ratios and exotic heavy-element arrangements, "certainly not by humans". *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 12:52-13:40)*
- 1933 [[magenta-italy-crash-1933]]: a partially intact vehicle recovered in Italy, held by the fascist regime until 1944-45, with [[pope-pius-xii]] back-channelling its existence to the Americans, who "ended up scooping it". *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 14:59-15:58)*
- [[roswell-incident-1947]]: he declines to give details but calls the 1994 Air Force report a "total hack job" that conflated crash-test dummies and Mogul balloons. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 16:08-17:55)*
- [[malmstrom-minuteman-incident-1967]]: a craft hovering over a silo and shutting down ten ICBMs, read by Grusch as "preparatory probing" of US nuclear technology. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 18:27-19:07)*
- Program personnel were physically injured by radiological/biological hazards from the material. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 19:16-19:33)*
- On the Pentagon videos: the 2015 Roosevelt "Gimbal"-type footage is "not a jet" (Raytheon ATFLIR pixel saturation argument); the 2004 Tic Tac is "absolutely a technical vehicle... something we didn't make. Not of this world, most likely"; only three videos were declassified and more concerning ones exist; witnesses describe craft "the size of a football field". *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 20:44-23:52)*
- A private aerospace company is storing craft: the government is custodian and "hand receipts" material to cleared defense contractors under sole-source arrangements, which he calls unethical, without naming companies. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 24:03-24:46)*
- He says he told [[sean-kirkpatrick]] (whom he has known about 8 years) about a year earlier what he was uncovering and got no follow-up, contradicting Kirkpatrick's April 2023 testimony that [[aaro]] found no evidence of off-world technology. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 25:01-26:12)*
- He was told of "malevolent events" in which people were harmed or killed by non-human craft, of attempts to bring craft down, and of "un-American" things he will not repeat, including a strong suspicion that people were murdered to protect the secret; he saw "substantive evidence that white collar crime was committed". *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 28:02-28:47,)*
- Russia and China run their own retrieval programs in a "multi-decade cold war"; he cites [[us-ussr-nuclear-accident-agreement-1971]] (via Coulthart) as showing both powers knew of UAP. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 28:50-31:56)*
- Asked whether the US has made agreements with NHI, he answers only that he hopes national leadership will "get to the bottom of it"; his internal document refers to agreements that risk putting the future in jeopardy. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 30:23-31:03)*
- He alleges a "sophisticated disinformation campaign" that let some truth leak while stigmatizing the subject. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 14:29,)*
- His [[intelligence-community-inspector-general]] complaint was found "urgent and credible" and he offers to brief Defense Secretary Austin and DNI Haines. He wants NHI technology and science shared like nuclear physics and plans a scientific foundation. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 37:15-38:04,)*

## Coulthart's framing

- Coulthart concedes Grusch has no smoking-gun documents, photos or official confirmation and often answers "I cannot comment"; he stresses NewsNation verified only his identity and role, and that "credible" refers to the IG's finding about the complaint, not proof of the claims. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 33:20-33:46,)*
