Claims that an Air Force rapid-reaction unit in blue fatigues and blue berets responds to crashed UFOs. Kecksburg witnesses describe US Army with M1 rifles, USAF personnel in blue fatigues and berets, men in civilian clothes giving orders, and men in "Moon suits". [[uap-gerb]] draws the Blue Beret idea from [[leonard-stringfield]] and promises a future video. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 5:33-5:57)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:09:13-1:09:45)*

## Claims

- Stringfield, in "Crash Retrievals of the Third Kind", wrote of a rapid-reaction special forces unit called Blue Beret that could "operate secretly and effectively by using diversionary tactics to prevent public interference", housed at certain bases in constant readiness. He met a former member in Florida who knew a fellow member who took part in a recovery and referred to a "big head" from an alien craft. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 6:12-7:21)*
- Stringfield's status report on fatal encounters at Fort Maguire (Fort Dix/McGuire) records Jeff Morse, a self-described former Blue Beret, saying in 1985-1987 that a non-human entity was shot to death there. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 7:21-7:45)*
- Gerb notes that real Air Force blue berets date to the 1041st Security Police Squadron (1965-66, Operation Safeside, Vietnam), and that USAF personnel in blue fatigues and berets go back to the Strategic Air Command Elite Guard created under [[curtis-lemay]] in 1956, active until 1966 as air police at SAC headquarters and bases including Lockbourne AFB. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 7:45-8:49)*
- Gerb suggests Blue Beret security police could act as a rapid reaction unit for domestic or foreign crash retrieval. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:09:22-1:09:45)*
