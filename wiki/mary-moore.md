Mary Moore is a longtime activist and researcher who lives near the [[bohemian-grove]] property
and has been protesting and researching the club for nearly half a century — described by More
Perfect Union as predating even Alex Jones's public-access attempts to expose the club.

## Claims

- Received a Bohemian Club membership list from an anonymous source who knew she was an activist,
  and used it to research who profited from the network's activities, aiming to show the movement
  "all our issues are connected by who profits from them"
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 4:11, 4:21, 4:26, 4:35, 4:45)*.
- Interviewed at her home, described as archiving materials in cold conditions without heat
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 4:02, 4:11)*.
- Stated that Donald Trump is "not welcome at the Grove," that he tried to join and is not a
  member and not on the list *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 13:07, 13:16)*.
  **Unverified claim**, attributed to Moore's own research/sources, not independently confirmed by
  this wiki.

## Relationships

- Investigates [[bohemian-grove]]; her decades of local research and an earlier, independently
  obtained membership list prompted More Perfect Union's broader national-influence framing of the
  video.
