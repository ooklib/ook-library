Peter Levenda tells [[jesse-michels]] that a New Year's Eve seance in Maine (1952 to 1953) run around Andrija Puharich, with a medium from India, drew old-money families and later connects, through a chain of relatives, to the household that hosted [[lee-harvey-oswald]]. Levenda offers this as an example of coordination "above the heads" of the participants; the individual facts are historical claims he says he drew from documents, while the meaning is his speculation.

## Claims

- Nine people attended, described as Du Pont, Astor and Forbes-type "blue bloods", plus [[arthur-young]] (Bell helicopter inventor) and his wife Ruth Forbes Paine Young. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 14:05, 15:16, 15:54)*
- The medium solved mathematical equations Puharich posed and materialized threads for each sitter, which supposedly made them "brahmins" appointed by nine forces later identified as residents of a UFO in low earth orbit, the same source later said to contact Uri Geller. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 16:56, 17:51)*
- Ruth Forbes Paine Young's best friend was said to be the partner of [[allen-dulles]]. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 16:19, 16:42)*
- Her daughter-in-law [[ruth-paine]] housed Oswald and his wife Marina and got him the Texas School Book Depository job; correspondence after the assassination has Young telling Ruth not to discuss it. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 18:33, 18:46, 19:55)*
- In her Warren Commission testimony Paine began to mention visiting the Youngs in Pennsylvania and Dulles cut her off with an unrelated joke, and nobody followed up, according to Levenda. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 21:45, 22:20)*
- Levenda frames this as "how is it that a bunch of people having a seance in Maine in 1952 are 10 years later implicated in the assassination of the 20th century". *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 20:11, 20:25)*
- Michels reads the chain as a Council of Nine "guardian" network and Oswald as a timeline-shifting agent; that gloss is Michels's, not Levenda's. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 18:57, 19:21)*
