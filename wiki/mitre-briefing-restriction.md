Gerb's claim that within [[mitre-corporation]] a "briefing embargo" (which he dates both 2007 and, in the same passage, "from 2011 onward") stopped incumbents on legacy-program work from being briefed on program history, successes, breakthroughs, records and milestones, so newcomers may be unaware of the legacy and even that their programs involve non-human technology. He reckons the embargo was not isolated to MITRE but enacted in other agencies and possibly other FFRDCs, and ties it to the catastrophic loss of institutional knowledge. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 2:07:07-2:07:50)*

Gerb notes the date inconsistency in his own narration (2007 vs 2011); the timeline places it in the 2009-2012 cluster with the [[air-force-go-public-campaign]] and [[operation-kona-blue]]. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 2:07:07-2:07:28, 2:16:23-2:16:39)*
