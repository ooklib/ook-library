[[uap-gerb]]'s outreach to Marines connected with [[jonathan-weygandt]]'s 1997 [[operation-laser-strike]] deployment, and the single reply he received from one of them. It bears on [[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]].

## Claims (gerb/weygandt)

- Gerb says he identified Staff Sergeant Montra (ASR "Montgre/Montil"), Sergeant Allen and Sergeant Atkins as real USMC veterans who had long careers, and that of about ten Laser Strike-connected people contacted only one replied, which he keeps anonymous. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:27:52-1:28:35, 1:49:36-1:50:09)*
- The sergeant replied (quoted by Gerb) that the operation is still classified and that no incident with a downed aircraft happened during his deployment; he acknowledged a Lance Corporal Weygandt in his platoon, said the Marines had no Hawk battery in 1997, that Weygandt never deployed to Laser Strike, that he had a medical condition and was never in any clandestine operation. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:29:02-1:31:09)*
- Gerb answers that Weygandt never claimed a clandestine role, that Weygandt himself agrees Hawks were not Marine and blames Peruvian forces, that Weygandt's Laser Strike description was accurate (the only oddity being the Bolivian border), that MACG-28 did deploy, and that dismissing whistleblowers over health claims resembles attacks on [[david-grush]]; he compares it to the team leader who called [[michael-herrera]] crazy ([[nathan-herrera-team-leader]]). *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:31:09-1:33:15, 1:50:39-1:51:39)*
- Gerb notes Weygandt left in 1998 admitting drug use to get discharged while the other Marines enjoyed long careers, and that Weygandt says internal USMC efforts made him look unstable. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 43:05-43:30, 1:50:09-1:50:25)*
