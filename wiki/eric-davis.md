Dr. Eric Davis is the physicist who transcribed the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]],
recounting a 2002 conversation with Vice Admiral [[thomas-wilson|Thomas Wilson]], leaked from
the estate of the late astronaut [[edgar-mitchell|Edgar Mitchell]].
*([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 34:55–35:19)*

## Claims

- Wrote *Teleportation Physics Study* (2001–2003) for the [[air-force-research-laboratory|Air
  Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)]], under Air Force Material Command at Edwards AFB, which
  devotes a section to "psychokinetic teleportation." *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:36:39–1:37:44)*
- Authored multiple of the 38 leaked Defense Intelligence Reference Documents (DIRDs), covering
  traversable wormholes, stargates and negative energy, anti-gravity for aerospace applications,
  and extracting energy from the quantum vacuum. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:36:16–1:36:32)*
- Stated during a 2024 symposium that his AFRL work uncovered "irrefutable evidence of UFO
  legacy program operations." *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:36:56–1:37:11)*

- Told Gerb directly, in a personal conversation, that alien reproduction vehicles do not exist
  and that no UFO technology has ever been successfully exploited for human aerospace use — a
  claim Gerb disputes and, given Davis's OAP work under [[james-t-ryder|James Ryder]] and Starfire
  Project collaboration with Ryder and [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]], speculates could be a program-
  protection strategy rather than an outright statement of fact.
  *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 2:05:36–2:06:55, 2:09:57–2:10:25)*
- Acknowledged in a 2019 interview that Ryder and Lockheed sought him out for the OAP project via
  an informal trust-building/"read into a SCIF" process typical of legacy-program recruitment.
  *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 2:06:55–2:09:57)*
- Separately stated UFO material-exploitation programs were shut down in 1989 due to lack of
  progress — a claim Gerb also disputes. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md)
  2:05:36–2:05:55)*
- Recently said in an interview he'd want to "string [[glenn-gaffney|Glenn Gaffney]] up like
  Jesus" over Gaffney's alleged role blocking the [[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]] transfer —
  which Gerb considers excessive. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 2:57:46–2:58:03)*

- In a 3 March 2001 journal entry, Jacques Vallee records Davis relaying the story of the alleged
  [[zodiac-crash-retrieval-program|Zodiac]] crash-retrieval program to him, including a "10
  Velobind volume" housed at Wright-Patterson AFB with data from the Roswell crash and a special
  study conducted by [[trw|TRW]]. Gerb calls his trust in Davis "wafer thin" but is keen to see
  Davis expand on this. *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 48:35–49:19)*
- In a recent debate/conversation with Eric Weinstein on *American Alchemy*, Davis said one of
  his primary sources for legacy-topic information was "a woman at TRW" — Gerb speculates this
  could be the pseudonymous Zodiac source "Sedge Masters"/Mary Elizabeth Elliot.
  *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 3:21:25–3:21:54)*
