Prescott Bush (born 1895, Columbus, Ohio) is [[eyes-wide-open]]'s "Bush the Father" Chapter 1 anchor
figure: a [[skull-and-bones]] member, [[brown-brothers-harriman]] partner, later Republican
Senator from Connecticut (1952) and father of [[george-h-w-bush]]. The host, drawing on Russ Baker's
*Family of Secrets*, Joseph Trento's *Prelude to Terror* and Whitney Webb, argues he is the bridge
between Wall Street "Yankee" finance and the early CIA. All of this is the host's/authors' framing,
not established fact.

## Background and Wall Street career

- Born 1895 in an old Anglo-German northeastern family; his father Samuel Bush was a steel company
  president who in World War I served as chief of "ordnance, small arms and ammunition" of the War
  Industries Board. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 5:31-6:04)*
- Attended Yale from 1913 and joined [[skull-and-bones]]; served in an Army artillery unit at the tail
  end of WWI; graduated 1918. Both his son [[george-h-w-bush]] and grandson George W. were later
  inducted into Bones. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 6:35-7:57)*
- Bonesman lore (host: "allegedly") has him taking part in the raid on Geronimo's tomb at Fort Sill,
  Oklahoma to steal the skull that remains the order's relic; the host notes the authenticity of the
  skull in the "tomb" is in question but that the grave-digging story is "considered real by
  historians." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 7:11-7:47)*
- Host: regarded by some old Brahman families as a mediocre man who "married his way" in; in 1921 he
  married Dorothy Walker, daughter of banker George Herbert Walker, who shortly afterward became
  president of W. A. Harriman and Co. Bush was hired there by his father-in-law in 1926. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 8:03-8:55)*
- Classmate of Roland Harriman at Yale and Bones member "alongside" Roland and older brother
  [[averell-harriman]]; also a colleague at the bank of [[robert-lovett]]. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 8:48-9:11)* *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 9:35-9:57)*

## Nazi-era banking (Trento, via host)

- Per Trento, Bush personally managed one of [[brown-brothers-harriman]]'s most significant clients,
  Fritz Thyssen's steel interests (United Steel Works), helping Thyssen "squirrel away" war profits
  through a Dutch shell bank under New York's Union Banking Corporation, on whose board Bush sat from
  1934. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 11:35-12:21)*
- By 1942 he sat on the boards of Brown Brothers Harriman, CBS, [[pan-american-airways]],
  Prudential Insurance (ASR "credential"; my inference), United States Guarantee Corporation and
  [[dresser-industries]]. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 12:21-12:45)*
- In 1942 Union Banking Corp was investigated by the Roosevelt administration under the Trading with
  the Enemy Act "which did not lead to any substantial penalties." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 12:45-13:08)*
- Host inference: the Bush and [[allen-dulles]]/[[john-foster-dulles]] families each held the
  other's wartime secrets (Dulles brothers via [[ig-farben]]), "almost like a small mutual blackmail
  network." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 15:59-16:24)*

## Link to the early CIA (host/Trento claims)

- Bush likely met the Dulles brothers because [[sullivan-and-cromwell]] represented Brown Brothers
  Harriman; the host says the two firms still work together (a 2021 Sullivan and Cromwell release).
  *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 16:17-16:57)*
- After the OSS was abolished, Allen Dulles allegedly ran a private intelligence agency in
  postwar Manhattan, keeping OSS staff on payroll until the CIA's 1947 formation, based not at
  [[sullivan-and-cromwell]] but at 44 Wall Street, the office rented by Brown Brothers Harriman and
  Bush; per Trento, Bush acted as an "unofficial advisor for early CIA money laundering activities."
  *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 16:57-17:57)* See [[office-of-strategic-services]] and [[central-intelligence-agency]].
- Close friend of [[william-pawley]] (ASR "Paulie"), a [[pan-american-airways]] executive whom the host
  says later played a significant role in [[operation-pbsuccess]] and the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]].
  *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 18:04-18:43)*
- A letter found by Russ Baker: Prescott wrote that [[neil-mallon]] was "well known to Alan Dulles and
  has tried to be helpful to him in the CIA especially in the procurement of individuals to serve in
  that important agency"; the host reads this as Bush indirectly admitting his son was recruited to
  the CIA via Mallon. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 22:24-22:54)*

## Politics, Eisenhower and Nixon

- Lost a 1950 run, then became Senator from Connecticut in 1952. Longtime friend and golf partner of
  [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] (perhaps met while Bush headed the US Golf Association) and, with the
  Dulles brothers and the Rockefellers, instrumental in the [[draft-eisenhower-movement]]. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 23:09-23:42)*
- Host speculation (after Russ Baker): the eastern establishment, possibly Bush or Mallon, sponsored
  [[richard-nixon]]'s 1946 first run; Bush and Nixon were photographed in matching Panama hats.
  See [[richard-nixon]]. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 24:51-28:36)*
- Senate Armed Services Committee seat, then its oversight subcommittee covering the
  [[central-intelligence-agency]] ("Alan Dulles's fiefdom"); served mostly under chairman Richard
  Russell, later a [[warren-commission]] member. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 31:20-32:46)*
- Host: with Bush holding "the levers of power" over the agency, Dulles could "cash in on his
  retainer" (his recruitment of the younger Bush). *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 33:25-33:50)*
