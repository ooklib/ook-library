In late 1979, according to AFOSI's own website, the Office of Special Projects (PJ) introduced
"an innovative new approach to help protect acknowledged black programs" — inserting a small
cadre of [[federal-bureau-of-investigation|FBI]] personnel into Air Force special-access-program
efforts. Approximately eight FBI agents in Los Angeles and select personnel at FBI headquarters
in Washington, DC were briefed into many of the Air Force's most critical SAPs under this
"Guardian Angel" program. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
2:17:56–2:18:29)*

## Claims

- Guardian Angel is credited with protecting conventional SAPs, including the arrest of Thomas
  Patrick Cavanagh, a Northrop B-2 engineer who attempted to sell classified stealth technology.
  Gerb finds it notable that Guardian Angel-protected sites clustered near what he considers the
  two most critical UFO-legacy-program regions in the continental United States.
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 2:18:29–2:18:58)*
- Gerb ties Guardian Angel (or "a similar continuation program") to non-public firsthand
  legacy-program witnesses who reportedly faced FBI harassment between 2021 and the present,
  including one individual who worked directly within UFO RDT&E under the Air Force. Gerb
  connects this to a December 2025 claim by Jeff Nusinovich (on a UFO podcast) that the FBI has
  been actively infiltrating UFO-podcast circles. Gerb explicitly recommends the current
  administration investigate whether AFOSI/PJ is actively operating against whistleblowers under
  Guardian Angel or a successor program. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  2:16:47–2:19:34)*
