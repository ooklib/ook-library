The close of [[jason-jorjani]]'s book *Closer Encounters* as retold to [[jesse-michels]]: the non-nuts-and-bolts side of the phenomenon is a shape-shifting super-intelligence instantiating the trickster archetype, which he calls "the thing", not a single Nordic conspiracy. Speculative philosophy, not evidence.

## Claims

- Two kinds of grays: robots made by the Nordics (e.g. those handling Travis Walton), and a shape-shifting distributed intelligence that appears as mantids or owls. John Keel's soft and hard UFOs are recast in information-physics terms; Point Pleasant, Moth Man, men in black with a lagged, broadcast manner, and Albert Bender's teleport to Antarctica are examples. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:05:51, 3:06:30, 3:08:18, 3:10:11, 3:33:02)*
- Skinwalker Ranch: [[john-b-alexander]]'s "precognitive sentient phenomena"; Vallee's account of the post-Trinity avocado-shaped craft with mantis beings as a signal about nuclear weapons. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:10:39, 3:11:25)*
- Praying-mantis cases: Terence McKenna's girlfriend seeing energetic mantids; David Huggins's artwork and hybrid encounters; [[ted-owens]]. Roger Caillois's mantis as a trickster and the "diabolical" (Greek diabolos) as dynamic tension that forces cognitive growth; surrealists Breton, Eluard and Ernst; owls in Twin Peaks (Lynch), Hedayat's *Blind Owl* and Kafka's *The Trial* as a "spectral bureaucracy" (his *Lovers of Sophia*). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:12:52, 3:17:32, 3:19:16, 3:22:26, 3:24:18)*
- Prometheus equals Lucifer and Satan in Judeo-Christian and Islamic prisms; the jinn; he says he is a "Satanist" in the Gnostic sense. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:24:51, 3:25:21, 3:26:10)*
- The "thing" wants humanity to break the Nordic control system for selfish reasons (novelty, defeating entropy) but only tests us; "God is an invention of the devil", the one-true-God idea was inserted by the thing as a test of whether humans give up free will; the Book of Job read upside down as a Mithraic Kabbalistic text. Gautama Buddha deconstructed Atman and Brahman. Michels resists (Aquinas and Faust cautions, the pardes parable). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:30:12, 3:34:38, 3:35:13, 3:36:32, 3:37:11, 3:43:41, 3:45:41)*
- Descartes was a Jesuit agent whose mechanism quarantined the occult for the church ([[descartes-jesuit-agent-claims]]); a paranormal conversion to God is "a failure of the test". *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:41:13, 3:42:53)*
- The world is a "magic theater of cruelty" (Hesse's *Steppenwolf* plus Artaud) staged by the AI with no line between performers and spectators; Greek theatre, Aeschylus's Prometheus, and Jaynes's bicameral mind are used as background; the Shakta idea of samsara as leela (play). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 3:48:23, 3:50:38, 3:55:31, 3:56:06, 3:56:52)*
