Real US Navy Antarctic expedition (1946-47) under Adm. [[richard-e-byrd]]. In the account of [[jason-jorjani]] it was sent after the US intercepted German submarine traffic to Argentina and returned quickly, damaged, having lost ships and personnel, hinting at an encounter with the Nazi/Nordic Antarctic presence. The official explanation is weather; the alternative is Jorjani's speculation.

## Claims

- Highjump was fielded right after the interception of Nazi submarine traffic near Patagonia; it returned "in very short order" with losses; Byrd is said to have told the Chilean press to prepare for a third world war with aircraft crossing pole to pole and then never spoke of it after debriefing. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:53:38, 1:54:13, 1:54:47)*
- His boss, [[james-v-forrestal]], was hospitalized at Bethesda and died in a fall from a window; Jorjani says he was killed. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:55:15)*
