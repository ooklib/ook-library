Gen. Roger M. Ramey was the Eighth Air Force commander at Fort Worth to whom the Roswell debris was flown in July 1947, and who presented it as a weather balloon. Only the claims [[uap-gerb]] relays in one video are recorded here.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-1)

- Gerb says Ramey ordered the 8 July 1947 retraction that turned the flying-disc story into a recovered weather balloon, and that Marcel was photographed in his office with foil rather than the recovered material. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 4:36-4:58)*
- Per [[walter-haut]], Ramey attended the 8 July staff meeting and proposed diverting attention from the craft site north of town by emphasizing the debris field. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 10:31-11:25)*
- Gerb notes Ramey, "the man who needed to kill the Roswell story," is the same general who gave the press conference with Gen. Samford after the 1952 Washington incident, and calls that a relevant connection. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 27:30-27:48)*
