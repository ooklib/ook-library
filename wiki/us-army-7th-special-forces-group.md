Fort Bragg-based Army Special Forces group that supports SOUTHCOM in Central and South America. [[uap-gerb]] wagers it supplied the unmarked black-clad armed men who detained [[jonathan-weygandt]] at the alleged 1997 Peru crash ([[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]]); this is Gerb's inference.

## Claims (gerb/weygandt)

- Gerb describes the group as specializing in unconventional warfare, direct action, special reconnaissance, counterproliferation and security force assistance, with earlier counter-narcotics work in Operation Snow Cap and a role in the 1995 Peru-Ecuador border dispute. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 9:34-10:10, 1:02:04-1:02:13)*
- He argues that they were "recruited by DOE NEST" as the enforcement arm of a retrieval, given their steady SOUTHCOM presence in northern Peru, and that Weygandt's statement that the men arrived first, on the ground before the helicopters, fits local forces arriving by vehicle. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:07:35-1:08:14, 1:18:05-1:18:21, 1:46:28-1:47:04)*
- Alternatively Gerb suggests a dedicated JSOC or former JSOC retrieval team stationed in South America (for example Howard Air Force Base, Panama), and links [[jsoc]], the [[intelligence-support-activity]] and [[albert-stubblebine]] to legacy-program retrieval claims by [[chris-sharp]] and others. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:04:47-1:06:29, 1:54:56-1:55:13)*
