The London house of the [[rothschild-family]], founded by [[nathan-mayer-rothschild]]. In the documentary its postwar strategy is to keep government financing at the core, stay a private club evading scrutiny, and remain wholly family owned.

## Claims

- After World War II it rebuilt on government financing, on the belief that good relations with strong governments guarantee business. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 33:55, 34:02, 34:15)*
- It preserved its model as "a private club, financing elites while evading public scrutiny". *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 34:28)*
- Financing of [[robert-maxwell]]: he sought its help in the 1970s and by the 1980s his investments looked good. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 34:41, 35:43)*
- A feud pitted Lord Victor Rothschild and his son Jacob (who wanted modern corporate expansion) against cousin Sir Evelyn, who led the bank and insisted it stay wholly Rothschild owned even at short-term cost. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 35:52, 36:14, 36:28, 36:34)*
- After Maxwell's 1991 death and the pension-fund scandal, Evelyn took it as confirming the bank's need for discretion and privacy; the documentary says the family's involvement in Maxwell's demise "remains unclear" and that it blemished their reputation. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 37:06, 37:46, 37:53, 37:58)*
