François de Grossouvre (ASR: 'Kosov', 'De Grossov') was, per Eyes Wide Open, the most famous person directly linked to Gladio's French underground: leader of the [[rose-des-vents]] cell around Lyon, later a close friend and counselor of President [[francois-mitterrand]], and found dead in 1994. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 40:34-40:52)*

## Claims

- Owned a small sugar company in Lyon and founded the firm later licensed to make Coca-Cola in France; during the war he was briefly in a Vichy militia (the Service d'Ordre Légionnaire), switched to the Resistance in 1943 and later claimed he had been a resistance spy. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 40:26-41:09)*
- In a 1993 interview he said he was in contact with at most ten people in the network and that everyone below was unaware of his involvement. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 41:21-41:53)*
- Mitterrand employed his close friend and mentor as personal counselor after 1981; in the Iran-Contra era he was known for arms-trade work in Lebanon and Syria, and by 1985 worked for an arms trader instead of the government. Gladio was revealed in 1990 by [[giulio-andreotti]]; in 1994 he was found dead in his apartment, ruled a suicide though investigators found two bullet holes. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 46:55-47:47)*
