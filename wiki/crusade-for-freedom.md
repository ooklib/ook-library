The Crusade for Freedom was, per [[eyes-wide-open]], a nationwide grassroots fundraising campaign for [[radio-free-europe]], established originally by General [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] in 1950 before he was president. The host calls it "a blatant cash grab funneling donations from hardworking Americans directly into the coffers of a CIA front." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 20:37-21:01)*

## Claims

- Fundraisers were hosted across the US "from California to Maine"; the Dallas chapter was hosted by [[neil-mallon]], Bush's boss at [[dresser-industries]]. The host connects this to Mallon thereby funding [[dimitri-von-mohrenschildt]]'s Radio Free Europe. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 21:01-21:17)*

## Relationships

- Funded [[radio-free-europe]]; Dallas chapter run by [[neil-mallon]].
