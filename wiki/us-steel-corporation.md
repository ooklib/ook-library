Steel company formed in 1901 by [[j-pierpont-morgan]] by merging Carnegie Steel with Federal Steel and others.

## Claims

- In 1901 Carnegie, ready to retire and netting 40 million dollars a year, named 480 million dollars for Carnegie Steel and Morgan agreed on the spot, later saying he would have paid 100 million more. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 9:26, 9:44, 9:55)*
- Merged with Federal Steel and others, it became the first billion-dollar corporation (1.4 billion dollar market cap versus 9 billion for all US manufacturing) and gave Morgan control of about two-thirds of American steel. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 10:03, 10:11, 10:20)*
- The narrator adds that Morgan financed Edison's early electricity work, helped merge Edison Electric into General Electric and was influential in creating what became AT&T. ([mm/jp-morgan](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-06-24 - J.P. Morgan - The Man Who Owned America.md>) 10:31, 10:40)*

## Relationships

- Created by [[j-pierpont-morgan]] of the [[house-of-morgan]]; Andrew Carnegie is the seller. See [[most-jp-morgan-owned-america-documentary-2023]].

## From MagnatesMedia on Rockefeller (mm/rockefeller)

- In this account US Steel arose from Rockefeller's Mesabi ore: [[j-pierpont-morgan]] bought the mines from [[john-d-rockefeller-jr]] for $88.5 million in US Steel stock, then bought Carnegie's business, and Rockefeller's stock rose to over $200 million: see [[mesabi-iron-range-deal]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 38:19, 39:14, 39:24, 39:31)*
