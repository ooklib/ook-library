Robert Burton Willingham (died 2015) was a US Army Air Force, Air Force Reserve and Civil Air Patrol pilot who claimed to have seen a UFO crash and its wreckage near the Texas-Mexico border in 1955. He signed an affidavit in 1977. [[uap-gerb]] treats the account as fascinating in context but weak in credibility. All below are claims as relayed by Gerb.

## Claims (gerb/del-rio)

- Gerb's biography: WWII and Korea service, a claimed Purple Heart for leg and head wounds from a mortar shell, which Gerb says he could not verify. Kevin Randle doubts the service record, but the St. Louis records center could not supply it and a 12 July 1973 fire destroyed most records for personnel serving 1947 to 1963; Noe Torres says he has over 50 documents proving the service. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 3:13-4:23)*
- Willingham first went public in 1965, in a Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania weekly article asking Civil Air Patrol pilots about UFO encounters. Researchers found the clippings in [[nicap]]'s files 12 years later, and Todd Zechel tracked him down and had him sign an affidavit. Gerb's main source is [[noe-torres]] and Ruben Uriarte's 2008 book *The Other Roswell*, based on interviews before Willingham's death in 2015. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 4:44-5:37)*
- Inconsistencies flagged by Gerb: the article and affidavit say an F-94 and 1948, whereas the book and other sources say an F-86 Sabre and 1955, which Willingham blamed on a faulty memory from the Korea head injury. Gerb says this hurts his credibility. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 5:37-6:17)*
- Body claims: the affidavit and the book quote him saying "I never saw bodies," yet in a 24 July 2012 Jeff Rense interview he said he "snuck around" and saw three (the lieutenant said four) non-human bodies, unclothed and in pieces, with broomstick-like arms. Gerb says he will not accept a failing-memory excuse and calls this a massive red flag, having asked Torres for comment. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 16:18-19:24)* *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 27:38-28:26)*
- Gerb's contrast: Roswell and even [[kingman-arizona-crash-1953]] (with [[arthur-stansel]]'s single affidavit) are better documented because their affidavits do not change details. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 30:11-30:42)*
