A 21-minute YouTube video (2025-07-23) by Brofessor Stein running through 24 companies with alleged Nazi-era ties, with the disclaimer that it is not accusing the companies of current ties and that details may be oversimplified. It has no UFO content; it is recorded here as background for the wiki's Nazi-era and business-elite threads.
