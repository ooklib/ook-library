[[uap-gerb]] does not claim proof; he says a strong case exists for interconnected systems in the American Southwest and East, and cites the historical record of defense interest as feasibility evidence. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 59:15, 59:23, 1:19:29)*
