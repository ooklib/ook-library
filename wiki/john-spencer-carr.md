Retired University of South Florida mass-communications professor who in October 1974 went public with claimed inside information on Aztec and named his Air Force source to [[leonard-stringfield]] on 6 February 1982. [[uap-gerb]] says Carr got details wrong and may have confused Aztec with another case.

## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Carr said five sources, including a senior USAF retrieval official, told him the craft and bodies went to Edwards AFB and then Wright-Patterson; he wrongly gave a 12 February 1948 date, a 30 ft saucer and 12 bodies dead of decompression. Mike McClellan's 1975 article "The UFO crash of 1948 is a hoax" answered him. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 2:07:54-2:09:20)*
- Carr asked Stringfield to keep the source's name private; Stringfield was stunned by the name, and Carr's claims plus Steinman's later rekindled his interest. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 2:09:29-2:10:11)*
