Alfred Cox was an OSS agent in the China theater, Hong Kong CIA station chief, and the CIA's
liaison at [[civil-air-transport]]; Alfred McCoy calls him a "guerrilla expert."

## Claims

- His 1967 history paper for the DCI (declassified, heavily redacted, "whitewashed" per the host)
  is the primary source the host leans on; it cites NSC 342 and meetings between CAT officials and
  KMT leaders in Taiwan. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 19:27-21:15)*
- Personally close to [[claire-chennault]] and [[li-mi]]; co-ran [[sea-supply-corporation]] operations with
  [[willis-bird]] and Sherman Joost. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 20:51-21:01, 32:34-32:47)*

## Relationships

- Liaison to [[civil-air-transport]]; participant in [[operation-paper]].

## Additional claims (The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE, ewa/jmwave)


- Shackley's memoir names him as the [[jm-wave-station]] chief before Shackley (acting chief when Harvey visited in February 1962), fired by [[william-harvey]] after Cox was sick and Shackley wouldn't drive Harvey to his house. The host finds this "dubious" and speculates Cox was shuffled back to Southeast Asia, where his airline-logistics and paramilitary skills were in demand as [[civil-air-transport]] became [[air-america]] in 1960. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 8:31-8:42, 8:49-9:27, 13:03-13:57)*
- 1973 New York Times obituary (host): OSS officer in North Africa, Italy and France as part of the Jedburgh teams under Col. Aaron Bank, later first commander of the Green Berets; alongside him in early special forces units were "Lucen Kane" (ASR; probably [[lucien-conein]], my inference) and John "Singlob" (probably [[john-singlaub]]); ended OSS service in China training nationalist commandos, so probably in contact with or under [[paul-helliwell]]. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 10:11-11:36)* Flagged: the host places the early-50s special forces service "at the OSS," which is chronologically muddled in the ASR.
- Met [[claire-chennault]] in China; special assistant to Chennault in 1949 while he presided over CAT; by 1952 president of "the CIA charter" (ASR; probably CAT/its charter arm) until 1955. The host cites his agency history of the airline: he "guided both the covert operations side and the public commercial side of the airline for a number of years." *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 11:41-12:59)* Unlike the Hong Kong-station description above, this episode mentions only Miami and airline roles.
