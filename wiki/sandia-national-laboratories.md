Sandia National Laboratories is a DOE/NSA National Laboratory FFRDC, established 1949, that
Gerb has separately investigated (in other projects) regarding UFO legacy-program involvement.

## Claims

- President Truman reportedly asked Bell Labs'/AT&T's president to spearhead Sandia in 1949,
  modeling its operations after Bell Labs. *(2:05:56–2:06:18)*
- Cited among AEC National Labs allegedly designated as custodians of recovered nonhuman
  technical vehicles by 1948 classified executive orders. *(19:45–20:05)*
- Gerb references his own prior work exploring Sandia's role and highlighting senior legacy
  officials who joined its board after federal service. *(8:32–8:49)*
- Cited in connection with 1953's "Kingman" retrieval as a Southwest systems intake site.
  *(2:04:13–2:04:30)*
- Along with [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence Livermore]], allegedly developed
  the reverse-engineered "Magnetic Field Disruptor" powering the
  [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b|TR-3B]]. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:32:39–1:33:10)*
- Located near [[kirtland-air-force-base|Kirtland AFB]], NM; has extensive contracts with
  [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]], including a 2013 $228M IT-
  consolidation contract. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 10:51–10:58, 57:44–58:14)*
- Established [[tonopah-test-range|Tonopah Test Range]] in 1957 for the Atomic Energy
  Commission; from 1993 (Martin Marietta's acquisition of Sandia's management contract) to 2017,
  Sandia — and by extension [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]], via the Sandia Corporation
  subsidiary — administered this classified DOE weapons-testing site. Sandia's post-2017
  administrator, National Technology and Engineering Solutions of Sandia LLC, is a Honeywell
  International subsidiary. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 38:39–40:16,
  52:09–52:54, 54:19–54:27)*
- Prior to the Honeywell-subsidiary era, Sandia was administrated by the Sandia Corporation, a
  subsidiary of Lockheed Martin. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 39:28–39:47)*
