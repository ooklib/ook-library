The tenth and final meeting of the National Intelligence Authority (pre-statutory predecessor of the NSC), 26 June 1947, two days after Kenneth Arnold's sighting. In the minutes DCI [[roscoe-hillenkoetter]] says there is "an urgent and important problem": the [[central-intelligence-group]] lacks qualified collectors of scientific intelligence, and he is working with [[vannevar-bush]] on solutions. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 19:39-20:36)*

## Claims

- [[jed-davis]]: the two solutions were scientific attaches abroad (an idea of [[lloyd-berkner]], approved by the IAB two months later) and obtaining scientists and giving them a "course of intelligence", which Davis reads as consistent with [[donald-menzel]]'s double life and never appears in later records, as one would expect of a covert program. Davis interprets this as the start of a secret infrastructure already aware of NHI; the host agrees the documents themselves never say so. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 20:44-24:29)*
- The next day (27 June) [[james-v-forrestal]], David Lilienthal, [[george-c-marshall]] and Robert Patterson allegedly held an off-record White House meeting with [[harry-truman]], with another the following Monday, per Forrestal's calendars. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 34:59-35:28)*
- Coulthart's devil's-advocate answer: these could have been about communism; Davis says early on officials wondered whether Roswell was Russian. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 35:32-36:53)*
