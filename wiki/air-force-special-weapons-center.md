The Air Force Special Weapons Center (AFSWC) operated 1949–1976 out of
[[kirtland-air-force-base|Kirtland AFB]], New Mexico, specializing in atomic, nuclear, and other
unconventional weaponry, first as a major command and then as a subordinate of the Air Research
and Development Command (ARDC) and, from 1 April 1952, of Air Research and Development Command
directly, before ARDC's 1961 rebranding as Air Force Systems Command (AFSC). When AFSWC was
disestablished in 1976, its office of primary responsibility (OPR) passed to the Air Force
Weapons Lab (AFWL), which merged into the [[air-force-research-laboratory|Air Force Research
Lab (AFRL)]] in 1992. Gerb argues this makes AFSWC part of the modern
[[air-force-material-command|Air Force Material Command (AFMC)]] through two lineages
simultaneously: via AFRL, and via AFSC (which itself became half of AFMC in 1992).
*([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 3:02:02–3:07:02)*
