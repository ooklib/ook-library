Nelson Rockefeller, per [[eyes-wide-open]]'s War Conspiracy 2, was brought in by [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] as special assistant on foreign policy in 1954, then led the private [[special-studies-project]] after resigning in 1955. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 10:20-10:28, 26:34-27:12)*

## Claims

- Named as part of the Guatemala-era interest group (with lobbyists like [[thomas-corcoran]] and the [[john-foster-dulles|Dulles]] brothers) threatened by [[jacobo-arbenz]]'s nationalization program. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 10:20-10:53)*
- Had been "one of the main voices" for the massive military aid regime in Southeast Asia. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 26:49-26:58)*
- Resigned in 1955 "allegedly due to apparent sidelining by the CIA and National Security Council"; then headed the Special Studies Project, co-directed with [[henry-kissinger]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 26:58-27:19)*

## Relationships

- Member of [[rockefeller-family]]; led [[special-studies-project]]; served [[dwight-d-eisenhower]]; co-director with [[henry-kissinger]]. Brother of [[laurance-rockefeller]].

## Additional claims (Operation 40)

- His WWII Office of the Coordinator of Inter-American Affairs is described as an intelligence front placing spies across Latin America; [[joseph-caldwell-king]] worked there before joining the CIA. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 8:51-9:17)*
