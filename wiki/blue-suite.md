The Blue Suite was a room at the Plaza Hotel in New York City — per Eyes Wide Open, alleged by
[[susan-kaufman]] to be the site of secret homosexual sex parties in the mid-1950s attended by
her husband [[lewis-rosenstiel]], FBI Director [[j-edgar-hoover]], Cardinal [[francis-spellman]],
and [[roy-cohn]]. The episode frames the Blue Suite as a founding "blueprint" for later alleged
intelligence-linked sexual-blackmail operations, drawing an explicit parallel to the Jeffrey
Epstein case.

## Claims

- Susan Kaufman, ex-wife of bootlegger-turned-Schenley-Liquor-Corporation-founder
  [[lewis-rosenstiel]], described witnessing secret homosexual sex parties in the 1950s at Room
  "2:33" of the Plaza Hotel — dubbed the Blue Suite — between her husband and other "powerful
  deep political actors," including FBI Director [[j-edgar-hoover]], Cardinal
  [[francis-spellman]], and [[roy-cohn]] (McCarthy's assistant counsel), plus several
  unidentified young men. She outlined the allegations in a 1970 affidavit to the New York State
  Legislative Committee on Crime, sealed to this day, and later testified publicly in the 1990s
  from her home in France. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 0:00-1:32)*
- Eyes Wide Open frames the alleged Blue Suite events as "especially revealing" of a long-standing
  tradition of sexual blackmail as an instrumental deep-state tool, and argues what allegedly
  happened there in the mid-1950s "would serve as a blueprint" for later intelligence-affiliated
  blackmail operations such as the Jeffrey Epstein case. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 1:33-1:57)*
- In a fuller recounting later in the episode, Kaufman is described as brought to Room "3:22" of
  the Plaza in 1958, where she witnessed Hoover dressed in drag going by the name "Mary Hoover" —
  the same female alter-name independently reported by Allen Dulles's mistress Mary Bancroft — as
  well as Spellman and Cohn; the transcript's two room-number citations (233 and 322) are not
  reconciled by the host, likely an ASR/verbal inconsistency in the source material. Cohn is
  additionally alleged to have procured the young men present. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 39:33-41:06)*
- The host's closing analysis argues the significance of the Blue Suite (and similar circles) was
  less about active blackmail — using dirt to coerce a specific act — than "passive" cohesion:
  because each powerful member had compromising material on the others, mutual exposure bound the
  group together and discouraged anyone from breaking with the network's shared interest in
  protecting organized crime and the drug trade, a dynamic tied to the wider [[operation-gladio]]
  narrative. Eyes Wide Open explicitly flags this as unverifiable speculation ("allegedly," "we
  will never truly know"). *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 41:20-42:24)*
- The host lists later alleged parallels in the same "sexual blackmail as network glue" pattern:
  the Marc Dutroux case in 1990s Belgium (and the Brabant attacks), a Cold War CIA-linked
  "honeypot" brothel in Stockholm allegedly tied to Olof Palme's assassination, the Turkish
  Susurluk car-crash scandal, and the Jeffrey Epstein case. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 43:02-44:55)*
