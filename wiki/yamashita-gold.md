The alleged Japanese war loot hoard ("Golden Lily", plundered across Asia under Prince Chichibu) that, per Sterling and Peggy Seagrave's *Gold Warriors* as summarized by [[eyes-wide-open]], was recovered by the Americans after WWII and converted into a secret worldwide covert political-action fund. The host offers it as a speculative, admittedly hard-to-substantiate explanation of how [[edward-lansdale]] rose so fast in US intelligence and how early covert operations were funded. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 19:08-19:20, 20:07-20:33)*

## Claims

- Per the Seagraves: the loot was taken by Emperor Hirohito's brother Prince Chichibu under an organization called Golden Lily, so that elites escaping prosecution could dig it up and return to prominence. The host says he can only call the portion he covers "fairly plausible" and concedes that its whereabouts and use are hard to substantiate; he gives credit to the Seagraves and to Andrew Gough's Golden Lily blog post. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 19:44-20:57, 25:43-26:03)*
- Lansdale (then a G2 officer in the Philippines, with Filipino American interrogator "Santi", who tortured captives) interrogated the entourage of General Yamashita; in October 1945 Yamashita's driver (ASR "major kojim makashi") allegedly directed them to over a dozen stashes north of Manila. Lansdale briefed MacArthur in Tokyo, MacArthur briefed Truman, and the White House agreed the loot should be taken but kept secret. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 21:06-22:38)*
- Quoting the Seagraves: the gold, platinum, and barrels of gems was combined with Axis loot from Europe "to create a worldwide covert political action fund to fight communism". Citing former CIA officer Ray Cline (ASR "kleene"), it was allegedly stored in 176 bank accounts in 42 countries, "possibly by CIA bankers such as former OSS agent" [[paul-helliwell]]. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 22:38-23:14)*
- Host's speculation: it may have funded covert activity between the dissolution of the OSS (1945) and creation of the CIA (1947), and may explain narcotics-adjacent funding questions around Lansdale, though "it will likely never be known" whether it was spent. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 23:14-24:16)*
- Marcos episode: in the early 1970s Filipino locksmith Roger Roxas (ASR "roas"/"rous") allegedly found a gold Buddha and about $22 billion in bullion under a hospital used by Yamashita; thugs working for Ferdinand Marcos stole it; a 1990s Hawaii civil suit awarded a record $22 billion judgment, but Roxas was assassinated before it. The host calls the Imelda Marcos wealth claims suspect. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 24:16-25:36)*

## Additional claims (ewa/willoughby-2)

- Host, citing Sterling and Peggy Seagrave (*Gold Warriors*, ch. 8 notes): the Japanese hid looted gold across the Pacific as a slush fund under "Golden Lily", administered by Hirohito's younger brother Prince Chichibu. On Biak (summer 1944) [[jack-cannon]], a CIC officer, earned a Silver Star for recovering "more than 11 cases of enemy documents" from Japanese caves; the Seagraves speculate he found stowed gold and that the document story was cover. Host: "purely speculation" with no further information about New Guinea gold; no Philippine link asserted for this incident. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:15:10-1:16:24)*
