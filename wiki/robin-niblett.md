Sir Robin Niblett ran [[chatham-house]] as director for 15 years (until 2022). Green & Pleasant
presents his subsequent career as the video's clearest documented example of the British
foreign-policy "revolving door."

## Claims

- Ran Chatham House for 15 years, tripling its size to over 200 staff; knighted for services to
  international relations *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 11:34, 11:42)*.
- On leaving Chatham House in 2022, became senior adviser at Hakluyt and Company, a strategic
  advisory firm founded by former MI6 officers — which is itself a Chatham House corporate
  member, i.e. Niblett moved to a firm that pays membership fees back to the institute he used to
  run *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 11:46, 11:54, 12:01)*.
- Also joined CSIS (Center for Strategic and International Studies) in Washington, co-chaired a
  World Economic Forum council, and sat on the FCDO's (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development
  Office's) own expert group — a role he had held since 2019, meaning he was simultaneously
  advising the Foreign Office while running the think tank the Foreign Office was funding
  *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 12:09, 12:17)*.
- In September 2026 becomes Warden of New College, Oxford
  *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 12:25)*.
