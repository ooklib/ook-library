Elizabethan court philosopher and scryer, co-author with Edward Kelley of the Enochian system and the Sigillum Dei Aemeth. In the [[matthew-brown-michels-interview-2026]], [[matthew-brown]] says that Dee and Kelley channelled what they took to be non-human intelligences, that this produced an "Enochian" cryptographic language and that it "led directly to the colonization" of America and a "New Atlantis"; Michels adds Dame Frances Yates's Rosicrucian Enlightenment. Brown says the angel "Abborymon" in his [[brown-tweet-to-elon-musk-2025]] comes from Dee's system, pointing to the length of non-human intervention in human affairs.
