The House Un-American Activities Committee (HUAC) was the congressional body through which
[[richard-nixon]] built his anti-communist political brand in the late 1940s, most notably by
prosecuting the [[alger-hiss]] perjury case.

## Claims

- Junior California congressman [[richard-nixon]] built his political brand as an ardent
  anti-communist prosecutor on HUAC. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 9:31-9:42)*
- Nixon's HUAC hearings "did open the door" for Senator [[joseph-mccarthy]] and his assistant
  counsel [[roy-cohn]]'s subsequent anti-communist inquisitions across the federal government.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 22:15-22:30)*
- Following testimony from Time magazine editor [[whittaker-chambers]] alleging a 1930s Soviet
  courier ring involving State Department official [[alger-hiss]], Nixon brought Hiss to trial
  before HUAC; Hiss was ultimately convicted only of perjury, not espionage or treason.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 15:37-16:01, 19:38-19:51)*
