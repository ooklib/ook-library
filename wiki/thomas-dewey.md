Thomas Dewey, governor of New York and twice a Republican presidential candidate, both prosecuted
[[lucky-luciano]] into prison in 1936 and, a decade later as governor, approved his parole and
deportation — a reversal Eyes Wide Open flags as suspicious in light of Dewey's subsequent
investment in what became [[resorts-international]].

## Claims

- As New York's Republican presidential hopeful in 1948, [[allen-dulles]] and
  [[john-foster-dulles]] attached themselves to his doomed campaign against incumbent Harry
  Truman, hoping to be installed as CIA director and secretary of state respectively.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 18:51-19:24, 19:51-19:57)*
- FBN agent [[george-white]] approached then-governor Dewey in 1946 to extradite
  [[lucky-luciano]] to Italy as a reward for Luciano's wartime cooperation via
  [[operation-underworld]]. The host notes it was Dewey himself, as a state attorney, who had
  originally imprisoned Luciano in 1936, calling it "strange" that Dewey would later release the
  same prisoner who had made his career as an anti-organized-crime prosecutor.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 47:19-47:55)*
- Per Whitney Webb and other authors, Dewey invested in the 1950s in the obscure Mary Carter Paint
  Company, later renamed [[resorts-international]], which would come to be rumored a CIA/Mafia
  front company. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 48:02-48:21)*
