Dr. Italo Venturelli is an active Brazilian neurosurgeon, head of Regional Hospital's neurosurgery and, per [[james-fox]], head of three hospitals, who appears on camera in the new [[moment-of-contact-documentary]] saying he was in the room with the captured being at Regional Hospital in January 1996 and exchanged telepathic communication with it for three to four minutes. Fox calls it "the biggest most significant development in the history of the phenomenon" if true. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:49:54-1:50:31)*

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Ramsey shows an exclusive clip from Venturelli (in Portuguese) and notes that he changed his story: earlier he said he had only seen a video of the being; now he says he was in the room. The reason given is that he did not want to enter the limelight; after a near-fatal heart attack and losing his partner, Marcos Venius (Fox: died of a heart attack in 2018), he decided not to take it to the grave. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 3:26-4:36, 1:47:42-1:48:37)*
- Fox says the doctor was first contacted by Marco Leal's contact inside a hospital, admitted seeing a video on a Zoom call about a year earlier, and then, after Dr. Armando Fortunato phoned him, met the crew at the hospital's back gate at 10 a.m. in scrubs and sneaked them into the ICU, pointing to the bed where he says the being lay. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:44:04-1:49:16)*
- Venturelli says it was in this room that Marcos Venius performed the procedure and that he himself was present with the being. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:49:16-1:49:41)*
- Fox recounts hospital staff catching the crew filming and the crew being kicked out; the doctor then talked in a cafe. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:51:09-1:54:33)*
- Described appearance: the being looked like a 7-year-old, pale-skinned with reddish (not glowing red) eyes, tucked in like a patient; it was "serene and calm", in his words it "had control of the room", read his thoughts, was far more advanced than anything on Earth, "did not have a mean bone in its body", "an angel". Fox suggests a brown look might be dried excretion. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:51:41-1:53:59)*
- Quote Fox says stayed with him: "we have a lot to learn through the eyes of that being"; the doctor cried, kissed Fox's hands and said "you can believe it". *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 1:54:53-1:57:33)*
- Fox says Venturelli was fired after appearing in the trailer but rehired within a week because the hospital could not run without him; Fox made a video saying he and others would expose any military attempt to visit him. Ramsey says Venturelli was in fact fired before the film release and has since been reinstated. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 4:45-5:06, 2:08:12-2:10:26)*
- Fox says he has since interviewed him for over an hour, met his wife and that the doctor plays piano; he calls him a hero. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 2:03:54-2:04:34, 2:05:37-2:07:31)*
- Fox says Venturelli agreed to a public polygraph. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 33:14-33:31)*
