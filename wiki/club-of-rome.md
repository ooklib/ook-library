The Club of Rome is a global think tank. In this wiki it appears only through [[john-coleman]]'s claims, relayed in the [[committee-of-300-video-2026]], that it is an executive arm of the [[committee-of-300]].

## Claims

- Coleman says the committee told Aurelio Peccei (ASR "Orurelio Pekky") to form it to bring down US industry and agriculture, blaming US development for overpopulation. *([va/1994-system](<../raw/va/Video Advice - 2026-01-25 - In 1994, He Exposed Their Entire System… and Predicted 2026 Perfectly.md>)  10:23, 10:40)*
- He says it produced a "zero growth post-industrial plan" for the US, aiming to destroy the middle class by crushing its industries. *([va/1994-system](<../raw/va/Video Advice - 2026-01-25 - In 1994, He Exposed Their Entire System… and Predicted 2026 Perfectly.md>)  11:09, 12:45)*
- He quotes its chairman Alexander King at an anniversary saying Americans must get used to never again having full employment. *([va/1994-system](<../raw/va/Video Advice - 2026-01-25 - In 1994, He Exposed Their Entire System… and Predicted 2026 Perfectly.md>)  13:44)*
