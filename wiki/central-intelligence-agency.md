The Central Intelligence Agency, one of the US's "big five" intelligence agencies, appears
throughout both videos as a top-level sponsor/participant in the alleged legacy program
ecosystem — via its [[cia-directorate-of-science-and-technology|Directorate of Science and
Technology]], [[cia-office-of-global-access|Office of Global Access]], and involvement in
[[project-stargate|Project Stargate]]. Separately, Eyes Wide Open's "History of the CIA" series
traces the agency's institutional origins to the WWII-era [[office-of-strategic-services|Office
of Strategic Services]] — an unrelated topic thread within this wiki, connected only via this
shared organization page.

## Claims

- Alleged in the MITRE video to have been "introduced" as a legacy-program mission partner in
  2011, coinciding with MITRE's briefing restrictions. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:34:41–1:35:12)*
- [[john-m-deutch|John M. Deutch]] served as CIA director under President Clinton; before that,
  as director of central intelligence. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 9:13–9:20,
  38:04–38:11)*
- [[bobby-ray-inman|Bobby Ray Inman]] served as CIA deputy director.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 9:06–9:13)*
- Sponsored [[project-stargate]] alongside the DIA and Army/INSCOM.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:42:34–1:43:21)*
- Per Eyes Wide Open, the CIA's institutional roots trace to the WWII
  [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]], with [[roscoe-hillenkoetter]] portrayed as becoming its
  first director after Truman dismissed OSS director [[william-donovan]]; [[allen-dulles]], an
  OSS officer under Donovan, later becomes (per the series' later episodes) the CIA's first
  civilian director. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 15:24-15:52, 16:39-17:44)*
- Eyes Wide Open casts the CIA as "arguably the center of th[e] deep state network," alongside
  the Department of Defense, FBI, and other institutions the host argues share interests in
  capital, US empire, and anti-communism. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 10:54-11:49)*

- Per Eyes Wide Open's Part 3, the OSS/CIA's alleged narcotics-funded covert-action model
  ([[paul-helliwell]]'s [[operation-x]], run via [[civil-air-transport]]/[[air-america]] and the
  [[meyer-lansky]]/[[lucky-luciano]] mafia network) persisted even after Congress formally funded
  the CIA in 1947, since the agency's black budget still faced theoretical Treasury/congressional
  oversight risk. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 14:06-16:32)*

- Eyes Wide Open argues the CIA's directors and agents were "for the most of its history"
  pulled from Wall Street, elite law firms, and multinational corporations that benefited from
  colonial-era "open door" economics — citing [[allen-dulles]] and [[john-foster-dulles]]
  (Sullivan & Cromwell), [[george-h-w-bush]] (Zapata Oil), and [[william-casey]] (Hall Casey
  Dickler and Howley) — and that this interplay drove operations such as the 1954 overthrow of
  Guatemalan president [[jacobo-arbenz]] (lobbied by [[united-fruit-company]]) and the 1953
  overthrow of Iranian PM [[mohammad-mossadegh]] (at the [[anglo-iranian-oil-company]]'s and
  British government's request). *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 5:29-7:12)*
- Created as an independent civilian intelligence agency by the
  [[national-security-act-1947|National Security Act of 1947]], reformed out of the
  [[central-intelligence-group]]. Per Eyes Wide Open, the act's operative "11 words" —
  authorizing "other functions and duties related to intelligence affecting national security" —
  became the vague legal basis the host says covered all the agency's subsequent illegal coups,
  assassinations, drug smuggling, blackmail, and extortion, beginning with the 1947 Greek Civil
  War. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 23:57-25:47)*

- Its newly formed covert-operations arm, the [[office-of-policy-coordination]] (headed by
  [[frank-wisner]]), ran the CIA's first operation abroad in the
  [[italian-election-1948|1948 Italian election]], allying with the Vatican and Sicilian Mafia to
  defeat a feared Communist/Socialist super-majority — framed by Eyes Wide Open as arguably the
  first [[operation-gladio]] operation. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 18:17-20:15)*
- Per Part 5.3, the CIA funded [[american-committee-on-united-europe]], [[le-cercle]] (alleged) and the French [[rose-des-vents]] stay-behind, and consolidated front funding in the [[cia-international-organizations-division]] under [[thomas-braden]]. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 22:58-27:16)*
- LAOS 1 claim: from 1964 to 1971 the CIA was primarily in charge of the US campaign in [[laos]], its first full-on secret war, run without Congressional approval; see [[secret-war-in-laos]]. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 3:55-4:26)*

- LAOS 3: per [[eyes-wide-open]], the CIA's first Vietnam team was the [[saigon-military-mission]] (June 1954), which the host argues installed [[ngo-dinh-diem]] as a proxy; the CIA is also alleged to have used the [[michigan-state-university-vietnam-advisory-group]] as a police-training cover and to have rigged the 1955 presidential election with Lansdale-delivered cash. The host repeats his "one never stops working for the CIA" mantra. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 0:29-0:42, 14:29-15:11, 28:11-28:25, 58:37-58:48)*

- LAOS 4: host's typology of CIA personnel: Georgetown old boys (Dulles, Helms, Angleton), operators, then paramilitary "Laos cowboys" such as [[bill-lair]]; after the Bay of Pigs a pool of Cuban exiles. Claims the CIA moved from spycraft to warcraft and used proprietaries to fund itself. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 56:47-59:32, 55:13-55:19, 1:02:40-1:04:38)*
- LAOS 4: host claims the CIA and State Department engineered the 1958-1960 Lao coups ([[laos-coups-1958-1960]]) and launched [[operation-momentum]]. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 28:16-29:56, 1:11:47-1:12:02)*

- War Conspiracy 1: host: the CIA bought [[civil-air-transport]] in 1950 and through it began pushing rollback via [[operation-paper]]; it is characterized as depending on an "illicit budget" from narcotics for "unapproved operations" (see [[cia-drug-paradigm]]); the "old boys" welcomed Eisenhower in January 1953 as a sympathetic president ([[dwight-d-eisenhower]]). In the [[war-conspiracy]] frame, intelligence personnel are the key actors in the buildup to Vietnam. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 2:00-2:21, 43:54-44:35, 47:48-48:01, 48:46-49:09)*
