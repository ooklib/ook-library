[[chris-ramsey]] raises the Chronovisor while discussing [[project-looking-glass-s4]]: a machine said to let the viewer see and photograph past events, claimed by the Benedictine monk Father Pellegrino Ernetti (first speaking publicly around 1970) to have been built in the 1950s with a team including physicist Enrico Fermi and Wernher von Braun, and supposedly confiscated by the Vatican. Ramsey himself says "is this real? Is it not real? I don't know" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 44:45-45:38)*. [[bob-lazar]] had never heard of it and offers no view beyond saying all three S4 gravity-related projects would be paradigm shifting *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 44:45-45:57)*. This entry records the claim as told; the Fermi/von Braun involvement is unverified and appears only in Ramsey's paraphrase.
