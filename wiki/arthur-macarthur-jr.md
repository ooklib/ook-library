Lieutenant General Arthur MacArthur Jr., father of [[douglas-macarthur]], is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the origin of the MacArthur family's Far East creed: the "China market" as the key to US commercial supremacy, the Philippines as a bulwark against Japan, and a lasting resentment of the Eastern civilian elite. The host says this background is essential to understanding both MacArthur and his intelligence chief [[charles-willoughby]].

## Claims

- Union Army lieutenant from Milwaukee who led a charge at Missionary Ridge (Nov 1863), was wounded at Franklin, and was awarded the Medal of Honor only in 1890 (the host suggests reconstruction-era politics or Army resentment). Remained a captain for nearly 25 years despite being a West Point graduate; a "pervasive feeling of being an outcast". Host says he took part in the Indian Wars, which the host calls genocidal campaigns. His own father, Arthur MacArthur Sr., was a Scottish-born judge who was Wisconsin governor for four days in 1856. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:22-2:47, 3:05-3:33)*
- In 1883 he went to China as military attache (taking three-year-old Douglas). Host, citing Michael Schaller, says he wrote a policy paper for former President Grant, "Chinese Memorandum and Notes", arguing the China market was "the real stepping stone to supremacy throughout the commercial world" and that the US should seek new markets in the Far East, as a paternalistic "new Monroe doctrine in Asia" that would also keep Russia out of the Pacific. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 4:21-5:27, 6:28-6:41)*
- Host contrasts this with the opium-based "China trade" of firms like Russell and Co. and Jardine Matheson, saying MacArthur envisioned more than the US acting as "China's dope dealer". *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 5:52-6:28)*
- 1899: promoted brigadier general and sent to Manila to lead US forces in the [[philippine-american-war]]; military governor for a little over a year until [[william-howard-taft]]'s Philippine Commission (June 1900) began the transition to civilian rule. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 6:56-8:39)*
- Host calls his counterinsurgency innovative: early psychological operations and a "fiesta" outreach to Filipino leaders; the host says he espoused ideas like those [[edward-lansdale]] would use four decades later against the Hukbalahap ([[hukbalahap]]). Taft acknowledged the fiestas' success but saw the Army as "the ultimate evil" to Filipinos. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 14:01-14:46, 15:47-16:14)*
- Elevated Captain (later General) John J. Pershing's career, personally introducing him to Douglas in 1903 -- a decade and a half before they served together in WWI (see [[pancho-villa-expedition]] for the Pershing lineage in Willoughby's career). *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 15:00-15:28)*
- Taft, with mentor [[elihu-root]], had him recalled in July 1901 (replaced by Gen. Adna Chaffee). Host says he resented Taft's election as president in 1908 and retired in 1909 as lieutenant general; he died three years later. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 17:34-17:47, 19:14-19:34)*
- Wrote to Douglas at West Point about the Philippines' strategic importance against Japan; per Schaller: a strong colony "would block Japan, secure vital economic interests, and enhance America's stature as a world power". *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 22:32-23:05)*
