Alleged June 1953 UFO crash retrieval near Garrison, Utah, less than a month after and about 200 miles north of the [[kingman-arizona-crash-1953]]. Rated a moderate-confidence case in [[ryan-s-wood]]'s book *MAJIC Eyes Only* and investigated by [[steve-jones]]. Gerb ultimately calls it "a dead end". *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 11:47, 11:55, 12:05, 14:03, 1:09:00)*)*

## Claims

- In 1993 the Phoenix Project group showed Jones 10-15 large aerial photos dated June 1953 of a valley with a triangular ground feature next to the Desert Range Experimental Station (DRES, US Forest Service), plus a 1979-80 DRES logbook listing USAF/Army/contractor visitors; they believed DRES fronted a research facility studying a buried craft. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 12:12, 12:20, 12:29, 12:37, 12:52, 13:01, 13:08)*)*
- Local resident Bart Wright claimed to have seen a blue light crash near the valley as a child; a geologist, Lamont Sorenson, hired in 1999 found no underground anomalies but thought the formation artificially dug. *(*([gerb/dugway](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-02-20 - Dugway Proving Ground - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 13:17, 13:24, 13:41, 13:49, 13:56)*)*
