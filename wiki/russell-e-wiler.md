Russell E. Wiler, a member of the Defense Intelligence Senior Executive Service (DISES), served
36 years in the Army before retiring in 2009, then served as director of
[[saf-aa|SAF/AAH ("sensitive activities")]] within the Office of the Administrative Assistant to
the Secretary of the Air Force from 2009 to 2024. From 2024 to 2025, following the 2024
Department of the Air Force "reoptimization" that morphed SAF/AAH into Air Force Competitive
Activities (SAF/OC), Wiler served as director of plans and effects within SAF/OCX — a
continuation of his former role. A now-deleted 2025 job posting for Wiler's SAF/OCX position was
listed as requiring tier-2 SES/DISES, confirming the position (and thus Wiler) held SES tier-2
status. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 1:20:41–1:23:15)*
