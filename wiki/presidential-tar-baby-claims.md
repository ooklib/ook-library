Cameron argues presidents deliberately stay away from the UFO subject (Bill Clinton likened it to the "tar baby"), that briefings are kept to what they need, and that Trump entered a box by publicly commenting on classified material. Cameron predicts a scripted Trump statement, and says the real coverup is the absence of answers.
