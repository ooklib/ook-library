Witness who testified under oath to observing a 100 ft black triangle above Langley Air Force
Base in 2012, seemingly originating from a nearby NASA hangar. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
1:54:39–1:56:04)*

## Claims

- Described a white light that stopped ~100 ft in the air, then flew across the base and manifested
  into a black metallic-flake equilateral triangle "one to two stories thick" with 90° edges,
  four lights (one per corner, larger center light), and a lava/plasma-like flowing surface over
  the metallic paint. His phone froze and overheated; after 2–3 minutes the craft's center light
  flashed and it shot away silently, leaving static electricity and an ozone-like smell.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:54:53–1:57:22)*
- Said the triangle "appeared around the light," unsure if this was active camouflage. Gerb
  interprets this as an electro-optic cloaking system layered over a human-built triangular
  airframe (possibly [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b|TR-3B]] or similar), reverse-engineered
  from nonhuman propulsion/cloaking principles. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  1:57:22–1:58:56)*
- When asked whether the triangle was US government technology, said he "did once upon a time,"
  but now would only answer "in a SCIF," directing questioners to "Arrow" (AARO) for the answer;
  denied it was a foreign government craft. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:58:01–1:58:31)*

  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:33:01–2:33:21)*
