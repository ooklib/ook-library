Jason Samosa is a UK-based UFO YouTuber (and former Christian preacher-in-training and call-centre worker) who was the guest on [[jesse-michels]]'s American Alchemy episode "The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event" (2026-07-14). Michels describes him as a strong explainer of complicated ufology threads and historical deep dives; he says he is going full-time on the channel. Nearly all substantive claims on this page's source are Samosa's own speculation or his reading of other authors, and the wiki records them as attributed claims.

## Claims and positions

- Considers the extraterrestrial hypothesis an inadequate fit for the data and leans on [[jacques-vallee]]: the phenomenon's effect on human belief and mythology may be the point, possibly via an AI-like control system, and everything encountered should not be taken at face value because it appears "very deceptive". *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 4:46, 5:52, 8:35)*
- Says he became a Christian at 19, trained as a preacher, then lost the faith after three years of theology and a call-centre job; he now warns against locking onto the Bible as an epistemological foundation when interpreting UFOs, citing viewer reactions to his [[collins-elite]] video. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 25:25, 15:47)*
- Says quote-unquote disclosure is a personal process, not a line in the sand, and that even full disclosure might leave people deeply unsatisfied. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 31:02, 33:08)*
- Reports that the UFO subreddit grew from about 70,000 members in 2017 to about 4 million in 2026. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 30:09)*
- Traces a chain from the 1944 [[maison-rouge-meeting-1944]] to the [[world-commerce-corporation]], the Bahamas, [[resorts-international]] and its subsidiary [[international-intelligence-inc]], and argues this network fits the [[maynard-consortium]] of [[tom-delonge]]'s novel; he says he is mid-investigation and flags parts as "could". *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:18:05, 1:24:10, 1:40:22)*
- Argues the Vatican, [[knights-of-malta]] and Catholic networks hold a seat at "the board of directors of the UFO issue" via intelligence posts such as [[air-force-office-of-special-investigations]], while stressing these networks are not one monolith. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 2:17:48, 2:27:02, 2:29:40)*
- Argues the secrecy side can handle prolific researchers such as UAP Gerb by cultivating them as sources and flooding the community with disinformation, and by manufacturing infighting; see [[manufactured-division-claims]]. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:53:02)*
- Believes a secret space program is plausible and that Corey Goode/Gaia-style claims discredit it. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:55:14)*
- Expects [[donald-trump]] is "not thinking deeply about UFOs" despite the Resorts International link. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 2:30:02)*
