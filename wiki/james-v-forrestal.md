James V. Forrestal, Secretary of the Navy (and later first US Secretary of Defense), co-founded
the [[armed-forces-special-weapons-project|Armed Forces Special Weapons Project]] with Secretary
of War Robert P. Patterson on 29 January 1947. He is named as an alleged original
[[majestic-12|Majestic 12]] member and died in 1949 after allegedly falling from a naval hospital
window — a death Gerb notes in passing as suspicious. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md)
18:25–18:42, 35:46–35:54)*

## Claims

- Per J. Andrew Kissner's account, Forrestal (alongside [[vannevar-bush]]) was authorized by a 24
  September 1947 memorandum from President Truman to proceed with Operation Majestic 12 — a
  meeting the Eisenhower Briefing Document (the first alleged MJ-12 leak) claims really occurred
  that day, per Truman's own appointment records. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md)
  35:46–36:19)*
- The Eisenhower Briefing Document states Gen. Walter B. Smith was appointed Forrestal's MJ-12
  replacement on 1 August 1950 — a date on which Truman's actual daily appointments show an
  off-record meeting with Smith, which Gerb cites as a suspicious parallel.
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 36:19–36:50)*
- Allegedly threw himself out of a naval hospital window on 22 May 1949. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 36:19–36:26)*
