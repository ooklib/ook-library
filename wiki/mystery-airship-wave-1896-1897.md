[[uap-gerb]] describes the mystery airship craze of 1896 to 1897 as thousands of reports of airship-style craft, some with human-looking crews. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 16:22-16:46)*

## Claims

- Gerb notes the contemporary belief in a secret inventor and that prototype airships existed (for example Solomon Andrews' 1863 Aereon), though capabilities were limited. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 16:46-17:35)*
- He says most sightings were explained as hoaxes or misidentified celestial phenomena, mentions the esoteric idea that the phenomenon presents itself in period technology, and leans toward a mixture of hoaxes and inventors' airship experiments. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 17:35-18:27)*
