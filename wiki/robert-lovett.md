Robert Lovett (Robert Lovett Jr. in the host's telling; ASR "LoveIt", "lit"; normalization is my
inference) was a [[brown-brothers-harriman]] partner and future Truman Defense Secretary,
recruited by [[eyes-wide-open]] as a Yankee-Texan bridge figure.

## Claims

- Texas family that connected to the Harriman family through his father Robert S. Lovett Sr., president
  of the railroads owned by the Harriman brothers' father; host: nepotism likely got Jr. his 1930s job
  at Harriman and Co. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 9:50-10:29)*
- Host's own theory: Lovett may have suggested to [[prescott-bush]] that George H. W. Bush go to
  Odessa, Texas after Yale (1948), the "clearest link" between Prescott and Texas. Speculation. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 10:29-11:01)*
- Rewarded under Eisenhower with a seat on the first [[presidents-intelligence-advisory-board]] (1956),
  alongside [[joseph-kennedy]]. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 28:58-30:10)*

## Relationships

- Partner at [[brown-brothers-harriman]]; PFIAB member; colleague of [[prescott-bush]].
