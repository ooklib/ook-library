Researcher and author of three books on underground bases, which [[uap-gerb]] calls "masterpieces" and cites throughout his DUMB video (ASR renders the name "Solder"). Gerb says Sauder's work is fact-based, built on FOIA, declassified and public records, plus occasional witness testimony, and that he plans a joint "part 2" project with him. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:54, 2:03, 2:10, 1:29:38)*

## Claims attributed to Sauder's research

- Obtained FOIA documents on two underground facilities beneath the Manzano mountains at [[kirtland-air-force-base]] (2001 book), confirming the rumored installation though the papers do not mention UFOs. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 31:59, 32:06)*
- DOE repeatedly denied underground facilities at [[los-alamos-national-laboratory]] before supplying a blurred article on an underground lab; a businessman told him 75% of Los Alamos is underground. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 34:40, 34:50, 35:20)*
- A Navy contact who served at [[china-lake]] told him the facility extends 1 mile deep and hosts "weapons more powerful than nuclear weapons". *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 21:47, 21:57)*
- A FOIA document estimated 7,800-8,700 personnel and about $17 billion over ten years to build and run a hundreds-of-miles deep tunnel system; his insiders say Bureau of Reclamation tunnels could double as cover for clandestine ones. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 59:32, 59:41, 59:56, 1:09:22, 1:09:38)*
- Heard of underground rail to Area 51 from Mercury, Nevada in the 1980s, and knows [[bill-hamilton]]'s informant network. *([gerb/dumbs](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-03-17 - Deep Underground Military Bases (D.U.M.Bs.) - UFO Legacy Programs.md>) 1:14:03, 1:14:10, 1:30:18)*
