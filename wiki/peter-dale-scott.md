Peter Dale Scott is a researcher credited by Eyes Wide Open as the originator of the "deep
politics"/[[deep-state|deep state]] theoretical framework that structures this Eyes Wide Open
series on the history of the CIA.

## Claims

- Developed the theory of deep states and deep politics, described by Eyes Wide Open as
  positing that behind the US's elected government (House, Senate, presidency) stands a largely
  unelected, unaccountable, often reactionary wing of government acting as guardian of elite
  interests and corporate profits — "the deep state." *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 8:23-8:59)*
- Cited via his book *Deep Politics and the Death of JFK*, listed in the video's bibliography as
  a key background source for the series' framing.

## Relationships

- Originated the [[deep-state]] concept used throughout this Eyes Wide Open series.
