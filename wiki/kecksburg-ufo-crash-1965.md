## Claims (gerb/kecksburg)

- Gerb's full treatment is [[gerb-kecksburg-video]]. On the evening of 9 December 1965 thousands across Michigan, Indiana, Ohio, West Virginia, Pennsylvania, New York and Canada saw a fireball with a smoke trail lasting about 20 minutes; pieces appeared to fall over Lapeer, Michigan and Elyria, Ohio (ASR "Leir", "Alyria"), later studied in the Blue Book file. Clark McClelland and Dr. Paul Annear (transcribed "anir") are cited for calculations that it changed course about 25 degrees over Ohio, was at 40-60 miles altitude over Michigan and moved far more slowly than a meteor. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:36-2:57)*
- Around 4:45 p.m. it descended near Greensburg and Latrobe, turned again near Loyalhanna and tracked northeast to Kecksburg. Blue Book sent a three-man team from the 662nd Radar Squadron at Oakdale near Pittsburgh (Aerospace Defense Command), presumably on Major Hector Quintanilla's orders; the 33-page file is filed under Acme, Pennsylvania and concluded meteor, finding no materials. Gerb notes Quintanilla testified on 5 April 1966 that Blue Book had no unexplained radar cases, which [[j-allen-hynek]] contradicted with three cases. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 2:57-4:36)*
- Townspeople and the volunteer fire department say military forces arrived within an hour, including Army soldiers with M1 rifles, USAF in blue fatigues and berets ([[blue-beret-claims]]), civilians giving orders and men in "Moon suits", contradicting the official three-man claim, which Gerb says Walker later contradicts. Local press (Pittsburgh Press, 10 December) also contradicts the denial. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 4:36-6:12)*
- WHJB's [[john-murphy-whjb]] was told "get your information from the Army" and his documentary was allegedly censored. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 9:06-13:12)*
- Witness accounts: [[jim-romansky]], [[bill-bulebush]], Jerry Betters (roadblock, flatbed with covered acorn, notarized statement), David Newhouse (M1 rifle pointed at him), Bill Weaver (Moon-suited men with a box), Mike Slater (an Army officer told him and a friend to send crash seekers in the wrong direction). Object: bell or acorn, 10-12 ft tall, 8-10 ft wide, dull brass or bronze, seamless, no visible propulsion, landing gear or windows, hieroglyphic-like writing around a collar or bumper. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 13:31-27:22)*
- Blue Book received fragmentary material that fell from the object, sent to Wright-Patterson and labeled chaff, which Gerb calls inconsistent with a meteor. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 27:41-28:16)*
- Rebuttal of the Soviet capsule theory: Cosmos 96 decayed and landed in Canada about 13 hours earlier per a 1965 NASA release; Stan Gordon's FOIA documents put its reentry at about 3:18 a.m. EST; James Oberg still argues a Soviet Venus probe capsule recovered for its heat shield; assistant fire chief Jim Mayer (transcribed "Mae") thought a Gemini or Orion capsule; NASA's Nicholas Johnson told [[leslie-kean]] it was impossible and no man-made object landed that day, also ruling out Corona. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 28:16-32:24)*
- The NASA files: Kean's lawsuit led to a 2007 settlement and, by 2009, 20 unrelated Moon Dust pages; NASA said the files sent to the National Archives had been marked lost since 1987. Gerb links this to [[project-moon-dust]] and notes the retrieval-oriented programs. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 32:24-33:34)*
- Gerb compares the bell shape to Blue Book sightings (Quincy, Louisiana 1952; Massachusetts 1956; New Jersey 1957 explained as weather balloon; Manhattan blackout, November 1965; Bunkie, Louisiana 1965), to the infographics witness ([[special-forces-witness-infographics]], who says crashes are mostly eggs and bells), and to a deleted 2018 Reddit post by an anonymous claimed NHI-program worker who describes a stored craft shaped like a dreidel without its handle, custom molded, with a missing external piece. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 33:34-38:13)*
