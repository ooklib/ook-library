Arthur Vandenberg was a Michigan Republican senator, an isolationist and New Deal critic in the 1930s-40s who became "a leading internationalist after World War II" (host). [[eyes-wide-open]] uses him twice: as the source of the 1930s remark that Congress wanted "not to free the Philippines from the United States, but to free the United States from the Philippines" (reflecting the cost of maintaining the colony), and as [[charles-willoughby]]'s first contact in Washington in summer 1943.

## Claims

- Host: the nephew of future Air Force general [[hoyt-vandenberg]], "the second director of CIA from 1947 to 48". *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:08:39-1:08:52)*
- Willoughby convinced him that MacArthur was the man for the Oval Office; he founded a "MacArthur for President" committee and published "Why I Am for MacArthur" in Collier's. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:08:22-1:09:13)*
- The 1944 Republican convention in Chicago went to Thomas Dewey ([[thomas-dewey]]) with 1,056 votes; MacArthur got one. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:10:17-1:10:37)*

## Relationships

- Uncle of [[hoyt-vandenberg]] (per host); ally of [[charles-willoughby]] in the 1944 MacArthur effort.
