US Army aviation battalion in Joint Task Force Bravo (SOUTHCOM), with CH-47 Chinooks based in Panama and Honduras during the 1990s. [[uap-gerb]] proposes its CH-47s were the helicopters that brought the DOE team to the alleged 1997 Peru crash site of [[jonathan-weygandt]] ([[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]]).

## Claims (gerb/weygandt)

- Gerb says provable CH-47s under this unit in SOUTHCOM's area make it more likely than the [[160th-special-operations-aviation-regiment]] Night Stalkers, who are based at Fort Campbell and could not have flown Chinooks to Peru in about nine hours (though elements might have been forward-stationed). *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:01:21-1:04:14)*
- JTF Bravo worked with the [[us-army-7th-special-forces-group]] in the 1995 Peru-Ecuador border dispute; Gerb also notes US Army South supported counterdrug operations with Black Hawks until 1 November 1997. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:01:13-1:02:13)*
