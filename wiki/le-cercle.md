Le Cercle ('the Circle', later Cercle Pinay; rendered by ASR as 'Le Salk'/'Laselk') is, per Eyes Wide Open, a secretive elite forum founded in 1952-53 by French PM [[antoine-pinay]] and West German Chancellor [[konrad-adenauer]] for Franco-German cooperation, that allegedly drifted into a CIA-funded, right-wing private-intelligence outfit. Eyes Wide Open stresses that little concrete information exists about its early years. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 29:38-30:52)*

## Claims

- Founded 1952 or 1953 by Pinay and Adenauer (per [[david-teacher]]'s *Rogue Agents*), at about the time of the [[bilderberg-group]]'s formation in 1954; Pinay was a Bilderberg member too. Helped found it and served as its second chairman (early 1970s, after Pinay) was Parisian lawyer [[jean-violet]]. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 30:46-31:12, 34:01-34:28)*
- Per the Serbian royal family website (which hosted a 2004 meeting), members are MPs, diplomats, intelligence figures, commentators, lawyers and businessmen from 25+ countries, meeting twice a year, once in Washington DC; everything is off the record. Per [[hennie-van-vuuren]], meetings were only in Washington and Europe apart from one in South Africa and one in Oman. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 31:12-32:56)*
- Van Vuuren claims it was deeply tied to Opus Dei and the Knights of Malta and used these networks as a blueprint; Eyes Wide Open connects this to the Vatican's alleged Gladio ties from Part 1. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 32:15-32:44)*
- Politics shifted from conservatism to hard right; causes included the French Empire (Algeria, Lebanon) and, in the 1970s-80s, apartheid South Africa, where per Van Vuuren it acted as a quasi-intelligence outfit circumventing South African intelligence through front groups and shell companies. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 32:56-33:30)*
- Alleged funding: [[reinhard-gehlen]] via Violet (see [[hans-langemann]]) and the CIA. Per Tory MP Alan Clark's diary (reported by The Independent), Le Cercle is 'an Atlantic society of right-wing dignitaries and a right-wing think tank funded by the CIA which churns out cold war concepts'. Alleged members include [[william-casey]], [[william-colby]], Henry Kissinger, [[richard-nixon]] after office, David Rockefeller and [[brian-crozier]]; Tory MP [[jonathan-aitken]] was a former chairman who was ousted in 1997. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 34:34-37:00)*
- A 1979 Crozier planning paper (authenticity flagged as uncertain by the host) listed objectives: undercover financial transactions for political aims, international campaigns to discredit hostile personalities, a (presumably private) intelligence service with a selective viewpoint, and cover offices in London, Washington, Paris, Munich and Madrid. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 36:55-37:50)*
- Eyes Wide Open argues it shows 'institutional depth': CIA fronts like [[american-committee-on-united-europe]] on the surface and forums like Le Cercle in the shadows, with Violet's closeness to Gehlen linking Franco-German integration to the stay-behind networks. Compared to the [[safari-club]] and [[western-goals-foundation]]. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 33:30-33:48, 38:14-39:24)*
