[[uap-gerb]] speculates, using [[grant-cameron]]'s 2024 files from the LBJ Library (Johnson's schedule for 9-11 December 1965, plus "James Webb Kecksburg" and "Johnson Weird Walker" files), that the morning meetings on 10 December 1965 at the Texas White House may have been briefings on the Kecksburg crash. No meeting record mentions Kecksburg, Soviet spy satellites or ICBMs. This is Gerb's inference. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 45:20-46:40)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 51:26-51:41)*

## Claims and observations

- The evening of 9 December shows leisure items (dinner, drive, evening news). At 9:50 a.m. on 10 December a large guest roster arrived, including the Joint Chiefs of Staff, the Secretary and Deputy Secretary of Defense, presidential science adviser Donald Hornig, NASA administrator James Webb and others. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 45:53-46:24)*
- From 10:10 a.m. to 12:51 p.m. Johnson met the JCS (Adm. Donald McDonald, Gen. Wallace Greene, Gen. J.P. McConnell who had relieved [[curtis-lemay]] on 1 February 1965, Gen. Earle Wheeler, Gen. Harold K. Johnson), Bill Moyers, Joe Califano, [[robert-mcnamara]] and Cyrus Vance. Gerb notes LeMay's contempt for Wheeler ("Polly parrot") and that Wheeler alone was left out of the car ride. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 46:48-48:24)*
- Gerb links Gen. Harold K. Johnson to the 1st Cavalry Division suspected in the [[special-forces-witness-infographics]] case. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 48:24-48:40)*
- Califano sat on the Federal Radiation Council and dealt with the AEC in a December 1965 mid-air collision case; an off-the-record Califano meeting with Buford Ellington of the Office of Emergency Planning four days later was sent to Cameron by the LBJ Library. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 49:04-49:43)*
- After lunch Johnson met McNamara, Vance, Moyers, Hornig and Agriculture Secretary Orville Freeman. Gerb finds Freeman's presence odd and connects Webb's contacts with an under secretary of agriculture (Norris Dodd) on 9 July 1947, two days after the Roswell crash, calling it a stretch. He notes Hornig's post was first held by [[vannevar-bush]]. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 49:51-51:19)*
- Gerb also mentions AEC chairman Glenn Seaborg and says he could make a whole video on the day. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 51:19-51:34)*
- Separately, a 1964 luncheon, 1964 and 1966 letters (the latter thanking [[eric-a-walker]] for his Defense Science Board tenure after the crash) and Walker's invitation to the 1967 Clean Air Act signing show a close Johnson-Walker relationship, and Johnson as a senator wanted a military subcommittee to report UFO sightings to him. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 43:13-45:13)*
