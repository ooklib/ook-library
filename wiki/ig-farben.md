IG Farben was a German chemical/pharmaceutical conglomerate formed in the 1920s that became one
of the largest companies in Nazi Germany, and a client of [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan &
Cromwell]] (the Dulles brothers' law firm), which Eyes Wide Open uses as its central example of
the Dulles brothers' Nazi-era business entanglements.

## Claims

- A monopoly conglomerate of the major German chemical and pharmaceutical producers, and one of
  the largest firms in the Reich; manufactured the Zyklon B gas used in Nazi concentration-camp
  gas chambers. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 19:32-19:39, 21:49-22:06)*
- Held the patent for synthetic fuel, which supplied a massive share of the fuel powering the
  Nazi war machine (Germany lacking access to large natural oil supplies); the Reich supplied
  price guarantees and bought a 5% equity stake in IG Farben to fund the expansion of synthetic
  fuel capacity — direct state control/linkage to the Nazi party. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 22:12-22:59)*
- Its legal representation was [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]], the Dulles
  brothers' firm; Eyes Wide Open treats this as the "cornerstone" of the Dulles brothers'
  alleged complicity in Nazi-era commerce. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 22:40-23:10)*
