Former McDonnell Douglas rocket scientist and father of [[ryan-s-wood]]; the two spent decades scrutinizing the Majestic documents. Not to be confused with [[robert-e-wood]] (America First Committee general).

## Claims

- Gerb introduces him as "a former McDonald Douglas rocket scientist" who has studied the whole Majestic catalog with his son. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:32)*
- Per Ryan Wood: around 1967 he read many UFO books and persuaded Douglas management to fund work on how UFOs work "before after [the Soviets] do"; Douglas (then McDonnell Douglas) spent the equivalent of about four and a half million on internal R&D gravity experiments and theory, including interviewing abductees and desert magnetometer trials, with no classified access. He hired [[stanton-friedman]] to study the nuclear physics of UFO propulsion. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:24:34, 1:26:08)*
- [[john-b-alexander]] writes that Dr. Bob told him the SOM1-01 was created in 1954 to be leaked to Soviet spies; Ryan Wood disputes this. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 49:37, 50:01)*
- Co-authored with Ryan the SOM1-01 authenticity analysis and Majestic document ratings. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 2:20)*
