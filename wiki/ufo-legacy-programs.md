Gerb's umbrella term for the alleged, decades-old, fragmented network of US government,
military, intelligence, national-lab, and contractor programs tasked with retrieval, storage,
exploitation, derivation, reverse engineering, and protection of "nonhuman technical
vehicles" (UFOs), running from 1947 to the present. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:55–2:24)*

## Claims — structure ("the pyramid")

- Described as "the greatest secret ever kept from humankind," nicknamed "Manhattan Project 2.0."
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:32–2:53)*
- Top layer: the [[ufo-control-group|UFO control group]], a "committee of 27" formed in 1994
  under President [[bill-clinton]]'s administration; historically the NSC's 5412 Committee /
  Special Group / 303 Committee, last run by [[dick-cheney]], succeeded by [[james-clapper]]
  after Cheney left office as VP in 2009. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 4:38–5:18)*
- Second layer: "program administrators" — elements of US armed forces/IC with covert-UFO-program
  capability, e.g. CIA Directorate of Science and Technology, Air Force Rapid Capabilities
  Office, [[office-of-naval-intelligence|ONI]], [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]],
  [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|NGA]], Air Force Test Center. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 5:18–5:50)*
- Bottom layer: defense-industrial prime contractors and subcontractors, mostly "cover
  capacity" personnel without true program knowledge. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 5:50–6:16)*
- The [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] runs through every layer via statutory
  Special Access Program authority that evades normal US classification oversight. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 6:16–6:30)*
- Program-protection/counterintelligence services safeguarding the pyramid: Air Force Office of
  Special Investigations Office of Special Projects (AFOSI/PJ), DOE Office of Intelligence and
  Counterintelligence, DARPA Security and Intelligence Directorate. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 6:39–6:54)*
- A "missing layer" Gerb emphasizes: [[ffrdc|FFRDCs]] — semi-private GOCO institutions that let
  the government keep strict oversight of records/materials/spending while nominally operating
  outside government, and which he argues have held program custody "since the days of the
  Roswell crash." *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 6:54–8:25, 20:05–20:13)*
- Information flows down the pyramid but "almost never up," and program personnel further down
  have exponentially less institutional knowledge. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 4:06–4:30)*
- The overall program set is described as fractured, siloed, and scattered "into the wind" over
  decades to adapt to oversight pressure. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 3:43–4:06)*

## Claims (from the "Secrecy" Vol. 1 video — origin narrative)

- UAP Gerb summarizes his entire ~90-plus-year catalog as fundamentally "a reverse engineering
  program to garner some kind of insight," where most resulting breakthroughs are not directly
  ripped-off alien technology but inspired innovations that fed into other US classified programs.
  He attributes a similar framing directly to [[david-grush|David Grush]].
  *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 0:16–0:32, 58:44–59:13)*
- Dates the origin to at least 1947 (immediately following Roswell), though he assesses earlier
  cases like the [[magenta-italy-crash-1933|1933 Magenta, Italy]] crash suggest some US officials
  may have had prior awareness of nonhuman craft. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  18:47–19:27)*
- Names the closest things to a single "centralized oversight figure" across the program's
  history: [[james-clapper|Lt. Gen. James Clapper]] (as the closest Cheney-esque figure post-2009
  while DNI), [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]] (during the 1940s–1950s "Manhattan Project 2.0"),
  [[terry-phillips|Terry Phillips]] (alleged whistleblower reprisals within Air Force SAP
  channels), and [[edward-c-aldridge|Edward C. Aldridge]] (evolution of UFO programs within
  special access programs in the early 1980s). *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  3:57–4:36)*
- Per Grush, oversight was conclusively lost as a centralized effort in the early 2000s, when
  certain contractors — in Gerb's informed opinion, [[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]] —
  allegedly harnessed company-funded independent research and development (IRAD) to establish
  breakaway UFO retrieval/exploitation projects unaccountable to their original government
  handlers; complete oversight was lost by 2009 when [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] left the vice
  presidency. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 3:14–3:57)*
- Restates the pyramid's FFRDC/GOCO layer as tracing back to the Truman administration's late-
  1940s decision to give custody of recovered vehicles to what would become DOE/AEC national labs
  (Sandia, Los Alamos, Oak Ridge), a layer Gerb calls "as important as program compartmentalization
  itself," letting program administrators offload materials/biologics for plausible deniability
  while retaining an iron grip on program deliverables. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 6:49–7:13, 1:00:55–1:01:39)*
- See [[manhattan-project-2]] for Gerb's full five-pillar breakdown (compartmentalization,
  organizational architecture, physical security, classification/information control, political
  shield/cover) of how this structure allegedly copied the original Manhattan Project's security
  architecture.

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2 — funding, secrecy, and decline)

- Gerb reiterates the pyramid's fractured, siloed, redundant state today, describing "severe
  redundancies and brain drain," a "self-licking ice cream cone" of accountability, and states
  he is unsure whether program progress has "stagnated" since [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] left
  office in 2009, but that a full reorganization under constitutional, reported oversight (while
  retaining necessary secrecy) "could be the dawn of a new age for US science, technology, and
  space programs." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 3:16:53–3:18:07)*
- Funding mechanisms alleged to keep legacy program spending off the books: "haircuts" diverted
  from other classified/black programs (and, per Gerb's own speculation, potentially from
  obscure agencies like the US Forest Service or Bureau of Land Management); Independent Research
  and Development (IRAD) misappropriation by contractors; self-funding cutout companies (e.g.
  [[e-systems|E-Systems]], evolved from LTV Aerospace, a CIA cutout; and Lt. Col. James Duncan's
  BSI consultancy from the [[yellow-fruit|Yellow Fruit]] scandal); and, per
  [[david-grush|David Grush]]'s Fox News interview, money laundered through criminal enterprises
  that were referred to the Department of Justice for investigation. See
  [[content-only-special-access-program]] for the structural mechanism enabling this.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:39:52–2:44:24)*
- Program-protection/exposure-mitigation offices Gerb names as the pyramid's "limestone casing":
  [[air-force-office-of-special-investigations|AFOSI/PJ]],
  [[darpa-security-and-intelligence-directorate|DARPA SID]],
  [[air-force-life-cycle-management-center|AFLCMC/IP]], and
  [[doe-office-of-intelligence-and-counterintelligence|DOE OICI]]. Alleged tactics include
  administrative terrorism (clearance revocation, career destruction, ostracization —
  corroborated by [[david-grush|Grush]]'s own account of reprisal against himself, his chief of
  staff, and his former boss at the NRO) and, in the most severe alleged cases, "wet works" —
  Gerb cites the mysterious 2024 death of Air Force intelligence officer
  [[matthew-sullivan|Matthew "Quake" Sullivan]], a Grush source and friend, as a case he considers
  suspicious. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:45:02–2:51:37)*
- Disinformation/narrative-control campaigns Gerb ties to the pyramid's outermost layer: the
  1953 [[robertson-panel-1953|Robertson Panel]], the 1969 Condon Committee, and 2022's
  [[aaro|AARO]] under [[sean-kirkpatrick|Sean Kirkpatrick]], plus a parallel effort he attributes
  to [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]]'s [[advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program|ATIP]]
  and the *Age of Disclosure* documentary's partial-disclosure campaign.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:53:37–2:55:07)*
