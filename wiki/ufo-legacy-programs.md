Gerb's umbrella term for the alleged, decades-old, fragmented network of US government,
military, intelligence, national-lab, and contractor programs tasked with retrieval, storage,
exploitation, derivation, reverse engineering, and protection of "nonhuman technical
vehicles" (UFOs), running from 1947 to the present. *(1:55–2:24)*

## Claims — structure ("the pyramid")

- Described as "the greatest secret ever kept from humankind," nicknamed "Manhattan Project 2.0."
  *(2:32–2:53)*
- Top layer: the [[ufo-control-group|UFO control group]], a "committee of 27" formed in 1994
  under President [[bill-clinton]]'s administration; historically the NSC's 5412 Committee /
  Special Group / 303 Committee, last run by [[dick-cheney]], succeeded by [[james-clapper]]
  after Cheney left office as VP in 2009. *(4:38–5:18)*
- Second layer: "program administrators" — elements of US armed forces/IC with covert-UFO-program
  capability, e.g. CIA Directorate of Science and Technology, Air Force Rapid Capabilities
  Office, [[office-of-naval-intelligence|ONI]], [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]],
  [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|NGA]], Air Force Test Center. *(5:18–5:50)*
- Bottom layer: defense-industrial prime contractors and subcontractors, mostly "cover
  capacity" personnel without true program knowledge. *(5:50–6:16)*
- The [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] runs through every layer via statutory
  Special Access Program authority that evades normal US classification oversight. *(6:16–6:30)*
- Program-protection/counterintelligence services safeguarding the pyramid: Air Force Office of
  Special Investigations Office of Special Projects (AFOSI/PJ), DOE Office of Intelligence and
  Counterintelligence, DARPA Security and Intelligence Directorate. *(6:39–6:54)*
- A "missing layer" Gerb emphasizes: [[ffrdc|FFRDCs]] — semi-private GOCO institutions that let
  the government keep strict oversight of records/materials/spending while nominally operating
  outside government, and which he argues have held program custody "since the days of the
  Roswell crash." *(6:54–8:25, 20:05–20:13)*
- Information flows down the pyramid but "almost never up," and program personnel further down
  have exponentially less institutional knowledge. *(4:06–4:30)*
- The overall program set is described as fractured, siloed, and scattered "into the wind" over
  decades to adapt to oversight pressure. *(3:43–4:06)*

## Related events/evidence cited

- 1970s SALT-era US–Soviet agreements to mutually alert on UFO detections that could be mistaken
  for hostile insertions, via a Moscow–Washington hotline using codes "atom 222"/"atom 333."
  *(33:12–33:51)*
- The [[tic-tac-factory-uso|Tic Tac Factory]] USO-tracking program near the Bahamas.
- The [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]] Navy reverse-engineering program tracing to Roswell materials.
- Claimed 2011–2017 shift folding [[mitre-corporation]]'s programs into IC/CIA and then National
  Security Council control under [[james-clapper]], possibly tied to
  [[immaculate-constellation]]. *(1:34:41–1:37:32)*
