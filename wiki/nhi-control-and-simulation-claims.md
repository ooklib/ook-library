The philosophical and theological end of [[borland-michels-interview-2026]]: Borland's claim that the intelligence community inspector general hinted humans are not in charge, plus a shared speculation on souls, the Book of Enoch, simulation and control of "exit paths". Highly speculative and attributed.

## Claims

- Borland says the [[intelligence-community-inspector-general]] "extremely hinted" that humans are not in charge of the government; he illustrates by the sequence of questions investigators asked (gray beings, where they came from, then who is running the show). He does not name the individual. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:30:57-2:34:12)* Contrast with the [[david-grush]] complaint found "urgent and credible".
- Michels wonders whether non-human intelligence controls the cover-up, since few smoking-gun items surface in the smartphone era; Borland answers "absolutely yes". *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:29:51-2:30:57)*
- Borland does not know of evidence that humans are a resource (a point [[matthew-brown]] made on Weaponized), but entertains a soul-based test-program hypothesis. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:34:40-2:37:10)*
- Enoch: Borland says the Catholic position (via Malachi Martin) is that Enoch was excluded not as false but as not wholly true, and he will not declare on Watchers or Nephilim. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:37:18-2:38:16)*
- Michels merges the simulation argument (Bostrom, Musk) with technology as a forcing function to argue that the world is a moral test; Borland agrees strongly. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:38:26-2:42:15)*
- Exit paths: Borland proposes a simulation's guards would control consciousness and extremely high-energy physics (nuclear, high voltage, particle accelerators), tying to [[ufo-nuclear-connection]]; Michels adds that UFOs may exist to expand the human mind and cites [[jacques-vallee]] on the Trinity test timing. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:59:22-3:01:09)*
- Borland says he believes in remote viewing, prophetic dreams and consciousness projection but avoids "woo" publicly, believing mixing fact with woo is a stigmatization tactic. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:19:43-2:20:33)* Both share synchronicity anecdotes (Nas sample dream, Flea dream). *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:50:37-2:55:23)*
- Borland says he cannot say what a childhood event was that made him Catholic and that the craft never made a mental connection with him. *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:49:51-2:50:29)*
- Borland calls Elon Musk's line ("no extraterrestrial evidence") equal to AARO's, asking about "non-human". *([jm/borland](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-08-05 - A Top Government Official Told Me Aliens Run Everything! - Dylan Borland UNLEASHED.md>) 2:48:58-2:49:32)*
