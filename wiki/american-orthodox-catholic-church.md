The American Orthodox Catholic Church, headquartered in the Bronx and legally incorporated in 1965, is described by [[peter-levenda]], who joined it in 1968 to 69, as a "big name for a very small church" of mostly bishops with no parishioners, allegedly a front for intelligence work. Everything below is Levenda's account; he says he found supporting letters in released Garrison files.

## Claims

- [[david-ferrie]] and Jack Martin were bishops under Carl Stanley of Kentucky, who called the FBI after the assassination naming them, then died within a month of visiting the Bronx to be consecrated. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 30:37, 31:54, 36:20)*
- The Bronx leader, a Ukrainian priest called Propheta, demanded other churches submit to FBI vetting and boasted that Hoover personally approved his priests and bishops. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 35:53, 54:55, 55:09)*
- Levenda says Propheta's letters in the Garrison files asked that Jack Martin be sent back when Garrison was finished; Martin was a go-to background-information man who stayed in clerical persona. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 48:51, 49:06, 34:36)*
- Levenda and a friend formed a draft-avoiding "Slavonic Orthodox" church at 17, crashed Robert Kennedy's funeral at St. Patrick's in June 1968 dressed as clergy, then met AOCC men outside and joined. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 37:32, 41:00, 46:51)*
- The Selective Service head allegedly told him to watch for Russian agents entering through Orthodox churches; Levenda also describes making unqualified foreign men bishops for consulates, including a Nigerian nationalist-side "bishop" during the Biafra war. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 47:48, 55:20, 55:49)*
- Levenda left in 1969 and was immediately given a draft notice, withdrawn after a phone call in which he threatened to talk. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 3:05:56, 3:06:10)*
