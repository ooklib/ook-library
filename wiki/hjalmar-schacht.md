Hjalmar Schacht was Hitler's personal banker, saved from Nuremberg sentencing by
[[allen-dulles]] and [[william-donovan]] as part of the broader [[operation-sunrise]] effort to
preserve useful Nazi elements after the war.

## Claims

- Hitler's personal banker, who came from the same elite banking world as Dulles and Donovan and
  remained a respected member of the international banking class in London and New York
  throughout the war; knew the location of hidden Nazi fortunes stashed away before the fall of
  Berlin. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 11:53-12:16)*
- Dulles and Donovan initially targeted [[hermann-goring]] as a potential separate-peace partner
  but judged him too high-profile to save; they focused instead on Schacht. Donovan reportedly
  circumvented Supreme Court Justice Robert Jackson (Roosevelt's overseer of Nuremberg) to save
  Schacht from sentencing, allegedly fabricating sympathetic stories that he had been exiled from
  the Nazi party and harbored no antisemitic views. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 11:17-12:44)*
- Framed by Eyes Wide Open as "only one of many Nazis saved" under the umbrella of
  [[operation-sunrise]]. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 12:49-12:53)*
