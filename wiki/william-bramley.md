William Bramley is the author of *The Gods of Eden*. [[jason-jorjani]] praises him as a non-grifter who wrote one masterful book and never returned to the topic.

## Claims

- Bramley began by researching war profiteering and found UFO reports in archival records across epochs. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 54:39, 55:07)*
- Medieval plague claim (as recounted by Jorjani): during Charlemagne's reign there were sightings and hysteria over "tyrants of the air", including the Lyons/Magonia incident where four people said to have descended from aerial ships were nearly lynched until Bishop Agobard declared it false; and that the Black Death broke out only after hooded beings landed and sprayed fields and doors with spray cans, seeding the scythe/Grim Reaper iconography, a biological attack causing a retreat into religious fundamentalism and ending the coming Renaissance. Jorjani says Bramley links these "plague promulgators" to the Enki/Anunnaki/Elohim/Deva faction. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 55:39, 56:07, 57:26, 58:52)*
- Claimed 1400s broadsheets of sky battles (Basel, Nuremberg; reprinted by Vallee in *Wonders in the Sky*) are read by Jorjani as the rebel faction winning a temporary reprieve. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 50:39, 51:36)*
