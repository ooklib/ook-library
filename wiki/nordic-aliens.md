Supposed humanoid extraterrestrials from the Pleiades (ASR "pades") resembling Scandinavians: 6-7 ft tall, blue eyed, fair skinned, long blond hair. Summarized by [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 30:39-31:00)*

## Claims

- Many 1950s contactees alleged encounters with benevolent, telepathic, affectionate Nordics who belong to a Galactic Federation and travel in spherical or cigar-shaped craft. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 31:00-31:34)*
- [[travis-walton]]'s account (film *Fire in the Sky*) includes several being types, including grays and a Nordic-like race of tall humanoids. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 31:34-32:07)*
- Gerb declines to opine on abduction or alien races; he focuses on the fact something non-human is in the sky. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 32:07-32:30)*
