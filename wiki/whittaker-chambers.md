Whittaker Chambers, a senior editor/writer at [[henry-luce]]'s [[time-life|Time magazine]],
provided the 1948 testimony accusing [[alger-hiss]] of Soviet espionage — testimony Eyes Wide
Open flags as "strangely coincidental" given Chambers's employer's closeness to
[[allen-dulles]].

## Claims

- A senior editor and writer at Time magazine, testified to having been a courier in a secret
  Soviet spy ring in 1930s Washington, DC alongside [[alger-hiss]], sparking [[richard-nixon]]'s
  HUAC prosecution of Hiss. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 15:35-15:55)*
- The host flags his employment under [[henry-luce]] — an arch-conservative close personal friend
  of [[allen-dulles]] who provided consistently positive coverage of Dulles's OSS/CIA activities —
  as suspicious context for his high-profile accusation against Hiss, whom [[john-foster-dulles]]
  had personally sponsored to the [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace]] presidency.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 16:41-16:54, 18:15-18:35)*

## Relationships
