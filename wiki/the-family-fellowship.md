The Family, also known as the Fellowship, is a Christian political network founded in the 1930s and prominent in the postwar era. In the `jm/cover-up` episode [[jason-samosa]] describes it as an invisible network of Protestant Christians throughout government (citing a book by Jeff Sharlet, given as "Charlotte") and asks how large it is and what it knows about UFOs. [[doug-coe]] is named as a leader; the National Prayer Breakfast is described as its event.

## Claims

- Members named: [[alonzo-mcdonald]] (Carter staff director) and [[james-baker]] (Reagan staff director), giving "a consistent presence at the nexus of power" across Democratic and Republican administrations. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 18:32, 19:07)*
- Both allegedly received UFO information in a briefing to Reagan, per [[nick-redfern]]. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 19:24, 19:38)*
- If someone in government shut down UFO investigations by calling them demonic, Samosa says it could be the Family rather than the 1952 [[collins-elite]] program. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 20:03)*
- Host [[jesse-michels]] notes Coe held the prayer breakfast that every president had to attend. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 20:23, 20:32)*
