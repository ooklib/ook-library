Operation 40 is, per [[eyes-wide-open]], the shorthand for an elite group of Cuban exile assets that CIA trainers split off from [[brigade-2506]] in 1960-61. The host uses "Operation 40" as a catch-all label (he says the name never appears in declassified or official CIA material, but exiles have referred to it) for a group he presents as simultaneously a would-be post-Castro intelligence service, an assassination and "purge" unit, and a private, deniable pool of contract operators for later CIA operations. This is the host's argument, not established fact. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 32:16-32:46)*

## Origin and structure

- Host: the seed is the Western Hemisphere Division's WH/4 branch, created after Allen Dulles's January 18, 1960 instruction to Bissell; CIA history says WH/4 "totaled 40 persons with 18 at headquarters, 20 at Havana station and two at Santiago base", which the host suspects is where the "40" name was born. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 11:16-12:04)* See [[joseph-caldwell-king]], [[richard-bissell]], [[allen-dulles]].
- Schlesinger memo to Richard Goodwin, June 9, 1961: "originally only 40 men were involved, later the group was enlarged to 70"; host adds other sources say ~90 by 1962. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 37:11-37:30)* The host says he first only had a typed copy from a Brigade 2506 veterans' site and later, via a Patreon supporter's National Archives request, a scan of the original; he believes this is its first full online appearance. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 35:30-37:04)*
- Selection: CIA trainers at Fort Trax separated the "hardened, ideological and battle-tested" exiles into a smaller commando group. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 31:53-32:16)* Per exile sources it was one of the CIA's first attempts to create from scratch the intelligence agency of another state, meant to become the intelligence apparatus of a post-Castro regime. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 33:48-34:07)*
- Host: with Castro still in power it became an "in-house private intelligence network" of non-US-citizen contract workers giving the CIA plausible deniability; many were former Batista-regime army or secret-police hardliners. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 34:07-34:47)*
- David Morales, in a May 25, 1961 interview, said the core was 39 highly educated Cubans trained as case officers (with Morales making 40) to form a future intelligence service; he called them "amots" (host: likely the Op 40 cryptonym) alongside "amfast" (counterintelligence) and "amchur" (political), together a projected ~200-man Cuban agency. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 44:59-45:48)* (ASR spellings; normalization to AMOTS/AMFAST/AMCHUR is the host's/my reading.)

## Purpose: assassins and "death squad"

- Schlesinger memo: the ostensible purpose was to administer liberated territories, but the CIA agent in charge, "Felix", trained members in "third degree interrogation, torture and general terrorism"; liberal exiles believed the real purpose was to "kill Communists", eliminate hardcore Fidelistas, then followers of Manuel Ray, then of Varona, and set up a right-wing dictatorship "presumably under Artime". *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 37:30-39:53)* Host identifies "Felix" as [[felix-rodriguez]] and says this memo is the only place he has seen Rodriguez named as Op 40's leader; the named exile leader, a former Cuban colonel the ASR renders "Vinante Leon", had died at the Bay of Pigs. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 37:48-39:12)* See [[manuel-artime]].
- William Turner told the House Select Committee on Assassinations (Dec 1977) of an interview with Op 40 exile Felipe de Diego, who said Op 40 "was the intelligence branch of the 2506 Brigade and brought along assassins since it was believed that some of the Cuban leaders would have to be eliminated permanently"; de Diego said he attended special forces training at Fort Benning before the invasion, later joined [[e-howard-hunt]]'s Watergate team (the Fielding break-in for Daniel Ellsberg's files). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 39:53-41:44)*
- Manuel Ray (MRP), May 1961: Op 40 men were to "follow behind the invading troops and purge the captured cities and towns"; he called one member a "war criminal in Cuba". *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 42:04-42:46)*
- Host: the same tactics reappeared in Vietnam under Phoenix, "administered once again by operation 40 agents and Exiles" (host's assertion). *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 41:50-42:04)*

## Trainers and alleged presence

- Trainers: [[e-howard-hunt]] (spycraft/regime-change expert) and [[david-sanchez-morales]] (assassination and counterinsurgency); the host says Hunt later brought Op 40 operators into the Nixon White House "plumber" unit. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 42:54-43:25, 44:52-44:59)*
- Per exiles, [[frank-sturgis]] was reportedly present; [[barry-seal]] is placed at the Guatemala camp area by a March 29, 1961 postcard (Lake Atitlan volcano) published by Daniel Hopsicker in *Barry and the Boys*; host: "almost indisputably" a trainer, likely aviation. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 47:21-48:14)*
- The host also notes J. C. King's name appears on a fictitious Intel item in the Call of Duty: Black Ops game about Operation 40 and calls that depiction a whitewash. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 33:05-33:48)*

## Alumni and afterlife (host's account)

- Rafael "Chi Chi" Cano (ASR "Cano"/"cantero"; the host's exact intended name is not resolved here): ex-Sturgis guerrilla, attached to Ted Shackley and Thomas Clines (ASR "Klein"), later embedded in Mexico's state oil company PEMEX in the early '70s with help of Naval-intelligence asset Ed Wilson to give cover for alleged arms and narcotics smuggling, and involved in late-'70s/early-'80s weapons deals tied to Iran-Contra. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 48:14-50:06)* See [[ted-shackley]].
- Ricardo Chavez, a Mexican CIA agent who led an Op 40 exile paramilitary squad in the Congo in the mid-1960s hunting Che Guevara, later at PEMEX. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 49:13-49:33)* Host notes [[george-h-w-bush]] had early-60s dealings with PEMEX's top staff and defers detail to the next part. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 49:33-49:53)*
- [[felix-rodriguez]] went private-assassin and Condor-era; [[orlando-bosch]], Orlando Garcia, Ignacio Novo and Ricardo "Monkey" Morales are presented as Op 40 veterans reused as deniable Condor-era terrorists; see that page for Letelier and Cubana 455. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 50:12-52:55)*
- Morales, at Bosch's 1968 trial, produced a tape in which a "fellow named Hunt" allegedly "gave $15,000 to some representatives of Cuban power to place bombs" (per Gaeton Fonzi's HSCA notes); the host asks whether this is part of Nixon's "whole Bay of Pigs thing" but also offers that Morales, dropped by the CIA after the Congo, may have been trying to implicate the agency. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 52:55-53:49)*

## The Hopsicker photo (speculative)

Host shows the cover photo of *Barry and the Boys*, labeled as the only extant photo of the Op 40 squad, allegedly taken January 22, 1963 in Mexico City. He says all identifications are speculation: front-left is "quite clearly" Felix Rodriguez; Hopsicker suggests the next man is Porter Goss (host not convinced); next is Barry Seal (host: near-undisputable); a man covering his face is proposed to be Sturgis (host not convinced; may be another Cuban). Host's own reading: Seal and Goss look like "guests of honor" briefing or checking in from Langley, and the timing 10 months before the Kennedy assassination fuels his speculation about its subject. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 53:56-58:06)* Discrepancy note: none with earlier episodes; War Conspiracy 2 used the same Hopsicker material for Seal.
