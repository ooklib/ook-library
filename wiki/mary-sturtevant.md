Extensive US intelligence/defense career; Gerb calls her online footprint "largely a ghost," with
sparse public information beyond an NRO "Leaders of the NRO" volume 3 celebration and old Senate
Select Committee on Intelligence (SSCI) documents. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
2:47:12–2:47:45)*

## Claims

- Worked at [[bdm-international|BDM]] (Braddock, Dunn & McDonald) on defense/arms-control
  research and technology transfer before government service.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:47:45–2:48:15)*
- Began CIA career in 1985 at the Directorate of Intelligence's Technology Transfer Assessment
  Center, focused on Soviet acquisition of Western dual-use technology; later worked in the CIA
  Counter Intelligence Center, then 8 years at SSCI (evaluating tactical military programs,
  advising on CIA programs, serving as budget director), then back to CIA as agency comptroller.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:48:19–2:49:13)*
- Appointed in 2001 as special assistant to the president and senior director for intelligence
  programs, National Security Council, under George W. Bush; then NRO deputy director for
  national support, 2004–2006. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:49:13–2:49:31)*
- Served as Lockheed Martin VP of government affairs (2006–2011) then VP of intelligence, joint,
  and science and technology programs (2011–2015) — active during the 2010–2011 "Kona Blue"
  technology-transfer attempt. Gerb wagers her NSC/CIA-DS&T allegiances would have commanded her
  to aid [[glenn-gaffney|Glenn Gaffney]] in blocking the transfer.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:49:39–2:50:18)*
- Gerb notes she and [[christopher-mellon|Christopher Mellon]] worked closely together at SSCI
  for years, but Mellon reportedly declined Gerb's request for help questioning her.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:50:18–2:50:35)*
- Gerb's Lockheed investigation formally identifies Sturtevant as a "high-level member of
  Lockheed UFO Legacy Program Operations." In 1999, prior to joining Lockheed, she was named
  Deputy Director of the CIA's Clandestine Information Technology Office — a joint venture
  between the CIA Directorate of Operations and CIA DS&T (the same directorate whose deputy
  director, [[glenn-gaffney|Glenn Gaffney]], later blocked the transfer). Gerb asks whether her
  prior agency allegiances outweighed her Lockheed coworker relationship with
  [[james-t-ryder|James Ryder]] in the blockage of [[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]].
  *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:22:52–2:23:12, 2:31:39–2:32:56)*
- Her online footprint largely disappears around the time she joined Lockheed; documents place
  her in the VP intelligence/joint/science & tech role at least 2011–2015 — 2011 being the year
  of the thwarted transfer. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:31:10–2:31:39)*
- A 1989 SSCI memorandum for record places her and [[christopher-mellon|Christopher Mellon]] at
  the same briefing; records through 1996 show them repeatedly attending the same series of
  meetings (identical per diem/travel costs, Jan–Mar 1996). *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:33:12–2:34:10)*
- Gerb compares her career to [[donald-kerr|Donald Kerr]]'s as among the most senior gatekeeper
  résumés he has documented. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 2:23:22–2:23:37)*
