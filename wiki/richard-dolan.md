Author of *UFOs and the National Security State*, praised by
[[katherine-austin-fitts|Katherine Austin Fitts]] as the best book describing the post-WWII
mechanisms behind the black-budget infrastructure. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
49:41–50:04)*

## Claims

- Has done research on the TRW/"Zodiac" story (pseudonym "Serge Monast" or similar, ASR: "SGE
  Masters"), which Gerb expresses interest in revisiting with him.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 2:10:38–2:10:46)*

- In Gerb's Northrop/TRW investigation, Dolan's [[zodiac-crash-retrieval-program|Zodiac]] research
  is credited with uncovering the identities behind the story's pseudonyms: attorney Jeffrey W.
  Griffith (behind "Greg Halifax") and Mary Elizabeth Elliot (behind "Sedge Masters"). Griffith
  served as corporate counsel for Hughes Aircraft and later for the former TRW Space Park in
  Redondo Beach, California. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md)
  46:15–47:29)*

- In 2021, detailed information leaked to him from a source familiar with a classified UAP Task
  Force report presented to Congress, describing energy-pulse, ion, anti-gravity, antimatter,
  ramjet-hydrogen, and compressed-nuclear propulsion research on recovered nonhuman technologies,
  spearheaded by DARPA and a group called "Advanced Group Six," funded partly via IRAD, with
  prototype craft flown at Area 51 and [[tonopah-test-range|Tonopah Test Range]] but never
  outside the Nevada Test and Training Range. Gerb calls Dolan the only researcher he's seen make
  this specific Tonopah connection. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md) 56:19–59:56,
  2:49:31–2:49:46)*

- In the Sandia video, Gerb revisits this claim alongside [[area-6|Area 6]], speculating Area 6
  — a top-secret NNSS UAV-testing site with DARPA as a customer and its aerial operations
  facility run by [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]] — may be where "Advanced Group Six" is
  based. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:19:29–2:19:35, 2:26:12–2:26:29)*
