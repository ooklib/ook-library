The Westall case is a 6 April 1966 sighting by 100-plus students and teachers at a Melbourne suburban school of one to three metallic elliptical discs, one of which was said to land in a nearby wooded area. [[ross-coulthart]] treats it as a key case in which witnesses were allegedly intimidated into silence.

## Claims

- Coulthart learned of it while writing his book from [[james-rigney]] of a Victorian UFO investigation group, whom he credits with securing the release of the [[wilson-davis-notes]]; Rigney also told him of the Kelly Cahill case, and Coulthart spent a weekend with him visiting the Cahill site and meeting the Westall students. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 22:15-24:39)*
- Coulthart says he judges the former students, now in their 60s, to be sincere, and that a group of kids got close enough to see the craft from about 5 metres; he doubts stories of people touching it. He cites an Australian researcher who has spoken to roughly 160 witnesses. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 24:39-26:13, 28:16-28:36)*
- The military arrived in droves and secured the site, but the kids knew the land better than the military did. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 25:42-25:57)*
- Science teacher [[andrew-greenwood]] told a suburban newspaper what he saw; late at night a man in Royal Australian Air Force uniform and a man in civilian clothes allegedly came to his door and threatened to spread a story that he was an alcoholic so he would lose his teaching job. Coulthart says Greenwood was not an alcoholic and stayed silent for 40-50 years, and gave Coulthart a television interview in his last two years. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 26:36-28:16, 30:33-30:47)*
- Students also describe American jeeps and uniforms at the school and being threatened by men in civilian clothes; the whole school assembly was told to keep quiet. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 28:36-29:35)*
- Coulthart notes RAAF Base Sale (his "Sail"), about 3-4 hours north of Melbourne, hosted a secret American U-2 program monitoring the southern oceans for radioactive plumes, offered as a possible reason for a US presence. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 28:36-29:13)*
- Why anyone would shut down the inquiry is unknown to Coulthart. The Australian Skeptics gave him a "bent fork" award for lending it credibility. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 29:54-30:07, 30:47-30:51)*
