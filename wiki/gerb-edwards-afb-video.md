A video (about 47m, dated 2024-10-02, [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_LSMLGBDNg)) in which [[uap-gerb]] analyzes [[witness-ed]], entry 11063 in [[steven-greer]]'s redacted witness list ("Vol. 2" of his redacted-list series, after the earlier video that produced [[witness-ms]] and TB). Chapters: Intro, 11063, Witness Testimony, Witness 11063 Notes, Edwards AFB, Blackjack, Conclusion. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 0:00-3:17)*

Gerb walks through two documents in the [[disclosure-project]] archive (a witness-testimony email and Greer's notes of a 25 November 2023 phone call), tests Ed's career claims against public sources on the Nevada Test and Training Range, the 412th Test Wing and USAF major generals, surveys other Edwards claims (Cooper, Project Outgrowth, Fouche, Project Heaven, Debbie Clayton, Project Red Light) and the alleged 1991 NRO document naming Blackjack. His thesis is a joint reverse-engineering program between [[nellis-air-force-base]] / [[groom-lake]] S4 and the [[edwards-air-force-base]] 412th Test Group. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 41:17-42:06)*
