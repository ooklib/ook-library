The first printing of [[the-day-after-roswell-book]] carried a foreword by him, which he reportedly angrily disowned when he learned the book centered on UAP, leading to an early reprint without it. The [[dawn-of-a-new-age-manuscript]] mentions Corso's relationship with him but no such endorsement. [[stanton-friedman]] notes the foreword says nothing about Roswell and was written for a different book about Corso's activities. *([gerb/corso](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-03 - Philip J.
