Ricardo Sicre is named ("Seeker" in ASR, pronunciation uncertain) by [[jason-samosa]] as a vice president of the [[world-commerce-corporation]], a Spanish spy employed by the OSS in WWII who seemed dodgy and organized-crime-connected. His friend Robert Rock wrote travel journals about their adventures and called them part of "a secret society which strides the world"; the two lived in the same Spanish apartment complex as oil magnate Jake Hamon and [[otto-skorzeny]]. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:33:01, 1:33:21, 1:33:54, 1:34:01)*
