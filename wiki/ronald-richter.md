German scientist who ran a fusion project on Huemul Island near Bariloche for Juan Peron around 1950. [[jason-jorjani]] and Michels treat him as a "former Nazi"; Jorjani says he promised fusion weapons but never delivered and that IAEA visitors found something unlike known fusion, energy-intensive and unclear. Jorjani speculates it was a fission-trigger-less "clean" fusion project. Details and his Nazi status are Jorjani's account.
