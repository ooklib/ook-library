- Bahnson wrote in his notes that something was going on he could not account for; he is Brown's second "reliable witness". *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 12:38-12:55)*
