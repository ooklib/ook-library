Courtney Whitney was a prominent Manila corporate lawyer who became [[douglas-macarthur]]'s personal attorney, fixer and political aide, and a general on his staff; [[eyes-wide-open]] portrays him as [[charles-willoughby]]'s main rival for MacArthur's ear, a rivalry that the host says continued in occupied Japan (to be covered in Willoughby 3).

## Claims

- Practiced in the Philippines from 1927; George Washington University law graduate; represented MacArthur personally and, the host speculates, possibly helped with the fallout of the Isabel Rosario Cooper affair. "Personal stooge" and fixer per the host; the Seagraves call him MacArthur's "intimate crony" (*Gold Warriors*). *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 45:44-46:23)*
- Close to Andres Soriano; his mandate overlapped Willoughby's. Willoughby later complained Whitney had "considerable influence on General MacArthur, which to us who had been with the general longer was mysterious to us to this day". *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 45:11-45:44, 46:23-46:35, 1:21:52-1:22:16)*
- Not on the PT-boat escape from Corregidor (March 1942); reached Australia later, which the host says "must have pleased Willoughby". *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 57:03-57:21)*
- In the 1944-45 Philippine campaign he headed the Philippine Regional Section, working with Filipino resistance groups (including the [[hukbalahap]], whom Willoughby thought communist); Willoughby saw this as a slight and was sidelined. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:21:39-1:22:23)*
- Host's discrepancy note: ASR renders him "Courtourtney Whitney" and "the rival Skull Dugurer" (likely "skull-duggerer"); normalization is my inference.
