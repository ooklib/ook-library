Harland F. Bentley claims to have been an Army private first class and radar operator at a Nike Ajax missile site near Olney, Maryland, in 1957-1959 and later a NASA classified contractor with an electrical engineering degree and graduate work in nuclear engineering. He first appeared in [[steven-greer]]'s [[disclosure-project]] in 2001. [[uap-gerb]] says he could not verify any of Bentley's military, education or contract records, so the claims are analyzed on their own merits. All below are Bentley's claims as relayed by Gerb.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-2)

- Bentley says he was stationed at a Nike Ajax site outside Olney, Maryland, in 1957-1959, either W-92 (Gaithersburg) or W-93 (north of Olney, now a FEMA federal support center); he later appeared at the National Press Club event (Gerb cites the recording from about 57 minutes in). *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 1:36-3:11)*
- His two main claims are the May 1958 [[nike-ajax-ufo-crash-1958]] and an alleged overheard [[apollo-8-ufo-encounter-claims]] radio exchange. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 2:08-2:31)*
- Gerb's assessment: he finds the claims "super interesting", especially the Ajax crash where the craft struck the ground and then departed in flight, but does not know what to make of the Apollo 8 story, and says there is no supporting evidence or testimony for May 1958. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 7:01-7:16,)*
