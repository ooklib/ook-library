A 5 March 2004 incident over the southern Mexican state of Campeche (ASR: "keee", "Cache") in which the Mexican Air Force filmed 11 lights with infrared equipment during an anti-drug patrol. [[uap-gerb]] narrates the date as "March 5th 2005" though the chapter title and video say 2004; this page uses 2004. He covers it in [[gerb-air-force-encounters-video]]. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 11:25-12:41)*

## Claims

- The pilots filmed 11 unidentified objects for minutes, appearing at about 3,500 m and surrounding the jet; only three showed on the plane's radar. Major Magdaleno Castanon said the jets chased the lights and "I believe they could feel we were pursuing them". *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 11:36-12:08)*
- Skeptics: Gerb says Michael Shermer (head of Skeptic magazine) and other detractors suggest the lights were burn-off flares from an offshore oil platform in the Gulf of Mexico. Gerb personally chooses to believe the military pilots but rates this the case with the highest probability of a prosaic explanation, adding that more analysis is needed. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 12:08-12:41,)*
