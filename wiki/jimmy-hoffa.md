Jimmy Hoffa, head of the Teamsters Union, is introduced by Eyes Wide Open only briefly here as
the mobster whose trucking network later distributed [[operation-x]] heroin across the United
States; the host flags him as "a very important character" for the series' future coverage of
John and Robert Kennedy.

## Claims

- "Later in this history the heroin would be shipped across the United States in trucks
  controlled by mobster Jimmy Hoffa's Teamsters Union." *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 28:16-28:30)*

## Relationships

- Teamsters Union trucking network distributed [[operation-x]] heroin domestically.

## Additional claims (CIA-Mafia Legend)

- Host (citing Peter Dale Scott): Hoffa's Teamsters gave the [[national-crime-syndicate]] union locals across the US (offices, do-nothing cover jobs), trucking logistics, and a looted pension fund; he leveraged influence between Giancana's outfit, the New York five families and Lansky's Florida network; Hoffa truckers allegedly delivered guns to mob/CIA-backed guerrillas in Florida for Cuba. [[robert-f-kennedy]]'s crusade began with Hoffa. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 54:07-55:33)*

## Additional claims (Bush and the Cowboys, Chapter 2)


- Davidson worked to prove Hoffa's innocence after his sentence to 13 years; per John Davis he had earlier used Hoffa to get a Teamsters pension loan for [[clint-murchison]]; Hoffa was freed Dec 23, 1971 by [[richard-nixon]]'s commutation ("time served"). Host speculates on a Nixon-Davidson link via Del Charro. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 41:24-42:41)* See [[irving-davidson]].

## Additional claims (Road to Dallas Ch. 4)

- Per Doc Stacher (via the host): [[meyer-lansky]] and the New York gangsters first met Hoffa through Moe Dalitz, who knew him from Detroit's Purple Gang as Hoffa began taking over the Teamsters there; Hoffa later commandeered the Teamsters Pension Fund, "one of the mob's most significant piggy banks". *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 6:15-6:48)*
