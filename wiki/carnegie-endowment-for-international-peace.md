The Carnegie Endowment for International Peace is described by Eyes Wide Open as "an influential
internationalist think tank" whose presidency was granted to [[alger-hiss]] by
[[john-foster-dulles]] in 1947 — a personal connection that, per the host, complicated Dulles's
position once Hiss was accused of espionage the following year.
