Halliburton (ASR "halberton"), described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as "an oil services and defense construction giant," is the company that in 1998 merged with [[dresser-industries]] (the former employer of [[neil-mallon]] and [[george-h-w-bush]]) and was at that time headed by [[dick-cheney]].

## Claims

- Dresser remained an independent oil services firm for five decades, "presumably continuing to serve as an intelligence front on the side" (host's speculation), until its 1998 merger with its "main rival" Halliburton. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 46:11-46:34)*
- At the time of the 1998 merger, Halliburton's CEO was "none other than" Dick Cheney, whom the host introduces as former Secretary of Defense in the George H. W. Bush administration and future vice president under [[george-h-w-bush]]'s son, and calls "the Lord of Darkness himself." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 46:34-46:52)*
- Halliburton had also absorbed Texas construction giant Brown and Root (host: in 1962), "one of the major supporters of Lyndon Johnson throughout his early political career"; the host promises the "Halliburton octopus" in a forthcoming Lyndon Johnson video. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 46:52-47:16)* See [[lyndon-b-johnson]].
- Host's point: "if they were loyal to him Bush kept them around usually for his whole career... from his CIA days to his time as vice president all the way to the White House and even his son's presidency, the same names continued to pop up"; Cheney is "but one example," and his "CIA buddies would also be brought along to the highest echelons of American intelligence and power." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 47:16-47:46)*
