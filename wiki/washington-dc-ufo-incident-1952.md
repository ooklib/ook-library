Mass sightings and radar tracks over Washington, D.C. on July 19-20 and July 26-27, 1952, per [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 36:27-36:42)*

## Claims

- At 11:40 pm on Saturday July 19, air traffic controller [[edward-nugent]] at Washington National spotted seven objects on radar moving erratically; two tower controllers saw a hovering bright light depart at great speed; objects moved over the White House; jets scrambled from New Castle AFB, Delaware, found nothing and returned low on fuel. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 36:32-37:38)*
- On July 26 a National Airlines pilot and stewardess saw erratic lights, and radar at National Airport and Andrews AFB tracked the objects; front-page headlines followed, [[harry-truman]] personally phoned his Air Force aides, and the CIA took much interest. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 37:38-38:17)*
- In response the [[robertson-panel-1953]] was formed to "strip the unidentified flying objects of their special status" and the aura of mystery; Gerb says the event thus led to the decades-long stigma around UFOs. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 38:17-38:41)*

## Claims (gerb/stigma)

- Gerb calls it one of the most famous mass UFO sightings in US history (December 1952 in his telling; the other flap was in July), with lights and radar signatures over several nights and, he says, a B-52 pilot among witnesses, initially given serious consideration as of ET origin; it drew 148 national newspapers over six months and led to the [[samford-press-conference-1952]]. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 8:12-9:02)*
