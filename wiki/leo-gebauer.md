Phoenix-based physical scientist specializing in magnetics, [[silas-newton]]'s friend and an inventor or early user of the oil-finding [[doodlebug-magnetic-detector]]. [[jp-cahn]] labeled him the single "Dr. G" behind [[frank-scully]]'s sources, which Gebauer denied under oath. He was convicted with Newton in 1953 but never sentenced. ASR renders the name "Gabau", "Gabauer" and "Jabau".

## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Cahn declared Gebauer the sole Dr. G on Newton's alleged word, with no basis; Gebauer stated in writing and in court he was not Dr. G, though Gerb allows he may have been one of the Gs relaying information. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 37:30-38:01, 42:01-43:06)*
- Gerb has seen reports that Newton and Gebauer said the doodlebug ran on recovered Aztec technology but says he has never seen such statements from Newton, Gebauer or Scully. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 42:18-42:32)*
- [[william-steinman]] proposes that [[carl-a-highland]] was the real Dr. G who leaked the story to Gebauer and Newton and that Gebauer and Highland lived and worked together in Antarctica in 1935 and later developed magnetic aerial detection; Gerb notes Steinman gives no evidence. Steinman also places Gebauer at the Phoenix and Los Angeles division of Air Research Company (the firm of Paul Schaerer), and the Ramsays say he held high positions in Air Research laboratories around 1946, which Gerb calls interesting but unsupported. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:06:52-1:09:07, 2:20:49-2:21:32)*
- At trial his defense showed successful water-well and oil finds by his device; Gerb thinks the doodlebug was based on real magnetic-anomaly technology, not a $445 surplus-store gadget. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 54:30-55:17, 1:20:44-1:21:10)*
