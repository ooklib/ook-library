A slide titled "Way Forward: UAP Campaign Plan, Lines of Effort" from [[karl-nell]]'s [[sol-foundation]] talk, partially leaked in November 2023 and described by [[uap-gerb]]. It proposes a controlled-disclosure campaign that reaches a post-disclosure world with oversight restored, catastrophic disclosure avoided and scientific understanding advanced. Nell contrasts controlled disclosure (a campaign plan) with catastrophic disclosure (floodgates opened, possibly by an adversary or the NHI themselves, to cause social disruption). *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 9:03-9:26,)*

## Claims

- Structure: five phases through four channels (public sector, philosophical investigation, scientific research, private sector). *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 11:40-11:57)*
- Phase 0 (shaping the narrative) is said to have been achieved by AATIP, the DoD videos, the 2017 New York Times article and the [[uap-task-force]]; Gerb notes [[aaro]] is not listed. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 12:04-12:21)*
- Phase 1, target date 1 January 2024, seems all but complete with the Schumer legislation: government acceptance achieved, UAP largely bipartisan, reactive hypothesis generation appearing. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 12:21-12:37)*
- Phase 2 (correlate signatures, harness academia) targets 1 January 2026 and is at risk because of stigma; phase 3 (public acceptance, possibly the disclosure point) targets 1 October 2030; phase 4 (answer who, what, when, where and why) targets 1 October 2034 and is off target; phase 5 has an indefinite timeline and an "interactive" end state, possibly integrating NHI and NHI technology with civilization. Dates and status are as read by Gerb from the slide. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 12:37-13:40)*
- Government line of effort ideas: amnesty, a Manhattan Project-style effort, centralized data, National Science Foundation grants, a National Intelligence Estimate on every country's position, and a UN summit or world congress on new norms of behavior. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 13:40-14:05)*
- Public and philosophical lines: a sociological model of disclosure impact with mitigation strategies prepared beforehand, and codifying ethics for a hierarchy of unequal civilizations (from "special relativity" to "general relativity" ethics). *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 14:05-14:54)*
- Scientific and private lines: methods to analyze materials (Nell mentions using roughly 339 isotopes instead of the 118 elements), wargaming consortiums to study recovered material, legal standards for intellectual property rights, and analysis of the economic impact if new energy sources were developed. *([gerb/nell](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-14 - SOL Foundation - Karl Nell - A Key Figure in UAP Disclosure.md>) 14:54-15:31)*
