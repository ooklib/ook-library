Gerb calls content-only SAPs "one of the biggest tools of the trade" and states that if viewers
take away only one concept from this two-part investigation, it should be this one. A
content-only SAP is **not a type of SAP and not a protection level** (unlike acknowledged/
unacknowledged/waived) — it is a **budgetary and administrative designation**.
*([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 31:06–31:21, 1:30:51–1:31:21)*

## Claims

- Defined (per AFI 116-701) as "a descriptive term used to describe a SAP or any sub element
  that contains information only and either has no funding associated with it or its funding is
  managed as part of the Air Force corporate budget process." DoDI 5205.11 similarly describes
  content SAPs as protecting "discrete Critical Program Information (CPI)."
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:31:52–1:32:33)*
- A content-only SAP holds/protects classified information (CPI) but carries **zero dedicated
  budget** — funding either doesn't exist or is rolled into generic DoD/armed-forces/executive
  budgets — and therefore carries **zero reporting requirements under Title 10 or Title 50**.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:30:51–1:31:52, 3:12:56–3:13:12)*
- Gerb's reconstructed mechanism: legacy UFO retrieval/exploitation activity, historically run
  out of the National Security Council's covert-action apparatus (see
  [[covert-action-program]]), is designated **non-covert action** as a White House
  [[special-access-program|SAP]]. Because it is not generated as a DoD SAP, it falls outside 10
  US Code 119; because it is designated non-covert, it falls outside 50 US Code 3093. The
  resulting White House SAP exists "in a gray area between Title 10 and Title 50" with **no
  statutory reporting obligation**, while still answering directly to the NSC or other cognizant
  executive authority — achieved in part via unitary-executive-theory arguments Gerb attributes
  to Eisenhower and Truman. Making that SAP **content-only** then removes the budget itself from
  the program record, eliminating the last remaining paper trail.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:29:00–1:34:20)*
- The actual crash-retrieval/reverse-engineering work is not conducted *within* the content-only
  SAP itself — the content-only SAP or its umbrella merely holds the CPI, while attributable
  activities/compartments sit below it. Gerb ties this structure directly to several alleged
  funding-obfuscation mechanisms: "haircuts" taken across other classified/black programs,
  Independent Research and Development (IRAD) misappropriation by contractors, self-funding
  cutout companies (e.g. [[e-systems|E-Systems]], formerly LTV Aerospace — a CIA cutout), and (per
  [[david-grush|David Grush]]'s Fox News interview) money laundered through criminal enterprises
  later referred to the Department of Justice. Gerb also floats "educated guesses" that haircut
  funds may pass through agencies like the US Forest Service or Bureau of Land Management.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:34:37–1:35:31, 2:39:52–2:44:24)*
- Corroborating detail from the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]]: Vice Admiral
  [[thomas-wilson|Thomas Wilson]] found the legacy program record within the then-OUSDA&T
  contained **zero budget information** (kept separately "for audit purposes") — which Gerb calls
  "probably the most critical piece of the entire Wilson-Davis notes," directly corroborating the
  content-only mechanism years before he had a name for it. Per the notes, Gen. Michael Costanik
  separately stated the legacy program's *security* budget alone ran 2–3x (at times 6–7x) the
  program's actual (attributable) budget — a figure Gerb argues is consistent with, not
  contradictory to, a content-only-SAP funding structure. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  57:48–58:31, 1:09:12–1:09:52)*
- Gerb states the same mechanism ("Yankee White"-style White House SAP made non-covert, then
  content-only) explains how [[david-grush|Grush]]'s Judicial Watch remark about "which reported
  special access programs and controlled access programs were essentially covering it up ...
  performing financial fraud ... misappropriating funds and then taking haircuts across other
  black programs to funnel the money ... to keep it off books" fits together.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:39:52–2:40:32)*
