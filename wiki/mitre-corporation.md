MITRE Corporation is a nonprofit, semi-private [[ffrdc|FFRDC]] operator, "spun up" by the US
Air Force in 1958 out of [[mit-lincoln-laboratory|MIT Lincoln Laboratory]] to build the SAGE
national air defense system. Publicly known as "the most important company you've never heard
of," MITRE operates six FFRDCs and 15 internal "MITRE Labs" innovation centers, headquartered
in McLean ("MLAN"), Virginia near [[crystal-city-virginia|Crystal City]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 9:41–11:33,
41:27–46:13)*

This page catalogs UAP Gerb's central allegation: that MITRE has, since its founding, secretly
served as the lead R&D/program-management institution for two Navy-sponsored UFO-related
legacy programs. **These are unverified claims from a single investigative source**, based on
Gerb's stated ~2 years of research and unnamed engineer/program-manager testimony.

## Public history

- Publicly stood up in 1958 by the Air Force out of MIT Lincoln Labs to architect/operate SAGE
  (Semi-Automatic Ground Environment), the US's first computer-based national air defense
  system, feeding NORAD. Workforce of 7,000+, 65%+ with advanced degrees. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 11:15–11:49,
  31:06–31:18)*
- Name is literally a play on "MIT" (founders liked how it sounded), **not** a reference to the
  bishop's mitre headwear. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 32:06–32:20, 2:09:04–2:09:19)*
- Modeled directly on [[rand-corporation|RAND Corporation]]: no capital stock, no profit
  motive, no hardware to sell, insulated from competitive bidding and conflicts of interest.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 32:20–32:48)*
- First technical director [[robert-r-everett]] came from sensitive MIT Navy programs
  including [[project-whirlwind]] — Gerb stresses MITRE's Navy technical bloodline predates
  its Air Force administrative origin. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 35:47–36:10)*
- Sixth Secretary of Defense under Eisenhower, [[neil-mcelroy]], asked MITRE's founders to
  build in "flexibility to take on the Department of Defense's requirements as we develop
  them." *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 35:12–35:26)*
- From founding, caught in an inter-service "roles and missions" power struggle among Army,
  Navy, and Air Force, each fearing loss of control over automated defense systems; MITRE acted
  as the neutral trusted integrator. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 36:17–37:31)*
- Receives ~99% of funding from the US federal government (per Gerb), like other FFRDCs.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 38:27–38:42)*
- Modern footprint: ~43 campuses/field offices across 28 states; HQ near Crystal City,
  McLean, VA. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 38:48–39:03)*
- Operates six FFRDCs today: National Cybersecurity FFRDC (NIST), Health FFRDC (HHS), Homeland
  Security Systems Engineering and Development Institute (DHS), Center for Enterprise
  Modernization (Treasury/IRS), Center for Advanced Aviation Systems Development (FAA), and the
  [[mitre-national-security-engineering-center|National Security Engineering Center]] (NSEC,
  formerly "C3I FFRDC," DoD/USD R&E — the one Gerb ties to legacy programs). *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 41:27–42:40)*
- Also runs 15 internal "MITRE Labs" applied-research/innovation centers, distinct from the
  government-sponsored FFRDCs. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 45:05–46:13)*

## Claim — 10th-anniversary Roswell founding thesis

- Gerb alleges MITRE was "partially established upon the 10-year anniversary of the Roswell,
  New Mexico crash retrieval" — an evaluation of recovered materials at
  [[wright-patterson-air-force-base|Wright-Patterson]] after 10 years allegedly created the
  need for a specialized think tank to derive "war-altering capabilities" from exploitable
  materials. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 12:07–12:32, 1:02:36–1:02:55)*
- Frames this as timed with [[air-force-material-command|Air Research and Development Command
  (ARDC)]] taking over R&D functions from Air Material Command in 1951 and being "practically
  inseparable" from MITRE during its formative years. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:06:56–1:07:34)*
- Parallels this timing to the founding of the [[aerospace-corporation|Aerospace Corporation]]
  (spun off from TRW ~10.5 years after Roswell) and to [[philip-j-corso]]'s claims about the
  Army's Foreign Technology Division under [[arthur-trudeau]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:03:23–1:04:55)*
- Gerb states this Roswell-exploitation program (Legacy Activity 1) does **not** concern
  producing craft, power/propulsion, or exotic materials — only technology exploitation and
  RDT&E of a "suite of war-altering capabilities," and has been Navy- (not Air Force-)
  administered since 1958 despite MITRE's Air Force founding. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 58:44–59:08, 1:07:41–1:07:54)*

## Claim — secrecy architecture ("the onion model")

- MITRE is described as "the paragon" that originated/innovated the legacy programs' "onion"
  layered-secrecy model. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 15:53–16:01, 48:39–48:57)*
- MITRE maintains an official Special Security Office (SSO) at its MLAN, VA campus for standard
  SCI/SAP work, but Gerb alleges a separate, **hidden** SSO exists at unlisted MITRE satellite
  "S-numbered" sites near Crystal City (not the famous "S4"), staffed by personnel operating
  under non-official cover (NOC) whose true identities are known only to the MITRE CEO and SSO
  director. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 51:54–53:55)*
- Claims the hidden SSO is exempt from audits, with members instructed to tell auditors "we are
  lollipop manufacturers" if questioned; program funding was allegedly skimmed from acknowledged
  MITRE/Navy projects. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 54:14–54:39)*
- Cites [[john-craven]]'s "seventh veil" analogy — even cleared personnel can't know if they've
  reached the innermost compartment — as apt to MITRE's internal structure, where e.g. an
  engineer tracking anomalous radar signatures may not know he's really tracking a USO.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 55:44–57:26)*
- Alleges that in **2011**, MITRE's hidden SSO ordered that new program personnel be briefed
  only on what they needed for their immediate task, cutting off newcomers from program history,
  chain of custody, and past breakthroughs — a change Gerb ties to the introduction of CIA as a
  new IC mission partner that year, and possibly untrusted by DoD/Navy incumbents.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:33:35–1:35:31)*
- Alleges that in **2017**, MITRE lost full control of/leadership over the reverse-engineering
  program, coinciding with [[james-clapper]] as DNI absorbing DoD legacy programs into a wider
  IC/[[national-security-council|NSC]] crash-retrieval portfolio, possibly connected to
  [[immaculate-constellation]]. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:35:39–1:37:20, 2:15:52–2:16:19)*
- Claims MITRE periodically destroys legacy-program records at the Fairfax County, VA Waste
  Management Depot, asking the depot to vacate for hours so MITRE personnel can shred/burn
  documents ("shred parties"). *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:22:29–1:22:40, 2:13:31–2:14:05)*
