Per [[eyes-wide-open]], the Saigon Military Mission was the first CIA team posted in the former French colony, sent by [[allen-dulles]] on 1 June 1954, weeks before Geneva was finalized, under the military cover of the Military Assistance Advisory Group (host abbreviation "MAG"; ASR "mag"). It was led by [[edward-lansdale]]. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 14:23-15:04)*

## Claims

- Unique because it "completely circumvented the usual CIA hierarchy": "more of an Allen Dulles operation than a CIA operation", his personal project; Lansdale reported only to Dulles. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 15:04-15:18)*
- Its monthly journal by Lansdale, leaked in the Pentagon Papers, covers attempts to build indigenous anti-communist paramilitaries in the South and stay-behind units in the North while bolstering Diem. It centers on Operation Passage to Freedom, a joint CIA and Naval intelligence operation resettling some 300,000-800,000 mostly Catholic refugees from North to South; the host says Diem then favored the Catholic newcomers in land redistribution over Buddhist residents. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 30:14-31:20)*
- Lansdale's journal admits the Viet Minh's relations with the population "has been exemplary" and calls the South Vietnamese army "adept at coercing the population" (ASR: "coing") and providing girls; the host reads this as Lansdale conceding the Viet Minh had won over most Vietnamese. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 31:26-31:58)*
- Lansdale and his team also "consistently came in conflict" with the Ngo brothers' independence; his role as Diem's premier adviser ended in 1957, before what the host calls widespread corruption and narcotics activity. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 52:16-52:46)*
- Discrepancy note: LAOS 2 glosses MAG as the Military Assistance Advisory Group set up by the CIA (see [[edward-lansdale]]); this episode says the CIA team operated "under the Saigon Military Mission" with the MAAG as cover. Host wording is loose about the two names. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 14:29-14:58)*
