A Pentagon press conference in July 1952 held by USAF Maj. Gen. John Samford (director of intelligence) with Roger M. Ramey (director of operations), in the wake of the [[washington-dc-ufo-incident-1952]]. Per [[uap-gerb]] (who says "Lt. General" in the chapter title).

## Claims

- Gerb says the Washington sightings drew 148 national newspapers over six months, and notes Ramey is the general who allegedly ordered Jesse Marcel to pose with fake wreckage at [[roswell-incident-1947]]. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 8:16-9:18)*
- Samford explained most reports away as hoaxes, misidentified friendly aircraft, meteorological or electrical phenomena or light apparitions, but conceded a percentage came from credible observers of "relatively incredible things", and denied the sightings related to any secret US development. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 9:18-9:56)*
- Gerb sets this against the [[robertson-panel-1953]], which he says reversed Samford's stance the following year. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 9:56-10:13)*

## Relationships

- Responds to the [[washington-dc-ufo-incident-1952]]; followed by the [[robertson-panel-1953]] in Gerb's narrative.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-1)

- Gerb notes Gen. [[roger-ramey]] gave the 1952 press release with Samford, connecting him to the 1947 Roswell retraction. *([gerb/whistleblowers-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-05 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.1].md>) 27:30-27:48)*
