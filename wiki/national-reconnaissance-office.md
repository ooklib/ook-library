The National Reconnaissance Office develops, acquires, launches, and operates US intelligence
satellites and ground systems ("America's eyes and ears in space"), reporting jointly to the
Secretary of Defense and the Director of National Intelligence. One of the US's "big six"
intelligence agencies (alongside CIA, DIA, NSA, NGA, and the unacknowledged
[[national-underwater-reconnaissance-office|NURO]]). Founded 1961 to consolidate US Air Force and
CIA reconnaissance activities after the 1960 U-2 shootdown; its own existence was denied/
classified for over 30 years, until official declassification in 1992.
*([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 9:52–10:43, 12:50–13:12, 14:52–16:20)*

**This page catalogs UAP Gerb's central allegation**, spread across two videos: that the NRO is
listed as a "program administrator" agency within his [[ufo-legacy-programs|legacy program
pyramid]], and (per this dedicated video) that it plays a unique, critical role among UFO legacy
programs — supplying satellite/space-based ISR to monitor craft of unknown origin, providing
direct intelligence support to crash-retrieval teams, and (Gerb argues) being among the first US
agencies equipped with derivative/alien-reproduction-vehicle technology for ISR missions. **These
are unverified claims from a single investigative source.**

## Public history

- Traces its heritage to WWII aerial IMINT/SIGINT collection; founded in response to the 1960 U-2
  shootdown, consolidating Air Force and CIA collection under one office. First co-directors:
  Joseph V. Charyk (Air Force) and CIA officer Richard Bissell Jr. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  15:54–17:06)*
- Operated the Corona spy-satellite program (launched 1960, declassified 1995); flew the A-12
  Oxcart with the CIA (1962); launched the Poppy SIGINT satellites with the Naval Research
  Laboratory; achieved the first orbital radar imagery via the Quill satellite (1964); launched
  the KH-11 Kennen "Keyhole" digital-imaging satellite (1976). *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  19:21–21:36)*
- Restructured from Programs A/B/C/D into functional directorates at 1992 declassification;
  Program B (joint NRO/CIA DS&T SIGINT satellites) was ground-based at
  [[pine-gap-australia|Pine Gap, Australia]]. As of 2025, has completed several hundred NROL
  launches; NRO director Chris Scales stated 100 satellites were launched June 2023–December
  2024. Partners with SpaceX and United Launch Alliance (a 2006 Lockheed Martin/Boeing joint
  venture) for rideshare launches. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 22:16–24:43)*
