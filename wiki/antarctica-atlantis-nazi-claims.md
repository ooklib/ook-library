Jorjani's claim that Thule/Atlantis was in Antarctica, that the Nazis knew it and went there in 1938-39, that Hess's flight and Highjump followed, and that Atlantean survivors may be the Nordics' base. Speculative; several elements (black octagon, Pirisi map, 2050 remote viewing) are second-hand or contested.

## Claims

- The [[thule-society]] took Vril from Bulwer-Lytton's *The Coming Race* seriously; Jorjani found no independent Vril Society, only claims of women mediums in contact with "supermen". Thule is the Germanic Atlantis, said by Blavatsky and Steiner in various senses to be the Aryan homeland; the Nazis viewed Steiner's Anthroposophy as their principal rival and burned the first Goetheanum. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:31:11, 1:38:16, 1:39:56, 1:41:04)*
- Plato's Atlantis, as an island continent in the "world ocean" the size of Libya plus Asia, fits Antarctica on the Piri Reis and Oronce Fine maps (two islands, later confirmed by subglacial topography); the dating (about 9,600 BC) matches the end of the ice age, meltwater pulse 1B, via Charles Hapgood's earth-crust displacement theory (Antarctica once where Argentina is). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:42:04, 1:43:46, 1:44:15)*
- The Maya legends Tullan/Atlan; Avestan flood myth of an Aryan island continent that froze over. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:46:47, 1:59:15)*
- The 1938-39 German Antarctic Expedition dropped swastika flags with spears; the nominal reason was margarine. Jorjani (incorrectly stating that Hess went) says the man sent to Antarctica later flew to Britain: see [[rudolf-hess]]. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:47:30, 1:48:24)*
- A Navy SEAL told Linda Moulton Howe of a black polished octagonal structure near McMurdo with a swastika on its 20-foot door, stacked octagons descending into ice-trapped antediluvian ruins, reachable also by US submarines under the ice; Jorjani says it may be disinformation. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:51:04, 1:52:14, 1:53:22)*
- [[operation-highjump]] (1946) followed interception of German submarine traffic to Patagonia; Byrd allegedly warned in Chile of a third world war with pole-to-pole aircraft; Antarctica has been a no-fly area since 1957 and skiers were allegedly turned away by black helicopters. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:53:38, 1:54:39, 1:56:36)*
- Jorjani suspects the Nordics' principal subterranean base is Antarctica, with surviving Atlantean facilities possibly warmed by thermal vents; a friend allegedly saw a black pyramid (Michels). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:57:16, 1:58:17, 1:58:31)*
- Remote viewers (Stefan Schwartz's group, [[lynn-buchanan]]) allegedly saw a permanent 200-300 ft sea-level rise by 2050, implying the Antarctic ice sheet melting via crustal slippage or an asteroid; Michels raises Eric Hecker's neutrino-emitter/earthquake claim (see [[neutrino-light-detector-claims]]), and Jorjani says he believes in tectonic weapons ([[nikola-tesla]]). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:03:18, 2:04:58, 2:05:45, 2:06:08)*
- Tunguska: an unnamed prominent Stargate remote viewer (maybe McMoneagle) allegedly saw people fleeing a totalitarian society in a saucer with an altitude-triggered bomb. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:07:10)*
