Santo Trafficante was a Mafia figure who controlled Cuban heroin-processing labs central to
[[operation-x]]'s supply chain. Eyes Wide Open flags him as a figure who "will continue to come
up especially in relation to the Kennedy assassination" in later episodes.

## Claims

- The Sicilian mob (organized by [[lucky-luciano]]) shipped pure heroin synthesized by
  [[schiaparelli]] in crates of fake wax oranges or sardine cans to Trafficante's Cuban labs,
  where it was cut with sugar to increase profit margins before being shipped on to New York.
  Cuba served as a heroin hub for this pipeline until Castro's 1959 revolution expelled the
  Italian-American mobsters, which the host says only strengthened the bond between the US
  security state and the Mafia. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 27:30-28:09)*

## Relationships

- Processed [[operation-x]] heroin at Cuban labs supplied by [[lucky-luciano]]'s Sicilian mob.

## Additional claims (War Conspiracy 2)


- Host: the CIA's major Mafia connection for dope smuggling from Laos and Burma in the 1960s; rumored to bring in Southeast Asian heroin; tied to [[jack-ruby]] and [[charles-rebozo]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 40:09-40:25, 50:08-50:31)*

## Additional claims (CIA-Mafia Legend)

- Host: introduced to [[robert-maheu]] as "Joe" at the Miami Kenilworth Hotel, head of the Florida outfit; a "Lansky associate"; among the hardest hit by Castro's nationalizations (Tampa, Florida). *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:19-1:44, 34:09-34:23, 59:03-59:20)*
- Host (citing [[peter-dale-scott]]): the CIA protected him and [[sam-giancana]] after [[robert-f-kennedy]]'s 1960 book named them. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:14:42-1:15:06)*
- Host (citing Hougan): possible double agent for Castro (cushy jailing and release; Castro skipped his favorite restaurant the day the poison-pill plot was to be executed); alternative: Roselli's remark (via 1981 Chicago Tribune / Jimmy Fratianno) that Trafficante "conned everybody" and "just sat on it". See [[cia-mafia-legend]]. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:16:19-1:19:33)* Named as the CIA's main mob ally later in Southeast Asian heroin importing. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:16:19-1:16:34, 1:17:23-1:17:32)*
- Not called before the [[church-committee]]; testified to the HSCA in 1978, downplaying his role as a "Spanish interpreter", denying involvement in JFK's murder; died of natural causes in 1987 at 72. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:21:40-1:22:28)*

## Additional claims (Road to Dallas Ch. 4)

- Moved to Cuba for a time after 1955 and became the second most prominent mob operator in Havana after [[meyer-lansky]], owning the entire Sans Souci club (ASR "Sasu Sea"), major portions of the Tropicana, and rumored to have put money into the Havana Hilton. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 13:16-13:48)*
- Stayed after Castro took power to test a deal; arrested by Castro's forces and detained for most of 1959. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 15:06-15:25)*
- Shared a Cuban cell with [[loran-hall]] (per Hall's FBI file: "Hall shared a cell in the Cuban jail with Santos Trafficante, well-known gambler and hoodlum of Tampa and Miami"); [[gerry-patrick-hemming]] first met Hall as a fellow prisoner around then and knew Hall as "the cellmate of the far more prominent" Trafficante. Host says the relationship carried over when both were back in Florida (promised for later). *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 15:25-17:13)*
- Per Jim Hougan (*Spooks*), citing a CIA report, he was "frequently visited by an American gangster named Ruby"; the host infers this was [[jack-ruby]] given Ruby's closeness to [[lewis-mcwillie]]. Host asks why Hall was never asked at the Warren Commission whether he recalled Ruby. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 17:13-18:35)*
- Ran, through [[norman-rothman]] and McWillie, the gun-running network to Cuba. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 21:57-22:30)*
