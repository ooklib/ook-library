NSC 68 (April 1950) is the [[national-security-council]] memorandum drafted by [[paul-nitze]] that
[[eyes-wide-open]] identifies as the policy blueprint for perpetual peacetime military spending and
the "rollback" strategy, and thus as a foundation of the [[military-industrial-complex]]. The
episode's bibliography links the document directly.

## Claims

- Two courses were open by 1950: the Truman internationalists' **containment** (encirclement of
  socialist states, NATO as a bulwark; solidified by the Soviet bomb, since aggression risked
  nuclear war) and hawks' **rollback**, coined in NSC 68 after the loss of China. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 34:19-35:28)*
- Host quotes it: (a) defend the Western Hemisphere and essential allied areas so their war-making
  capabilities can develop; (b) provide and protect a mobilization base while offensive forces are
  built up; (c) conduct offensive operations to destroy vital elements of Soviet war-making capacity
  and keep the enemy off balance until the full offensive strength of the US and allies can be
  brought to bear. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 36:41-37:19)*
- It recommended a "substantial increase in expenditures for military purposes" and in military
  assistance programs, with a "reduction of federal expenditures for purposes other than defense and
  foreign assistance," plus "increased taxes." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 37:19-37:57)*
- The host calls it "any hawk's wet dream" and the roadmap to a militarized state; June 1950's
  invasion of South Korea gave the Truman administration an immediate opportunity to act on it
  ([[korean-war]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 37:57-38:35)*
