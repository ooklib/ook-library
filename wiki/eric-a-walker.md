Eric A. Walker was president of Penn State University 1956-1971, chairman of the Institute for Defense Analyses, and executive secretary of the Defense Research Board 1950-51. *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 14:00-14:30)* UAP Gerb treats him as the man [[robert-sarbacher]] described as "smug" from Philadelphia at the Wright Field crash meeting. *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 12:37-13:10)*

## Claims

- Walker told researchers he attended the c.1949-50 meeting on military crash and body retrieval; per Gerb he also confirmed a Majestic-12-like group, contact with NHI, that reverse engineering was a waste because the technology is far beyond human understanding, and involvement in the 1965 Kecksburg crash ("we went there with two from the military but not on duty"). *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 14:39-15:37, 17:17-17:55)*
- 1980s interview with [[william-steinman]]: said he had known of MJ-12 for 40 years, that Steinman was "fighting windmills" and delving into an area he could "do absolutely nothing about". *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 15:37-16:09, 19:56-20:44)*
- 1990: three recorded calls with [[armen-victorian]] covering the 1978 Bolivia crash and insect-like bodies; Walker said MJ-12 exists but the documents are likely forgeries, was cryptic about ESP, and confirmed Bush's involvement. *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 16:09-17:17)*
