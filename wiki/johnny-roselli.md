Johnny Roselli ("handsome Johnny") is the Chicago-outfit mobster [[eyes-wide-open]] names as the mob's counterpart in the 1960 Brown Derby meeting with [[robert-maheu]]. The host calls him "an operator", not merely a mobster, and one of the major liaisons between the [[national-crime-syndicate]] and the "respectable American establishment".

## Claims

- Born in south-central Italy in 1905 as "Philipo Sako" (ASR; normalization to Filippo Sacco is my inference); emigrated to Boston as a child, joined [[sam-giancana]]'s Chicago outfit in the early 1920s, and cut ties with his family. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 57:24-57:45)*
- Sent to Los Angeles in the late 1920s to expand Chicago's gambling interests; Giancana's west-coast emissary during the 1940s wire-service takeovers; forged ties between Hollywood stars and the mob, including the Rat Pack (Sinatra, Dean Martin, Sammy Davis Jr., Peter Lawford). Jim Hougan calls him "the Don Giovanni of the mafia". *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 57:45-58:48)*
- Made his way to Cuba in the 1940s as a casino operative in the Lansky and Giancana joints, then to Las Vegas by the mid-1950s as one of Giancana's main men; had contacts in Cuba the CIA could tap. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 34:39-34:55, 58:48-59:11)*
- Sent to Los Angeles as part of the wire-service expansion (the host cites [[peter-dale-scott]]), the same process that sent [[jack-ruby]] to Dallas. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 53:22-53:53)*
- Met [[robert-maheu]] at the Brown Derby in September 1960 and arranged the Miami meeting with [[sam-giancana]] and [[santo-trafficante]]. See [[cia-mafia-assassination-plots]].
- Testified to the [[church-committee]] in June and September 1975 (and again in April 1976), claiming he himself had traveled to Cuba in the 1960s to kill [[fidel-castro]]; missing from July 28 1976, found August 7 1976, aged 71, in a 55-gallon oil drum floating in a bay near Miami, days before a fourth scheduled testimony. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:20:45-1:21:40)*
- **The "scam" remark:** the host cites a 7 Jan 1981 Chicago Tribune interview with LA mobster Jimmy "the Weasel" Fratianno ("frano"/"Frano" per ASR), who said Roselli told him the whole thing "had been a scam" and that Trafficante "never did nothing but bull everybody". See [[cia-mafia-legend]]. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:17:40-1:19:08)*
