The US primary ballistic missile early-warning satellite system, first launched in 1970 (DSP-1) and now part of the US Space Force. In [[gerb-fastwalkers-video]] [[uap-gerb]] argues it is the collection system behind fastwalker data since 1972, and cites two alleged cases ([[fastwalker-dsp-case-1984]], [[tehran-ufo-incident-1976]]) plus the email from [[bob-fish]].

## Claims

- Gerb says the 1972 database build-up is a result of DSP; DSP serves as the primary missile-warning system, its first satellite went up in 1970, information is relayed to [[norad]] and it is now part of the Space Force. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 5:56-6:21)*
- Fish's 2015 email says the USAF DSP program is one of the government programs collecting hard data on unidentified flying objects and cites the 2014 DSP fact sheet as evidence collection continues. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 8:43-8:50, 10:06-10:31)*
- The DSP satellites have infrared capacity to spot small heat sources on Earth, which Gerb aligns with the 1972 paper's reflected light and heat detection. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 12:02-12:26)*
- Gerb reads a DSP scan cadence of once per 10 seconds from a quoted email (238 scans, so 39.7 minutes of tracking in the Tehran case). *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 16:29-16:46)*
- Aerospace Corporation: the fact sheet line that researchers at [[aerospace-corporation]] used DSP to develop parts of a hazard support system is read by Gerb as a possible link to covert UAP study. *([gerb/fastwalkers](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-27 - FASTWALKERS - UFOs Outside Earth.md>) 10:14-10:39, 20:24-20:47)*
