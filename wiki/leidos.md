Leidos is the entity that resulted from [[science-applications-international-corporation|
SAIC]]'s 27 September 2013 split: SAIC kept its name for a new $4B spinoff, while the original
company was renamed Leidos — driven by federal acquisition conflict-of-interest rules that
barred bidding on new contracts given SAIC's existing contract volume.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 16:59–17:41)*

## Claims

- 16 August 2016: merged with Lockheed Martin's Information Systems & Global Solutions
  division, acquiring it for $5B; that division was renamed Leidos Government Services Inc. on
  30 November 2017. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 17:41–17:58)*
- Gerb treats "SAIC" as shorthand for both SAIC and Leidos throughout his investigation.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 17:33–17:41)*
