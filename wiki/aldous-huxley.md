Aldous Huxley (1894–1963), later the author of *Brave New World*, briefly taught French at Eton
around 1917. Green & Pleasant highlights this as the point of contact between [[george-orwell]]
(one of his pupils) and the eugenics-adjacent circles surrounding the [[fabian-society]], since
Huxley's brother [[julian-huxley]] led the British [[eugenics-society]]. Transcribed in the raw
source as "Aldis Huxley."

## Claims

- Around 1917, taught French at Eton for about a year — described as nearly blind and unable to
  keep classroom order, but brilliant with language. One of his pupils was Eric Blair, later
  known as George Orwell
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 4:06, 4:21)*.
- Wrote *Brave New World*, which the video says drew directly on eugenic ideas circulating in
  his brother Julian's circles
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 4:45)*.

## Relationships

- Brother of [[julian-huxley]] (president of the [[eugenics-society]]).
- Taught [[george-orwell]] French at Eton, c. 1917.
