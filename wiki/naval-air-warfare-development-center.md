The Navy's center of excellence for air combat training at Fallon, Nevada, formed in 1996 by merging the Naval Strike Warfare Center, the Fighter Weapons School (Top Gun) and the Carrier Airborne Early Warning Weapons School (Top Dome), and running the Fallon Range Training Complex. In [[gerb-castle-video]] [[uap-gerb]] treats it as the main Navy player in his [[castle-triangle-origin-thesis]]. ASR renders it "Nautic" and "N AWDC".

## Claims (gerb/castle)

- Gerb notes Gen. Krulak asked its predecessor to send air crews to the [[hunter-warrior-advanced-warfighting-experiment-1997]], and calls this an adjunct bolted on late, when the Navy allegedly saw a chance to exercise its ARV and special mission units. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 58:57-1:00:43)*
- He says NAWDC works closely with naval intelligence ([[office-of-naval-intelligence]] and [[office-of-naval-research]] are ones he identifies with Navy legacy programs) and runs a range complex just north of the [[nevada-test-and-training-range]] used by [[groom-lake]], and notes a 2002 Fallon triangle sighting report of a huge, silent 20 mph craft with seven to nine dim nodes. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:04:26-1:07:18)*
- He points to Randy Anderson's claim about electrogravitic triangles stored at Area 51 auxiliary sites and to Keyport's mine-storage depot near Fallon, and argues the triangle could have been stationed at Fallon, [[naval-air-station-lemoore]] or Edwards. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:53:32-1:54:34)*
- Gerb notes Top Gun is a fighter school rather than a test pilot school, undercutting [[edgar-fuche]]'s claim of test pilot elites from it. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:55:13-1:55:34)*
