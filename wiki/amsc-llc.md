AMSC LLC, jointly owned by Newport News Shipbuilding (a [[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]]
division) and [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]], was formed in 1999 — the
same year Northrop purchased Teledyne Ryan. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:28:26–1:28:51)*

## Claims

- Grew from $100M revenue (2000) to $500M (2007), contracting primarily with the Navy for naval
  architecture, marine engineering, ship-systems assessment, maintenance engineering, waterfront
  maintenance, acquisition support, and C4I installation. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:28:51–1:29:23)*
- By the 2010s, a major contractor for Navy modernization; 2015 IDIQ contracts north of $83M
  for naval surface warfare centers. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:29:37–1:30:01)*
- Gerb connects this to [[randy-anderson|Randy Anderson]]'s 2012–2015 testimony about the
  off-world technologies division below [[naval-surface-warfare-center-crane|NSWC Crane]].
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:30:01–1:30:18)*
