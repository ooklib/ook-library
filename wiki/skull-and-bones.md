Skull and Bones is the Yale secret society that [[eyes-wide-open]] uses in this episode as an
emblem of how "ingrained narcotics smuggling was within the circles of the US power elite." It is
the first secret-society reference in the Eyes Wide Open thread with a substantive claim (earlier
mentions on [[henry-luce]] and [[george-h-w-bush]] were passing).

## Claims

- [[henry-luce]] joined the society when he enrolled at Yale in 1916; the host mentions it to show
  Luce was "already a certified member of the American ruling class." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 11:24-11:44)*
- The host says Skull and Bones "traces its history back to the Chinese opium trade": its founder and
  first benefactor, William H. Russell, scion of the Russell family, made the family fortune in the
  opium trade; the "Russell Trust" is the business entity behind the society and funded the
  construction of the "tomb" that allegedly still holds meetings. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 11:36-12:09)*
- Host's inference: its history as "a byproduct of American opium profiteering" shows narcotics
  smuggling was long ingrained among the US elite, so when the Cold War revived the opium trade in
  Southeast Asia "nothing had really changed." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 12:09-12:29)*
- Video description lists a Washington Post article on Skull and Bones among its sources. (Source
  header only.)
