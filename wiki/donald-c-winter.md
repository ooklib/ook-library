Donald C. Winter joined [[trw|TRW]] in 1972 and by the 1980s had joined [[darpa|DARPA]] under
Ronald Reagan's [[strategic-defense-initiative|Strategic Defense Initiative (SDI)]], where his
primary responsibility was the [[talon-gold|Talon Gold]] program. He later returned to TRW as
CEO, served as the 74th United States Secretary of the Navy, and — after Northrop's 2002
acquisition of TRW — became corporate VP and lead of mission systems at
[[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]], the sector Gerb notes was called "the coolest and most
dynamic portfolio in the company" by Northrop senior director of security
[[john-c-freestone|John Freestone]]. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md)
39:00–39:24, 41:26–41:37, 43:19–43:51)*
