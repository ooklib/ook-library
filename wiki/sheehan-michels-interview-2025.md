A roughly 2h46m episode of American Alchemy (published 2025-05-31) in which [[jesse-michels]] and Jeremy Rys ([[jeremy-rys]]) talk with lawyer [[danny-sheehan]]. It opens with clips on the 2025 JFK file release, then spends about 1h45m on Sheehan's chronological account of how the assassination arose from a Nixon-Hughes-mafia Castro plot, followed by shorter segments on lost Philippine treasure, a deathbed S-4 alien account, Scientology and remote viewing, John Mack, and disclosure legislation. Sponsor reads (Ground News, Perfect Jean, Ridge Wallet, iRestore) are ignored.
