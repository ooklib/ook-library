The family of [[osama-bin-laden]] is described in the 911mmry compilation as investors in the [[carlyle-group]], including one of its defense funds, and as beneficiaries of higher post-9/11 defense spending. *(*([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 0:57)*)* [[shafik-bin-laden]], Osama's half-brother, is said to have been at the 9/11-morning Carlyle conference. The compilation says the family had to withdraw from Carlyle once attention focused on the link. *(*([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 1:57)*)*

## Claims

- A commentator finds it "very discomforting" that [[george-h-w-bush]] met the bin Laden family while Osama was a wanted terrorist, well before 9/11. *([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 2:14)*
