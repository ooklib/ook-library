Archival re-upload (Yogsoggeth, April 2011, originally on Google Video) of [[phil-schneider]]'s November 1995 lecture at a Las Vegas UFO conference, about 70 minutes with audience Q&A. He is introduced as having given over 30 lectures and surviving 13 attempts on his life *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 1:01, 1:59)*. The talk is a "mixed bag" of claims: an alien-human treaty, hundreds of deep underground bases, a hidden black budget, alien-derived materials, a New World Order taking orders from hostile aliens, and a survivor's account of a battle at Dulce. He repeatedly urges listeners to do their own homework and "put your skeptical on". All content below is attributed claim, not established fact.

## Claims

- [[greada-treaty-1954]]: alleged Eisenhower-era alien-human treaty of technology for occasional humans and cattle, which he says broke down. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 8:08)*
- Early history: 1909 US cavalry encounter with "horseshoe craft" and small gray "demons" at Truth or Consequences, New Mexico; 1933 government cooperation with French and English on aerial phenomena; a shallow underwater alien base at Bikini used as the reason for the 1946 atomic tests (Operation Crossroads). *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 5:22)*
- [[deep-underground-military-bases]]: 131 bases in the US (127 in the lower 48), average 4.25 cubic miles and 5,700 feet deep, costing 17 to 31 billion dollars each, employing 1,800 to 10,000 people, two new bases per year; all linked by roads and near-Mach-2 maglev trains, coast to coast in about 90 minutes; a 30-cubic-mile base under construction in Sweden costing two trillion dollars, funded by the UN. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 19:46, 22:09, 51:27)*
- Locations named: Nellis (nine bases), Denver area (three, plus [[denver-international-airport]] with eight levels), Fort Collins, Colorado Springs (which he links to Tesla's lab) and a new prison "boot camp" at Cheyenne. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 47:39, 53:13, 53:38)*
- Black budget: over 500 billion dollars per year, one quarter spent on underground bases, funded by CIA drug activity, NSA operations in Panama, South America and Russia; 1.0 to 1.3 trillion dollars every two years; 39 prison camps, two more built every seven months; "United Nations prisoner transfer cars" with 143 shackle positions. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 22:09, 23:25, 37:39, 1:08:51)*
- [[corbomite-and-alien-materials-claims]]: samples of alien-derived materials from crashed saucers, a 140-element periodic table, Phoenix-class submarines, scalenohedral crystals and "Aurora drops", and a Roswell hull fragment given to him at age 14. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 27:29, 36:00)*
- Says the widely circulated Roswell alien-autopsy photographs fail computer grain analysis, giving instead a photograph of a charred alien via Skip Frach of Seattle, whose physician father he says did autopsy work. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 11:40)*
- [[dulce-underground-base-incident-1979]]: his first-person account of the alleged August 1979 Dulce "alien-human war". *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 43:24)*
- [[new-world-order-alien-agenda-claims]]: 11 alien civilizations known to the US military, 9 hostile; the UN and New World Order take orders from hostile aliens; population reduction, martial law and forced labor. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 55:53, 57:22)*
- Weather: the US has modified weather with Russia since 1972 or as early as 1966 and could dissipate hurricanes and tornadoes. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 59:51)*
- Technology gap: military technology runs 44 to 45 years ahead per calendar year, about 1,200 years ahead in total; he suggests the 1943 [[philadelphia-experiment]] as an example. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 1:07:00, 1:07:17)*
- Tunneling: lasers that melt out a tunnel 28 feet wide and 28 feet high at 7 miles per day, versus slow public boring machines. *([yogsoggeth/schneider](<../raw/yogsoggeth/Yogsoggeth - 2011-04-17 - Phil Schneider - November 1995 (Full Video).md>) 1:04:43)*
