Christine Maxwell is [[robert-maxwell]]'s daughter and the twin of [[isabelle-maxwell]], described by oompaville as the sibling who built information businesses. She is alive and has never been charged with anything, as the host stresses. ASR renders the data-mining firm "Kilad" (real name not given in the source). See [[maxwells-data-dynasty-video-2026]].

## Claims

- Her father handed her an information-on-demand business, later plugged into over 250 databases serving businesses, hospitals and labs. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 16:51)*
- She built the Magellan search engine (ASR "Mellin") with Isabelle and Isabelle's husband David Hayden, one of the early-internet search-engine competitors; sold in 1996 to Excite for about $18 million. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 17:05,)*
- In the late 1990s she co-founded a data-mining firm ("Kilad" in ASR) whose software, after 9/11, is said to have powered the FBI's counterterrorism investigative data warehouse of 659 million records. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 17:31,)*
- [[whitney-webb]] reads these businesses as a deliberate intelligence operation controlling sensitive information; the host says the documented facts are public and the intent is Webb's inference. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 19:34,)*
