Alleged July 1953 crash of an egg-shaped craft near Camp Polk, Louisiana, reported by an Army veteran identified only as "HJ" to [[leonard-stringfield]] between 1980 and 1982 (Crash Retrieval Status Report 3). [[uap-gerb]] cites it as a close parallel to [[jonathan-weygandt]]'s 1997 Peru craft ([[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]]). Single anonymous witness; unverified.

## Claims (gerb/weygandt)

- As told by Gerb, then-17-year-old private HJ of B Company saw the egg-shaped object crash at about 7 p.m. during maneuvers; at 15 yards Lieutenant Whitting (whom Gerb says he can place at Camp Polk) ordered his company back to 100 yards. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:33:49-1:34:49)*
- The ground was burned to a brick-like powder and still hot; the craft was as wide as a three-bedroom ranch house (Gerb estimates 35 to 70 ft, close to Weygandt's roughly 66 ft) with no windows and a still-rotating fin along its equator, which Gerb likens to Weygandt's circling light. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:34:49-1:35:22)*
- A special detachment with medics and an ambulance arrived; a body was carried off and three injured occupants, 3.5 to 4 feet tall with large featureless heads and dull green tight suits, were led out "like children" and reacted only to basic stimuli. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:35:22-1:37:18)*
- HJ later heard they were isolated in a hospital and all died, with corpses sent to a medical center near Washington, which Gerb speculates could be Fort Detrick (site of a [[battelle-memorial-institute]]-managed biodefense center) and links to his earlier Dugway claims; he compares the passive occupants to the biological android idea from [[special-forces-witness-infographics]]. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:36:04-1:38:05)*
