The Rendlesham Forest and RAF Bentwaters case is a UK sighting where a craft allegedly landed in a forest in Suffolk near a nuclear-armed US Air Force base during the Cold War. Here it appears via [[ross-coulthart]]'s interview with [[larry-warren]].

## Claims

- Coulthart describes Colonel Charles Halt, deputy base commander, as impeccable and as having seen a craft moving through the trees; Halt was angry with Warren for allegedly confabulating a separate event. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:55:45-1:57:18)*
- Warren describes a later event in an open field, with beings emerging from the craft, British policemen photographing, and US military filming, in an elaborate cover-up, and places Halt there. Coulthart offers a prosaic reconciliation, that Warren conflated the forest incident earlier that evening with the later field event. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:57:18-1:58:31)*
