A secret trust that [[danny-sheehan]] says Harry Truman set up in Houston in 1945 to hold about US$1.2 trillion (12 of 176 buried caches) of Japanese gold, platinum, silver and jewels found in the Philippines, used afterwards to fund covert operations and anti-communist politicians; see also [[yamashita-gold]]. Sheehan is explicit it has nothing to do with UFOs, contrary to what Michels heard. Unverified.
