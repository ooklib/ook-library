The 1561 Nuremberg celestial phenomenon is described by [[uap-gerb]] as one of the first mass UFO sightings in recorded history (the video's chapter title says 1562; Gerb says 1561). *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 22:00-22:14)*

## Claims

- A broadsheet in the Holy Roman Empire reportedly described hundreds of globes, cylinders, rods, crosses, crescents, a black spear and cylinders emitting small spheres at sunrise, resembling a battle, plus a black triangular object near the ground followed by a crash. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 22:07-22:47)*
- Skeptics offer Jungian mass hysteria or a sun dog; Gerb finds the sun-dog explanation unlikely and the case perplexing. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 22:47-23:12)*
