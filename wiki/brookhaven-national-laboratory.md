Brookhaven National Laboratory, in Upton, New York, is a DOE-sponsored FFRDC.

## Claims

- Performed [[strategic-defense-initiative|SDI]] lethality/target-hardening work (assessing
  X-ray laser weapon lethality against SDI threats) alongside White Sands Missile Range and
  [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|Los Alamos]], contracted alongside
  [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]]. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:15:22–1:16:06)*
- Alleged (per UFO researcher Leonard Stringfield and the Long Island UFO Network) recipient of
  a large wedge/boomerang-shaped object with unusually low mass, downed by an SDI weapon over
  [[mattituck-bay-crash-1989|Mattituck Bay, Long Island]] on 29 September 1989 and transported
  to Brookhaven; a Brookhaven scientist source ("Dr. Nick") stated the downing weapon was built
  by AIL Electronics. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:14:38–1:15:22)*
- Gerb separately flags a "perplexing" 1992 Long Island UFO crash case also possibly involving
  Brookhaven, which he says he may revisit. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:13:05–1:13:23)*
