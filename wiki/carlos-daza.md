Carlos Daza is, per [[james-fox]], a geography professor and ultralight pilot who says he watched a cigar-shaped craft crash near Varginha in January 1996, handled a piece of its wreckage, and was threatened at gunpoint and then by men in suits. Fox says he vanished from public view for 26 years.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox says Daza was the original crash witness who came forward once in 1996 and then disappeared; researchers found him and got him to consider coming forward around 2021. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 20:51-23:27, 30:04-30:27)*
- The film shows Daza returning to the crash site for the first time in 26-27 years; he initially could not find it, and after a drone showed a small white house he ran to the spot and broke down in tears. Fox says the moment was captured live and not reenacted. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 23:36-26:10)*
- Daza's account (relayed by Fox): about 4:30-5:00 a.m. on the way to fly he saw a cigar-shaped craft going down and thought of a military experimental craft; he assumed the crew was in trouble and never thought of extraterrestrials. He found half of the craft intact, roughly half the size of a large school bus, and the rest scattered as torn aluminum-like pieces over a football-field area. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 30:43-31:38)*
- The smell was overwhelming, like ammonia and sulfur, his eyes watered and something like acid burned the grass. He picked up a piece so light he could not feel it that crumpled and reformed when released (Ramsey compares Roswell memory metal). *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 31:38-32:58)*
- Military trucks from the base (the base is transcribed "Eza") arrived from the opposite direction and threatened him at gunpoint to leave; ten to fifteen minutes later, at a gas-station cafe, men in suits told him not to talk. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 32:23-32:47, 30:04-30:27)*
- The craft that crashed had a gash at the rear and white vapour, matching the farm family's account; Fox keeps Daza's stick used to show the craft's motion in his edit room. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 26:36-27:02)*
