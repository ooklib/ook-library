The Special Studies Project (1956-1960) was a Rockefeller Brothers Fund-financed panel to "clarify national objectives" for the post-war era, led by [[nelson-rockefeller]] and co-directed by [[henry-kissinger]]; per [[eyes-wide-open]] it drafted six reports across six panels. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 26:16-27:35)*

## Claims

- Host: it turned the Rockefeller Brothers Fund from philanthropy into "a massive lobbying effort," a private think tank "set on institutionalizing the perpetual war economy and military Keynesianism." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 26:01-26:26)*
- Panel II, "International Security: the Military Aspect," was the most consequential and directed by Kissinger. Named panelists per the host: [[henry-luce]] (Skull and Bones; also called a prominent China lobbyist), [[laurance-rockefeller]], Roswell Gilpatric (future deputy secretary of defense), Townsend Hoopes (ASR "won towns and Hoops"; Skull and Bones; Air Force under secretary 1967-69), and 15 others of 20 named contributors, a "who's who" of contractors and defense researchers: Gordon Dean (General Dynamics VP nuclear energy), James B. Fisk (Executive VP, [[bell-labs]]), Ellis Johnson (Johns Hopkins), Gen. James McCormack (MIT VP) and Thaddeus Walkowicz of [[itek]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 27:44-29:37)*
- The panel reaffirmed [[paul-nitze]]'s 1950 [[nsc-68]] and called for forces for "limited war" and more airlift, and decried post-[[korean-war]] spending cuts; the host reads this as the ruling class wanting "a limited war with unlimited war spending." Fulbright's "nice little war" quote is cited. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 29:52-31:59)*
- Skull and Bones cross-link: two panelists identified by the host as members ([[skull-and-bones]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 27:51-28:22)*
