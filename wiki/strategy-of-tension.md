The 'strategy of tension', per Eyes Wide Open, was the use of false-flag terror attacks by Gladio cells that were blamed on the left in order to build popular support for security measures; the host says it was a core component of Gladio, most famously in Italy's 'years of lead' (1960s-80s). *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 17:14-17:40)*

## Claims

- Plan Bleu papers seized in 1947 allegedly contained an early mention of the strategy. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 17:00-17:14)*
- The main primary evidence is U.S. Army Field Manual 30-31B, a classified appendix; the State Department called it a forgery and likely Soviet hoax, while CIA analyst Ray Cline and many Italian whistleblowers claimed it is real. Eyes Wide Open notes the dispute but says circumstantial evidence from Italy corroborates the strategy. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 17:40-18:28)*
