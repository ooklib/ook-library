The Gehlen Organization was a US-backed paramilitary/intelligence network in West Germany, built
by [[reinhard-gehlen]] out of his WWII military-intelligence apparatus and run under US handlers
after [[allen-dulles]] recruited him as a postwar anti-communist stay-behind asset.

## Claims

- Formed clandestine guerrilla armies out of former fascists and Hitler Youth, organized into
  "werewolf squads" that Eyes Wide Open describes as "ordinary citizens by day that turn into
  ruthless communist killers at night." *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 4:25-4:38)*
- Its "werewolves" were eventually renamed "Gladiators" under the future CIA operation known as
  [[operation-gladio]], and would form the backbone of the postwar European political climate,
  activated at war's end and playing an instrumental role in West German and broader European
  politics; similar secret armies were allegedly formed across the continent.
  *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 4:45-5:07)*

## Relationships

- Founded/led by [[reinhard-gehlen]], recruited by [[allen-dulles]] into US service.
- Its fighters seeded the future [[operation-gladio]] network.

## Additional claims (JFK and the CIA)


- Host: Helms saw memos (Operation Rusty) on absorbing Gehlen's Eastern Front network, opposing integration "largely on the grounds of security"; the network entered West German intelligence and became the BND with [[reinhard-gehlen]] as first director. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 23:20-24:03)*
