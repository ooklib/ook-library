Claims by Ross Andrews (author) and the narration that Himmler and Hitler sought the Ark of the Covenant, Holy Grail and Spear of Destiny, and that the Templars may have hidden such relics in Royston Cave, Hertfordshire. The Templar-hoard speculation is explicitly unproven ("we don't have proof of that").

## Claims

- Himmler wanted to absorb all religion into his own and collect relics; the Nazis already held the Spear of Destiny; millions were spent on expeditions to Tibet and Africa. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 35:15,)*
- Royston Cave may have been a Templar vault en route to Rosslyn Chapel; Himmler may have sent agents, who found it empty; its circular recessed floor resembles the initiation chamber at [[wewelsburg-castle]]. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 34:43,)*
- The Nazis are said to have seen themselves as crusading Aryan knights; had they found the Ark or Grail it would have been announced as a morale boost. *([banijay/nazi-elite](<../raw/banijay/Banijay Documentaries - 2023-07-20 - Secrets of the Nazi Elite - Inside Secret Societies - S01 EP1 - Investigative Documentary.md>) 37:57,)*
