Harry Truman was President of the United States when, per Eyes Wide Open, he lost faith in
[[william-donovan]]'s leadership of the [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]] and replaced him
with [[roscoe-hillenkoetter]] as (per the host) first director of the CIA.

## Claims

- Commissioned a 1945 report from Colonel Park on the OSS while weighing whether to create a
  new postwar spy agency; the report portrayed Donovan as an ineffective, drunk administrator
  who hired via Wall Street connections. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 14:32-14:50)*
- Dismissed Donovan and replaced him with naval officer [[roscoe-hillenkoetter]], per Eyes Wide
  Open reflecting greater trust in an actual military officer over Donovan. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 15:26-15:52)*

- Received a letter from SS General [[karl-wolff]] outlining details of [[allen-dulles]]'s
  insubordinate [[operation-sunrise]] scheme, allegedly sent as leverage in Wolff's blackmail of
  Dulles. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 19:49-19:54)*

- His 1945 postwar federal budget allocated no funds for a new intelligence agency or covert
  operations, having earlier voiced fear that a peacetime CIA-like agency could become an
  "American Gestapo"; Eyes Wide Open frames this funding gap as the direct catalyst for
  [[paul-helliwell]]'s narcotics-funded [[operation-x]] scheme. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 14:15-14:27, 19:11-19:27)*

- Two weeks after Japan's surrender, ordered the wartime OSS largely disbanded (August 1945).
  Reluctantly created the [[central-intelligence-group|Central Intelligence Group]] on 24 January
  1946 under pressure from the FBI ([[j-edgar-hoover]]) and the Pentagon, both lobbying to become
  the sole postwar US intelligence authority; per Eyes Wide Open, Truman's own stated goal was
  merely a daily intelligence brief, and he did not anticipate having opened the door to the
  future CIA. His liberal leanings and fear of creating an "American Gestapo"-style unaccountable
  agency had made him reluctant to establish any peacetime intelligence service at all.
  *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 14:09-16:23, 17:01-17:24)*
- In March 1947, delivered the [[truman-doctrine]] to Congress while requesting aid for
  anti-communist forces in Greece, marking (per Eyes Wide Open) the start of the Cold War proper.
  Later that year signed the [[national-security-act-1947|National Security Act of 1947]] after
  years of lobbying from the FBI, the Pentagon, and former OSS agents, creating the
  [[national-security-council|National Security Council]], the Secretary of Defense position, the
  US Air Force, and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] itself. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 21:52-22:39, 22:57-24:27)*

- Approved [[operation-paper]] in late 1950 on Joint Chiefs/OPC advice over DCI Smith's objection; NSC 342 was approved 3 March 1949. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 20:16-22:17)*
- LAOS 1: largely ignored [[ho-chi-minh]]'s Feb 1946 cable requesting support for Vietnamese independence, focused on the Cold War; host calls this a 'massive foreign policy mistake'. *([ewa/laos-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-01 - The Secret War in Laos - South East Asia's Heart of Darkness - LAOS 1.md) 21:15-22:51)*

- War Conspiracy 1: his administration committed to anti-communism in Europe (overt Marshall Plan; covert campaigns in the Greek civil war and the 1948 Italian election) while "turning attention away" from Asia, so per the anticommunist press "allowed China to go red" in 1949; hawks pushed rollback ([[nsc-68]]); he entered the [[korean-war]] in June 1950, and his $50B first post-entry defense budget began peacetime military spending ([[military-industrial-complex]]). He left office in January 1953. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 30:41-31:13, 38:21-39:07, 48:46-48:52)*
