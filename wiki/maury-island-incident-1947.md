The Maury Island affair was a June 1947 Puget Sound UFO report that [[peter-levenda]] recounts to [[jesse-michels]] as the hidden link between the early UFO era and the Kennedy assassination. It is presented here as Levenda's retelling; details such as the bugged hotel and the fate of the journalist are his claims.

## Claims

- Kenneth Arnold's Cascade sighting (a wing-shaped craft with a bite out of the end, closer to a Horten design in Levenda's view) coincided with a man in a boat near Maury Island who reported a damaged UFO raining slag that killed his dog and wounded his son. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 24:36, 25:33)*
- The man's boss was [[fred-crisman]], whose phone calls attracted two Army Air Force intelligence officers; Arnold was invited to the meeting in Seattle and found the most expensive hotel already had a reservation for him. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 25:48, 27:45, 28:16)*
- Levenda says the room was bugged, a local journalist obtained a tape and died soon after; Crisman gave the officers a box of the slag, and their plane exploded in midair, giving the "first fatality" of the modern UFO era. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 28:34, 28:45, 29:19)*
- [[guy-banister]], then an FBI special agent in the Pacific Northwest, reported on UFO sightings to J. Edgar Hoover by teletype under a "special operation" designation Levenda calls "the actual X-files". *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 23:27, 23:52, 24:08)*
- Crisman was later investigated by Jim Garrison, and [[clay-shaw]] reportedly phoned him during the Garrison probe; Levenda says [[e-howard-hunt]] may also have been one of the "hobos" arrested in Dallas, calling that a rumor. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 22:51, 26:24, 27:17)*
