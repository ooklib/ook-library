Alleged airman who told [[mark-mccandlish]] he saw the flux liner ARV at Edwards AFB in 1973. Named "Kent sellin" in the ASR and "Kellin" once; the spelling Selin is a guess.

## Claims (gerb/mccandlish)

- McCandlish met him at the 1992 Edwards air show (first public B-2 unveiling). In 1973 he was a crew chief on experimental aircraft, working for the air intelligence agency at Edwards North Base, and he wandered into an area with a classified aircraft, the ARV, while cutting across the base to fetch a ground power unit. He described it in detail and added configuration and operation details Sorenson did not know. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 33:12-34:10)*
- McCandlish used his account, along with Sorenson's, for the March 1989 cutaway drawing (the ASR says "Kellin" as an additional source). *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 32:26-32:50)*
- Gerb links it to [[leonard-stringfield]]'s files via Michael Schratt: a pilot given permission to cut across Edwards saw a disc with a transparent dome in an open hangar, and Mr. Hines relayed a Bell Labs source who saw a retrieved or test craft and cutaway at a California airbase in the early 1950s. *([gerb/mccandlish](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-21 - Alien Reproduction Vehicle - the Testimony of Mark McCandlish.md>) 34:10-35:22)*
