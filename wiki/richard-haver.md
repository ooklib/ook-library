Richard L. Haver joined [[trw|TRW]] in 1999 as vice president and director for intelligence
programs and, following Northrop's 2002 acquisition of TRW, continued as vice president for
intelligence programs at [[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman]] until his 2010 retirement. Gerb
calls him a "labeled protege" of [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] and argues his role is a near-mirror
to [[mary-sturdivant|Mary K. Sturdivant]]'s VP for intelligence programs post at rival prime
[[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]]. *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md)
1:12:14–1:12:31, 1:46:16–1:47:12)*

## Claims

- Career: active-duty Navy officer, then civilian intelligence analyst in the anti-submarine
  warfare systems branch at the Naval Intelligence Support Center, rising to deputy director of
  naval intelligence, 1985–1989 — likely overlapping there with
  [[stephanie-osullivan|Stephanie O'Sullivan]]. *([northrop-trw](../raw/
  0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 1:47:36–1:48:02)*
- In 1989, [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] (then Secretary of Defense) hand-selected Haver for a
  newly created position, assistant to the Secretary of Defense for intelligence policy, giving
  Haver broad authority over military intelligence policy, DoD intelligence/counterintelligence/
  security/covert-action review, and recommendations to the SecDef. Gerb notes the same position
  was later held (1997–1998) by [[christopher-mellon|Christopher Mellon]].
  *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 1:48:02–1:49:02)*
- Gerb wagers Haver, with intimate access to clandestine intelligence programs as Cheney's special
  aide, was "a critical member in Cheney's inner circle of legacy activity personnel" from roughly
  1992 to 1995. *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 1:49:23–1:49:39)*
- Served as executive director for intelligence community affairs, then (1998) chief of staff of
  the National Intelligence Council and deputy to the assistant director of central intelligence
  for analysis and production, before moving to TRW in 1999.
  *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 1:49:40–1:49:56)*
- While at TRW (business development for TRW's intelligence activities, systems and information
  technology group, and space and electronics group) and then Northrop Grumman, Haver was again
  selected by then-VP Dick Cheney in the early 2000s to head the incoming administration's
  intelligence transition team, and separately chosen by SecDef Donald Rumsfeld as special
  assistant to the Secretary of Defense for intelligence.
  *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 1:50:16–1:50:57)*
- Gerb states there are "no direct links between Haver and UFO Legacy programs," but argues "an
  extremely strong argument can be made" given the chain from [[office-of-naval-intelligence|ONI]]
  to TRW to Northrop Grumman to being repeatedly hand-picked by Cheney — whom Grush names as the
  legacy-program "head of the snake." *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md)
  1:51:00–1:51:12)*
