First of [[uap-gerb]]'s three epochs of the [[ufo-legacy-programs]] ([[legacy-program-timeline]]): 1947-1978, "a unified national effort driven by a cohesive mission" run by presidents and senior officials inside the NSC covert-action oversight structure, with sub-compartments hidden in any agency behind early code-word controls ("Wild Wild West days"); Gerb names [[vannevar-bush]], [[curtis-lemay]] and [[henry-kissinger]] among its talent. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 20:58-23:06)*

## Structure claimed by Gerb

- **[[manhattan-project-2]] (1947-1954):** formation and consolidation, copying compartmentalization, organizational architecture, physical security, information control and political cover from the atomic project; personnel [[vannevar-bush]], [[george-c-marshall]], possibly [[j-robert-oppenheimer]]; predecessors the [[alsos-mission]], T-Force and [[top-policy-group]]. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 23:08-24:43, 2:29:26-2:30:46)*
- **Milestones:** [[roswell-incident-1947]], [[national-security-act-1947]], [[nsc-10-2]], PEADs ([[presidential-emergency-action-documents]]), [[kingman-arizona-crash-1953]], [[executive-order-10501]], [[nsc-5412-committee]], [[atomic-energy-act-1954]], the 1958-63 R&D golden age ([[philip-j-corso]], [[mitre-corporation]], [[aerospace-corporation]]), [[cia-directorate-of-science-and-technology]], the 303 Committee, [[deep-submergence-systems-project]], [[executive-order-11652]].
- **Reverse engineering begins late:** Gerb argues real exploitation/derivation started only in the late 1950s, after 1954-1960 possibly saw (unverified, excluded from his timeline) agreements with non-human intelligence under Eisenhower. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 27:47-27:56, 53:14-53:45)*
- **Presidents:** Truman and Eisenhower set the structure; afterwards, Gerb says, the question became which presidents to trust: [[lyndon-b-johnson]] and [[richard-nixon]] involved, [[john-f-kennedy]] and Jimmy Carter ([[jimmy-carter]]) not. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 2:35:38-2:36:20)*
- **Estimate of the situation:** [[wilbert-b-smith]]'s 1950 memo relaying [[robert-sarbacher]]: the subject rated above the H-bomb, a small group headed by [[vannevar-bush]]. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 34:57-36:48, 39:40-39:53)*
- **What ended it:** [[executive-order-11652]] (1972) and [[executive-order-12036]] (1978). *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:04:39-1:04:53)*

Gerb admits many golden-era entries are missing (rapid-recovery units in the Air Force and special forces, [[trw]] and its alleged [[zodiac-crash-retrieval-program]], prototyping of UFO "low performance mode" capabilities) because he cannot date them. *([gerb/timeline](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2026-09-18 - 80 Years of UFO Crash Retrieval and Reverse Engineering - A Timeline.md>) 1:07:37-1:08:16)*
