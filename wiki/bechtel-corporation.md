The Bechtel Corporation is a large, privately held US construction/engineering conglomerate.
More Perfect Union presents the Bechtel family as the entity "more deeply tied to the [[bohemian-grove]]
and elite collusion than any other" named in the video, tracing generations of family members and
executives appearing on the club's membership/attendance lists alongside a pattern of major
government contracts.

## Claims

- Two Bechtel family members appear on the leaked attendance/membership list; the family has been
  involved with the club for generations
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 8:23, 8:31, 8:37)*.
- A photo allegedly shows Steven Bechtel Sr. at the Grove encampment with Henry Kissinger,
  accompanied by a memo from Bechtel to Kissinger
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 8:37, 8:46)*.
- Named as part of a claimed "Venn diagram" of Bechtel executives, government officials, and
  Bohemian Club members: Caspar Weinberger, George Shultz, Edwin Meese, and John McComb (credited
  by the video with inventing the "cost plus" government-contracting model, in which contractors
  report costs and receive a profit margin on top, removing any incentive to control costs)
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 8:52, 9:00, 9:05, 9:16)*.
- More Perfect Union alleges the "cost plus" model has driven excess costs across government
  contracting broadly, including in Medicare Advantage
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 9:16, 9:24)*.
- Bechtel is alleged to have received millions of dollars in contracts to "clean up" after the
  early-2000s Iraq invasion, which the video attributes to club members George W. Bush, Dick
  Cheney, Donald Rumsfeld, and Colin Powell
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 9:30, 9:41, 9:45)*.
- Current family CEO Brendan Bechtel appeared alongside Senator [[david-mccormick]] and President
  Trump at an announcement of a major AI-investment push for Pennsylvania data centers and power
  plants, from which Bechtel is said to be poised to receive billions in construction contracts.
  The video further alleges Bechtel has financial interests tied to Venezuelan pipelines Trump has
  discussed seizing, and has been floated as a beneficiary of a Kushner/Trump plan to rebuild Gaza
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 15:18, 15:28, 15:31, 15:38, 15:44, 15:50, 15:56, 16:00)*.

## Relationships

- Overlapping membership with [[bohemian-grove]] across multiple generations of the Bechtel
  family and its senior executives.
- Connected to Senator [[david-mccormick]] via the 2020s Pennsylvania AI-infrastructure deal.

## Speculation flag

The claimed link between Bechtel's government-contract history (Iraq, Pennsylvania AI
infrastructure, Venezuela, Gaza) and Bohemian Grove attendance is More Perfect Union's own
editorial argument connecting separately documented contracts to separately documented club
attendance; the video does not claim a specific contract was negotiated at the Grove itself
(unlike its Social Security/Reagan claim on the [[bohemian-grove]] page), and this wiki records
the connection as an alleged pattern, not a proven causal link.
