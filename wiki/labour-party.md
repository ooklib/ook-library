The UK Labour Party traces to the 1900 Labour Representation Committee, co-founded by the
[[fabian-society]] among other socialist/trade-union organizations. Green & Pleasant alleges the
Fabian Society has continuously and disproportionately shaped Labour's leadership, constitution,
and policy program for over a century via [[permeation]].

## Claims

- The Fabian Society, trade unions, and the Independent Labour Party founded the Labour
  Representation Committee within 16 years of the Fabians' 1884 founding; it became the Labour
  Party. Of these founding organizations, only the Fabian Society remains affiliated to Labour
  today, unchanged
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 1:46, 1:54)*.
- Sidney Webb wrote Clause IV of Labour's 1918 constitution (common ownership of the means of
  production), in force until Tony Blair replaced it, using a Fabian pamphlet as his
  justification *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 9:00, 9:14)*.
- The Fabian Society's own website claims every Labour prime minister has been a Fabian:
  Ramsay MacDonald, Clement Attlee, Harold Wilson, James Callaghan, Tony Blair, Gordon Brown, and
  Keir Starmer are each named with specific Fabian affiliations
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 23:35, 24:24)*.
- As of the video (March 2026): Fabian Society membership (~6,500) supplies 141 of 411 Labour
  MPs (34%) and reportedly more than half the cabinet, despite being 1.6% of Labour's ~400,000
  membership *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 11:06)*.
- The 2016 attempt to remove Jeremy Corbyn as leader (24 shadow-cabinet resignations) is framed
  as the Fabian wing of the party moving against the Corbyn left; Keir Starmer resigned from
  Corbyn's shadow cabinet that year and became leader within four years, the first Fabian
  executive-committee member to do so
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 12:50, 13:19)*.
- After the 2024 election, the Fabian Society stated Labour's manifesto was "shaped by Fabian
  values and filled with Fabian values"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 13:42)*.

## Relationships

- Descended from the 1900 Labour Representation Committee, co-founded by the [[fabian-society]].
- Constitution (Clause IV, 1918) authored by [[sidney-webb]].
- Welfare-state policy lineage traced to [[beatrice-webb]]'s 1909 Minority Report via
  [[william-beveridge]]'s 1942 report.

## Speculation flag

The claim that "every Labour PM has been a Fabian" and current cabinet-composition figures are
the Fabian Society's own self-description as relayed by Green & Pleasant; this page records them
as an attributed claim, not independently verified fact.
