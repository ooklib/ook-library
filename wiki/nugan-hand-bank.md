Nugan Hand Bank of Sydney, Australia is described by [[eyes-wide-open]] as a "suspected major vector and money launder of illicit narco funds from the wars in Southeast Asia" and as the successor to [[castle-bank-and-trust]] in the CIA's shadow banking. ASR spellings "Nuggan/Nugganhand." The host defers the detail ("as we will come to see much later"); this page holds only the brief `ewa/jmwave` account.

## Claims

- Castle Bank "would come to be superseded by the CIA's next narco bank," Nugan Hand. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 20:53-21:09)*
- [[ted-shackley]] "and his close associates at CIA dating all the way back to the JM wave days would play prominent roles in the founding and operations" of the bank; the host calls Shackley "a silent administrator of the shell companies and firms used in the laundering network of the CIA." *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 21:09-21:33)*
- Photo in Jonathan Kwitny's *The Crimes of Patriots* shows an elderly [[paul-helliwell]] shaking hands with Frank Nugan, a co-founder; the host reads it as a "passing of the torch" from Castle Bank to Nugan Hand, "the evolution of CIA shadow banking from Helwell to Shockley." *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 21:33-22:09)*

## Relationships

- Successor to [[castle-bank-and-trust]]; tied by the host to [[ted-shackley]] and [[paul-helliwell]].
