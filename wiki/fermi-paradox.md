The Fermi paradox, posed by Enrico Fermi in the summer of 1950 ("where is everybody?"), is the tension between the high likelihood of extraterrestrials and the absence of conclusive evidence; [[uap-gerb]] says he "despises" it. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 26:25-26:58)*

## Claims

- Gerb argues SETI searches a tiny conical slice of a 92-billion-light-year universe, assumes radio (not, say, neutrinos), and that Dysonian SETI's technosignature search is a better approach. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 26:58-28:06)*
- He lists proposed solutions: the [[great-filter-hypothesis]]; the Dark Forest (civilizations hide from something bigger); aquatic aliens undetectable at range; we are early; we are late (a graveyard). He thinks the paradox is limited and ignorant. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 28:06-29:54)*
