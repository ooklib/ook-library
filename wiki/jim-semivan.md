Jim Semivan is described in the `jm/cover-up` episode as a To The Stars Academy adviser and a senior CIA executive ("two people under the CIA director"). Attributed claims:

- Says [[sekret-machines-project]]'s Maynard Consortium book is very interesting but would not say why. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:15:39, 1:15:47)*

- Michels notes that a senior ex-CIA figure talking about alien-human hybrids may function as slow dissemination of an idea. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 2:13:15, 2:14:13)*
