The Defense Advanced Research Center (DARC) — not to be confused with DARPA — is a UFO-lore
institution [[edgar-fuchs|Edgar Fuchs]] claimed was stationed underground near Area 51/Groom
Lake, working on UFO reverse engineering and material exploitation.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:09:20–1:09:43)*

## Claims

- A declassified 1973 ARPA historical study (of an agency then said to have "remarkably little
  in the way of an official record or institutional memory") shows, on page 82, a DoD directive
  considering the Army Ballistic Missile Agency for transfer into DARC, linking it to the Jet
  Propulsion Laboratory. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:09:43–1:10:22)* See
  [[army-ballistic-missile-agency]].
- Was "actually a proposed institution" rather than confirmed as built — Gerb frames DARC's
  documented status as a genuine ARPA-era proposal, not a confirmed operational site.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:09:35–1:09:50)*
- Fuchs claimed DARC was "bolstered" with [[strategic-defense-initiative|SDI]] funding in the
  mid-1980s. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:11:13–1:11:22)*

## Relationships

- Alleged by [[edgar-fuchs]]; linked in ARPA documentation to
  [[army-ballistic-missile-agency]]; allegedly SDI-funded.
