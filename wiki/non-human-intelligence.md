"Non-human intelligence" (NHI) is the term used by [[david-grush]] and others in the disclosure community for the alleged intelligent originators of recovered craft, preferred over "extraterrestrial" because origin is unknown. Grusch says he does not want to "denote origin" and floats a higher-dimensional, collocated source as a theory. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 7:42-8:13,)*

## Claims

- Grusch says NHI have been around "at least multiple decades in modern history" and appear neutral to human affairs, having "allowed ourselves to annihilate ourselves". *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 40:19-40:41)*
- He rejects the inference that advanced means benevolent: "what appears to be malevolent activity has happened", citing nuclear-site probing and witness testimony. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 27:22-28:47)*
- He has never met NHI himself and bases his view on testimony. *([rc/grusch](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2025-04-02 - UFO whistleblower David Grusch_ We are not alone _ Official Ross Coulthart NewsNation interview.md>) 8:31)*
