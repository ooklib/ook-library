The Office of Secure Transportation (OST), established in 1975 (successor to the Albuquerque
Operations Office's nuclear-transport role, dating to 1947), is the [[national-nuclear-security-
administration|NNSA]] command responsible for moving nuclear weapons, weapons components, and
special nuclear material (SNM) across the continental United States. Gerb theorizes OST — along
with the Special Response Team (SRT) and [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team|NEST]] — forms part
of an NNSA logistics/enforcement apparatus for continental-US UFO-materials transport, operating
under 1954 Atomic Energy Act "transclassified foreign nuclear information" and SNM cover.
*([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 1:38:42–1:41:25)*

## Claims

- OST convoys use unmarked escort and conventional-looking tractor-trailer vehicles, identifiable
  mainly by US government license plates; agents are NNSA federal officers who complete 1,000+
  hours of training and are authorized to use deadly force; OST logs 4.5M+ miles annually.
  Personnel were historically trained/staffed by Wackenhut Services, Inc. ("nuclear commandos").
  *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 1:41:37–1:43:06)*
- OST developed the Safeguards Transporter (SGT), engineered to survive severe highway accidents
  including long-duration fires, and the newer Mobile Guardian Transporter (MGT) — built by
  [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]] — a third-generation 18-wheeler for moving nuclear
  materials, special components, and weapons. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md)
  1:39:19–1:40:12)*
- Gerb speculates that, since at least 1975, UFO craft and debris could have been transported
  within the continental US by OST under SNM/transclassified-nuclear-information cover, following
  an earlier era (per Kner's disclosures) of Army lowboy-trailer transport under the Atomic Energy
  Commission. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 1:40:43–1:41:16)*
- OST works with an enforcement federal protective force called the Special Response Team (SRT),
  which has provided security for sensitive DOE defense-nuclear facilities including Los Alamos,
  Lawrence Livermore, and Sandia since at least the late 1990s; Gerb has separately (with low
  confidence) theorized SRT was the on-site team alongside NEST that detained
  [[jonathan-wagant|Jonathan Wagant]] in 1997, and (with medium-to-high confidence) that SRT was
  the ground-based team below a triangular craft that apprehended USMC Sgt. Rodri Castle during
  the Hunter Warrior exercise. *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 1:41:25–1:41:37,
  1:43:06–1:44:01)*
- Per DOE Order 153.1A, OST "may provide transportation for [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team|
  NEST]] nuclear weapons, nuclear weapon components, and SNM within the continental United
  States." *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 1:44:10–1:44:27)*
- Convoys, wearing "Nevada Test Site" and NNSA badges, allegedly responded to the 2008 Needles,
  California object-crash incident investigated by journalist George Knapp.
  *([sandia](../raw/mHkPHd_eOXI_sandia.md) 1:37:17–1:38:26)*
