Luigi Vendittelli (spelled "Venditelli" in the video description) is the filmmaker behind *S4: The Bob Lazar Story* and its working title Project Gravitaur ([[project-gravitaur-s4-documentary]]). Lazar says Luigi kept calling and eventually persuaded him to "show exactly what you saw", and that Lazar corrected the CGI reconstruction until it matched his memory *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 5:22-6:15)*. Luigi built the bus ride, the S4 door, the infirmary, the lab and a physically lit model of the craft, and Lazar rates the reconstruction "100%" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 38:12-38:42, 1:04:14-1:05:16)*. His images include the alleged S4 hangar-door photograph *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 26:15-26:47)*, and he gave Ramsey a Billy Meier photo book and owns a poster of one Meier photo that Lazar says matches the sport model ([[billy-meier]]) *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:44:30-1:44:49, 1:51:20-1:51:41)*. Lazar mentions Luigi's spectral analyst Ron Masters, who reportedly judged the hangar-door pixels anomalous, as reported by the host *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 26:28-26:47)*.
