War loan of 1870-71 that, in [[empire-of-shadows-rothschild-documentary-2023]], made the [[house-of-morgan]].

## Claims

- After Napoleon III declared war on 19 July 1870 the [[rothschild-family]] and Barings judged France would lose and refused to lend. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 20:37, 21:27, 21:39)*
- [[junius-spencer-morgan]] reasoned that repayment, not victory, mattered, and raised over 10 million pounds; France lost (Treaty of Frankfurt 1871, German Empire proclaimed at Versailles) but prepaid the bonds in 1873, netting Morgan 1.5 million pounds. *([finaius/rothschild](<../raw/finaius/FINAiUS - 2023-11-18 - Empire of Shadows - True Story of the Richest Family in History.md>) 21:52, 22:23, 22:40, 23:00, 23:14)*
