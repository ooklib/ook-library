Operation X, per Eyes Wide Open, was [[paul-helliwell]]'s postwar plan (presented to
[[william-donovan]], [[allen-dulles]], and [[james-jesus-angleton]]) for the OSS/CIA to
facilitate the global heroin trade through organized-crime networks and warlords, using the
1940s Harlem jazz-club heroin market as an initial "testing ground." The host frames it as the
origin of the trade route later known as "The French Connection," and flags it as a topic the
channel will revisit in more depth in future installments (the videos catalogued as `ewa/laos-2`
and `ewa/laos-3` in `raw/index.md` explicitly cover "Operation X and the French Indochina War"
and "The French Connection in South East Asia").

## Claims

- Conceived after Truman's 1945 postwar budget allocated no funding for a new intelligence
  agency, leaving Donovan and the OSS's elite "furious" and casting about for an unaccountable
  funding source; modeled on Helliwell's prior success facilitating the KMT's opium trade in
  China. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 19:11-19:55)*
- [[frank-wisner]] (future CIA head of covert operations), alongside Angleton, developed the
  operational relationship with [[meyer-lansky]], "often considered the most prolific and
  wealthy Mafia boss in US history," who had united the Jewish, Italian, and Irish mobs into a
  nationwide "National Crime Syndicate" and served as its financial mastermind. Lansky ran
  Operation X's finances in partnership with Helliwell through the shell company
  [[general-development-corporation]] in Miami. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 22:31-23:44)*
- Eyes Wide Open lays out the alleged simplified pipeline: opium grown in Nationalist-Chinese/Thai-
  controlled plateaus of Indochina was shipped overland through Afghanistan; for Helliwell's
  first test run the opium was synthesized into heroin in Sicilian labs run by the Italian
  pharmaceutical firm [[schiaparelli]], smuggled by the Sicilian mob (organized by
  [[lucky-luciano]]) in fake wax-orange crates and sardine cans to Cuban labs controlled by
  [[santo-trafficante]], cut with sugar there, then shipped to Luciano-controlled New York City
  docks and sold to Harlem jazz-scene addicts; proceeds flowed into
  [[general-development-corporation]] and from there to Luciano's Sicilian mob, which
  distributed funds to the far-right underground network that made up [[operation-gladio]]
  (Mafia elements, former fascists, secret armies, and Vatican officials, per the host's teaser
  for the next episode). In the 1950s-60s, the equivalent role was played by heroin labs in
  Marseille run by the Corsican mob, feeding what became known as "The French Connection." Later
  in this history, heroin distribution within the US ran through [[jimmy-hoffa]]'s Teamsters
  trucking network. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 26:14-29:21)*
- The host explicitly flags scholarly disagreement over how directly narcotics profits funded
  CIA operations: Alfred McCoy (*The Politics of Heroin*) argues the CIA never used drug money
  to directly fund operations, acting only as a facilitator guaranteeing allies' unimpeded
  smuggling; Paul L. Williams (*Operation Gladio*) argues the CIA acted as a direct middleman,
  funneling drug funds into secret accounts and shell companies owned by the Mafia, the Vatican,
  and the CIA itself to fund Gladio. Eyes Wide Open says the distinction is "irrelevant" to the
  larger point that the Mafia, warlords, and narco-states shared the American deep state's
  material anti-communist interests. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 16:37-18:21)*
- Its test run in the late 1940s was, per Eyes Wide Open, "a massive success": heroin became a
  mainstay of the Harlem jazz scene and spread through the US and Europe, cementing narcotics as
  "one of the main currencies used by the future CIA to fund its actions across the globe," at
  the cost of a new generation of addicts. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 29:21-29:56)*

## Relationships

- Devised by [[paul-helliwell]]; presented to [[william-donovan]], [[allen-dulles]], and
  [[james-jesus-angleton]].
- Financial/liaison relationship with [[meyer-lansky]] developed by [[frank-wisner]] and
  Angleton; run through [[general-development-corporation]].
- Heroin supply chain organized by [[lucky-luciano]] (Sicily/New York) and
  [[santo-trafficante]] (Cuba); domestic distribution later via [[jimmy-hoffa]]'s Teamsters.
- Proceeds allegedly helped fund [[operation-gladio]] via the Sicilian mob.
- Analogous to the wartime [[operation-underworld]] precedent; evolved into "The French
  Connection" trade route.
