**Note (LAOS 2):** the host later retracted the Helliwell attribution and says Operation X was actually a French intelligence program in Laos and North Vietnam; see "Attribution correction" and "Operation X in French Indochina" below.

Operation X, per Eyes Wide Open's Pt. 3, was [[paul-helliwell]]'s postwar plan (presented to
[[william-donovan]], [[allen-dulles]], and [[james-jesus-angleton]]) for the OSS/CIA to
facilitate the global heroin trade through organized-crime networks and warlords, using the
1940s Harlem jazz-club heroin market as an initial "testing ground." The host frames it as the
origin of the trade route later known as "The French Connection," and flagged it as a topic to revisit; `ewa/laos-2` is that revisit, and `ewa/laos-3` ("The French Connection in South East Asia") is still to be ingested.

## Attribution correction (LAOS 2)

**Flagged discrepancy.** In *ewa/mafia-allies* (Pt. 3) the host presented "Operation X" as [[paul-helliwell]]'s postwar OSS/CIA plan. In *ewa/laos-2* he explicitly retracts this: he says he "mistakenly called the American Narco smuggling operation in China and Burma right at the end of the second World War as Operation X" when it was "likely an early iteration of operation paper", blames the source (Paul L. Williams, *Operation Gladio*, which he now calls rushed and full of "factual inaccuracies", to be avoided for academic use), and states: "for the record Operation X was a French intelligence operation in Laos and North Vietnam" that "surely influenced US operations down the line". He says all other details of the earlier video "remain correct". *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 19:30-20:55)* The Helliwell/Lansky/Luciano material below (and on the many pages that link here, e.g. [[paul-helliwell]], [[meyer-lansky]], [[general-development-corporation]]) is therefore best read as attributed to the earlier episode and is unresolved: the host does not say which of those claims he considers to belong to [[operation-paper]] instead.

## Operation X in French Indochina (LAOS 2)

Per Eyes Wide Open, Operation X was the French narco-counterinsurgency program of the [[first-indochina-war]], "refined later by the CIA and Edward Lansdale". Under it French intelligence, via Major [[roger-trinquier]], contracted the [[hmong-people]] (the host's ASR: "Hong") to fight for France; they became future CIA allies. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 17:41-18:26; episode description)*

### Claims

- **Concept.** In 1951 Trinquier, needing a cheap war, proposed that "the war paid for itself" and be fought by indigenous allies, the Hmong, "the main purveyors of the French opium monopoly". *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 17:41-18:26)* After the war the legitimate colonial trade went underground to organized crime; the Hmong kept growing opium and sold it to the [[binh-xuyen]] and to Chinese mafia remnants moving to Hong Kong and Bangkok. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 18:26-19:13)*
- **Masterminds.** [[roger-trinquier]] (tactics: use the profits to arm, feed and pay Hmong mercenaries) and Corsican Captain [[antoine-savani]] (logistics: ship the dope and liaise with criminals). Stated goal: Hmong mercenaries in northern Laos as a second strike force to divert the enemy in North Vietnam. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 21:04-22:28)*
- **Funding model.** Buy the Hmong's raw opium at a competitive rate and market it in Saigon, giving France a fighting force paid for by illicit profit and using locals against each other. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 22:36-23:15)* Savani made contact with the [[binh-xuyen]], the French having "disbanded" the opium monopoly in 1946 only cosmetically (dens renamed detox clinics); the French allied with the pirates for an \$85,000/year stipend. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 23:15-25:04)* The [[corsican-mafia]] in Saigon likely handled export (host speculation), and Corsican charter airlines "likely" began shipping dope north-to-south, though most raw opium moved on [[mixed-airborne-commando-group]] planes; the host says these airlines later became key partners of the CIA and South Vietnam. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 25:26-26:17)*
- **The black box.** Profits went to a "caisse" (black box), an unaccounted safe of dirty cash in Trinquier's office at Cap Saint-Jacques, used to pay Hmong recruits (chiefs could come to Saigon and access it directly): an off-the-book slush fund. The host contrasts it with the later "far more sophisticated" CIA system of shell banks and front companies run by paymasters such as [[paul-helliwell]] and his shadow banking empire. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 26:17-27:32)*
- **Four-phase recruitment (McCoy).** (1) A small plane overflew hill-tribe territory: shooting meant Viet Minh sympathizers, waving meant potential allies. (2) Four or five MACG units parachuted in to recruit about 50 irregulars, the "maquis" (Trinquier's borrowed term from the French/Belgian WWII resistance), who were flown to Cap Saint-Jacques for about 2.5 months' training. (3) Split into four-man teams with cash, guns and radios, each recruited 100 more via propaganda and cash. (4) The war bands consolidated and rooted out Viet Minh influence. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 27:39-29:53)*
- **Scale.** Three major maquis: two in Tonkin (one Hmong, one Thai) and one Hmong in Laos; about 40,000 fighters by 1954, mostly Hmong. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 29:53-30:25)* The Lao maquis leader was [[touby-lyfoung]] (ASR "tuby"). *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 30:25-30:48)*
- **Opium flow.** Opium from Hmong villages went out on DC-3s that had brought arms and food, to dirt strips (later "Lima sites"), then to Cap Saint-Jacques where Savani worked with the Binh Xuyen and local Corsicans; proceeds went back to pay village leaders and buy weapons. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 30:48-31:19)*
- **Uses.** The maquis served as an assassination/strike force and radio-equipped reconnaissance; spring 1953 backup at the Plain of Jars let the French build a base housing 12,000 troops. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 31:19-32:23)* The host calls these "some of the only successful French military operations in the war". *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 32:16-32:30)*
- **Dien Bien Phu.** In April 1954 Trinquier bought 3,000 of Touby's men with silver; they helped at [[battle-of-dien-bien-phu]] but were "not a decisive factor". *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 36:34-37:38)*
- **Aftermath.** Trinquier left in July 1954 after [[geneva-conference-1954]]; his textbook omitted the narco funding, which he told to [[alfred-mccoy]] in the early 1970s. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 42:45-45:15)*
- **Handover to the US.** In Saigon in July 1954 Trinquier briefed [[edward-lansdale]] (head of what the host calls "MAG", the Military Assistance Advisory Group, which the host says was set up under the civilian CIA to evade the Geneva limits) and [[lucien-conein]], saying the infrastructure remained ready to reactivate, and [[vang-pao]] "surely came up". Lansdale later claimed he disapproved of narco funding (the host doubts this); [[allen-dulles]] was "particularly impressed" and the host says the CIA would soon create "an operation X of their own", "much larger... more efficient... much more deadly", after its own similar experience in Burma with [[operation-paper]]. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 45:39-48:39)*

## Earlier claims (ewa/mafia-allies; attribution retracted, see above)

- Conceived after Truman's 1945 postwar budget allocated no funding for a new intelligence
  agency, leaving Donovan and the OSS's elite "furious" and casting about for an unaccountable
  funding source; modeled on Helliwell's prior success facilitating the KMT's opium trade in
  China. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 19:11-19:55)*
- [[frank-wisner]] (future CIA head of covert operations), alongside Angleton, developed the
  operational relationship with [[meyer-lansky]], "often considered the most prolific and
  wealthy Mafia boss in US history," who had united the Jewish, Italian, and Irish mobs into a
  nationwide "National Crime Syndicate" and served as its financial mastermind. Lansky ran
  Operation X's finances in partnership with Helliwell through the shell company
  [[general-development-corporation]] in Miami. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 22:31-23:44)*
- Eyes Wide Open lays out the alleged simplified pipeline: opium grown in Nationalist-Chinese/Thai-
  controlled plateaus of Indochina was shipped overland through Afghanistan; for Helliwell's
  first test run the opium was synthesized into heroin in Sicilian labs run by the Italian
  pharmaceutical firm [[schiaparelli]], smuggled by the Sicilian mob (organized by
  [[lucky-luciano]]) in fake wax-orange crates and sardine cans to Cuban labs controlled by
  [[santo-trafficante]], cut with sugar there, then shipped to Luciano-controlled New York City
  docks and sold to Harlem jazz-scene addicts; proceeds flowed into
  [[general-development-corporation]] and from there to Luciano's Sicilian mob, which
  distributed funds to the far-right underground network that made up [[operation-gladio]]
  (Mafia elements, former fascists, secret armies, and Vatican officials, per the host's teaser
  for the next episode). In the 1950s-60s, the equivalent role was played by heroin labs in
  Marseille run by the Corsican mob, feeding what became known as "The French Connection." Later
  in this history, heroin distribution within the US ran through [[jimmy-hoffa]]'s Teamsters
  trucking network. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 26:14-29:21)*
- The host explicitly flags scholarly disagreement over how directly narcotics profits funded
  CIA operations: Alfred McCoy (*The Politics of Heroin*) argues the CIA never used drug money
  to directly fund operations, acting only as a facilitator guaranteeing allies' unimpeded
  smuggling; Paul L. Williams (*Operation Gladio*) argues the CIA acted as a direct middleman,
  funneling drug funds into secret accounts and shell companies owned by the Mafia, the Vatican,
  and the CIA itself to fund Gladio. Eyes Wide Open says the distinction is "irrelevant" to the
  larger point that the Mafia, warlords, and narco-states shared the American deep state's
  material anti-communist interests. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 16:37-18:21)*
- Its test run in the late 1940s was, per Eyes Wide Open, "a massive success": heroin became a
  mainstay of the Harlem jazz scene and spread through the US and Europe, cementing narcotics as
  "one of the main currencies used by the future CIA to fund its actions across the globe," at
  the cost of a new generation of addicts. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 29:21-29:56)*
