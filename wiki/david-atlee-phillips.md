David Atlee Phillips (born Fort Worth, Texas, 1923) was, per [[eyes-wide-open]], a CIA officer for about 25 years, almost exclusively in Latin America, retiring in 1975, and "a key player in nearly every CIA-JFK assassination theory." Chapter 2 of [[road-to-dallas]] introduces him as the "archetypal" CIA officer, a "spy's spy": a company man whose loyalty was to the agency first and the country second. This page records only what that episode says about his early career (Chile, Guatemala, Cuba, Lebanon, Cuba again). The episode does not reach any Mexico City or Oswald-related claim about him. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 5:44)*

## Claims

### Character and role (host's framing)

- Host equates Phillips with [[e-howard-hunt]]: both largely worked Latin America, rose through the agency at the same pace, and were PBSuccess veterans who "tended to appear wherever there was smoke related to a controversial CIA operation," as the visible face of CIA meddling covering for the "respectable plotters" behind the scenes, named as [[allen-dulles]], [[richard-bissell]] and [[ted-shackley]]. Hunt was a personal friend of the far more senior Dulles. Presented as the host's personal observation. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 5:49)*
- Host: Phillips originally wanted to be an actor and moved to New York before World War II; the acting background shaped a career of deep cover and shifting identities. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 9:44)*

### Early life and Chile

- Served in World War II, "apparently being shot down over Austria" and making his way back to an Allied POW camp (host hedges with "apparently"). *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 9:51)*
- After the war he bought a small English-language newspaper in Chile and was first brought on as a CIA business asset. Host cites Dick Russell (*The Man Who Knew Too Much*) quoting Phillips calling his first agency work a "dangle operation": a well-positioned asset planted abroad to be recruited by a foreign service, ideally the KGB. Host: apparently successful, and it began a roughly 25-year Latin America career. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 10:19)*
- Started off the books in Chile and was put on the payroll in time for 1954; fluent in several languages. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 11:01)*

### Guatemala (PBSuccess)

- In the 1954 coup ([[operation-pbsuccess]]) he served in deep cover posing as an American businessman in Guatemala City, "making sure everything ran smoothly." *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 11:15)*
- Host frames the PBSuccess veterans, Phillips among them, as the core team reunited at the Havana station in 1959 and later on the Gulf Coast in the secret war against Castro. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 5:00)*

### Posting history in his HSCA testimony

- Host reads his testimony to the House Select Committee on Assassinations (April 25, 1978), asked for his full CIA posting history: Chile 1950, Guatemala 1954, Cuba 1955-56 (the height of Batista's repression). The original transcript redacted 1957-58; the unredacted version, released in 2003, shows Lebanon between his two Cuba tours. Host calls the redaction "somewhat suspicious." *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 13:52)*
- His New York Times obituary says he was in Lebanon as a "businessman," which the host assumes was the same cover used in Chile and Guatemala. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 14:59)*

### Lebanon, 1957-58

- Per his memoir *The Night Watch*, he and fellow CIA officers in Beirut in 1957 worked to support the US-chosen Maronite candidates; host says this was likely under "Operation PECTATE," speculated (citing a memo) to be the CIA's Lebanon operation of 1957-58, and pictures him "distributing bags of cash to the right opposition leaders." Fellow officer in Beirut: [[miles-copeland]] ("Miles Copeland Jr." in the host's words). See [[lebanon-crisis-1958]]. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 17:12, 17:48)*
- Host speculation (labeled as such): Phillips may have been clued into other Middle East agency affairs, in particular the Turkey-Beirut-Marseilles heroin route. Because Batista's Cuba was a mob and narcotics transshipment hub, the host argues that keeping the Beirut-Marseilles connection intact meant "ensuring the opiates kept flowing" to Batista's mafia allies. Drawn from Alfred McCoy's *The Politics of Heroin*; the Phillips link is the host's inference, not a documented claim. See [[corsican-mafia]], [[alfred-mccoy]]. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 18:51, 20:32)*

### Second Cuba tour, 1959-60

- Host says Phillips was reposted to the CIA base inside the US embassy in Havana in 1959, where the station was staffed by many former PBSuccess planners; David Morales is described as "a Phillips colleague in Cuba." See [[cuban-revolution]] (Havana station section). *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 5:26, 9:10, 26:11)*
- Host speculates that [[jack-cannon]], an Army intelligence officer, may have been scouted by or met Phillips in the Middle East and that Cannon's work with the Cuban underground in Havana "would coincide and complement" Phillips's role there in 1959-60. Explicitly the host's venture ("I would venture"), not documented. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 25:25)*

### Association of Former Intelligence Officers

- Phillips founded the [[association-of-former-intelligence-officers]] in 1975 in response to the 1975 "year of intelligence" hearings ([[church-committee]]). *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 7:33)*

## Discrepancies and cautions

- Name spellings in the ASR vary ("Atley", "Adley", "Oddly"); "David Atlee Phillips" follows the video title and description.
- The host says his Cuba tours were 1955-56 and, after Lebanon, 1959 (video description: "second tour in Cuba during the early days of the Castro Government"). [[david-sanchez-morales]] (earlier page) has Morales at Havana 1958-60 alongside Phillips; this episode dates only Phillips's arrival, so this is a difference in dating, not a contradiction.
- Host adds "Yet another Texan to add to our list." *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 9:44)*
- Wiki-internal observation (not from the transcript): [[e-howard-hunt]]'s alleged deathbed list names Phillips; this episode does not mention that list.
