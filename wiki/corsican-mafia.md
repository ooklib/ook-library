Corsican organized crime in Marseille and Saigon; the subject of the upcoming French Connection series. Per Eyes Wide Open, [[lucien-conein]] allegedly forged ties with Corsican mobsters in Vietnam (some met in Marseille during WWII) and connected them to [[vang-pao]]'s army for opium trafficking; he later admitted working with them at the DEA. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 15:45-15:57, 16:17-16:24, 17:02-17:10)*

## Claims

- Fuller coverage promised in the French Connection series. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 17:26-17:30)*

- LAOS 2: the host calls the Corsican Brotherhood "the prominent criminal organization" of Marseille, controlling a major share of the East-West narcotics trade; by the mid-1950s Marseille was "Europe's heroin laboratory" (raw opium paste refined into heroin). Saigon Corsicans handled shipping logistics and were valuable allies to French intelligence; they allegedly began small charter airlines to fly dope from northern villages to southern ports and would become key partners of the CIA and South Vietnam. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 2:18-3:56, 25:42-26:17)*
- Captain [[antoine-savani]], a Corsican French intelligence officer, ran the drug-marketing half of [[operation-x]], working with the [[binh-xuyen]] and local Corsicans. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 21:04-21:47, 31:05-31:19)*
- The Corsican connection is also drawn on the American side: [[lucien-conein]] is described as a short intelligence officer with a Corsican name who had supported the Corsican mob in southern France in WWII. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 46:57-47:06)*
