A very long investigation by [[uap-gerb]] ([the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QJxbyu-9Tj0), dated 2025-07-19) into the alleged 25 March 1948 crash retrieval of a 99.9 ft disc near Aztec, New Mexico, an event Gerb calls infamous and controversial but "refusing to die". He builds it chiefly on [[frank-scully]]'s 1950 book *Behind the Flying Saucers*, [[william-steinman]]'s 1986 *UFO Crash at Aztec: A Well-Kept Secret*, and Scott and Suzanne Ramsay's book ([[scott-and-suzanne-ramsay]]), plus [[leonard-stringfield]]'s status reports and the two main debunks, [[jp-cahn]]'s 1952 book and Mike McClellan's 1975 article. Gerb ends leaning that the case "likely happened" while stressing it is complicated. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 0:00-8:41, 2:12:39-2:13:58)*

- He says his earlier view on Aztec, "I'm not so sure", changed because respected people said there was more than the Cahn debunk, notably Coulthart on Jesse Michels saying Nat Kobitz strongly implied or told him Aztec was real, and Stringfield's own reversal. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 2:12:47-2:13:34, 2:23:00-2:23:16)*

- His main critique of Steinman is the absence of sourcing for the IPU, Marshall and scientist roster; his hoped-for proofs are that Gebauer worked for Highland or Paul Schaerer's Air Research. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 2:19:11-2:19:52, 2:20:49-2:21:32)*
