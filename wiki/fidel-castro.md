Fidel Castro's 1958-59 overthrow of Batista is cast by [[eyes-wide-open]] as the trigger for the Bay of Pigs planning and the formal CIA-mafia alliance. The host calls him a "closeted communist" and calls the CIA's assisting him unrealistic to explain by ignorance. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 35:28-35:53, 46:11-46:19)*

## Claims

- During the revolution (~1956-58) the CIA funneled weapons and money to both Batista and Castro's Sierra Maestra guerrillas via unofficial gunrunners such as [[frank-sturgis]] and [[jack-ruby]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 36:19-36:52, 39:29-39:44)*
- Host offers explanations (Castro concealed communism; the CIA hedged its bets) but says he finds them hard to accept and notes the CIA benefited: absorbed Cuban exiles as later contractors, a training ground for paramilitary experts, and closer ties to Havana mobsters. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 45:05-46:34, 46:41-48:12)*
- 1959-60 nationalizations: United Fruit plantations, Rockefeller-owned Jersey Standard refineries, casinos; banned gambling and prostitution, hurting [[meyer-lansky]] and the mob, propelling the "formal marriage" of syndicate and CIA. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 48:27-49:11)*
- Eisenhower/CIA planning: first secret task force by Nixon and Dulles (host says behind Eisenhower's back, per Kruger); Eisenhower gave green light on March 17, 1960; original assassination timetable aimed before the Nov. 1960 election. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 52:15-53:32, 59:02-59:16)*

See [[cuban-revolution]]. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 2:54-4:06)*
