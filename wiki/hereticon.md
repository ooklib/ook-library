Michels thanks Founders Fund, Michael Solana and Pirate Wires in the episode description, and says [[peter-thiel]] was "helping host" the event and gave a talk on the Antichrist beforehand. Michels frames the "real heresy" here as linking UFOs with past religious phenomena. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft.
