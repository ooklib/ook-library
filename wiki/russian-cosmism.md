Russian cosmism is the late-19th-century philosophy that [[jesse-michels]] presents as the Soviet counterpart to Western mystical rocketry. Nikolai Fyodorov held that humanity's task was to resurrect all the dead by technology and then colonize the cosmos; his follower Konstantin Tsiolkovsky (father of Russian spaceflight, rocket equation 1903) saw space travel as necessary. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 32:29-33:45)*

## Claims

- Citing [[diana-pasulka]]'s *American Cosmic*: Tsiolkovsky believed "ethereal beings" communicate with us via celestial symbols and that a genius channels these messages into technology. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 34:02-35:02)*
- Soviet thinkers such as Alexander Kazantsev and Felix Zigel proposed ancient alien contact ("paleocontact"), which was discussed in academic circles, state media and reportedly investigated by military and intelligence services. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 35:12-36:21)*
