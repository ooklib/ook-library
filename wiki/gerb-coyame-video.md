A video (about 46 minutes, dated 2024-08-09, [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bL3tMByq_WM)) in which [[uap-gerb]] examines the alleged 1974 [[coyame-crash-1974]], "Mexico's Roswell", a midair collision between a civilian light plane and a disc that supposedly led to the death of a Mexican military retrieval team and a covert US recovery. Chapters: Intro, 1974 Coyame Mexico UFO Crash, Exploring the Deneb Report, Plane Crash?, American Recovery Team, What Killed the Mexican Soldiers?, Wikipedia and Leaked Retrieval Team, Witnesses, Conclusion. *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 0:00-1:50)*
