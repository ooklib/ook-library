President Ronald Reagan's 1983 Strategic Defense Initiative (SDI, "Star Wars") was a proposed
space-and-ground-based missile-defense system against ballistic nuclear missiles. Gerb argues
SDI's enormous, poorly-audited funding served as a cover/funding channel for covert UFO-related
programs.

## Claims

- Since 1986, SDI scientist Aldrich Saufley alleged the program showed a pattern of
  "mismanagement and abuse and wasteful spending" and "misreporting and misleading Congress" —
  he was fired for "poor performance," investigated by the FBI, but given whistleblower
  protection by Dick Cheney (then SecDef) in 1992 after Rep. John Conyers found his firing
  retaliatory. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:06:04–1:06:43)*
- [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] was, by 1985, SDI's largest
  contractor by value — more than General Dynamics, Northrop, Raytheon, and RAND combined —
  and "the best represented contractor" for particle-beam weapons work from 1978 onward.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:07:16–1:07:51)* Gerb argues SAIC's history of
  diverting funds (per [[katherine-austin-fitts]] and [[denise-mckenzie]]) extends to SDI
  itself, given its scale as an SDI contractor. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:07:58–1:08:20)*
- Edgar Fuchs ("Fuché") claimed up to 35% of SDI's funding was siphoned to support UFO black
  programs, and that the reverse-engineered [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b|TR3B]]'s first
  operational flight (mid-1990s) was funded by SDI money. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:11:13–1:11:38)* See [[edgar-fuchs]].
- The [[defense-advanced-research-center|Defense Advanced Research Center (DARC)]] was
  allegedly "bolstered" in the mid-1980s with SDI funding. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:11:13–1:11:22)*
- [[project-brilliant-buzzard|Project Brilliant Buzzard]], an alleged airborne
  electromagnetic-shield surveillance program (per researcher Joseph Jones, 2013), was also
  "allegedly a program born from" SDI. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:11:46–1:12:27)*
- [[brookhaven-national-laboratory|Brookhaven National Laboratory]] performed SDI lethality/
  target-hardening work alongside White Sands Missile Range and Los Alamos, contracted alongside
  SAIC, in the same period Brookhaven is alleged to have received a downed UFO from the
  1989 [[mattituck-bay-crash-1989|Mattituck Bay, Long Island]] incident.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:15:22–1:16:20)*
- 1985: SAIC employee, retired USAF Maj. Gen. Jasper Welch, exclusively coordinated Star Wars
  efforts for the company. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:07:10–1:07:16)*
- 1992: then-SecDef [[dick-cheney]] investigated whistleblower fraud/waste claims regarding SDI
  (Saufley's case). *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:05:57–1:06:14)*
- 1988: SAIC's *Electric Propulsion Study* report for Edwards AFB's Astronautics/Aeronautics
  Laboratory (later part of Phillips Lab) — discussed at the top of the source video as
  investigating anti-gravity propulsion via electromagnetic/gravitational coupling.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:19–2:09, 1:31:14–1:32:29)* See
  [[science-applications-international-corporation]] and [[biefeld-brown-effect]].
