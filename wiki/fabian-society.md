The Fabian Society is Britain's oldest political think tank, founded on 4 January 1884 by nine
people meeting above a shop on Osnaburgh Street, London, after splitting from the self-improvement
oriented Fellowship of the New Life. Green & Pleasant alleges it has functioned for 140 years as
the intellectual and personnel pipeline behind the Labour Party — supplying, on its own website's
claim, every Labour prime minister in history — via a deliberate strategy of "permeation":
infiltrating existing institutions rather than building competing ones. This is the wiki's entry
point into a new sub-graph on British power structures, distinct from and currently unconnected to
the wiki's existing UFO-legacy-program material.

## Claims

### Founding, name, and symbols

- Founded 4 January 1884 by nine people, including a children's author, a stockbroker, and a
  civil servant, after splitting from the Fellowship of the New Life
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 0:55, 1:20)*.
- Named after the Roman general Quintus Fabius Maximus, who defeated Hannibal through attrition
  rather than pitched battle; the society adopted the motto "For the right moment you must wait,
  but when the time comes you must strike hard," reportedly written by co-founder Frank Podmore
  rather than an authentic Roman source
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 2:09)*.
- Used three successive coats of arms/logos: a tortoise ("when I strike, I strike hard"), then —
  until dropping it around 1900 for the optics — a literal wolf in sheep's clothing, then today's
  plain red square
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 2:42)*.
- Within 16 years of founding, helped found the Labour Representation Committee (1900), which
  became the Labour Party; of the original founding organizations (Fabians, trade unions,
  Independent Labour Party), Green & Pleasant says only the Fabian Society remains affiliated to
  Labour today, unchanged
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 1:46, 1:54)*.

### Permeation strategy

- Published *Fabian Essays in Socialism* (1889, edited by George Bernard Shaw); the first print
  run of 1,000 sold out, reaching 27,000 within two years and 46,000 (translated into German,
  Dutch, Norwegian) before WWI. Sidney Webb coined the phrase "the inevitability of gradualness"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 3:15)*.
- Shaw, in an 1892 published Fabian tract, wrote: "We permeated the party organizations and
  pulled all the wires we could lay our hands on with our utmost adroitness and energy." In 1908
  he wrote the Fabians had set themselves the task of making socialism as ordinary as being
  liberal or conservative
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 5:18, 5:48)*.
- Fabian historian Margaret Cole explained permeation as converting "key persons... in a
  position to take action," adding "it was not necessary that these key persons should be
  members of the Fabian Society. Often it was as well they should not"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 6:18)*.
- See [[permeation]] for the full concept writeup, including the LSE/New Statesman/Labour
  pipeline and international reach (Nehru, Lee Kuan Yew, Jinnah).

### Modern Labour Party presence (Green & Pleasant's claims, current as of March 2026)

- Fabian Society membership ~6,500 (1.6% of Labour's ~400,000 members) but supplying 141 of 411
  Labour MPs (34%) and reportedly more than half the cabinet as of 2024
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 11:06)*.
- Keir Starmer, Rachel Reeves, Wes Streeting, David Lammy, Angela Rayner, Yvette Cooper, Ed
  Miliband, Pat McFadden, Bridget Phillipson and Shabana Mahmood are named as current/former
  Fabian executive-committee members or Fabians; Starmer is described as the first person to win
  the Labour leadership while sitting on the society's executive committee, having written his
  political vision document "The Road Ahead" as a Fabian pamphlet
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 11:31, 11:55)*.
- January 2025: appointed Joe Dromey (son of former Labour MP/deputy leader Harriet Harman and
  former Labour MP Jack Dromey) as general secretary; annual income cited as £811,000 with 16
  staff *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 12:23)*.
- Alleges the Fabian wing of Labour was "openly at war" with Jeremy Corbyn's leadership: 24
  shadow-cabinet resignations in 2016, a critical paper by then-general-secretary Andrew Harrap,
  Starmer's own 2016 resignation from Corbyn's shadow cabinet followed by his leadership win
  within four years, and Harrap's 2023 "Plans for Power" policy compilation. After the 2024
  election, the Fabian Society itself stated Labour's manifesto was "shaped by Fabian values and
  filled with Fabian values"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 12:50, 13:34, 13:42)*.

### Conveyor belt of Labour prime ministers (per the society's own claimed history)

Green & Pleasant lists, citing the Fabian Society's own website: Ramsay MacDonald (joined
1880s, executive from 1894, first Labour PM 1924); Clement Attlee (LSE lecturer, 1945 landslide
with 229 Fabian MPs elected); Harold Wilson (Fabian chairman, 12 of 20 cabinet ministers Fabians);
James Callaghan (reportedly entire cabinet Fabian); Tony Blair (Fabian pamphlets including "The
Third Way"); Gordon Brown (Bank of England independence originated in a Fabian pamphlet by Ed
Balls, enacted within Brown's first week as Chancellor); and Keir Starmer
*([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 9:38, 23:35, 24:24)*.

### The Fabian Window

Designed by Shaw in 1910, a stained-glass window depicts Shaw and Sidney Webb hammering out "the
new world" on an anvil, a wolf in sheep's clothing above them on a red shield, small figures
worshipping a pile of Fabian books below, and H.G. Wells (who had by then quit the society)
thumbing his nose at the whole scene. Stolen from a Fabian country house in 1978, it resurfaced at
Sotheby's in July 2006 and was unveiled on 20 April 2006 at the LSE by then-Prime-Minister Tony
Blair, who said Fabian/Shaw "values would be very recognizable in today's Labour Party"
*([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 24:56, 25:53)*.
