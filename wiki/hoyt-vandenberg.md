Air Marshal Hoyt Vandenberg was, per Eyes Wide Open, the second director of the
[[central-intelligence-group|Central Intelligence Group]], under whom the agency began its
reform into the modern CIA, and was subsequently named the first leader of the newly created
US Air Force under the [[national-security-act-1947|National Security Act of 1947]].

## Claims

- As CIG's second director, oversaw the beginning of the agency's reformation into the modern
  CIA, aided by [[allen-dulles]]'s continued liaison work with former OSS colleagues.
  *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 20:29-20:40)*
- Named leader of the newly created US Air Force under the 1947 act, which split the Air Force
  out from the Army. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 24:04-24:11)*
- Wrote a November 1946 memo to Truman that 'the Communists have sufficient strength to seize power in France whenever they may deem it desirable', while noting they preferred legal means and that a seizure would contradict Kremlin policy. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 13:57-14:50)*
