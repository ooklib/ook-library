Giulio Andreotti (ASR-misspelled "Julio Andriotti" in the transcript) was a Christian Democratic
Party figure who served as Italian prime minister in seven governments and as a minister 21
times, and who in August 1990 publicly admitted before the Italian parliament that Operation
Gladio's secret armies had existed.

## Claims

- In August 1990, as the Cold War was ending, Prime Minister Andreotti — "a harrowing figure of
  Cold War Italy and the Christian Democratic Party," having served as PM in seven governments and
  21 times as a minister — stood before the Italian parliament and admitted bluntly that secret
  armies had existed across Europe and Italy, promising a public report on these networks.
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 3:31-3:58)*
- His eventual report was delivered late and heavily redacted by the Italian security apparatus;
  parallel investigations in Belgium, the Netherlands, and Denmark largely went nowhere, and the
  US/NATO stayed conspicuously silent. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 4:06-4:30)*
