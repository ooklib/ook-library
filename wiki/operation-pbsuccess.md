Operation PBSuccess (1954) is the CIA coup that overthrew Guatemala's elected government of [[jacobo-arbenz]]; per Eyes Wide Open it was the second of the "big three" coups under [[allen-dulles]] (after Iran 1953, before Lumumba 1960), largely at the behest of [[united-fruit-company]], and put [[e-howard-hunt]] on the map. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 5:29-6:16)*

## Claims

- Installed the military dictatorship of Castillo Armas; Guatemala was later used by the CIA and organized crime as a rallying point for the Bay of Pigs invasion. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 5:58-6:09)*

## Relationships

- Involved [[e-howard-hunt]]; targeted [[jacobo-arbenz]]; linked to [[united-fruit-company]] and [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]].

## Additional claims (War Conspiracy 2)


- Host: coup done on behalf of the [[united-fruit-company]], which felt threatened by [[jacobo-arbenz]]'s land reform; followed a failed CIA plot, PB Fortune, a year earlier (planned by [[walter-bedell-smith]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 8:44-8:52, 9:52-10:11)*
- Lobbying: UFC hired [[thomas-corcoran]] ("Tommy the Cork"), who wrote to Eisenhower and the Dulles brothers; [[allen-dulles]] and Bedell Smith later joined UFC's board. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 9:23-10:11)*
- Whiting Willauer ([[whiting-willauer]], ASR "Willow"), CAT co-founder and US ambassador to Honduras, let CIA-financed forces stage from Honduras and procured pilots via [[civil-air-transport]] at about $2,000/month plus $250 per mission (per a 1994 CIA history). Air base allegedly in Managua under Somoza. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 11:31-12:45)*
- Partly funded by [[william-pawley]] ("Paulie" ASR), who also funded the Bay of Pigs. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 13:01-13:16)*
- [[richard-bissell]] planned it; [[e-howard-hunt]] ran propaganda from the Mexico City station. The host calls it an "unofficial dry run" for the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]]; force of ~150 Guatemalan exiles. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 13:23-14:41, 53:03-53:17)*
- Aftermath: Castillo Armas installed, later assassinated by his guard in 1957; civil war from 1960; Guatemala City mob casinos and [[carlos-marcello]]'s second base in 1958. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 14:48-17:12)*
- Host's thesis: the China Lobby was "only one cog" in an amorphous American ruling class; PBSuccess overlapped China Lobby aviation, [[rockefeller-family]] oil and banana interests. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 9:00-9:16, 14:56-15:21)*

## Additional claims (The Road to Dallas Chapter 2, ewa/dallas-2)

- [[david-atlee-phillips]] served in deep cover as an American businessman in Guatemala City during the coup; host: he had been an off-the-books asset in Chile, on payroll by 1954. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 11:15)*

- Host: PBSuccess was the blueprint for the Bay of Pigs ([[bay-of-pigs-invasion]]) and "ground zero" for the CIA Caribbean team on the Gulf Coast; many PBSuccess planners were staffed at the 1959 Havana station. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 1:06, 5:00)*

- Che Guevara claim: per Guevara's own words as quoted by the host, the overthrow of [[jacobo-arbenz]] (he was in Guatemala at the time and tried in vain to organize resistance) was the definitive event that inspired him to begin guerrilla activity, ending in his support of [[fidel-castro]]. Guevara's quote names [[john-foster-dulles]] as the visible head of the aggression and as a stockholder and attorney of [[united-fruit-company]]; the host adds that Allen Dulles ran the CIA side. Host: PBSuccess thus formed the CIA team behind anti-Castro activity and also "radicalized" a key revolutionary, making it a "fundamental deep event" of Cold War Latin American history. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 11:52, 13:17)*
