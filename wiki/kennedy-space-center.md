Kennedy Space Center (KSC) is [[nasa]]'s Florida launch site and home of Swamp Works and the [[nasa-electrostatics-and-surface-physics-laboratory]] led by [[charles-buhler]]. In [[buhler-michels-interview-2026]] it is background for Buhler's credentials and for his Cocoa Beach residence, near Patrick Air Force Base.

## Claims

- KSC hosts NASA's only electrostatics research because of 1960s fatal accidents there. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 15:42-16:08)*
- Buhler says he lives in Cocoa Beach and will host the Electrostatics Society of America conference there, with a live demo of the Exodus thrusters. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:03:35-1:03:42)*
- The [[cocoa-beach-lights-encounter]] occurred on a beach south of the Cape, near Patrick Air Force Base. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:15:09-1:15:46)*
