- Samosa cites [[peter-levenda]] as calling him the public face of the huge Nazi underground. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:35:04)*
