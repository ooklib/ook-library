The Disclosure Project is [[steven-greer]]'s whistleblower-testimony organization, launched publicly at a National Press Club event in 2001 after a private phase codenamed Project Starlight in the mid-1990s. Greer says it holds terabytes of documents and about 760-plus debriefed whistleblowers, published at dpiarchive.com, and that most identities are redacted unless a witness agrees to come forward. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 4:22-4:58, 7:31-7:49, 18:21-18:30, 12:55-13:01, 1:40:19-1:40:28)*

## Claims

- Greer describes the archive as a "disclosure mosaic" and gives [[jesse-michels]] a redacted "Disclosure Project briefing document 2025", a one-page myth-versus-truth sheet and a map of alleged black sites, which he says gives Michels about 90 percent of what the new president would receive. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 3:50-4:06, 58:20-58:29, 2:14:44-2:15:06, 2:27:22-2:27:34)*
- Greer says the 2001 event was followed months later by 9/11, which he calls a diversion created because the story was about to become public; this is his assertion with no support given in the interview. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 7:40-8:07)*
- In late 1997 Greer says he sent a "UNOD" (unless otherwise directed) assessment declaring the programs illegal and unconstitutional, arguing this voided their non-disclosure agreements. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:38:07-1:39:24)*
- Named witnesses and materials from the archive mentioned: [[michael-herrera]], the late Naval Research Laboratory scientist Richard Foch (who allegedly saw the vault document dating mastery of gravity control to October 1954), Merl Shane McDow, Steven Lovekin, [[bob-beckwith]], the 1960s "nuclear tunneling device" patent, and a 1997 Bill Kirby episode. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 11:08-11:14, 2:45:34, 3:30:12, 1:16:16, 2:27:48-2:28:04, 1:45:56)*
- Greer says the 1997 congressional briefings at a hotel included a witness whom he had removed after finding provably false embellishments. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 1:00:19-1:01:43)*
