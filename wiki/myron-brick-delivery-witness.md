"Myron" (transcribed "myON"; surname withheld in the transcript, as in Stringfield's reports) is a trucker and brick-business worker who told [[stan-gordon]] and [[leonard-stringfield]] after the 1990 Unsolved Mysteries broadcast that he saw the Kecksburg object at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base. [[leonard-stringfield]] wrote he was convinced of Myron's sincerity after lengthy interviews with Dr. Brian Thompson filming him. All details below are attributed claims. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 52:39-53:27)*

## Claims

- He and his cousin "JS" worked for a family shale and tile brick business in Ohio. Two or three days after the crash the firm was allegedly contracted for a large order of "double glazed engineered" bricks for Wright-Patterson, the customer being the US Navy, which Myron later said were to build a double thick lead-lined structure around the Kecksburg object against radiation. Receipts were lost when the business was sold. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 54:13-54:55)*
- At the base a two- or three-star general in blue led them to a brick warehouse 50-80 ft inside, where he saw an Army 35 ft triaxle lowboy with a tarp still draped over it, and four or five personnel in white coveralls, visors, gloves and boots with pistols. Navy insignia, not Air Force. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 55:03-55:58)*
- Peering in, he says he saw scaffolding around a bell-shaped object about 10 ft tall (in the taped account 14 ft, 10-12 ft wide at the bottom) of dull copper or bronze color, charred or sooty, under parachute silks lit by one floodlight, with a collar bearing writing and a welded-looking triangle. A visor-wearing man said he was trying to enter with an acetylene torch, diamond bits and acid, and failed, and remarked that if there were bodies inside they might be too hot for the mortician. A guard threatened to lock him up. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 56:07-58:59)*
- Gerb links the failed-entry detail to the [[marine-rb-craft-guard-1963]] account. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 56:53-57:10)*
- Cousin JS in October 1990 confirmed the brick delivery and guards in protective clothing but recalled few details, then in 1991 said he had made an earlier solo delivery and seen a Liberty Bell-shaped object upright under a tarp on a lowboy, 8-10 ft tall. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 59:16-1:00:44)*
- In 1998, filming for "Kecksburg: The Untold Story", Myron added that he saw a body on a workbench ([[kecksburg-bodies-claims]]). Gerb initially found the late addition a red flag. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:01:30-1:02:02)* *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 1:11:24-1:12:21)*
