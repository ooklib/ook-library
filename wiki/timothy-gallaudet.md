Retired US Navy Rear Admiral, former assistant secretary of commerce for oceans and atmosphere and former NOAA administrator, presented by [[uap-gerb]] as the most credible figure to speak on USOs and one of the biggest inspirations for the video. He spoke on the [[sol-foundation]]'s November 2023 panel. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 10:33-10:57,)*

## Claims

- Gerb quotes him as saying the ocean-science community's lack of curiosity about this "story of the century" mystifies him, and, in a NewsNation interview with [[ross-coulthart]], that "we're being visited by non-human intelligence" and that it is time to disclose contact. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 10:57-11:43)*
- Gerb says Gallaudet described viewing the Gimbal and Go Fast videos and having them mysteriously removed from his inbox, and commented that the "SoCal anomaly", possibly USO-related, was scrubbed from Google Earth (both as reported by Gerb, unverified). *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 11:51-12:08)*
- His 2024 SOL white paper "Beneath the Surface: We May Learn More About UAP by Looking in the Ocean" frames USO study as an opportunity for maritime science, urges elevating it to national research priorities, and stresses the maritime security threat and the DoD's "non-reaction". It states that pilots, credible observers and calibrated instruments recorded objects accelerating and crossing the air-sea interface in ways not possible for human-made objects, implying materials science and physics beyond the state of the art. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 12:08-14:01)*
- It notes the disparity between investigating aerial and ocean UAP, highlighted by [[aaro]]'s Historical Report Volume 1 having zero references to USO or maritime cases or whistleblowers (Gerb's characterization). *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 14:01-14:26)*
- The paper cites the 2004 Tic Tac case ([[2004-nimitz-encounter]]), the 2013 Aguadilla CBP video, a body of literature on USO diversity, and the 1960 Shelburne Harbour divers case, which Gallaudet reportedly thought nonsensical until recent whistleblower revelations; details on [[uso-unidentified-submerged-objects]]. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 14:26-16:29)*
