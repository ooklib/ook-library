[[ross-coulthart]] has publicly alluded to a "giant UFO" and in this interview narrows it to a building shaped like a craft, outside the continental US, that he says serves an important national-security purpose.

## Claims

- He says he is "pretty confident it's real", not speculative in his book, based on multiple first-hand sources (more than two) about the purpose of the location, though he has never seen its location himself. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:14:50-2:17:44)*
- It is visible from satellite, "a building", "the shape of the craft", outside the continental US, and he says he will not reveal it because linking the location to its purpose could cause serious problems for the people working there. Asked whether it is outside the Five Eyes he laughs and says only that "it's outside of the continental United States". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:13:37-2:15:52)*
- Representative [[eric-burlison]] has indicated willingness to investigate the allegation and has said publicly he too heard it is classified. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:13:58-2:14:16)*
- Coulthart regrets mentioning it at times because of speculation online (people searching Korea and Greenland). *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:14:33-2:14:50)*
- He says there are other alleged recovery locations not yet public. See [[legacy-program-insider-claims-coulthart]].
