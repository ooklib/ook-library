Norman "Roughhouse" Rothman (ASR: "Ruff House Rothman") is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] in `ewa/dallas-4` as "the key figure that links US intelligence and the mafia during the Cuban Revolution" (the host's view). Born in New York, based mainly in Florida.

## Claims

- Operated in [[santo-trafficante]]'s gambling sphere and had close ties to [[meyer-lansky]] from his early New York years; came to manage all of Trafficante's Havana casino properties, making him [[lewis-mcwillie]]'s boss (McWillie being pit boss at the Sans Souci club); per the book *Warrior* he even owned a portion of the Sans Souci. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 21:51-22:30)*
- Ran the procurement side of the mob's Cuba gun-running networks. Per Larry Hancock, he was revealed to be behind the theft of 317 weapons from an Ohio National Guard armory (Oct. 14, 1958), intercepted in West Virginia en route to Florida (Nov. 4, 1958). *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 22:30-22:55)*
- Host speculation: because that shipment was intercepted about a month before [[jack-cannon]]'s Dec. 1958 Fort Hood court-martial acquittal and January 1959 ordnance-officer post, Rothman may have shifted supply to Fort Hood and Cannon. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 22:55-23:35)*
- Per Hancock, Jim Hunt/Bob Risch (*Warrior*) and Peter Dale Scott, the primary emissary between the mafia casino interests and Castro's new gambling czar [[frank-sturgis]] in 1959-60; both relocated to Miami in 1960 and Rothman came to own the Biltmore Hotel in Miami Beach (ASR "Builtmore"), "a future hot spot for mob activity". *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 23:35-24:18)*
- Host: through the Rothman-Sturgis link one sees "a coalition of sorts" forming among mob, mercenary and intelligence networks. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 24:13-24:24)*

## Relationships

- Associate of [[santo-trafficante]], [[meyer-lansky]] and [[frank-sturgis]].
- Boss of [[lewis-mcwillie]] at the Sans Souci in the host's account.
- Part of the [[cuban-revolution]]-era arms networks; see [[jack-ruby]], [[edward-browder]].
