Claims by [[jason-jorjani]] that the Moon is an artificial, hollow satellite with a megalithic city on its far side, built by Mars-based Nordics as a transport and terraforming device. Highly speculative.

## Claims

- Carl Wolf, a photographic repair technician and Air Force sergeant in the early 1960s, says he was taken to what he thought was a NASA but was an NSA facility processing Lunar Orbiter photos and saw mosaics of a huge megalithic city of polygonal, poured-stone-like structures on the far side; Jorjani says Wolf was run over just before the 2017-18 disclosure wave, "just the way John Mack was run over". *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:03:25, 1:04:51)*
- Jorjani argues from geometry: the Moon is 1/400 the Earth-Sun distance and 1/400 the Sun's size (perfect eclipses), craters never deeper than five or six miles although up to 186 miles wide, convex hard surfaces at crater bases, and Apollo impact seismometry that "rang like a bell" (once for three hours). He also says its density falls with depth and that Gordon MacDonald, Eisenhower's space adviser, said only a hollow Moon would fit. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:13:51, 1:15:22, 2:03:35)*
- Purpose: he argues the Martians built the Moon to move a population and to stabilize and terraform Earth (spin, tides, storms). *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:16:19, 1:16:56)*
- Michels adds a Carl Sagan anecdote: security clearance via Project A119 (a plan to nuke the Moon), then a flip to "priestly" skeptic. *([jm/jorjani-religion](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-27 - Aliens Invented Religion To Control Humanity! - Top Philosopher Jason Jorjani.md>) 1:09:26)*
