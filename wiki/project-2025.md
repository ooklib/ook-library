Project 2025 (formally "Mandate for Leadership 7... the Conservative Promise") is a policy
document published by the [[heritage-foundation]]. This wiki page currently records only the
single claim about it made in the More Perfect Union Bohemian Grove video — its authorship by
Heritage and an alleged personnel overlap with the [[bohemian-grove]] club during its drafting —
not an independent treatment of the document's contents.

## Claims

- Introduced on camera by [[edwin-feulner]] as "Mandate for Leadership 7... called Project 2025,
  Mandate for Leadership, the Conservative Promise," in a clip More Perfect Union dates to shortly
  before a Bohemian Grove encampment
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 13:48, 13:56, 14:08)*.
- During the months Project 2025 was being written, More Perfect Union alleges the Heritage
  Foundation's board was "like a quarter Bohemian Club guys"
  *([mpu/secret-society](../raw/mpu/More Perfect Union - 2026-02-24 - We Uncovered The Most Infamous Secret Society_ And The Truth Is Shocking.md) 14:24, 14:32)*.

## Relationships

- Authored/published by the [[heritage-foundation]].
