A 12 April 1969 encounter near Pori airport in Finland, which [[uap-gerb]] says is the only UFO observation acknowledged by the Finnish air force. He tells it briefly in [[gerb-air-force-encounters-video]] because Scandinavian countries rarely report UFO cases. The names in the ASR transcript are garbled. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 5:59-7:39)*

## Claims

- During a Fouga Magister jet training mission, a flight controller radioed pilot Tarmo Tua (spelling unverified) to check seven assumed weather balloons floating at 500-3,000 m above the airport. Tua reported the objects were not balloons but disc-shaped, "like balls with no extremities" and pale yellow, and that they accelerated away at great speed against a 180 km/h headwind. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 6:04-6:51)*
- A second pilot, Jau Corona (spelling unverified), also observed them, confirming the anomalous shape and speed. Pori airport made no radar signatures, but radar about 200 km away at Vaasa detected the seven objects after they accelerated away, for an estimated speed Gerb gives as "3,218 m/second". *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 6:51-7:29)*
- Gerb calls it a short case whose "trail ends here" and says he leans towards a UFO explanation because of the pilots' ironclad testimony. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 7:22-7:39,)*
