Cardinal Francis Spellman of New York worked as an American propagandist against the PCI during
the 1948 Italian election, distributing pamphlets and arranging celebrity radio broadcasts; Eyes
Wide Open flags his career as illustrative of the broader Gladio parapolitical network and
teases deeper New York-connection coverage in Part 2.

- The most indicative propaganda effort in the 1948 campaign was Cardinal Francis Spellman of New
  York's; the host states Spellman's career from the 1940s to the 1960s "displays the true depth
  of this parapolitical network surrounding Gladio and the intelligence agencies." Spellman worked
  as an American propagandist against the PCI, distributing pamphlets across Catholic churches in
  the US and arranging radio messages from Frank Sinatra, Bing Crosby, and Gary Cooper urging
  Italians to fight communism. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 27:03-27:55)*

  "ultimate say" on city bureaucratic appointments; rumors held that his homosexuality was an
