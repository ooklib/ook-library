Asset-management subsidiary of Deutsche Bank (about 800 billion euros under management by Fixler's figures, "roughly $1 trillion" in her other phrasing). Recorded here only via [[desiree-fixler]]'s account in [[wef-insider-interview-2025]].

## Claims

- Fixler says its CEO publicly claimed over 450 billion euros of ESG-aligned assets while internal estimates suggested about 120 billion, with no tracking system, and that the firm was already on US probation for a separate matter. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 27:47, 28:17, 28:49)*
- She alleges technology-heavy portfolios were relabelled as sustainable-development-goal aligned, gender ratios were fudged, and AI risk systems were claimed that did not exist. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 29:17, 29:52, 30:24)*
- She says the firm fired her, smeared her through Bloomberg and revoked her work permit, then faced a Frankfurt raid, US and German guilty findings and a share-price drop after the Wall Street Journal story. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 33:00, 36:54, 37:20)*
- She says it spent heavily on consultants (Boston Consulting Group, PwC, Accenture) and data feeds (Bloomberg, MSCI) on ESG, half a million each. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 41:04, 41:41)*
