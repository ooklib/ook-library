Researcher and co-author, with [[noe-torres]], of *Mexico's Roswell: The Chihuahua UFO Crash* and the follow-up *The Coyame Incident*, the main first-hand investigation of the [[coyame-crash-1974]]. Gerb's transcript spells the surname "yuriar", "Yuri Arte" and similar.

## Claims (gerb/coyame)

- Uriarte and Torres hypothesize "JS", the signer of the [[deneb-report]], stands for a US government department such as Joint Staff or Joint Services, guess the civilian plane was a Cessna 180, cite the death of Mexican pilot Enrique Riviera Gutierrez (30 August 1974), and found aircraft parts near the alleged crash region. *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 16:19-16:34)* *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 19:30-21:19)*
- They corresponded with the Wikipedia editor "Mercy11" in 2011-2013 about his additions; see [[coyame-wikipedia-mercy11-edits]]. *([gerb/coyame](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-09 - The 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash.md>) 33:47-34:44)*
