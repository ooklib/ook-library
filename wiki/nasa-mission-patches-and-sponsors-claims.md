In the follow-up conversation [[jesse-michels]] and [[diana-pasulka]] discuss why early NASA missions and current Space Force mission patches carry Roman and Egyptian deities (Horus, Osiris) and opaque first-century Latin mottos. This is a continuation of [[nasa-occult-symbolism-claims]]. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:02:31-1:03:12)*

## Claims

- Pasulka says people doing the work told her the symbolism pays homage to "the sponsors", and that they read Latin; she calls it a "large tradition that goes unacknowledged", hermetically passed, "not a Christianized veneer" but Greco-Roman with recognition of deities. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:03:12-1:05:19)*
- She compares [[russian-cosmism]] (Tsiolkovsky as believer in NHI, Christian framing) with [[jack-parsons]] (not Christian) as different veneers on the same impetus. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:04:32-1:05:11)*
- Michels' own view: there is "no one in charge", but a loosely distributed emergent thing with a mind of its own, and some people tapped into deeper layers; Pasulka agrees but says it cannot be proven. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:05:35-1:06:30)*
- Pasulka says people she knew figured out their missions on their own, only learning they got them right when given an award or patch, which she likens to an oral tradition (compared with Australian Aboriginal ones said to be about 30,000 years old and involving NHI); she says one person has 150-plus such patches. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:06:38-1:09:35)*
- Michels quotes [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s image of fish that see only the hulls of ships and not what they carry. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 1:08:15-1:08:38)*
