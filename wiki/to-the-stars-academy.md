To the Stars Academy, founded by musician [[tom-delonge|Tom DeLonge]] ("Tom Dong" per ASR),
publicly launched on Joe Rogan Experience episode 1029 (26 October 2017). Its advisory group
included Air Force Maj. Gen. Neil McCasland, Air Force Maj. Gen. Michael Kerry, John Podesta,
[[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]], Chris Mellon, [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]], Lockheed Skunk Works
EVP Rob Weiss, and 30+-year Skunk Works veteran Steve Justice. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
49:25–50:10)*

## Claims

- Sought to build, "from scratch," free-energy technology for aerospace and energy programs —
  which Gerb calls suspicious given several advisers' likely direct exposure to UFO
  legacy-program derivative technologies. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 50:18–50:47)*
- Gerb theorizes TTSA/DeLonge's project traces to the 2009 Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space
  Studies 10-month report and a "leftover" 2010 initiative called Project Forum, which proposed
  raising public awareness of "ET" and disclosure potential by targeting popular figures in
  California — and that [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]] and Chris Mellon "targeted" DeLonge to
  execute an updated version of this plan. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 51:03–51:29)*
- [[luis-elizondo|Elizondo]] performed liaison duties between TTSA and the DIA, CIA, and NRO
  while director of global security and special programs (2017–2021); Gerb theorizes Elizondo
  informed DeLonge of [[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]]-style "episodic
  visit" data around the same time IMCON was established (2017), constituting an authorized (or
  unauthorized) disclosure. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 56:19–57:18)*

- Gerb's Lockheed investigation elaborates on Skunk Works EVP Rob Weiss and 30+-year Skunk Works
  veteran Steve Justice as advisers, and further theorizes TTSA traces to the 2009
  [[bigelow-aerospace-advanced-space-studies|BASS]] 10-month report and a "leftover idea" called
  Project Forum, and that Elizondo and Mellon "targeted" [[tom-delonge|DeLonge]] to execute an
  updated version. By 2021, Mellon, Elizondo, and Justice left TTSA, and the group deemphasized
  its scientific focus for entertainment projects. *([gerb/lockheed](../raw/gerb/lockheed.md)
  1:24:14–1:25:41, 1:29:39–1:30:12, 1:26:37–1:26:48)*

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2)

- Gerb frames TTSA as the operational vehicle through which
  [[luis-elizondo|Elizondo]]'s [[advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program|ATIP]]
  partial-disclosure mission was executed, alongside [[christopher-mellon|Christopher Mellon]],
  Maj. Gen. Neil McCasland, and John Podesta (Hillary Clinton's 2016 campaign manager) — an
  effort aimed at positioning Clinton as "the disclosure president," which failed with her 2016
  election loss. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:29:23–2:30:00, 3:00:59–3:01:07)*
- Gerb states he does not blame [[tom-delonge|DeLonge]] himself, characterizing Elizondo and
  others as having "parasitically attached themselves" to DeLonge's genuine curiosity about the
  topic. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:30:40–2:30:51)*
