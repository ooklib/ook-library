Official USAF group proposed in 1961, with companion Project Blue Fly and Foreign Technology Division, tasked to "locate, recover and deliver descended foreign space vehicles". Gerb covered cases in Nepal, Afghanistan, Canada and a 1965 Congo metal fragment in a separate video. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:26:23, 1:26:39)*

## Claims

- Wood: primarily a space-junk recovery group but for a clear saucer a different team goes; Moon Dust personnel were involved and are connected to the majestic documents through the [[bowen-manuscript]] and an alleged IPU/"moondust officer" source. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:27:11, 42:38)*
- Blue Fly delivers items of technological intelligence interest to the Foreign Technology Division. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:26:39)*

## Additional claims (jm/kissinger)

- [[jesse-michels]]: described as a Cold War USAF program to recover foreign space debris that, per declassified documents, extended to unidentified objects; cites New Zealand spheres, a Bolivian fireball/silent aircraft site, and a 1957 Afghanistan event report sent to Wright-Patterson. [[henry-kissinger]]'s January 1977 [[kissinger-moon-dust-telegram-1977]] to Ottawa used "Moon Dust" as its subject line. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 52:00, 52:09, 52:18, 52:35, 52:43, 53:07, 53:14)*

## Claims (gerb/moondust)

- Gerb's dedicated video ([[gerb-moondust-video]]) presents Moon Dust as a possible DoD UFO crash retrieval program with deep ties to the Air Force and [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]], based on FOIA documents. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 0:26-1:37,)*
- Origin: a 1961 USAF draft policy proposes that the 1127th USAF Field Activities Group take collection responsibility for the Air Force's UFO investigation program and lists Moon Dust as the project with authority to "locate, recover and deliver descended foreign space vehicles". *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 3:00-3:39)*
- A 1967 review for the assistant chief of staff for intelligence, describing the 1127th, shows Moon Dust existed and that in 1967 it captured 49 fallen Soviet space objects and 17 US ones, followed by a large redacted portion. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 3:39-4:29)*
- 25 April 1961, Karachi: an internal memo records Moon Dust observing three cigar-shaped UFOs in close delta formation. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 4:35-4:58)*
- 1968, Nepal: a Defense Intelligence Agency memo monitors a crashed circular metal disc discovered in Nepal four months after a State Department cable headed "Moon Dust" to the DIA, the 1127th and Wright-Patterson called a diplomatic visit to Kathmandu a "fishing expedition" for information on space objects; the Royal Nepalese Army showed photos to US offices, and a satellite-crash cover story was allegedly built despite NORAD and NASA data showing a 100% satellite launch success rate in 1968. Gerb does not know whether the disc was recovered. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 5:05-6:04)*
- 1976, Canada: a FOIA memo shows Moon Dust asking Canadian officials for photos of space-related metals from a non-US satellite, of interest to the USAF [[air-force-foreign-technology-division]] and the CIA's Office of Weapons Intelligence, a year before the [[kissinger-moon-dust-telegram-1977]]. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 6:13-6:35)*
- 1978, New Zealand: Moon Dust investigated spherical, pressure-vessel-like fragments. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 7:39-7:49)*
- 1978, Bolivia: [[bolivia-ufo-crash-1978]]. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 8:13-10:56)*
- 1992-1994 denials: [[moon-dust-senate-inquiries-1992-1994]]. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 10:56-13:32)*
- Gerb notes the Wright-Patterson recipient pattern across the memos, tying it to the Roswell debris and the Goldwater story of being refused access to the storage room. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 6:52-7:32)*
- He personally rejects the reading that Moon Dust only collected foreign and domestic space vehicles and lists Zodiac, Blue Fly and the [[cia-office-of-global-access]] as other unacknowledged programs to cover. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 14:04-14:29)*
