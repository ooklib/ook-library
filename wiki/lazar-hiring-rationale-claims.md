Chris Ramsey asks the "big hump" question skeptics raise: why would a top-secret reverse-engineering program hire [[bob-lazar]], an acquaintance of the loud conspiracy figure [[john-lear]]? *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 15:53-16:45)*. This page collects the answers offered in [[lazar-ramsey-followup-interview-2026]]; all are speculation.

## Lazar's explanations

- Lear's claims at the time (over a billion aliens living in mountains near Las Vegas, a civilization inside a cool sun, breathable air on Venus) were so extreme that Lazar says nobody took him seriously and he was no threat; Lazar says he told Lear to his face it was nonsense and that Lear grew "crazier" over time: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 16:45-17:36)*
- The likeliest reason is [[edward-teller]], whose weight made a referral of "this kid in Los Alamos" enough; Lazar adds he may simply have "lucked out" and that a program might want someone "outside the box" who would not follow protocol: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 18:00-19:47)*, see [[los-alamos-national-laboratory]] and [[lazar-eg-and-g-recruitment-and-s4-access-claims]]

## Ramsey's long shot

Ramsey, flagged as "complete speculation" and "a wild grasp at straws", suggests [[dennis-mariani]] may have wanted Lazar hired precisely because Lazar knew the conspiracy world, as an insider who wanted to "blow the lid off"; he points to Mariani's later friendly-seeming call and to Mariani's last-minute invitation to watch the test flight: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 17:36-19:09,)*. Lazar calls this a long shot but "not impossible", and notes the test-flight invitation was a last-minute thought that made little sense to him, possibly due to compartmentalization: *([a52/lazar-secret](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-05-08 - Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87.md>) 19:09-19:17,)*. Lazar's own first reading of the Mariani meeting was negative, see [[lazar-union-plaza-meeting-claims]].
