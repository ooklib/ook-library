[[matthew-pines]] speculates that Mormon-network institutions are an underexamined thread linking Howard Hughes, Elon Musk and US intelligence. He says he does not know what dots to connect and that it is just facts to stitch together.

## Claims

- Pines says [[howard-hughes]] was deeply connected to the Mormon church: his senior managers (the "Mormon mafia", including Frank Gay) took over his affairs as he lost his grip and were the same group doing covert actions for the CIA, the Glomar Explorer, and potentially "hits" on both Kennedys (per Michels). *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:22:29-3:23:13)*
- Pines says Musk's manager [[jared-birchall]] is a Mormon elder who sits on xAI and Neuralink, manages his family office and crypto, and runs a private security company, "isomorphic" to the Hughes story. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:24:19-3:25:13)*
- He says the church has penetrated the intelligence community (recruiting traits: language skills, missions, sobriety, loyalty, hierarchy; "Mormons, Marines and MBAs"), that Brigham Young is a top FBI source, and that the NSA's Utah data center code name was Bumblehive, the symbol of Deseret. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:25:21-3:26:18)*
- Michels and Pines call Mormonism "a UFO religion" (Joseph Smith "gets downloads" and codifies them), and Pines says the church controls hundreds of billions of dollars and has lasted longer than the CIA or Carlyle. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:26:18-3:27:45)*
- Pines says Joseph Smith may have been assassinated by government agents; this is framed as speculation. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:23:54-3:24:02)*
- Musk is described by Pines as skeptical of aliens (the Starlink dodge remark), but recently saying that if the government has interesting technology it should be made available, then walking it back. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:21:15-3:22:21)*
