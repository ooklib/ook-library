[[michael-herrera]], a Marine formerly of the 31st Marine Expeditionary Unit, testified at the [[national-press-club-disclosure-event-2023]] to a 2009 humanitarian mission in Indonesia (the transcript also says Philippines and "operation Kitsana"; the location is inconsistent in the source) after an earthquake and tsunami, during which his team saw a large craft and was detained by an unmarked paramilitary unit. The account is Herrera's as retold by [[jesse-michels]] (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 38:44-41:18)*); [[jake-barber]] says he has no firsthand experience of that operation but recognizes the craft type and containers (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 41:34-41:52)*).

## Claims

- Herrera's unit, second battalion, fifth Marines, was flown with no comms into the jungle; his six-man team pushed to a high hill where they saw a craft rotating and shifting between light matte gray and dark matte black, about 300 ft across (three helicopters could fit beneath), "octagonal" and vanta-black, hovering just above the canopy with a subsonic hum. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 38:53-40:37)*
- An eight-man paramilitary unit with no insignia disarmed them, scanned them with a bioscanner, held them at gunpoint and threatened to kill them if they talked. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 40:46-41:07)*
- Trucks arrived with shipping containers with HVAC units on top; Herrera assumed drugs. Barber says the units match mini-split HVAC on containers "used up there" and "they weren't hauling drugs. They were hauling people, kids." *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 41:18-41:59)*
- Michels speculates the paramilitary might be DOE protective forces (recruited by private companies, commanded by Defense Nuclear Security under [[department-of-energy]]'s NNSA) or a private-contractor black-world unit, and notes the CIA's Office of Global Access is rumored to coordinate with [[jsoc]] and NEST ([[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team]]). *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 43:57-44:53)*
- AARO's alleged misrepresentation of Herrera's testimony is recorded at [[michael-herrera]].

The unmarked men wore black fatigues like those in [[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]]. *([gerb/marines](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-18 - The Marines Who Got too Close to UFOs.md>) 9:11-10:49)*

## Claims (gerb/herrera-2)

- Approach and interception: a team member pointed at the top of the craft above the tree line, which the team first thought was a building, and the acting team leader chose to descend; they had magazines in but no round chambered. At the clearing eight men with no insignia (moving in a "nose bed" interlocking-fire arrangement) screamed at them to raise their hands, flipped safeties off audibly, stripped weapons and magazines and checked only their military IDs on a device Herrera compares to a thin modern smartphone after first trying a biometric "BATS"-type system. The men had NVG-mounted rifles with PEQ-16 illuminators, and a lead man with a chest-mounted radio antenna; a standoff man stayed more than ten feet away. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:39-12:40, 25:53-27:13)*
- Drawing: Herrera sketches on camera an octagonal or possibly round craft with a pyramid-like top, shaded panels, vents on each corner, a scale pattern, and red, yellow, green and blue lights that came on at takeoff; he calls it "the blackest darkest thing" he had seen and says it changed between light matte gray and dark matte black. A raised platform underneath was about as long as the craft and rose or tilted to slide beneath it. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 12:40-16:37)*
- Trucks: four up-armored black trucks (Herrera guesses F-350s with unmarked bodies and night-vision-style housings), each carrying pelican-style weapons cases and towing a four-wheel black container with ridges, barred rear hatch doors and a black cylinder on the front that he later learned was an oxygen supply; he first hypothesized humidity control for drugs, which he told AARO. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 16:37-25:45)*
- Aftermath: the six Marines were told to go back up the hill and not look back, ran with their weapons slung uncomfortably tight (one gash on the neck), and at the LZ met a gunnery sergeant he had never seen who was upset they looked unsquared away; a CH-53 then took them away. No one discussed it except a brief exchange at Subic Bay, where one Marine said he would tell no one. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 29:11-37:01)*
- Herrera says there was no rotor wash on vegetation and no disturbance of the environment when it left, and that it was unlike any Marine, Air Force or foreign aircraft he knew. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 1:11:16-1:12:37)*
- Dating: earthquake at the end of September 2009 and the Denver arriving on the 9th; Herrera cannot recall the day but says early afternoon or late morning, sunny and hot. Joey speculates about why such an operation would occur nine days after the quake. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 27:21-28:31, 52:05-53:16, 1:07:36-1:08:00)*
- Helicopter details: a CH-53 landed at an airport (blue roofs, never left the aircraft), then flew to the hasty LZ; a second helicopter dropped a cargo net elsewhere and Herrera filmed it. *([gerb/herrera-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-06-30 - Michael Herrera - Insights into UAP Encounter and Black Program Insiders.md>) 34:23-35:17, 55:29-1:03:45)*
