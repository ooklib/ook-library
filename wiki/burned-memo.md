Document set that [[timothy-cooper]] received in June 1999 from an anonymous "Source S-1" claiming to be a counter-intelligence officer, saying a Nixon executive order ordered all MJ-12 documents destroyed and moved onto computers; the memo was allegedly snatched from a fire. Wood rates it among the highest authenticity ratings. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:02:19, 1:02:43)*

## Claims

- Original paper memo from MJ-1 (the Director of Central Intelligence) to MJ-2 through MJ-7 regarding "Project Majestic in Jehovah" (Vero parasite and Parhelion): "Lancer" (code word for JFK) "has made some inquiries regarding our activities which we cannot allow". *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:03:02, 1:03:10)*
- Gerb reads this as connecting [[majestic-12]] to the [[kennedy-assassination]]. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 1:02:19)*

## Additional claims (rc/mj12)

- [[geoffrey-cruickshank]] is "99% sure" the memo and its accompanying letter were written by [[scotty-miller]]; he says Miller fed documents to the incinerator after [[richard-helms]] ordered them burned in 1973 and saved the burned memo, angry at [[richard-nixon]]. Gerb's wiki source gave only "Source S-1". *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 26:02-26:40, 48:18-48:58)*
