An event before SkyWatcher was established at the Esalen Institute retreat on the California coast (spelled "Eslon" and "Eselin" in the transcript), where a psionic "inviting" session was staged for wealthy potential investors. [[ross-coulthart]] calls it his "ontological moment" and the one that made him think there might be something to psionic contact. It is Coulthart's first-hand account; no date is given.

## Claims

- Alex Klokus, a co-founder of [[skywatcher]] with [[jake-barber]] and others (Coulthart names James Fowler), invited Coulthart to speak; guests were high-net-worth people arriving by private jet, and Barber and [[david-grush]] came too. Coulthart introduced psionic [[jordan-joac]] to Barber and Klokus. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:20:28-2:22:07)*
- About 4 pm, Jordan said he had a clear image of a blue hexagonal craft, telling this in front of Grusch, Klokus and investors in Klokus's hotel room. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:22:28-2:23:06)*
- At about 10:30 pm the group meditated on yoga mats on the lawn; Jordan covered his eyes with a blindfold and within seconds said "I'm engaged" and "I'm in a craft", said "Horizon", and two golden-white orbs appeared over the ocean. A businessman said "We love you, come closer," and the objects came closer, within an estimated 150-200 m, hovered over the ocean and slowly left. Coulthart describes an emotional, loving feeling and credits the 50/50 gender balance. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:23:37-2:27:05)*
- Disappointed that the blue hexagon had not appeared, Coulthart says that at that moment someone ran up; photographs show swirling faces in translucent mist by the hot springs (others at the site reported seeing it too) and a blue hexagonal orb that had settled on the beach, seen by most people up at 10:30-11:00 pm. Coulthart cannot remember how it left. Ramsey notes the photo clearly shows hexagon sides and something around it. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:27:05-2:29:48)*
- Coulthart says this moved him from thinking psionic inviting "woo woo", and suspects it gave Jordan the credibility to become a lead psionic for SkyWatcher, "shortlived though". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:22:07-2:22:28, 2:29:48-2:30:28)*
- Hot-spring nudity at the nearby pools is mentioned as background. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:23:12-2:24:10)*
