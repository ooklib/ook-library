The climactic sequence of [[sentinels-of-ether]] as retold by [[jesse-michels]] in [[sentinels-of-ether-michels-episode-2026]]: covert helicopters carrying a shipping container of refugees are chased by US Navy F-18s into a canyon at [[coyame-mexico]], and vanish into a mountain that pixelates away. It is a work of fiction attributed to [[jake-barber]]; Michels treats it as possibly encoding real events but offers no evidence.

## Narrative beats

- After the operator's execution, JSOC and the Secretary of Defense conclude the killers are "black on black" (covert versus covert) rather than blue-on-blue friendly fire (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 26:26-28:00)*). See [[jsoc]].
- F-18s "Eagle 1 and 2" scramble from White Sands, unarmed ("fast, but not fatal"), intercept the helicopters at nap-of-the-earth altitude and order them to land on 243 MHz. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 28:10-30:37)*
- The gorge dead-ends in a 1,500 ft mountain; the drone feed, Eagle 1's flight controls and engines and Eagle 2's oxygen and generators fail; Eagle 1 ejects. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 33:39-35:04)*
- The mountainside "dissolves from 3D to 2D to nothing", the helicopters pass through into a chamber of stone and steel beams, and an electromagnetic force field re-energizes the illusion of solid rock. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 35:16-35:54)*
- Michels' reading: the general asking the Secretary of Defense whether another agency runs an unsanctioned op means "the enemy isn't foreign. It's domestic, but it's not under normal chains of command". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 36:54-37:10)*

## Michels' analysis

- A hologram is possible (he cites a 1994 Air Force holographic projector concept reported in 1999, Bob Lazar's holographic book, a Raytheon projection claim relayed by [[steven-greer]], and [[randy-anderson]]'s Crane display) but mountain-scale 3D holography is an "insane power bill" and fragile in monsoon weather. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 45:02-48:01)* See [[bob-lazar]], [[raytheon]], [[naval-surface-warfare-center-crane]].
- He proposes [[foglets-and-programmable-matter]] instead, matching the pixelation, the low-frequency pulsing (audio rectification) and the selective EM power kill. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 49:49-55:26)*
- Crafts vanishing into mountains is called a documented phenomenon at the Uinta Basin ([[skinwalker-ranch]]) and Bradshaw Ranch ([[bradshaw-ranch-sedona]]). *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 55:16-58:40)*
