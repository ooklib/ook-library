The May 1955 Battelle/ATIC statistical analysis of 3,200 UFO sightings, which concluded that UFOs are not aerial vehicles beyond human scientific knowledge. [[uap-gerb]] presents it as a public-facing product of the [[project-stork]] indexing effort.

## Claims (gerb/battelle)

- Gerb says Battelle and ATIC published the analysis on 5 May 1955 and that it concluded no physical matter had ever been recorded from any UFO sighting, which he calls a "massive contradiction" given [[battelle-nitinol-study-1949]]. *([gerb/battelle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-18 - UFOs in the Private Sector - Battelle Memorial Institute.md>) 3:49-4:21)*
