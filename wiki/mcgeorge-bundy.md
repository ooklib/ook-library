McGeorge Bundy, John F. Kennedy's national security adviser (January 1961 to February 1966), is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as a hawkish Skull-and-Bones-pedigreed insider who straddled both tracks of Kennedy's Cuba policy and who, in the host's speculation, may have been one of the "White House" voices urging the CIA to keep its assassination capability alive. This page is scoped to the `ewa/jfk-cia` primer; the ASR renders the name as "Mc George"/"McGorge"/"Mcjorge." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 34:50-35:16, 42:52-43:03)*

## Claims

- Family: the Yankee Bundy family of lawyers and public servants; father Harvey Hollister Bundy served as assistant secretary of state under Henry L. Stimson (Hoover administration) and again under Stimson as secretary of war (FDR). Host: all three (Harvey, McGeorge, William) attended Yale and "were members of the ubiquitous Skull and Bones secret society, which was a key gateway to the CIA and spycraft." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 35:38-36:43)*
- "Stimson's Kindergarten": per Kris Millegan's 2000 article on Skull and Bones, Stimson (himself a Bonesman) mentored a class of early Cold Warriors including [[george-c-marshall]], John J. McCloy, Dean Acheson and [[robert-lovett]] (the host calls Lovett a "fellow Bonesman"), and "of course" the Bundy brothers. The host does not say which of the others were Bonesmen beyond Stimson, Lovett and the Bundys. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 36:43-38:13)* See [[skull-and-bones]].
- William Bundy: Bones, then a career at the CIA after a few years at Covington and Burling; under Kennedy he moved to the Pentagon as deputy to [[paul-nitze]] (assistant secretary of defense for international security affairs) and stayed under him when Nitze became Secretary of the Navy on November 29, 1963 (host's date), during the Gulf of Tonkin incident that the host calls a "false flag." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 38:13-40:23)*
- McGeorge's own path: US Army intelligence officer in WWII (never an official CIA employee); ghostwrote a memoir for Stimson; worked on Thomas Dewey's 1948 campaign; joined the Rockefeller-dominated [[council-on-foreign-relations]]; became the youngest tenured Harvard professor (1953, government department) alongside [[henry-kissinger]]. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 40:29-41:50)*
- Host's pattern claim: Bundy was the first of a series of national security advisers (Bundy, Kissinger, Brzezinski) from Rockefeller-affiliated institutions (CFR, Harvard), all "outsized and hawkish." Kissinger was called by [[nelson-rockefeller]] to the [[special-studies-project]]; the host frames the two brothers as orbiting the theorists of the militarized Cold War (Nitze/[[nsc-68]] and Kissinger/Special Studies). *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 41:57-42:40, 44:36-45:01)*
- As adviser he oversaw the transformation of the post into "one of the most influential national security posts in the entire government." See [[national-security-council]]. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 43:03-43:23)*
- On Cuba he sat on the Special Group Augmented (the White House Cuba task force) but, through ties to CIA "old boys, including his own brother," likely also had an inside view of the CIA's own plots: "a partial view of both tracks." *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 45:14-45:43)*
- Assassination: per Hinckle and Turner (*The Fish Is Red*), Bundy spoke briefly with outgoing DDP [[richard-bissell]] about assassination programs, kept it to himself, and had "less qualms" than JFK. Bissell had taught him at Yale. Host speculates Bundy may have asked Bissell to proceed with [[zr-rifle]]; RFK is the other "prime suspect." Not established by the source. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 34:50-35:38, 1:08:01-1:08:31, 1:09:48-1:09:55)*
- Host: hardliners such as Bundy and his CIA acquaintances found [[operation-mongoose]] inadequate and favored the CIA's approach. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:01:43-1:01:54)*
