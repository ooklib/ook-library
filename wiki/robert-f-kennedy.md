Robert F. Kennedy appears briefly in this episode as a young Justice Department attorney who lost
out to [[roy-cohn]] for the senior chief-counsel role on [[joseph-mccarthy]]'s committee, serving
instead in a junior capacity — an experience the host speculates may have shaped RFK's later
anti-Mafia campaign as Attorney General.

## Claims

- [[roy-cohn]] was appointed chief counsel on McCarthy's team over another young Justice
  Department attorney, Robert Kennedy, who would famously feud with [[j-edgar-hoover]] throughout
  his political career. RFK did not get the senior counsel position but still participated in a
  junior role in the McCarthy hearings; the host speculates this proximity to Cohn and exposure to
  "the corrupt machinations of DC politics" may have partially inspired RFK's later anti-Mafia
  campaign as Attorney General, while conceding "we'll never truly know." *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 32:31-33:08)*

## Relationships

- Lost the McCarthy chief-counsel role to [[roy-cohn]]; served in a junior role on the committee.

## Additional claims (Operation 40)

- Host: after the Bay of Pigs the guerrilla operations were reorganized under Operation Mongoose, "Bobby Kennedy's show and his obsession"; Morales allegedly claimed a role in killing both Kennedys. *([ewa/operation-40](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-13 - The CIA's OPERATION 40.md) 5:10-5:29, 46:12-46:32)*

## Additional claims (CIA-Mafia Legend)

- Host: an "archenemy of the mob"; his 1960 book *The Enemy Within* named Giancana and Trafficante (per Scott, the CIA then protected them via the plots); his zealous pursuit of the national crime syndicate "in many ways ... spelled he and his brother's undoing". *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 3:11-3:26, 1:14:42-1:16:19, 55:25-55:49)* See [[cia-mafia-assassination-plots]].

## Additional claims (JFK and the CIA)


- JFK put him in charge of the White House Cuba task force after April 1961, as Eisenhower had done with Nixon; his Justice Department office was already overloaded with organized-crime cases (McClellan Committee veteran; feuds with [[jimmy-hoffa]], plus [[carlos-marcello]] and [[sam-giancana]]). *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 45:43-46:47)*
- Host: unlike JFK, RFK was "a bit more lenient" on executive action against Castro and "may have even sanctioned the continued use of mafia figures in CIA murder plots"; citing Douglas Valentine, in June 1961 his office proposed covert clemency deals to mobster Norman Rothman in return for help with Castro attempts. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 33:38-34:20, 1:08:50-1:09:21)*
- Host: he and [[william-harvey]] clashed "near weekly" over the Miami operations. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:17:59-1:18:06)*
- Host lists him with [[mcgeorge-bundy]] as "prime candidates" for the White House voice urging Bissell to build [[zr-rifle]] (speculation). *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 1:07:41-1:09:55)* See [[operation-mongoose]].
