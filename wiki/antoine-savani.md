Captain Antoine Savani (ASR: "antoan savani", "anwan savani", "anine savini", "Anan saini"; my normalization) was a Corsican French intelligence officer whom [[alfred-mccoy]] calls, per Eyes Wide Open, the "Corsican Machiavelli". *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 19:13-19:21, 21:04-21:22)*

## Claims

- Co-masterminded [[operation-x]] with [[roger-trinquier]]; he represented the logistics side: shipping the dope and liaising with criminal enterprises to turn the profit that funded the guerrilla armies. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 21:31-21:47)*
- Made the initial contact with Saigon's major opium dealers, the [[binh-xuyen]], and allied French intelligence with them via an $85,000-a-year stipend. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 23:15-25:04)*
- His Corsican lineage "likely" ingratiated him with [[corsican-mafia]] mobsters in Saigon who sold excess supply for export, "likely for Marseilles" (host speculation). *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 25:26-25:42)*
- Coordinated at Cap Saint-Jacques the marketing of the opium flown in from Laos. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 31:05-31:19)*
- The host draws a parallel: Lansdale/Conein mirror Trinquier/Savani. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 47:06-47:22)*

## Relationships

- Partner of [[roger-trinquier]] in [[operation-x]]; allied with [[binh-xuyen]] and [[corsican-mafia]]; parallel of [[lucien-conein]].
