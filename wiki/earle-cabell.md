Earle Cabell was mayor of Dallas when [[john-f-kennedy]] was shot in November 1963 and, per [[eyes-wide-open]], graduated Texas A&M in the same class as [[jack-crichton]]. His brother was CIA deputy director [[charles-cabell]], a "direct link between the CIA and the Dallas mayor's office" at the time of the [[kennedy-assassination]] (host's framing). *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 26:36-27:15)*
