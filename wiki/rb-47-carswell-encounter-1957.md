A 19 September 1957 encounter in which a bright fireball chased a USAF RB-47 for over 600 miles. [[uap-gerb]] cites it as context for the [[willingham-langtry-crash-1955]] because it is another Carswell AFB-linked military case, and as material the [[condon-committee]] included. All below are as described by Gerb from the Condon report.

## Claims (gerb/del-rio)

- The fireball, alternating red and white, was seen by the crew, detected by Carswell radar and picked up on the RB-47's radar; electronic countermeasures gear registered a strong radar signal of about 2800 megacycles. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 8:08-8:48)*
- Pilot Lewis D. Chase is quoted saying the light crossed in front of the plane at a velocity far above airplane speeds, appearing as "a glowing body as big as a barn." The Condon report says the ECM and radar data were removed on landing by intelligence personnel. Gerb points to a related CIA document. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 8:48-9:22)*
