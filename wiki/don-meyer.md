Don Meyer (the ASR transcript renders the surname inconsistently as "Meyer," "Myrix," and "Myer
Ricks" — likely a single individual with a transcription-garbled name) served as Assistant
Director of National Intelligence for Acquisition, Technology and Facilities (ADNI ATNF) from
2009 to 2013, a role Gerb pairs with its DoD counterpart, the Under Secretary of Defense for
Acquisition and Sustainment (USD A&S), as imperative to legacy-program operations, specifically
through NRO acquisitions. *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:42:24–1:42:55)*

## Claims

- Career: TRW technical staff, JPL project manager, CTO of Defense Information Systems Agency,
  ADNI ATNF, then Deputy Director of CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology, 2013–2021.
  *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:42:55–1:43:14)*
- Immediately after leaving CIA DS&T, joined [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]]'s corporate board.
  *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:43:14–1:43:22)*

- Career included TRW technical staff (1981–1993), overlapping roughly two years there with
  [[stephanie-osullivan|Stephanie O'Sullivan]] (TRW c. 1982–1985), and likely also with
  [[richard-haver|Richard Haver]], per Gerb's Northrop/TRW investigation.
  *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 1:43:52–1:44:01)*
