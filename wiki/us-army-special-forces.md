The US Army's unconventional-warfare force. [[eyes-wide-open]] (`ewa/shackley`) argues, as a self-labeled speculative thesis, that the first Green Berets grew out of the same Wisner-funded plan as [[reinhard-gehlen]]'s commando program, and stresses that [[ted-shackley]] later worked closely with special forces in Laos.

## Claims

- Charles Simpson's *Inside the Green Berets* (quoted): the 10th Special Forces Group (Airborne) was established at Fort Bragg in summer 1952 under former OSS Colonel Aaron Bank with the "highly secret mission ... to exploit the resistance potential behind the Iron Curtain in the event of the expected Russian invasion of Western Europe," infiltrating small teams usually by parachute. Host: this "sounds oddly similar" to the Gehlen commandos. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:01:41-1:03:00)*
- The Lodge-Philbin Act (June 1950) let the Army recruit foreign nationals; early SF rosters were foreign-born, with a "foreign legion flavor." The 10th relocated to Bad Tolz in June 1953, where Gehlen's commandos had trained. See [[galen-commando-program]]. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:03:25-1:05:18)*
- Example: [[lauri-torni]], ex-Waffen-SS, served in the 10th Group. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:09:44-1:09:52)*
- [[lucien-conein]]: after his first Vietnam tour with [[edward-lansdale]], called to Fort Bragg in 1956 to set up the first low-altitude paratrooper training at the Psywar Center (later the JFK Special Warfare Center and School); host wonders whether he had a direct role in the Gehlen program. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:06:40-1:07:26)*
- Laos: the only official US military forces in the nominally neutral country were special-forces contingents; MACV-SOG (ASR "Mach V SOG"; "Makave SOG") is described as a secretive group of Green Berets, Navy SEALs, Marines and CIA operators running "wet work" along the Ho Chi Minh Trail. Per Corn, Shackley, as Laos station chief 1966-68, held weekly meetings with General Westmoreland attended by Colonel [[john-singlaub]], MACV-SOG commander, who had served under Paul Helliwell's OSS Detachment 202 with Conein; Shackley and Singlaub reappear on the funding side of Iran-Contra. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 1:07:34-1:08:11, 1:10:32-1:11:45)* See [[oss-detachment-202]], [[paul-helliwell]], [[secret-war-in-laos]].
