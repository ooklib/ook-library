Charles "Lucky" Luciano was a Sicilian-American Mafia boss within the
[[national-crime-syndicate]], a close associate of [[meyer-lansky]], and, per Eyes Wide Open, the
central organizer of the Sicilian-mob heroin pipeline that fed [[operation-x]]. His early
release from prison via [[operation-underworld]] is presented as the wartime precedent for the
CIA's later mafia alliances.

## Claims

- Had worked since the end of Prohibition with Chinese warlords in Shanghai and Tianjin to import
  heroin to the US, undeterred by other mobsters' taboo against drug smuggling; became a major
  heroin dealer and pimp in New York City until his 1936 arrest and 35-year sentence on 62 counts
  of prostitution. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 23:53-24:22)*
- His criminal associates devised a scheme to secure his early release: in 1942 they set the SS
  Normandie (a French luxury liner converted to a US troop transport) ablaze in New York Harbor
  and blamed the Nazis, using their control of the New York Dock Workers Union. The embarrassed
  [[office-of-naval-intelligence|Office of Naval Intelligence]] approached the Luciano/Lansky-
  controlled union for help securing the docks, striking a deal — later declassified as
  [[operation-underworld]] — in which Luciano secured the docks from his cell in exchange for
  early release and deportation to Sicily in 1946, guaranteeing the free flow of narcotics from
  European and Cuban heroin labs. In Sicily, Luciano reorganized the Sicilian Mafia, which had
  been decimated under Mussolini. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 24:22-26:14)*
- Organized the [[operation-x]] heroin pipeline from Sicily: pure heroin synthesized by
  [[schiaparelli]] was shipped by the Sicilian mob in crates of fake wax oranges or sardine cans
  to Cuban labs controlled by [[santo-trafficante]]; the cut heroin was then shipped to
  Luciano-controlled New York docks and sold to Harlem jazz-scene addicts, with proceeds funneled
  back through [[general-development-corporation]] to the Sicilian mob, which distributed funds
  to the far-right network that made up [[operation-gladio]]. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 26:51-29:12)*

- From his prison cell, shared the locations/identities of key Mafia figures who could help
  administer Sicily for the Allies during [[operation-husky]]; along with [[giovanni-montini]],
  endorsed [[calogero-vizzini]] as an OSS ally, having grown up 15 minutes from Vizzini's estate.
  In 1946, FBN agent [[george-white]] struck a deal releasing Luciano on parole to Sicily,
  coordinated with [[james-jesus-angleton]]. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 12:47-13:19, 16:14-16:22, 25:16-25:57)*

- [[thomas-dewey]] had originally imprisoned Luciano in 1936 as a state attorney; a decade later,
  as New York governor, Dewey approved [[george-white]]'s request to extradite Luciano to Italy in
  1946 as a wartime reward — a reversal the host calls "strange" given Dewey's earlier
  organized-crime-prosecutor fame. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 47:26-47:55)*
- In 1950, [[meyer-lansky]] took a trip to Sicily to meet with Luciano; the trip's purpose remains
  obscure, though it coincided with CIA moves to place southern French docks under Corsican Mafia
  control. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 55:10-55:30)*

## Relationships

- Mafia boss within the [[national-crime-syndicate]]; close associate of [[meyer-lansky]].
- Recruited by [[office-of-naval-intelligence]] via [[operation-underworld]] for early release
  and deportation to Sicily.
- Organized the Sicily-Cuba-New York heroin pipeline for [[operation-x]], working with
  [[santo-trafficante]] and funding [[operation-gladio]] through the Sicilian mob.
- Provided Mafia intelligence for [[operation-husky]]; endorsed [[calogero-vizzini]]; paroled to
  Sicily via [[george-white]] and [[james-jesus-angleton]].

## Additional claims (CIA-Mafia Legend)

- Host: NY Dewey prosecution, 1936 arrest on prostitution charges, deported to Sicily in 1946; in exile helped establish the French heroin connection through Corsican gangsters in Marseille, in the host's telling reflecting the mob's origin as strikebreakers; the host classifies Luciano as a prosecutable "street" figure unlike Lansky. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 40:32-40:40, 47:44-48:48)*
