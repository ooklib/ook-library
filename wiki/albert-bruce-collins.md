Albert Bruce Collins (alias "Barnabas") claimed to be a metallurgical engineer and retired
government employee who developed metal alloys for electromagnetic propagation and magnetic
propulsion from 1942 to the late 1950s. His testimony was collected via researcher Tim Cooper and
analyzed by [[leonard-stringfield|Leonard Stringfield]] in Crash Retrieval Status Report 6 ("The
Inner Sanctum," 1991). Collins died 30 December 1990; Gerb can only confirm from obituaries that
he was an NRA and Nature Conservancy member — not his claimed engineering career.
*([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 26:33–27:41)*

## Claims

- Claimed to have observed, from ~100 ft away, a recovered oval-shaped craft (30–40 ft diameter,
  ~15 ft high) resting on a flatbed truck in a UC Berkeley warehouse in 1947 — metallic,
  polished-aluminum finish, with a large gash suggesting internal explosion, multi-layered
  honeycomb bulkhead skin, and a shiny sphere at the center surrounded by another bulkhead. Gerb
  notes the "multi-layered honeycomb"/nesting-bulkhead detail recurs across other crash-retrieval
  testimonies he covers. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 27:41–28:56)*
- Claimed (as of 1949) awareness of a multitude of agencies researching "technologies of unknown
  origin," including US Army/Air Force/Navy intelligence offices, the CIA, [[rand-corporation|RAND
  Corporation]] (founded 1948), and the Vatican; also claimed Los Alamos studied green fireballs
  and desert debris — which Gerb identifies as the real Project Twinkle at Sandia Base.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 29:08–29:52)*
- Claimed the Air Force wanted to "counter the black sky UFO threat" via early-1960s
  high-frontier space-weapon programs, and that Talon Gold and Teal Ruby (real 1970s DARPA/Air
  Force Strategic Defense Initiative-era projects) resulted from satellite recon photos of
  unidentified orbiting spacecraft — leading, per Collins, to the NRO's 1960 establishment. Gerb
  cautions Collins's 1990 NRO awareness could stem from 1970s newspaper leaks about the still-
  classified agency. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 29:52–31:19, 33:44–34:03)*
