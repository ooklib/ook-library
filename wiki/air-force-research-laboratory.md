AFRL, under [[air-force-material-command|Air Force Material Command]] at Edwards AFB, is where
[[eric-davis|Eric Davis]] wrote *Teleportation Physics Study* and later worked, stating he
uncovered "irrefutable evidence of UFO legacy program operations."
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:36:56–1:37:11)*

## Claims

- [[sean-kirkpatrick|Sean Kirkpatrick]] worked at AFRL in 1997, between his SAIC and NRO/CIA
  stints. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 31:34–31:49)*

## Claims (from the Hidden Wing video)

- AFRL absorbed the office of primary responsibility (OPR) of the disestablished
  [[air-force-special-weapons-center|Air Force Special Weapons Center (AFSWC)]] via the Air Force
  Weapons Lab (AFWL) merger in 1992 — the same institutional lineage that once employed
  [[eric-henry-wang|Dr. Eric Henry Wang]]. AFRL's commander is appointed by
  [[saf-aq|SAF/AQ]] as a technology executive officer for select programs.
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 34:10–34:33, 3:06:07–3:07:02)*
- Gerb highlights AFRL's Directed Energy and Space Vehicles directorates at
  [[kirtland-air-force-base|Kirtland AFB]] and its Aerospace Systems directorate at
  [[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards AFB]] (which he has separately tied to a possible deep
  underground military base network near Haystack Butte). *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 2:47:38–2:48:09)*
- Maj. Gen. Neil McCasland commanded AFRL from 2011 to 2013 (having earlier served as AFRL Space
  Vehicles Directorate materiel wing director and Ogden Air Logistics Center vice commander under
  AFMC), and reportedly advised [[tom-delonge|Tom DeLonge]]'s [[to-the-stars-academy|To The Stars
  Academy]] while publicly posing as a UFO skeptic; per a DeLonge–John Podesta communication,
  McCasland allegedly said Roswell wreckage was shipped to the Wright-Patterson laboratory he
  later ran. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 2:53:12–2:55:17)*
- Eric Davis (in a September 2024 SOL conference talk and a separate Gerb interview) stated much
  of his "concrete proof" of UFO legacy-program operations came from AFRL. Gerb, skeptical of
  Davis's simultaneous denial that ARVs or successful nonhuman-technology adaptation exist,
  suggests Davis's public denials may constitute program protection mandated by AFRL.
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 2:48:24–2:51:32)*
