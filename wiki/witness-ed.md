Anonymous retired Air Force lieutenant colonel, entry 11063 in [[steven-greer]]'s redacted witness list, whom [[uap-gerb]] calls "Ed" (the same witness that underlies the "Ed" section of [[hidden-wing-air-force-program]]). Gerb says he has ascertained Ed's identity through public sources and tried repeatedly to contact him but will not disclose it; he cautions that Greer's retelling may contain embellishment and that Ed has "gone off books". All claims below are Greer's or Gerb's account of Ed. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 2:19-3:17, 44:10-44:34)*

## Listing and career

- Greer's list entry reads: career Air Force, Nellis, Edwards, test director of electronic warfare group, tested reverse-engineered technology, at the Pentagon, DOE, wing commander at Edwards, contacted us in 2023. No files existed until late summer 2024 when the DPI archive added intelligence briefing modules. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 3:17-3:56)*
- Module 3 ("Covert government retrieval and handling of ET vehicles") describes a lieutenant colonel who managed pilots at Edwards and trained pilots who operated man-made UFOs; Greer introduced him to the Senate intelligence and armed services committees, and he wants to testify to the House Oversight Committee under oath. He left the Air Force in 2009 per that text, believes the operations were illegal and so free to discuss. Gerb notes this conflicts with the email's dates. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 4:56-5:46)*
- A late-2023 internal DPI email says Ed served in the USAF 1999 to an early retirement in 2015, has an engineering background, worked with the Department of Energy and served seven years at the Pentagon; three years at Nellis, three at Hanscom and five at Edwards. He went to the Edwards test pilot school in 2006 and says pilots there are trained for unacknowledged special access programs using reverse-engineered non-human technology. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 6:04-7:01)*
- After test pilot school he headed an electronic warfare group testing NHI reverse-engineered technology, acting as intermediary between engineers reporting to him and test pilots when they had a "mancraft" they were testing. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 7:43-7:59)*
- He agrees with Greer on free-energy and zero-point systems from firsthand involvement in projects, was tracking legacy-program amnesty legislation, and would testify to [[aaro]] only if [[sean-kirkpatrick]] were removed because Kirkpatrick "is an insider and is meant to squash information". *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 7:59-8:08, 10:12-10:38)*

## Greer's call notes (25 November 2023)

- Ed threatened legal action against [[lockheed-martin]] and [[boeing]] because he was read into programs overlapping the two at the start of his career. Gerb confirms Ed worked with Lockheed as a range technical contractor at Nellis in the early 2000s but finds no public Boeing link. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 12:55-13:19)*
- As a first lieutenant at [[nellis-air-force-base]] (1999-2002) he was briefed at Area 51 S4 via a 30-minute video showing "inspection aircraft that were essentially invisible"; a major said they had "captured lots of aircraft". Gerb speculates these were crash retrievals or ARVs and compares the [[flux-liner-arv-saga]]. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 14:19-15:53)*
- At Nellis Building 200 he worked under the (redacted) first commander of the 98th Range Wing (transcribed "998th"), now the Nevada Test and Training Range, and "saw man-made craft on the range and was there quite a bit". See [[nevada-test-and-training-range]] and [[christopher-have]]. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 16:00-16:14, 19:26-19:41)*
- 2002-2005 at [[hanscom-air-force-base]] he worked with MIT on foreign military assets but saw only terrestrial craft. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 21:02-21:18)*
- 2006-2010 at [[edwards-air-force-base]] he was "formally" read into multiple advanced aircraft programs and served as director (redacted) of an electronic warfare group of the 412th Test Wing, the same wing as the test pilot school. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 19:50-20:22, 22:26-22:42)*
- In 2008, as director of a redacted program, he was part of a go-public plan via the Air Force that was called off in 2009-2010 by a two-star general, after which he moved to the Pentagon. Gerb finds no confirmation and lists candidate major generals (Thomas K. Anderson, Eugene "Hos" (as transcribed), Casey D. Blake, and others) without concluding. See [[air-force-go-public-campaign]]. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 22:50-25:45)*
- He says [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]] has a tight connection to Edwards and guesses Hangar 6, held by "Logics", as a storage site; Gerb finds almost no public information on the hangar and says he will prioritize it, looking for infrastructure for living or deceased biologics. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 26:35-27:28)*
- 2010-2015 at the Pentagon he worked in a research-and-development panel chain; the notes record "$34 billion then $40 billion to non-Air Force authorization non-blue TOA", which Gerb reads as off-book black-budget funding and links to [[katherine-austin-fitts]]'s $21 trillion claim. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 27:36-28:41)*

## Gerb's inferences

- Gerb concludes with "high confidence" that Ed's work centered on using NHI technology to build and test ARVs, and that a joint reverse-engineering program spans Nellis/S4 and the Edwards 412th Test Group (test pilot school plus electronic warfare group), involving contractors such as Lockheed Martin. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 28:51-29:24, 41:17-41:58)*
- He contrasts this with the [[off-world-technologies-division]] testimony (singular devices, weapons-like) and links Ed to [[edgar-fuche]]'s claim that TR-3B pilots graduate from the 412th test pilot school, to [[mark-mccandlish]]'s flux liners and to the craft-donation theory. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 42:06-43:12)*
