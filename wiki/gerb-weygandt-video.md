A long-form investigation by [[uap-gerb]] ([the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Jc2G5aEH0A), dated 2025-05-16) into the alleged March-April 1997 crash retrieval described by USMC Lance Corporal [[jonathan-weygandt]] during [[operation-laser-strike]] in Peru, built as a precursor to a promised in-person Weygandt interview. Gerb recaps the testimony using Weygandt's 2000 [[steven-greer]] interview tapes and new 2025 footage, then reconstructs where the crash and the detention site might have been and who the DOE team and the black-clad operators were. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 0:00-6:20)*

## Notable claims by Gerb

- Gerb says Weygandt is a close friend, is a private man who is uncomfortable on camera, and does not appear to suffer from delusions; he says he met Weygandt in person in the American South with help from a friend (streamer "Kermit Frog and Badger"). *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:33:00-1:33:15, 1:44:30-1:44:54, 1:56:07-1:56:25)*
