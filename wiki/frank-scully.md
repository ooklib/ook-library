Author of *Behind the Flying Saucers* (1950), which popularized the alleged Aztec, New Mexico crash. Per Smith's notes, [[robert-sarbacher]] said its contents were "fundamentally correct"; Gerb has reservations about Aztec and reserves judgment. *([gerb/sarbacher](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-08 - Dr. Robert Sarbacher & the US Government's Secret UFO Crash Retrieval Group.md>) 6:47-7:33)* See [[aztec-new-mexico-crash-1948]].


## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Scully's 1950 *Behind the Flying Saucers*, after two 1949 Variety columns, was the second real book on saucers after Donald Keyhoe's; it reported four recoveries to 1950 and magnetic propulsion, and prompted the [[wilbert-b-smith]]-[[robert-sarbacher]] exchange behind Canada's Project Magnet and Second Story. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 30:40-32:14)*
- His sources were [[silas-newton]] and the [[dr-gee-scientists]]; Newton took him to test a doodlebug, and Dr. G showed him a cube radio and gears; his widow said she saw crash photographs. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 32:21-34:41)*
- [[jp-cahn]] tried and failed to buy the story in 1949 and threatened Scully with hoax accusations; per Sarbacher, the book's facts were "substantially correct". *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 39:14-39:44, 45:07-45:52, 1:43:06-1:43:21)*
