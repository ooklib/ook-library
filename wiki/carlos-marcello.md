Carlos Marcello (ASR "Marcelo") is the New Orleans crime boss whom [[eyes-wide-open]] calls a prominent player in the CIA's future anti-Castro operations; Louisiana became "arguably the second most important node" after South Florida. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 16:00-16:15, 41:11-41:29)*

## Claims

- Per [[peter-dale-scott]], mob-owned Guatemala City casinos were established by [[meyer-lansky]]'s representatives and Marcello; he set up his second base of operations in Guatemala in 1958. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 15:52-16:15)*
- His lawyer Antonio Valadas (ASR "valadas") chaired the regional conference of the CIA front "anti-communist Federation" in Guatemala City, which [[e-howard-hunt]] attended. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 16:15-16:47)*
