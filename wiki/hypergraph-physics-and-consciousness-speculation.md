Roughly two of the four hours of [[pines-michels-interview-2024]] are [[matthew-pines]] explaining, in a non-expert capacity, the Wolfram Physics Project and Jonathan Gorard's hypergraph/category-theory models (discrete graph rewriting yielding Einstein's field equations, causal and branchial graphs, observers as bounded slicers), then applying them to consciousness, autism ("non-verbal autistics" who may live in branchial space, per Pines) and NHI. It is speculation, not UFO testimony.

## Claims

- Pines says the brain may be a room-temperature quantum system, consciousness "may be the only anti-entropic force", and he mentions the "Telepathy Tapes" as anomalous evidence he takes seriously, while admitting paranormal research is hard to replicate. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 1:33:10-1:34:31, 1:42:13-1:45:20, 1:51:33-1:55:54, 2:20:11-2:20:50)*
- He speculates that NHI may have "gone through" a transition to exploring branchial space, and that elites' turn to psychedelics and consciousness is a cultural phase shift. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 2:02:36-2:03:16)*
- He entertains a simulation ("someone has virtually matrixed us"), a test for virtue, and timeline manipulation ("CI wizards"), and, after this abstract discussion, returns to a recent report from DoD intelligence contacts of a craft coming out of the water, saying he cannot explain what is actually happening; see [[nhi-counterintelligence-and-polygraph-claims]]. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 2:12:38-2:17:14, 2:14:45-2:16:11)*
- Pines says the UFO and consciousness stories are intertwined in American intelligence history. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 2:33:31-2:35:08)*
- Rocket-program mysticism: Pines cites [[jack-parsons]] and [[l-ron-hubbard]], [[wernher-von-braun]] and [[hermann-oberth]], Tsiolkovsky's cosmism ([[russian-cosmism]]) and Buzz Aldrin's Masonic flag as evidence that 20th-century technological paradigms carry spiritual overtones, extending to AGI-as-god belief among tech leaders. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:16:45-3:19:35)*
