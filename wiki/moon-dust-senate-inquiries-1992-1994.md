Gerb's account of New Mexico senators Jeff Bingaman and Pete Domenici (ASR "dominicci", "domich") asking the Air Force about Project Moon Dust in 1992 and 1994 and receiving denials and a claim that records were destroyed. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 10:56-11:50)*

## Claims

- In 1992 the senators inquired; the Air Force first said there was no Project Moon Dust and such missions never existed, then, after the senators answered with documents, revised its statement to acknowledge Moon Dust's existence and function with regard to UFOs. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 11:03-11:28)*
- In 1994 Domenici requested 11 documents; the Air Force said the projects no longer existed and that any classified reports had been destroyed, which Gerb calls "categorically false". *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 11:35-12:04)*
- Gerb's speculation: the denials and destruction may reflect the early-1990s special access program reorganization that [[thomas-wilson]] describes in the Wilson-Davis material, hiding crash retrieval and reverse engineering inside compartmented programs (unverified). *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 12:14-12:54)*
- He concludes it is quite plausible the Air Force destroyed most Moon Dust files to hide a UFO crash retrieval program. *([gerb/moondust](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-24 - MOON DUST - The Pentagon's Secret UFO Programs.md>) 13:16-13:32)*
