USAF range complex run by the 98th Range Wing (transcribed "998th") from [[nellis-air-force-base]], the largest contiguous air and ground space available for peacetime military operations in the Free World, with about 2.9 million acres and 5,000 square miles of restricted airspace, which also hosts Department of Energy testing. It is one of the "Western Ranges" of the [[hidden-wing-air-force-program]].

## Claims (gerb/edwards-afb)

- Per [[uap-gerb]] the range began operations in 2001, and its South Range contains [[groom-lake]] (Area 51). He calls it an ideal place for DOD and contractors to test reverse-engineered technology. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 16:14-17:39)*
- [[witness-ed]] worked under its first commander and says he saw man-made craft on the range; Gerb infers the range commander may have oversight of reverse-engineering programs and examines the 2005-2007 commander, [[christopher-have]]. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 16:08-19:41)*
- Gerb concludes the Edwards 412th Test Wing program works in conjunction with the NTTR. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 20:30-20:47)*
