According to [[jake-barber]] (as presented in [[sentinels-of-ether-michels-episode-2026]]), the origin of his manuscript was a private-contractor operation to recover missing Panasonic Toughbook laptops believed to hold evidence of crash retrieval programs. The employer is unnamed; Barber says of it "if you took two guesses, you'd get both of them right on the first try" (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 7:50-8:04)*), and elsewhere in the wiki the guess is Lockheed Martin or a similar contractor (see [[lockheed-martin]]; not confirmed). All details are Barber's or Michels' unverified claims.

## Claims

- Barber worked for a private company embedded in legacy aerospace on need-to-know operations; he was sent to track down missing ruggedized Toughbooks believed to hold sensor data and video footage of crash retrieval (CR) programs. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 12:32-13:16)*
- A human-intelligence package led a recovery team to the first Toughbooks, which had no hard drives; a human asset led to a steel container of hard drives about 25 ft underwater in a glacier lake in the Sierras. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 13:27-14:04)*
- Don Paul Bales, a 25-year military and law enforcement veteran, says he worked under Barber on the same operation. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 14:04-14:15)*
- The next mission had a new intel contact, coordinates and chain of command; an ambush awaited ("shots were fired"), and Barber's team set out to learn who they were working for. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 14:15-14:46)*
- Per an "insider leak" cited by Michels, the programs documented on the Toughbooks used temporary flight restrictions to clear the skies, lure UFOs in and bring them down with EMP weaponry, and civilian aircraft that entered the restricted airspace were allegedly downed with bodies and wreckage disposed of. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 14:46-15:26)* See [[ufo-baiting-and-directed-energy-claims]] and [[wall-street-journal-malmstrom-emp-claims]].
- The employer allegedly wanted to destroy both the Toughbooks and Barber, the "plausible deniability button ... would sever us as the hidden hand", so he turned his spycraft against the employer by publishing through [[dopsr-prepublication-review-claims]]. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 15:26-16:25)*
- At the [[national-press-club-disclosure-event-2023]] Barber says he was sent on behalf of his employer to threat-assess whistleblowers and report them, while the missing Toughbooks were on his mind. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 38:04-38:44)*
