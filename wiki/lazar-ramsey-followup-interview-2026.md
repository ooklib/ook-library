Chris Ramsey's DEBRIEFED episode 87 (published 2026-05-08, roughly a 35-minute follow-up) is a second sit-down with [[bob-lazar]], recorded a few months after [[lazar-ramsey-interview-2025]] and aired once Luigi Vendittelli's documentary *S4: The Bob Lazar Story* ([[project-gravitator-s4-documentary]]) was out. Its hook is a "new name" in connection with S4, security guard [[chuck-payne]], which Lazar had withheld for decades and which appeared in a deleted documentary scene. The rest revisits [[dennis-mariani]], the [[eg-and-g]] escort, and asks why S4 would hire Lazar. All content is Lazar's or the host's uncorroborated statement.
