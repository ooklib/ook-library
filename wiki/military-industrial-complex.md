The military-industrial complex (MIC) is the nexus of arms industry, aviation and aerospace firms,
lobbyists, the military and the intelligence community that [[eyes-wide-open]] presents as one
driver of the [[war-conspiracy]]. In War Conspiracy 1 the host traces its formation from World War
II aviation contracts, through [[nsc-68]] and the [[korean-war]], to a permanent peacetime
arms economy.

## Claims

- Episode opens with an audio excerpt (the transcript does not name the speaker; it is the
  "unwarranted influence" passage of [[dwight-d-eisenhower]]'s farewell address, identification is
  mine by inference): "this conjunction of an immense military establishment and a large arms
  industry is new in the American experience... we must guard against the acquisition of
  unwarranted influence, whether sought or unsought, by the military industrial complex."
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 4:14-5:00)*
- The host describes a nexus of intelligence agents, business elites and military figures organized
  into informal elite circles; the first is the [[china-lobby]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 5:00-5:22)*
- WWII massively increased investment in the domestic arms industry, above all a rigorous aviation
  manufacturing sector; air power and air superiority became fundamental to modern warfare, and
  the industrialists who profited formed a powerful lobby bloc in the Cold War (see
  [[howard-hughes]], [[pan-american-airways]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 22:30-23:09)*
- [[nsc-68]] (April 1950) is credited with laying the foundation of the MIC "as we know it today"
  and of perpetual peacetime military spending. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 36:14-36:27, 38:04-38:14)*
- The [[korean-war]] institutionalized peacetime military spending: per James Bradley, Truman's
  first post-entry defense budget was $50 billion, nearly double the prior year's and the first
  substantial increase since 1946; the defense industry was "reignited, not to be turned off ever
  again." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 38:35-39:20)*
- "Military Keynesianism": the host explains Keynesian pump-priming, applied by FDR's New Deal to
  civilian works and then diverted to weapons manufacturing after 1941, and says its peacetime
  revival with the Korean War is when "the military industrial complex was born." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 39:20-41:08)*
- The Truman administration began awarding large contracts to arms manufacturers, many in aviation
  overlapping with China Lobby ventures; aerospace (airplanes, helicopters, rockets, "eventually
  space shuttles") boomed, with Boeing, Lockheed and Northrop emerging as "the crown jewels" of the
  new MIC ([[boeing]], [[lockheed-martin]], [[northrop-grumman]]). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 41:08-41:33)*
- Rocketry, "first developed by the Nazis and imported to the US through the smuggling of Operation
  Paperclip scientists," opened the door to theoretically infinite spending on nuclear-armed and
  eventually space-bound rockets. (Paperclip has no page here; compare [[operation-safe-haven]] for
  other post-war Nazi transfers as covered by the series.) *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 41:33-41:56)*
- The host ties the 1950s-60s MIC to the "golden era" of American capitalism: high-paying unionized
  factory jobs plus the GI Bill opened the middle class (segregated, he notes), creating a "new social
  contract" of taxpayers funding a peacetime arms industry in exchange for protected manufacturing
  jobs; he says defense remains one of the few protected domestic manufacturing sectors. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 41:56-43:36)*
- Recurring beneficiaries named: PanAm, TWA, Boeing and Hughes Aircraft, which "would all benefit
  massively from the Vietnam War" as providers of military aircraft and air services in the theater.
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 28:37-28:58)*
- Preview: the next episode covers "Rockefeller funding of CIA spy satellites," which the host
  calls some of his deepest and most obscure research; the series is to end with
  [[lyndon-b-johnson]] and the MIC. (Possible future bridge to the [[national-reconnaissance-office]]
  thread; nothing is claimed about the NRO in this episode.) *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 50:29-50:54, 51:19-51:39)*
