A method of ranking a civilization's technological advancement by the energy it can harness, proposed by Soviet astronomer Nikolai Kardashev in 1964, as summarized by [[uap-gerb]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 19:32-19:52)*

## Claims

- Type I harnesses all energy of its planet (control of weather and natural forces); Type II all energy of its host star (for example a Dyson sphere, occupying other planets and moons); Type III galactic-scale energy and colonization of its galaxy; extensions IV and V (galactic cluster, universe) are speculative. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 19:52-21:08)*
- Gerb says humanity is currently around 0.7 on the scale. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 21:15-21:24)*
