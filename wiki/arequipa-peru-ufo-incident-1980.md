An 11 April 1980 encounter at La Joya air base near Arequipa, Peru, in which Lieutenant Oscar Santa Maria Huertas (ASR renders it "Hueras", "Herrera", "quas", and Gerb's "Whereas/Querto") fired on and chased a silvery object in a Russian-made Su-22. [[uap-gerb]] says he found the case in the same DOD Joint Chiefs briefing that highlights the 1968 Nepal disc when he covered [[project-moon-dust]], and ranks it among the two most compelling cases in [[gerb-air-force-encounters-video]]. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 7:33-11:27)*

## Claims

- The pilot was ordered to intercept a silvery object floating near the end of the runway in restricted airspace, described as a grave challenge to national sovereignty during a period of high espionage concern in Peru. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 7:48-8:29)*
- He climbed to 2,500 m and fired a 64-shell burst of 30 mm rounds that had no effect; the object shot skyward, and he chased it on afterburners at Mach 1.6 over 84 km until it stopped instantly and he had to turn sharply to avoid collision. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 8:29-9:18)*
- Re-engaging, he says he locked the target but it climbed fast and evaded; he tried to get above it as Gorman did, but it shadowed him from 14,000 to 19,200 ft. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 9:18-10:04)*
- Low on fuel he closed to 100 m and says he saw an object about 10 m across with a cream-colored shiny dome like a half light bulb on a wide silver circular metallic base, with no wings, jets, exhaust, windows or antenna, and no visible propulsion; frightened, he zigzagged away. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 10:04-10:55)*
- Multiple eyewitnesses and base personnel also saw the object, leading to an official DOD investigation whose document corroborates the pilot's account of firing 30 mm shells without effect. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 10:55-11:27)*
