Sidney Webb (1859–1947) was a leading [[fabian-society]] theorist, co-founder of the
[[london-school-of-economics]], author of Labour's Clause IV, and — with his wife
[[beatrice-webb]] — a prominent published eugenicist and, later, an apologist for Stalin's
purges.

## Claims

- Coined the phrase "the inevitability of gradualness" to describe the Fabian approach to
  socialism as evolutionary rather than revolutionary
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 3:50)*.
- Stated intention in founding the LSE (1895): to teach political economy "on more modern and
  more socialist lines"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 7:00)*.
- Wrote Clause IV of the Labour Party's 1918 constitution, committing the party to common
  ownership of the means of production — in force for 77 years until Tony Blair replaced it,
  using a Fabian pamphlet as justification
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 9:00, 9:14)*.
- In a 1907 Fabian tract, "The Decline in the Birth Rate," warned of "degenerate hordes" and the
  country "falling victim" to Irish and Jewish population growth; in the (mainstream academic)
  Eugenics Review wrote that "no consistent eugenist" could be a passive individualist and "must
  interfere, interfere, interfere"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 17:23, 17:40)*.
- With Beatrice Webb, published *Soviet Communism: A New Civilization* (1935, with a question
  mark in the title); the 1937 second edition — published the year of Stalin's Great Terror
  (an estimated 680,000 executions) — dropped the question mark; the 1941 third edition defended
  the purges as having "cut out the dead wood"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 21:32, 21:49, 22:02)*.
- Received a 1938 letter from George Bernard Shaw proposing state eugenic experiments "weeding
  out" undesirable strains as an answer to "the Jewish question"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 16:36)*.

## Relationships

- Founding-era [[fabian-society]] member and co-founder of [[london-school-of-economics]].
- Married to [[beatrice-webb]]; co-author of *Soviet Communism: A New Civilization*.
- Author of Labour's original Clause IV, later replaced by Tony Blair.
- Trained [[william-beveridge]] as a researcher (with Beatrice Webb).
- Critiqued (as one of "the Webbs") by [[george-orwell]] in *The Road to Wigan Pier*.
