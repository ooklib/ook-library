Hans-Adam II, Prince of Liechtenstein, is discussed in the `jm/cover-up` episode as a UFO-interested royal ("Likenstein" in ASR). Claims (unverified, from hearsay in the conversation):

- [[jesse-michels]] says the prince believes Jesus will show up on a flying saucer; [[jason-samosa]] reads it as an eschatological belief. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 2:02:44, 2:03:10)*
- Shirley MacLaine's book Going Within is said to describe him at a Washington gathering with Senator Claiborne Pell and Budd Hopkins; he reportedly convened Roswell witnesses and was friends with Whitley Strieber. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 2:00:43, 2:01:50)*
- Ed May says he worked with the prince, and Samosa wonders whether he financially supported the [[lone-stars-group]]. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 2:02:10, 2:02:44)*
- Samosa says the prince was not involved in the [[world-commerce-corporation]] as far as he has seen. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:29:01)*
