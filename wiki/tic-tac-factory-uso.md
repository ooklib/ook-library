The "Tic Tac Factory" is Gerb's name (given to him by an unnamed source) for an alleged, several
decades-old, waived unacknowledged Navy/MITRE program that monitors, tracks, and tasks for
intercept an enormous unidentified submerged object (USO) that permanently or
semi-permanently resides in the Atlantic near the Bahamas.

## Claims

- The object is described as roughly "the size of 10 city blocks / a football field," deep
  under the Atlantic, appearing as an "opaque blob" — possibly its true shape, or possibly a
  sensor/cloaking artifact. *(13:39–14:15, 1:23:19–1:24:06)*
- It allegedly deploys/collects its own "subordinate vehicles" — tic-tac-shaped, cylindrical,
  disc-shaped, and blobby craft capable of transmedium travel (leaving water to fly, sometimes
  returning to the "mother ship"). *(14:03–14:15, 1:24:06–1:24:28)*
- Geographically situated near the Blake–Bahama Basin / "Tongue of the Ocean," where Atlantic
  depths off the Bahamas shelf plunge to 4,000+ m, near the Navy's
  [[atlantic-undersea-test-and-evaluation-center|AUTEC]] range. *(1:24:38–1:25:34)*
- Gerb connects Rep. [[eric-burlison]]'s public account (from an interview clip embedded in the
  video) of meeting a Navy-source whistleblower alongside Rep. Anna Paulina Luna, whose account
  reminded Burlison of the film *The Abyss* and described "a structure in the deep ocean," as
  likely referring to this same object. *(14:39–15:53)*
- Corroborating incident testimony cited: Cdr. [[david-fravor]]'s 2004 "Tic Tac" encounter off
  San Diego, where a large submerged object appeared to be "churning the surface" of the ocean
  before the tic-tac object itself was observed; and Senior Chief Wiggins' testimony of a
  self-luminous tic-tac object emerging from the ocean, linking with three similar objects, then
  departing in synchronized near-instantaneous acceleration. *(1:26:20–1:28:19)*
  See [[2004-nimitz-encounter]].
- 18 Bravo weapons Sergeant [[randy-anderson]] claimed (mid-2010s) he was taken to an "off-world
  technologies division" below [[naval-surface-warfare-center-crane|NSWC Crane]] and shown
  recovered NHI technology — a consciousness-reactive sphere and a gauntlet-like device. Gerb
  states he believes/stands by Anderson's account as related to this MITRE program.
  *(1:13:12–1:13:41)*
- A pseudonymous **April 2023 4chan poster** made several claims about a "mobile construction
  facility" hiding in the ocean that builds UFOs to spec; see [[4chan-whistleblower-2023]] for
  the itemized claims and Gerb's point-by-point agreement/disagreement.

## Gerb's assessment of the 4chan claims (summary)

- Agrees: UFOs are primarily unmanned drones; a mobile ocean-based construction facility is
  (one of several) sources of UFOs; the object tracked by MITRE matches this description.
  *(1:29:06–1:31:16)*
- Disagrees / skeptical: that the facility destroys anything approaching it; that downing
  attempts against it have failed; that its craft use "element 115." *(1:29:57–1:32:13)*
- No independent knowledge either way: that craft are built to spec each deployment; that the
  facility has been active on Earth for 100+ years. *(1:29:26–1:30:12)*
- Notes the poster claimed unusually deep/broad knowledge of the program compared to Gerb's
  own MITRE-engineer sources, raising some suspicion, but a specific detail — the poster's
  answer that he "moved on from the project because of new management that didn't trust the
  older proven methods" — struck Gerb as meaningful corroboration of the alleged 2011–2017
  MITRE leadership/briefing changes. *(1:30:34–1:33:06)* See [[mitre-corporation]].
