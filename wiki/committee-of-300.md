The Committee of 300, also called "the Olympians" in intelligence circles per [[john-coleman]], is the alleged supranational body of 300 unelected men that secretly runs the world. Its existence is asserted by Coleman and in the [[committee-of-300-video-2026]]; nothing here is verified.

## Claims

- The first public announcement was allegedly by Walther Rathenau (ASR "Rathnau"), financial adviser to the Kaiser and to the Rothschilds, who is quoted saying in 1934 that "a committee of 300 men rule the world, known only to each other" (the video's date; Rathenau died in 1922, so the date is suspect, flagged here). *([va/1994-system](<../raw/va/Video Advice - 2026-01-25 - In 1994, He Exposed Their Entire System… and Predicted 2026 Perfectly.md>)  1:02, 1:35)*
- It is allegedly above the CIA and other agencies in a hierarchy, is beyond national law, and is protected by a "secret upper level parallel government" in the US. *([va/1994-system](<../raw/va/Video Advice - 2026-01-25 - In 1994, He Exposed Their Entire System… and Predicted 2026 Perfectly.md>)  1:02, 7:14)*
- Its "executive arms" are said to be [[chatham-house]], the [[club-of-rome]], the Montelleran (Mont Pelerin) Society, the Order of St. John and other societies. *([va/1994-system](<../raw/va/Video Advice - 2026-01-25 - In 1994, He Exposed Their Entire System… and Predicted 2026 Perfectly.md>)  8:11)*
- Its alleged goals are a world order with the US middle class destroyed through deindustrialization and zero growth. *([va/1994-system](<../raw/va/Video Advice - 2026-01-25 - In 1994, He Exposed Their Entire System… and Predicted 2026 Perfectly.md>)  11:59)*
