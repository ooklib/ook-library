Teledyne Ryan (San Diego), a maker of aerial targets, surveillance systems and unmanned aircraft, bought by [[northrop-grumman]] in 1999. In [[uap-gerb]]'s reading it is one of the acquisitions that carried legacy-program work into Northrop. All items are attributed claims.

## Claims

- Acquired by Northrop in 1999; Gerb calls it one of Northrop's "premium purchases". *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 9:23-9:39,)*
- [[edgar-fuche]] names Lockheed, Boeing, Teledyne Ryan and Northrop as the primary contractors of the [[alien-reproduction-vehicle-tr3b]]. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 36:05-36:19)*
- [[colonel-steve-wilson]] said the anti-gravity "XH-75D" or "XH Shark" helicopter was designed by Teledyne Ryan's San Diego division and assigned to Delta and the [[national-reconnaissance-office]] for UFO crash retrievals; Gerb sees only a visual resemblance to Sikorsky's 2019 Raider X concept and calls it a dead end. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 41:47-44:11)*
- Wilson's "Star Wars City" chart lists Teledyne Ryan (and Ford Aerospace, now Northrop) among contractors whose black-budget directors sit in [[decision-science-applications]] under the [[strategic-defense-initiative]]. *([gerb/northrop](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-01-27 - UFO Legacy Programs - Northrop Grumman.md>) 46:31-47:04)*
- Also mentioned in the SAIC video as bought by Northrop in 1999; see [[northrop-grumman]].
