Successor to [[allen-dulles]] as CIA director; per David Talbot via Eyes Wide Open, one of the CIA guests hosted at Dulles's house in 1962-63. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 8:50-8:57)*

## Claims

- Listed among Dulles's household guests alongside [[james-jesus-angleton]], [[richard-helms]] and [[cord-meyer]]. *([ewa/detachment-202](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-09-04 - SPY STORIES OSS Detachment 202 - Secret Cold Warriors [pt. 5.4].md) 8:33-8:57)*

- War Conspiracy 1: in the early 1940s headed the company that became Bechtel, whose lobbyist [[thomas-corcoran]] won government contracts; the host adds Kennedy later chose him to replace [[allen-dulles]] after the Bay of Pigs. Bibliography cites the John McCone papers (Eisenhower Library). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 16:18-16:48)*

## Additional claims (JFK and the CIA)


- Appointed DCI (announced September 1961, two months before Dulles's official resignation): Republican businessman and engineer, shipbuilder before and during WWII (whose firm employed "Tommy the Cork" [[thomas-corcoran]] as a wartime lobbyist, host recalling War Conspiracy 1), then an executive at ITT (host: often a CIA front in Latin America, notably in the 1973 Chile coup), briefly under secretary of the Air Force (1950-51), then chairman of the Atomic Energy Commission replacing Lewis Strauss. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 8:34-11:08)* See [[atomic-energy-commission]].
- Host's explanation for the appointment: the AEC work uncovering Israel's nuclear program in 1960. At a December 19, 1960 meeting with Eisenhower, Dulles and Gordon Gray (January 12, 1961 memo), the group discussed information that "the Israelis are operating a plutonium production plant," and McCone proposed insisting on IAEA safeguards; JFK was impressed. The host says the Israeli-nuclear issue "would continue to plague" JFK and feeds speculation of an Israeli element in the assassination. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 11:15-13:13)*
- Host: "maverick McCone" largely failed to impose Kennedy policy on the CIA; per Seigenthaler, "McCone was out of the loop. Dick Helms was running the agency." Replaced by [[richard-helms]] in 1966 under [[lyndon-b-johnson]]. *([ewa/jfk-cia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-09-05 - JFK and the CIA.md) 13:13-13:35, 27:13-27:25, 28:22-28:42)*
- Wiki-internal observation: the AEC also appears in the UFO-legacy thread ([[sandia-national-laboratories]]), and McCone's prior AEC chairmanship is stated here only as a resume item; the transcript makes no UFO link.
