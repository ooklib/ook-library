Founder and longtime head of the [[world-economic-forum]] and author of the stakeholder-capitalism doctrine. This page records only claims from [[wef-insider-interview-2025]], where he is criticized by [[desiree-fixler]] and [[winston-marshall]].

## Claims

- Fixler says Schwab's stakeholder capitalism ("profit with purpose") replaced Milton Friedman's shareholder-primacy model and is what she and others sold to institutions. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 4:16, 13:08)*
- Marshall reads Schwab's 2020 book The Great Reset as being about "global governance" rather than free markets, and says its democracy/sovereignty/globalization trilemma is resolved in favour of global governance. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 56:07, 57:03)*
- Marshall says Schwab's 2022 The Great Narrative doubled down on climate-catastrophe rhetoric as the ESG backlash began, and has a chapter on "morality and values" that Fixler compares to a religious doctrine. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:09:26, 1:24:00)*
- Marshall says the Wall Street Journal reported inappropriate funding use and discrimination cases and that Schwab was forced to step down; Fixler agrees he did not live up to his transparency ideals. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:19:43, 1:20:10)*
- Fixler met him in San Francisco and Dubai and describes him as awkward and stiff. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:20:28)*
