The Office of the Director of Air Force Test and Evaluation (AFTTE), within Air Staff under
compliance in the headquarters of the Air Force (HAF), serves per HAF mission directive 1-52
(20 April 2021) as the senior test executive for both the Air Force and Space Force, and "the
chief T&E adviser to the DAF senior leadership," setting T&E policy/guidance, advocating for test
resources, and resolving developmental/operational test programmatic issues. AFTTE has authority
over specific DoD T&E activities delegated to the Secretary of the Air Force pursuant to DoD
Directive 3200.11 (major range and test facility bases, MRTFBs). Gerb argues AFTTE's T&E
programs include the reverse engineering and derivation of nonhuman technologies, and — after
building his case across the video — names AFTTE as "the real top of the chain of command" for
actual hands-on test-and-evaluation work on the [[hidden-wing-air-force-program|Hidden Wing]]
program. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:28:32–1:30:36, 3:19:14–3:19:46)*

## Claims

- Two subordinate divisions share AFTTE responsibility: AFTTEP (Policy, Programs, and Resources
  Division) and AFTTEZ (Special Programs Division). AFTTEZ represents Air Force T&E to the
  Special Programs Review Group, overseeing all Air Force SAPs surrounding test and evaluation,
  and deals heavily with "foreign material programs" — per HAF mission directive 1-52,
  responsible for "providing leadership, direction, policy, and resources to ensure DAF, foreign
  material, and classified test programs meet documented warfighter requirements" and "management
  and oversight of the life cycle process for foreign material acquisition and ... exploitation of
  foreign aerospace-related weapon systems and components." Gerb suggests "foreign material
  programs" may literally refer to UFOs.
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:37:16–1:38:55)*
- AFTTEP acts as the T&E policy focal point for all Air/Space Force testing conducted by
  [[air-force-operational-test-and-evaluation-center|AFOTEC]], Air Force MAJCOMs, Space Force
  field commands, and forward operating agencies. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:39:06–1:39:21)*
- Witness "Ed," while serving within the [[edwards-air-force-base|Edwards 412th Test Wing]] on a
  T&E program for reverse-engineered/derivative vehicles, had AFTTE as his Office of Primary
  Responsibility (OPR). *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 2:44:05–2:45:02)*
- [[randall-g-walden|Randall G. Walden]] served as director of AFTTE immediately before becoming
  director/PEO of the [[rapid-capabilities-office|RCO]].
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 56:59–57:16)*
- In his conclusion, Gerb specifically flags a former AFTTE leader "by the last name Clark" as
  someone he'd be interested in seeing more research on, regarding infrastructure set-up for the
  Hidden Wing program. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 3:19:46–3:20:02)*
- Maj. Gen. [[dawn-m-dunlop|Dawn M. Dunlop]], a former director of the Special Access Program
  Central Office (SAPCO) and vice commander/commander of the Edwards 412th Test Wing
  (2009–2012), is alleged by Gerb to have possibly gatekept or operated within Hidden Wing
  programs at a T&E level; witness "Ed" reportedly received commendation letters signed by
  Dunlop during his time at the 412th. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  1:35:42–1:37:16)*
