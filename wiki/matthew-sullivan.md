Matthew "Quake" Sullivan, a decorated Air Force veteran (Bronze Star for Valor, Operation
Enduring Freedom) who served in Air Force Intelligence, the National Security Agency, and the
National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC), was reportedly set to deliver protected
disclosures on UFO legacy programs to Congress (per Rep. [[eric-burlison|Eric Burlison]]) when he
died of a reported "accidental overdose" roughly two weeks before he was to testify. Gerb treats
this as a possible example of "wet works" against a prospective whistleblower.
*([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:45:02–2:45:24)*
