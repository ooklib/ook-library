The 1916-17 US Army expedition into Mexico under General John "Blackjack" Pershing after Pancho Villa's March 1916 raid on Columbus, New Mexico. [[eyes-wide-open]] treats it as Charles Willoughby's first army posting and as a meeting ground for future intelligence chiefs. ASR spelling: "Ponchovilla".

## Claims

- Context (host): part of the on-again, off-again Mexican Border War, a sideshow to the Mexican Revolution; Wilson recognized Carranza in 1915, Villa raided Columbus, and Pershing was sent to capture Villa. The expedition pushed Villa's men back into Mexico but did not capture him. *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 9:46-13:13)*
- Willoughby was posted at Nogales, Arizona ("Ngalas" in ASR); the host calls it "barely action at all." *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 13:13-13:27)*
- Per author Dick Russell, [[william-donovan]] was also assigned to the expedition, as was future General Walter "Beetle" Smith ([[walter-bedell-smith]]); the host says it brought together future heads of Army G-2 intelligence (Willoughby), the OSS (Donovan) and the CIA (Smith). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 13:27-15:13)*
- Host on Pershing: mentor to nearly all WWII generals (Marshall, Eisenhower, Bradley, Patton, MacArthur); after WWI he awarded the Distinguished Service Cross to [[douglas-macarthur]] and Donovan (host describes a photo). *([ewa/willoughby-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-19 - America’s Fascist Spymaster - WILLOUGHBY 1.md) 11:13-11:44,)*
- Wiki-internal observation: the host's claim that all three attended the expedition rests on one cited author (Russell) and is not corroborated elsewhere in the wiki.
