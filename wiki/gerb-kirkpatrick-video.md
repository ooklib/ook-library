An off-the-cuff, roughly 13-minute rant (dated 2024-01-26) in which [[uap-gerb]] rebuts [[sean-kirkpatrick]]'s Scientific American op-ed and calls him a "modern-day disinformation agent" and mouthpiece of the DoD. The source page is [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hK24ZdkvwN4). Details of the op-ed and Gerb's rebuttals are on [[kirkpatrick-scientific-american-op-ed-2024]]. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 0:00-1:11)*

## Claims

- Framing: [[aaro]] was the 2022 official DoD UFO investigation office, succeeding the [[uap-task-force]] (to which [[david-grush]] was liaison from the NRO and NGA), in a line of official programs from Sign, Grudge and Blue Book to AATIP. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 0:23-1:05)*
- AARO reported to [[ronald-moultrie]], had analyzed about 650 cases as of April 2023 with no published reports, and until the end of 2023 had no web page or contact route beyond a single sheet. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 1:12-1:36)*
- Gerb says Kirkpatrick avoids the term non-human intelligence used in the 2024 NDAA under Chuck Schumer and speaks only of "aliens" or "extraterrestrials" to sidestep the wording. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 2:07-2:37)*
- Gerb argues secret programs limited to 50 to 100 people are plausible given how acknowledged and unacknowledged SAPs work, citing the senior review group, SAPOC and the Manhattan Project's compartmentalization. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 3:24-3:39)*
- He says the 1954 atomic energy agreement, referenced multiple times in the NDAA and tied to misclassification of UAP records under the [[atomic-energy-commission]], contradicts Kirkpatrick's "2008 conspiracy" story. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 4:34-5:23)*
- Whistleblower dispute: Kirkpatrick said none came to AARO; Gerb cites Grusch (who says he tried), [[christopher-mellon]]'s and [[luis-elizondo]]'s tweets. See [[kirkpatrick-scientific-american-op-ed-2024]]. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 5:23-7:08)*
- A DoD Inspector General release says the DoD has no overarching UAP policy; Gerb contrasts this with Kirkpatrick's claim of AARO's commitment (credited to a tweet by Ross Coulthart). *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 7:17-7:56)*
- Gerb compares Kirkpatrick's explaining UAP as Chinese drones (including a "cube within a sphere" reported to Ryan Graves as a Chinese ball drone) with the [[robertson-panel-1953]]'s swamp gas and weather balloons. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 7:56-8:56)*
- Conflict-of-interest claims: he says Kirkpatrick left AARO possibly as a scapegoat, is registered with [[nonlinear-solutions]] (North Carolina address held with his wife since 2017; registration dated 16 October 2023 per a Coulthart tweet) and with DOE's Oak Ridge National Laboratory run by Battelle. He links the DOE to Jonathan Weygandt's account in his marines video. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 8:56-9:58)*
- Gerb calls a 2022 award of $1.9 million to [[sancorp]] for AARO support services the "most damning" evidence that AARO is a disinformation front, since Sancorp specializes in preventing leaks and stopping whistleblowers; he says Moultrie's office helped secure the contract. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 10:05-10:52)*
- A LinkedIn post by Anthony Miller (quoted) says that at Kirkpatrick's last public event as AARO director at George Mason University, Pentagon spokesperson Susan Gough shadowed him for an hour like a Soviet-era minder, and he looked at her before answering. *([gerb/kirkpatrick](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-26 - The Modern Day UFO Disinformation Agent - Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick's Lies.md>) 10:52-11:48)*
