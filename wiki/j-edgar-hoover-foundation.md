The J. Edgar Hoover Foundation, a charitable foundation dedicated to the FBI chief, was
co-founded in 1960 by [[lewis-rosenstiel]] and [[joseph-kennedy]] — two former bootleggers turned
legitimate businessmen, five years after the alleged [[blue-suite]] events, which Eyes Wide Open
calls "strange and fascinating" given how interwoven these worlds were.
