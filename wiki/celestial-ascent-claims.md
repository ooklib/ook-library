[[peter-levenda]]'s book *Stairway to Heaven* traces a recurring seven-level ascent to heaven across traditions. In the interview he calls it the underlying subject of all his books, and treats it as an inner, non-repeatable practice, not a technology.

## Claims

- Ascent traditions named: Merkabah in Judaism, Shangqing Daoism, Islam, Zoroastrianism, ziggurats and Egypt; Michels links the seven levels to the Big Dipper and the Space Delta 7 patch. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 3:35:22, 3:35:38, 3:36:12)*
- Levenda speculates the seven levels and the double-helix serpents (the caduceus, a Chinese diagram) may be DNA "imprinting itself" on consciousness. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 3:36:44, 3:36:53, 3:38:33)*
- He says the process is real and has been done, warns against attempting it unready, and recommends a neuroscience-oriented, co-authored Kabbalah book by Moshe Idel (title not given, name transcribed "Moa Adele"). *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 3:39:20, 3:42:35, 3:43:28)*
- He rejects Traditionalism (Guenon, Evola) and implicitly [[jason-jorjani]]'s stance, saying he wants to go toward a golden age, not back. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 4:14:29, 4:16:03, 4:17:43)*
