Bill Bulebush is a Kecksburg witness whom the transcript renders as "Bill bully Bush" (ASR/Gerb's spelling; the usual spelling, Bulebush, is supplied from outside the transcript and is not certain here). He says he saw the object land and walked to it before the firemen arrived. All details below are attributed claims.

## Claims

- He says he saw the object streak over, "hesitate" over the Loyalhanna area (transcribed "luraville"), turn and descend toward Kecksburg, then went to the top of what is now Meteor Road and saw blue lights flashing in the woods. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 21:33-21:57)*
- He says he walked to the crash location and saw a metallic acorn-shaped object embedded about a foot to a foot and a half in the soil, with the same writing noticed by [[jim-romansky]] and blue arcing light coming from it at intervals. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 22:06-22:23)*
- He describes a red ball moving as if "radio controlled", making a figure eight and belly-landing, knocking the tops off trees, smelling like rotten eggs and sizzling as if cooling from red hot. Gerb notes he is the only witness he knows to report a smell and compares it to the strong ammonia smell reported at the Varginha case. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 22:39-23:20)*
- He says the object had no welding or window and a ring at the back with Egyptian-like writing. He fled when the firemen approached, so by Gerb's reading the object was in view for about 15 minutes before the men in the trench coats ordered the firemen away. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 23:28-23:59)*
- Gerb argues that his description independently matches Romansky's, and supports a non-meteorite explanation. *([gerb/kecksburg](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-09-12 - The 1965 Kecksburg, Pennsylvania UFO Crash.md>) 22:23-22:31)*
