Zapata Offshore was the offshore-drilling spin-off of [[zapata-petroleum]], founded in 1954 by [[george-h-w-bush]] with [[bill-clements]]. [[eyes-wide-open]] argues it was a money-losing "CIA pet project" and a "CIA front" used to give corporate cover to CIA assets, especially anti-Castro Cuban exiles, before the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]]. All of this is the host's inference.

## Claims

- Founded 1954, "only a year after" Zapata Petroleum; Bush relocated from Midland to Houston a year later and teamed with Clements to develop it. The host also notes he used this company's logo throughout the video as a stand-in for Zapata Petroleum. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 9:38-10:34)*
- Per Joseph Trento (via host): small profit 1955-56, over half a million dollars in losses in 1957-58. The [[zapata-petroleum]] partners (the Liedtke brothers) took an alleged $800,000 buyout, allegedly provided by Bush's uncle Herbert Walker Jr., and later built Pennzoil with South Penn Oil (1963). Host: "the only explanation" for Bush walking away from profit is that Zapata Offshore was a CIA project providing corporate cover for intelligence assets. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 12:24-14:09)*
- Per Russ Baker (via host): rigs in the Gulf of Mexico, the Persian Gulf and off Borneo, Indonesia; host notes the Indonesian rig coincides with the failed 1957-58 CIA coup there, and that renting one of the first offshore rigs to Kuwait began Bush's long Gulf-monarchy relationship. Host: this was "the first activation of Bush by Dulles as a proper intelligence asset," a "friendly and cooperative business contact" under Devine's guidance. See [[allen-dulles]], [[thomas-devine]]. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 14:09-15:21)*
- Quotes: William Corson (via Trento) said Bush was "perfect at talent spotting"; John Sherwood, an anti-Castro operations chief in 1962-63, said Bush was one of "hundreds of other businessmen" giving "nuts and bolts assistance," "no spy," mainly a discreet "place to park people." Host frames these as describing a front. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 15:21-15:53)*
- Host classification: a CIA "front" (sham business giving agents mail, phones and an answer to "what are you doing here"), as opposed to a "proprietary". See [[cia-fronts-and-proprietaries]]. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 17:31-18:03)*
- Cuba: expanded in 1958 with a plot off [[cay-sal]] leased next to one leased by [[howard-hughes]]; offices in Marathon, Florida, near No Name Key; Bush's rig "Scorpion" installed off Cay Sal in 1958 and speculated (Trento, Baker) to have been an impromptu training site for Cuban exiles given cover at Zapata Offshore or in the Mexican oil company PEMEX (ASR "PMX"). *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 19:31-25:09)*
- Host's speculation: exiles trained there were likely elite [[operation-40]] recruits. *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 25:09-25:42)*
- Registry: [[george-de-mohrenschildt]]'s Rolodex listed Bush as "president of Zapata Offshore." *([ewa/bush-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-04-04 - BUSH AND THE CIA - CHAPTER 3.md) 31:44-32:08)*
