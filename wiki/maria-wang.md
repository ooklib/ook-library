Maria Wang was the widow of [[eric-henry-wang]], the Wright-Patterson "Special Studies" chief. In the account [[jesse-michels]] relays from [[william-steinman]]'s 1980s research, she is the source of the claim that her husband reported directly to [[henry-kissinger]].

## Claims

- Maria Wang confirmed to Steinman that her late husband worked on classified projects at Wright-Patterson involving technology "not of this world" and confided before his death that the US government was studying recovered extraterrestrial craft. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 5:00, 5:08, 5:15)*
- She said Eric Wang reported directly to Kissinger, that Kissinger was deeply involved in the flying saucer program and oversaw much of Wang's work, and that he visited the couple's home on occasion. Michels notes that if true, this puts Kissinger at the center of the program before he held public office (Wang died in 1960); he calls it "a huge claim" and treats it as unverified. *([jm/kissinger](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-17 - Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program.md>) 5:22, 5:32, 6:14, 6:22)*
