La Cagoule ('the Hood'; formally the Comité Secret d’Action Révolutionnaire) was, per Eyes Wide Open, a secret fascist paramilitary organization founded in 1936 by [[eugene-deloncle]], which carried out political assassinations and bombings in pre-war France; Eyes Wide Open presents it as the pre-war ancestor of France's post-war Gladio-style networks. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 2:58)*

## Claims

- Founded in 1936 by former naval engineer [[eugene-deloncle]]; members wore ritualistic cowls and met in secret underground venues, with initiation rites resembling Masonic esoteric rituals (per academic Bertram Gordon). *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 2:58-3:56)*
- Carried out late-1930s political assassinations (notably the Rosselli brothers, anti-fascist exiles from Mussolini's Italy) and set off two bombs near the Arc de Triomphe; reportedly closer to Italian Fascism than to Germany. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 4:02-4:38)*
- Per a 2011 New Yorker article, heavily funded by French industrialists such as [[eugene-schueller]], founder of [[loreal]], who allegedly hired members after the war and let La Cagoule and its associated collaborationist party hold meetings at L'Oreal offices. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 4:50-5:16)*
- Its intelligence arm was the [[corvignolles]], organized by [[georges-loustaunau-lacau]]. Police infiltration initially blunted the group; many members were jailed, then released in 1939 to return to the army. After the 1940 defeat some followed Deloncle to Vichy while others joined the Free French in London. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 5:22-7:00)*
- Eyes Wide Open argues the Cagoule's cell structure was the blueprint for later Gladio operations, that the Corvignolles' integration into the French security apparatus foreshadowed a deep symbiosis between the security community and far-right networks, and that its esoteric forums 'reproduced themselves' after the war in obscure clandestine groups (a setup for [[le-cercle]]). *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 7:57-8:40)*
