Journalist whose serialized monthly articles exposed [[standard-oil]] and [[john-d-rockefeller]]; the documentary says her father was a refiner ruined by Standard and his partner took his own life.

## Claims

- Her articles broke Rockefeller's secrecy, revealing the rebate deals, intimidation, bribed politicians, his father's scams and second marriage; Rockefeller never responded, which angered the public. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 41:01, 41:28, 41:37, 41:46, 41:54)*
- The resulting public hatred let [[theodore-roosevelt]] make Standard Oil his target: see [[standard-oil-dissolution-1911]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 42:13, 42:26)*
