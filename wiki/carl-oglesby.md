Carl Oglesby, author of the 1976 book *The Yankee and Cowboys War* (available free via the
Internet Archive), is credited by Eyes Wide Open with developing the "Yankee vs. Cowboy" framework
for understanding US elite power politics — a lens the host adopts, loosely, throughout the
series (see [[yankee-cowboy-thesis]]).

## Claims

- Wrote *The Yankee and Cowboys War* (1976), described by the host as "a fantastic and largely
  forgotten book" and "a really quick, engaging read," proposing that the US establishment split
  into two competing elite camps transcending party lines. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 11:39-12:04)*
- His book is mainly focused on the Kennedy assassination and Watergate, which Oglesby frames as
  the titular "Yankee Cowboy War" — starting with JFK's assassination and ending with Nixon's
  impeachment. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 14:46-14:57)*

## Relationships
