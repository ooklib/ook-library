Carlos Prio Socarras was the Cuban president overthrown by Fulgencio Batista's 1952 coup, then a wealthy exile in Miami and, per [[eyes-wide-open]], the major financial backer of anti-Batista forces from 1953. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 17:40-18:03)*

## Claims

- Overthrown by Batista in 1952; from 1953 the "major benefactor" of oppositional elements to Batista from Miami; Warren Hinckle and William Turner (*The Fish Is Red*) call Miami under him "a sort of Antillean Switzerland" where rich Cubans funneled fortunes into bank accounts. He hoped to return to Cuba to reclaim the presidency. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 17:40-18:26)*
- Per *The Fish Is Red* as summarized by the host: [[fidel-castro]], fresh out of a Batista jail, met Prio at the Hotel Casa de Palmas in McAllen, Texas, and Prio agreed to fund the movement provided he was returned as president; Fidel agreed and left with a reported $100,000 pledge. The host calls this "the legend" of how the 26th of July movement got off the ground. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 18:26-19:39)*
- The host says [[frank-sturgis]] met Castro at a Miami fundraiser while visiting his aunt and uncle, whose family was close to Prio. *([ewa/dallas-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-14 - The Road to Dallas Chapter 1 - Frank Sturgis and the Soldiers of Fortune (JFK ASSASSINATION SERIES).md) 17:34-17:46, 19:45-20:04)*
