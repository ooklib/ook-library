William E. McClure, a member of the senior executive service tier 2 (SES2), served as director
of [[saf-aa|SAF/AAZ ("security/special program oversight/information protection")]] from 2019 to
2021, providing "security leadership, policy direction, integration, and oversight of the Air
Force special access program and information protection enterprise" and serving as functional
manager of the Air Force's 2,000+ member security community.
*([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 1:26:28–1:27:18)*

## Claims

- Gerb volunteers McClure as a second candidate for the redacted "SCES2" Air Force gatekeeper
  named in [[christopher-mellon|Christopher Mellon]]'s 23 April 2024 leaked Signal exchange
  (see [[russell-e-wiler|Russell E. Wiler]] for the primary account). Researcher Alex Katz's
  analysis implied a 12-character name; "William McClure" is 14 characters and "Will McClure" 11,
  but Gerb notes McClure goes by "Bill McClure" on LinkedIn, and "Bill E. McClure" is exactly 12
  characters — leaving the character-count condition "still satisfied" under some naming
  variants. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 1:26:19–1:28:24)*
- Gerb personally leans 60/40 toward Wiler over McClure for the SCES2 identification, but states
  both men have documented involvement in Air Force legacy-program history.
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 3:23:04–3:23:20)*
- SAF/AAZ, which McClure directed, "manages the Air Force information security program to
  include nuclear information security" (a category Gerb repeatedly ties to
  transclassification-based UFO material secrecy), the personnel and industrial security
  programs, the Air Force insider-threat program, and serves as the Air Force Special Access
  Program Central Office (SAPCO) and authorizing official for Air Force SAP systems.
  *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 1:25:13–1:26:11)*
