Home of the 412th Test Wing and an MRTFB (Major Range and Test Facility Base), listed by Gerb
alongside Naval/Army test ranges. *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 1:16:48–1:17:03)*
Alleged site of the [[hidden-wing-air-force-program|"Hidden Wing"]] program below its western
ranges. *([mitre](../raw/107BtDYhTkA_mitre.md) 52:21–52:55)*

## Claims (from the Hidden Wing video)

- The Air Force Test Center (AFTC, formerly the Air Force Flight Test Center), housed at Edwards
  under [[air-force-material-command|AFMC]], "has flight tested every aircraft in the Army Air
  Forces and the Air Force's inventory since World War II" and operates the Air Force Test Pilot
  School (TPS). It is enforced/administered by the Edwards 412th Test Wing, which "plans,
  conducts, analyzes and reports on all flight and ground testing of aircraft, weapon systems,
  software and components." *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md)
  2:42:45–2:44:05)*
- Gerb's central thesis: the 412th Test Wing, AFTC, TPS, [[aftte|AFTTE]], and
  [[air-force-research-laboratory|AFRL]] together, under AFMC, comprise the "Hidden Wing" — a
  T&E program for nonhuman-derived and alien-reproduction vehicles run across Edwards, the Utah
  Test and Training Range, and the Nevada Test and Training Range ("Western Ranges"), drawing
  R&D support from Antelope Valley contractors at [[air-force-plant-42|Air Force Plant 42]]. See
  [[hidden-wing-air-force-program]]. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md)
  2:45:20–2:46:18)*
- A separate corroborating account: former Marine Sgt. Rodrick Castle described a 1997 sighting
  of a large, silent black triangle during the Hunter Warrior exercises near Twentynine Palms;
  the craft departed in the direction of Edwards AFB, specifically toward the restricted R-2508
  airspace complex. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md) 3:18:17–3:19:07)*
- Gerb theorizes a connected network of deep underground military bases beneath Edwards, China
  Lake, and the broader Antelope Valley, linking to Lockheed's Helendale and Northrop's Tehachapi
  radar cross-section ranges. *([hidden-wing](../raw/-IXSZe4xVv4_hidden_wing.md)
  2:04:44–2:05:15)*
