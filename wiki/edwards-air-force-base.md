  conducts, analyzes and reports on all flight and ground testing of aircraft, weapon systems,
  software and components." *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  2:42:45–2:44:05)*

  [[air-force-research-laboratory|AFRL]] together, under AFMC, comprise the "Hidden Wing" — a
  T&E program for nonhuman-derived and alien-reproduction vehicles run across Edwards, the Utah
  Test and Training Range, and the Nevada Test and Training Range ("Western Ranges"), drawing
  R&D support from Antelope Valley contractors at [[air-force-plant-42|Air Force Plant 42]]. See
  [[hidden-wing-air-force-program]]. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  2:45:20–2:46:18)*
- A separate corroborating account: former Marine Sgt. Rodrick Castle described a 1997 sighting
  of a large, silent black triangle during the Hunter Warrior exercises near Twentynine Palms;
  the craft departed in the direction of Edwards AFB, specifically toward the restricted R-2508
  airspace complex. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 3:18:17–3:19:07)*
- Gerb theorizes a connected network of deep underground military bases beneath Edwards, China
  Lake, and the broader Antelope Valley, linking to Lockheed's Helendale and Northrop's Tehachapi
  radar cross-section ranges. *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md)
  2:04:44–2:05:15)*
