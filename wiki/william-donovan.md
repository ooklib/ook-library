William "Wild Bill" Donovan was a WWI-hero-turned-Wall-Street-lawyer who became the first (and
only) director of the [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]] under President
[[franklin-d-roosevelt|FDR]], and is characterized by Eyes Wide Open as "the godfather of modern
American intelligence" despite being, in the host's telling, a largely ineffective
administrator.

## Claims

- His nickname "Wild Bill" comes from baseball player Wild Bill Donovan, who coached the
  Yankees 1915-1917. He was a WWI hero and self-made millionaire Wall Street lawyer, and served
  as US Coordinator of Information (an early DoD intelligence arm) during WWI, background which
  made him a likely pick when FDR created the OSS in 1942. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 12:43-13:23)*
- As director, his "army bonafides" gave the new OSS legitimacy and cover, since the security
  establishment was wary the OSS/CIA could try to usurp their role; he was "largely overshadowed
  by his subordinates," especially [[allen-dulles]]. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 13:26-14:16)*
- Despite an "honorable man" public image, most accounts (per a 1945 report by Colonel Park,
  written for President Truman) portray Donovan as a drunk who hired via the Wall Street "old
  boy network" rather than merit, who left a briefcase of critical OSS intel in a brothel in
  Romania (later intercepted by the Gestapo), forgot about an agent stranded in Liberia, and
  accidentally ordered OSS agents in France blown up. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 14:20-16:00)*
- Truman, lacking faith in Donovan's effectiveness, dismissed him and replaced him with
  [[roscoe-hillenkoetter]], a US naval officer, as (per Eyes Wide Open) "the first director of
  the CIA." *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 15:26-15:52)*
- Alongside [[allen-dulles]], worked to save select Nazi war criminals from Nuremberg justice
  under [[operation-sunrise]]: circumvented Supreme Court Justice Robert Jackson (FDR's overseer
  of Nuremberg) to secure [[hjalmar-schacht]]'s release, allegedly fabricating sympathetic
  stories about Schacht's supposed exile from the Nazi party and lack of antisemitism; judged
  [[hermann-goring]] too high-profile a target to save despite considering him.
  *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 10:05-12:44)*
- Alongside [[james-jesus-angleton]], briefly considered installing [[junio-valerio-borghese|the
  Black Prince]] as king of a Catholic-dominated southern Italy after his rescue from war-crimes
  prosecution, before opting for the lower-profile plan of making him commander of
  [[operation-gladio|Gladio]] forces in Italy. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 25:12-25:24)*
- Illustrates a recurring theme the host identifies in US intelligence history — "you never
  really leave the CIA" — by remaining an informal advisor to Allen Dulles throughout Dulles's
  CIA directorship even after officially returning to his law practice, and being present for
  "many landmark operations." Eyes Wide Open frames him as personally "fairly irrelevant" but
  as the connective "anchor" who brought together the agents who went on to run the CIA's
  formative, "most notable and criminal acts." *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 16:00-16:59)*

- Alongside Dulles, "loved" [[paul-helliwell]]'s narcotics-funded covert-action plan as a way to
  keep OSS-style operations running "without the formal ascent of the US government"; the OSS
  elite were "thrilled" with Helliwell's earlier China opium-facilitation plan and immediately
  funneled funds (including looted Nazi fortunes) to his office. *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 10:56-11:08, 14:27-14:41)*

- After the OSS's August 1945 disbandment, returned (along with [[allen-dulles]], [[james-jesus-angleton]],
  and other "OSS boys") to private life; his days leading American intelligence were over, though
  he remained connected to the future CIA by drafting a 1946-47 work plan with Dulles for the
  agency, based largely on his own vision of "a highly funded top secret unaccountable agency"
  with unlimited global reach — the outline Dulles later presented to the Senate Armed Services
  Committee. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 14:09-14:38, 20:59-21:44)*
- His wartime OSS network in Romania, run there by [[frank-wisner]], later formed the basis of
  the [[central-intelligence-group|Central Intelligence Group]]'s first (failed) operation. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 17:47-17:58)*

- Following orders from Donovan, OSS Italy director [[earl-brennan]] liaised with Vatican
  official [[giovanni-montini]] to identify anti-communist allies; on Montini's (and
  [[lucky-luciano]]'s) suggestion, Donovan ordered an airdrop communique to Sicilian Mafia boss
  [[calogero-vizzini]] requesting his help with [[operation-husky]], the invasion of Sicily.
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 15:50-16:36)*
- Met [[pope-pius-xii]] at the Vatican in 1945; was decorated as a "crusader against communism"
  and granted the Grand Cross of the Order of Saint Sylvester, becoming a "Knight of the Vatican"
  — setting the pieces in motion for future Gladio money laundering through the
  [[vatican-bank]]. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 22:02-22:31)*
- According to some sources, as a private citizen, allegedly funded the
  [[portella-della-ginestra-massacre-1947|1947 Portella della Ginestra massacre]] directly via his
  private [[world-commerce-corporation]], which he used to funnel money to mob associates.
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 26:19-26:31)*
- Per [[george-white]]'s own diary, met with White in Rome in 1948, while Donovan was a private
  citizen; White separately had allegations of funding Mafia elements in Italy shortly before that
  year's election. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 54:43-55:03)*
- Founded the [[american-committee-on-united-europe]] in 1948 as chairman while legally a private Wall Street lawyer but still working with the CIA, hiring [[allen-dulles]] and [[walter-bedell-smith]] and other ex-OSS figures. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 22:58-23:50)*

- Per McCoy, an insider in Thai circles in the late 1940s; appointed US ambassador to Thailand 1953-54, during the withdrawal of [[li-mi]]'s force, which the host says raises questions. *([ewa/operation-paper](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-10-04 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation PAPER and Trouble in the Golden Triangle [pt. 6].md) 44:49-45:20)*

- LAOS 3: introduced to [[ngo-dinh-diem]] in spring 1951; the host speculates that as a fellow Catholic he could have recommended Diem to [[allen-dulles]], and says some speculate he had a role in Diem's 1954 appointment as he (then ambassador to Thailand) arrived in Saigon alongside [[edward-lansdale]]. In 1945 he also sent 50 OSS men to the Pentagon to be posted to the Philippines under a military-intelligence G2 designation, Lansdale among them. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 11:47-12:11, 18:44-19:08, 27:36-27:53)*

- LAOS 4: host calls Donovan "in a sense the first CIA cowboy" and links him to [[world-commerce-corporation]] and SEA Supply; [[desmond-fitzgerald]] was reportedly his friend. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:03:12-1:03:26, 1:10:43-1:10:54)*

- War Conspiracy 1: host: had a "particular fixation" on Southeast Asia, set up in Thailand after the war and briefly served as US ambassador to Thailand in the 1950s; OSS Detachment 202 under [[paul-helliwell]] was his China-hand operation. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 20:40-21:07)*
