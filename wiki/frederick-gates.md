Baptist minister hired by [[john-d-rockefeller]] in the 1890s to manage his fortune; Rockefeller reportedly called him the greatest businessman he ever met.

## Claims

- Hired to fix Rockefeller's losing personal investments; the narrator notes the oddity of a former priest administering the fortune. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 34:35, 34:43, 34:52)*
- With [[john-d-rockefeller-jr]] made railroad and other purchases and the Mesabi investment: see [[mesabi-iron-range-deal]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 35:08, 35:16, 36:16)*
- Co-founded the [[rockefeller-foundation]] in 1913 and directed about $537 million through it with Junior. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 47:16, 47:30)*

## Relationships

- Adviser to [[john-d-rockefeller]] and [[john-d-rockefeller-jr]]; co-founder of the [[rockefeller-foundation]].
