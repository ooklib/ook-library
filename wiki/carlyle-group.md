The Carlyle Group is described in the 911mmry compilation as a multinational conglomerate that invests in heavily government-regulated industries: telecommunications, healthcare and particularly defense. The compilation says both [[george-w-bush]] and [[george-h-w-bush]] worked for it and that the [[bin-laden-family]] counted among its investors. A speaker calls it in effect the 11th largest US defense contractor. *(*([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 0:00)*)* *(*([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 1:22)*)*

## Claims

- Carlyle held its annual investor conference on the morning of 9/11 at the Ritz-Carlton in Washington DC. The speaker says the regulars were there: [[james-baker]] "likely", John Major "definitely", and [[george-h-w-bush]], who left that morning, along with [[shafik-bin-laden]] (Osama's half-brother), in town to watch the family's Carlyle investments. All were said to be in one room watching the planes hit. *([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 0:24)*
- The bin Laden family was invested in a Carlyle defense fund, which the speaker calls ironic because rising US defense spending after 9/11 benefited it. *([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 0:57)*
- Carlyle owned [[united-defense-industries]] (maker of the Bradley fighting vehicle); it filed to take the company public six weeks after 9/11 and made a one-day profit of $237 million in December. *([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 1:29)*
- Because of the attention on the bin Laden connection the family eventually had to withdraw, but Bush Sr. stayed on as senior adviser to Carlyle's Asia board for another two years. *([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 1:57)*
- A commentator's counter-framing: the company "is about money, it's not about conspiracies to run the world", and it has done very well. *([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 3:10)*
- A White House spokesman is quoted as seeing no ethical conflict in a former president and former Secretary of State [[james-baker]] using their contacts with world leaders to represent "one of the most well-known military arms dealers". *([911mmry/carlyle](<../raw/911mmry/911MmRy - 2008-02-12 - Carlyle Group - Bush connection to Bin Laden.md>) 3:25)*
