Vince Divine is director of security and SAPCO (special access program central office) for
[[northrop-grumman|Northrop Grumman's]] space sector — a title Gerb reads as marking him as one
of the executive seniors within Northrop's internal SAP security offices, working under
[[terry-phillips|Terry Phillips]]. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md)
2:26:42–2:27:05)*

## Claims

- Prior career: ManTech, liaising intimately with [[darpa|DARPA]]'s Strategic Technology Office
  as a SAP functional area lead; deputy director of security supporting USDI SAPCO and DoD SAPCO;
  director of security for East Coast operations for ACOM, the successor to
  [[eg-and-g|EG&G]]. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 2:27:05–2:27:44)*
- From 2019 to 2021, served as deputy director of security for the Air Force Rapid Capabilities
  Office (RCO) under [[randall-g-walden|Randall G. Walden]]. *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 2:27:57–2:28:14)*
- Gerb argues that if Northrop's alleged breakaway UFO programs still answer to any government
  handler at all, Divine — given his top-secret SAP-channel career spanning DARPA, RCO, and
  Northrop's space sector — "would indeed be the man to serve a critical role coordinating such
  [WOAP] activities." *([gerb/northrop_trw](../raw/gerb/northrop_trw.md) 2:28:19–2:28:34)*
