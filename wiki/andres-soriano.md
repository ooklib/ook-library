Andres Soriano (ASR "Andres Sorano") was the most prominent member of Manila's Spanish Club, president of San Miguel Brewery, founder of the Soriano Corporation (Anscor) holding company, and head of the Philippine branch of Franco's Falange, per [[eyes-wide-open]]. He is the host's example of the "atypical" nation-building officers and Franco sympathizers around [[douglas-macarthur]] and [[charles-willoughby]].

## Claims

- The Spanish Club (upscale, Spanish Filipinos) was "filled with Falangist sympathizers" who, per a 1952 article on Willoughby, controlled an estimated 80% of the islands' wealth; families named by the host: Soriano, Elizalde, "Brias Roases" (ASR; possibly Roxas -- my inference), Zobel. Willoughby, a Franco fan ([[francisco-franco]]), became a mainstay there on arrival in 1940. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 43:13-44:13)*
- Anscor held major stakes in mining, oil exploration, airlines; Coca-Cola bottling. Soriano was Franco's number-one Philippine donor in the Spanish Civil War; in WWII he became Quezon's Secretary of Commerce and a colonel on MacArthur's staff. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 44:13-45:11)*
- Close to [[courtney-whitney]]; befriended MacArthur's staff in the late 1930s. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 45:24-45:49)*
