AFIO is, per [[eyes-wide-open]] in chapter 2 of [[road-to-dallas]], the Association of Former Intelligence Officers, founded in 1975 by [[david-atlee-phillips]] in response to the scrutiny of the 1975 "year of intelligence" hearings ([[church-committee]]); host notes it "still exists to this day." *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 7:33)*

## Claims

- Ostensible purpose: a lobbying group and defense fund to protect intelligence officers from prosecution under the ongoing investigations of CIA crimes. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 7:57)*
- Host's reading: beyond that, it created a network linking not only ex-CIA officers but ex-members of the Office of Naval Intelligence, the Defense Intelligence Agency and Army Intelligence, which "would become especially significant" in the 1980s when intelligence operations were largely privatized under Reagan-Bush (as covered in his earlier videos; see [[cia-fronts-and-proprietaries]]). Socially, it shows Phillips viewing colleagues as a family worth protecting. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 8:10)*
- Host shows photos from the Tango Down 63 blog of Phillips at seemingly AFIO events, one standing with a man who looks similar to "Poppy" Bush; host says he is "not certain it is him" ([[george-h-w-bush]]). He adds that the JM/WAVE-episode photo of [[david-sanchez-morales]] and [[thomas-clines]] (ASR "Tom Klein") could plausibly have been taken at an AFIO event; speculative. *([ewa/dallas-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-21 - David Atlee Phillips_ the Spy's Spy - The Road to Dallas Chapter 2.md) 8:48)*

## Relationships

- Founded by [[david-atlee-phillips]]; members drawn from [[central-intelligence-agency]] and other services.
