TREAT is, per [[edgar-fuche]], the "Men in Black": a team under NSA control that watched cleared employees at Nevada Test Site, Nellis, Los Alamos, Sandia and Area 51. Gerb can find only one public mention, a 31 Aug 1997 letter to [[steven-greer]] accusing [[hans-adam-ii]] of funding TREAT and "its Gerald", who was married to a former head of Army intelligence and security command, which Gerb reads as [[albert-stubblebine]]. Gerb concludes TREAT may be the real-life Men in Black housed in [[army-intelligence-and-security-command]]. Unverified.
