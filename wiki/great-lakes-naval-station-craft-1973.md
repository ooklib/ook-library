Case A7 in [[leonard-stringfield]]'s crash retrieval status report 3: witness "RK", an E4 gunnery instructor at Great Lakes Naval Base from March 1973 to September 1974, told Stringfield on 14 July 1980 that in September 1973 he saw a metallic craft in a Quonset hut. Gerb says he knows RK's true name and is trying to vet him. Unverified secondhand claim.

## Claims (gerb/navy)

- RK was ordered to guard a large Quonset hut, letting nobody within 100 yards. A naval officer gave him a sealed envelope for the commander inside. Inside he saw a 30 ft long, 10 ft high metallic craft on a wooden platform, "light silvery blue and shimmering", tear-drop tapered with a flange along the top and no windows. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:17:31-1:18:40)*
- Weeks earlier in San Diego a sailor told RK that an identical UFO was shot down by the Navy between Hawaii and the mainland in June 1973 after picking it up on destroyer radar, crashing into 350 ft of water, and was retrieved by the [[hughes-glomar-explorer]], shipped to Hawaii, then stateside and finally to Chicago. The sailor gave RK a matching sketch. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:18:40-1:19:29)*
- Gerb argues this matches Chris Sharp's 2024 claims (NURO and the Navy downing and retrieving craft, then storing at an ONR location), noting the Office of Naval Research's Chicago department is under 40 miles away, and that [[bobby-ray-inman]] said he learned of Glomar activities in Hawaii in the early 1970s. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:19:29-1:20:10, 1:43:08-1:44:07)*
