Project Sidekick is, per [[bob-lazar]], the S4 project concerning the weapon potential of the craft, as described in the blue briefing folders he read on arrival *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 42:02-42:09)*. Lazar says that of the three projects he naturally likes propulsion but, given a "destructive streak", finds Sidekick appealing *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 44:10-44:35)*. He and the host frame it as one of three uses of gravity control (propulsion, time, weapons) whose mastery would "rule the world"; secrecy is justified because if an adversary got a time-dilating weapon "you lose" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 42:40-44:09)*. See [[lazar-briefing-projects-trio]].
