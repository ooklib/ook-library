Robert Bigelow is the Las Vegas hotel-and-aerospace billionaire behind [[bigelow-aerospace-advanced-space-studies]]. [[steven-greer]] says he knew him at the 1993 [[laurance-rockefeller]] ranch gathering and that Bigelow wanted access to the secret technology. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 44:04-44:14, 2:10:20-2:11:03)*

## Claims

- Greer says the $22 million [[harry-reid]] helped direct to Bigelow was a setup run by Colonel [[john-b-alexander]], who he says was also mentor to [[luis-elizondo]], and that Reid's people told him nothing of value came of it. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:08:37-2:09:44)*
- Michels asks whether Bigelow has UFOs in a Vegas warehouse; Greer says no. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:08:32-2:08:37)*
- Greer says Bigelow "was innocent" and that people are hooked by their soft spots (money, power or inside access), and warns Joe Firmage and Peter Thiel similarly. *([jm/greer](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-10 - UFO Secrets Are Held By A Global Cabal (Ft. Steven Greer).md>) 2:08:52-2:11:48)*
