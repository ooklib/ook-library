Tonopah Test Range (TTR), established in 1957 by the Sandia Corporation for the Atomic Energy
Commission to test ballistics and non-nuclear features of atomic weapons, is a classified
restricted military installation within the Great Basin Desert, part of the highly restricted
northern Nevada Test and Training Range. Gerb dubs it a "mini Area 51" and publicly accuses it
of operating UFO crash-retrieval and material-exploitation programs, at least prior to 2017.
*([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 52:09–52:54, 53:26–54:27)*

## Claims

- Managed by Sandia National Laboratories from 1957; from 1993 (Martin Marietta's acquisition of
  Sandia's management contract) to 2017, effectively administered by
  [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]]. Naval operations at TTR ceased in 1993, shortly before
  Lockheed took over range administration. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md)
  54:19–54:27, 1:06:20–1:06:28)*
- Lockheed's F-117 Nighthawk was initially developed and tested in secret at TTR for nearly a
  decade; Lockheed radio telemetry data was detected at TTR beginning in 2007, linked to
  Lockheed's Palmdale, California facility (1011 Lockheed Way, building 601).
  *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 55:22–56:13)*
- Per [[richard-dolan|Richard Dolan]], a 2021 leaked source described a classified UAP Task
  Force report discussing energy-pulse, ion, anti-gravity, antimatter, ramjet-hydrogen, and
  compressed-nuclear propulsion systems, along with exotic-element energy research on "recovered
  nonhuman technologies," spearheaded by DARPA and a group called "Advanced Group Six," funded
  partly through IRAD, with prototype craft flown at Area 51 and Tonopah, observed UAP activity
  in the area attributed to these craft, but none flown outside the Nevada Test and Training
  Range. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 56:19–59:56)*
- TTR hosts its own acknowledged "Site 4," managed by the Air Force 554th Range Squadron, distinct
  from Bob Lazar's Area 51/S4 near Papoose Lake — though Gerb's private sources claim Tonopah's
  Site 4 is directly involved in UFO operations. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md)
  1:07:33–1:10:16)*
- The Naval Air Special Weapons Facility (now NAWCWD, primarily at China Lake) recommended
  TTR's site in 1956; the Navy later conducted special-weapons testing
  there, including work connected to [[william-f-raborn|Admiral William F. Raborn]]'s Polaris
  program and [[john-craven|John Piña Craven]]'s [[operation-sand-dollar|Operation Sand Dollar]]
  — Lockheed's Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicles (DSRVs) Mystic and Avalon were built for this
  program. *([lockheed](../raw/X6JfbfmvgMo_lockheed.md) 1:02:47–1:06:09)*
