Senator Joseph McCarthy of Wisconsin, per Eyes Wide Open, was the public face of the early-1950s
anti-communist "Red Scare" — but the host, following authors Anthony Summers and Peter Dale
Scott, argues McCarthy's rise and supposedly ironclad intelligence on communist subversives were
secretly orchestrated by [[j-edgar-hoover]] and financier [[lewis-rosenstiel]].

## Claims

- In February 1950, the then-unknown junior senator made his iconic claim that the State
  Department was riddled with some 200 communists, always producing lists of subversives that
  seemed to appear "out of thin air." *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 22:36-22:57)*
- Per journalist/author Anthony Summers, citing William Sullivan (1960s head of the FBI's domestic
  intelligence program), McCarthy's "meteoric rise and irrefutable intel" was secretly fed to him
  by [[j-edgar-hoover]], who used McCarthy as a public-facing front for a political crusade Hoover
  had no authority to undertake himself as ostensibly neutral FBI director. Peter Dale Scott goes
  further, arguing Hoover and [[lewis-rosenstiel]] together effectively created McCarthy's entire
  witch trial: Hoover's subversive-name lists were routed through US Army intelligence and then
  leaked via Rosenstiel's Jewish League Against Communism front group before reaching McCarthy.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 23:00-24:16)*
- His anti-communist "crusade in earnest" beginning in 1950 was inspired by [[richard-nixon]]'s
  prior [[house-un-american-activities-committee|HUAC]] prosecutions, including the Alger Hiss
  case. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 15:25-15:41)*
- [[roy-cohn]] served as his chief counsel, beating out [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert Kennedy]] for
  the senior role; Cohn's career had been engineered largely by [[j-edgar-hoover]].
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 32:23-32:48)*
- George White, an FBN/OSS/CIA agent nicknamed "Mr. Deep Politics," worked with McCarthy on the
  1950 committee investigating communist influence in the State Department, in an unclear
  capacity the host speculates may have been as a CIA attaché. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 53:19-53:57)*
