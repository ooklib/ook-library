The Manhattan Engineer District (MED) was the US Army Corps of Engineers organization that
served as the legal and administrative entity for the Manhattan Project, giving the program its
name. UAP Gerb treats the MED's cover-name/cover-organization strategy as the direct template for
how he alleges the "Manhattan Project 2.0" UFO legacy program apparatus hides administratively
within the modern US Air Force. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 42:38–43:23)*

## Claims

- Created by exploiting the Army Corps of Engineers' convention of organizing into geographic
  "districts" (e.g. the Omaha District, Pittsburgh District); the "Manhattan" name was chosen to
  be deliberately mundane and unconnected to nuclear weapons. *([secrecy-1](../raw/
  jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 42:47–43:23)*
- Served as the legal/administrative entity handling acquisitions, contracting, construction,
  personnel security, and infrastructure for the atomic bomb program, under the command of
  General [[leslie-groves|Leslie Groves]], who reported through a short chain of command directly
  to Secretary of War Henry Stimson and Army Chief of Staff [[george-c-marshall|George Marshall]].
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 43:23–44:06)*
- In 1943, established Special Engineer Detachments (SEDs) — a designation for military personnel
  with scientific/technical backgrounds assigned to work alongside civilian scientists at Los
  Alamos, Sandia's Z Division, and Oak Ridge; most SED personnel went on to prominent physics,
  engineering, and academic careers after the war. Disputed [[interplanetary-phenomenon-unit|IPU]]
  documents place an SED at the 1947 Roswell recovery. *([secrecy-1](../raw/
  jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 2:10:46–2:11:20)*
- Under the MED, early nuclear secrets were governed by an internal self-censorship/publication-
  control system spearheaded by [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]]'s Office of Scientific Research
  and Development, formalizing voluntary-then-mandatory censorship of nuclear-physics discussions.
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:07:39–1:08:04)*
- UAP Gerb argues the MED's function — a mundanely-named cover organization letting Groves bypass
  standard Army bureaucracy and acquisition oversight while retaining near-total operational
  autonomy — is directly mirrored by the modern Air Force [[rapid-capabilities-office|Rapid
  Capabilities Office (RCO)]], which he alleges administers the [[hidden-wing-air-force-program|
  Hidden Wing]] UFO test-and-evaluation effort with similarly minimal chain-of-command friction
  and "near limitless budgets." *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 47:16–48:04)*
- Proposed and oversaw, via General Marshall, the [[alsos-mission|ALSOS missions]] — a Manhattan
  Project intelligence operation to secure Nazi atomic-program personnel, facilities, and
  materials, run entirely outside standard military-intelligence channels.
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 2:14:08–2:14:24)*
