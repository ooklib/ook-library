The "mixed airborne commando group" (ASR "Mach G", "Mac G"; acronym MACG is the host's spoken form as transcribed), described as a newly formed special-forces division under French intelligence, the "French equivalent of Green Berets". *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 17:04-17:25, 27:32-27:47)*

## Claims

- [[roger-trinquier]] served nominally under it from late 1951; its commandos parachuted into Hmong villages to raise the maquis, and its planes flew most of the raw opium out. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 17:04-17:11, 25:56-26:02, 28:09-28:16)*
- [[roger-trinquier]] was later its commander. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 36:34-36:41)*
- Its parachute commandos paid the tribesmen out of the black-box slush fund. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 26:17-26:33)*
- The host says the link to [[sdece]] is that it was "under French intelligence" (the transcript names the SDECE earlier, at 3:48-3:56, but does not tie the MACG to it explicitly).
