ITT is an American industrial manufacturer based in Connecticut. The video alleges deep business ties with Nazi Germany.

## Claims

- In August 1933 Hitler met ITT CEO Sosthenes Behn, one of his first meetings with an American businessman. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 20:29-20:38)*
- ITT subsidiaries allegedly made payments to SS leader [[heinrich-himmler]] and held a 25% stake in Focke-Wulf, maker of Luftwaffe fighters; it also owned shares of a firm transcribed 'Ziggnalba' (probably Signalbau), producing radar and communications gear for the Wehrmacht. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 20:41-20:56)*
- In the 1960s ITT received $27 million compensation from the US government for Allied bombing damage to its Focke-Wulf plant share. *([brof_stein/nazi-brands](<../raw/brof_stein/Brofessor Stein - 2025-07-23 - Every Major Brand with Nazi Ties Explained By a German.md>) 20:56-21:05)*
