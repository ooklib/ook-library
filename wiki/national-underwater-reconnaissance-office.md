Described as the US's "most secret intelligence agency," co-founded by the
[[cia-directorate-of-science-and-technology|CIA Directorate of Science and Technology]] and the
US Navy. Gerb connects it to underwater UFO crash retrievals.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 47:38–47:54)*

## Claims

- Referred to elsewhere in Gerb's work as one of the US's intelligence agencies alongside NSA,
  CIA, DIA, NGA, NRO — sometimes called "the hidden neuro" for its obscurity.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 21:35–21:45)*
- Cited as a partner (with the Navy, CIA DS&T, SOCOM, DOE, and others) in worldwide undersea UFO
  retrieval operations, and specifically connected to
  [[naval-surface-warfare-center-crane|NSWC Crane]]'s alleged role in the broader complex of
  naval centers engaged in "technologies of unknown origin" housing/exploitation.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 19:00–19:14, 1:50:09–1:50:24)*

- Created secretly in 1969 to consolidate reconnaissance between the CIA and Navy, still
  classified today; sister agency to the [[national-reconnaissance-office|NRO]] under the
  National Reconnaissance Program (NRP). [[bobby-ray-inman|Bobby Ray Inman]] directed it starting
  13 September 1974. One of NURO's first operations (1972) recovered wreckage of Soviet submarine
  K-129 using the CIA vessel Glomar Explorer. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 12:12–12:22,
  2:43:44–2:46:00)*
- [[glenn-gaffney|Glenn Gaffney]], later CIA DS&T deputy director, reportedly began his career at
  NURO. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 2:44:52–2:45:09)*
