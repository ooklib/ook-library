On 11 November 1979 a TAE Super Caravelle (flight JK 297) carrying 109 passengers made an emergency landing at Manises airport, Valencia, Spain, after its crew saw two red lights that seemed to follow the aircraft, while a Spanish Air Force Mirage F1 sent to intercept an object allegedly had its systems jammed. [[uap-gerb]] presents it as Spain's most famous UFO case and the first recorded commercial flight grounded by a UFO sighting. All details below are Gerb's retelling of the case, largely relayed from the MPC Flight Recreations video; the source does not give references.

## Claims

- Captain "Javier Leo de Taha" (ASR; probably Javier Lerdo de Tejada, unverified) landed the flight after a refueling stop on the way from Salzburg, Austria, to Las Palmas; the co-pilot is given as Jose Ramon "Zazangore" (spelling unverified) and the flight mechanic as Francisco Javier Rodriguez. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 1:19-2:10)*
- At about 23:05 over the Mediterranean the flight mechanic warned of two powerful red lights to the front left at roughly 23,000 ft. Barcelona air traffic control said there was no other traffic nearby. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 2:10-2:59)*
- The lights approached, appeared to be two powerful sources of light with no visible solid body, and mirrored the aircraft when it changed altitude, staying between half a mile and five miles away. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 3:24-3:47)*
- The captain judged that flying near the object broke safety rules and left no room for evasive action, so he aborted and landed at Manises. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 4:41-5:35)*
- Just before landing the crew detected three new signals on radar, each with an estimated diameter of 200 m. Airport personnel and Marines at the nearby Manises air base also saw the lights, and one signature passed close to the runways, so emergency lights were turned on in case it was an aircraft in trouble. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 5:35-6:09)*
- Captain Fernando "Kamaro" (Camara, spelling unverified) was scrambled in a Mirage F1 from the Los Llanos airbase. He had to fly at Mach 1.4 (given as 1,074 mph) to get visual contact with a truncated cone-shaped object with a bright, changing color, which disappeared. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 6:09-6:53)*
- He was vectored to a second radar echo near Sagunto; the object darted away as he approached, then seemed to respond to the jet: his avionics were scrambled, the alert system warned of a continuous-wave missile radar lock, and the UFO accelerated away toward Africa. After nearly an hour and a half of pursuit and low on fuel he returned. Gerb also says the object jammed his attempted infrared missile lock. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 6:53-7:48,)*
- The sighting reached the Spanish parliament in September 1980, where representatives asked for an official explanation; it was dismissed as a series of freak optical illusions. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 7:48-8:10)*
- Gerb says the most striking element is the fighter's sensor failure, not the crew's decision to land. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 9:26-9:44)*

## Skeptical explanations (as reported by Gerb)

- The red lights were combustion towers of the Esso (transcribed "Esom") refinery near Cartagena (transcribed "cardia", unverified), the crew had an anxiety attack, and the airport and Marine sightings were stars and planets. Gerb answers that stationary flares do not explain lights that approached and matched maneuvers. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 12:04-13:29)*
- The Mirage jamming was blamed on electronic emissions from two US Navy Sixth Fleet helicopter carriers in the Mediterranean, at a time when the Iranian hostage crisis was being resolved; Camara rejects this, saying he was too far from the fleet and it does not explain the cone shape, the speed-away behavior or the missile-lock jamming. *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 13:29-14:34)*
- Gerb attributes this "debunk panel" to "the McWest level" of skeptic and concludes it "doesn't hold up". *([gerb/flight-grounded](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-21 - The First Commercial Flight Grounded Due to UFOs.md>) 11:58-12:04,)*
