T. V. Soong is presented by [[eyes-wide-open]] as Chiang Kai-shek's brother-in-law and the central
KMT figure of the [[china-lobby]] in Washington. **Name note:** the ASR renders "TVs" / "soon"; the
normalization is my inference from the initials and role (the source's page never spells it).

## Claims

- One of the KMT's largest sponsors, long-time China Lobby leader, "purportedly one of the richest
  men in Asia"; worked as the Generalissimo's foreign agent and, for a time in the mid-1940s, as
  premier of the Republic of China. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 9:26-9:51)*
- Entered the US in 1940, three years before the repeal of the 1882 Chinese Exclusion Act, which the
  host takes as a sign of his power; assembled the modern China Lobby's foreign backers in Washington
  and reconnected with [[henry-luce]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 9:51-10:54)*
- Per [[peter-dale-scott]], early post-WWII funding for [[civil-air-transport]] "likely came
  directly" from Soong and his wealthy wife (the host says "Chang's wealthy wife" in the same
  sentence; ASR ambiguity about which spouse, flagged). *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 30:06-30:20)*

## Relationships
