[[peter-levenda]] says the first pages he wrote for [[tom-delonge]] became the "cargo cult" prologue of *Sekret Machines: Gods*: modern civilization began as a cargo cult, imitating gods who once descended. The idea reportedly resonated with senior figures around DeLonge.

## Claims

- Levenda says DeLonge contacted him in November 2014, likely after his Amsterdam "secret space program" symposium talk about the JFK-UFO link and classifying their own information, which drew a standing ovation and many online views; he met [[richard-dolan]] and Timothy Good there. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 1:13:24, 1:14:46, 1:15:32, 1:17:08)*
- The Sumerian story of a visitor who emerges from a river vehicle wearing a fish-head hat (Levenda compares it to a bishop's miter) and teaches mathematics, writing and agriculture is presented as a creation myth; he says "everybody has a piece of the story". *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 1:40:04, 1:41:31, 1:45:24)*
- In 2015 a trusted adviser of DeLonge (unnamed) allegedly emailed asking how much they knew of Greek mythology, pointing at Prometheus as an encrypted contact story; Levenda calls it a "deep throat moment" and reads Prometheus as a god transgressing on humanity's behalf. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 1:47:22, 1:48:14, 1:49:34)*
- He rejects the Corey Goode-style secret space program and the "Eisenhower treaty" as lacking language, enforcement or evidence. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 1:19:10, 3:48:04, 4:04:29)*
- He says he would not want a golden age and that "you are the disclosure", because NHI would not privilege generals or presidents. *([jm/levenda](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-22 - CIA Historian Aliens Are Controlling Human Timelines! (ft. Peter Levenda).md>) 4:03:33, 4:04:29)*
