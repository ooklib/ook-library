Peter Levenda is an occult-history author cited by [[jesse-michels]] as co-author with [[tom-delonge]] of *Secret Machines* and as editor or publisher-associated with the 1977 *Necronomicon* (the source states he has received letters from military officers, including Strategic Air Command, calling it "spot on", and that it was a bestseller at Fort Benning; unverified). He suggests figures near the NROL-16 launch encouraged DeLonge to study Prometheus. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 50:32-52:08)*
