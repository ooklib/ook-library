General Leslie Groves commanded the Manhattan Project as head of the
[[manhattan-engineer-district|Manhattan Engineer District (MED)]]. UAP Gerb credits Groves with
outlining the conceptual security blueprint — compartmentalization, organizational cover, and a
short chain of command — that he argues was directly copied onto the alleged "Manhattan Project
2.0" UFO legacy-program apparatus. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
2:20:57–2:21:13)*

## Claims

- Used the Army Corps of Engineers' geographic-"district" administrative convention to create the
  deliberately mundane-sounding Manhattan Engineer District as the legal/administrative entity
  through which the Manhattan Project operated, handling acquisitions, contracting, construction,
  and personnel security while obscuring the project's true nuclear-weapons purpose.
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 42:38–43:40)*
- Reported through an extremely short chain of command directly to Secretary of War Henry Stimson
  and Army Chief of Staff [[george-c-marshall|George Marshall]], which let Groves aggressively
  skirt standard acquisition channels and bureaucratic red tape. UAP Gerb draws a direct parallel
  to the modern Air Force Rapid Capabilities Office (RCO) — an office that similarly operates
  with near-limitless budget and minimal oversight/chain-of-command friction, which Gerb connects
  to the alleged [[hidden-wing-air-force-program|Hidden Wing]] UFO test-and-evaluation effort.
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 43:40–48:04)*
- Implemented early iterations of what would become Sensitive Compartmented Information (SCI) and
  Special Access Program-style compartmentalization on the Manhattan Project, granting access to
  core program secrets on a strict tiered, need-to-know basis — a practice UAP Gerb says was
  "quite literally" ported onto UFO legacy program secrecy. *([secrecy-1](../raw/
  jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:08:04–1:08:28)*
- Practiced an informal doctrine of information "born secret" — a scientist's own written notes
  on nuclear physics were inherently classified by subject matter, not by formal review — later
  codified into law as "restricted data" under the 1946 Atomic Energy Act.
  *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 1:09:16–1:09:45)*
- Appropriated the remote Los Alamos school site and installed [[j-robert-oppenheimer|J. Robert
  Oppenheimer]] as scientific director at Oppenheimer's request, creating a single isolated lab
  where personnel could be recruited to a location and project they were not permitted to
  identify or describe. Oppenheimer periodically complained that Groves's need-to-know apparatus
  hindered scientific progress. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 39:37–40:01,
  1:51:38–1:51:53)*
- Commanded the [[alsos-mission|ALSOS missions]] alongside [[george-c-marshall|Marshall]],
  Marshall's G2 Colonel Boris Pash, and [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]] — the Manhattan
  Engineer District's dedicated intelligence operation to secure Nazi atomic-program personnel,
  facilities, and materials. UAP Gerb notes Groves reportedly disliked the ALSOS name (a pun on
  his own surname in Greek). *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md) 2:14:08–2:14:24,
  3:16:17–3:16:32)*
- UAP Gerb states he does not believe Groves personally carried over into the alleged Manhattan
  Project 2.0 legacy-program apparatus, as Groves was forced to retire shortly after the Manhattan
  Project — distinguishing him from [[vannevar-bush|Bush]] and [[george-c-marshall|Marshall]],
  whom Gerb argues did carry forward. *([secrecy-1](../raw/jR-h5p2bd-A_secrecy_1.md)
  3:15:32–3:15:48)*
