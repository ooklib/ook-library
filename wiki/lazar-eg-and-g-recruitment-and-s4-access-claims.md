[[bob-lazar]]'s account, in the 2025 Area52 interview, of how he was hired and inducted into S4. All items are Lazar's uncorroborated claims unless noted.

## Meeting Teller and getting the job

- Lazar was working at [[los-alamos-national-laboratory]] and had become locally famous for putting a jet engine in his Honda; the lab newspaper ran him on its front page the same day [[edward-teller]] lectured at the lab. Ramsey says the documentary found proof that Teller lectured that day. Lazar met Teller sitting outside, introduced himself as "the guy on the front" and chatted *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 12:13-14:01)*.
- After leaving the lab, running his own business and moving to Las Vegas, Lazar sent out resumes, including to Teller; Teller replied with a contact at [[eg-and-g]] special projects at McCarran Airport *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 14:11-14:54)*. See also the Teller hiring story on [[bob-lazar]].
- Two interviews followed: the first technical, the second almost entirely about his spare time, drinking and social life, which Lazar found "really, really unusual"; EG&G then hired him for "advanced propulsion technology" with an eventual rotation of two weeks on, one off *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 15:14-16:50)*.

## Travel and induction

- EG&G's "Janet" 727/737 flights with red stripes left from a building behind the McCarran EG&G facility and served the test sites; Lazar's first flight landed at [[groom-lake]], where he signed security forms; his supervisor [[dennis-mariani]] escorted him *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 18:37-19:36)*.
- On later trips a Navy-painted school bus with blacked-out windows took them about 15-20 minutes south; Mariani stared at him throughout; the bus passed the hangar doors and stopped at a single unmarked door in a hillside *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 23:39-25:33)*.
- Inside was a small guard room, then a hand scanner (Lazar recalls an "Idenomat"-type device) that measured bone lengths and issued a magnetic-stripe badge *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 28:32-29:50)*.
- Security guards were aggressive: a badge slipping out of sight brought a wall-slamming interrogation, and guards allegedly watched him urinate; Lazar says it "destroyed your will to work there" and thinks not everybody wanted him hired *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 30:11-32:23)*.
- A nurse gave a checkup and an arm-grid allergen test for unknown exotic materials, and he drank a translucent pine-colored liquid she gave him "for your immune system" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 33:06-34:36)*. Compare the drink noted on [[jacques-vallee]] and [[bob-lazar]].
- Call-ins were unpredictable: EG&G phoned at random (including weekends) with a tarmac time. Lazar wonders if it was a flexibility test; Ramsey compares it with stress inoculation *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 36:18-37:52)*.
- Lazar was told to "follow the rules" and not be a jerk, or he would regret it *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:58:55-1:59:07)*.
