Thomas Braden, per Eyes Wide Open, persuaded [[allen-dulles]] to create the [[cia-international-organizations-division]] and headed it from spring 1951, funding the [[american-committee-on-united-europe]] and associated pan-European groups. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 25:51-26:20)*

## Claims

- Special assistant to Dulles who convinced him to consolidate CIA front-group and propaganda funding into one directorate; made its head in spring 1951, replaced in 1954 by [[cord-meyer]]. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 25:51-27:16)*
- Later prominent in Operation Mockingbird (CIA domestic propaganda): bought the Oceanside newspaper in 1954 with a $100,000 loan from Nelson Rockefeller and was the liberal host on CNN's Crossfire opposite Pat Buchanan. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 26:20-26:57)*
- Will reappear in the French Connection episodes, allegedly helping pacify the docks and enabling the Corsican Mafia takeover in the early 1950s. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 26:57-27:10)*
