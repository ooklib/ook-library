The New Statesman is a British political magazine founded in 1913 by the [[fabian-society]],
per Green & Pleasant, as part of the society's [[permeation]] strategy of building institutions
to spread its influence through the civil service and political class.

## Claims

- Founded 1913 by the Fabian Society
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 7:34)*.
- Fabian Margaret Hay Thomas observed that the magazine was "being taken in by an enormous number
  of civil servants and was penetrating right through Whitehall"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 7:43)*.

## Relationships

- Founded by the [[fabian-society]] (1913) as a [[permeation]] vehicle.

## Speculation flag

Green & Pleasant cites this as an example of deliberate institutional infiltration via civil
servant readership; the magazine's later editorial independence from the Fabian Society is not
addressed by this source.
