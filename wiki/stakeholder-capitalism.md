Economic doctrine associated with [[klaus-schwab]] in which firms serve employees, community, society and the planet, not just shareholders (contrasted with Milton Friedman's shareholder primacy). Claims below are from [[wef-insider-interview-2025]].

## Claims

- Fixler says she was raised on Friedman, then found Schwab's version attractive ("profit with purpose") and saw it as saleable. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 4:16, 4:36)*
- She says it shifted after 2020 (COVID, George Floyd, social unrest) from option to obligation, blurring business and government. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 11:28, 13:08)*
- She says the existing elite rebranded through it and that it created a new class of consultants, regulators and academics. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 43:14, 44:11)*
- She argues it "isn't stakeholder capitalism, this is socialism": top-down state control of resources, production, distribution and demographics, and says it is taught in schools and business programs while Friedman is "banished". *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 54:39, 55:30, 1:22:39)*
- Marshall notes that with everyone a stakeholder in every decision, effectively no one decides. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 13:50)*
