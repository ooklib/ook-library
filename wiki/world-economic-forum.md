Swiss-based international forum best known for its annual Davos meeting. This page records claims made in [[wef-insider-interview-2025]] by [[desiree-fixler]] and [[winston-marshall]], not verified fact; see [[davos-agenda-control-claims]] for the control thesis.

## Claims

- Fixler says the WEF is "much more than Davos": about 800 staff in Switzerland (over a thousand in her estimate) with four other offices, 22 industry sector groups, 37 "future councils" of about 20 external experts each (academia, business, politics, regulators, media), monthly meetings, and over 140 projects, all disclosed on its website. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 57:43, 59:08, 1:00:02)*
- She says it runs an annual risk report through its expert network and so defines the world's problems (climate, misinformation, social injustice) and their solutions (net zero, censorship of "hate speech", border controls, DEI). *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:00:17, 1:00:44)*
- She says she sat on a futures council on responsible investing and left in December 2024. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 45:16, 1:18:46)*
- She says WEF works with large asset managers, consultants and governments who all profit from the agenda. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:03:24)*
- Marshall says [[klaus-schwab]] stepped down after a Wall Street Journal story on inappropriate funding and discrimination cases. *([wm/wef-insider](<../raw/wm/Winston Marshall - 2025-12-13 - I Helped Build It! A WEF-Davos Insider EXPOSES The Great Reset.md>) 1:19:43)*
