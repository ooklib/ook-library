Lao Army general who, per [[eyes-wide-open]], became the main Royal Lao Army officer in charge of opium production and export in the mid-1960s and a major sponsor of heroin refineries across the western border. The transcript renders him only as "General Juan radicon" / "Juan rakon" / "General Juan"; normalizing this to Ouane Rattikone is my inference, not stated in the video. The host promises much more on him in future episodes. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 42:11-42:24, 50:23-50:38)*

## Claims

- Photographed in Sam Neua in 1959 with journalist Joseph Alsop, when he was "relatively unknown"; the host finds this meeting with a future figure of the CIA "Narco complex" eyebrow-raising. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 42:06-42:37)*
- In 1962 [[phoumi-nosavan]] assigned him the covert opium funding model. In an early-1970s interview with [[alfred-mccoy]] he admitted that his boss's opium profits in early 1962 totalled at least $35,000 a month. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 50:16-50:56)*
