Colloquial name for the 1977 dismissal of long-standing CIA "old boys" after Jimmy Carter took office. In [[eyes-wide-open]]'s `ewa/shackley` it ends the careers of [[george-h-w-bush]] as director and [[ted-shackley]] as associate deputy director for operations.

## Claims

- Carter entered office January 1977; [[george-h-w-bush]] (director since 1976, who had promoted Shackley two rungs under the directorship) was replaced by outsider Admiral Stansfield Turner (ASR "Stanfield"), ordered to "clean house." *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 9:16-10:15)*
- Several long-standing CIA old boys were terminated in "another attempt to reform the agency"; host: "oddly similar" to JFK's attempted CIA restructuring. Shackley was demoted in December 1977 and officially retired by 1979. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 10:06-10:40)*
- Shackley's *The Third Option* opens by denouncing Carter over this ([[the-third-option]]); host says the CIA returned to its "pre-Carter, pre-Halloween massacre glory" under [[william-casey]] and Reagan. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 13:48-13:58, 16:02-16:11)*
