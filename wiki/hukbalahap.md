The Hukbalahap ("People's Army", ASR "Hook Balah"), a Filipino resistance organization founded in 1942 to fight the Japanese, one of some 64 distinct resistance groups according to [[eyes-wide-open]] (citing David Foy).

## Claims

- Many around [[douglas-macarthur]] regarded the Huks as a dependable ally against the Japanese; [[courtney-whitney]] favored working with them. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:21:14-1:21:52)*
- [[charles-willoughby]] believed the Huks were communists and opposed supporting them, which left him marginalized in the Philippines campaign. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:22:10-1:22:29)*
- Host: [[edward-lansdale]] later "put down the Hukbalahap rebellion" in the Philippines right after WWII, "positioning him as a rising star"; the host contrasts this with Willoughby's hostility. *([ewa/willoughby-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-25 - The MacArthur Legacy - 50 Years of Empire Building in the Pacific - WILLOUGHBY 2.md) 1:19:37-1:20:06)*

## Relationships

- Opposed by [[charles-willoughby]] and later [[edward-lansdale]] (per host).
