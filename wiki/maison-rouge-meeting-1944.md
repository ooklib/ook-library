The Maison Rouge meeting is a purported secret meeting, dated by [[jason-samosa]] to around August 10, 1944 and placed in Paris, at which Martin Bormann assembled German industry leaders to plan the mass evacuation of German industry, technology, people and money as the war turned. The wiki records it as Samosa's account, built on [[peter-levenda]]'s documentation and a State Department document.

## Claims

- Bormann swept the rooms for bugs and planned the evacuation; per documentation Levenda has seen, a State Department document says by 1945 hundreds of new companies had been set up in Nicaragua and Argentina tied to Nazi industrialists. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:18:13, 1:18:39)*
- Attendees named: Hermann Schmidt of [[ig-farben]], heads of the German subsidiaries of Ford and Standard Oil, people from the [[bank-for-international-settlements]], and a representative of Baron von Schroeder; Alfred Krupp is also said to have been there. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:19:08, 1:41:09)*
- Samosa frames these firms as long tied to the [[rockefeller-family]], Morgan and Hambro interests (Standard Oil/IG Farben monopoly arrangements, Rockefeller-Schroeder & Co.) and cites the 1930s "Business Plot" against FDR as evidence of transatlantic elite collaboration; he also claims [[prescott-bush]] was president of the Union Bank and that Ford prioritized trucks for Nazi Germany over UK jet engines. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:19:40, 1:20:28, 1:21:13, 1:21:37)*
- He argues the intelligence chiefs who could have monitored the evacuation were the same elite figures: [[william-donovan]] and [[allen-dulles]] at the OSS, [[nelson-rockefeller]] at the Office for the Coordination of Interamerican Affairs, [[william-stevenson]] at British Security Coordination, and [[charles-hambro]] at the Special Operations Executive. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:22:43, 1:23:51)*
- After the war, he says, those networks reconstituted as the [[world-commerce-corporation]], and the best technology taken out of Germany would have been relocated under their cover, possibly including saucer-related technology. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:24:00, 1:26:02)*
- Samosa adds that the Nazis had planned to get out of Europe nearly a year earlier, with a mass evacuation of technology, people, gold and money starting in 1943 and certainly 1944. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:14:33, 1:14:59)*
