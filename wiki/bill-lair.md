Oklahoma-born, Texas-raised CIA officer James William "Bill" Lair, the first of what [[eyes-wide-open]] calls the "Laos cowboys" and the officer who built the Thai [[paru]] and then ran the CIA's Hmong army program. Sources cited include Lair's Texas Tech Vietnam Center interviews. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 56:22-56:41)*

## Claims

- Advised the Thai border police paramilitary program 1951-1958 and helped create the PARU; funded through [[sea-supply-corporation]] (officially on its payroll). Lair claims [[allen-dulles]] visited his Thai jungle camp in 1953, which he says later helped him head the Laos program. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 56:16-56:41, 1:00:01-1:01:13, 1:01:44-1:01:52)*
- Host's typology: old-boys "spies" (Dulles, Helms, Angleton) versus "operators" (former law enforcement) versus a new class of paramilitary "cowboys" from Middle America, Army intelligence or OSS, later joined by Cuban exiles after the Bay of Pigs. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 56:47-59:32)*
- Heard rumors in Bangkok of Hmong wanting arms; J. Vinton Lawrence says Stuart Methven and another officer told Lair to meet [[vang-pao]]. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:04:51-1:06:11)*
- Moved to Savannakhet in late 1960 with PARU commandos, helping [[phoumi-nosavan]] against [[kong-le]]; first met Vang Pao in January 1961 ("we fight the Communists or we got to get out of Thailand"; reply: "you give us the weapons and we'll fight the Communists"). *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:09:16-1:10:26)*
- Met [[desmond-fitzgerald]] in Vientiane, drafted an 18-page cable that went to [[allen-dulles]], and got a short authorization to arm the first thousand men: the start of [[operation-momentum]]. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:10:32-1:11:47)*
