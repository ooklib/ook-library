Air Force colonel who commanded the 98th Range Wing (the [[nevada-test-and-training-range]]) from 2005 to 2007. The surname is as transcribed ("Have"/"Ha"), so it may be an ASR variant. [[uap-gerb]] examines him only because [[witness-ed]] served under a range commander and Gerb could not find the first commander's name. Gerb presents him as a "possible reverse engineering program official", which is speculation.

- He spent 26 years in the Air Force in fighter aviation roles. After the service he directed global trade controls at [[boeing]] in Washington, DC (trade policy and technology licensing) and led a team at "Ron Technologies" (Gerb's contractor "implicated with UAP study", read as [[raytheon]]) managing relationships with the Office of the Secretary of Defense and the military departments. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 18:03-18:52)*
