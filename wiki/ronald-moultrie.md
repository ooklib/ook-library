Ronald Moultrie, former Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security, a close
friend of [[sean-kirkpatrick]]'s, played a major role in establishing [[aaro]] and provided
oversight/direction to it in its early years. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:49:54–1:50:08)*

## Claims

- Gerb notes, "comically," that Moultrie removed MITRE from his public CV — implied by Gerb to
  be a suspicious omission given the broader MITRE-legacy-program thesis of the video.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:50:08–1:50:14)*
- Per the SAIC video, it was Moultrie, then USD for Intelligence and Security, upon whose
  recommendation [[aaro|AARO]] was formed, while himself sitting on the board of
  [[battelle-memorial-institute|Battelle]] — a connection allegedly scrubbed from his LinkedIn,
  called by an embedded guest speaker "a total conflict of interest."
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 29:29–29:54)*
- Also served on [[bdm-international|BDM International]]'s board.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 29:13–29:29)*

## Claims (from the Hidden Wing video)

- As [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-and-security|USD I&S]] (2021–2023), on 20
  July 2022, Moultrie informed the DoD of the creation of [[aaro|AARO]] and the installation of
  [[sean-kirkpatrick|Sean Kirkpatrick]] as its director, providing oversight/direction over
  several primary lines of operation (surveillance/collection/reporting, intelligence
  operations/analysis, science and technology, etc.). Gerb suggests AARO served as a "honeypot
  for whistleblowers" that Moultrie engaged with, and that the two men "would enjoy gallivanting
  around together in 2022, dismissing the topic of UFOs outright."
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:09:11–1:09:59)*
- Also served as a board member for the Better Angel Society (owned by the Carlyle Group), an
  intelligence advisory board member of [[mitre-corporation|MITRE]], and — separately from his
  documented BDM/Battelle board roles — allegedly erased [[battelle-memorial-institute|Battelle
  Memorial Institute]] (nearly two years as an advisory board member) from his public CV.
  *([gerb/hidden_wing](../raw/gerb/hidden_wing.md) 1:11:16–1:11:39)*

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2)

- Gerb alleges what was originally supposed to become a joint UAP investigation office (the "UAP
  JPO") was "distorted and twisted, likely by" Moultrie as USD I&S, into AARO as it exists today.
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:11:48–2:12:03)*
- Actual selection of [[sean-kirkpatrick|Sean Kirkpatrick]] as AARO director is attributed by Gerb
  more specifically to [[david-m-taylor|David M. Taylor]] (Deputy USD I&S) rather than Moultrie
  alone. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:18:47–2:19:20)*
