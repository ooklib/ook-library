"The war conspiracy" is [[peter-dale-scott]]'s meta-theory, as presented by [[eyes-wide-open]], that
private capital, the [[military-industrial-complex]] and above all the intelligence community
worked (consciously or by proxy) to prime, instigate and build up the United States' Asian wars.
It is the organizing frame of the "War Conspiracy" series that follows the Laos arc.

## Claims

- Scott published *The War Conspiracy: The Secret Road to the Second Indochina War* in 1972; the
  host presents its thesis as the "real and irrefutable role" of private capital, the
  military-industrial complex and the intelligence community in the buildup to the endless wars.
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 1:27-2:00)*
- Scott's own definition, quoted by the host: the war conspiracy is "the sustained resort to
  collusion and conspiracy, unauthorized provocations and fraud by US personnel, particularly
  intelligence personnel, in order to sustain or increase our military commitment in Asia."
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 2:00-2:21)*
- The host says the concept stays universally applicable: since the end of the Cold War the
  omnipresent communist threat was "shuffled out" for the global war on terror. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 2:27-2:39)*
- The theory sees a "blob" of ostensibly independent but overlapping actors, mostly members of the
  American ruling class in overlapping social circles, who share an interest in a nation in a
  perpetual state of war and so, consciously or by proxy, coalesce to make war "a constant necessity
  and reality." *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 2:39-3:11)*
- Host's stated aim: after the on-the-ground Laos episodes, take a macro view of the forces
  (intelligence agencies, lobbyists, corporate interests, the military-industrial complex,
  political operatives and the corporate media) that made a Vietnam war seem inevitable by 1964.
  *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 3:19-4:14)*
- The host contrasts this with the establishment domino-theory account; see below.

## Domino theory as the "official" explanation

The host describes the Cold War domino theory (each East Asian state a domino waiting to fall to
Red China and the Soviet Union, so that if South Vietnam or Laos fell every other would follow) as
the "definitive silver bullet" of establishment histories, and says skeptical researchers asked
whether Vietnam was truly inevitable. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 0:32-1:27)*
