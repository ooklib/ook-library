A little-documented US Navy office that Gerb describes as an umbrella control system for several naval clandestine undersea operations, first found in a 1974 omnibus NSCID alongside the National Reconnaissance Program. Gerb theorizes that a naval UFO/USO retrieval program would be compartmented inside it, possibly together with NURO. Speculation from sparse documents.

## Claims (gerb/navy)

- The first mention Gerb can find is the 1974 omnibus National Security Council intelligence directive, which discusses it next to the National Reconnaissance Program in a way meant not to draw attention. Gerb links it to the "special Navy control program" for submarine imagery and signals collection that the Richelson book ties to NURO. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:22:16-1:23:34)*
- An 11 December 1975 declassified memo shows it was designated to run parallel to the NRP under the Executive Committee for the DCI and the Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence; Gerb suggests asking [[christopher-mellon]], himself a former ASD for intelligence. Gerb reads its collection role as showing NURO is probably hidden within it. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:23:34-1:24:30)*
- As of 26 May 2023 requests for approval of special access program facilities by the Navy Special Program Central Office can be found. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:25:04-1:25:23)*
- Gerb reasons that admiral [[timothy-gallaudet]]'s statement that he knew of testimony but was never briefed on naval legacy programs fits their being hidden in the NSP. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:25:23-1:25:49)*
- A 29 October 1974 CIA memo on clearances for NSC intelligence committee working group members discusses overlap with compartmented areas of the NSP, and a 14 December 1984 memo to Rear Admiral and DNI John Butts mentions a letter from Navy Secretary John Lehman (also a NURO director, per Gerb) about the NSP and suggests adding a representative from [[everett-heinman]]'s department. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:29:09-1:30:36)*
- Gerb says he has contacted former NSP personnel, one a diver with the Mobile Diving and Salvage Unit One, one (Mr. PR) a project lead for the NSP office and SOCOM acquisition program office, and one (Mr. WH) an ONI chief information security officer for the NSP; he withholds their identities. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:30:36-1:31:35)*
- Samuel Gene Potit, technical director of the Navy Special Program Office, ran [[project-palladium]]. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:25:49-1:26:38)*
