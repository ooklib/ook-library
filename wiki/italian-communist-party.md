The Italian Communist Party (Partito Comunista Italiano, PCI) was, per Eyes Wide Open, the
largest and most organized Communist party outside the Iron Curtain at war's end, and the target
of the CIA/Vatican/Mafia's alleged 1948 election-interference campaign.

## Claims

- Doubled in size between 1943 and 1944; by war's end, the Italian partisan network it anchored
  comprised over 150,000 armed communist sympathizers, mostly in Italy's north. Italy was
  described as divided not by race or culture but by ideology: the communist/socialist north
  (where partisan fighting was concentrated) versus the Catholic, Mafia-influenced south and
  Sicily. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 6:26-6:32, 10:47-11:32)*
- Per future CIA director [[william-colby]], had the Mafia not intervened in the 1948 election,
  the communists would have gained 60% of the vote. Peter Dale Scott states roughly 498 people,
  mostly PCI or Italian Socialist Party members, were murdered in 1948 alone as part of the
  Mafia's terror campaign against leftists and labor activists. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 24:50-25:16, 26:31-26:46)*
- Allegations exist (from CIA agent [[f-mark-wyatt]], deemed dubious by Eyes Wide Open and
  historian Alessandro Brogi) that the PCI was directly funded by "black bags of money" from the
  Soviet compound in Rome. *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 23:15-24:41)*
- Final 1948 election results: the [[christian-democracy-italy|Christian Democrats]] won 48% of
  the vote and 307 parliamentary seats; the PCI-led Communist/Socialist coalition reached only
  31% of the vote (200 seats), nearly 30% lower than its projected total.
  *([ewa/italy-1948](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-17 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Italy 1948 - How to Steal an Election [pt. 5.1].md) 29:18-29:38)*
