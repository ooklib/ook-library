Paratrooper unit inside the Thai Border Patrol Police, built and trained by [[bill-lair]] and CIA associates, per [[eyes-wide-open]] LAOS 4. The host calls it a semi-private independent element of the border patrol, "arguably Thailand's most versatile fighting force" (Timothy Castle). *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:00:06-1:01:26)*

## Claims

- The Thai border police under General "Po Cenan" (ASR; [[phao-sriyanon]] by inference) were, per several researchers, deeply invested in the north-south opium trade from the Shan states; US aid to Thailand rose from $4.5M in 1951 to $56M in 1953. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 55:40-56:16)*
- Lair's operations were funded through [[sea-supply-corporation]], a CIA cutout founded by [[paul-helliwell]] under [[world-commerce-corporation]]; the host says SEA Supply, with Lair on payroll, dispersed about $35 million a year to Phao's police. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:01:32-1:02:05, 1:03:07-1:03:19)*
- Training centre at Hua Hin (1958). Lair also claims [[allen-dulles]] visited it in 1953. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:00:26-1:01:13)*
- The PARU model of a private-looking, CIA-run force inspired the later Hmong program; PARU commandos moved into Laos with Lair in late 1960. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 1:02:19-1:02:32, 1:09:16-1:09:29)*
