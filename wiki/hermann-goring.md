Hermann Göring (ASR-transcribed "Gurring" in Eyes Wide Open's captions) was the Reichsmarschall
of the Third Reich and Hitler's named successor, considered and rejected by [[allen-dulles]] and
[[william-donovan]] as too high-profile a target to save from Nuremberg under
[[operation-sunrise]].

## Claims

- Reichsmarschall of the Third Reich and Hitler's named successor; amassed massive wealth during
  the war organizing and consolidating the German steel, mining, and weapons industries under the
  Reich, making him — in the Wall Street OSS agents' eyes — a plausible separate-peace partner
  with resources to rebuild an anti-communist Europe. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 11:17-11:46)*
- Ultimately judged too high-profile for even Dulles and Donovan to save, as it "would have raised
  too many alarm bells at the White House"; they redirected their rescue efforts to
  [[hjalmar-schacht]] instead. Later, [[karl-wolff]] testified at Nuremberg specifically as a
  witness against Göring. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 11:46-11:53, 19:05-19:08)*
