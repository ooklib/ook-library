The Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP, "OAP"), discussed alongside
[[advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program|AATIP]] as a more classified counterpart —
Gerb's earlier (SAIC video) theory suggested AATIP was likely a "white world cover" for AAWSAP.
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:34:32–1:35:01)*

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2 — clarified relationship to ATIP)

- OAP ran out of the [[defense-intelligence-agency|DIA]] from 2008 to 2012, funded by then-Senate
  Majority Leader [[harry-reid|Harry Reid]] with a budget of roughly $22 million, contracted
  primarily through [[bigelow-aerospace-advanced-space-studies|Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space
  Studies (BASS)]]. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 2:21:32–2:21:47)*
- Per [[david-grush|David Grush]] (on Rogan), one of OAP's express missions was to serve as the
  intended recipient of the [[operation-kona-blue|Kona Blue]] DHS prospective-SAP transfer of UFO
  materials from [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]]. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  2:21:47–2:22:03)*
- Gerb **revises his earlier claim** that [[advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program|
  ATIP]] was a sub-SAP or cover layer *of* AAWSAP/OAP: he now states plainly that ATIP was an
  unfunded informal working group, distinct from and not organizationally nested under OAP —
  directly rebutting [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]]'s own public claim that ATIP was a "sub-SAP
  to OAP." *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 3:07:08–3:07:56)*
- Gerb reckons OAP was likely structured as an **intelligence SAP** (protecting how the
  underlying NHI technology/materials were assessed as exploitable), by analogy with his SAP-type
  framework (acquisition/intelligence/operations-support) — the same category he assigns to
  [[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]]. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  3:06:41–3:07:08)*
