The Jeddah Agreements ("jeda" in the ASR) were, per [[eyes-wide-open]] citing Jim Hougan, a 1954 secret negotiation between [[aristotle-onassis]] and the Saudi government for the shipping of Aramco oil, which the host uses as the first documented collaboration of [[richard-nixon]] and [[robert-maheu]].

## Claims

- By January 1954 Onassis was in the final stages of the deal: his firm would get an initial 10% of Saudi oil exports, escalating potentially to 100%, making him the gatekeeper of Saudi crude. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 14:37-15:16)*
- Before it, exports were handled by the four American Aramco firms (Standard Oil of California/Chevron, Texaco, Mobil, Exxon; the host says all but Texaco were Rockefeller Standard Oil descendants, "Yankee" oil concerns versus independent "Cowboy" firms), which saw Onassis as a threat. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 15:16-16:21)*
- Context the host gives: [[nelson-rockefeller]] was Eisenhower's Special Assistant on Foreign Affairs; the CIA under [[allen-dulles]] had just carried out the 1953 Iran coup on behalf of what became BP ([[anglo-iranian-oil-company]]); Nixon cultivated the oil lobby. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 16:38-17:24)*
- Per Hougan, Nixon worked with the CIA in early 1954 to bring in Maheu's firm; Maheu was on CIA retainer and "brought right into the White House". A Maheu-firm journalist (ASR "John G" / "John gity"; name not normalized) is quoted: "Nixon ran the thing but he was never more than a frontman for the multinational oil companies ... the only string tied to it was Aristotle Onassis." Nixon was to be updated weekly. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 17:32-18:29)*
- Tactics: illegal wiretaps ("the perfect foreshadowing to Watergate") and a smear campaign using Onassis's intermediary [[hjalmar-schacht]] (Hitler's economic minister and Reichsbank president) to buy newspaper space alleging complicity in German rearmament. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 18:29-20:58)*
- Maheu later claimed the deal was foiled not by the wiretaps or smears but by evidence of a bribe between Onassis and the Saudi royal family that he convinced the CIA was a national-security issue. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 20:58-21:23)*
