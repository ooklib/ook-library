Die Glocke ("the Bell"), a legendary Nazi bell-shaped high-voltage machine, is raised as a model for Brown-style time manipulation.

## Claims (jm/townsend-brown)

- Jesse Michels says SS officer Jakob Sporrenberg documented magnetic-field separation and vortex compression around it; Walter Gerlach and Ernst Grawitz were reportedly involved, and Polish journalist Igor Witkowski believes it existed. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 1:28:16-1:29:14)*
- Nick Cook's pseudonymous source "Dan Marcus" claimed time inside its torsion-field chamber ran at 1/1000 the outside rate. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 1:29:25-1:29:54)*
- Host thesis: high-voltage, low-current fields may conceivably slow time, linking to Brown's time-travel interest and Ferris Williams' 5D theory. *([jm/townsend-brown](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-02-22 - The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary) .md>) 1:30:05-1:31:51)*
