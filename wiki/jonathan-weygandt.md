USMC Lance Corporal who, during 1997 testimony, described encountering a large egg-shaped UFO
crash while stationed in Peru during Operation Laser Strike (an aerial counter-narcotics
program). *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:12:23–1:13:05)*

## Claims

- Described the craft as egg-shaped, embedded in jungle rock, with hatches, dark interior, and
  large vents that cast no shadow despite direct sunlight — the craft appeared to absorb light
  with no reflection. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:12:40–1:13:39)*
- He and fellow Marines were held at gunpoint by unmarked men in black fatigues and Army helmets
  (no insignia); two US Army CH-47s then landed carrying DOE personnel in "MOP gear"
  (mission-oriented protective posture) to secure the site — which Gerb identifies with the
  [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team|DOE Nuclear Emergency Support Team (NEST)]].
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:13:39–1:15:16)*
- Separately described the craft's skin as surrounded by a shimmering green/purple "mother of
  pearl"-like field, which Gerb compares to [[dylan-borland|Dylan Borland]]'s later triangle
  sighting and speculates may represent an electro-optic cloaking effect.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:59:03–1:59:31)*
- Gerb identifies the DOE personnel in MOP gear as [[doe-nuclear-emergency-support-team|NEST]],
  and notes NEST considered (but did not execute) a deployment to countries neighboring Peru in
  November 1997 — months after Wagant's encounter — over debris risk from the failed Mars 96
  probe, acting on intelligence from US Space Command's DSP satellites, whose ground-processing
  systems [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]] has helped field; Gerb speculates Sandia may
  have separately aided intelligence-gathering for UFO legacy programs via DSP.
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 1:30:48–1:33:40)*
