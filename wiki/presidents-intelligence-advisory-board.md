The President's Intelligence Advisory Board (host: "PAB"; the bibliography cites an Obama White House
archive page on "PFIAB") is an advisory body created by [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] in 1956 that
[[eyes-wide-open]] calls "one of the most unaccountable and opaque pieces of the US intelligence
apparatus."

## Claims

- Accountable only to the president; its official function (per the White House archive quoted by the
  host) is to give the president "an independent source of advice on the effectiveness with which the
  intelligence community is meeting the nation's intelligence needs." Host: in practice
  presidentially selected operatives with "intimate access" to the intelligence community. *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 29:06-29:45)*
- First iteration (1956) included [[robert-lovett]] and [[joseph-kennedy]]; host notes Kennedy's seat
  could plausibly have leaked Bay of Pigs invasion plans to the JFK campaign, as "speculated by many
  researchers." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 29:45-30:32)*
- Host: later presidents, "most prominently both Poppy Bush and his son George W.," used it to induct
  friends and associates into the intelligence bubble without CIA or Pentagon bureaucracy; "we'll keep
  coming back to it." *([ewa/bush-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-25 - BUSH THE FATHER - CHAPTER 1.md) 30:40-31:12)*
