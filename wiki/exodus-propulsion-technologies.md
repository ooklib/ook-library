Exodus Propulsion Technologies is the private company (website exoduspropulsion.space) through which [[charles-buhler]] and [[andrew-arjima]] develop and patent electrostatic thrusters based on the [[exodus-force-claims]]. It is described as separate from [[nasa]], although all the founders work at "the space agency at different levels" or its periphery.

## Claims

- Goal: first commercial markets are space (orbit maintenance, moving satellites between orbits as a kick stage), with a longer-term aim of a "self launcher" that replaces rockets; there are no blueprints or energy budget yet. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 39:26-41:37)*
- Buhler says they have not yet lifted a device in Earth gravity because the voltages, wires and framing are not ready, but that thrusters could in principle lift themselves. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:08:49-1:09:30)*
- The site hosts DIY videos so anyone can build one; Buhler wants people to replicate the effect and notes an investor who already built a 1 mN device from the videos. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:08:01-1:08:29, 1:02:07-1:02:34)*
- A first patent may have received a national-security review (Buhler: "we don't know"); a second patent is under examiner-level review that Buhler equates to peer review, with examiners contacting people who signed affidavits of replication. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:10:00-1:10:31, 1:28:37-1:29:11)*
- Funding is needed to bring a second, nearly walk-in vacuum chamber online. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 44:36-44:57)*
- A separate group in Germany is said to be reproducing the effect. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:51:53-1:52:02)*
