[[bob-lazar]]'s description of the disc-shaped craft he worked on at S4, later nicknamed the "sport model" ("I referred to it as model and everybody picked that up" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:51:45-1:51:55)*). All of this is his recollection of about 1989, recreated in [[project-gravitaur-s4-documentary]] with 100% fidelity in his opinion *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:04:33-1:05:16)*.

## First sight and first entry

- He says on about his third day the bus stopped at an open hangar door, [[dennis-mariani]] led him in, he brushed the craft and a guard shouted "hands down, eyes forward"; the craft had a reversed American flag stuck at the hatch, so he thought it a fighter *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 59:56-1:01:12)*.
- On a later day he entered with [[barry-s4-lab-partner]] to check where the components sat, climbed stairs built for it and crawled in; you could barely stand except near the center, and it clearly was not built for a human *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 58:34-59:24, 1:01:30-1:02:16)*.

## Interior

- Everything inside is one color and sheen, with the same radius of curvature and no sharp edges except the reactor plate, giving an "uncanny valley", "ominous" feeling that he says was "not cool at all" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:02:33-1:03:36, 9:09-9:51)*.
- Halogen tripod lights and orange Black & Decker extension cords ran from outside; the lights lit only where aimed and did not refract, which Lazar attributes to the geometry rather than the material *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 9:58-11:52, 1:21:47-1:22:05)*.
- A triad of "chairs" (three cutouts facing one archway, one directly behind a waveguide) is what Lazar and Barry called seats but might not be; he speculates they place occupants inside the field for inertia protection or a short trip *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:13:40-1:14:38, 1:18:13-1:19:45)*. He thinks the craft may be autonomous with occupants as "a biological component" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:20:28-1:20:53)*.
- Two technicians once made an archway turn transparent, then blue, showing writing that looked like italic Korean-like circles and lines; after seeing writing samples from other cases he says Luigi's recreation is much closer *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:14:47-1:17:24)*. [[chris-ramsey]] links this to a witness of the Aztec case who saw a craft turn translucent under high voltage; Lazar says it looked like "a shower door", milky and blurry *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:22:54-1:23:56)*.
- Lazar says he once saw men in lab coats studying a chair with what he thinks was a mannequin to measure how big a creature would be, and denies seeing aliens there *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:12:05-1:13:32)*.

## Test flight and invisibility effect

- Once Dennis invited Lazar and Barry to see a test flight; the craft was out on the lake bed, lifted silently with a bluish-purple corona glow, and hovered *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:05:39-1:07:31)*.
- Dennis told Lazar to walk under it; as he approached, the craft "turned into sky" from light bending around it, and stepping back restored the view *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:07:31-1:08:11)*.
- The craft did not transfer weight to the ground: no wind, no sound, no crushing, "the weight is just gone" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:08:11-1:09:09)*.

## Comparisons

- [[billy-meier]]'s early photos match the sport model "down to the antenna", by Lazar's judgment *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:44:30-1:44:49, 1:51:59-1:52:11)*; [[phil-schneider]] also described a small "sport model" saucer at S-4.
- [[travis-walton]]'s craft reportedly had similar arches *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 1:43:26-1:44:14)*.
- [[chris-ramsey]] compares the transparent panel with a claimed Wright-Patterson witness of the [[aztec-new-mexico-crash-1948]] craft turning translucent under high voltage; Lazar says he does not follow such cases.
