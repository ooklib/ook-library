Nell and Pasulka argue that the esoteric versions of many religions describe similar initiatic encounters with non-human beings. These are interpretive claims by the speakers, not established scholarship, and Pasulka explicitly says she is not a New Testament scholar. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 31:40-32:06, 52:33-52:48)*

## Claims

- Nell: the Gnostic, Sufi, Kabbalistic and Advaita Vedanta traditions all describe inward, initiatic transcendence and connection with a deeper reality; Meister Eckhart is quoted "theologians may quarrel, but mystics all speak the same language"; he says Jewish kabbalists and the Dalai Lama once compared notes on non-human intelligences and their colors. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 31:57-34:08)*
- Nell: the Egyptian pyramid texts (fifth dynasty, about 2500 BC) are conventionally read as funerary, but a counter-scholarship reads them as initiatory (via the Sed festival kingship ritual), creating a direct line from Egypt to the Hermetic and Gnostic traditions and on to classical antiquity and early Christian theology. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 32:06-33:52)*
- Michels and Pasulka: Paul's conversion resembles merkabah mysticism (Ezekiel tradition); the seven heavenly palaces (hekhalot) parallel the seven Egyptian arits and a Rosicrucian ascent through seven levels, with good and bad beings testing the initiate; Pasulka says some New Testament scholars say in private that Jesus' cloud ascension fits this. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 52:33-53:38)*
- Pasulka: early Christianity is Hellenized Judaism, the scriptures are in Greek, and she suggests the Greek Orthodox Church knows the Socratic-to-Christian lineage. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 51:49-52:33)*
- Michels: the Last Supper may have been a mystery ritual (with a nod to Elaine Pagels) and religions have "outer ring" observers and mystics, with the four-rabbis parable as the danger of the mystical path. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 51:28-51:49, 55:31-56:25)*
- Pasulka: Paul changed his name after a spontaneous merkabah experience and built communities (ecclesia), a model for what a post-disclosure community might look like. *([jm/nell-pasulka](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-12-19 - The Biblical Truth of UFOs & Angels (Ft. Karl Nell & Diana Pasulka).md>) 53:47-55:24)*
