Admiral William A. Owens, former Vice Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, was a permanent
member of the [[special-access-program-oversight-committee|SAPOC]] post-restructuring. Outside
his naval career, served as senior military assistant to [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] and later
as CEO of [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]].
