Talon Gold was [[donald-c-winter|Donald C. Winter]]'s primary responsibility within the
[[strategic-defense-initiative|Strategic Defense Initiative (SDI)]]: "a project to develop a low
energy laser to demonstrate acquisition and tracking of targets from space and to precisely point
a narrow laser beam," serving as an essential prerequisite for deploying a high-energy laser
weapon system within SDI. *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md)
41:26–41:57)*

## Claims

- Metallurgical engineer Albert Bruce Collins mentioned Talon Gold and [[teal-ruby|Teal Ruby]] to
  the late Leonard Stringfield as programs oriented to track and possibly down UFOs during the
  1970s, and connected SDI to NRO satellites allegedly detecting UFOs as far back as the 1960s.
  *([northrop-trw](../raw/0kLSp0lH-JQ_northrop_trw.md) 41:57–42:20)*
