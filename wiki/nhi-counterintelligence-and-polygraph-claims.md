[[matthew-pines]] relays several unverified second-hand reports and reasons about non-human intelligence (NHI) as a possible counterintelligence problem. He says he has not had them confirmed.

## Claims

- Pines was told that the UAP Task Force's screening polygraph added a question about "any relationships or loyalty to any non-human faction"; he first thought it was a joke and says he has no evidence it is true. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:05:28-3:06:40)*
- He reasons that if human-NHI interaction exists, similar communication could exist with elements of the legacy program, which is more uncomfortable than random anomalous encounters such as Chris Bledsoe's, and that a senior US intelligence official having such an experience would be a different story. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:06:49-3:07:54)*
- Pines says he knows of an extremely senior intelligence official who had a "pretty gnarly abduction experience" along with his wife and may go public. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:08:22-3:08:35)*
- He argues disclosure's real threat is to the sanctity of the mind and sovereignty, and models it as a "more exotic flavor of a traditional counterintelligence issue": NHI would have their own tradecraft and infiltration, like Childhood's End. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:09:04-3:10:37)*
- Michels quotes Pines (opening clip) and Pines describes being told by DoD intelligence people that a craft "came out of the water medium and zapped" somebody, mentioned incidentally in an unrelated meeting "not infrequently"; Pines gives no details. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 0:00-0:09, 2:14:45-2:15:33)*
- Michels notes Jeremy Corbell and [[ross-coulthart]] hear from legacy-program contacts "if you knew what I know, you wouldn't be pro-disclosure", and Pines calls it an epistemic impasse. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 4:07:59-4:08:35)*
- On "agreements" with NHI, Pines calls the question a possible category error: he once dismissed the Eisenhower story about the grays collecting biological specimens, but now says "senior impressive people in the Navy" told him it might be real and that genetic collection recently ended, which worries them as a possible pre-cataclysm signal (see [[eisenhower-et-meeting-claims]]). *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:10:57-3:12:41)*
- Pines says Eisenhower's granddaughter "stands by that fully" but is into other quackery. *([jm/pines](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2024-11-15 - UFO Physics & Disclosure Under Trump (ft. Matthew Pines).md>) 3:11:47-3:11:58)*
