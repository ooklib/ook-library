The alleged January 1996 crash of a cigar-shaped craft near Varginha, as reconstructed by [[james-fox]] from [[carlos-daza]] and a nearby farm family.

## Claims (a52/fox)

- Fox names the farmers as Enrico and Oralini Freitas (spelling as transcribed). They and others say animals on their farm went crazy around 2:00-3:00 a.m.; they went outside and saw a cigar-shaped, aluminium-like craft about the size of a large school bus with a gash at the back trailing white vapour like a non-fire smoke, and the woman said she waited for it to hit the ground because it was struggling to stay in the air silently. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 27:09-27:50)*
- Ramsey and Fox note that these descriptions independently match Daza's "torn back" description. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 26:53-27:26)*
- New in the 2025 cut: Google maps and graphics placing where each witness stood relative to the crash site; Fox says it all lined up, which they could not have known in advance. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 27:50-28:35)*
- Fox says the crash was several days before the blockade in town; whether other crashes occurred that week is unknown to anyone he asked. *([a52/fox](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-01-09 - The Greatest Case of an Alien Encounter of our Time - James Fox _ DEBRIEFED ep. 70.md>) 34:31-35:23)*
