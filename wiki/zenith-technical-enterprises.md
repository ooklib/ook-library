Zenith Technical Enterprises, Inc. was, per [[eyes-wide-open]], the "nondescript firm" and CIA cutout that served as the corporate cover for [[jm-wave-station]] after the station moved in early 1962 from a block of houses in Coral Gables to an abandoned naval air base in Richmond, on Miami's southern outskirts. Everything below is the host's account.

## Claims

- The base had been leased by the Navy to the [[university-of-miami]] for research after World War II; in early 1962 the university "welcomed new tenants," Zenith, in reality a CIA cutout. The move "signaled the massive increase in agency covert activity on the Florida coast." *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 0:00-0:43)*
- [[ted-shackley]] entered the "Zenith Technical Enterprises lot" for the first time in February 1962. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 6:41-6:54)*
- Host quotes Joseph Trento (*Prelude to Terror*): [[paul-helliwell]] showed Shackley the importance of income from agency business fronts and helped make sure fronts "like Zenith Technical Enterprises were the perfect cover for JM Wave." The host calls Trento's sources unverifiable testimony but the link compelling. *([ewa/jmwave](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-11-07 - The CIA's Miami Station - JMWAVE.md) 20:04-20:28)*

## Relationships

- Front for [[jm-wave-station]]; see also [[cia-fronts-and-proprietaries]]. Located on the [[university-of-miami]]-leased former naval air base.
