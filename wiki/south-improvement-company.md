Secret 1872 alliance of the largest railroads (Pennsylvania, New York Central, Erie) and a select group of refiners including [[standard-oil]], described by MagnatesMedia as a scheme to shut out competing refiners. It was dissolved after about two months.

## Claims

- [[thomas-a-scott]] proposed it; in return for a secret 50% rebate Rockefeller shipped exclusively through the three railroads. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 18:01, 18:18, 18:39)*
- Standard was also to get "drawbacks", a 50% rebate on rivals' shipments, plus detailed data on competitors' shipments. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 18:53, 19:11)*
- Rumors of a cartel and a doubling of rates led to street protests, seized shipments and torn-up rails, and an oil-miners' boycott killed it two months after formation. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 20:03, 20:18, 20:29)*
- The narrator says Rockefeller sincerely believed it would save the industry from oversupply, while critics called it a power grab. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 19:32, 19:48)*

## Relationships

- Formed by [[standard-oil]] and [[thomas-a-scott]]'s Pennsylvania Railroad; its aftermath is the Cleveland Massacre described in [[standard-oil]].
