## Claims (from the "Secrecy" Vol. 1 video — founding narrative)

- Created August 1947 under the National Security Act, the NSC "originates from within the White
  House as part of the Executive Office" and is composed of senior national security advisers and
  cabinet officials. UAP Gerb argues Truman relegated the fledgling UFO legacy-program's control
  group to this newly formed body, carrying forward the Manhattan Project's
  [[top-policy-group|Top Policy Group]] model. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md)
  1:27:32–1:27:50)*
- Eisenhower deepened this arrangement on 15 March 1954 by establishing the
  [[nsc-5412-committee|5412 Committee ("Special Group")]] — formally defining "covert operations"
  and designating the CIA as executive agent for them — which UAP Gerb argues concealed the UFO
  control group even further than the original NSC structure, exploiting the fact that informal
  meetings among relevant NSC members (unlike full council sessions) are often held off the books
  and go unrecorded. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 1:32:53–1:38:04)*
- UAP Gerb believes an NSC element retains oversight of a singular UFO crash-retrieval portfolio
  to the present day, spanning DOE, the Air Force, and the CIA's Directorate of Science and
  Technology and select primes/FFRDCs — a role he argues the *Age of Disclosure* documentary
  understated to protect [[james-clapper|James Clapper]]. *([gerb/secrecy_1](../raw/gerb/secrecy_1.md) 1:34:34–1:35:04)*

- War Conspiracy 1: [[nsc-68]] (April 1950) is presented as a National Security Council memo by State Department policy planning director [[paul-nitze]] recommending rollback and permanently higher military spending. Unrelated to the UFO-thread NSC pages beyond the shared institution. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-1](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-29 - The Origins of the Military-Industrial Complex - War Conspiracy 1.md) 35:15-35:41)*
