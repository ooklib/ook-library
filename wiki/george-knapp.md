George Knapp, Las Vegas investigative journalist (KLAS-TV), has reported for decades on Area 51,
[[eg-and-g|EG&G]], and related classified-site UFO claims.

## Claims

- Circa 1990, while researching a follow-up investigation on Area 51/S4/EG&G, was tipped by a
  family member of a senior EG&G manager and eventually interviewed the late Alfred O'Donnell, a
  senior EG&G manager. O'Donnell claimed DoD/IC elements (inferrably including EG&G) possessed a
  flying saucer recovered from New Mexico, along with a live being that could not be
  communicated with; the disc was allegedly kept at Indian Springs Air Force Base (now Creech
  AFB) until 1955, then transferred to a facility adjacent to Area 51.
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:06:04–2:07:19)*
- After an EG&G co-founder arranged a meeting between Knapp, O'Donnell, and congressional
  staffer Dick D'Amato (who oversaw special access programs), O'Donnell's demeanor changed and
  he became tight-lipped. Near the end of his life, interviewed for Annie Jacobsen's *Area 51: An
  Uncensored History*, O'Donnell instead claimed Roswell was a Soviet craft carrying
  "grotesque child-sized avatars" from Joseph Mengele's human experiments — a story Gerb
  disbelieves, favoring O'Donnell's earlier non-human account. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:07:19–2:08:22, 2:43:10–2:43:36)*
- Published a 14 May 2020 article, "Strange object crashes in Needles; investigation leads to
  secretive government agency," on a 14 May 2008 object crash on the Colorado River near
  Needles, California, witnessed by ex-police officer Frank Costigan and others; identified
  responding "men in black"-type personnel and convoys as [[national-nuclear-security-
  administration|NNSA]] [[office-of-secure-transportation|Office of Secure Transportation]].
  *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 1:36:55–1:38:26)*
