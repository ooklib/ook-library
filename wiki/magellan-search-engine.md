Magellan was a mid-1990s web search engine built by [[christine-maxwell]] with [[isabelle-maxwell]] and David Hayden (ASR "Mellin"); the video calls it a contender in the early search-engine wars before Google, sold to Excite in 1996 for about $18 million. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies.
