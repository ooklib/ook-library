Time Life, run by publisher [[henry-luce]], is presented by Eyes Wide Open as a consistent
pro-Dulles media platform — providing favorable coverage of [[allen-dulles]]'s wartime and CIA
activities, employing [[whittaker-chambers]] (the main witness against [[alger-hiss]]), and later
controlling the Zapruder film.

## Claims

- [[henry-luce]] "could always be counted on to provide positive coverage" of [[allen-dulles]]'s
  OSS activities and future CIA operations; Time wrote a glowing article praising Dulles's
  [[operation-sunrise|Operation Sunrise]] and continued praising him throughout his CIA tenure.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 16:47-17:07)*
- [[whittaker-chambers]], the main witness against [[alger-hiss]] in the HUAC case, was Time's
  employee, which the host flags as "strangely coincidental" given the outlet's closeness to the
  Dulles brothers. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 18:15-18:35)*
- Time Life would later purchase the Zapruder film — the sole film documenting the JFK
  assassination — and hold it out of public view for five and a half years until 1969, which the
  host calls a likely "massive favor for parties that were allegedly involved" in the
  assassination, namely the CIA and the mob. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 17:47-18:15)*
