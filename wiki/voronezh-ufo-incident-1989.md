The Voronezh incident is a 27 September 1989 Soviet-era alleged extraterrestrial encounter that [[uap-gerb]] says he had never heard of before researching the iceberg. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 9:10-9:32)*

## Claims

- Gerb says boys playing football in a city park saw a pink glowing object and then a deep-red ball about 3 m across that vanished and reappeared, and claimed a roughly 10 ft tall three-eyed alien in bronze boots with a disc on its chest and a robot exited it; a police sergeant (name garbled) reportedly saw a "flying body". *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 9:24-10:22)*
- The being reportedly used a weapon to make a 16-year-old boy disappear before leaving. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 10:22-10:38)*
- Gerb suggests the Soviet media frenzy came from newly freed press under Gorbachev going tabloid; the Soviet scientific commission found no proof of a landing, radioactive cesium too weak to indicate one, and no abnormal microorganisms or spectrochemical results. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 10:38-11:37)*
- His verdict: an interesting-looking ET but he doubts the case's validity. *([gerb/iceberg-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-16 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 2).md>) 11:37-11:44)*
