The Babalon Working (rendered "Babylon working" by the transcript) was a 1946 ritual series conducted by [[jack-parsons]] with [[l-ron-hubbard]] in Pasadena, described by [[jesse-michels]] as an attempt to summon a divine feminine archetype, inspired by Aleister Crowley's novel *Moonchild*, aimed at a symbolic "magical child" and self-transformation.

## Claims

- Hubbard then allegedly left with Parsons's girlfriend and money; Parsons's magical counterattack invoked Bartzabel in a Miami hotel room, and Hubbard's yacht hit a storm and a court made him repay Parsons. *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 18:52-20:52)*
- Michels asks whether the working "had actually succeeded, if it had opened something up in 1946" because UAP reports subsequently "skyrocketed" (speculation; compare [[golden-era-1947-1978]]). *([jm/nasa-pagan](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-09-20 - NASA & UFOs - Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel.md>) 20:52-21:08)*
