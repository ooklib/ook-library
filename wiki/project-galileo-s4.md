Project Galileo is, per [[bob-lazar]], the S4 project dealing with the craft's power and propulsion system, where staff "talked about reverse engineering extraterrestrial equipment"; it was the one he was assigned to, working with [[barry-s4-lab-partner]] *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 41:34-41:54, 46:32-46:40)*. Lazar says it "became real" when Barry demonstrated the reactor's force field and gravity emitter ([[lazar-gravity-amplifier-lab-claims]]) *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 55:45-56:02)*. He notes that everything he later verified hands-on about Galileo matched what the briefing binders said, which slightly raises the credence of the rest, while reminding himself the binders may include deliberate falsehoods *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 50:53-51:28)*. Other groups allegedly worked on materials and metallurgy *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 58:14-58:24)*.

The same trio of projects is described on [[lazar-briefing-projects-trio]]; its siblings are [[project-sidekick-s4]] and [[project-looking-glass-s4]]. Do not confuse with unrelated uses of "Galileo" elsewhere in the wiki.
