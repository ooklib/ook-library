The Sovereign Military Order of Malta, a Vatican-linked order with sovereign-state status. In the Malmgren interview it is put forward, as speculation, as a hidden guardian of UFO secrecy. This wiki records the claims only.

## Claims

- [[harald-malmgren]]: the relationship between [[james-jesus-angleton]]'s father Hugh and the Knights "needs to be uncovered"; the Knights, tied to the Vatican and holding an international passport-issuing status ("a sovereign state for a handful of people"), are involved in the UFO story; he does not know what form the residue takes today and says he is speculating. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 1:35:09-1:36:01, 1:42:16-1:43:10, 1:49:47-1:50:27)*
- Host list of alleged Knights: Hugh and [[james-jesus-angleton]], [[philip-j-corso]] (also liaison to a future pope), [[douglas-macarthur]] and his intelligence staff, [[william-donovan]], [[john-mccone]], [[william-casey]], FDR's Vatican envoy Myron Taylor; also, per Seymour Hersh as cited by the host, JSOC head Stanley McChrystal and many JSOC members. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 1:39:13-1:41:59)*
- Host: Malmgren's Knights thread connects to the alleged 1933 [[magenta-italy-crash-1933]] retrieval, Pius XII's back-channel and OSS operation McGregor. *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 1:36:01-1:38:55)*
- Malmgren: many top business leaders and politicians are involved with the Knights; "they still have the sovereign state identity". *([jm/malmgren](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-04-22 - Presidential Advisor - I Directly Handled UFO Material (Ft. Harald Malmgren).md>) 1:50:22-1:50:44)*
