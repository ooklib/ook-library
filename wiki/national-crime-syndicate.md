The National Crime Syndicate, per Eyes Wide Open, was the nationwide organized-crime structure
[[meyer-lansky]] built by uniting the Jewish, Italian, and Irish mobs, with [[lucky-luciano]]
among its other bosses.

## Claims

- United by [[meyer-lansky]], who became "the mob's accountant" and the mastermind behind its
  sprawling criminal social network across the US. [[lucky-luciano]] is described as "one of the
  other bosses in this national crime syndicate." *([ewa/mafia-allies](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-20 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Mafia Allies and the Beginning of the Global Drug Connection [pt. 3].md) 22:47-23:53)*

## Relationships

- Founded/led financially by [[meyer-lansky]]; [[lucky-luciano]] a fellow boss.

## Additional claims (War Conspiracy 2)


- Host: Castro's 1959 nationalization propelled the "formal marriage" between the syndicate and the CIA; see [[carlos-marcello]], [[meyer-lansky]], [[santo-trafficante]]. *([ewa/war-conspiracy-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-06 - Eisenhower, Bananas, and the CIA Imperium - War Conspiracy 2.md) 48:47-49:20)*

## Additional claims (CIA-Mafia Legend)

- **Host's definitional argument:** "La Cosa Nostra" (Sicilian, 1930s usage) then "Mafia" (Italian) are limited, ethnically Italian frames; the host says these terms shielded the Jewish and Irish business interests (Canadian liquor distributors such as Seagram's/Bronfman and Schenley/Rosenstiel; ASR "Shenley/Seagrams", "Rosen steel/bronfman") that profited from Prohibition and directed attention away from the multiethnic national crime syndicate that emerged in the late WWII years; he ties this to [[j-edgar-hoover]] refusing to acknowledge organized crime until 1961 and to Whitney Webb's phrase about laundering "dirty money into seemingly clean investment capital". *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 35:46-46:26)* The host calls the concept "disputed by reputed sources as a conspiracy theory of sorts". *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 44:44-45:13)*
- Host's rough three-node model (self-described "cheat sheet", his own research): (1) New York's five families (Bonanno, Colombo, Gambino, Genovese, Lucchese; ASR variants) with ties to Montreal's Cotroni family for French Connection dope; (2) the Chicago outfit under [[sam-giancana]], expanded to the Southwest via wire services; (3) the Florida mob around [[meyer-lansky]] in Miami, with [[carlos-marcello]] of New Orleans (also close to the Texas mob) and [[santo-trafficante]] of Tampa; neutral turf = pre-revolutionary Cuba and Las Vegas. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 49:00-51:37)*
- Host (citing Peter Dale Scott): gambling via the horse-racing wire service expanded local mobs nationally; wire-service wars in the 1930s; [[jimmy-hoffa]]'s Teamsters supplied a national network of union offices, cover jobs, trucking logistics and a pension fund "slush fund"; Hoffa truckers allegedly delivered guns to mob- and CIA-backed guerrillas in Florida bound for Cuba. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 51:53-55:25)*
- Host: the syndicate acts as a "subterfuge appendage" and "slush fund and enforcement arm" for the legitimate Overworld; [[robert-f-kennedy]]'s crusade therefore threatened Overworld figures too; "emissaries" like [[robert-maheu]] and [[johnny-roselli]] bridge the worlds. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 36:16-36:42, 48:46-49:00, 55:42-56:57)*
- Host: after Castro, Havana losses and the mob's alliance with the CIA against him; Las Vegas became the replacement and "massive money laundry". *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 33:32-33:48, 34:55-35:22)* See [[cia-mafia-assassination-plots]].
