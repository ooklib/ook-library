J. Edgar Hoover was the first, long-time director of the
[[federal-bureau-of-investigation|FBI]], noted by Eyes Wide Open as leading the agency that
handled US domestic intelligence before and after the creation of the
[[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]], and as lobbying (unsuccessfully) for the FBI to take over
US foreign intelligence functions during the run-up to the CIA's creation.

## Claims

- Led the FBI as its long-time and first director; the FBI handled domestic intelligence (as it
  still does), while foreign intelligence before WWII was largely handled by the State
  Department and Department of Defense. *([ewa/oss](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-03-23 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The OSS and the Birth of American Intelligence [Pt. 1].md) 6:06-6:19)*
- Proposed the FBI take over America's new postwar foreign-spying functions, leveraging the
  FBI's existing domestic intelligence network built spying on and subverting leftist political
  action in the US. Eyes Wide Open alleges Hoover "actively sabotaged" the creation of a civilian
  intelligence agency by doctoring a report delivered to Truman that portrayed OSS wartime
  activities as largely ineffective — a report the host concedes wasn't factually wrong, but
  frames as a self-interested attempt to win Hoover ultimate control of US intelligence rather
  than a "noble" act. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 15:37-16:20)*
- Eyes Wide Open flags that Hoover would go on to have a "mixed relationship" with the CIA,
  previewing a later episode's coverage of a "shadowy blackmail network" allegedly tying the FBI
  and CIA together. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 16:20-16:31)*
- In January 1946, gave a proclamation to a Catholic youth group in New York City (accompanied
  on stage by anti-communist Cardinal [[francis-spellman]]) claiming there were "only 100,000
  Communists who are organized and articulate but they are motivated by fanatical frenzy." Hoover
  went on to become a key instigator and prosecutor of domestic communists, representing (per the
  host) "the tip of the spear for domestic repression of leftist organizations." Hoover and
  Spellman were two of the four alleged [[blue-suite]] party guests reported by
  [[susan-kaufman]], showing (per the host) "a long enduring relationship" between them.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 6:48-8:43)*
- Per journalist Anthony Summers (citing William Sullivan, 1960s head of FBI domestic
  intelligence), secretly fed [[joseph-mccarthy]] dozens of names of suspected subversives, using
  McCarthy as a public-facing front for a political crusade Hoover had no authority to undertake
  himself as ostensibly neutral FBI director. Per Peter Dale Scott, Hoover and
  [[lewis-rosenstiel]] together effectively created McCarthy's entire campaign, routing subversive
  lists through US Army intelligence and Rosenstiel's Jewish League Against Communism front group.
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 23:06-24:16)*
- Remained a close ally of [[lewis-rosenstiel]], who likely acted as Hoover's liaison/middleman to
  mob boss [[meyer-lansky]]. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 25:25-25:36)*
- Per Whitney Webb's transcription of an alleged first-ever correspondence with [[roy-cohn]] —
  strikingly informal for a first contact between a federal police chief and a Senate counsel —
  Hoover effectively created Cohn's career, getting him his Justice Department job via Hoover
  contacts and personally recommending him to [[joseph-mccarthy]]. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 31:02-33:13)*
- Widely speculated (by historians) to have been the lifelong companion/lover of his long-time
  deputy [[clyde-tolson]]; as early as 1946, sources allege [[james-jesus-angleton]] and
  [[meyer-lansky]] separately possessed compromising photographs of Hoover receiving oral sex from
  Tolson. Allen Dulles's mistress Mary Bancroft allegedly referred to Hoover as "that Virgin Mary
  in pants," using the same female alter-name ("Mary Hoover") that [[susan-kaufman]] separately
  reported hearing at the [[blue-suite]] parties, where she alleged witnessing Hoover in drag.
  Eyes Wide Open frames this alleged CIA/Mafia leverage over the FBI's director as evidence the
  CIA and mob had shared interests in the continued free flow of narcotics and organized-crime
  activity, with the FBI allegedly looking the other way as a result. *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 36:21-38:44, 39:33-39:57)*
- Alleged (per [[susan-kaufman]]) to have attended the [[blue-suite]] parties at the Plaza Hotel
  alongside [[francis-spellman]] and [[roy-cohn]], hosted by [[lewis-rosenstiel]].
  *([ewa/red-scare](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-06-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Red Scare, Blackmail, and the Blue Suite [pt. 5.2].md) 0:50-1:21)*
