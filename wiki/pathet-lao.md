The leftist Lao faction, led by the "Red Prince" Souphanouvong, which the host (per [[eyes-wide-open]]) describes as the Lao Communist Party / Lao Patriotic Front, founded in the mid-1940s and always reliant on Vietnam. ASR variants: "patet Lao", "potet Lao", "PED Lao". *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 13:33-14:00)*

## Claims

- Nominally fought with the Viet Minh in the [[first-indochina-war]] but spent the later years building a power base in Sam Neua and Phongsaly. Just before Geneva the Viet Minh formed "Group 100" to make the Pathet Lao self-sufficient militarily and politically (quoting Frederick Benson). *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 14:05-14:57)*
- Between 1954 and 1959 it competed in elections; it won 9 of 21 seats in the May 1958 special election, alarming Washington. After being banned in 1958 its fighters scattered back to the countryside and the northern provinces were once again taken by the leftist paramilitary. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 15:03-15:41, 33:47-34:23, 36:42-37:01)*
- Host says its return to arms in the late 1950s and the Ho Chi Minh Trail activity followed CIA pressure on the neutralist government; if it prevailed in the opium-growing north it would hit the finances of the pro-US regimes in Bangkok, Saigon and Vientiane. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 15:20-15:41, 54:21-54:47)*
- Host quotes the 1960 Sam Neua result (13 votes for the Pathet Lao against 6,108) as evidence the April 1960 election was rigged; see [[laos-coups-1958-1960]]. *([ewa/laos-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-22 - The Paramilitary CIA and the Seeds of a Secret War - LAOS 4.md) 44:33-44:59)*
