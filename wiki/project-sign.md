Project Sign was the first US Air Force UFO investigation project (1948), the predecessor of Project Grudge and [[project-blue-book]]. This page so far records only what [[uap-gerb]] says about it in [[gerb-air-force-encounters-video]].

## Claims (gerb/air-force-encounters)

- Gerb says Sign was created because Lt. Gen. [[nathan-twining]] asserted, in a letter to Air Force commanding general George Schulgen, that flying discs were "real and not visionary or fictitious", and that all Sign reports were required to go to the Army and Navy Research and Development Board, the USAF Scientific Advisory Board and the [[atomic-energy-commission]]. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 4:41-5:13)*
- Sign investigated the [[gorman-dogfight-1948]], interviewing Gorman and witnesses and checking his P-51 for radiation, then rolled out prosaic explanations. *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 5:13-5:59)*
- Gerb notes some cases in the video were found in official Project Sign or [[project-moon-dust]] documents (per the video description). *([gerb/air-force-encounters](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-01 - Global Air Force UFO Encounters You've Probably Never Heard of.md>) 0:00-0:47)*

## Relationships

- Related to [[project-blue-book]] (successor lineage via Grudge, per [[edward-j-ruppelt]] directing Sign, Grudge and Blue Book).
- Investigated the [[gorman-dogfight-1948]].

## Claims (gerb/stigma)

- Gerb says it began in 1948 and ran to 1949, established by Gen. [[nathan-twining]], initially named Project Saucer because Twining asserted flying discs are "real and not visionary or fictitious" (letter to AAF Commanding General George Schulgen, spelled "lugan" in ASR); its report said some UFO reports present actual objects with undeterminable origins, and [[edward-j-ruppelt]] claimed it endorsed the interplanetary explanation for some cases. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 2:53-3:51)*
- All sign reports were required to go to the Army and Navy Research and Development Board, the USAF Scientific Advisory Group and the [[atomic-energy-commission]], which Gerb connects to his thesis that UFO investigations trace to the AEC. Gen. [[hoyt-vandenberg]] shut it down in 1949 for "lack of proof", and [[project-grudge]] was commissioned the same year. *([gerb/stigma](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-02-09 - The Origin of the UFO Stigma.md>) 3:51-4:32)*
