A roughly 2013 (Buhler: "maybe 10 or 12 years ago") night-time encounter on Cocoa Beach, Florida, reported by [[charles-buhler]] and his wife: a bright light that exploded, then split into rotating pinkish-orange lights that approached them.

## Claims

- About 9:30-10 pm, a red beacon about 3 miles offshore brightened until it exploded into a light so bright it lit the beach from Cape Canaveral southward; about 5-10 minutes later a helicopter from Patrick Air Force Base hovered over it and returned without action. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:15:09-1:17:28)*
- The light approached, split into six or seven rotating orange-pink lights "like bicycle spokes", going under water and re-emerging, coming to about 50 yards, mirroring the couple's movements; they walked away for about 12 blocks over 40 minutes. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:17:28-1:19:56)*
- Buhler says such lights are common there and follow cruise ships; friends saw them 7-8 years later; [[steven-greer]] takes groups to a beach about 40 minutes south. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:20:24-1:21:00)* He does not link it to his propulsion work. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:21:47-1:22:01)*
- Michels notes [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s teenage Catalina orb encounter as a parallel; Buhler wishes he had received a "download". *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 1:22:09-1:23:17)*
