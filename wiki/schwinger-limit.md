The Schwinger limit is an established concept in quantum electrodynamics — the field strength at which the quantum vacuum becomes unstable to electron-positron pair production. In [[pais-michels-interview-2025]], [[salvatore-pais]] and the host use it as the physical threshold that his propulsion concept is meant to approach or exceed; this speculative extension (that exceeding it forms a black hole/wormhole usable for propulsion) is Pais's own claim, not a mainstream physics conclusion.

## Claims (jm/pais)

- Described by Pais as representing how much energy density the "quantum vacuum" or spacetime fabric can withstand before it "breaks apart"; he cites values on the order of 10^33 W/m^2, corresponding to an energy density of ~10^25 J/m^3, with electric field strength ~10^18 V/m and magnetic field ~10^9 V/m equivalent. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 31:00-31:26)*
- Says that once this limit is exceeded ("breaking the Schwinger"/forming a black hole), a paired white hole and wormhole form between the locality where the fabric broke and "somewhere else that it talks to" — his proposed propulsion mechanism, explicitly framed as not requiring or implying faster-than-light travel, which he says is impossible under Einsteinian physics. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 32:00-32:32, 51:33-52:07)*
- Says this generation-of-particle-antiparticle-pairs mechanism is exactly "what the Schwinger limit needs," tying it to work citing Max Born and Paul Dirac. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 30:21-30:53)*
- Host and co-host connect Pais's framing to Alcubierre-drive/negative-energy concepts, noting the two approaches (quantum-vacuum-energy and Alcubierre) might describe the same underlying physics from different paradigms, though Alcubierre stays within general relativity while Pais's approach requires "new physics." *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 52:55-53:48)*
- Michels analogizes Pais's black-hole/wormhole propulsion claim to [[bob-lazar]]'s account of a "sport model" craft appearing "3D printed" out of spacetime, and to [[ken-shoulders]]'s exotic-vacuum-object (EVO) work, which Pais says requires a fifth-dimensional space. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 15:52-16:44, 1:09:54-1:10:20)*
