The "Binh Xuyen river pirates" (ASR: "binu Yen", "benu Yen", "bzu Yen"; normalization is my inference), a Saigon-area organized-crime syndicate. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 18:50-18:58)*

## Claims

- Bought the Hmong's raw opium once the legitimate colonial trade went underground. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 18:43-18:58)*
- After the French formally closed the opium monopoly and dens (1946, under international pressure), the dens were renamed "detoxification clinics" and stayed with Corsican and Vietnamese organized crime; the Binh Xuyen ran them, two boiling plants, and a quasi-militia around Saigon. *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 23:22-24:41)*
- [[antoine-savani]] allied French intelligence with them for an $85,000-a-year stipend. The host says the militarized pirates would challenge [[ngo-dinh-diem]]'s authority almost as soon as he became president (deferred to the next episode). *([ewa/laos-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-08 - Operation X and the French Indochina War - LAOS 2.md) 24:41-25:19)*

- LAOS 3: put in charge of Saigon-area policing by Bao Dai in the war's last years, they joined the Cao Dai as the armed "sects" that fought [[ngo-dinh-diem]] in 1955; Diem's forces, advised by [[edward-lansdale]] with Filipino forces, cleared them from Saigon in the week-long [[battle-of-saigon-1955]]. The host says Laos opium exports dropped 1954-58 as Diem cracked down on the Binh Xuyen-controlled opium dens. *([ewa/laos-3](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-11-15 - The French Connection in South East Asia - LAOS 3.md) 32:33-33:12, 35:19-35:35, 47:17-47:33)*
