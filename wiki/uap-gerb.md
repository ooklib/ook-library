UAP Gerb is a YouTube creator/investigator who self-describes as spending years "unmasking"
and mapping the alleged US "UFO legacy program" apparatus from 1947 to today, with heavy focus
on [[ffrdc|FFRDCs]] as the "missing link" in the covert UFO retrieval/exploitation ecosystem.

## Claims about self / methodology

- States his mission is to "meticulously map the legacy programs and their evolution from 1947
  to today," operating as "investigator, unmasker, and historian." *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 2:24–2:53)*
- Claims to have spent nearly 7 hours across a prior two-part series specifically investigating
  the MITRE Corporation before this video. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 9:14–9:28)*
- Says his MITRE-related investigation has been ongoing "for about 2 years," during which he
  took testimony from several unnamed MITRE engineers and program managers, whom he says he
  spent years vetting, including travel to the South Pacific to meet a source. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 49:05–50:13)*
- States he will not name the individual engineers briefed on the program, distinguishing them
  ("patriots") from named "gatekeepers" like [[james-clapper]], [[donald-kerr]], and others,
  though he says he will provide their names to congressional elements. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 59:58–1:00:26)*
- Explicitly frames his claims about specific reverse-engineered technologies as intentionally
  vague, citing personal national-security concerns. *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 13:22–13:32, 1:02:55–1:03:14)*
- Reports upcoming media appearances on NewsNation and The Hill around this video's release.
  *([gerb/mitre](../raw/gerb/mitre.md) 1:58:37–1:58:53)*
