The CIA-built deep-sea recovery ship launched in 1972 to raise the sunken Soviet submarine K-129 under Project Azorian (Project Jennifer), which Gerb treats as a creation of [[national-underwater-reconnaissance-office]]. This page records Gerb's claims that it was also used for UFO recovery, none of them verified.

## Claims (gerb/navy)

- The ship carried a modern price tag of about 1.68 billion dollars. Gerb credits a Medium author for an article exploring its involvement in hidden programs beyond K-129. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 25:34-26:12)*
- In 1978 Ocean Minerals Company, whose prime contractor was Lockheed Missiles and Space Company, leased it for a "deep sea mining" operation, and Gerb argues it was used by [[lockheed-martin]] for nearly two decades; he asks whether Lockheed, NURO and the Glomar also took part in undersea UFO retrievals. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 26:12-26:53)*
- [[bobby-ray-inman]] said NURO had engaged before his time in the effort to pick up a submarine with the Glomar, and that he had been exposed to the program while in Hawaii. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 30:48-31:26)*
- The alleged 1973 sailor's account says a UFO shot down between Hawaii and the mainland was retrieved by the Glomar Explorer, shipped to Hawaii and then stateside, ending at Great Lakes; see [[great-lakes-naval-station-craft-1973]]. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:18:56-1:19:56)*
- Gerb notes the Glomar was publicly known only for K-129 when [[leonard-stringfield]] published the Great Lakes story in the 1980s. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:43:16-1:43:33)*
