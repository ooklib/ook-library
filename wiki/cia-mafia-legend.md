"The CIA-Mafia Legend" is [[eyes-wide-open]]'s framing, in his December 2024 episode, of the popular narrative around the [[cia-mafia-assassination-plots]]. He uses "legend" in the intelligence-trade sense of a cover story built to hide an asset's true identity and purpose, and argues the mainstream account of a blundering agency is such a legend. This is his thesis, explicitly labelled as speculation ("the one that I'm positing").

## The argument

- The "legend": the CIA is a well-meaning but bumbling bureaucracy that floundered under its own plots; the host names Tim Weiner's *Legacy of Ashes* as an exemplar (ASR "Tim winer"; normalization mine). Evidence offered: the wacky, filmic schemes (poison cigars, exploding conch shell, LSD microphone, depilatory), the claimed ~600 failed attempts, and the fact that Maheu's firm reportedly inspired *Mission Impossible*. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:10:06-1:13:47)*
- Why it is convenient: it lets the CIA (which the host says overthrew "half a dozen legitimate governments" in the 1950s and was ruthlessly effective at assassinations in the 1960s-70s) look as a failing "Mission Impossible" outfit, especially as the agency and allies turned on Kennedy after the Bay of Pigs failure. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:13:00-1:14:18, 1:19:57-1:20:19)*
- Two theories about why the plots "failed": (1) Trafficante as double agent who kept Castro informed; (2) the Assassination Legend Theory: the CIA invented the far-fetched plots as a cover for bringing top mobsters into the agency as assets, with Trafficante "playing his side of the ruse" by telling wild stories. Support: Roselli's 1981-reported remark (via Fratianno) that it was "a scam", plus the Scott-quoted protection motive. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:14:27-1:19:57)*
- Timing: the shift of Bay of Pigs planning from an exile guerrilla campaign to a conventional amphibious assault, the assassination plots, the 1960 election and early 1961 all coincide; the host says "it's almost as if the agency was reorienting their plans towards failure" that would land "squarely on the young president's lap" and enable plots against Kennedy. *([ewa/cia-mafia](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2024-12-20 - The CIA-Mafia Legend.md) 1:22:53-1:23:44)* Flagged: this is the host's suggestive framing; he offers no document for it.
