In [[sentinels-of-ether-michels-episode-2026]] [[jake-barber]]'s claims that black aerospace programs recruit, drug and wire psychic "assets" to summon and fly UFOs are assembled by [[jesse-michels]] into a three-layer stack: brain-machine interfaces, craft that need real-time field control, and psionic humans. The stack is Michels' argument by documented R&D lineage; he says "I'm not saying that we have a verified Salvatore Pais saucer in a hangar synced to a psionic asset" (*([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:21:41-1:21:57)*). It complements the P3 and breeding claims in [[psionic-assets-and-breeding-claims]].

## Barber's claims (as relayed)

- P3 = "psionic predisposition potential"; recruits are "intuitives" trained in remote viewing, telekinesis and tapping the "consciousness field", the P3 being "the Wi-Fi antenna"; Barber sees it as elite recruitment rather than trafficking. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 59:11-1:00:30)*
- Two attraction methods: machine-based "dog whistle" signals that make UAP "come to us day after day", and man-based psionics projecting an invitation; children are naturally better, and psychic kids are allegedly tested at DOE national labs ([[department-of-energy]]). *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:00:30-1:01:08)*
- Prospecting for psionic assets happens abroad because people from simpler lives are supposedly better at "disassociating and heightening their intuition"; natural disasters, unaccompanied-minor caravans and the Ukraine war are named as cover. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 42:07-43:24)*
- Barber recounts flying a retrieved craft when overwhelmed by "a feminine energy that was loving and sad", possibly a psionic asset's consciousness or the craft's own. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:05:08-1:06:07)*
- Per leaks passed via [[michael-herrera]] (relayed to a pseudonymous researcher) from an insider Michels assumes is Barber: assets are chipped with a tracking device and given heavy drugs to stay calm, many die or slip into a coma after years, and the informal term is "pink assets". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:06:07-1:07:14)* Michels notes programs may differ and some may be more ethical.
- A non-psionic pilot cannot fly an ET craft; a psionic asset is connected via an advanced brain machine interface so a pilot's intent is converted to neural patterns the craft reads, making the asset "a psychic joystick" with "a pilot behind" them. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 1:07:22-1:08:44)*
