Debate, on Coulthart's "Reality Check", over why anyone would fabricate or plant the [[majestic-documents]]. Coulthart's stated question is "if they're a hoax, for what purpose?" *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 24:29-25:50)*

## Official position (as recited by Coulthart)

- 1988 FBI consulted the USAF OSI, was told no such committee existed and called the document "completely bogus"; USAF and ISOO found no records; the [[cutler-twining-memo]] was declared a forgery; a GAO investigation "confirmed the hoax" (compare [[gao-roswell-report-1995]]); the National Archives, Truman and Eisenhower libraries and the NSC found nothing. Coulthart says he accepted the denials until listener requests. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 3:44-6:28)*

## Arguments

- Cruickshank: the source was a disgruntled CIA officer ([[scotty-miller]]); in 1984 the USSR existed but in 1994 (the Miller/Cooper documents) it did not, so the only target would be US citizens, forbidden to CIA; "majestic derangement syndrome" among online critics, many of them bots. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 25:55-27:38, 31:06-31:41)*
- Davis: a counterintelligence purpose could be leak-tracing (see who routes what to whom, 1985 USSR still there); after MK-Ultra and FOIA, a custodian expecting leaks would "make the whole thing radioactive" by seeding a bad document, the [[cutler-twining-memo]]. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 47:38-48:09, 1:10:51-1:11:39)*
- Coulthart: a controlled disinformation operation makes little sense given detail consistent with the declassified record; a rogue staffer's leak fits better. Someone "deep in the intelligence community" is presumably frustrated as more matches surface. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 30:08-30:49, 1:08:21-1:08:53, 1:18:11-1:18:43)*
- The Angleton-CI-foil theory is raised by Coulthart and treated skeptically. *([rc/mj12](<../raw/rc/NewsNation - 2026-04-14 - A secret history - Inside shadow intelligence, the MJ-12 documents  Reality Check.md>) 46:59-47:30)*
