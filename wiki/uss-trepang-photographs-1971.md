A series of controversial images said to have been taken in March 1971 from the submarine USS Trepang (SSN-674) between Iceland and Jan Mayen Island, purporting to show a triangle and a cigar-shaped craft, possibly also an egg-shaped one. [[uap-gerb]] reports the debate and is himself undecided. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 21:10-21:28,)*

## Claims

- The images first appeared in the French magazine *Top Secret*, and original scans were sent to investigator Alex Mrea (spelling as transcribed); the boat, under Admiral Dean Ransacket (as transcribed, "Sackett"), allegedly came upon the objects by accident and officer John Clicka first spotted them by periscope. Some images show hovering, entering or leaving the water, others the objects apparently in distress. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 21:44-22:18)*
- Explanations debated: hoax, real, test barrage balloons, or a Fata Morgana mirage. Gerb cannot find whether attack submarines carry balloons, only a 1955 joint USN-CIA document on balloon launches from submarines, and no live-fire records, which casts doubt on the balloon theory. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 21:28-22:59)*
- Investigator and Navy pilot Steve Mario (spelling as transcribed) spoke with the admiral, who steered away from UFO questions and said he and no one else saw anything unusual, only ice; Clicka said he found the investigation interesting and did not know what the pictures represented. Gerb says this kills the training-range story for him, leaving a hoax or the real deal with witnesses bound by oaths or unaware. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 22:59-23:42)*
- One of the best images shows signs of tampering (credit to Wim van Utrecht); Gerb agrees with John Greenwald ([[black-vault]]) that publications enhance photographs for print, so an altered magazine image does not discredit the others. *([gerb/uso](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-04-17 - USO - Unidentified Submerged Objects.md>) 23:42-24:28)*
