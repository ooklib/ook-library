The Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering (USD R&E) is, per Gerb, "the
evolution of [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]]'s Research and Development Board (RDB)," and one of
three Office of the Secretary of Defense elements (alongside
[[under-secretary-of-defense-for-acquisition-and-sustainment|USD A&S]] and
[[under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-and-security|USD I&S]]) Gerb repeatedly names as
imperative to UFO legacy program operations. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
58:57–59:31, 1:04:03–1:04:44)*

## Claims

- Per DoDI 5205.11, USD R&E "identifies and cultivates cutting-edge technology development,
  technology transition, developmental prototyping, experimentation, and developmental testing
  activities and programs requiring SAP protection to ensure continued US warfighter advantage."
  Gerb highlights that USD R&E's stated mission overlaps almost exactly with what would be
  required to run covert UFO reverse-engineering RDT&E. *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md)
  1:04:03–1:04:44, 1:53:03–1:53:34)*
- [[darpa|DARPA]] reports through USD R&E; Gerb names [[peter-highnam|Dr. Peter Highnam]] —
  principal deputy assistant secretary of defense for critical technologies within USD R&E, and
  former deputy/acting director of DARPA (2018–2022) — as a "legacy program senior" occupying the
  administrative line of accountability connecting USD R&E, DARPA, and
  [[darpa-security-and-intelligence-directorate|DARPA's Security and Intelligence Directorate]].
  *([gerb/secrecy_2](../raw/gerb/secrecy_2.md) 1:52:44–1:53:51)*

  [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-acquisition-and-sustainment]] and
  [[under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-and-security]]) Gerb calls central to legacy
