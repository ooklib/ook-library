An elite Air Force/National Reconnaissance Organization special-forces unit, per
[[colonel-steve-wilson|Colonel Steve Wilson]]'s claims, that retrieved downed UFOs with
elements of JSOC/Delta Force. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 12:49–12:56)*

## Claims

- Can allegedly be observed referenced in the disputed Majestic 12 "Project Aquarius" document
  and, oddly, the 1953 [[robertson-panel-1953|Robertson Panel]] report.
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 13:04–13:11)*
- Gerb traces the "Pounce" name to Air Force Brig. Gen. [[william-a-matan|William A. Matan]]'s
  1952 proposal for a dedicated F-94C interceptor unit to photograph UFOs over Albuquerque/
  Kirtland AFB — a proposal shelved for lack of spare aircraft, not credibility. He theorizes this
  IMINT-tasking concept was later revived as an NRO-era retrieval task force.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:41:10–1:43:10)*
- Per [[colonel-steve-wilson|Wilson]], Pounce's "Delta" NRO detachment deployed a truck-mounted,
  6 ft EMP "equalizer" weapon (allegedly built by [[e-systems|LTV Electrosystems]]) to shoot down
  UFOs, and Teledyne Ryan (later Northrop Grumman) built an anti-gravity helicopter, the
  "XH-7D"/"XH Shark," assigned to Pounce's Delta team. Gerb finds no corroborating evidence for
  either claim. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:32:11–1:34:37, 2:00:43–2:01:07)*
- Gerb notes an anonymous firsthand witness's account of an NRO-run Nevada-based crash-retrieval
  rapid-reaction team, and a separate 2024 Reddit "ex-OGA" account of a siloed tier-1 task force,
  both structurally resemble Wilson's Pounce/Delta claims — leading Gerb to conclude the NRO is
  likely the umbrella authority behind such task forces. See
  [[nevada-national-security-site]]. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 1:28:02–1:28:27)*
