Naval Support Activity Crane, Indiana, hosts [[naval-surface-warfare-center-crane|NSWC Crane]]
and the US Army Ammunition Activity (stockpiling much of the US mainland's munitions). Chosen
during WWII for its landlocked, hard-to-target location.
