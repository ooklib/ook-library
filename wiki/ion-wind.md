Ion wind (electrohydrodynamic thrust) is the conventional explanation for lifter and asymmetric-capacitor thrust: corona ionizes air, the ions collide with neutral molecules, and momentum is conserved through the air. It is the "first-order debunk" of the [[biefeld-brown-effect]] and of [[exodus-force-claims]].

## Claims

- [[jesse-michels]]: skeptics dismiss capacitor experiments as ionized air; Brown allegedly used EHD ion-wind effects as cover for electrogravitics because they are "95% similar but not the same thing". *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 47:11-47:41, 49:26-49:57)*
- [[charles-buhler]]: ion wind devices move with the exhaust like a rocket; when he puts a lifter in a sealed plastic box on a scale the weight "flat lines" (momentum conserved), while his force still shows on the scale in an enclosure; he says he can tell the two apart. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 48:03-48:57)*
- Vacuum removes ion wind and gives more thrust, contradicting the ion-wind account; Brown also did oil-immersion tests, and Musha's oil test is cited. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 52:26-53:25, 1:50:58-1:51:31)*
- Chester's residual worries: ions or electrons emitted by field emission even in vacuum could carry momentum. *([jm/nasa-chief](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-03-30 - NASA Chief - We Just Built Antigravity Propulsion!.md>) 2:22:12-2:23:40)*
