Musician who founded [[to-the-stars-academy|To the Stars Academy]], publicly launched on Joe
Rogan Experience episode 1029 (26 October 2017). ASR-transcribed as "Tom Dong" in this source.
*([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 49:25–49:39)*

## Claims

- On JRE 1029, discussed advisers including people "at the National Reconnaissance Office" with
  satellite-feed perspectives on objects entering/leaving the atmosphere, describing forward-
  looking infrared detection systems programmed to flag anomalous heat/speed signatures
  distinguishable from ICBMs, aircraft, and meteorites. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  51:42–53:40)*
- Stated one of his advisers, "high up" at the NRO, told him about "episodic visits" — periodic
  time windows during which UFOs (including "mother ships" and smaller collector craft) arrive,
  with a DoD physicist having reportedly developed an algorithm predicting their arrival
  longitude/latitude/orbit with apparent success. *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md)
  53:48–54:39)*
- Gerb theorizes DeLonge was informed of [[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]]-
  style episodic-visit data by [[luis-elizondo|Luis Elizondo]] in 2017, the same year IMCON was
  established, constituting an authorized or unauthorized disclosure 7 years before
  [[matthew-brown|Matthew Brown]] publicly disclosed IMCON's existence.
  *([gerb/nro](../raw/gerb/nro.md) 55:58–56:19)*

## Claims (from "Special Access Required" Vol. 2)

- Gerb re-airs the same JRE 1029 "episodic visits" clip and reiterates his theory that DeLonge's
  founding of [[to-the-stars-academy|TTSA]] traces to a 2010 "Project Forum" idea from the
  [[bigelow-aerospace-advanced-space-studies|BASS]] 10-month report, executed via
  [[luis-elizondo|Elizondo]] as part of the broader

  2:29:23–2:30:51)*
