[[uap-gerb]]'s speculative timeline and organizational reconstruction of how the alleged 1997 crash in Peru ([[weygandt-peru-crash-encounter-1997]]) was retrieved. It is Gerb's inference layered on [[jonathan-weygandt]]'s testimony, not a claim by Weygandt, and Gerb repeatedly says it rests on many assumptions and is open to correction. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:55:13-1:55:37)*

## The thesis

- Laser Strike radar, and probably [[national-reconnaissance-office]]-run [[defense-support-program]] satellites, tracked the UFO; Weygandt suspects it was downed by Peruvian Hawk MIM-23 missile fragmentation (Gerb notes Hawks can use air-burst rounds) and Gerb says a rapid-reaction team got perhaps an hour of advance notice. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 18:05-19:54, 1:09:54-1:10:40, 1:19:19-1:19:32)*
- A NEST team flew from a DOE lab (Gerb proposes [[sandia-national-laboratories]]; Santa Fe to Iquitos is 3,446 miles, about 7.5 hours in a Cessna Citation II) then about 30 minutes by CH-47 (180-196 mph) to the site, totalling roughly 8 hours of travel inside a roughly 9-hour window; Weygandt's Marines took 2 to 4 hours from alert to site. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 47:13-47:48, 1:19:32-1:21:23)*
- Gerb calls the roughly 30-person team a rapid recovery unit comparable to the alleged Blue Berets at [[kecksburg-ufo-crash-1965]], Stringfield's "Blue Boys" and the [[kingman-arizona-crash-1953]] response, and in its hazmat gear and armed escorts to the [[coyame-crash-1974]] team. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 47:56-49:15)*
- NEST tie-ins: NEST began in 1974, was governed by the 1954 Atomic Energy Act ([[atomic-energy-act-1954]]) which Gerb says was used to reclassify UFO data as transclassified foreign nuclear information, and in February 1997 created the Joint Technical Operations team; helicopters would be Army CH-47s from the [[first-battalion-228th-aviation-regiment]] or the [[160th-special-operations-aviation-regiment]]; armed escorts from the [[us-army-7th-special-forces-group]], JSOC assets or the [[doe-special-response-team]]. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 50:12-52:21, 55:42-56:26, 56:52-57:16, 1:00:49-1:09:46)*
- Detention: Weygandt was contaminated by the craft's liquid, stripped by suited personnel, and held at a secure base with permanent structures, possibly [[namru-south]] in [[iquitos-peru]]. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:11:19-1:12:05, 1:23:47-1:24:20)*
- Open questions Gerb raises: how a craft embedded in a granite cliff was removed, what happened to the crew and the other seven or so Marines, and whether the craft went to the [[office-of-naval-research]]; he says the DOE/NEST identification is now hard to argue against. *([gerb/weygandt](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-05-16 - 1997 Peru UFO Crash Retrieval - the Story of Jonathan Weygandt.md>) 1:48:09-1:49:00, 1:52:40-1:53:30)*
