The Service d'Action Civique (SAC) was, per Eyes Wide Open, a secret police force founded in December 1959 by [[jacques-foccart]] and controlled directly by the French president, effectively replacing the CIA/NATO-linked [[rose-des-vents]] with a Gaullist paramilitary network until its 1982 disbandment. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 45:36-46:55)*

## Claims

- Embroiled in scandals 1959-1981; many researchers speculate it was responsible for the 1965 disappearance of Moroccan nationalist Mehdi Ben Barka. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 46:13-46:33)*
- In 1981 five SAC commandos received 15-year and life sentences for murdering Jacques Massie and his family after his investigation into the organization's corruption; the massacre led to SAC's disbandment in 1982 (the year after [[francois-mitterrand]]'s election). *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 46:33-46:55)*
- Eyes Wide Open notes much of the pacification of French Communists was done by other means such as the Mafia and rival unions, to be covered in the French Connection episodes. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 45:10-45:21)*
