Alfred, 1st Viscount Milner, was British High Commissioner for Southern Africa and Governor of
Cape Colony. Green & Pleasant presents him as the founding figure of the [[round-table-movement]]
and, through his protégé [[lionel-curtis]], the ideological root of [[chatham-house]]'s founding
network.

## Claims

- His policies as High Commissioner for Southern Africa are alleged to have directly precipitated
  the Second Boer War *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 14:10)*.
- After the British victory, he assembled a group of young Oxford-educated men — "Milner's
  Kindergarten," including [[lionel-curtis]], Philip Kerr (later Lord Lothian), and Geoffrey
  Dawson (later editor of the Times) — to administer the conquered Transvaal territories
  *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 14:19, 14:26, 14:34)*.
- Wrote in 1897: "You have only to sacrifice 'the nigger' absolutely and the game is easy."
  A personal credo found among his papers after his death is quoted as declaring him "a British —
  indeed primarily an English — nationalist," whose imperial project was framed as "the destiny
  of the English race" *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 14:43, 14:51, 14:59)*.
- Historian Keith Breckenridge is cited documenting that the administrative systems his
  Kindergarten built in the Transvaal formed a system designed to control the identities,
  movements and behavior of Black workers; the 1905 Lagden Commission, established under his
  administration, formalized racial segregation and is described as "a blueprint for apartheid"
  *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 15:08, 15:13)*.

## Relationships

- Founded/assembled the [[round-table-movement]] ("Milner's Kindergarten").
- Mentor of [[lionel-curtis]], who later convened [[chatham-house]]'s founding meeting.
