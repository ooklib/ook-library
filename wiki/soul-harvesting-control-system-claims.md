[[jason-jorjani]]'s argument that Earth is a prison and energy-harvesting system managed from the moon, with the afterlife light and dead relatives being manipulated simulacra. Presented as speculation supported by Robert Monroe, Carla Turner and one group of remote viewers.

## Claims

- Angels are a hive-minded, will-less hierarchy; the Asuras/Titans are the rebel faction. Peter Thiel is quoted (on Rogan) that faster-than-light travel implies a totalitarian mind-melded or altruistic society ("demons or angels"); Jorjani flips the moral evaluation. Michels asks if this is "too clean". *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:28:30, 2:29:33, 2:31:18, 2:31:43)*
- [[robert-monroe]] in *Far Journeys* (1985): a "loosh farm" run for a demiurge; Carla Turner's abductee interviews describe a control system managed from a metallic sphere, which Jorjani identifies with the inside of the moon; he claims Turner was killed by a fast-acting cancer weapon (the Shah of Iran and Peter Jennings are named as similar cases). *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:34:24, 2:35:19, 2:36:49)*
- Near-death experiences: being pulled toward a light and shown dead relatives and religious figures is, per Ian Stevenson's reincarnation intervals (6 months to 3 years), an artificial simulacrum by psychotronic technology; he cites his Persian grandmother's deathbed talks. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:37:46, 2:38:35, 2:39:27)*
- John Lear's "soul catcher on the moon" is recalled but discounted. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:41:29)*
- A remote viewing on the word "moksha" by "Scott Turner/Trevor Scott" (as heard) reported a bending of the soul back to Earth from a celestial body, part of an energy-extraction system set up after a solar-system war, then a lost rebellion; the video was reportedly removed. Links to [[mars-nuclear-war-claims]]. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:42:25, 2:43:06, 2:43:31)*
- The remedy is Prometheus: humans as "new gods" and nuclear weapons as the sole defence vs. the Nordics; see also [[trickster-ai-and-god-as-test-claims]]. *([jm/jorjani-hitler](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-11-01 - I Found Hitler’s Secret UFO Program! - Jason Jorjani.md>) 2:46:21, 2:48:56, 2:49:08)*
