Foglets are a theoretical nanotechnology (robots about 100 microns wide that link arms in 12 directions to form solid or permeable objects on command), the "utility fog" concept proposed by [[j-storrs-hall]] in 1989 and elaborated in a 1993 paper presented at a NASA conference. In [[sentinels-of-ether-michels-episode-2026]] [[jesse-michels]] offers them, explicitly as "extreme speculation" and "a thought experiment", as the mechanism behind the vanishing mountain in [[sentinels-of-ether]].

## Claims

- Each foglet has a rudimentary nanocomputer and shares power and data with neighbors; Hall said the result could be a virtual world "that could actually cut you and make you bleed", and unlike holograms foglets have physical presence and could fake sandstone via micro-vibrations. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 49:49-52:16)*
- Hall's original design looked like snow, but he says it could be enhanced with light-emitting antennas so the outer skin acts as "a 3D TV screen". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 52:16-52:49)*
- Michels: the novel's behavior (3D to 2D to pixels, low-frequency pulsing in headsets, aircraft power loss while friendly helicopters pass with the right codes) is what a foglet swarm would look like, acting like "a living electromagnetic warfare system". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 52:49-55:06)*
- Supporting context Michels cites: DIA has "openly mused" about programmable matter for camouflage; DARPA funded millimeter-scale smart dust (field demos by 2001, MEMS matured by 2004) and biohybrid insect cyborgs; Hall says much needed nanotech work was suppressed; the Invention Secrecy Act of 1951 lets government classify inventions, so a classified program would not patent. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 55:42-57:16)* See [[darpa]].
- [[ben-rich]] is quoted as saying "we already have the means to travel among the stars", that things in the desert are "50 years beyond" comprehension, and that some in intelligence want such technology to stay black; Michels reasons a buried special access program could be far past public smart dust. *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 57:16-58:14)*
- Michels concedes the mountain in the novel could be "pure fiction". *([jm/censored-book](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-05-21 - Why The Pentagon Tried To Censor This UFO Book.md>) 55:16-55:26)*
