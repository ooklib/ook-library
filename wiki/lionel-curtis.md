Lionel Curtis was a British colonial administrator, protégé of [[alfred-milner]] and member of
the "Milner's Kindergarten" cohort of the [[round-table-movement]]. Green & Pleasant credits him
as the direct founder of [[chatham-house]], convening its founding meeting at the Paris Peace
Conference in 1919.

## Claims

- On 30 May 1919, at the Hotel Majestic in Paris — where the British delegation to the Paris
  Peace Conference was staying — Curtis gathered roughly 30 British and American delegates to
  propose "a permanent Anglo-American Institute of International Affairs," one body with London
  and New York offices. Historian Katharina Rietzler, writing in Chatham House's own centenary
  account, called it "an English-speaking common market of ideas"
  *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 13:18, 13:34, 13:43, 13:50, 13:57)*.
- Was a protégé of [[alfred-milner]] and one of "Milner's Kindergarten," the young Oxford-educated
  men who administered the conquered Transvaal after the Second Boer War
  *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 14:03, 14:26)*.
- Spent his career advocating imperial federation and world government under Anglo-Saxon
  leadership. His three-volume work *Civitas Dei* is described, per one biographical summary
  quoted in the video, in "messianic terms" as proclaiming the unique destiny of the multi-nation,
  multi-race British Commonwealth as "the very manifestation of the city of God upon earth";
  another summary described his life's project as "a blueprint for the transition from imperial
  direct rule to globalized neo-colonialism"
  *([gp/chatham-house](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-04-10 - Britain's Most Powerful Secret Organisation - Chatham House Exposed.md) 15:20, 15:29, 15:38, 15:45, 15:52)*.

## Relationships

- Member of the [[round-table-movement]] ("Milner's Kindergarten"), under [[alfred-milner]].
- Founded [[chatham-house]] via the 30 May 1919 Hotel Majestic meeting.
