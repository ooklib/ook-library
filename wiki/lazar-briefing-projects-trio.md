On arrival at S4, [[bob-lazar]] says [[dennis-mariani]] sat him in a briefing room with a stack of blue folders summarizing the running projects; he read them before meeting [[barry-s4-lab-partner]] *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 40:33-41:34)*. Three projects are named: [[project-galileo-s4]] (power and propulsion), [[project-sidekick-s4]] (weapons) and [[project-looking-glass-s4]] (time distortion). Ramsey's synthesis is that these are the three directions of gravity manipulation, space, time and weapons, "the holy trinity" that would let a holder "rule the world" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 42:40-43:14)*. Lazar agrees, saying a machine that makes and modulates gravity gives you force fields, time travel and warp drive, and if you have one "they really want to know how to make more of those" *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 43:14-43:51)*. Lazar suggests this is a good reason to keep it secret from adversaries *([a52/lazar-everything](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-04-10 - Bob Lazar Tells me Everything in NEW Interview - DEBRIEFED ep. 83.md>) 43:51-44:09)*.

The folders also allegedly contained non-technical material ([[lazar-zeta-reticuli-briefing-document-claims]]) that Lazar treats as unreliable.
