EG&G (Edgerton, Germeshausen, and Grier, Inc.), founded in 1931 by MIT professor
[[harold-edgerton|Harold Edgerton]], is a contractor "legendary in the UFO community."
*([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:21:05–1:21:27)*

## Claims

- Historically shared responsibility at the Nevada Test Site (including Nellis AFB and Area 51)
  for weapons development and high-tech testing, alongside
  [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence Livermore]] and [[raytheon|Raytheon]].
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:21:36–1:21:52)*
- [[bob-lazar|Bob Lazar]] claimed EG&G hired him to reverse-engineer UFO propulsion systems at
  Area 51's S4 site. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:21:52–1:22:08)*
- Cited by Gerb as one of multiple components (with the DOE's Nuclear Emergency Support Team,
  NEST) connected to UFO legacy programs; Gerb wagers NEST/EG&G was the on-site technical team
  that recovered a UFO in the Peruvian jungle in 1997, witnessed by USMC Lance Cpl. Jonathan
  Weygandt. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:22:08–1:22:31)*
- Per journalist George Knapp, deceased former senior EG&G manager Alfred O'Donnell said EG&G
  "had in its possession a flying saucer recovered in New Mexico, as well as live nonhuman
  beings that looked vaguely human." *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 1:22:31–1:22:46)*
- Site of the 2002 parking-lot meeting between [[eric-davis|Eric Davis]] and
  [[thomas-wilson|VADM Thomas Wilson]] central to the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]].
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 35:02–35:20)*
- 2004: entered a joint venture with SAIC and Parsons Infrastructure and Technology Group to
  form RDS (Research and Development Solutions, LLC), assigned to the DOE's National Energy
  Technology Laboratory ($217M+ total value). *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:24:50–1:25:22)*
- Acquired by URS in 2002, then by AECOM in 2014. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  1:21:27–1:21:36)*
