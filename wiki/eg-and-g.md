EG&G (Edgerton, Germeshausen, and Grier, Inc.), founded in 1931 by MIT professor
[[harold-edgerton|Harold Edgerton]], is a contractor "legendary in the UFO community."
*([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:21:05–1:21:27)*

## Claims

- Historically shared responsibility at the Nevada Test Site (including Nellis AFB and Area 51)
  for weapons development and high-tech testing, alongside
  [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence Livermore]] and [[raytheon|Raytheon]].
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:21:36–1:21:52)*
- [[bob-lazar|Bob Lazar]] claimed EG&G hired him to reverse-engineer UFO propulsion systems at
  Area 51's S4 site. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:21:52–1:22:08)*
- Cited by Gerb as one of multiple components (with the DOE's Nuclear Emergency Support Team,
  NEST) connected to UFO legacy programs; Gerb wagers NEST/EG&G was the on-site technical team
  that recovered a UFO in the Peruvian jungle in 1997, witnessed by USMC Lance Cpl. Jonathan
  Weygandt. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:22:08–1:22:31)*
- Per journalist George Knapp, deceased former senior EG&G manager Alfred O'Donnell said EG&G
  "had in its possession a flying saucer recovered in New Mexico, as well as live nonhuman
  beings that looked vaguely human." *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 1:22:31–1:22:46)*
- Site of the 2002 parking-lot meeting between [[eric-davis|Eric Davis]] and
  [[thomas-wilson|VADM Thomas Wilson]] central to the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]].
  *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md) 35:02–35:20)*
- From the 1970s to 1993, EG&G's Special Projects Division worked closely with
  [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia]] at the Nevada Test Site, providing timing-control and
  high-speed photographic support for nuclear weapons testing and classified SAPs; a 20,000+-
  image EG&G/NTS photo-lab collection (1972–1992+) documents this work, including images of
  [[area-6|Area 6]] and Area 19. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:03:01–2:04:21,
  2:13:22–2:14:32)*
- Per journalist [[george-knapp|George Knapp]], senior EG&G manager Alfred O'Donnell (circa 1990)
  claimed DoD/IC elements — inferrably including EG&G — possessed a recovered New Mexico disc
  and a live non-human being, housed at Indian Springs AFB (now Creech AFB) until 1955 then moved
  to an Area 51-adjacent facility. O'Donnell later gave Annie Jacobsen a different, Soviet/Mengele-
  origin account of Roswell that Gerb disbelieves. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md)
  2:06:04–2:08:22)*
- [[donald-kerr|Donald Kerr]] — separately a top Gerb-alleged legacy-program gatekeeper — was
  president/director of EG&G 1989–1992. *([gerb/sandia](../raw/gerb/sandia.md) 2:05:12–2:05:52)*
- 2004: entered a joint venture with SAIC and Parsons Infrastructure and Technology Group to
  form RDS (Research and Development Solutions, LLC), assigned to the DOE's National Energy
  Technology Laboratory ($217M+ total value). *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:24:50–1:25:22)*
- Acquired by URS in 2002, then by AECOM in 2014. *([gerb/saic](../raw/gerb/saic.md)
  1:21:27–1:21:36)*
