Son and heir of [[john-d-rockefeller]]; in this documentary the negotiator of the Mesabi sale to Morgan. He is the father of the brothers described in [[rockefeller-family]], though this source does not name them.

## Claims

- Graduated in the 1890s and was taught investing and negotiating by [[frederick-gates]]. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 35:00, 35:08)*
- Aged 27 he told [[j-pierpont-morgan]] "I did not come here to sell. I understood you wished to buy", forcing Morgan to name the price: $88.5 million in US Steel stock. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 38:40, 38:57, 39:14)*
- Co-founded the [[rockefeller-foundation]] and gave $540 million of his own inheritance. *([mm/rockefeller](<../raw/mm/MagnatesMedia - 2023-11-19 - Rockefeller - The World’s First Billionaire.md>) 47:16, 47:37)*

## Relationships

- Son of [[john-d-rockefeller]]; associate of [[frederick-gates]]; counterpart of [[j-pierpont-morgan]] in [[mesabi-iron-range-deal]].
