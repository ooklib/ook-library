## Claims (gerb/aztec)

- Valentin Archuleta (pseudonym "VA", Steinman 1982), a rancher and former POW, heard an explosion and saw a huge domed disc wobble past within 200 yards and strike a cliff; he phoned Kirtland and was never called back. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:28:45-1:30:05)*
- Doug Noland, 19, El Paso Oil Company, said oil workers found a molded, seamless brushed-aluminum disc about 100 ft across and 18-20 ft high with three gold rings, a mirror-like porthole with a quarter-sized hole, two charred bodies over a control panel, a walkway opened with a fire pole, a circling helicopter and Army-looking uniformed men who ordered silence for national security. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:30:12-1:34:17)*
- Police officer Manuel Sandoval of Cuba, NM, followed the fluttering disc and cleared people from it; ranchers Mr and Mrs Knight warned the workers off. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:32:43-1:33:24)*
- Ken Farley (deathbed, 1999, to Scott Ramsay) described a dull-aluminum disc like two pie pans tilted about 12 degrees, with a larger top bubble. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:34:17-1:35:58)*
- An airman using the pseudonym "George" (2001, known to [[stanton-friedman]]) said he was at Walker AFB, formerly Roswell Army Airfield, where the retrieval was coordinated; it was compartmentalized, involved about 200 people, took two weeks, included Fifth Army in Colorado, followed Roswell's failures with better professionals, and he saw photographs and lots of trucks at Los Alamos. He said the Air Force worried about who leaked, not about Scully. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:35:58-1:40:22)*
- Lee Crane recalled Elvado Air Force radar-site personnel bringing in ducks they said microwave radar had knocked out of the air, fitting the theory that radar downed the craft. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 11:17-11:54)*
