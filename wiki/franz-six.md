Nazi professor, SS officer and death-squad commander who, per [[eyes-wide-open]] (`ewa/shackley`, drawing on Cookridge, Loftus and a 1982 Washington Post article), was freed early from a war-crimes sentence and became the main recruiter of [[reinhard-gehlen]]'s CIA-funded commando force. ASR renders him "Dr. France Alfred 6" ("Six"); "Kingsburg"/"Kunnigburg" is the ASR of the university city (Konigsberg by my inference).

## Claims

- Described as the most prominent of Gehlen's three-man academic "triumvirate" (with Akhmeteli and Augsburg, see [[galen-commando-program]]): Konigsberg professor and former SS brigade-level officer (host: equivalent of brigadier general). *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 48:25-48:33)*
- Hitler allegedly selected him to head intelligence functions in a Nazi-occupied Britain, in charge of tracking targets on a blacklist reportedly including Churchill; later put in charge of "Vorkommando Moscow," a unit of Einsatzgruppe B, tasked with liquidating communists, Jews and dissidents and with purging Moscow if it fell. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 48:33-49:21)*
- An ideologue of Lebensraum ("living space") from his university posts; also head of ideological research at Himmler's Reich Security Main Office. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 49:37-50:07)*
- A 1982 Washington Post article "Nazi Cold War advisers went unpunished" (per host) covers Six and Friedrich Buchardt (ASR "Bukart"), a Latvian academic who led his own Einsatzgruppen unit and later worked for MI6 and then the CIA. Host: the Einsatzgruppen trial defendants were mostly highly educated men. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 50:07-51:16)*
- Caught in 1948 and tried at Nuremberg that year; sentenced to 20 years but served four, the sentence commuted in 1952 on the recommendation of US High Commissioner John J. McCloy (ASR "Mcclo"; normalization by my inference), whom the host calls a good friend of [[allen-dulles]] and an architect of the OSS; host speculates a personal favor by Dulles but says "we'll never know." *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 51:59-52:37)*
- Released in 1952, Gehlen enlisted him as main recruiter for his "special forces," tapping former Eastern Front commando squads scattered in Western Europe. *([ewa/shackley](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-10-03 - The CIA's Blond Ghost - Ted Shackley.md) 52:37-52:54)*
