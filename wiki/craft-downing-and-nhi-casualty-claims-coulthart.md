[[ross-coulthart]] says people still in the legacy program told him the US uses high-power microwave (HPM) weapons to bring down craft, after psionics is used to lure them, and asks whether beings have been killed as a result.

## Claims

- His most shocking moment: people in the program admitted "we're using high pulse microwave weapons" to take craft down, using psionics to attract or invite them, and "for some obscure reason they keep on coming". *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 0:32-0:47, 1:00:26-1:00:53)*
- Ramsey compares the repeated lure to a white-blood-cell or immune-system response, possibly an AI. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:00:53-1:01:23)*
- Asked "have we purposely murdered other beings?", Coulthart says he suspects so; when he asks retrieval people what happened to the beings he gets a squeamish look, and he does not know if there was a being aboard. He calls it the worst possible crime and the group-think of a military rationale. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:01:23-1:02:29)*
- He says he thinks bodies described to him are some kind of android or biological synthetic intelligence, "constructed objects", possibly a different class from what was recovered in the 1940s and 1950s. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:04:32-1:05:11)*
- [[jake-barber]] spoke of overwhelming love from the intelligence associated with a craft he recovered, which Coulthart says changed his life. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 1:02:29-1:03:21)*
- Before his research he thought shooting down craft "preposterous" and psionics "woo woo"; multiple corroborating sources changed that. *([a52/coulthart](<../raw/a52/Area52 - 2026-02-20 - The DANGEROUS Truth About UFOs - Ross Coulthart _ DEBRIEFED ep. 76.md>) 2:31:08-2:35:17)*
- Related: the [[esalen-inviting-session-skywatcher]] is where Coulthart says he first saw psionic inviting work.
