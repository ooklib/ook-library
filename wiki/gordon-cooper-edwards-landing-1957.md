Mercury astronaut Gordon Cooper's claim that in 1957, while a project manager at [[edwards-air-force-base]], a camera crew filming a precision landing facility at the edge of the dry lake bed saw a saucer land there. Retold by [[uap-gerb]] with audio of Cooper.

## Claims (gerb/edwards-afb)

- The crew filmed a shiny silver craft about 30 ft in diameter with three landing gear that hovered, sat on the lake bed for a few minutes and left at very high speed after the crew came within 20 yards. Cooper says he saw the film's close-up shots against a window, and it was sent to Washington by courier under orders not to run prints. Gerb notes the evidence was confiscated. *([gerb/edwards-afb](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-10-02 - UAP Reverse Engineering at Edwards Air Force Base [Redacted List Vol.2].md>) 29:41-32:08)*
