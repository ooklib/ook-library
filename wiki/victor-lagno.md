Dr. Victor Lagno, described by [[salvatore-pais]] as of the Russian Academy of Sciences, is cited by Pais as the author of a short (three-page) archived paper on a bipolaron mechanism that Pais says validates his own claimed room-temperature-superconductor concept. Name transcribed inconsistently in the ASR ("Lnau", "Lacno"); no independent corroboration of this person or paper is given in the source.

## Claims (jm/pais)

- Pais says Lagno published an archived paper (never accepted to arXiv despite Lagno's attempts) showing that a bipolaron mechanism — not the standard BCS electron-pairing mechanism — could make a room-temperature superconductor work along the lines of the [[pais-effect]], and that this validated his own patent's mechanism even though the USPTO examiner rejected the patent. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 23:28-24:01, 41:02-41:10)*
- Pais describes a bipolaron as a composite quasiparticle of two polarons, and says Lagno's mechanism does not depend on chemical composition, unlike type-I/II BCS superconductors; Pais chose lead zirconate titanate (a "piezoelectric material") for his own patent because all its components are superconducting at very low temperature. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 40:16-40:31, 42:55-43:47)*
- Pais says Lagno, whom he regards as an exceptionally capable mathematical physicist ("physics drives the mathematics, not vice versa"), believes the mechanism could work and that "it should be looked at"; Pais says this validation "means so much" to him personally. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 46:42-47:04, 1:38:36-1:38:46)*
- Pais notes that despite the English-language patent, only Korean and Chinese teams have publicly claimed replication attempts of a related room-temperature-superconductor result, with reported reproduction issues; he does not connect this specifically to LK-99 by name but the description (six-author then three-author papers, claimed 2023-era replications) matches that episode. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 47:04-47:58)*
