The Dallas-area woman who hosted Marina Oswald and helped Lee Harvey Oswald get work at the Texas School Book Depository in 1963. The Sheehan episode (transcribed "Ruth Payne") raises a chain of possible connections; this is speculation by the participants.

## Claims

- Speakers say she was said to have vacationed in summer 1963 on Naushon Island (Forbes family) with Allen Dulles's mistress, that her sister worked for the CIA, that she brought the Oswalds from New Orleans to Dallas, got Lee the depository job (owned by D. Harold Byrd), and only after the FBI search produced a note used to link Oswald to the shooting of General Edwin Walker.
