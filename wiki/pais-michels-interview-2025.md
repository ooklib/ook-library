Jesse Michels interviews Navy physicist [[salvatore-pais]] on American Alchemy, published 2025-01-24 (video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8TYMQOUDQBo), joined by a pseudonymous physicist co-host referred to only as "Jack," who works in "sensitive areas" and helps drive the technical conversation. Pais states at the outset that he speaks only as a private citizen, not for the Navy or Space Force, and repeatedly declines to go further on weaponization-adjacent questions ("EQ3ed" — his shorthand for not wanting to "educate and enlighten the enemy"). All substantive physics and disclosure claims below are Pais's own, attributed and unverified; see [[salvatore-pais]] for the detailed claim list.

## Structure and themes

- Pais's biography (Romania to Queens, Case Western Reserve, Northrop Grumman, USPTO, Navy strategic systems programs, Space Force) and the origin/examination history of his three granted and two rejected patents are covered on [[salvatore-pais]]. *([jm/pais](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2025-01-24 - Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais).md>) 3:05-24:01)*
- The core physics threads — the [[pais-effect]], the [[schwinger-limit]], high-frequency gravitational waves, and the disputed room-temperature superconductor (with [[victor-lagno]]'s bipolaron mechanism) — run through the middle of the interview.
- Man-made-versus-ET technology framing, and comparisons to [[bob-lazar]]'s "sport model" account and [[ken-shoulders]]'s exotic-vacuum-object work, recur throughout.
- A digression on alternate gravity-manipulation lineages (Ning Li, Eugene Podkletnov, POEMS theory, Alzofon) is recorded on [[salvatore-pais]].
- Host commentary and tangents — remote viewing/[[hal-puthoff]], the [[ashton-forbes]] MH370-orbs claim, torsion physics (Einstein-Cartan), Nikola Tesla and scalar-wave theory, the Chapel Hill conference (Agnew Bahnson, John Wheeler, Bryce DeWitt, Cecile DeWitt-Morette, "Lulu"/Louis Witten), and the "children of light/darkness" and "external enemy" framing — are recorded on [[jesse-michels]].
- The interview closes with Pais's call for global human unification against a possible external/non-human threat, and his message that non-specialists ("Joe the plumber," citing [[ashton-forbes]]) could still contribute a key idea.
