Barry Goldwater was a US Air Force Reserve major general, senator from Arizona (1953-65 and 1969-87) and the 1964 Republican presidential nominee, who publicly said he tried to enter a restricted room at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base where UFO material was rumored to be stored. [[uap-gerb]] presents his statements as powerful claims by an important figure.

## Claims (gerb/whistleblowers-2)

- Goldwater's 1975 interview: "I think the government does know. I can't back that up," and he said at Wright-Patterson, if you could get into certain places, you would find out what the Air Force and government know about UFOs; he said reportedly a spaceship landed and it was all hushed up. He says he called Gen. [[curtis-lemay]] to ask to go into the room, and LeMay "got mad in hell", cussed him out and said never ask that question again. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 16:00-17:14)*
- Letters compiled by [[leonard-stringfield]]: to Stringfield (3 December 1974) he said he had been agnostic about UFOs and was denied when he tried to get into the room where the information was stored; to Lee Graham (19 October 1981) he said he knew of no one with access to the "blue room" and the matter had become so highly classified that it was "impossible to get anything on it"; in a 28 March 1975 letter to an unknown researcher he said he was denied entry to the building at Wright-Patterson where information collected by the Air Force is stored, and that it is still classified "Above Top Secret". Gerb dates his interest and attempt to the early 1960s, perhaps 1963. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 17:14-19:05)*
- Gerb's analysis: rumors of craft and bodies at Wright-Patterson were common in the era, citing a 27 April 1953 Air Force OSI document in which Master Sgt. Ralph Brown relayed that three flying saucers (one damaged, two functional) and pilot bodies were at Wright-Patterson. See [[blue-room-wright-patterson]]. *([gerb/whistleblowers-2](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-26 - UFO Whistleblowers [Vol.2].md>) 19:05-19:45)*
