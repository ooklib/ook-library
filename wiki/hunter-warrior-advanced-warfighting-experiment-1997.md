A large USMC exercise run by the newly renamed Marine Corps Warfighting Laboratory around [[marine-corps-air-ground-combat-center-29-palms]] in southern California, dated by [[uap-gerb]] to 2-14 March 1997 (he misspeaks "May" once). It is the context of [[castle-29-palms-triangle-encounter-1997]].

## Claims (gerb/castle)

- Gerb describes it as the largest experiment to date by the Marine Corps Warfighting Laboratory, testing experimental equipment and tactics along three objectives: tactical operations on a dispersed non-contiguous battlefield, C4I and a single shared digital network, and fire support and targeting. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 14:18-15:53, 46:20-46:58)*
- Per a 1997 USMC news release it ran in three phases: reconnaissance, surveillance, shaping and deception; targeting for initial engagements; and major night movement, more targeting and a mobile raid. Castle's unit belonged to an experimental special-purpose Marine air-ground task force; Gerb guesses the encounter fell in phase three. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 47:02-48:28)*
- The findings were summarized for the Marine Corps Combat Development Center by the [[center-for-naval-analyses]], a Navy FFRDC, which Gerb reads as possibly a secondary reporting channel that could carry ARV results back to the Navy while avoiding the Marine chain of command. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 54:51-58:44)*
- The Navy's role: a 1995 report by Vice Adm. Robert Spain on sea-based aerial fire support, and at the request of Adm. J. Johnson, Gen. Charles Krulak asked Naval Strike Air Warfare Center Fallon ([[naval-air-warfare-development-center]]) to assemble air crews from Fallon and [[naval-air-station-lemoore]] to explore a "Hunter Air" concept; Gerb calls this an adjunct bolted on late in planning and the opening he thinks the Navy used for its ARV teams. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 58:44-1:00:43)*
- Gerb argues the black team chose an active experiment zone on purpose to train alongside "blue" assets unseen, and that the Navy's 2004 [[2004-nimitz-encounter]] Tic Tac may be a similar test on unwitting forces, a hypothesis he says he neither stakes out nor advocates. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 49:23-54:19, 1:49:35-1:50:51)*
- Gerb sees no evidence the USMC itself runs UFO legacy programs and says the analysis turns away from the Marines entirely. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 12:23-13:06, 46:04-46:20)*
