Robert Maxwell, the publisher and father of Ghislaine Maxwell, appears in the `jm/cover-up` episode as a proposed node of the transnational network. Claims by [[jason-samosa]] and [[jesse-michels]] (speculative):

- Michels: he had Israeli intelligence ties and, he believes, MI6 ties; interrogated Nazi scientists after WWII and set up a scientific publisher (Pergamon), and his protege [[jeffrey-epstein]] allegedly suppressed science such as cold fusion (Pons and Fleischmann). *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:46:50, 1:47:13)*
- Samosa: Maxwell appears "adjacent" to [[resorts-international]], Permindex and the [[world-commerce-corporation]]; the PROMIS software scandal (sold to Sandia Labs) supposedly siphoned American nuclear secrets, and networks shifted toward Israeli power by the 1970s-80s. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:47:45, 1:48:26, 1:48:53)*
