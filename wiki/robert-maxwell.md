Robert Maxwell, the publisher and father of Ghislaine Maxwell, appears in the `jm/cover-up` episode as a proposed node of the transnational network. Claims by [[jason-samosa]] and [[jesse-michels]] (speculative):

- Michels: he had Israeli intelligence ties and, he believes, MI6 ties; interrogated Nazi scientists after WWII and set up a scientific publisher (Pergamon), and his protege [[jeffrey-epstein]] allegedly suppressed science such as cold fusion (Pons and Fleischmann). *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:46:50, 1:47:13)*
- Samosa: Maxwell appears "adjacent" to [[resorts-international]], Permindex and the [[world-commerce-corporation]]; the PROMIS software scandal (sold to Sandia Labs) supposedly siphoned American nuclear secrets, and networks shifted toward Israeli power by the 1970s-80s. *([jm/cover-up](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-07-14 - The UFO Cover-Up Leads to One Secret Society - and One Final Event.md>) 1:47:45, 1:48:26, 1:48:53)*

## From oompaville on the Maxwell "data dynasty" (oompa/maxwells)

- Life: born Jan (Ludvik Hoch) on 1923-06-10 in Slatinske Doly, Czechoslovakia, a Yiddish-speaking Orthodox Jew; left at about 15 in 1939, lost most of his family at Auschwitz, reached Britain, taught himself an upper-class English accent, served as "Lance Corporal Leslie Smith", won the Military Cross in January 1945 (pinned by Montgomery) and later denied being Jewish. Per John Preston's *Fall*, he watched Auschwitz arrival footage looking for his parents. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 4:59, 5:16, 6:40, 7:13, 7:37, 7:48, 9:06)*
- War crimes: he admitted in a 1945 letter shooting a German mayor and later said he shot surrendered soldiers in a farmhouse; a war-crimes inquiry in his final years closed in 1992 without a charge. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 8:04, 8:20, 8:35)*
- Business: [[pergamon-press]] (1951, 400+ journals by the early 1990s), the printing firm behind Maxwell Communication Corporation (1981), Mirror Group (1984), Macmillan (\$2.6 billion, 1988), The European (1990), New York Daily News (1991); a 1971 British government inquiry judged he could not be relied on to steward a public company. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 12:15, 13:56, 14:26, 14:43, 15:21)*
- Family: children Michael (car crash coma, died 1968) and Karine (leukemia, 1957) died young; sons Ian and Kevin were charged with fraud after his death and acquitted; daughters [[christine-maxwell]], [[isabelle-maxwell]] and [[ghislaine-maxwell]] are covered on their own pages. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 15:54, 16:19)*
- Death: official ruling heart attack and drowning, but three pathologists disagreed; Preston refuses to call it murder ("most likely fell or jumped"), ex-editor Roy Greenslade thinks suicide. He was buried on the Mount of Olives with Israeli president Herzog's eulogy. *([oompa/maxwells](<../raw/oompa/oompaville - 2026-07-08 - The Maxwells Weren't Spies. They Were Something Worse.md>) 29:09, 29:17, 29:34, 30:49)*
