Colonel Steve Wilson, a "controversial" whistleblower **distinct from** VADM
[[thomas-wilson|Thomas Wilson]] of the [[wilson-davis-notes|Wilson-Davis notes]], disclosed
claims to UFO researcher Dr. Richard Boland. Gerb treats his claims with more skepticism,
recommending a critical take by author Kevin Randle. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
12:21–13:52)*

## Claims

- Claimed [[decision-science-applications|DSAI]] and
  [[science-applications-international-corporation|SAIC]] "were one and the same," with DSAI's
  board including UFO-black-budget-division directors from IBM, Lockheed, Teledyne Ryan, and
  Aerojet Electro Systems, and that [[bobby-ray-inman|Bobby Ray Inman]] also ran DSAI.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 11:45–12:13)*
- Claimed to have been stationed at Area 51/S4, where reverse-engineered anti-gravity vehicles
  were tested, and to have spearheaded [[project-pounce|Project Pounce]], an elite Air
  Force/National Reconnaissance Organization special-forces unit that retrieved downed UFOs with
  elements of JSOC/Delta Force. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 12:29–13:04)*
- Project Pounce allegedly appears in the disputed Majestic 12 "Project Aquarius" document and,
  oddly, the 1953 [[robertson-panel-1953|Robertson Panel]] report.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 13:04–13:11)*
- Claimed the first successful flight of man-made anti-gravity ARVs occurred on 18 July 1971,
  with [[bobby-ray-inman|Bobby Ray Inman]] present. *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md)
  13:18–13:33)*
- Produced a "map of Star Wars City" depicting UFO legacy-program operations within the
  [[strategic-defense-initiative|Strategic Defense Initiative]] (SDI, created 1983), naming
  contractors including Rockwell, Martin Marietta, Lockheed, Boeing, McDonnell Douglas, and IBM
  operating alongside the SDI Colorado HQ, "MAGI," the 1010th Special Security Squadron
  (protecting NORAD/SPACECOM resources at Cheyenne Mountain), and
  [[decision-science-applications|DSAI]] as the paramount contractor.
  *([saic](../raw/9p99lTsC7wQ_saic.md) 13:52–15:07)*

## Relationships

- Claims connect [[decision-science-applications]], [[bobby-ray-inman]], and
