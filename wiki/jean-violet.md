Jean Violet (ASR variants 'Viada', 'Villare', 'Viola'; spelling normalized from the wiki's best reading) was a Parisian lawyer and, per Eyes Wide Open, 'deep state operative' who helped found [[le-cercle]] and chaired it after [[antoine-pinay]]. Eyes Wide Open says information on him is scarce, 'an empty void in history'. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 33:55-34:15)*

## Claims

- Per a 1982 Der Spiegel article, kept intimate contacts with American and French intelligence and was a close confidant of [[reinhard-gehlen]], who allegedly called him a 'special connection' and paid him some 6,000 Deutsche marks a year. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 34:28-35:01)*
- German intelligence officer turned whistleblower [[hans-langemann]] claimed he once paid Violet 30,000 DM on Gehlen's behalf for the purposes of Le Cercle. *([ewa/pan-europa](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-07-28 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - France’s Secret Armies and the Pan-Europa Project [pt. 5.3].md) 35:08-35:41)*
