Cameron describes more than 80 telephone messages from an entity called "Audrey", addressing people as "star seed", received by Stan Romanek and by several people investigating his case, as a little-known layer of the Romanek story. Everything here is Cameron's second-hand account; Michels does not endorse it.

## Claims

- Romanek (a contactee whose father allegedly worked on missiles in North Dakota) later faced pornography charges he said were a set-up; Cameron regards the case as "a real deal". *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 16:48-17:26, 22:00-22:12)*
- A New Jersey businessman who backed Romanek and hosted Cameron's meeting with [[tim-taylor-nasa]] says he knew before the phone rang that Audrey was calling; a public affairs person from a UFO group, Paula Harris and a MUFON board member also allegedly received messages. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 18:42-22:00)*
- Paula Harris allegedly got a call reassuring her she would return to Crestone, Colorado, after attending a [[steven-greer]] event there. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 20:17-21:16)*
- [[john-b-alexander]] allegedly visited Romanek's home; Cameron says Alexander told him a flashlight answered questions in binary code and dismissed it as "just paranormal". *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 22:37-25:12)*
- Cameron says Romanek's electromagnetic effects match those [[chris-bledsoe]] reports. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 23:09-23:38)*
