Seismic exploration company founded in 1930 by J. Clarence Carter and Eugene McDermott that became [[texas-instruments]] in 1951. [[uap-gerb]] proposes it as a plausible workplace of one or more "Mr. G" scientists ([[dr-gee-scientists]]) because it moved from oil-detection devices to [[vannevar-bush]]'s wartime anti-submarine programs. ASR calls it "Geoysical Services" and once "Geospatial Services". Speculative link.

- After Pearl Harbor GSI was pulled into National Defense Research Committee/OSRD anti-submarine work, built electronics for the Army Signal Corps and a Navy airborne magnetic submarine detector contract, and by 1942 was a Navy war production contractor under [[vannevar-bush]]. *([gerb/aztec](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-07-19 - The 1948 Aztec, New Mexico UFO Crash Retrieval.md>) 1:11:45-1:14:57)*
