The Anglo Iranian Oil Company (AIOC) is the British oil corporation Eyes Wide Open names, along
with [[united-fruit-company|United Fruit]], as a [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]]
client whose interests the CIA allegedly served in the 1953 overthrow of Iranian Prime Minister
[[mohammad-mossadegh]].

## Claims

- A client of [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]], alongside [[united-fruit-company|
  United Fruit]]. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 5:50-5:54)*
- At the request of the British government and the AIOC, the CIA toppled the democratically
  elected government of Iranian Prime Minister [[mohammad-mossadegh]] in 1953, one year before
  the CIA-backed [[jacobo-arbenz|Arbenz]] overthrow in Guatemala. *([ewa/cold-war](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-05-03 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - The Real Cold War and the Creation of CIA [pt. 4].md) 6:24-6:37)*

## Relationships

  [[mohammad-mossadegh]] in Iran, 1953.
