The Bank for International Settlements (BIS), headquartered in Bern, was founded in the 1920s to
administer German WWI reparations payments but, per Eyes Wide Open, became fully integrated into
the Nazi financial system by the 1930s and was used to store Nazi-linked and Holocaust-victim
assets — a role central to the [[allen-dulles]]/[[john-foster-dulles]] shell-company scheme
described in this episode.

## Claims

- Founded in the 1920s to administer German reparations disbursements under the Treaty of
  Versailles; by the 1930s had been fully integrated into the Nazi financial system, with five of
  its board members card-carrying Nazis in 1940. Used to store stolen gold, art, and Nazi profits,
  much of it stolen from Holocaust victims. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 6:29-6:53)*
- Targeted by the Roosevelt administration's Operation Safe Haven policy, which sought to
  confiscate Nazi assets held at banks worldwide, fearing they would fund a future German
  resurgence. Two of Sullivan & Cromwell's major German clients, [[ig-farben]] and Merck KGaA, had
  funds stored at BIS. *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 6:56-7:32)*
- Allen and John Foster Dulles allegedly worked to see that these BIS funds remained with their
  Nazi clients until the end of the war: Foster set up a network of shell companies to shuffle the
  funds through complex transactions, while Allen (headquartered nearby in Bern as OSS station
  chief) destroyed paper records of the transactions to shield his brother. Some of these assets
  were never recovered, allegedly ending up funding far-right networks across Cold War Europe.
  *([ewa/sunrise](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2023-04-05 - THE HISTORY OF THE CIA - Operation Sunrise and the Plan for a New Europe [pt. 2].md) 7:32-8:24)*
