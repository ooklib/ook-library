George Bernard Shaw (1856–1950), the Nobel Prize-winning playwright, was a founding member of the
[[fabian-society]] (1884) and co-founder of the [[london-school-of-economics]] (1895). Green &
Pleasant presents him as both the most quotable evangelist of Fabian [[permeation]] tactics and
the most extreme documented eugenics advocate among the society's founders, including praise for
Stalin and denial of the Holodomor.

## Claims

### Permeation and Fabian activism

- Edited *Fabian Essays in Socialism* (1889), which sold out its first 1,000-copy print run and
  reached 27,000 copies within two years
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 3:15)*.
- Wrote in an 1892 published Fabian tract: "We permeated the party organizations and pulled all
  the wires we could lay our hands on with our utmost adroitness and energy." In 1908 he
  described the Fabians' explicit two-part strategy of providing a socialist parliamentary
  program and normalizing socialism as an ordinary political choice
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 5:18, 5:48)*.
- Designed the 1910 "Fabian Window" stained-glass artwork depicting himself and Sidney Webb
  hammering out "the new world," with a wolf in sheep's clothing above them
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 24:56)*.

### Eugenics advocacy — unverified claim, per source

Green & Pleasant presents the following as direct quotes from primary sources:

- 1903, appendix to *Man and Superman*: "The only fundamental and possible socialism is the
  socialism of the selective breeding of man"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 14:40)*.
- 1910, lecture to the Eugenics Education Society (reported in the Daily Express, 4 March): "A
  part of eugenic politics would finally land us in an extensive use of the lethal chamber"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 15:04)*.
- 1931, filmed Paramount interview: said there are "an extraordinary number of people whom I
  want to kill," proposing a board every five to seven years before which people would have to
  "justify your existence"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 15:21)*.
- 1938, letter to [[beatrice-webb]] (five years after Hitler took power): proposed "tackling the
  Jewish question" by "admitting the right of the state to make eugenic experiments by weeding
  out any strains they think undesirable"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 16:36)*.
- 1948, in the Atlantic Monthly, after the liberation of the camps and the Nuremberg trials:
  "Do not punish them. Kill, kill, kill, kill, kill them"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 17:00)*.

### Soviet sympathies — unverified claim, per source

- Visited the USSR in 1931, met Stalin for three hours, called him "a Georgian gentleman," and
  described the USSR as "a land of hope" versus Western "countries of despair"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 22:12)*.
- Signed a 2 March 1933 letter to the Manchester Guardian denying the Ukrainian famine while
  journalist Gareth Jones was reporting mass starvation on the ground (the Holodomor is estimated
  to have killed 3.5–5 million people)
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 22:27)*.
- Kept photographs of Felix Dzerzhinsky, Lenin, and Stalin on his mantelpiece; in a December 1933
  Fabian lecture (reported by the New York Times) praised Hitler, Mussolini, and Stalin together,
  saying "they're trying to get something done"
  *([gp/secret-society](../raw/gp/Green & Pleasant - 2026-03-28 - The Secret Society Running Britain for 140 Years.md) 22:44, 22:51)*.

## Relationships

- Founding member of the [[fabian-society]] and co-founder of the [[london-school-of-economics]].
- Designed the Fabian Window, later unveiled at LSE by Tony Blair.
- Corresponded with [[beatrice-webb]] on eugenics ("the Jewish question," 1938).
- Praised Stalin, Hitler, and Mussolini; denied the Holodomor.
- Critiqued (alongside other Fabian intellectuals) by [[george-orwell]] in *The Road to Wigan
  Pier*.
