The alleged night encounter, during the [[hunter-warrior-advanced-warfighting-experiment-1997]] in early March 1997, in which USMC Sgt [[roderick-castle]] and other VMA-513 Marines on Harrier crash-retrieval duty came over a hill near Emerson Dry Lake and saw a huge silent black triangle hovering over an armed, unmarked team of about 30 men and five or six black trucks. [[uap-gerb]] analyses it as a glimpse of an alien reproduction vehicle (ARV) operating alongside a black special mission unit; see [[castle-triangle-origin-thesis]].

## Claims (gerb/castle)

### Setting and date

- Castle's team was on 12-hour overnight shifts (7 pm to 7 am) as an emergency crash-retrieval crew with a Humvee carrying fire suppression equipment and a trailer, and got a radio call about "unexplained or unauthorized" flare activity three to five miles out in the desert. The Marines carried unloaded M16s in the back. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 17:00-17:42, 27:40-28:05)*
- Gerb and Castle traced the site to within a 7.5-mile radius of Emerson Dry Lake, northwest of 29 Palms and about 90 miles southeast of Edwards, inside FAA restricted airspace R-2501C. Gerb dates the event to 2-14 March 1997 (the video description says March, though his narration once says May) and guesses phase three of Hunter Warrior. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 14:18-14:25, 19:48-21:25, 48:14-48:28)*

### The craft

- Castle says five Marines in the Humvee all saw a black, equilateral triangle from the underside with a whitish-yellowish embedded spherical light at each rounded corner, possibly with venting or striations on the sides and a concave center, hanging at 150-200 feet and slowly turning counterclockwise. The lights did not cast light on the ground or the team. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 0:40-2:28, 18:03-19:48, 21:38-22:56)*
- Gerb's summary: about 200-300 feet across (he also says 300 feet), silent, no exhaust, seams, bolts or rivets, matte black or darker with no reflectivity, no wobble, unlike a Harrier or helicopter. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 23:20-24:09)*
- After roughly two minutes the triangle stopped rotating, rose at an angle and darted northwest at very high speed, with a slow buildup then rapid acceleration, "a very tiny light to nothing", and no noise, exhaust or heat signature. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 31:01-32:27)*

### The black team

- About 30 men in black uniforms, black army-style helmets, goggles or ski masks and black tactical gear, armed with submachine guns (Gerb later says they looked like MP5s, not M16s or M4s), with no insignia, name tags or markings and American accents; five or six matte-black F-150 to F-350 type trucks, some with canopies and antennas, 150-200 feet from the Marines. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 1:52-2:28, 24:09-25:04, 26:10-26:45, 1:46:19-1:46:34)*
- About 15 of them ran up the hill in V formations, barked "get your heads down", disarmed and held the Marines at gunpoint, with muzzles very close to their heads; Castle says the aim seemed to be that the Marines not look at anything. Castle says the whole event lasted three to four minutes. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 25:34-26:04, 27:11-28:19)*
- Castle guessed the team was in some kind of overwatch role with the craft. When it left, the team ran back down the hill, boarded their trucks and drove northwest, the same direction as the craft. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 30:38-31:01, 32:36-33:05)*
- The Marines' Humvee, a diesel, would not start for about five minutes; Gerb links this to [[kevin-knuth]]'s 2023 talk on UFO vehicle interference, which cites a 1983 automobile-interference typology (ASR "Mccell", probably McCampbell) and infers the black team's vehicles had countermeasures, perhaps Faraday-cage-like. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 33:37-35:40)*

### Aftermath

- Back at the Quonset hut, Castle's gunnery sergeant and a chief warrant officer waited at 7 am and told them it was "just flare activity" and, by implication, to say nothing; Gerb thinks those officers were not briefed and were relaying an instruction from the USAP chain. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 35:40-37:34)*
- The next day the group was given an anthrax booster, with a man with a rifle near the medical area and papers signed; Gerb says Castle had already been vaccinated about a year earlier and that the DoD mandate only began in December 1997. He notes that [[michael-herrera]] and [[jonathan-weygandt]] both reported anthrax boosters right after their encounters and calls it a data point he cannot ignore, while admitting it may be too conspiratorial. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 37:43-40:27)*
- Castle says nobody explicitly told him not to talk and he signed nothing about the incident; the silence was implied. *([gerb/castle](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-08-17 - 1997 ALIEN REPRODUCTION VEHICLE ENCOUNTER AT 29 PALMS, CA - THE TESTIMONY OF SGT RODERICK CASTLE.md>) 39:35-39:55)*
