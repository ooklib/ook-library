Per *Mogul of the Mob* as relayed by [[eyes-wide-open]] in `ewa/dallas-4`, a week-long December 1946 summit at Havana's Hotel Nacional, convened by [[meyer-lansky]] as the first mob summit of the postwar years.

## Claims

- [[lucky-luciano]], freshly freed and exiled to Sicily, was smuggled by Lansky to Havana. The ostensible reason was Frank Sinatra's arrival; the real purpose was reaffirming Luciano's place as top boss from Sicily. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 9:22-10:03)*
- Attendees named: Joe Adonis, Frank Costello (ASR "Castello"), Joe Profaci, Willie Moretti, Mike Miranda, Albert Anastasia, Joe Bananas, Vito Genovese. Each swore loyalty to Luciano and gave him an envelope of cash as compensation for his prison years. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 10:03-10:34)*
- Host speculation: the conference may have discussed the French heroin connection through Marseilles (Corsican-run labs; opium via Turkey and Lebanon), with much Marseilles dope reaching Cuba; many speculate Luciano oversaw it into the early 1950s. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 10:34-11:20)*
- Lansky was reportedly disturbed by plans to kill Bugsy Siegel, accused of embezzling mob funds from Las Vegas; Siegel was gunned down in June 1947, six months later. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 11:20-12:06)*
- "Most importantly", the supposed establishment of the [[national-crime-syndicate]] as an informal arrangement. Quoting the book: Luciano "again emphasized the philosophy he and Meyer had worked out back in the 20s. Avoid bad publicity and dissension in the ranks. No unnecessary wars." Host: this arrangement mostly held until at least the mid-1970s. *([ewa/dallas-4](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-12-05 - Meyer Lansky - Cuban Tabloid - The Road to Dallas Chapter 4.md) 12:06-13:05)*

## Relationships
