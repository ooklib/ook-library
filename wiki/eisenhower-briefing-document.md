The first Majestic document to surface: an alleged 18 November 1952 briefing of President-elect [[dwight-d-eisenhower]] on "Operation Majestic 12", received on undeveloped film by Jaime Shandera in 1984 and authenticated as far as possible by [[stanton-friedman]]. Gerb reviews it in the 2024 mj12 video. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 7:24, 5:21)*

## Claims (as described by Gerb)

- Labels Operation Majestic 12 a top secret R&D intelligence operation reporting solely to the president, set up by classified executive order of [[harry-truman]] on 24 Sept 1947 on the recommendation of [[vannevar-bush]] and [[james-v-forrestal]]; Truman's appointment log shows a Forrestal/Bush meeting that day. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 7:41, 8:04)*
- Lists all 12 members, including [[walter-bedell-smith]], who replaced the deceased Forrestal on 1 Aug 1950; Truman's log shows an off-record meeting with Smith that day. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 8:11, 8:27)*
- Recounts the Kenneth Arnold-era hysteria and the Roswell crash northwest of Roswell Army Airfield in July 1947, with craft and body recovery by Gen. [[nathan-twining]] and Bush; [[detlev-bronk]] allegedly autopsied bodies and coined "EBE" (extraterrestrial biological entity); Bronk and Donald Menzel concluded the craft was non-human. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 8:34, 8:52, 9:09)*
- Says the Roswell report led to Projects Sign, Grudge and [[project-blue-book]], with very limited contact with MJ-12; mentions writing on the craft (as claimed by Jesse Marcel) and the December 1950 El Indio/Guerrero crash near the Texas-Mexico border, referencing the Atomic Energy Commission and [[sandia-national-laboratories]]. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 9:17, 9:35, 9:57)*
- Names a contingency plan MJ-1 1949-56, also cited in the [[special-operations-manual-som1-01]]; the plan is not available. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 10:05, 36:56)*
- Friedman's checks: he found in 1989 at the National Archives numerous 18 Nov 1952 briefings for Eisenhower (none on MJ-12), and showed [[roscoe-hillenkoetter]] (briefing officer) was in New York in late 1952, contradicting a claimed 6-year Korea posting. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 10:26, 10:58)*
- Wood notes the copies are not "stunningly authentic" as originals and only film/photocopies exist. *([gerb/mj12](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-08-25 - The Majestic-12 Documents [With Ryan S. Wood].md>) 6:50)*
