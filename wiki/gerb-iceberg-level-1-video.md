[[uap-gerb]]'s 2024-01-12 video walks through level one (the "Observer" layer) of the Reddit "Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg" made by user Blyke-sama (ASR "Blake Sama"), the first of a promised six-part series. An iceberg meme ranks topics from best known at the top to most obscure at the bottom; Gerb says he did not make it and will say so when an entry is bogus or when he strongly agrees. Level one covers well-known material, so the video is a survey of opinions more than of new claims. All statements below are Gerb's, attributed. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 0:01-1:40)*

## Claims

- Gerb lists 20 entries in order: extraterrestrial life, [[crop-circles-claims]], [[mcminnville-ufo-photographs-1950]], [[alien-invasion-scenario]], [[panspermia]], [[men-in-black-claims]], [[nazca-lines-ancient-astronaut-claims]], [[betty-and-barney-hill-abduction-1961]], [[raelism]], [[kardashev-scale]], [[phoenix-lights-1997]], [[battle-of-los-angeles-1942]], [[reptilian-claims]], [[pentagon-ufo-videos-2017]], Area 51 (see [[groom-lake]]), [[nordic-aliens]], [[grey-aliens-claims]], [[little-green-men]], [[hoia-baciu-forest]], and [[washington-dc-ufo-incident-1952]]. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 0:00-38:44)*
- Gerb defines extraterrestrial life as any life, carbon or silicon, single-celled or a star-travelling civilization, that does not originate on Earth. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 1:40-2:14)*
- His stance on each entry: crop circles are human-made and uninteresting; an alien invasion is unlikely; the suited Men in Black are a hoax; the Nazca lines are religious; Little Green Men are only a politicians' dismissal; while McMinnville, Phoenix Lights, the Pentagon videos, the Hill case and the 1952 Washington sightings he treats as genuinely interesting. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 3:19-3:34, 7:05, 10:41-11:06, 12:41-12:58, 35:29-35:54)*
- He says the 1952 Washington sightings led directly to the decades-long UFO stigma and promises follow-up videos on the Condon Committee, Area 51, Roswell and that event. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 4:29-4:37, 28:37, 33:49, 38:33-38:41)*
- He plugs his first video, the [[wilson-davis-notes]] documentary. *([gerb/iceberg-1](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2024-01-12 - The Alien and UFO Obscure Oddities Iceberg (Level 1).md>) 1:16-1:30)*
