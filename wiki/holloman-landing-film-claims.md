Cameron recounts the Holloman Air Force Base landing story: three craft arrive, two leave, one lands near a hangar and a tall, northern-European-looking humanoid meets officials. He says Emenegger and Sandler were given Air Force footage that was taken back, and that the 8 seconds that appears in their documentary is misleading. Dates conflict: Emenegger said May 1971, while [[richard-doty]] and Linda Howe are said to place it in 1964.

## Claims

- Emenegger was dealing with Blue Book's Colonel Coleman and was allegedly given the Holloman film, which Cameron says inspired "Close Encounters of the Third Kind". *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:00:31-1:01:08)*
- Cameron says the Air Force had long tried to signal UFOs with fighter lights; he cites Lacatski and Walker as refusing to say whether communication occurred ("we promised not to tell"). *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:01:10-1:02:12)*
- The Defense Audiovisual Agency reportedly intercepted attempts to let Blue Book's Hynek see the film. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:05:35-1:05:52)*
- The 8 seconds in the documentary shows what looks like an F-4 in high-definition, which online debunkers used; Cameron says he initially disagreed. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:06:20-1:07:06)*
- Cameron reports Michael Luckman's claim that the entire film was a Pentagon-supplied hoax, because a storyboard artist drew the being from a description and the drawing matches a British Museum figure; Emenegger and Sandler may not have known it was fake. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:07:06-1:09:12)*
- Cameron still believes a real event occurred, that Sandler saw the true video, and that the storyboarded version was fed to them: [[disclosure-by-disinformation-claims]]. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:08:55-1:09:20)*
- Cameron says Emenegger's film crew filming at Holloman drew a base commander's refusal, with Major Verona and a CIA man acting as fixers. *([jm/cameron](<../raw/jm/Jesse Michels - 2026-09-15 - I Met The Head Of The Entire UFO Program! - Grant Cameron.md>) 1:02:49-1:03:42)*
