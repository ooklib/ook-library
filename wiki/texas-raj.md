"Texas Raj" is Russ Baker's phrase (via [[eyes-wide-open]]) for the new East Texas oil-independent moneyed class that came to dominate Texas civil and political life and, "a generation later," US politics under [[george-h-w-bush]] and his son. It is the concrete social group behind the "Cowboys" in the [[yankee-cowboy-thesis]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 0:00-0:29, 6:00-6:16)*

## Claims

- East Texas oil field: discovered October 30, 1930 by a man the ASR renders "Maran DOD Joiner" (surname Joiner) after failed crowd-funded attempts; per Bryce it was an anomaly in size and ownership, containing an ASR-stated "52 billion barrels" of clean crude (a third of all US crude produced to that time). It powered WWII and the postwar boom and became linked to US energy independence. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 3:10-4:32)* The earlier Permian Basin (Odessa, later Midland) had pushed Texas to top US producer. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 2:32-3:01)*
- Unlike the earlier oil majors ("Seven Sisters," Northeastern and British owned), East Texas was bought up by Southern wildcatters, creating independents who were "kings of the new Cowboy Coalition." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 4:41-6:00)*
- Three principals: [[clint-murchison]], [[sid-richardson]], [[h-l-hunt]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 6:00-11:37)*
- Oil depletion allowance: a federal tax break from 1913 (host's date; he also says it was 5% in 1927 and raised to 27.5% where it stood until 1969 (ASR garbles "275%"; flagged, the transcript is internally muddled on the original 5% date)), letting owners deduct up to 27.5% of well income, even for foreign wells if the company is US-incorporated. Critics were mostly Northeastern "Yankee" interests. The Texans backed any politician who defended it; as Truman and then [[john-f-kennedy]] criticized/threatened it, they drifted from the Democrats toward [[richard-nixon]] and the GOP. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 7:26-9:48)*
- Host: the independents were "largely excluded from the northeastern intelligence boom and the Yankee dominated CIA," so they built their own private intelligence network (centered on [[jack-crichton]], Murchison's Del Charro property and Washington lobbyist [[irving-davidson]]), until "they ran in with George Bush of the CIA." *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 15:04-15:30, 25:12-26:28, 25:36-25:43)*
- Cuba/Latin America was their first attempt at international expansion into "the Dominion of the Yankees" (Rockefellers, Morgans, duPonts, Mellons), via [[cuban-venezuelan-oil-voting-trust]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 23:32-24:40)*
- Citing Kirkpatrick Sale's "Southern Rim" concept, organized crime allegedly also benefited from the Southern Rim's ascendancy (tourism, real estate, gambling, narcotics from Mexico); see [[del-charro-set]]. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 29:39-31:26)*
- Host: what the Raj wielded in Washington through lobbyists, its members would "one generation later under the likes of George HW Bush" wield themselves. *([ewa/bush-2](../raw/ewa/Eyes Wide Open - 2025-02-16 - BUSH AND THE COWBOYS - CHAPTER 2.md) 44:34-44:50)*
