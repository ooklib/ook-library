Author, with Ruben Uriarte, of *The Other Roswell UFO Crash on the Texas Border* (2008), the main source on [[robert-willingham]]'s alleged 1955 sighting. Gerb spells the first name "Noah" in places; transcript spellings vary.

## Claims (gerb/del-rio)

- Torres says he holds over 50 documents proving Willingham's Air Force service, which Gerb has not seen. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 4:09-4:23)*
- The book has a specific chapter on alien bodies per Gerb's reading, yet quotes Willingham as never seeing bodies; a 2023 Torres presentation mentions bodies. Gerb says he contacted Torres for comment and highly recommends the book for showing there were two separate Del Rio incidents. *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 4:44-4:59)* *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 18:36-19:20)* *([gerb/del-rio](<../raw/UAP Gerb - 2024-05-17 - The 1950s Del Rio, Texas UFO Crashes.md>) 27:23-27:47)*
