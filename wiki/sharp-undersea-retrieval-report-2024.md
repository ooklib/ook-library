A Liberation Times article by [[chris-sharp]], first posted 20 September 2024 under the title "Paradigm changing UFO transparency legislation fails in Congress for second consecutive year", which per [[uap-gerb]] disclosed sourced claims about an undersea UFO retrieval program. Gerb credits it with kicking off his Navy investigation. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 6:16-6:33, 1:38:00-1:38:16)*

## Claims

- The article began as a report on the UAP Disclosure Act being dropped from the FY2025 NDAA, then pivoted to programs said to be orchestrated by the CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology and Directorate of Operations (including its maritime branch) with components of the NURO, US Navy, NRO, Space Command, NGA and JSOC, and to retrieval of objects from beneath the oceans with SOCOM and Woods Hole submersibles, and abroad with the CIA's Office of Global Access and JSOC. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 6:40-8:58)*
- By 22 September the article was revised to remove all mention of the Navy, NURO, Woods Hole, NGA, NRO and JSOC. Sharp answered accusations of "stealth edits" by saying the content was removed because of threats to him. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 9:15-9:58)*
- On 25 September Sharp restored and expanded the details, saying someone had "recklessly put me at extreme risk". The third version says Woods Hole provided deep submergence vehicles to support retrieval and that recovered craft are transferred to the [[office-of-naval-research]], which hands them to defense contractors for analysis. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 10:06-10:58)*
- Gerb's inference: CIA DS&T acts as logistics coordinator and JSOC as security and human intelligence, the NRO and NGA supply satellite signals intelligence, NURO supplies unknown underwater reconnaissance, and the Navy and contractors such as [[lockheed-martin]] (Gerb cites its DSRV and "dry submersible") do the retrieval. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 11:15-12:25)*
- Gerb adds his own thesis that a missing intermediary layer, FFRDCs and UARCs, sits between the government and contractors. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 12:25-13:19)*
- Gerb notes that since 2023 SOCOM has declared operational capability with Lockheed's dry submersible, which he finds intriguing given Sharp's claims. *([gerb/navy](<../raw/gerb/UAP Gerb - 2025-04-11 - US Navy UFO Crash Retrieval & Reverse Engineering Programs.md>) 1:31:03-1:31:25)*
